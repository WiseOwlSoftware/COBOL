## Entry 3129 — 2026-11-04 19:01 PST — kb/Work PB4117 decided: WiseOwl implements the asynchronous messaging facility (PB4700)

PB4117 asked the owner how to treat the message-tag operands of SEND and RECEIVE (§14.9.38.3 SR4, §14.9.31.3 SR1) while
the messaging facility is declined and USAGE MESSAGE-TAG is refused (COBOL1943). On 2026-11-04 the owner chose to
implement the facility. PB4117 is closed with that decision. The work is filed as PB4700, a MAJOR rejects-legal-source
defect, which asks for the design doc first, then USAGE MESSAGE-TAG, SEND and RECEIVE with their screens, and the
retirement of the declined-facility arms.
