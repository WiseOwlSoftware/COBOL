## PB4296: FUNCTION ABS is a 2002 function, and the catalog's introduction years are held to the older standards' own lists

**The defect.** `IntrinsicCatalog` windowed `FUNCTION ABS` at `IntroducedIn 2014`. ISO/IEC 1989:2002 Annex F.2 item 78 ("Intrinsic functions. New intrinsic functions are:") heads its list with ABS, its section 8.11 lists it, and section 15.6 is "ABS function"; the 2009 committee draft's Annex D.2 item 4 names eleven 2014 functions and ABS is not one. So `--std 2002` refused a legal 2002 program with COBOL1502, and at 85 the refusal named the wrong edition. The wrong year had spread: PB3173 moved inventory rows AR-15.7.3-1 and RV-15.7.4-1 to 2014 to match the catalog (its drift test trusts the catalog), FMT-15.7.2 recorded "2002 or 2014" as an open question, and PB3538, PB3951 and PB3953 each wrote a golden or a negative that assumed "ABS is a 2014 function".

**Re-probe.** On this branch's build, before the change: `cobol abs.cob --std 2002 --run` gave `error COBOL1502: FUNCTION ABS was introduced by ISO/IEC 1989:2014 (§15) — it requires --std 2014 or later (targeting COBOL-2002)`, rc 65; `--std 2014` printed `+3`.

**The fix.** The ABS row moved to the 2002 block of `IntrinsicCatalog.Build`. COBOL1502 reads `sig.IntroducedIn`, so at 85 it now says 2002 with no binder change. The three rows were re-banded `2002,2014,2023` through `record_verdicts` (verdicts unchanged, GAP 19 to 19). `pb3953_function_argument_int128_window` and `pb3951_length_of_numeric_function_result` now accept from 2002, their output byte-identical at 2002 (measured); `pb3538_zero_operand_precision` keeps 2014 because its other rule GR-11.9.11.2-1 is a 2014 rule (the suite generator refuses a 2002 accept). The two negatives were renamed `pb3953_function_argument_below_2002` and `pb3951_length_of_abs_below_2002` and refuse at 85 only.

**The structure, not just the row.** The 2002 and 2009 drafts are not tracked in git and the 2023 text carries no edition data below 2023, so nothing could have caught a mis-dated row. `tests/version-matrix/intrinsic-introductions.json` now commits each edition's new-function list with the passage it was read from, and `IntrinsicIntroductionDriftTests` holds `IntroducedIn` equal to it for all 94 clause-15 functions in both directions (a function with no list, a listed name with no catalog row, a name in two lists) and re-derives the 2023 list from Annex E.2 item 13 of `specs/ISO_COBOL.md` on every run. The 85 list is not a memory: the 2002 edition's section 8.11 (76 names) less its F.2 item 78 (34 names) is exactly the catalog's 42 rows windowed at 85. With ABS put back at 2014 the test fails with `ABS: IntrinsicCatalog says IntroducedIn 2014, the committed list of the standard says 2002`, so it can fail. Sibling sweep: every other row of the 94 agrees with its list.

**Self-review.** Architecture: one mechanism (the catalog column) now has one committed authority, and the guard sits beside the other catalog drift tests. Code: the `AbsScaled` summary still said "COBOL-2014+"; fixed. Duplication: no second copy of the windows; the test reads the catalog. Closed notes PB3538, PB3951 and PB3953 still say "ABS is a 2014 function" in their historical prose; they were left as the record of what they believed.
