## The units of unnumbered text: one segmentation for every edition, every unit pending, and the audit of their dispositions (kb/Work PB5449, first landing)

The rule catalogs held a row for every numbered rule and for the 18 PB284 paragraphs of record locking, and nothing for
the rest of the standard's unnumbered prose, its rule blocks' lead-ins and its tables. Decision PB5448 makes the margin
paragraph the unit of that text; this landing builds the mechanism (PB479 step 1) and changes no row.

**Re-probe.** Before the change `cite.py --check "§2023:8.4.2.1" "The same name shall not be used both as the name of an
external record"` answered only "the text is in the clause", the session probe printed 2023's 4,214 rules as the whole
denominator, and `prose_paragraphs` read §2023:8.4.2.1 as two paragraphs (no closing parenthesis after a stop) and cut
§2023:14.6.2.2's dash list from its lead.

**The mechanism.** `scripts/spec/text_units.py` segments a clause's unnumbered text: a sentence ends in a stop and any
closing brackets; an unterminated unit continues across a blank line into a lowercase or continuation-word opening (the
42 such breaks of the 2023 text are its case data); a colon unit owns its dash or lettered list and the list's closing
tail; a numbered prose item is a unit by its printed number; a NOTE stays inside its unit and a general format is none;
a table's units follow its kind (entries and matrix by body row, data whole). Units are keyed by clause, the digest of
their normalized words and their order. The ISO reader (`iso_units`, replacing `prose_paragraphs`) and the 1985 reader
(`rule_catalog_1985.units`) feed it; each manifest's `paragraph-blocks` now holds a disposition per unit, regenerated
by the extractor (a new unit enters pending, a pending entry that lost its unit leaves, a decided one stays for the audit
to refuse), with the 18 PB284 rows and 2002's 8.8.4.1.5.2 migrated from the positional `kind`/`kinds` maps.

**The audit.** It refuses a unit with no entry, an entry no unit has, a moved unit, a kind the clause does not take, a
`restates` naming no existing rule, a `not-a-rule` failing its class test (lead-in, cross-reference, data) or the verbal
forms (`scripts/spec/data/verbal-forms.json`) or the shape guard derived from every row's sentence openings, either one
without an UPHELD refuter record of its digest, per-class counts unequal, and (once a manifest sets
`not-rules-refuted`) a `not-rules` block without its record. Each refusal fires on planted data in
`scripts/selftests/spec/text_units/test_text_units.py` (10 cases, 46 expectations).

**What moved.** Every catalog's `rules` array regenerated byte-identical; each `outside-denominator` gained
`pending-units`: 2023 2,298, 2002 1,766, 1985 911. The session probe prints them as lower bounds. `cite.py` answers
`(unnumbered prose, pending)` for prose in no row (kb/Work PB222's repair) and reads `p<n>` and `Table <t> row <r>`;
`audit_catalog_coverage.py` treats as admitted only a clause whose every unit is a row. A prose item's `item <n>`
designator was tried and withdrawn: six existing citations use `item <n>` for lists that are no unit, and they are
re-pointed first.

**Not built yet** (PB5449 stays open): the 2023 margin-level measurement, the move batch and `record_verdicts.py split`
with the parent recheck, the `item <n>` designator, a 1985 unit's `box` and a 2002 unit's `candidate`. No rows
re-verdicted; `closes_rows: []`.
