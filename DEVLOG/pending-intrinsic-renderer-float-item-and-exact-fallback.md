## PB4622 float-item function values land exactly; the exact form gets its arbitrary-precision third form (PB4587, PB4592, PB4701, PB4702, PB4729), mixed selections keep a float's content (PB4730), TRIM of a computed operand (PB4710)

Wave 20261872, group A, one root: the intrinsic renderer and the exact form it settles on.

**PB4622 (MAJOR, wrong answer).** `MOVE FUNCTION ABS (DN) TO X5` stored 00000 where `MOVE DN TO X5` stores 73792, `FRACTION-PART (F)` landed .9999999996000000000 for .9999999995999999669, `COMPUTE R ROUNDED MODE IS PROHIBITED = FUNCTION ABS (A1)` took NOT ON SIZE ERROR where `= A1` raises. Re-probed on the build (`p4w.cob`, `p6.cob`): every row of the determination reproduced. Cause: `FunctionValueLane.Of`'s last arm and `IntrinsicRenderer.RenderNum` classified the binary64 a NON-floating-math function computes from a float ITEM as an engine approximation, and PB4516 lands every approximation by its 17-digit shortest decimal. Fix: one predicate, `IntrinsicCarrierRules.Binary64ResultIsApproximation`, asked by the binder and the renderer; a value derived from a float item is `Real` and not `Approximate`, lands through `FloatToScaled` (the landing `COMPUTE R = F` takes), enters the standard-decimal intermediate through the one operand lift (`DecOperand`), and `SendingValueTemp` marks a multi-receiver MOVE's intermediate an approximation only for `ReturnsApproximation`. Goldens `pb4622_float_item_function_value_exact` / `_prohibited` / `_standard_decimal` and the 85 refusal; the twelve claimed RV rows were re-witnessed and moved to `closes_rows`.

**PB4592, PB4587, PB4701, PB4702, PB4729, PB4730: the exact form gets its third form (owner decision of 2026-11-04, "Exact, lift the ban").** Six notes, one root: `CobolExact` fell to the operands' 34-digit images the moment a step left the 256-bit `CobolWide`, and five compile-time arms decided that fallback on a digit BOUND and not on the value. Re-probes (`pc/q1..q5`, `pbw/p1..p5`, `pd/rem2, rem3, g01`) reproduced every defect on the main build (99001, -999.0000, 0 for 1, `1.01 ** 119` ...852, REMD 000003000100000000, R2 0 for 7, MAX (DP ONE) 00000) and read the determinations' expected column on this one. The fix is `CobolBig` (new file): an arbitrary-precision integer at a scale, reached only where the wide operation fails and normalized back when the result fits, so `Add`, `Mul`, `DivTruncated`, `SubsidiaryQuotient`, `Pow`, `Compare`, `Image`, `ToUnscaled`, the SUM/RANGE/MEAN/MEDIAN/MIDRANGE/MOD/REM bodies and the integer-part members are exact at any size inside the published bound (|value| < 10^6145, 2^22 bits). It replaces the determinations' separate designs: PB4587's two-bound 512-bit routine (with a residue) is unnecessary, `PowNativeDec` and its round-to-odd chain are deleted, and `mode` left every exact operation. PB4701 lands the final-transfer quotient from the exact quotient (`QuotientAtScale`); PB4730 takes a float item as its exact expansion (`FromBinary64`) and makes MAX/MIN/MEDIAN return the selected argument whole; PB4729 hands a bound past 77 digits to `CobolExact` instead of the SDIDI. Goldens: `pb4592_exact_past_the_wide_form` (+ STANDARD-DECIMAL twin), `pb4587_native_power_reduced_once`, `pb4702_remainder_dividend_past_the_wide_form`, `pb4730_mixed_selection_keeps_a_float_items_content` (each with its 85 refusal), the WiseOwl case `pb4701_size_error_stores_the_exact_quotient_digits`, and `CobolBigExactFormTests` against a `BigInteger` oracle. Two old goldens pinned PB4622's defect and were re-derived (`pb77_move_past_the_carrier`'s CMP-ABS-X5 is 73792, `pb3829_random_fraction_rounded_store`'s FRACTION-PART is .9999999995999999669). Semgrep `wiseowl-cobol-no-biginteger` exempts the one file, counts unchanged.

**PB4710.** `TRIM (I2 + 1)` under `--permissive` compiled after the demoted COBOL1627 and died at run time in `StrArgVisitor.Visit(BoundComputedOperand)` (reproduced, `pd/t10.cob`). The coercion existed for a parenthesized item and for a numeric function's value, not for a compound arithmetic expression. The visitor now renders a binary, negated or power expression through `OperandText.AsString` (whose `NumericExprText` arm is widened to `BoundNegate` and `BoundPower`): the value's text, "3", "-2", "8". A unit test beside the PB4635 permissive tests holds it (the shape is refused in every strict edition, so no conformance golden can run it).

**Self-review.** Architecture: one mechanism per job (`CobolBig` only behind `CobolExact`; the approximation predicate asked by the binder and the renderer; the float-item lift is `DecOperand` alone).
