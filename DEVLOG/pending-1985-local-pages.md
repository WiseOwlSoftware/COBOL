## The five filtered 1985 pages converted with a local model

The API's output filter refused five pages of the 1985 standard: the approval notice on page 4 and glossary pages 122, 132, 137 and 142. The owner directed converting them with local tools. `local1985.py` sends the batch's page render and prompt to a local vision model through Ollama; no Anthropic model saw those pages. Of the three local models tried, qwen3.6:35b alone kept the glossary terms' underlining and left out the per-copy stamp (one model copied the stamp; its output was deleted). After the outer code fence was removed and one omitted page footer was restored, each page agrees with the PDF's OCR text layer at 99.3 % or better, and the rendered page confirms the one term the OCR layer lacks. The raw 1985 conversion now stands at 976 of 976 pages. The record is in kb/Work PB4812.
