## Entry 3134 — 2026-11-04 21:41 PST — PB4622, PB4587 and PB4592: three determinations become defects

Wave 20261870 determined three open analysis notes (read-only adjudicators, pin ccf466600, GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 as the second authority) and the register files the results. No compiler code changes in this entry.

PB4622 (value-preserving functions over a float item) is a MAJOR silent wrong-answer defect. The deciding citation is `OK  §15.4.1   (Numeric and integer functions)`, read with §15.59.4 1), §15.63.4 1), §15.61.1 and §15.44.4 1): the standard fixes these values exactly, the owner's 2026-11-04 ruling on PB4569 closes the native approximation latitude for them, and GnuCOBOL agrees. The defect is the classification: `FunctionValueLane.Of` and `IntrinsicRenderer.RenderNum` call the binary64 of a non-floating-math function over a float item an approximation, so `MOVE FUNCTION ABS (DN) TO X5` stores 00000 where `MOVE DN TO X5` stores 73792. The note claims twelve RV rows, and it gained the determination and the owed fix (one predicate on `IntrinsicCarrierRules`). New notes: PB4730 (a mixed list such as `MAX (DP ONE)` holds a float item's content to 34 digits), PB4731 (an analysis: does the exactness ruling reach D16's binary64 arithmetic on float items), PB4732 (an upstream candidate: GnuCOBOL's lossy double-to-decimal intake, 49088 for 73792, unreported and unposted).

PB4587 (a native power past the 77-digit wide form) is a MINOR silent wrong-answer defect. The deciding citation is `OK  §8.8.1.3   (Native arithmetic)`, with §11.9.11.2 1) and §A.1 123): native evaluation is the implementor's, §8.8.1.5.4 2) e)'s chain binds STANDARD-DECIMAL only, and GnuCOBOL's power is exact. The owed value is the exact power reduced once by round-to-odd, not the `MulToOdd` chain, which drifts 2 to 8 units in the 34th digit (`1.01 ** 119` stores ...852 for ...851). The note's "...0124" was the nearest-even rounding; the owed value ends ...0125. Siblings S1 to S4 (the wide base, the negative exponent, the wide product, the scaled base) are in the fix. No owner question.

PB4592 (a value past 77 digits keeps a 34-digit residue) is a MINOR silent wrong-answer defect. The deciding citation is `OK  §8.8.1.3   (Native arithmetic)`: the standard fixes no native value, GnuCOBOL is exact at every size, and widening past 256 bits conflicts with DESIGN §1.2 invariant 2's `BigInteger` ban. Owner decision (2026-11-04): asked whether a native intermediate past 77 digits should be computed exactly as GnuCOBOL does, which lifts that ban for one fallback in `CobolExact.cs`, or keep the 34-digit rounding and document the residue, the owner answered "Exact, lift the ban". The note records the exact arm: an arbitrary-precision third form in `CobolExact.cs`, used only where the 256-bit form fails, with the ban and the semgrep rule `wiseowl-cobol-no-biginteger` narrowed to that file. Siblings S2 to S5 are in the fix, and S6 (the power) belongs to PB4587. New note: PB4729 (the compile-time wide lane falls back on the digit bound, not the value: `A*A*A*A + 1 - A*A*A*A` with A = 10^19 + 1 stores 0 for 1), decided without the owner.
