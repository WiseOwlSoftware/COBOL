## PB4641: a native final-transfer quotient of two SDIDI values is rounded once, at the receiver

Re-probe: `COMPUTE R ROUNDED = 9.999999999999999999999999999999999E-1 / 2.0E0` stored 1 and a truncating `PIC V9(30)` receiver stored .5000..., where the exact quotient .49999999999999999999999999999999995 (a tie at the 35th digit) is below .5. The native lane's `DecBinary` "/" called `CobolDec.Div` at the program's INTERMEDIATE ROUNDING mode (nearest-even took the tie up to .5 at 34 digits) and the receiver rounded again, against DOC-A.1-123's single rounding.

Fix: `CobolDec.DivFinal` reduces the quotient to 34 digits by round-to-odd (PROHIBITED keeps its screening, §2023:11.9.11.2 GR3d), emitted through `RuntimeApi.DecDivFinal` for every non-STANDARD-DECIMAL final-transfer quotient. STANDARD-DECIMAL keeps `Div`: its quotient is an SDIDI value. A nested quotient (`Divide`) and the exact-operand quotient (`ExactFinalQuotient`) already rounded once, so the sweep found no further arm. Golden `pb4641_final_transfer_quotient_of_sdidi_operands` (2002+, refused at 85). DOC-A.1-123 carries the determination. No inventory row closes. Self-review (architecture, code, performance, duplication): one kernel for one rule; no new allocation on the path.

Review round 1 (R1-1): a floating-point edited receiver may hold 33 to 36 digits (2023:13.18.40.3 SR15), where round-to-odd at 34 digits no longer preserves the receiver rounding, so DecBinary keeps `Div` for a FloatEdited receiver. Golden `pb4641_float_edited_receiver_quotient` (2.0E0 / 9.0E0 into 33- and 34-digit receivers). R1-2: citation corrected to §2023:11.9.11.2 GR3d. The FloatEdited bypass is narrowed (R2-1) to a receiver whose mask significand holds 33 or more digits (ReceiverContext.FloatSignificandDigits); a narrower float-edited receiver takes the one-rounding DivFinal.
