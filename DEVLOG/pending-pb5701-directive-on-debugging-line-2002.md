## PB5701: a compiler directive on a debugging line is refused at --std 2002

**PB5701.** ISO/IEC 1989:2002 clause 7.2.2 SR8 d) (cite.py OK on the SR8 lead-in) forbids a compiler directive on a debugging line. The re-probe on the build reproduced the note: `D>>DEFINE VV AS 1` gave only COBOL0903 and the program printed NOT DEFINED. The debugging line is carried by `LogicalLineBuilder.EmitDebugging`, the one place the fixed `D` line and the floating `>>D` line (either reference format) pass through; nothing asked whether the carried text was a directive. It now does (`CheckNoDirective`, which leaves a fixed D line whose text begins `>>D` to COBOL5770), reporting COBOL8092 through `ReferenceFormatDiagnostics.OnDirectiveOnDebuggingLine`, at 2002 only: the 2014 text has no debugging-line rule and 2023 has no D indicator, and 85 has no directive. Probes: 2002 refuses both spellings; 2014 keeps COBOL0902 only. SR8 items b) and c) were probed at 2002 and are already refused (COBOL2697; COBOL2696/COBOL5563).

Goldens: `_unassigned/pb5701-directive-on-debugging-line`, `_unassigned/pb5701-floating-directive-on-debugging-line` (refused at 2002, diagnostics JSON); unit test `LogicalLineBuilderTests.DirectiveOnADebuggingLine_IsRefusedAt2002` (105 pass in the class). Row `2002-SR-7.2.2-8` re-verdicted CONFORMS (batch pb5701-sr8-2002, GAP 1137 to 1136).
