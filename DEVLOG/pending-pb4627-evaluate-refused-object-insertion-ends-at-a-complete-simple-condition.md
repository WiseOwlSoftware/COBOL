## A refused or surplus EVALUATE object judges its bare operands and abbreviated relations by where the insertion ends

PB4627 (the lead of PB4606's review): `ConditionBinder.BindBareOperandStandalone` bound every plain value in a refused (Table 15, COBOL1634) or surplus (SR2, COBOL2106) EVALUATE partial-expression object as the object of the inserted subject's relation. ISO 8.8.4.12.4 GR1 ends the insertion "once a complete simple condition is encountered within a complex condition", so `WHEN > 1 AND C88 AND 5` has a `5` that is no object and no condition either. The same condition in an IF draws COBOL2318; in a refused pair the verdict about the pair hid it.

Re-probe (branch build, before): `WHEN > 1 AND S88 AND 5`, `WHEN NUMERIC AND 5`, `WHEN > 1 AND NOT S88 AND 5`, `WHEN > 1 AND (S88) AND WS-X` drew COBOL1634 alone; the surplus `EVALUATE N WHEN 1 ALSO > 1 AND S88 AND 5` drew COBOL2106 alone. The sibling sweep of the same walk found three more hidden diagnostics with the same root: `WHEN NUMERIC AND > 1` (COBOL2552), `WHEN > 1 AND TRUE` (COBOL2318) and a bare address-identifier after a complete simple condition (COBOL2318).

Fix: the walk carries `StandaloneInsertion` (insertion in flight, boolean relation, a leading class-name allowed), the part of `AbbrevCarry` a walk with no subject operand can keep. The leading relational shape and every stated relation turn it on; a condition-name, switch-status, boolean operand, class or sign shape, a parenthesized group and a truth word turn it off. Judged by it, a bare value is the object of the carried relation while the insertion lasts and otherwise goes through `RefuseNonCondition`; an abbreviated relation without it reports COBOL2552, and after a boolean relation COBOL2723. The three reports (COBOL2552, the truth word and the address-identifier as a condition) are extracted from `BindAbbreviatedRelation`, `BindPrimary` and `BindComparison` into one member each, so the walk and the bound condition cannot word a rule twice. `docs/CONDITIONS_EXCEPTIONS_DESIGN.md` D5a carries the paragraph.

Goldens: `pb4627-evaluate-refused-pair-value-after-condition-name` (COBOL2318) and `pb4627-evaluate-refused-pair-abbreviated-relation-after-class-condition` (COBOL2552), refused at 85, 2002, 2014 and 2023; `EvaluateObjectOperandFaultTests` gained 23 refused-pair rows (4 over a class-name) and 2 surplus rows, including the controls that must keep drawing the Table 15 verdict alone (`> 1 AND N = 2 AND 5`, `> 1 AND 5`). No inventory row is claimed or closed.
