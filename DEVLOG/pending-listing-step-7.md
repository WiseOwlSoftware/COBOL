## PB5925: the source listing, step 7a - every name slot of the grammar carries a classifying label

Step 7 of `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-listing.md` (kb/Work PB5925; the owner's order of 2026-11-18 to build the full listing)
is the cross-reference: every use of every name, classified defined, read, written or modified. Its occurrences are recorded by
the one `SymbolTable.Identify` that step 6b makes the only way from a written name to a symbol, and step 6b is not built, so
step 7 is split (kb/Work PB5932's dated file `2026-11-18-step-7-split`). This entry is step 7a, the half that stands on nothing
6b changes: the grammar now says, at every place the programmer can write a name, what the name does there, and a drift test
computed from the parser holds it. Step 7b, the occurrences and the cross-reference section, follows step 6b.

**What was built.**
- `GrammarNameSlotDriftTests` (Unit), written first and red (commit 224a9f2e27: 269 rules, 452 unlabelled slots). Its counter,
  `GrammarNameSlots`, reads `CobolParserCore._ATN`: the name rules are the closure from `cobolWord` and `reservedGatedWord`
  (21), the reference rules the rules some path of which begins with a slot (36, `procedureName` and `dataReference` among
  them), and each rule's slot transitions are counted against the classified label fields of its generated context classes.
  Nothing lists a slot or a rule. A fixture grammar, `NameSlotFixture.g4`, generated at test time with the vendored ANTLR jar
  and compiled in memory, shows each of five planted ways a slot can hide reported at its slot.
- The labels: 452 slots in the 11 parser grammar files, labelled 281 `ref`, 73 `nn`, 64 `def`, 25 `name` and 9 `qual`, each a
  prefix, a name and an ordinal (`refDataReference1=dataReference`, `defProcedureName1=procedureName`). Removing them from each
  file gives the previous text byte for byte; the emitted C# snapshots (Characterization, 37 of 37) are unchanged.
- `tests/_shared/GrammarSource.cs`: the label's one form and the grammar's text without labels. Nine Unit classes (13 facts)
  that read a rule's shape with regular expressions went red on the labels and now read through it; the diagram generator
  drops a label when it cleans a fragment.

**Corrections the code proved** (marked "(step 7a)" in the design): "a rule that holds slots and other elements" made every
statement rule a reference rule, so a reference rule is one some path of which BEGINS with a slot; a token beside a name that
names nothing (FILLER, NUMERIC, a literal kind) is `nn` even in a name rule's body, and a use-site label overrides only an inner
`ref` head; labels are `=`, never `+=` (a list in every context of every parse); the fixture is generated by the test, not by
the product's generator; an FD's or SD's file-name is a reference to its SELECT declaration (§2023:13.4.5.3 SR1, §2023:13.4.6.3
SR1) and the data-name of a CAPACITY phrase a definition (§2023:14.9.39.3 SR29); the design's "257 `cobolWord` occurrences"
are 452 slots. DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md section 3.17 states the convention for every later grammar change.

**Measured** (DESIGN-listing.md section 9): the labels are fields of the parser's context classes, so they cost every parse. Two runs of `CompileBenchmarks.FrontEndAndBind` on the branch and two on its parent, alternating: with no listing the branch allocates a fixed amount more on both runs, about +0.3 MB (0.4 to 0.5 %) on IX113A and +0.2 MB on NC218A, where the parent repeats itself within 0.1 MB. The budget of section 9 now names the label fields beside the line-kind arrays (the README's step-7a table).
