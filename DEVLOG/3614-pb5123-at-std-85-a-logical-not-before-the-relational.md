## Entry 3614 — 2026-11-14 11:12 PST — PB5123: at --std 85 a logical NOT before the relational operator of an abbreviated relation (A > B AND NOT >= C OR D) parses and negates the relation; from 2002 it is COBOL0902

**The defect.** §1985:VI.6.3.3 reads the NOT of an abbreviated combined relation condition by the word after it: before GREATER, >, LESS, <, EQUAL or = it is part of the relational operator (GRL2.1); otherwise "The NOT is interpreted as a logical operator and, therefore, the implied insertion of subject or relational operator results in a negated relation condition" (GRL2.2). So at 85 `A > B AND NOT >= C OR D` is ((A > B) AND (NOT (A >= C))) OR (A >= D). The grammar admitted NOT after a connective only inside `comparisonOperator` (the 2023 printed set, kb/Work PB1034) or before a bare object, so the 1985 form was `COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near '>='` at every edition, and `AND NOT IS > C` was COBOL0001 near 'IS'.

**Re-probe (base b4339bf403).** The note's program at `--std 85`: COBOL0001 at lines 62, 69, 76; the same at 2002 and 2023. With the fix: T1 TRUE … T10 FALSE, as the note derives.

**The fix.** `succeedingCondition` gains a middle alternative, `{logicalNotBeforeRelationalOperator()}? negatedAbbreviatedRelation`, with `negatedAbbreviatedRelation : NOT abbreviatedRelation`. The left-edge predicate (CobolParserCoreBase) is GRL2.1 itself, plus NOT after NOT, which no reading admits, so the new alternative never competes with the `comparisonOperator` alternatives that open on NOT. `ConditionBinder.BindCondition` binds it as `BoundNot` over the one `BindAbbreviatedRelation`, so the stated operator is the one the insertion carries on. ISO 2002 prohibited the form (§2002:F.1 item 22) and the 2014/2023 formats have no NOT prefix to a relational operator, so the grammar stays the superset and `VersionConformancePass.VisitNegatedAbbreviatedRelation` refuses it from 2002 with COBOL0902 through the new construct `abbreviated-not-logical-removed-2002` (a warning under `--permissive`, the 85 meaning kept). `SearchAllFormat2Rules.TryConjuncts` names it an abbreviated combined relation, as it does the plain one. Design doc (`docs/CONDITIONS_EXCEPTIONS_DESIGN.md`) and VCR row 7.73 updated; `PartialExpressionSpineDriftTests` pins the new `succeedingCondition` body and `negatedAbbreviatedRelation` as a wrapper of the one `abbreviatedRelation`.

**Goldens.** `cases/VI.6.3@1985-CONDITIONAL-EXPRESSIONS/pb5123_logical_not_before_relop` (85: T1-T13 over NOT >=, NOT <= and NOT IS >, each leg failing if the form is refused or the negated operator is carried) and the existing refusal `cases/8.7.5-Relational-operators/pb1034-abbreviated-not-gteq`, the same program, which pinned COBOL0001 at every edition and now expects COBOL0902 at 2002, 2014 and 2023 (gate run 000004 caught it at all four editions; a duplicate refusal this branch had added beside it was deleted).
