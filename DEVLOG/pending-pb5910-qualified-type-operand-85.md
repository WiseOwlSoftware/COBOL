## PB5910: at 1985 a qualified TYPE CONTROL HEADING / CONTROL FOOTING operand is refused; the code reads of slices 1985-250 and 1985-251

The Mythos lane's rulings on read pass 21 (batch 23, `kb/Work/PB5683/2026-11-17-rulings-code-reads-batch-23.md`) upheld the refuter's doubt on `1985-SR-XIII.3.20.3-4` as a defect of the FORMAT row. `§1985:III.2` says a general format's data-name "must not be reference-modified, subscripted, or qualified unless specifically permitted by the rules of the format". The CONTROL clause permits qualification (`§1985:XIII.3.7.3 SR1`); the printed TYPE clause (folio XIII-55) does not. Yet `TYPE CH WS-KEY OF WS-GRP` compiled and ran at `--std 85` (qualneedless.cob, chqual.cob). kb/Work PB5573 had admitted it by a reading recorded while the folio was missing; a dated file beside that note now says the printed page replaces the reading, and why. The batch also withdrew the `§1985:IV.4.3.8.1` basis the row's previous record gave.

The fix is in `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.GateCobol85ControlName`, the one place that holds a control name's 1985 shape. It now takes each clause's answer to qualification: the CONTROL operand may be qualified, the TYPE operand may not. A qualifier on the TYPE operand at 85 is COBOL0900 under the new construct row `report-type-operand-qualification-2002` (VCR row 7.45 (h)). `§2002:13.16.55.2 SR10`, `§2014:13.18.57.2 SR10` and `§2023:13.18.57.3 SR10` each permit "qualified and reference modified", so the later editions are unchanged; 2002 is frozen and got no verdict or case.

New negatives at 85: the qualified TYPE operand (OF and IN), the subscripted one (COBOL0899, as at every edition) and an unqualified reference-modified one. The positive `pb5573_qualified_control_name_85` had qualified its TYPE operands. It is rewritten to qualify only the major CONTROL operand and to name the unique minor control plain in the TYPE clause; its output is derived from `§1985:XIII.3.20.4 GR4`, `GR6` and `GR13a`. Compiling all 931 REPORT SECTION programs in the conformance cases and the NIST suite at 85 found three that draw the new diagnostic: the new negative; `pb5573-type-control-ref-mod-85`, already refused, which now gets a second COBOL0900 for its qualifier; and `g2-control-forms-cd`, which is accepted at 2002 only. No NIST program is newly refused. `1985-FMT-XIII.3.20.2` was re-recorded CONFORMS in one `record_verdicts --edition 1985` batch.
