## PB5100 (interim): a wave runs at most 3 implementers, the gate-slot count

Wave 20261979 ran 11 groups against 3 gate slots, and the 1985 placement gate waited 42 minutes in the queue behind
them. Until PB5100 derives a wave's concurrency from the free gate slots, `model_rules.json` sets `wave.concurrency`
to 3, the slot cap, so no wave starts more implementers than can gate at once (Mythos's decision under the owner's
delegation, PB5112). The workstream skill's argument lists name the setting instead of a number.
