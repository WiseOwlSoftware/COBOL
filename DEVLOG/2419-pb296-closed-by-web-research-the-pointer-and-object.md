## Entry 2419 — 2026-10-23 14:37 PDT — PB296 closed by web research: the pointer and object-reference placement rules are unchanged since 2002; two edition edges and a re-derivation lead filed

An Opus agent read the archived COBOL committee texts to settle whether the three §13.18.60.3 placement rules (SR14, SR15, SR4) were worded the same before 2023. The sources were the FCD 1989:2001 text (P1), the post-ballot 2001-12-04 text of ISO/IEC 1989:2002 (P2) and the 2009-08-23 committee draft of the 2014 line (P3), checked against 2023 Annex E.2. The rules are unchanged in substance, so PB296 closes with the ungated posture confirmed. The research also showed that the CONSTANT RECORD clause is absent from P1 and P2, so its edge is 2014 (PB3600), and that `POINTER TO type-name` is in P2, so its edge is 2002 (PB3601). PB3602 files the re-derivation of the 45 provisional pre-2023 edges from the same three texts.
