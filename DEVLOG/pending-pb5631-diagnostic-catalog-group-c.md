## PB5631, PB5630, PB5538, PB5641, PB5642: five diagnostic mechanisms fixed at their roots; PB5554 re-probed and left open

Wave 20262044 group C. Each note was re-probed on the branch's build before any change.

**PB5631 (SD before FD).** `BindFileSection` bound every FD before any SD, so an SD that came first in source was reported as the repeat while the FD that followed was the entry kept. `FirstDescriptionPositions` now finds each file-name's first description in source order across both kinds, and `DescriptionRepeated` refuses any other entry under COBOL4250. Three refusal goldens (`pb5631-sd-then-sd`, `-fd-then-sd`, `-sd-then-fd`) pin the mixed orders; each reports line 22, the later entry. The COBOL4250 doc comment names the file-name case as decided outside the declaration funnel.

**PB5630 (subordinate group of a USAGE IS INDEX group, 85).** `Level85Forbidden` walked every ancestor. §1985:VI.5.14.4 GR1 gives a group-level USAGE to the elementary items, so the operand's own entry is what GR3 of §1985:VI.6.4.3 asks about. A golden accepts a subordinate group at 85 (ADD, MOVE and SUBTRACT CORRESPONDING); the existing refusal of the group that itself carries the clause still passes. The PB5105 entry's remark that both goldens carry a legal control line is history: the older golden has none, and the new golden is that control.

**PB5538 (ENTER drew two errors at 2002+).** `ConstructRegistry.GateErrorCodeAt` is the quiet twin of the gate (the code it reports when that report is an error). `BindEnter` stays silent through `BoundRejected.ByEditionGate` when it answers and keeps COBOL7240 at 85 and under `--permissive` (the gate only warns there, and the statement is not provided at any edition). The strict version-matrix theory now fails a declined row that draws its decline from the removal edition on.

**PB5641 (table SORT subject at 2002).** §2002:14.8.36.2 SR13 requires the rightmost subscript of a nested table subject to be ALL. New COBOL7369, raised by `SubscriptBinder.ScreenTableSubjectRightmostAll` below COBOL-2014 (2014 and 2023 let the subject omit its own level). `pb1175_table_sort_shared_storage` (accept 2002) used the omitted spelling and now writes `E(3, ALL)`. Goldens: a 2002 accept with ALL, a 2014 accept with the omission, a 2002 refusal. Whether SEARCH has a twin rule at 2002 was not examined and is a lead in the note's addendum.

**PB5642 (float literal in the composite at 2002).** `CheckComposite` excluded a floating-point literal at every edition. §2014:14.7.6 rule 2 added the exclusion (the 2014 Annex lists it as a change from the previous standard); §2002:14.6.6 rule 2 does not exclude it. Below 2014 the literal counts by the digits of its significand. The 36-digit `ADD 1.234567890123456789E0 TO X` is refused COBOL0805 at 2002 and still runs at 2014 and 2023. Two 2002 rows (ADD, DIVIDE) and the SORT row are recorded CONFORMS in the 2002 store (GAP 2517 to 2514).

**PB5554, re-probed, left open.** `MOVE FUNCTION ... (B / Z)` is a 2002 construct and is refused at 85 before any arithmetic; the COMPUTE form already continues. The reference-modifier half still ends the run unit and needs a value for the leftmost position that no 1985 text gives. **PB5628, PB5579 and PB5578** were not worked in this change (PB5628 needs the adjudication its note names; the other two keep rows claimed for later 2002 twin tests).
