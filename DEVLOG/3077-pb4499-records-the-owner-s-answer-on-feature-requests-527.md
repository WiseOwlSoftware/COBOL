## Entry 3077 — 2026-11-04 08:33 PST — PB4499 records the owner's answer on feature-requests #527

Simon Sobisch had asked whether OMITTED belongs in GnuCOBOL's COBOL-85 word list. On 2026-11-04 the owner answered on the ticket that it does, because of `LABEL RECORDS ARE OMITTED`, which ISO/IEC 1989:2002 deleted (NIST OBNC1M cited). The reply also said that OMITTED as a CALL argument came with the 2002 program prototypes, and that without the 1985 text this rests on IBM's and HP's documentation. PB4499's Upstream state section now records the reply.
