## PB5256: the 2002 rule catalog and inventory, read by the one ISO extraction

**Mechanism.** ISO/IEC 1989:2002 had a transcription (PB4813) and no catalog: `extract_rule_catalog.py --edition 2002` exited "no rule catalog arm", and the schema held no 2002 store, so 2002 had no GAP count. Measured first, the 2023 arm read the 2002 text unchanged into 2,990 rows with 13 findings, every one a fact of 2002's structure or a defect of its transcription. So the 2023 arm became the ONE ISO extraction (`extract`) over a table of per-edition facts (`ISO`, `IsoEdition`: a rule-bearing annex, the implementor-defined list's annex, the excluded and known-empty blocks, the conventions clause, the table of contents' form, the row form, reconciled or not). The 1985 reader stays its own (Roman sections, modules, boxes, amendments) and keeps sharing the kind table, heading rules, content-keyed scan and standing lists. The 2023 and 1985 catalogs regenerate byte-identical.

**What 2002 reads.** 3,290 rows from 548 blocks: SR 1,168, GR 1,361, AR 142, RV 142, DOC 184 (Annex B.1), FMT 293; Annex A (the communications facility) 117 of them. Each row carries `2002-<kind>-<clause>-<n>`, its `citation` (`§2002:14.8.25.2 SR1`) and PDF `page`. Standing (§2002:G.1, G.2): 11 archaic, 129 obsolete. Parse findings, recorded in the catalog for the reconciliation: 6 (unrecognised-heading 4: §2002:8.8.1.3.1.3 "Rounding rules" and three "Rules" under §2002:8.8.4.1, which wait for the manifest's adjudication; unexplained-ordinal 1: a table cell of §2002:15.16.2 wrapped to read "99."; flattened-tail 1: §2002:14.8.1.3 GR25 c) 2. at column 0). An ordinal no list explains is no longer a guessed row in any edition. The content-keyed manifest measures 92 clauses (395 ordinals); none is word for word a 2023 manifest clause, so all are pending, each with its nearest 2023 candidate.

**Inventory and lineage.** The schema holds 2002 (`tests/version-matrix/inventory-2002`, 1985's fields) as held but not linked (`linked: false`), so 1985's 1,937 KEPT and CHANGED records keep naming 2023 and the EDITION GATES audit spans no edge at 2002; Python, C# and edition_gates read the flag. The probe prints `2023 4214 rules · 0 GAP | 2002 3290 rules · 3290 GAP | 1985 2274 rules · 689 GAP`. `lineage.py --candidates` pairs 1,758 of the 3,290 rules with 2023 rules of the same construct, kind and text, erasing typography only (1,732 exact, 26 normalized, 2 with two candidates); 1,532 are unpaired. No verdict is recorded.

**Branch review round 1 (8 findings, all fixed).** The pairing's normalization is defined (typography only; never a bracket, brace, bar, ellipsis, underline, word, case or number): `cite.norm`'s words-only key had paired 2002's `{character-1} ...` with 2023's `[character-1] ...`, and 197 of 223 normalized pairs fell out. A change record carries its targets' digests (`next.digests`, stamped by `record_verdicts.py`; 1985's 1,937 records stamped, nothing else in them changed), so a reconciliation that inserts a rule into READ marks the 27 records whose target text moved STALE by name; and an edition is linked only once its reconciliation ledger records every page of its PDF (`verify_publishable.reconciled`, `inventory_schema.link_errors`). The gate's RULE CATALOGS, LINEAGE CANDIDATES and CATALOG COVERAGE audits (and CI's) read the held editions from the schema. The probe and the ledger print 2002's count as a lower bound (92 clauses pending adjudication, 4 headings untyped). `cite.norm` strips the underline tag only (`<for classes>` in §2023:3.103 is content). CONFORMANCE.md's two "no 1985 text" sentences now say where the held editions are listed and that the two determinations are still to be read against them.

**Found on the way.** `citation.iso_regions` read Annex A's preamble as §2002:16.1.2.1 (an annex title now opens its own region), and `cite.norm` kept the `u` of `<u>` as a word, so no quotation of two underlined words could pass.

**Merged main after records-85d and the 2014 transcription.** The 1985 store merged clean and needed no restamp: records-85d changed no record's `next`, and each of its 128 records is main's plus this branch's `next.digests` (all 1,937 digests current against the 2023 view). PB5268's split dash lists in the 2002 transcription staled the 2002 catalog (regenerated, 10 lines) and showed that the pairing kept the transcription's markup: a Markdown hard break and a character entity are now typography (`lineage.pairing_text`), so 2002 to 2023 pairs 1,771 rules (1,730 exact, 41 normalized), none lost. `spec_pdf`'s 2014 row gains its page count, 952. A records branch writes 1985 change records through `record_verdicts.py --edition 1985`, which stamps the digests, and INVENTORY VIEW refuses one without them (kb/Work PB5256 addendum).

**Proof.** `extract_rule_catalog.py --self-test` (a planted page and a planted correction: ids stable where clause and number did not change), `--edition 2002 --check` 0, `--check` and `--edition 1985` byte-identical; `cite.py --check` on 284 sampled 2002 citations over 236 clauses, 284 OK; self-tests of citation, cite, element_standing, inventory_schema, inventory_store, lineage, rule_catalog_1985, spec_pdf, verify_publishable, edition_gates, test_audit_batch green; `inventory_view.py --check` and `edition_gates.py --check` GREEN.
