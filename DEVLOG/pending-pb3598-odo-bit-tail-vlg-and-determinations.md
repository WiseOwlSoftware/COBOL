## PB3598 closes: a variable-length group's receiving store keeps the implicit filler past an ODO bit table's maximum, and the two questions the first landing left to the owner are answered by the standard

PB3598 registered three shapes where the implicit filler of a USAGE BIT run is not stored. The first landing (entry 2422) stored shape 3 and the half of shape 1 that lies past the table's maximum extent, and left shape 2 and an "owner question" open. This change closes the note. No inventory row is claimed or closed.

**Re-probe.** Shape 3 and the stored half of shape 1 still held on a fresh build. Shape 1 did not hold in a variable-length group (a group with a dynamic-length item): `MOVE S TO R` of two groups `DS PIC X DYNAMIC LENGTH LIMIT 5`, `B PIC 1(2)`, `T PIC 1(3) OCCURS 1 TO 4 DEPENDING ON N`, the sender at count 3 with filler 10 past the maximum extent, left the receiver's filler 00 (second character 0x60, 0x62 required). `OdoTail.KeepUnused`, the splice every variable-length receiving store asks (the declared group's `FromVarImage`, a cell-backed group's `StoreVarGroupAt`, the CALL carrier), kept the receiver's bits from the first unused occurrence to the END of the member, so the stored tail was dropped at every count below the maximum.

**Fix.** `KeepUnused` keeps the receiver's bits from the first unused occurrence to the end of the table at its maximum extent (`CobolTable.TrimmedExtent`) or of the receiver's current characters, whichever ends first. The filler past the maximum that lies in the characters the receiver uses is the sender's, as it is at a count equal to the maximum; the characters past the current extent are the receiver's own. Basis: §14.9.25.4 GR9 a) moves "each character position that is not occupied by a corresponding table", and the filler past a table's maximum occupies none.

**The open question dissolves.** The bits that end the current extent's last character at a count below the maximum were called both filler and unused occurrence. They are the occurrence's: §8.5.1.6.3 generates the filler after "the last data item in a record", the table at its maximum; their content "is undefined" (§13.18.38.4 7)); §13.18.38.4 8) a) leaves them out of the operation. The two receiving stores differ there only where the standard leaves the content undefined, which is implementor latitude (a group with no dynamic-length item or dynamic-capacity table is a fixed-length group, §8.5.1.12.1, so its move is GR4's alphanumeric move of whole characters; a variable-length group moves by GR9 a), and an OCCURS DEPENDING table is no corresponding table, §8.5.1.12.2, so GR9 a) moves every character position as GR4 does; the keep-the-receiver's-bits choice dates from PB3030), and both conform. The first draft of this entry, the code comment and the golden header called that choice GR9 a)'s and §14.6.9.2's table move; branch review round 1 (R1-1) showed §14.6.9.2 applies only to dynamic-capacity tables, and every text now says latitude. No golden changes, and INITIALIZE ... WITH FILLER has no in-range filler to reset (a depending item inside the group makes it a receiver at its maximum length, GR8 b)).

**Shape 2 is a determination.** The padding of an element of a dynamic-capacity bit table is out-of-line allocation slack and the rounding of the element's byte length (§8.5.1.9.1 3), §15.14.4 4), §8.5.1.6.3's NOTE), not a filler item: no statement can write it, since §14.6.9.2 moves tables element by element. It is not stored and reads zero in every image; recorded in `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (USAGE BIT) as a determination the owner may overturn, pinned by `DynamicBitElementPadTests` (INITIALIZE ... WITH FILLER leaves it zero while setting the one-bit item's filler). A stored pad would need a parallel pad column in `CobolDynTable` across every capacity change, EXTERNAL cell tables and BASED storage.

**Goldens.** `14.9.25-MOVE/pb3598_odo_bit_run_tail_variable_group` (2014, 2023: V1 and V4, a working-storage and a cell-backed receiver, read 0x60 before the fix and 0x62 after; V2 and V3 are the controls) and the negative `pb3598-odo-bit-run-tail-variable-group-below-2014` (COBOLNET0900 at 85 and 2002). The first landing's golden comment now states the rule path instead of an open question.

**Self-review.** Architecture: one splice law in `OdoTail`, the three consumers read it; the ordinary group's `PlaceRenderer.ReceiveInto` stays GR4's whole-character store by design. Full code: the strided-table trailing filler (`Trailing`) is given back by the same `TrimmedExtent` the extent channels read; a count at the maximum and a zero-width keep window both return the received run. Performance: the splice runs per variable-length group MOVE with an ODO bit run, one `WriteWindow` as before. Duplication: no second copy of the rule.
