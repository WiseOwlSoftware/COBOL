## Entry 3821 — 2026-11-17 11:22 PST — PB5894 and PB5895: at 1985 a control footing crossfoots in the order its sum counters are defined, and a counter may be its own addend

The printed folio XIII-54 (kb/Work PB5789) gave `1985-GR-XIII.3.19.4-9` two paragraphs the compiler did not keep. §1985:XIII.3.19.4 GR9a: "Crossfooting is performed according to the sequence in which sum counters are defined within the control footing report group", and "When one of the addends is the sum counter defined by the data description entry in which that SUM clause appears, the initial value of that sum counter at the time of summation is used in the summing operation". The compiler crossfooted in dependency order, the 2002 and later rule (§2023:13.18.54.4 GR6), so `SA SUM SB SC`, `SB SUM SC`, `SC SUM WS-A` printed SA = 6 where 1985 gives 3 (PB5894, wrong answer), and it refused `S5 SUM WS-A S5` with COBOL2730 under §2023:13.18.54.3 SR4e (PB5895, legal source refused).

One edition behaviour carries both: `DialectBehavior.SumAdditionsInDependencyOrder`, changed in 2002, VCR row 7.87. The 2002, 2014 and 2023 texts each state GR6's dependency order and the SR3e/SR4e refusal, so those editions are unchanged. At `--std 85`, `ReportWriterEmitter.RegisterInDefinitionOrder` registers a group's crossfooting counter by counter in counter-id order, with every addend read as it stands at that moment. All of one counter's crossfoot addends form a single addition, so a self addend reads its value from before that addition. Rolling forward follows (§1985:XIII.3.20.4 GR10 a)/b)). `ScreenRolledChains` runs only from 2002.
