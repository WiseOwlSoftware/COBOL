## PB4708: a declared PROGRAM specifier's CALL refusal says why it has no prototype, on all three routes

kb/Work PB4708 (two review leads of the PB4633 branch). Re-probe on the tree: `PROGRAM R4708P AS ""` (a zero-length literal-3, refused by ISO §12.3.8.3 SR2) registers no `ProgramSpecifiers` row, so `CALL R4708P` and `CANCEL R4708P` said the word was "neither declared by a PROGRAM specifier", and the USAGE PROGRAM-POINTER TO screen added a second "not a program-prototype-name" refusal for the same declared word. Where the external repository's own finding refused a specifier (REPO-3, -4 and the like) the sentence cited an SR14 refusal that `RepositoryResolver.ReportUnanswered` never reports.

Fix shape: `ConfigurationSlice.RefusedProgramSpecifiers` (word to `ProgramSpecifierRefusal`) remembers a declared word whose literal-3 was refused, or whose unanswered specifier the external repository's finding refused (inherited by contained units with the rest of the REPOSITORY paragraph), and `ConfigurationSlice.DeclaresProgramSpecifier` is the one declared-word question that the CALL spelling-(b) arm, `CallBinder.ResolvePrototype` and the USAGE screen in `DataBinder` ask; the private copy in `CallBinder` is deleted. `CallBinder.WhyNoPrototype` names the three routes: SR14 with COBOL5962, the external repository's own finding (recorded by `BinderDriver.ProgramPrototypesOf` from `RepositoryResolver.External`), and SR2's literal-3 refusal. A first cut that passed the resolver to `StatementBinder` failed `ArchitectureEdgesDriftTests` (a new Bound to Binding edge) at the first gate and was replaced by this table. SR14's three alternatives are worded as the standard writes them (a program prototype of the group, a program definition "specified previously in this compilation group", information in the external repository); the citations were checked with `cite.py --check`.

Tests: `ExternalRepositoryResolutionTests.ASpecifierWhoseLiteral3IsRefused_IsDeclaredAndNamesSr2` (CALL, CANCEL, CALL AS, and no extra PROGRAM-POINTER refusal) and added assertions in `AFileNamedAfterTheUnitThatIsNoAssembly_IsRepo3` (the repository-refused route names no SR14). The change is a diagnostic sentence, so no conformance-suite case differs by edition. No inventory row is claimed or closed.
