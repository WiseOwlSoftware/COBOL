## PB4298 the 2002 floating debugging indicator >>D in both reference formats; PB4281 re-probed (the residue is one stage-order root)

Wave 20261799 group B, two notes in `ReferenceFormatProcessor.LogicalConversion.cs`.

**PB4298.** The 2002 floating debugging indicator `>>D` (ISO/IEC 1989:2002 6.1.2 "a debugging line when followed by at least one space character", 6.3.4, 6.1.2.1 SR7 and SR8; ISO 2023 6.2.3.1 lets a floating indicator stand in either reference format and lists none for debugging) was refused with COBOL0001 "unexpected '>'" in both formats at `--std 2002`, where `cobc -std=cobol2002` accepts it. The re-probe on the build reproduced all four of the review's programs (`a1` `a2` `a5` free, `a3` fixed), and the fixed-D twin `a6` ran. The two converters each classify a line; neither asked the question. It is now ONE decision, `LogicalLineBuilder.TryFloatingDebugging` / `TryEmitFloatingDebugging`, called by `FixedFormConverter.AddSourceLine` and `FreeFormConverter.Convert`: the line is carried exactly as a fixed `D` line (`EmitDebugging`), the indicator written as three spaces so that every later column holds, so `DebuggingLineRewriter`, the PICTURE and subscript re-lex and the PB2490 between-literal-parts rule apply unchanged. Registry row `floating-debugging-indicator-2002` (introduced 2002 with the floating indicators, obsolete 2002, removed 2014, so `>>D` is COBOL0900 at 85, COBOL0903 at 2002, COBOL0902 from 2014); SR8 (`>>D` on a line whose indicator area holds `D` or the continuation hyphen) is the new COBOL5770. Fixed in the same arm: the COBOL3473 diagnostic named column 7 for every debugging line, and now names the indicator's own column. Determinations in docs/CONFORMANCE.md D-DEBUG: `>>D` or `>>d` with no space inside and a space after it; in free form a line that ends in `>>D` is not one (the 2002 table asks for a space), in fixed form the space-filled program-text area makes a bare `>>D` a blank debugging line. The 2002 clauses are not in the repository, so none is `cite.py`-checkable; 2023 6.2.3.1 was. Goldens: `_unassigned/pb4298_floating_debugging_free` (runs under WITH DEBUGGING MODE), `pb4298_floating_debugging_fixed` (a comment without it), the refusals `pb4298-floating-debugging-below-2002`, `-at-2014`, `-on-fixed-indicator`; unit tests `LogicalLineBuilderTests.FloatingDebuggingIndicator_*`. They sit in `_unassigned` (rules `[]`) as the PB1494 D-line goldens do, because 2023 has no rule for the indicator and the generator refuses a clause folder for a rule-less test. No inventory row is claimed or closed.
