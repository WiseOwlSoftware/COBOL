## Entry 1806 — 2026-09-29 23:57 PDT — PB1759 + PB1760 (+ PB1093): HIGH-VALUE is U+FFFF, one storage-byte law, LINE SEQUENTIAL is UTF-8

Measured before any edit (PB1759 step 0): the 47 files naming `AsImage`/`FromImage` reach the codec through two renderers (`PlaceRenderer`, `OperandText`), and those renderers serve file statements and in-memory statements alike — READ's store and a FILE STATUS group store are the same `WriteFullGroupImage`; the sort work store never touches a medium. More decisive: every in-memory byte-form decoder already truncates a character to its low byte, the record areas that matter most (multi-01 FD areas, Tier-B REDEFINES backings, elementary records) have no leaves a per-leaf `AsMedium` could dispatch on, and a probe showed the real collision is IN MEMORY: with HIGH-VALUE = U+FFFF and binary bytes still imaged as U+00bb, `MOVE HIGH-VALUES TO REC` then `IF REC = HIGH-VALUES` turns FALSE for a record with a COMP field, because the struct re-images FF FF as U+00FF U+00FF — content changed by a store, where §14.9.25.4 GR4 moves a group "without conversion of data from one form of internal representation to another". So D29 was corrected (owner addendum: the design is improvable): not a second generated image per record, but ONE law — byte 0xFF IS U+FFFF, every other byte b is U+00bb, a character's byte is its low byte (`StorageByte`) — used by every byte-form encoder/decoder and by the fixed-record medium (`FileCharacterSet.ToChannel/FromChannel`, the only mapping at the two medium sites). The medium then needs no layout knowledge at all.

HIGH-VALUE is U+FFFF at every R52 site from one member (`NativeCollatingSequence`): the collating extremes walk the whole repertoire (the `national` parameters of `CollatingTable.Build`, `AlphabetFigurative` and `LiteralEnvironment.SpecialNames` are gone — GR10's phrase now selects the same pair), `FigurativeConstants.Fill` became one rendering of `FillChar` (its byte-identity second switch deleted), the collating fingerprint hashes all 65,536 weights. ORD(HIGH-VALUE) = 65536; `ALPHABET AL IS HIGH-VALUE "B" "A"` puts U+FFFF first and X"FF" at ORD 257; on a CODE-SET medium HIGH-VALUE is the set's highest code unit (R51 item 4). Accepted cost, generalized and documented (DOC-A.1-31): a real `ÿ` that passes through any byte reads back as HIGH-VALUE.
