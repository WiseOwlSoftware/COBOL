## PB5575: compile-time arithmetic at --std 2002 is the 32-digit truncating standard arithmetic

Wave 20262043, group C. `CompileTimeArithmetic.For` knew two modes, a 34-digit SDIDI for 2002 and 2014 and System.Decimal for 2023. The standards say three things. §2002:7.2.5.2 GR1: "Standard arithmetic shall be used for all arithmetic operations", whose standard intermediate data item has "a precision of 32 decimal digits" (§2002:8.8.1.3.1.1) and whose sum, product and quotient are "the exact ... truncated to 32 significant digits" (§2002:8.8.1.3.2, §2002:8.8.1.3.4, §2002:8.8.1.3.5). §2014:7.3.5.2 GR1: "as if ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD-DECIMAL were in effect at compile time". 2023: implementor-defined.

**Re-probe (my build, before the fix).** `--std 2002`: `K1 CONSTANT AS 10^30 * 100 + 9 - 10^30 * 100` printed `00009` (2002 gives 0: 10^32 + 9 has 33 digits and loses its 9), `2 / 3 * 3` printed 2 (2002 gives 1). After the fix `--std 2002` prints `00000` and `00001`; `--std 2014` still prints `00009` and `00002`.

**Fix shape.** Three modes in `CompileTimeArithmetic`: `Standard2002` (new), `StandardDecimal` (the old `Standard`, now 2014 only) and `SystemDecimal`. The 2002 mode truncates each operand and each `+ - * /` result to 32 significant digits and refuses a value outside 10^-999 through 10^1000 - 10^968 (§2002:8.8.1.3.1 r2: underflow is a size error, "not rounded to zero"). It runs the engine's `CobolRounding.Truncation` and cuts the 34-digit result to 32, which composes because truncation does. Two members on `CobolDec` carry it, `TruncateToSignificantDigits(int)` and `AdjustedExponent`; the run-time slice of this note reuses them. The 2014 edge is a new `DialectBehavior` row, `CompileTimeArithmeticIsStandardDecimal` (2014, VCR row 7.77, `compiler-facts.json`); `For` reads both edges. `DESIGN-compile-time-expressions.md` and `DOC-A.1-29` now state the three modes.

**Goldens.** `7.2.5@2002-COMPILE-TIME-ARITHMETIC-EXPRESSIONS/pb5575-ct-arith-32-digit-truncation` (accepted at 2002) runs the constant entry, `>>IF` true and false, `>>DEFINE` same value and OVERRIDE, `>>EVALUATE` and a constant as a VALUE; every expected line is derived by hand from the cited rules. `pb5575-ct-arith-before-2002` is refused at 85 (COBOL0900). `pb1592_ct_arith_standard` asserted 34-digit values at 2002, so it is now accepted at 2014 only. `CompileTimeArithmeticModeTests` gained the per-edition division, sum, tie and range cases (31 and 33 operand products and quotients around 10^999).

**Rows.** Seven 2002 rows recorded CONFORMS in the inventory-2002 store (batch `pb5575-ct-arith-2002`, GAP 3297 to 3290): 7.2.5.2 GR1, 7.2.9.2 SR2, 7.2.9.3 GR3, 7.2.10.3 GR4, 7.2.13.3 GR2 and GR3, 13.9.3 GR3. `work.py check` refuses a 2002 id in `closes_rows` (PB5094), so the note's `closes_rows_reason` names them. The 25 run-time rows stay in `inventory_rows`: the run-time ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD engine and the intrinsic functions still use the 34-digit model, which is its own redesign (a precision and range parameter on the engine with the two models as instances).

**Found on the way.** §2014:D.16.1 says the default intermediate rounding is truncation; this compiler's 2014 standard-decimal rounds NEAREST-AWAY-FROM-ZERO (the 2023 default). Recorded in the note's addendum for the 2014 lane.
