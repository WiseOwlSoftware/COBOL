## Entry 3612 — 2026-11-14 10:08 PST — PB5481: at --std 85 an EVALUATE with a literal subject and a literal object compiles; the combination table starts at 2002

At `--std 85` `EVALUATE 5 WHEN 5`, `EVALUATE ZERO WHEN 0`, `EVALUATE "A" WHEN "A"`, `EVALUATE 5 WHEN NOT 4` and an ALSO position of the same were refused COBOL1634, because `EvaluateBinder.ScreenPairing` asked `EvaluateOperandCombinations.IsPermitted`, which is ISO's Table 15 (§2023:14.9.13.3 SR10), at every edition. The table is printed from 2002 (§2002:14.8.12.2 SR9, Table 16) and its literal subject x literal object cell is blank. 1985 prints no table: §1985:VI.6.13.3 SR6 a asks that an object be a valid operand for comparison to its subject, and §1985:VI.6.13.4 GR1 gives each literal "the value and class of the specified literal". The text keeps two readings alive, so by CLAUDE.md rule 1 GnuCOBOL decides, and 3.2 with -std=cobol85 compiles and runs each of them.

The fix makes the table an edition fact. `IsPermitted` now takes the edition year; the new `DialectBehavior.EvaluateRefusesLiteralSubjectLiteralObject` (2002, VCR row 7.75) is inactive at 85, and then the one blank cell is permitted. No other cell changes: the 1985 operand kinds pair as Table 15 prints them. The re-probe on the branch's build showed six COBOL1634 at 85, 2002 and 2023; after the fix the 85 program prints SELECTED-1 to SELECTED-5, OTHER-6, OTHER-7 (values derived from the selection rule) and 2002, 2014 and 2023 still refuse. docs/CONFORMANCE.md gets §3.12 for the latitude, CONDITIONS_EXCEPTIONS_DESIGN D5a the design paragraph, and `EvaluateOperandCombinationsDriftTests` pins the table at 2002 and 2023 and the one 85 difference.

Goldens: `pb5481_literal_subject_literal_object_85` (VI.6.13 1985 folder) and `pb5481_literal_subject_literal_object_2002` (14.9.13-EVALUATE, refused 2002 2014 2023). Row 1985-SR-VI.6.13.3-6 PARTIAL to CONFORMS (1985 GAP 176 to 175).
