## The GnuCOBOL 85 measurement names the cause of 351 of its 484 failures

PB6054's first half recorded the 484 tests cobc 3.2.0 fails at `-std=cobol85`, each with its mechanical bucket and the cause `unclassified`. This slice reads the buckets and sets the cause of 351 of them in `conformance-suite/measurements/gnucobol.json`: 185 `gnucobol-nonconformance`, each with a `§1985:` citation, and 166 `non-portable-test`. The second-compiler term of the suite status line falls from 484 to 133.

What the re-probe showed. The first diagnostic a compile bucket is keyed on is not always the failure: the `_FORTIFY_SOURCE redefined` and `Control field ... is not referenced in report` buckets are invalid C that cobc generated for valid COBOL (`b_6`, `f_5`, `h_SORT_A` undeclared), and the OPEN REVERSED bucket is a warning followed by a syntax error. `ALPHABET ... IS NATIVE | STANDARD-1 | literal` compile at 85 while `IS EBCDIC` and `UPSI-7` do not, which is the line between a cobc defect and an implementor-defined name. The Debug module (an optional module, `§1985:I.1.4`) and the Communication module (104 compile failures) cannot be portable: the 48 output failures of the Debug and Communication folders need the implementor's switch (the one sampled, `1985-GR-XV_3_2_4-1`, prints its DEBUG lines under `cobc -debug`): the object-time switch is outside the program (`§1985:XV.1.3.4 p1`), so the test is non-portable until the adapter passes the flag. Each classified output folder was sampled on two tests and diffed against its `.out`; a Report Writer RF group prints on page 1 line 34 where NEXT PAGE puts it on page 2 line 4, and cobc returns 00 where 02, 39 and 46 are the file statuses the rule names.

The citation of each nonconformance is the rule its test names, or the prose of its clause, checked with `cite.resolve` before it was written. The remaining 133 are output buckets of single-clause folders whose diffs were not read; PB6054 stays `half` and its addendum `kb/Work/PB6054/2026-11-22-cause-adjudication.md` records the evidence and the reading. `generate.py --check`: GREEN, 11098 tests.
