## Third fuzz night triaged: seven defects filed (PB3562–PB3568) and three leads

The third overnight differential fuzz run (fuzz-20261022-163846, qwen3.6:35b, 4756 programs, 804 `WE_REJECT` and 377 `OUTPUT_DIFFERS` rows among them) was re-judged on main at e7cf38ce4 and read by twelve Opus adjudicator slices of at most 25 signatures each, run four at a time: 231 signatures, 2,145,771 subagent tokens, 45 minutes, no slice at its turn cap. Seven defects came out of ten signatures, three of them wrong answers: a boolean function sent to a group of bit items stores its '0'/'1' text instead of its bits (PB3562), a MOVE into a RENAMES THROUGH alias converts the numeric leaf it spans (PB3563), and the implicit filler bits after a USAGE BIT item have no storage, so a group MOVE loses them (PB3564). One refuses legal source: `COMPUTE bit-item = FUNCTION BOOLEAN-OF-INTEGER(...)` (PB3565). Three accept illegal source: the literal `VALUE 0` on a numeric-edited item at 2002 and 2014 (PB3566, which overturns the zero exemption PB921 landed), a `+`-signed literal as the VALUE of an unsigned item (PB3567), and the operands of VALIDATE, which are never bound (PB3568). Three leads were filed as well: a misleading COBOLNET0864 message on `READ ... KEY` under sequential access (PB3569), an owner question on what a numeric literal moved to a group stores (PB3570), and the undocumented `0.00` text of a zero standard-decimal function result (PB3571).
