rule telling a session to load a skill before dispatching anything, and the retired wave-local gate wording; it gains Proven

gains "Prove your work". PB5112 records the measured size of the rest of the agent-instruction surface (skills, brief
templates, the 80 KB practices file, agent definitions, loop unit prompts) and the order of its rewrite, after PB5099, PB5100
