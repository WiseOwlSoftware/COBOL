## Entry 1897 — 2026-10-05 20:23 PDT — The §8.13 external-repository design is approved and landed (PB1099 Q3): the repository is the compiled assembly's metadata, COBOL-to-COBOL calls keep the CobolArg boundary, and every program gets a generated host entry; slices PB2097-PB2104 filed

**What was decided.** Seven review rounds (an author, then an adversarial Opus refuter each round) produced `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-external-repository.md`. The owner chose "option 1": the typed COBOL-to-COBOL boundary the first rounds designed (a typed `Entry`, prototype interfaces, signature carriers, a digest, a numeric cell family, a shared group area) was deleted, because each round found a new place where a typed C# boundary could not honour COBOL's byte-level aliasing (§14.2.3 GR8, §14.8.2.3.2 rule 1, REDEFINES). COBOL-to-COBOL calls keep `ICobolProgram.Call(CobolArg[])`; the repository is ECMA-335 metadata plus COBOL attributes (no bespoke file), read without loading; every outermost program gets one generated `public static class Cobol.<asm>.<NAME>` host entry with `Call` and `Cancel`, and the untyped host route is retired. Group-formal aliasing is not part of it: it is PB2087 (landed for CALL and functions in train 1022, INVOKE arm and PB2089 in wave 1023).

**Owner decisions recorded (design section 19).** One host surface; AOT builds are host-composed (no compile-time module reference, §7.3 intact); every host value loss is refused with `ArgumentOutOfRangeException`; the function-name rule follows GnuCOBOL where ISO conflicts with itself (REPO-13); the Annex C fold for AS literals; no arity warning on literal CALLs; groups cross as record images.

**Round history in one line each.** Round 3 found the `ref` alias dies past `Entry`; round 4 found numeric formals cross as images by §14.8.2.3.2 rule 1 and groups still copy; round 5 found the carrier identity carries names ISO ignores (pb1115) and the group area costs 22x-367x at record size; round 6 found the host entry's RETURNING and naming holes; round 7 closed them. Findings that dissolved with the typed boundary are recorded in the review reports, not in the document.

**Filed.** Slices PB2097-PB2104 (design section 21); PB2093 (a legal subordinate named AsImage or Equals breaks today's record struct). PB1086 keeps the 18 inventory rows; PB989's two rows close in slice 6. PB1940 is blocked by PB2087 (item 5 supersedes its fix).

**Lessons.** (1) An experiment that hides its own crash (`Select-Object -First 4`) is not evidence; the round-5 refuter caught it. (2) The measured cost of a mechanism at one record size does not transfer to another (the group area was 36 ns at 20 characters and 4 microseconds at 32,000). (3) Do not pass a blanket `-BorrowDays`; the design allowance is for the design only (entry 1894).
