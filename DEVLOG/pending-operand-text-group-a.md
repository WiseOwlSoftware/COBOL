## Operand text group A: READ INTO the whole area, RECEIVE INTO a group at its maximum, INSPECT of a scaled item, SEND in standard data format, a numeric literal opposite a 1985 group

Five open defects of the operand and image channels (PB5309, PB5377, PB5375, PB5378, PB5419), each re-probed on a fresh build first (every note reproduced), each fixed at its root, one golden each at --std 85.

**PB5309 (READ INTO).** A record area whose first record description holds an OCCURS DEPENDING ON table was read through `SendingGroupImage`, which cuts the table to its current count, so `READ F INTO W` sent `AB` and ten spaces for a 12-character record with the count at 2 (§1985:VII.3.8.4 GR12 fixes the sender's positions alone). `OperandText.RecordAreaImage` now takes an `AreaUse` (`Sending`, `Receiving`, `Whole`), the one vocabulary of how much of an OCCURS DEPENDING group an operation uses of its record area, and `CurrentRecordImage` reads `Whole`. The first attempt, a `maximum` flag on `VarGroupCurrentImage`, changed nothing: a classic OCCURS DEPENDING ON record is not a "variable-length" record in the compiler's sense (that is the 2023 dynamic-length and dynamic-table notion), and the generated C# showed the cut came from `SendingImage`. The WRITE twin (PB5217) is still open.

**PB5377 (RECEIVE INTO).** `EmitReceive` handed the MCS the receiving group at the DEPENDING ON item's current count, so a nine-character message into a ten-position group was split at four (TEXT LENGTH 0004, END KEY 0). It now passes `AreaUse.Receiving`: the maximum length when the group holds its own DEPENDING ON object (§1985:VI.5.8.4 GR3b), the current count when the item lies outside (GR3a), as `WriteGroupImage` already stored it back. The golden also checks the positions past a short message (the amendment).

**PB5375 (INSPECT of a P item).** `INSPECT ... TALLYING` over `PIC 9PP VALUE 500` counted the three characters of the algebraic image and `REPLACING` wrote five characters into a one-character item. PB5187's `AsItemSizeTemporary` became `AsItemCharacters` (it also cuts a leading P, which the comparison never reaches because a leading P makes the item non-integer), and INSPECT reads identifier-1 and every operand through it. STRING refuses a P item (COBOL1651), UNSTRING takes an alphanumeric sender, the class test and reference modification read the stored image, DISPLAY's conversion is the implementor's: no sibling.

**PB5378 (SEND size).** The elementary binary or packed item was already its standard digits; the group was its storage bytes. At --std 85 `CommunicationEmitter.SendingText` composes a group holding such an item in standard data format (§1985:IV.4.3.4): `ItemLength.StandardLayout` states the layout (a part per item, tables repeated, plain runs merged), `RuntimeApi.StandardDataImage` carries it and the new runtime `CobolStandardData.Image` replaces each binary or packed item by its digits, so a group of `XX` and `9(4) BINARY` is six characters and sends `AB0012`. From 2002 a group stays its storage image (the 2002 text is not in the carried specs); the reading is in `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` section 3.3 and the design doc.

**PB5419 (numeric literal opposite a group).** `IF G2 = -5` compared the literal's source text with the group: true for `-5`, false for `5 `. `ConditionRenderer.ComparisonText` now compares a numeric literal as its digits whatever the pair's sign choice, the image a MOVE of the literal to a group stores (a literal has no stored image; its size is its digit count, §1985:IV.4.2.2.2.2 GR4). The held-row addendum's second claim, that `MOVE -5 TO G2` depends on unrelated lines, is a misreading: in `x1.cob` line 21 is cut at column 72 (COBOL6688), which nests the MOVE in an ELSE the wrong comparison skipped. No MOVE defect.

**Goldens** (all `accept: [85]`, at the rule's own edition; no earlier edition exists): `VII.3.8@1985-RECORD-CLAUSE/pb5309-read-into-odo-first-record`, `XIV.3.5@1985-RECEIVE-STATEMENT/pb5377-receive-into-group-with-own-odo`, `VI.6.18@1985-INSPECT-STATEMENT/pb5375-inspect-scaled-numeric-characters`, `XIV.3.6@1985-SEND-STATEMENT/pb5378-send-group-size-standard-data-format`, `VI.6.3.1.1@1985-Relation-Condition/pb5419-signed-literal-opposite-group`; units `StandardDataFormatTests`, `RecordAreaUseDriftTests`.

**Rows.** Seven 1985 rows recorded CONFORMS in the 1985 store (`record_verdicts --edition 1985`, GAP 318 to 311): VII.3.8.4-12, XIV.3.5.4-3, -8, -9, VI.5.9.4-8, XIV.3.6.4-3, VI.6.3.1.1.2-2. The notes close no inventory row (the inventory has no 1985 rows, PB5094).
