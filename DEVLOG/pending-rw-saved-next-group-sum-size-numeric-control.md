## Report Writer: the group after a saved NEXT GROUP at 85, the raise time of a SUM size error at 2002, and a signed zero in a numeric CONTROL item

Wave 20262063, group C (kb/Work PB5823, PB5643, PB5782). Each note was re-probed on this branch's Debug build before any edit, and each reproduced.

**PB5782 (every edition).** A numeric CONTROL item that went from -0 to +0 between two GENERATEs broke, because the engine compared the saved and current character images of every control it had no collating comparison for. `negzctl.cob` printed `CFX` twice. §1985:XIII.3.7.4 GR3 a asks for "the comparison of two numeric operands" and §2023:8.8.4.2.4 makes zero one value whatever its sign. The emitter now passes `CobolReport.NumericControlEqual` for an image-carried numeric control (zoned, packed, a REDEFINES view): it decodes both images through the item's profile. A native numeric item's image is a function of its value and keeps the image compare; the comment on `ControlEntry.Equal` said the same of all numeric items and is corrected. Golden `13.18.16-CONTROL/pb5782_numeric_control_signed_zero` (zoned sign-separate and packed controls, 85 to 2023). Witness recorded on `1985-GR-XIII.3.7.4-3` and `GR-13.18.16.4-3`.

**PB5823 (85).** `PresentBody` took the saved-integer arm of the fit test for every body group while the save location was set, which is the 2002 and 2023 forced page advance (§2023:13.18.37.4 GR4a). §1985:XIII.3.10.8 GR3b applies the trial sum while a body group is on the page and reads the saved integer only after a page advance, so a LINE PLUS 0 control footing at LINE-COUNTER = FOOTING stays on the page. New dialect behavior `SavedNextGroupForcesPageAdvance` (2002, VCR row 7.81): below it, the ordinary test runs first and a group that fails it advances and meets the saved integer in `PlaceAfterSavedNextGroup`. `m1save0.cob` at `--std 85` now ends `PC 02 LC 12` (it ended `PC 03 LC 09`); 2023 is unchanged. Golden `13.18.37-NEXT-GROUP/pb5823_saved_next_group_fit_test` (`.85.out`, `.out`). The existing PB5245 golden is unchanged by construction (its TERMINATE footing fails the test at 17 > 16).

**PB5643 (2002).** `Accumulate` raised EC-REPORT-SUM-SIZE at the addition at every edition. §2002:13.16.52.3 GR3 only sets the indicator and GR4 raises when the entry is presented; §2014:13.18.54.3 GR3 and §2023:13.18.54.4 GR3 raise at the addition. New dialect behavior `SumSizeErrorRaisedAtAddition` (2014, VCR row 7.82). Golden `13.18.54-SUM/pb5643_sum_size_error_raise_time` (`.2002.out`: GEN1 DONE, GEN2 DONE, RAISED 1; `.out`: GEN1 DONE, RAISED 1). `2002-GR-13.16.52.3-3` recorded CONFORMS (GAP 1234 to 1233); the 1985 witness additions took GAP 99 to 98.
