## The edition band of a clause 15 function rule is read from each edition's own text

kb/Work PB3321 asked whether a function rule's `editions` band starts at its facility's introduction ("core obligation") or at the edition whose wording states it ("as worded"). The answer, recorded in `docs/VERSION_TEST_MATRIX_DESIGN.md` P2.9, is neither as a blanket: the band starts at the first edition whose own text states the same obligation. A rule that an earlier edition states in other words starts there; a rule that names a facility an edition lacks (class national, the LOCALE phrase, standard-binary arithmetic) or states a figure the earlier text gives differently starts at the edition that first says it.

The re-probe on the landed per-rule store found 57 late-start rows (0 early) among the 94 function sections. Each was read against the 1985 file's Intrinsic Function module, `ISO_COBOL_2002.md` and `ISO_COBOL_2014.md`. 43 stand. 14 started later than the first edition that states the same obligation and were re-banded in one `record_verdicts` batch (GAP 4 to 4, `adjudication/pb3321-function-rule-bands/`): the argument rules of DATE-OF-INTEGER, DAY-OF-INTEGER, INTEGER-OF-DATE, INTEGER-OF-DAY, LOWER-CASE, UPPER-CASE, ORD, REVERSE, NUMVAL and NUMVAL-C and the NUMVAL-C format now start at 85 (the compiler already refuses each at `--std 85` with COBOL1627 citing the 1989 module; the 1985 inventory's `1989-AR-A.2.*` rows hold their witnesses), BYTE-LENGTH's format and its DEPENDING rule (§2002:15.12.3 RV2, word for word) and SQRT's "argument-1 is not rounded" (§2002:15.68.3 RV1, standard arithmetic) start at 2002. Two goldens witness the 2002 starts (`pb3321-byte-length-depending-2002`, `pb3321-sqrt-standard-arithmetic-2002`, SQRT(1.5129) = 1.23).

The other direction is now a drift test: `EveryFunctionRule_StartsNoLaterThanItsFirstVerbatimStatement` reads each late rule's sentence from the 2023 text and refuses a band that starts after the 2002 or 2014 text of the same function carries it word for word (before the fix it named `RV-15.14.4-2`). A rule an earlier edition states in other words stays the adjudicator's reading. One row, `RV-15.68.4-2`, is the lower-confidence call (worded first in 2023, implied by the earlier formats) and stays at 2002. PB3320 is no longer blocked on this note.
