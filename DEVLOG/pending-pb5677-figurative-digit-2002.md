## PB5677: HIGH-VALUE, LOW-VALUE and QUOTE into a noninteger numeric or numeric-edited item are an integer at 2002 and 2014

With a collating sequence that makes LOW-VALUE the digit "5", `MOVE LOW-VALUE TO` a `PIC 9(3)V99` item stored 55555 through the image fill and a `ZZ9.99` item showed 555555. The 85 landing already routed this through `MoveEmitter.AllLiteralNumber`; its figurative arm was gated to 1985. At 2002 and 2014 the rule (§2002:14.8.24.3 GR4b, §2014:14.9.24.3 GR6d.3.b) makes the sender an unsigned integer of the receiver's digit count, moved by the numeric rules, so the value is 555.00. The fix drops the edition gate on the figurative arm and gives a figurative sender scale 0 at every edition (the ALL literal keeps its positional 2002 reading, kb/Work PB5351). Re-probe: before `NV=55555 NE=[555555]`, after `NV=55500 NE=[555.00] NEB=[$55.00]`, `NS=55500+ NI=555 NP=555.00 NB=555.00`. Golden `pb5677_figurative_digit_to_noninteger_2002`. Row 2002-GR-14.8.24.3-4 stays open on the ALL-literal leg (PB5351), so no row is closed.
