## Entry 3022 — 2026-11-02 23:49 PST — PB4456 and PB4457: the free-form writer of an expansion assembly never leaves a continued literal line empty, and its diagnostics are reported at the EXPANDS entry

Both notes came out of the PB2104 branch review and share one file, `ExpansionAssemblies.cs`, the placement-(a) arm that compiles an expansion's text apart.

**PB4456 (rejects legal source).** `FreeForm` wrote an expansion's text as free-form lines of at most 200 positions and tested the continuation point before EVERY content unit, the first included. A short literal that fits its line whole but opens within two positions of the `"-` reserve (`"A"` at 198, `""""` at 197, `N""""` at 196-197, the apostrophe forms) was therefore closed as `""-`: the opening delimiter and no content. ISO 6.4.2 (`cite.py --check`) requires "at least one alphanumeric character, national character, or hexadecimal digit of the literal content" on the continued line and on each continuation line, and a doubled quotation symbol on one line; the compiler's own COBOL2690 refused the writer's output. Re-probe on this tree: the sweep test reproduced COBOL2690 on the old code for short literals opened within two positions of the reserve. The fix tracks where the open literal begins (prefix letters included) and how many units it has on the line; a first unit that does not fit moves the whole literal to a line of its own, a later one is continued as before, and a continuation line opens with a whole unit (at most two positions), so it always has content. The line-81 comment ("a longer one starts where it is") is corrected. Sibling sweep: no other writer of a free-form continuation in `src`.

Witnesses: `ExpansionFreeFormTests` (every kind: alphanumeric and national in both delimiters and letter cases, boolean, X, BX, NX; short bodies that fit a line and long ones; every opening column 1..199; each result read back through the reference-format stage with zero diagnostics and a join equal to the input; plus the exact repro columns), and the multi-unit golden `pb4456_expansion_literals_at_every_column` (library `pb4456_literal_run_lib.cbl`, a 1199-character run of short literals through a real expansion assembly) with its negative `pb4456_expansion_literals_refused_at_85`. With the fix disabled the golden fails with COBOL2690.

**PB4457 (misleading diagnostics).** The sub-compilation's errors named the deleted temp file and its line; its warnings were dropped; the CLI printed `generated C# at ` with no path when the sub-compilation failed before writing C#. `ExpansionToWrite` now carries the EXPANDS entry's `SourceLocation` (the position `edition.At(entry)` gives an expansion made in the group), `Write` returns a `Written` record with the warnings, `AtTheEntry` relabels every error and warning to the entry and names the expansion, `CompilerDriver` carries the warnings on every outcome, and `Program.BackendFailureHeadline` names the C# only when the file exists. Tests: the shapes of `AtTheEntry`, a real library at 2023 whose skeleton uses NEXT SENTENCE (COBOL0903 reported at the client's `(8,5)` entry), and the headline. One observation: a warning the group's bind also raises for the expansion text (COBOL3146 in the probe) now appears twice, once from the group's bind and once from the sub-compilation under the recorded state.

DESIGN-external-repository section 10.5 states both. No inventory row changes (ISO 6.4.2 has no catalog rule; PB4457 is a reporting path). No diagnostic code allocated.
