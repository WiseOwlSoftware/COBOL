## PB5238: a READ of a record outside the record descriptions' sizes answers 04, for relative and indexed below 2023 and for record sequential at every edition

Re-probe: the note's program (`p16u.cob`, a relative file `RECORD IS VARYING 1 TO 30` over one `PIC X(20)` description, records written with lengths 3, 25 and 20) printed `R1 00`, `R2 00`, `R3 00` at `--std 85`. The held-rows adjudication's pairs showed the same for the record sequential file (`p04s.cob`, descriptions of 10 and 15 bytes under a 6 to 20 clause) and the indexed file (`p04w.cob`/`p04r.cob`).

Root cause: the connectors measured a READ against the RECORD clause's integer-2 and integer-3 (`VaryMin`/`VaryMax`), which a WRITE is also measured against, so the clause's bounds and the descriptions' bounds were one number to the runtime. §1985:VII.3.8.4 GR10 refuses a WRITE only outside integer-2..integer-3, so a file can hold records its reading program's descriptions do not span; §1985:VIII.4.5.4 GR16, §1985:IX.4.5.4 GR18, §1985:VII.4.4.4 GR13 and §2023:14.9.30.4 GR14 measure the READ against "the record description entries". The relative and indexed READ also had no length test at all.

Fix: `FileModel.RecordDescriptionSizes` (smallest `MinRecordSize`, largest description) reaches the connector through a new registration `RegisterRecordDescriptionSizes`, emitted by `EmitAreaRegistrations` (the one place every organization's registration passes) only when the descriptions differ from the clause's bounds. `FileConnector.ReadLengthOutsideDescriptions` is the one test; the record sequential READ calls it, and `KeyedConnector.RecordReadSuccessStatus` serves the relative and indexed READs (`ReadSequential` and `ReadRandom` of both). The 2002 and 2014 texts state the rule for every organization and 2023 limits it to a record sequential file, so the keyed arm is gated by the new `DialectBehavior.RecordLengthConflictRecordSequentialOnly` (VCR row 7.61): 04 below 2023, 00 at 2023. An indexed record that also has an equal next key keeps 02, the status stated after the length rule. The citation the note carried for the descriptions lying inside the clause (§1985:VII.3.8.3 SR2) is the external-file-connector rule; the right one is §1985:VII.3.8.4 GR4 and GR10 (`cite.py --check` on both).

Goldens: `14.9.30-READ/pb5238_keyed_read_length_conflict` (85 2002 2014: relative and indexed, random and NEXT, 04/00/04), `pb5238_sequential_read_length_conflict` (all four editions), `wiseowl/cases/14.9.30-READ/pb5238_keyed_read_length_conflict_2023` (00, an implementor reading of a text that names no status). No refusal test: nothing is refused. `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` records the reading beside the '04' entry. No inventory rows closed (the inventory has no 1985 rows, PB5094).
