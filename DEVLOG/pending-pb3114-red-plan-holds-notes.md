## PB3114: a RED wave plan still holds its notes and burns its codes

PB3114 (process/orchestrator, MINOR, silent): `plan_wave.py` reserves the wave's codes and records its groups in the dispatch ledger before `make_dispatch_specs.py` and `check_practices.py` run. A RED plan therefore exits 1 with its notes held as in flight, so the next plan skips them until someone runs `--release`, and with three codes per group reserved and never used. It also accepts a drive-relative `--scratch`. The unit released the dispatch, removed the folder and re-planned from PowerShell. Filed only; no code changed.
