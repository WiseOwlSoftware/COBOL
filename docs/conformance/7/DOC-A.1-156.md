The end of the source text also ends its last line. **In comments and literals:** the terminator cannot appear in a comment or in the content of an alphanumeric or national literal — it ends the line (a literal that must span lines ends the first line with the floating literal continuation indicator, §2023:6.2.3.1) — while every other control character, a lone CR included, MAY appear in comment-text and in literal content and is taken as that character: `\"A<FF>B\"` and `\"A<CR>B\"` each hold three characters, and a lone CR in a `*>` comment does not end it. The one exception is the horizontal tab, which is never a character of a comment or a literal because it is expanded to spaces at line entry (item 157; owner decision kb/Work R55).
