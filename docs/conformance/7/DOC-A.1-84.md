(b) **A numeric item** is its sign — `+` or `-`, always present for a signed item, \"Signs are presented as SIGN IS LEADING SEPARATE\" (GR8) — and its UNSCALED digits, zero-filled to the digits the item's representation can hold: its PICTURE's digit count, or more for a binary item (`PIC S9(4) COMP` holds 16 bits, so five digits, 32768) and a packed one (2n-1 digits in n bytes, 2n unsigned); the decimal point stays implied. An unsigned item has no sign character. A national numeric item is the same text in national positions (UTF-16BE pairs). (c) **A floating-point item** is a sign and the exponent form of 17 (binary64: `E16`) or 9 (binary32: `E8`) significant digits, `+1.2345678901234567E+003`, 24 and 16 characters, which restores the value exactly; an infinity or a NaN has no external text and the WRITE is refused (I-O status 30). (d) **A bit item** is one `0` or `1` for each of its bits, in the order of the item, the items of a byte of bit items one after another. (e) **A character item** is itself, converted by the CODE-SET alphabet when the file has one — formatting first on output, conversion first on input (§2023:13.18.13.4 GR5). (f) **The record on the medium** is these in the order of the record description entry, with no control information; its length is fixed by the entry and the data it holds, and it is the length the RECORD clause bounds (§2023:14.9.51.4 GR14: I-O status 44 outside) — so a FORMAT file's records are variable-length (§2023:13.4.5.3 SR6) and its WRITE of a record is the record formatted at its own length. A record on a line sequential file is the line of the text. (g) **Reading** restores each selected item to its internal representation (GR9), the entry chosen by the SELECT WHEN clauses (§2023:13.18.51.4 GR3); text that is not the representation of a value of the item is an unsuccessful READ with I-O status 30. Annex A.1 item 85 states the exclusions.
