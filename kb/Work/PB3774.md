# PB3774 — route a bounce to the lane that dispatched the branch

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` (`reap`, `tick`, `conflict_brief`, `write_conflict_brief`, `notify_operator`, `watch_queue`: the bounce and CONFLICT handling, the conflict brief, the queue watch's alerts), `scripts/orchestrator/units/enqueue.md` (the finisher and re-arm steps), `scripts/orchestrator/dispatch_ledger.py` (`record_dispatch`), `scripts/orchestrator/reports.py` (`ledger_slug`: the dispatch ledger, each group's wave, slug and scratch), `scripts/orchestrator/mailbox.py` (whole: the change adds a new member and its call sites in this file) (the `to-mythos` inbox).

## What happens

On 2026-10-25 two lanes dispatched implementers: the loop (account 2) and the Mythos lane (account 1, waves from 20261500, scratch `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/mythos`). The landing lane lands every CLEAN branch of both, which is intended, but it names "the enqueue unit's finisher" for every bounce and posts every stall to the operator. So at 13:27-13:30 the loop's enqueue unit, the Mythos lane and the operator each re-armed the same 10 outage bounces, and at 14:06 the lane assigned PR #602 (worktree-wf_1341dc87-1c4-14, a Mythos branch) to the loop's finisher. The owner had the lanes split ownership by origin (memory lane-split-2026-10-25); the operator had to repeat that split to each new loop unit by message, because no unit reads it.

## A standalone tick reports no STOP at all (2026-10-25 evening)

With the loop stopped from 22:05Z, the operator ran `land_lane.py tick` every 5 minutes from a background script (log `E:/COBOL-coord/logs/land-lane-ticker-20261025.log`). Three attempts ended in exit 4: worktree-wf_6e0a917c-500-26 (attempt 000160, started 22:15Z), worktree-wf_a588e886-609-2 (000162, 22:27Z) and worktree-wf_94698d58-de0-2 (000169, 22:48Z). The tick log carries only each attempt's `enqueue.py land started` line, and neither inbox got a message: `reap()` speaks only for a runner that DIED and for the exit-8 halt, and `tick()` prints a waiting reason only for an attempt it classifies FREE. A CONFLICT STOP is visible only in `land_lane.py status`, whose reader is the loop's enqueue unit, and no enqueue unit runs while the loop is stopped. The Mythos lane found 500-26 itself 33 minutes later, when the owner asked why four PRs were not merging. The operator forwarded 609-2 and de0-2 to `to-mythos` at 23:17Z.

## Fix direction

Ownership by construction: the dispatch ledger already records which wave (and scratch) dispatched each branch's group. The lane reads the owning lane from it (a wave in the Mythos lane's range, or a scratch under its directory, or an explicit `lane` field the renderer records) and sends that lane's bounces, conflict briefs and stalls to its own inbox (`mailbox.py send --to mythos`); the enqueue unit acts only on the loop's. Self-tests: a Mythos-lane branch's CONFLICT goes to `to-mythos` and no loop finisher is planned; a loop branch's still goes to the enqueue unit. The same route closes the gap above: a tick prints one line for each attempt it finds newly finished (branch, head, exit and verdict), and posts a STOPPED branch's conflict brief to the owning lane's inbox once per head, whether or not an enqueue unit is running; the unit closes the message when it acts. Self-test: a standalone tick whose attempt ended exit 4 prints the line and sends one message, and a second tick at the same head sends none.
