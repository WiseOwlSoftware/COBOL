## Landed (wave 1045 group E, 2026-10-08)

Re-probed first on a fresh build (`>>LEAP-SECOND ON`, S = 86400.5): `FORMATTED-TIME("hhmmss.sZ", S)` gave `2359605Z`,
`(S, 0)` and `(S, Z0)` gave `0000005Z`, `(S, 60)` gave `2300005Z`; `FORMATTED-DATETIME(.., 1, S, 0)` moved the date to
1601-01-02, and at integer date 3,067,671 with offset 0 it raised EC-ARGUMENT-FUNCTION and returned "".

Fixed at the root, in two parts:

- **One path for the omitted and the zero offset (15.41.3 r6 / 15.40.3 r7).** The `hasOffset` flag is gone from
  `CobolDate.EmitFormatted`, `FormattedTime`, `FormattedDatetime` and `OffsetOutOfRange`; the renderer's new
  `IntrinsicRenderer.OffsetMinutesArg` passes `0` for an omitted offset, so the UTC adjustment runs for every UTC format
  and "as though 0 were specified" holds by construction, not by range.
- **The leap second keeps its place (7.3.17.4 GR4, 15.3.3.3).** `EmitFormatted` sets a value in [86 400, 86 401) aside as
  23:59:59 plus its fraction BEFORE the 15.41.4 r2 / 15.40.4 r2 adjustment, then shows the shifted minute's second as 60.
  Offset 0 gives 23:59:60.5Z, +60 gives 22:59:60.5Z, -60 gives 00:59:60.5Z of the next day.

Goldens `conformance:2014/pb2629_leap_second_utc_offset` (FORMATTED-TIME and FORMATTED-DATETIME, omitted / literal 0 /
item 0 / plus / minus offsets, the offset format, the last integer date, under EC-ARGUMENT-FUNCTION CHECKING ON) and its
negative twin `negative/pb2629-formatted-time-offset-below-2014`; `unit:CobolDateUtcRollRangeTests` (the leap-second
case). Sibling sweep: `FORMATTED-CURRENT-DATE` shares `EmitFormatted` (it always passed an offset and never sees 86 400);
`LOCALE-TIME-FROM-SECONDS` and `COMBINED-DATETIME` take no offset; `TimeOfDay` stays the one reading of a whole number of
seconds.

## Landing

- `CobolDate.cs#EmitFormatted` owns the UTC adjustment and the leap second's place in it; there is no "has offset" flag.
- `IntrinsicRenderer.cs#OffsetMinutesArg` is the one producer of the offset argument for FORMATTED-TIME and
  FORMATTED-DATETIME (an omitted offset is the literal 0).
