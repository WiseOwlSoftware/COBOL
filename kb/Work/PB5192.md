# PB5192 - at --std 85 an entry with an OCCURS clause written between an OCCURS ... KEY IS subject and its key entry compiles: OccursKeyItemFault walks only the key's own hierarchy, the 2002 narrowing

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/OccursKeyResolvePass.cs` (`OccursKeyItemFault`, `Run`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:VI.5.8.3 SR12 bars "any entry that contains an OCCURS clause" between the descriptions of the KEY data-names and the subject of the entry. `OccursKeyResolvePass.OccursKeyItemFault` walks from the key's parent up to the table entry and reports COBOL2787 only for a group on that path, the 2002 rule (an entry between may carry OCCURS if it is not a group in the key's hierarchy), with no edition arm. An earlier sibling table, elementary or group, or a table between two keys compiles at --std 85.

**Found by.** The 1985 code read (2026-11-10; its records are `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/for-register.json`): each rule below was read against the code sites it names and probed at `--std 85`, and found non-conforming. Verdict, harm, evidence, repro and observed text are the records'; the rows' verdicts are recorded on another branch.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids of the list below.

Checked: §1985:VI.5.8.3 SR12: "There must not be any entry that contains an OCCURS clause between the descriptions of the data items identified by the data-names in the KEY IS phrase and the subject of the entry.". Code sites read: src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/OccursKeyResolvePass.cs#OccursKeyItemFault; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/OccursKeyResolvePass.cs#Run. Code read: No kb/Work note owns the fix. §1985:VI.5.8.3 SR12: "There must not be any entry that contains an OCCURS clause between the descriptions of the data items identified by the data-names in the KEY IS phrase and the subject of the entry." Implemented at 85 for one kind of entry in between: OccursKeyResolvePass.Run resolves each KEY data-name within the table and OccursKeyItemFault walks from the key's parent up to the table entry, reporting COBOL2787 when a group on that path has an OCCURS clause. That walk is the 2023 rule (§2023:13.18.38.3 SR4, a group the key is subordinate to) and has no edition arm. Not implemented for 85: an entry with an OCCURS clause that stands between the subject entry and the key's entry without containing the key (an earlier sibling table, elementary or group, or a table written between two keys). The 1985 sentence bars "any entry", and the narrowing to the key's own hierarchy is the 2002 change §2002:F.2 item 84: "An entry between a data description entry that contains an OCCURS clause and the entry describing a data item referenced in the KEY phrase of the OCCURS may itself contain an OCCURS clause, as long as it is not a group item in the hierarchy of the data item referenced in the KEY phrase." Probed at --std 85: refused are a key under 10 G OCCURS 2 (COBOL2787) and a key whose own entry has OCCURS (the neighbouring rule, §1985:VI.5.8.3 SR11); accepted and legal are a table written after the last key, the subject as its own key, and a keyed table inside an outer table; accepted and not legal are 10 S PIC X OCCURS 2 before 10 K, a group 10 GS OCCURS 2 before 10 K3, and 10 S4 PIC X OCCURS 2 between the keys K4A and K4B. The witness puts the key K under 10 G OCCURS 2 TIMES and is refused with COBOL2787.
