# PB1686 — LANDED

## Landed (wave w0930-numeric — one bundled commit with the seven siblings of this note)

Re-probed first: a 9V99 counter fed 1.000 then −0.005 printed 1.00 (the ADD of the same two values gives 0.99).

**Root cause.** §13.18.54.4 GR3 says the adding "is consistent with the general rules of the ADD statement" and GR9 sums a
group's addends together; the emitter aligned EACH addend to the counter's scale (rounding it there) BEFORE adding, so an
addend finer than the counter lost its extra digits one addend at a time. **Fix (the mechanism, not a wider scale):** a term's
addition is now EMITTED BY THE ADD STATEMENT'S OWN MACHINERY — `NumericRenderer.Fold` over the addends, `Combine(counter, "+",
sum)` at the wider of the two scales in the unit's lane (Int128, or the SDIDI under a standard mode), `StoreExpr(…,
raiseOnSizeError: true)` once through the counter's profile with the clause's ROUNDED mode — and `ReportWriter.SumTerm` is a
`Func<Int128, Int128>` (counter content in, content after the ADD out; any `CobolSizeError` / `OverflowException` is the GR3
size error). `AddSum` lost its digit count (capacity is the store's, through the profile). Citations: §13.18.54.4 3) (the
quoted sentence, `cite.py --check`). Golden `2023/pb1686_sum_counter_adds_like_add` (three counters: single addend,
multi-addend GR9, ROUNDED); docs COBOLNET_REPORT_WRITER_DESIGN.md.

**Siblings swept:** every `AddSumTerm` producer (one: `ReportWriterEmitter`), the VARYING counters (integers by GR5 — no
scale to cut), GR12's procedure-division alterations (stored through the counter's profile already).


Filed by the train-70 lander from wave 70 V3's report (implementer of PB1509; `w70v3-PB1509-report.md`, lead 2).

## Finding

**Repro.** `E:\Temp\claude\E--COBOL\73cac64c-391d-42c0-bcdf-c880e3e10305\scratchpad\w70v3\p\sumtr.cob`: a SUM
counter of PICTURE 9V99 accumulating an addend of three decimal places, 1.000 then −0.005. The report prints 1.00;
the same two additions written as `ADD` statements into a 9V99 item give 0.99.

**Rule** (`cite.py --check` OK). §13.18.54.4 3): "The current content of each addend is implicitly added into the
sum counter during execution of GENERATE and TERMINATE statements at specific times defined below. The adding is
consistent with the general rules of the ADD statement …" — ADD forms the exact sum and only then stores it to the
receiver's scale.

**Code site.** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ReportWriterEmitter.cs:555` —
`NumericRenderer.Align(num.Render(v, …), sum.Scale, sum.Rounding)` is applied PER ADDEND, inside the addend delegate,
so the fraction below the counter's scale is lost before the addition.

**Mechanism.** The engine's counter (`CobolReport.SumEntry`, Int128 since PB1509) holds the value at the counter's
scale, so the addend delegate aligns first. ADD-consistency needs the addition at the wider of the two scales and the
store (truncation, or GR4's ROUNDED) after it — the engine would carry the addend's own scale or a wider working scale.
