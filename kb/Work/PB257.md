WHAT IS ARITHMETICALLY FAITHFUL, re-derived and upheld by the refuter so the PARTIAL is not mistaken for a wider doubt: `VarianceDec` is the EAE's operation TREE in the EAE's order on the SDIDI carrier — `mean = MeanDec(mode, xs)`, then `acc = Add(acc, Mul(d, d, mode), mode)` over `d = Sub(x, mean, mode)`, then `Div(acc, n, mode)`. §8.8.1.5.4 rule 2b makes `operand ** 2` exactly `(operand * operand)`, so `Mul(d,d,mode)` is the EAE's own operation and not a substitution; `CobolDec.AddSigned:175-176` short-circuits a zero significand so the leading `Add(0, t)` cannot round; `CobolDec.Div:227-235` pre-scales in two exact steps and folds the true remainder into sticky before ONE rounding, so `Div(x, 1)` is exact and case (a) returns literally (0); `Round34Wide:370-391` enforces the decimal128 range with EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW/UNDERFLOW per §8.8.1.5.2 r2. ⛔ FINDING 1 — TWO BODIES, ONE GUARD, AND THE STATED REACHABILITY EVIDENCE IS FALSE. The adjudication asserted 'Divide-by-zero on n is unreachable: MinArgs 1 (COBOLNET1504) guarantees xs.Length >= 1'. The real guarantee is a THIRD body the adjudication never located — `CobolTable.cs#AllArgs:130-135` raises EC-ARGUMENT-FUNCTION and throws on an empty enumeration, citing §15.3's 'the evaluation of an ALL subscript shall result in at least one argument'.
