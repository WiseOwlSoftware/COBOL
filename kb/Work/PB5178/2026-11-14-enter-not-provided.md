**Landing paragraph (2026-11-14, kb/Work PB5308): ENTER is refused at --std 85, which makes this note's period and routine-name arms moot.**

The ENTER statement is not provided (§1985:I.1.5.2.5.5; docs/conformance/3.7.1), so `StatementBinder.BindEnter` refuses every ENTER with COBOL7240. What PB5178 added for a statement that compiled to nothing is gone with it: the `PlacementRules.EnterEndsSentence` row and its diagnostic COBOL6973, the `UserWordKind.RoutineName` member and the routine-name's census entry, and the three goldens (`pb5178-enter-not-end-of-sentence`, `pb5178-routine-name-is-paragraph-name`, `pb5178_routine_name_later_verb_word`). The grammar keeps `routineName` (the cobolWord and reservedGatedWord arms), so a sentence such as `ENTER FORTRAN FREE.` still parses whole and is refused once, at the statement, instead of at its routine-name. The rows `1985-FMT-VI.6.12.2` (PARTIAL) and `1985-SR-VI.6.12.3-2` (PARTIAL) were decided against the accepting compiler and carry prose about it; they are not re-verdicted here.
