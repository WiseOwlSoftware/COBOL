r2_ids: runtime-control-2--performance--f1#1

**Wave kind:** restructure. **Model:** Opus (the control-flow protocol of a CALL return).

**Mechanism.** A GOBACK or EXIT PROGRAM in a called program, and a GOBACK in a method, leaves through a thrown `ProgramReturn` or
`MethodReturn` (`CallEmitter.EmitGoback`, `CallEmitter.EmitExitProgram`, `StatementEmitter.Visit(BoundMethodReturn)`). Each return
allocates an exception and unwinds the stack. The R2 measurement on the pin was +1.09 us per CALL return, about double the cost of the
CALL itself.

**Target.** Decide how the return leaves a nested PERFORM or declarative frame without a throw, keep the GOBACK and EXIT PROGRAM
semantics unchanged, and prove neutrality with the §4 oracle and a benchmark that carries a witness.
