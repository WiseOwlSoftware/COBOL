## Finding — `write-statement-p2` (wrong-answer; rows `GR-14.9.51.4-21`, `GR-14.9.51.4-22`)

**Mechanism.** The line-sequential trailing-space strip (FileConnector.TrimRecordEnd, called unconditionally from every line-sequential WRITE arm) is keyed on none of the three things §14.9.51.4 GR21/GR22 key it on: (1) it runs on a DEPENDING-phrase FD too, where GR21 does not apply and GR22 keeps the spaces out to data-name-1's length; (2) its alphanumeric arm is string.TrimEnd(), which strips every Unicode White_Space character, not the alphanumeric space character; (3) its national/alphanumeric choice is a per-connector flag (any national record description marks the whole FD), not record-name-1's category.

**Repro.** (a) FD LV RECORD IS VARYING IN SIZE FROM 1 TO 10 DEPENDING ON VL. 01 LVR PIC X(10). MOVE 'AB' TO LVR. MOVE 6 TO VL. WRITE LVR. (b) FD LF. 01 LR PIC X(8). MOVE 'AB' TO LR. MOVE X'A0' TO LR(3:1). WRITE LR. (c) FD LM. 01 LMN PIC N(4). 01 LMX PIC X(8). MOVE 'AB' TO LMX. WRITE LMX. All ORGANIZATION LINE SEQUENTIAL, --std 2023. (E:/Temp/claude/E--CobolSharp/a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec/scratchpad/adj-d1/out/probe/write-p2/pc.cob, pd.cob)

**Expected from the spec.** (a) the line is 'AB' followed by four spaces (six characters, GR22); (b) the line is 'AB' U+00A0 — U+00A0 is not the alphanumeric space character and is a member of the line sequential character set (CONFORMANCE DOC-A.1-115), so it is transferred (GR21); (c) the line is 'AB' — record-name-1 LMX is alphanumeric, so its trailing alphanumeric spaces are not transferred (GR21).

**Observed / reasoning.** Measured: (a) 'AB' CRLF; (b) 'AB' CRLF (U+00A0 dropped); (c) 'AB' + six 0x20 + CRLF (the national pair test finds no 00 20 pairs and strips nothing). SequentialIoEmitter.EmitNationalAreaRegistration's own comment records (3) as a known residual.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/FileConnector.cs#FileConnector.TrimRecordEnd; src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/SequentialConnector.cs#SequentialConnector.Write (IsVarying && _lineSequential arm); src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/SequentialIoEmitter.cs#SequentialIoEmitter.EmitNationalAreaRegistration
