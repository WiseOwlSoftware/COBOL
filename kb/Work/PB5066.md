# PB5066 - refit the Fable/Mythos rate from a measured spend

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` (whole: the calibration constants).

**Measured (owner's usage page, account 1).** Before the triage: weekly 16-19 %, Fable 0-2 %. After it: weekly 26 %, Fable 17 %.
The Fable row rose 15 while the weekly rose 7, so the Fable row is a percentage of the Fable allowance (half the weekly limit):
the triage cost about 8.5 % of the week. Its own telemetry: 1,821 requests on claude-mythos-5-2, 8.53 M counted tokens
(input + output + cache writes; 213 M cache reads uncounted), which the 800,000 rate makes 10.7 points (11.3 with the canary).
So the meter fits about 1.0-1.1 M counted Mythos tokens per weekly point. Refit under the calibration rule (two readings
bracketing a measured spend) on the OWNER'S GO: the owner said "do not change anything" during the run.
