# PB4972 - the 1985 transcription corrects misprints and lists them in an Errata

**Decision (owner 2026-11-08).** `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` corrects the misprints of the printed standard and lists
every correction in a closing section. Until now the file transcribed "a printed slip included" (its Preface).

**Shape: the 2023 convention, one mechanism (kb/Work R66).** A correction is made in place, flagged with a
`> ⚠ **CORRECTED — see the Errata (Cn).**` note that quotes the printed form and names the page, and listed in the
closing section with its printed form and page. `verify_publishable.check_corrections` holds the three together for
both editions. Only the section's name is per edition: `verify_publishable.CORRECTIONS_SECTION` is the Addendum for 2023
and the Errata for 1985, because the 1985 text already has an Addendum of its own (Amendment 1, Addendum 1). A 1985 page
is its PDF page (`## Page N`), with the folio after it, since the scan numbers its folios per section. The
acknowledgment is never corrected (its slips are entries D1 and D2).

**The corrections (C1-C19).** Found by a scan of the transcription for rare words one edit from a common word, and a
sweep of the body for every accented or foreign letter (review R1-1 found C18 and C19 that way), and each read on the rendered page (crops from the scan's text layer, location only).
Seven misspellings: suppported, constitutent, statment (twice), coresponding, concernings, upppercase. Twelve damaged
letters the transcription had reproduced: piovides, _ables, comment-entrıes, BEFCRE (in INSPECT's general format),
Fil'es, omi-ted, fopting, thac, techñique, ıeturns, rêport, desçription (review R1-2 reclassed fopting and thac: the
rendered page shows a stray descender and a lost ascender). One candidate was right as printed (ADDDDC, example data). The scan finds a misprint
that makes a non-word; one that makes another real word needs a reader.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/verify_publishable.py` (`CORRECTIONS_SECTION`, `corrections_section`, `flag_reference`,
`check_corrections`, `REQUIRED`, `main`, `self_test`), `scripts/spec/sweep_figures.py` (`main`: the one flag pattern;
`ADDENDUM_REF` deleted), `scripts/spec/rule_catalog_1985.py` (`parse`: a correction note is not rule text; `PLANTED`,
`self_test`), `scripts/spec/cite.py` (module docstring), `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (the Preface's note, 19 flags,
`# Errata`), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json` (9 rule texts), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/figures-1985/p217-1.json`
(`figure_lines`, `decided`: neither), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/LEDGER-1985.json` (the page-634 KEPT
claim of 2026-11-07 removed: the word is now corrected), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/TRANSCRIPTION-STATE.md`,
`specs/README.md`.

## Landing
- A new 1985 correction is a flag, an Errata entry and (in a general format) its verdict's `figure_lines` with
  `decided: neither`, in one change; `verify_publishable.py --edition 1985` and `sweep_figures.py --edition 1985 --check`
  refuse a partial one.
- The section's name lives only in `verify_publishable.CORRECTIONS_SECTION`; read it through `flag_reference`, never
  a second regex.
