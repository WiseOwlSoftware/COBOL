No spill path exists. The O(n) in-memory footprint is real; whether it matters is a measurement the perf baseline cannot make today (no SORT row). Not tracked: no open kb/Work note names SortStore memory, spill or external sort (PB1570, landed, is the run-unit scoping of the same store).

## Obsolete by PB3339 (registrar, 2026-10-17)

Owner 2026-10-17: closed in the SORT and file I/O campaign (kb/Work PB3353). Main at 3f3e0514a does what this analysis
asked about. The store that held every record is gone: `SortStore` holds no record collection
(`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/SortFileTable.cs:14-20`; its records live in `SortWork`), and `SortWork` (`SortWork.cs:30`)
seals its chunk at `COBOLNET_SORT_MEMORY` (default 128 MiB, `SortMemory.cs:25` and `:34`) into sorted runs on temporary
files (`SortRunFile.cs`) that it merges k-way. The capacity is measured, not assumed: `SortWorkBoundTests` asserts the
measured peak resident bytes stay within the limit for an input at least eight times it
(`tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/SortWorkBoundTests.cs:130`), `SortWorkBoundDriftTests` refuses an unbounded record field, and
`SortWorkSpillConformanceTests` runs a 30,000-record SORT and MERGE at the 64K limit and at the default. The perf-baseline
SORT row this note proposed was its way to choose between a documented capacity limit and a spill design. PB3339 built the
spill design, so the row is not carried forward.
