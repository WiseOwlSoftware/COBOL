**The mechanism.** The subscript list had the declaration-informed cut since kb/Work PB136 (`SubscriptBinder.SegmentsOf`:
a '(' after a name that cannot be subscripted begins the next subscript), and the two argument lists did not: both
function-identifier forms bound the parse's items as they stood. The cut was also read in only one place (the position
segment's `Cut` field, honoured by the direct walk alone), so neither an argument's bind nor the edition gate could see
it. The fix makes it ONE list reader, `SubscriptBinder.GiveAwayClosingList`, asked by `SegmentsOf` and by
`IntrinsicBinder.ArgumentsOf` (both forms), and marks the paren it gives away on the parse node
(`SubscriptPartOwnership.GiveToNextItem`), which `ReferenceResolver.ReadWritten` and `VersionConformancePass.VisitSubscriptPart`
honour, so every reader of the reference agrees. `PositionSegment.Cut` is deleted; a materialized subscript whose head
carries a cut (`D2 (X / 1 (B - 1))`) now binds instead of being refused.
