# PB1627

Filed 2026-09-26 by the train-63 lander from the w62k (PB1406) implementer's lead 1.

**Repro** (w62k probes, `E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\ba65f072-4d30-4868-9bd3-4e5c60087107\scratchpad\w62k\probe\`):
`q15.cob`, `MOVE ALL "A" & K TO X` with K an alphanumeric constant-name, and `q16.cob`, `MOVE ALL "A" & SPACE TO X`.
Both draw COBOLNET1541. Not probed yet: `ALL K` for a constant-name parses as `ALL cobolWord` and draws the
symbolic-character error at `ExpressionBinder.cs:296`, although §13.10.3 SR2 lets a constant-name stand wherever a
literal of its class may be written.

**Rule.** §8.3.3.6.3 SR2 lets ALL's literal-1 be a concatenation expression, and §8.8.3.2 SR1 admits a figurative
constant as one or both operands of a concatenation expression. Only an ALL figurative is barred as an operand.
§13.10.3 SR2 admits a constant-name. So both probes are legal source.

**Code sites.** `CobolExpressions.g4#allLiteralOperand` (quoted literals only); `ConcatFolder.FoldAll`, which
should ride the same `ConcatFolder.Walk` and `LiteralEnvironment` PB1406 introduced rather than a second
operand model; and `VersionConformancePass.VisitFigurativeConstant`'s ALL-arm reporting (PB1393).

## Landing (w62q, 2026-09-26)

Re-probed on the w62q build: q15 and q16 drew COBOLNET1541, `ALL K` (scratch `w62q\probe\q17.cob`) drew COBOLNET1670.
**Fix — one operand model.** `allLiteral : concatenationExpression | STRINGLIT | HEXLIT | NATLIT | BOOLLIT` — the
concatenated literal-1 IS the §8.8.3 rule (`allLiteralOperand` deleted). `ConcatFolder.FoldAllLiteral(fig, env,
report?)` is the ONE Format 6 reader: a quoted literal, a concatenation through the same pairwise `Walk`, or a
constant-name written alone (the `ALL cobolWord` arm, resolved as Format 6 before the Format 7 readers). It reports
SR2 — COBOLNET1648 zero-length (structural), new COBOLNET2491 for a numeric constant-name — and the concatenation's
§8.8.3.2 rules. Its callers: `ExpressionBinder.FigurativeOperand` (`BoundAllLiteral.Of(Folded)`), the boolean
channel and its predicate (`ConditionBinder`, now classing by the GR1 fold, not "every operand is B"…""), the VALUE
reader (`DataBinder.RawValueOperandText` + `IsLiteralValueOperand`) and the ALPHABET/CLASS literal phrase
(`DataBinder.Switches.AllLiteralCharacters`, class asked of the folded literal). The version pass's ALL arm keeps only
the national/boolean 2002 gate; the SR2/§8.8.3.2 checks it asked over quoted tokens moved to the reader (they need
the constant-name table). Goldens: `tests/conformance/2002/pb1627_all_literal_concat_operands`, negatives
`pb1627-all-literal-concat-below-2002` (0900 at 85) and `pb1627-all-numeric-constant-name` (2491).
