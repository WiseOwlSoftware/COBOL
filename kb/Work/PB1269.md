## Measured

### Finding occurs-clause-p2 — §8.5.1.9.6 1) (via §13.18.38.4 GR14 / GR17) (wrong-answer; rows GR-13.18.38.4-14, GR-13.18.38.4-17)

**Mechanism.** F3 — When EC-BOUND-OVERFLOW is raised on implicit growth past the expected capacity and the declarative ends with RESUME AT NEXT STATEMENT, NonfatalIfEnabled throws RaiseResumeSignal. That unwinds RefReceiving BEFORE GrowTo runs, so the growth §8.5.1.9.6 requires never happens. Every later receiving reference past the old capacity then raises again.

**Repro.** --std 2023 (E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d1\out13\probe\occurs-p2\gr14.cob): >>TURN EC-BOUND CHECKING ON; 01 T. 05 R PIC X OCCURS DYNAMIC CAPACITY IN RCAP FROM 2 TO 3.  A declarative on EC-BOUND counts calls and ends RESUME AT NEXT STATEMENT. MOVE "A" TO R(4). DISPLAY RCAP. MOVE "B" TO R(5). DISPLAY RCAP.

**Expected, from the standard.** One handler call; RCAP 4 (R(4)='A') and then RCAP 5 — §8.5.1.9.6 1): 'If checking for the EC-BOUND-OVERFLOW exception condition … results in the execution of a declarative procedure … that executes a RESUME statement with the NEXT STATEMENT phrase, the operation shall be allowed to continue, thus exceeding the receiving table's specified expected capacity', and 'If the change in capacity was implicit and the expected capacity had already been exceeded before the operation, no exception shall exist' (so R(5) raises nothing).

**Observed.** Handler runs twice and RCAP stays 2 after both MOVEs: the MOVE is abandoned and the table never grows. When the declarative completes normally (gr14b.cob) the growth does happen (CAP 4, then 5, one call). So the defect is only the RESUME NEXT STATEMENT arm. NonfatalIfEnabled's documented reading (RESUME = transfer out of the statement) is right in general, but §8.5.1.9.6 names this condition's continuation explicitly. Fix direction: for EC-BOUND-OVERFLOW, complete the growth (and the store) before honouring the resume, or treat the resume as a normal return at this raise site.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Values/Tables/CobolDynTable.cs#CobolDynTable.RefReceiving; src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Exceptions/ExceptionState.cs#ExceptionEngine.NonfatalIfEnabled
