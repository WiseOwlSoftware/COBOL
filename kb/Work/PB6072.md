# PB6072 - each edition is judged by its own text, and the message must cite it

CLAUDE.md memory rule "each edition stands on its own text": a refusal at an edition cites that edition's rule. The reports name
these sites that do not (the classes PB5627 and PB5644 swept one edition at a time):

1. `ScreenFromFunctionClass` cites the 2023 wording at 2002 for an intrinsic refusal: the 2002 locator is added for REWRITE only;
   WRITE and RELEASE still cite 2023 numbering at 2002 (PB5678). And `MoveBinder.cs` (~line 140) REWRITE FROM now cites
   `§2002:14.8.33.2 SR9`/`SR8` beside the 2023 quotation "If identifier-1 references a function ...", which is not the 2002
   text ("an intrinsic function"): the diagnostic attributes words to a rule that does not contain them.
2. `CounterResolver.LinageFileOf`: the ambiguity arm cites the running edition, but the zero-file arm of the same ternary and the
   qualified arm (`FileKeyClauseRule`, ~line 188) still cite ISO 2023:8.4.3.14 at 85 and 2002 (PB5787).
3. `ResolveRedefinesPass.cs` (~line 527): the level-66 arm of COBOL2740 is reachable only at --std 85 yet cites ISO §2023:13.18.45.3
   SR5; the rule refusing it at 85 is §1985:VI.5.11.3 SR4 (PB5801).
4. `FileControlBinder.Resolve.cs` COBOL2270 at 2002 and 2014 cites the 2023 locator (§2023:8.4.2.3.3 SR5) while the sibling
   `DataBinder.ScreenOperandOccurrence` cites §2002:8.4.1.2.2 SR5 for the same refusal (PB5810; main's current text also names
   §1985, re-read before editing).
5. At 2014, several 2023-cited refusals (`FileControlKeyRules` OCCURS rows) cite §2023 where §2014 prints the SR numbers
   differently (PB5775; PB5627's class one edition up). `FileControlKeyRules.cs` `Of1985` rows restate the file kind, role, applies
   and violated predicate of the 2023 rows they supersede instead of taking them from the superseded row (PB5775 R1-2); take them
   from one source so the editions cannot drift.
6. COBOL0901 at a system-name slot (`OBJECT-COMPUTER. CLASS-ID.` at 2002) says "cannot be used as a user-defined word", while
   §2023:8.3.2.1 SR1 also names system-names (PB5795; `ReservedWordSet.UserWordViolationMessage` through
   `VersionConformancePass.VisitReservedGatedWord` and `CobolErrorStrategy`).
7. Comments with a wrong rule or text: `ConditionRenderer.cs` (~502) quotes "the comparison proceeds by the rules of native
   arithmetic" as both §2002:8.8.4.1.1.3 and §2023:8.8.4.2.4 though the 2023 text reads "comparison proceeds by ..." (cite.py
   FAILS text-not-found), and says the float at 85 "lifts to its exact value" where an engine approximation lifts to its shortest
   round-trip decimal (`DecOperandExact`); `DataBinder.cs` `QualifiedCandidates` (~6005) quotes SR3 text under §1985:IV.4.3.8.1
   SRL2.1; `IntrinsicArgumentRules.cs` (~556) and `EvaluateBinder.cs` (~399) cite §2023:8.3.3.6.4 GR1 for ZERO standing for the
   numeric value 0, where GR1 governs the character-value representation and the numeric-value rule is GR4 (PB5813 R2-1; DEVLOG
   entry line 9 repeats it).

Fix by a sweep with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check` over every diagnostic and comment citation of these sites, and look for a
structure (one citation per edition carried on the rule row) rather than seven more hand edits, per CLAUDE.md rule 5.

**Landed 2026-11-22 (wave 20262143, group G).** Re-probed each item on the build. Item 2 (`LinageFileOf`) and the level-66 arm of item 3 and
the `DataBinder` comment of item 7 no longer reproduce (every arm cites the running edition through `LinageCounterRule` and
`Edition.Cite`; `QualifiedCandidates` is gone and `UniqueOrReportAmbiguous` quotes SR3 under §1985:IV.4.3.8.1 SR3, which holds it). Fixed:
(1) `EditionCite` carries `Iso2002` and `Iso2014`, so every FROM-phrase row (`FromPhraseRules`) cites the clause and the words of the edition
compiled (the 2002 REWRITE and WRITE FILE texts say "an intrinsic function", the 2002 WRITE text "alphanumeric, or national"), and the
zero-length-literal rule is an `EditionCite`; (3) the RENAMES refusals of `ResolveRedefinesPass` (SR3, SR4, SR5, SR6, the range rule, the integral-bytes
rule and the window rule) cite §2002:13.16.43.2 and §2014:13.18.45.2; (4) `ScreenRecordDependingOccurs` cites §2002:8.4.1.2.2 SR5 / §2014:8.4.1.2.2 SR5;
(5) `FileControlKeyRules.Restating` makes the `Of1985` rows and the new `Rules2014` rows take kind, role, precondition and predicate from the 2023 row
they supersede, and the three OCCURS bans at 2014 are §2014:8.4.1.2.2 SR5's table-element rule (the 2014 key clauses say "shall not be subscripted");
(6) `ReservedWordSet.UserWordViolationMessage` names "user-defined word or system-name" and cites §2002:8.3.1.1, §2014:8.3.1.1 and §2023:8.3.2.1 SR1;
(7) the `ConditionRenderer` comment, `IntrinsicArgumentRules` and `EvaluateBinder` (ZERO is GR4's numeric value). Unfixed remainder: the other
`FileControlKeyRules` rows and the FILE STATUS, LINAGE and ASSIGN USING messages of `FileControlBinder.Resolve.cs` still cite the 2023 clause at
2014 and 2002 (see the wave report).
