# PB5981 landed, 2026-11-20 (wave 20262115, group A)

**Re-probe before the fix** (tree a2bd139087). `rhlim.cob`: an RD with `PAGE LIMIT 20 HEADING 1 FIRST DETAIL 8`, RH1
at `LINE 4` and PH1 at `LINE 3`. At `--std 85` it was refused with `COBOL2199 ... LINE 4 of report heading report group
'RH1' lies below the lower limit 2 ... limits per §1985:XIII.3.8.3 SR8`. SR8a puts that limit at FIRST DETAIL minus 1 = 7,
so a program that the 1985 rule admits was refused under a borrowed limit. That makes the harm `rejects_legal_source`
as well as `misleads_diagnosis`.

**Fix.** `ReportSectionBinder.GroupLimits`, report heading arm: from 2002 a page heading's first absolute line bounds the
report heading (§2023:13.18.57.4 GR8b). At 85 the bound is FIRST DETAIL minus 1, whatever the page heading
(§1985:XIII.3.10.6 GR2a; §1985:XIII.3.8.3 SR8a). With that change, `RelativeGroupLowerLimit` gives every 85 report heading
that is not on a page by itself the 1985 words; its guard `lower == model.FirstDetail - 1` is gone.

**After:** `rhlim.cob` compiles at 85 and is still refused at 2023 (limit 2). At 85, `pb5981-report-heading-past-first-detail-85`
(RH `LINE 8`, FIRST DETAIL 8) is refused with `lies below the lower limit 7 ... §1985:XIII.3.8.3 SR8`, and
`pb5981_report_heading_lower_limit_first_detail_85` (RH `LINE 4`) translates.

**Lead (not this note):** at 85 the page heading printed after the report heading on page 1 has the upper limit "one greater
than the final LINE-COUNTER setting established by the report heading" (§1985:XIII.3.10.7 GR1). `rhlim.cob` breaks that limit
(PH LINE 3 after RH LINE 4), compiles, and at run time prints P on the line below R. That is kb/Work PB5789/PB5830's
ground (the folio XIII-30 reading), so it was left alone.
