# PB2505

Filed by the w1033 clerk from the PB867 refuter (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1033-adj\PB867-refute.md` section 2, "Harm 'none' holds for conformance, but say
what users will see"). The refuter calls it "an owner/design choice, not a conformance defect", so it is an analysis.

**Facts.** `PB867` was decided CONFORMS: an un-TURNed `RAISE EXCEPTION EC-USER-x` raises nothing (`§14.6.13.1.1`, `§14.6.13.1.4`). Annex D.2.5 (informative)
prints a "Commit and rollback" sample program with a `USE AFTER EXCEPTION CONDITION EC-USER-FATAL-ERROR` declarative and eleven
`RAISE EXCEPTION EC-USER-FATAL-ERROR` statements and no `>>TURN` directive (rendered PDF pages 1053-1054, printed 1023-1024). On this compiler a user who
copies that sample gets a program that silently runs on past its fatal errors (the first RAISE, after "ERROR OPENING FILES", does nothing).

**Owed (decisions, not implementation).**
1. Whether to add a compile-time warning for a RAISE whose checking is provably off at that statement (the compile-time TURN mask in
   `EcBinder.cs`/`ctx.EcState.Turn.Enabled` already knows; lexical TURN does not reach out-of-line paragraphs, so "provably" must be defined).
2. Whether the D.2.5 sample is reported as a defect against the standard (it contradicts `§14.6.13.1.4` under every reading of `§14.9.29.4 1)`).
