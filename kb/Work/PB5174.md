# PB5174 - at --std 85 a lowercase letter in a PICTURE is taken as the uppercase currency symbol (PIC w99 under CURRENCY SIGN IS W): TryExpandRepeats folds the whole character-string to uppercase at every edition

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs` (`TryExpandRepeats`, `AnalyzeCharacterString`; landed: `FoldPictureCase`, the one edition fold)

**Mechanism.** §1985:VI.5.9.3 SR3 makes the lowercase forms of A, B, P, S, V, X, Z, CR and DB equivalent to their uppercase symbols and says all other lowercase letters are not. `PictureAnalyzer.TryExpandRepeats` folds the whole character-string with ToUpperInvariant before any symbol is read, and `AnalyzeCharacterString` folds again when it scans for the currency symbol, with no 1985 arm. So under CURRENCY SIGN IS "W" the string w99 binds and edits as W99 at --std 85, where w is no PICTURE symbol (the 1985 currency symbol is never lowercase, §1985:VI.4.5.4 GR11). kb/Work PB534 (landed) settled the 2023 equivalence.

**Found by.** The 1985 code read (2026-11-10; its records are `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/for-register.json`): each rule below was read against the code sites it names and probed at `--std 85`, and found non-conforming. Verdict, harm, evidence, repro and observed text are the records'; the rows' verdicts are recorded on another branch.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids of the list below.

**Rules (1; every citation checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `1985-SR-VI.5.9.3-3` - `§1985:VI.5.9.3 SR3` (record verdict: DIVERGES, harm: under-rejects). Checked: §1985:VI.5.9.3 SR3: "The lowercase letters corresponding to the uppercase letters representing the PICTURE symbols A, B, P, S, V, X, Z, CR, and DB are equivalent to their uppercase representations". Code sites read: src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs#TryExpandRepeats; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs#AnalyzeCharacterString. Code read: No kb/Work note owns the fix. §1985:VI.5.9.3 SR3 has two sentences. The first ("The lowercase letters corresponding to the uppercase letters representing the PICTURE symbols A, B, P, S, V, X, Z, CR, and DB are equivalent to their uppercase representations") is implemented: TryExpandRepeats folds the whole character-string with ToUpperInvariant before any symbol is read, so pic aaa, 9ppp, s9v9, zz9, xbx, 99db and 99Cr bind as their uppercase forms at 85 (probed; the witness runs x, s, v, z, cr and b). The second ("All other lowercase letters are not equivalent to their corresponding uppercase representations") is not: the same blanket fold, and the char.ToUpperInvariant in AnalyzeCharacterString's scan for the currency symbol, apply at every edition with no 1985 arm, so a lowercase letter is taken as the uppercase letter the CURRENCY SIGN clause names. §1985:VI.4.5.4 GR11 lets the currency symbol be an uppercase letter other than A, B, C, D, P, R, S, V, X, Z and never a lowercase one ("the lowercase letters a through z" are excepted), so under CURRENCY SIGN IS "W" the character w is no PICTURE symbol at all and PIC w99 is not an allowable combination (§1985:VI.5.9.3 SR2). At --std 85 it compiles and edits as PIC W99. The fold is the 2023 rule (kb/Work PB534, landed, measured the lowercase currency letter binding and judged it by the 2023 text only).

The currency symbol the program declares is the uppercase W, so w is not the currency symbol and is none of the PICTURE symbols; §1985:VI.5.9.3 SR2 "A character-string consists of certain allowable combinations of characters in the COBOL character set used as symbols."
