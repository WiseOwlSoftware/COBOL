# PB5043 - cite.py cannot check the 1993 page citations (such as §1993:p.B-16) the 1985 catalog emits

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/cite.py#load`, `scripts/spec/citation.py#regions` (the edition-to-file map).

**Found (wave 20261948 group A, PB4952).** `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check "§1993:p.B-16" "<text>"` exits with "the 1993
transcription does not exist", yet the 1985 rule catalog cites its amendment rows by page (`§1993:p.B-10`, `§1993:p.B-16`) and the catalog's own
`cite_check` accepts them. So CLAUDE.md rule 1's mechanical check cannot be run on a citation the tree itself emits, and
any such citation copied into a comment, golden or DEVLOG is unchecked. The amendments are transcribed inside
`specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (Amendment 1 and 2 pages), so the fix is most likely a region of that file for edition 1993
in `citation.regions`, not a new transcription; confirm against how `cite_check` resolves them, and keep ONE resolver
(the catalog's check and the CLI must read the same map).

**Not probed** beyond the one command the report ran.

**Resolved (wave 20261956 group A).** Re-probe on the unchanged tree: `cite.py --check "§1993:p.B-16" "x"` exited "the 1993 transcription does
not exist". The catalog's `cite_check` did NOT really accept those rows: `citation.validate` returns None for an edition with no
transcription ("parsed only"), so all 201 amendment rows (190 Section A paragraph rows of 1989, 2 page rows of 1989, 9 page rows of 1993) were never checked by anyone.
Fix at the one resolver: `citation.amendment_regions` reads `regions_1985` over the lines of one `# AMENDMENT n:` heading
(`AMENDMENT_EDITIONS`: 1989 is Amendment 1 with its Section A, 1993 is Amendment 2 with pages only), `SOURCE_EDITION` maps both tokens to the
1985 file, and `cite.py load`, `validate`, the catalog and both audits all read it. After the fix every one of the 2,266 catalog citations
validates, a 1993 page is not a 1989 page, and a phantom (`§1993:p.B-999`, `§1989:A.9.9.9`) is refused. **Sites:**
`scripts/spec/citation.py#amendment_regions`, `#regions_1985`, `#_loaded`, `#transcription`; `scripts/spec/cite.py#load`.
Sibling found, not fixed: the 1985 universe still holds the amendments' folios (`§1985:p.B-16` validates against Amendment 2's page); see the report.
