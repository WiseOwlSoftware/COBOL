# PB3336 — the performance witnesses

Implement FROM `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-runtime-library.md` §2.2.1.11 (the table of bounds and the four witnesses (a)-(d);
(d) lands with PB3335). Follows PB3335 (the engine complete). No wall-clock assertion anywhere (the repository's rule): every
witness reads `PagedStore.IoCounters`.

## Mechanism

`tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/Store/KeyedStorePerformanceWitnessTests.cs`, parameterized over {RELATIVE, INDEXED with 0 and
with 2 alternate keys} × {sequential keys, random keys} × {NO OTHER, ALL OTHER}:

- (a) linearity: load N = 20 000 and 4N records; assert pages written per statement at 4N ≤ pages written per statement at N +
  (depth(4N) − depth(N)), and every statement within the §2.2.1.11 bound for its row;
- (b) OPEN reads the header: after a clean CLOSE, OPEN of the 4N store reads ≤ 2 pages; after a simulated process death (no
  CLOSE), ≤ 2 + the ledger's fresh pages;
- (c) random READ: with `PagedStore.DropCache` before each, 1 000 random READs on the 4N store read ≤ depth + 1 pages each
  (+ 1 under ALL OTHER);
- the sequential-load in-place rule (R3): between promotions a sequential WRITE writes ≤ k + 3 pages (indexed, k alternates)
  or ≤ 3 (relative).

The design doc's §2.2.1.2 keeps the prototype's measured numbers; this group adds nothing wall-clock to the tests. If a bound
in §2.2.1.11 is wrong by the measured counts, the doc's table is corrected in the same change and the reason recorded.
