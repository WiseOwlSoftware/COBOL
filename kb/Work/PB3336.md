# PB3336 — the performance witnesses

Implement FROM `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-runtime-library.md` §2.2.1.11 (the table of bounds and the witnesses (a)-(d);
(d) lands with PB3335; witness (e), a transaction's cost, needs the transaction runtime and is PB3337's). Follows PB3335 (the engine complete). No wall-clock assertion anywhere (the repository's rule): every
witness reads `PagedStore.IoCounters`.

## Mechanism

`tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/Store/KeyedStorePerformanceWitnessTests.cs`, parameterized over {RELATIVE, INDEXED with 0 and
with 2 alternate keys} × {sequential keys, random keys} × {NO OTHER, ALL OTHER}:

- (a) linearity: load N = 20 000 and 4N records; assert pages written per statement at 4N ≤ pages written per statement at N +
  (depth(4N) − depth(N)), and every statement within the 2.2.1.11 bound for its row;
- (b) OPEN reads the header: after a CLOSE, OPEN of the 4N store reads ≤ 3 + 7 pages whatever N (the three slots, the
  retirement's ledger and free-tree pages, the transactions tree) and writes 0 pages through an INPUT handle and ≤ 8 through
  a writable one (the fence's claim, its slot and its retirement); after a simulated process death (no CLOSE), ≤ 3 + the
  ledger's fresh pages;
- (c) random READ: with `PagedStore.DropCache` before each, 1 000 random READs on the 4N store read ≤ depth + 1 pages each
  (+ 1 under ALL OTHER: the coherent entry's page 2; + ≤ 2 only when a transaction exists on the file, 2.2.1.11's entry row; + h, the HOLDINGS tree's depth, only when a holding exists and the READ is not IGNORING LOCK, round 4);
- the sequential-load bound (R1 path-copies every statement; there is no in-place rewrite): a sequential WRITE writes
  ≤ (k + 1) × depth + 5 pages (indexed, k alternate keys) or ≤ depth + 5 (relative), + 1 under ALL OTHER (the claim, round 3), + u × (1 + the unique values freed, none for a WRITE) + h × (the same count) + the before-image's overflow pages under
  commit control (the UNDO and HOLDINGS path copies, one per leaf the entries land on; rounds 4 and 5, 2.2.1.11's rows);
- Witness (e), a transaction costs its own records only, is PB3337's (it lands with the transaction runtime, which may be
  narrowed or deferred by the owner's decision on that slice); this group's bounds for commit control are the rows above.

The design doc's §2.2.1.2 keeps the prototype's measured numbers; this group adds nothing wall-clock to the tests. If a bound
in 2.2.1.11 is wrong by the measured counts, the doc's table is corrected in the same change and the reason recorded.
