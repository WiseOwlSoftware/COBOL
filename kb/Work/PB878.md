and skipped SR8/SR9. **On this tree it does not.** `CorrespondingBinder.cs:253` now reads

  OK  §14.8.2.3.3 2) d)  (Elementary items passed by content or by value)

"the same as for a MOVE statement" is the WHOLE question, and `Refusal` is SR10 only. A `BINARY-LONG`
argument against a non-numeric formal (SR8) and a variable-length-group argument against an incompatible
formal (SR9) are therefore admitted where the written MOVE refuses them with COBOLNET0819.

## The sibling sweep the fixer owes

Every caller of `MoveTable16.Refusal` that holds two DATA ITEMS (rather than a literal or a function result)
is a candidate for the same subset bug. At `80dd97e2` the call sites are `MoveBinder` (the written MOVE, which
asks the whole chain through `StatementValidation`), `CorrespondingBinder` (closed by PB391),
`InitializeBinder` (above), `OoConformance` (above) and `OoConformance.cs:607`'s literal-sender probe (a
literal has no SR8/SR9 exposure — correctly `Refusal`-only). **Add a drift test** asserting that no caller
outside `MoveTable16` composes `ShapeRefusal`/`VariableLengthRefusal`/`Refusal` by hand: that is the shape
that makes the NEXT rule automatic, and it is why `DataItemRefusal` was written.
