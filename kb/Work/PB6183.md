# PB6183 — an owner hold lives only in a note's prose and title

**Mechanism.** `kb/Work/PB5900.md` is held for 2014 by the owner's order of 2026-11-15 (the other three editions first); its title says
"NOT to be fixed now" and its body opens **NOT TO BE FIXED NOW**. Its frontmatter says `status: open`, `blocked_by: []`,
`wrong_answer`-class harm flags set, `editions: [2002, 2014]`. The one answer to "is this note blocked" is `work.waiting_on`, which
reads only `blocked_by` entries and `status: owner` / `status: blocked` (PB3389); `work.actionable`, `clusters.ready_notes_view`
(the planner's population) and `work.py next` all read it. A hold written in the title is invisible to all of them, so PB5900 was
planned in wave 20262165 (group D), the implementer read the note, returned BLOCKED with no commits, and the dispatch was spent.
(Report: "Re-dispatch PB5900 only when the owner lifts the 2014 hold.")

**Fix shape.** One machine-readable hold on a note (a frontmatter key such as `hold: "<reason, owner date>"`, or the existing
`status: owner` with its reason), read by `waiting_on` (so the one answer stays one), listed in `work.py next` under "held" with its
reason and not planned by `plan_wave.py`; `work.py check` refuses a note whose title or first line says "NOT to be fixed now" with
no hold. PB5900 gets the hold in the same change; the hold clears when the owner lifts the 2014 order (a drift test pins that no open
note carries the hold phrase without the key).

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/work.py:1107` (`waiting_on`, `actionable`), `scripts/orchestrator/clusters.py:166` (`ready_notes_view`), `kb/Work/PB5900.md` (whole: gains the hold key)
