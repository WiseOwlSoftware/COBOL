## Finding — `concatenation-expressions` (rejects-legal-source; rows `FMT-8.8.3.1`, `SR-8.8.3.2-1`)

**Mechanism.** concat-operand-word-substitution: the concatOperand grammar rule admits only lexical literal tokens and the KEYWORD figurative constants, so the two WORD spellings of a literal — a symbolic-character figurative constant and a constant-name — are parse errors as concatenation operands, although every other literal position resolves them through the symbolic/constant-name substitution seams

**Repro.** SPECIAL-NAMES. SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS SYM-A IS 66.  01 K CONSTANT AS "Q".  01 W-X PIC X(8).  PROCEDURE: MOVE "X" & SYM-A TO W-X. / MOVE SYM-A & "X" TO W-X. / MOVE "X" & K TO W-X.  (--std 2023; probe dir out/probe/concat CCN1, CCN7, c5)

**Expected from the spec.** Compile clean; W-X = "XA", "AX", "XQ". §8.3.2.2.29: a symbolic-character is a user-defined figurative constant; §8.8.3.2 SR1: a figurative constant may be specified as one or both operands (class taken from the other operand, §8.8.3.3 GR1a; one character, §8.3.3.6.4 GR3a); §13.10.3 SR2: a constant-name may be used anywhere a format specifies a literal of its class and category — the §8.8.3.1 format specifies literal-1 / literal-2.

**Observed / reasoning.** COBOL0309 "unexpected 'SYM-A'. A literal value is expected here, not a data-name." for "X" & SYM-A; COBOL0001 "unexpected '&'" for SYM-A & "X"; COBOL0309 for "X" & K. The same SYM-A as a plain MOVE/DISPLAY operand works (MOVE SYM-A -> AAAAAAAA, DISPLAY "X" SYM-A -> XA), so the gap is the concat operand only. The ALL SYM-A form parses (figurativeConstant's `ALL cobolWord` arm) and is correctly refused COBOLNET1541, proving the bare-word arm is what is missing. ConcatFolder.FigurativeChar also has no symbolic-character arm, so the fix spans grammar + fold (resolve the word through the SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS / constant-name tables before classification).

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolExpressions.g4#concatOperand (line ~693); src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/ConcatFolder.cs#Fold / #ClassOf / #FigurativeChar

## Finding — `concatenation-expressions` (under-rejects; rows `SR-8.8.3.2-1`, `GR-8.8.3.3-1`)

**Mechanism.** concat-flattened-class: the grammar flattens the left-recursive format to operand (& operand)+ and ConcatFolder classifies the whole chain by its FIRST non-figurative operand, so a chain that opens with two or more figurative constants and then meets a national or boolean literal is accepted, although the inner all-figurative concatenation-expression-1 has class alphanumeric and is not itself a figurative constant

**Repro.** 01 W-N PIC N(4).  MOVE SPACE & SPACE & N"AB" TO W-N.  (--std 2023; probe dir out/probe/concat CCN2) — likewise ZERO & ZERO & B"1".

**Expected from the spec.** COBOLNET1540. Format §8.8.3.1 is {literal-1 | concatenation-expression-1} & literal-2, so SPACE & SPACE & N"AB" is (SPACE & SPACE) & N"AB"; by §8.8.3.3 GR1b the inner expression is class alphanumeric; the outer operands are a concatenation expression (not a figurative constant) of class alphanumeric and a national literal, which §8.8.3.2 SR1 forbids (both operands shall be of the same class).

**Observed / reasoning.** Compiles clean and runs, W-N = [  AB] (class national). ClassOf returns the first non-figurative operand's class for the entire flattened chain; every LEGAL chain classifies identically under both readings, so the defect is confined to accepting this illegal shape (and, in it, HIGH-/LOW-VALUE would take national rather than alphanumeric extremes). A pairwise left fold of the class (apply GR1 a/b/c to each (accumulated, next) pair) makes SR1 exact.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/ConcatFolder.cs#ClassOf and #Fold

### `GR-8.8.3.3-2` — DIVERGES (adjudicator + refuter record, verbatim)

--check 8.8.3.3 "If both literal-1 and literal-2 are specified and both are zero-length literals the value of the concatenation expression is a zero-length literal" -> OK (first sentence in the same GR, OK). Fold appends each operand's decoded value; figurative = one character (§8.3.3.6.4 GR3a, --check OK). PROBE: embedded delimiters 'IT''S' & "A""B" -> IT'SA"B; NX"00410042" & N"C" -> ABC; "" & "" moved to X(8) -> all spaces (zero-length source, §8.3.3 padding); FUNCTION LENGTH("A" & "" & "B") = 2; golden values AB&CD, 88 VALUE, national/boolean VALUE, FIG chain all correct. The value half is covered by conformance:2002/literal_concat, but the zero-length second sentence has no golden — test-ref left empty (test-needed for the zero-length leg). || REFUTED: DIVERGES — GR2 makes the value of a concatenation the concatenation of the values of its literals AND figurative constants, so a figurative operand must fold to the character that figurative stands for in its context. In the SPECIAL-NAMES paragraph, §12.3.7.4 GR10 binds HIGH-VALUE under the NATIONAL phrase to the highest character of the native NATIONAL collating sequence. The implementation realizes that as U+FFFF: DataBinder.Switches.cs AlphabetFigurative returns ￿ for national. But the SPECIAL-NAMES concat sites call ConcatFolder.Fold(cat, Edition, collate: null), at DataBinder.Switches.cs:1014 (CLASS/literal operand) and :1664 (LiteralCharsOf). With no national table, FigurativeChar returns the ALPHANUMERIC pin U+00FF for class national. So N"A" & HIGH-VALUE folds to a value different from the one the same HIGH-VALUE has as a bare literal in the same clause. The adjudicator's probes covered only SPACE/ZERO/QUOTE, embedded delimiters, NX and zero-length. All of those are correct (re-probed RZ1: [AB" 0], [A 0"], ""&"" -> [] and MOVE -> spaces). No HIGH-/LOW-VALUE value was probed in a context-dependent position. Secondary sites also drop the national table: ControlFlowBinder.cs:37 (STOP literal) and OoBinder.cs:181 call Fold without natCollate, so a national concat with HIGH-/LOW-VALUE under a declared national PCS takes U+00FF/U+0000 there. ExpressionBinder and SetBinder do pass it.

## What the fixer owes

(1) concatOperand resolves symbolic-character and constant-name words through the existing substitution seams; (2) fold the left-recursive format pairwise so each inner concatenation-expression has its own class (§8.8.3.3 GR1); (3) every Fold / FoldAll caller passes the context's national collating table (DataBinder.Switches.cs:1014, :1664, ControlFlowBinder.cs:37, OoBinder.cs:181) — better, make the table a required parameter.
