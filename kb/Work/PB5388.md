# PB5388 - the doc comment of FileControlBinder.IsSequentiallyOrganized sits between ScreenRerunClauses' own <summary> and that method

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/FileControlBinder.IoControl.cs:166` (`FileControlBinder.IsSequentiallyOrganized`, `FileControlBinder.ScreenRerunClauses`)

**Mechanism.** On origin/main (5237495315), the `<summary>` that documents the RERUN clause (its format, SR1-SR5, COBOL6496/6529/6530/6531) ends with `</summary>` just before a second `<summary>` that documents `IsSequentiallyOrganized` (line 166 onward); `IsSequentiallyOrganized` follows at line 169 and `ScreenRerunClauses` at line 171. The IDE and the generated XML documentation therefore give `IsSequentiallyOrganized` two summaries (the RERUN prose is the first) and `ScreenRerunClauses` none. The compiler is unaffected; the comment misleads a reader and tooling. The lead holds on main: PB5312 touched this code and did not move the block.

**Fix direction.** Put `IsSequentiallyOrganized` and its summary above the RERUN `<summary>` block, so that block sits directly on `ScreenRerunClauses`. Sweep the file's other helpers for a summary orphaned the same way. No behavior change; no golden.
