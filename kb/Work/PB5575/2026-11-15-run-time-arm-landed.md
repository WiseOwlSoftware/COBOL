**The finding that reshapes the note.** The 2014 text keeps standard arithmetic too: §2014:8.8.1.3.1.1 is "a precision of 32 decimal digits", §2014:8.8.1.3.2/.4/.5 truncate each result to 32 significant digits, and §2014:8.8.1.3.1.3.1 to .4 round to 31 digits per INTERMEDIATE ROUNDING. So `ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD` at run time is the 32-digit model at BOTH 2002 and 2014 (2023 refuses it), and no edition arm was needed: the mode itself (`ArithmeticMode.Standard`) selects it. The note's title ("2014's 34-digit model instead of 2002's") was right about the code and wrong about 2014's text; the 34-digit SDIDI model is STANDARD-DECIMAL's alone.

**Re-probe (`pa.cbl`, `--std 2002`, the note's `COMPUTE A = B * 100 + 9 - B * 100`).** Before: `00009`; after: `00000` (10^32 + 9 has 33 digits, is cut to 10^32, minus 10^32 is 0). At 2014 the same. `COMPUTE C = E / D * D` (2 / 3 * 3) into PIC 9V9(30): before `2.000...` by luck of the 34-digit rounding; after `2.000...` by the 31-digit rounding of 1.9999999999999999999999999999999. `IF B * 100 + 9 - B * 100 IS ZERO`: before NOT ZERO, after ZERO. `COMPUTE X = 10 ** N / Z` with N = 1000 under EC-SIZE checking: before EC-SIZE-ZERO-DIVIDE, after EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW first.

**Shape (the design the earlier addendum asked for, smaller than it estimated).** The decimal engine already truncates (`CobolRounding.Truncation`) at 34 digits, and truncation composes, so the 32-digit model needs no engine parameter: `CobolDec.ToSidi` cuts each operation result to 32 digits and checks the 10^-999..10^1000 - 10^968 range (EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW / EC-SIZE-UNDERFLOW; PROHIBITED an inexact one), `CobolDec.ToSidiRounded` rounds to 31 digits. The emitter (`NumericRenderer.StandardIntermediate`) runs the operations at TRUNCATION, wraps `DecBinary` and the exponentiation result in `ToSidi`, an unbounded operand lift (`SidiOperand`) likewise, and applies `ToSidiRounded` where §2002:8.8.1.3.1.3 situations 1 and 3 say: a relation and a sign condition (`SidiRounded`, `ConditionRenderer`), and the final transfer into a receiver without ROUNDED (`SidiAtTransfer`, in `Settle`). The compile-time 2002 mode now calls the same `ToSidi` (one spelling of the model).
