# PB93 — LANDED — `01 CPY-B REDEFINES NOPE PIC 9(2).` with no `NOPE` anywhere compiled clean

> ⚙ **flags:** under-rejects (illegal source accepted), silent · found 2026-08-18 probing [[PB82]]'s copybook
> positions with a deliberately-broken copybook: the "broken" entry drew nothing at all · **landed 2026-08-18**
> (DEVLOG 1318).

**Measured (before)** (`scratchpad/pb82/redef.cob`, `--std 2023`): a WORKING-STORAGE `01 CPY-B REDEFINES NOPE PIC 9(2).`
where no entry named `NOPE` exists compiled with exit 0 and no diagnostic; the program ran.

**Spec:** §13.18.44.3 SR4 ("No entry having a level-number numerically lower than the level-number of data-name-2
may occur between the data description entries of data-name-2 and the subject of the entry"), SR7 ("Multiple
redefinitions of the same storage area shall each specify as data-name-2 the data-name of the entry that
originally defined the area") and SR10 ("The entries giving the new descriptions of the storage area shall follow
the entries defining the area of data-name-2") all presuppose that data-name-2 names a PRECEDING entry, and
§8.4.2.1 requires that "a statement shall contain a reference that uniquely identifies that resource".
§13.18.45.3 SR4: RENAMES data-name-2/-3 "shall be names of elementary items or groups of elementary items in the
same record".

**Mechanism (as found):** `DataBinder.ResolveRedefines` looked the target up in the sibling scope with a
whole-scope `FirstOrDefault` and, when nothing matched, reported ONLY the METHOD-scope case (COBOLNET1577); a
program-scope miss left `RedefinesTarget` null while `RedefinesTargetName` stayed set — and the two disagreed
downstream: `BitLayout` / `ImageWidth` / `ByteWidth` skipped the item as an overlay while the emitter gave it its
own field. The whole-scope lookup also bound a REDEFINES to a LATER sibling (illegal, SR10) and picked the FIRST
of duplicate names (NOTE 1 wants the nearest preceding). The RENAMES loop `continue`d silently on an unresolved
FROM/THRU, leaving a picture-less alias every reference failed on downstream.

## As built (DEVLOG 1318)

- The lookup is `scope.TakeWhile(s => !ReferenceEquals(s, item)).LastOrDefault(name match)` — the NEAREST PRECEDING
  same-level sibling (SR4/SR7/SR10 + NOTE 1). A miss is **COBOLNET1654** (`RedefinesTargetUnresolved`) and the
  entry binds as an ORDINARY item (`RedefinesTargetName` cleared — settable now — so no consumer sees the
  half-state); the method-scope miss keeps its 1577.
- SR7 sweep: a REDEFINES naming an entry that is itself a redefinition is **COBOLNET1656**
  (`RedefinesOfRedefinition`) — error strict, warning + the anchor-chased chain under `--permissive`
  (`EditionContext.Removed`, the documented-dialect-leniency seam; GnuCOBOL/IBM accept chains).
- RENAMES: an unresolved FROM / THRU is **COBOLNET1655** (`RenamesOperandUnresolved`), positioned at the level-66
  entry.
- Sweep result: OCCURS DEPENDING ON (0851), SAME AS (`SameAsReferencedEntry`), TYPE (1530) already diagnosed
  their unresolved names — no change.

**Evidence:** negatives `pb93-redefines-undefined-target`, `pb93-redefines-later-sibling` (1654),
`pb93-redefines-of-redefinition` (1656), `pb93-renames-undefined-operand` (1655), all at 85 and 2023;
`RedefinesTargetTests` (the permissive chain runs with the shared area: `MOVE "xy" TO C` shows `xyxyxy`; the legal
shapes still bind). Inventory SR-13.18.44.3-4/-7/-10 and SR-13.18.45.3-4 → CONFORMS.
