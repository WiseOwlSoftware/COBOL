# PB5730 - widen from the stored KEPT rows; the 1985 widening batch

**What is wrong.** Kb/Work PB5675 makes a KEPT row of every older edition close only on a witness of its own, a
conformance-suite case that runs at the row's edition. With it 1985 reads 835 GAP, 803 of them KEPT rows with no such
witness (571 with no target witnessed at 85, 232 with some). The tool that gives a row that witness without writing a
new program is `python scripts/spec/lineage_reads.py widen`: it finds the suite cases of the row's targets that could
run at the edition (every rule they cite exists there), proves each by running it at the edition, and adds the edition
to the band of the ones that pass. But `widen_plan` takes its rows from READ DECISIONS (`decide(...)` over the read
records of an `import-reads` run, `--state` is required), and 1985 has no read records: its 1,373 KEPT records are
stored records of the earlier comparison audit. So the 803 rows have no widening path at all.

**Measured** (the source line above; a scratch prototype that imports the branch's own `case_status`, `Witnesses` and
`Lineage`, and proves nothing by running): of the 803 rows, 483 are `widenable` (every target that lacks a witness has
a case that would help) and 320 `needs-own-witness`. The cases: 322 `widen`, 43 `widen-refusal`, 175 `band`, 149 that
already run at 85; a greedy cover of the widenable rows needs 200 cases. Why a row needs its own witness, by lacking
target: 180 `band` (its cases cite a rule 1985 does not have), 131 no suite case at all, 62 a case that runs at 85 but
is a diagnosis for a rule whose kind takes none, 13 refusal-only. At 2002 the same path proved 158 of 184 and 75 of 100
candidates (the operator's two runs, `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-20261115/FOR-MYTHOS-gl2002-lessons.md` item 8).

**Fix direction.** One population for `widen`: the KEPT rows of the edition that lack a witness of their own, whether
the record is stored or is a read decision refused only for its evidence; `--state` needed only where reads exist. The
classification (`case_status`, the helping rule) and the proof-first `--apply` are unchanged. Each row that needs its
own witness says why, because that list is the input of the 1985 witness lane. Then the batch: widen at 1985, write
the proven witnesses as the rows' own test-refs (`witness-batch`, `record_verdicts.py --edition 1985`), and classify
every widening that did not hold.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/lineage_reads.py` (`widen_plan`, `witness_batch`, `main`), `scripts/selftests/spec/lineage_reads/test_reads.py` (`case_06_the_widening_plans_and_edits_only_what_can_witness_the_rule`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md` (§10.6)
