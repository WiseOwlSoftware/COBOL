# PB3570 — a numeric literal moved to a group: its characters, sign included

## What the fuzz run saw

`SIGN` signature, 6 programs, first fz00177: `MOVE -456 TO group` stores the literal's characters, `-45`. This is kb/Work PB1113's
determination, also used by CALL (BY CONTENT to a group formal) and INVOKE.

### The probe (the B1 adjudicator's, verbatim: `adj\B1\probes\litgrp\litgrp.cob`)

```cobol
IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
PROGRAM-ID. LITGRP.
DATA DIVISION.
WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
01 G.
   05 G1 PIC X(3).
01 E PIC X(3).
01 N PIC S99 VALUE -15.
PROCEDURE DIVISION.
MAIN-PARA.
    MOVE -15 TO G
    DISPLAY "LIT-GROUP [" G "]"
    MOVE -15 TO E
    DISPLAY "LIT-ELEM  [" E "]"
    MOVE N TO G
    DISPLAY "ITEM-GROUP [" G "]"
    MOVE N TO E
    DISPLAY "ITEM-ELEM  [" E "]"
    STOP RUN.
```

Output on judge e7cf38ce4: `LIT-GROUP [-15]`, `LIT-ELEM  [15 ]`, `ITEM-GROUP [1N ]`, `ITEM-ELEM  [15 ]`.

## The rules (each re-run through `cite.py --check`, OK)

- §14.9.25.4 4): a move to a group "is treated exactly as if it were an alphanumeric to alphanumeric elementary move, except
  that there is no conversion of data from one form of internal representation to another". An `S99` sender sends its overpunch
  image (`1N`).
- §14.9.25.4 6) (OK): "When an alphanumeric, alphanumeric-edited, national, or national-edited data item is a receiving operand,
  alignment and any necessary space filling shall take place as defined in 14.6.8 …"; its item a) drops the operational sign for
  an elementary receiver, as the `PIC X` line shows.
- §14.9.25.4 4) gives a numeric literal no internal representation to preserve, so the adjudicator recorded LATITUDE: the
  standard does not say what a literal sent to a group stores.

## Why it is a decision

The determination (characters, sign included) is not in `docs/CONFORMANCE.md`. CLAUDE.md rule 1 says that, where the spec leaves
latitude, the precedence is the ISO spec, then GnuCOBOL, then IBM or Micro Focus; GnuCOBOL drops the sign (as WiseOwl does for an
elementary `PIC X` receiver). The choice is an owner question, to be asked by the session, not decided by the clerk. Whatever is
decided, the rule belongs in CONFORMANCE.md with a golden.

## Owner question (bare, wording by the clerk from the sources)

Should a numeric literal moved to a group keep its sign character (today's PB1113 determination, `MOVE -15 TO G` stores `-15`),
or drop the sign as GnuCOBOL does and as WiseOwl does for an elementary alphanumeric receiver?
