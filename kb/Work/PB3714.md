# PB3714 — one verdict-line format, three definitions

**Sites:** `scripts/verdict_line.py` (new: `render`, `parse`, `outcomes`, `conflict_outcome`, `outcome_files`, `conflict_files`, `self_test`), `scripts/orchestrator/enqueue.py` (`land`: `end`; `self_test`), `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` (`last_verdict`, `write_conflict_brief`, `self_test`), `scripts/merge_drivers.py` (`conflict_report`, `main`, `self_test`), `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/LandingLaneDriftTests.cs`, `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/MergeDriverDriftTests.cs`.

## What happens

Every landing outcome ends `=== ENQUEUE: <OUTCOME> (<branch>) ===` (MANDATORY-PRACTICES P2 blocks on it). The prefix is a literal in
`enqueue.py`, a regex over it in `land_lane.py`, and a second literal in `merge_drivers.py` (added by PB3640 so the conflict reader needs no
import of the landing script, which imports the drivers). PB3640's review R1-3 made the CONFLICT outcome one writer and one reader inside
`merge_drivers`, but the line around it still has three homes. A change of the line's shape (a new field, a different terminator) must be
made in all three, and no test fails if one is missed, so a reader and a writer can drift apart unseen.

## Fix

One module owns the verdict line: its prefix, `format(outcome, branch)` and `parse(line)`. `enqueue.py` writes through it, `land_lane.py` and
`merge_drivers.py` read through it, and the other two definitions are deleted in the same change (no alias, CLAUDE.md rule 4). The import
direction needs a leaf module neither script depends on the other for (`merge_drivers.py` must not import `enqueue.py`). A drift test
asserts no other file in `scripts/` spells the literal `=== ENQUEUE: ` outside that module and the documentation.

## Landed (wave 20261107 group D)

**Re-probe.** `grep` confirmed the three spellings (`enqueue.VERDICT`, `land_lane.VERDICT_LINE` over it, `merge_drivers.ENQUEUE_VERDICT`) and the CONFLICT outcome's writer and reader in `merge_drivers`; `enqueue.py`'s own self-test also spelled the prefix through `VERDICT`, and `land_lane.py`'s two fake runners spelled it as a literal.

**Fix.** One leaf module, `scripts/verdict_line.py`, owns the line (`PREFIX`, `render(outcome, branch)`, `parse(line)`, `outcomes(text)`) and the CONFLICT outcome inside it (`conflict_outcome(files)`, `outcome_files(outcome)`, `conflict_files(text)`). `enqueue.py` writes through it, `land_lane.py` and `merge_drivers.py` read through it, and the three old definitions are DELETED (no alias): `enqueue.VERDICT`, `land_lane.VERDICT_LINE`, `merge_drivers.CONFLICT_OUTCOME`/`ENQUEUE_VERDICT`/`conflict_outcome`/`outcome_files`/`conflict_files`. The module is a leaf, so `merge_drivers.py` (which `enqueue.py` imports) needs no import of the landing script. Its own `--self-test` pins writer/reader round trips, a line without a branch, prose that names the prefix, and the CONFLICT outcome. Drift test `LandingLaneDriftTests.NoScriptSpellsTheVerdictLine_OutsideItsOneHome` (and its planted-spelling arm) holds that no other script under `scripts/` opens a string literal with the prefix; `MergeDriverDriftTests` follows the moved names. Docs: `DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` step 2 names the one home.
