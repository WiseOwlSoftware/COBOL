# PB3641 — the group cap's measurement provenance is wrong

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` (`wave.max_notes_per_group_source`, `wave.cost.per_group_tokens`, `wave.cost.per_note_tokens`), `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`group_cost`).

## What happens

PB3611 raised `wave.max_notes_per_group` from 5 to 8. Two review leads on that landing remain open on origin/main.

- **The source string overstates its evidence.** The 66 + 13 turns-per-note line is fitted on the 128 reports whose title names notes (188 of the 316 reports count 0 notes); n >= 3 rests on 12 reports and 6 notes on ONE. 8 is extrapolated past any observed group. The 190-turn SPLIT onset is not computed by `turns_by_group_size.py` and the data contradicts it. The date `2026-10-29` is the wave id, not the date (2026-10-24).
- **The cost constants stay the 2026-10-04 seed**, calibrated on five-note groups. The implementer's own turns line (66 per group : 13 per note, about 5:1) contradicts the seed split `per_group_tokens : per_note_tokens` of 400k : 240k (1.67:1). The PB3611 fix shape asked for the constants to be re-measured with the cap.

Both are one mechanism: the provenance of the group-cap numbers in `model_rules.json`.

## Fix direction

Recompute the fit on the reports that name their notes and state n per group size; state 8 as an extrapolation or measure a group of 8; fix the date; re-fit `per_group_tokens` and `per_note_tokens` from transcripts (the `r2_cost.py` shape) or state in the `_source` strings that they are an unmeasured seed. Keep one key, one cap.

## Source

Report `w20261029c-PB3611-report.md` (PB3611, landed in PR #495), review leads R1-1 and R1-2.

## Landed

The measurement is tracked now: `python scripts/orchestrator/turns_by_group_size.py` (with a `--self-test`) joins each
report in `coord.reports_dir()` to its group's dispatch spec by wave and letter and counts the notes the spec's first
line names (the planner's own list), never the report title's ids. On 2026-10-24 it joined 287 reports (378 have no
turns figure, 29 no spec, 5 an ambiguous spec): 1 note n=185 mean 81 turns, 2 notes n=59 mean 115, 3 notes n=16 mean
158, 4 notes n=7 mean 161, 5 notes n=18 mean 159, 6 and 7 notes one report each; line about 61 + 23 per note. So the
old string was wrong in its direction, too: a group of 8 sits near 245 turns, past the 220-turn cap, and groups of 3 to 5
SPLIT about half the time. `wave.max_notes_per_group_source` now states that, says 8 is extrapolated past the largest
observed group, gives the cap's real reason (the landing rate, PB3611), fixes the date (2026-10-24) and drops the
190-turn claim. `cost.source` states that `per_group_tokens` and `per_note_tokens` are still the unmeasured 2026-10-04
seed, why the turns split disagrees with it, and what a token re-fit needs (per-agent transcript tokens joined to the
group size, which no record carries). The cap stays one key at 8.
