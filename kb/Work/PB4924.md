# PB4924 - one MEDIAN mean, two kinds on the two channels

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/FunctionValueField.cs` (`FunctionValueField.Median`, `FunctionValueField.OfBinary64`, `FunctionValueField.OfFloat`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.ExactValue.cs` (`CobolIntrinsics.Median`)

**Report (low; ordering only; not probed).** After PB4891, `CobolIntrinsics.Median` (COMPUTE) returns the binary64 mean as `CobolExact.FromBinary64`, a float item's
content, which compares by its exact value, while `FunctionValueField.Median` (DISPLAY) returns `OfFloat`, an approximation temporary that compares by
its shortest image. `§2023:15.61.4 GR2` (checked: "the arithmetic mean of the two middle values") fixes the value, not the kind. A MAX or MIN over a nested
even MEDIAN beside a fixed value equal to the mean's shortest image can therefore select differently on DISPLAY and COMPUTE. Expected: one kind on both
channels. The implementer's suggested fix is `OfBinary64(mean, mode)`, which needs a rounding/intermediate mode threaded into `FunctionValueField.Median`
(its callers `FunctionDeviceText` already pass `num.IntermediateMode` elsewhere). The fix adds a probe: a fixed value equal to the mean's shortest
image, `MAX (MEDIAN (F M2 M3 N) V)`, DISPLAY against COMPUTE, golden 2002/2014/2023.

Source report and lead: `w20261905b-PB4891-report.md`, New defects 2.

**Fix (landed, wave 20261915).** Re-probed on f19719cfaf: `DISPLAY MAX (MEDIAN (F M) V)` with F the double of 0.1, M 0.5, V PIC 9V99 0.30 showed 0.3
by the old image ordering where COMPUTE selects V (the mean's double 0.29999999999999998889 is below 0.30). `FunctionValueField.Median` now takes the
intermediate mode and returns `OfBinary64(mean, mode)`, ordered by its exact value as `CobolIntrinsics.Median`'s `FromBinary64` is; DISPLAY now shows 0.30,
and `MIN (V MEDIAN (F M))` shows 0.3. Golden `pb4924_even_median_mean_is_one_binary64_kind_on_display_and_compute` plus its `_below_2002` refusal.
Sibling sweep: `OfFloat` has no other binary64-mean producer (`FunctionValueField.OfExact` already routes a marked value to `OfBinary64`).

**Sites (as changed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/FunctionValueField.cs` (`FunctionValueField.Median`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Roslyn/RuntimeApi.cs` (`RuntimeApi.FunctionFieldMedian`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/FunctionDeviceText.cs` (the MedianScaled arm), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt`.
