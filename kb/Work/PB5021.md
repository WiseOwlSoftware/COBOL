# PB5021 - one convention for KEPT and CHANGED when 2023 adds only what 1985 cannot write

**Sites:** `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md` (section 10.2, the change kinds), `scripts/spec/audit_batch.py` (`PROMPTS`: adjudicate, recheck)

**Report.** Nine of the 36 adjudicators of PB4995's contested records asked the coordinator for one rule. Most slices
(08, 09, 11, 13, 24, 27, 36) recorded KEPT when the 2023 change is reachable only through syntax or a feature 1985
lacks (a new phrase, national or boolean operands, RECURSIVE, a feature off by default): a conforming 1985 program means
the same. General formats follow PB4996's doctrine, CHANGED when the 2023 diagram accepts other programs. Slice 21
recorded a lifted 1985 refusal (looser syntax a 1985 program could be refused for) as CHANGED, and slice 20 noted the
two conventions differ. A lifted refusal matters at `--std=85`: the 1985 compiler must still refuse what 1985 refused,
so whether its record is KEPT or CHANGED decides whether the lineage carries the 2023 test there.

**Cause (Mythos session, 2026-11-09).** The design already states the rule: KEPT is "the same programs are accepted or
refused" (DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md section 10.2's table, and the adjudicate and recheck prompts in
`audit_batch.PROMPTS`). The fleet's brief, written by the coordinating session, paraphrased it as "a conforming 1985
program means the same under 2023", which drops the refusals, so most slices recorded a 2023 addition reachable only
through 1985-absent syntax as KEPT. The 1985 compiler must still refuse that syntax, so those records are CHANGED. The
gate's EDITION GATES audit caught 8 (START reverse operators, INSPECT BACKWARD, zero-length reference modification,
word length 63, underscore in user words, PROCEDURE RETURNING, VALUE table format, REPLACE ALSO/LAST). The seeded
recheck (`state-adj712`), whose prompt carries the design's rule, contests every such record, and each goes back to a
`cobol-adjudicator` whose brief quotes the design's definition verbatim.

**Expected:** DESIGN section 10.2 states the rule once (KEPT: every program the 1985 rule admits or refuses is admitted
or refused, and means the same, under 2023; a 2023 addition reachable only through 1985-absent syntax does not change
that; a lifted refusal does), the adjudicate and recheck prompts carry it, and the records PB4995's seeded recheck
contests on this ground are decided by it.

**Conflict seen 2026-11-09 (review lead R1-2 of w20261974a, PB5062 and PB5092).** Two conventions now sit side by side in one
1985 section: `1985-GR-VI.6.18.4-3` (INSPECT GR3) is KEPT on the ground that this rule refuses neither of the cases 2023 adds
(national and boolean categories, which 1985 SR1's USAGE IS DISPLAY excludes), while the PB5092 INSPECT family ruling
(`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/sib-85/out/inspect-leftmost-r2.json`) made five siblings CHANGED for BACKWARD, rejecting the ground
that the general format, not the rule, refuses the 2023-only case. The proposed rule above (a 2023 addition reachable only
through 1985-absent syntax does not change KEPT) would make the BACKWARD five KEPT again; the family ruling would make GR3
CHANGED. Deciding this note decides both, and the records follow.

**Re-probe, and what the placement needs (wave 20261977 group B, 2026-11-10).** Still open on main b91ec90f41: section
10.2's table says KEPT is "the next edition states the same requirement", and the adjudicate and recheck prompts
(`audit_batch.PROMPTS`) "the same programs are accepted or refused, with the same effect"; neither says whether that
compares the programs the 1985 RULE governs or every program, so neither settles a 2023 case reachable only through syntax
1985 lacks. This note's own Cause (those records are CHANGED, as the EDITION GATES audit needs) and Expected (such an
addition does not change KEPT) answer it oppositely, so the choice is the owner's, one question:

> When 2023 adds to a 1985 rule only a case that 1985's general format cannot write (INSPECT BACKWARD, national or
> boolean operands), is the 1985 rule KEPT or CHANGED?

The placement of PB4995 item 3 does not wait on it: a placed test names the 1985 rule, whatever its kind, and only the
recording follows the kind (a CHANGED or REMOVED rule's witness is recorded as its `test-ref`; a KEPT rule's is its
chain's). Of the 709 witnesses placed, 141 sit on KEPT records and are unrecorded; the answer re-records whichever of them
it makes CHANGED with `place_witnesses.py`'s batch, rerun, and no test moves.
