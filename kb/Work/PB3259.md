# PB3259 — r2_collect.same_mechanism merges distinct defects into one note on site overlap

**Symptom:** the loop's review of notes-PB1754-r2b4b-20261016
(E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/review-notes-PB1754-r2b4b-20261016.jsonl, 2026-10-16) found PB3232 filing three distinct
defects as one (SUM-counter ambiguity in SumCounterFamilyFor; a contained program's ADDRESS OF / BY REFERENCE of a
GLOBAL record resolved in the container scope; ResolveForAddressOf dropping the 13.7.3 SR4 screen), PB3234 merging
validation-1--architecture--f2#1 (FlagConformancePass.Run builds compilation-group-wide base-name lookups) into a
StripLiteral-to-CobolLiteral.Decode note, and PB3226 carrying `rejects_legal_source: true` from a merged sub-finding
that PB2586 fixed on main after the pin (4b7966db0). The review of notes-PB1754-r2b5b-20261016
(E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/review-notes-PB1754-r2b5b-20261016.jsonl, R1-4 and R1-5) found the second shape: a
TYPE-LEVEL member prefix match. data-binding-8--architecture--f1#1 (the TierCIsland boundary decided by 14 emit-time
guards) merged into PB3214 (DataBinder.RangeCollations) because its member CobolNet.CodeGen.Emit.ConditionRenderer
prefix-matched PB3214's ConditionRenderer.RangeCollateArg, and data-binding-7--duplication--f1#2 (the separate-sign
width rule written three times) merged into PB3221 (picture geometry recomputed) because its member
CobolNet.Binding.PictureAnalyzer prefix-matched PictureAnalyzer.AnalyzeCharacterString; in both, collected.json's
`existing_note` was empty and the filer's `same_mechanism` match against the existing note did the merge.
The finisher of that branch split all five out (PB3286 to PB3289; the sign finding moved into PB3252 by judgment).

**Measurement:** `scripts/arch/r2_collect.py` `same_mechanism` merges two findings of one wave kind when their sites overlap
OR when `members_overlap` finds one member a dotted prefix of the other (so a finding that names a whole type,
such as CobolNet.CodeGen.Emit.ConditionRenderer, matches every note naming any member of that type); the merged
ones are kept only as "Also found as" lines with no target. `file_census_notes.file_r2` applies the same test
against already-filed notes, so the merge also crosses batches.

**Root cause:** overlap of code sites, or of a type and one of its members, is not sameness of mechanism (rule,
target and scenario).

**Target:** merge only when the finder's rule AND target statements match; otherwise file separately and cross-link; a
merged finding whose claim is stale on main (fixed after the pin) sets no harm flag; a self-test with the PB3232 shape,
seen failing once, and one with the type-prefix shape (a type-level member against a member of that type, rules
and targets distinct), seen failing once.

**Sites:**
- `scripts/arch/r2_collect.py` (same_mechanism)
- `scripts/arch/file_census_notes.py`
