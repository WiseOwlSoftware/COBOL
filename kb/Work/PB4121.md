# PB4121 — three SetBinder guards still open their message with an elided 'SET …' (SET … TO ADDRESS OF FUNCTION, SET … TO

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SetBinder.cs:809` (`SetBinder.BindSetFunctionAddress`, `SetBinder.BindSetEntry`, `SetBinder.BindSetProgramAddress`, `SetBinder.BindSetLocale`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/SetDiagnosticNamesReceiversDriftTests.cs:46` (`SetDiagnosticNamesReceiversDriftTests.ElidedEcho`, `SetDiagnosticNamesReceiversDriftTests.NoSetDiagnosticOpensWithAnElidedReceiverList`).

Review lead R1-1 (low, round 1) of PB3966. The PB1314 sweep reworded `PtrBinder.BindSetUpDown`'s no-receiver message to drop its U+2026
and the DEVLOG and kb/Work/PB1314 call it "the one other defect of the kind". Three identical sibling guards in SetBinder.cs still
open with an elided SET: line 828 (`SET … TO ADDRESS OF FUNCTION`, in `BindSetFunctionAddress`), 1011 (`SET … TO ENTRY`, in
`BindSetEntry`) and 1109 (`SET … TO ADDRESS OF PROGRAM`, in `BindSetProgramAddress`), each with no receiving operand (lines as on the
reviewed branch; members span 809-911, 987-1038, 1079-1160 on main). `SetDiagnosticNamesReceiversDriftTests.ElidedEcho` (the regex at
line 50) keys on `Error(` only, so it cannot see any `BoundRejected.Report(ctx.Edition, code, "SET …")` site: the drift test's blind
spot is the mechanism, the three guards are its instances.

**Fix:** (1) make the three messages name the receivers as the written statement has them (or the operand it does have); (2) widen the
drift test to the `BoundRejected.Report(` call form, so the next one is automatic (rule 5); (3) sweep other binders' `Report(` sites
for a message opening `VERB …`.

Source: report `w20261769g-PB3966-report.md`, review lead R1-1; PB3966 (landed). Related PB1314, PB388.

## Landing

Re-probe on the branch build: the three guards are unreachable from source (`setEntryStatement`, `setFunctionAddressStatement` and
`setProgramAddressStatement` all print `setReceivingIdentifier+`, so `SET TO ENTRY 'X'` is `COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near 'TO'`);
they are defensive guards, so no source golden can reach them and the drift test is their witness. Fixed: the three messages now read
`SET TO ADDRESS OF FUNCTION / TO ENTRY / TO ADDRESS OF PROGRAM with no receiving operand` (the statement as written, as PtrBinder's
`SET UP BY / DOWN BY with no receiving operand` does). `SetDiagnosticNamesReceiversDriftTests.ElidedEcho` now keys on `Error(` and
`Report(` call forms, and its fires-on-the-defect test feeds it the `BoundRejected.Report` form (proved RED with one message reverted).
Sibling found in the same file and fixed: `BindSetLocale`'s duplicate-category message opened `SET LOCALE … {w}` with the earlier
categories in hand; it now names them (`SET LOCALE LC_TIME LC_TIME: …`). Sweep (3): about forty messages in other binders open
`VERB … PHRASE` (CALL, READ, WRITE, START, INSPECT, OCCURS, CODE-SET); they elide a phrase list, not a receiver list, and are reported as a
lead, not fixed here.
