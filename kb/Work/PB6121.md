# PB6121 - three leftovers of the PB5712 float-edited digit-count fix

1. **Wrong designator.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs:1451` cites the exponent digit-position rule as
   "§2002:13.16.38.4 ER8", and so do `g2002-pic-float-edited-digits-total-refused.cob` (line 10), `g2002-pic-float-edited-bounds-accepted.cob`
   (line 12), the row 2002-SR-13.16.38.2-6 notes and the PB5712 pending DEVLOG. ER is not a rule designator; the 2002 editing rules are
   general rules and the designator is GR8. It was inherited from the round-1 finding text and not re-derived. Re-derive with `cite.py --check`.
2. **COBOL1658 message.** `PictureAnalyzer.cs:1458` prints "the PICTURE counts {countedDigits} digit position(s) (significand {digits},
   exponent {exponentDigits})" at every edition. From 2014 `countedDigits` is the significand alone, so the message states a total that is
   not the sum of the parts it lists and implies the exponent counts.
3. **Docs stale.** `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md:530` (VCR row 7.118) still says a 31-digit significand at 2002; the 2002 bound is
   significand plus exponent digits totalling 31 (largest significand 30 with `+9`). The `DialectBehavior.FloatEditedPictureWideBounds` summary
   (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DialectBehavior.cs`, near line 588) does not say the exponent counts (rule 6).

**Landed (wave 20262158 E).** (1) `ER8` is now `§2002:13.16.38.4 GR8` (cite.py --check OK: "all digit positions of the significand and all digit positions of the exponent shall be zero") in `PictureAnalyzer.AnalyzeFloatEdited`, the two goldens and the row 2002-SR-13.16.38.2-6 notes. (2) COBOL1658 prints "counts N digit position(s) (significand S + exponent E)" at 2002 and "the PICTURE's significand has S digit position(s)" from 2014, through `EditionInfo.Cite`. (3) VCR row 7.119 (the note's "7.118" after the renumbering) and the `FloatEditedPictureWideBounds` summary already said "significand and exponent counted together" when this wave began; no edit was needed. `closes_rows` stays empty: the row's state does not change.
