# PB5995 - LANDED - an open note's editions are read from its text, not declared

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/edition_status.py` (`work_part`), `scripts/spec/work.py` (`declared_editions`, `register_problems`, `main`), `scripts/spec/work_editions.py` (new), `scripts/spec/gen_ledger.py` (`note_editions`, `branch_of`, `landings`)

## What is wrong
Part 3 of `edition_status.py` (the open notes that touch an edition) decides "touches" by regular expressions over the
note's text. Until the review of 2026-11-20 it read the frontmatter only, and 24 open notes with three or more body
mentions of 1985 (PB4996, PB5692, PB5449 among them) were invisible to it: a false-COMPLETE route (review R1-1). The fix
landed with kb/Work PB5994 reads the body and the addenda too, with a year match for 1985, 2002 and 2014 and a
citation, `--std` or rule-id match for 2023 (whose bare year is the standard's own number in nearly every note). On the
tree of 2026-11-20 that counts 164 open notes for 1985 (93 before), 326 for 2002, 518 for 2023 and 254 for 2014.

Text matching is still the wrong shape (CLAUDE.md rule 5): it counts a note that only mentions an edition in passing,
which buries the notes that block it, and it misses a note about an edition that never spells it (a listing step whose
body says "the listing" and nothing else).

## The structural fix (proposed, not implemented)
1. A frontmatter field `editions: [1985, 2002, 2014, 2023]` (a list, possibly `[]` for a note no edition owns) on every
   note.
2. `work.py check` refuses an OPEN note without it, and refuses a value outside `edition_status.EDITIONS`.
3. A one-time backfill writes the field from today's `EDITION_PATTERNS` match (over-counting is the safe direction),
   then the owners of the most-cited notes narrow it by hand where the match was incidental.
4. `edition_status.touches` reads the field and nothing else; the patterns are deleted with the backfill (rule 4: no
   second mechanism). A drift test keeps an open note whose body cites an edition's text (`§1985:`, `§2002:` ...)
   from declaring `editions` without it.
