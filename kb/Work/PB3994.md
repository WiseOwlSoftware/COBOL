# PB3994 — a `--cluster` wave binds every group to the campaign's requirements, members or not

Found 2026-10-28 by the operator lane. Grepped `kb/Work` for `OWNER REQUIREMENTS`, `always` and `--cluster` wave: PB3359
(landed) made requirements bind by MEMBERSHIP (`cluster:` lists) but kept the `always` parameter for the campaign a
`--cluster` wave runs; PB3379 and PB2120 are other planner-campaign notes and hold neither. No open note holds this.

**Failure scenario (verbatim from the spec).** Wave 20261731 was planned with `plan_wave.py --cluster PB3838`, groups
A PB3972 and B PB2100. PB2100 is a member of campaign PB1086, not PB3838. Group B's rendered spec
`msg-w20261731-b.txt` carries "⛔ OWNER REQUIREMENTS (campaign PB3838, standing; the branch reviewer raises a finding when
your branch lacks one of campaign PB3838's): (1) Every record meets the eight-item bar above ..." That is the
upstream-record bar of campaign PB3838 imposed on a compiler-codec implementer, with the branch reviewer told to raise a
finding when the branch lacks it.

**Mechanism.** `plan_wave.py` main passes `[a.cluster] if a.cluster else []` as `group_requirements`' `always` argument
(`scripts/orchestrator/groups.py`), documented there as "campaigns that bind every group (the one a `--cluster` wave
runs)". A `--cluster` wave can plan a group that is not a member (a fix-lane note absorbed into a finisher on a shared
primary file, or a blocked_by successor), and that group inherits the requirements all the same.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/groups.py` (`group_requirements`, `requirements_text`),
`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`main`, `plan_main`: the group_reqs computation).

**Acceptance.** A group's spec carries the OWNER REQUIREMENTS of the campaigns its own notes belong to (`cluster:`) and
no other: `always` is removed, the planner and `check_practices.py` agree, and a group a `--cluster` wave plans from
the campaign's own members is bound through its membership. A self-test in `test_plan_wave.py` builds a wave of one
member and one non-member group, is seen RED first (the non-member group carries the lead-in), then GREEN.
