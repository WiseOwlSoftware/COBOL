# PB5414 - a USE FOR DEBUGGING section-name subject runs its debugging section on every transfer to the section's first paragraph (GO TO P1, PERFORM P1, a loop back to P1, GO TO P1 OF S2), not once per execution of the section: the section is a trigger at its first pc

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs#AddDebugSectionSubject`
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs#FinalizeDebug`
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/DispatchEmitter.cs#EmitDispatchMethod`

**Mechanism.** `ProcedureTableBuilder.AddDebugSectionSubject` records a section-name subject as a trigger at the pc of the section's first paragraph, and `DispatchEmitter.EmitDispatchMethod` fires every trigger on every entry to its pc. So any transfer to the first paragraph runs the section's debugging section with DEBUG-NAME the section: a loop `GO TO P1` inside S2 re-runs it on each pass though S2 executes once, a `PERFORM P1` runs it as if S2 were performed, and a qualified `GO TO P1 OF S2` runs it though GR9 says a qualifier is no reference. ALL PROCEDURES makes every section such a subject. `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` D-DEBUG-PROCEDURE encodes the behavior ("runs it once, when control reaches the section's first paragraph"); kb/Work PB5083 (landed) fixed only entry at a later paragraph.

**Observed (the pinned compiler of `E:/COBOL-wt/coderead85-tree-c` (fafa91a83c, the head of witnesses-85g; `git diff --stat fafa91a83c origin/main -- src` is empty at 3c4f6fe652), rerun 2026-11-12; `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/reprobe-85i/log.txt`).** `d14` prints `DS [    18][S2` then `DS [    22][S2` twice and `N=3`: S2's section runs three times for one execution of S2. `d11` prints `[S1 ...]` and `[S1-1 ...]` on each of three passes. `d13` prints `DS [    16][S2 ...][PERFORM LOOP` and `DS [    17][S2 ...]` for transfers to P1.

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** The section-name subject's debugging section runs immediately before each execution of the section, that is when control enters the section as a whole, and not for a transfer to its first paragraph from within or by a qualified paragraph reference: `d14` prints one `DS` line.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Make a section-name subject a trigger on the section's own entry (the transfer whose target is the section, or fall-through into it), separate from its first paragraph's trigger, in `ProcedureTableBuilder` and the dispatcher; correct D-DEBUG-PROCEDURE in `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` in the same change.
