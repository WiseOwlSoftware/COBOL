# PB4827 - an even MEDIAN over a table(ALL) with a run-time count (OCCURS DEPENDING or dynamic capacity) keeps the exact-lane selection, because its parity is not a bind-time fact

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** New defects 2. Even MEDIAN over a table(ALL) with a run-time count: see the PB4791 report of the reviewed branch (IntrinsicCarrierRules.cs ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair). The landed PB4791 note words it: a table(ALL) over an OCCURS DEPENDING or dynamic-capacity range has a RUN-TIME count: its parity is not a bind-time fact, and that list stays a selection, the lane it had (a lead in the wave report: the exact lane's even mean would have to land as a binary64 when both middle values are binary64-derived). Re-read: IntrinsicCarrierRules.cs:167 still returns false when `all.StaticCount` is null.

**R1.** kb/Work PB4791 (landed) and PB4753 (landed) are the two earlier arms.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/IntrinsicCarrierRules.cs` (`IntrinsicCarrierRules.ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/Place.cs` (`TableAllPlace.StaticCount`).

Filed from a review lead of a merged fix-lane branch (wave 20261879 to 20261882) by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

**Fix (landed, wave 20261885).** Re-probed on the wave build: `COMPUTE AF = FUNCTION MEDIAN (U (ALL) FUNCTION EXP10 (FUN3))` over an OCCURS DEPENDING table of three FLOAT-LONG items printed `EVEN NE` against `(U (2) + FUNCTION EXP10 (FUN3)) / 2`. The parity of a run-time table(ALL) count is decided at run time, so the exact lane (which `SelectionNeedsExactLane` picks for two kinds of binary64) now calls `CobolIntrinsics.MedianBinary64Exact`: an odd count returns the selected argument whole as its own kind, an even count is the binary64 mean of the two middle values (`MedianReal`'s rule) landed as a float item. `IntrinsicCarrierRules.MedianParityIsRunTime` is the one reader of "the count is not a bind-time fact" (`ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair` asks it too). Goldens: `pb4827_even_median_through_a_run_time_table_all_lands_by_the_binary64` (OCCURS DEPENDING, 2002) and `pb4827_even_median_through_a_dynamic_capacity_table_all_lands_by_the_binary64` (OCCURS DYNAMIC, 2014), each with a refused twin.

**Sites (as changed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/IntrinsicCarrierRules.cs` (`IntrinsicCarrierRules.MedianParityIsRunTime`, `IntrinsicCarrierRules.ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs` (`IntrinsicRenderer.RenderDec`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.ExactValue.cs` (`CobolIntrinsics.MedianBinary64Exact`).
