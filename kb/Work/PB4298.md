# PB4298 - The 2002 floating debugging indicator `>>D` is not recognized in either reference format: COBOL0001 "unexpected '>'" at `--std 2002`

Found by the Mythos front-end review, gap pass, 2026-10-31 (GAP-REVIEW.md, G1-1; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/KaxkLe85tfZBZ3nZ1tDGTs). Pin d7daee1cf.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.LogicalConversion.cs` (`FreeFormConverter.Convert` :250-266, `FixedFormConverter.ConvertLine` default arm, `KindOf` 'D' :62-72, the `LineKind.Debugging` case :103-111, the arm to share); `CompilerDirectiveLine.TryParse` :48-70; `CompilerDirectiveCatalog.Find` :45-47; `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` row debugging-line-removed-2014 :593-599; `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` D-DEBUG :1980; `Parsing/DebuggingLineRewriter.cs` (consumer, unchanged).

**Finding.** The 2002 floating debugging indicator `>>D` (t2002.txt:2163 "may be used in fixed-form or free-form reference format", :2190, SR7 :2221, SR8 :2222) is refused with COBOL0001 "unexpected '>'" in BOTH reference formats (`G1-refute/a1`, `a2` free; `a3` fixed, at (7,8)). `cobc -std=cobol2002` accepts all three; `-std=cobol2014` refuses ("debugging indicator does not conform"), the compiler's own debugging-line-removed-2014 edge. 2023 section 6.2.3.1 has no `>>D`, so the window is 2002 only. The fixed-D twin `a6` compiles with COBOL0903 and runs DEBUG/ALWAYS. PB1802:103-106 and PB1705:64 flagged `>>D` as unimplemented without filing it: a dropped thread.

**Fix direction.** Both converters classify a line whose program text begins, after spaces only (SR7), with `>>D` plus at least one space (the 2002 table requires the space; "or end of line" is not in the text) as LineKind.Debugging feeding the one DebugLineCarrier. SR8 refuses a floating indicator on a line with a fixed D or `-`. Widen row debugging-line-removed-2014 and D-DEBUG to both spellings.

**Citations checked** (`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`): section 6.2.3.1 "Floating indicators may be used in fixed-form or free-form reference format".

**Evidence.** E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/GAP-REVIEW.md (sections 2 and 5.1); G1-refute/a1, a2, a3, a6; review-G1.md. Probe names are copied from the shard reports; the refuter folders are E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/G*-refute/. Shard reports: E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/review-G*.md.
