# PB273 — the native Dec lane's stale census

Found by the arithmetic-wave review fleet over the D-B/D-C/PB194/PB195 working tree, 2026-08-31, and fixed in the change set that landed the wave. Every number below was re-derived independently by the implementer (a 34-digit emulation of `CobolDec.cs` validated against the shipped `pb167` goldens, and 80–120-digit `Decimal` truth) BEFORE the confirming run, and then confirmed by it.

Three producers, not one: an integer power (`PowNativeIntDec`), a floating-point numeric-EDITED sender lifted by
`FieldNum` in every mode, and — much the most common since owner decision D-B — a floating-point LITERAL operand
(`LiteralNum`). All three reach the same `(a.Dec || b.Dec) && !a.Real && !b.Real && !_rcv.Real` branch, for the
same reason: a Dec has no compile-time scale.

TWO SITES, not one — the same sentence was written down twice (feedback_one_rule_one_place):
`NumericRenderer.CombineCore` and `IntrinsicRenderer.RenderNum`'s native-Dec-argument arm. Both are
rewritten to the census, and `CombineCore` also gains the cost the branch actually carries, which is now a
published determination rather than an aside — see PB274.
