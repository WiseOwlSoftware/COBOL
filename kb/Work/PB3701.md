# PB3701 — the turn cap is 400 in the rule and 220 in the spec

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/coord.py` (`agent_turn_cap`, `AGENTS_DIR`, `_self_test`), `.claude/skills/workstream/make_dispatch_specs.py` (`render`, `IMPLEMENTER`), `.claude/skills/workstream/check_practices.py` (`turn_cap_copies`, `CAP_COPIES`, `RENDERED_TURN_CAP`, `SPEC`, `BRIEFS`, `main`), `scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py` (the 7c2 block), `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/dispatch-spec-implementer.md` (the two `{turn_cap}` places), `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/fix-lane-implementer-brief.md` and `implementer-brief.md` (no typed number), `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/MANDATORY-PRACTICES.md` (P5, O4), `.claude/skills/workstream/references/cost-law-and-budget.md` (the cap table), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md` (section 4.1), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` (3.14.9).

The owner raised the implementer cap from 220 to 400 on 2026-09-30 (kb/Work R67) because a batch of fixes does not fit 220; MANDATORY-PRACTICES P5 and `.claude/agents/cobol-implementer.md` (`maxTurns: 400`) say so. Every rendered implementer spec still says `Turn cap 220 — at the cap, checkpoint, write NEXT, return SPLIT`, and `check_practices.py` REQUIRES that text, so the rule the owner set cannot reach an agent. PB3641's measurement (`scripts/orchestrator/turns_by_group_size.py`, 287 reports) puts a group of eight notes at about 245 turns, past 220: groups of three to five split about half the time, and each split is a finisher dispatch. One value in one place: the cap lives in `model_rules.json` (or P5) and the template, the check and the brief read it, with a drift test that fails when a copy differs. Whether eight notes per group still fits once the cap is 400 is the owner's question (PB3641's lead).
