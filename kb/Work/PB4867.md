# PB4867 - MessagingProtocol.OnActionFailed: a RECEIVE ended with a CLAIM in flight leaves the later GRANT unacked

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R2-3 LEAD (low): `MessagingProtocol.cs:378` ActionFailed that ends a RECEIVE with a CLAIM in flight leaves the later GRANT unacked; left for the clerk (design 5.4.4 deadline wait).

The report names the design's section 5.4.4 deadline wait as the rule that applies.

**Not probed.** The clerk reproduced nothing and did not read design 5.4.4.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Messaging/MessagingProtocol.cs` (`MessagingProtocol.OnActionFailed`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-message-control.md` (`section 5.4.4`).

**Landed (wave 20261887 D).** Re-probe: `MessagingProtocolTests.AReceiveEndedImpWithAClaimInFlightClosesTheClaimedLinkSoTheRequestorGoesOnToANewRound` failed before the fix (the SEND never completed and the later GRANT was dropped). `CompleteReceive` now closes the claimed link of a RECEIVE that ends with its CLAIM in flight (design 5.4.2; 5.4.1 step 9: the requestor, still holding the message, starts a new round), except when the server still owes a reply on that requestor's link (`_active` holds an entry for it): then the offer is marked `Offer.InFlight`, the next RECEIVE adopts it without a second CLAIM, and a GRANT that arrives between two RECEIVEs is held on the offer (`Offer.Held`) and taken by that RECEIVE (review round 2 of wave 20261887 D, R1-1; the note text corrected from its R2-2 lead). The deadline wait (5.4.4) is unchanged: that RECEIVE does not complete with a claim in flight. Sites added: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Messaging/MessagingProtocol.cs` (`MessagingProtocol.CompleteReceive`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/MessagingProtocolTests.cs`, `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-message-control.md` (`section 5.4.2`).

Filed from review leads of waves 20261884 and 20261885 by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.
