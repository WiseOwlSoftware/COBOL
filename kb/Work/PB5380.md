# PB5380 - the second and later iterations of a PERFORM ... TIMES, UNTIL or VARYING whose range lies in an independent segment re-enter that segment in its initial state: DispatchEmitter resets __seg after each pass, so the loop head calls __EnterSegment and undoes the range's own ALTER

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/DispatchEmitter.cs#EmitDispatchMethod` (the loop head's `if (__SegOf[__pc] != __seg) __EnterSegment(...)` and the `finally` that restores `__seg`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/AlterSwitchEmitter.cs#EmitSegmentState` (emits `__EnterSegment`, which resets the segment's alterable GO TOs)

**Mechanism.** An iterative PERFORM calls the dispatch method once per iteration. Each call's `finally` restores `__seg` to the caller's segment, so on the next iteration the loop head sees a segment change and calls `__EnterSegment`, which treats the transfer as explicit and returns the independent segment's alterable GO TOs to their initial targets. kb/Work PB5084 (landed) fixed the other INITIAL-state arms of the same rule.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids below. Its row is not recorded: the refuter's OVERTURNED verdict is held for adjudication in `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/held-7.json`.

**Rules (1 claimed; every citation checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `1985-GR-XVI.1.4.3-L2.1` - `§1985:XVI.1.4.3 GRL2.1` (refuter: OVERTURNED, harm: wrong answer): an independent segment is in its last-used state when "Control is transferred implicitly to that segment from a segment with a different segment-number". Under an iterative PERFORM, "an implicit transfer of control occurs between the control mechanism associated with that PERFORM statement and the first statement in that paragraph" for each iteration (`§1985:IV.4.4.2 GR1`).

**Repro.** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/out-reread2/refute-rr-08/p1-iter.cob`: from segment 0, `PERFORM S60 2 TIMES` where S60 (segment 60) ALTERs its own GO TO on the first pass, then `PERFORM T70 VARYING K FROM 1 BY 1 UNTIL K > 2` with the same shape in segment 70; `cobol.exe --std 85 --source-format fixed --run p1-iter.cob`.

**Observed (the pinned compiler of `E:/COBOL-wt/coderead85-tree-b` (origin/main 8147f008b3; `git diff --stat 8147f008b3 origin/main -- src` is empty at 5237495315), rerun 2026-11-12; `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/reprobe-85f/log.txt`).** `S60 INITIAL`, `S60 INITIAL`, `VARYING`, `T70 INITIAL 1`, `T70 INITIAL 2`.

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** `S60 INITIAL`, `S60 ALTERED`, `VARYING`, `T70 INITIAL 1`, `T70 ALTERED 2`: the PERFORM enters the range explicitly once, and each later iteration enters it implicitly in its last-used state.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Enter the independent segment once per PERFORM statement, not once per dispatch call: the iteration's re-entry must not pass through `__EnterSegment`. Sweep the other callers of the dispatch method (SORT and MERGE procedures, USE procedures) for the same per-call reset.
