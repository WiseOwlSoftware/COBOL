# PB2558 — R2's subsystem table, partition check and shards

**Why.** The owner held R2's dispatch (2026-10-07 20:40 PDT) for a Mythos adversarial review of its first brief
(`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1034-R2-brief-adversarial-review.md`). Its B1: the brief's sixteen subsystems were
names, not file sets — `ReferenceResolver.cs` sat in both "data binding" and "reference resolution", and the runtime's
collation, Unicode and globalization code (about 6,000 lines) and the source generator sat in none. B2: one agent per
(subsystem × dimension) could not read the binding family's ~76,000 lines, and nothing recorded what went unread. B7:
no census or member index existed for the pinned commit 8be230068.

**What landed.**
- `scripts/arch/r2_subsystems.py`: the table (17 subsystems — the runtime's collation, Unicode and globalization is
  the seventeenth; each with its kb/Work `area`), the reviewed DOMAIN and its stated exclusions, the PARTITION check
  (a hole or an overlap fails), balanced shards of at most 6,000 physical lines, the census god-class rows per shard,
  and the design block it renders (`--write-design`; `--check` fails when the block is stale). At 8be230068: 1,503
  files, 0 holes, 0 overlaps, 75 shards.
- The census record and member index of 8be230068
  (`docs/rearchitecture/evidence/arch-census/8be23006851a46a17fef584893b00fcbaad3641f.{json,findings.json,members.json.gz}`).
- `ArchReviewFleetDriftTests` (Unit): the partition and the design block on the committed tree, and every fleet
  instrument's self-test.
- DESIGN-architecture-review §3 R2 rewritten to describe the fleet as built.
