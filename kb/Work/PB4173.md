## Landed, 2026-10-30 (wave 20261780, group F)

The repro the note lacked, found by probing every COBOL5506 shape with a following phrase word: an operand REFUSED inside its phrase
returns the rule that failed, and the phrase's remaining word is met by the enclosing list as a clause or a statement of its own.
`05 E PIC X OCCURS "A" TIMES.` drew COBOL5506 and a false COBOL1941 at TIMES (INDEXED likewise); `FD F RECORD CONTAINS "A"
CHARACTERS` / `LINAGE IS "A" LINES` / `BLOCK CONTAINS "A" RECORDS` a false COBOL1970; `PERFORM "A" TIMES ... END-PERFORM` a false
COBOL0306 at the END-PERFORM. Fix at the root: such a fault (hint 0i, COBOL5506, any report kind but the missing token, which
conjures and goes on) is one whose tail the parser cannot place (`OrphansItsTail`, the generalization of PB3843's
`EndsAConstructEarly`), and its recovery discards to the period of the nearest enclosing UNIT, any rule that ends at a separator
period (`EndsWithPeriod`, read from the ATN: the sentence and the entries, no longer the sentence alone). The unit's own period is
consumed when the unit recovers (`FinishUnitTailDiscard`; a stale mark of a loop recovery is reset at every `Recover`).
