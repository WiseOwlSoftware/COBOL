# PB845 — OPEN — one exemption, two possible scopes, and the difference is an over-acceptance

Source: the `PB415` implementer report, item (b). Filed as an adjudication rather than a defect because the
exemption is deliberate and its narrow reading is what the standard's keyword-omitted function reference needs.

## The facts

`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolWords.g4:82` carries a bare `| NATIONAL` alternative in `cobolWord`
while its §8.9-straddling siblings carry `{userWordHere("W")}?`. `reserved-words.json` has NATIONAL
`r85=false` / `r2002=true`, so `01 NATIONAL PIC X.` should be refused from 2002 onward. The exemption is BY
CONSTRUCTION: `scripts/gen-cobol-words.ps1` step 4b excludes the §15 intrinsic function names that collide with
a reserved word (LENGTH, NATIONAL, BIT) because a `cobolWord` occurrence of one of them is the keyword-omitted
function reference §15 permits.

## The two readings

1. **Total exemption (today's tree).** The word is a user-defined word everywhere, at every edition. Cost: an
   over-acceptance of §8.3.2.1 rule 1 in every context — a data-name, a paragraph-name, a section-name may be
   spelled NATIONAL.
2. **Positional exemption.** The word is reserved everywhere EXCEPT where a function-identifier operand is
   expected, which is the only place §15's keyword-omitted reference can appear. Cost: the predicate must know
   it is in a function-argument slot, which is a parser-state question the current `userWordHere` gate does not
   answer.

## What the standard says

§8.9's reserved-word list and §8.3.2.1's rule that a user-defined word shall not be a reserved word are the bar;
§15 is the permission that creates the collision. Neither text scopes the permission explicitly, which is why
this is an adjudication and not a defect: the tree's behaviour is DEFENSIBLE and undocumented, and
`feedback_green_gates_arent_evidence` applies — nothing today would notice if the exemption widened.

## Owner question

Is the intrinsic-name exemption positional or total? (Reading 2 is what §4.2.1 conformance implies; reading 1 is
what every surveyed compiler does today — check GnuCOBOL before answering, per
`feedback_survey_compilers_on_latitude`.)

## Code site

`scripts/gen-cobol-words.ps1` step 4b; `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolWords.g4#cobolWord`. Three
words move together — LENGTH, NATIONAL, BIT — so whichever reading wins needs the
`feedback_edition_gate_sweep` treatment across all three.
