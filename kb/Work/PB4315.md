# PB4315 - Hint 14 (COBOL0307 "a period may be missing") appears or not depending on whether the next sentence's own fault lies within its first 16 tokens

Found by the Mythos front-end review, gap pass, 2026-10-31 (GAP-REVIEW.md, G4-6; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/KaxkLe85tfZBZ3nZ1tDGTs). Pin d7daee1cf.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (hint 14 guard `APeriodLetsTheSentenceFollow()` :950-957, `MaxResimulatedTokens = 16` :1109, `OffendingPosition.APeriodLetsTheSentenceFollow`, `SentenceTypesFrom`); `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/ParseRecoveryHintTruthTests.cs` (PBHT38, PBHT41-51).

**Finding.** Hint 14 (COBOL0307 "A period may be missing") is withheld when the sentence after the missing period has its own fault within the first 16 tokens (MaxResimulatedTokens, CobolErrorStrategy.cs:1109) and given when the fault lies further on (`G4-refute/H1` draws it, H2 and `i10` do not): which hint a user gets depends on that distance. The hint is lost, nothing false is reported. PB906/PB3844 shaped the guard.

**Fix direction.** Replace the 16-token whole-sentence walk in OffendingPosition.APeriodLetsTheSentenceFollow (hint 14 guard :950-957) with the shortest prefix that separates a sentence START from a phrase continuation: a period, then at least TWO tokens taken (PBHT42's `IGNORING LOCK` dies at the second token; a 1-token prefix would break it; PBHT38 and PBHT41-51 all die at token 1 or 2). Pin i10, H1, H2 beside PBHT38/42.

**Evidence.** E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/GAP-REVIEW.md (sections 2 and 5.1); G4/i4, i5, i10, i11; G4-refute/H1, H2; review-G4.md. Probe names are copied from the shard reports; the refuter folders are E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/G*-refute/. Shard reports: E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/review-G*.md.
