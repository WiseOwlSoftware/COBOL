# PB5932 - decision: listing step 6 is split, the one store of names is step 6b

## What step 6 said
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-listing.md` section 11 put two bodies of work in step 6: (a) the ONE store of names, written
first (`NameTableOwnershipDriftTests` red on the branch, then every name table of the binder moved into `SymbolTable` and made
private, every lookup converted to `Identify` or classified, every `CobolNames.Same` / `Comparer` / name comparison moved in
or classified, `SymbolTable.Declarations` returning only `DeclarationFact`, the `CobolName` key), with "the step lands only with
`NameTableOwnershipDriftTests` green"; and (b) the data map, the file map and the margin as views of the finished table.

## The decision
Step 6 builds (b) and the finished table it reads; (a) is step 6b, built and landed before step 7 branches.

## Why
- (a) is the restructuring of the binder's whole name resolution that section 6.7.2's evidence measures: 65 string-keyed
  tables (31 non-private), 194 non-private string-taking members, 124 `CobolNames.Same` calls, 130 `Comparer` uses and 40
  name-property comparisons, across `DataBinder`, `ReferenceResolver`, `NameResolution`, `ProcedureTableBuilder`, the file,
  report, CD, configuration, OO and constant binders. It changes how every reference of every program resolves, so it is a
  landing of its own with its own differential (rule 5: a stated scope is an estimate, the restructuring is the answer; the
  answer here is the restructuring in its own step, not a smaller one).
- (b) does not stand on (a). The data map's entries and attributes are complete by construction: `SymbolTableFinish.Of` reads
  every declared name off the frozen bound model of every unit (the forest's roots in order, each record's subordinates, its
  level-88 and level-66 entries and INDEXED BY index-names, the constant entries and the file-names), whatever table the binder
  kept them in. What (a) guarantees is that every REFERENCE goes through one `Identify` and is recorded, which is step 7's
  occurrences: the cross-reference stands on it, the data map does not.
- Holding (b) until (a) lands would hold the 1985-first listing's data map behind a binder-wide restructuring for no
  correctness gain.

## What step 6b must do (unchanged from the design, section 11 "Step 6b" and section 6.7.1)
`NameTableOwnershipDriftTests` first and red, its failures matching section 6.7.2's evidence; every name table private to the
grown `SymbolTable`; `Identify` the only lookup; `Declarations` returning only `DeclarationFact`; `CobolName`; the
`string-parameter-members.json` and `name-comparisons.json` classifications; `SymbolTable.Declare` replacing
`SymbolTableWriter.Declare` (the attributes stay read off each symbol, section 6.6's step-6 correction); where the grown
`SymbolTable` lives (it is in `compiler.model` today, which is backend input and may not name the binder's constant table:
step 6's first build was refused there by `ArchitectureEdgesDriftTests` and `BackendContractDriftTests`). The data map is
unchanged by it.

## Leads step 6 leaves for 6b or later
- The bound class, factory, object and method units carry no position, so their program attributes (the .NET type above all)
  are not joined to the program map; `OoClassSymbol` needs a declaration position like `BoundUnit.DeclaredAt`.
- (Answered on the step-6 branch by review round 1, kb/Work/PB5925/2026-11-18-listing-step-6-review-r1.md:) the data map
  first listed only each forest's and linkage's roots, its constants and files. Step 6 now walks every declaring structure:
  each method's own LOCAL-STORAGE and LINKAGE roots and type declarations, each interface's methods, the type declarations,
  the CAPACITY registers, the REPORT SECTION (`ReportScope.Declared`) and the COMMUNICATION SECTION (`CommunicationSlice`,
  the cd-name and its implicit area). A SCREEN SECTION is refused (COBOL1560), so its compilation has no data map. Step 6b
  changes where the finishing lives, not what the data map holds.
