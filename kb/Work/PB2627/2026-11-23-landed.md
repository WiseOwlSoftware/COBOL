## Landed (wave 20262154, group C)

Re-probed at ccb23d13e4: `IntrinsicSig.RuntimeMethod` was still the renderer's dispatch key (the catalog wrote `"MaxScaled"` for MAX,
which emits `MaxAt`, `MaxDec`, `MaxExact`, `MaxWide` or `MaxReal`, never `MaxScaled`), and a grep of `src` and `tests` found no
emitter of `CobolIntrinsics.OrdMax` or `OrdMin` (now `params ReadOnlySpan<Int128>`): their only callers were
`SelectionRuleCarrierAgreementTests`. `MaxScaled` and `MinScaled` were called only by `RangeScaled` and `MidrangeScaled`.

The row names no runtime member now. Every consumer keys on the function's catalog name: the renderer's switches
(`case "MAX" or "MIN":`), `RenderDec`, `RenderExactUnary`, `RenderWide`, `RenderWholeAligned`, the string channel,
`FunctionDeviceText`, `FunctionValueLane`'s four sets and the renderer's `StandardValueFixedByRule` and
`EquivalentExpressionUnaryArms`. Each body the renderer emits is named through `nameof`, and the exact-to-binary64 name
convention (`RealMethod`) is the one function-keyed table `IntrinsicRenderer.Binary64Bodies`. The binder's choice of the
string-comparison body is a fact on the call, `BoundIntrinsicCall.ComparesStrings`, so the bound tree carries no dispatch key.
`OrdMax` and `OrdMin` are deleted. `MaxScaled` and `MinScaled` are private helpers of RANGE and MIDRANGE. They were Unshipped
API, so the shipped surface and the ABI pin are unchanged.

`IntrinsicCarrierRuleKeyDriftTests.EveryBackendFunctionKey_IsACatalogFunctionName` holds every function-keyed table and every
string arm label of the renderer and `FunctionDeviceText` to a catalog name, so a body's spelling cannot come back as a key.
`IntrinsicRealArgDriftTests` holds `Binary64Bodies` to every function whose argument run can carry a binary64, and fails on a
`…Real` body no row names.

The string form used to be excluded from the numeric bodies by its key (`"OrdMaxString"`). Each numeric arm keyed on MAX, MIN,
ORD-MAX or ORD-MIN now asks `ComparesStrings` instead. Self-review found that `RenderDec` had been missed: under ARITHMETIC IS
STANDARD, ORD-MAX over strings answered 001 for 002. The guard fixes it, and the golden
`conformance-suite/cases/15.58@2002-ORD-MAX-FUNCTION/g2-ord-max-strings-arithmetic-standard` pins it, because the oracle corpus
has no such call.
