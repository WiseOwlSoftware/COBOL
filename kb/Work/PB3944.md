# PB3944 — merge_drivers.merge_note keeps BOTH versions of a Sites line one side rewrote and the other side changed; work.py check does not see the stale copy

**Sites:** `scripts/merge_drivers.py` (`merge_note`), `scripts/spec/work.py` (the Sites check)

The work-note merge driver's union body merge keeps both versions of a body line that one side rewrote and the other
changed (by design: "kept twice, visible to the reader"). For a `**Sites:**` line the duplicate is silent to
`work.py check`, and a stale site misleads the wave planner (it plans against the wrong file). Seen in PB3837's finisher
merge: the branch's renamed copy of PB3830's OLD Sites line stayed beside main's new one (repaired by hand at `17f5b7c3a`,
review finding R3-1). Repro: merge `722ba0d99` with `6df7e46b6` through the drivers and read `kb/Work/PB3830.md` lines 34-35.

## Acceptance
- Either the driver resolves a doubled `**Sites:**` paragraph (keeps the side that is not a pure rewrite of the other), or
  `work.py check` refuses a note with two `**Sites:**` paragraphs; each seen RED first on the repro above.
- Sweep the other note paragraphs the planner reads (`**Model:**`, `**Fix shape:**`) for the same doubling.
