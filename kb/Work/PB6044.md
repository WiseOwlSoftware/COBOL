# PB6044 - the shard jobs outgrew PB5841's two-times headroom

**Defect.** PB5841 set every CI job's limit at twice its longest recent run and made the nightly check it
(`ci_bounds.py --durations`). The population grew (Conformance 29,080 cases), and the longest shard runs reached 13.5 min
(Windows, nightly 44926331041) and 12.7 / 12.8 min (Linux / Windows, nightly 45047687789) against the 25-minute limit of
`Linux shard` and `Windows shard` in `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml`. The nightly went red on that check both nights.

**Fix.** Both shard limits go to 30 minutes, at least twice the longest run (27 min). `python scripts/ci_bounds.py
--durations` at the change: `=== CI JOB DURATIONS: GREEN (20 runs a workflow, limit at least 2x the longest) ===`.
The growth itself is PB4333's (the program differential, GATE GROWTH step 10); this note only restores the headroom.
