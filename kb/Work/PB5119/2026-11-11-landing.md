## Landing (wave 20261998, group B)

**Re-probe on the build.** `INSPECT G TALLYING N FOR ALL "A" REPLACING CHARACTERS BY "Z"` (G = `E PIC X OCCURS 1 TO 6 DEPENDING ON N`, N 3, "ABABAB") printed `LENGTH 5 ZZZ  ` and `FULL ZZZ  B` at `--std 85` and `--std 2023`: the image was read at length 3 and written back at length 5.

**Rule, per edition (every citation checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).** §2023:14.9.22.4 GR19 fixes "Item identification of any identifier in the format 2 statement ... only once before executing the format 1 statement", and §2023:14.6.4 step 6 puts "length evaluation for an occurs-depending group item" in item identification (the 2002 text, §2002:14.8.21.3 GR18 with §2002:14.5.4 step 6, says the same of a variable-length data item). From 2002 the result is `ZZZBA`. §1985:VI.6.18.4 GR17 fixes only the subscripting; the length is read "at the start of the operation" (§1985:VI.5.8.4 GR3) of each of the two successive statements, so at `--std 85` the result is `ZZZZZ`.

**Fix.** `DialectBehavior.InspectFormat3LengthIdentifiedOnce` (ChangedIn 2002, VCR row 7.56). `InspectBinder.Bind` reads it: from 2002 it pins the DEPENDING ON object of a format 3's occurs-depending identifier-1 through `StatementBinder.FreezeOdoExtentForStatement`, a thin entry over the existing `FreezeOdoExtentBeforeRefMod`, a pre-op copy placed after identifier-1's own subscripts and before every later operand, so the read and the write-back share one extent; below 2002 it sets `BoundInspect.ReplacingTakesLengthAnew`, and `InspectEmitter.EmitBody` re-reads identifier-1 after the TALLYING counters are stored. Goldens: `14.9.22-INSPECT/pb5119_format3_odo_length` (2002 2014 2023) and `pb5119_format3_odo_length_1985` (85). No refusal golden: nothing is refused.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/InspectBinder.cs` (`Bind`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundInspect.cs` (`BoundInspect.ReplacingTakesLengthAnew`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/StatementBinder.SubscriptSegments.cs` (`FreezeOdoExtentForStatement`, over the existing `FreezeOdoExtentBeforeRefMod`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/InspectEmitter.cs` (`EmitBody`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DialectBehavior.cs` (`InspectFormat3LengthIdentifiedOnce`).
