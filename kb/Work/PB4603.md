`CheckAlignedClausesPass.cs#DescribeAlignedSubject` (about line 95) writes "an elementary boolean item whose usage is DISPLAY" for every `PicCategory.Boolean` subject. A boolean item can have usage national (ISO 13.18.60.3 SR12: "An elementary data item with usage national shall be described with a picture character-string that describes a boolean, national, national-edited, numeric, or numeric-edited data item"), and that subject is told something untrue. PB1654 fixed the same untrue face in the group arms and left this one.

**Fix shape.** The sentence states the item's real usage, or does not state one. Add a golden for a USAGE NATIONAL boolean subject.

**Site:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/CheckAlignedClausesPass.cs#DescribeAlignedSubject`.
