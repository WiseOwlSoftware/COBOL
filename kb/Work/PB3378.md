# PB3378 — a drain the owner ordered is carried by prose, and prose gets dropped

Found by the operator (account 2), 2026-10-18 08:56 PDT. Grepped `kb/Work` for `drain` with `handoff` or `steer`.
PB3150 (landed) made `plan_wave.py --drain` possible, and PB2699 is about a stale register; no note covers how the drain
reaches a unit.

**The rule.** The owner's drain decision (2026-10-13): over the daily pace, no new work items; only in-process work
lands. The loop's scope for 2026-10-17/18 (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\operator-steer-restart-20261017.md` section 00 item
6) applies it: ordinary waves plan finishers of started work only, and campaign waves plan only the campaign's notes.

**How it reaches a unit today.** `scripts/orchestrator/units/wave.md` step 2: "Under the owner's DRAIN (a steer or the
last handoff names it) add `--drain`." A steer is consumed by the one unit it names (PB2596). After that the drain lives
only as long as every handoff's summary repeats it. `orchestrate.ps1` has no drain setting of its own; it substitutes
`{BORROW_DAYS}` and the cluster into the unit's prompt, but nothing for the drain.

**What happened.**
1. Unit `wave` (session 80335ee2, 08:54:14) was named by a handoff whose summary did not mention the drain.
2. It ran `plan_wave.py` without `--drain` and planned wave 1120:
   - A, the PB1154 review finisher (started work);
   - B PB3365, C PB3358, D PB3273 and E PB3355: new fix-lane groups in no campaign, outside the owner's SORT and
     file I/O overage scope.
3. The Workflow `wf_ebc64425-1e2` launched all five at 08:56:31. The operator created `STOP-UNIT` at 08:57 and the
   loop's fleet stop `scratch\STOP-loop` at 08:58, and steered the next unit to a drain-only re-plan.

**Harm.** A standing owner decision is enforced only by a sentence surviving every handoff. The first dropped sentence
spends quota on work the owner did not approve, here on a day already past its allowance.

**The bar for the fix.**
- The drain is a supervisor setting, like `-BorrowDays` and `-Cluster`: for example `orchestrate.ps1 -Drain`. It is
  recorded in the loop's state and substituted into the wave unit's `plan_wave.py` command, so no handoff can drop it.
  Campaign units keep planning their cluster.
- The operator turns it on and off with the supervisor's own switch, never with prose.
- A test pins that a wave unit's rendered prompt carries `--drain` whenever the supervisor runs with the setting.

**Skills to update in the same change** (owner 2026-10-18: keep the skills in sync with every fix):
- `scripts/orchestrator/units/wave.md` step 2: the drain comes from the substituted argument, not from prose;
- `.claude/skills/workstream/SKILL.md`: the loop paragraph's switches and the Steering bullet;
- the public base's `skills/agent-fleet/references/orchestrator-loop.md`: the steering section. It already says a
