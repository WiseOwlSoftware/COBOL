# PB4230 — procedureDivision's `sentence* procedureUnit*` makes every paragraph after a section header ambiguous (section's or division's); full-context LL scans to the next section header at each paragraph, so the LL pass is quadratic in a section's paragraphs: 99.6 s for a LEGAL 400-paragraph program with one `SET p TO NULL`, DB201A 55 s

Found by the Mythos front-end review 2026-10-31 (GRAMMAR-REVIEW.md, N1; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/VpxTVcaFUsArrq59EvUe5r). Pin 84cdb410f.

**Harm:** none of the four flags (legal output unchanged); the cost is minutes-long compiles of legal source.

**Mechanism.** `procedureDivision : sentence* procedureUnit*` with `procedureUnit = section | paragraph` leaves every paragraph after a section header ambiguous between the section's paragraph and the division's. The review measured 0 context sensitivities and 290 ambiguities; the alt-1 configurations merge into a context that never equals alt 2's until the next SECTION header, so full-context LL scans to that header at each paragraph and the LL pass is quadratic in the number of paragraphs of a section.

**Measurements** (copied from the review): S6 N-series; S8 `null_sec_*` (a legal 400-paragraph program with one `SET p TO NULL` takes 99.6 s); S2-refute bin-ctl vs bin-mod on 6,201 files; DB201A 55 s (and 1.1 s with its two `USE ... ALL` lines spelled out, see N8).

**Fix direction.** The review states the fix and its proof: remove the ambiguity from the procedureDivision/procedureUnit/sectionDefinition shape. PB1146's COBOL2797 (Format 1 vs Format 2) is kept. Structural guard: decision 133, LL_MaxLook at most one paragraph (see N24 for the harness that measures it).

**Cross-reference.** PB1146 (landed) introduced the Format 1/2 diagnostic that this restructuring must preserve. On legal source the LL retry is triggered by N2 (SET TO NULL) and N8 (USE FOR DEBUGGING ALL).

## Landing (wave 20261795 group A)

Re-probed on the w20261795a build (Debug `cobol.exe`, wall time): `null_sec_400.cob` (a legal 400-paragraph section
with one `SET W-P TO NULL`) 112,412 ms, rc 0; CCVS `DB201A` (`--nist DB201A`) 58,272 ms, rc 65 with its two COBOL5506s.
After the fix: 6,801 ms and 1,380 ms, with the same diagnostics and a byte-identical `null_sec_400.dll`.
`null_nosec_400` (no section) 3,563 and 3,215 ms, unchanged.

**Fix.** `procedureDivision` is `declarativePart* sentence* paragraphDefinition* sectionDefinition*` and `procedureUnit`
is deleted: every paragraph after a section header is that section's in the shape (ISO §14.4.2), so no decision has a
second derivation to scan for. The two-format union that PB1146 refuses by name (COBOL2797) is kept: leading sentences
and paragraphs are still the division's own children.
