# PB5173 - at --std 85 CURRENCY SIGN IS E and N are refused (COBOL0891) and a lowercase letter such as m is accepted: ValidateCurrencyChar holds the 2023 exclusion list with no edition arm

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.Currency.cs` (`ValidateCurrencyChar`, `SwitchBindCurrency`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:VI.4.5.4 GR11 excludes from literal-6 the digits, the uppercase letters A, B, C, D, P, R, S, V, X, Z, every lowercase letter, the space and a list of special characters. `SpecialNamesBinder.Currency.ValidateCurrencyChar` holds ONE exclusion list, the 2023 one (A B C D E N P R S V X Z in either case), with no edition arm. So at --std 85 CURRENCY SIGN IS "E" and "N" are refused although 1985 allows them, and "m" or "l" compiles although 1985 excludes every lowercase letter.

**Found by.** The 1985 code read (2026-11-10; its records are `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/for-register.json`): each rule below was read against the code sites it names and probed at `--std 85`, and found non-conforming. Verdict, harm, evidence, repro and observed text are the records'; the rows' verdicts are recorded on another branch.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids of the list below.

SpecialNamesBinder.SwitchBindCurrency binds the bare one-character form at every edition (CurrencyOperandOf refuses a numeric literal and a figurative constant, COBOL0892; a longer literal is COBOL0890), RegisterCurrency records the symbol, PictureAnalyzer.Analyze reads it in a PICTURE, FinalizeCurrencySigns keeps the currency sign as the default, and the 2002 WITH PICTURE SYMBOL phrase is refused at 85 by VisitCurrencySignClause (COBOL0893). Probed right at 85: L, U, G, the colon and > as symbols (fixed and floating), the excluded digits, space, =, A and z refused (COBOL0891), PIC L99 without the clause refused (COBOL0808). Also seen, the format row's matter: two CURRENCY SIGN clauses in one paragraph compile at 85 (the 2002 form; §1985:VI.4.5.2 prints the clause once). **Expected (from the 1985 text, the record's).** --std 85 compiles the program and it displays [E05]. §1985:VI.4.5.4 GR11: "It may be any character from the computer's character set except one of the following: a. digits 0 through 9; b. alphabetic characters consisting of the uppercase letters A, B, C, D, P, R, S, V, X, Z; the lowercase letters a through z; or the space; c. special characters * + - , . ; ( ) " = /". E (and N) is not in the list, so it is a legal literal-6 and "is used in the PICTURE clause to represent the currency symbol"; by the same list a lowercase letter ("m") is refused.

**Observed (the record's).** COBOL0891 at line 6 ("CURRENCY SIGN literal 'E': not a valid currency symbol — digits, the picture letters A B C D E N P R S V X Z, space, and + - , . * / ; ( ) \" = are excluded (ISO §2023:12.3.7.3 SR22/SR27)") and COBOL1658 at line 9 (the PICTURE read as a 2002 floating-point picture); CURRENCY SIGN IS "N" with PIC N99 draws COBOL0891 and COBOL1935. The other direction, harm under-rejects: the same program with CURRENCY SIGN IS "m" and PIC m99 compiles at --std 85 with no diagnostic and displays [m05] ("l" with PIC L99 displays [l05]), where 1985 excludes "the lowercase letters a through z". Sibling: CURRENCY SIGN IS "L" followed by CURRENCY SIGN IS "K" compiles at 85 and PIC K99 displays [K05].

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Make the exclusion set a function of the edition, held once (one table keyed by edition, read by ValidateCurrencyChar), so the 1985, 2002 and 2023 lists each have one home and a drift test pins them to their rules.
