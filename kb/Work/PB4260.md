# PB4260 — the new enqueue handoff steps tell a unit to name enqueue for a DECISION, STOPPED or BOUNCED row, but rule 6 (land_lane.agent_work + review_lane.agent_work) counts none of them, and DECISION rows belong to the next wave planner

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/units/enqueue.md` (whole: the unit prompt, step 8 at line 138 and the statement at line 30), `scripts/orchestrator/units/resume.md` (whole: step 3 and line 10), `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` (`agent_work`).

The new step 8 of units/enqueue.md (line 138) and step 3 of units/resume.md tell a unit to name enqueue for a DECISION row
(and any STOPPED or BOUNCED row), and say next_unit.py runs a named enqueue for that work. But rule 6
(`land_lane.agent_work` plus `review_lane.agent_work`) counts no DECISION row, no collected STOPPED attempt and no BOUNCED
row with conflicts. DECISION rows belong to the next wave planner (enqueue.md line 30), and resume.md line 10 says to
DECIDE every DECISION row in that unit and never carry it forward. The prompts and the rule disagree; one of them changes.
