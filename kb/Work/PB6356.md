# PB6356 — COBOL2049 cites a 2023 rule at --std 85

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SetConditionBinder.cs` (`BindSetCondition`, the `SetFalseWithoutFalsePhrase` refusal near line 63)

`SET condition-name TO FALSE` is a construct COBOL-85 does not have (SET TO FALSE arrived in 2002), so at
`--std 85` the edition gate COBOL0900 is the right and sufficient refusal. The binder ALSO raises
COBOL2049 for a level-88 with no FALSE phrase, and its text cites `ISO §2023:14.9.39.3 SR7`, a rule of
another edition, at `--std 85`: the PB4950 rule (a diagnostic cites the rule of the edition in force)
is broken, and the extra refusal adds noise beside the gate.

**Expected:** at `--std 85` only the edition gate fires (COBOL2049 is suppressed below the construct's
introducing edition, the way other post-gate screens are); at 2002, 2014 and 2023 COBOL2049 cites each
edition's own SET rule through the edition-citation mechanism PB6219 extended (CiteRule / CiteRuleFrom2002),
each checked with `cite.py --check`. Re-probe at all four editions. PB6219 (landing 2026-11-27) owns the
edition-citation drift net: extend it so this site is covered.
