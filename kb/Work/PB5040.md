# PB5040 - Annex citations carry no § and no edition

**Mechanism.** PB4887 (landed, both phases) made `§<edition>:<locator>[ <rule>]` the one form and added the BARE classifier for dotted-clause citations. An Annex locator (`Annex A.1 item 158`, `ISO Annex E.2 item 1c`) has no leading clause number, so BARE never matches it and the phase-2 rewrite did not touch it. CLAUDE.md rule 1 requires the edition form on every citation.

**Extent.** About 1,047 sites under `src/` and `tests/`, for example `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Cli/Program.cs:104` and `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundCall.cs:113`. No open note owns this: PB4887 is landed and PB4976 covers only clause-form sites.

**Needed.** Add a classifier for `Annex <letter>.<n>` (without `§<edition>:`) beside BARE in `audit_code_citations.py`; rewrite the tree once (alone, tree-wide) to `§2023:<Annex locator>` with each site's item checked via `cite.py --check`. Do the rewrite after PB5039 settles how list items are written, since Annex entries are list items and would otherwise inherit the `N)` shape.
