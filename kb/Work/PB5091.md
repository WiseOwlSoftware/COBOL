# PB5091 - COBOL2122 and the ambiguous-procedure-name diagnostic name the duplicate paragraphs at the wrong lines: the line of the paragraph's first sentence, or none for an empty paragraph

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs#AtLine` (line 414 renders `_paraLine[pc]`, which lines 66 and 96 fill with the source line of the paragraph's FIRST SENTENCE, kept for DEBUG-LINE, and with 0 for a paragraph of no sentences); every message built from the candidates inherits it, COBOL2122 and `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs#RejectAmbiguousProcedureName` among them.

**Mechanism.** The refusal itself is right (§1985:IV.4.3.8.1 qualification rule 6) but the message tells the user "paragraph P1 at line 9; paragraph P1 at line 11" where the paragraph-names are on lines 8 and 10 (lines 9 and 11 hold the first statements), and a paragraph with no sentence gets no line at all. The message also cites a 2023 rule at --std 85 (kb/Work/PB4950).

**Found by.** The 1985 `--std=85` draft triage (kb/Work PB4995 item 3): each rule below is a conformance-suite draft that did not MATCH at `--std 85`, and its adjudicator decided COMPILER-DEFECT. Harm and observed text are the records'; nothing here was re-derived.

**Inventory.** The traceability inventory carries no 1985 rows yet, so `inventory_rows` is empty; the rows this note covers are the 1985 rule ids listed below (catalog `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json`).

**Rules (1; every citation checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `1985-SR-IV.4.3.8.1-L2.6` - `§1985:IV.4.3.8.1 SRL2.6` (record harm: misleads-diagnosis, slice-02): §1985:IV.4.3.8.1 SRL2.6 (rule 6 of the rules for qualification): "If explicitly referenced, a paragraph-name must not be duplicated within a section." The draft declares P1 twice in section S1 (lines 8 and 10) and references it explicitly (`PERFORM P1`, line 6), so the 1985 text requires the refusal the draft expects, and the compiler refuses. Two things then differ. (1) The site: the rule relates three places (the reference and the two declarations) and gives a diagnostic no line; the compiler reports at the reference (line 6) by design, because the rule is conditioned on "If explicitly referenced", and the draft marks only the second declaration (line 10). Marked at line 6 the same program and control MATCH. (2) The defect: the message names the two declarations by line and both line numbers are wrong, so a user is sent to lines that hold no paragraph-name.

**Repro (`1985-SR-IV.4.3.8.1-L2.6`, slice-02).**

```cobol
000100 IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
000200 PROGRAM-ID. DUPPAR.
000300 PROCEDURE DIVISION.
000400 S1 SECTION.
000500 P0.
000600     PERFORM P1.
000700     STOP RUN.
000800 P1.
000900     DISPLAY "A".
001000 P1.
001100     DISPLAY "B".
```

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** The message names the lines that hold the paragraph-names. The deciding words: "If explicitly referenced, a paragraph-name must not be duplicated within a section." (§1985:IV.4.3.8.1 SRL2.6).

**Observed (copied from `1985-SR-IV.4.3.8.1-L2.6`).** cobol --std 85 --source-format fixed --flag-extensions: exit 65: t.cob(6,12): error COBOL2122: PERFORM 'P1': that paragraph-name is declared 2 times within section 'S1' (paragraph 'P1' at line 9; paragraph 'P1' at line 11), and it is explicitly referenced here (ISO §2023:8.4.2.2.3 SR7 — "If explicitly referenced, a paragraph-name shall not be duplicated within a section"). No qualifier can separate the two: §2023:8.4.2.2.2 format 4 gives a paragraph-name only its section-name as a qualifier, and both declarations carry the same one — rename one of the paragraphs. The paragraph-names are on lines 8 and 10; lines 9 and 11 hold the DISPLAY statements. Second probe (P1 at line 8 followed by a blank line, a comment line and its first statement at line 11; a second, empty P1 at line 12): the message reads "(paragraph 'P1' at line 11; paragraph 'P1')", three lines off for the first and no line at all for the empty paragraph. Code site: src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs, AtLine (line 414) renders `_paraLine[pc]`, which lines 66 and 96 fill with the source line of the paragraph's FIRST SENTENCE (kept for DEBUG-LINE) and with 0 for a paragraph of no sentences; every message built from the candidates (COBOL2122 and the ambiguous-procedure-name diagnostic in Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs, RejectAmbiguousProcedureName) inherits it. The message also cites 2023 rules for a --std=85 compile (kb/Work PB4950's population). Controls: the draft's control (second P1 renamed P2) compiles, runs and displays 'A'; duplicate P1 paragraphs with no reference compile (exit 0), as the rule's condition allows; `PERFORM P1 OF S1` and `GO TO P1` against the duplicates are each refused with COBOL2122 at the reference.
