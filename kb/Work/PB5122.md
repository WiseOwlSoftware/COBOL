# PB5122 - at --std 85 the class condition applies the 2023 operand rule: ALPHABETIC and class-name tests of a numeric edited item are refused (COBOL6000), and the 1985 bars on NUMERIC (alphabetic item, signed group, non-DISPLAY usage) are not applied

**Sites:** none (not located: the record names the COBOL6000 check, the 2023 rule §2023:8.8.4.4.3 SR4 applied at every edition, and no member; the implementer locates the class-condition operand binder); the fix's sites: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ClassConditionModel.cs` (`ClassOperandRule`, `ClassAlternative.RulesAt`, `ClassOperandFacts`, `Violates`, `Wording`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ConditionBinder.cs` (`CheckClassConditionOperand`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (COBOL6916-COBOL6918)

**Mechanism.** Two directions, one mechanism: the class-condition operand check is the 2023 rule at every edition. (1) REJECTS LEGAL SOURCE: a numeric edited item is not numeric in 1985 (§1985:IV.4.3.3 puts numeric edited in the alphanumeric class), so ALPHABETIC, ALPHABETIC-LOWER and a class-name test of a PIC ZZZ item are legal 1985; 2023 refuses them (§2023:8.8.4.4.3 SR4). (2) UNDER-REJECTS: the 1985 operand restrictions that 2023 lifted are not applied: `IF AL IS NUMERIC` (item PIC AAA), `IF GS IS NUMERIC` (a group of signed items) and `IF CP IS NUMERIC` (PIC 999 COMP) compile with no diagnostic and run (F, F, T). The numeric-operand bars (ALPHABETIC, ALPHABETIC-LOWER, ALPHABETIC-UPPER, class-name on PIC 999) and a USAGE INDEX operand are refused as 1985 requires. The draft below witnesses direction (1); direction (2) is the record's second defect and is not in the draft.

**Found by.** The 1985 witness-drafting pass (kb/Work PB4995 item 3): each rule below is a witness program drafted from the 1985 text alone, run at `--std 85`, and its result differs from what the text requires (outcome MISMATCH). Harm and observed text are the record's; nothing here was re-derived.
