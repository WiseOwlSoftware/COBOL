# PB3639 — the tokenless landing lane is bound to the life of an LLM unit

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/orchestrate.ps1` (`Wait-Until`, the unit loop and its `LaneTick`), `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` (`tick`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md` section 4.9.

Found 2026-10-24 by the operator's stall-class survey (`docs/rearchitecture/evidence/fleet-optimization/2026-10-24-stall-class-survey.md`,
class LANE_TICK_UNIT_BOUND).

## Defect

The supervisor ticks `land_lane.py tick` "as each unit starts and every -LandLaneSeconds while it runs"
(`orchestrate.ps1` header, lines ~29 and ~81). Between units it sleeps in `Wait-Until` (lines ~142-148), which checks
STOP every minute and never ticks. So the lane, which spends no tokens, stops whenever no LLM unit runs: during a budget
hold-day, after a breaker trip, while the loop waits out a meter failure, and in the gap between two units. A branch
reviewed CLEAN, or re-armed, in such a window waits for the next unit's start.

The survey's refuter confirmed the code claim and showed that most of the minutes first charged to it belonged to
other mechanisms (night STOPs the lane honours by design, PB3181 survey misreads, PB3186 over-holds, the WSL disk
outage); the residual, about 1,000 minutes of holds and breaker pauses with no STOP, is unmeasured as landable time. The
BREAKER_METER and QUOTA_STOP classes in the same survey name this as their real mechanism. On 2026-10-24 the operator
re-armed PB3112 at about 12:04 PDT and the lane had not ticked by 12:10, so the operator ran `land_lane.py tick` by hand.

## Fix direction

The lane ticks on its own timer for as long as the supervisor process lives, unit or no unit: in `Wait-Until`, in a
hold-day, and after a breaker trip. Only the owner's STOP and the loop's fleet stop end it, exactly as now
(`land_lane.py` already refuses new attempts under them, PB3402). It spends no tokens and gates under the shared slot,
so a quota hold is no reason to stop it. A drift test: a supervisor fixture in `Wait-Until` with a WAITING TO LAND row
starts one `land_lane.py tick` within -LandLaneSeconds, and none once STOP exists.
