# PB5119 - at --std 85 (and 2023) a format 3 INSPECT of an OCCURS DEPENDING ON group whose TALLYING changes the DEPENDING ON item writes the image back at the new length and space-fills it (ZZZ and two spaces); 1985 gives ZZZZZ, 2023 gives ZZZBA

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/InspectEmitter.cs#EmitStore` (the group arm: `PlaceRenderer.WriteGroupValue(p, img, ..., AccessDir.Sending)` sizes the write-back by the current count) and `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Roslyn/PlaceIdentification.cs#Freeze` (line 147: the Freeze of an OdoGroupPlace freezes the inner address, not the extent)

**Mechanism.** A format 3 INSPECT is two successive INSPECTs of the same identifier; only subscripting of the second is fixed beforehand (§1985:VI.6.18.4 GR17), so the second statement takes the length of an OCCURS DEPENDING ON group anew. The compiler reads the group image at length 3 for the tally (which raises N, the DEPENDING ON item, from 3 to 5), then writes it back at length 5 and space-fills it, destroying characters 4 and 5; it is neither the 1985 result nor the 2023 one (§2023:14.9.22.4 GR19 fixes item identification, keeping length 3). --std 2023 prints the same. Two separate statements (tally, then replace) give ZZZZZ. The ORDER and SUBSCRIPT statements of the draft match (PB1123, landed, froze the subscripts of format 3; it did not cover the occurs-depending extent).

**Found by.** The 1985 witness-drafting pass (kb/Work PB4995 item 3): each rule below is a witness program drafted from the 1985 text alone, run at `--std 85`, and its result differs from what the text requires (outcome MISMATCH). Harm and observed text are the record's; nothing here was re-derived.

**Observed (copied from `1985-GR-VI.6.18.4-17`).** The pinned compiler prints 'ORDER 02 BBBB', 'SUBSCRIPT 3 AXAX', 'LENGTH 5 ZZZ' (G is ZZZ followed by two SPACES; with N then set to 6 it is 'ZZZ  B'). Two separate statements (INSPECT G TALLYING N FOR ALL "A". INSPECT G REPLACING CHARACTERS BY "Z".) give ZZZZZ.
