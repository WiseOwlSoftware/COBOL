# PB5167 - at --std 85 an INPUT-OUTPUT SECTION with no FILE-CONTROL paragraph, a FILE-CONTROL paragraph with no entry and an empty REPOSITORY paragraph compile: the grammar takes the 2023 optional forms with no edition arm

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolIO.g4` (`inputOutputSection`, `fileControlParagraph`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4` (`repositoryParagraph`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/FileControlBinder.cs` (`BindFileControl`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`VisitRepositoryEntry`)

**Mechanism.** The 1985 Environment Division format does not bracket FILE-CONTROL inside the INPUT-OUTPUT SECTION, and the FILE-CONTROL paragraph takes one file-control-entry at least (braces with an ellipsis, §1985:IV.2.1.4). The grammar writes `inputOutputSection` with `fileControlParagraph?` and `fileControlParagraph` with `fileControlClauseGroup*`, the 2023 shapes, and `BindFileControl` iterates whatever entries exist; nothing asks the edition. So at --std 85 the section followed by I-O-CONTROL alone, FILE-CONTROL with no SELECT and an empty REPOSITORY paragraph (whose entries are refused, kb/Work PB2589, but not the empty paragraph) compile clean.
