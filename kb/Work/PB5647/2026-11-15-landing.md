Re-probe on the Debug build (`cur8_long_s.cob --std 2002 --run`): COBOL0891, as the note says. Root cause was wider than the exclusion test: the invariant upper case (`char.ToUpperInvariant`) stood for COBOL case equivalence in three places (the currency set's key, the PICTURE fold, the exclusion list's letter test), so even with the exclusion lifted U+017F folded to `S` in the PICTURE and `PIC ſſ9.99` bound as `SS9.99` and displayed `2.50` (a wrong answer, seen while fixing). The 1985 and 2002 texts define case equivalence between COBOL basic letters only (§2002:8.1.2.1 GR3, the Annex D mapping); the implementor's additional-character specification (§2014:8.1.1) and the currency-symbol equivalence (§2014:12.3.6.2 SR20) first appear in 2014.

Fix: one function, `CurrencySymbolEquivalence.Fold(c, dialectLevel)`, asked by `RegisterCurrency` (the key), `PictureAnalyzer.FoldPictureCase` and the legacy single-symbol path (the fold), and `CurrencySymbolExclusion.Excludes` (the letter test). A non-COBOL character folds to its invariant upper case from 2014 and is equivalent to no COBOL letter below 2014. `CurrencySymbolExclusion` gained rows per edition (2002 / 2014 / 2023, each citing its own edition's SR) with the year it asks `Fold` with; the mask canonicalization compares the already-folded expansion with the key (it had folded a second time). DOC-A.1-43 and DOC-A.1-44 state the edition boundary.
