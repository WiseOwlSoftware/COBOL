## Landed (kb/Work PB3334, wave 1118 group A4)

The keyed lifecycle is written once, in `KeyedConnector`, now that both organizations drive the keyed store engine: the
§14.9.27.4 arm skeleton (`OpenCore`, sealed), the store's attach and adoption (`Attach`, through the organizations'
`AttachStore`/`ForgetStore` hooks over ONE generic `KeyedStoreTable.Attach<T>` and `AttachCreated<T>`), the CLOSE's promotion
and the §9.1.15 release (`CloseCore`, sealed), `AbandonOpen`, the file position indicator's validity bit (`FpiValid`),
`StartFail` and the one `InvalidateFilePosition` override. The organizations keep only their position state
(`ResetPosition`, `Opened`) and their verbs. `KeyedStoreTable.AttachRelative`/`AttachIndexed` are deleted (every caller moved).
