# PB6132 - the bound nodes are named for one of their four producers

**Sites.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundTree.cs` (`BoundUdfEvaluated`, `BoundUdfEvaluatedExpr`) and
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/OperandEvaluationWindow.cs` (`AttachPerEvaluation`). The window now feeds property GETs and
subscript temporaries as well as UDF activations, so a reader looking for the property-GET step searches the wrong name.

PB3223 left the rename as a separate behavior-neutral change: it touches the emitters and the condition renderer. Rename the nodes with every
caller and delete the old names in one change (rule 4), proven by an output differential.
