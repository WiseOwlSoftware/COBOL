# PB4180 — chained enqueue units starve the waves

## Measured

`E:/COBOL-coord/units.jsonl`, every unit since 2026-10-01: **28 chains** of two or more consecutive `enqueue` units,
**75 units, 49.6 hours** of the one supervisor's time. The latest is 2026-10-30, 16:41 to 17:21 PDT: three units of
1,868 s, 499 s and another. Each handoff named `enqueue` again: "f7f-29 and 17b-15 land once #851 merges",
"f7f-29 lands once #811 merges", "#811 merges, then the lane lands f7f-29 and 468-1". Meanwhile campaigns PB4147
(3 notes ready) and PB2911 (8 ready) had no turn. Earlier chains: 2026-10-27 18:45 (2 units, 72 min), 10-29 15:03
(2, 88 min), 10-29 18:06 (3, 138 min). The operator broke the 10-30 chain by steering a campaign.

## The mechanism

`scripts/orchestrator/next_unit.py#base_choice` returns the handoff's `next_unit` before every other rule (rule 2: "named
by the last handoff"). The enqueue unit's own instructions let it name `enqueue` again whenever a branch it handled is
still in the merge queue. But since PB3103 and PB3608, the landing lane lands every WAITING TO LAND branch, and the
review lane reviews every DONE branch, on their own ticks between units. Waiting for a merge is no unit's work, so a
unit spent waiting is a whole Opus session that produces nothing, and the waves and campaigns wait behind it.

## Acceptance

A unit's handoff may name a successor only for work no lane owns: a STOPPED, BOUNCED or DECISION row, a finisher to
collect, or leads to file. "A pull request will merge" or "the lane will land X" is never a reason to name `enqueue`.
Either `next_unit.py` refuses such a handoff-named unit when the survey shows nothing but lane-owned rows (and falls
through to the wave/campaign rules), or the enqueue unit's instructions (`scripts/orchestrator/units/enqueue.md`) forbid
naming itself for that reason. Pin it with a test that replays a handoff naming `enqueue` with only WAITING TO LAND /
ENQUEUED rows and expects a wave or campaign. A drift measure over `units.jsonl` should report consecutive enqueue chains.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/next_unit.py#base_choice`, `scripts/orchestrator/units/enqueue.md` (whole: its handoff
rules), `scripts/orchestrator/test_next_unit.py` (whole: the replay arm).
