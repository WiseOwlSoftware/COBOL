# PB3966 — ScreenImplicitSet's data-pointer arm names neither the INITIALIZE nor the refused receiver, and PointerAssignmentReasons' doc comment is false with an unreachable Pointer arm

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SetBinder.cs:739` (`SetBinder.ScreenImplicitSet`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SetBinder.cs:683` (`SetBinder.PointerAssignmentReason`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/PtrBinder.cs:142` (`PtrBinder.ScreenPointerReceiverRestriction`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/PtrBinder.cs:226` (`PtrBinder.RejectRestriction`, its lead now a parameter; its four other callers `PtrBinder.ScreenBasedReceiverRestriction`, `PtrBinder.BindAllocate`, `PtrBinder.BindSetUpDown` quote their operand themselves), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/PtrBinder.cs:517` (`PtrBinder.BindSetUpDown`, a U+2026 in the no-receiver message, which the renderer transliterates)

Two leads of PB3817, one site family (`SetBinder`).

1. `SetBinder.ScreenImplicitSet`'s data-pointer arm (line 743) ignores its `statement` argument and the receiver name, so an
   INITIALIZE refusal under SR19 names neither the INITIALIZE nor which subordinate receiver was refused, unlike the
   program-pointer, function-pointer and object-reference arms of the same method.
2. The doc comment of `SetBinder.PointerAssignmentReasons` (line 687) says BY CONTENT / BY VALUE arguments call it; only
   the two SET sites do (the argument lane lives in `ActivationRelations`). Its `PicCategory.Pointer` arm, a second copy of
   SR19 beside `PtrBinder.ScreenPointerReceiverRestriction`, is reached only by the new `ScreenImplicitSet` fall-through
   guard order, so it is unused otherwise. One rule, two places (CLAUDE.md rule 5).

## Acceptance
- The data-pointer arm names the statement and receiver like its siblings (golden expect text for an INITIALIZE of a group
  holding a restricted pointer); the doc comment is corrected; the Pointer arm and `PtrBinder`'s copy become one.

## Landed (wave 20261769, group G)

Re-probed on a Debug build of main 802352531 (scratch `w20261769g/init1.cob`: `INITIALIZE GRP REPLACING
DATA-POINTER DATA BY PU`, GRP a STRONG group holding a restricted `PR` and an unrestricted `PQ`): COBOL0869
`'PU': the receiving data-pointer is restricted to type 'TT' …` named neither the INITIALIZE nor `PR`.

- `PtrBinder.ScreenPointerReceiverRestriction` gained `implicitSet`, the lead of an implicit SET's diagnostic;
  `SetBinder.ScreenImplicitSet` builds ONE lead (`<statement> — the implicit SET 'recv' TO 'send' (ISO §14.9.20.4
  GR4)`) and hands it to all four arms (data-pointer, object-reference, program-/function-pointer), so they agree.
- `SetBinder.PointerAssignmentReason`'s `PicCategory.Pointer` arm, the second copy of SR19, is deleted (only
  `ScreenImplicitSet`'s guard order ever kept it unreachable); its doc comment now says what is true: Formats 8 and 9
  and `ScreenImplicitSet` call it, and the BY CONTENT / BY VALUE argument lane asks `ActivationRelations.MaySet`.
- Goldens: `pb3966-initialize-replacing-data-pointer-names-receiver` (SR19 sentence 1, receiver subordinate to the
  group) and `pb3966-initialize-replacing-data-pointer-restricted-sender-names-receiver` (the converse sentence).
