# PB6162 — LANDED — a currency symbol's case partner is accepted in a PICTURE at COBOL-85

At `--std 85`, with `CURRENCY SIGN IS "é"`, `PIC É9.99` compiles and edits 1.5 as `é1.50`. 1985 gives no such equivalence:
"All other lowercase letters are not equivalent to their corresponding uppercase representations" (§1985:VI.5.9.3 SR3), and 'É'
is neither the currency symbol nor a PICTURE symbol, so the PICTURE character-string is not one 1985 allows. The equivalence
latitude is 2014 text (§2014:12.3.6.2 SR20; DOC-A.1-43, band 2014,2023 since PB6130).

**Repro.** The former l1c06_currency_non_cobol_equivalence_85 (git history before PB6130's landing) at `--std 85`: `UP=[é1.50]`.
**Code site.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.Currency.cs#SwitchBindCurrency` and
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs#Analyze` (the `ToUpperInvariant` key, not edition-gated).
**Sibling to check.** 2002 has neither the sentence nor an Annex B item for it (PB6130's refuter), and the compiler applies the
same mapping there: `l1c06-currency-non-cobol-equivalent-rebound` (two CURRENCY clauses binding 'é' and 'É' to different strings)
is refused COBOL0891 at 2002 too, where the two symbols are not equivalent; its refusal file now names only 2014 and 2023.

## Fixed (2026-11-24, PB6161's branch, review finding R1-2)

`CurrencySymbolEquivalence.Fold` now folds a character outside the repertoire only from 2014 (§2014:12.3.6.2 SR20); below 2014
it is itself, so 'é' is no 'É' at 85 and 2002. The one fold serves the currency set's key, the PICTURE fold and the exclusion test,
so all three changed together. At 2002 the rule left is "No two occurrences of literal-8 may be the same value" (§2002:12.2.6.2 SR22),
and 'é' and 'É' are two values; §2002:12.2.6.3 GR13's lowercase-letter rule reaches the Annex D letters only. Witnesses:
`cases/13.18.40-PICTURE/pb6162_currency_case_partner_refused` (refused COBOL0808 at 85 and 2002) and
`cases/12.3.7-SPECIAL-NAMES/pb6162_currency_case_partners_distinct_2002` (accepted at 2002: `LO=[EUR1.50]`, `UP=[USD1.50]`);
`CurrencySymbolExclusionDriftTests` pins the fold at each edition. The rebound refusal names 2014 and 2023 only.
