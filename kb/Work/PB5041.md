# PB5041 - Amendment 1 underlined heads were sampled, not measured

**Found.** The review of PB5028's branch (PR #1410) found that the Amendment 1 pages (842-906) of
`specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` were checked for underlined heads only by eye on 20 samples. No note in the register covers this:
PB4953 and PB5028 cover the body headings of pages 85-906 by the text-layer match and the 212 unconfirmed lines, and PB4848
covers the raster measurer's accuracy, not an Amendment 1 sweep.

**Scenario.** An underlined head on pages 842-906 that is not among the 20 sampled is missed, so the transcription silently drops
its underline.

**Sites:** `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (PDF pages 842-906); the raster measurer `scripts/spec/figure_raster.py` (`find_rules`,
line 856, exists; PB4848 records that it is tuned for the monospaced general-format band and that the amendments' Times text
reads serif chains as fused rules).

**Fix.** A deskewed horizontal-rule detector run over every Amendment 1 page, calibrated on the 20 by-eye samples, with each
page's head lines compared to the transcription's `<u>` marks; repairs carry their `LEDGER-1985.json` sweep entry in the same
change.
