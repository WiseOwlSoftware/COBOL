# PB3108 — the harness's fixed run budget turns host load into reds

> ⚙ **flags:** process-only

Source: kb/Work PB3104 "Separately worth recording".

`tests/_shared/ProcessObservation.cs:64-71` (`TimeoutMs`, 120 s default, override `COBOLNET_RUN_TIMEOUT_MS`) and the
non-observation report near line 174: a run is killed after the budget and the case reads HARNESS NON-OBSERVATION (RED).
The budget is a fixed number, not scaled from the measured cost of the case.

**Measured 2026-10-12** under PB3104's seven uncapped Linux gates:
`RelationPairShapeTests.StrongGroupWithAThousandLeaves_CompilesAndComparesInOrder` (6.96 s in the shared timings store) took
8 m 14 s, 8 m 2 s and 16 m 56 s; `ActivationResourcesTests.RecursiveFunction_PastTheStack_IsEcProgramResources` (3.77 s) took
4 m 37 s and 4 m 32 s. Both went RED on load alone; the same two tests passed on another gate in the same window.

Owner rules: a timing failure is a failure (memory timing-failure-is-a-failure); no fixed time limit in a test (PB3022).
PB3022 item 5 and PB2882 cover the drift tests' own fixed limits; neither owns the harness budget for compiled-program runs.
Also mentioning the budget: `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/SelfTestArmSource.cs:18` and PB1957 (survey row 30).
PB3104's fix (Linux row under the gate slot) removes this load source but not the fixed budget. The implementer decides the
shape (a budget derived from the case's recorded baseline, or an observation that is not wall-clock).
