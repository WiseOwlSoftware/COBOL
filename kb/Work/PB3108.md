## Landed (wave 1155, group A)

**Re-probe (on the branch's own build, 2026-10-22).** The suite is 39 tests at four editions (14 tests "in the suites"),
not 2,000 programs: `run.py` over it starts 78 processes (35 accepted tests compile and run, 4 flagged tests compile
their control and themselves), 8 at a time, and took 29.9 s on a quiet 32-processor host, its slowest test 9.3 s. One
observation of that under a budget sized for one program fails at any load above four-fold: with the unit set to 20 s
(`WISEOWL_COBOL_RUN_TIMEOUT_MS=20000`, still twice the slowest test) the old drift test was a HARNESS NON-OBSERVATION on both
attempts, and the log named the command as a bare `python` (the observer read `ProcessStartInfo.Arguments`, which
`ArgumentList` leaves empty).

**Fix: one budget rule over the work.** `tests/_shared/ObservationBudget.cs` is the rule for every observed process: one
harness unit (`UnitMs`, 120 s, `WISEOWL_COBOL_RUN_TIMEOUT_MS`) for the process plus one per serial round of the child
processes it runs, `1 + ceil(children / parallel)`; `OneProcess` for a program or a script. `ProcessObserver.TimeoutMs`
is deleted and every caller moved; `Observe`, `ObserveOrThrow` and `ObserveWithRetry` take a budget, and
`PythonInstrument.Run` takes it as its first parameter, so every caller states its work. The two-runner drift test
observes `run.py` once per generated edition suite, with `--jobs` stated, at `ForChildren(2 × tests, jobs)`: with the
same 20 s unit it passes (3/3, 40 s). A timeout's report now carries the budget and the work it covered, the process's
own processor time and the host's load across the attempt (`tests/_shared/HostLoad.cs`: busy share from `/proc/stat` or
`GetSystemTimes`, Linux load averages), and the command line with its `ArgumentList`. The unit stays the floor for one
program: 120 s against a recorded cost of at most 9.3 s (one suite test, compile and run) and 7.0 s and 3.8 s (the two
single-program witnesses above), a multiple of 13 to 32.

**The observation log is kept.** Every CI `dotnet test` step sets `WISEOWL_COBOL_HARNESS_LOG` to
`<its results directory>/harness-observations.log` and its job uploads it with the TRX (`CiHarnessLogDriftTests` holds
it); the local gate writes `<run>/leg-<n>-<assembly>.observations.log` (`run_gate_legs.py`'s `leg_env`) and the Linux
gate `TestResults/linux-gate/<leg>.observations.log`.

**Not changed, by design.** `run.py`'s own per-process hang guard (600 s) is the tool's, for any compiler's user; the
outer budget gives each round of its children the same unit an observed program gets. The single-program witnesses
(8 to 17 minutes under seven uncapped Linux gates) stay non-observations at one unit: they now name the load.

**For the next implementer.**
- `tests/_shared/ObservationBudget.cs#ObservationBudget` owns the budget rule; a caller that runs children states them
  through `ForChildren(children, parallel, work)`, a program or script takes `OneProcess`; never pass a literal unit
  (`NoWallClockAssertionDriftTests` refuses `ObservationBudget.For(<digits>` outside the observer's own tests).
- `tests/_shared/ProcessObservation.cs#ProcessObserver.Observe` reads the host load (`HostLoad`) and the child's own
  processor time on a timeout; `CommandLine` prints `ArgumentList`. Reuse them, never a second launcher.
- `tests/_shared/PythonInstrument.cs#Run` takes the budget first; every caller states it.
- `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/ConformanceSuiteRunnerDriftTests.cs#RunPy` runs one edition suite per observation;
  trap: `suites/wiseowl` is the WiseOwl manifest, not a suite, and run.py refuses an edition suite with no test.
- `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CiHarnessLogDriftTests.cs`: a new CI `dotnet test` step sets `WISEOWL_COBOL_HARNESS_LOG` into
  its results directory and uploads it; trap: a class that reads one file carries no `[Trait("Sweep", "tree")]`.
