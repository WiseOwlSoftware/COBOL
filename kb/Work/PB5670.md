# PB5670 - SCREEN SECTION operand refusal cites Of2023 clauses at 2002/2014

**The lead.** "Text only: at 2002/2014 the INVOKE/CALL ScreenSection message cites Of2023 clauses."

The messages are the `DiagnosticCatalog.CallOperandSection` (COBOL1677) and `DiagnosticCatalog.InvokeOperandSection` descriptors, raised through
`host.Params.ScreenSection` from `CallBinder.cs` (lines 1574, 1577) and `OoBinder.cs` (lines 561, 1089, 1440, 1723). The CALL descriptor carries a 1985 clause for
`--std 85` but `ISO §2023:14.9.4.3 SR3/SR7` at 2002 and 2014, where the compiler's active edition is a different text. The fix names the active edition's clause
(`EditionInfo`) in each message, every citation checked with `cite.py --check`.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (`DiagnosticCatalog.CallOperandSection`, `DiagnosticCatalog.InvokeOperandSection`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs` (`CallBinder`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.cs` (`OoBinder`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ParameterConformance.cs` (`StorageSectionRule.For`, `StorageSectionRule.CallUsing`, `StorageSectionRule.InvokeUsing`)

**Sibling sweep.** Every `§2023:` text in a message that fires at `--std 2002`/`2014` (PB5659 is the same mechanism in `OoConformance`).

**Landed (wave 20262165 group A, 2026-11-24): the INVOKE and CALL storage-section refusals cite their edition's rule and section list.** `StorageSectionRule.For` (`ParameterConformance.cs`) builds the rule and the section list from ONE edition choice, with the named rules `CallUsing`, `CallReturning`, `InvokeUsing` and `InvokeReturning` (§2002:14.8.4.2 SR3/SR7 and §2002:14.8.22.2 SR9/SR11 keep the communication section in the list; §2014:14.9.4.2 and §2014:14.9.22.2 drop it). `CallBinder.ScreenCallOperand`, `CallBinder.OperandUnresolved` and the four `OoBinder` screens ask it. Re-probe before the fix: COBOL1677 at --std 2002 cited `ISO §2023:14.9.4.3 SR3`; after: `ISO §2002:14.8.4.2 SR3`. The net (`EditionCitationNet`, PB5922) now refuses COBOL1677 citing only a later edition at 2002 and 2014.
