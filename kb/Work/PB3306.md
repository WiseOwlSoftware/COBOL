# PB3306 — cite.py region for G.2 runs through the Bibliography and the Index

Source: `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1103z-PB3149-report.md`, lead L1. Harm: process_only; it let a false citation validate (PB3149's "§G.2 3)" was really the alphabetic Index).

**Mechanism.** Unnumbered `##` headings (`## BIBLIOGRAPHY`, `## Index`) do not end a clause region in `scripts/spec/cite.py`, so everything after §G.2 is attributed to §G.2 3). Every Index line therefore validates as a G.2 citation.

**Repro:** `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check G.2 "## Index"` passes (also `--check G.2 '<a id="index"></a>'`). In `specs/ISO_COBOL.md`, `## BIBLIOGRAPHY` is at line 44154 and `## Index` at line 44177.

**Fix shape.** A region ends at the next heading of any level, with a self-test case that `--check G.2 "## Index"` is refused. Then re-check any citation that names G.2 for the same inheritance (the report found PB3149's).
