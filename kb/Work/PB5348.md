# PB5348 - extract_rule_catalog.py's stale check of an ISO catalog compares only the committed rules and findings, so the catalog's outside-denominator field (the lower-bound note the probe, inventory_view --summary and the ledger print) can go stale while --check stays GREEN

**Sites:**

- `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (`main_iso`)

**Mechanism.** In `main_iso` the stale test builds `committed`/`fresh` from the rules and compares `findings`, and never compares `outside-denominator` (`OUTSIDE`), though the probe's `invent` line, `inventory_view --summary` and the ledger print it beside the 2002 count (`inventory_view.lower_bound`). A change to the content-keyed manifest or the untyped headings that moves the lower bound passes `--check` with a stale committed field.

**Fix direction.** Include the field in the comparison (`old.get(OUTSIDE) != doc.get(OUTSIDE)` appended to `stale` as `(its outside-denominator)`), with a self-test arm that plants a changed manifest clause and expects STALE.

**Source.** Review lead R1-1, low, not blocking at round 1.
