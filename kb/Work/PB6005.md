# PB6005 - A spurious cascade COBOL7672 'relational operator END-IF' follows the correct COBOL5506 refusal of AND NOT NOT 2: the spacing rule judged a token error recovery consumed

Probe E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/pb5453/probes/s-11/c3-insert-not-not.cob at --std 85: COBOL5506 at the `2` (right), then
COBOL7672 "the relational operator 'END-IF' is not immediately followed by a separator space" at line 19 (wrong). Root:
`SeparatorRule.ConditionOperatorViolations` judged every terminal child of a comparison-operator or logical-expression context,
and error recovery puts the tokens it consumed there as error nodes. The fix skips `IErrorNode` in both arms. Witness:
`OperatorSpacingDiagnosticTests.ErrorRecoveredToken_IsNotJudgedAsAConditionOperator_At85`. Sibling sweep: `Violations` and
`ArithmeticOperatorViolations` walk tokens, not the tree, so error nodes do not reach them.

**Sites (as changed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/SeparatorRule.cs` (`ConditionOperatorViolations`).
