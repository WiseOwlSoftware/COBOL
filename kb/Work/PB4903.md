# PB4903 - the DEBUG-ITEM group is modeled as one elementary item

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R3-2 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 3):
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DebugItem.cs:163` A store into the whole DEBUG-ITEM is not a store into the group D-DEBUG-STORE
describes: StoreImage drops the FILLER positions, and INITIALIZE DEBUG-ITEM reaches it as one elementary alphanumeric store, so
DEBUG-SUB-n become spaces where ISO/IEC 1989:2023 14.9.20.4 GR4 initializes each elementary item by category (numeric to zero).
The root is the binder's elementary model of the group: MOVE CORRESPONDING to DEBUG-ITEM is also refused COBOL1757 as an
elementary item (`r2/r5.cob` of that group's review scratch). D-DEBUG-STORE names GnuCOBOL 3.2 as the authority and differs from
it on these; no golden displays the group after a store. Flags: wrong_answer (a candidate: INITIALIZE DEBUG-ITEM).

**Citation re-checked.** `§2023:14.9.20.4 GR4` OK for "the effect of the execution of the INITIALIZE statement is as though a
series of implicit MOVE or SET statements, each of which has an elementary data item as its receiving operand, were written". The
report's gloss "(numeric to zero)" is the category table of `§2023:14.9.20.4 GR6` (OK), not GR4's own words. The repro file
`r2/r5.cob` is no longer in that group's scratch tree; re-derive it first.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DebugItem.cs` (`DebugItem.StoreImage`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/Place.cs` (`DebugRegisterPlace`).
