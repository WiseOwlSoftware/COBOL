# PB1090 — OPEN — `specialNamesParagraph : SPECIAL_NAMES DOT specialNameEntry*` has no optional trailing period, so the full two-period spelling of an EMPTY paragraph (`SPECIAL-N

## Rule text (every citation re-run by the registrar)
- `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 12.3.7.3 "One of the separator periods may be omitted if none of the clauses in the SPECIAL-NAMES paragraph is specified"` → `OK  §12.3.7.3 31)  (Syntax rules)`

### Adjudicator finding (special-names-paragraph-p1, harm rejects-legal-source)
- **Mechanism:** specialNamesParagraph has no optional second separator period, so the full two-period spelling of an empty paragraph (`SPECIAL-NAMES. .`) is a parse error, while its SOURCE-COMPUTER and OBJECT-COMPUTER siblings accept theirs
- **Repro:** ENVIRONMENT DIVISION. CONFIGURATION SECTION. SPECIAL-NAMES. .   (also `SPECIAL-NAMES..` and the second period on the next line) at --std 85 and --std 2023
- **Expected (spec):** §12.3.7.2 prints `SPECIAL-NAMES. [clauses] .`; §12.3.7.3 SR31: 'One of the separator periods may be omitted if none of the clauses in the SPECIAL-NAMES paragraph is specified' - so with no clause both `SPECIAL-NAMES. .` and `SPECIAL-NAMES.` are conforming and shall compile.
- **Observed:** COBOL0001 "unexpected '.'" at both editions for all three two-period spellings; `SPECIAL-NAMES.` alone compiles. The grammar `SPECIAL_NAMES DOT specialNameEntry*` has no trailing `DOT?` for the empty case. `SOURCE-COMPUTER. .` and `OBJECT-COMPUTER. .` compile (their grammar carries the optional second DOT), so this paragraph is the one arm of the three period-omission rules not modeled.
- **Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolSpecialNames.g4#specialNamesParagraph
- **Clause (adjudicator):** §12.3.7.3 SR31

## What the fixer owes
Model the paragraph as `SPECIAL-NAMES. [clauses] .` with the SR31 omission, as the two sibling paragraphs do (their grammar carries `DOT?`); keep the non-empty form's terminating period required. Positive golden at 85.
