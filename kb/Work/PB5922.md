# PB5922 - a diagnostic at --std 2002 or 2014 cites that edition's rule

**The decision this carries out** is kb/Work PB4950's (owner, 2026-11-07): "Yes a diagnostic cite the rule of the edition the
program was compiled under". PB4950 landed the 1985 direction: `EditionInfo.Cite(y1985, later, y2002, y2014)` is the one
decider, every refusal the wiseowl manifest compiles at `--std 85` cites a 1985 rule, an edition gate, or a descriptor's
`No1985Rule`, and `EditionCitationNet` is a plain rule at 85 with no allowance list (docs/VERSION_TEST_MATRIX_DESIGN.md P2.3a).

**What is left.** At `--std 2002` and `--std 2014` most sites still pass the 2023 citation as their 2002 and 2014 arms
(the 2023 text passed to `Cite` as `later` with no `y2002` or `y2014` override), so a 2002 program is told a 2023 rule number. The 2002 read found these instances (its
dated files under kb/Work/PB4950: `2026-11-15-read-2002-b.md`, `2026-11-15-read-2002-c.md`, `2026-11-15-cobol0859-use-eo-parameterized.md`):
COBOL1560, COBOL1707, COBOL1628, COBOL1688, COBOL1627, COBOL1655, COBOL1553, COBOL2749, COBOL0891, COBOL1914, COBOL2143, the
EC-REPORT-INACTIVE run-time text, COBOL0859 and COBOL0813. The rows this note claims are theirs.

**The work.** The same sweep at 2002 and at 2014, each locator through `cite.py --check`, and `EditionCitationNet` extended to
ask `--std 2002` (a `§2014:` or `§2023:` citation with no `§2002:` one) and `--std 2014` (a `§2023:` citation with no `§2014:`
one). PB4950's design note says the per-edition citations are to come from the rule lineage
(kb/Work PB4991) when its chains exist.

**Sites:** `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/EditionCitationNet.cs` (`EditionCitationNet.AssertCitesTheEditionInForce`, `EditionCitationNet.CitesOnlyALaterEdition`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (whole: every descriptor's 2002 and 2014 citation arm is the work).
