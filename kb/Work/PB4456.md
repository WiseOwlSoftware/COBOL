# PB4456 - FreeForm checks the continuation point before the first content unit of a literal, so a doubled quotation symbol at the line end yields an empty continued line

**Site:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/ExpansionAssemblies.cs:94` (`ExpansionAssemblies.FreeForm`, the `column + unit > LineLimit - 2` test; the review cited line 91). The comment at line 81 ("a longer one (a long literal) starts where it is and is continued") is also inaccurate. Sites are at the head of branch `worktree-wf_d44be67f-63e-4` (PB2104, PR #1046); line numbers are that head's.

**Defect.** `FreeForm` writes an expansion's skeleton text as free-form lines of at most `LineLimit` (200) positions and splits a long literal with a floating continuation (`"-` then a new line opening with the quotation symbol). The split test runs for EVERY content unit, including the first one after the opening delimiter. When the opening delimiter lands so that the first unit does not fit (a doubled quotation symbol, `""` or `N""""`, is two positions: delimiter at position 197 gives 197 + 2 > 198; a one-character first unit does the same with the delimiter at 198), the line is closed as `""-`: the opening delimiter and NO content before the continuation indicator. ISO 6.4.2 (continuation of lines) requires a continued literal line to carry literal content (the compiler's own COBOL2690, `literal-continuation-part-empty`, refuses the empty part), so the expansion assembly of legal skeleton text fails to compile with that error.

**Fix shape.** Test the continuation point only once at least one content unit of the literal is on the line (or start the literal on a fresh line when its first unit cannot fit), correct the line-81 comment, and add a case with a literal whose opening delimiter sits at 197 and 198 and whose first unit is a doubled quotation symbol, in both the `"` and `N"` forms. Sweep for any other writer of free-form continuation.

## Landed (wave 20261840, group A)

`ExpansionAssemblies.FreeForm` now tracks where an open literal begins (prefix letters included, `PrefixLengthBefore`) and how many content units sit on the line being written. A content unit that does not fit before the `"-` reserve is either the first one of the literal on its line, in which case the literal moves to a line of its own (the preceding space becomes the line break), or a later one, in which case the continuation indicator and the continuation line's opening quotation symbol are written as before. A continuation line opens with a whole content unit (at most two positions) so it always holds content. ISO 6.4.2 (cite.py --check): "At least one alphanumeric character, national character, or hexadecimal digit of the literal content shall be specified on the continued line and on each continuation line" and "A pair of quotation symbols indicating a single quotation symbol within a literal shall be specified on the same line". The comment that called the long-literal start "where it is" is corrected: a word longer than half a line starts at most halfway along a line.

The report's repro columns were not the whole space: the defect shows for any short literal that fits a line whole and opens within two positions of the reserve (`"A"` at 198, `""""` at 197, `N""""` at 196-197, and the apostrophe forms). `ExpansionFreeFormTests` sweeps every literal kind (alphanumeric and national in both delimiters and both letter cases, boolean, X, BX, NX) over every opening column 1..199, reads each result back through the reference-format stage (zero diagnostics, the join equals the input), and the multi-unit golden `pb4456_expansion_literals_at_every_column` (with `pb4456_expansion_literals_refused_at_85`) runs a 1200-character literal run through a real expansion assembly. Sibling sweep: no other writer of a free-form continuation exists in `src` (grep for `LineLimit` and the floating continuation indicator outside the preprocessor).
