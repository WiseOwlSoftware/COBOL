# PB4849 - every 1985 general format, regenerated and cross-checked

**Why.** PB4812 rule 1: every general format is regenerated from the measured page, never taken from extracted text. The Opus
batch drew each 1985 general format from the page image (`<pre>`, `<u>`, box-drawing brackets and braces). That drawing is a
second, independent source. Where the measured figure and the drawn figure agree on all four things a general format carries
(grouping, choice bars, underlines, vertical alignment), the figure is accepted. Where they disagree, the printed page decides.

**What to do.**
- **Wire the raster arm in.** PB4815 left the figure pipeline vector-only and guarded by `spec_pdf`'s vector check (PB4815's
  lead: "figure_extract.extract reads underlines from vector drawings only; 1985 needs an underline raster arm in
  render_figure/sweep/audits"). For a raster edition, `figure_extract.extract`, `figure_geometry.classify_page`,
  `render_figure.build` and `sweep_figures.generated` take their strokes and underlines from `figure_raster`. That is one
  dispatch on the edition's kind, not a second pipeline, and no tool is copied.
- **Regenerate** every general format in the assembled `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (PB4816) with `sweep_figures.py --edition
  1985`, and record per figure: agree, or disagree on which of the four things.
- **Adversarial check.** Each disagreement goes to a refuter, which reads the rendered page (`render-spec-page.py --edition
  1985`) and decides between the two drawings, or finds both wrong. Run it as a `spec-grammar-conformance`-style workflow, with
  verdicts written to a ledger under `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/`.
- **Write the decided figure** into the file, drawn by FIGURE-STYLE.md, and run the four figure audits
  (`audit_underlining.py`, `audit_figure_structure.py`, `audit_figure_text.py`, `audit_grammar_optional_words.py`) with
  `--edition 1985`.

**Boxes (found 2026-11-06, PB4850's figure-only pass).** A drawn box marks syntax unique to level 2 of a module (PDF pages 23 and
147; PB4848). The raw pages lose every box: the figure-only pass reported 68 lost boxes on 315 general-format pages. Every
regenerated general format carries its boxes. Draw them by a FIGURE-STYLE.md rule that this note adds: a glyph set distinct from
brackets and braces, so that a reader and the grammar audits can tell a level-2 span from optional syntax. Check each of the
figure pass's box claims against the measured rectangles.

**Done when** every general format in the 1985 file is either agreed or decided by a recorded verdict, `sweep_figures.py
--edition 1985 --check` is clean, and the four audits are GREEN.

**Landed in part (wave 20261890, the raster arm and the cross-check).** A scan has no text layer to find a figure by
or read its words from, so the raster arm reads both from the transcription's own drawing:
- `figure_extract.page_extractor` and `figure_geometry.page_classifier` are the one dispatch on `spec_pdf.geometry`; the
  raster arm's page is a `figure_raster.RasterPage` (the old raster arm of `page_classifier` passed a PyMuPDF page to
  `measure_page`, which takes a page number: it could not have run).
- `figure_raster.measure_figures(edition, page, figures)` measures the page once, places each drawn figure on it
  (`locate`: the page's figures placed together, in order, as disjoint runs of printed lines whose OCR words match each
  figure's; the OCR text is a position hint only) and pairs each drawn word with its ink (`align`: one assignment over
  the figure by glyph count and pitch, relative row and column, and the OCR hint; typed delimiters second, by the words
  beside them; equal words and delimiter runs dealt in their drawn order). Every unpaired word or box, a pair too
  unlike, and a line paired out of order is a disagreement and the word stays unread. `find_words` now joins an i's dot
  to its letter, keeps an ellipsis one word, splits a typed `[`/`{` off its word, and groups lines by the letters
  nearest each glyph (a typed line runs uphill: page 310); the skew is measured clear of the stamp's lines. The
  assignment is `figure_raster.hungarian` (numpy only: CI's self-tests install no scipy).
- `figure_reading.read` turns either drawing (the house style or the batch's) into words with their row, column,
  underlined letters and holders; `compare` names every word on which two drawings differ, per thing.
- `sweep_figures.py --edition 1985 --cross-check [--ledger F]` regenerates every drawn figure and gives each a verdict:
  agree, disagree (on which things), unpaired (a word the pairing could not read), unlocated. `render_figure.py <page>
  --edition 1985` prints a page's regenerated figures with what stayed unread, for the refuter. `--report`, `--apply`
  and `--check` refuse a scanned edition until the verdicts exist.

**Landed in part (wave 20261891, the level-2 boxes).** FIGURE-STYLE.md rule 9 draws a box in DOUBLE lines, its two
sides only (`╔ ║ ╚` / `╗ ║ ╝`, and `║ … ║` one row tall), a fourth family tellable from brackets, braces and
parentheses. `figure_raster.classify_page` gives each measured box as two stems of kind `box` (`box_side`), and
`render_figure.build` pairs, seats and draws them like a bracket's stems in a column of their own; a box is no group,
so it adds and removes no blank row. `figure_reading` reads a box from either drawing (these sides, or the batch's
full `╔═╗`/`╚═╝` rectangle; a corner-less `║` pair is a one-row box) and `compare` names a fifth thing, `boxes`.
`sweep_figures.regenerated` names a measured box the drawing lost (its words stayed unread) instead of dropping it.
`find_boxes` now requires the rectangle its rules enclose to be a box's size: an underline fused to a descender
(page 222) and one side of a drawn diagram (page 353) were read as boxes 1.3 pt tall or 1.4 pt wide, and their ink
erased (page 222's crop is now a calibration case). The batch drew some boxes as brackets (page 662: `┌DESTINATION
TABLE …┐`, dropping a typed `[`), so those figures come out unpaired, for the refuter.

**Landed in part (wave 20261893, wrapped boxes: PB4898).** `figure_raster.find_boxes` now measures a box that wraps a
line (one outline around two lines' boxes) as a box per line; the re-run cross-check (ledger in the wave report) keeps
every verdict (181 agree, 68 disagree, 212 unpaired, 40 unlocated) and measures 114 boxes reaching a figure, not 104.
The page 533 figure's `[TO·integer-5]` is a speck PRINTED on the page, not a batch typo: writing the decided figure
(`--apply`) drops it.

**Landed in part (wave 20261897, the decided figures written).** `sweep_figures.py --edition 1985 --report | --apply |
--check` now run (`raster_sweep`): `decide` writes a `<pre>` block in the house style when a refuter's verdict decides
it (`load_verdicts`: one JSON file per block, `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/figures-1985/p<page>-<figure>.json`,
keyed by the page and the block's ordinal on it, carrying `drawn_lines` (the block's lines as drawn, its identity:
`decide` refuses a verdict whose ordinal now names another block, review R1-1), `decided` = generated | drawn | neither |
not-a-format, `figure_lines` and `reason`; the format is in that directory's README.md row), or when the block is a
general format (`Drawn.general_format`: under a heading or after a sentence naming one, or with a `Format N` label;
`spec_headings.GENERAL_FORMAT_NAMED`) whose words were all read and whose two drawings agree on every word and thing.
The batch set COBOL examples, flow diagrams and tables in `<pre>` too (PDF pages 96-100, 763-954): the first apply,
keyed on agreement alone, flattened their indentation and merged two-column examples, so no agreement rewrites a block
that is not where a general format is printed; it awaits a `not-a-format` verdict. A written figure regenerates to
itself (`--check` clean on every written figure): `figure_reading.token_rows` reads the house `…` as the page's `...`
(65 written figures stopped pairing without it), `figure_raster._Frame.cost` takes an OCR hint of any delimiter of a
typed delimiter's side (`DELIM_SIDE`: the OCR reads `]}` as `])`), and `figure_reading.compare` names a stroke that
pairs with nothing (`strays`; page 522's page-wide level-2 box side came out a lone `│` the comparison could not see).
The cross-check ledger now carries each block's `figure` ordinal and `general_format`. The written count, the ledger
and the awaiting count are in the wave report.

reading, not the printed page.
