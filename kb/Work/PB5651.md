# PB5651 - COBOL2021 counts a multiple-operand LINE clause as an OCCURS clause

**Site (lead, from the writer):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReportSectionBinder.cs#ReportOccursOf`: the SR10 check
fires on `depending is not null && st.Repetitions.Count > 0`, and `Repetitions` holds the repetitions of a multiple LINE or
COLUMN clause as well as those of OCCURS clauses. The diagnostic cites §2023:13.18.38.3 SR10 at --std 2002.

**Rules (each checked with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`, verdict OK).**

- `§2002:13.16.36.2 SR9`: "If the DEPENDING ON phrase is not specified, an OCCURS clause may be subordinate to a data description entry that contains another OCCURS clause" - it limits only an OCCURS clause under an entry containing another OCCURS clause.
- `§2002:13.13.3 GR3`: "an OCCURS clause or a LINE or COLUMN clause with more than one operand" - the two kinds of repeating entry are named apart.
- `§2002:13.16.51.3 GR4`: "SOURCE operands are nevertheless assigned to them, even though they are not printed" - the rule whose third sentence the repro witnesses.

**Repro** (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/gl2002/lane1/out/g2002-13-16-52-sum/defects/2002-GR-13.16.51.3-4/g2src02_source_suppressed_before_printed.cob`,
its header comments omitted here; `cobol.exe <file> --std 2002 --run`):

```cobol
       REPORT SECTION.
       RD  R1.
       01  DMULTI TYPE DE.
           02  LINE PLUS 1 PLUS 1.
               03  COLUMN 1 PIC X(3) OCCURS 1 TO 2 TIMES
                   DEPENDING ON WS-N STEP 4
                   SOURCES ARE CA CB CC CE.
```

with `WS-N PIC 9 VALUE 1`, `CA`..`CE` = "AAA" "BBB" "CCC" "DDD", one GENERATE DMULTI, and the print file read back line by line.

Expected (derived, beside the repro in `.expected`): it compiles and prints `[AAA]` then `[CCC]`: the suppressed second
occurrences of each line still take CB and CE. Observed (main b15bb9088f): `(47,16): error COBOL2021: RD 'R1' entry
'FILLER': an OCCURS clause may be subordinate to an entry containing another OCCURS clause only if the DEPENDING ON phrase
is not specified (ISO §2023:13.18.38.3 SR10)`, rc 65.

**Rows.** 2002-GR-13.16.51.3-4 (its first two sentences are witnessed by G2SRC01; the third waits on this fix; the repro
becomes its golden).

**Sibling sweep.** Whether the 2023 SR10 is worded OCCURS-only too (then 2023 refuses the same legal shape); every other
check that reads `Repetitions` as "an OCCURS clause" (subscript counting, SR24 sequencing).
