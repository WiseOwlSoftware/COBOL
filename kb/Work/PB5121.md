# PB5121 - at --std 85 a format 1 SEARCH whose index is below 1 (left one decrement below the table by PERFORM VARYING ... BY -1) goes to AT END; 1985 tests only the upper bound and evaluates the WHEN conditions

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ControlFlowEmitter.cs#EmitSerialScan` (line 704): the start test `{IndexField} < 1 || {IndexField} > {bound}` (§2023:14.9.37.4 GR4), emitted at every edition. LANDED: the start test is gated by `DialectBehavior.SearchIndexBelowTableUnsuccessful` (2002, VCR 7.55): `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DialectBehavior.cs` (`DialectBehaviors.Table`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SearchBinder.cs` (`BindSearch`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundTree.cs` (`BoundSearch.IndexBelowTableIsUnsuccessful`) and `ControlFlowEmitter.EmitSerialScan`; goldens `14.9.37-SEARCH/pb5121_search_index_below_table_85` and `pb5121_search_index_below_table`.

**Mechanism.** The 1985 SEARCH figure compares the index setting with the highest permissible occurrence number: greater goes to AT END, otherwise the conditions are evaluated; an index outside the table by one decrement after PERFORM or SEARCH is expressly allowed (§1985:VI.6.21.4 GR10, §1985:VI.6.23.4 GR2). §2023:14.9.37.4 GR4 instead makes a negative, zero or too-large index unsuccessful. The compiler emits the 2023 start test at every edition. The first eight lines of the draft (WHEN order, V stepping, index at 5 going to AT END at once) match.
