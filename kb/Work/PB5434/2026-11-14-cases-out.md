## 2026-11-14: land_lane.py's self-test is case files (kb/Work PB5434)

**Built.** `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` lost `self_test` (1,267 lines, one `try` body of 602 statements), its `--self-test` flag and
its two imports only the self-test used (`time`, `PR_LIST_FIELDS`); 3,038 lines became 1,750. The cases are
`scripts/selftests/orchestrator/land_lane/`: `fixtures.py` (`world`, `fresh`, `row`, `Fake`, `finish`, `wait_runners`) and six case files, one per
fixture world: `test_tick.py` (planted surveys, the attempts' lifecycle, the stops, the lock), `test_detached_runner.py` (the real launch path,
a reused pid, a runner that died after commit), `test_stranded_branches.py`, `test_queue_watch.py` (the watch, one writer per field, a hung
call, the class of a merge-queue red), `test_conflict_brief.py`, `test_source_shape.py`. `scripts/selftest_layout.py` `REMAINING` lost the
subject's line; `DECLARED` and `WIRING` never held it.

**Oracle (DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md 16.1).** The labels, by a syntax-tree walk of every `expect(` call's first argument: 97 before, 97 after,
multiset equal. The old self-test passed 100 `PASS` lines; the six files are GREEN under `scripts/self_tests.py --only`.

**Three things the conversion found.**

1. The self-test's `try` body is one order-dependent world (names `c`, `f`, `out`, `a` rebound and read across sections). The converter
   (a reaching-definitions walk over the statements) found the five groups of cases that share no state and made each a file; inside a file the
   case order is load-bearing, as in plan_wave's `test_planner.py`, and the module globals each file starts with are the names a later case
   reads.
2. One accidental coupling was cut: the conflict-brief case read `out`, the list the real-launch case filled. It plants its own list (the arms
   append to it and assert nothing of it).
3. The stopped-clock case replaced the subject's `now_utc` with `globals()["now_utc"] = ...`, which inside the subject was the subject's
   namespace; in a case file that rebinds the case file's. It now sets `ll.now_utc`, the subject's attribute, and restores it.

**Design correction (section 16.2).** The design and the note called the detached runner's world Windows-only and asked for a
`# SELF-TEST-PLATFORM:` header on its file. The self-test it came from carried no header and ran on Linux (the reused-pid arm reads `/proc`, kb/Work
PB3973), so the file `test_detached_runner.py` carries none: a header would drop that coverage. Section 16.2 says so.

**Sites the diff changed beyond the note's list:** `scripts/selftest_layout.py` (`REMAINING`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md`
(sections 12 and 16.2).
