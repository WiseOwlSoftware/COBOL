# PB4611 — the ENTRY-CONVENTION clause is gated as a 2014 construct; only its COBOL alternative is

**What is wrong.** The golden `conformance-suite/cases/11.9.7-ENTRY-CONVENTION/pb232-entry-convention-name.cob` at
`--std 2002` prints COBOL2385 (correct: WiseOwl defines no entry-convention-name) AND COBOL0900 "OPTIONS ENTRY-CONVENTION
requires COBOL-2014" (probe, main's `cobol.exe`, 2026-11-04). The second diagnostic is false. The 2002 OPTIONS paragraph has the
clause: `t2002.txt:10939` and `10979` (11.8.5 ENTRY-CONVENTION clause), `10984` `ENTRY-CONVENTION IS entry-convention-name-1`,
GR2 at `10997` (the name's meaning is implementor-defined). The 2014 FCD adds the COBOL alternative:
`t2014.txt:13144-13146` `ENTRY-CONVENTION IS { COBOL | entry-convention-name-1 }`, GR2 at `13166` "When COBOL is specified,
the naming convention and mapping of method-names and program-names are as specified in 8.3.1.1.1". 2023 §11.9.7.4 GR2 is the
same rule (`cite.py --check` OK).

**Expected.** At 2002 a named convention draws COBOL2385 alone; `ENTRY-CONVENTION IS COBOL` draws COBOL0900 (the alternative is
2014). At 2014 and 2023 nothing changes.

**Fix shape.** The gate moves to the COBOL alternative (the refuter: "the COBOL formatWord arm of entryConventionName"). Row
`options-entry-convention-2014` stays at 2014 with its description, citation and VCR text re-scoped to "the COBOL alternative of
the ENTRY-CONVENTION clause (§11.9.7.2, §11.9.7.4 GR2)"; the VCR records that the clause itself, in the form
`ENTRY-CONVENTION IS entry-convention-name-1`, is 2002 (settling the pending 2002 clause inventory entry for ARITHMETIC and
ENTRY-CONVENTION). The golden's header comment ("Below 2014 the clause does not exist") is corrected.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`Constructs.OptionsEntryConvention2014`, about
line 2270, on the whole clause), `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (row `options-entry-convention-2014`),
`docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md`, `conformance-suite/cases/11.9.7-ENTRY-CONVENTION/pb232-entry-convention-name.cob`.

**Assignment.** The owner assigned this fix to the Mythos lane (2026-11-04).

**Landed (wave 20261866 A, branch worktree-wf_4dd35aaa-2ac-1).** Re-probe on main's build: `ENTRY-CONVENTION IS STDCALL` at
`--std 2002` drew COBOL2385 AND COBOL0900 ("OPTIONS ENTRY-CONVENTION requires COBOL-2014"); `IS COBOL` drew 0900 at 2002.
The gate moves from the whole clause to the COBOL alternative: `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitEntryConventionClause`
asks `Constructs.OptionsEntryConvention2014` only when `entryConventionName().formatWord()` is present (the grammar rule's COBOL
alternative). After the fix: STDCALL at 2002, 2014, 2023 draws COBOL2385 alone; `IS COBOL` draws 0900 at 85 (beside the
OPTIONS paragraph's own COBOL0804) and 2002, nothing at 2014 and 2023. The row id stays (`options-entry-convention-2014`);
its description, display, citation and VCR text are re-scoped; VCR row 7.50 records that the clause is 2002 (t2002.txt:10979-10997)
and the COBOL alternative 2014 (t2014.txt:13144-13146, GR2 at 13166), settling the pending 2002 clause inventory entry. The
inventory rows FMT-11.9.7.2, SR-11.9.7.3-1, GR-11.9.7.4-1 to -4 are re-recorded (editions 2002 where the 2002 text has the rule;
the "conservative choice" sentence replaced). Goldens: `pb4611-entry-convention-cobol-below-2014` (refused at 85, 2002),
`pb232-entry-convention-name` now refuses at 2002 too; the absence of the false 0900 is pinned by `EntryConventionGateTests`.
Sibling sweep: the other OPTIONS clause rows (DEFAULT ROUNDED, INTERMEDIATE ROUNDING, FLOAT-BINARY, FLOAT-DECIMAL) are PB4608's
(FLOAT-* UNDECIDABLE) and were not touched.
