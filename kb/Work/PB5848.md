# PB5848 - a COPY text-name is never checked against the reserved words (every edition)

**Ruling: UPHELD (b), harm under_rejects.** The row `1985-SR-IV.4.2.2.1-2` does not close on its inherited verdict. `COPY LENGTH.` and `COPY ADD.` compile: a text-name is never checked against the reserved words. It is the sibling of kb/Work PB5482. The checker's confidence: high at 85; medium on the 2023 half (it rests on text-name being a system-name, `§2023:8.3.2.3.12`, not separately run through cite.py). Cause (b): the same programs run at 2023, so the hole is not a missing 85 arm.

**The checker's evidence (rulings.json, copied).** 1985: text-name and library-name are user-defined words (`§1985:IV.4.2.2.1.1`, list items 9 and 19), and the COBOL words LENGTH, RANDOM and SUM may not be used as a system-name or as a user-defined word regardless of context (`§1985:IV.4.2.2.1 SR2b`); every other reserved word is barred by SR1a (`§1985:IV.4.2.2.1 SR1`). `COPY LENGTH.` and `COPY ADD.` compile and run at 85. The reader probed every user-defined-word class reached by the COBOL0901 funnel but not the two the preprocessor consumes. The same programs run at 2023, where text-name is a system-name and `§2023:8.3.2.4.1` bars a reserved word as a system-name or user-defined word, so the COPY names are screened for reserved words at no edition (cause b). kb/Work PB5482 (landed) fed 1985 text-names and library-names into the disjoint-set check only (CopyNameCensus, gated by EditionInfo.CopyNamesAreUserDefinedWords); the reserved-word half was never added. The refuter's library-name observation (`COPY KTLIB OF SUM` reported as copybook not found) is the same hole seen through the library lookup; not re-probed.
