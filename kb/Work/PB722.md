## Why it is refuted

The rule is real and correctly cited — `cite.py --check 14.9.34.4 "The size of the current record is determined by
rules specified for the RECORD clause"` → **OK §14.9.34.4 5) b)**, and
`--check 13.18.43.4 "the number of bytes in the current record that participate as the sending operands in the
implicit MOVE statement is determined by the following conditions"` → **OK §13.18.43.4 16)**. What is wrong is the
**conclusion drawn from the probe**, and the reason is the probe's own shape.

`p11`'s receiver is `PIC X(12)` — **left-justified**, the default alignment.
`--check 14.6.8.5 "aligned at the leftmost character position in the data item with space fill or truncation to the
right"` → **OK §14.6.8.5**, so a 6-byte sender and a 12-byte space-padded sender land the same 12 characters.
The two hypotheses are indistinguishable on that receiver **by construction**: p11 cannot fail, whichever sender
the emitter uses. That is `feedback_probe_the_shape_the_subject_hides` exactly — the
probe held fixed the very axis the difference lives on.

Flip the axis and the two separate immediately. §13.18.32.4 governs a JUSTIFIED receiver:

- `--check 13.18.32.4 "the leftmost character positions or boolean positions of the sending operand shall be
  truncated"` → **OK §13.18.32.4 1)** — an over-long sender loses its **LEFTMOST** characters.
- `--check 13.18.32.4 "the data is aligned at the rightmost character position or boolean position in the data item
  with zero fill for the leftmost boolean positions and space fill for the leftmost character positions"` → **OK
  §13.18.32.4 2)** — a shorter sender is right-aligned with left space fill.

So into a `PIC X(12) JUSTIFIED RIGHT` receiver, the **current record** (6 bytes) gives `      2BB   `-style right
alignment, while the **whole 20-byte space-padded area** is an over-long sender whose leftmost 12 characters — all
spaces — are what survive GR1. PB339 measured precisely that on the RETURN arm
(`…\a9d94900…\scratchpad\PB339\pb339ret.cob`, SD `RECORD VARYING 3 TO 20 DEPENDING`, an 8-byte released record,
`RETURN … INTO` a `PIC X(12) JUSTIFIED`): `JUST=[            ]` at `c34f01ad`, `JUST=[    K01PQRST]` after its fix.
Not identical; the whole content is destroyed.

## The tell that makes this a pattern and not a one-off

The twin row records the SAME observation and gets it RIGHT. `GR-14.9.30.4-4` (READ … INTO, owner PB339,
verdict `DIVERGES`) says in its own notes:

> For a left-justified receiver the two are observationally identical (the emitter's own comment says so, and that
> is true), but for a JUSTIFIED receiver §13.18.32.4 GR1 truncates the LEFTMOST characters of an over-long sender,
> so the defined characters are dropped.

Two rows, one shared sentence of the standard (§14.9.30.4 GR4 b) and §14.9.34.4 GR5 b) are the same words twice),
one emitter shape — and only one of them was measured on the discriminating receiver. `feedback_two_arm_dispatch`:
ask which arm you measured. The READ arm got a JUSTIFIED probe; the RETURN arm got p11.

## Why it matters

A verdict is auditable; a sentence inside a row's `notes` is not. `GR-14.9.34.4-5` stays `PARTIAL`/`GAP` for
PB348's own binder-side reason (the implied MOVE is synthesized in the emitter, downstream of `CheckStrongMove`),
so the row **looks** open and nothing draws attention to the one sub-conclusion inside it that is wrong. The next
reader — including PB348's own implementer — inherits "observationally identical", concludes the size half of GR5 b)
needs no work, and closes the row having fixed only the MOVE-rules half. That is how the size half survived to be
found by an unrelated fix lane: `feedback_a_dead_lookup_is_also_unverified` at the level of a row's evidence, and
CLAUDE.md rule 1's actual failure mode — not inventing a claim but **inheriting** one.

## The fix shape

**PB348's implementer rewrites the row's notes, and re-derives rather than inherits.**

⛔ **Through a `record_verdicts.py` batch, never a hand edit of `tests/version-matrix/traceability-inventory.json`.**
The script's own docstring states the rule: *"the alternative — reviewers hand-editing a 1 MB JSON array — loses a
batch the first time two of them overlap, and offers no point at which anything is checked. So verdicts are never
written by hand."* The batch is all-or-nothing and validated before merge. `--dry-run` first, then apply, and read
the **GAP delta** on the output (`reference_verdict_recording_forms` — `record_verdicts.py` drops bad rows per
record, so a silently-dropped record looks like success).

What the replacement text must do, in order:

1. **Delete the identity claim and the p11 evidence for it.** p11 is not wrong as a run; it is not evidence for
   this proposition, and leaving it attached to a corrected sentence would re-license the same inference.
2. **State the size half against the tree the batch lands on.** ⛔ Sequencing matters and is measurable: PB339's
   fix is **not on main** as of `0db46983` (`grep -rn "BoundCurrentRecord" src/` returns nothing), so today the
   emitter really does send the whole area — `SortEmitter.cs:178`,
   `move.Emit(new BoundMove(new BoundFieldOperand(rt.RecordArea), [into]))` — and the size half **DIVERGES**. Once
   PB339 lands, the sender becomes `BoundCurrentRecord` via `SequentialIoEmitter.IntoSender` and the size half
   CONFORMS, leaving only PB348's own MOVE-rules half open. The notes must describe whichever of those two trees
   the batch is written against, and say which.
3. **Carry a discriminating witness.** Whatever verdict the size half gets, its evidence has to be a JUSTIFIED
   receiver (or an equivalent right-aligned shape), because a left-justified one cannot fail either way. PB339's
   goldens `tests/conformance/{85,2002,2014,2023}/pb339_into_current_record.*` already contain that witness on the
   RETURN arm (leg G, `PIC X(12) JUSTIFIED RIGHT`) and are the natural `test-ref`.
4. **Fix the owning note too.** `kb/Work/PB348.md:87-88` lists the size half among the obligations that *"DO hold
   and were verified"*. A corrected row with an uncorrected note is the same defect in the other register.

## Sweep

- **Every row whose evidence is a MOVE into a receiver the probe chose.** The blind shape is not "p11" but
  "left-justified receiver", and any row justifying a sender-size or sender-length claim through one has the same
  hole. The mechanical form: a probe asserting *what* was sent must vary the receiver's alignment, since
  §13.18.32.4 is the only place the sender's length becomes observable through a plain MOVE. `GR-14.9.30.4-4` and
  `GR-14.9.34.4-5` are the pair the standard writes twice; `GR-13.18.43.4-16` (GR16 itself, named in no note's

  another blind one.
