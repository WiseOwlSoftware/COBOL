- `OK  §1985:XIII.3.8.3 SR8b  (3.8.3 Syntax Rules)  the text is in SR8b` — "A page heading report group, if defined, must be defined such that it can be presented in the vertical subdivision" (from integer-2 to integer-3 minus 1)

Reasoning: The 1985 text has two kinds of limit. One kind is the static vertical subdivisions "as defined by the PAGE clause": SR8b gives a page heading HEADING..FIRST DETAIL−1, and SR1 forbids a LINE integer that puts a line outside them. These are syntax rules, and the compiler enforces both ends (phhd2, phfd6). The other kind is GR1's page-1 limit, which depends on the report heading's final LINE-COUNTER. It is a general (presentation) rule, and neither SR8 nor SR1 restates it, because neither refers to another group. A program that breaks GR1 is not a conforming source program, and §1985:I.1.6 makes "the results of violating the formats or rules" undefined. Placing the page heading on its written line (GR3a) is therefore a permitted result, and no diagnostic is required. Whether to add one anyway for consistency is a codebase design choice (Claude's under CLAUDE.md Ownership), not a conformance question and not the owner's.

Lead (separate from this verdict; the owner of the screen should file it): at 85, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReportSectionBinder.cs#GroupLimits` (ReportHeading arm) sets a report heading's lower limit to "the page heading's first absolute line − 1". That is the 2023 GR8 b) shape. The COBOL2199 text then attributes that limit to "§1985:XIII.3.15.3 SR1 ... limits per §1985:XIII.3.8.3 SR8", but the 1985 rules put the RH limit at FIRST DETAIL − 1 (SR8a; §1985:XIII.3.10.6 GR2a). Every program it refuses (phov3, the RH half of phhd2) does break §1985:XIII.3.10.7 GR1, so nothing legal is rejected. The message blames the report heading and cites rules that do not state the limit (candidate harm: misleads_diagnosis). The same GR1 violation also goes unreported when it comes from the report heading's NEXT GROUP (phup4) rather than its lines. If a diagnostic is wanted, one shape covers both: at 85, check the page heading's page-1 upper limit, `Cobol85FinalLineCounter(RH) + 1`, in the PageHeading arm of `GroupLimits`, cite §1985:XIII.3.10.7 GR1, and drop the borrowed RH lower limit at 85. Every GR1 violation is then reported once, on the page heading, whether it comes from the RH's lines or from its NEXT GROUP. Goldens at 85 would be phup4 and phov3 (refused, naming PHG and GR1) and phfd6 (unchanged).
