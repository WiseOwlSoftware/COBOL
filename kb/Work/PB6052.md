**Asked by the owner, 2026-11-22 (account 1 session):** "I read a recommendation that CLAUDE.md should be deleted entirely
given the ability of the new models. That overconstraining the new models lessens their usefulness. Should we do that in
whole or in part? Question applies to both accounts". On the recommendation below: "yes, do it on a branch".

**Decision (Claude's, under CLAUDE.md Ownership: tooling and process shape).** Trim, do not delete. CLAUDE.md goes from
16,076 to about 6,900 characters. It is the one instruction file both accounts and every agent load on every turn, so its
size and tone are paid for everywhere.

**Kept (not derivable from the code or the tools):** the Ownership section and the owner's reservations, word for word;
the quota plan in force; rules 1–8 under their numbers (scripts and docs cite them by number); the spec precedence and the
`cite.py --check` requirement; no byte `ProgramState`; one work register; prove your work; do not redo work at unchanged
inputs; the project's names, mission and where things live.

**Removed, and where it lives instead:**
- The gate's lane rules, the merge-group explanation and the "every Windows case ran green" rule: `scripts/gate.py`,
  `scripts/lane_classification.py` and `enqueue.py land` enforce them, and the `gate` skill and plan §9 explain them.
  CLAUDE.md keeps the one gate command, the one landing command, and "classify a red before changing anything".
- Incident narrative inside rules (the five registers of 2026-08-04, the OCR history, the toolchain detail): history
  belongs in DEVLOG and the cited notes.
- Repetition of `PROMPT.md`, the skills and the session-start hook.
- The ⛔/capitals emphasis. Current models over-apply a shouted rule to cases it was not written for; plain statements
  carry the same rule.

**THE RULE moved to the `gate` skill.** The full statement (every Windows merge-group case already green at the pushed
head, or byte-identical to one by the program differential; one `toolchain.env`) is now in `.claude/skills/gate/SKILL.md`,
and the nine references to CLAUDE.md "Testing" (MANDATORY-PRACTICES I1/I12, DOC_INDEX, DESIGN-test-build-ci,
DESIGN-orchestrator-loop) point there.

**The example citation changed** from `§2023:13.18.35.4 GR1` to `§2023:8.4.2.3.4 GR1b`, which `cite.py --check` passes
with its quotation ("EC-BOUND-SUBSCRIPT exception condition is set to exist"); §2023:4.2.1 also checked.

**Not in this change:** the session-start hook's injected text and each account's memory index (MEMORY.md) are the other
per-turn context. The account 1 memory index was cut under its 6,000-character cap on 2026-11-22; the hook output is a
follow-up for the same reason.
