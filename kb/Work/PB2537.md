# PB2537 — one lander on main at a time, mechanically

The rule "one lander on main at a time" (workstream SKILL §3) is enforced only at the last step: `push-main.sh` refuses a
push whose base is not origin/main. A landing's own cost before that step is ≈ 50 min (rebase, lander gate, Linux gate,
oracle, CI). On 2026-10-07 the owner approved R1 at 17:42 PDT; its lander (an attended Opus agent, report
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1031-PB2118-land-report.md`) rebased three times as the loop landed train 1034
(DEVLOG 1924) and train 1034b (DEVLOG 1925) inside its window, re-gated each time, and ended SPLIT at its 150-turn cap
with lander GREEN, Linux GREEN, oracle IDENTICAL, landing check PASS and CI run 37714677935 green — and still not on main.
Each lost race costs a full re-gate (≈ 30 min of the whole machine) plus a DEVLOG renumber and conflict resolution.

## Shape
A landing LEASE in the coordination dir, taken by every lander (the loop's `land` unit and attended landers alike)
BEFORE its final rebase and gates, released after push-main succeeds or fails, with an owner/holder, a reason, a heartbeat
and an expiry so a dead lander cannot hold main; a second lander waits (or the loop's supervisor picks a `wave` unit
instead of `land` while another lease is held). One implementation (`scripts/orchestrator/landing_lease.py`), called by
`push-main.sh`'s caller steps in the lander briefs and by the loop's land unit; `stop.ps1 -Status` and `gate_slot.py
status` show the holder. A self-test plants two landers and shows the second waits instead of re-gating. Rule 6: the
workstream skill §3, MANDATORY-PRACTICES L-rows and the orchestrator design describe it.

## Landed: the landing lease (wave 1037 group P, 2026-10-07, branch `landing-lease`)

- **`scripts/orchestrator/landing_lease.py`** (`acquire | renew | release | check | status | --self-test`), the one
  implementation. The lease is `<coord>\landing-lease.json`: holder, reason, WORKTREE (its owner: a lander resuming in
  the same worktree re-takes it), branch, host, acquired/heartbeat/expiry (30 min after the last renew). A lease whose
  expiry passed or whose worktree no longer exists is DEAD and the next acquire takes it over (logged with the dead
  holder). `acquire --wait-min 9` polls while another worktree holds a live lease and exits 4 (`WAIT`) after the wait;
  renew and release are the holder's only (exit 4 otherwise, `LOST` on a renew). Every change AND every read runs under
  `coord.locked(cdir, "landing-lease.lock")` (on Windows a reader holding the file open makes a writer's replace fail);
  a file that is not a lease exits 2 from every verb, never 0/1/4; events go to `<coord>\landing-lease.jsonl`.
- **`coord.py` hardening** found by the self-review: `coord_dir()` refuses a relative coordination directory (the
  Windows default is a relative name on Linux, so a lease there would bind no one); the mutex's access-denied branch is
  now bounded by its deadline instead of spinning; reads, replaces and the lock's removal retry a Windows sharing
  refusal briefly.
- **`coord.locked(cdir, name)`**: the coordination directory's one short mutex, moved out of `alloc.py` (which, with
  `test_alloc.py`, now calls it) rather than written a second time.
- **`scripts/push-main.sh` is the backstop** every landing passes: it re-takes the caller's lease or takes a free one,
  refuses the landing with exit 4 while another worktree holds it (before a CI run is spent) and dies (exit 2) when it
  cannot read it, renews it every minute while CI runs (retrying a transient failure, stopping only on `LOST`, logging
  to a file), checks it again before the push to main (free: takes it back; taken over: exit 4 with the renewer's
  log), and releases it on exit, landed or not, loudly when the release fails; a re-run that finds its sha already on
  main releases its worktree's lease too. Its UNVERIFIED
  main-run exit moved from 3 to 5: exit 3 is the landing check's STOP ("never retry as is"), so a landed commit whose
  main-run verdict could not be read was reported as a blocked landing.
- **The callers**: lander-train-brief and lander-brief step 2b (acquire before the final rebase and every gate, renew at
  every step and before every wait, release when stopping before push-main; a SPLIT keeps it for the resumer),
  golden-lander-brief step 4, registrar-brief (push-main's exit 4), `wf_rolling_wave.js`'s train prompt, `units/land.md`;
  `check_practices.py` requires the lease in the lander briefs, the workflow and MANDATORY-PRACTICES. `next_unit.py`
  defers `land` (rule 6, and a handoff naming `land`, rule 2) while a lease is live, naming the holder in the reason;
  `stop.ps1 -Status` prints the lease line. `gate_slot.py status` does not: the gate slots live in the git common dir
  and no gate consults the lease, and the dispatch named `stop.ps1 -Status` as the one view.
- **Tests**: `landing_lease.py --self-test`, 9 arms (two landers: the second waits and gates only after the first
  releases; takeover of an expired lease and of one whose worktree is gone; holder-only renew/release; the resuming
  holder by any spelling of its path; six parallel acquires, exactly one wins; push-main's `check`; a corrupt lease;
  next_unit defers `land`), each seen to fail
