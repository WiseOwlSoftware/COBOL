# PB4156 — the extended-letter gate is skipped for the whole word, and the pass half of PB4130 is unpinned

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs:2691` (`VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitCobolWord`), `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/pb4130-no-break-space-in-a-word.json`, `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/pb1402-not-an-annex-b-character.json`, `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/pb1402-annex-b-2023-letter-before-2023.json` (the COBOL2773 refusals).

Two review leads of PB4130's branch, one change, one site.

**R1-1 (behaviour, low).** In `VisitCobolWord` the 2002 extended-letter gate is
`if (character is null || _p._edition.Year >= 2002) _p.Check(Constructs.UserWordExtendedLetter2002, ...)`, where `character` is
`CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation(raw, year)`: the first character in NO edition's Annex B. Below 2002 the gate is therefore
skipped for the whole word as soon as it holds any such character, even when the same word also holds a real extended letter
(a character the 2002 Annex B admits and COBOL-85 does not). That letter goes unreported at COBOL-85; the word is still refused,
with COBOL2773 for the other character alone. The decision should be per character: the gate asks whether the word holds an extended
letter, not whether it holds a non-Annex-B character. Probe: a word with one extended letter and one euro sign (or U+00A0) at
`--std 85` and `--std 2002`; the implementer decides from the spec's COBOL-word and Annex B rules which diagnostics the word draws
at 85 (validate each clause with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).

**R1-2 (test gap, low).** Nothing pins the `VersionConformancePass` half of PB4130's fix (COBOL0900 no longer fires at 85 for a
non-Annex-B character): the refusals in `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/` assert only that COBOL2773 is present
(`EditionHarness.AssertHasDiagnostic`), and the unit test calls `CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation`, not the pass. A regression that
brings the misleading COBOL0900 back passes every gate. Add a case that asserts COBOL0900 is ABSENT for that word at 85 and present
at 2002 where the character is a real extended letter.

Not mine: PB3845 (a U+00A0-only line breaks the parse before the word check) remains open and separate.
