# PB4156 — the extended-letter gate is skipped for the whole word, and the pass half of PB4130 is unpinned

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/WordCharacterScreen.cs:110` (`WordCharacterScreen.Check`; PB3845 moved the word check out of `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitCobolWord`, where this note first located it), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/CobolCharacterRepertoire.cs` (`CobolCharacterRepertoire.HasExtendedLetter`, `CobolCharacterRepertoire.IsInSomeAnnexB`, `CobolCharacterRepertoire.ViolationAtEveryEdition`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/WordCharacterScreenTests.cs`, `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/pb4156-extended-letter-beside-euro-sign.json`, `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/pb4130-no-break-space-in-a-word.json`, `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/pb1402-not-an-annex-b-character.json`, `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/pb1402-annex-b-2023-letter-before-2023.json` (the COBOL2773 refusals).

Two review leads of PB4130's branch, one change, one site.

**R1-1 (behaviour, low).** In `VisitCobolWord` the 2002 extended-letter gate is
`if (character is null || _p._edition.Year >= 2002) _p.Check(Constructs.UserWordExtendedLetter2002, ...)`, where `character` is
`CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation(raw, year)`: the first character in NO edition's Annex B. Below 2002 the gate is therefore
skipped for the whole word as soon as it holds any such character, even when the same word also holds a real extended letter
(a character the 2002 Annex B admits and COBOL-85 does not). That letter goes unreported at COBOL-85; the word is still refused,
with COBOL2773 for the other character alone. The decision should be per character: the gate asks whether the word holds an extended
letter, not whether it holds a non-Annex-B character. Probe: a word with one extended letter and one euro sign (or U+00A0) at
`--std 85` and `--std 2002`; the implementer decides from the spec's COBOL-word and Annex B rules which diagnostics the word draws
at 85 (validate each clause with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).

**R1-2 (test gap, low).** Nothing pins the `VersionConformancePass` half of PB4130's fix (COBOL0900 no longer fires at 85 for a
non-Annex-B character): the refusals in `conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/` assert only that COBOL2773 is present
(`EditionHarness.AssertHasDiagnostic`), and the unit test calls `CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation`, not the pass. A regression that
brings the misleading COBOL0900 back passes every gate. Add a case that asserts COBOL0900 is ABSENT for that word at 85 and present
at 2002 where the character is a real extended letter.

Not mine: PB3845 (a U+00A0-only line breaks the parse before the word check) remains open and separate.

Source: report `w20261773c-PB4130-report.md`, review findings R1-1 and R1-2; PB4130 (landed).

## Landing (wave 20261783, group D)

R1-1 fixed at its root. The re-probe on the landing tree reproduced it: a `CAFÉ€` word at `--std 85` drew only COBOL2773 (for the euro
sign), and the gate was already in `WordCharacterScreen.Check`, not `VersionConformancePass.VisitCobolWord` (PB3845 moved it). The gate
now asks `CobolCharacterRepertoire.HasExtendedLetter(word)`: any character some edition's Annex B holds, decided per character.
`IsInSomeAnnexB` is the one place that distinction is drawn (`ViolationAtEveryEdition` asks it too). From 2002 the construct is
introduced, so asking it was and remains a no-op. At 85 the word now draws COBOL0900 for the É and COBOL2773 for the euro sign.

R1-2 was already pinned by PB3845's landing (`WordCharacterScreenTests.ACharacterInNoAnnexB_IsTheWordCharacterError_NotTheExtendedLetterGate_At85`
asserts COBOL0900 absent for a euro or U+00A0 word at 85, parsed and unparsed). The note's "present at 2002" cannot hold: the gate is a
2002 introduction and silent from 2002. The new test `AnExtendedLetterBesideACharacterInNoAnnexB_IsGatedAt85AndRefusedAtEveryTarget`
pins COBOL0900 present at 85 and absent from 2002 for a word with a real extended letter, and the golden
`pb4156-extended-letter-beside-euro-sign` pins it at 85.
