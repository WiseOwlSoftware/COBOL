# PB5683 - the 1985 adjudications (PB5679 step, D4)

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/audit_batch.py` (`import_reads`), `scripts/spec/lineage_reads.py` (`import_records`, `load`,
`kept_problems`): the import of 1985 reads, whose pairing check, for an edition with no word-identity candidates file
(1985 pairs 0 of 2,274 rules), takes the stored record's own targets. The rest is reads, code reads and
`record_verdicts.py --edition 1985` batches.

**What is stored** (2026-11-15). 2,260 1985 records, all `next` into 2023 over a bridge: KEPT 1,373, CHANGED 564, REMOVED
323 (881 rows carry a verdict of their own); 14 of the 2,274 1985 rules have no record.

**Work, in today's format, nothing withdrawn (D5).**
- Each 1985 KEPT rule is read in 1985's own terms and adjudicated on the code at `--std 85` by the code-read pipeline
  (reader and refuter), sized by PB5680's measured rate and by PB5692's answer.
- A rule the read finds to state a different requirement is re-recorded CHANGED with its own verdict through the
  existing path; the landing states every gate proof that moves and why (PB5679 Q4).
- A rule the read confirms keeps its KEPT record until the switch; its adjudication is kept as a tracked record of the
  read, and how it becomes the row's `adjudicated` verdict is the switch's design (PB5682).
- The 14 rules with no record are classified.

**Done when.** Every 1985 rule has an adjudication in 1985's own terms (recorded, or tracked for the switch); each
landing states every held edition's GAP before and after and every moved gate proof.

**Rows.** None claimed here; each landing's batch names its rows.
