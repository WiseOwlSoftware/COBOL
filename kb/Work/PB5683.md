# PB5683 - the 1985 own verdicts and the 1985 to 2002 pair (PB5679 step 1b)

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/lineage.py` (`next_errors`, `chain_bands`, `band_drift`, `spanned`),
`scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py` (`ChangeKind`, `ChangeRecord`, `change_record_of`), `scripts/spec/edition_gates.py` (`pair_of`, `newer_change`,
`older_change`), `tests/_shared/TraceabilityInventory.cs` (`TraceabilityInventory.ChangeRecord`,
`TraceabilityInventory.NextOf`): the readers of the legacy `next` record, deleted in the landing that drains the last
one; the rest of the work is read records and `record_verdicts.py` batches.

**What is stored.** 2,260 1985 records, all `next` into 2023 over a bridge: KEPT 1,373, CHANGED 564, REMOVED 323 (881
of them carry a verdict of their own). The 1985 candidate pairing against 2023 pairs 0 of 2,274 rules.

**Work.** One read per 1985 rule, in 1985's own terms, sized by PB5680's measured rate:
- its own verdict and evidence at `--std 85` (L6), the 2023 records serving only as pointers to code and cases;
- its relation to 2002: the read decides, for each 2002 candidate, as-is, altered (with `change`) or no counterpart.
  Candidates come first from the word-identical pairing of 1985 with 2002, then from composition (the 1985 KEPT records
  and the 2002 pairing meet on 1,100 pairs: 864 1985 rules, 648 2002 rules), then from routing and wording;
- the 2002 rows' `from` records (pair 1985:2002) written through `record_verdicts.py --edition 2002`, removals backed by
  `spec-change-list-2002.json` (Annex F).

**Done when.** Every 1985 row has its own verdict or is open with a work item; the pair 1985:2002 is complete; the
EDITION GATES audit proves its 2002 edges from that pair; the `next` field, `ChangeRecord`, `chain_bands`' bridge and
every reader of the 1985 legacy records are deleted in the landing that drains the last one; the view's legacy count is 0.
Each landing states every held edition's GAP before and after.

**Rows.** None claimed here; each landing's batch names its rows.
