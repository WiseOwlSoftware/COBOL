# PB5683 - the 1985 reads and the pair reads, before the switch (PB5679 step 2)

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/audit_batch.py` (`import_reads`, `change_batch`), `scripts/spec/lineage_reads.py` (`reads_dir`,
`import_records`, `load`, `normal_read`, `kept_problems`, `main`), `scripts/spec/lineage.py` (`candidates`,
`candidates_document`): the pair on the read-record path (the read records named by both years,
`lineage-reads-2002/` renamed `lineage-reads-2002-2023/` with every caller), the `examined` field, the pairing check from
the stored record's own targets for an edition with no word-identity candidates (1985), and `lineage_reads.py coverage
--switch`. The rest is read records and `record_verdicts.py` batches.

**What is stored** (2026-11-15). 2,260 1985 records, all `next` into 2023 over a bridge: KEPT 1,373, CHANGED 564, REMOVED
323; 14 of the 2,274 1985 rules have no record. The 1985 candidate pairing against 2023 pairs 0 rules.

**In today's format, nothing withdrawn** (section 10.7.6):
- each 1985 KEPT rule is read in 1985's own terms (sized by PB5680's measured rate) and its record is CONFIRMED in place
  (a tracked read record and the row's own `test-ref`; it stays KEPT) or re-recorded through `record_verdicts.py
  --edition 1985` (CHANGED with its own verdict, for one the read finds different), each landing showing
  `edition_gates.py --list` unchanged at base and head;
- the 14 unclassified 1985 rules are classified, so the NEW set the switch records is derived over a complete 1985 side;
- the reads the switch can use to place a relation at 1985 to 2002 are kept as tracked read records of that pair
  (evidence only, read by no state function, gate or writer before the switch).

**Done when.** `lineage_reads.py coverage --switch` prints 0 stored KEPT records without a read in their own edition's
terms and 0 unclassified 1985 rules; each landing states every held edition's GAP before and after.
