# PB3305 — a dated cap override for the owner's special cases

## The owner's decision (operator session c95c0b86, 2026-10-16 ~15:25 PDT, verbatim)
"We can run to 100% today, without triggering aborts, as the reset is 10 AM tomorrow and we won't need to work tonight"

Account 2 read 92 % weekly at 15:23 on day 7 of its week, which resets Saturday 2026-10-17 at 10:00. Day 7's
allowance is `min(weekly_cap_pct 97, 7 × 14.3)` = 97, so the loop would have stopped near 97 % at about 20:00 and
left about 3 points unused.

## Why a new mechanism
The 2026-10-14 pacing decision ([[owner-decision-pacing-2026-10-14]]) allows overages per case, and the loop has two
per-run levers for that: `-OverrideHold` lifts a hold-session or hold-day, and `-BorrowDays N` moves the allowance.
On day 7 neither helps. The cap is the binding limit there, and `budget.py` returns `stop-week` at the cap, which ends
the loop under both levers. Adding a per-run `-WeeklyCapPct` argument would not reach every reader either: the wave
planner (`plan_wave.py --from-budget`) and a unit's own `budget.py` call compute the headroom themselves, from the
quota table.

## The mechanism
- `model_rules.json` `accounts.list[].quota_overrides`: one entry per owner special case, with `until` (and optionally
  `from`; ISO times with a zone), the quota keys it sets, and `_source` (the owner's words).
- `Account.quota_at(now)` (`scripts/orchestrator/account.py`) is the one resolver. It applies each override whose
  window holds `now`, in table order. `_overrides` refuses an entry with no `_source`, no quota key, an unknown key or
  a time without a zone. An ignored override would leave the loop on the cap the owner lifted, and one without its
  source would be an overage nobody approved.
- Every quota reader asks `quota_at`: `budget.estimate` (the supervisor's check, the planner's headroom and the units'
  calls), `next_unit.py` (the meter's maximum age) and `ledger_plan.py` (the ledger's pacing row). An override
  therefore takes effect at each reader's next call, with no loop restart.
- An override past its `until` is inert, so nobody has to remember to take it back. An expired entry is litter; the
  next change that touches the table deletes it.
- The first entry: account 2, `weekly_cap_pct` 99 until 2026-10-17T10:00-07:00. It is 99, not 100, because the cap
  ends the loop only between units. The last point covers the running unit's spend and the estimate's drift between
  meter readings: the 15:23 reading was 2.6 points above the estimate. The operator re-reads the meter through Chrome
  at each unit's end while the account is within 3 points of the cap.
