# PB79 — OPEN — `01 NG GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL.` / `01 BG GROUP-USAGE BIT.` do not parse

> ⚙ **flags:** rejects-legal-source (loud, at parse) · **7 inventory rows** (`FMT-13.18.29.2`, `SR-13.18.29.3-1..3`,
> `GR-13.18.29.4-1..3`, all GAP) **plus the group legs of two PB61 rows** (`RV-15.50.4-1` "a bit group item …
> in boolean positions", `RV-15.50.4-2` "a national group item … in national character positions" — recorded
> PARTIAL on PB61's landing, 2026-08-17, naming this note). Registered while landing PB61: the LENGTH fold's r1/r2
> group legs are unreachable because the clause that CREATES a bit/national group cannot be written.

**Measured repro** (`--std 2023`, `scratchpad/sr6/gu.cob`):
```
       01 NG GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL.        ->  error COBOL0307: unexpected 'NATIONAL'
       01 BG GROUP-USAGE BIT.             ->  error COBOL0307: unexpected 'BIT'
```
`GROUP-USAGE` is a §8.9 reserved word (it lexes), but no data-description clause consumes it, so the parser dies
on the phrase word.

**Spec:** §13.18.29.2 general format `GROUP-USAGE IS { BIT | NATIONAL }` (figure notes: BIT/NATIONAL/GROUP-USAGE
underlined, IS optional). SR1: only on a group item that is not strongly typed and not a variable-length group.
SR2 (BIT): USAGE BIT implied for the subject; every subordinate elementary item usage bit / category boolean;
every subordinate group GROUP-USAGE BIT (explicit or implied). SR3 (NATIONAL): USAGE NATIONAL implied; every
subordinate elementary item usage national; signed numerics SIGN IS SEPARATE; subordinate groups GROUP-USAGE
NATIONAL. GR1 (BIT): the subject is a bit group AND a bit data item, class/category boolean, treated as
`PICTURE 1(m)` (m = the bit length), subordinates allocated per §8.5.1.6.3. GR2 (NATIONAL): the subject is a
national group, class/category national, treated as `PICTURE N(m)` — the NOTE gives the purpose: truncation,
padding and INSPECT in national characters rather than as an alphanumeric group. GR3: without the clause a group
(not strongly typed, not variable-length) is an alphanumeric group item — which is what every group is today, and
why PB43 (USAGE BIT) and PB15 (result types) could land without it.

**Blast radius of the clause once modelled** — it is a CATEGORY change for the group, not a lexical one: MOVE
(§14.9.24 — a national group is category national, so Table 16's national row governs; padding/truncation in
national characters), comparisons (§8.8.4.1.2 national vs alphanumeric group), INSPECT/STRING/UNSTRING sending
and receiving, `FUNCTION LENGTH` r1/r2 (the PB61 legs), `BYTE-LENGTH` (unchanged — bytes are bytes), the
class/category classifiers (`OperandCategory`, `IsStringOperand`), the layout (a bit group packs per §8.5.1.6.3 —
PB43's split-run layout already handles subordinate USAGE BIT items; the group's own image becomes the packed
bit image), and the §13.18.49.3 SR9 SIGN-clause guard in `DataBinder` that today says "GROUP-USAGE is not
modeled". §8.5.2.10 (national group) and §8.5.1.6.3 (bit alignment) are the concept sections.

**Fix shape:** grammar `groupUsageClause : GROUP_USAGE IS? (BIT | NATIONAL)` on the data-description entry
(introducedIn 2002 — GROUP-USAGE is a COBOL 2002 word; VCR row + `constructs.json` + the edition-gate sweep,
`new-construct` skill); `DataItem` gains a `GroupUsage` (None/Bit/National) that the classifier maps to
category boolean/national for the GROUP; SR1–SR3 as bind-time diagnostics (next-free code from session-probe);
`LengthPositions` (IntrinsicBinder) grows the r1/r2 group arms — it already carries the comment naming this note;
goldens for the MOVE/compare/INSPECT/LENGTH behaviours from the GR2 NOTE. Survey GnuCOBOL (it accepts
GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL; check its bit support) before choosing the implementor-defined corners.
