# PB5622 landing

**Re-probe (Debug build, `--std 2002`, `>>TURN EC-ARGUMENT-FUNCTION CHECKING ON`, CNT = 0):** before, `LEN=000000000` then
`AFTER R1=      EXC=` and rc 0; after, `abnormal run-unit termination: EC-ARGUMENT-FUNCTION (fatal): UPPER-CASE argument-1 is of
zero length (§2023:15.97.3 AR1 ...)` and rc 1.

**Root cause.** The static screen (`MinWidth(1)`) only sees a width known at bind time; `CobolIntrinsics.UpperCase`, `LowerCase`
and `Reverse`, and the `CobolLocale` carriers that the LOCALE phrase and CHARACTER CLASSIFICATION route to, had no empty-argument
arm (`Ord` and `Convert` and `Substitute` do).

**Fix.** One guard, `CobolIntrinsics.CaseArgumentZeroLength`, raised through `ExceptionState.ArgumentErrorZeroLength` (row
DOC-A.1-90's zero-length class: the result's length is the argument's), called by all five entries. `CobolLocale.LowerCase`
was also the ANYCASE fold of FIND-STRING, SUBSTITUTE and NUMVAL-C, whose arguments may legally be zero length, so the fold is split
off as `CobolLocale.FoldLower` and those callers read it.

**Sibling sweep.** Spec sentences asking a minimum length (`awk` over §2023 clause 15): 15.19.3 CONVERT (guard exists),
15.57.3 / 15.78.3 / 15.97.3 (this fix), 15.70.3 ORD (guard exists), 15.87.4 SUBSTITUTE (guard exists); 15.37.4 r5 FIND-STRING
returns zero (no exception); DISPLAY-OF and NATIONAL-OF state no minimum length.

**Goldens.** `cases/15.65@2002-REVERSE-FUNCTION/pb5622-reverse-zero-length-2002`,
`cases/15.78@2002-UPPER-CASE-FUNCTION/pb5622-upper-lower-zero-length-2002`, `cases/15.78.3-Argument-rule/pb5622-case-reverse-zero-length`
(2014, 2023). No negative below 2002: the refusal at 85 is of the `>>TURN` directive, not of this rule.
