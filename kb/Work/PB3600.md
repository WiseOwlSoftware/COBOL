# PB3600 — the CONSTANT RECORD edge is 2014, not 2002

**Sites:** `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (row `constant-record-2014`, formerly `constant-record-2002`), `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` (row 7.48 and the status index), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs:1339` (`VisitConstantRecordClause`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ConstructRegistry.g.cs` and `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Constructs.g.cs` (regenerated: `Constructs.ConstantRecord2014`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4:378,457` (comments), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/SameAsTests.cs:167` (`SameAsOfConstantRecord_Rejected1556`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/ValueClauseScreenTests.cs:39` (`WithConstantRecord`)

## Scenario

`tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` row `constant-record-2002` has `"introducedIn": 2002`, and its `vcr` text says
"provisional edge per decision 1". The archived 2002 committee texts do not contain the clause: P1 (ISO/IEC FCD
1989:2001, WG4 N0147) and P2 (the post-ballot text dated 2001-12-04) have zero hits for "CONSTANT RECORD" after
whitespace normalization. The 2009 committee draft P3 (CD 1.2, 2009-08-23, §2023:13.18.15) has it, and the 2023 Annex E.2
item 10 says the 2014 edition already had the clause: `cite.py --check E.2 "CONSTANT RECORD"` returns OK on item 10
("External items. The CONSTANT RECORD clause may now only be specified for external items that are strongly typed,
where previously external items could not be strongly typed"). The edge is therefore 2014.

Harm: at `--std 2002` the program below compiles clean where the 2002 edition has no such clause and the compiler
must refuse it with COBOL0900, as it does for the other 2014 edges.

### Repro

The row's own source (program id `KCR`), compiled at `--std 2002`:

```cobol
IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
PROGRAM-ID. KCR.
DATA DIVISION.
WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
01 CFG CONSTANT RECORD.
   05 CFG-TAG PIC X(4) VALUE "COBL".
PROCEDURE DIVISION.
MAIN.
    DISPLAY CFG-TAG.
    STOP RUN.
```

Expected: COBOL0900 at `--std 2002`; clean at 2014 and 2023.

## Fix (for the fix lane)

1. The row's `introducedIn` becomes 2014 and its `vcr` text cites P2 (zero hits), P3 (§2023:13.18.15) and Annex E.2 item 10
   instead of "provisional edge per decision 1". Sources and URLs: [[PB296]] "Closed by the research".
2. The edition-gate sweep (`new-construct` skill): re-run the corpus at 85 / 2002 / 2014 / 2023; any golden or
   conformance program that uses CONSTANT RECORD below 2014 moves to the right edition.
3. A 2002 refusal golden for the repro, and the VCR row.
4. Siblings: the SR rules tied to the clause (§2023:13.18.15 SR1, §2023:13.16.3 SR3/SR6/SR13, the COBOL1548 / 1549 arms)
   inherit the edge. [[PB3602]] re-derives the other provisional edges.

## Landing

The row is `constant-record-2014` with `introducedIn` 2014 (the id carried the edge, so it was renamed and every caller
changed: the generated registry, `VersionConformancePass.VisitConstantRecordClause`, the inventory and the docs). The
repro compiled clean at `--std 2002` on the pre-fix tree and now draws `COBOL0900: the CONSTANT RECORD clause requires
COBOL-2014 (targeting COBOL-2002)`. The edition-gate sweep moved the nine accepted goldens that use the clause from
`accept: [2002]` to `[2014, 2023]` (each re-run at both and equal to its `.out`), dropped `2002` from the `refuse-at` of
the 27 existing negatives that the clause's own rules refuse (below 2014 they are COBOL0900, not their SR code), and moved
the `_unassigned` negative to `13.18.15-CONSTANT-RECORD/constant-record-below-2014` (refused at 85 and 2002). Twelve
inventory rows that are rules of the clause carry `editions` 2014,2023.
