# PB6177 — one trailing floating extended string is accepted and loses the sign

**Mechanism.** PB6116 added the SR27 count to `PictureComposition.Validate`: two floating extended strings in one PICTURE are
refused COBOL1934. A PICTURE with a single floating extended string placed after the digits (`PIC 99LL`) still binds, and the
sign the editing character is meant to carry is dropped. Reviewer R1-2 (low) says the picture is not refused and the sign is lost;
the implementer left it for the clerk. Reachability and the exact legality (is a trailing-only floating extended string legal under
the §2023:13.18.40.3 rules at all?) are unprobed: the first step is the probe, then a verdict from the spec.

**Recorded rationale to reconcile (R2-1).** `DEVLOG/pending-pb6116-second-extended-floating-string.md` (line 20) keeps `PIC LLZZ9`
legal because "SR27's list names only +, -, currency, * and Z strings; a floating extended string is not on it", yet the same
entry and the COBOL1934 message refuse `LL99FF` precisely because GR6 puts a floating extended string on that list. The behaviour
is right (SR12 b admits Z and the currency symbol beside an extended character-1; the adjudicated golden
`pb4755_deedit_for_floating_blank_landing_before_z`, PIC LLLZZ9, accepts it), but the two statements of the basis contradict each
other. The consistent reading, from the reviewer: SR12 b and SR26 admit Z / currency symbol beside character-1, and SR27 via GR6
limits extended strings to one. Settle the reading here and state it once.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureComposition.cs:441` (`PictureComposition.Validate`)

**Landed (PB6177, wave 20262171 A).** Re-probe: `PIC 99LL EDITING L FOR NEGATIVE IS "-"` and `PIC 99LL9` bound clean, while `PIC 99++` and
`PIC 99$$` are refused COBOL1935 (Table 10's blank cell: a digit position may not precede a floating insertion symbol). Verdict: a floating
extended string is a floating insertion string (§2023:13.18.40.5 GR6 lists the extended editing sign control symbols among the floating
insertion symbols) that stands for "the leading numeric character positions", so the Table-10 floating rows' question about what
PRECEDES it applies. Fix: `PictureComposition.Precedence` gives a floating extended string the floating currency role (left or right of
the decimal point position), asks it of the walk with the union of the floating-currency and floating-sign rows, and does not add it to the
`seen` set, so what FOLLOWS it stays unasked (Annex D.24 `LLLL9.99F`, the adjudicated `PIC LLLZZ9`). `99LL`, `99LL9`, `ZZLL9`, `XXLL`
are COBOL1935; `LL99`, `$LL99`, `-LL99`, `LLLZZ9`, `LLLL9.99F` stay legal. Goldens `pb6177_picture_floating_extended_after_insertion_2023`
and negative `pb6177-picture-floating-extended-after-digit`; unit theory `PictureCompositionTests.AFloatingExtendedString_FollowingADigitPositionIsRefusedByTable10`.

**R2-1 settled (one statement of the basis).** SR27 (§2023:13.18.40.3) lists only '+', '-', currency, '*' and 'Z' strings, so a floating
extended string is not one of ITS list and `PIC LLZZ9` / `LLLZZ9` are not two SR27 strings: SR12 b admits Z and the currency symbol
beside an extended character-1 (the adjudicated golden `pb4755_deedit_for_floating_blank_landing_before_z`). The refusal of `LL99FF` /
`LL9LL` is the other statement: GR6 makes a floating extended string a floating insertion string, and a character-string holds one such
string (SR27's own principle, NOTE 5), which `PictureComposition.Validate` counts beside the Table-10 walk. DEVLOG entry 4041's line is the
history; this paragraph is the current reading.
