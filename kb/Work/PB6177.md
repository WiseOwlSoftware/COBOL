# PB6177 — one trailing floating extended string is accepted and loses the sign

**Mechanism.** PB6116 added the SR27 count to `PictureComposition.Validate`: two floating extended strings in one PICTURE are
refused COBOL1934. A PICTURE with a single floating extended string placed after the digits (`PIC 99LL`) still binds, and the
sign the editing character is meant to carry is dropped. Reviewer R1-2 (low) says the picture is not refused and the sign is lost;
the implementer left it for the clerk. Reachability and the exact legality (is a trailing-only floating extended string legal under
the §2023:13.18.40.3 rules at all?) are unprobed: the first step is the probe, then a verdict from the spec.

**Recorded rationale to reconcile (R2-1).** `DEVLOG/pending-pb6116-second-extended-floating-string.md` (line 20) keeps `PIC LLZZ9`
legal because "SR27's list names only +, -, currency, * and Z strings; a floating extended string is not on it", yet the same
entry and the COBOL1934 message refuse `LL99FF` precisely because GR6 puts a floating extended string on that list. The behaviour
is right (SR12 b admits Z and the currency symbol beside an extended character-1; the adjudicated golden
`pb4755_deedit_for_floating_blank_landing_before_z`, PIC LLLZZ9, accepts it), but the two statements of the basis contradict each
other. The consistent reading, from the reviewer: SR12 b and SR26 admit Z / currency symbol beside character-1, and SR27 via GR6
limits extended strings to one. Settle the reading here and state it once.
