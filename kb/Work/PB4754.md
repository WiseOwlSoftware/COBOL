# PB4754 - BinaryMagnitude always forms CobolBig

**What the report says (R2-2, low).** R1-3's landing added the shape R1-4 removed: `BinaryMagnitude` always forms the value on
`CobolBig` (`AsBig`), and `FloatResultant.LandExactValue` writes it out through `BigInteger.ToString` and `double.Parse`, though
the value is usually on the 256-bit or 128-bit form. The class header, DESIGN section 1.2 and the owner decision on PB4592 say
the third form is reached only where the 256-bit form fails. No wrong answer is named; the harm is the always-on third form.

**R1.** No open note covers it. PB2639 (landed) mentions a different `BinaryMagnitude` (the unsigned BinaryCapacity arm).

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolExact.cs#BinaryMagnitude`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/FloatResultant.cs#LandExactValue`.
