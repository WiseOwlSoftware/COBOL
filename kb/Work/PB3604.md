# PB3604 — the `<>` operator edge is 2014, not 2002

**Sites:** `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (row `not-equal-operator-2014`, renamed from `not-equal-operator-2002`), `src/Cobol.Net.Editions/Constructs.g.cs`, `src/Cobol.Net.Editions/ConstructRegistry.g.cs`, `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` (row 7.26), `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitComparisonOperator`), `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Expressions/RelationalOperatorGate.cs` (`RelationalOperatorGate.Check`), `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ConditionalCompilationProcessor.cs` (`ConditionalCompilationProcessor.EvaluateCceText`, `DirectiveDiag.GateRelationalOperators`)

## Scenario

Row `not-equal-operator-2002` carries edge 2002. The `<>` relational operator is a 2014 addition. P3 Annex D.3 item 16:
"Relational operator enhancement. The symbol <> has been added as an extended relational operator. It is an
abbreviation for 'not equal'." P1 and P2 have no `<>`: P2 clause 8.7.5.2's abbreviation rules end at `<=`, and its
special-character word list is `>= <= *> >> ::`.

2023: `cite.py --check 8.7.5.2 "<> is an abbreviation for NOT EQUAL"` reports OK, but on syntax rule 8 ("NOT = is an
abbreviation for NOT EQUAL"), not on the rule the quotation belongs to ([[PB3606]]). `cite.py --find` places the text
in §8.7.5.2 syntax rule 11 ("<> is an abbreviation for NOT EQUAL."), and the rule number is confirmed by the `--find`
output, not by `--check`.

Harm: `IF A <> B` compiles clean at `--std 2002`; the 2002 edition has no such operator and the compiler must refuse it
with COBOLNET0900, as it does for the other 2014 edges.

## Fix (for the fix lane)

1. The row's edge becomes 2014 and its `vcr` text cites P2 (no `<>`), P3 Annex D.3 item 16 and 2023 §8.7.5.2 syntax
   rule 11 in place of "provisional".
2. The edition-gate sweep (`new-construct` skill): re-run the corpus at 85 / 2002 / 2014 / 2023; any golden or
   conformance program using `<>` below 2014 moves to the right edition.
3. A 2002 refusal golden for `IF A <> B`, and the VCR row.

## Landed

Re-probed on the worktree build (main 204718793): `IF A <> 3 DISPLAY "NE".` compiled clean at 2002, 2014 and 2023 and was
COBOLNET0900 at 85 only. The 2014 edge is confirmed against the archived texts (`research/pb296-dl`): the post-ballot 2002
text has no `<>` (its clause 8.7.5.2 ends the abbreviation rules at SR8 `<=`, and its special character words read
`>= <= *> >> ::`), and Annex D.3 (2002->2014 changes) item 16 of the 2009 committee draft reads "Relational operator
enhancement. The symbol <> has been added as an extended relational operator. It is an abbreviation for 'not equal'."
`cite.py --find` places "<> is an abbreviation for NOT EQUAL." at 8.7.5.2 SR11; `--check` reports SR8 for the same words
(PB3606), so SR11 is read from `--find`.

The row is `not-equal-operator-2014` (id renamed in every caller: the generated registry, the parse arm, the VCR row 7.26 and its
generated index, the goldens, the inventory). SIBLING ARM FOUND AND FIXED: the symbol is also written in a `>>IF` / `>>EVALUATE`
constant-conditional-expression (ISO 7.3.8.2 SR1 a), "formed according to the rules in 8.8.4.2"), a fragment the
conditional-compilation stage parses apart and the compilation-unit walk never reaches, and that lane had no gate at all:
`>>IF 5 <> 3` compiled at 2002 and (once the row moved) would still have. The gate is now one body, `RelationalOperatorGate`
(the `LogicalOperatorGate` precedent), asked per `comparisonOperator` node by the parse arm and per evaluated fragment by
`DirectiveDiag.GateRelationalOperators`. `RelationalOperatorGateDriftTests` pins that the row is read only by the body, that both
lanes call it, and that the NOTEQUAL token is spelled only by `comparisonOperator`.

Goldens: positives moved to 2014 (`accept: [2014]`): `l1c26_relational_operator_not_equal_symbol`,
`pb1034_is_not_equal_symbol`, `pb1034_cce_printed_relational_operators`, `pb1021_address_identifier_operands`,
`pb1391_boolean_relations_stated_in_full`; negatives `pb1459-not-equal-operator-below-2014` (renamed from `...-below-2002`, now
refused at 85 and 2002) and `pb3604-cce-not-equal-operator-below-2014` (2002, the directive lane). The word forms `NOT EQUAL TO` and
`NOT =` stay legal at 85 (probed).

**For the next implementer.** `RelationalOperatorGate.cs#Check` is the one body; a new place that spells a relation calls it. Trap:
a new `constructs.json` row needs `scripts/gen-constructs.ps1` and `scripts/gen-vcr.ps1` after the last edit.
