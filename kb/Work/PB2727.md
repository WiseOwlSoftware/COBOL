## Landed (w1055a, 2026-10-10) — the period half

The three paragraph rules keep a punctuation superset, `(entry DOT?)*`, now written on the paragraph rule itself
(SPECIAL-NAMES' periods moved up out of `specialNameEntry`), so the periods are the paragraph's own children.
`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Cst/ParagraphPeriods.cs` is the one table of such paragraphs (subject, clause, what the format
prints, and the one clause printed as a sentence of its own: §12.4.6.2's APPLY COMMIT) and splits a paragraph into its
sentences; `ClosedFormatPass.ScreenParagraphPeriods` refuses a period that splits a sentence (**COBOL3407**, at the
period) and a paragraph that ends without its period (**COBOL3408**, at the last entry), Error at every edition.
`ParagraphPeriodDriftTests` holds every grammar rule that writes `DOT?` to a row, in both directions.
Measured on the built compiler: `I-O-CONTROL. SAME AREA FOR F1 F2. SAME RECORD AREA FOR F3 F4.` → COBOL3407 at the
first period; `SPECIAL-NAMES. CLASS HEXD IS "0" THRU "9". DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA.` → COBOL3407; `REPOSITORY. FUNCTION
PI INTRINSIC FUNCTION E INTRINSIC` before `DATA DIVISION.` → COBOL3408; the legal 2023 two-sentence form
`I-O-CONTROL. APPLY COMMIT ON F1. SAME AREA FOR F1 F2 SAME RECORD AREA FOR F3 F4.` draws no period diagnostic (only the
declined clause's COBOL1709). The repository's own corpus carried the per-entry period in 52 sources (72 periods,
found by compiling every source that names one of the three paragraphs and reading the new diagnostic); each period
was deleted mechanically at the diagnostic's position. Golden `2002/pb2727_paragraph_single_period` (expected from
§12.3.7.4, §12.3.8.4 and the I-O-CONTROL format), negatives `pb2727-{io-control,special-names}-period-{per-clause,
missing}` (85 2002 2014 2023) and `pb2727-repository-period-{per-specifier,missing}` (2002 2014 2023). Witness batch
`w1055a-pb2727-paragraph-periods` (FMT-12.4.6.2, FMT-12.3.7.2, FMT-12.3.8.2; GAP 103 → 103). Inline C# sources: 14
REPOSITORY periods in OoSpineTests / OoPortedTests and one ClassClauseForPhraseTests case. The external GnuCOBOL corpus
has three affected cases (run_misc:1759, syn_definition:1849, syn_definition:1872), all already rejected in the
baseline, so no differential flip is predicted.
