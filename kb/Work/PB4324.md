# PB4324 — step 1 of PB4323: bound and cache CI

**Sites:** `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml` (whole: every job gains `timeout-minutes`, every checkout gains a 5 min step timeout and one retry, `setup-toolchain` steps a 5 min timeout, the NuGet and GnuCOBOL-tarball caches become restore-only, the new `package-cache` job and `toolchain-cache` save them under main, `supersede` reads either checkout), `.github/workflows/toolchain-freshness.yml` (whole: the same bounds), `.github/actions/setup-toolchain/action.yml` (the manifest step, the cache restore and save: Python's tool-cache directory joins the entry, the save requires the main ref), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CiBoundsDriftTests.cs` (new: `CiBoundsDriftTests`, the every-job-states-a-timeout arm and its siblings), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/ToolchainManifestDriftTests.cs` (`AuditJobs`, the toolchain step after the checkout and its retry), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/SparseCheckoutAudit.cs` (`SparseJobs`, one list per job), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/SparseCheckoutDriftTests.cs`, `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/LandingPipelineDriftTests.cs` (`TheMergeGroupRun_CancelsTheRunsTheQueueSuperseded_AndNothingElse`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` (1.4), `docs/DOC_INDEX.md`.

**Change.** `timeout-minutes` on every job, about 2 times the measured p90: Linux shards 15, Windows shards 20, `changes`/`ci-gate`/`audits` 10, `windows-self-tests` 20, nightly 60. Step-level 5 min on `actions/checkout` and the toolchain step, with one retry of the checkout (`if: steps.checkout.outcome != 'success'`, the shape `supersede` uses). Python into the toolchain cache. Caches saved from main only (the push run's `toolchain-cache`, restore-or-save) so a merge group can restore them; queue branches stop saving NuGet copies (10.77 GB held against the 10 GB cap). The drift-test arm goes red the day a job lacks a timeout.

**Adds / deletes.** About +40 / 0.

**Effect.** No hang past 20 min; about 49 s less on each of 8 Windows jobs; the SDK-outage class stays closed.

**Closes.** PB4227 (which names the drift-test arm).

**Order.** Any order with steps 2 and 9; before step 7 (the cache scope).

## Landed

Re-probed on this build: `grep -c timeout-minutes .github/workflows/*.yml` printed 0 for both workflows; the repro of PB4227
was the same. Fixed at the root, one reader and one drift test (`CiBoundsDriftTests`):

- every job of both workflows states `timeout-minutes`, sized from the p90 of 18 to 70 successful runs per job (2026-10-31
  merge-group data, E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-pl1/M2/data). It is about twice p90 and NOT the review's 15 (Linux shard) and 20
  (Windows shard): the Guard job's NIST loop alone is 14.1 minutes p50 and the Linux unit job 12.2 p90, so 15 would fail
  them every run; `guard` 30, `windows-build-test` 30, `greenfield-unit` 25, `windows-conformance` 25, `windows-self-tests` 25,
  `greenfield-conformance` 15, `toolchain-cache` and `package-cache` 20, the small jobs 10. Step 6 (PB4329) re-shards and
  tightens them together.
- every `actions/checkout` and `setup-toolchain` step is killed after 5 minutes; every job's first step is a checkout with
  `id: checkout` + `continue-on-error: true` and its second the same checkout (the same `with:` block, compared by the
  drift test) run `if: steps.checkout.outcome != 'success'`. `supersede`'s retry is `checkout-retry` and `continue-on-error`.
- Python joins the toolchain cache: `$RUNNER_TOOL_CACHE/Python/<version>` is in the cache entry and restored BEFORE
  `actions/setup-python`, which then downloads nothing.
- caches are saved from main only: the matrix jobs' NuGet and tarball steps are `actions/cache/restore`, the
  `setup-toolchain` save requires `github.ref == 'refs/heads/main'`, and the new `package-cache` job (push to main, both
  OSes) saves NuGet and the tarball. A separate job, because `toolchain-cache` is a sparse-checkout job and NuGet's key
  hashes every csproj.

Not verifiable off CI: the Windows hosted-tool-cache restore (setup-python finding the restored directory) and the
`package-cache` job run for the first time on the first push to main after landing; the first merge group after it reads
their effect. `CiBoundsDriftTests` holds the shapes, not the runner behaviour.
