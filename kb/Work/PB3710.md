# PB3710 — a re-enqueue's landing record is refused by its own validator

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/landing_measure.py` (`record_enqueued`, `patch_record`, `stage_times`, `validate`; the stage-time code is on branch `worktree-wf_12e98f2a-957-1`, PB3446, and lands with it).

## What happens

`record_enqueued` bounds the stage times by this call's enqueue time, but `patch_record` keeps the record's first `enqueued` and overlays
`review_clean`. Scenario: PR #90 enqueued 09:00 (record: `review_clean` 08:40, `enqueued` 09:00); it bounces, a review finisher fixes it,
round 2 stamps CLEAN at 10:00 and `enqueue.py land` re-runs at 10:10 reusing the PR. `stage_times(..., enqueued=10:10)` accepts clean 10:00;
`patch_record` keeps `enqueued` 09:00 and sets `review_clean` 10:00; `validate` raises "enqueued 09:00 is before review_clean 10:00". `queue()`
prints the warning "landing record was NOT written" and the update is lost (the reviewer probed `patch_record` directly: REFUSED). It
contradicts `stage_times`' docstring, which promises it never yields a record `validate` refuses.

## Fix

Bound `stage_times` by the record's existing first `enqueued` (read the record before computing the stages), or have `patch_record` treat
`review_clean` as it treats `enqueued` (first wins). Add a self-test arm with the bounce, re-review and re-enqueue sequence above.
