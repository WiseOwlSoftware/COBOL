# PB4214 — the data-entry tail discard cannot tell the next named entry from the entry's own tail

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs#UnitTailDiscard` (`Resumes`). Until PB4133's branch lands the site exists only on branch `worktree-wf_34371f8b-c21-5`.

## What happens

When a data entry is faulted and loses its period, the tail discard consumes tokens until `UnitTailDiscard.Resumes` says the parser can resume. `Resumes` decides on an ATN pair test: whether the unit can hold the (previous token, next token) pair. That test cannot tell a following named entry (`01 Y`) from the entry's own tail, because the entry format itself holds an integer followed by a word: `OCCURS integer STEP integer` (STEP is a `cobolWord` behind the predicate `occursStepAhead()`, CobolData.g4:793) and `EDITING literal valueClauseOperand` (CobolData.g4:578). The pair (integer, word) is legal inside the entry, so `01 Y` is read as more tail and discarded.

## Repro (review round 1)

WORKING-STORAGE `01 X PIC X OCCURS "A" TIMES` (no period), then `01 Y PIC 9 VALUE IS.`, then `MOVE 1 W-N.` in the procedure. The fault in Y is lost: 2 diagnostics where 3 are due.

## What the finisher tried

A walk from the unit's own end (the entry list) with a test of the pairs the unit can hold (an ATN FOLLOW within the unit, error productions excluded, the unit's opening excluded). It fixes `01 FILLER ...` but not `01 Y ...`. The attempt was reverted; the branch carries none of it.

## Fix shape

Evaluate the pairs with the predicates run (so `occursStepAhead()` decides whether the word is STEP's operand), or use a three-token test. Pin with the repro above as a recovery golden.

## Landed (wave 20261788 D, 2026-10-31)

Re-probed on the tree first, and the note's mechanism had moved: PB4133 landed (PR #890) with a `UnitTailDiscard.Resumes` that has NO pair test
of the unit's own tail. It resumed only where a rule AROUND the unit takes the next two tokens, and never at the next element of the unit's OWN
list, so the following entry was eaten up to its period whatever its first tokens: `01 X PIC X OCCURS "A" TIMES` then `01 Y PIC 9 VALUE IS.`
drew two diagnostics (the refused operand, the later MOVE), the fault in Y lost, as filed.

**Fix.** `UnitTailDiscard` also resumes at the next element of the unit's own list where the unit opens with ONE token type
(`SoleOpener`, read from the ATN: the level-number of an entry; a sentence opens with any verb and its next sentence is told from its own
statements by nothing but the period). The opener alone is not enough, the entry holds integers (`OCCURS 2 STEP 3`, STEP a word behind a text
predicate that no walk evaluates), so the opener and the two tokens after it must be what the unit's own follow takes
(`ResimulationWalk.After(…, skipsErrorProductions: true)`: the three-token test the note named). The walk leaves the error productions out
(`WithoutErrorProductions`; ANTLR's closure is not overridable): a residue run takes any word, and `2 STEP W-STEP` would read as an entry with an
unrecognized clause. A head the list does not take (`01 Y PIK 9.`) is not resumed at (that entry is eaten with the tail, as before).
