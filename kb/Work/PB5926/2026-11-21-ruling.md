# PB5926 - ruling D-B (2026-11-21)

Decided by the controller, account 2, from the adjudication `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w20262132\questions\finding.md`
(decision D-B), adopting its recommendation.

**Question.** How does the KEPT row 1989-RV-A.2.46.3-3 (WHEN-COMPILED denotes the compilation time the listing
prints) get its own-edition witness, now that the premise of DRV-1989-RV-A.2.46.3-3 ("no listing in any edition") is
false?

**Ruling.** The row gets its own witness: a conformance-suite case that compiles with its listing and checks that the
instant the listing's page heading prints equals the program's WHEN-COMPILED. The derivation whose premise is false is
deleted, and nothing is re-derived in its place.

**Why.** §1989:A.2.46.3 RV3 asks that the returned value "denote the same time as the compilation date and time if
provided in the listing of the source program". Its antecedent is now populated at 85: §1985:IV.7.1 says each compiler
"produces an output listing of the source program", and the full listing landed (#1906), printing the compilation instant in its page heading. So the rule can
fail at 85, and the unpopulatable-antecedent argument no longer holds. A KEPT row's witness must be a conformance-suite
case that runs at its edition (kb/Work PB5675), so the suite harness gains an option that compiles a case with its
listing and compares the listing's heading instant with the program's output.
