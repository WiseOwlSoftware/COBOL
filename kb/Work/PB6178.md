# PB6178 — the anchor of a later operand when a whole field is absent, and two stale comments

**Open question (the implementer, unfixed, "ambiguous in spec").** When a whole report field is absent (its presence test fails), the
hoisted anchors of the later operands (`EmitAnchorSeeds`) still use the constant sum, assuming the earlier operands present.
§2023:13.18.38.4 GR12 "would occupy" does not settle it. An adjudication reads GR12 (and GR13) and decides what column a later
operand's step anchor is measured from when the whole field is absent; a probe pair (field present vs absent, with a STEP'd
multiple COLUMN) shows today's column. The compiler's answer, once decided, is pinned by a golden.

**Stale comments (reviewer R1-1).** After PB6146 a later operand (repetition > 0) of a present field has its anchor rewritten from the
live `__hc` INSIDE the field-level guard (lines 269-270). Two comments say otherwise: line 254 ("EVERY operand's seed is written OUTSIDE
every presence test, the field guard included") and the AnchorSeed arm at lines 297-299 ("the seed itself was written before the
presence tests (EmitAnchorSeeds)"). The AnchorSeed arm places at the rewrite, not at the hoist. Correct both comments with the fix of the
open question.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ReportWriterEmitter.cs:254` (`ReportWriterEmitter.EmitFieldPlacements`, `ReportWriterEmitter.EmitAnchorSeeds`)

**Decision (2026-11-25, wave 20262171 C).** When a WHOLE field is absent its hoisted anchors stay the constant sum that assumes every earlier
operand present: a later occurrence is positioned "integer-3 columns to the right of the column they occupy in the preceding occurrence"
(§2023:13.18.38.4 GR12b), the absent occurrence occupies none, and GR2b (§2023:13.18.41.4, "as though the entry were omitted") does not say
where an omitted entry's step is measured from. The reading that keeps the entry's own layout (operand 1 at counter + 2, operand 2 at operand 1's
column + 2) is the one a STEPped table needs: the live-counter reading would put every operand of the absent occurrence on one column and the
present occurrence's operands on top of each other. Probe (this build, STEP 10, `PRESENT WHEN G NOT = 1`): `01 [HELLO` + 11 spaces + `2 2]`,
operands at 17 and 19. Pinned by golden `pb6178-absent-field-anchor-layout` (2002) and its refusal below 2002
(`pb6178-absent-field-anchor-layout-85`, COBOL0900). Both stale comments corrected. No code path changed.
