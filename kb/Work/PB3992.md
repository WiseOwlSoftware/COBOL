## What to decide

1. **Triage a finding by the change's own claim.** A finding that the change's core claim is wrong blocks. A finding on
   adjacent scope (a sibling behaviour, a doc sentence's precision about another implementation, a pre-existing bug the
   sweep found) becomes a kb/Work note and the change lands with the text made true, never expanded into new code in
   the same branch. The review skill's branch form and MANDATORY-PRACTICES would carry the rule.
2. **A finisher answers a doc-precision finding with doc text**, not with a behaviour change, unless the finding says
   the behaviour is wrong.
3. **The leg-1 gaps that turn into merge-queue bounces** are defects of their own (PB3991), and the bounce's hour is
   their cost.
4. **The attended session does not STOP the loop while a branch the owner is waiting for is in flight** without saying
   what it delays.
