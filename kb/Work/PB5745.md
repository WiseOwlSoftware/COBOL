# PB5745 - FLAG-85 b CORRESPONDING treats a subscript that is a constant-name as non-constant (HasNonConstantSubscript)

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/FlagConformancePass.Flag85.cs` (`HasNonConstantSubscript`, `IsIntegerLiteralText`, `FlagCorresponding`).

**Mechanism.** `§2002:7.2.11.3 GR4` b) flags an ADD, MOVE or SUBTRACT with CORRESPONDING "if subscripting with other than a constant is specified on any of the operands". The detector takes a constant to be an integer literal only (`IsIntegerLiteralText`), so a subscript written as a constant-name reads as non-constant and the statement is flagged although every subscript is a constant. Whether a constant-name is a "constant" here is for the determination against the 2002 text; the comment on `FlagCorresponding` states the literal-only reading without a citation.

**Repro.** A 2002 source unit with `>>FLAG-85 CORRESPONDING`, a constant entry `01 K CONSTANT AS 2.` and `MOVE CORRESPONDING G1(K) TO G2(1)`; expected per the determination, observed a flag.

**Direction.** Determine, then resolve a constant-name subscript through the binder's constant table rather than by text; add accepted and refused goldens under `cases/7.2.11@2002-FLAG-85/`.
