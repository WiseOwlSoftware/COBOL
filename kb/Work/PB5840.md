# PB5840 - the 2002 text dropped the 1985 sentence that treats sequential mass storage files as files on tape, its annex of changes does not list it, and docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md has no row for it

**Sites:** none (a recorded fact about the edition texts; no change is planned from this note).

**What 1985 says, and where.** The CLOSE general rules of both modules say that a sequential mass storage file is treated as a file on tape: "Treatment of sequential mass storage files is logically equivalent to the treatment of a file on tape or analogous sequential media" in the preamble of `§1985:VII.4.2.4` and again in the preamble of `§1985:XIII.4.2.4`. The hardware-dependent list ties the REEL/UNIT, FOR REMOVAL and NO REWIND phrases to "a reel or mass storage type of device" (`§1985:C.3` items 9, 10, 11, 15).

**What 2002 and 2023 say instead, and where.** The 1985 preamble sentence is gone. `§2002:9.1.9` instead confines "reel" and "unit" to sequential files "associated with a physical file that may be contained on multiple physical devices" (its NOTE's examples: multiple magnetic tapes, multiple removable disk packs); `§2002:14.8.6.3 GR2a` keeps the three categories plus d; `§2002:9.1.12.1` keeps 07 for a non-reel/unit medium; `§2002:14.8.26.3 GR9` adds that an ignored NO REWIND gives 07. The processor-dependent list (`§2002:B.3`, items 12 to 17) still ties these phrases to "a reel or mass storage type of device". As 2002: `§2023:9.1.9` carries the same confining sentence (the brief's `§2023:14.9.6.4` for it is wrong: the sentence is `§2023:9.1.9`; `§2023:14.9.6.4 GR2a` carries the categories); `§2023:9.1.13.2` restricts 07 to OPEN/CLOSE on a non-reel/unit medium; `§2023:14.9.27.4 GR11` gives 07 for an ignored NO REWIND. 2023 does not say what a single-device disk file is beyond 9.1.9; DOC-A.1-24 records the compiler's placement there.

**What records the change, and what does not.** Annex F (substantive changes) of the 2002 text does not record the removal of the sentence, and `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` has no row for it. The 2002 edition's work is frozen by the owner (2026-11-16), so this note records a fact, not a task to start.
