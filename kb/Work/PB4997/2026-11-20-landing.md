# PB4997 addendum, 2026-11-20: the COBOL-85 conformance statement is docs/CONFORMANCE.md section 9

**What landed.** `docs/conformance/9/` is the statement §1985:I.1.5 asks for. Its one hand-written claim is PB4885's: the
high subset with the highest level of every optional module. Everything it says the compiler meets is computed on every render
by `scripts/spec/conformance_1985.py` from the 1985 view (`inventory_view.inventory(1985)`, each rule's own verdict or its KEPT
chain's): the module-by-module table (rules, level 2 rules, OK, GAP, own verdict, KEPT into 2023, not supported), every GAP
row by citation (none at this head), and the obsolete elements of §1985:C.1 item by item, with the rules each marks and
their states and the warning read from the construct catalogue (`obsoleteIn` 85). Section 9.1 names, with its ground, each
of the 14 rules the claim does not support (own verdict DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT, or KEPT into a 2023 rule with that verdict),
and `conformance_doc.py --check` holds it to that set both ways (a missing row, a row of a supported rule, a row whose
citation or subject is not its rule's). Section 9.2 specifies the hardware components item by item from §1985:C.3, and 9.3
the character set.

**Design.** Not a new view, as the note's site line planned (`scripts/rendered_views.py`, whole): a second rendered file
would be a second copy of the statement to drift. The computed text is part of the one conformance view through a new
mechanism of the reader, `conformance_source.GENERATORS` (an index's `generated:` frontmatter names one); its findings are
the sources' findings, so the RENDERED VIEWS and DOC AUDITS gate audits fail on a disagreement. Self-tests:
`scripts/selftests/spec/conformance_1985/test_render.py` (each way 9.1 can part from the rows is a finding) and the planted
section 9 of `conformance_source/test_sources.py`.

**The fifth optional module (decision, mine).** PB4885 says "every level of all four optional modules", decided on the
unamended text; Amendment 1 made Intrinsic Function the fifth (§1985:I.1.5: "Standard COBOL consists of 12 modules, seven of
which are required and five of which are optional"), and all 190 of its rules are in the 1985 inventory, OK. The claim
covers it, which is what "every optional module" means on the amended text. Its rows carry the one departure the statement
states as a departure rather than a qualification of §1985:I.1.5.2.5: D-UTC-OFFSET (kb/Work PB191, PB5304), CURRENT-DATE and
WHEN-COMPILED reporting a true offset beyond +13/-12 hours.

**blocked_by PB4995 dropped.** The statement is computed, so it waits on no adjudication: whatever slice 4 leaves open shows
as a GAP row the moment it is open. At this head the 1985 view is 2,275 rules, 0 GAP (`inventory_view.py --summary`).
