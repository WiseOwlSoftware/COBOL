# PB5807 - a group item named in a report SOURCE clause is printed wrongly: '4X7' prints as '047' where a MOVE of the same group gives '4X7' (every edition)

**What is wrong.** A report SOURCE clause that names a group item is moved into a numeric or numeric-edited printable item by numeric conversion, where the implicit MOVE is a group move with no conversion: the group `WS-G` holding `4X7`, as the SOURCE of a `PIC 999` printable item, prints `047`, while a procedure `MOVE WS-G TO N3` of the same group gives `4X7` in the same run. Same at `--std 85` and `--std 2023`. Ruling: UPHELD (b), harm wrong_answer. The checker's confidence: high.

**Mechanism (the checker's reading).** SOURCE identifier-1 is the sender of an implicit MOVE (`§1985:XIII.3.18.3 SR2`); a move with a group sender is alphanumeric to alphanumeric with no conversion (`§1985:VI.6.19.4 GR5`), so WS-G `4X7` into `PIC 999` prints `4X7`. 2023 makes SOURCE an implicit MOVE too (`§2023:13.18.53.4 GR1`).
