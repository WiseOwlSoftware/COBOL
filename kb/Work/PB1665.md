# PB1665 — OPEN

Filed 2026-09-27 by the train-68 lander from the wave-68 T implementer report (`w68t-PB1401-report.md`, new
defect 1), which carried it forward from wave-65 group E's lead 1 (never filed until now).

**Repro.** `scratchpad/w65e/p/p10.cob`: `01 G5. 05 A PIC X(2). 05 N PIC N(2). 05 B PIC X(2).` and a same-shaped
`G6`, then `MOVE G5 TO G6` — `B` of `G6` is left as spaces. Wrong answer, silent.

**Rule.** §14.9.25.4 GR4 (`cite.py --check 14.9.25.4` OK §14.9.25.4 4)): a group move is "treated exactly as if it
were an alphanumeric to alphanumeric elementary move", and "the receiving area will be filled without consideration
for the individual elementary or group items contained within either the sending or receiving area". The whole
sending area must arrive; the move width is the group's storage size.

**Code sites.** The group-MOVE width (`DataItem.ImageWidth` as the MOVE emitter's group arm reads it) against
`GroupImageCodec.AsImageOf`, which emits two image positions per national character. One width authority must
serve both, or the national leaf's image form must match the width the MOVE counts.

**What the fixer owes.** Fix the width at its one authority (sweep every consumer of the group image width: MOVE,
compare, CALL/INVOKE boundary windows — `CallEmitter.BoundaryImageWidth`, `ElementaryFormalWindow`). Then drop
`Usage.National` from `StorageFormPass.IsImagePromotable`'s exclusion and land PB1466's national arm (row
GR-8.8.4.4.4-L2.1; w65e probes p2, p8, p9). Golden: the p10 shape with a `.out`.

## Analysis 2026-09-29 (batch 8, not landed)

Reproduced (`G5`: X(2) "AB", N(2) N"CD", X(2) "EF"; `MOVE G5 TO G6`) -> `[AB][CD][  ]`, B lost. `DataItem.ImageWidth` counts a national leaf in POSITIONS (DataItem.cs:814 "never byte-doubled") while `GroupImageCodec.AsImageOf`'s national arm writes each position as a UTF-16 byte pair, so the group image is longer than the width the MOVE copies. Every consumer of a group image width must move to one authority (MOVE group arm, compare, `CallEmitter.BoundaryImageWidth`, `ElementaryFormalWindow`); not a one-site fix, hence not taken in a token-frugal batch.
