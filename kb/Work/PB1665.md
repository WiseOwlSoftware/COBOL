# PB1665 — LANDED

Filed 2026-09-27 by the train-68 lander from the wave-68 T implementer report (`w68t-PB1401-report.md`, new
defect 1), which carried it forward from wave-65 group E's lead 1 (never filed until now).

**Repro.** `scratchpad/w65e/p/p10.cob`: `01 G5. 05 A PIC X(2). 05 N PIC N(2). 05 B PIC X(2).` and a same-shaped
`G6`, then `MOVE G5 TO G6` — `B` of `G6` is left as spaces. Wrong answer, silent.

**Rule.** §14.9.25.4 GR4 (`cite.py --check 14.9.25.4` OK §14.9.25.4 4)): a group move is "treated exactly as if it
were an alphanumeric to alphanumeric elementary move", and "the receiving area will be filled without consideration
for the individual elementary or group items contained within either the sending or receiving area". The whole
sending area must arrive; the move width is the group's storage size.

**Code sites.** The group-MOVE width (`DataItem.ImageWidth` as the MOVE emitter's group arm reads it) against
`GroupImageCodec.AsImageOf`, which emits two image positions per national character. One width authority must
serve both, or the national leaf's image form must match the width the MOVE counts.

**What the fixer owes.** Fix the width at its one authority (sweep every consumer of the group image width: MOVE,
compare, CALL/INVOKE boundary windows — `CallEmitter.BoundaryImageWidth`, `ElementaryFormalWindow`). Then drop
`Usage.National` from `StorageFormPass.IsImagePromotable`'s exclusion and land PB1466's national arm (row
GR-8.8.4.4.4-L2.1; w65e probes p2, p8, p9). Golden: the p10 shape with a `.out`.

## Analysis 2026-09-29 (batch 8, not landed)

Reproduced (`G5`: X(2) "AB", N(2) N"CD", X(2) "EF"; `MOVE G5 TO G6`) -> `[AB][CD][  ]`, B lost. `DataItem.ImageWidth` counts a national leaf in POSITIONS (DataItem.cs:814 "never byte-doubled") while `GroupImageCodec.AsImageOf`'s national arm writes each position as a UTF-16 byte pair, so the group image is longer than the width the MOVE copies. Every consumer of a group image width must move to one authority (MOVE group arm, compare, `CallEmitter.BoundaryImageWidth`, `ElementaryFormalWindow`); not a one-site fix, hence not taken in a token-frugal batch.

## Analysis 2026-09-29, batch 9 (not landed — wider than one site)

Emitted for the repro: `G6.FromImage(CobolString.Store(G5.AsImage(), 6))` — the group MOVE stores the sender's image at the RECEIVER's width `ReceivingStore` (line ~114, `target.Item.AsIfPic?.Length ?? target.Item.ImageWidth`) = 6 POSITIONS, while `AsImage()` is 8 characters because `GroupImageCodec.AsImageOf`'s national arm writes each position as a UTF-16BE byte pair (PB327) — so `Store` truncates the last two. `DataItem.ByteWidth` already is the byte-image length (national x2; `PhysicalModel` uses it). The width rule is written in FOUR places that must move together — `DataItem.ImageWidth`, its mirror `RecordLayout.ImageWidth`, `GroupValueSlicer` (line ~196 offsets per child `ImageWidth`), and the group consumers in `MoveEmitter` / `ReceivingStore` / `CallEmitter.BoundaryImageWidth` — and `GroupImageCodec` line ~565 reads the GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL as-if `PIC N(m)` from the same `ImageWidth` (positions), so simply doubling a group's `ImageWidth` would break national groups. Shape of the fix: a group-image width that is `ByteWidth` for an alphanumeric group and positions for a GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL one, asked through ONE member every consumer above reads (delete the mirrored copies), with `ImageWidthIsStorageWidthTests` extended to a mixed group. Not taken alone.

## Landed — wave 1001 group D (w1001d)

**Re-probed first** on the w1001d Debug build of `b62408c25`: `MOVE G5 TO G6` over `X(2), N(2), X(2)` -> `[AB][CD][  ]`,
`IF G5 = G6` -> NE, and the same group across `CALL ... USING G5 G6` lost its tail at the formal.

**Root (the one-width rule, written twice).** `DataItem.ImageWidth` and its mirror `RecordLayout.ImageWidth` summed a
national leaf as POSITIONS inside an ALPHANUMERIC group, while the group's image (`AsImage`, the record codec,
`FUNCTION LENGTH`, `PhysicalWidth`) is in STORAGE characters, a national position being `CobolBits.BytesPerNational`
(D-N1). Every group-image consumer sized by `ImageWidth` (the MOVE width and `ReceivingStore`, the comparison, the
boundary windows, ref-mod positions, `KnownWidth`, the Tier-B strides) therefore disagreed with the image it sized.
The note's four places were four readers of those two copies.

**Fix.** The rule is ONE copy, `RecordLayout.ImageWidth`, and `DataItem.ImageWidth` IS it (the mirror and the
StorageFormPass identity #3 that compared them are deleted). The unit of a group's width is its class's: a
`GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL` group (§13.18.29.4 GR2b, as-if `PICTURE N(m)`) counts national POSITIONS (so its `AsIfPic`
length m and `GroupImageCodec`'s `AsNat` are unchanged), any other group counts storage characters
(§8.5.2.1 "an alphanumeric group item has class and category alphanumeric"; §14.9.25.4 GR4 moves it "exactly as if it
were an alphanumeric to alphanumeric elementary move"). So for an alphanumeric group `ImageWidth == ByteWidth`.
`ImageWidthIsStorageWidthTests` gains the group arm (alphanumeric group with a national leaf, national group with a
nested group, national group inside an alphanumeric group).

**Sweep (every `.ImageWidth` reader classified).** MOVE group arm / `ReceivingStore.CharacterPositions` /
`CallEmitter.BoundaryImageWidth` / `OoEmitter` var-group windows / `Place.PositionCount` / `IntrinsicBinder.KnownWidth` /
`PlaceCursor.Indexed` / `OoConformance` size compare / `VariableLengthCompatibility` signature atoms: group readers,
now in the image's unit, no edit. `GroupValueSlicer`: SR14 forbids a national leaf under a VALUE-bearing alphanumeric
group, and a national group's children are positions - unchanged. **A second consumer the same disagreement hid: RENAMES
THROUGH.** Its window arithmetic (`DataBinder` tiling, `ReferenceResolver` widths, `RenamesPlace` read/write) mixed
elementary positions with group widths; over a record holding a national leaf `RENAMES P1 THRU P2` was 7 characters
(storage is 9) and `MOVE RA TO W` read the wrong characters (measured). It is now tiled in storage bytes
(`RenamesSpanPart.FromBytes` / `.Bytes` convert to the leaf's positions; a boundary inside a national character is
refused as untileable) and a national cell contributes `NatBytes` to the alias image / is decoded with `NatReadWindow`
on write. The group ref-mod position check (`G5(7:2)` over 8 storage characters) is now measured against the right
count: it was refused as "a position of the 6-position item".

**Goldens.** `conformance:2002/pb1665_group_image_national_leaf` (MOVE, comparison, round trip through `X(8)`,
CALL BY REFERENCE, RENAMES alias length and tail). Unblocks kb/Work PB1466's national arm, landed in the same change.
