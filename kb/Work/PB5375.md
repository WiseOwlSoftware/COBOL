# PB5375 - INSPECT of an unsigned item whose PICTURE has P inspects the algebraic expansion, not the stored characters: 9PP VALUE 500 tallies two zeros, PP9 counts three CHARACTERS, and REPLACING writes five characters into a one-character item and corrupts the record around it

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/InspectEmitter.cs#Emit` and `#EmitBody` (the inspected image of the subject)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/InspectEmitter.cs#EmitStore` (the store of the REPLACING result)

**Mechanism.** An unsigned numeric item is inspected "as though it had been redefined as alphanumeric", which is its stored character positions, and the scaling positions a P describes are not among them. The emitter builds the subject's image from the item's value (500 for `9PP` holding 5), so TALLYING counts characters the item does not hold, and REPLACING stores a five-character result back through the numeric item, which spills into the bytes after it.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids below. Its row is not recorded: the refuter's OVERTURNED verdict is held for adjudication in `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/held-7.json`.

- `1985-GR-VI.5.9.4-8` - `§1985:VI.5.9.4 GR8` (refuter: OVERTURNED the read's CONFORMS, harm: wrong answer): outside the operations the rule lists, the P positions "are ignored and are not counted in the size of the operand". With `§1985:VI.6.18.4 GR3b`: "the data item is inspected as though it had been redefined as alphanumeric".

**Observed (the pinned compiler of `E:/COBOL-wt/coderead85-tree-b` (origin/main 8147f008b3; `git diff --stat 8147f008b3 origin/main -- src` is empty at 5237495315), rerun 2026-11-12; `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/reprobe-85f/log.txt`).** p1 prints `T02`; p2 prints `R[51100]` and `CH03`; p3 prints `B[5ABCD]` before and `A[511ABCD] N=51100` after the REPLACING.

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** p1 tallies 00 (the item holds one character, "5"); p2's `PP9` counts 01 CHARACTERS; p3 leaves the item and its group unchanged, `[5ABCD]`.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** The inspected subject of an unsigned numeric DISPLAY item is its storage image, the same image a group move and a nonnumeric comparison already read (p1's `G[5  ]` and `EQ5` are right); REPLACING stores back through that image. Sweep the other statements that read a numeric item "as alphanumeric" (STRING, UNSTRING sender, the class test) for the same expansion.
