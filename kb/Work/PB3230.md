**Re-probe at 27faa4c114:** the state the note names was all present: `ReferenceResolver.Probing { get; set; }` saved, set and

five settable delegates (`ReferenceResolver.FreezeOdoExtent`, `SubscriptBinder.MaterializeSegment`,
`RefModBinder.RefModCheckingAt`, `ObjectPropertyResolver.BindPropertyObject`, `ApplyObjectView`) installed by
`StatementBinder.AttachSegmentMaterializer` with `??=`; the statement binder calling back into the resolver's mutable
`SegmentDeferred` (`NoteSegmentDeferred`); report sets `ReferenceResolver.Diagnosed`, `NameResolution._indexRefsReported`,
`_qualifiedIndexGated`, `_linkageLegReported`, and the same discipline in `ConditionBinder` (three sets).

**Built:** (1) the five hooks are ONE immutable interface, `IStatementSite`, a constructor argument of `ReferenceResolver`;
`StatementBinder` implements it and constructs its own procedure resolver (`StatementBinder.Refs`), and every data-division
resolver is constructed with `site: null`, so the two are two constructions. The materializer answers `SegmentValue`
(temp, or unbuilt), and the resolver marks its own deferral. (2) A probe is a second construction of the funnel
(`ReferenceResolver.Prober`) over the same binder, site and ledger with its own parts, so `Probing` is get-only: every
speculative ask goes to the prober and a commit asked while probing goes back to the committer; no flag is saved or
restored anywhere. (3) One report per written reference is one ledger, `ReferenceReports`, keyed by the written node's
identity and a `ReferenceReportRule`, asked through `ReferenceResolver.ReportOnce` (false on the prober); it absorbed the
seven sets. `ResolutionModeDriftTests` pins all three by reflection.

**Not built, by decision (recorded in DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3 (4)):** effects returned as data for one commit
step, with `Probe` discarding them and the probe-only readings of `OoBinder.OoBindPropertyObject` deleted. The effects of a
commit are not the resolver's to return: the D18 materializer binds a whole expression through the statement binder
(diagnostics, pre-ops, compiler temps whose names are numbered in allocation order), so a probe that ran it and dropped the
effects would renumber every later temporary and reorder the in-order diagnostic stream the DESIGN-architecture-review §4.1 oracle compares. The
probe keeps its effect-free readings.
