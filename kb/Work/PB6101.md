# PB6101 - PB3322's 2002 citations are unchecked prose

CLAUDE.md rule 1 requires a citation in the edition form `§2002:<clause> <rule>`, checked with `cite.py --check`. The PB3322 report says `cite.py` does not
apply to 2002 text; it does (`cite.py --check` takes an edition-named 2002 citation and `--edition 2002` is supported). Re-verified on main ae1136f781:

- `tests/version-matrix/inventory/DOC-A.1-64.json` (notes, line 8) says "11.8.5 ENTRY-CONVENTION clause IS entry-convention-name-1, general rule 2 ...
  Annex A item 44 (optional, 11.8.5 general rule 2)". The check
  `cite.py --check` of that quotation against clause 11.8.5 GR2 answers `FAIL [no-rule-list]` (clause 11.8.5 has no general rules; its subclause 11.8.5.3 has GR2), and the same quotation against §2002:11.8.5.3 GR2 is OK. The locator the row carries is
  the clause number inherited from prose, the failure mode rule 1 names.
- `kb/Work/PB3322.md` (the lines for DOC-A.1-34/-37 "2002 8.1.1", DOC-A.1-112 "2002 7.2.14 ... Annex A item 87", DOC-A.1-64 "2002 11.8.5 (general rule 2 ...)"), the
  batch `adjudication/pb3322-refute-pb3173/overturn-batch.json` and `DEVLOG/3936-pb3322-the-refuter-pass-over-pb3173-s-edition-decisions.md` carry the
  same prose forms. `8.1.1` and `7.2.14` were not re-derived here (a bare quote of fewer than four words cannot be checked); each needs its own `cite.py --check`.

**Sites:**
- `tests/version-matrix/inventory/DOC-A.1-64.json#notes`
- `kb/Work/PB3322.md`
- `adjudication/pb3322-refute-pb3173/overturn-batch.json`

**Next:** re-derive each 2002 locator through `cite.py --check`, rewrite the row's notes in the edition form (a re-record through `record_verdicts.py`, never a hand edit
of a stored row), and correct the PB3322 note beside it. The DEVLOG entry is history and stays as written.

**Resolution (wave 20262148 group C).** Every locator re-derived with `cite.py --check`, edition-named:
- DOC-A.1-64: `§2002:11.8.5.3 GR2` (11.8.5 itself has no rules), the default paragraph in `§2002:11.8.5.3`, and the item list is `§2002:B.1 item 44`
  (2002 Annex A is the communications facility; the implementor-defined list is Annex B, so "Annex A item 44" was a second wrong locator). Re-recorded
  through `record_verdicts.py` (batch `pb6101-cite-2002`, 1 row changed, GAP 2 -> 2).
- `8.1.1` and `7.2.14` were right as clauses: `§2002:8.1.1` and `§2002:7.2.14` check OK against their prose.
- DOC-A.1-112 "Annex A item 87" is `§2002:B.1 item 87`; DOC-A.1-188 "Annex A item 151, general rules 7f and 7h" is `§2002:B.1 item 151`, `§2002:12.2.6.3 GR7f`
  (UCS-4), `GR7g` (UTF-8), `GR7h` (UTF-16); DOC-A.1-202 "2014 Annex A item 184" is `§2014:A.1 item 184` (2014 keeps the list in A.1).
- `kb/Work/PB3322.md` and `adjudication/pb3322-refute-pb3173/overturn-batch.json` carry the corrected forms; the DEVLOG entry stays as written.
