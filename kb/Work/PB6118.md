# PB6118 - a two-arm Cite gives 2002 and 2014 the 2023 clause

**Mechanism.** A diagnostic names the rule of the edition it runs at (PB4950). Where the call site passes only a 1985 text and a "later"
text (`EditionInfo.Cite(y1985, later)`, `CiteRule(...)`), `--std 2002` and `--std 2014` fall into the "later" arm, which holds the 2023
locator. The PB5658 report counted the shape with `grep -rnoE '\.Cite(Rule)?\("[^"]*", "[^"]*"\)' src`: 401 calls. PB5978 and PB6072 fixed
single sites of the same shape; no note owns the sweep.

**Sightings (each a site of the sweep, not a separate defect).**
- `IndexNameRule.NotAnIdentifier` (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/IndexNameRule.cs:19`): COBOL1637 at --std 2002 cites §2023:8.4.3.1.2 and
  §2023:13.18.38.3 SR7 (repro `p5978.cob`, PB5658 wave).
- `FileControlKeyRules.Rules`: at --std 2002 the RECORD KEY / ALTERNATE RECORD KEY category rows, SOURCE same-category, the FILE STATUS and
  PADDING messages print §2023:12.4.5.12.3 SR2 and kin, although the 2002 clauses 12.3.4.12.2, 12.3.4.5.2 and 12.3.4.7.2 hold the words (repro
  `p/keyfs.cob`, PB5735 wave; restating rows exist for 85 and 2014 only).
- Cascading diagnostics after an already refused construct: a zero-length literal as a report VALUE draws COBOL2247 citing §2023:13.15.3 SR14
  (`ReportSectionBinder.cs`, VALUE-only entry arm near line 2700); a refused DYNAMIC LENGTH item draws COBOL1561 citing §2023:13.18.19.3 SR1.
- `MoveTable16.Sr1ClassRefusal` pointer, message-tag and object arms word §2023 Table 2 and §2023:13.18.60.3 SR8/SR9 at 2002 and 2014.
- COBOL1543 (keyword-omitted intrinsic not declared) at --std 2002 and 2014 cites `ISO §2023:8.4.3.2.3 SR2`, not §2002:12.2.7.3 GR13 or the
  2014 rule: `IntrinsicBinder.cs#BindIntrinsic` passes only the 1985 and 2023 texts to `CiteRule` (repro
  `cases/12.2.7@2002-REPOSITORY-PARAGRAPH/g2sum08_sum_unprefixed_undeclared.cob` at --std 2002).

**Fix shape (rule 5).** Replace the two-arm form with a form that cannot omit an edition (every arm required, or a per-edition table), sweep
the call sites, and pair it with a drift test that fails on a diagnostic whose citation edition differs from its `--std`. Each re-cited clause
is derived from its edition's text and checked with `cite.py --check`.
