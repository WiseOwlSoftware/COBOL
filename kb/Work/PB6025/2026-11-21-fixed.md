## 2026-11-21 — adjudicated and fixed: the relative NEXT GROUP integer is unbounded by the page limit at 85 (group y, train85c-rw)

Ruling from the 1985 text: §1985:XIII.3.16.3 SR2 bounds both integers to three significant digits and states no page-limit bound;
each group type's presentation rules bound the rest. A body group's absolute integer lies between FIRST DETAIL and FOOTING
(§1985:XIII.3.10.8 GR5, "The integer of the absolute NEXT GROUP clause must specify a line number that is not less than..."), a
report heading's below FIRST DETAIL (§1985:XIII.3.10.6 GR4a/b), a page footing's at most the page limit (§1985:XIII.3.10.9 GR4a/b);
a body group's RELATIVE integer is bounded by nothing, because the final LINE-COUNTER setting stops at the FOOTING integer
(§1985:XIII.3.10.8 GR6b, "If the sum is equal to or greater than the line number specified by the FOOTING phrase..."), and an
unpaged report has no vertical subdivisions. So `NEXT GROUP PLUS 50` under `PAGE LIMIT 20` is legal 1985 source; the page-limit
bound is COBOL-2002's (2002 13.16.35.2 SR1). Fix in `BindNextGroupClauses`: the SR1 arm is asked from 2002 only (its message
cites §2023:13.18.37.3 SR1 alone), and at 85 the page footing's absolute integer keeps GR4a's page-limit half (the one bound SR1
covered that no 85 arm stated). Witnesses: `XIII.3.16@1985-NEXT-GROUP-CLAUSE/pb6025_relative_next_group_past_page_limit_85`
