# PB2840 — OPEN — an INVOKE literal compares by the 2023 comparer at every edition

Found by the w1047a implementer (PB1965, report lead 2) and Filed by the train 1047 lander, 2026-10-08 20:53 PDT.

## Repro
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1047a\p\d.cob --std 2014`: `INVOKE O "X<final sigma>"` against `METHOD-ID. X<sigma>.`
is refused COBOLNET0825 (method not found), though below 2023 the Annex C fold makes final sigma and sigma one name
(PB1965's batch, row GR-8.1.3.2-4: below 2023 two METHOD-IDs of those names are one name, COBOLNET0822).

## Mechanism
The literal method-name compares through the 2023 comparer; `CobolNames.EditionSpelling` reaches only lexed words, so
an alphanumeric literal naming a method is never edition-spelled. The literal's content should be folded by the same
edition rule as the word it names.

## Code sites
The INVOKE method-selector resolution (`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Oo/` INVOKE binding, the COBOLNET0825 site) and
`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/CobolNames.cs#EditionSpelling`.
