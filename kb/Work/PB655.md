# PB655 — OPEN — the whole lexer, joined against §8.9

Found 2026-09-05 by the `PB300` implementer (report §"New defects" D1), whose fix made the next case a one-line
data edit and whose sweep then measured how many next cases there are. The 22 §8.10 context-sensitive words PB301 measured are a subset of the fourth
class below, and are this note's alone: [[PB301]] LANDED on 2026-09-06 over the screen surface it owns (its
22 screen words all carry `nameSlot` rows and its population is now DERIVED from the grammar), handing the
non-screen §8.10 words here rather than holding a second register open on them. PB300 had already closed
PB301's CRT/CURSOR declaration gap for free.

## The rule

§8.9 is the reserved-word list per edition (`OK §8.9`); §8.10 the context-sensitive words, which are reserved only
in their context (`OK §8.10`). A word §8.9 does not reserve at edition E is a legal user-defined word at E; a §8.10
word is a legal user-defined word outside its context at every edition. The compiler's lexer makes a token of
every word its grammar needs, and a token that is not admitted to `cobolWord` (or `reservedGatedWord` at the
editions that free it) is refused as a user-defined word everywhere — a raw COBOL0001, never a §8.9 diagnostic.

## The measurement (`python <a9d94900 scratch>/PB300/sweep2.py`)

101 hits, 99 real (two are the `MINUS`/`SUB_MINUS` punctuation tokens, false positives of the script's literal
match), in four classes:

1. **Vendor usages the standard never had** — `COMP-1`…`COMP-5`, `COMPUTATIONAL-1`…`-5`, `PACKED`. A deliberate
   vendor surface; whether the dialect doctrine licenses TAKING the word as reserved is the owner's reading of
   [[follow_gnucobol_on_split_latitude]] (GnuCOBOL reserves them).
2. **Post-85 words refused at `--std 85`** — `ALLOCATE`, `GOBACK`, `BASED`, `OBJECT`, `TYPEDEF`, `LOCAL-STORAGE`,
   `RETURNING`, `REPOSITORY`, `SELF`, `SUPER`, `FUNCTION-ID`, `METHOD-ID`, `CLASS-ID`, `INTERFACE-ID`, `INHERITS`,
   `INVOKE`, `OPTIONS`, `NULL`, `ADDRESS`, `FREE`, `END-ACCEPT`, `END-DISPLAY`, the `FLOAT-*` and `BINARY-*`
   families. `01 GOBACK PIC X.` is legal COBOL-85 source and is refused today.
3. **Words §8.9 dropped, used at the later edition** — `ALTER`, `AUTHOR`, `DATE-WRITTEN`, `DATE-COMPILED`,
   `INSTALLATION`, `SECURITY`, `LABEL`, `MULTIPLE`, `REVERSED`, `TAPE` (free 2002+); `PAGE`, `POSITION`, `PROCEED`
   (free 2023); `REPORTS` (2002/2014); `B-SHIFT-*`, `FINALLY` (below 2023). PB300's own second harm, for ~25 more
   words: `01 PAGE PIC X.` is legal COBOL-2023 source.
4. **Words with no §8.9 row at all** — `AWAY-FROM-ZERO`, `BACKWARD`, `CAPACITY`, `CHANNEL`, `CYCLE`, `EDITED`,
   `GENERIC`, `INITIALIZED`, `INTRINSIC`, `JSON`, `XML`, `PARAGRAPH`, `PREVIOUS`, `PROHIBITED`, `RECURSIVE`,
   `SIGNED`, `STRONG`, `TRUNCATION`, `UNSIGNED`, `YYYYDDD`, `YYYYMMDD`, `NEAREST-*`, `TOWARD-*`, the `END-*` family.
   Free at every edition; PB301's 22 are a subset.

## The mechanism, and the fix shape

`tests/version-matrix/cobol-words.json` → the generated `cobolWord` / `reservedGatedWord` rules in
`CobolWords.g4`; the tokens in `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4`. After PB300 each case is a
`reservationGated` row (classes 2 and 3: gated to the editions §8.9 reserves the word) or a plain `cobolWord`
admission (class 4: free everywhere; §8.10 words keep their context through the parser predicates PB250 routed
through `CobolWordsMap`). Class 1 is the owner's call and is recorded, not decided, here. Do it as ONE landing per
class, not one per word, and measure the ALL(*) prediction cost PB301 warned about once per class with the
grammar-diagram generator's timing (PB736's load-sensitive test is the canary; PB588 was the same defect and is
retired into it). Drift: turn `sweep2.py` into a
unit test — the join of the lexer's single-word tokens against `cobolWord ∪ reservedGatedWord ∪ §8.9-at-every-
edition` must be EMPTY except for a cited exemption list (class 1 until the owner answers, and the punctuation
tokens), so the next token the grammar adds cannot cost a user's word silently. Goldens: one program per class
declaring a representative word as a data item at the edition that frees it (the PB300 golden is the model),
plus the negative at the edition that reserves it (COBOLNET0901, never COBOL0001). Inventory row `SR-8.3.2.1-3` ("Context-sensitive words may be used
as user-defined words and system-names in contexts other than the language construct in which they are
defined") is GAP and is what this note's class 4 landing closes; `SR-8.3.2.1-1`, the reserved-word half of
the same clause, went CONFORMS with PB301.

## 2026-09-06 — registrar append (class 3): `REPORTS` is the one `confidence: medium` row, and it is not inert

Filed into this note rather than as a new id, because `REPORTS` is already named in class 3 above. It is singled
out here because it is the one word in the whole data file whose *provenance*, not just its gating, is unsettled
— and the provenance note that says so calls it "inert", which the measurements below refute. Found by the PB301
implementer (report §"New defects" D2); every fact re-derived on main at `9fad13e5`.

**The row is unique.** `tests/version-matrix/reserved-words.json`, read programmatically — `REPORTS` is the
**only** row in the file with `confidence` other than `high`:

```json
{ "word": "REPORTS", "r85": true, "r2002": false, "r2014": false, "r2023": true,
  "confidence": "medium",
  "provenance": "in ISO 2023 §8.9 but not the 2014 source list and not an Annex-E addition — needs review (inert)" }
```

**It is not inert, and the word "inert" in that provenance string is the thing to delete first.** Three measured
facts, all static:

1. `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4:643` — `REPORTS : 'REPORTS' ;`. It is a real lexer token,
   so an occurrence never reaches `IDENTIFIER`. ⚠ The report gives this as `:651`; on main it is **:643**.
2. `grep -n REPORTS tests/version-matrix/cobol-words.json` and the same over
   `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolWords.g4` both return **nothing** — the word is in neither
   `cobolWord` nor the generated `reservedGatedWord`, at any edition.
3. The generator excludes it BY DESIGN, and says so: `scripts/gen-cobol-words.ps1:195-202` —
   *"The grammar gate keys on `userWordHere()` = `!IsReservedAt` (confidence-blind), while the §8.9 funnel only
   REPORTS high-confidence rows (`ReservedWordSet.RejectsAt`). Gating a lower-confidence word would reject them
   with no COBOLNET0901"* — and `:198` is the skip (`if ($e.confidence -ne 'high') { … continue }`), with an
   RW-3 `throw` guarding the combination.

So the two harms are both live, and they are on *opposite* sides of the same word:

| edition | §8.9 (2023 text) | the JSON row | what the compiler does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | **lists REPORTS** (`cite.py --check 8.9 "REPORTS"` → OK) | reserved | refuses `01 REPORTS PIC X.` — but as a raw `COBOL0001` + `COBOL0307` that never names §8.9. Right verdict, and precisely the diagnostic [[PB693]] existed to remove. |
| 2002 / 2014 | not derivable from this spec | **free** | refuses it anyway, because the token exists and no `cobolWord` admission does. **This note's class 3 on legal source.** |
| 85 | not derivable | reserved | refuses it; consistent. |

⚠ *Provenance of the diagnostic codes in that table:* the `COBOL0001` + `COBOL0307` pair is the PB301
implementer's measurement on his own worktree, not a probe re-run here — building was out of scope for this pass
and facts 1–3 above, which are the mechanism, are static and were re-derived on main. The refusal ITSELF follows
from fact 2 alone: a word that is a lexer token and appears in neither `cobolWord` nor `reservedGatedWord` cannot
occupy a user-word slot at any edition.

**And it is the single row that makes `SR-8.3.2.1-1`'s caveat non-empty.** PB301's landing (train 18 — ⛔ **not on
main at `9fad13e5`**, where that row is still blank and GAP) verdicts it CONFORMS on the strength of a gate
DERIVED from this file — but derived from its *high-confidence* rows only. One medium row is the whole difference
between "the gate is derived" and "the gate is derived, except for one word nobody adjudicated". Closing this

**What the fixer owes, and it is not a one-line data edit.** The gating half is one `reservationGated` row once

**Premise corrections (landed with PB805 in this train).**
1. ⛔ **PAGE is NOT free at 2023.** `- PAGE` sits in the 2023 §8.9 list between PACKED-DECIMAL and PAGE-COUNTER;

2. **REPORTS is settled, not an owner question.** ISO E.3.1: "Such incompatible additions are documented in

   branch now covers r85+r2023 with Annex E silent; REPORTS is `high`, r2002/r2014 true, "(inert)" gone.
3. Class 4 splits: 22 words ARE §8.10 context-sensitive; the other 23 (COMP-1..5, COMPUTATIONAL-1..5, PACKED,
   CHANNEL, ENTRY, JSON, XML, GENERIC, REMARKS, END-INVOKE/-JSON/-MERGE/-METHOD/-SORT/-XML) are in NEITHER list —
   vendor words ISO §4.2.10 lets an implementation reserve if documented (`cite.py --check 4.2.10 "shall specify
   any reserved words added for nonstandard extensions"` → OK).

**What the admission needs, measured.** WIP commit `5d6234dd7` on branch `worktree-wf_c843b87e-3e3-1` adds the 73
rows, the `extensionReserved` array, CONFORMANCE.md D-RW1, the lexer-join drift test and PB805's declared-name rule.
The whole Conformance assembly then went 115 red, and the dominant cause is structural, not per word: at the
editions §8.9 RESERVES an admitted word, its `{userWordHere}?` gate is a predicate past the LEFT EDGE of the
enclosing decision, ANTLR does not evaluate it there, and the enclosing decision commits to the reference reading —
`SET P TO NULL` at 2002 picks the SET-to-value format and the gate then fails the parse (re-coded COBOLNET0901).
NULL, SELF, SUPER, ADDRESS, TYPEDEF, BASED, ACTIVE-CLASS-adjacent programs all fell this way. The gate for these
words must be visible to prediction — a TOKEN-level gate (e.g. the post-lex rewriter retyping a free, DECLARED word
to IDENTIFIER, the `>>COBOL-WORDS` UNDEFINE mechanism) rather than a predicate. An ATN-derived "operand-position
keyword" probe (in the WIP commit) over-selects (68 words, incl. already-gated COLUMN/COMMIT/DEFAULT) and needs the
narrower definition. Finisher: design the token-level gate, then re-apply `5d6234dd7`'s rows/tests on it.
