## Finding — `reserved-words` (under-rejects; rows `SR-8.3.2.4.2-2`)

**Mechanism.** Post-85 special character words *> (inline comment indicator) and <> (not-equal relational operator) have no edition-introduction gate: COMMENT_START and NOTEQUAL are plain lexer tokens with no constructs.json row and no VersionConformancePass arm. Their siblings in the same §8.3.2.4.2 list, & (concat-operator-2002), >> (the *-directive-2002 rows) and :: (inline-method-invocation-2002), are all gated at 2002 with COBOLNET0900. So --std 85 silently accepts two words that did not exist in COBOL-85.

**Repro.** out/probe/reserved-words/rw85a.cob: `DISPLAY A *> inline comment` compiled with --std 85 --run gives no diagnostic and prints 7. rw85b.cob: `IF A <> 3 DISPLAY "NE".` compiled with --std 85 --run gives no diagnostic and prints NE. Control: rwall.cob --std 85 gives COBOLNET0900 on `"AB" & "CD"`, and rw85c.cob --std 85 gives COBOLNET0900 on `>>IF`.

**Expected from the spec.** §8.3.2.4.2 2) (cite.py --check OK for 'Comment indicator' and 'Relational operator - not equal') lists *> and <> as special character words of the 2023 language. Neither is a COBOL-85 word. The repo's own derivation (docs/ISO2023_CONFORMANCE_PLAN.md M2 WS-2002-FORMAT, which constructs.json's directive rows reuse) places *> inline comments in COBOL-2002. Annex E does not list <> as a 2014->2023 addition, so <> predates 2023, but its 2002-vs-2014 edge cannot be derived in-repo. Expected: --std 85 rejects each word with COBOLNET0900, exactly as it rejects & and >>; --std 2002/2014/2023 accept them. Pick the <> edge by the VCR 'derived, not quoted' discipline, or make it an owner decision.

**Observed / reasoning.** Probed with the pinned compiler (see repro). grep of constructs.json finds no row for inline comments or <>. NOTEQUAL is referenced only by the comparisonOperator grammar and SearchAllFormat2Rules. COMMENT_START is `'*>' -> skip` in DEFAULT mode with no gate. This is the two-arm shape: three of the five post-85 special character words gate and two do not.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4 (COMMENT_START line 188, NOTEQUAL line 987); tests/version-matrix/constructs.json (no rows); VersionConformancePass (no arms)
