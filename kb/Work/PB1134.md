### GR-14.6.2.3.2-L2.1 (PARTIAL)
|| REFUTED: PARTIAL — Action 1 sets 'the storage allocated for the implied or associated sections' to the specified-fill-character. Annex E.3.3 item 33 says 'The content is explicitly defined when this clause is specified'. InitialStateBackground.Seed only fills CharacterFormed pictures (alphanumeric, national, numeric-edited, DISPLAY boolean). Every numeric item keeps its zero, including USAGE DISPLAY numeric, whose storage IS character positions. Measured: out/probe/refute-isl/f1.cob, with OPTIONS INITIALIZE WORKING-STORAGE SECTION TO X"41" and VALUE-less 01 G (05 N PIC 9(4), 05 X PIC X(2), 05 B PIC 9(4) COMP). DISPLAY G gives '0000AA' plus two NUL bytes; the spec requires every storage position to be X"41" ('AAAAAA' plus 0x41 0x41). N=[0000] where 'AAAA' is required, and NN PIC 9(3)=[000]. The CONFORMANCE.md D23 determination (PB152) justifies this with §13.18.63.4 GR4 c), which is the no-clause 'undefined' baseline. It answers a different question from action 1, and §11.9.10.4 GR6 limits that latitude to 'If the INITIALIZE clause is not specified'. The determination was self-chosen, not an owner decision. The alphanumeric, national and edited arms, the section selectivity and the fill-under-VALUE ordering do hold (probe/is1 G=AA07).
