# PB3397 — the public base held a same-file predecessor; the project no longer does

Filed by the enqueue unit's leads clerk, 2026-10-18, from the PB3381 report
(`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w1125a-PB3381-report.md`, "New defects", first bullet). PB3381's own body
(`kb/Work/PB3381.md`, "Out of scope" paragraph, on `worktree-wf_9b787306-369-7`) names this as a lead of its landing.

**The contradiction.** The owner's rule of 2026-10-18 ~12:10 (kb/Work PB3381): finished work lands the moment it is
reviewed CLEAN; no predecessor is held. The project's `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/wf_rolling_wave.js` follows
it. The public base (`tools/claude-skills`, pinned at `b57b900`) taught and implemented the hold.

**Sites at the former pin `b57b900`** (`git -C tools/claude-skills grep`):
- `skills/agent-fleet/references/rolling-wave.js`: `held` line 122, `hasSuccessorQueued` line 129, the hold at line 214
  ("held — lands through its successor"), release at lines 217-219, drain at line 259 (the cited block is 115-132).
- `skills/agent-fleet/SKILL.md` line 124: "review a held chain once at its tail".
- `skills/agent-fleet/references/grouping-fixes.md` lines 27, 37, 41 (SAME-FILE SUCCESSORS: "predecessor's branch is held
  from the enqueue step and lands ...", "review a held chain only at its tail").
- `skills/agent-fleet/README.md` line 118 (the same-file-successors table row: "review a held chain at its tail").

**Related, not merged.** PB3380 is a different mechanism (a predecessor's review fixes never reach its successors) and
says the base "holds the chain correctly"; once the hold goes, PB3380's base-side item and this note touch the same
files, so the fixer should read both.

**The fix.** Change the public repository `E:/claude-skills` (rolling-wave.js, SKILL.md section 7, grouping-fixes.md,
README.md row) to the PB3381 rule, then move the `tools/claude-skills` pin (PB1699's pattern).

**Landing (2026-10-18).** Landed by the pin move to brent-tools 1.22.0 (`0b6e663`) on branch
`skills-pin-1-22-0-20261018` (kb/Work PB1699). v1.22.0 removes every site listed above: `rolling-wave.js` no longer
holds a predecessor through its successor (a successor starts after the predecessor's review loop ends and merges the
REVIEWED head), `SKILL.md` section 7 no longer says "review a held chain once at its tail", `grouping-fixes.md`
SAME-FILE SUCCESSORS now reads "Nothing is held for a successor", and the README row now reads "Finished work is never
held".
