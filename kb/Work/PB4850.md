# PB4850 - the 1985 transcription, reconciled and published

**Why.** PB4812's process is the 2023 one: every page is compared against its rendered image before it is trusted. Because the
1985 PDF is a scan, its OCR layer is no authority, so this reconciliation leans on the rendered page.

**What to do.**
- **Reconcile** every page of `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` against the PDF with the `spec-reconcile` skill and
  `.claude/workflows/spec-reconcile.js`, taking the edition. Every claim gets an adversarial verify before a repair. The ledger
  lives under `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/`, kept apart from the 2023 ledger by edition. Size the runs to the
  weekly quota: the workflow runs in page ranges, and each range is one checkpoint.
- **Front matter.** Write a Preface like `specs/ISO_COBOL_2023.md`'s. It names the edition and the CODASYL permission it is
  reproduced under, reproduces the acknowledgment paragraphs in their entirety, and names no person. The X3.23a-1989 and
  X3.23b-1993 amendments stay in the file, each marked as an amendment.
- **Gates,** all with `--edition 1985`: `lint_rendering.py`, `sweep_figures.py --check`, `verify_publishable.py` (no stamp,
  order number or licensee name anywhere) and `verify_acknowledgment.py`.
- **Connect.** `scripts/spec/cite.py --edition 1985 --check` validates a citation against the file. Record the transcription
  in `docs/DOC_INDEX.md` and in `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md`, and check that document's 1985 claims against the
  edition's own text, filing each disagreement as its own note. Then tell PB4759 (the cd-name debugging trigger, Debug module
  around PDF page 698) that it can cite the file.

**Done when** every page has a reconciliation verdict, the gates are GREEN, cite.py checks a 1985 citation, and PB4812 can
close.

**Sites:** `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (whole: the reconciled transcription), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/README.md` (whole: the 1985 ledger's place), `docs/DOC_INDEX.md` (whole: one row), `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` (whole: its 1985 claims are checked).
