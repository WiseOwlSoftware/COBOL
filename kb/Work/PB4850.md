# PB4850 - the 1985 transcription, reconciled and published

**Why.** PB4812's process is the 2023 one: every page is compared against its rendered image before it is trusted. Because the
1985 PDF is a scan, its OCR layer is no authority, so this reconciliation leans on the rendered page.

- **Then the 1985 audit** (PB4886): the reconciled file becomes the authority for `--std=1985`, through a rule catalog and a

**Boxes are normative.** The 1985 standard draws a box around every specification unique to level 2 ("the high level") of a
module (PDF page 23; page 147), and the amendments edit boxes (page 848). The batch prompt never asked for boxes, so the raw pages
lose every one. TRANSCRIPTION-STATE.md gains the rule for a boxed span in prose: one markup, used everywhere and explained in the
Preface's notation note. Every boxed prose span is then restored. The boxes in general formats are PB4849's.

**The first reconciliation pass (2026-11-06).** The owner asked to "use the batch api remaining credit for part of the
reconciliation today". The inputs and outputs are in `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reconcile-1985\`: `recon1985.py` (restartable; Opus
5.5 through the Message Batches API), about $26.30 in all. Each request sent a page image and that page's raw Markdown, and asked
for the discrepancies only, as JSON.
- **Canary.** Four pages were copied and four errors planted: a dropped underline, a blanked bracket corner, a changed word and a
  misspelled term. The prompt reported all four and reported nothing on the untouched control page. A first prompt, which had no
  counting step, answered `[]` on 8 of 10 pages after 4 output tokens, and was replaced.
- **Full pass** (976 pages, $18.39): 377 claims on 162 pages. On 21 pages the batch model had left a false start and its own
  chatter ("Wait - header should be plain text. Let me produce properly."). `repair_chatter.py` cut each false start, kept the
  original in `pages/orig/` and recorded the cut in `repairs.json`. Pages 709 and 824 had been paraphrased as bullet summaries,
  and page 908 was a refusal. Those three were converted again with the local model (`local.json`), not an Anthropic one. A
  recheck of the 24 pages ($0.47) found no chatter left.
- **Adversarial verify** ($2.41). Each of the 292 remaining claims, on 146 pages, went back with the page image to be refuted.
  The result: 143 CONFIRMED on 82 pages (85 of them underlines); 142 REFUTED (including all 110 heading-level claims, as
  convention); 7 UNSURE. `verdicts.json` holds every claim with its verdict, reason and proposed fix. A spot check against the
  rendered page agreed: in BLOCK CONTAINS on page 531, CHARACTERS is not underlined, but the transcription underlines it.
- **Figure-only pass** ($4.77; 315 general-format pages, densest first): 77 claims on 52 pages, 68 of them lost boxes.
- **Second opinion** ($1.50; the owner raised the month's cap to $400 and added $200 of credit). The 150 CONFIRMED and UNSURE
  claims went back to the same model in a fresh context. It confirmed 139 again, refuted 6 and left 5 unsure. In
  `verdicts.json` each claim now carries `second` and a `final` field: 139 CONFIRMED (both verdicts), 142 REFUTED, 11 DISPUTED.
  A disputed claim is decided against the rendered page in the repair step.
- **Box pass** ($13.29, all 976 pages). For each page it listed every printed box: where it is (format, rule, paragraph or
  table), and its first and last words as the transcription spells them. The prompt was proved on a 5-page pilot checked by
  eye: page 389's five boxes and page 531's `[IS EXTERNAL]`/`[IS GLOBAL]` box were found, and no box was reported on a
  glossary page or on page 147, which only describes boxes. The pass found 722 boxes on 297 pages (673 high certainty): 423
  in rules, 163 in paragraphs, 115 in general formats, 8 in tables and 13 other. `boxes.json` holds them, as the
  restoration list for the prose boxes and the cross-check for PB4849's measured rectangles.
These claims and boxes are detections, and every verdict came from one model family. Repairs are made against the rendered page
through this note's workflow, starting from the `final` CONFIRMED verdicts and `boxes.json`, once PB4816 has assembled the file.
