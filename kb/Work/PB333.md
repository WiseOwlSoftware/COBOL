# PB333 — a positioning direction the source never legally expressed

**Rows:** `SR-14.9.41.3-3`.

## The program

`<lane3 scratch>/out-b1/probe/sr3-ops/prb.cob` — an `ORGANIZATION INDEXED` file with two records, then
`START IXF KEY IS NOT >= IX-KEY ... END-START` and `START IXF KEY IS NOT LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO IX-KEY ...
END-START`. Both compile and run: `P2-OK ST=00`, `P3-OK ST=00`.

## The rule

> §14.9.41.3 SR3: In the KEY phrase, relational-operator is a relational operator specified in the
> general-relation format of 8.8.4.2, Simple relation conditions, with the exception of the relational operators
> 'IS NOT EQUAL TO' or 'IS NOT='.

`--check 14.9.41.3 "relational-operator is a relational operator specified in the general-relation format of
8.8.4.2"` → **OK**; `--check 8.8.4.2 "Simple relation conditions"` → **OK**.

The §8.8.4.2 Format-1 diagram brackets NOT on SIX alternatives only — GREATER THAN, `>`, LESS THAN, `<`,
EQUAL TO, `=` — while GREATER THAN OR EQUAL TO, `>=`, LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO, `<=` and `<>` carry no NOT bracket
(its figure note: *Each of the three brace groups requires exactly one of its enclosed alternatives; the brackets
around NOT make it optional*). `NOT >=`, `NOT <=`, `NOT GREATER THAN OR EQUAL TO` and `NOT LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO`
are therefore outside the format, and SR3 forbids them in a START. Both statements must be rejected.

## What the compiler does

`comparisonOperator` in `CobolExpressions.g4` carries `IS? NOT GTEQUAL`, `IS? NOT LTEQUAL`,
`IS? NOT GREATER THAN? OR EQUAL TO?` and `IS? NOT LESS THAN? OR EQUAL TO?` as alternatives, and `BindStart`
applies exactly one filter to the result — it rejects only the mapped `!=`. `ConditionBinder.MapOperator` then
folds `NOT >=` to `<` and `NOT <=` to `>`, so the program runs with a positioning direction the source never
legally expressed.

**The EXCLUDED half of SR3 is implemented** — all three not-equal spellings (NOT EQUAL TO, NOT =, `<>`) draw
COBOLNET0862, verified in `out-b1/probe/sr3-ne` — so it is the POSITIVE MEMBERSHIP half that has no
implementation. That asymmetry is the tell: the rule was read as its exception clause only.

## The fix shape

The narrowing belongs in a START-specific operator screen (or a dedicated `startRelationalOperator` grammar
rule), never in `comparisonOperator`, which is shared with every condition in the language. The same phrase's
required-vs-optional defect — relational-operator made optional where the printed format requires it — is
**PB332**, and both land in `startKeyPhrase` together.

**Code sites:** `CobolExpressions.g4#comparisonOperator` · `KeyedIoBinder.cs#BindStart` ·
`ConditionBinder.cs#MapOperator`.

## Landed (wave 56, group PB318/PB330/PB333/PB357)

The MEMBERSHIP half of §14.9.41.3 SR3 now has its site: `ConditionBinder.InGeneralRelationFormat` (beside
`MapOperator`) answers "is this one of the §8.8.4.2.2 Format 1 alternatives" from the operator's TOKENS — NOT with
any OR-EQUAL spelling, and EQUAL THAN, are outside — and `KeyedIoBinder.BindStart` asks it of the written operator
before `MapOperator` folds the spelling away (COBOLNET0862, the existing START operand-rule code). ⚠ Citation
correction: the figure note this note quoted is in §8.8.4.2.2, not §8.8.4.2 (`--check 8.8.4.2` FAILS on it,
`--check 8.8.4.2.2` → OK). Re-probed (`w56a/probe/stk1.cob`, `stk2.cob`): BEFORE `P3-OK ST=00` for
`NOT LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO`; AFTER five spellings rejected and five legal ones accepted. Goldens
`conformance:85/pb333_start_general_relation_operators` (positions for NOT LESS THAN, NOT <, GREATER THAN,
GREATER THAN OR EQUAL TO, >=, EQUAL TO) and `conformance:negative/pb333-start-not-or-equal-operator` (85–2023).
The same NOT-on-OR-EQUAL spellings are still accepted in ordinary relation conditions (IF, EVALUATE, …) — a
lead for the registrar, not this rule.
