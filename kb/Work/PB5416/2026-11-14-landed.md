# PB5416 landed (wave 20262029 group C)

**Sites (landed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/LiteralScreen.cs` (`GateFormat`: the national and boolean literal gates, beside the hexadecimal one), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`VisitNonNumericLiteral`, `VisitFigurativeConstant` and `InStatement` deleted; the Step 14h.4b comment), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ExpressionBinder.cs` (`NationalLiteralOperand`, `BooleanLiteralOperand` comments), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ConditionBinder.cs` (comment), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/EditionGateArmDriftTests.cs` (`AdjudicatedDualArm`: the NationalData2002 and BooleanData2002 entries go, the literal arm no longer being in the version pass).

**Re-probe (this worktree's build, before the fix).** `VALUE OF ID IS N"AB"`, `B"1"`, `NX"0041"` and `BX"4"` compiled and ran `FS=00` at --std 85; `X"4142"` was COBOL0900. Sibling positions: a level-88 `VALUE N"A"` of a PIC X item, `01 X PIC X(2) VALUE N"AB"`, `VALUE B"1"`, `SPECIAL-NAMES. CLASS CL IS N"A"` and `ALPHABET AL IS N"A"` were each refused by a category rule alone (COBOL0898, COBOL5982, COBOL1671, COBOL1906), with no edition diagnostic.

**Mechanism and fix.** The national and boolean literal gates were the version pass's, scoped to a procedure-division statement on the reasoning that a data-division literal seeds an item whose category is gated; a literal in VALUE OF, a level-88 of another category or SPECIAL-NAMES seeds none. The literal's format is a property of its token, so the gates now sit in `LiteralScreen.GateFormat` beside the `X"…"` gate, position-blind (the same walk already screened every literal token). Every position above now draws COBOL0900. A national item's own national VALUE literal draws two reports, the item's and the literal's, each at its own position.

**Amendment taken.** The 19-digit arm is dropped (the held-row adjudication): 18 digits is the least a 1985 implementor must allow (`§1985:IV.4.2.2.2.2`), so a longer literal is not a 1985 violation. The compiler's own 18-digit literal limit at 85 is asked only of procedure-division literals (`ExpressionBinder` → `EditionContext.CheckDigitCapacity`); that is a consistency lead in the landing report, not this note.

**Goldens.** `pb5416-value-of-national-literal`, `pb5416-value-of-boolean-literal` (VII.3.9@1985), `pb5416-condition-national-literal` (_unassigned), each refused at 85 with COBOL0900. Row `1985-FMT-VII.3.9.2` re-verdicted PARTIAL → CONFORMS; seven rows that named the deleted `VisitNonNumericLiteral`, and four that named `VisitFigurativeConstant`, re-sited to `LiteralScreen.cs#GateFormat` (or retired) by record_verdicts batches.
