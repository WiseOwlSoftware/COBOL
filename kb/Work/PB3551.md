# PB3551 — the run.py budget undercounts the serial depth

## Lead (R1-1, low, not blocking at round 1; verbatim from the PB3108 report)

tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance/ConformanceSuiteRunnerDriftTests.cs:89 ForChildren(2*tests, RunPyJobs) models run.py's 2T processes as independently schedulable, but run.py's ThreadPoolExecutor schedules TESTS and runs each test's two processes (compile then run, or control then flag) in sequence on one worker, so the serial depth is 2*ceil(T/J), not ceil(2T/J); the budget undercounts by up to one unit, contradicting ObservationBudget's and PB3108's claim that each child gets the same unit an observed program gets.
