# PB4494 - the position of a bare-verb fault depends on which verb

**Observed (as reported by PB4283's finisher).** A paragraph whose sentence is a bare verb with no operand: `FREE.` draws
`cannot parse construct near 'FREE'` at the verb, `DISPLAY.` draws its fault at the period. Both programs are refused and the
message names a token of the sentence, so the harm is only a column that points at different places for two verbs of one
shape (misleads_diagnosis: low).

**Cause (as reported, not traced).** The report names ANTLR's failed prediction (`ErrorKind.NoViableAlternative`, whose message
`BuildMessage` builds) and nothing more; which token each verb's prediction dies on was not traced and its probe file was not
kept. The implementer reproduces the two sentences at `--std 2023` first, finds where the two positions diverge, and reports both
at the same token (the fault is the missing operand, which PB4283's bare-verb evidence already places at the period).

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`CobolErrorStrategy.BuildMessage`).
