# PB4494 - the position of a bare-verb fault depends on which verb

**Observed (as reported by PB4283's finisher).** A paragraph whose sentence is a bare verb with no operand: `FREE.` draws
`cannot parse construct near 'FREE'` at the verb, `DISPLAY.` draws its fault at the period. Both programs are refused and the
message names a token of the sentence, so the harm is only a column that points at different places for two verbs of one
shape (misleads_diagnosis: low).

**Cause (as reported, not traced).** The report names ANTLR's failed prediction (`ErrorKind.NoViableAlternative`, whose message
`BuildMessage` builds) and nothing more; which token each verb's prediction dies on was not traced and its probe file was not
kept. The implementer reproduces the two sentences at `--std 2023` first, finds where the two positions diverge, and reports both
at the same token (the fault is the missing operand, which PB4283's bare-verb evidence already places at the period).

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`CobolErrorStrategy.BuildMessage` is not where the position is
decided; the fix is in `ReportNoViableAlternative`, `FaultOfABareVerb`, `Sync`, `SkipsBareVerbSentences`, `ReservedWordUse`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs` (`IsIncompleteVerbSentence`, `HeaderReadingRefused`,
`PeriodOfAnIncompleteVerbSentence`, `procedureHeaderHere`, `ReservedProcedureName`),
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.cs`.

## Landed (wave 20261849, group G)

**Re-probe** (built tree, `--std 2023`, a paragraph holding the one sentence `VERB.` then `STOP RUN.`): `FREE.` drew `(5,12) cannot
parse construct near 'FREE'`; `DISPLAY.` `(5,19) unexpected '.'`; `READ.` `(5,16) unexpected '.'`; `MOVE.`, `ADD.`, `STOP.`
`cannot parse construct near '.'` at the period. Reproduced for the gated verbs.

**Cause (traced).** Not `BuildMessage`. A verb the edition reserves keeps a paragraph-header reading beside the statement's through
the `sentence*` loop's prediction: the statement dies at the period, the header (refused by `procedureHeaderHere`'s predicate,
PB4283) is the sole survivor, and ANTLR rewinds to the decision's start before it evaluates the predicate, so the
`NoViableAltException` stands at the verb (`IsPredictionFailedAt`). A verb reserved at every edition has no header reading, so the
prediction commits to the statement and the failure is the statement's own, at the period. **A second harm of the same cause that
the note did not name:** the exception is caught in the paragraph's rule, whose recovery resumes at what follows the paragraph, so
every sentence after the verb was discarded with its faults (`FREE.` then `MOVE.` then `MOVE.` reported one fault, not three).

**Fix.** One decision, `CobolParserCoreBase.IsIncompleteVerbSentence` (the bare verb that no procedure reference names; three
copies of `IsBareVerbSentence && !NamedAsAProcedureElsewhere` became one call). `CobolErrorStrategy.Sync` reports a gated bare-verb
sentence at its period and consumes it whole (`SkipsBareVerbSentences`, each of a run), ahead of the failing prediction, so the loop
predicts the next sentence; `ReportNoViableAlternative` names the period for a failure that still reaches it (`FaultOfABareVerb`).
Design: `DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md`, the bare-verb paragraph.

**Goldens.** `ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.ABareVerbSentence_IsAnIncompleteStatement_NeverNamedCobol0901` (rows for FREE,
VALIDATE, ALLOCATE, RAISE, INVOKE), `ARunOfBareVerbSentences_IsFaultedEachAtItsPeriod` (three sentences, three diagnostics, each at its
own period); the PB4283 refusal golden's `expect` now reads `near '.'`.
