**Decided 2026-11-10 (Mythos session, under the owner's delegation of that day: "What it's for and how it's built are your call").**

The question this note carried to the owner is now the codebase owner's to take, and the answer is: **the 1985 rule is KEPT.**

Two rules of adjacent editions are compared on the programs the OLDER edition admits (its general formats and syntax rules).

- A case the newer edition adds that no such program can write (INSPECT's BACKWARD, national or boolean operands, a new
  phrase) does not make the older RULE CHANGED. The difference is recorded once, on the older edition's general format or
  syntax rule that excludes the syntax: that record is CHANGED, and it is the one that owes the older compiler's refusal.
- A refusal the older edition makes of a program its own formats admit, which the newer edition lifts (or the reverse, or
  a refusal made an exception condition), IS a change of that rule.
- A `to` list names only rules that state the older requirement, whole or in part. A newer rule about syntax the older
  edition lacks is never a target, whatever subject it shares.

**Why this way and not the family ruling's (CHANGED for the BACKWARD five).** Each edition gate of the compiler needs
exactly one record to rest on. Under the other reading every general rule that mentions a phrase 1985 cannot write would
be CHANGED too, each owing its own 1985 witness for a behaviour identical to 2023's on every program 1985 admits: the
same refusal proven once per rule that mentions the phrase. The EDITION GATES audit's eight catches (START's reverse
operators, INSPECT BACKWARD, zero-length reference modification, word length 63, the underscore, PROCEDURE RETURNING, the
VALUE table format, REPLACE ALSO and LAST) are all formats or syntax rules, so they stay CHANGED under this decision.

**Where it is written.** `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md` section 10.2 (the paragraph after the
kinds table) and `scripts/spec/audit_batch.py` `PROMPTS` adjudicate and recheck.

**What it was applied to.** The band adjudication of kb/Work PB4996 (113 rules of 2023) used it verbatim; the 18 targets
it judged wrong were all 2023 rules about syntax or a case 1985 lacks (16 removed from a KEPT record; two records
re-decided CHANGED, which keep the rule as their same-subject successor). The INSPECT BACKWARD five of PB5092 are re-read under it
with the next recheck of the 1985 records; that re-read is PB4996's, not this note's.
