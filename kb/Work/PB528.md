# PB528

## Mechanism — picture-clause-p1
There is no COMPOSITION validator for a format-1 PICTURE. PictureAnalyzer.Analyze validates symbol MEMBERSHIP (the SR2 whitelist loop, :107-166) and then derives geometry from the symbol MULTISET - `expanded.Contains('S')` (:259), `expanded.Contains('V')` (:260), `expanded.Count(c => c is '9' or 'Z' or '*')` (:289), the leading/trailing-P classifier (:283-290), the floating-string detector (:276-281). Symbol POSITION is read only to COMPUTE those quantities, never to VALIDATE, and no site anywhere tests two symbols for mutual exclusion or for count. The result is that every one of 13.18.40.3's composition rules is unenforced at once: SR2's second obligation (an allowable COMBINATION per 13.18.40.6 Table 10), SR12's 'If the FOR phrase is not specified' sub-rules a) and b) - which ValidateEditing cannot even reach, because it returns at :407 for any picture with no EDITING phrase - SR16, SR17, SR18, SR19, SR20, SR21, SR22 and both sentences of SR24. Two arms of the SAME method already do this correctly and are the model to follow: AnalyzeFloatEdited (:640-695) walks Table 10 row E, and AnalyzeLocaleEdited (:712-780) walks Table 11 and checks SR33/SR34/SR35 inline. SR13 belongs here too: its second sentence ('the rules for the symbol period apply to the symbol comma') is currently VACUOUS because the period-specific rules it would transfer do not exist, so the composition validator must be written with the comma/period interchange built in (13.18.40.6's closing paragraph says the precedence rules themselves interchange) or SR13 regresses the moment the rules land. The shape that makes the next case automatic is a single table-and-rule-driven composition pass over the repeat-expanded string - Table 10 as data, the exclusion/ordering/count rules as declarative entries, the comma/period roles as a parameter - not eleven hand-written `if`s. This is the same mechanism the sibling part-2 adjudication reported for SR25/SR26/SR27/SR29 and part-2's SR4 (the 63-character limit, also unmeasured): ONE register note, not several.

### Expected, from the standard
ISO/IEC 1989:2023 13.18.40.3, all verified with scripts/spec/cite.py --check against clause 13.18.40.3: SR2 "Character-string-1 shall consist of an allowable combination of characters used as picture symbols" + "The allowable combinations of symbols for a PICTURE clause are specified in 13.18.40.6, Precedence rules"; SR12 (FOR phrase not specified) a) character-string-1 shall contain at least one of A N X Z 1 9 * or at least two occurrences of one of character-1, '+', '-', the currency symbol, and b) "Each of the symbols from the set 'CR', 'DB', 'E', 'S', 'V' '.' may appear only once in character-string-1"; SR13 "The rules for the symbol period apply to the symbol comma, and the rules for the symbol comma apply to the symbol period"; SR16 "The symbol 'P' may appear only as a continuous string of 'P's in the leftmost or rightmost digit positions in character-string-1"; SR17 "The symbol 'P' and the symbol '.' are mutually exclusive in character-string-1"; SR18 "The symbol 'S', if present, shall be the first symbol in character-string-1"; SR19 "the symbol 'V' shall either immediately precede the first symbol 'P' or immediately follow the last symbol 'P'"; SR20 "The symbol 'V' and the symbol '.' are mutually exclusive in character-string-1"; SR21 "The symbol 'Z' and the symbol '*' are mutually exclusive in character-string-1"; SR22 "Neither the symbol 'S' nor the symbol '*' shall be specified in character-string-1 when the BLANK WHEN ZERO clause is specified for the subject of the entry"; SR24 "For fixed insertion with editing sign control symbols, only one currency symbol and only one editing sign control symbol may be used in character-string-1. For extended editing sign control symbols, either one or two extended editing sign control symbols may be used in character-string-1". 13.18.40.6 supplies Table 10 and, for an EDITING phrase, "the precedence of 'es' ... has the same precedence as the 'cs' symbol in the column and row of non-floating insertion symbols", and "When the DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA clause is specified, the precedence rules for the symbols comma and period are interchanged". Every one of these is illegal source that shall draw a named diagnostic.

### Observed
All accepted, at all four editions, and several do not merely compile - they run and produce an image no rule defines, which is the reason this is worse than a missing check. `PIC 99.99.99` turns 123456 into `56.00.00`. `PIC ZZ**9` renders 7 as `  **7`, mixing space fill and asterisk fill in one item where 13.18.40.5 defines exactly one replacement character. `PIC +999+` and `PIC $999$` are silently REINTERPRETED: the :276-281 detector sees two occurrences of the same symbol and reads them as a floating string, so the compiler answers a question the source did not ask instead of rejecting it. `PIC 9P9` loses its P from BOTH the scale (:290) and the digit-position count (:298, where 13.18.40.4 GR14 row P requires it to be counted). `PIC S9(5) BLANK WHEN ZERO` silently DISCARDS the clause, because the promotion at :345 requires `!signed`. Legal complements were probed alongside each violation and all compile and render correctly (`PIC ZZ9`, `PIC 99PP`, `PIC PP99`, `PIC S999`, `PIC 99VPP`, `PIC ***9`, `PIC $999+`, `PIC $$$9.99`, `PIC +$$99`, `PIC 999$+`, `PIC ++`, `PIC LL EDITING "L" IS ":"`, `PIC L999F` with two FOR phrases), so the gap is one-sided: the compiler under-rejects and does not over-reject, and closing it carries a real regression risk that a drift test over the legal shapes should pin.
