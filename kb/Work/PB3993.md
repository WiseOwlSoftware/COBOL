# PB3993 — PB3989's keep for a COMP-5 / COMP-X / COMP-N receiver has no witness

**Sites:** `conformance-suite/cases/14.7.5-SIZE-ERROR-phrase-and-size-error/pb3869_not_size_error_alone.cob` (no
binary-capacity receiver row); `SizeErrorDispositionTests`' NOT-only theories (none for COMP-5 / COMP-X / COMP-N).

**The determination.** PB3989 (the owner, 2026-10-27): WiseOwl COBOL keeps every receiver of a [NOT] SIZE ERROR statement
that meets a size error, every usage alike, and does not copy GnuCOBOL 3.2's exemption of COMP-5, COMP-X and COMP-N
receivers of a NOT-only statement (`cobc/typeck.c` `build_store_option`). `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` DOC-A.1-70 states it.

**The gap.** No golden row and no test row has a binary-capacity receiver under a NOT-only statement. A change that follows
the exemption (exactly what the abandoned `worktree-wf_5cf68b99-205-1` did) makes `ADD 200 TO X5 NOT ON SIZE ERROR ...` (X5
`PIC 99 COMP-5 VALUE 99`) store the residue 043 instead of keeping 099, and nothing goes red; the CCVS85 NC programs carry
no COMP-5 receiver.

**Fix.** Add the NOT-only overflow rows for COMP-5, COMP-X and COMP-N (each the compiler accepts under the edition) beside a
DISPLAY row, to the golden (expected values derived from DOC-A.1-70: the receiver keeps its value) and to
`SizeErrorDispositionTests` (both lanes). The golden departs from GnuCOBOL on those rows by the owner's decision; record
that in the golden's header so the differential's OUTPUT_DIFFERS on it reads as expected.
