# PB4206 — `PERFORM RECEIVE.` is not named as a reserved-word misuse

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4:902` (`procedureName`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolControlFlow.g4:18` (`performStatement`: its out-of-line alternatives and the
inline alternative that reads `PERFORM RECEIVE` as the start of a RECEIVE statement).

**The rule.** `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 8.3.2.1 "Reserved words"` OK: §8.3.2.1 1) "Reserved words shall not be used as
user-defined words or system-names." A paragraph named RECEIVE is a reserved-word misuse, and every other operand slot
reports it as COBOL0901 (PB3248).

**The defect.** At --std 2023, `PERFORM RECEIVE.` draws `COBOL0001: unexpected '.'` and never COBOL0901. PB3248's sweep found
it ("`PERFORM RECEIVE.` draws one diagnostic, but it is the inline PERFORM's reading (`unexpected '.'` inside a RECEIVE
statement), never a 0901, because no error falls at the word; that is a different mechanism (the out-of-line procedure-name
slot has no reserved-word arm) and is reported as a lead, not fixed here"). RECEIVE begins a statement, so the inline PERFORM
alternative wins the parse, the error falls at the period after it, and the word is never reported.

**Repro** (--std 2023; originally `probe/rv3.cob` under E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/mythos/w-g7/w20261780c/, not durable):

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. PRV3.
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01 WS-A PIC X(8).
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       MAIN-P.
           PERFORM RECEIVE.
           STOP RUN.
```

**Fix direction.** Give the procedure-name slot the reserved-word arm the operand slots have (the `reservedGatedWord`
alternative that sits beside `cobolWord` just above `procedureName` in CobolParserCore.g4), or re-code the error at the period to
the COBOL0901 naming RECEIVE. Check SEND and VALIDATE (2002) and `PERFORM ... THRU RECEIVE` the same way, and add the rows to
`ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.AReservedVerbWrittenAsAnOperand_DrawsOneDiagnostic`. PB4132 (a gated keyword in
statement position) is the neighbouring open note.
