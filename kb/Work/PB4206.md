# PB4206 — `PERFORM RECEIVE.` is not named as a reserved-word misuse

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4:912` (`procedureName`; `sectionName` :873 and
`paragraphName` :890 lost their now-redundant `reservedGatedWord` alternative),
`scripts/gen-cobol-words.ps1:235` (the `reservedGatedWord` rule's emitted comment) and its output
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolWords.g4:88` (`reservedGatedWord`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs:403` (`gatedDeclaration`, `FreeGatedDeclarations` :393, doc),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/ReservationGateRewriter.cs:51` (`ReservationGateRewriter`, doc),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs:2784` (`VisitReservedGatedWord`, doc),
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.cs:478`
(`AReservedVerbWrittenAsAProcedureName_IsNamedByCobol0901`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md` (the
token-level reservation gate). As filed:
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolControlFlow.g4:18` (`performStatement`: its out-of-line alternatives and the
inline alternative that reads `PERFORM RECEIVE` as the start of a RECEIVE statement).

**The rule.** `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 8.3.2.1 "Reserved words"` OK: §8.3.2.1 1) "Reserved words shall not be used as
user-defined words or system-names." A paragraph named RECEIVE is a reserved-word misuse, and every other operand slot
reports it as COBOL0901 (PB3248).

**The defect.** At --std 2023, `PERFORM RECEIVE.` draws `COBOL0001: unexpected '.'` and never COBOL0901. PB3248's sweep found
it ("`PERFORM RECEIVE.` draws one diagnostic, but it is the inline PERFORM's reading (`unexpected '.'` inside a RECEIVE
statement), never a 0901, because no error falls at the word; that is a different mechanism (the out-of-line procedure-name
slot has no reserved-word arm) and is reported as a lead, not fixed here"). RECEIVE begins a statement, so the inline PERFORM
alternative wins the parse, the error falls at the period after it, and the word is never reported.

**Repro** (--std 2023; originally `probe/rv3.cob` under E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/mythos/w-g7/w20261780c/, not durable):

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. PRV3.
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01 WS-A PIC X(8).
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       MAIN-P.
           PERFORM RECEIVE.
           STOP RUN.
```

**Fix direction.** Give the procedure-name slot the reserved-word arm the operand slots have (the `reservedGatedWord`
alternative that sits beside `cobolWord` just above `procedureName` in CobolParserCore.g4), or re-code the error at the period to
the COBOL0901 naming RECEIVE. Check SEND and VALIDATE (2002) and `PERFORM ... THRU RECEIVE` the same way, and add the rows to
`ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.AReservedVerbWrittenAsAnOperand_DrawsOneDiagnostic`. PB4132 (a gated keyword in
statement position) is the neighbouring open note.

## Landed (wave 20261786 A, 2026-10-31)

**Re-probe** (build of 84cdb410f, --std 2023): `PERFORM RECEIVE.` -> `COBOL0001: unexpected '.'`, as filed. The sweep showed
the mechanism is wider than PERFORM's simple form: `PERFORM SEND.`, `PERFORM VALIDATE.`, `PERFORM RECEIVE 3 TIMES.`,
`PERFORM RECEIVE UNTIL ...`, `PERFORM RECEIVE VARYING ...`, `PERFORM RECEIVE THRU P1.`, `PERFORM RECEIVE OF S1.`, and GO TO
(`GO TO RECEIVE.`, `GO RECEIVE.`, `GO TO SEND.`, `GO TO P1 RECEIVE DEPENDING ON N.`) each drew a COBOL0001 (at the period, at
THRU / UNTIL / VARYING / OF, or at DEPENDING) and never named the word. The slots where nothing else can read the word
already drew COBOL0901: `PERFORM P1 THRU RECEIVE`, `ALTER RECEIVE TO P1`, `ALTER P1 TO PROCEED TO RECEIVE`, `RESUME AT
RECEIVE`, `SORT ... INPUT PROCEDURE IS RECEIVE`.

**Mechanism.** The procedure-name REFERENCE slot had no arm for a reservation-gated word (PB693 put `reservedGatedWord` on
the definition wrappers only), so where another reading of the word exists (RECEIVE, SEND and VALIDATE begin statements)
the parse took it: the inline PERFORM for PERFORM, the 1985 target-less GO TO followed by a statement for GO TO. The error
then fell after the word, where the listener's §8.9 re-coding cannot see it.

**Fix.** `procedureName` itself takes `reservedGatedWord` (head and qualifier), the one rule every procedure-name slot
shares, so the definition wrappers `sectionName` / `paragraphName` drop their copy. Where §8.9 reserves the word the parse
now succeeds and `VersionConformancePass.VisitReservedGatedWord` names it (COBOL0901); where §8.9 leaves it free
(RECEIVE and SEND at 2014, GOBACK and VALIDATE at 85) the match is the user-word reading the gate retypes the word for, as
the second witness (a syntax error at the word) already did. The full-context prediction keeps every reading that parses:
the inline PERFORM is the earlier alternative (`PERFORM GOBACK END-PERFORM` and `PERFORM VALIDATE W END-PERFORM` still
bind as inline PERFORMs), and the only procedure-name lists (GO TO DEPENDING's, ALTER's entries) end at DEPENDING / TO, so
neither can absorb the next statement's verb (the PB693 risk). With no procedure named by the word, §14.9.28.3 SR12 /
§8.4.2.1 are broken too and the binder's COBOL1639 stands beside the COBOL0901, as the project posture for two true rules
(VersionConformancePass ParseArm: "both are true"); with the procedure defined, exactly one diagnostic.

**Witnesses.** `ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.AReservedVerbWrittenAsAProcedureName_IsNamedByCobol0901` (13 rows:
every PERFORM format, both THRU ends, both GO TO formats, RECEIVE/SEND at 85 and 2023, VALIDATE at 2002, each with the
procedure defined and not); goldens `cases/8.3.2-COBOL-words/pb4206-perform-reserved-verb-as-procedure-name` and
`pb4206-go-to-reserved-verb-as-procedure-name` (refused at 85/2002/2023, COBOL0901), and
`pb4206_free_verbs_as_procedure_names` (accepted at 2014, where RECEIVE and SEND are user words).

**For the next implementer.**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#procedureName` owns "a reserved word in a procedure-name slot": its
  `reservedGatedWord` arm feeds `VersionConformancePass.VisitReservedGatedWord` (COBOL0901) and, at a free edition, the
  gate's retype. A new procedure-name slot needs nothing; do not re-add `reservedGatedWord` to `sectionName`/`paragraphName`.
- `scripts/gen-cobol-words.ps1` writes the `reservedGatedWord` rule and its comment; edit the generator, never CobolWords.g4.
- Trap: the binder's refusal ledger (`EditionContext.NoteRefusal`, COBOL2362) runs BEFORE the edition pass, so a binder arm
  cannot stay silent on the strength of a COBOL0901 the edition pass will report later.
