> Comment lines and blank lines may be interspersed among lines containing the parts of a literal.

`OK  §6.3.5 2)  (Continuation of lines)`

> In the case of continuation with a fixed continuation indicator, any spaces at the end of the fixed-form continued line are part of the literal.

`OK  §6.3.5 2)  (Continuation of lines)`

> 2) If the line is a comment line or a blank line, that line is logically discarded.

`OK  §6.5 2)  (Logical conversion)`

> 3) If the line contains an inline comment, the inline comment is replaced by spaces and processing of that line continues.

`OK  §6.5 3)  (Logical conversion)`

`OK  §6.5 6) a)  (Logical conversion)`


## Finding — `fixed-form-reference-format` (crashes; rows `SR-6.3.5-1`, `SR-6.3.5-2`)

**Mechanism.** F1 - fixed-form literal continuation joins onto the immediately preceding PHYSICAL output line and resets the literal state on every comment or blank line, so the §6.3.5 permission to intersperse comment lines and blank lines among the parts of a literal is broken: a comment line in between is rejected, and a blank line in between crashes the compiler

**Repro.** Fixed form, numeric sequence area. `000100 01 X PIC X(80) VALUE "ABCDE` (padded with spaces to col 72), then an EMPTY line, then `000100-    "FGH".`, then DISPLAY "[" X "]". Probe ffp2 (also ffp2b, where the blank line is 72 spaces). Probe ffp1: the blank line replaced by `000100* a comment`.

**Expected from the spec.** §6.3.5: 'the next line that is not a comment line or a blank line is the continuation line' and 'Comment lines and blank lines may be interspersed among lines containing the parts of a literal.' The program compiles and prints [ABCDE, then the spaces from column 13 through column 72 of the continued line, then FGH, then spaces up to 80].

**Observed / reasoning.** ffp2/ffp2b: Unhandled exception System.InvalidOperationException 'ConvertFixedToFree origin tracking: 8 origin(s) for 9 output line(s) (kb/Work PB82)', at every edition. ffp1: COBOL0001 'cannot parse construct near '"'' at --std 85, 2002 and 2023. In ConvertFixedToFree, the '*'/'/' arm and both blank arms set inLiteral=false. The '-' arm and HandleContinuation then strip only trailing newlines from the StringBuilder and append to whatever output line is last: the `*> comment` line, or, for a blank line, the head line with its trailing literal spaces trimmed because inLiteral is now false. The origin bookkeeping pops one origin per stripped newline only while nlSeen > 0, so two stripped newlines desynchronize it and trip the PB82 invariant. Fix shape: the continued-literal state (the open line and its quote) must survive comment and blank lines, and the join must target the last non-comment, non-blank logical line.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ConvertFixedToFree (case '*' or '/' at l.555-559, blank arms at l.502-508 and l.626-634, the '-' arm at l.596-624); #HandleContinuation l.758-773

## Finding — `reference-format` (rejects-legal-source; rows `GR-6.5-2`, `GR-6.5-6`, `GR-6.1-2`)

**Mechanism.** Fixed-form continuation joins onto the LAST OUTPUT line, even when that line is an interspersed comment or blank line, whose processing has also reset the literal state; so the continuation text is spliced into a '*>' comment (or a blank), not into the continued literal

**Repro.** E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2b\out\probe\reference-format\rf3.cob: `000100 01 X PIC X(80) VALUE \"ABCDEFGHH…H` (literal filled to col 72), `000100* a comment`, `000100-    \"XYZ\".` -> COBOL0001 cannot parse construct near '\"' + COBOL0307. rf4.cob with an empty line in place of the comment -> COBOL0301 unexpected '\"'. Control rf5.cob (nothing interspersed) -> correct.

**Expected from the spec.** §6.3.5: 'Comment lines and blank lines may be interspersed among lines containing the parts of a literal' and 'the next line that is not a comment line or a blank line is the continuation line'; §6.5 2) discards them; §6.5 6) a) appends after the quote. X = 'ABCDEFG' + 'H' x 37 + 'XYZ' padded to 80.

**Observed / reasoning.** ConvertFixedToFree emits '*> text' / '' for the comment/blank line and sets inLiteral=false (lines 505-506, 557-558); HandleContinuation then strips the newline of THAT line and appends — in the !inLiteral branch, keeping the resume quote — so the literal on the real continued line stays unterminated.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ConvertFixedToFree (the '*'/'/' and short/blank-line arms); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#HandleContinuation

## Finding — `reference-format` (wrong-answer; rows `GR-6.5-6`)

**Mechanism.** A fixed-form continued literal on a physically short record is not padded to margin R: the program-text area is taken as the record's actual length rather than columns 8-72, so the spaces between the record end and margin R are lost from the literal

**Repro.** E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2b\out\probe\reference-format\rf6.cob: `000100 01 X PIC X(80) VALUE \"AB` (record ends at col 29), `000100-    \"CD\".`, DISPLAY \"[\" X \"]\" -> [ABCD<76 spaces>].

**Expected from the spec.** §6.3.5: 'any spaces at the end of the fixed-form continued line are part of the literal'; §6.3.1 + DOC-A.1-158: the program-text area ends at col 72. X = 'AB' + 43 spaces (cols 30-72) + 'CD'.

**Observed / reasoning.** sourceArea = line[7..] truncated to 65 but never padded. Whether a short text record has positions up to margin R is the implementor's 'meaning of lines and character positions' (§6.1 1) c), A.1 item 157), which CONFORMANCE.md does not document; GnuCOBOL and IBM pad to the margin, so under the owner's latitude precedence the padding is the expected result, and the choice must be documented as DOC-A.1-157 either way.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ConvertFixedToFree (sourceArea extraction, lines 510-513); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#HandleContinuation

## Row `GR-6.5-3` — DIVERGES (editions 2002/2014/2023 (the floating comment indicator is a 2002 introduction — absent from 2023 Annex E, present at 2014))

'If the line contains an inline comment, the inline comment is replaced by spaces and processing of that line continues.' (--check OK §6.5 3)). Fixed form: StripInlineComment truncates the program-text area at a '*>' outside any literal (doubled quotes tracked) BEFORE the cross-line literal scan, so a quote inside the comment cannot disturb continuation; free form: the lexer's COMMENT_START '*>' -> COMMENT_MODE skips to end of line, and a '*>' inside a literal is inside STRINGLIT by maximal munch. Probe rf11.cob: `01 X PIC X(3) VALUE "*>A". *> inline "quote` -> [*>A] (literal kept, comment with a stray quote dropped). Truncation vs replacement-by-spaces is equivalent for the tokens. No spec-derived golden isolates the rule. || REFUTED: DIVERGES — Step 3 applies to EVERY line, and §6.3.7.3 permits an inline comment on any fixed-form line (the only exception is a line carrying a floating literal continuation indicator). ReferenceFormatProcessor.ConvertFixedToFree calls StripInlineComment only in the default (normal-line) arm; the col-7 '-' arm hands the raw sourceArea to HandleContinuation, which appends it with the '*>' text intact. When that continuation line is itself continued, the next continuation's content is appended AFTER the unstripped comment on the same logical line and the lexer's COMMENT_MODE swallows it. A legal program is rejected.

## What the fixer owes

Restructure ConvertFixedToFree around the §6.5 model instead of the physical output buffer: comment and blank lines are DISCARDED (rule 2) without touching the literal state or becoming the join target, the join target is the latest LOGICAL line, the program-text area of a continued line is columns 8–72 padded to margin R whatever the record's physical length (§6.3.5's trailing-spaces sentence; A.1 item 157 names the line meaning — PB1496), and rule 3 strips an inline comment on EVERY line including a continuation line before the next continuation is appended. Then the blank-line crash, the comment-line rejection and the lost-trailing-spaces wrong answer are one fix. Goldens: literal continued across a `*` comment line, across an empty line and a 72-space line, a short-record continued literal (DISPLAY brackets the value), a continuation line carrying `*>` that is itself continued. Sweep: CopyProcessor's copybook path (ConvertFixedToFreeMapped) must share the same converter — PB1067 owns its directive half.
