# PB4572 - a spelling test of a reserved word ignores its COBOL-WORDS synonym

**Mechanism (one, two witnesses).** Code that decides by the SPELLING of a reserved word does not see a synonym made by `>>COBOL-WORDS EQUATE`. §7.3.10.4 2)
(`cite.py --check` OK): the literal-2 word "shall be treated as a synonym for the COBOL word that is the content of literal-1, and may be used in any syntax requiring
the use of the reserved word".

**Witness 1 (R1-2, on main before #1103).** `DECIMAL-POINT IS CMA` after `>>COBOL-WORDS EQUATE "COMMA" WITH "CMA"` is refused with COBOL0894.
Site `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.cs#SwitchBindDecimalPoint` (about line 127), which compares the written word to COMMA by spelling.

**Witness 2 (R1-1, introduced by #1103).** `>>COBOL-WORDS EQUATE "EXCEPTION-OBJECT" WITH "EXO"` then `IF EXO = NULL` draws a false COBOL0901 beside the existing COBOL1639.
Site `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (about line 2921, the funnel branch that exempts EXCEPTION-OBJECT through
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.cs#OoIsExceptionObject`, a spelling test). Probe: `q10.cob` in the directory above.

**Fix shape.** Such tests compare the canonical word (the synonym map resolves it), and a sweep finds every spelling test of a reserved word in the compiler.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.cs` (`SwitchBindDecimalPoint`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.Currency.cs`
(`SwitchBindCurrency`, the PICTURE SYMBOL word), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.cs` (`OoIsExceptionObject`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs` (`IsExceptionObjectRegister`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs`
(`ParseArm.VisitCobolWord`, the EXCEPTION-OBJECT exemption), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-cobol-words-directive.md` (§2.1 "Where the name resolution is called");
witnesses `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CobolWordsReachDriftTests.cs`, `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CobolWordsSpellingSweepDriftTests.cs`, `conformance-suite/cases/7.3.10-COBOL-WORDS/pb4572*`,
`conformance-suite/wiseowl/diagnostics/pb4572*`.

**Landed (wave 20261859 B).** Both witnesses reproduced on 9f5f0a1ae and a third, the `PICTURE SYMBOL` word of the CURRENCY clause
(`EQUATE "SYMBOL" WITH "SYM"`, COBOL0892), fell to the same sweep. The fix is the map: each site asks `CobolWordsMap.Is(written, word)` (the
ONE reading of GR2-GR4), and `OoIsExceptionObject` takes the map. `IF EXO = NULL` drew COBOL1639 as well as COBOL0901 (the resolver's
`IsExceptionObjectRegister` was the second spelling test), and an UNDEFINE of COMMA had been ACCEPTED by the clause (the opposite direction of
the same defect); it is refused now, naming the directive. The drift test `CobolWordsSpellingSweepDriftTests.NoCompilerSourceComparesAnUnlexedDirectiveWord_ByItsSpelling` keeps the next
case automatic. Sweep: every quoted §8.9/§8.10 word the lexer has no token for, across Compiler, Frontend and Editions: the rest ask the map already
(`KeywordWordOf`, `SetBinder`, `CodeSetNameOf`, `Locales`), compare a canonical value (`sig.Name`, a phrase through `Resolve`), or are not words the
directive can name at 2023 (`OptionsBinder`'s `COBOL` is reserved at 1985 only, SR3 of 7.3.10.3 admits no such literal-1).
