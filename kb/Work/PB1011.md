# PB1011 — OPEN

## The measurement

Histogram by code: 0900 ×57, 0806 ×10, 1699 ×7, 0821 ×6, 1624 ×4, 0849 ×4, 0899 ×3, 2144/1727/1710/1582/0902 ×2, and
twelve singletons. PB975 owns ONE producer (OoClassTable pass 1); the rest come from data-division and edition passes
that report without an `Edition.At(context)` scope.

## What the fixer owes
Make a position mandatory at the sink: a report issued outside any positioned scope is a compiler bug (a drift test that
greps the negative-golden corpus for position-less diagnostics and fails above zero — make it fail once first), then
give each producer its declaration's context. The .err goldens change with it.
