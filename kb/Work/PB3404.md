# PB3404 — an UNANSWERED predecessor's findings are answered twice, and the successor still waits

**Sites:** `scripts/prune_worktrees.py` (`carrying_findings`, `review_answers`, `findings_answered_by`,
`answered_by_carrier`), `scripts/orchestrator/review_findings.py` (`reading`, `capped`),
`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`plan`, `main`), `scripts/orchestrator/test_rolling_wave.py`,
`scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py`, and `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/wf_rolling_wave.js`
(`predecessorEnd`, `successorNote`, `predecessorScope`, case `unanswered`).

Filed by the operator from PB3381's branch review (round 4 lead, medium, not blocking; `w1125a-PB3381-report.md`,
"Review findings"), which PB3381 left open ("The round-4 leads stay leads", `kb/Work/PB3381.md`).

**The two owners (on main at 49e1c6b79).**
- `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/wf_rolling_wave.js` `predecessorScope`, case `unanswered`: a same-file
  predecessor whose review ended FINDINGS and whose review finisher came back without answering them. The successor's
  reviewer is told to review the predecessor's commits as part of the successor, read `review_findings.py show <file>`,
  and "record again here each of those findings that still holds on this head". The successor's own review finisher then
  fixes them on the successor's branch.
- `scripts/prune_worktrees.py` `carrying_findings`: a WAITING TO LAND branch whose tip contains a head at which ANOTHER
  note's last verdict is FINDINGS with blocking findings reads IN FLIGHT and waits until "a later round of that review
  records CLEAN or that head is on main". Its docstring gives the findings to "the predecessor's next-wave finisher
  (plan_wave.py plans it)".

So in the `unanswered` case the successor fixes the findings, then waits for the predecessor's own next-wave finisher to
fix them again on the predecessor's branch and record CLEAN there, or for that head to reach main. Two finishers do one
fix, and finished work waits on the slower one.

**The fix.** Give each FINDINGS case one owner. One shape: in the `unanswered` case the successor owns them. Its review
records them in the successor's own findings file, its finisher answers them, and `carrying_findings` releases a
successor whose own findings file covers the predecessor's head with a later CLEAN verdict. The planner then plans no
second finisher for the predecessor. The other shape: the predecessor owns them, and the wave stops telling the
successor's reviewer to record them again. Either way, `test_rolling_wave.py` and the prune self-test pin one owner
and no hold once that owner records CLEAN.

## Landed (2026-10-25, wave 20261107 group E)

**Re-probe.** Both owners were still live on this tree. The `unanswered` case of `predecessorEnd` told the successor's
reviewer to record the predecessor's findings again (so the successor's finisher fixed them), while
`carrying_findings` held the successor on the predecessor's findings file until that file recorded CLEAN or the
predecessor's head reached main: the self-test's `rsucc` (CLEAN over `rpred`'s uncapped round-1 FINDINGS) read IN
FLIGHT, waiting on `rpred`. That wait could not end by itself: the next wave's finisher of `rpred` writes its review
into a NEW wave's file, so the old file never records CLEAN, and `rpred`'s head reaches main only through the
successor that was waiting.

**The shape: the successor owns an UNCAPPED verdict, the predecessor a CAPPED one, and the file says which.**
`review_findings.capped(verdict)` is the one definition (the verdict blocked on `BLOCKING_AT_CAP`, the severities
the wave passes for exactly the round after the last review finisher); `last_verdict` carries it as `capped`, and the
wave's `predecessorEnd` reads it instead of its own `round > REVIEW_FIX_ROUNDS` test. A capped verdict keeps both
rules as they were (no finisher followed it; the predecessor's next-wave finisher answers it; the successor is held
until then). For an uncapped one, `prune_worktrees.review_answers` / `findings_answered_by` release a successor whose
own review is CLEAN over a head that contains the verdict's head (the wave's round 1 reviews the whole branch, the
predecessor's commits as its own), and the planner reads the same rule through `answered_by_carrier` (a new `answered`
argument of `plan_wave.plan`, built in `main`): a carried FINDINGS branch whose carrier's CLEAN review answers it waits
naming the carrier instead of getting a second finisher. A torn file answers nothing. The successor's note
(`successorNote`) now says the findings are its own. Self-test arms: `rsucc` WAITING TO LAND and `review_answers` at
each edge (capped, torn, an unrelated head, no head, a row without a CLEAN review), the tpred cut after an UNCAPPED
round 2 released and after a CAPPED one held, `answered_by_carrier` on the real files, `capped` in
`review_findings.py`'s self-test, `test_rolling_wave.py` (the file's verdict capped / not, the note's wording),
`test_plan_wave.py` (the planner plans no second finisher). `DESIGN-test-build-ci.md`, `DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md`
and the workstream `SKILL.md` carry the rule.
