# PB3181 — the survey's report admission rejects two finished shapes

Source: enqueue unit 2026-10-15 (note-ready text, `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\enqueue-unit\PB3181-note-ready.md`).

carried-train form. Neither owns the two shapes below, so this note files them and cross-links.

## The two shapes

1. **A DISCHARGED report whose branch carries commits** (the note's retirement and its DEVLOG entry) is finished work
   by the wave's own rule (`wf_rolling_wave.js` `hasWork()`, line 184:
   `r.status === 'DONE' || (r.status === 'DISCHARGED' && r.head && r.head !== r.base)`), and the wave reviews it. The
   survey admits only DONE, so the reviewed branch can never read WAITING TO LAND and the lane never lands it.
   Repro: `worktree-wf_15ec6338-3a0-11` (PB934 retired as superseded by PB3102), report `w1078h-PB934-report.md`
   `Status: DISCHARGED`, `**Review:** CLEAN w1078h-review-r1 0b54dba684de` = its tip: `prune_worktrees.py --brief`
   lists it DECISION. Two arms of one dispatch (memory `feedback_two_arm_dispatch`): one predicate "a finished branch"
   must be defined once, and the JS and Python arms pinned by a drift test.

2. **A DONE report whose branch gained only enqueue.py's commits after it** (the origin/main merge and the
   `DEVLOG NNNN: stamp the entry` commit, made before `landing_check.py` refuses with exit 3) fails the age test,
   because the stamp commit is 11 to 15 min newer than the report: `worktree-wf_15ec6338-3a0-19` (last commit 16:31:57
   PDT, report 16:20:25), `worktree-wf_15ec6338-3a0-4` (17:23:17 vs 17:10:46), `worktree-wf_5579bfb3-423-5` (18:42:31
   vs 18:27:15), all 2026-10-14. All three are Review CLEAN with only mechanical commits since
   (`row.review_holds`, line 581, already computes exactly that), and the landing lane holds them RETRY-WHEN-FREE
   (exit 3), yet the survey lists them DECISION. Proposed fix: measure the report's freshness against the newest
   NON-mechanical commit (the `unreviewed_since()` classification `review_holds` uses), never the raw tip.

## Conflict to adjudicate before implementing shape 2

PB3125 (open; the same age test, the oracle-refused / STOPPED form, exit 7) records a REJECTED shape: "measuring the
report's age against the last non-mechanical commit alone ... would make the row WAITING TO LAND, and the lane would
re-run a landing that the oracle refuses until a finisher re-records the baseline (PB3107)". Shape 2 here is the exit-3
(RETRY-WHEN-FREE) form, where re-running is the intended behaviour, so the two may take different arms, but the
implementer must take both notes together and decide one rule (state per lane-attempt outcome) rather than two
patches. That is a design judgment (NEEDS-OPUS for the fix shape; the filing above is what the reports state).
