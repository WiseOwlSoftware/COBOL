# PB5736 - a semicolon after the compile-time expression of >>IF (and after >>EVALUATE's and >>WHEN's operand) vanishes in the cce fragment lexer, so '>>IF 1 = 1;' compiles while '>>IF 1 = 1,' and '>>ELSE;' / '>>END-IF;' are refused: one of two interchangeable separators is judged wrongly (2002 and 2023)

**Sites:**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/ConditionalCompilationProcessor.cs#ParseCceFragment` (hands the operand text to the main lexer, whose `SEMICOLON : ';' -> channel(HIDDEN)` drops a trailing semicolon; a comma reaches the fragment parse and is refused COBOL1619)

**Mechanism.** The directive line checks for `>>ELSE` and `>>END-IF` refuse any text after the directive (COBOL1911), a
semicolon included; the operand of `>>IF`, `>>EVALUATE` and `>>WHEN` is parsed as a fragment by the main lexer, which hides
a semicolon. A separator comma or semicolon is a text-word (§2002:7.1.1.4) and the two are interchangeable (§2002:5.1.7).
Under the reading the compiler's own `>>ELSE;` refusal takes (a trailing separator is text after the directive, so text-1 on
the IF line, which SR2 forbids) the semicolon form is under-rejected; under the other (it counts as a space) the comma's
refusal rejects legal source. Either way the two separators are judged differently, which no reading allows; the fix decides
the reading once (citing §2002:7.2.2 SR3 and §2023:7.3.3 SR3) and applies it to every directive operand.

**Inventory.** `2002-SR-7.2.13.2-2` (a KEPT record into `SR-7.3.16.3-2`; it reads GAP through its chain once the 2023 row is
non-conforming), `SR-7.3.16.3-2` (>>IF text-1) and `SR-7.3.13.3-4` (>>WHEN's text-1), both re-recorded PARTIAL naming this note by
the registrar landing records-sample-pb5692. `>>EVALUATE 1;` (SR-7.3.13.3-2, its operand "specified entirely on that line") is
the same mechanism; its row was not re-recorded here because whether a trailing separator is part of "operand-1" is the reading
the fix decides.

**Rules (each citation checked with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`, verdict OK).**
- `§2002:7.2.13.2 SR2` and `§2023:7.3.16.3 SR2`: "Text-1 shall begin on a new line"
- `§2002:7.2.2 SR3` and `§2023:7.3.3 SR3`: "may be followed only by space characters and an optional inline comment"
- `§2002:7.1.1.4`: "A text-word is a character-string in source text or in library text that constitutes an element processed by text manipulation"
- `§2002:5.1.7`: "In the compilation group, these separators are interchangeable"
