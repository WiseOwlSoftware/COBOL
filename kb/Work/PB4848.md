# PB4848 - the raster measurer, calibrated on real 1985 pages

**Why.** `scripts/spec/figure_raster.py` (PB4817) has only been tested on PLANTED images. Its constants were set without a real
1985 page. The owner lifted the hold on running tools against `specs-private/ANSI_COBOL_1985.pdf` on 2026-11-06 ("yes, go ahead
with all the steps"). PB4812's "1985 is a SCAN" section requires a calibration against about a dozen pages checked by eye before
the measurer regenerates any figure (PB4849).

**What to do.**
- Choose about a dozen general-format pages that together hold every feature the measurer reads: one-line and multi-line
  brackets, braces with three and with five or more lines, nested brackets inside braces, choice-indicator bars, ellipses,
  underlined words with descenders (g, p, y, q), and words underlined in part. Take them from different modules (Nucleus,
  Sequential I-O, Relative, Indexed, Sort-Merge, Report Writer, Communication, Debug) and from both amendments. PDF page 196
  (the Environment Division general format) is one.
- For each page, run the measurer and render the page with `scripts/render-spec-page.py --edition 1985`. Compare every stroke
  the measurer reports with the image, and record the result in a calibration table in the note body or in a fixture beside
  `scripts/spec/test_figure_raster.py`.
- Settle the two open leads from PB4817's review:
  - `BRACE_GLYPH_H` (13 pt) and the `brace_pieces` glyph y positions are assumed values. Measure them.
  - `find_rules` misses an underline fused to a descender. Fix it so the underline is found, and add a planted case.
  - `BRACE_STRAIGHT`, `BRACE_ALIGNED` and `BRACE_POINT` are unverified on a real scan. Verify each or correct it.
- Turn the calibration into self-test cases. Crop the measured regions to small planted images with the stamp margin
  excluded, so no per-copy stamp text reaches the repository (PB4812's standing requirement). Assert each crop's expected
  strokes in the self-test.

**Boxes are normative (found 2026-11-06 by the reconciliation pass, PB4850).** The 1985 standard draws a BOX around every
specification unique to level 2 ("the high level") of a module. PDF page 23: "specifications unique to level 2 of the modules are
enclosed in boxes". Page 147: "specifications unique to the high level are enclosed in boxes". The amendments edit boxes too:
page 848 reads "delete the box around the first occurrence of [INDEXED BY ...]". The measurer must detect a box as a rectangle
of four joined rules and report which words it encloses. Include boxed formats among the calibration pages; PDF pages 252, 389,
396, 426 and 493 hold boxes.

**Done when** every chosen page's strokes match the image, the constants carry their measured values with the page that set
each, and the self-test holds the real-page crops.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/figure_raster.py` (`_classify_curly`, `_stack_pieces`, `find_braces`, `_straight`, `_side`, `find_boxes`, `split_fused_rules`, `is_hyphen`, `find_rules`, `find_stems`, `_foot_side`, `measure_image`, `brace_pieces`, `overlay`, `RasterPage`, `BRACE_GLYPH_H`, `BRACE_POINT`, `BRACE_SHOULDER`, `BRACE_MID`, `BRACE_SYMMETRY`, `BRACE_HOOK`, `BRACKET_STRAIGHT`, `BOX_SIDE`, `BOX_TILT`, `FUSED_MIN_RUN`, `RULE_MEAN_THICK`, `HYPHEN_MAX_W`), `scripts/spec/test_figure_raster.py` (whole: a module-level self-test; the real-page cases and the planted cases for each mechanism are added), `scripts/spec/figure_raster_pages/` (new: twelve crops and `calibration.json`).

**Landed (2026-11-06, wave 20261886, owner: "yes, go ahead with all the steps").** The measurer was run on the licensed 1985 scan for the first time and
was wrong in six ways, each fixed at its root and each pinned by a planted case AND by the real crops (`test_figure_raster.py` measures the twelve crops
below and asserts what the eye counted):

| mechanism | what the real pages showed | fix |
|---|---|---|
| braces | printed braces have NO straight halves (smooth S, 0.9 to 1.5 pt of wander); `BRACE_STRAIGHT` and `BRACE_ALIGNED` rejected every brace taller than two lines | the point is a departure of the middle of the edge from its shoulders (`BRACE_POINT` 1.0; 2.5 pt at 22 pt tall), the side is the hooks' direction on the centre line, the halves mirror within `BRACE_SYMMETRY` 1.6; both straightness constants deleted |
| stacked braces | a tall brace is overstruck from two glyphs with a 1.8 pt gap (page 217) | `_stack_pieces` joins pieces that are not braces alone; a brace alone stays one (page 844's two small braces one above the other are two) |
| brackets vs braces | a five-line brace has a straight middle over 75 % of its height and hooks that read as feet; a 52 to 72 pt bracket LEANS 1.8 pt (page 196) so its stem began 4.7 pt late and its feet were lost | stems take the whole run's extent, feet are anchored on the stroke's own edge per row, and `_straight` (a line fit that may lean, `BRACKET_STRAIGHT` 1.1) decides bracket against brace |
| underlines | the fill test (0.7) refused ragged tilted rules (box fill 0.42 to 0.76); hyphens (4.5 to 5.0 pt bars) were read as underlines and removed from their words; an underline fused to its word was never found | `RULE_MEAN_THICK` 1.5 (area over width: 0.5 to 1.1 for a rule, 2.7 for a letter), `is_hyphen`, `split_fused_rules` (a run longer than `FUSED_MIN_RUN` 9.0, one row more erased each side, a descender's tail rejoined) |
| boxes | the 1985 standard boxes level-2 specifications (page 23, 147; the amendments delete boxes); the measurer drew their rules as underlines and their sides as choice bars | `find_boxes`: four rules, each level to `BOX_TILT` and thin, inked along `BOX_SIDE` 0.6 of the side, found even when a brace or bracket touches an edge; `RasterPage.boxes` lists the words each encloses |
| glyph height | `BRACE_GLYPH_H` was 13 (the 2023 vector value) | measured 10.2: a brace's last enclosed row begins 10.24 pt above its bottom (median of 120 braces on pages 196, 217, 252, 389, 396, 426; p10 7.2, p90 11.9); the first row sits 1.84 pt below its top |
