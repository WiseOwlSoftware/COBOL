# PB5003 - EXTERNAL FD record of an EXTERNAL strong type declares its cell and backing twice

**Found.** Wave 20261930 group a, probing PB4957. The program `FD F IS EXTERNAL.` / `01 R TYPE TG.` with
`01 TG IS TYPEDEF STRONG EXTERNAL.` holding a POINTER compiles to C# that declares `_scell_R` and `_redef_R` twice:
`cobol p2.cob` stops at CS0102 (exit 70). A plain EXTERNAL FD record (the report's p3.cob) compiles. Repro:
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w20261930a\probe\p2.cob`.

**Mechanism (likely, not yet traced).** The record is EXTERNAL twice over, once through the file's EXTERNAL clause and
once through the type's, so the storage cell and its redefines backing are claimed once for each. The same shape was
closed for BASED x EXTERNAL (the screen at `DataBinder.BindEntry`, kb/Work PB519) and for a contained program's GLOBAL
record (`PtrBindBasedAndAddressablesPass.ResolveStorageClaimHead`): one backing name, two claimants.

**Owed.** One claimant per record: find the two claims, make the second see the first, and cover the sibling shapes
(an EXTERNAL group record TYPEd by a strong EXTERNAL type with and without a pointer; a non-FD 01 of the same type).
Golden under `conformance-suite/cases`, citing the clause that makes both EXTERNAL legal (check it with `cite.py`).

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/RedefinesModel.cs` (`RedefinesClass.BackingClrName`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/LinkageBinder.cs` (`CallExternalBacking`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.cs` (`DataBinder.BindEntry`)
