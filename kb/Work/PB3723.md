# PB3723 — parallel branches conflict at the append points

**Sites:** `scripts/merge_drivers.py` (`hunk_kind`, `conflict_hunks`, `union_text`, `DRIVERS`), `scripts/orchestrator/enqueue.py` (`merge_main`), the `self_test` function of each `scripts/**/*.py` that has one, `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`unit_meet`, `collision`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` (the merge drivers' section).

## The owner's question and decision

The owner, 2026-10-25 ~11:10 PDT: "I do not understand why I'm reading occasional output suggesting that we serialize some parts … I thought that issue was precisely what branches were for. Why can't we parallize using multiple branches then merge". The operator explained that branches isolate the work but not the merge, that the planner serializes only two groups on one member and a change that rewrites what the others touch, and proposed measuring wave 20261107's two same-file pairs, which PB3696's gap let run side by side. The owner: "letting that result decide."

## The measurement (2026-10-25 ~11:25)

- **E (`worktree-wf_6e0a917c-500-20`, PB3386 group) and F (`500-6`, PB3367 group), planned to share `prune_worktrees.py#contained`.** F never edited `contained` (its hunks: the module docstring, `survey`, `carrying_findings`, `self_test`). F merged first (PR #567, 11:04). E's landing stopped `CONFLICT in scripts/prune_worktrees.py` (land-lane attempt 000122, conflict brief `E:/COBOL-coord/land-lane/conflicts/000122-20261025T180901Z-worktree-wf_6e0a917c-500-20.md`). Where the two diffs meet, against their merge base: three places, all inside `self_test()` (base lines 1752-1753, 1842, 2134-2136): both groups added test arms at one place.
- **D (`500-4`, PB3113 group) and H (`500-9`, PB3643 group), planned to share `merge_drivers.py#DRIVERS`.** Neither edited `DRIVERS`; their `merge_drivers.py` changes merge cleanly (`git merge-tree`). The simulated merge still conflicts in `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md`: both appended a paragraph after the same sentence ("… a hand resolution is not."), D for PB3714's verdict-line home and H for PB3643's measurement.

So the member partition reasoned about the wrong units. The conflicts come from points every change touches by rule and never declares: a script's single `self_test()` (MANDATORY-PRACTICES: a fix ships its test) and the design-doc paragraph of its mechanism (CLAUDE.md rule 6: docs current in the same change). Serializing by member would not have prevented either conflict; only serializing by file would, which PB3670 removed at the owner's direction. Each conflict costs a CONFLICT finisher, a re-gate, a review of the finisher's diff and another trip through the queue (PR #505 on 2026-10-24 was the same shape).

## Fix direction (branches and merge, the owner's model)

1. **Insert/insert hunks resolve themselves where both sides only added lines at one place.** PB3643's classifier (`merge_drivers.py --hunks`, `hunk_kind`) already tells an insert/insert hunk from a revise/revise one. For a test arm (an independent block in `self_test`), keeping both insertions is correct by construction; the merge queue's CI then runs the merged tests. Either give the drivers an insert-only resolution scoped to that, or restructure the self-tests so two additions never land at one place (one function per case, collected by a registry), whichever makes the next case automatic (rule 5).
2. **Prose.** PB3643 measured 37 insert/insert and 89 revise/revise conflict hunks over `CONFORMANCE.md`, `DESIGN-runtime-library.md` and `DESIGN-test-build-ci.md`, and refuted a general prose driver. The D/H case is insert/insert; decide from that same history whether an insert-only resolution (refusing every revise/revise hunk) would have matched the person's resolutions, and adopt it only if it would.
3. **Measure.** Conflicts per landing among same-file parallel groups, before and after, from the landing records and the land-lane conflict briefs.

Any resolution a driver makes stays visible to the review's coverage rule (`prune_worktrees.mechanical`, `is_drivers_merge`), and a revise/revise hunk always stays a conflict for a finisher.
