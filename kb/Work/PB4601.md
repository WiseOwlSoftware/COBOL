`PIC 9(9) ** 2` is 18 digits, and `Multiply` narrows the same product to an Int128 multiply. The literal-exponent arm of `NumericRenderer.cs` (about line 1460) takes the wide lane regardless. PB4569's implementer measured 6M powers at 0.58 s, against 0.42 s before.

**Fix shape.** Choose the lane from the digit bound, as `Multiply` does, and pin the choice structurally rather than with a time limit.

**Site:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs#Power`.
