# PB3172 — conformance suite slice S0a

Owner approved `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-conformance-suite.md` on 2026-10-14 ("approved", revision 2; kb/Work PB3160 holds the decisions verbatim).

**Prerequisites:** none.

**Scope:** Design §13 S0a and §6 step 0. The field is written two ways today (683 rows use ", "), so every reader parses it its own way. Every row's `editions` becomes the comma-separated ascending list with no spaces (`85,2002,2014,2023`); `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py`, the one validator, refuses any other spelling; every writer (`scripts/spec/build_inventory.py`, `record_verdicts.py`, `derive_verdict_batch.py`, `grammar_findings_to_batch.py`) writes it, and each reader that split on ", " is simplified to the one form. An empty field stays legal until PB3173. Tests: the validator's self-test refuses a spaced and an out-of-order list; `SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests` holds the whole inventory to it.

**Rules for the implementer:** implement this slice FROM the design (the sections named above), not from this note, which is its register entry; the complete slice with its tests and drift tests (CLAUDE.md rules 3, 5); a design correction lands in the design in the same change (rules 2, 6); every ISO citation through `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`; no wrapper, alias or compatibility layer (rule 4); the gate is `pwsh scripts/build-local.ps1 -Priority BelowNormal`; flip this note to `landed` and write `closes_rows` (or `closes_rows_reason`) in the commit that lands it.

## Landed

Re-probed at 1a9d0435f: 4,195 rows; 3,402 already in the one spelling, 645 spaced (37 of them out of order as well:
`2002, 2014, 2023, 85` ×19, `2014, 2023, 85, 2002` ×13, `2014, 2023, 2002` ×3, `2023, 2002, 2014` ×2), 148 empty.
No row named an unknown or repeated edition.

- **The rule is data**: the schema's `editions` entry became `{values, separator}` (ascending values, `,`), the way
  `code-location` and `test-ref` carry their separators.
- **One validator, one writer** in `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py`: `Schema.editions_error` (empty, or exactly the
  spelling of the editions it names; refuses a space, an out-of-order list, a doubled separator, an unknown or a
  repeated edition, naming the spelling to write) and `Schema.spell_editions` (raises on an unknown or repeated
  edition). `inventory_schema.py --self-test` (discovered by `scripts/self_tests.py`) proves both.
- **Writers**: `record_verdicts.py` asks the validator of every record (its own `split(ed, ",")` reader is gone) and
  its list-typed hint now spells the value the validator accepts; `build_inventory.py` carries `editions` through the
  writer (the migration: 645 rows respelled, nothing else in the 6.8 MB file changed, GAP 61 → 61) and stops on an
  unspellable carried field; `normalize_batch.py` respells an agent's batch (string or list) and logs it;
  `grammar_findings_to_batch.py` spells a finding's editions (an unknown edition is a problem) and now reads the
  catalog through `inventory_schema.load_catalog` instead of its own path; `derive_verdict_batch.py` carries the
  row's own value, which the drift test holds to the rule.
- **The C# twin**: `SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests.EditionsError` mirrors the rule from the same schema entry;
  `EveryEditionsField_IsTheOneSpelling` (was `EveryEditionsField_NamesOnlyKnownEditions`) holds the whole inventory to
  it, red with 645 findings on the old inventory and green on the new; the fabricated-inventory self-test adds the
  spaced, out-of-order, doubled-separator and repeated cases.
- Design §2, §6 step 0 and §13 S0a updated to the landed shape.
