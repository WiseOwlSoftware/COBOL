## Finding — `fixed-form-reference-format` (under-rejects; rows `SR-6.3.4-2`)

**Mechanism.** F5 - the column-7 indicator dispatch is a NIST-CCVS-shaped hand list that is not dialect-gated: S/Y/P/J/H/E/U (and D at 2023, where §6.2.2 no longer lists it) silently turn the line into a comment, and every other non-indicator character silently makes the line ordinary source; §6.2.2 admits only * / - and space, and a line with any other character in the indicator area is not conforming reference format, yet neither case draws a diagnostic outside --nist

**Repro.** Fixed form PROCEDURE DIVISION lines `000100     DISPLAY "LINE1".`, `000100E    DISPLAY "E-LINE".`, `000100X    DISPLAY "X-LINE".`, `000100     DISPLAY "LINE3".` (probe ffp10), at --std 2023 and --std 85.

**Expected from the spec.** §6.3.3: 'The indicator area identifies the type of a source line in accordance with the indicators specified in 6.2.2'. §6.2.2 lists * / - and space (D as the debugging indicator only in editions that still have it). §6.3.4 defines program-text content only for comment, continuation and source indicators. A line whose indicator area holds E or X must be diagnosed. It must not be silently dropped or silently compiled.

**Observed / reasoning.** Output is LINE1, X-LINE, LINE3, with no diagnostic at either edition. The E line's statement vanished, and the X line was compiled as source. ConvertFixedToFree has `case 'P' or 'p' or 'J' ... 'U' or 'u'` and `case 'D' or 'd' or 'S' or 's' or 'Y' or 'y'`, both of which emit `*>`. `default` treats every other character as the source indicator. The comments justify the letters by CCVS member conventions (IX207A etc.), but the switch runs for every compilation, not only under --nist, which breaks the 'every leniency is dialect-gated' doctrine. The excludedIndicator list at l.518-520 repeats the same letters a second time.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ConvertFixedToFree (l.518-520 excludedIndicator; l.561-594 the D/S/Y and P/J/H/E/U arms; l.626 default)

## Finding — `reference-format` (wrong-answer; rows `GR-6.5-5`)

**Mechanism.** The COBOL-85 comment-entry heuristic (CommentEntryParagraphs: AUTHOR, INSTALLATION, DATE-WRITTEN, DATE-COMPILED, SECURITY, REMARKS) is unscoped: in ANY division at ANY edition, a source-indicator line whose first Area-A word is one of those names, and every line after it up to the next Area-A word, is rewritten as a comment instead of being copied

**Repro.** E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2b\out\probe\reference-format\rf18.cob (fixed): PROCEDURE DIVISION. MAIN-PARA. DISPLAY \"START\". REMARKS. DISPLAY \"IN-REMARKS\". LAST-PARA. STOP RUN. -> prints only START. rf17.cob `PERFORM REMARKS` -> COBOL0001.

**Expected from the spec.** §6.5 5): a source-indicator line's program-text area is copied. REMARKS is not a reserved word in ISO 1989:2023, so it is a legal paragraph-name; output START then IN-REMARKS.

**Observed / reasoning.** ConvertFixedToFree applies FirstAreaAWord/CommentEntryParagraphs to every non-excluded-indicator line with no division or edition scope, silently dropping executable code. At 85 comment-entries exist only in the IDENTIFICATION DIVISION; from 2002 they are gone. Sibling: indicators D/S/Y/P/J/H/E/U silently comment the line (NIST accommodations) with no diagnostic.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ConvertFixedToFree (lines 518-551); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#CommentEntryParagraphs; src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#FirstAreaAWord
