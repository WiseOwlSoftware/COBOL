# PB4975 - PB4641 residue: wide float-edited receivers still double-round

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs` (line about 846, the `DecBinary` bypass keyed on `_rcv.FloatSignificandDigits <= 32`; `DivFinal` vs `CobolDec.Div`), `ReceiverContext.FloatSignificandDigits`, `PicInfo.FloatSignificandDigits`.

**Report (R3-1, medium, not blocking at round 3).** PB4641's R2-1 fix is half done: `DivFinal` (round-to-odd at 34 digits) now serves every native final-transfer
quotient except a float-edited receiver whose significand holds 33 or more digits, which keeps `CobolDec.Div`. For a significand of 33 to 36 digits
(`FloatSignificandDigits > 32`) `Div` rounds the quotient at 34 digits in the program's mode and the receiver then rounds again, so the double rounding PB4641 removed
survives. Round 2 asked for these to land from the exact quotient (as `ExactFinalQuotient` does) or a wider kernel; neither the report, the DEVLOG entry nor kb/Work
registered the remaining case (PB4641 is landed). Existing probe: p6.cob in `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261921d/` (F1 `+.499999999999999999999999999999E+00`, F2 ROUNDED `+.500000000000000000000000000000E+00`, R `.499999999999999999999999999999`)
is the narrow (32 or fewer digits) case; the 33-36 digit case needs a new probe and golden (the report states the 33/34-digit golden `pb4641_float_edited_receiver_quotient` still passes; whether it pins the single-rounding answer is for the implementer to check).

## Landed (wave 20261925, group B)

Re-probed on the build (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261925b/a/p86.cob`): `COMPUTE F33 = 9.999999999999999999999999999999999E-1 / 2.0E0` (PIC `+.9(33)E+99`) stored `+.5000...` where the exact quotient .49999999999999999999999999999999995 truncates to a 4 and 32 nines; the 34, 35 and 36 digit significands stored .5 too, and `A * A / D` of a 21-digit A lost its digits past the 34th. The earlier golden `pb4641_float_edited_receiver_quotient` pins only 2/9, a non-terminating quotient where the double rounding is invisible. Cause: the final-transfer quotient was reduced to 34 digits (`CobolDec.Div` in the program's mode, or the exact lane's 34-digit round-to-odd), and round-to-odd at 34 digits serves the receiver's one rounding only while the receiver is at least two digits coarser. Fix: `CobolBig.QuotientToOdd` forms the exact quotient reduced by round-to-odd to the significand plus two digits (up to 38, an `Int128` significand); `CobolDec.DivFinal` and `CobolExact.DivToOdd` take the receiver's significand (`floatSignificandDigits`) and use it past 32 (PROHIBITED excepted, which screens at 34 as before); `NumericRenderer.FinalTransferSignificandDigits` hands it over only for the final transfer. The nested quotient and every non-float-edited receiver are unchanged.

**Goldens:** `conformance-suite/cases/8.8.1.2-Native-standard-binary-and-standard-decimal/pb4975_float_edited_wide_significand_quotient` (accept 2002, 2014, 2023; refused at 85 by `wiseowl/diagnostics/pb4975_float_edited_wide_significand_quotient_85.json`); unit `CobolDecFinalQuotientWideTests`.
