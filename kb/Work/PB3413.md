# PB3413 — a remote-only branch is invisible to the branch survey

**Sites:** `scripts/prune_worktrees.py` (`survey`, `drop_remote`), `scripts/session-probe.ps1` (the BRANCH LIFECYCLE count).

**What happened (2026-10-18 ~22:58, operator session c95c0b86).** During the owner's drain for shutdown, the operator
ran `git branch -r --no-merged origin/main`, a query no tool runs. It listed eleven branches of 2026-10-09 to 10-11 that
exist only on origin: `ollama-eval-record`, `land-train-cap-r2100`, `pb2590-watchdog`, `worktree-wf_aba5c7ac-b61-1`,
`-b61-2`, `-b61-7`, `-b61-8`, `worktree-wf_7ea6bc1f-05f-1`, `-05f-2`, `-05f-6` and `-05f-12`. None had ever appeared in
`prune_worktrees.py`, the session probe's BRANCH LIFECYCLE, or the landing lane. Nobody could say from any record whether
needed work was in them. The owner asked: "Why is the other session just now discovering week old branches and having
no idea whether needed work is in them? Something in the process is badly broken".

**What they hold (checked by hand that night).** All eleven are carried on main. Their commits are not
patch-identical, because train landers rewrote them (`git cherry` reads `+`), but every note they name is landed:
- `-05f-12`, `-05f-6`, `-05f-1`: PB2987, PB3016, PB3023-PB3025, PB3027 (PB3023 names them);
- `-05f-2`: PB3024 phase 1 and PB3028 (`enqueue.py`, `ruleset.py`, `landing_measure.py` on main, `train_measure.py`
  gone);
- `-b61-1`, `-b61-2`, `-b61-7`, `-b61-8`: train 1063 (DEVLOG 1962: PB3022 part 1, PB3027, PB3026, PB3061). PB3022 stays
  open only for its item 10, which no branch carries;
- `pb2590-watchdog`: PB2590 (landed: `orchestrate.ps1`'s Write-Checkpoint);
- `land-train-cap-r2100`: its one commit is patch-identical on main;
- `ollama-eval-record`: PB2886 (landed) and a plan §0 refresh that later ones superseded.

**Why the survey never showed them (`scripts/prune_worktrees.py`, on main at 49e1c6b79).** `survey()` covers "Every local
branch but main, and every detached worktree". `origin` enters only in `drop_remote`, at `--apply`, for a local branch
being removed as LANDED. It deletes `origin/<branch>` only when that remote is the local tip or an ancestor of it, and
otherwise prints "kept origin/<branch>: it holds commits the local branch did not" once and never looks again. A local
branch deleted any other way leaves its remote with no row anywhere:
- by hand (PB2708 records the operator removing landed branches by hand on 10-08 and 10-10);
- by a train lander or a Workflow worktree cleanup;
- before `drop_remote` landed (2026-10-11 01:20, 8deb3dad2);
- when its remote had moved on.
PB2708 (status half) recorded "nothing in the lifecycle looks at `origin`" on 10-08. Its fix covered only the `--apply`
path. PB2600's rule, that every branch ends LANDED or ABANDONED, is therefore enforced only for local branches.

**The fix.** `survey()` lists `refs/remotes/origin/*` too. A remote-only branch, or one whose remote differs from its
local tip, gets a row and a lifecycle state by the same rules:
- LANDED by content or by its notes (`carried`, the train records PB2708 added): `--apply` deletes the remote;
- otherwise DECISION, naming what is not on main;
- the `gh-readonly-queue/*` refs are the merge queue's and are skipped.
The session probe's BRANCH LIFECYCLE line counts them, so a stray remote appears at every session start. The self-test
plants a remote-only landed branch (deleted by `--apply`), a remote-only unlanded one (DECISION, kept) and a remote that
moved on past its local tip (DECISION).

**The owner's decision on the eleven (2026-10-18 ~23:55, relayed by the Mythos lane):** "delete the eleven origin-only
branches of 10-09 to 10-11 now". The Mythos lane deleted all eleven on origin (`git push origin --delete`, 11 of 11).
It also deleted the wave-1118 remotes `worktree-wf_14a1e62b-326-19`, `-326-26` and `-326-32`, whose content is on main
(`-26`'s two commits outside main were verified carried by `-326-31`). The owner then judged the local `park/<branch>`
tags to have no forensic value, since their work is on main, and all 16 were deleted (relayed by the Mythos lane,
2026-10-18 ~23:51). The rule going forward: verify that a branch's content is on main, record that in its note, and
delete the branch. Never park it as a tag. Still on origin afterwards: `worktree-wf_ff4f514b-355-29`, merged into main
on 2026-10-16 with its remote never deleted, which is the case this note's survey must also list.
