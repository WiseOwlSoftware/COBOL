# PB6215 — an implementor-latitude determination lives outside the design doc

**Mechanism.** PB6178 decided that a wholly absent first occurrence of a STEPped entry keeps the layout its entry takes when
present: hoisted constant-sum anchors, rewritten from the live counter only inside a present field. That is recorded only in
`kb/Work/PB6178.md` and code comments. `docs/REPORT_WRITER_DESIGN.md` (the STEP Placement paragraph, AnchorSeed and AnchorStep) and
`docs/conformance/` say nothing about an absent occurrence. CLAUDE.md rule 1 sets the precedence for implementor latitude (the ISO
spec, then GnuCOBOL, then IBM or Micro Focus) and requires the choice in `docs/conformance/`; the GnuCOBOL tier was not consulted
for this gap (the report names GR12b and GR2b).

**Fix shape.** Probe GnuCOBOL on the absent-first-occurrence case, record the result and the choice in `docs/conformance/`, and add
the determination to the STEP Placement paragraph of the design doc in the same change (rule 2, rule 6).

**Sites:** `docs/REPORT_WRITER_DESIGN.md:249`, `docs/conformance/`
