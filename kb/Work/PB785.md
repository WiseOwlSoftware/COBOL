## The bare question for the owner

> **Is `docs/rearchitecture/evidence/*.json` frozen evidence — a record of what an agent said, needing a
> machine-readable "superseded by" marker — or a document that CLAUDE.md rule 6 makes current?**

## The default, if the owner does not answer — and the convention it follows

Per `feedback_owner_decision_protocol` (follow the surveyed convention; ask only on significant considerations),
the default is **FROZEN, plus a marker**, because three independent parts of this repo already treat the
directory that way:

1. ⭐ **`scripts/spec/citation_corpus.py:19-25` names this exact directory in a `FROZEN` tuple** — a MECHANICAL
   commitment, not prose: *"Directories that hold FROZEN evidence — an agent's raw output, recorded as it was
   said. Repairing a citation inside one would rewrite the record of what was found, which is the opposite of
   what these files are for. (The findings they contain are actioned through `kb/Work/`, never by editing the
   transcript.)"* Both citation audits skip `docs/rearchitecture/evidence` because of it. Under the alternative
   answer (rule 6 makes it current) that exclusion would itself be a defect.
2. **`docs/DOC_INDEX.md:74`** already classifies the one evidence file it indexes exactly so — and shows the
   house form of the marker, which is a POINTER: *"⛔ Frozen evidence: never edited to stay current. The CURRENT
   process is `.claude/skills/workstream/SKILL.md`; the six questions still owed the owner are `kb/Work/PB468`."*
3. **The banner precedent** the dispatch names: `docs/ISO2023_CONFORMANCE_PLAN.md:3` and
   `docs/PHASE4_RECONCILIATION.md:3` both open with *"⚠ **FROZEN HISTORICAL LEDGER (2026-07-19):** … Live state =
   the plan §0 + …"* — date, freeze, and the pointer to what IS live, in that order.

⛔ **What the default does NOT cover, and why this is a question at all:** the house marker is a Markdown banner,
and a `.json` file has nowhere to put one. So adopting the default still requires choosing a machine-readable
form — a top-level `"_frozen"` / `"_superseded_by"` object carrying the date, the reason and the pointers (for
this file: `kb/Work/PB712`, and `docs/rearchitecture/PHASE-13-wave-c-scout.md`'s refutation once train 20 lands),
which the schema-free evidence JSONs can all take. That is a shape decision the owner may want, which is why the
question is asked rather than silently applied.

## ⛔ What a future scout reads first — measured, not assumed

This is the part that decides whether a marker in the JSON is enough:

- `docs/DOC_INDEX.md` indexes **neither** file. `grep -n "grammar-batch-research\|wave-c-scout" docs/DOC_INDEX.md`
  → no hits; the only `evidence/` entry in the whole index is `PROCESS-REVIEW-2026-09-04.md` (`:74`).
- So nothing routes a reader from one to the other. A scout arrives by grep or by listing
  `docs/rearchitecture/`, and a keyword-dense JSON full of `LANDED` verdicts is at least as likely a hit as a
  728-line prose scout doc — **which is exactly how PB712 was built**: the refuted design was read out of the
  research artefact and implemented as fact.

Therefore, whichever way the owner answers, the marker has to live **inside the JSON**, and
`docs/rearchitecture/evidence/` needs a row of its own in `docs/DOC_INDEX.md` saying what the directory is. A
banner added only to the prose scout doc does not reach the reader who never opens it.
