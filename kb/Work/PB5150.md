# PB5150 - adjudicate: subordinates of a GLOBAL record and the uniqueness screen

**Sites:** none (an adjudication changes no code file; its result may change the screen or the pb1047 golden).

**Question.** §2023:8.4.2.1 (checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`) treats a subordinate of a GLOBAL record as global too. The PB5073 screen nevertheless leaves two same-named subordinates of GLOBAL records to qualification, as the pb1047 golden expects. Read strictly, the golden and the screen disagree with the clause, so the golden is itself in question.

**Owed.** An adjudicator decides, from the spec text and the printed page, whether two such subordinates are a uniqueness violation or legal qualified names, and whether the pb1047 golden stands. Rule 1: the standard decides, not the golden. The result updates the screen or the golden.
