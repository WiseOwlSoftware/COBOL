**Found by:** the attended session that dispatched R1 (wave 1031) and its reviser: the loop's own wave of 05:40 on
2026-10-07 was also numbered 1031.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py:540` (`next_wave`: `max(report waves, "wave N" in the newest 200 DEVLOG
lines) + 1`), `plan_wave.py:64` (`REPORT_NAME = ^w(?P<wave>\d+)(?P<letter>[a-z0-9]*)-(?P<lead>PB\d+)-report\.md$` —
a name with a suffix after the lead, such as `w1031-PB2118-r1-report.md`, `w1031-PB2118-refute-report.md` and
`w1031-PB2118-revise-report.md`, does not match, so it is not a report and its wave number is never seen), and
`scripts/orchestrator/alloc.py:37` (`KINDS = ("devlog", "pb", "code")` — the central allocator the ids and codes
already come from has no wave kind).

**Repro:** with `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w1031-PB2118-r1-report.md` present and no other 1031 report,
`python -I scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py --cluster PB2119 --dry-run --budget-points 10 --no-branches` heads its plan
`=== WAVE 1031 PLAN` (seen 2026-10-07 by the R1 refuter's driver: `c9_plan.txt` line 2).

**The rule it breaks:** MANDATORY-PRACTICES P8 ("ids and diagnostic codes only from the orchestrator's allocation"):
a wave number is an id that names reports, scratch directories and DEVLOG entries, and two dispatchers deriving it
independently collide — the failure P8 was written against (five id collisions in one day).

**Scenario:** two waves share a number; their reports, `w<n>…` scratch directories and DEVLOG "wave N" references
interleave, and a later `next_wave` or a lander reading `w1031*` mixes an attended dispatch's report into the loop's
train.

**Fix shape:** `alloc.py wave` (a fourth kind, its truth scan = today's `next_wave` sources plus any `w<n>*` file or
directory under the coordination scratch), called by `plan_wave.py` (replacing `next_wave`) and by the attended
dispatch path (`make_dispatch_specs.py` / the workstream templates); `test_alloc.py` gains the wave kind and
`test_plan_wave.py` a case where a suffixed report raises the next number. **Model:** Sonnet (mechanical once the
truth scan is agreed; NEEDS-OPUS if the scan's sources are contested).
