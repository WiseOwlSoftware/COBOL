# PB3815 — the usage pair is still spelled inline beside its one predicate

**Sites** (re-grepped on origin/main 33e3dcf21, `git grep -nE "Usage\.Display or Usage\.National" -- src`):
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/ItemCategory.cs#IsDisplayOrNationalUsage` (230) and `#UsageOutsideDisplayOrNational`
(248, 252), `Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ControlFlowBinder.cs` (194, 200), `Binding/Procedure/Verbs/IntrinsicBinder.cs` (1530,
1664, 2041, 2058), `Binding/Procedure/Verbs/MoveBinder.cs` (615), `Binding/Procedure/Verbs/StringUnstringBinder.cs` (656),
`Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs` (789), `Binding/RelationComparability.cs` (88), `Binding/DataBinder.cs` (7361),
`Binding/DataBinder.SignClause.cs` (140), `Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs` (1139), `Binding/IntrinsicArgumentRules.cs` (766),
`Binding/Model/PicInfo.cs` (1001, a switch arm, likely stays).
The predicate is `PicInfo.cs:227 IsDisplayOrNational(Usage u)` (`UsageFamilies`).

## Measured

PB498/PB508 made the pair one structure (`UsageFamilies.IsDisplayOrNational`) and said so in its doc comment. The PB498
reviewer's R1-1: its item-level twin `ItemCategory.IsDisplayOrNationalUsage` and `UsageOutsideDisplayOrNational` still
spell the pair inline, as do the binder sites above, so the new predicate is a second spelling beside them. Each site
is correct for its rule (the report: "moving them to UsageFamilies.IsDisplayOrNational is mechanical"); PB3228 had the
same shape for STRING/INSPECT and fixed one site.

## Acceptance

- Every site above that asks the question calls `UsageFamilies.IsDisplayOrNational` (or the negated form); the item-level
  twins in ItemCategory.cs call it too. A switch arm that selects a form (PicInfo.cs:1001) may stay and is named in the landing.
- A drift test fails on a new inline `Usage.Display or Usage.National` outside `UsageFamilies`.
- No output changes: the conformance suite and the unit tests stay as they are.
