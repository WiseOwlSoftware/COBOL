# PB3248 — one reserved-word reference, two diagnostics

Filed 2026-10-16 by the enqueue unit's clerk from the PB656 implementer's report
(`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w1084b-PB656-report.md`, "New defects (note-ready)"). The report's repro is
`probe/rv.cob` in the w1084b scratch directory; it was not re-run for this filing (R2: a runnable repro and the
sites are given).

**Symptom.** At `--std 85`, `2002` and `2023`, `DISPLAY RECEIVE.` (RECEIVE reserved at that edition) reports
COBOL0901 (the section 8.9 user-word message) and then ALSO `COBOL0001: unexpected '.'` at (7,27). The
declaration of the word on line 5 is not the one reported; the reference is. Harm: noise only, the program is
refused anyway (`process_only`).

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`ConsumeUntil`, `IsReportedReservedOperand`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs` (`LookaheadBeginsAStatementThatParses`, `ReadingParses`, `entryNameHere`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/EditionReadDriftTests.cs` (the new adjudicated read).

**Code sites (as filed).**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs:2583` `VisitCobolWord`, the funnel that raises
  COBOL0901 for a reserved word in a user-word slot (reporter `FlagReservedUserWord`, `:2695`).
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4:185,869,887` and `Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4:273`: the generated
  `reservedGatedWord` alternative (from `cobol-words.json` `reservationGated`) that re-admits a gated word in a
  DECLARATION slot but not in a statement operand slot.
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs:620` converts a parse error at a gated word to the
  COBOL0901 text; the token that follows (`.`) is then reported separately as COBOL0001 because the statement
  parse was abandoned at the reference.

**Mechanism.** The reference to a gated word is unparseable by design (PB693), so the parser raises one error at
the word and a second at the next token. The listener folds only the first into 0901. The report says this shares
its root with the class-3 parse path PB655 made data-driven (PB655 is landed): rejection of a gated reference and
the recovery that follows are one path, so the fix belongs in the listener's recovery (suppress the follow-on error
for a reference already reported as 0901), not in a per-word grammar edit. The exact recovery site was not traced
for this filing; the implementer owes that.

**Sweep owed.** Probe the other statement operand slots (MOVE, ADD, IF, PERFORM) with a gated word and confirm
each yields exactly one diagnostic.

## Landed (wave 20261780 C, 2026-10-30)

Re-probed on the tree first: `DISPLAY RECEIVE.` gave COBOL0901 and `COBOL0001: unexpected '.'` at 85, 2002 and 2023, as filed.
The sweep the note owed showed the shape exactly: `DISPLAY CONSTANT.` / `MOVE CONSTANT TO W` drew ONE diagnostic, while every
operand slot holding RECEIVE or SEND drew two or three (`MOVE W TO RECEIVE.` -> `unexpected '.'`; `ADD RECEIVE TO N.` ->
`unexpected 'TO'` and `COBOL5506 ... expected GIVING`; `IF RECEIVE = 1` -> `unexpected '='`).

**Mechanism (traced).** Not the listener: the error STRATEGY's resynchronization. RECEIVE and SEND (and VALIDATE from 2002) begin
a statement, so they are in every statement-level recovery set, and `DefaultErrorStrategy`'s `ConsumeUntil` stopped AT the word
the report had just named; the statement loop then parsed it as a RECEIVE / SEND statement and refused the token after it.
CONSTANT begins nothing, which is why it never cascaded.

**Fix.** `CobolErrorStrategy.ConsumeUntil` consumes the token the last report named when the listener re-codes it to COBOL0901
(`IsReportedReservedOperand`: the report's token, and `ReservedUserWordViolation` non-null, the listener's own test), as the
operand it stood for, before resynchronizing. One rule: the report and the recovery read the word the same way (ISO §8.3.2.1 1),
broken once by one occurrence). Every other refused token is still a resynchronization point.

**Witness.** `ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.AReservedVerbWrittenAsAnOperand_DrawsOneDiagnostic` (DISPLAY / MOVE sender and
receiver / ADD / IF / a two-operand DISPLAY, RECEIVE at 85 and 2023, SEND at 2023, VALIDATE at 2002): exactly one diagnostic, the
COBOL0901 naming the word. **PERFORM** (the sweep's fourth verb): `PERFORM RECEIVE.` draws one diagnostic, but it is the inline
PERFORM's reading (`unexpected '.'` inside a RECEIVE statement), never a 0901, because no error falls at the word; that is a
different mechanism (the out-of-line procedure-name slot has no reserved-word arm) and is reported as a lead, not fixed here.

**Review finding R1-1 (branch review round 1, fixed by the review finisher).** Consuming every word the listener re-codes to
COBOL0901 was too wide: the re-code also fires at a verb that correctly begins the NEXT statement after an incomplete one, and
skipping that verb resumed the recovery inside the correct statement. At --std 2023, after `MOVE W-X TO` (receiver missing),
`RECEIVE FROM W-X GIVING W-Y W-N` drew a second `COBOL0001: unexpected 'GIVING'`, `SEND TO W-X FROM W-Y RETURNING W-N END-SEND`
(also inside IF ... END-IF) `unexpected 'FROM'`, and `RESUME AT NEXT STATEMENT` a COBOL5506 telling the user to write SENTENCE.
The two cases are told apart by the grammar, never by a list: the word is consumed only when the statement it would begin does
not parse (`CobolParserCoreBase.LookaheadBeginsAStatementThatParses`, a speculative `ParserInterpreter` parse of `statement` up
to the separator period). `entryNameHere`'s clause probe and this one now share one helper, `ReadingParses`. The recovery
asks the resynchronization set first, so the probe runs only where its answer decides something. Witness:
`ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.AReservedVerbBeginningTheNextStatement_DrawsOneDiagnostic` (six rows; four of them fail on
the reviewed head be53df75b, and the `SEND TO W-X.` row is the control, a reserved verb whose statement does not parse and is
still consumed).
