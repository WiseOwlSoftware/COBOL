# PB5667 - SEND WITH function-identifier: two diagnostics at 85

**The lead.** "R1-1 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 1): src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CommunicationBinder.cs:205 At --std 85 a
function-identifier in SEND WITH draws two errors (COBOL0900 placement, then COBOL6171 SR2), contrary to the report self-review claim that the 85 path gives no
double report; the claim holds only for ADVANCING, where describedAs is null."

One cause (a function-identifier is not a legal SEND WITH operand at 85), two reports: `BindSendIdentifier` reports the placement (COBOL0900) and returns an item,
and the `IsIntegerItem` check at the line cited then reports `DiagnosticCatalog.CommunicationStatementOperand` (COBOL6171, SR2) on the same operand. The fix makes
the first refusal return a rejected item so the SR2 check does not run on it (one cause, one report), and a golden at `--std 85` pins one diagnostic.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CommunicationBinder.cs:205` (`CommunicationBinder.BindSendIdentifier`)

**Sibling sweep.** Every caller of `BindSendIdentifier` and every `IsIntegerItem` check that follows a placement refusal in the communication binders (RECEIVE, SEND ADVANCING).
