# PB4980 - ANNUITY over a nested selection differs between one receiver and several

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/FunctionValueLane.cs` (`FunctionValueLane.OnSdidiBody`, `FunctionValueLane.NativeSdidiBodiesWithExactResult`, `FunctionValueLane.NativeSdidiBodiesWithWholeResult`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/FunctionValueLaneDriftTests.cs` (`FunctionValueLaneDriftTests.NativeRenderDecArms`, `FunctionValueLaneDriftTests.NativeSdidiBodiesWithExactResult_AreTheRenderDecArmsNotBuiltByDec`).

**Report.** A multi-receiver MOVE of `ANNUITY (MEDIAN (T (ALL)) 3)` or `ANNUITY (MAX (T (ALL)) 3)` gives Q1 and Q2 values that differ from the single-receiver
result. Probe: `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261921f/an.cob`. Not probed beyond that; the report gives no values, so re-run the probe first. Likely a sibling of PB4979
(ANNUITY keeps the exact value over a nested float selection) but surfaced through the lane that renders one body per receiver, so decide whether the body is evaluated once or per receiver (a function is evaluated once, §2023:14.6.4 GR7).

## Determination and fix (landed)

Re-probe on the fix-lane build (E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261925e/p2.cob): the one-receiver MOVE of `ANNUITY (MEDIAN (T (ALL)) 3)` stored the exact
`0.3333333333333333333333333` and the two-receiver MOVE the binary64 `0.3333333333333333000000000`; `ANNUITY (MAX (U 1.5E0) 3)` stored `1.6025641025641025641025641`
alone and `1.6025641025641026000000000` beside a second receiver. `ANNUITY (MAX (T (ALL)) 3)` agreed with itself (both binary64: a plain MAX is a scale-0 value, and ANNUITY
of an exact operand is on the float lane, §2023:15.4.1).

Root: the renderer's native MAX / MIN / MEDIAN arms (`RenderDec`: `MaxExact`, `MinExact`, `MedianExact`; `RenderWholeAligned`: `MedianWhole`) return `NumX.Exact`, which the
function above reads through `NumericRenderer.LowerWide` as an SDIDI operand, so ANNUITY (and PRESENT-VALUE, VARIANCE ...) computes on the SDIDI. `FunctionValueLane`
listed the three in `NativeSdidiBodiesWithExactResult` (a scale-0 value) and predicted `CarrierKind.Fixed`, so the multi-receiver intermediate was a COMP-2 item
(`ReturnsBinary64`) where the single-receiver MOVE was exact. They now sit in `NativeSdidiBodiesWithWholeResult` with SUM / RANGE / MEAN / MIDRANGE (predicted `Dec`),
and the drift test tells the three arm shapes apart (`Dec(...)`, `Exact(...)`, `new NumX(...)`), so a body added to the renderer without its line fails. A function is
evaluated once (§2023:14.9.25.4 GR1) and its value is the same for all instances (§2023:15.4.1). Golden
`pb4980_a_function_over_a_nested_selection_is_the_same_for_every_receiver` (+ `_85`).
