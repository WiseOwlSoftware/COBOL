## Reading 1

**Item.** SET index-name TO / UP BY / DOWN BY a value outside the table's range stores it (S2=11, U2=15, U3=05) at --std 2002 (rows 2002-GR-14.8.35.3-2, 2002-GR-14.8.35.3-3; repro E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-20261114/read-2002/defects-work/p3/p20_set_to_out_of_range.cob prints S1=03 S2=11)

**Outcome.** conditional: the 2002 text conflicts with itself

**What the text says.** normative §2002:14.8.35.3 GR2 makes an out-of-range TO value an unsuccessful SET: 'otherwise the execution of the SET statement is unsuccessful and the content of the receiving operand is unchanged' (OK); GR3b raises EC-BOUND-SUBSCRIPT for UP/DOWN: 'otherwise the EC-BOUND-SUBSCRIPT exception condition is set to exist' (OK). Informative §2002:F.2 item 121, among the substantive changes NOT affecting existing programs, says the opposite: 'The result of setting an index can be outside of the range of the associated table' (OK).

**What is unsettled.** whether 2002's normative GR2/GR3b (index unchanged, EC-BOUND-SUBSCRIPT) or its own Annex F account (outside the range permitted, as 2014/2023 do) states the 2002 behaviour; normative text ordinarily governs, but the annex records the intent of the change. Decides the two rows; extends adjudication PB5625.

## Reading 2

**What the text says.** §2002:8.5.2.7 item 3 puts in category national 'An elementary data item explicitly or implicitly described as usage national.' (OK); §2002:8.5.2.9 puts in category numeric 'An elementary data item described as numeric by its PICTURE character-string and not descr' (OK); §2002:13.16.22.3 GR4 speaks of '— class numeric with usage national.' **What is unsettled.** whether 8.5.2.7 item 3 (read literally: every usage-national elementary item is national) or the class-numeric-with-usage-national reading of 13.16.22.3 GR4 and 14.8.23.3 GR2a governs a PIC 9 / PIC 1 USAGE NATIONAL item; render the PDF pages of 8.5.2.7 and 8.5.2.9 before deciding.
