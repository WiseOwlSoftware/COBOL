**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Repository/ExpansionPlacementPass.cs:184` (`WillWrite`, the `TargetFile.Skewed` arm: warning `RepositoryPreviousBuildSkew`, REPO-12; the review cited line 172), `:232` (`ReportForeignState`, COBOL2240), `:217` (`ReportSameName`, the REPO-2 warning and the `shallNot` COBOL2240 error), `:244` (`RepositoryWord`).
