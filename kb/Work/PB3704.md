# PB3704 — how many notes per group, given what a note costs in turns

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` (`wave.max_notes_per_group` and `max_notes_per_group_source`), `scripts/orchestrator/turns_by_group_size.py` (`measure`, `TURNS`; the measurement, on main since PB3641), `scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py` and `scripts/spec/ledger_plan.py` (read the cap; unchanged).

## The measurement

`turns_by_group_size.py` reads the dispatched reports and the turn counts of their implementers. Its fit is about 61 turns for
the group's fixed cost plus about 23 turns per note. At the group cap of 8 notes that is about 245 turns. Groups of three to five notes
split (hit the turn cap and return SPLIT) about half the time, and each split costs a finisher dispatch (the finisher's own gate and
review included). PB3701 records that the implementer cap is written as 220 in the spec template and check but is 400 in MANDATORY-PRACTICES
P5 and the agent definition; the cap the planner assumes decides what group size fits.

## The owner decision

Choose the group cap and the turn cap together:

1. Keep 8 notes per group and make the cap 400 everywhere (PB3701's fix): one value, 8 notes fit with room.
2. Keep the cap at 220 and cut the group cap to about 5 notes (the measured fit for 220 turns is `(220 - 61) / 23`, about 6, so 5 leaves a margin).
3. Size groups by the notes' predicted turns, not by a count (`turns_by_group_size.py`'s per-note cost differs by area): a larger change, but the next case is automatic.

Whichever is chosen, `model_rules.json` should carry the number once, `max_notes_per_group_source` should quote the re-measured fit, and a
drift test should pin the template, the check and P5 to it (PB3701).

## Decided (2026-10-25, the owner)

The owner, 2026-10-25 ~00:05 PDT, answering the operator's explanation (keep 8, cut to 4-5, or size by predicted turns; recommended: cut to 4-5 now, predicted-turns sizing as the follow-on, and the 400 cap applied regardless): "Both your recommendations".

**The bar.** `model_rules.json` `wave.max_notes_per_group` becomes 4 (the owner, ~00:15 PDT: "No. The group cap drops from 8 to 4 notes, about 153 turns, so a group lands within the hour"), the one place the cap lives (PB3611), and `max_notes_per_group_source` quotes the re-measured fit from `turns_by_group_size.py` (about 61 + 23 turns per note, so 4 notes is about 153 turns) and this decision. Every reader takes the value from there; `test_plan_wave.py`'s planning cases and the ledger's estimate (`ledger_plan.py`, counted at this cap) follow it. Measure the split rate and the dispatch-to-merge time per group over the first day of waves, before and after.

## Correction (2026-10-25 ~00:15, the owner)

An interim reading recorded a cap of 5 (groups of 4-5) from the owner's "Cut size to 4-5. Plan predicted turns later". The owner then settled it: "No. The group cap drops from 8 to 4 notes, about 153 turns, so a group lands within the hour (PB3704, top priority). Sizing groups by predicted turns is filed as the follow-on (tier 2)." The cap is 4.

## Landed (2026-10-25, wave 20261107 group A)

`model_rules.json` `wave.max_notes_per_group` is 4, the one place the cap lives; every reader takes it from there
(`plan_wave.py` hands it to `fix_clusters.py` and the dispatch grouping, `ledger_plan.py` counts the ledger at it; the
`test_plan_wave.py` self-test, 425 checks, and the `ledger_plan.py` and `gen_ledger.py` self-tests pass at 4). Its
`max_notes_per_group_source` quotes this decision, the owner's words and the RE-MEASURED fit (PB3711 first made the
measurement complete): on 2026-10-25, 322 joined reports, about 65 turns per group plus 20.5 per note, so 4 notes is about
147 turns (the owner's 153 is the earlier 61 + 23 x 4). `cost.source` quotes the same line. The 400-turn cap (PB3701) and
the predicted-turns sizing (PB3718) are their own notes and are not done here. The split-rate and dispatch-to-merge
measurement over the first day at cap 4 is the follow-up the decision names; `turns_by_group_size.py` is its tool.
