**Landed (2026-11-13, wave 20262025).** The area of a word a replacement places and whether it begins its line are now two facts with two sources. WHETHER a token begins its line is read from the RESULTANT token stream (`AreaPlacementPass.Run`: no token before it on its resultant line), because the program is judged only after all COPY and REPLACE processing (§1985:XII.2.4 GR8, §1985:XII.3.4 GR9). The AREA it begins in is that of the place it was laid out at: a stretch of resultant text carries an optional `Layout` beside its origin, and `ApplyReplacements` writes pseudo-text-2 with `OriginWriter.AppendLaidOut`, so each of its words stands at the replaced text (its origin, which every diagnostic and EXCEPTION-LOCATION still names; kb/Work PB1801) and is laid out where pseudo-text-2 wrote it (§1985:XII.2.4 GR9 "begins in the same area of the resultant program as it appears in pseudo-text-2"). Identifier-2, literal-2 and word-2 keep the replaced word's area (the same rule). One mechanism serves REPLACE and COPY REPLACING.

Determination for REPLACE (recorded in the 1985-GR-XII.3.4-10 store record): §1985:XII.3.4 GR10's "placed in the source program according to the rules for reference format" is the sentence the COPY statement's GR10 also carries beside GR9's area rule, so REPLACE keeps the area each word was written in, as COPY does; the compiler never moves a word. RA5 (a single `P2.` written in area B of the REPLACE line, replacing `XXX`) is therefore refused, which the held-rows adjudication had left open.
