corrected (diagram and figure note).

**Fix — closed alternatives in the grammar, one rule each, no bind screen.** The parser now refuses the complement,
the `PrintedFormatAlternativeDriftTests` shape and the `CONTROL IS? | CONTROLS ARE?` precedent in the same file:
- `reportLineClause` opens `(LINE (NUMBERS ARE? | ARE | NUMBER? IS?) | LINES ARE?)` — §13.18.35.2's brace.
- `reportColumnClause` opens with a PLURAL half `((COLUMN | COL) NUMBERS | COLUMNS | COLS) reportColumnAlignment? ARE?`
  and a SINGULAR half `(COLUMN | COL) NUMBER? reportColumnAlignment? IS?` — §13.18.14.2's six-way spelling brace with
  §13.18.14.3 SR4/SR5 splitting [IS | ARE] across the alignment word. The alignment word became its own rule,
  `reportColumnAlignment`, so both halves read one spelling of it (`DataBinder.Reports` and the version pass now read
  that node).
- Sibling sweep: every `(IS | ARE)` in the grammar was listed. `reportSourceClause` had the same cross product and now
  opens `(SOURCE IS? | SOURCES ARE?)` (§13.18.53.2). The VALUE clause's pairing is already screened at bind by
  `DataBinder.CheckValueConnective` (PB559: its format depends on the level-number, which a parse rule cannot see);
  `CONTROL`, `PAGE LIMIT`, `REPORT`, `LABEL RECORD` and `DATA RECORD` are already closed; the SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS
  `IS | ARE` is a genuine free choice; the screen-section LINE/COLUMN rules are the declined A.4.2 module.
- A NUMBERS the format does not permit at its position is a user-defined word by §8.10, so `COLS NUMBERS 5` parses as
  a two-operand clause naming a constant NUMBERS (refused at bind when none exists) — the standard's reading, not an
  acceptance of the prefix.
- Found on the way and fixed (I9): the 2002 introduction gate called a single-operand `LINES 3` "the multiple LINE
  clause form"; `VisitReportLineClause` / `VisitReportColumnClause` now name the written form, and the two constructs'
  displays in `constructs.json` name the spellings they gate.
