**Re-probed first** on the wave-1032 tree (`d625d3b84`, its own `cobol.exe`, `--std 2023`): `MOVE A1 :: "GETNAME" () TO W`
and `( )` compiled and ran (`W=ACCOUNT`); the §8.7.4 sibling `MOVE A1:: "GETNAME" TO W` (no space BEFORE the operator)
compiled and ran too. `A1::"GETNAME"` and `A1 ::"GETNAME"` were already refused, but only incidentally, by §8.3.5 rule
5's literal-delimiter check (COBOLNET2633). The note's claim that `MethodOmittedArgumentTests` pins a spaceless
`O::"CHK"(OMITTED)` was stale: PB1394 had already respaced that test.

**The rule.** The rendered figure (PDF page 163) brackets the whole `( … )` group but puts the five argument forms in a
BRACE, and the ellipsis repeats that brace inside the one pair. `cite.py --check 5.2.6.3 "one of the alternatives
contained within the braces shall be explicitly specified"` OK, so a written pair holds at least one argument. The
function-identifier is the contrast: §8.4.3.2.2 BRACKETS its argument group inside the parentheses (`FUNCTION RANDOM ()`
is printed in its SR6 note), so `functionCall`'s `functionArgList?` stays.

**The fix.** No edition admits the empty pair, so it leaves the grammar: `inlineInvocationSegment : COLONCOLON literal
(LPAREN argumentList RPAREN)?`. `CobolErrorStrategy.IsEmptyInlineArgumentList` names the refused shape (the `::`
token has no other role, so `:: literal ( )` is this format and nothing else): **COBOLNET2994**. Before this, the shape
was a bare `COBOL0001 unexpected '('`. `InlineMethodInvocationOperandDriftTests.TheRuleMatchesThePrintedGeneralFormat`
used to assert only that `argumentList` appeared; it now pins the required list.

**The sibling (§8.7.4, and its §8.7.3 twin).** The rule is "shall be immediately preceded and followed by a separator
space" (`cite.py --check 8.7.4` and `8.7.3` OK). The lexer comments called both operators deliberately lenient. They
are now decided where every separator-context rule lives, `SeparatorRule.Violations`, as **COBOLNET2993**, with the
side named. When the touching neighbour is a literal, the operator owns the boundary and the literal's COBOLNET2633
stands down (one report per boundary). The `&` arm was a live under-reject of its own: `SPACE&SPACE` and constant-name
operands have no delimiter for rule 5 to see. §8.7.1 (arithmetic operators, with a unary exception) stays with
kb/Work PB2017; the class comment says so.

**Goldens.** Positive `2002/pb1430_operator_separator_spaces` (a line end on either side of `::` and `&` is a separator
space; `(3 4)` and `(5, 6)` argument separators; expected values from §8.4.3.4.4 GR1 and §8.8.3.3 GR2). Negatives
`pb1430-inline-invocation-empty-arguments` (2994), `pb1430-invocation-operator-spacing` and
`pb1430-concatenation-operator-spacing` (2993), all 2002/2014/2023. The below-2002 gate is the existing
