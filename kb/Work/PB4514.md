# PB4514 — De-editing a FLOATING '+' edited item drops its minus: PIC ++++9.99 holding '-1234.50' MOVEd to S9(5)V99 gives +1234.50

**Found:** 2026-11-03 by the Sonnet-written fuzz batch; adjudicated WISEOWL-DEFECT (Opus adjudicator, billed to the API key), and its reduced repro re-run on a judge built from main 3c8b2845c (`fuzz_triage.py verify`) still shows it.

**The adjudicator's finding.** De-editing a FLOATING '+' string drops the minus: PIC ++++9.99 holding '-1234.50' MOVEd to S9(5)V99 gives +1234.50 (expected -1234.50; ----9.99 is right) — fz00671, fz02080; repro probes/deed/prog.cob, fresh judge 'PLUS [-1234.50] +01234.50'. Site CobolEdit.cs:554 (DeEdit sets negative from a floating sign only when the mask has a floating '-', minus > 1).

**The rule** (each marked checked by the adjudicator; re-run `cite.py --check` before writing one into code): §14.9.25.4 r6 1. 'de-editing establishes the operand's numeric value, which may be signed' (checked); §3.56 de-editing (checked); §13.18.40.5 r7 b) '... all character positions of the item will contain the character asterisk, but the decimal separator ... will appear' (checked)

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolEdit.cs`:554 (`DeEdit`: the whole-image `minus > 1 && image.Contains('-')` scan is deleted; the sign of a floating '+' or '-' string is now read AT its digit-position branch, `c == '-' && p is '+' or '-'`), the golden `conformance-suite/cases/13.18.40.5-Editing-rules/pb4514_deedit_floating_plus_sign`, `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CobolEditStoredSignTests.cs` (`DeEdit_FloatingSignString_ReadsTheLandedSign`)

**Landed (2026-11-03).** The re-probe on this build printed `PLUS [-1234.50] +01234.50` before the fix and `-01234.50` after. The floating-point form (`CobolEdit.Float.cs` `DeEditFloat`) already read the sign at the position, so it needed nothing.
