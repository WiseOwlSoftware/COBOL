# PB4854 - the debug reach walk counts an implicit OCCURS DEPENDING ON item as a written reference: INITIALIZE leaves AllCount.Odo.Depending out of ImplicitOperands and SEARCH counts BoundSearch.DependItem while the table it names holds no place

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R3-1 LEAD (medium, not blocking at round 3): `BoundInitialize.cs:30` R2-3 is only partly fixed for INITIALIZE: `ImplicitOperands` names the receiving places but not the OCCURS DEPENDING ON item an `InitializeLoop` holds in `AllCount.Odo.Depending`, so the walk still takes it as written.

R3-2 LEAD (medium, not blocking at round 3): `BoundTree.cs:1974` The R2-3 sibling sweep missed SEARCH: `BoundSearch.DependItem`, the lowered ODO depending item, counts as a written reference, and the table the statement does name holds no place, so the section for the table does not run.

**Not probed.** Neither lead was reproduced by the clerk, and no program was compiled for this note.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundInitialize.cs` (`BoundInitialize.ImplicitOperands`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundTree.cs` (`BoundSearch.DependItem`).

Filed from review leads of waves 20261884 and 20261885 by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

**Landed (wave 20261887 A).** Re-probed on the current tree (`INITIALIZE TB` and `SEARCH E` over `E OCCURS 1 TO 5 DEPENDING ON N`, with ALL REFERENCES OF
N): both ran the section for N, which neither statement wrote. Fix: `AllCount.Places` is the one list of the places a count reads, and every
node that holds a count names them implicit (`IBoundImplicitOperands`): `BoundInitialize.ImplicitOperands` (the loops' counts, also with no
written place), `BoundSearch.ImplicitOperands` (`DependItem`), `BoundTableSort.ImplicitOperands` (`Count`), and, by the sibling sweep,
`BoundMove.ImplicitOperands` (the element moves of a group move, whose counts and element places the program did not write). Witness:
`pb4854-debug-odo-count-is-implicit` (accept 85) and `pb4854-debug-odo-count-removed-2002`. The lead's second sentence (the TABLE that SEARCH
names holds no place, so its own section does not run) is the whole-table family of PB4855, which stays open for it.
