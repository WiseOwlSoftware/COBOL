# PB3158 — ALIGNED in a bit group that starts MID-byte aligns to the group, not the record

Filed by the clerk from the "New defects" section of `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1077d-PB3156-report.md`
(branch worktree-wf_84f0c364-28e-4, landed as PR #62). cites PB3156, which left this open as its lead.
Grepped kb/Work for `RoundUpToByte|mid-byte` before filing: PB43, PB1277, PB1280, PB1572 are all landed and a different
mechanism; no open note covers it.

**Mechanism.** When a GROUP-USAGE BIT group itself begins in the middle of a byte of the record (it follows bit items in an
enclosing bit group), an ALIGNED item inside it is aligned on a byte boundary counted from the inner group's origin, not
from a byte boundary of the record. §13.18.1.4 GR1 requires the record's own byte boundary.
**Symptoms.** Wrong answer: `FUNCTION LENGTH` 31 where 29 is right. Crash: `MOVE` of the alphanumeric-group form throws
`ArgumentOutOfRangeException`. Stage: `FUNCTION BYTE-LENGTH` refuses with "fixed extent is negative".
**Sites.** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/BitLayout.cs` `Walk`, `RoundUpToByte(cursor)` taken relative to the group
(it also rounds "all other bit data items" inside a mid-byte bit group relative to its origin, ~:266 and ~:319), and
`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/IntrinsicBinder.cs` `VariableLengthGroupSum` char path. The walk needs to
take an absolute origin (the offset in bits of the group within the record).

**Rule.** §13.18.1.4 GR1, checked with `cite.py --check 13.18.1.4 "first available byte boundary"` (OK, "General rules" item 1):
"An ALIGNED clause causes the subject of the entry to be aligned on the first bit of the first available byte boundary.
Implicit filler bits may be generated to complete the assignment of bits as described in 8.5.1.6." Layout filler: §8.5.1.6.3.

## Landed 2026-10-14 (w1078 E)

Re-probed on a fresh build, both repros reproduced: repro 1 answered LENGTH 31 for both the ODO group and its fixed twin
(29 is right: BA bits 0-1, SA bit 2, TA(k) at bits 8, 16, 24, the last ending at bit 28) and staged BYTE-LENGTH ("fixed
extent is negative"); repro 2 threw `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` at the MOVE.
**Root cause.** `BitLayout.Walk` kept its cursor RELATIVE to the group it walked, so every `RoundUpToByte` was a boundary of
the GROUP. A GROUP-USAGE BIT group that follows a same-level bit item begins mid-byte (§8.5.1.6.3), and an ALIGNED subject
(§13.18.1.4 GR1) or any "other bit data item" belongs on a byte of the RECORD. The layout also rounded the group's FIRST
child up, which the old origin (0) hid. **Fix.** `BitLayout.PhaseOf(item)` is where in a byte a group begins; the walk starts
its cursor there (extents and starts it reports stay group-relative, so every caller that adds an origin is unchanged) and a
first bit child begins where its group begins (`KeepsPosition`, the subject conjuncts of `SharesByteWith`, §8.5.1.6.3 "the
alignment of the start of a group item and the alignment of the first item within that group ... are at the same bit
position"). The public `WidthBits` / `StrideBits` / `RunBits` / `TableBits` / `ExtentBits` read the explicit-phase `...At`
forms at `PhaseOf(item)`; the walk passes the phase down and never asks `PhaseOf` per child (that is exponential in a run of
same-level groups; `BitGroupPhaseDriftTests`). `IntrinsicBinder.VariableLengthGroupSum` needed no change: its prefix is
`StartBitOf`, now the record's. Determination (docs/CONFORMANCE.md 4.2.6 USAGE BIT): a TABLE of mid-byte bit groups lays every
occurrence out as the first, so only the first occurrence's ALIGNED members sit on record byte boundaries.
Sibling sweep: no code outside `BitLayout.cs` rounds a bit placement (`BitRunImageDriftTests.NoCodeOutsideBitLayout...`); the
by-reference alignment screen (`ParameterConformance`, `StartBitWithin` summed up the chain) now accepts an ALIGNED item
in a mid-byte group (it refused it at bit 10) - negative golden `pb3158-call-bit-unaligned-after-aligned`. Golden
`2002/pb3158_aligned_in_mid_byte_bit_group` (DISPLAY, MOVE to a bit item and to an alphanumeric item, LENGTH, BYTE-LENGTH,
an ODO table, the fixed twin, a three-level nest); `BitRunImageDriftTests` BRD09 / BRD10 (the Tier-B alias lane).
Found and left open (a different mechanism, reported as a lead): `05 RSUB2 REDEFINES RSUB GROUP-USAGE BIT` where RSUB is a
GROUP-USAGE BIT group does not compile (CS0103 'RSUB' does not exist), with or without ALIGNED.
