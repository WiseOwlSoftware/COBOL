# PB4858 - USE FOR DEBUGGING ON DEBUG-CONTENTS (1:12) is refused COBOL1639 and a special register or sum counter target is refused COBOL1571; the legality of both is unadjudicated; DB205A does not finish

**What the report says.** Round-1 lead (stands): DEBUG-CONTENTS `(1:12)` is COBOL1639 (`ReferenceResolver.cs`, `DebugRegisters` early return); a special register or sum counter target is COBOL1571 (`ProcedureTableBuilder.AddDebugDataTarget`); DB205A does not finish (communication facility, PB4677 neighbour); PB4811 should reuse `DebugTriggerEmitter`/`DebugWhen`.

**Legality is unadjudicated.** Whether the standard (or the 1985 debug module) permits a reference-modified DEBUG-CONTENTS, a special register or a sum counter as a debugging target has not been decided; this note records the refusals only. That is why `rejects_legal_source` is a claim to settle, not a finding.

**Not probed.** The clerk reproduced none of the three. DB205A not finishing is recorded as the report states it, a neighbour of PB4677; its cause is not known.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs` (`ReferenceResolver (DebugRegisters early return)`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs` (`ProcedureTableBuilder.AddDebugDataTarget`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ImplicitRegisters.cs` (`ImplicitRegisters.DebugRegisters`).

Filed from review leads of waves 20261884 and 20261885 by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

**Adjudicated and partly landed (wave 20261887 A).** The 1985 text decides all three (PDF page 693, syntax rules 9 to 12; page 696, rules 10 and 11):
- `DEBUG-CONTENTS (1:12)` is LEGAL. Syntax rule 12 bars a reference modifier on identifier-1 of a USE statement, and rule 11 restricts DEBUG-ITEM
  references to a debugging section, but a statement in the section may reference-modify DEBUG-CONTENTS, which rule 10 describes as `PICTURE X(n)`.
  Fixed: `ReferenceResolver.Identify` (the DEBUG-ITEM arm) lets the register meet the reference-modification tail (`Identified.ImplicitPlace`); witness
  `pb4811-debug-file-name-triggers` reads `DEBUG-CONTENTS (1:8)` and `DEBUG-NAME (1:2)`.
- A REPORT SECTION sum counter as identifier-1 is LEGAL: syntax rule 9, "Identifier-1 must not reference any data item defined in the Report Section
  except sum counters". Fixed: `ProcedureTableBuilder.SumCounterOf` names the counter's register, which every `ReportSumCounterPlace` carries, so
  the planner matches its references and stores; witness `pb4858-debug-sum-counter-target` (accept 85), `pb4858-debug-sum-counter-removed-2002`.
- DB205A does not finish because it is an OPERATOR-DRIVEN program, not because of the debugging facility: with the MCS of `tests/nist/data/mcs.json` it
  compiles and runs to RECEIVE-TEST-2, writes "ENTER ONE MESSAGE NOW." to its terminal file and waits for a message (a RECEIVE with no NO DATA phrase
  waits for data), exactly as CM101M, CM104M and CM202M do, which `CommunicationCcvsTests` therefore only compiles. Its debugging section runs.
- STILL OPEN: LINAGE-COUNTER, LINE-COUNTER and PAGE-COUNTER as identifier-1 (legal on the text: they are special-register data items, and nothing in
  the format excludes them) stay the staged COBOL1571. The statement binder routes them to `BoundLinageCounterRef` and `BoundReportCounterRef`, which are
  no places, so the reach walk has nothing to match a target against; the fix is a reference identity for those two nodes
  (`BoundReferences` yields a place or a register identity), after which `AddDebugDataTarget` accepts them.
- Sites (landed): `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs` (`ReferenceResolver.Identify`, `ResolveImplCore`, `Identified`),
  `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs` (`ProcedureTableBuilder.SumCounterOf`, `NamesDataItem`, `AddDebugDataTarget`).
- Review round 1 (R1-3): a store into DEBUG-ITEM or a member, whole or reference-modified, is COBOL6361 (general rule 11: the facility alone updates the register). Sites: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ExpressionBinder.cs` (`ExpressionBinder.ReceivingPlaceBar`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (`DiagnosticCatalog.DebugRegisterReceiving`, `DebugSubFacilityStaged`); witness `pb4858-debug-register-receiving`.
- Still open, lead R1-4: syntax rule 11 (DEBUG-ITEM references only from within a debugging section) is not enforced; `DEBUG-CONTENTS` in nondeclarative code compiles at `--std 85` (`ReferenceResolver.cs` the DEBUG-ITEM arm of `Identify`, which has no bind-position section to test).
