# PB3112 — the Sweep-trait drift check never runs in Windows leg 1

Source: `w1069l-PB3103-report.md` "## Finisher"; enqueue unit 1517's late lead L6.

`[Trait("Sweep", "tree")]` classes are over 2.0 s, in 93 classes**. The figure moves with the store: the review's
count against the 05:44Z store was 282 cases in 97 classes. This note first said "31 classes, 246 cases", which was a

wrong metric: 53 classes have a case outside tier 0c while their class mean is under the cap, e.g.

121 s, `ProcessObservationDriftTests` 84 s, `ExternalCorpusPopulationDriftTests` 82 s, `BoundDeferralDriftTests`
and `OperandClassScreenDriftTests` 62 s. The probe is `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\resume-2026-10-12\sweep_cap_probe.py
<repo>` (it imports `gate_plan`'s own `name_key`, `class_of`, `class_traits`, `trait_sets` and `Timings`). Each such
red costs a whole landing attempt (both gates), a finisher and a review. The fix must cover every sweep over the cap,
