# PB1687 — OPEN

Filed by the train-70 lander from wave 70 V3's report (implementer of PB1509; `w70v3-PB1509-report.md`, lead 3).

## Finding

**Repro.** `E:\Temp\claude\E--COBOL\73cac64c-391d-42c0-bcdf-c880e3e10305\scratchpad\w70v3\p\neg85.cob`: a report
entry `PIC 9(20) SUM …` compiles at `--std 85` with no diagnostic.

**Rule** (`cite.py --check` OK). §13.18.40.3 14): "For data items of category numeric, and for fixed-point data items
of category numeric-edited, the number of digit positions described by character-string-1 shall range from 1 through
31." — the 2023 limit; the COBOL-85 edition's limit is 18 digits, which WORKING-STORAGE items already enforce through
`Edition.CheckDigitCapacity` → COBOLNET0802.

**Code sites.** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs:1909` and `:2490` bind a report entry's
PICTURE with no `Edition.CheckDigitCapacity` call (compare the data-division path, `DataBinder.cs:4877`).

**Mechanism.** The digit-capacity gate is written at the data-division binder only; the REPORT SECTION's own
picture binding is a second place the PICTURE rule is applied without it. Route report-entry pictures through the one
gate (a sweep of every other PICTURE-binding site belongs to the fix).
