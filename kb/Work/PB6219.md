# PB6219 — one mechanism, five sites still pinned to the 2023 rule

**Mechanism.** The PB4950 rule: a diagnostic cites the rule of the edition being compiled under (`EditionInfo.Cite` / `CiteRule`,
one argument per edition). PB6175 (landed) fixed three sites; two later branches (PB5753, PB6082) found more of the same, so this is
one open register of them rather than a finding on the landed note. PB4950 and PB6175 are not edited.

1. **`OoExpansion`** (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Oo/OoExpansion.cs`, `Validate`, `ReparseFailed`, `ImportedSkeleton` and the
   duplicate-name arms of `Expand`) prints `§2023:12.3.8.3 SR4/SR7`, `§2023:12.3.8.4 GR5/GR8`, `§2023:9.3.12/9.3.13`,
   `§2023:8.4.6.4 / §2023:8.3.2.2` at every edition. 2002's own are `§2002:12.2.7.2 SR4/SR7` and `§2002:12.2.7.3 GR5/GR8`
   (re-derive each, and 2014's, with `cite.py --check` before it goes in). Fix shape: a `HeaderRules`-style per-edition table.
2. **`UniqueReferenceRule.Citation`** (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/UniqueReferenceRule.cs`):
   `edition.Cite("§1985:IV.4.3.8", "§2023:8.4.2.1")` has no 2002 (`§2002:8.4.1`) or 2014 (`§2014:8.4.1`) argument. About 40 test files
   assert the 2023 text and move with it.
3. **`EcBinder.EcBindRaising`** (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/EcBinder.cs:258`, "; GR1 b) 2.") and the COBOL0717
   text (`:292`, "Table 13") cite 2023's general-rule and table numbers at 2002 and 2014.
4. **`RaisingPhrase`** (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/RaisingPhrase.cs:62`): the procedure-division-header RAISING refusal
   COBOL0858, and the COBOL text at `:97`, hard-codes §2023:14.2.2 SR7 at every edition.
5. **`CiteRule` calls whose argument for an edition is another edition's rule.** The scan
   `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20262171i/scan_citerule.py src` lists: `CallBinder.cs:1104` (the 1985 argument is §2023:14.9.4.3 SR14),
   `FileControlBinder.Resolve.cs:435`, `ReportWriterBinder.cs:321`, `PictureAnalyzer.cs:581`, `:1412`, `:1467`,
   `ResolveRedefinesPass.cs:209`, `:532`, `IntrinsicBinder.cs:80`, `DataBinder.cs:1233`, and `ExternalFileConnectorRules.cs:90` (the
   later-edition argument is a 2002 rule). Each should be the edition's own rule or `CiteRuleFrom2002`.

**Fix shape.** Fix the five sites and add the drift test the PB6082 report names: every literal argument of a `CiteRule` call
carries its own edition prefix (a Roslyn or text scan, with the scan script as the starting point). Re-run the scan after the fix;
its zero is the evidence.

**Sites:** the files and lines above.
