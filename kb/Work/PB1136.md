### Refuter evidence — row `GR-8.3.3.5.4-4` (PARTIAL)

PARTIAL — The rule gives an NX literal's value as one national character per hex-character-sequence (4 digits under D-N1) in EVERY context. The main codec (CobolLiteral.Decode -> DecodeHexGroups(body,4)) does this. But the INVOKE / inline-invocation method-name path OoBinder.OoDecodeMethodNameLiteral does not route an NX literal through Decode: it strips the first character and decodes the remaining X"..." with 2-digit (alphanumeric) groups. So NX"00530048004F0057" becomes NUL,S,NUL,H,NUL,O,NUL,W instead of SHOW. Â§14.9.23.3 SR2 makes a national literal legal there, so legal source is refused because of the wrong value. This is a sibling-copy (two-arm dispatch) defect of the codec this row cites.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Common/CobolLiteral.cs#Decode; src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Common/CobolLiteral.cs#DecodeHexGroups
