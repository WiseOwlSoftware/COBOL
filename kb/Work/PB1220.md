## Landed (wave 1005 group A, 2026-10-03)

**Surface.** `reportColumnClause` is now `(COLUMN | COLUMNS | COL | COLS) (NUMBER | NUMBERS)? (LEFT | CENTER | RIGHT)? (IS | ARE)? reportColumnOperand+`
(`CobolReportWriter.g4`). The PDF diagram (printed p386, PDF p416, rendered) prints the alignment as a brace, but SR9's second sentence
("If any of the operands is absolute and neither LEFT, CENTER, nor RIGHT is specified, LEFT is assumed", `cite.py --check` OK 9)) licenses
its omission, so the grammar keeps it optional. CENTER is a context-sensitive word (§8.10) and became a lexer token through the
data-driven path (`tests/version-matrix/cobol-words.json` -> `scripts/gen-cobol-words.ps1`: `CobolWords.g4`, `CobolLexerWordSet.g.cs`), so
`01 CENTER PIC X(3)` and `CENTER-X` still compile at every edition (probed at 2023 and 85).

**Mechanism (the ONE computation).** `ReportColumnSpec` gained `Alignment`; `ReportColumnSpec.AbsoluteLeftmost(printableSize)` is GR6 b)-d)
written once (LEFT integer-1; RIGHT integer-1 - size + 1; CENTER integer-1 - (size - 1) / 2, which equals both the odd and the even
printed formulas) and `AbsoluteRightmost` is GR9's counter value. The emitter's single-absolute fast path, its Absolute arm and the
bind-time line-width walk all call it (the old `ReportFieldModel.Column` property, which returned the bare integer-1, was deleted with its one
caller). SR9's absolute-only sentence is COBOLNET2711; an aligned item whose leftmost column falls before column 1 is COBOLNET2712 (the
standard states no outcome; `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` section 3 "COLUMN alignment"). The phrase gates 2002 with the other COLUMN forms (construct
`report-multi-column-2002`: "everything else gates 2002" past the 85 `COLUMN NUMBER IS integer-1`).

**Goldens.** Positive `conformance:2002/pb1220_column_alignment` (RIGHT / CENTER odd / CENTER even / LEFT IS / bare, then relative items after
aligned ones for GR9, then `COLUMNS CENTER ARE 5 15 25`; expected text derived by hand from the quoted formulas BEFORE the run). Negatives
`pb1220-column-alignment-relative` (COBOLNET2711), `pb1220-column-alignment-left-of-line` (COBOLNET2712) and `pb1220-column-alignment-at-85`
(COBOLNET0900).

**Siblings swept.** The `LINE` clause (`#reportLineClause`, PB1221) has no alignment word; `NEXT GROUP` none. The screen COLUMN clause (Format 2)
is a different production (`screenColumnClause`) and unchanged. The `COLUMN`-clause consumers of `Columns[].Value`
(`RepeatedPlacements` shifts an absolute operand by STEP, which moves an aligned item's leftmost column by the same amount, so alignment
survives a repeating entry; the diagnostic text at `col`) were read and need no change.
