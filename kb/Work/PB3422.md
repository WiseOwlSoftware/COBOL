# PB3422 — the leads of one branch are filed once per filer, not once

Found by the orchestrator loop's enqueue unit on 2026-10-19 (~00:55 PDT). It had just filed two leads that already
existed. The operator retired them on `notes-retire-pb3417-pb3418-20261019`.

**What happened (PR #268, `worktree-wf_14a1e62b-326-31`, PB3336 with PB3334 and PB3335).**
- 23:08 PDT 10-18: the previous enqueue unit's clerk filed the A6 report's round-2 LEADs as PB3409-PB3411 (`eca78ac98`,
  PR #275). The branch had not landed yet; its PR bounced and was re-landed by a finisher.
- 23:23: the Mythos lane filed the A5 report's R5-L1 and R5-L2 as PB3414 and PB3415 (`19fe6fde9`, on
  `notes-pb3381-leads-pb3380-landed-20261018`, PR #276, then awaiting review).
- 00:10 10-19: this enqueue unit read `land_lane.py status`. The newest attempt for -326-31
  (`20261019T060449380904Z`, ENQUEUED after the bounce finisher) was not yet collected, so it took that attempt's
  report leads as unfiled. It handed the same leads to a clerk, which filed them again as PB3417 (= PB3410) and PB3418
  (= PB3414 + PB3415) in PR #278.

**The mechanism.**
1. Step 5 of `scripts/orchestrator/units/enqueue.md` selects "the reports of the branches the lane queued (step 3's
   DONE lines, not yet collected)". But `land_lane.py collect` marks an ATTEMPT. A branch landed again (after a
   bounce, a conflict or a red gate) gets a new attempt, which reads uncollected while its report's leads were already
   filed against the earlier one. Any filer outside the unit (the Mythos lane, the operator) collects nothing at all.
2. The report keeps no record that a lead was filed. The `LEAD` lines under `## Review findings` and the `## New
   defects` paragraphs stay as written, with no `filed as PBnnnn` beside them, so every reader of the report sees the
   same open leads.
3. MANDATORY-PRACTICES R1 (grep `kb/Work/` first) cannot catch either case. The clerk's worktree was cut at
   `0eba56a75` (06:19Z), before PR #275 merged PB3410 at 07:14Z. PB3414 and PB3415 were on an unlanded branch. The
   enqueue unit's own check grepped one in-flight notes branch, but piped the output through `head`, which cut the
   PB3410 hit (memory `search-register-before-filing`: untruncated). A grep of main alone never sees a lead filed by
   a branch still in the queue.

**Harm.** No user's program is affected. It costs register churn (two notes filed, reviewed, landed and retired), a
clerk dispatch (~98k tokens), and a reviewer and a landing for the retirement. It recurs whenever a branch lands
twice or two lanes read one report.

**Fix (one record, at the source).** The report records the filing: whoever files a lead writes `filed as PBnnnn` (or
`extends PBnnnn`) on that lead's line, through ONE writer (a `review_findings.py`-style command, so the format stays
machine-readable). Step 5, the clerk brief and the Mythos lane skip a lead marked that way. Step 5 then selects by
"report leads not yet marked", never by "attempt not yet collected". R1's grep also covers the open notes branches
(`origin/notes-*`, and every branch the survey lists IN FLIGHT or ENQUEUED), not only the worktree's base. Add a
drift test: a report with a marked lead yields no lead to step 5.

## Second instance 2026-10-19 (PB3395, wave 1127): the register search before filing was skipped again

The filer of PB3395 did no register search before filing (none by `--release` or `record_dispatch`, the mechanism's own
terms), and PB3395 duplicated PB3114. This repeats PB3360's miss (retired the same way on 2026-10-17: its filing grepped
for `--release` and `record_dispatch` and found only PB3186, though PB3114 contains both). PB3395 was retired as a
duplicate and its instance folded into PB3114. Two retirements of one duplicate in two days show the search is left to
each filer's discipline. The fix shape of this note applies: R1's search is run by the filing tool (one command that
greps `kb/Work/` on main and the open notes branches for the note's terms and refuses to allocate while a hit is
unread), not by prose in a brief. No new note is filed.
