# PB4435 - operand-due COBOL5506 offers a constant-name at --std 85

**Defect.** The new operand-due arm in `AdmittedKinds` (AdmittedKinds.cs:160) names 'a user-defined word' whenever the
parser's superset admits IDENTIFIER, without asking whether the compile edition prints one there, so under `--std 85`
the OCCURS refusal offers a constant-name; constant entries first exist at 2002 (the report's words).

**Witness.** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w20261823a-PB4299-report.md`, R1-2 and New defects 2 (same root).

**Fix direction (the report's).** A new edition read in `CobolParserCoreBase` beside `PrintsKeyword`, plus a verdict row
in `EditionReadDriftTests`. The finisher left it because AdmittedKinds.cs is not a file its change touches.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs#PrintsKeyword`
