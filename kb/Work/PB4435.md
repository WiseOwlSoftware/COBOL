# PB4435 - operand-due COBOL5506 offers a constant-name at --std 85

**Defect.** The new operand-due arm in `AdmittedKinds` (AdmittedKinds.cs:160) names 'a user-defined word' whenever the
parser's superset admits IDENTIFIER, without asking whether the compile edition prints one there, so under `--std 85`
the OCCURS refusal offers a constant-name; constant entries first exist at 2002 (the report's words).

**Witness.** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w20261823a-PB4299-report.md`, R1-2 and New defects 2 (same root).

**Fix direction (the report's).** A new edition read in `CobolParserCoreBase` beside `PrintsKeyword`, plus a verdict row
in `EditionReadDriftTests`. The finisher left it because AdmittedKinds.cs is not a file its change touches.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs#PrintsKeyword`

## Landed (wave 20261826 group A)

**Re-probe** (`OCCURS 0 TO TIMES DEPENDING ON N` at `--std 85`, built tree 20f9715ba): "The word TIMES is not expected
here: expected a user-defined word or an integer literal (ISO §5.2.1)." Reproduced. The sibling sweep found the same
fault in the binder: at 85, `OCCURS K TIMES` with K a data-name drew COBOL1547 "an integer-n position admits an integer
literal or an integer constant-name", and the PICTURE repetition refusal said the same (with a wrong citation,
§13.18.40.2, where the rule is §13.18.40.3 SR6).

**Rule.** §13.10.3 SR2: "constant-name-1 may be used anywhere that a format specifies a literal of the class and
category of constant-name-1"; §5.5 1) makes integer-n a fixed-point integer literal; the constant entry is COBOL-2002's
(constant-entry-2002). So a user-defined word in a literal position is a constant-name, and COBOL-85 has none.

**Fix.** The walk decides whether the word is a constant-name, from the ATN, never from a list of positions:
`ResimulationWalk.EveryWordStandsForALiteral` asks whether every configuration taking IDENTIFIER takes it in
`integerOperand` or `valueTableSubscript` (the two rules whose word stands only for a literal; a symbolic-character, an
85 word, may stand in the others), carried as `Admitted.WordsStandForLiterals`. `CobolParserCoreBase.PrintsConstantName`
is the edition read (recorded in `EditionReadDriftTests` under a new verdict, `DiagnosisOnly`, which `PrintsKeyword`
now shares). `AdmittedKinds.Refusal` takes `aUserWordIsPrinted` and leaves the word out of the operand-due sentence.
The binder's twin is `DataBinder.ConstantNamesExist`, read by `ConstantIntegerOperand` and `ExpandPicConstants`.

**Goldens.** `13.18.38-OCCURS/pb4435-occurs-bound-omitted-at-85` (refused at 85, the literal alone),
`pb4301-occurs-to-without-integer-2` narrowed to 2002+ (the word and the literal), and
`pb4435-occurs-bound-data-name-at-85` (COBOL1547 at 85 without a constant-name). Unit rows in `WrittenKindRefusalTests`
(85, 2002, and an ADD data-name control at 85 that still names the word).
