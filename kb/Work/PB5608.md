# PB5608 - at --std 2002 a REPLACE partial-word holding a character outside the COBOL character repertoire is accepted

**Sites:**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#Content` (ReplacingOperandContent, COBOL2572)

**Mechanism.** A 2002 text-word is a sequence of contiguous COBOL characters; the 2023 text-word is any sequence from the compile-time coded character set. The REPLACE/COPY REPLACING operand content check counts text-words on the 2023 definition at every edition, so ==Y¤Z== is one text-word at 2002.

**Inventory.** Rows claimed: `2002-SR-7.1.3.2-6`.

**Rules (each citation checked with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`, verdict OK).**

- `§2002:7.1.3.2 SR6`: "Partial-word-2 shall consist of zero or one text-word"
- `§2002:7.1.1.4`: "any other sequence of contiguous COBOL characters bounded by separators"

**Repro.** Scratch originals under `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-20261114/read-2002/defects-work/` (p/rp-nonc.cob); the text is inline. Command: `cobol.exe p/rp-nonc.cob --std 2002 --run`

`p/rp-nonc.cob`:

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. RPNONC.
       REPLACE LEADING ==XX== BY ==Y¤Z==.
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01  W   PIC X VALUE "A".
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           DISPLAY W.
           STOP RUN.
```

**Observed.** compiles and prints A, rc=0 (the same compiler refuses U+00A4 in a COBOL word at 2002, COBOL2773).

**Expected (from the 2002 text).** a diagnostic: partial-word-2 is not zero or one text-word.

**Sibling sweep.** every 7.1.2/7.1.3 pseudo-text and partial-word rule (the SR4 sibling: reader's rp-nonc3.cob for pseudo-text-2).

**Other editions.** 85: unknown (1985 text-word definition). 2014: unknown.
