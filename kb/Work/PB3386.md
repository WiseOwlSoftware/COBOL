# PB3386 — a merged SPLIT predecessor reads DECISION

A lead from the Mythos lane's PB3381 implementer (report `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1125a-PB3381-report.md`,
"New defects"), assigned to the operator's clerk. It predates PB3381.

**The site.** `scripts/prune_worktrees.py` `contained()` (about lines 678-695 at main 38926bffb) marks a branch IN
FLIGHT, carried, when its tip is an ancestor of a carrier's tip. Its skip condition passes over every DECISION row that
has a report unless that report is FINDINGS-stamped:

    or (r.state == 'DECISION' and r.report is not None and not findings)

**What goes wrong.** A SPLIT report puts its branch in DECISION (lifecycle: "a SPLIT report (DECISION)"). When a
successor or a finisher MERGES that branch, the predecessor's work lands through the carrier. Yet `contained()` skips the row because its
report is SPLIT, not FINDINGS, so the survey still prints a DECISION row. The operator is asked to land or abandon
work that is already on its way to main, and `--abandon` on it would record a false decision. PB3170 is the planner's
side of the same blind spot, for a cherry-picked SPLIT predecessor, where ancestry cannot see the carry at all.

**The fix.** `contained()` carries any DECISION row whose tip a carrier contains, whatever its report says. The
FINDINGS arm stays as it is, and a SPLIT report is carried the same way. A self-test pins a SPLIT-stamped predecessor
merged into a WAITING TO LAND successor, which must read IN FLIGHT with the successor named as its carrier.
