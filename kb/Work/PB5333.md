# PB5333 - the citations RESOLVE refuses that no mechanical rewrite owns

**Sites:**

- `scripts/spec/data/cite-resolve-baseline.json` (whole: the entries outside PB5318's class, below, each naming its file,
  citation and failure kind; each repair deletes its entry)

The CITATIONS audit's `--sweep <json>` prints each entry's message and, where the checker finds it, the right citation.

**Mechanism.** kb/Work PB5317 made `cite.resolve` check every part of a citation in every edition and gave the CITATIONS
audit its RESOLVE check, whose baseline holds every citation the repository held that the checker refuses, as debt that may
only shrink. PB5318 owns one class of it: a 2023 citation of a rule written under the statement's clause or a sibling
clause (`§2023:14.9.39 SR17` for `§2023:14.9.39.3 SR17`), whose repair RESOLVE prints and a scripted rewrite can apply. The
rest is this note's: citations whose rule number, format, table or sub-item is wrong, or whose quotation sits under another
rule, each a judgment (is the citation wrong, or the quotation?), so no rewrite can apply them blind.

**The population** (measured at the branch's head, 2026-11-11, origin/main merged, after review round 1: RESOLVE reads a
citation in backticks or quotes, R1-3, and no longer pairs a citation with a quotation that belongs to the next sentence or
the next rule named): the baseline's 1,617 entries are PB5318's 1,514 (edition 2023, `no-rule-list`, the message naming
the cited clause's subclause or sibling clause) and this note's 103: 60 distinct citations in 66 files, 62 of them with
the right citation printed.

- by kind: no-rule-list 35 (a clause with no rules of the cited kind, no repair found) · no-format 28 · no-table 21 ·
  no-ordinal 10 · text-not-in-rule 6 · no-sub-item 3
- by edition: 2023 92 · 1985 7 · 2002 4
- by directory: src 38 · kb 33 · docs 24 · tests 5 · conformance-suite 3

**The 1985 seven.** PB5317's sweep found eight 1985 failures. Three of them were the pairing's false positives, not wrong
citations (§1985:XIV.3.3.4 GR2 in PB5286 is right: the quotation it was read against is GR3's; §1985:VI.6.19.4 GR3 in
PB5176 and its store record is right: the quotation is the GR4b the prose names next), and RESOLVE no longer refuses
them. Five remain, and PB4887's lines 53 and 61 joined them when RESOLVE began reading backticks:

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ConstructRegistry.g.cs`: §1985:IV.4.2.2.2.3 SR1 and SR2 (no-ordinal; RESOLVE suggests GR1 and
  GR2: the catalog numbers that mixed block's syntax rules as its third list, `SRL3.1`, so either the generator's citation
  or the catalog's numbering of a mixed block is wrong, and that is decided first)
- `kb/Work/PB4887.md` lines 2, 53 and 61: §1985:VI.5.3.2 GR3 (no-rule-list; VI.5.3.2 is a general format: §1985:VI.5.3.4 GR3)
- the store records `tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/1985-SR-II.6.4.1.2-3.json` and `1985-SR-II.6.4.1.2-4.json`:
  §1985:II.6.4.1.3 SR3 and SR2 (no-rule-list; the paragraph has no syntax rules; RESOLVE suggests §1985:II.6.4.1.2 SR3 and
  SR2, the rows' own clause)

**The 2002 four**, refused against the 2002 rule catalog (kb/Work PB5256): §2002:9.1.12.1 GR2a
(`docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md`), §2002:14.8.4 SR2 (`kb/Work/PB4887.md`; §2002:14.8.4.2 SR2), §2002:13.16.55 SR9
(`kb/Work/PB4952.md`; §2002:13.16.55.2 SR9) and §2002:14.5.2.1.2.1 GR1 (the store record
`tests/version-matrix/inventory/GR-14.6.2.3.2-1.json`).

**Why it matters.** The inventory records are adjudication evidence, and the src and docs/CONFORMANCE.md entries are the
citations a reader looks up: `§2023:14.6.2.3.2 GR7` (InitializeBinder.cs, ProgramEmitter.cs, docs/CONFORMANCE.md) names a
rule the clause does not have, and `§2023:14.2.3 GR8` (CallEmitter.cs, CobolVarGroup.cs) quotes the text of GR9. The
baseline holds every one GREEN, so nothing else schedules the repair, and the next reader inherits the wrong number
(PB5317's failure mode).

**Fix direction.** Per entry: read the message (`--sweep`), check the cited text with `cite.py --check`, and correct the
citation where it is wrong (or the quotation, where the citation is right); a store record's citation is corrected through
its tool, never by hand, and the ConstructRegistry pair is fixed in its generator after the catalog's mixed-block numbering
is decided. Each repair deletes its entry (`audit_code_citations.py --write-baseline`, which refuses to add one). Done when
no baseline entry outside PB5318's class remains. The 1,514 PB5318 entries are not rewritten here: a scripted rewrite
takes them.
