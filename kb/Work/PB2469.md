**Shape.** §8.5.1.12.1 defines a variable-length group ("a group item whose data description has at least one
dynamic-length elementary item or dynamic-capacity table as a subordinate item"). The ONE spelling of it is
`VariableLengthCompatibility.IsVariableLength(item)` (`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/VariableLengthCompatibility.cs`),
which `ItemCategory.GroupKindsOf` also subtracts from the alphanumeric group. These sites restate it as
`item.IsGroup && ReferenceResolver.HasVariableLengthSubordinate(item)` (line numbers as of the train-1034 base):

* `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:1153`
* `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SortKeyAdmission.cs:89`
* `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/StringUnstringBinder.cs:115`, `:189`, `:253`
* `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/FileModel.cs:627`
* `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/RecordLayout.cs:302`
* `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/DataItem.cs:974` (`ComposesCurrentExtent`, after PB244's wave-1033 landing)

(re-located by the lander on the train-1034 tree; `grep -rn "IsGroup && .*HasVariableLengthSubordinate" src`

**Why it matters.** Two spellings of one definition drift: PB947 was exactly that drift (a paraphrase of §3.11's
four exclusions gave a wrong letter). Today the two spellings agree, so the change is behavior-neutral.

**Fix shape.** A Roslyn rewrite of every `X.IsGroup && ReferenceResolver.HasVariableLengthSubordinate(X)` to
`VariableLengthCompatibility.IsVariableLength(X)` (roslyn-analysis skill), proven by the arch oracle
(`scripts/arch/compare_oracle.py` IDENTICAL), plus a drift test that refuses the two-term spelling outside
`VariableLengthCompatibility`. Natural home: the R1 architecture pass.
