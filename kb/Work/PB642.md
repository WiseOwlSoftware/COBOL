# PB642 — OPEN — a regression the instrument invented

Found 2026-09-05 attributing battery #48 (`<NEW scratch>/battery-48/`, run in the detached worktree at `6eaa98cf`,
train 5's head): `=== NIST: 352 MATCH, 1 REGRESSION(S) ===` naming IF137A (STANDARD-DEVIATION), the audit's one
finding being the same line, every other leg green and the GnuCOBOL differential at zero flips. This note is the
third face of [[PB473]] (the compare arm scoring a report it could not read as a wrong answer, battery #43) and
[[PB478]]: the evidence rules now separate "wrong answer" from "no answer", and this run produced a wrong answer
that was not one.

## The measurement (all on the battery worktree's own build at `6eaa98cf`)

- `/tmp/gf_forensics/IF137A/if137a.txt` (the program's print file, `cp -p`-preserved, mtime 03:55:17 = the
  run's end): 44 lines, "017 OF 017 TESTS WERE EXECUTED SUCCESSFULLY", and `tr -d '\r' | sed 's/ *$//; …'` of it
  is BYTE-EQUAL to the preserved `expected.norm` (1983 bytes). The compiler passed IF137A.
- `actual.norm` in the same forensics: 0 bytes, mtime 03:55:23 — six seconds after the print file's last write.
- `IF137A-stdout.txt` and `-stderr.txt`: both empty; the run's rc was 0 (the verdict took the "ran to
  completion, produced bytes, comparison said they differ" branch).
- Serial re-observation through the same group runner, three times: `IF137A: MATCH`. Twelve group runners for
  IF137A launched concurrently on the same worktree: `IF137A: MATCH` ×12, no stderr.

So at the moment the guard normalized `$WORK/if137a.txt` (between 03:55:19 and :23) the comparison returned a
difference for a file whose last write was at :17 and whose content equals the golden. The mechanism was not
reproduced; the shape is what the note records.

## Three holes, each its own fix

1. **The forensics do not preserve the evidence the verdict was scored on.** `guard_output_verdict` normalizes
   EVERY candidate into the one `$CMP/actual.norm`, so after the loop the file holds the LAST candidate tried
   (the empty stdout), and `guard_preserve` copies that. Candidate 1's normalized form — the thing that "differed"
   — is gone. Preserve per candidate (`actual.<basename>.norm`) and record which candidate each diff rc came from.
2. **A REGRESSION is scored on ONE observation under contention.** `guard-fast.sh` step 3b re-takes LOST
   observations ("contention can no longer corrupt a verdict — it can only LOSE one") — the premise this battery
   refutes. A non-match whose preserved report normalizes EQUAL to the golden is a contradiction the harness can
   detect itself, and any regression is rare enough to re-observe once at serial isolation before scoring:
   route `regression` verdicts through the same retry path as `no-verdict`, and score the second observation.
3. **The mechanism is unmeasured.** The candidates for a transient difference on a complete file: a truncated
   read while the writer's handle was not yet released (the .NET process exited, but the runtime's PRINT-FILE
   writer may be closed by a process-exit hook rather than the program's CLOSE — the compile log's
   `CBL0702: I/O operation on file 'PRINT-FILE' which has not been OPENed` warning says the file's lifecycle is
   unusual in this program), a Defender/indexer sharing conflict on a freshly written file, or MSYS `mktemp -d`
   returning a directory another group was still using. A stress probe (N-wide, hundreds of iterations, logging
   candidate-1's normalized form and the diff rc on every non-match) decides; hole 1 is its prerequisite.
