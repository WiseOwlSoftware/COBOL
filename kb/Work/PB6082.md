# PB6082 - an invalid EditionInfo reaches the compiler unchecked (CLAUDE.md rule 4; unresolvable fails loudly)

**Scenario.** The `Options.Edition` doc comment, the PB2619 note and its DEVLOG say a year outside `EditionInfo.All` is
unrepresentable at the driver. But `EditionInfo` is a public positional readonly record struct: `new EditionInfo(1999)`
and `default(EditionInfo)` (Year 0) both construct, and `CompilerDriver.Compile` validates nothing; it reads
`options.Edition.Year` straight into `Frontend.DialectLevel` and `EditionContext`. The required parameter makes `default` a
one-word way to pass Year 0, which the old `int DialectLevel = 2023` never produced.

**Target.** Either `EditionInfo` cannot hold a year outside `EditionInfo.All` (a private constructor behind
`EditionInfo.Of`, `default` refused at the first read), or `CompilerDriver.Compile` refuses loudly; the claim in the
comment, note and DEVLOG then holds. A test constructs each bad value and expects the refusal.
