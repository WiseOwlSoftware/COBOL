# PB5171 - at --std 85 the SPECIAL-NAMES paragraph takes its clauses in any order and a second CURRENCY SIGN or DECIMAL-POINT clause: no edition arm holds the 1985 sequence or the once-only clauses

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.cs` (`BindSpecialNames`, `SwitchBindDecimalPoint`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.Currency.cs` (`SwitchBindCurrency`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`VisitCurrencySignClause`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/ClosedFormatPass.cs` (`ScreenParagraphPeriods`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:VI.4.5.2 prints the switch entries, then ALPHABET, SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS and CLASS (each repeatable), then one CURRENCY SIGN and one DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA, and §1985:IV.2.1 fixes that sequence. At --std 85 the compiler accepts (1) a second CURRENCY SIGN clause (SwitchBindCurrency registers every clause; both signs then work in a PICTURE), (2) DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA twice, at every edition (no count in SwitchBindDecimalPoint or ClosedFormatPass), and (3) the clauses in any order. kb/Work PB1578 is the open analysis of the order for 2023; the 1985 text settles it for 1985. The row also records CLASS ... IN alphabet-name (kb/Work PB5172) and the lone second period (kb/Work PB5170); both close with this row.

**Expected (from the 1985 text, the record's).** a diagnostic at the second CURRENCY SIGN clause (line 7): §1985:VI.4.5.2 prints "[CURRENCY SIGN IS literal-6]" with no ellipsis, and §1985:IV.2.1.4 lets a bracketed portion be specified or omitted, §1985:IV.2.1.5 giving repetition only where an ellipsis stands ("the ellipsis (...) represents the position at which the user elects repetition of a portion of a format"). The same holds for the other four arms: the 1985 CLASS clause prints no IN phrase; "[DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA]" has no ellipsis; §1985:IV.2.1: "Clauses must be written in the sequence given in the general formats."; and the paragraph's second period stands inside the bracket that holds the clauses.

**Observed (the record's).** exit 0, no diagnostic; the program displays L12 M12. Also exit 0 with no diagnostic at --std 85 for a SPECIAL-NAMES paragraph that holds: ALPHABET A1 IS STANDARD-1 CLASS V IS 66 THRU 68 IN A1. (the class test then answers by the alphabet); DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA.; DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA CURRENCY SIGN IS "L" CLASS V IS "AEIOU" ALPHABET A1 IS NATIVE SWITCH-1 IS SW1.; and only a second period.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Give the SPECIAL-NAMES clauses a cardinality and sequence row in the ClauseCardinalities table (the mechanism ClosedFormatPass.ScreenClauseRepetition already applies to the other configuration paragraphs), with the 1985 sequence and once-only CURRENCY SIGN and DECIMAL-POINT as its 1985 arm, instead of per-clause counts in the binder.
