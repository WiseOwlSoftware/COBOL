For each candidate, render the printed format page (`scripts/render-spec-page.py <page>`), decide whether the word is plain
(optional) or underlined (required) there, and file or fix: a plain word the grammar demands is a PB3751-class defect
(rejects legal source), a word the page underlines is an audit false positive and gets an `ANCHORINGS` or examined-list entry.
