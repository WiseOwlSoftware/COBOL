# PB4501 — GnuCOBOL -std=cobol85 accepts sentences before the first paragraph-name

## Summary

ISO/IEC 1989:2002 Annex F.2 (substantive changes from the 1985 standard) item 90: "Paragraph-name. A paragraph-name is
not required at the beginning of the procedure division or a section." COBOL-85 therefore requires one. cobc 3.2 under
`-std=cobol85` accepts a procedure division that opens with a sentence, with no diagnostic even under `-Wall`; no dialect
option covers it.

## Minimal test case

Written for this note (not taken from the GnuCOBOL testsuite):

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. NOPARA.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           DISPLAY "NO PARAGRAPH-NAME".
           STOP RUN.
```

## Environment

GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 (`cobc (GnuCOBOL) 3.2.0`), Ubuntu under WSL2 on Windows 11, 2026-11-03.

## Observed result

`cobc -fsyntax-only -Wall -std=cobol85 nopara.cob`: no output, exit 0.

## Expected result (per ISO)

Under `-std=cobol85`, a diagnostic that the procedure division (or section) must begin with a paragraph-name (the 1985
format of the procedure division; the relaxation is 2002 Annex F.2 item 90, read in the archived 2002 text). From 2002 on
the program conforms (ISO/IEC 1989:2023 §14.4.3).

## Our compiler (WiseOwl)

COBOL0900 at `--std 85`: "sentences before the first paragraph-name of the procedure division or of a section requires
COBOL-2002 (targeting COBOL-85) — ... (ISO §14.4.3; ISO/IEC 1989:2002 Annex F.2 item 90)", from the F.2 edition edges of
kb/Work PB4305. Accepted at 2002 and later.

## Fix site (GnuCOBOL 3.2 source, read observationally)

`cobc/parser.y`, rule `procedure`: the `statements` alternative is accepted wherever `paragraph_header` is, with no
check that a paragraph or section header came first. A new dialect support option (unconformable in cobol85.conf, ok
elsewhere), verified when `statements` reduces before any header, would close it; `cobc/config.def` has no such option
today.

**Title:** -std=cobol85 accepts a PROCEDURE DIVISION that does not begin with a paragraph-name

cobc 3.2 with `-std=cobol85 -Wall` compiles a procedure division whose first sentence has no paragraph-name (and the
same for a section) with no diagnostic. COBOL-85 requires the paragraph-name; ISO/IEC 1989:2002 lists its omission as a
new feature (Annex F.2, item 90: "A paragraph-name is not required at the beginning of the procedure division or a
section"). Expected: under `-std=cobol85`, a dialect-controlled error or warning; `-std=cobol2002` and later accept it.
The `procedure` rule in parser.y accepts `statements` before any `paragraph_header`, and config.def has no support
option for it.
