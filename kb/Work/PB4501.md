# PB4501 — GnuCOBOL -std=cobol85 accepts sentences before the first paragraph-name

## Summary

ISO/IEC 1989:2002 Annex F.2 (substantive changes from the 1985 standard) item 90: "Paragraph-name. A paragraph-name is
not required at the beginning of the procedure division or a section." COBOL-85 therefore requires one. cobc 3.2 under
`-std=cobol85` accepts a procedure division that opens with a sentence, with no diagnostic even under `-Wall`; no dialect
option covers it.

## Minimal test case

Written for this note (not taken from the GnuCOBOL testsuite):

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. NOPARA.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           DISPLAY "NO PARAGRAPH-NAME".
           STOP RUN.
```

## Environment

GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 (`cobc (GnuCOBOL) 3.2.0`), Ubuntu under WSL2 on Windows 11, 2026-11-03.

## Observed result

`cobc -fsyntax-only -Wall -std=cobol85 nopara.cob`: no output, exit 0.

## Expected result (per ISO)

Under `-std=cobol85`, a diagnostic that the procedure division (or section) must begin with a paragraph-name (the 1985
format of the procedure division; the relaxation is 2002 Annex F.2 item 90, read in the archived 2002 text). From 2002 on
the program conforms (ISO/IEC 1989:2023 §14.4.3).

## Our compiler (WiseOwl)

COBOL0900 at `--std 85`: "sentences before the first paragraph-name of the procedure division or of a section requires
COBOL-2002 (targeting COBOL-85) — ... (ISO §14.4.3; ISO/IEC 1989:2002 Annex F.2 item 90)", from the F.2 edition edges of
kb/Work PB4305. Accepted at 2002 and later.

## Fix site (GnuCOBOL 3.2 source, read observationally)

`cobc/parser.y`, rule `procedure`: the `statements` alternative is accepted wherever `paragraph_header` is, with no
check that a paragraph or section header came first. A new dialect support option (unconformable in cobol85.conf, ok
elsewhere), verified when `statements` reduces before any header, would close it; `cobc/config.def` has no such option
today.

On 2026-11-04 Simon Sobisch (sf-mensch) moved the ticket to [feature-requests #526](https://sourceforge.net/p/gnucobol/feature-requests/526/), accepted it at priority 6 with the labels dialect, cobol-85, ibm and good-first-issue, and retitled it "dialect configuration: accepting a PROCEDURE DIVISION that does not begin with a paragraph-name". He wrote that it is not a bug, since GnuCOBOL does not claim conformance, but a reasonable feature request. His planned fix is the one this note names: a new `config.def` value, ok for default, cobol2002 and later and probably mf, unconformable for the other dialects, a warning in the lax includes, and a `cb_verify` call where cobc inserts the missing pseudo-paragraph. Two users added IBM history. arn79 recalls no IBM mainframe compiler, from OS/VS COBOL to Enterprise COBOL V4, that refused or warned on the omission, and thinks a warning is enough. jamesbwhite reports that z/OS Enterprise COBOL 6.5 compiles and runs both attached programs with no warning. That is IBM's extension and changes nothing here: COBOL-85 still requires the paragraph-name, as the 2002 Annex F.2 item 90 quoted above records.

## Draft issue

**Title:** -std=cobol85 accepts a PROCEDURE DIVISION that does not begin with a paragraph-name

As posted (owner-reviewed, 2026-11-03):

Under `-std=cobol85`, cobc compiles a procedure division whose first sentence has no paragraph-name in front of it, with no diagnostic, even with `-Wall`. The same applies at the start of a section. COBOL-85 requires the paragraph-name; dropping that requirement is one of the changes ISO/IEC 1989:2002 made. So `-std=cobol85` does not report a program that COBOL-85 does not allow.

## To reproduce

`nopara.cob` (attached):

~~~~
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. NOPARA.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           DISPLAY "NO PARAGRAPH-NAME".
           STOP RUN.
~~~~

    cobc -fsyntax-only -Wall -std=cobol85 nopara.cob

`nopara-sect.cob` (attached) is the same case at the start of a section:

~~~~
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. NOPARAS.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       MAIN-SECT SECTION.
           DISPLAY "NO PARAGRAPH-NAME AFTER SECTION".
           STOP RUN.
~~~~

    cobc -fsyntax-only -Wall -std=cobol85 nopara-sect.cob

## What happens

For both programs, 3.2.0 and 3.3-dev r5751 print nothing and exit 0.

## What should happen

Under `-std=cobol85`, an error, or a warning controlled by a dialect support option, saying that the procedure division (or the section) must begin with a paragraph-name. `-std=cobol2002` and later accept the program as they do now.

- ISO/IEC 1989:2002 Annex F.2 ("Substantive changes not affecting existing programs"), item 90: "Paragraph-name. A paragraph-name is not required at the beginning of the procedure division or a section."
- ISO/IEC 1989:2023 §14.4.3 keeps that relaxation: a paragraph consists of a paragraph-name and its sentences "or, if the paragraph-name is omitted, one or more successive sentences following the procedure division header".

## Where the fix belongs

In `cobc/parser.y`, the `procedure` rule accepts `statements` wherever it accepts `paragraph_header`, so nothing checks that a header came first. `cobc/config.def` has no support option for this. A new one (unconformable in `cobol85.conf`, ok elsewhere), checked when `statements` reduces before any section or paragraph header, would cover it. The option's name and which dialects allow it are the maintainers' choice, so there is no patch.
