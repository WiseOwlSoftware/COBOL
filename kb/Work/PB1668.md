# PB1668 — LANDED (wave 1005 D)

**Landed.** `ConditionBinder.BindPrimary`'s `booleanLiteral` arm now reports COBOLNET2318 (`OperandIsNotACondition`)
naming the word (`'TRUE' is used as a condition, but it is the word TRUE, which is written only as an EVALUATE
selection subject or selection object (ISO §14.9.13.3 SR7 b)`), through `ReportNotACondition` — the ONE wording of the
not-a-condition report, which the address-identifier arm and `RefuseNonCondition` now share (three copies of one
sentence became one). No new code: COBOLNET2318 is this rule's code, and the allocated COBOLNET2724-2726 are returned.
Negatives `pb1668-if-truth-word` and `pb1668-and-truth-word` (all four editions).

**The sweep found a legal-source sibling of the same arm.** `EVALUATE TRUE WHEN TRUE`, `EVALUATE FALSE WHEN TRUE` and
every TRUE/FALSE × TRUE/FALSE pair (Table 15's two `Y` cells for the words) reached that arm through
`EvaluateBinder.BindWhenItem`, which bound the OBJECT word as a condition, and drew COBOLNET2319 / COBOLNET2362 on
conforming source. §14.9.13.4 GR3 f) gives the subject word its truth value and GR4 a) 4. makes the pair true exactly
when the two match, so the pair is a constant: `EvaluateBinder.Constant(subjTrue == objectWordTrue)` (the ANY arm's
empty conjunction, now one helper). Positive golden `pb1668_evaluate_truth_word_pairs` at 85 (EVALUATE is 85).

**Sweep of the other `Refused(...)` sites** (queries `Cond\.Refused\(|[^.]Refused\("|Unresolved\(` over
`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler`): every site with a rule behind it has a catalogued `Edition.Error` beside it
(`AbbreviatedRelationWithoutSubject`, `OperandIsNotACondition`, `LocaleAlphabetNotACharacterSet`, `UndefinedReference`,
`OmittedConditionOperand`, `ReportMisqualifiedCondition`); the five that remain are genuine internal invariants the
COBOLNET2319 net is for (`unsupported condition form`, `partial-expression with no selection subject`, `sign condition
with no operand`, and EVALUATE's post-screen `value WHEN object` / `WHEN object` fall-throughs, each behind
`ScreenPairing`'s Table 15 diagnostics). The COBOLNET2318 description now names the truth words and the parenthesis
rule (docs/DIAGNOSTICS.md regenerated).

Filed 2026-09-27 by the train-68 lander from the wave-68 S implementer report (`w68s-PB1390-report.md`, new
defect 1).

**Repro.** `scratchpad/w68s/probe/t4.cob` (`IF TRUE`) and `t5.cob` (`IF A = 1 AND TRUE`), at any edition: both draw

**Rule.** §8.8.4.2.1 (`cite.py --check 8.8.4.2.1` OK §8.8.4.2.1): "The simple conditions are the relation, boolean,
class, condition-name, switch-status, sign, and omitted-argument conditions." TRUE/FALSE are not a condition; they
appear only as EVALUATE subjects and objects (§14.9.13). The rejection is right; the diagnostic must name the rule.

`CobolExpressions.g4#primaryCondition`'s `booleanLiteral` arm.
