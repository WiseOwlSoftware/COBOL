# PB3773 — CI's concurrency budget is 60 on Team

**Sites:** `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml` (the comment at the `pull_request` trigger, about lines 23-24: "a public repository's 20-concurrent-job limit"), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` (section 3.17.2 step 5 and section 3.17.6), `scripts/orchestrator/ruleset-main.json` (`merge_queue.parameters.max_entries_to_build`, 5) with `ruleset.py`.

## What changed

On 2026-10-25 the operator measured CI saturated at the Free plan's 20 concurrent jobs: five merge groups of about 20 jobs each shared 20 slots, so a run that takes 12-14 minutes alone took 25-30 (runs #3750-#3752: jobs started about 20 minutes after their runs were created). The owner upgraded the org to GitHub Team (`gh api orgs/WiseOwlSoftware`: plan team, 1 seat); GitHub's limits page gives Team 60 concurrent jobs on standard hosted runners, and the org's runners page showed 21 active jobs minutes later. Docs that state the 20-job limit now describe the old plan (CLAUDE.md rule 6).

## Fix direction

Update the two references to the plan's budget (60, with the source). Re-measure the merge queue's build width against 60 slots: at about 20 jobs per merge group, three groups run unthrottled; measure enqueued-to-merged and the merge-group CI wall at widths 3 and 5 over a day of landings before changing `max_entries_to_build` (ruleset.py applies and checks the committed JSON).

## Landed (wave 20261787, group C)

Re-probe on the live repository 2026-10-31: `gh api orgs/WiseOwlSoftware` plan `team`; the day's 60 merge-group runs have a full group at 23 jobs (peak 18-19 running at once) and an inert-lane group at 13; the busiest moment (five groups at once, runs 41231268883, 41231270319, 41231270803, 41231271575, 41231400422) peaked at 40 running jobs, every job started within a second of its run's creation, and a full group's wall was 13-16 minutes, its unthrottled time. Two references fixed: the workflow comment at the `pull_request` trigger and `DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` section 3.17.2 step 5; section 3.17.6 now carries the 60-job budget and the width measurement. `max_entries_to_build` stays 5: the budget holds three full groups (about 57 jobs), the day never put more than two full groups in flight (the driver-file rule of PB4147 lands a refusal-adding wave one at a time), and a fourth or fifth full group costs no more than the serial order a narrower width imposes. Narrow it to 3 only if a measured day shows full groups waiting for runners (`ruleset.py check` binds the value).
