# PB2934 — semgrep verify is a prose-only landing step

**Found:** the PB2885 sweep of every lander-brief step for prose that nothing checks (wave 1053 group P, 2026-10-10).

**The defect.** `python scripts/semgrep/verify.py` (rules fire on `scripts/semgrep/testdata/Violations.cs`; the
greenfield tree's counts may not exceed `scripts/semgrep/baseline.json`) is required of every implementer (P9) and of
the lander before and after a train (lander-train-brief step 3), and it is run by nothing mechanical:
`grep -n semgrep .github/workflows/*.yml scripts/run_gate_legs.py scripts/guard*.sh scripts/push-main.sh` prints
nothing (repro, 2026-10-10 on 4f070d054). Train 57 dropped a cluster for +20 BigInteger findings nobody reported, which
is what the rule exists to stop; today it stops it only when a lander remembers.

**Measured:** `python scripts/semgrep/verify.py` took 97 s on the Windows host under a concurrent gate's load and
printed `PASS` (e.g. `cobolnet-raw-diagnostic-code-literal: 268 -> 268`). It needs the external `semgrep` tool
(`C:\Users\brent\AppData\Local\Programs\Python\Python314\Scripts\semgrep`), which no CI job installs.

**Why it was not fixed in PB2885:** where it belongs is its own decision with a cost: a refusal in `push-main.sh` (97 s
per landing, the semgrep version unpinned there), one of the gate's audits (97 s on every implementer gate), or a CI job
(a pinned install, and then the local gates must run the same pinned version under the CI invariant, PB1957). Each
shape is a mechanism of its own beyond PB2885's two.

**Code sites:** `scripts/semgrep/verify.py` (the check), `scripts/push-main.sh` (the landing checks, exits 3, 6, 7),
`scripts/run_gate_legs.py` AUDITS, `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml` `audits` job.

**Rule:** none (process tooling, no ISO clause).
