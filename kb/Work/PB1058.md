# PB1058 — OPEN

## Measured
`05 COLUMN 77777777777 PIC X(3) VALUE "ABC".` → `Unhandled exception: System.OverflowException: Value was either too
large or too small for an Int32. at System.Int32.Parse … at CobolNet.Binding.DataBinder.BindReportEntry
(DataBinder.Reports.cs:line 1624)`. `RD RR PAGE LIMIT 77777777777 LINES.` → the same exception at
`BindReportDescriptionClauses` (DataBinder.Reports.cs:line 781). Exit 1, no diagnostic.

## What the fixer owes
Route every report-writer integer through the one checked integer decoder PB1002 introduced for LINE / NEXT GROUP
(`LineInteger`), reporting the clause's own range rule (LINE: §13.18.35.3 3); COLUMN: §13.18.14.3 SR6 via PB1059;
PAGE: §13.18.39.3) instead of throwing. Grep `int.Parse(` over `Binding/` for the rest (the lead says the PAGE
subclauses share it). Negative golden per clause. Legal source cannot reach it, but a compiler crash on ANY source is
a MAJOR by the register's rule.
