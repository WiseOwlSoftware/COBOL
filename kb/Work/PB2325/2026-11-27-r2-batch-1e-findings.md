### `procedure-binding-2--architecture--f2#1` (finding, extract, votes upheld/upheld/upheld)

**Finding:** CallBinder binds GOBACK and owns three cross-binder activation rules; neither is in the CallBinder row of section 8.3 or in PB2325

**Rule:** single responsibility per type and one home per rule (DESIGN-architecture-review 5.1, 8.3 method: every concern of a god class is assigned a target type); 8.3 row Binding.Procedure.CallBinder assigns only arguments, target and statement (CallArgumentBinder, CallTargetResolver, CallBinder)

**Scenario:** At the pin CallBinder is 1638 lines and 32 members (R0-0031), against the 1139 lines and 17 members that PB2325 was written from (R0-0030). Two concerns are not CALL and have no target. (1) GOBACK (2023:14.9.18): BindGoback, DecodeGobackPhrases and the nested record GobackPhrases (CallBinder.cs:1349-1444). Its termination-statement siblings STOP and EXIT bind in ControlFlowBinder (StatementBinder.cs:718,767; ControlFlowBinder.cs:28,417), and GOBACK already calls back into ControlFlowBinder for BindTerminationStatus and ReturningAsStatus. OoBinder.OoBindMethodGoback(in CallBinder.GobackPhrases) (OoBinder.cs:2186) takes a type nested in CallBinder. An extract that follows the 8.3 row as written would carry GOBACK into the residual CallBinder or split it arbitrarily, and the residual would still fail the god-class rule. (2) Rules that other binders call as CallBinder statics: ByValueLiteralAdmitted (SR23, also INVOKE SR16; OoBinder.cs:530,1172), Described (UdfBinder.cs:283) and BarePrototypeWord (StorageClaimScan.cs:190). The INVOKE and function-reference activations therefore depend on the CALL statement binder, and the extract has no stated home for these rules. CANCEL (BindCancel, 1257-1347) shares literal-1, prototype and SR1 category resolution with the CALL target and is not named in the row either.

**Target:** Amend the 8.3 CallBinder row and PB2325: GOBACK moves to the termination-statement binder beside STOP and EXIT (ControlFlowBinder, or a GobackBinder it composes), and GobackPhrases becomes a top-level type named for GOBACK that OoBinder takes. The shared activation rules move to ParameterConformance, which already holds the 14.8.2 half CALL, INVOKE and UdfBinder share (ByValueLiteralAdmitted, Described), or to CallTargetResolver (BarePrototypeWord). CANCEL is bound through CallTargetResolver, as CALL is, so the two verbs keep one resolution.

**Finding sites (the pin's):** src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:1349-1444 (CallBinder.BindGoback, DecodeGobackPhrases, GobackPhrases); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:893-902 (CallBinder.Described); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:1254-1255 (CallBinder.BarePrototypeWord); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:1461-1468 (CallBinder.ByValueLiteralAdmitted); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:1257-1347 (CallBinder.BindCancel); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.cs:2186 (OoBinder.OoBindMethodGoback)

### `procedure-binding-2--architecture--f2#2` (finding, extract, votes upheld/upheld/upheld)

**Finding:** A CALL argument is classified in five places, and each place dispatches the callArgument parse alternatives again; the CallArgumentBinder of section 8.3 should take one classified argument

**Rule:** one mechanism per job and one rule in one place (DESIGN-architecture-review 5.1); the shape 8.3 gives CallArgumentBinder (PB2325) states no argument model, so an extract would move the five dispatches as they are

**Scenario:** One USING argument is classified by five separate readers, and each reader re-runs the 2023:14.9.4.4 GR8 reduction (ConditionBinder.ReduceArgument) and re-dispatches callByReference, callByContent, callByValue and the bare alternatives. The readers are the up-front 85 judgement (CallBinder.cs:68-91), the bind loop and its arms (273-663), NullSpellingOf (941-951), Format2OnlySpellingOf (974-1019) and FunctionIdentifierArgumentOf (1026-1032). ReduceArgument is called at lines 75, 280, 371, 439, 945, 948, 987 and 1029, up to five times for one argument. CallArgumentFormatDriftTests pins that Format2OnlySpellingOf reads every alternative. Nothing pins the other readers. Their order has already disagreed: PB4634 (open) records that at 85 the judgedUpFront pass runs Format2OnlySpellingOf before the free-NULL verdict, against what the loop says, and PB4155 and PB4508 were NULL arms that one reader had and another lacked. A new argument alternative or edition rule has to be written in every reader, and a missed one is a silent drift.

**Target:** Within the section 8.3 extract (PB2325), CallArgumentBinder reduces each callArgument once into one closed classified-argument record: the written phrase, the GR8-reduced operand, the NULL spelling, the function-identifier operand and the Format-2-only spelling. A closed hierarchy in the 8.5 sense fits here. The up-front judgement, Format1Narrowing and the bind arms switch over that record. CallArgumentFormatDriftTests then pins the one classifier and not one of five readers.

**Finding sites (the pin's):** src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:68-91 (CallBinder.BindCall, up-front judgement); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:273-663 (CallBinder.BindCall, argument loop); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:941-951 (CallBinder.NullSpellingOf); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:974-1019 (CallBinder.Format2OnlySpellingOf); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs:1026-1032 (CallBinder.FunctionIdentifierArgumentOf)
