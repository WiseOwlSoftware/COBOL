# PB1600 — LANDED

## What happened
In w61f's gate 3 (BelowNormal priority, a loaded host) `PictureCompositionTests.ALargeRepeatExpandedPicture_CostsLinearTime`
failed at **36x** against its bound of 30; gates 1, 2 and 4 on the same tree were green, and no cluster touched
`PictureComposition.cs`.

## Mechanism
PB1590 turned the test's absolute 5-second ceiling into a GROWTH RATIO — best-of-three `Stopwatch.GetTimestamp()`
readings at 3 000 and at 30 000 symbols — on the premise that "load that slows both sizes alike cannot move a ratio".
Load does not slow both sizes alike: the two readings are taken at different moments, the small one is a few
milliseconds that a single scheduler preemption dominates, and the ratio moves by whatever landed on either one.
The measured quantity was still wall-clock time. `NoWallClockAssertionDriftTests`' summary named that ratio as "the
one permitted timing shape", so the drift rule endorsed the flaky shape.

## Fix (landed in train 61)
Count the WORK: `PictureComposition.WalkObserver` (an `AsyncLocal<Action<long>?>` seam, the `PhysicalModel.Observer`
pattern) reports each Table-10 walk's role tests — one per live symbol per candidate assignment, plus the culprit
scan on a refusal. The test (now `ALargeRepeatExpandedPicture_CostsLinearWork`) sums them over the same five shapes
at 3 000 and 30 000 symbols and asserts large ≤ 11 × small (the linear walk is exactly ~10x, a pair walk ~100x),
plus a floor (every symbol walked) so a disconnected seam cannot pass. The count is deterministic — no host can move
it. `NoWallClockAssertionDriftTests`' summary no longer permits a stopwatch ratio.

Sibling sweep: `Stopwatch` in tests/ — `CldrLocaleLoaderTests` (reports only), `CompiledProgramCache` (reports only),
the legacy `FixedFormTests` (ceilings removed by PB1590, reports only). No other assertion reads a clock.

Limit (stated, not hidden): the regex drift test cannot recognise a ratio of two timestamps as a timing assertion
(`CompiledProgramCache` legitimately reads `GetTimestamp` for its report), so the rule against the ratio shape lives
in the drift test's summary and `docs/DRIFT_RULES.md`, not in its pattern.
