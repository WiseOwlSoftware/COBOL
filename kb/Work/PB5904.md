# PB5904 - decision: keep or delete the cite.py RECONSTRUCTED machinery and the catalog's provisional field

Filed from the lead of wave 20262099 group F (kb/Work PB5789 and PB4952: the four folios XIII-30, XIII-31, XIII-54 and XIII-55 are now printed text). The implementer's words, unchanged:

> The `RECONSTRUCTED` machinery has nothing left to read: `cite.RECONSTRUCTED`/`RECONSTRUCTED_END`/`RECONSTRUCTED_MARK`,
> `reconstructions`, `in_reconstruction` and `reconstructed`; the catalog's `provisional` field and `PROVISIONAL`;
> `inventory_view`'s provisional count; `audit_batch`'s provisional prompts; and `sweep_figures`'s exclusion. It still
> guards a future gap through `check_reconstructions`. Whether to delete it is a decision for you; I left it.

**The question.** Keep the machinery to catch a future gap in a transcription, or delete it. CLAUDE.md rule 4 (no shims, no dead code kept for a case that may never come) bears on it, and so does the fact that `check_reconstructions` still guards a future gap, as the report says. Nobody has decided it.

**Sites (as the report names them):** `scripts/spec/cite.py` (`RECONSTRUCTED`, `RECONSTRUCTED_END`, `RECONSTRUCTED_MARK`, `reconstructions`, `in_reconstruction`, `reconstructed`, `check_reconstructions`), the rule catalog's `provisional` field and `PROVISIONAL`, `inventory_view` (its provisional count), `audit_batch` (its provisional prompts) and `sweep_figures` (its exclusion).

**Not decided here.** The clerk files the question only.
