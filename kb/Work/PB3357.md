# PB3357 — ledger trend point dates mix UTC and the committer's local offset

> flags: process-only, silent

Source: enqueue unit 2026-10-17 ~20:05 PDT.

**Symptom.** Measured 2026-10-17 on `docs/rearchitecture/evidence/ledger-trend.json` (983 points): the `date` field steps
BACKWARD between consecutive points at least 13 times, e.g. index 243 `92cfea45 2026-10-13 -> a40774c2 2026-10-12`, index 358
`b83de9c4 2026-10-14 -> 8a319aa9 2026-10-13`, index 477 `6452ed98 2026-10-15 -> 57d95844 2026-10-14`. The branch review of
notes-pb3356 also noticed `efe7d904` dated 2026-10-18 followed by a 2026-10-17 point. The series order is right
(`git log --reverse`); the date labels are not monotonic.

**Mechanism.** `scripts/spec/gen_ledger.py` `missed_points()` (line ~468-480) reads `git log --format=%h|%cI` and stores
`iso[:10]`, the committer's LOCAL calendar date. GitHub's merge-queue merge commits carry +00:00 (UTC), local commits carry
-07:00 (PDT), so a merge at 01:54Z on 10-18 is labelled 2026-10-18 and a local commit made later that evening (19:09 -07:00,
02:09Z on 10-18) is labelled 2026-10-17. Sibling: line ~177 (`git log -1 --format=%h|%cI` for the head stamp) takes the date the
same way; check whether the current point's `head["date"]` is derived from it.

**Harm.** The owner's ledger page (axis labels `p["date"][5:]` at line ~609/683/684 and the descriptions at ~627/686) shows
dates out of order, and any reading of "what moved on day D" from the page attributes points to the wrong day around the UTC
midnight (17:00 PDT). Silent: the page shows it without any warning.

**Fix shape.** Normalise every point's date to ONE clock in one place: a helper used by `missed_points` and by the head stamp,
`datetime.fromisoformat(iso).astimezone(<the owner's zone or UTC>).date().isoformat()`. Re-derive the existing points' dates from
their shas once (the series is generated data). Add a drift test: the series' dates are non-decreasing.
