# PB5361 - at --std 85 the refusals of a sum counter named in LINAGE, RECORD DEPENDING, RECORD KEY and ALTERNATE RECORD KEY cite 2023 clause rules where a 1985 rule governs

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/FileControlBinder.Resolve.cs:299` (`FileControlBinder.ResolveLinage`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/FileControlBinder.Resolve.cs:70` (`FileControlBinder.ResolveFiles`, the file-key arm and its ReportEntryReferenceRefused call)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/RecordClauseRules.cs:221` (`RecordClauseRules`)

**Mechanism (one).** The refusal of a sum counter named as an operand of a file or record clause is raised with 2023 clause-rule citations at every edition. PB5240 gave the OCCURS DEPENDING and ResolveClauseOperand arms an 85 edge, but the sibling arms still ask `ReportEntryReferenceRefused` unchanged. The report states that at `--std 85`: LINAGE and RECORD DEPENDING refusals (lines 299 and 221) cite 2023 rules where "§1985:VII.3.7.3 SR1 governs", and a RECORD KEY / ALTERNATE RECORD KEY naming a sum counter (line 70) is refused citing "§2023:13.8.6.2.3", the section rule the branch records as having no 1985 counterpart. The refusals are right (a key must be in the file's record); the cited rule is wrong, so the diagnostic misleads. The section and rule designators are the report's claim and were not re-derived here; the implementer runs `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check` on each before writing it.

**Fix direction.** Make the edition-keyed citation one structure all arms share, so the next clause arm gets its 85 citation automatically (CLAUDE.md rule 5), with a drift test that every arm calling it has an 85 edge.

**Source.** PB5240's report, New defects item 2 and R1-2.
