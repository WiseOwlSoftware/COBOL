# PB56 — OPEN — `IntrinsicRenderer.Landed` converts an SDIDI operand at ws = max(receiver scale, 6) with truncation, so under STANDARD-DECIMAL a nonzero value finer than 10⁻⁶ lands as ZERO in every exact-family intrinsic

> ⚙ **flags:** wrong-answer, silent

> **Found by the batch-6 SIGN adjudication (RV-15.81.4-1, 2026-08-08), derived by reading — the mechanism
> is the DOCUMENTED PB32-interim approximation at the ONE landing.** `IntrinsicRenderer.Landed` converts a
> standard-decimal intermediate (SDIDI) argument via `DecToUnscaled` at working scale `max(receiver scale,
> 6)`, truncating, BEFORE the intrinsic body sees the operand. §15.4.1 r1 — "the returned value shall equal
> the value of the equivalent arithmetic expression" — makes the truncation a wrong answer wherever the
> operand's true value is nonzero below the working scale.
>
> **FAILING SHAPE (SIGN instance):**
> ```
> OPTIONS. ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD-DECIMAL.
> 01 A PIC S9V9(9) VALUE 0.000000001.  01 I PIC S9.
> COMPUTE I = FUNCTION SIGN(A - 0)
> ```
> The SDIDI value 1e-9 is exact and > 0; §15.81.4 r1a selects EAE = +1; **actual 0** (trunc at scale 6).
> The same shape under NATIVE arithmetic is inside §15.4.1's implementor-defined intermediate latitude and
> is NOT a divergence — the defect is STANDARD-DECIMAL-specific, where §8.8.1.5 fixes the intermediate.
>
> ⛔ **FAMILY-WIDE, NOT SIGN-SPECIFIC** — every exact-family intrinsic fed a Dec operand through `Landed`
> shares it (the landing is the ONE choke point PB32 built, which is why the fix belongs there and only
> there). The recorded end state at the landing's own remark: a **Dec-carrier body** — the operand crosses
> into the intrinsic AS a Dec value, no unscaled quantization at all — rather than a wider working scale
> (any fixed ws is the same defect at a smaller epsilon; PB13's receiver-blind scale is the precedent).
> **The sweep when fixing:** enumerate every `Landed` caller and probe each with a sub-working-scale
> operand; add the family drift guard beside `IntrinsicCarrierAgreementDriftTests`.
> Fix lands with: spec-derived golden (the SIGN shape + at least one sibling function), re-verdict of
> RV-15.81.4-1 and any triage-confirmed siblings.
