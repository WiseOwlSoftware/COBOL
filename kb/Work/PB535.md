# PB535

## Mechanism — picture-clause-p3
pic-symbol-combination-unvalidated - PictureAnalyzer validates symbol MEMBERSHIP (the 13.18.40.3 SR2 whitelist, COBOLNET0808) and never symbol COMBINATION, so SR2's second sentence ('The allowable combinations of symbols for a PICTURE clause are specified in 13.18.40.6, Precedence rules'), the definitional GRs 5-11, and SR12's minimum-content leg are all unenforced. Two consequences travel together. (a) A character-string that defines NO category reaches the analyzer's FALL-THROUGH arm and binds as category numeric with zero digits and zero length. (b) A character-string that mixes A/X with symbols outside {A,X,9,B,0,/} binds as category alphanumeric and its LENGTH is computed from a hard-coded whitelist (`expanded.Count(c => c is 'X' or 'A' or '9' or 'B' or '0' or '/' || char1Set.Contains(c))`), so the offending symbols vanish from the item's size - the item is SHORTER than its picture, which shifts every following member of a group image (the PB188 shape). The analyzer's own comment concedes the gap: 'The SR2/13.18.40.6 precedence-rule validation (symbol ORDER/multiplicity - PIC 99.99.99 etc.) is a separate self-contained table walk, queued (adversarial-review minor, DEVLOG 595)'. No kb/Work note owns it. The right shape is the Table-10 walk as a generated table plus a category-definition check derived from GR5-GR11, not more special cases: today the numeric-edited arm counts positions correctly (everything but V/S/P) while the alphanumeric arm counts a list, which is one rule written down twice.

### Expected, from the standard
ISO/IEC 1989:2023 13.18.40.3 SR2: 'Character-string-1 shall consist of an allowable combination of characters used as picture symbols. The allowable combinations of symbols for a PICTURE clause are specified in 13.18.40.6, Precedence rules.' 13.18.40.4 GR11: to define an item as fixed-point numeric, character-string-1 'shall include at least one symbol 9'. 13.18.40.3 SR12's 'If the FOR phrase is not specified' leg a): character-string-1 shall contain at least one of the symbols from the set A, N, X, Z, 1, 9, * or at least two occurrences of one of the symbols from the set character-1, x, +, - and the currency symbol. A string of S/V/P alone satisfies none of these, and none of GR3's eight categories is defined by it, so each of `PIC S`, `PIC PPP`, `PIC SV`, `PIC SPPP` shall be rejected. Table 10 admits no ',' after 'A X' and no 'A X' after 'Z *' or 'CR DB', so `PIC XX,XX`, `PIC AAZZ`, `PIC XXCR` and `PIC ZZ9AA` shall be rejected too. If any of them were legal, 13.18.40.4 GR4 with GR14 would still make the size the count of every symbol representing a character position - 5, 4, 4 and 5 respectively - never the counts observed.

### Observed
Observed by probe at all four editions. The mechanism was then read out of Analyze: after the SR2 membership loop the code branches national -> boolean -> USAGE guards -> `anyAlpha` -> `anyEdit || blankWhenZero...` -> pure numeric, and the last arm has no precondition at all, so any string that reaches it is 'numeric'. Both the zero-length numeric case and the short alphanumeric case are silent, and a zero-length or short elementary item inside a group silently mis-lays every following member - the harm is larger than a missing diagnostic.

## Landed — 2026-09-13

**The behaviour half was already closed, and that was measured first.** The eight repro pictures were re-run on
this worktree's own freshly built `cobol.exe` before anything was changed: `PIC S`, `PIC PPP`, `PIC SV` and
`PIC SPPP` each draw COBOLNET1934 (§13.18.40.3 SR12 a) and `PIC XX,XX`, `PIC AAZZ`, `PIC XXCR` and `PIC ZZ9AA`
each draw COBOLNET1935 (the §13.18.40.6 Table 10 pair), at 85, 2002, 2014 and 2023 alike, while the legal
controls still bind (`PIC V9` 1, `PIC AAA9` 4, `PIC 99` 2). The landing was **PB528** (08ba8f9b, train 26) —
`PictureComposition`, which read SR2's second obligation for the first time. All four inventory rows this note
owns were nevertheless still GAP, with no test-ref behind any of them, so what PB535 lands is the architectural
residue the composition validator did not touch and the evidence the rows owe.

**GR4 is now written ONCE.** `PictureAnalyzer.CharacterPositions` states ISO §13.18.40.4 GR4 — "the number of
symbols in character-string-1 that represent either boolean positions or character positions" — as GR14's
EXCLUSION: `'P'` ("not counted in the size of the item"), `'V'` ("not counted in the size of the item") and the
`'S'` of an item with no SIGN SEPARATE phrase, every other symbol carrying GR14's own "is counted in the size of
the item", plus GR14's currency widening. Four arms had spelled that one rule four ways — the national arm
`expanded.Length`, the numeric-edited arm the exclusion, the float-edited arm `expanded.Length` again, and the
alphanumeric arm a hard-coded WHITELIST of the symbols it expected. The whitelist is the spelling that caused
the harm: it answers a symbol it does not know about by DROPPING it, which is why `PIC XX,XX` bound LENGTH 4 and
`PIC XXCR` bound 2 — an item shorter than its picture, shifting every following member of a group image. Stated
as an exclusion the rule cannot do that, and all five sites (the boolean arm included) now call the one
function.

**Each category arm states its own general rule.** The alphanumeric arm's predicate was "contains an 'A' or an
'X'", which is not GR6's antecedent, and the pure-numeric arm — the FALL-THROUGH, reached by exhaustion — had no
precondition whatever. Both now carry the loud COBOLNET0808 assertion the national (GR9/GR10) and boolean (GR8)
arms beside them already carried: GR6/GR7's alphabet for the first, GR11's "shall include at least one symbol
'9'" for the second. Both are unreachable while Table 10 and GR5–GR13 agree; they are written down so that the
agreement is asserted rather than assumed, which is the same reason the national arm's else branch exists.

**The property is measured over the ACCEPTED SET, not over a list of cases.**
`tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/PictureCategoryDriftTests.cs` walks 7 239 character-strings over the whole Format-1
symbol alphabet plus 22 shapes three symbols cannot spell, and asks of every string the compiler ACCEPTS: does
the category the analyzer assigned satisfy the general rule that defines it (GR5–GR13), and is its size GR4's
count? That is what makes the next symbol automatic — a prohibition relaxed in `PictureComposition`, or a
category arm widened here, changes the accepted set and is measured against the standard rather than against a
list someone remembered to extend. All three legs were proved failing once: the POPULATION guard (1 142 accepted
strings; histogram alphabetic 3, alphanumeric 34, alphanumeric-edited 140, boolean 4, national 4,
national-edited 43, numeric 26, numeric-edited 888), the size property (dropping one symbol from the count put
256 strings wrong) and the category property (widening the alphanumeric arm by one symbol put 57 strings wrong).

**Goldens.** `tests/conformance/85/pb535_picture_category_size_85` measures GR4 as DISPLACEMENT inside a group —
the harm itself — for 'A', 'X', '9', the GR7 insertions '0' and '/', the uncounted 'S', 'P' and 'V', and the two
character positions GR14 gives 'CR'. `tests/conformance/2023/pb535_picture_category_size_2023` constructs all
EIGHT of GR3's categories and reads each size with FUNCTION LENGTH. The two negatives
`pb535-picture-no-category` (COBOLNET1934) and `pb535-picture-alphanumeric-combination` (COBOLNET1935) are the
note's own eight repro strings, rejecting at all four editions.

**What did NOT hold on today's tree.** Two claims in the row notes were stale and are corrected in the

sizes 5), and the GR6/GR11 under-rejections (PB528 closed them). The one leg that remains unreachable is GR3's
BLANK WHEN ZERO sentence under USAGE NATIONAL, and not for a reason of this rule: `PIC 9(5) USAGE NATIONAL` is
itself COBOLNET0899 national-form numeric data, the staged residue owned by row `SR-13.18.60.3-12`.
