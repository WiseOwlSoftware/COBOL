# PB535

## Mechanism — picture-clause-p3
pic-symbol-combination-unvalidated - PictureAnalyzer validates symbol MEMBERSHIP (the 13.18.40.3 SR2 whitelist, COBOLNET0808) and never symbol COMBINATION, so SR2's second sentence ('The allowable combinations of symbols for a PICTURE clause are specified in 13.18.40.6, Precedence rules'), the definitional GRs 5-11, and SR12's minimum-content leg are all unenforced. Two consequences travel together. (a) A character-string that defines NO category reaches the analyzer's FALL-THROUGH arm and binds as category numeric with zero digits and zero length. (b) A character-string that mixes A/X with symbols outside {A,X,9,B,0,/} binds as category alphanumeric and its LENGTH is computed from a hard-coded whitelist (`expanded.Count(c => c is 'X' or 'A' or '9' or 'B' or '0' or '/' || char1Set.Contains(c))`), so the offending symbols vanish from the item's size - the item is SHORTER than its picture, which shifts every following member of a group image (the PB188 shape). The analyzer's own comment concedes the gap: 'The SR2/13.18.40.6 precedence-rule validation (symbol ORDER/multiplicity - PIC 99.99.99 etc.) is a separate self-contained table walk, queued (adversarial-review minor, DEVLOG 595)'. No kb/Work note owns it. The right shape is the Table-10 walk as a generated table plus a category-definition check derived from GR5-GR11, not more special cases: today the numeric-edited arm counts positions correctly (everything but V/S/P) while the alphanumeric arm counts a list, which is one rule written down twice.

### Expected, from the standard
ISO/IEC 1989:2023 13.18.40.3 SR2: 'Character-string-1 shall consist of an allowable combination of characters used as picture symbols. The allowable combinations of symbols for a PICTURE clause are specified in 13.18.40.6, Precedence rules.' 13.18.40.4 GR11: to define an item as fixed-point numeric, character-string-1 'shall include at least one symbol 9'. 13.18.40.3 SR12's 'If the FOR phrase is not specified' leg a): character-string-1 shall contain at least one of the symbols from the set A, N, X, Z, 1, 9, * or at least two occurrences of one of the symbols from the set character-1, x, +, - and the currency symbol. A string of S/V/P alone satisfies none of these, and none of GR3's eight categories is defined by it, so each of `PIC S`, `PIC PPP`, `PIC SV`, `PIC SPPP` shall be rejected. Table 10 admits no ',' after 'A X' and no 'A X' after 'Z *' or 'CR DB', so `PIC XX,XX`, `PIC AAZZ`, `PIC XXCR` and `PIC ZZ9AA` shall be rejected too. If any of them were legal, 13.18.40.4 GR4 with GR14 would still make the size the count of every symbol representing a character position - 5, 4, 4 and 5 respectively - never the counts observed.

### Observed
Observed by probe at all four editions. The mechanism was then read out of Analyze: after the SR2 membership loop the code branches national -> boolean -> USAGE guards -> `anyAlpha` -> `anyEdit || blankWhenZero...` -> pure numeric, and the last arm has no precondition at all, so any string that reaches it is 'numeric'. Both the zero-length numeric case and the short alphanumeric case are silent, and a zero-length or short elementary item inside a group silently mis-lays every following member - the harm is larger than a missing diagnostic.
