## Finding — replace-statement-p1 (rejects-legal-source)

REPLACE is located only as the first significant word of a line (FindKeywordAtLineStart), so a REPLACE statement that follows other text on the same line is never recognized and is handed to the parser.

**Rows:** SR-7.2.4.3-1, SR-7.2.4.3-2

**Repro:**

```
DISPLAY "START". REPLACE ==XX1== BY ==AAA==.
           DISPLAY XX1.  (probe midline.cob, 01 AAA PIC X(3) VALUE "AAA")
```

**Expected from the spec:** Compiles; prints START then AAA -- 7.2.4.3 1) a REPLACE may be specified anywhere a character-string or separator may appear, and 2) it is preceded by a space.

**Observed:** error COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near '=' at (7,37).

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#FindKeywordAtLineStart; #ApplyReplaceStatements line 121
