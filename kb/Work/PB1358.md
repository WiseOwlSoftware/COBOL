# PB1358 — LANDED

## Landed 2026-09-27 (wave 68 X, with PB1357 — one REPLACE statement model)

A REPLACE statement is located by the same §7.2.2.5 text-word walk that finds COPY — `FindStatementKeyword(text, pos,
keyword, onGlued)`, which replaced both `FindKeywordAtLineStart` (REPLACE, first word of a line only) and
`FindCopyKeyword` — so it is recognized anywhere REPLACE is a text-word (§7.2.4.3 SR1, cite.py OK 1)): after another
statement on the same line, inside a statement, in library text; never inside a literal, a directive line or a longer
word (REPLACE-FLAG). SR2 (cite.py OK 2)): REPLACE directly after `(`, `:`, a literal or `==` is COBOLNET2449 naming SR2,
and REPLACE glued behind a period/comma/semicolon is reported by the finder's glued arm (the COPY twin's). The
line-map replay (`ReplaceLineMap`) uses the same finder. Probe `midline.cob`: was COBOL0001 near '=', now `START` /
`AAA`. Golden `85/pb1358_replace_anywhere` ([START] [AAA] [B-C] [Y]), negative `pb1358-replace-not-preceded-by-space`.
Sweep: no other text-manipulation keyword was located by line position (`FindKeywordAtLineStart` and `MatchWord` had
no other callers and are deleted).

## Finding — replace-statement-p1 (rejects-legal-source)

REPLACE is located only as the first significant word of a line (FindKeywordAtLineStart), so a REPLACE statement that follows other text on the same line is never recognized and is handed to the parser.

**Rows:** SR-7.2.4.3-1, SR-7.2.4.3-2

**Repro:**

```
DISPLAY "START". REPLACE ==XX1== BY ==AAA==.
           DISPLAY XX1.  (probe midline.cob, 01 AAA PIC X(3) VALUE "AAA")
```

**Expected from the spec:** Compiles; prints START then AAA -- 7.2.4.3 1) a REPLACE may be specified anywhere a character-string or separator may appear, and 2) it is preceded by a space.

**Observed:** error COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near '=' at (7,37).

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#FindKeywordAtLineStart; #ApplyReplaceStatements line 121
