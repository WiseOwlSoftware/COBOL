# PB4349 - CiBoundsDriftTests's summary points at DESIGN-test-build-ci.md section 3.17, but the design paragraph it pins was written into section 1.4

**Sites:** `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CiBoundsDriftTests.cs` (`CiBoundsDriftTests`, `TheSummaryCitesTheDesignSectionThatPinsThisClass`, `CitedDesignSection`, `DesignSection`).

Found by the branch review of PB4324 (w20261792b-review-r1, lead R1-2, low). The summary names `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` section 3.17 as the design of the CI bounds. Section 3.17 is "THE LANDING PIPELINE" (line 2480); the paragraph that states the bounds and pins `CiBoundsDriftTests` ("CI is bounded, and every cache is saved from main only", line 170) sits in section 1.4, "CI (`.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml`)" (line 92). `scripts/spec/drift_rules.py` prints the summary to every implementer who touches a workflow or the toolchain action, so each of them is sent to the wrong section. The test reads correctly; only the pointer is wrong, which is why `misleads_diagnosis` stays false (no compiler diagnostic is involved).

Fix: change the citation to §1.4, and sweep the repo for other comments, goldens and notes that cite 3.17 for the CI bounds (`grep -rn "3.17" tests scripts .github docs`), since the number was inherited by PB4324's brief.
