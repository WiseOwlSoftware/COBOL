# PB4130 — a COBOL-85 target and a non-Annex-B character in a word (U+00A0, the euro sign)

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Common/CobolWordRule.cs:52` (`CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/CobolCharacterRepertoire.cs` (`ViolationAtEveryEdition`, `NextCodePoint`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`VisitCobolWord`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (`WordCharacterNotPermitted`).

New defect 1 of PB3845's implementer (wave 20261764 group D; the note PB3845 is held by an in-flight group, so this is a new note that
names it). Under a COBOL-85 target a word containing U+00A0 or the euro sign draws COBOL0900 worded "the extended letter", although no
edition's Annex B admits the character: it is not a letter of any set. Misleading message only (the program is refused either way).
Sites: `WordCharacterScreen.Check` (new with PB3845's branch, not on main when this was filed) and `CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation`.

**Fix:** word the diagnostic by what the character is (not in Annex B of the target edition) and not "the extended letter"; keep the
two-edition split (a 2002+ extended letter is legal where §8.3.2 allows it). Pin with goldens at 85 and 2002.

Source: report `w20261764d-PB3845-report.md`, "New defects" item 1; PB3845 (open, in-flight).

## Landed

Re-probed on the worktree build: a word with U+00A0 or U+20AC at `--std 85` drew COBOL0900 'the extended letter'; at 2002/2014/2023
COBOL2773. Fix at the root: `CobolCharacterRepertoire.ViolationAtEveryEdition` answers 'is this character in NO edition's Annex B'
(NotPermitted in both the 2023 and the 2002/2014 tables); `CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation` asks it below 2002 (where there is no
edition Annex B to judge by), and `VisitCobolWord` skips the 2002 extended-letter gate for such a word so COBOL2773 is the only
diagnostic. A real extended letter (CAFE-acute) at 85 is still COBOL0900. Goldens: `pb4130-no-break-space-in-a-word` (refused at all
four editions, COBOL2773) and `pb1402-not-an-annex-b-character` (euro sign) now also refused at 85.
