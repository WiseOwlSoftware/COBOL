**Sites:** `scripts/spec/audit_derivations.py` (whole: every arm's checker, the signature check and the row reader change),
`tests/version-matrix/inventory-schema.json` (`derivation`: the arms

## What is missing

kb/Work PB5373 decides that nine of the 21 no-witness rows of the 1985 edition close on a derivation (its classes 1 and
2). Measured on 2026-11-12: `git grep -l '"derivation"' -- tests/version-matrix` finds 45 rows in the 2023 inventory and
none in `inventory-1985/`. Three things stand in the way.

1. **No arm fits a 1985 rule that declares its result undefined.** `undefined-A.2` is checked against Annex A.2 of the
   2023 standard, resolving an item's trailing citation to the row. The 1985 standard has no such list; its rules say
   "undefined" in their own text (49 lines of `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json` contain the word).
2. **The signature is a dated owner decision.** The schema's `signatures` map accepts only keys of that form. PB5373
   moves the signature to the deciding session (`claude: <date>`, mapped to its decision note) and requires an UPHELD
   `cobol-refuter` record for each argued arm.
3. **Section 8 and its checkers read 2023 rule ids.** Whether `audit_derivations.py`, `Schema.state_for` and the drift
   test accept a row keyed `DRV-1985-...` and resolve it against the 1985 catalog and store is not established; no 1985
   row has tried.

## The shape to build

- A fifth arm, `undefined-in-rule`, MECHANICAL: the `Names` cell quotes the words of the row's own catalog text that
  declare the result undefined or unpredictable, and the checker requires the quotation to lie inside that rule's text in
  that edition's catalog (the check `cite.py --check` already makes for a quotation). It is available to every edition;
  a 2023 row whose rule Annex A.2 lists keeps `undefined-A.2`, and the checker refuses `undefined-in-rule` on a row that
  an A.2 item resolves to, so one fact has one arm.
- The `signatures` map gains the `claude: <date>` form, each key mapped to a `kind: decision` note that exists; a row of
  an argued arm signed with such a key names its refuter record, and the checker requires that record to exist and read
  UPHELD. Rows signed by the owner are unchanged.
- Section 8 holds rows of every edition, keyed `DRV-<rule-id>` (a 1985 id carries its edition), each checked against its
  own edition's catalog and store. A drift test holds that every edition's store is read: a derivation on a row of an
  edition the checker does not open is refused, never skipped.
- DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md section 1.1 and the head of `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` section 8 state the fifth arm and
  the signature rule in the same change.
