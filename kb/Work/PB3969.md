# PB3969 — a Format-1 CALL sends a computed numeric operand to a numeric formal as zero

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs#ArgCarrierText` (the `BoundComputedOperand expr` arm
with `ReceiverContext.None`) and the callee's `CobolArgAdapt` Format-1 adaptation (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/CallAbi.cs`).

Filed 2026-10-27 by the enqueue unit's clerk from the PB3760 branch review report (the implementer's lead, carried
unchanged). Not re-probed by the clerk.

## Repro (measured by the PB3760 implementer)

- `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261720e/q6.cob`: `CALL "Q6X" USING BY CONTENT FUNCTION MAX(W 0)` with `W` = 1.25; the
  callee's formal `PIC S9V99` shows `+0.00`.
- `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261720e/q5.cob` and `q5b.cob` (`--permissive`): `BY CONTENT (1.25 + 0)` gives `+000.00`.

## Mechanism (suspected, from the report's named site)

The computed operand is rendered without a receiver (`ReceiverContext.None`) and adapted by `CobolArgAdapt`'s Format-1
path; the value is lost between the two. Pre-existing on main.

## Related, distinct (both landed)

- PB3677: a STANDARD-DECIMAL function evaluated receiver-less with 6 fraction digits (the same arm, a different loss).
- PB1113: cross-reference only; a different mechanism at the same call-argument carrier.

Fix at the root: one Format-1 numeric-operand path, a golden per repro above, and a sweep of the sibling arms.
