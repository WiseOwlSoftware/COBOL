# PB5450 - the 2023 dispositions (PB479 step 2)

**Sites:**
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-unharvested-rule-blocks.json` (`paragraph-blocks`: every 2023 unit leaves `pending`)
- the refuter records of every `restates` and `not-a-rule` (the store PB5449 or PB5374 builds)

**What.** First, a small stratified sample of the tables (by kind: entries, matrix, data; by block: prose clause, rule
block), so the table-row estimate the owner was given (unmeasured, about 250 to 450) is replaced by a measured one
before the rest is decided. Then, for every unit PB5449's segmentation lists in clauses 6 to 16 and Annexes A to C,
decide its disposition by §2023:5.3.2 and §2023:5.3.3 ("Phase A, continued" of section 3 of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md`, point 3): a kind (a ROW: a unit that states or defines anything
normative is a row, and one that obliges the implementation to nothing is still a row, closing later through the
`definitional` arm of section 1.1 of that document), `restates` (naming the rule ids), or `not-a-rule` of class
`lead-in`, `cross-reference` or `data` (each passing its mechanical test). A NOTE, example or format line the
transcription failed to mark is a transcription repair, recorded here and made in PB5451, not a disposition. One
request per clause, the clause's whole text and its existing rows in it; the five carriers of the held 1985 rules
(§2023:14.5.1 with Table 12, §2023:14.5.2, §2023:10.4, §2023:14.6.2.2, §2023:8.4.2.1) and the 121 measured glued
paragraphs first.

- **Table 12** is an entries table, one unit per body row keyed by its statement name (`GR-14.5.1-T12.<r>`). Expected,
  each decided here: the 21 rows with a conditional phrase and PERFORM's (scope terminator only) are rows; a cell with
  two phrases (DELETE, IF, READ, SEARCH, WRITE) stays one row, the statement being the unit; the 28 rows with both
  cells empty (ALLOCATE, CANCEL, CLOSE, ...) are `restates` of their statement's general-format row when that row holds
  no conditional phrase and no `END-` terminator (checked mechanically, then refuted).
- **Annex A.2 items** are `restates` of the ids `extract_annex_a2.py` resolves from their own trailing citation; an
  item whose citation names a clause with no rule (item 11, §2023:14.6.3, resolves to nothing today) is decided against
  that clause's units by reading. A.3 and A.4 items are decided like any unit (A.4's documentation obligation makes
  its items DOC rows where they are rows).
- **Every `restates` and `not-a-rule`** goes to an independent refuter, which tries to state the obligation the unit
  imposes that the named rules (or nothing) do not, and its UPHELD record is stored with the digest it read; an
  overturned one becomes a row. The four existing block dispositions `not-rules` of
  the 2023 manifest are refuted the same way. A sample of the rows goes to the refuter too (the refute stage is not optional,
  design section 9).

**Oracle.** The standard's text, read against §2023:5.3.2 and §2023:5.3.3; every citation a disposition writes passes
`cite.py --check`; the class tests and guards of PB5449; the refuter records.

**Done when** no 2023 unit is `pending` (`inventory_view.lower_bound` prints 2023 with none pending on the branch),
every `restates` and `not-a-rule` has its UPHELD refuter record and the audit's per-class counts agree, and the branch
carries the exact minted-row count (paragraphs, carved paragraphs, table units, numbered items, data) that replaces
the design's estimate (about 500 to 1,850) in PB5451 and in the owner's report.
