# PB5450 - the 2023 dispositions (PB479 step 2)

**Sites:**
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-unharvested-rule-blocks.json` (`paragraph-blocks`: every 2023 entry leaves `pending`)

**What.** For every margin paragraph and table PB5449's segmentation lists in clauses 6 to 16 and Annexes A to C,
decide its disposition by §2023:5.3.2 and §2023:5.3.3 (design section 3, "Phase A, continued", point 3): a kind, `lead`,
`restates` (naming the rule ids), `not-rule` (a closed reason), a table's `rows`/`whole`, a data clause's `whole`. One
request per clause, the clause's whole text and its existing rows in it; the five carriers of the held 1985 rules
(§2023:14.5.1 with Table 12, §2023:14.5.2, §2023:10.4, §2023:14.6.2.2, §2023:8.4.2.1) and the 121 measured glued
paragraphs first. Every `not-rule` and `restates` and a sample of the rows go through an independent refuter (the
refute stage is not optional, design section 9).

**Oracle.** The standard's text, read against §2023:5.3.2 and §2023:5.3.3; every citation a disposition writes passes
`cite.py --check`. A `restates` names rules whose text states the same requirement, checked by the refuter.

**Done when** no 2023 paragraph is `pending` (`inventory_view.lower_bound` prints 2023 with none pending on the branch),
every refutation is answered, and the branch carries the exact minted-row count that replaces the design's estimate
(950 to 1,330) in PB5451 and in the owner's report.
