# PB5573 - at --std 85 a reference-modified control name compiles in TYPE CONTROL HEADING / CONTROL FOOTING and in the RD CONTROL clause (WS-KEY OF WS-REC (1:1)): the 1985 data-name operand is not gated

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitReportGroupType`, `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitReportControlName`)

**Mechanism.** The 1985 TYPE clause names its CONTROL HEADING and CONTROL FOOTING operand data-name-1 / data-name-2, and in a general format a 'data-name' "must not be reference-modified, subscripted, or qualified unless specifically permitted by the rules of the format". The CONTROL clause permits qualification only: "Data-name-1 may be qualified". The permission to qualify and reference-modify the TYPE operand first appears in 2002 (§2002:13.16.55.2 SR10, §2023:13.18.57.3 SR10). At --std 85, `VersionConformancePass.VisitReportGroupType` gates only the omitted operand and OR PAGE, and `VisitReportControlName` gates only the words ON and FOR. Nothing gates a reference-modified operand, so `RD ... CONTROLS ARE WS-KEY OF WS-REC (1:1)` with `01 TYPE CH WS-KEY OF WS-REC (1:1)` compiles and runs at 85.

**Open half, not claimed as a defect.** Whether a QUALIFIED TYPE operand is a 1985 refusal depends on the reconstructed folio XIII-55. Our scan lacks the printed pages XIII-54 and XIII-55 (kb/Work PB4952), and the transcription's reconstruction drops 'the qualification and reference modification of data-name-1 and data-name-2' as later additions. Programs `qual.cob` (TYPE CH WS-KEY OF WS-REC) and `qualin.cob` (TYPE IS CONTROL FOOTING WS-KEY IN WS-REC) compile and run. The fixer decides the qualified half once the printed page is obtained, or records it as a reading. The reference-modification half does not rest on the reconstruction: no 1985 rule permits reference modification of any control name.

**Found by.** The 1985 read 6 (`kb/Work/PB4996/2026-11-14-read-6.md`): the refuter of `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-20261114/out-read6/rd-04-refute.json` OVERTURNED the reader's CONFORMS of `1985-FMT-XIII.3.20.2`. The row had no verdict.
