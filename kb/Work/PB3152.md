## What happens

Wave 1076 (2026-10-14 02:40 PDT, drain), planned from the `plan-wave-drain` worktree at 6013789b2 with
`python scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py --drain --from-budget --borrow-days 6 --dry-run`:

```
A  opus  finisher  notes PB2921, PB2988 (lead PB2921)   src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/DataItem.cs
A2 opus  after A  finisher  notes PB3101 (lead PB3101)  src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/DataItem.cs
```

A is right: the e99-4 R4-1 review finisher. A2 is the review finisher of `plan-wave-drain` (report
`w1075z-PB3150-report.md`, DONE, `Review: FINDINGS 2`, lead PB3150), planned under the wrong identity: PB3101's
note, files and design-heavy model, serialized behind A because PB3101's primary file is A's. Nothing in the plan
names PB3150 or `plan-wave-drain`, so the two review findings (R1-1, R1-2 in
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\review-plan-wave-drain.jsonl`) look unplanned, and a finisher dispatched from A2's spec is
handed PB3101 (a not-started redesign the report never claimed) as its subject. Under the owner's drain that invites
new work; the drain text in the pred covers only notes the report lists as NOT started, and w1075z does not list
PB3101 at all, it only mentions it. The wave unit dropped A2 (`--max-groups 1`) for that reason.

## Mechanism

- `parse_report` collects `ids` from every PB id in the report's text: w1075z yields `['PB3150', 'PB3101',
  'PB3146']` (measured with `plan_wave.load_reports`).
- `plan()`'s `newest` loop (`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py`, the `for r in reports: for i in r.ids: if i in
  open_ids` block) keys each OPEN id on the newest report that mentions it, so w1075z becomes PB3101's newest
  report, and its FINDINGS verdict files PB3101 into `finisher_by_rep[w1075z]`.
- PB3150's unseen-lead arm (the `newest_of_branch` loop, same function) then skips w1075z because
  `r.path in finisher_by_rep`, so the report's real lead is never planned.
- The group is built from `ids` (PB3101), so its note, files, model and same-file `after:` are PB3101's.

The `newest` keying is on main too: any FINDINGS or SPLIT report that merely mentions another open note (a merge
note, a "not started" aside about a different group, a cross-reference) can capture that note's finisher.

## Fix direction

Key planning on the notes a report's group was DISPATCHED with (its header's notes, or the dispatch ledger
`inflight-groups.json`), never on every id the prose mentions; a report's finisher is planned under its own lead.
Regression test in `scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py`: a FINDINGS report whose body mentions another open
note plans one finisher under its own lead and leaves the mentioned note to its own report. The natural fixer is
`plan-wave-drain`'s review finisher (PB3150; the same function, I9).
