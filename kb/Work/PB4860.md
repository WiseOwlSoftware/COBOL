# PB4860 - a cross-aligning intrinsic list whose argument is itself a whole-value call lands on the 34-digit SumExact body

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R1-3 LEAD (low): `IntrinsicRenderer.cs:1381` A cross-aligning list whose argument is itself a whole-value call still lands on the 34-digit SumExact body; same on main, not a regression.

**Not probed.** The clerk reproduced nothing; the report names no program.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs` (`IntrinsicRenderer (line 1381, SumExact body)`).

Filed from review leads of waves 20261884 and 20261885 by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

**Fix (landed, wave 20261887).** Re-probed on the build: `COMPUTE R ROUNDED = FUNCTION SUM (FUNCTION SUM (A B) Z)` with `A PIC 9(31)` holding 26 digits, `B PIC V9(9)` = .004999999, `Z PIC V9` = 0 and `R PIC 9(26)V99` stored `.01`, where `A + B + Z` stores `.00`; RANGE and MIDRANGE over the nested call and DISPLAY (`.005`, the 34-digit image) agreed. Root: the inner call returns a whole value (`NumX.Exact`), `RawArg` lowers it to its 34-digit SDIDI image, and the renderer's `AnyDecRaw` arm read that as a genuine SDIDI operand and sent the list to `SumExact` (rounded to 34 digits) before the cross-aligning whole-value arm could ask its question. The arm now precedes the SDIDI-operand arm (its standard-mode half was dead: `RenderDec` already returns for the always-SDIDI family there), and `AlignedOperands` reads each argument unlowered (`RawWideArg`): a `NumX.Exact` operand is an `AlignedOperand` of unknown bound and run-time scale (`RunTimeScaled`, which `AlignmentShiftsUnboundedOperand` reads as shifting), so the list takes `RenderWholeAligned`. A genuine SDIDI or binary64 operand still answers null (pb4592 keeps its 34-digit rounding). Golden `pb4860_a_whole_value_argument_keeps_its_value_in_a_list_that_leaves_the_carrier` plus its `_85` refusal.
