# PB3819 — one workflow, six job splitters

**Sites:** `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CiHarnessLogDriftTests.cs#Jobs`, `DotnetSdkInstallDriftTests.cs#Jobs`,
`FilterPopulationGuardDriftTests.cs#SplitJobs`, `InertLaneDriftTests.cs#Jobs`, `SelfTestDiscoveryDriftTests.cs#Jobs`, and
the inline regex in `LandingPipelineDriftTests.cs#TheMergeGroupRun_CancelsTheRunsTheQueueSuperseded_AndNothingElse`.
(`SparseCheckoutAudit.Jobs` is a seventh reader, and PB3810 item 2 files the disagreement between it and
`DotnetSdkInstallDriftTests.Jobs`; this note is the whole family.)

## Measured

Each test parses `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml` into jobs its own way: comment attribution, step blocks and how
`jobs:` is found differ (PB3810 measured one consequence: a reader over the whole file returns the `on:` triggers as jobs).
A workflow edit one reader tolerates, another misreads, and a drift test then passes vacuously or reds on a legal change.
PB3769's implementer left it: it means a semantic redesign across five other drift-test classes, outside that slice.

## Acceptance

- One `CiWorkflow` helper (in `tests/_shared/` beside TrackedTree, or the reader PB3810 settles on) yields the jobs, their
  steps and their comments; every class above uses it and no private splitter remains (`git grep -nE "Jobs\(|SplitJobs"
  -- tests` shows one definition).
- The helper has its own drift test over a fixture workflow carrying a comment between jobs, a multi-line `run:` block and a
  trigger block.
- Land after or with PB3810 (it shares `SparseCheckoutAudit`).

## Landed (wave 20261752, group F)

Re-probe on this build (before the change, `git grep -nE "Jobs\(|SplitJobs" -- tests`): eight job readers, not seven.
The note named five classes, `SparseCheckoutAudit.Jobs` and the inline regex of `LandingPipelineDriftTests`; the sweep
found `ToolchainManifestDriftTests.Jobs` and `Steps` (a copy of `DotnetSdkInstallDriftTests.Jobs` plus its own step
reader) and two `Regex.Split` readers in `LinuxGateDriftTests` as well. They disagreed on: the `jobs:` anchor (one over
the whole file, so the `on:` triggers read as jobs; one an `IndexOf` of a newline-prefixed anchor; one `StartsWith`; one
`TrimEnd() ==`), the job-id characters (`.` allowed in one), where a banner goes (the job above in six, the job
below in one), comments inside a step (dropped, kept, or kept only deeper than `steps:`), and what a step is (a line
at exactly six spaces, or the indentation of the first item).

Fix at the root: `tests/_shared/CiWorkflow.cs` is the one reader (jobs, steps, each step's own keys, comments attributed
to what follows them; a shape it cannot read raises). All eight sites ask it, and `CiWorkflowDriftTests` holds its arms
on a fixture (a comment between jobs, a multi-line `run:` block, a trigger block, a list at the key's indentation, CRLF,
the refusals) and sweeps `tests/` so no other file carries a job-key regex or a `jobs:` anchor. Also closed by it:
PB3810 item 2 (`SparseCheckoutAudit.Jobs` over the whole workflow); item 1 of PB3810 is untouched.
