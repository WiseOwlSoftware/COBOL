# PB4702 — TruncatedQuotient past the 256-bit form: a wrong remainder though the quotient fits

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolExact.cs:155` (`CobolExact.TruncatedQuotient`)

Reported by the branch review, not probed. R1-3, copied: when the dividend alignment leaves the 256-bit form, `TruncatedQuotient`
falls to the operands' 34-digit images, and the back-multiply overflows there too, so a quotient that FITS identifier-3 with no size
error still gets a wrong remainder. Not introduced by the PB4640 diff (the base build prints the same value) and within the
past-77-digits determination of DOC-A.1-123, though the dividend itself has only 75 digits. The review cited `CobolExact.cs:164`.
