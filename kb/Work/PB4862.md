# PB4862 - the seeded DeviceEnds row is not synced, so "durable before the statement writes" (McsStore comment, design section 6) is false for power loss

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R2-2 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 2): `McsStore.cs:142` The comment and design section 6 say the seeded DeviceEnds row is durable before the statement writes; the seeding landing does not sync, so that holds for process death and is false for power loss.

**Not probed.** No power-loss witness exists (the report records no seam for a kill between a device write and its flush); the clerk reproduced nothing.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Communication/McsStore.cs` (`McsStore (line 142, DeviceEnds seeding)`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-communication-facility.md` (`section 6`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Communication/McsStore.cs` (`Prepare`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/McsStoreCrashMatrixTests.cs`.

Filed from review leads of waves 20261884 and 20261885 by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

**Landed (wave 20261887 group C).** Re-probed on the build: the seeding landing was a plain statement (no sync), so the committed slot a power loss returns to lacked the seeded `DeviceEnds` rows (witness `McsStoreCrashMatrixTests.Seeding_IsPromoted_SoAPowerLossKeepsItsDeviceEndsRows`: committed prime root differed from the work slot's). Fix: `McsStore.Prepare` promotes the seeding (`PagedFile.Promote`) before any device is written; a refused promotion answers 92 with the seeding landed and the next statement's writable open fences it. Design section 6 "Devices" says so. Sites also: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Communication/McsStore.cs` (`Prepare`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/McsStoreCrashMatrixTests.cs`.
