**The mechanism (the checker's reading held).** Inside a list region the lexer kept every comma or semicolon followed by
a space as the `FNARG_SEPARATOR` token, and the grammar admits the token only between two items. The token exists only
to mark an item boundary (a '(' or a signed literal after it begins a new item, kb/Work PB2113); where it stands as a
space would — after an opening parenthesis, an operator, a qualifier connective or another separator, before a closing
parenthesis or a spaced operator — it is now skipped exactly as the space is (`CobolLexer.ListItemBoundary`, the set
`ListOperandFollows`), so the grammar needs no separator slot at every operand position. The function-argument list and
the reference modifier had the same shape and are fixed by the same decision (probed: `FUNCTION MAX (, A I)`,
`MAX (A, )`, `MAX (A +, 1; I)`, `W-T (2:, 2)`, `W-T (, 1 :2)`, `E OF, G (J)`, keyword-omitted `MIN (A; , J ; )`).
