**Landed.** The arm is `ALL REFERENCES? OF? dataReference`. Re-probe on 05fdfc2bd: `ALL W-A` and `ALL OF W-A` drew
COBOL5506 at 85; after the fix all five `nodbg_*` spellings run inert (`W-A=00007 W-B=00000`). The binder had told the
arm by `REFERENCES()`, so `ALL W-A` under WITH DEBUGGING MODE would have fallen to the bare-name arm (and `ALL P1` would
have silently debugged paragraph P1); it now reads `ALL()`. Two siblings in `FinalizeDebug` fixed with it: its COBOL1571
diagnostics printed with no file and line (it runs after every walker; the PB2034 rule), and the ALL message named no
operand, so DB201A's two ALL operands in one USE were deduplicated into one. DB201A `--nist`: 37.4 s with two COBOL5506
before, 1.8 s with ten positioned COBOL1571 (the staged data/file subjects, VCR row 7.17) after. Goldens
`conformance-suite/cases/_unassigned/pb4237_debug_all_identifier` (85) and
`pb4237-debug-all-identifier-removed-2002` (COBOL0902 from 2002).
