# An insertion is anchored at its place in the entry (2026-11-24)

**Change** (`scripts/spec/text_layer_diff.py`, case_15 of its self-test). `amendment_accounts_for` checks a side with no words
at its place through one rule, `_stands_between`: the page's words on each side of the hunk must stand consecutively around
it in the amended text (`before`, the hunk's words, `after`). A drop (the Markdown side empty) is the empty-hunk case, as
before; an insertion (the printed side empty) now needs the same, where it had passed as any run of three or more words
found anywhere in the entry. A hunk at the page's edge has no neighbour on one side and is never anchored. `entry_text` ends
an entry before the next row's page cell (and a `page` printed with it) when the layer prints that cell in the last three
words before the next row's instruction (`_before_cell`), so the cell is no word of the amended text.

**Fail closed.** An insertion that cannot be anchored is not excused: its Markdown-only words fall through to the existing
classes (UNCOVERED, or UNEXPLAINED for a single moved word), never AMENDMENT-APPLIED.

**Evidence.** The round-4 probe (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/pb6135/r4/probe_wrongspot4.py`, pointed at this worktree): the
misplaced duplicate (`insert elsewhere`, `dup-line 3w`) and the stray `page` (`trail page`) are now UNCOVERED; the control
replacement stays AMENDMENT-APPLIED. case_15 plants both probes, a stray next-row page cell, a one-sided anchor, an insertion
at the page's edge, and two correct insertions (one word, four words) that still pass; on the tool before this change seven
of its expectations fail.
