# An insertion is anchored at its place in the entry (2026-11-24)

**Change** (`scripts/spec/text_layer_diff.py`, case_15 of its self-test). `amendment_accounts_for` checks a side with no words
at its place through one rule, `_stands_between`: the page's words on each side of the hunk must stand consecutively around
it in the amended text (`before`, the hunk's words, `after`). A drop (the Markdown side empty) is the empty-hunk case, as
before; an insertion (the printed side empty) now needs the same, where it had passed as any run of three or more words
found anywhere in the entry. A hunk at the page's edge has no neighbour on one side and is never anchored. `entry_text` ends
an entry before the next row's page cell (and a `page` printed with it) when the layer prints that cell in the last three
words before the next row's instruction (`_before_cell`), so the cell is no word of the amended text.

**The governed block (review round 1).** A neighbour on each side is not a place: a short insertion still passed wherever
its two neighbours recurred on the page (the reviewer's probe: `not` gained by a look-alike sentence the Amendment does not
touch, `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/pb6144-r1/probe_not.py`). So a note now excuses only differences inside the Markdown block it
is attached to. `amendment_notes` records, for each note, the nearest block above it on its page, past any other note: a run
of non-blank lines, a `<pre>` or fenced figure being one block with its blank lines. `_inside` checks every Markdown word of
the difference on that block's lines, and for a drop a word beside the gap on them; it runs before the entry and word checks,
for replacements, drops and insertions alike. A note with no block above it on its page governs nothing (none of the 300 notes
is of that kind today). case_16 plants the probe with the look-alike in another block, both sentences changed (only the
governed one applied), the amended sentence in a block the note does not follow, and the block rules (top of page, stacked
notes, figures). Within one block the neighbour rule is still the only locator: the probe written as one paragraph still
passes, and the block is as narrow as the transcription's own structure allows.

**Fail closed.** An insertion that cannot be anchored is not excused: its Markdown-only words fall through to the existing
classes (UNCOVERED, or UNEXPLAINED for a single moved word), never AMENDMENT-APPLIED.
