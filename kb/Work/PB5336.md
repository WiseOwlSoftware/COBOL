# PB5336 - the PAGE clause's LAST lines lose their printed indent

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/render_figure.py` (whole: the continuation-indent rule; locate it with `orient.py`).

The PAGE clause's general format (the clause of §2023:13.18.39.3 SR4, §2014:13.18.39.2 SR3 and §2002:13.16.37.2 SR3,
each "The HEADING, FIRST DETAIL, LAST CONTROL HEADING, LAST DETAIL, and FOOTING phrases may be written in any order",
`cite.py --check` OK in all three) prints its two `LAST` lines indented under `PAGE`. The generator draws them flush,
because its continuation-indent rule measures only lines that begin with a word, and these begin with a bracket. Every
word and every bracket is right, so no syntax changes; only the printed indent is lost. The finisher cited the clause as
"§2014:13.14.1"; `cite.py` refutes that locator, and the three above are the checked ones.

**Fix:** measure a bracket-led line's indent the way a word-led one is measured, redraw with `sweep_figures.py --apply`
per edition, check each redraw against its rendered page, and confirm no other figure moves unexpectedly.
