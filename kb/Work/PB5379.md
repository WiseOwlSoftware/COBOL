# PB5379 - at --std 85 a segment-number written with three or more digits (SEGMENT-LIMIT IS 010, a section header SECTION 020) compiles, though 1985 defines a segment-number as a one or two digit number and the level-number of the same definition list is refused COBOL1746

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs#ScreenSegmentNumber` (checks the value 1 through 49 or 0 through 99, never the form)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/LevelNumberPass.cs#VisitLevelNumber` (the form check a level-number gets, COBOL1746)

**Mechanism.** `ScreenSegmentNumber` reads the integer and applies the range rules (`VisitSegmentLimitClause` passes 1 and 49), so `010`, `001` and an 18-digit `000000000000000010` pass as 10 and 1. The definition limits the word itself to one or two digits, as it does for a level-number, which `LevelNumberPass` already enforces.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids below. Its row is not recorded: the refuter's OVERTURNED verdict is held for adjudication in `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/held-7.json`.

**Rules (1 claimed; every citation checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `1985-FMT-XVI.2.3.2` - `§1985:XVI.2.3.2` (refuter: OVERTURNED, harm: under-rejects): "SEGMENT-LIMIT IS segment-number", and `§1985:III.2`: "A segment-number may be expressed either as a one or two digit number". The section header's operand is the same word (`§1985:XVI.3.2.2`: "section-name SECTION [segment-number]"), outside this row.

**Repro.** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/out-reread2/refute-rr-08/p2-lim-010.cob` (`SEGMENT-LIMIT IS 010`), `p2-lim-001.cob` (`IS 001`), `p2-sec-020.cob` (`OVL-SECT SECTION 020`), `p2-lim-09.cob` (`IS 09`, legal), `p3-lvl-001.cob` (`001 X PIC 9`); `cobol.exe --std 85 --source-format fixed --run <file>`.

**Observed (the pinned compiler of `E:/COBOL-wt/coderead85-tree-b` (origin/main 8147f008b3; `git diff --stat 8147f008b3 origin/main -- src` is empty at 5237495315), rerun 2026-11-12; `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/reprobe-85f/log.txt`).** p2-lim-010, p2-lim-001 and p2-sec-020 compile and print `FIXED`, `OVERLAY`, as does the legal p2-lim-09; p3-lvl-001 is refused `COBOL1746: level-number 001 on 'X': a level-number is expressed as a one-digit or two-digit number`.

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** The three-digit forms are refused as not a segment-number; `09` compiles.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Check the written form in `ScreenSegmentNumber` (one place for both the clause and the section header), with the level-number's form rule as the model.
