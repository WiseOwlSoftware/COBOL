`NumericRenderer.cs#Power` sends an integer or exact base to `CobolExact.Pow` (`NumX.Exact`) and a scaled Int128 base to `PowNativeDec` (`NumX.Dec`). Sending the scaled base through `ExactPow` too would delete the Dec arm. It shares its root with PB2628.

The comment above `CombineCore` (`NumericRenderer.cs`, about line 736) lists "a power with an exact base (CobolExact.Pow, or PowNativeDec on the SDIDI for a scaled base ...)" among the three Dec producers. `CobolExact.Pow` yields `NumX.Exact`, never `NumX.Dec`. An integer power now produces no Dec at all; the only Dec power left is `PowNativeDec`, and its base is scaled, not exact. Correct the comment whichever way the carrier question lands.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs#Power`, `#CombineCore`; `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.RealArgs.cs#PowNativeDec`.
