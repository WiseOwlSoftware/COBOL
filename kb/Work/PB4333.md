# PB4333 — step 10 of PB4323: growth trigger, then the emitted-assembly differential

**Sites:** `scripts/gate.py` (whole: new in step 5, PB4328; would gain the manifest comparison; `Gate._finish`, `Gate._record_growth` and `Host.record_growth` write the growth sample), `scripts/gate_growth.py` (whole: the trigger, its sample store and its verdict line), `scripts/arch/emit_fraction.py` (whole: the changed-program fraction), `scripts/session-probe.ps1` (whole: one probe line added), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` (section 3.14.13). The merge group's shard-spread report is NOT a site: it measures CI artifacts, and the trigger's sample (a local gate's test phase) is not there.

**Trigger.** Conformance grew 94 % in 27 days (10,503 to 20,406). Built when the weekly median of the code lane's test phase (recorded in `verdict.json`) exceeds 6 min, or the population exceeds 1.5 times today's (about 30,000 Conformance cases). Until the trigger fires, nothing is built.

**If built.** The merge group writes a manifest of case to hash of the emitted program bytes for each main commit. A local code-lane gate whose diff does not touch `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime` compiles the population (46-84 s) and runs only the Conformance cases whose program bytes differ from main's manifest, plus every case that is not a compiled program. A runtime change runs everything. It is a sound selection: a case whose program bytes, runtime and harness inputs are byte-identical to main's cannot be affected. Measured first over 20 recent non-runtime landings; built only if the median fraction of changed programs is under 10 %.

**Adds / deletes.** 0 until it fires.

**Effect.** Conformance in a non-runtime gate from about 290 s to under 100 s if built.

**Closes.** Nothing.

**Order.** After step 5 (PB4328); step 5's acceptance measurement names this as the remedy after a thread cap and 2 slots.

**Landed (kb/Work PB4333, wave 20261807, 2026-11-01): the trigger became an instrument, and the second criterion was measured.** The note stays `half`: the differential is built only when the trigger fires, and it has not.

- **Re-probe.** `verdict.json` is written by `Gate._finish` and lives in the gate's worktree, so it is deleted with the worktree (63 records survived across 41 worktrees on 2026-11-01; only two carry `timings.tests_s`, the others `legs_s` of schema 4). A weekly median read from `verdict.json` could never be computed. Fixed at the root: every GREEN code-lane gate that ran its hosts writes one sample file beside the gate slots (`<git common dir>/cobol-gate-growth/`, `scripts/gate_growth.py`), `gate_growth.py status` reads the trailing 7 days and `session-probe.ps1` prints the line. A RED, a REUSED verdict and the inert lane are no sample (gate.py self-test arms, 70 now).
- **Time arm, first reading: not a weekly median yet.** The 16 whole-population GREEN gates of 2026-11-01 (all that exist; the shape began that day) have a median test phase of 464 s = 7.7 min, over the 6-minute limit, but they span one day and ran beside up to five fleets (three gates together measured 503 s each in the acceptance measurement of PB4328). The trigger judges only a complete window (7 days, 10 samples); until then it prints PARTIAL with the median it does not judge. It can fire from 2026-11-08.
- **Population arm: not fired.** Conformance 20,482 cases against the limit 30,609 (1.5 x 20,406), 1.00 times the review's basis.
- **Second criterion, measured now so the build decision has it: the changed-program fraction.** `scripts/arch/emit_fraction.py` over the 20 newest non-runtime landings of `8fd4ad387` (each and its first parent through the architecture oracle, about 4,700 programs of about 15,700 cases): median 0.00 % of programs changed (0.03 % counting added programs), maximum 0.08 % (0.34 %); 2 of the 20 changed any program's C# (1 and 4 programs). Validation: the review's landing `a95e97138` gives 0 changed, as the review measured. The criterion (under 10 %) is met by two orders of magnitude.
- **What the criterion does not measure, for whoever builds.** Of the 54 code-lane landings since 2026-10-31, 28 leave the runtime AND the Conformance harness (`tests/_shared`, the Conformance assembly's code, `conformance-suite/harness`, build properties) alone. The differential is sound only for those; the other 26 must run everything unless a finer rule is proved, and the finer rule is the classification that failed the impact map (PB2696, PB4228). The build also needs a mapping from an xunit case to its oracle id, a manifest store the merge group writes and a gate reads, and a population check that accepts a proved selection.
