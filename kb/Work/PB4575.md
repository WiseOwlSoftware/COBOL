# PB4575 - a wide product beside an SDIDI operand enters an expression or relation as its image

The expression-lane sibling of PB4568: a native expression or relation that mixes a wide product (here BIG * BIG * K3, 39 digits) with an SDIDI operand lowers the product
to its 34-digit image.

**Sites.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs#CombineCore` and `#CompareWide`.

**Probe** `lead.cob` (run 2026-11-03 from `p0/lead.dll`):
```
       01 BIG PIC 9(18) VALUE 999999999999999999.
       01 K3  PIC 999 VALUE 999.
       01 R   PIC S9(9).
           COMPUTE R = FUNCTION MAX (BIG * BIG * K3 - 1,
                       BIG * BIG * K3, 2.0E0) - (BIG * BIG * K3 - 5)
           DISPLAY "MAXOP " R
           COMPUTE R = (BIG * BIG * K3 + 2.0E0) - BIG * BIG * K3
           DISPLAY "EXPR  " R
           IF BIG * BIG * K3 - 1 < 2.0E0 + BIG * BIG * K3
              DISPLAY "REL   LT" ELSE DISPLAY "REL   NOT-LT".
```
Observed: `MAXOP -000100000`, `EXPR  +000000000`, `REL   NOT-LT`.
Expected (exact arithmetic, §8.8.4.2.4 comparison by algebraic value): `MAXOP +000000005` (MAX is P, and P - (P - 5) = 5), `EXPR  +000000002`, `REL   LT`.
MAXOP is the function-in-expression case, so it may close with PB4568's lane; EXPR and REL are the lead.
