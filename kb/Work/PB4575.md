# PB4575 - a wide product beside an SDIDI operand enters an expression or relation as its image

to its 34-digit image.

**Probe** `lead.cob` (run 2026-11-03 from `p0/lead.dll`):
```
       01 BIG PIC 9(18) VALUE 999999999999999999.
       01 K3  PIC 999 VALUE 999.
       01 R   PIC S9(9).
           COMPUTE R = FUNCTION MAX (BIG * BIG * K3 - 1,
                       BIG * BIG * K3, 2.0E0) - (BIG * BIG * K3 - 5)
           DISPLAY "MAXOP " R
           COMPUTE R = (BIG * BIG * K3 + 2.0E0) - BIG * BIG * K3
```

MAXOP is the function-in-expression case, so it may close with PB4568's lane; EXPR and REL are the lead.

## Landing (wave 20261859 group A)

**Re-probe on the branch base (9f5f0a1ae), `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-m6/w20261859a/p0/lead.cob`:** `MAXOP -000100000`, `EXPR +000000000`,
`REL NOT-LT` (as filed), plus `POW` `(P + 2 ** 2) - P` 0, `FRAC` `(P + 2.5E-1) - P` 0.0000, `MUL` `P * 2.0E0 - P * 2` -1000000.

**Root.** Under native arithmetic `CombineCore` sent every operation with an SDIDI-carried operand to the SDIDI lane (`DecBinary` /
`Divide`), whose operand lift (`DecOperandExact`) lowers an exact wide intermediate to its 34-digit round-to-odd image; and `CompareWide`
declined a wide value beside an SDIDI or unsigned 16-byte value, so the relation compared the two images. The wide form could not take the
pair because its scale is the compiler's and the SDIDI value's a run-time fact.

**Fix.** A run-time-scaled exact carrier, `NumX.Exact` (`CobolExact`): a +, -, * or NESTED quotient in which a wide value meets an SDIDI
operand (and every one over such a value) is exact on the 256-bit form at the scale the run time aligns, the SDIDI only past 77 digits
(DOC-A.1-123's determination), and carries its precision so a nested quotient's working scale is the one rule (D2). It settles where a wide
value settles (final transfer once, relation exactly, intrinsic argument, several-receiver snapshot, DISPLAY's temporary; round-to-odd
lowering otherwise). The relation of a wide value beside an SDIDI or unsigned value now compares the exact values too (a sibling: P's image
EQUALS the 34-digit literal `9.989999999999999980020000000000001E38` that P is below). A scaled Int128 operand beside an SDIDI one keeps the
SDIDI lane: DOC-A.1-82's published lane determination (PB274).

**After:** `EXPR +000000002`, `REL LT`, `POW +000000004`, `FRAC +000000000.2500`, `MUL +000000000`; golden
`conformance:cases/8.8.1.2-Native-standard-binary-and-standard-decimal/pb4575_wide_beside_sdidi_operand` (19 legs) and its 85 refusal
`pb4575_wide_beside_sdidi_operand_85`; `unit:CobolExactIntrinsicsTests` (the carrier's five facts); `ExactWideSettlementDriftTests`
pins the `NumX.Exact` arms at every settling site.

**Not closed here: MAXOP** (`MAX (P - 1, P, 2.0E0) - (P - 5)`, now -994, owed 5). MAX's returned value is held on the SDIDI carrier, rounded
once to 34 digits (P's nearest image), and the expression above it is now exact, so it shows that rounding: the returned-value carrier
question of PB4570, not this lane.
