# PB1560 — LANDED 2026-09-28 (wave 70 V3, with PB1509 + PB1666 — one carrier)

## Landing (wave 70 V3)

- **SUM counters:** re-probed (`PIC 9(20) SUM` of a 20-digit addend printed spaces); fixed by the ONE Int128 carrier
  PB1509's landing describes — `ReportWriter.SumEntry` / `SumTerm` / `SumValue` / `SetSumValue` and
  `RuntimeApi.ReportSumWrite` (`(Int128)(…)`, was `(long)(…)`). Goldens as PB1509.
- **PAGE-COUNTER — NOT a defect (re-verdicted with evidence).** docs/CONFORMANCE.md (the counter registers' declared
  capacity, kb/Work R26) pins PAGE-COUNTER / LINE-COUNTER as **PIC 9(18)** — the standard gives them no size
  (§8.4.3.15.4 GR1: "temporary unsigned integer data items"), and 18 digits is exactly what the `long` carrier holds.
  Probe: `MOVE 123456789012345678901 TO PAGE-COUNTER` (a 21-digit sender) then `SOURCE PAGE-COUNTER` into PIC 9(18)
  prints 456789012345678901 — the §14.9.25 MOVE truncation of an 18-digit receiver, stored through the register's
  profile before `ReportPageCounterWrite`'s `(long)` cast, which is therefore exact.

Found 2026-09-24 by the w59b implementer (integer-narrowing group) outside its mechanism; **probe first**:
`05 SUM X` with `PIC 9(20)` and a 20-digit addend. §13.18.54 (SUM clause) gives the sum counter the size of its
PICTURE; the carrier must be the item's own typed-native numeric (the same carrier as any other numeric item of that
PICTURE), never a host long. Sweep every report-writer counter store/read.
