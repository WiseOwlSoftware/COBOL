# PB1560

Found 2026-09-24 by the w59b implementer (integer-narrowing group) outside its mechanism; **probe first**:
`05 SUM X` with `PIC 9(20)` and a 20-digit addend. §13.18.54 (SUM clause) gives the sum counter the size of its
PICTURE; the carrier must be the item's own typed-native numeric (the same carrier as any other numeric item of that
PICTURE), never a host long. Sweep every report-writer counter store/read.
