# PB331 — the spec spelling of READ is a syntax error and the reverse parses

**Rows:** `FMT-14.9.30.2`, `SR-14.9.30.3-3`, `SR-14.9.30.3-10`, `SR-14.9.30.3-11`, `SR-14.9.30.3-12`.

Both halves were measured from the PRINTED page, not the transcription: `clause_page.py 14.9.30.2` → PDF page
722, then `figure_geometry.py 722` for the bracket rectangles.

## Part 1 — the KEY phrase is two slots too early

```cobol
READ IXF RECORD WITH NO LOCK KEY IS IX-KEY
    INVALID KEY DISPLAY "BAD"
    NOT INVALID KEY DISPLAY "OK " IX-DATA
END-READ
```

→ `error COBOL0001: no viable alternative at input 'KEY'` (plus cascade COBOL0307/0306). Identical failure with
`IGNORING LOCK KEY IS ...` and `RETRY 3 TIMES KEY IS ...`. The NON-printed order `KEY IS IX-KEY WITH NO LOCK`
compiles. Probes: `out-b1/probe/keyorder/spec_nolock.cob`, `spec_ignoring.cob`, `spec_retry.cob`, `rev.cob`.

**The printed order.** §14.9.30.2 Format 2 lists, top to bottom: `READ file-name-1 RECORD [INTO identifier-1]` /
`[IGNORING LOCK | retry-phrase]` / `[WITH LOCK | WITH NO LOCK]` / `[KEY IS {data-name-1 | record-key-name-1}]` /
`[INVALID KEY ... | NOT INVALID KEY ...]` / `[END-READ]`. `figure_geometry.py 722` puts the two lock brackets at
y=507.57 and y=547.48 and the KEY bracket at y=589.03 — the KEY phrase FOLLOWS them.

`readStatement` is written `READ (fileName|FILE fileName) readDirection? RECORD? readInto? readKey?
readAdvancingOnLock? retryPhrase? recordLockPhrase? readAtEnd? readInvalidKey? END_READ?`. ANTLR takes the first
matching alternative in written order and each phrase is a separate optional, so the parser has already passed
the `readKey` slot by the time it consumes the lock phrase and the trailing KEY tokens have nowhere to go. All
three programs above are conforming source and shall compile.

## Part 2 — the two Format-1 brackets, both cardinalities inverted at once

**a) OVER-REJECTION.** A record-sequential file with `LOCK MODE IS MANUAL` and
`READ SQF NEXT RECORD IGNORING LOCK WITH NO LOCK AT END ... NOT AT END ... END-READ`
→ `error COBOL0001: no viable alternative at input 'WITH'` (`out-b1/probe/lockcombo/seq.cob`).

**b) UNDER-REJECTION.** The same program with
`READ SQF NEXT RECORD ADVANCING ON LOCK RETRY 3 TIMES IGNORING LOCK ...`
→ compiles clean (`out-b1/probe/lockcombo/seq3.cob`).

`figure_geometry.py 722` measures Format 1 as a bracket y=280.90 h=48.79 over THREE stacked alternatives
(`ADVANCING ON LOCK` / `IGNORING LOCK` / retry-phrase) and a SEPARATE bracket y=339.26 h=31.98 over TWO
(`WITH LOCK` / `WITH NO LOCK`); neither carries choice-indicator bars — contrast the AT END group at y=377.08,
which the same tool flags `<-- CHOICE INDICATORS (5.2.6.4)`.

> §5.2.6.2: [a plain bracket admits] the syntax element contained within the brackets, or one of the
> alternatives contained within the brackets

`--check 5.2.6.2 "or one of the alternatives contained within the brackets"` → **OK**. So: at most ONE from each
bracket, and the two brackets are INDEPENDENT. (a) is therefore legal and shall compile; (b) is illegal and
shall be rejected. The union of the two errors is exactly backwards — what the brackets make mutually exclusive
is free, and what they make independent is exclusive.

`recordLockPhrase : IGNORING LOCK | WITH? NO LOCK | WITH? LOCK` merges bracket-1's `IGNORING LOCK` into
bracket-2's alternatives, and `readStatement` then offers `readAdvancingOnLock? retryPhrase? recordLockPhrase?`
as three free optionals.

## The trap in fixing part 2 — SR3 today is enforced only by the collapse

`FileLockBinder.CheckRecordLockPhrase`'s summary says it validates that *IGNORING LOCK and WITH LOCK are mutually
exclusive*, but the body computes `kind` and tests nothing except `file.LockMode is { Kind: LockKind.Automatic }`
(which is SR4). SR3 forbids `IGNORING LOCK` with *the LOCK phrase*, and §14.9.30.4 GR11 b)/d) name *the NO LOCK
phrase* and *the LOCK phrase* as DIFFERENT phrases — so the forbidden pair is unwritable today ONLY because of
the collapse. **Splitting the brackets correctly removes the only thing enforcing SR3 unless a real SR3 check
lands in the same change set.**

## SR10 / SR11 / SR12

They ride the KEY phrase and cannot be reached in the printed spelling while part 1 stands. SR10 —
*The KEY phrase may be specified only if ORGANIZATION IS INDEXED is specified in the file control entry for
file-name-1* (`--check 14.9.30.3` → **OK**) — is additionally unenforced on the sequential arm for a second,
independent reason: **PB334**.

**Code sites:** `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolIO.g4#readStatement`, `#recordLockPhrase` ·
`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/FileLockBinder.cs#CheckRecordLockPhrase`.

## Landed 2026-09-05

**Both halves, plus two the measurement found on the same page.** The printed figure was re-rendered
(`render-spec-page.py 722`) and re-measured before anything was edited, and the transcription was found already
CORRECT on every point — the grammar had diverged from it, so `specs/ISO_COBOL.md` needed no repair.

- **Part 1 (order).** `readStatement` now spells the phrases in the printed sequence: `readInto?`,
  `readLockContentionPhrase?`, `recordLockPhrase?`, `readKey?`. §5.2.1 — *"The words, phrases, clauses,
  punctuation, and operands in each general format shall be written in the compilation group in the sequence
  given in the general format"* (`--check 5.2.1` OK) — is why the printed order is the conforming one and the
  reverse is not: `spec_nolock` / `spec_ignoring` / `spec_retry` all compile and run, and the previously
  accepted `KEY IS IX-KEY WITH NO LOCK` is now rejected (`negative/pb331-read-key-before-lock`).
- **Part 2 (the two brackets).** `readLockContentionPhrase : readAdvancingOnLock | readIgnoringLock |
  retryPhrase` is bracket 1 and `recordLockPhrase : WITH? NO LOCK | WITH? LOCK` is bracket 2 — ONE grammar
  object per printed bracket, so §5.2.6.2's at-most-one is written down once and §5.2.6.1's independence is the
  shape rather than a rule anyone has to remember. `IGNORING LOCK WITH NO LOCK` compiles;
  `ADVANCING ON LOCK RETRY 3 TIMES IGNORING LOCK` is rejected.
- **The trap the note flagged was real, and is closed in the same change set.**
  `FileLockBinder.CheckIgnoringLock` → **COBOLNET1818** is SR3's first actual check; its doc comment no longer
  claims a rule it does not enforce. `negative/pb331-read-ignoring-with-lock` pins the rejection at all four
  editions and `2002/pb331_read_lock_brackets` pins the PERMITTED pair, so a compiler that rejected any two
  lock phrases would fail the pair rather than pass the negative.
- **Two siblings the sweep found** (rule 4 — every bug is a pattern). (a) WRITE and REWRITE referenced the
  merged rule, so `WRITE R IGNORING LOCK` and `REWRITE R IGNORING LOCK` compiled although §14.9.51.2 (pages
  815/816) and §14.9.35.2 (page 740) print no such alternative; both are now rejected, with negatives.
  (b) `figure_extract.py 722` measures `_ADVANCING_ ON _LOCK_` — ON is NOT underlined, so §5.2.3 makes it an
  optional word and `READ … ADVANCING LOCK` is conforming source that drew
  `error COBOL0001: missing token before 'LOCK'`. The rule is now `ADVANCING ON? LOCK`.
- **A third, found by sweeping the whole figure rather than the two defects this note named.** The one
  printed optional word `RECORD` was written in TWO grammar places — inside `readDirection` and again as
  `readStatement`'s own `RECORD?` — so `READ SQF NEXT RECORD RECORD … END-READ` compiled clean (measured: exit
  0, no diagnostics). `readDirection` is now `NEXT | PREVIOUS`; `negative/pb331-read-record-word-twice` pins it.
  This one had to be fixed here rather than filed: `FMT-14.9.30.2` is the row for the FORMAT, and a second
  live over-acceptance of the same figure would have made the CONFORMS verdict this note records false.

**The re-architecture the estimate did not carry (rule 5).** A single `BoundRecordLock` enum cannot represent
`IGNORING LOCK WITH NO LOCK`, because the two facts come from two independent brackets — so the enum is now the
RETENTION bracket alone (`None | WithLock | WithNoLock`), `BoundRead`/`BoundKeyedRead` carry `IgnoringLock`
beside `AdvancingOnLock`/`Retry`, and the runtime's `FileRecordLock` / `CobolFile.ReadShared` /
`ReadLockGovern` take the two separately. That is also a live correctness fix: with IGNORING folded into the
enum, `FileRegistry.ApplyReadLockDiscipline` could never reach §14.9.30.4 GR11 b)'s release for the combined
phrase. `CobolFileLockTests.ReadShared_IgnoringLockWithNoLock_…` drives it with a falsifying control arm.
Recorded as `docs/COBOLNET_FILES_DESIGN.md` **D16**.

**Edition gates swept** (`feedback_edition_gate_sweep`). Moving IGNORING LOCK out of `recordLockPhrase` would
have shipped it UNGATED below 2002 — its only gate was membership of that rule. `VersionConformancePass`
gained `VisitReadIgnoringLock` with the same where-string, and `--std 85` was re-measured for IGNORING LOCK,
WITH NO LOCK and ADVANCING LOCK: each still draws COBOLNET0900, once per phrase.

**Rows.** `FMT-14.9.30.2` DIVERGES → **CONFORMS** and `SR-14.9.30.3-3` PARTIAL → **CONFORMS** (GAP 2821 →
2819). The other three rows this note used to claim are RE-ATTRIBUTED, because their remaining halves are other
notes' mechanisms and not this format defect: `SR-14.9.30.3-10` → **PB334** (the sequential READ arm never
calls `r.readKey()`, one of PB334's three unread phrases — the parse tree still carries the phrase at
`ReadStatementContext.readKey()` for it to read), and `SR-14.9.30.3-11` / `SR-14.9.30.3-12` → **PB358** (the
`record-key-name SOURCE IS` form has no grammar and no named decline; PB358 already owns START's twins). Both
rows keep a `test-ref` they never had: the new positive golden is the first corpus program to write the KEY
phrase in the printed order at all.
