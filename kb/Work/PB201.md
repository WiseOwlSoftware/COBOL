# PB201

Found 2026-08-31 while MEASURING the reachability of `NumericRenderer.FieldNumCore`'s group arm for
[[PB178]] (the cluster-A burn-down). PRE-EXISTING; nothing in that landing touched the subscript path.

**MEASURED, both shapes, at `--std 2023` strict AND `--permissive` (identical output — no dialect gate is
involved):**

- a WORKING-STORAGE group as a subscript, `01 WG. 05 WF1 PIC 9(2).` … `MOVE TE(WG) TO R` →
  `error CS1503: Argument 1: cannot convert from '_PRG_NRSUB2._T_0' to 'long'` — the group's record STRUCT
  is handed to `CobolTable.At` where an index is wanted.
- a BASED group as a subscript, `01 BODO BASED. 05 BF1 PIC 9(2). 05 BT OCCURS 1 TO 5 DEPENDING ON NN …` …
  `MOVE TE(BODO) TO R` → `error CS0103: The name 'BODO' does not exist in the current context` — the raw
  COBOL word is emitted verbatim, because a class-tier group has no C# field of that name at all.

**THE MECHANISM, and why it is not [[PB178]]'s.** The subscript index is the D10 TRANSITIONAL string carrier
(a rendered index expression on the `AccessPath` segment, per the PHASE-07 Step 11 plan / plan D10), so the
subscript path renders the reference DIRECTLY rather than routing it through `NumericRenderer.FieldNum`. That
is why the group arm of `FieldNumCore` — which decodes a group's image as an unsigned integer, and which
`--permissive` does reach for an ARITHMETIC operand — never sees a subscript. Two consequences, both bad:

1. `ExpressionBinder`'s §8.8.1.1 group-operand rejection (COBOLNET0844) does not fire either, so the
   construct is neither properly REJECTED nor properly COMPILED — it is accepted at bind and then breaks the
   backend. Under-rejects, then crashes.
2. Whatever the eventual verdict, the two shapes must not diverge: today one emits a struct and the other
   emits an undefined identifier.

**DERIVE BEFORE FIXING.** §8.4.2.3 governs what a subscript may be (the subscript forms and their integer
requirement) — read it and cite the exact rule before choosing between (a) a bind rejection, the likely
answer, since a group is not an integer numeric item, or (b) routing the subscript through the ONE numeric
read the way every other numeric context does. ⛔ Do NOT infer the answer from §8.8.1.1: that clause governs
ARITHMETIC EXPRESSIONS, and a subscript is not one — the real clause may permit or forbid on different
grounds (`feedback_a_real_clause_can_answer_a_different_question`).

**WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND THE CRASH.** This is the last unmeasured arm of `FieldNum`'s 9 callers. The PB178
landing established that a group operand reaching a numeric context is reachable under `--permissive`; the
subscript path is the one caller where it does not reach at all, and the reason is a transitional carrier
that D10 is scheduled to remove. Fixing it before D10 lands means fixing it twice; fixing it after means the
crash ships until PHASE 15. That trade-off is the owner's, not the implementer's.
