# PB821 — OPEN — a valid picture the category rules do not reach

Filed 2026-09-09 by the registrar (second pass) from the **PB528 implementer's report**, "New defects" (b) —
`scratchpad/reports/PB528-report.md`. **Re-probed on this worktree's own build at `d3ba5795`.**

## The measurement

```
$ cobol.exe p17_editing.cob --std 2023 --run
[  ]
exit=0
```

`registrar2/probe/p17_editing.cob` is `01 E1 PIC LL EDITING "L" IS ":".` displayed between brackets. It
compiles, occupies two character positions, and initialises to spaces — i.e. it is classified as an *edited*
item (`anyEdit`), which is why it has a character image at all.

## The two rules, both `--check`ed

```
$ python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 13.18.40.3 "at least two occurrences of one of the symbols from the set character-1"
OK  §13.18.40.3 12) a)  (Syntax rules)
    — at least two occurrences of one of the symbols from the set character-1, 'x', '+', '-', and the currency
      symbol.
```

So `LL` — two occurrences of character-1 — makes character-string-1 **valid**. Now the category rules,
verbatim from `specs/ISO_COBOL.md` §13.18.40.4:

> 7) To define an item as alphanumeric-edited, character-string-1 shall include
> — at least one symbol 'A' or one symbol 'X', and
> — at least one instance of character-1 or one of the symbols from the set 'B', '0', '/'.
>
> 8) To define an item as boolean, character-string-1 shall contain only one or more occurrences of the symbol '1'.
>
> 9) To define an item as national, character-string-1 shall contain only one or more occurrences of the symbol 'N'.
>
> 13) a) To define a fixed-point numeric-edited item, character-string-1 shall include:
> — at least one symbol 'Z'; or — at least one symbol '\*'; or — at least two identical symbols from the set
> '+', '-', currency symbol; or — at least one symbol '9' and at least one instance of character-1 or at least
> one of the symbols from the set 'B', 'CR', 'DB', '0', '/', ',', '.', '+', '-', the currency symbol.

`LL` satisfies none of them: no 'A'/'X' (GR7), not only '1' (GR8), not only 'N' (GR9), no Z / \* / two identical
sign-or-currency symbols / '9' (GR13 a)). **The string is valid and categoryless.**

## What the compiler does, and why that is the finding

`PictureAnalyzer.Analyze` computes `anyEdit` as `char1Set.Count > 0` and returns **alphanumeric-edited**. That
answer may well be the right one — an EDITING character-1 is an insertion symbol and alphanumeric-edited is
where insertions live — but it rests on **a code path, not on a rule**. Nothing in §13.18.40.4 says so, and
nothing in the register records a determination that it should. That is the difference between *correct* and
*unverified*, and it is why this is an `analysis` note and not a defect: the observable behaviour is harmless
today (the item initialises to spaces; no corpus program MOVEs to it), and what is missing is the derivation.

## What the analysis owes

1. Decide whether §13.18.40.4's category list is intended to be **exhaustive** over valid character-strings —
   i.e. whether a valid-but-categoryless string is a defect in the standard, a case SR12 a) over-admits, or a
   category the rules assign elsewhere (check §13.18.40.6's precedence tables and the EDITING clause's own
   §13.18.40.3 SR24/SR25 before concluding).
2. If the answer is "alphanumeric-edited", write the derivation down at `PictureAnalyzer.Analyze` with a
   `--check`ed citation, so the next reader does not have to re-derive it — and add the golden.
3. If the answer is "no category", decide what the compiler shall do with it: it is legal source, so a
   rejection is not available without an owner decision (`feedback_no_deferral_default`).
