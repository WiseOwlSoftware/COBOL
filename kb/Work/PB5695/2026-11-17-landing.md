## 2026-11-17: landed — the limit on the size of one data item (COBOL7641), at every edition

**Decided (the note's "To decide").** A compile-time refusal against a documented implementor limit. No edition's text bounds
the size of a data item: X3.23-1985 states no limit (its Section I conformance clauses, §1985:I.1.5.2, say nothing of
capacities), and §2023:4.5 ("Translation may be unsuccessful due to factors other than lack of conformance of a compilation
group", whose NOTE names "the limits of an implementation"; `cite.py --check` OK) admits one. The run-time alternative
(EC-STORAGE-NOT-AVAIL on NEW) does not reach the 1985 or WORKING-STORAGE form of the defect, which allocates at activation,
before any statement, and has no exception model at 85.

**The limit is one number.** The compiler already held an ELEMENTARY item to 134,217,728 (2^27) character positions
(COBOL2148, kb/Work PB531: the largest power of two whose UTF-16 image stays inside .NET's single-object ceiling). A group's
image is the same kind of string, so the same limit holds for it. The constant moved from `PictureAnalyzer` to
`RecordLayout.MaxCharacterPositions`; `RecordLayout.ExceedsSizeLimit` is the one decider over a laid-out item, and
`CheckStorageLimitsPass` (after `InheritSignClauses`, before the REDEFINES passes) reports COBOL7641 at the outermost item
past it, in every section the unit's forest holds (WORKING-STORAGE, LOCAL-STORAGE, FILE, LINKAGE, COMMUNICATION, object and
method data). Documented as determination `docs/conformance/3/D-ITEM-SIZE.md`.

**The wrap is gone.** `RecordLayout.ImageWidth` and `PhysicalWidth` summed `int` products; they now sum in `long`
(`ExactImageWidth`, saturating through `TableValuePlan.SaturatingAdd`/`SaturatingMultiply`) and saturate to `int`, so the
diagnostic names the true size (2,000,000,000,000 for the incident's entry; "more than 9,223,372,036,854,775,806" for a
product past `long`) and no reader sees a wrapped one.

**Siblings swept (the note's list).** WORKING-STORAGE (`big-ws`, `big-two`), FILE record, an OCCURS DEPENDING maximum and
LINKAGE (`big-sections`, three reports), nested tables past `long` (`big-nest`), a REDEFINES subordinate larger than its
group (`big-redef`, COBOL7641 beside COBOL1539), a national table in an alphanumeric group (`big-national`, 2,023 only), and
the note's own OBJECT WORKING-STORAGE class (`big-oo`, compile-only on the fixed compiler). LOCAL-STORAGE and SORT records
are records of the same forest. ALLOCATE of a giant BASED item (2002+) is the same item size and the same pass; its run-time
storage request is PB5696's side. A host that cannot allocate an item WITHIN the limit is §2023:4.6's limit of an
implementation, not this note.

**Witnesses.** `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/DataItemSizeLimitTests.cs` (at the limit accepted, one past it, the incident
and a product past `long` refused, each at 85, 2002, 2014 and 2023; CheckOnly, so nothing is allocated);
`conformance-suite/wiseowl/cases/_unassigned/pb5695-group-beyond-size-limit` (WS, FILE and LINKAGE, refused at every edition).

**Evidence.** Probes under `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/my-20261115/w20262102/b/p/` and runs under `.../b/runs-new/`; the
differential `check-batch` over 20,717 (source, edition) entries (every NIST program at 85, every conformance case at 85 and
2023, the probes) against a main build changed the verdict of the probes alone.

**Review round 1, R1-1 (high), fixed at the class.** The first landing narrowed only `ImageWidth` and `PhysicalWidth`;
`DataItem.ByteWidth` still summed `int` products with a checked `Enumerable.Sum`, so an over-limit group with a REDEFINES over
it, or an FD with a second record, crashed the compiler with an OverflowException in `ClassifyRedefinesClassesPass` before
COBOL7641 printed, and five more sites kept the shape. The ruling: one computation, one narrowing, the check before any
narrowing pass, an enforceable shape. Done:
- `RecordLayout`'s size section is the ONE computation in 64 bits: `ExactImageWidth`, `ExactByteWidth`, `ExactExtent` (storage
  with every overlay), `ElementaryOccurrences`, `OccupiedBytes`/`OccupiedBits` (the REDEFINES comparisons), the offset walks,
  `Times` (the OCCURS product), `Product`, `Plus`, `OverStorageChildren` (the group sum); `BitLayout`'s walk runs in `long` too
  (`ExactExtentBits`). Every other site (ByteWidth, ItemLength, Place, DataBinder.Ptr, ResolveRedefinesPass, the record plans,
  FileModel's record lengths, GroupImageCodec, PhysicalModel, PublishedRecordEmitter, IntrinsicBinder, ParameterConformance,
  VariableLengthCompatibility, the CONSTANT entry's LENGTH OF) calls it.
- `RecordLayout.ItemSize` is the ONE narrowing: an internal compiler error above `MaxMeasure` (2^30, the bits of the largest
  storage an admitted item has). `SaturatedBound` is the one conversion of a RUN-TIME bound that is no item's size (a
  dynamic-length maximum in a record length, the debug register's width), saturating as before.
- The limit gained two measures beside the image: storage with overlays (at most 2^27 bytes, so every bit offset is an `int`)
  and the count of elementary items (a table of dynamic-length items). `CheckStorageLimitsPass` also screens TYPEDEF templates
  and, once it reports, throws `DataDivisionRefused`; `CompilerDriver` answers with the diagnostics so far. The CONSTANT entry's
  LENGTH OF, which binds before the check, reads the 64-bit length.
- `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/Architecture/SizeArithmeticDriftTests.cs`: a TEXT test (chosen over a Roslyn one for cost)
  banning `Occurs ?? 1`, an OCCURS value or a width multiplied, a width summed by `Sum` or by `+=`, a width clamped by
  `(int)Math.Min`, outside `RecordLayout.cs`; a theory proves it catches each shape the review found; a fact pins `RecordLayout.ItemSize` (then named `Narrow`).
- Refusal cases (all four editions, COBOL7641, no crash): `wiseowl/cases/_unassigned/pb5695-over-limit-group-redefined`,
  `-file-record-two-records`, `-occurs-depending`, `-renames-range`, `-sort-record`, `-linkage`; unit cases for the stop, the
  storage measure (overlays, a national group) and the elementary count.

**Review round 2.** All three findings were fixed: R2-2 (high), R2-1 (medium), and the lead R2-3, fixed as the ruling's point 1.
- **R2-2: the narrowing of an ITEM'S OWN size.** The round-1 narrowing (`Narrow`) was also reached by a computation over
  HYPOTHETICAL counts, which no limit had proved in range. `SequentialIoEmitter.OdoRecordExceedsMaximum` searches for the
  largest DEPENDING count that a 2023 READ's record maximum admits, and it sized the table through `BitLayout.TableBits` at counts
  past the table's maximum. So a legal record (USAGE BIT plus OCCURS DEPENDING, RECORD VARYING TO 500000000) crashed the
  compiler with the internal compiler error. Main compiled it. The API is split so the two cannot be confused:
  - `RecordLayout.ItemSize` (renamed from `Narrow`) narrows only an item's own measure.
  - `BitLayout.TableBitsAtCount(table, long count)` sizes a hypothetical count and stays 64-bit.
  - `RecordLayout.SaturatedBound` converts a run-time bound.
  - The search computes in 64 bits.
