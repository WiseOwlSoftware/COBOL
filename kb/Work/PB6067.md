# PB6067 - three gaps the 1985 widening left in the generator and the 2023 store

1. **`check_editions` never asks `refuse-at`.** Free form before 2002 is refused over `accept + flag.at`, not over a refusal entry's
   `refuse-at`. The widening wrote 34 free-form `cases/` refusal tests (for example `pb128-compute-alphanumeric-receiver`,
   `constant-record-level`) to refuse-at 85, and they close 1985 rows (for example `1985-SR-VI.6.8.3-1`) as own witnesses, while
   33 free-form ACCEPTED cases at 85 were discarded as "not 1985 programs". Pre-existing on main (1290 free-form `cases/`
   refusal tests refuse at 85 on that tree). `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-conformance-suite.md` section 4 lists free form at 85 in
   `cases/` as a refusal without the restriction, so the rule is unsettled: a free-form program at 85 is refused for its FORMAT,
   which is not the refusal of the rule the row names, so it witnesses nothing of that rule. Settle the doctrine (see PB6033 and
   PB6037 for the free-form-at-85 ruling), then make the generator and the design agree.
2. **22 of 74 cases carry `accept: [85, 2023]`** although their rules exist at 2002 (and 2014): a 2002 widening the operator's
   lane can make, with the 2002 and 2014 expectations proven.
3. **The 2023 store's test-refs name cases whose headers do not cite the rule.** 25 KEPT references inherit it, for example
   `GR-14.9.43.4-5` to `cases/14.9.43-STRING/pb664_string_operand_screen`, whose header cites SR-14.9.43.3-1 only. The generator's
   `rules` field and the 2023 store disagree; this is also the blocker for the widening's addition 1.

**Sites:** `conformance-suite/harness/generate.py` (`_check_store_witnesses`, `check_cases`, `self_test`),
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-conformance-suite.md` (section 3.6, section 4), the `accept:` lines of the 22 cases, the `rules`
header of 165 cases, `tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/` (eight rows' retired witnesses).

**Landed (wave 20262151 group F).**
1. Re-probed on main ccb23d13e4: the premise is stale. `tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/` names ONE free-form case,
   `cases/13.18.33-Level-number/pb485-level-number-missing`, already retired from 1985-SR-VI.5.7.3-1 (PB5754); no 1985 row
   closes through a free-form refusal test, and `lineage_reads.format_problem` (the writer, the audit and the planner) already
   refuses one. DESIGN-conformance-suite section 3.6 already rules that a free-form REFUSAL test may refuse at 85 (about 1,150
   do) as a regression test of `--source-format free`, witnessing no row; the section 4 list now says "ACCEPTED or FLAGGED".
   `check_editions` stays as it is.
2. The 22 cases were run at 2002 and 2014 (`run_probe.py --expect`): every `.out` matched at both, and `generate.py --check`
   accepts them at `[85, 2002, 2014, 2023]`.
3. The 2023 store named 172 cases (285 pairs) whose headers did not cite the row: the headers now do (the citeable pairs, 165 headers in all with the 12 the chained rows needed; the pairs
   whose case is accepted outside the row's band are introduction gates, which a case cannot cite). `generate.py` refuses
   the difference (`_check_store_witnesses`), the drift test for the rule. The chained rows' unrelated witnesses (eight, each on
   a row with other own witnesses) are retired through `record_verdicts.py --edition 1985`; the 2002 rows whose case cannot
   cite a 2023-only band stay exempt until their band is widened.
