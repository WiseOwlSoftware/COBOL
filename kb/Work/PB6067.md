# PB6067 - three gaps the 1985 widening left in the generator and the 2023 store

1. **`check_editions` never asks `refuse-at`.** Free form before 2002 is refused over `accept + flag.at`, not over a refusal entry's
   `refuse-at`. The widening wrote 34 free-form `cases/` refusal tests (for example `pb128-compute-alphanumeric-receiver`,
   `constant-record-level`) to refuse-at 85, and they close 1985 rows (for example `1985-SR-VI.6.8.3-1`) as own witnesses, while
   33 free-form ACCEPTED cases at 85 were discarded as "not 1985 programs". Pre-existing on main (1290 free-form `cases/`
   refusal tests refuse at 85 on that tree). `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-conformance-suite.md` section 4 lists free form at 85 in
   `cases/` as a refusal without the restriction, so the rule is unsettled: a free-form program at 85 is refused for its FORMAT,
   which is not the refusal of the rule the row names, so it witnesses nothing of that rule. Settle the doctrine (see PB6033 and
   PB6037 for the free-form-at-85 ruling), then make the generator and the design agree.
2. **22 of 74 cases carry `accept: [85, 2023]`** although their rules exist at 2002 (and 2014): a 2002 widening the operator's
   lane can make, with the 2002 and 2014 expectations proven.
3. **The 2023 store's test-refs name cases whose headers do not cite the rule.** 25 KEPT references inherit it, for example
   `GR-14.9.43.4-5` to `cases/14.9.43-STRING/pb664_string_operand_screen`, whose header cites SR-14.9.43.3-1 only. The generator's
   `rules` field and the 2023 store disagree; this is also the blocker for the widening's addition 1.

**Sites:** `conformance-suite/harness/generate.py#check_editions`, the 2023 store under `tests/version-matrix/inventory`, the
`accept:` lines of the 22 cases (list them with `grep -l "accept: \[85, 2023\]"`).
