# PB173

§13.18.29.4 GR1b makes a bit group an as-if elementary PICTURE 1(m) and §8.4.3.3 gives its ref-mod slice
boolean positions — but the resolver wraps EVERY group ref-mod in GroupImagePlace, whose Read/Write are the
PACKED byte image (`AsImage`/`FromImage`, ceil(m/8) characters), while `RefModPlace.Category` (OperandPic)
answers Boolean and the boolean channel's widths (Gr3Width/RefModLen) count BITS. The units disagree.

PB157's landing posture: the boolean-COMPUTE RECEIVER arm stages COBOLNET0899 (recognized-not-implemented —
legal source, loud, never a bit-into-byte splice), and the relation/COMPUTE SENDING classifications exclude
a ref-mod-of-bit-group so it keeps the byte-consistent GENERAL relation channel it always rode. PB157's
review fleet also measured: PlaceRenderer.Write's ref-mod boolean pad reads RAW Pic (a bit-group slice
would pad with SPACES where §14.6.8.6 wants boolean zeros), and the MOVE surface (MoveReceiverCategory →
rm.Category) has admitted the slice since PB70/PB79 — the same missing model, pre-existing.

WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT: a bit-position slice place for as-if-elementary groups (ref-mod over the UNPACKED bit
string, repack on write — the §8.4.3.3 unique data item), the §14.6.8.6 boolean pad keyed on OperandPic,
the 0899 stage and the channel exclusions removed, and goldens for `COMPUTE G(1:3) = …`, `MOVE B"11" TO
G(1:2)` and `IF G(1:3) = B"101"`.
