# PB3755 — GateSr5 has two askers; the activation arm is a third

**Sites:** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs#GateSr5`, `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs#GateMove`, `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs#GateInitialize`.

Filed 2026-10-25 by the enqueue clerk from PB3605's R1-1 LEAD (low, pre-existing, not a PB3605 regression).
`GateSr5` (VersionConformancePass.cs about line 401) holds the SR5 edition rows (HIGH-VALUE, LOW-VALUE, QUOTE and a non-digit ALL
literal as the sender of a MOVE to a numeric item) and has exactly two askers: `GateMove` (line 375) and `GateInitialize` (INITIALIZE REPLACING's hypothetical MOVE).

Rule (read in `specs/ISO_COBOL.md` on 2026-10-25):
§14.8.2.3.3 rule 2 d) "Otherwise, the conformance rules are the same as for a MOVE statement with the argument as the sending operand and the corresponding
formal parameter as the receiving operand" (rule 2 applies to a program with a REPOSITORY program-specifier, a NESTED CALL, a method, a function). So a figurative argument BY CONTENT
into a non-numeric elementary formal is under the same MOVE rules as the explicit statement. PB3605 fixed the SPACE arm in both phases (the MOVE chain and
`ActivationRelations`), but the edition rows are not asked of the activation arm.

## What the user's program sees
A nested CALL (or a method or function call) that passes a 2023-removed figurative BY CONTENT into the formal the removal row names compiles clean at
`--std 2023`, where the equivalent MOVE is refused by the SR5 edition row. No diagnostic; the source is accepted where the edition forbids it
(under-rejection, silent).

## Fix direction
Make the activation arm a third asker of `GateSr5` (the edition rows live in one place, PB879), or better, make the rows part of the conformance relation the
activation arm already asks (the MOVE rules), so a fourth asker is automatic. Golden per edition (85, 2002, 2014, 2023). Cross-link PB3605.
