# PB4444 — COBOL1639 for an unreserved SELF/SUPER lands at the statement

**Defect.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.cs:650` (`OoBinder.OoPredefinedSearchRoot`)
reports the PB4153 COBOL1639 at `qualifier`, which is null for a bare SELF or SUPER (an INVOKE receiver, an object
property's object). The diagnostic then lands at the enclosing statement or data reference instead of at the word, while
the PB4153 DEVLOG says every reader now reports at the word.

**Repro.** Not given in the report: any bare SELF/SUPER reader at `--std 85` (the PB4153 probes in
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261824b/probe-after.txt`); compare the COBOL1639 column with the word's. Report text:
"positions the new COBOL1639 at `qualifier`, which is null for a bare SELF/SUPER, so the diagnostic lands at the enclosing
statement or data reference instead of at the word".

**Fix direction.** Position it at the SELF/SUPER token (the receiver's own token when `qualifier` is null); add a golden
that pins the column, and sweep the other readers `OoBinder.RefuseUnreservedPredefinedObject` serves for the same position
habit.

**Landed (wave 20261849 group D).** Re-probe on the build (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261849d/probe.py 85`) confirmed the
note: `INVOKE SELF "M"` / `INVOKE SUPER "M"` drew COBOL1639 at column 12 (the INVOKE) and `MOVE P OF SELF TO X.` at column 17 (the
`P`), the word sitting at 19 and 22. Fixed at the root: `OoBinder.OoPredefinedSearchRoot` takes the SELF / SUPER **token** (the
bare `selfAndSuper` term's `Start`, the SUPER that ends `object-class-name-1 OF SUPER`, or the property object's `SELF()` /
`SUPER()` symbol) and positions every refusal it makes there: the PB4153 unreserved word, SR1 outside a method (COBOL6002, the same
habit, found on the way), SR4 and the root-class SUPER. `RefuseUnreservedPredefinedObject` now takes the token, so its other five
readers (the OO identifier door, SET's sender, both `arg.Self` readers, the object view, RAISE) name the word by construction.
Goldens: the two PB4153 negatives that were the bug (`pb4153-invoke-receiver-super-undeclared-at-85`,
`pb4153-property-of-self-undeclared-at-85`) now pin `(line,column)`, and `pb4444-invoke-self-outside-method-position` pins the
COBOL6002 sibling at 2002+.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.cs` (`OoBinder.OoPredefinedSearchRoot`, `OoBinder.RefuseUnreservedPredefinedObject`, `OoBinder.UnreservedPredefinedObjectRefusal`, `OoBinder.OoBindByReceiver`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.PropertyObject.cs` (`OoBinder.OoBindPropertyObject`).
