# PB5319 - a general-format row miscounts its formats and loses its tail

**Sites:**

- `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (`main_2023`, the FMT rows: `"formats": labels or 1` and `"text": ...[:4000]`)

**Observed (branch fix-cite-1985-ordinals at its PB5317 checkpoint, 2026-11-11).** `FMT_LABEL` is `^\s*Format\s+\d+` with
`re.M`, so a label the transcription writes bold (`**Format 1 (simple-relational-operator):**`, §2023:8.7.5.1) does not
match, and the row of 8.7.5.1 records `formats: 1` where the clause prints Format 1 and Format 2 (8.8.4.7.2 likewise: 2 of
322 FMT rows). Separately the row's `text` is cut at 4,000 characters, so the rows of 10.6.1, 12.3.7.2, 12.4.5.1, 13.17.2,
14.9.12.2, 14.9.28.2 and 14.9.39.2 lose their later formats (14.9.39.2, SET, prints 17).

**Harm.** `cite.resolve` (kb/Work PB5317) first counted a citation's `Format n` from these fields and refused
`§2023:8.7.5.1 Format 2`, a real format; it now reads the general format from the transcription, the catalog choosing only
which clause prints it. Any other reader of `formats` (described in the extractor as "a size signal for the verification
work") reads a short count, and a reader of an FMT row's `text` a truncated diagram.

**Fix direction.** Count the labels with the bold form allowed (one `Format n` label pattern, shared with
`cite.FORMAT_LABEL`), and drop the cut or record the row's full text; regenerate the catalog in the same change
(kb/Work PB5256 restructures `main_2023` into `extract`: fix it there or after it lands).
