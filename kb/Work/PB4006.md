# PB4006 — a cumulative fuzz history and its page, updated every night

**Owner, 2026-10-28:** "Publish a cummulative summary as an artifact. It should briefly describe how the fuzz testing and
analysis works, then produce a cummulatively summary with tables and/or graphs, as best illustrates the data. It should make
clear that the artifact is machine-generated and maintained automatically as an artifact of each fuzz testing run. So we
should add that updating to the process." Then: "Write the artifact from the PoV of someone totally unfamilar with my COBOL
project."

**The gap.** Seven nights ran (2026-10-20 to 10-27). Each night's numbers were in its run directory (git-ignored) and its
evidence record's prose; its verdict files were session scratch. No reader could see the trend (WiseOwl defects per night
falling from 7 to 1 or 2) or the cumulative GnuCOBOL record.

**The fix (design `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-differential-fuzz.md` §6).**
- `conformance-suite/fuzz-history.json` — one tracked record per night, and the page's `artifact` URL.
  Backfilled for nights 1 to 7 (verdict tallies for nights 3 to 7 from the surviving verdict files).
- `scripts/fuzz_history.py` — `render` draws the page from the history and the register (live statuses); `show` lists it.
- `scripts/fuzz_triage.py history --triage <dir> --judge-commit <sha> --write` — the morning's last step: records the night,
  writes `<coord>/scratch/fuzz-summary.html`; the session publishes it to the `artifact` URL.
- `scripts/test_fuzz_history.py` — the self-test.
- The page: https://claude.ai/artifact/UBCptoMp1GohHrvpUFRS7g, written for a reader new to the project, stating that it is
  machine-generated.
