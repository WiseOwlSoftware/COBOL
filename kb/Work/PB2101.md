## Residual question answered (2026-10-27, Mythos lane determination (b), design 11.2 and 19)

A registrar fault other than a RegisterModule refusal is NOT-FOUND-with-reason, and that is intended: §14.9.4.4 GR3 b) sets EC-PROGRAM-NOT-FOUND when "the program cannot be located", and a program is located when it is registered in the run unit (DOC-A.1-14 / -89), which a faulting registrar never achieves (RegisterModule commits its staged nodes only after Register returns). A load or initialization fault of the program's class or a dependency after registration, at instance creation, is also EC-PROGRAM-NOT-FOUND (corrected 2026-10-27 after the branch review, finding R1-5): GR3 c)'s checked resources are the implementor's, CLAUDE.md rule 1 takes GnuCOBOL's answer, and DOC-A.1-14 / -89 record it (a module that cannot be loaded is not located), so one missing assembly never gives NOT-FOUND or RESOURCES depending on when the CLR loads it. EC-PROGRAM-RESOURCES stays the stack check only. Nothing escapes as a fatal of another kind. Measured while deciding: the landed probe already classifies a throwing registrar as NOT-FOUND (its TargetInvocationException arm); a registrar fault reached through EnsureRegistered() from a host or the emitted Main, and a failure of `n.Factory(…)` in ProgramTable.CallProgram, still escape as the CLR's own exceptions. Both are this slice's work.
