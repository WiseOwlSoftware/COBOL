# PB6055 - a second selection loop survives the MAX/MIN unification

The reader of slice 1985-c05 found that `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.ExactValue.cs#SelectExact` holds
its own leftmost-tie selection loop next to the one kernel PB2635 (5f7b73d0df) made, `CobolIntrinsics.Selection.cs#ExtremeIndex`
over a per-carrier `ISelectionOrder`; the native-arithmetic forms of MAX, MIN, ORD-MAX and ORD-MIN take the copy. The
five 1985 rows read CONFORM on both paths today, so this is duplication (one rule written twice), not a wrong answer:
the copy can drift from the kernel. Fix: route SelectExact through ExtremeIndex with an exact-value order, and delete the loop.
The PB1754 campaign (account 1) owns the area.
