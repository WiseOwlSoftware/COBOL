# PB5600 - landing

Re-probed on a fresh Debug build: `>>FLAG-85 ALL ON JUNK` compiled clean (A, B, rc 0) and `>>FLAG-85 NUMVAL ON` +
`FUNCTION NUMVAL(Y)` printed 3 with no warning; both reproduced.

Root cause: the two construct rows were `directiveOperand.form = text` ("content unchecked by decision") because the 2023 text
has no format, so `CompilerDirectiveCatalog.CheckOperand` checked nothing and no stage held their state. The 2002 and 2014
texts print both (§2002:7.2.11.1, §2002:7.2.12.1). Fix: both are now `stage` rows owned by `FlagDirectiveProcessor`, two more
values of `FlagDirective` served by the one `FlagDirectiveLine` parse, `FlagState` fold and `FlagConformancePass` walk, with
the placement rule (SR1) as data on the row and the omitted-branch syntax check of `StageOperandChecks` for free. Ten FLAG-85
options (GR4 b-k) and the one FLAG-NATIVE-ARITHMETIC option have detectors in `FlagConformancePass.Flag85.cs`; codes
COBOL7390 and COBOL7391 (COBOL7392 returned). Goldens: `cases/7.2.11@2002-FLAG-85/` and
`cases/7.2.12@2002-FLAG-NATIVE-ARITHMETIC/` (an accepted program and a refused one each; `wiseowl/diagnostics/pb5600-*.json`).

Determinations (each a reading of the 2002 text, recorded in `docs/conformance/3/D-IGNORED-DIRECTIVES.md` and
`DESIGN-flag-directives.md` section 6): "constant" in GR4 b is a numeric literal; GR4 d reads the dividend and divisor as the
statement's other two operands, whichever of INTO / BY names them; GR4 e's "function that allows multiple arithmetic
expressions" is an `IntrinsicCatalog` row with two or more numeric-kind arguments; GR4 h's "variable length group" is a group
holding an OCCURS DEPENDING table; GR4 k reads "could be a zero-length item" off `DataItem.MinimumLengthIsZero` and
`FileModel.VaryMin`, once per statement; FLAG-NATIVE-ARITHMETIC counts COMP-1 / COMP-2 as float-short / float-long.
