# PB4721 — a word the client wrote into a repository text draws a second error

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/BinderDriver.cs:255` (`BinderDriver.Bind`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Common/ExpansionToken.cs` (`ClientWritten`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Common/ClientWrittenTokens.cs` (`Contains`, `OfTexts`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/WordCharacterScreen.cs` (`RunOnTree`, `WordScanner.Add`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/LiteralScreen.cs` (`Run`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Oo/OoExpansion.cs` (`SubstitutedTokens`, `ClientWrittenTexts`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Oo/ExpansionOrigin.cs` (`ExpansionOrigin`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/ExpansionPlacements.cs` (`ExpansionToWrite`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Repository/ExpansionPlacementPass.cs` (`ExpansionPlacementPass`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/ExpansionAssemblies.cs` (`Write`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CompilerDriver.cs` (`Options.ClientWritten`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/EditionContext.cs` (`ClientWritten`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`ParseArm.VisitCobolWord`)

Reported by the branch review, not probed. R1-4 (info), copied: walking repository texts also screens the words the client itself wrote into the text (an actual, the expansion name), so one illegal word the client wrote draws a second error at the EXPANDS entry where placement (b) printed one before.

**Landed (w20261875e).** Re-probe: a client with `CLASS HOLD-BOX_ EXPANDS HOLD USING BOX` over a repository skeleton printed COBOL2773 twice (`CL1.cob(8,11)` and `CL1.cob(8,5) ... [in the expansion 'HOLD-BOX_']`). Fix at the root: `OoExpansion.SubstitutedTokens` marks every token the client wrote (actuals, renamed headers, the entry's and an actual's externalized-name phrase) `ExpansionToken.ClientWritten`; `WordCharacterScreen` and `LiteralScreen` skip them, the written tree having screened them where written. Witness: `ExternalRepositoryExpansionTests.AnIllegalWordTheClientWroteIntoAnExpansion_IsReportedOnce_WhereItIsWritten`.

Review round 1 (the same landing): the first fix left two arms. (R1-1) `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitCobolWord` walked the same texts and asked the word-length rule of a client word (COBOL1567 twice for a 70-character expansion name in the group placement); it now asks `ClientWrittenTokens.Contains` first. (R1-2) an expansion compiled in an assembly of its own is lexed again from `ExpansionOrigin.Text`, whose tokens carry no flag, so its compilation reported the client's word again at the entry; the origin now records the texts of the client's tokens, `ExpansionToWrite` carries them, and that compilation's `Options.ClientWritten` hands them to the screens. Witnesses: `AnOverlongNameTheClientWroteIntoAnExpansion_IsReportedOnce_InTheGroupPlacement`, `ARemovedCharacterTheClientWroteIntoAnExpansion_IsReportedOnce_InTheOwnAssemblyPlacement`, `ATokenTheClientWroteIntoAnExpansion_IsScreenedOnce_InTheGroupPlacement` (the literal arm and the renamed header), `AnIllegalActualTheClientWrote_IsReportedWhereItIsWritten_NotInTheExpansion`.
