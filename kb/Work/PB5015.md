# PB5015 - SetFormatSelection.BraceOf says a row outside its edition is unreachable in practice, but a dynamic-length item is declarable at 2014 while Format 16 begins at 2023, so the labelled arm is reached and no test pins its wording

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R1-1 LEAD (low): src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SetFormatSelection.cs:177 BraceOf doc comment says a row reached outside its edition is unreachable in practice because its declaration was refused earlier, but a dynamic-length item is declarable at 2014 (dynamic-length-item-2014) while Format 16 begins at 2023, so the labelled arm is reached and no test pins its wording.

**Verified.** `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` row `dynamic-length-item-2014` has `introducedIn` 2014; `SetFormatSelection.Formats` row F16 (`SetOperandKind.DynamicLength`) has `IntroducedIn` 2023. Under `--std=2014` a dynamic-length item declares, so a `SET dyn-item TO n` selects F16 and `BraceOf` takes its `!InEdition(row)` arm and prints "(a format first printed in COBOL-2023, absent from this edition)".

**R1.** No open note covers it (grep of kb/Work for BraceOf and the key phrase).

**Needed.** Correct the `BraceOf` doc comment to say when the arm is reached (the F16 / dynamic-length-at-2014 case), and add a test pinning the wording of that diagnostic at 2014.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SetFormatSelection.cs` (`SetFormatSelection.BraceOf`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/SetFormatSelectionDriftTests.cs` (`ASelectedRowTheEditionLacks_IsLabelledWithItsIntroducingEdition`)

**Re-probe (wave 20261936 group C, 2026-11-08).** Reproduced, and wider than the note: the arm is reached through
`ReportNotAdmitted` and `ReportNotSoleReceiver`, which name the SELECTED row (`ReportNoFormat` filters its list by
edition). `SET D N TO 5` and `SET D E TO 5` over dynamic-length items at `--std 2014` draw COBOL2112 "Format 16's ...
(a format first printed in COBOL-2023, absent from this edition)"; `SET TC N TO 5` over a CAPACITY register at
`--std 2002` (the OCCURS DYNAMIC declaration refused COBOL0900, the item still bound) draws the same label for
Format 14 ("first printed in COBOL-2014"). The comment's "its declaration was refused earlier, so this is unreachable"
was false twice: a refused declaration does not stop binding, and the dynamic-length declaration is legal at 2014.

**Fix.** `BraceOf`'s doc comment says which arms reach it and why (selection reads kinds, not the edition), with both
witnesses; the theory `ASelectedRowTheEditionLacks_IsLabelledWithItsIntroducingEdition` pins the label below each
row's introducing edition and its absence at it, on both report arms. The wording itself was right and is unchanged.
