# PB5059 - the 1985 citation universe includes the amendment pages

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/citation.py` (`_loaded`, `regions_1985`, `amendment_regions`), `scripts/spec/cite.py` (`find`, `main`).

**Found (PB5043's implementer and reviewer, wave 20261956).** `citation._loaded` runs `regions_1985` over the whole of
`specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md`, so the 1985 universe also holds Amendment 1's and Amendment 2's folios: `python scripts/spec/citation.py
"§1985:p.A-20"` and `"§1985:p.B-16"` validate against amendment pages, a wrong-edition citation the check accepts. And
`cite.py --find` takes only `spec_pdf.EDITIONS`, so it cannot search the 1989/1993 amendments; `--find --edition 1985` steers
amendment text to a wrong-edition §1985 page locator that `--check` then accepts, while a failed 1989/1993 `--check` still says
"run --find". Cutting the 1985 universe at the first `# AMENDMENT` heading also touches `element_standing.py` and
`extract_rule_catalog.py`, which share `regions_1985`. Note PB5045 since applied both amendments in place in the base text:
decide what a §1985 citation of amended text means (the amended base rule) and keep ONE resolver.
