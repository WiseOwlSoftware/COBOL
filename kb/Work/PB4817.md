# PB4817 - measure the scanned diagrams

**The defect.** `scripts/spec/figure_geometry.py` measures a general format's strokes from the PDF's VECTOR drawings. The 1985
PDF is a scan: its underlines, brackets, braces and choice bars exist only as pixels, so the measurer finds nothing and
the 2023 pipeline (measure, read the words with coordinates, `render_figure.py` draws, `sweep_figures.py --check`
regenerates and diffs) cannot run on it. PB4812 "The general formats" states why the drawing must come from measurement:
the 2023 formats were wrong until they were regenerated from the measured page.

Example (1985 PDF page 196, Environment Division general format; the Mythos session's pilot, 2026-11-06): Opus 5.5 read every
word and every underline correctly from the page image but drifted the columns of the nested braces after EVERY; Sonnet 5.5
broke the one-line brackets. The words come from the model; the geometry must come from the pixels.

**The fix.** A raster measurer, `scripts/spec/figure_raster.py`, producing the SAME geometry structure `figure_geometry.py`
returns (so `render_figure.py` and the audits consume it unchanged), selected per edition by `spec_pdf`'s edition table
(PB4815: an edition records whether its general formats are vector or raster), never by a second pipeline:
- render the general-format region at high resolution (grayscale, at least 400 dpi);
- detect horizontal runs under words: the UNDERLINE (a required word); separate them from bracket and brace serifs;
- detect vertical runs and their end serifs: brackets (square serifs), braces (the curled ends and the middle point),
  choice-indicator bars;
- map each stroke to the words by coordinates. The words and their boxes come from a source the tool can trust: the
  converted page's words (PB4816) aligned to boxes found in the image (connected components), not the PDF's OCR text
  layer, which has errors (the permission sentence reads "OI" for "or"). Where the two disagree the tool reports it,
  never picks silently.
- a self-test on PLANTED images (drawn with known underlines, nested brackets and braces, bars, with noise and a slight
  skew like a scan) asserting the measured structure exactly; `test_figure_geometry.py` is the model.

**Calibration** against a dozen 1985 pages checked by eye is part of running the tool, which waits for the owner (PB4812's
standing requirements); this note delivers the tool, its self-test and a `--calibrate <pages>` mode that writes the
side-by-side images for that check.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/figure_raster.py` (new: `measure_page`, `measure_image`, `classify_page`, `extract`, `calibrate`, `find_stems`, `_foot_side`, `find_braces`, `brace_pieces`, `find_rules`, `find_words`, `align`); `scripts/spec/test_figure_raster.py` (new); `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml` and `.github/workflows/nightly.yml` (the pinned `pip install` lines of the self-test jobs gain `numpy==2.5.4`, which the measurer reads pixels with). It edits no existing script and nothing calls it yet: PB4815 (open) is to dispatch on the edition and call it.

**Landed (2026-11-06).** The measurer, its planted-image self-test and `--calibrate` exist. Not done, by the owner's rule: no run against the real PDF, so the thresholds (`BRACE_*`, `WORD_GAP_PT`) are calibrated on planted shapes only and the calibration against a dozen real pages waits for the owner. Known limit: an underline fused to a descender is not a pure bar and is not found (uppercase required words rarely have one).

**Do not run it** against the real PDF (PB4812's standing requirements).
