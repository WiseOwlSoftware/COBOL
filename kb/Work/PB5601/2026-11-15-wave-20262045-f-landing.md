**2026-11-15, wave 20262045 group F: a boolean expression as the DEFINE operand is a 2023 format.**

Re-probe on a Debug build at the base: `>>DEFINE V AS B"1100" B-OR B"0011"` then `>>IF V = B"1111"` printed DEFINE-BOOL-EXPR, rc 0, at 2002, 2014 and 2023. Reproduced. §2014:7.3.9.1 has the same three alternatives as §2002:7.2.9.1 (the note's "2014: unknown" is answered: 2014 refuses too).

Fix. Construct row `define-boolean-expression-2023` (introducedIn 2023, COBOL0900). `ConditionalCompilationProcessor.EvaluateOperandText(..., defineValue: true)`, passed by the DEFINE value arm only, asks `DirectiveDiag.GateDefineBooleanExpression`, which fires when the parsed operand took the grammar's `booleanExpression` alternative (an operator or a parenthesized boolean literal; a boolean literal is literal-1) and the edition lacks the row. It reports through `ConstructRegistry.Check`, still evaluates the operand so every violation is reported, and the directive is a refused DEFINE (`DefineEffect.RefusalOf`). EVALUATE and WHEN operands and constant conditional expressions keep their 2002 admission (§2002:7.2.6). Goldens: `pb5601_define_boolean_expression` (2023) with its refusal at 85, 2002 and 2014. The two cases that used the 2023 form under `accept: [2002]` (`pb1367_define_general_format_alternatives`, `directive_expressions`) now write the boolean LITERAL B"1111". Sibling sweep: the other operand positions that read the shared `compileTimeOperand` rule are EVALUATE, WHEN, the IF/EVALUATE relation operands and the DISPLAY directive (2023 whole), each admitted by its own edition's text.
