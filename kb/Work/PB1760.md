# PB1760 — a per-file encoding attribute; LINE SEQUENTIAL defaults to UTF-8 (owner R51)

Design: `docs/COBOLNET_FILES_DESIGN.md` D29. Depends on PB1759's medium image for the record path; independent for the
text path.

## Work

- A file ENCODING attribute (default UTF-8 for LINE SEQUENTIAL; ISO 8859-1 one byte per position for the fixed
  record organizations unless CODE-SET says otherwise), carried by the connector; `FileCharacterSet` becomes the
  Latin-1 case of it and `LineSequentialCharacterSet` (Annex A.1 item 115) states which characters a text line may
  hold in UTF-8 (control characters and the noncharacter U+FFFF stay refused with '71').
- DISPLAY / ACCEPT and the standard streams: UTF-8 (verify what `StandardStreams` does today — measure first).
- Line length and the record-size rules: a LINE SEQUENTIAL record of n CHARACTERS has a variable BYTE length; the
  §14.9.30.4 GR14/15 truncation and status-06 rules are in CHARACTER positions — decide and document, do not inherit.
- BOM policy on read (accept and strip) and write (none), and invalid UTF-8 on read (status 09 vs replacement — the
  spec decides; derive before coding).

## Revisit before starting

R47 said "never re-ask" for the Latin-1 default; R51 supersedes it for line sequential files only, in the owner's words
of 2026-09-29. Update R47's note (superseded-by R51) in the landing commit, plus docs/CONFORMANCE.md DOC-A.1-31 and
DOC-A.1-115, and the golden(s) that pin the refusal of a character above U+00FF on a line sequential file.
