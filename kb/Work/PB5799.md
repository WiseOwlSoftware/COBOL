# PB5799 - OPEN: does REDEFINES SR5 (include a variable occurrence data item) bar a redefinition inside an OCCURS DEPENDING ON table at --std 85? The text admits both readings

**Ruling: OPEN.** A REDEFINES pair inside an OCCURS DEPENDING ON table compiles at `--std 85`. Whether `§1985:VI.5.10.3 SR5` ("Neither the original definition nor the redefinition can include a variable occurrence data item") bars it turns on whether "include" covers the item itself, which the glossary makes a variable occurrence data item. The row `1985-SR-VI.5.10.3-5` stays `undecided`. Harm: under_rejects under reading 1 (not set while the row is open). The checker's confidence: medium.

**Mechanism 4, left open, and how it closes (the ruling's paragraph, copied).** The glossary makes every item subordinate to an OCCURS DEPENDING ON entry a variable occurrence data item, so an item of the table that is redefined IS one; SR5 forbids a definition that "includes" one. Both readings are honest: "include" as "contain within it" (then the pair is legal, and the rule keeps redefined areas a fixed size, which they are), or "include" as covering the item itself (then no redefinition may stand inside such a table). The text does not decide it. CLAUDE.md rule 1 then gives the order: what GnuCOBOL does, measured; failing that IBM Enterprise COBOL or Micro Focus, from their manuals; and the choice recorded in `docs/conformance/` for 1985 (a 1985 conformance entry). The same glossary sentence is the hinge of the sample's cause (c) row `1985-SR-VI.5.8.3-1` (kb/Work PB5692's result): one determination must cover both. That row is held by the open note kb/Work PB5737, named in `related`.

**The two readings (the checker's).**
- Reading 1 (the refuter's): by the 1985 glossary (`§1985:III.2`) A and B, being subordinate to an ODO entry, ARE variable occurrence data items, so "neither the original definition nor the redefinition can include a variable occurrence data item" bars the pair: under_rejects at 85.
- Reading 2 (the reader's): a definition "includes" an item that lies within the area it describes; A's and B's areas are fixed (2 and 1 characters per occurrence) and contain no variable-occurrence item, while sentence 2 of the same rule expressly lets data-name-2 be subordinate to an OCCURS item without excluding the DEPENDING form.
- Both fit the 1985 words. 2002 keeps "variable-occurrence data item" but defines it without the "or be subordinate to such an item" sentence; 2023 says "occurs-depending table" (`§2023:13.18.44.3 SR5`), under which `r1.cob` is legal and the 2023 row is right. These are pointers only. If reading 1 is chosen the 85 arm under-rejects (cause a: an 85 arm is needed, since 2023's text admits `r1.cob`).
- What settles it: a ruling on whether "include" in SR5 sentence 4 covers the entry itself (and its subordinates) or only items within its area.
