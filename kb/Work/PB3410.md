# PB3410 — a documented ledger bound with no witness

A lead from round 2 of the PB3336 branch review (`mythos-a6-review-r2`, report
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1118a6-PB3336-report.md`, `## Review findings`, "LEAD (low, doc bound without
witness)"). The code landed with PB3336 (PR #268, 0eba56a75); the
sites below are read on origin/main.

**Mechanism.** `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-runtime-library.md:1804` says the ledger's length ℓ grows with the ledger's
fill "at most 9 pages at R2's checkpoint (1 024 entries plus the pages of the one statement that begins at it;
measured, ...". No case asserts it. `Load` in `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/Store/KeyedStorePerformanceWitnessTests.cs`
computes `mostLedgerPages` (lines 288 and 297) but only prints it (line 309). The asserted bounds are the per-statement
row with ℓ read from the file, and the entry count. A ledger layout change that let ℓ reach 20 pages at the checkpoint
would stay green while the row's number went false.

**Fix.** Assert `mostLedgerPages` against the stated bound, or restate the bound in the doc as the measured value and
assert that. Rule: requirement 8, a doc bound needs a witness (2.2.1.11 of the design).
