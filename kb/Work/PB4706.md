# PB4706 — a missing period before a header word names the word as the entry (COBOL1941)

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs:3511` (`CobolErrorStrategy.BeginsWhatFollows`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs:3522`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolData.g4` (`dataName`)

Copied from the PB4646 report (wave 20261868 e). Leads 1 and 2 carry a run repro by the implementer; R1-1 and R1-2 are the branch review's, not probed.

1. `01` then `PROCEDURE DIVISION.` (a dangling level-number before the division header): PROCEDURE is a legal data-name (NC205A, `CobolData.g4` dataName alternative), so it is taken as the entry's name and DIVISION draws COBOL1941 "not a clause ... for 'PROCEDURE'" (probe `p2.cob`; same for `MAIN SECTION`, a name plus SECTION). A missing period is the likelier reading; the fix is a grammar/predicate choice on the dataName PROCEDURE alternative, outside the PB4646 strategy. Same family as PB4646.
2. `01 LOCAL-STORAGE SECTION.` and `01 SCREEN SECTION.` in WORKING-STORAGE read the first word as the entry's name (not reserved there) and draw COBOL1941 at SECTION "for 'LOCAL-STORAGE'"; same missing-period reading, same family as lead 1.
3. R1-1: `BeginsWhatFollows` (line 3511) asks about two tokens while hint 14 asks a stricter question (`APeriodLetsTheSentenceFollow`), so a word that passes the first and fails the second gets neither the reserved-word report nor the missing-period report.
4. R1-2: the PB4645 fallback (line 3522) names a header word as the entry in the same missing-period family the report registers, and that family is wider than the report lists and one of its reasons is inaccurate.

## Landed (wave 20261875 group B)

Re-probed on a fresh build: every lead reproduced (`01` then `PROCEDURE DIVISION.`: COBOL1941 at DIVISION "for 'PROCEDURE'" and a second COBOL0001 at the next paragraph; `01 LOCAL-STORAGE SECTION.` and `01 SCREEN SECTION.`: COBOL1941 at SECTION; `01 MAIN SECTION.`: COBOL1941 at SECTION; `01 LINKAGE SECTION FOO.`: neither report, COBOL0001). The sweep widened the family and found one under-rejection: `01 PROCEDURE PIC X.` COMPILED at every edition.

**The PROCEDURE alternative was the root, and a defect of its own.** `dataName` held a hand-written `PROCEDURE` alternative, kept for NC205A (PB300 "legally names a data item with it"). NC205A does not: its `77 PROCEDURE` continues on the next line with `DIVISION`, the one word PROCEDUREDIVISION (a word continuation; the compiler joined it before this change and still does, and NC205A compiles with the alternative gone). So §8.3.2.1 1) ("Reserved words shall not be used as user-defined words or system-names") and §8.9 refuse the name, and the alternative is deleted: `01 PROCEDURE PIC X.` is COBOL0901, and `01` then `PROCEDURE DIVISION.` stops at the word as every other header does, one COBOL0307 and no cascade, at every slot that takes a `dataName` (the data description, report group and constant entries). `FOLLOW(dataName)` and `FIRST(dataName)` are pinned in `ReadingParsesFollowDriftTests`.

**A gated word before SECTION or DIVISION names no entry.** `dataName`'s `reservedGatedWord` alternative carries `CobolParserCoreBase.noHeaderWrittenHere`: no format follows a data-name with either word (the same drift test), so no conforming source loses a reading, and `01 LOCAL-STORAGE SECTION.` at 2002 and later is COBOL0307 at LOCAL-STORAGE. At 85 LOCAL-STORAGE is a user word and no such section exists, so it is the name and the report is the plain COBOL0001 at SECTION.

**A header is never a clause or a name (R1-2's wider family).** `OffendingPosition.WritesAHeader` (the token is SECTION or DIVISION, or the word before one) keeps `ClosedFormatResidue` from COBOL1941 (`01 MAIN SECTION.`, `01 A` then `MAIN SECTION.`, `FD F` then `MAIN SECTION.`) and `EntrySlotYields` keeps the first word from COBOL0901 (`01 FILE SECTION.`, `01 WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.`, `01 DATA DIVISION.`, each now the plain report that header draws after a complete entry). `ReservedWordUse` also stands down for a header keyword a period lets follow (`01 A PIC X` then `LOCAL-STORAGE SECTION.`, which drew COBOL0901). The PB4645 fallback (`OwnerName`) no longer meets a header word: PROCEDURE left `EntryNameOnlyWords` with the alternative.

**One question (R1-1).** `BeginsWhatFollows` is gone. The slot and hint 14 both ask `OffendingPosition.PeriodIsMissingBeforeTheEntrySlot`: a period, the word and the one after it read on from the slot. `01 LINKAGE SECTION FOO.` and `01 END PROGRAM P FOO.` are COBOL0307 and then the fault after the header. `SentenceTypesFrom` ends a reading at a header's closing word, so the same holds with a clause or a name before the header (`01 A PIC X` then `LINKAGE SECTION FOO.`).

Goldens: `conformance-suite/cases/13.16-Data-description/pb4706-*` (seven).
