## The rule, and the open question inside it

```
$ python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 13.18.63.3 "may be numeric"
OK  §13.18.63.3 6)  (Syntax rules)
    6) If the item is of category numeric-edited, then, subject to Syntax rules 2 and 3, literals in
       formats 1, 2, and 4 of the VALUE clause may be numeric when they shall be converted to their
       numeric-edited forms according to the rules of the PICTURE clause …
```

SR6 names **formats 1, 2 and 4** — the level-88 condition-name VALUE is Format 3, and it is NOT in the
list. Two readings, and they give opposite answers:

* **SR6's list is exhaustive.** Then a numeric literal on a Format-3 VALUE over a numeric-edited subject is
  not admitted AT ANY EDITION, and `m_numed_fmt3` should be refused at 2023 too. The whole 2023 numeric-
  edited golden set that rides Format 3 would move.
* **SR6's list is editorial** (formats 1, 2 and 4 are the *data-item* VALUE formats, and Format 3 inherits
  through §13.18.63.4 GR19 "the characteristics of a condition-name are implicitly those of its conditional
  variable"). Then Format 3 needs the SAME Annex E.3.3 item 43 gate as Format 1, and today it has none.

**Either reading makes the current behaviour wrong**, which is why this is a defect note and not an
adjudication: the fixer derives the answer from the rendered §13.18.63.2 figure and the E.3.3 entry, and
implements it. The PB560 report flags the choice as a DETERMINATION and is the reason every numeric-edited
golden in that change set rides 2023 — that dependency is real and is recorded here so it is not rediscovered.

## Landed (2026-09-21, wave 42 group E)

**It was a THREE-arm dispatch, not two, and the root was one rule written down in two places.** §13.18.63.3 SR6
has two halves: the literal-FORM rule (fixed-point vs floating-point) and the EDITION on which a numeric literal
on a numeric-edited subject became writable at all. The form half sat in `DataBinder.ValidateValueCategory`,
which the item VALUE, the level-88 set AND the group VALUE all funnel through; the edition half sat INLINE in
`DataBinder.ScreenValueLiteral`, which only the item VALUE reaches. One rule, two places, and the two places
covered different formats — so the format enumeration the rule itself carries was enforced on some of its
formats and not others.

### The measurement (this worktree's own build, before)

|---|---|---|

Annex E.3.3 item 43 dates the permission: "VALUE clause, numeric-edited items and numeric literals. It is now
permitted to allow numeric-edited data items to be assigned values specified as numeric literals" — a COBOL-2023
addition, so below 2023 the edited image had to be written as the alphanumeric or national literal SR7 governs.
The §13.18.63.2 figures were RENDERED from the printed page (`scripts/render-spec-page.py 546 547`, folios 516
and 517) rather than read from the OCR, because the format enumeration is the whole verdict.

### DETERMINATION — format 3 rides the same gate

SR6's text names formats 1, 2 and 4 and omits format 3 (and format 5). Read strictly that forbids a numeric
literal on a condition-name over a numeric-edited conditional variable at EVERY edition, and would invalidate
the landed `2023/pb560_condition_value_numeric_edited_2023`. The standard does not sustain the strict reading on
its own terms:

* §13.18.63.4 GR19 — "The characteristics of a condition-name are implicitly those of its conditional variable."
* §14.9.39.4 GR6 — `SET condition-name TO TRUE` places the literal "according to the rules for the VALUE
  clause", i.e. through SR6's own MOVE-rules conversion. Under the strict reading that placement has NO rule.
* §13.18.63.3 SR36 carries SR11 — "Editing characters in a picture character-string for a numeric-edited data
  item are used in editing of the initial value … when the literal is numeric" — into FORMAT 5, whose subject is
  a level-88 condition-name exactly as format 3's is. The drafters do contemplate numeric-edited editing in a
  condition-name-subject format, which makes SR6's omission read as naming the DATA-ITEM formats rather than as
  excluding the condition-name one.

The two readings AGREE below 2023 — both refuse — and that agreement is the half this landing closes. At 2023
the reading that does not reject source is taken. Recorded here so an owner decision can revisit it rather than
the question being rediscovered; the counter-argument is written out above in full.

### What changed

* `DataBinder.ScreenNumericEditedNumericLiteral` — the ONE SR6 edition screen, taking the subject's picture and
  the folded literal. A literal that is not numeric answers no, so no caller pre-classifies.
* `DataBinder.ValidateValueCategory` asks it before its own category switch — which gives formats 1 and 2 (via
  `ScreenValueLiteral`), format 3 (the three level-88 call sites) and the group-level VALUE the same answer by
  construction. The inline copy in `ScreenValueLiteral` is gone.
* `DataBinder.Reports.cs` asks it per VALUE operand once the printable item's picture is settled — format 4, and
  a multi-operand clause (SR35) gates each of its literals.

### Complement, measured

SR6's zero exemption — "the figurative constant ZERO or ZEROES and the integer and decimal forms of the literal
zero" — and SR7's alphanumeric/national edited image are NOT gated: `VALUE 0`, `VALUE ZERO` and `VALUE " 10.00"`
all compile at `--std 85` in both the item and the condition-name spellings, and a non-numeric-edited subject
(`PIC 9(3) VALUE 10.`) is untouched. All seven are asserted.
