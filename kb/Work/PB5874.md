# PB5874 - two finished branches that cannot both merge are found one at a time, by the landing of the second, after each has paid a full gate

**Sites:** none (a process finding whose shape is proposed below for the loop's owner to decide; no change is planned until that decision).

Noticed on Monday 2026-11-16 at 11:25 PST by the Mythos lane, an hour and a half after the owner asked "why the
fuck are we waiting for a known red to appear, then handle the error?".

kind: defect. process_only: true. No harm flag. Severity: MAJOR is the filer's to weigh (each case costs a stop at
landing, a finisher, a second full gate of about 40 minutes and a second review).
area: tooling/landing. related: PB5872, PB5863, PB5099, PB5110, PB2602, PB3103.

## What was measured
`git merge-tree --write-tree` over the ten branches that were finished or in their gate and not on main
(`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/my-20261115/predict_merge.py`, which writes to no worktree), at 11:25:

  - `worktree-agent-a89782e0eb62cf8d7` (kb/Work PB5844, gated GREEN) and `records-1985-code-reads-2` (in its gate):
    `kb/Work/PB5844.md` and five 2023 inventory rows of the RECORD DELIMITER clause, which one branch re-records
    PARTIAL and the other CONFORMS;
  - `worktree-agent-ae0ab66656ae8c895` (PB5852, not gated) and `worktree-agent-a7838843ae4411b36` (PB5845, gated
    GREEN): `DialectBehavior.cs`, `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md`, `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt`,
    `compiler-facts.json`;
  - PB5852's branch and the records branch: `tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/1985-GR-VI.6.19.4-4.json`;
  - PB5844's branch and `worktree-wf_df842f2c-278-1` (the loop's): `compiler-facts.json`;
  - `worktree-wf_f60b9cb3-27d-27` (gated GREEN) and `worktree-wf_1d5ba7f2-7a4-9` (the loop's, in its gate):
    `EditionInfo.cs`.
- TWO identifiers were taken twice with no shared allocator: `DialectBehavior` value 28 and
  `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` row 7.84, by PB5845 and by PB5852, an hour apart.
Three of the ten branches had already paid a full gate that their landing must repeat: `enqueue.py land` merges
`origin/main` in, meets the conflict, stops, a finisher resolves it, and the new tip needs a gate and a review of
its own. CLAUDE.md already names the waste ("a gate on a branch that must merge main again before it lands"); no
tool says WHICH branch that is before the gate is spent.

## Why nothing saw it
The dispatch ledger (`inflight-groups.json`, `check_practices.py`) refuses two groups that edit the same MEMBER
and deliberately lets two groups share a file (kb/Work PB4452). That holds for code. It does not hold for the
files many fixes rewrite whole or append to at one place: generated registers (`compiler-facts.json`,
`PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt`), numbered tables (`DialectBehavior`, the version change reference), an inventory record
two branches re-record, a note's frontmatter. And two numbered resources have no allocator (`alloc.py` gives ids,
codes, waves and DEVLOG numbers only).

## The shape proposed (the loop's owner decides; the Mythos lane builds none of it without that)
1. THE SURVEY PREDICTS. `prune_worktrees.py` already holds every unlanded branch with its state; it runs the
   pairwise `merge-tree` over the branches that are DONE, in a gate, WAITING FOR REVIEW or WAITING TO LAND, and
   prints each conflicting pair with its files. No worktree is touched and nothing is fetched.
2. THE GATE REFUSES THE WASTE. A gate of a branch that conflicts with a branch AHEAD of it (reviewed, or gated
   GREEN earlier) does not start: it prints the pair and says to land the other first. The landing lane lands a
   conflicting pair in the order that leaves the re-merge to the branch that has not gated.
3. THE NUMBERED TABLES GET THE ALLOCATOR: `alloc.py dialect 1` and `alloc.py vcr 1`, as for ids and codes, with
   the same drift test that the tree's highest number is below the allocator's next.
4. GENERATED REGISTERS STOP CONFLICTING: a file a tool regenerates whole is resolved by regenerating it; the
   landing's merge takes main's copy of each such file and runs its generator, from a declared list (the
   rendered-views tool already knows which files are rendered).
