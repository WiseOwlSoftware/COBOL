# PB4953 - residue of the 1985 reconciliation

**Sites:** `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (the page-114 Figure 1 block; the element-summary table rows of pages 31-83; the Amendment 1 running heads and change tables of pages 837-855; the underlined body headings of pages 96-724), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/LEDGER-1985.json` (`sweeps`), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/figures-1985/p114-0.json` (`drawn_lines`), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/TRANSCRIPTION-STATE.md` (the 1985 section), `scripts/spec/rule_catalog_1985.py` (`CHANGE_ADDS`), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json` (regenerated).

**Report (PB4850 finisher, items 3-5; one mechanism: the reconciliation checked a page only where a claim pointed).**
- Figure 1 (PDF page 114) lacks two connectors: the first SEND connects to an output queue, and the third device connects back to an input queue.
- The element-summary tables (pages 31-84) are only partly reconciled: their underlined group labels and indentation were checked only where a claim pointed (all titles were swept). Body section headings (`1. <u>INTRODUCTION</u>`) are underlined only where a claim pointed.
- Amendment 1's change-list head is inconsistent: six different shapes, and page 837's reads `| Page No. | Change |`.

Expected: each is read against the rendered page and repaired, with its LEDGER-1985 entry in the same change (`verify_publishable.check_ledger`).

**Landed (wave 20261937, group G).**
- Figure 1 (page 114): the first SEND now reaches the communication description, a second line runs from it to the output queue, and the third device is joined back to the left input queue. The figure verdict `p114-0.json` carries the same lines; `sweep_figures.py --edition 1985 --check` stays clean.
- The element-summary tables (pages 31-83) were measured against the scanned page (a row's left edge, its underline run) and matched to the table row by row: 462 rows gained their printed indentation or group underline, among them bold group labels that the page underlines. TRANSCRIPTION-STATE.md names the convention.
- Body headings (pages 85-906): 2,374 heading-shaped lines were matched to the PDF's text layer and checked for an underline; 33 top-level headings the page underlines (`4. <u>TABLE HANDLING</u>`, `<u>SECTION XI: ...</u>`, the `GENERAL FORMAT FOR ...` titles, the condition-format labels) gained `<u>`. 49 headings the scan could not match (mostly the `# SECTION` dividers that sit after a folio) were not checked.
- Amendment 1's change list (pages 831-856): the running head is one of two shapes (the table header row, or the single line on a prose page), page 837's `| Page No. | Change |` reads as the printed head, and the three tables written without outer pipes (pages 850-852) have them.
- Root cause found on the way: those three tables were invisible to the 1985 rule catalog, which reads change rows by their leading pipe. Their rows X-27 and XIV-26 ("add new syntax rule 4 as follows") showed that `CHANGE_ADDS` knew only "as new ... rule n". It reads "add new ... rule n" now, and the committed catalog was regenerated.
- Ledger: 533 sweep entries (`summary-rows`, `heading-underlines`, `amendment-1-heads`, `figure-1-connectors`); the page-853 claim and sweep entries whose `now` the new head superseded were updated.
