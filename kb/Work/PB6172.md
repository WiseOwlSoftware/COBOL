**Wave kind:** move-and-rename. **Model:** Sonnet. A namespace move of one enum, with every caller changed (CLAUDE.md
rule 4).

**Found 2026-11-24** by the census at 9e65164eb, recorded for R2's new pin. The `WiseOwl.Cobol.CodeGen ->
WiseOwl.Cobol.Binding` tolerated row measured 70 uses against the 68 the edges file allowed.
`AcceptDisplayEmitter` (`ctx.NumericDisplay is NumericDisplayForm.Decimal`) reads the `--numeric-display` option's enum,
declared in `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/NumericDisplayForm.cs`. An enum constant compiles to an integer, so the
IL walk of `ArchitectureEdgesDriftTests` never saw the pair. Only the census, which reads the source, did.

The growth was admitted by hand in the change that recorded the census (`docs/rearchitecture/architecture-edges.json`,
the row's plan). This note removes it.

**Target:** `NumericDisplayForm` moves to compiler.model, as PB2402 moved the other OPTIONS types. Its readers change
in the same change: `EditionContext.NumericDisplay`, the CLI's `CliOptions` and `NumericDisplayFormOption`, and
`AcceptDisplayEmitter`. Then ratchet the row back to 68.

**Removes:** `WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler.CodeGen.Verbs → WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler.Binding` (its pair added at the 9e65164eb census)

**Sites:**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/NumericDisplayForm.cs` (whole: the file moves with its enum)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/AcceptDisplayEmitter.cs` (`AcceptDisplayEmitter`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/EditionContext.cs` (`EditionContext.NumericDisplay`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Cli/CliOptions.cs` (`CliOptions`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Cli/Program.cs` (the `--numeric-display` option's validator and resolve; its `using WiseOwl.Cobol.Binding` deleted)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CompilerDriver.cs` (the options record's `NumericDisplay`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/OptionsSectionBuilder.cs` (`NumericDisplayFormOption.OptionSpellings`)
- `docs/rearchitecture/architecture-edges.json` (the `cli → compiler.binding` allowance becomes `cli → compiler.model`; the ratchet and the census lower the rows)
- `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-architecture-review.md` §8.1 "The file as measured" (the allowance's reason)

**Landed (wave 20262172, group A).** The enum and `NumericDisplayFormOption` are in `Binding/Model/NumericDisplayForm.cs`
(namespace `WiseOwl.Cobol.Binding.Model`). The census at d0c58f441211, recorded in the landing, measures `CodeGen → Binding`
at 69 (was 70), `CodeGen.Emit → Binding` at 23 (was 25) and `Binding.Model → Binding` at 15 (was 16). The walk deleted the
three pairs that named `Binding.NumericDisplayForm`. The CLI no longer names compiler.binding, so its allowance is now
`cli → compiler.model`. The census still counts one use above the 68 the note expected. That use does not come from this
enum: no source under `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen` names `NumericDisplayForm` outside `Binding.Model`.
