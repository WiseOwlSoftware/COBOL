## Row GR-7.3.17.4-4 — PARTIAL (adjudicator + refuter record)

Rule: 'When ON ... a standard numeric time form value shall be greater than or equal to zero and less than 86,401. The implementor defines whether a value greater than or equal to 86,400 may be returned from the SECONDS-PAST-MIDNIGHT intrinsic function.' (cite.py --check 7.3.17.4 OK). Half 1: SecondsOutOfStandardForm bound = leapSecond ? 86401 : 86400 on an exact CobolDec compare; every standard-numeric-time-form consumer (FORMATTED-TIME, FORMATTED-DATETIME, COMBINED-DATETIME dec and real, LOCALE-TIME-FROM-SECONDS) gets LeapSecondFlag. Probe j_bound.cob under ON: FORMATTED-TIME('hhmmss.ss',86400.99)=23596099; 86401 -> zero-length default for FORMATTED-TIME and LOCALE-TIME-FROM-SECONDS; pb65_leap_second_on T5-T8 (86400 accepted, rendered 23:59:60). Half 2: determination 'never' documented as DOC-A.1-112 (Clock ticks < 86,400 s). || REFUTED: PARTIAL — Half 1's floor ('greater than or equal to zero') is violated under ON on the float/SDIDI carriers of FORMATTED-TIME, FORMATTED-DATETIME and LOCALE-TIME-FROM-SECONDS: a tiny negative is truncated to 0 before the screen. The ceiling (86,401) and fixed-point carriers are correct. Also noted: LOCALE-TIME-FROM-SECONDS(86400) under ON is accepted but renders '12:00:00 AM' (the leap second presented as midnight) - a §15.54 returned-value question, not this rule's, but the adjudicator's 'every consumer' claim did not look at it. The test pb65_leap_second_on pins only the accepted side (86400), not the 86401 rejection nor the floor.
