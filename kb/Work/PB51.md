# PB51 — OPEN — the commonest way to write zero is a compile error

> ⚙ **FOUND BY [[PB48]]'s PROBE MATRIX, AND MEASURED ON THE PRE-PB48 TREE — pre-existing, not a regression.**
> It was found only because the probe swept the ARITHMETIC controls as well as the intrinsic cases; nothing in
> PB48's subject would have touched it.

## MEASURED (2026-08-05)

```
01 WS-N PIC S9(9)V99.
COMPUTE WS-N = ZERO
  ⛔ error COBOLNET1511: a boolean COMPUTE expression shall not consist solely of an
     ALL literal (ISO §14.9.8 Format 2 SR3)
```

For contrast, every neighbouring form compiles and runs correctly: `COMPUTE WS-N = (ZERO)`,
`COMPUTE WS-N = ZERO + 7`, `COMPUTE WS-N = 7 + ZERO`, `MOVE ZERO TO WS-N`.

## WHY IT IS WRONG, TWICE OVER

§8.8.1.1 (`--check`ed): "An arithmetic expression may be an identifier referencing a numeric data item, a numeric
literal, **the figurative constant ZERO** (ZEROS, ZEROES), …" — so `ZERO` alone is a well-formed
arithmetic-expression-1 for §14.9.8 Format 1, and `WS-N` is a legal Format-1 receiver.

The diagnostic is wrong on its own terms as well: **the source contains no ALL literal.** The message names
§14.9.8 Format 2 SR3, a rule about `ALL literal-1`, for a statement whose operand is the bare figurative.

## THE MECHANISM (hypothesis — MEASURE IT, do not inherit it)

`ArithmeticBinder.BindComputeBoolean` rejects when the bound RHS is a `BoundBoolAll`, and `BoundBoolAll`'s own
doc says it carries "the figurative ZERO, normalized to `ALL B"0"` at bind". A bare `ZERO` is not adjacent to any
arithmetic operator or paren, so `ZeroTokenRewriter` leaves it figurative, `primaryExpression` cannot match it,
and the parser takes the COMPUTE Format-2 boolean alternative instead — where the normalization then makes it
indistinguishable from `ALL B"0"`. ⚠ `ArithmeticBinder` documents an "F1→F2 sole-boolean re-route"; the reverse
re-route appears not to exist, and SR2 (a Format-2 receiver shall be an elementary boolean item) should have
rejected the `PIC S9(9)V99` receiver before SR3 was ever consulted — **that ordering is itself worth checking.**

## ▶ WHAT TO CHECK FIRST

⛔ **THIS SHARES A ROOT WITH [[PB50]] AND THE DECISION IS THE SAME ONE: `primaryExpression` cannot express
§8.8.1.1's figurative ZERO, and a token-adjacency pass has been patching over the gap.** PB48 fixed the
adjacency pass where it fired WRONGLY; these two are where it does not fire at all. The structural answer is to
let the grammar carry the rule the standard states — `ZERO` as a `primaryExpression` alternative, with
`ZeroTokenRewriter` retired.
⚠ **That change is NOT small and must be measured, not assumed, in both directions.** (a) The rewriter exists
because adding `ZERO` to `primaryExpression` "causes exponential ANTLR prediction time" — a claim in a source
comment that has never been re-measured, and §0's standing rule is that a named root cause is a claim until it
is measured. (b) More importantly it injects a real ambiguity everywhere `figurativeConstant` and
`arithmeticExpression` are sibling alternatives — relation conditions, `valueOperand`, `booleanFactor` — where
ANTLR's first-match would silently decide figurative-vs-numeric semantics that differ observably (`IF X = ZERO`
over `PIC X(5)` compares against `"00000"`, not against 0). Enumerate those sites BEFORE editing the grammar.
