# PB3897 — national-items-displayed-as-utf-16-bytes--national-space-fill: National items filled with SPACE hold 0x20 bytes and are not equal to N" "

Campaign PB3838, tier 4: filed 2026-10-27 from the tier 2 harvest and the tier 3 adjudication (slice S4) and refutation (UPHELD). Upstream status `unreported`: the tracker has not been searched (tier 6, owner-gated). Evidence, outside git on one machine: `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\op\pb3838\records\national-items-displayed-as-utf-16-bytes--national-space-fill.md` and `.cob`, the refuter's reruns under `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\op\pb3838\refute\national-items-displayed-as-utf-16-bytes--national-space-fill\`.

## Summary

Under `-std=cobol2014`, every place that fills a national item with spaces stores the byte 0x20 in every byte:
`VALUE SPACE`, `MOVE SPACE`, and the space fill of a MOVE whose sender is shorter than the receiver. (A default
INITIALIZE of a national item does nothing at all; that is the separate record
`national-items-displayed-as-utf-16-bytes--initialize-national-no-op`.) GnuCOBOL's own national literal N" " is `00 20` (big-endian UTF-16), so a
national item "filled with spaces" holds `20 20` per character, a different character, and compares unequal to N" ".
Class: wrong output. One of the mechanisms the harvest grouped under "national items displayed as UTF-16 bytes": in
the fuzz output, the padding of national items prints as plain spaces while their letters print with NUL bytes
(fz00451, fz02549, fz03256, fz03848, fz00076, fz01163, fz02571, fz02809, fz00508, fz00509 and others, nights 3 to 5).

## Minimal test case

Free reference format (`-fformat=free`), 8 lines, written for this record.

```cobol
IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
PROGRAM-ID. NSPACE.
DATA DIVISION.
WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
01 NAT PIC N(1) VALUE SPACE.
PROCEDURE DIVISION.
IF NAT = N" " DISPLAY "EQUAL" ELSE DISPLAY "NOT EQUAL" END-IF
STOP RUN.
```

File: `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\op\pb3838\records\national-items-displayed-as-utf-16-bytes--national-space-fill.cob`.

The probe `work\national-items-displayed-as-utf-16-bytes\sprobe.cob` shows the other fill paths: after `MOVE SPACES TO
NM` (PIC N(2)), `NM = N"  "` is false; after `MOVE N"A" TO NP` (PIC N(2)), `NP = N"A "` is false (while `NP = N"A"` is
true, because the comparison also pads the shorter operand with 0x20 bytes).

## Environment

- `cobc (GnuCOBOL) 3.2.0`, Ubuntu package `gnucobol3 3.2-4build1`, WSL2 (Linux 6.18.54.1-microsoft-standard-WSL2 x86_64).
- Commands:

## Observed result

Compile exit code 0, with `-Wunfinished` warnings for USAGE NATIONAL (line 5) and the national literal (line 7). Run
exit code 0, output `NOT EQUAL`. The generated C (`cobc -C`) initializes the item with `memset (b_1, 32, 4)` for
`PIC N(2) VALUE SPACES`, and `MOVE SPACES` is `cob_move (&cob_all_space, ...)`.

## Expected result (per ISO)

Output `EQUAL`.

- ISO/IEC 1989:2023 §8.3.3.6.4 1) (checked): "When a figurative constant is used in a context requiring national
  characters, the figurative constant represents a national character value." In the VALUE clause of a national item
  SPACE is the national space, the same character as N" ".
- §14.6.8.5 (checked): data moved to a national receiver is "aligned at the leftmost character position in the data
  item with space fill or truncation to the right, as required"; the receiver's character positions are national, so
  the fill is national spaces.
- §14.9.20.4 6) (checked): a national receiving operand of a default INITIALIZE gets "Figurative constant national
  SPACES".
- §8.8.4.2.10 1) (checked): "The operands are determined to be equal if all pairs of corresponding national characters
  are equal." NAT holds one national space and N" " is one national space.

Edition: national data, national figurative constants and the INITIALIZE table entry are 2002 features; the 2009
committee draft for ISO/IEC 1989:2014 (CD 1.2) has the same figurative-constant rule ("When a figurative constant is
used in a context requiring national characters, the figurative constant represents ...") and the INITIALIZE entry
"National: Figurative constant national SPACES".

Edition as recorded by the adjudicator: 2014 (national figurative constants 2002; same rules in the 2009 CD). `upstream_edition: 2014` is the edition `-std=cobol2014` claims; the cited clauses were validated with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check` by the adjudicator and again by the refuter.

## Fix site (GnuCOBOL 3.2 source, read observationally)

One rule, written in several places, all filling with the byte `' '`:

- `cobc/codegen.c`, `output_initialize_to_value` (line 4935): the figurative-constant branch for `cb_space` calls
  `output_figurative (x, f, ' ', ...)`, a byte fill, whatever the category of the field.
- `libcob/move.c`, `cob_move_alphanum_to_alphanum` (line 444): pads the receiver with `memset (..., ' ', ...)`
  (lines 465 and 469); `cob_move` (line 1446) routes a national receiver there (no national case), for the figurative
  `cob_all_space` and for any shorter sender.
- `cobc/codegen.c`, `output_initialize_to_default` (line 5224): national categories use `output_move (cb_space, x)`,
  which would reach the same byte fill once INITIALIZE of a national item emits code at all (it does not today; see the
  INITIALIZE record).

The national space has to be a national character (`00 20` in GnuCOBOL's representation); `libcob/move.c` also needs
a national-to-national move that pads with it. Related records: `move-alphanumeric-to-national-copies-bytes` (the same
missing national case in `cob_move`) and `national-items-displayed-as-utf-16-bytes--initialize-national-no-op`.

## Draft issue

**Title:** National items filled with SPACE hold 0x20 bytes and are not equal to N" "

**Body:**

Environment: GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 (Ubuntu gnucobol3 3.2-4build1), x86_64 Linux.

Steps: `cobc -std=cobol2014 -fformat=free -x nspace.cob && ./nspace` with

```cobol
IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
PROGRAM-ID. NSPACE.
DATA DIVISION.
WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
01 NAT PIC N(1) VALUE SPACE.
PROCEDURE DIVISION.
IF NAT = N" " DISPLAY "EQUAL" ELSE DISPLAY "NOT EQUAL" END-IF
STOP RUN.
```

Observed: `NOT EQUAL`. The item holds `20 20`; the literal N" " is `00 20`. The same happens after `MOVE SPACES` to a
national item, and in the right-hand fill of `MOVE N"A" TO` a `PIC N(2)` item (which then is not equal to N"A ").

Expected: `EQUAL`. ISO/IEC 1989:2014 (2023 §8.3.3.6.4 rule 1): "When a figurative constant is used in a context
requiring national characters, the figurative constant represents a national character value", and a move to a national
item is space-filled in national character positions (2023 §14.6.8.5).

Cause: the VALUE initialization (`output_initialize_to_value` / `output_figurative` in cobc/codegen.c) and the move
padding (`cob_move_alphanum_to_alphanum` in libcob/move.c, reached because `cob_move` has no national case) fill with
the byte `' '`.
