# PB3700 — no allocator hands out a wave number to a hand dispatch, and a hand report's wave number is invisible to plan_wave's next-wave count

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/alloc.py` (`wave_top`, `truth`, `render`, `allocate`, `peek`, `main`), `scripts/orchestrator/coord.py` (`ledger_groups`), `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`main`), `scripts/orchestrator/landing_check.py` (`check`, `self_test`), `scripts/orchestrator/landing_measure.py` (`stage_times`), `scripts/orchestrator/progress.py` (`progress`, `self_test`), `scripts/orchestrator/turns_by_group_size.py` (`REPORT_NAME` reader), `.claude/skills/workstream/make_dispatch_specs.py` (`main`), `scripts/orchestrator/test_alloc.py`, `scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py`, `.claude/skills/workstream/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/MANDATORY-PRACTICES.md`, `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md`.

The operator named hand reports `w20261103a-PB3671` and `w20261104a-PB3690` without an allocator. The loop then planned wave 20261104, whose group a's findings file `review-w20261104-a.jsonl` already held a round of `worktree-wf_5669d89a-9c0-1`; the PB3690 reviewer noticed and wrote to another file. Central allocation (the workstream skill section 4) needs a `wave` kind in `alloc.py` that both `plan_wave.py` and a hand dispatch take from, and `--wave next` must read it.

## Landed (2026-10-25, fix-lane wave 20261107 group C)

**Re-probe.** `plan_wave.next_wave` read the highest wave in the reports directory, the ledger and the DEVLOG at plan
time, and a hand dispatch had no allocator for its number, so the window between the operator choosing `w20261104a` and
writing its report let the loop plan wave 20261104 too.

**Fix.** `alloc.py` has a fourth kind, `wave`: truth is `wave_top` (the highest wave or train number in a report's name,
the ledger or the newest DEVLOG labels: `next_wave`'s rule, moved here), reserved like the others in `alloc.json`;
`alloc.py wave` is what a hand dispatch runs before it names its report, and `plan_wave --wave next` PEEKS it while it
plans and RESERVES it once its specs check GREEN (as for the codes, PB3114), re-deriving the stop file if the number moved.
`next_wave` is deleted. To keep `alloc` a leaf of `coord`, the coordination directory's two file formats moved there from
`plan_wave`: `REPORT_NAME`, `DISPATCH_LEDGER` and `ledger_groups` (every caller changed, no alias left). The workstream
skill section 4, MANDATORY-PRACTICES O11 and DESIGN-orchestrator-loop sections 6 and 9 name the kind. **Tests.**
`test_alloc.py` (reports, hand reports, ledger, retired hand entries, seed, DEVLOG labels, reservation) and
`test_plan_wave.py` (a RED check with `--wave next` reserves nothing; GREEN reserves it and the next asker gets the one
after).
