# PB4075 — a wide function argument's consumers read the 34-digit image instead of the exact value

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs` (`IntrinsicRenderer.RenderWide` - the `MeanScaled` arm, `IntrinsicRenderer.FloatBody`, `IntrinsicRenderer.WideLeadArg`, `IntrinsicRenderer.LeadArg`, `IntrinsicRenderer.WholeRangeBodies`, `IntrinsicRenderer.ExactIntake`, `IntrinsicRenderer.RawArg` - comment only), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Roslyn/RuntimeApi.cs` (`RuntimeApi.DomainWideAdmitted`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Wide.cs` (`CobolIntrinsics.MeanWide`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.FloatWide.cs` (new: `CobolIntrinsics.SinWide`, `CosWide`, `TanWide`, `LogWide`, `Log10Wide`, `AcosWide`, `AsinWide`, `DomainWideAdmitted`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Float.cs` (`CobolIntrinsics.SinReduced`, `CobolIntrinsics.CosReduced`, `CobolIntrinsics.TanReduced`, `CobolIntrinsics.AcosOfGap`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.TwoPi.cs` (`CobolIntrinsics.ReduceQuarterTurns`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolWide.cs` (`CobolWide.QuotientDec`, `CobolWide.MagnitudeLimbs`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolDec.cs` (`CobolDec.Round34Wide`, `CobolDec.ToPreferredExponent` - now internal), `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (DOC-A.1-123), `docs/INTRINSICS_DESIGN.md`

One mechanism, filed as one note (MANDATORY-PRACTICES R3): an arithmetic expression wider than 38 digits inside a function argument is lowered to the SDIDI by round-to-odd (`RawArg`), and the consumers then use that image as if it were the argument's value. The two symptoms below are two consumers of the same image; the fix is the exact wide carrier PB3953 started, taken by each consumer (the MEAN consumer needs the exact wide sum, the binary64 consumer needs the exact value at the angle reduction).

**1. MEAN is off in the 34th digit** (wrong_answer, silent). `FUNCTION MEAN (BIG * BIG * K3, 1)` with BIG `9(18)` all 9s and K3 = 999 shows `499499999999999999001000000000000100000`; one 34-digit rounding of the exact mean gives `...000000000000000000000`. Cause: the `"MeanScaled"` arm of `RenderDec` lifts the round-to-odd image (`DecArg` -> `RawArg`), then the SDIDI quotient in `MeanDec` rounds again, so the result is rounded twice where PB3860 requires one SDIDI quotient. Fix: an exact wide sum, one SDIDI division.

**2. The binary64 family reads a wide argument's image** (wrong_answer). `FUNCTION SIN (BIG * BIG * K3)` prints `-0.929164729629822`, the sine of the 34-digit image, about 999 radians away from the exact argument. `LeadArg`/`ExactArg` (the `WholeRangeBodies` path) hand `ReduceQuarterTurns` the image. Fix: the exact value reaches the quarter-turn reduction.

Both repros are the report's own and were not re-probed (R2). The PB3953 fix is not yet on main in full (its finisher's tip `0941c35dc` on `worktree-wf_b7b6d335-6b8-4` waits to land; PR #787 carries the earlier head `5d4e0ac1d`), so the sites above are named as that report gives them and the implementer re-reads them on the landed code.

## Resolution (2026-10-30, wave 20261766)

Both repros reproduced on the PB3953-landed tree and are fixed at the one root: the consumer takes the argument's exact wide
value (`NumX.Wide`, `RawWideArg`), not its round-to-odd image.

- **MEAN.** `RenderWide` gained a `MeanScaled` arm: `CobolIntrinsics.MeanWide` sums the aligned arguments on `CobolWide` and
  `CobolWide.QuotientDec` rounds the exact quotient to the SDIDI's 34 digits once (remainder as the sticky bit, intermediate rounding
  mode, preferred exponent for an exact quotient). `FUNCTION MEAN (BIG * BIG * K3, 1)` now shows `499499999999999999001000000000000000000`.
- **The binary64 family.** `ExactIntake.Wide` marks SIN, COS, TAN, LOG, LOG10, ACOS and ASIN; `FloatBody` names their `...Wide`
  overload (`CobolIntrinsics.FloatWide.cs`) for a wide argument. The quarter-turn reduction takes the 77-digit magnitude's five
  limbs (`ReduceQuarterTurns(CobolWide, int)`, three limbs of 1/(2 pi) per significand limb), and LOG / LOG10 / ACOS / ASIN form
  x - 1 and 1 - |x| on the exact value, through `CobolIntrinsics.DomainWideAdmitted` for the 15.x.3 screen. EXP, EXP10 and SQRT keep
  the image: condition numbers of at most 709, 709 and 1/2 cannot carry a 10^-34 relative error to binary64's last bit.
- `FUNCTION SIN (BIG * BIG * K3)` is now `0.932559711390065` (oracle 0.93255971139006498686).

**Left open (a lead in the wave report):** VARIANCE, STANDARD-DEVIATION and PRESENT-VALUE still read the wide argument's image through
`DecArgList` (`RenderDec`): `FUNCTION VARIANCE (BIG * BIG * K3, BIG * BIG * K3 - 1)` shows `00000000010000000000` where the exact value
is 0.25, and `PRESENT-VALUE (0, P, 1 - P)` shows 0 where the sum is 1. Their value depends on the precision of the MEAN they deviate
from, which no clause fixes, so a body needs a determination first.
