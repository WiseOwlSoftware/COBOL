# PB4791 - an even MEDIAN through a table(ALL) argument still lands one binary64 low

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R1-1 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 1): `IntrinsicCarrierRules.cs:157` `ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair` answers false for any table(ALL) argument, so an even MEDIAN through a table stays on the exact lane and still lands one binary64 low; its comment calls the count unknown although `TableAllPlace.StaticCount` knows it for a fixed OCCURS, and PB4753 flips to landed with this arm reproducing and no note.

**Link.** PB4753 (landed) is the note whose fix introduced `ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair`; it was marked landed with this arm reproducing. This note is the arm it left.

**R1.** A grep of kb/Work for `ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair` finds only PB4753 (landed). No open note covers it.

**Fix (landed, wave 20261881).** `ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair` counted the arguments as written and answered false for any table(ALL) argument. It now counts the elements a table(ALL) stands for (§2023:15.3 14), `TableAllPlace.StaticCount`, the product of a fixed OCCURS's counts), so a list of fixed tables has a bind-time parity and an even one is the mean on the float lane. Re-probe before: `COMPUTE AF = FUNCTION MEDIAN (T (ALL) FUNCTION EXP10 (FUN3))` (T one FLOAT-LONG element 0.0001) compared NE to `(T (1) + FUNCTION EXP10 (FUN3)) / 2`; after, EQ. Golden `pb4791_even_median_through_a_table_all_lands_by_the_binary64` (and its `_below_2002` refusal). A table(ALL) over an OCCURS DEPENDING or dynamic-capacity range has a RUN-TIME count: its parity is not a bind-time fact, and that list stays a selection, the lane it had (a lead in the wave report: the exact lane's even mean would have to land as a binary64 when both middle values are binary64-derived).

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/IntrinsicCarrierRules.cs#ReturnsMeanOfMiddlePair`.
