# PB2885 — a stale arch-oracle baseline reaches main unrefused

**Found:** train 1050 (2026-10-09, the operator landing PB2880). `python scripts/arch/compare_oracle.py` on
f3387ba8d printed `=== ARCH-ORACLE: DIFFERENT — 6 of 7695 cases differ (baseline e345db425878, new f3387ba8d9b5) ===`,
every one `ADDED` and none `CHANGED`:
`corpus/2002/pb2739_copy_after_directive_line`, `corpus/85/pb2739_data_name_ending_id_then_copy`,
`corpus/85/pb2739_indented_division_header_remarks` (PB2739, 792f3d338), `corpus/2002/pb2844_constant_length_same_as_subordinate`,
`corpus/2002/pb2844_constant_length_type_subordinate` (PB2844, ca2580c0d), `corpus/2023/pb2757_medium_bytes_not_bom_sniffed`
(PB2757, bce5de48f). All three landed in train 1049 (DEVLOG 1947), an operator hand-landing that ran the gates but
not lander step 3c (`compare_oracle.py`, then `capture_oracle.py --record`); the baseline stayed at train 1047's
e345db425878. Trains 1049b and 1049c (also operator landings) skipped it too, harmlessly, because they added no case.

**The defect:** step 3c lives only in prose (`lander-train-brief.md` 3c, MANDATORY-PRACTICES L11). Nothing mechanical
refuses a landing whose committed baseline is not the tree's: `scripts/push-main.sh` and
`scripts/orchestrator/landing_check.py` never look at `docs/rearchitecture/evidence/arch-oracle/`. A landing that skips
the step leaves the next train to discover the drift, attribute cases it did not make, and re-record for it.

**What closes it:** make the step mechanical where every landing passes, the way PB2605 made the DEVLOG entry
mechanical: a landing that changes a compiled program (`src/`, `tests/conformance/`, the negative fixtures) must carry
a re-recorded baseline named for a commit in the landing, or push-main refuses with the command to run. Sweep for the
other lander-brief steps that exist only as prose (3c is one; check 3d onward) and give each the same treatment or a
recorded reason it cannot be checked.

**Rule:** none (process tooling, no ISO clause).
