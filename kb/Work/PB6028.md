Each is a SECOND report on a program already refused (or about to be) by the edition gate or another rule, so the compile fails
either way; the defect is the extra line, which cites a 2023 rule at 85 under a descriptor whose `No1985Rule` says the construct is
not 1985's. One mechanism likely serves them all: a diagnostic of a construct the edition lacks is not emitted once the edition
gate (or the rule that refused the construct) has spoken.
- COBOL1644 `numval-c-ambiguous-currency` (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/IntrinsicBinder.cs:2046`) fires at
  85 after COBOL2423 refused the second CURRENCY SIGN clause (probes `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20262131q/work-q-01/p1.cob`..`p4.cob`).
- COBOL1504 at `IntrinsicBinder.cs` about line 1221 (`UPPER-CASE (A LOCALE)`) fires before the construct gate.
- COBOL1640 `copy-replacing-nested-copy` (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs:737`) may report after the
  COBOL0900 nesting gate (not probed).
- COBOL2630 `literal-content-repertoire` (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/LiteralScreen.cs:191`) and COBOL5986
  `alphabet-code-set-branch` (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.Alphabets.cs:275`) fire beside their gates.
- COBOL2574 `replacing-result-forbidden` (`CopyProcessor.cs:537`): its comment arm is not edition-gated at 85.
Probe each at 85 first (`scripts/orchestrator/run_probe.py`); the net's `EditionCitationNet.Contradicted` and `Offences` see a
line only when a suite case runs it at 85.
