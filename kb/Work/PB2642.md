That checker spells the simple-insertion set again (case 'B' and the default arm at 244-245; CobolEditSimpleInsertionDriftTests does not cover it), the fixed-insertion sign arms of RenderFloat again (238-243) and the exponent form again (248-257), and it has no BLANK WHEN ZERO input.
