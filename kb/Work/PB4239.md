FOR: section 7.3.10.1, section 7.3.10.4 GR3 "shall no longer be reserved or restricted in any way", section 4.2.10 3) "additional reserved words" (all cite OK), plus two the first report missed: D.12.1 (the directive lets a programmer "conform to previously existing implementor extensions") and D.12.3 (UNDEFINE "can be useful when a change of compiler that includes new reserved words that conflict with existing code") - the collision itself. GnuCOBOL 3.2 default/ibm/mf accept `UNDEFINE 'COMP-3'` and compile `01 COMP-3`, while -std=cobol2002/2014 say it "is not a reserved word": its population is the active configuration's reserved set. AGAINST: section 8.3.2.4.1 defines the term by the section 8.9 list; section 4.2.10's remedy is documentation (D-RW1). Pinned: `UNDEFINE 'COMP-3'` and `UNDEFINE 'JSON'` both draw COBOL1623 at 2023. Evidence correction: `01 COMP-3 PIC X.` does NOT draw COBOL0313 (COMP-3 is consumed as USAGE of an implicit FILLER, COBOL0881); `01 JSON PIC X` does. Recommendation: include the 15 extensionReserved words in the SR3 population, with goldens for UNDEFINE 'JSON' / 'COMP-3' each followed by the word as a data-name, recorded in D-RW1; if excluded, COBOL1623 must say the word is reserved as a nonstandard extension (section 4.2.10) that the directive cannot modify. ## Adjudicated (wave 20261861 d, 2026-11-04, pin eafd32083)

**Adjudicated (wave 20261861 d, E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-w20261861/d/determination.md; pin eafd32083).** The standard
answers it the way the owner did. SR3 and SR4 of 7.3.10.3 speak of "a reserved word", and 8.3.2.1 rule 1 makes a 4.2.10
addition a reserved word of this implementation. 7.3.10.1 and D.12.1 say the directive exists to change which words are
reserved, and D.12.3 names this collision ("a change of compiler that includes new reserved words"). So the population SR3
and SR4 share is 8.9 at 2023, plus 8.10, plus 8.11, plus the 15 D-RW1 words, and the defect has two sides.
- (A) `UNDEFINE`, `EQUATE` or `SUBSTITUTE` of JSON, XML, COMP-3, ENTRY and the other 11 draws COBOL1623 SR3 (probes r01-r04,
  r06, r09, r11, r12, r14-r16). The lexer retype has already freed the word, and only the category test refuses.
- (B) `RESERVE "JSON"` and `EQUATE "MOVE" WITH "COMP-3"` compile clean (r07, r13), and `EQUATE "DISPLAY" WITH "JSON"` is
  refused only at its use (r08).

GnuCOBOL 3.2's default dialect accepts (A) and refuses `EQUATE ... WITH "COMP-3"`. It accepts `RESERVE "JSON"`, where SR4 rules.
**Fix:** `VersionConformancePass.IsReservedContextOrIntrinsic` asks `NonstandardExtensionRegister.ReservesWord`, a new
lookup over the register's `ReservedWords`. The SR4 message names 4.2.10 and D-RW1. D-RW1 and the SR3/SR4 bullets of
DESIGN-cobol-words-directive.md say so. The SR3/SR4 unit theories iterate the register. The note's title and Owner question
said "SR3" for the clause; SR3 and SR4 live in §2023:7.3.10.3 (`spec_refs` now names it).

Siblings, in this fix:
- **S1, SR4 under-reject.** The same predicate is the cause. r13 (`EQUATE "MOVE" WITH "COMP-3"`) compiles clean. r07
  (`RESERVE "JSON"`) compiles clean. r08 (`EQUATE "DISPLAY" WITH "JSON"`, then `JSON A.`) gives only COBOL0313 at the use. r20
  (`RESERVE "JSON"` plus `01 JSON`) gives only COBOL0313. GnuCOBOL default refuses r08 and r13 with "already reserved".
- **S2, EQUATE/SUBSTITUTE of an extension word as literal-1/4.** r06 (`EQUATE "COMP-3" WITH "PACK3"`), r09 and r16
  (`SUBSTITUTE "COMP-3" BY "PACK3"`) and r14 (`SUBSTITUTE "JSON" BY "JSN"`) each give COBOL1623 SR3 today and are legal.

Siblings, elsewhere:
- **S3**, the directive's SR3/SR4 COBOL1623 is dropped whenever the parse fails (r19): PB4586.
- **S4, a PB4573 witness** (the Mythos lane adds it to PB4573): r17 (`EQUATE "JSON" WITH "JS"`, then `01 JS`) reports COBOL0313
  "unexpected 'JSON'", which names the original word for a retyped synonym. It is not fixed under PB4239.
- No overlap with PB4488 (its `CobolWordsMap.ReservedSynonyms` covers only originals with no lexer token, and every extension
  word has one) or PB4572 (s01 and s02 show a usage synonym reaches `ParseUsage` as the canonical spelling). The COBOL1501 half
  of the gap addendum landed in PB4309.

Owed goldens (conformance-suite/cases/7.3.10-COBOL-WORDS/): E1 `pb4239_cobol_words_undefine_json_is_a_data_name` (`J`),
E2 `pb4239_cobol_words_undefine_comp_3_keeps_computational_3` (`C +00012 3`), E3 `pb4239_cobol_words_substitute_comp_3`
(`C +00012 3`), E5 `pb4239-cobol-words-extension-word-is-no-fresh-word` (COBOL1623 SR4), E6
`pb4239-cobol-words-reserve-extension-word` (COBOL1623 SR4); and the unit theories over `NonstandardExtensionRegister.Entries`.
`UNDEFINE "COMP-3"` followed by `USAGE COMP-3` stays refused (COBOL5926 warning, COBOL5506 error). Fix shape, legs and
doc edits (D-RW1, DESIGN-cobol-words-directive.md, the SR-7.3.10.3-3 and SR-7.3.10.3-4 inventory files) are in the

**S4 after PB4573 (Mythos lane, 2026-11-04).** PB4573 (wave 20261860) fixed S4's naming: at its head r17 reports COBOL0313

## Landing (wave 20261863 A)

**Fix.** `NonstandardExtensionRegister.ReservesWord` (a lazy set over the rows' `ReservedWords`, compared by the keyword fold) is the
fourth disjunct of the one SR3/SR4 population, `CobolWordsCategoryPass.IsReservedContextOrIntrinsic` (the predicate moved out of
`VersionConformancePass` under PB4586). `UNDEFINE`, `EQUATE` and `SUBSTITUTE` of JSON, XML, COMP-3, ENTRY and the other 11 are legal;
`RESERVE "JSON"` and `EQUATE "MOVE" WITH "COMP-3"` are COBOL1623 SR4 with a sentence that names section 4.2.10 and D-RW1. S4 (the
witness PB4573 left): the parser's COBOL0313 vendor hint for the JSON and XML tokens (`CobolErrorStrategy`, hint 0) now says the word is
reserved for a nonstandard extension and so is no user-defined word, and names the synonym as written, so `01 JS` after `EQUATE "JSON"
WITH "JS"` reads "JSON is a word this implementation reserves ... and 'JS' is its synonym here (ISO 7.3.10.4 GR2), so neither is a
user-defined word". Goldens: `conformance:cases/7.3.10-COBOL-WORDS/pb4239_*` (E1, E2, E3), negatives `pb4239-cobol-words-*` (E5, E6, E7).
Unit theories iterate the register (`CobolWordsDirectiveTests.Sr3_AnExtensionReservedWord_IsAnExistingWord`,
`Sr4_AnExtensionReservedWord_IsNotAFreshWord`). Rows SR-7.3.10.3-3 and -4 stay CONFORMS with the new witnesses.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/NonstandardExtension.cs:153` (`NonstandardExtensionRegister.ReservesWord`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/CobolWordsCategoryPass.cs` (`IsReservedContextOrIntrinsic`, `NewWordRefusal`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`GuessCobolIntent`, hint 0), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/CobolWordsMap.cs`
(`SynonymBy`, `NoteSynonym`), `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` D-RW1,

**Review round 1 (R1-1).** The hint-0 sentence for a synonym named EQUATE's GR2 whatever wrote the synonym; a SUBSTITUTE-written
synonym (`SUBSTITUTE "JSON" BY "JSN"`, `01 JSN`) was told "neither is a user-defined word" while `01 JSON` is accepted, as GR4 frees
it. `CobolWordsMap.SynonymBy` returns the directive that wrote the synonym; the hint reads it from the parser's map and names GR4
and the SUBSTITUTE for that arm. Goldens `pb4239_cobol_words_substitute_json_is_a_data_name` (JSON freed, prints D) and
`pb4239-cobol-words-substitute-synonym-of-extension-word-is-reserved` (COBOL0313), unit
`ASubstituteSynonymOfAnExtensionWord_UsedAsADataName_NamesGr4AndFreesTheOriginal`.

**Left open (not this note's mechanism).** The second sentence of the same COBOL0313 report, "'JS' is not a clause of the data description
entry", is the closed-format residue (`ClosedFormats` / `CobolErrorStrategy.ClosedFormatResidue`) and fires for ANY reserved word in the
position of a data-name (`01 MOVE PIC X.` reads the same, COBOL1941); that is a separate defect of the residue hint, filed by the registrar
from the wave report.
