**Fix.** `writeStatement` parses ONE `retryLockPhrases` element (new; shared with REWRITE) in either printed place:
`retryLockPhrases conditionalPhrases | conditionalPhrases retryLockPhrases?`. `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.GateRetryLockPlace`

(COBOL0902 from 2014, a warning under --permissive), before one is `write-lock-phrases-before-end-of-page-2014` (COBOL0900
at 2002: each edition is read from its own text), after INVALID KEY phrases is COBOL2987 at every edition. After the change:
`ord2002.cob --std 2002 --run` → `N=5`, rc=0; at 2014 and 2023 COBOL0902 at both WRITEs.

(§2002:14.8.37.1) and WRITE Format 2 all print retry/lock before the conditional phrases, as the grammar does; WRITE Format 1
is the only arm whose place moved.
