# PB1350 — OPEN

ONE tokenizer (`TokenizeTextWords`, shared by REPLACE and COPY REPLACING, and `ReadTextWord` sharing its predicates) decides what a text-word is, and it decides it by its own predicates instead of §7.2.2.5 + §8.3.5. Five adjudicators and seven refuters reached it independently from three subjects (text-manipulation-elements, copy-statement-p2, replace-statement-p2). Every matching rule downstream is only as right as the words this function forms.

## Finding — text-manipulation-elements (wrong-answer)

F1 - CopyProcessor.TokenizeTextWords classifies separators by its own predicates instead of the §8.3.5 set plus the §7.2.2.5 1) colon/parenthesis rule: the colon is never a separator, and period/comma/semicolon are separators when 'not followed by a digit' instead of when 'followed by a space', so REPLACE / COPY REPLACING match against the wrong text-words (misses in reference modifiers, spurious hits inside picture strings)

**Rows:** GR-7.2.2.5-1, GR-7.2.2.5-3

**Repro:**

```
probe/textword/tw1.cob: REPLACE ==5== BY ==2==. 01 WA PIC X(6) VALUE "ABCDEF". 01 WB PIC X(6). MOVE WA(1:5) TO WB DISPLAY "[" WB "]" (any --std). probe/textword/tw2.cob: REPLACE ==ZZ== BY ==99==. 01 WA PIC ZZ.ZZ. MOVE 5 TO WA DISPLAY "[" WA "]".
```

**Expected from the spec:** tw1: the colon is a separator (§7.2.2.5 1)), so 5 is a text-word, matches, and the statement becomes MOVE WA(1:2) -> [AB    ]. tw2: a period not followed by a space is not a separator (§8.3.5 3)), so ZZ.ZZ is one text-word (§7.2.2.5 3)), does not equal ZZ, and stays -> [ 5.00]. Likewise Z,ZZ9 is one text-word (§8.3.5 2)).

**Observed:** tw1 prints [ABCDE ] (the word '1:5' never matches); tw2 prints [05.00] (ZZ.ZZ split into ZZ . ZZ, both replaced). A PIC Z,ZZ9 under REPLACE ==ZZ9== BY ==999== printed [  005] instead of [    5]: the embedded comma is treated as a separator comma and even skipped as a space.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#TokenizeTextWords (ordinary-word loop and the '(' ')' arm), #IsSeparatorPunctuation, #IsSpaceEquivalentSeparator; ReadTextWord shares the same predicates

## Finding — text-manipulation-elements (wrong-answer)

F2 - CopyProcessor.TokenizeTextWords' literal arm recognizes only a bare quote/apostrophe opening delimiter and closes at the first matching quote, so prefixed literals (X" N" B" NX" BX") are split into a prefix word plus a quoted word and a literal with a doubled quote is split in two; REPLACE / COPY REPLACING then match inside literals

**Rows:** GR-7.2.2.5-2

**Repro:**

```
probe/textword/tw3.cob (--std 2002+): REPLACE =="41"== BY =="42"== =="PQ"== BY =="RS"== =="Q"== BY =="Z"==. DISPLAY "[" X"41" "]" DISPLAY "[" N"PQ" "]" DISPLAY "[" "P""Q" "]". probe/textword/tw5.cob (--std 85): REPLACE =="Q"== BY =="Z"==. DISPLAY "[" "P""Q" "]".
```

**Expected from the spec:** Each literal INCLUDING its opening delimiter (X", N" per §8.3.5 5)) and with its doubled-quote content is ONE text-word (§7.2.2.5 2)); none equals a pseudo-text-1 operand, so nothing is replaced: [A] [PQ] [P"Q].

**Observed:** Observed [B] [RS] [P"Z] at 2002/2014/2023 and [P"Z] at 85: the quoted tail of each literal is matched as its own text-word and rewritten.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#TokenizeTextWords (the c is '"' or '\'' arm and the ordinary-word loop that stops at a quote)

## Finding — replace-statement-p2 (wrong-answer)

replace-text-word-tokenizer: TokenizeTextWords does not form §7.2.2.5 text-words - a literal's N/X/B/NX prefix is a separate word, a literal ends at the first quote so a doubled quote splits it in two, and the colon is not a separator - so pseudo-text matches inside literals and fails on subscripts/ref-mods

**Rows:** GR-7.2.4.4-8, GR-7.2.4.4-L2.4

**Repro:**

```
REPLACE =="AB"== BY =="CD"==. DISPLAY N"AB"  -> prints CD; REPLACE =="A"== BY =="Y"== =="41"== BY =="42"==. DISPLAY "A""B" DISPLAY X"41" -> prints Y"B and B; REPLACE ==2 : 3== BY ==3 : 2==. DISPLAY TBL(2:3) with TBL VALUE 'ABCDEF' -> prints BCD
```

**Expected from the spec:** §7.2.2.5 2) a literal 'including the opening and closing delimiters' is ONE text-word, so N"AB", X"41" and "A""B" never equal "AB"/"41"/"A" (GR8 b): prints AB, A"B, A. §7.2.2.5 1) colon is a separator text-word, so TBL(2:3) holds text-words 2 : 3 and is replaced -> TBL(3:2) prints CD.

**Observed:** Probes out/probe/replace-p2/nat.cob, lit.cob, match2.cob on the pinned compiler: CD, Y"B, B, BCD. The literal arm (lines 300-309) stops at the first quote with no doubled-quote handling and no prefix; the word arm (319-327) breaks on quotes but not on ':'.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#TokenizeTextWords (262-330) - shared with COPY REPLACING (ResolveOneCopy -> ApplyReplacements), so COPY ... REPLACING has the same defect
