# PB5334 - a `Base/Head:` report header was read with its base as its head

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/reports.py` (`sha_field`, `header_sha`, `BASE_HEAD`); `scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py`
(the PB3476 head checks).

## What happened

The wave-20262000 implementers wrote their headers as `Base/Head: <base sha> / <head sha>` (6 reports on disk use the
form; the template says `**base:** x · **HEAD:** y`). `sha_field("head")`'s field pattern needs `head` at a field
position, so it missed, and its fallback, the first `head <sha>` anywhere, read `Head: <base sha>`. The branch survey
then reported each of w20262000b (worktree-wf_07022f79-76e-2), w20262000c (-76e-6) and w20262000e (-76e-8) as a
DECISION ("the report's HEAD 74bd6713166c is not a commit of this branch" / "2 commits since the report's HEAD that no
report describes"), so the review lane never reviewed them, and the progress mails called each a STALL at 130, 171 and
153 minutes. Nothing failed: the misread base looked like a real head.

## Fix

`BASE_HEAD` reads the combined field before either single field, its first sha the base and its second the head;
`sha_field` carries each word's group in it. The three reports now parse to their real heads (f92b5a6629, 0a97e37a1c,
dbadc71dee). Self-test: a `Base/Head:` header gives HEAD the second sha and BASE the first (`test_plan_wave.py`,
552/552).
