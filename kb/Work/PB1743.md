# PB1743 — the blocking wait idles to its timeout

## Found (2026-09-29, wave 72)

The owner noticed that the fleet was using ~5 % CPU and that wave 72 was taking "far far far longer than it should".
- Group H's final implementer gate went GREEN at 11:56. Its wait,
  `timeout 590 bash -c 'tail -n +1 -f "<log>" | grep -m1 -E "=== BUILD-LOCAL GATE: "'`, returned only at 12:03:48,
  terminated by the timeout.
- The orchestrator's own `push-main` wait earlier that morning did the same (exit 124 with `PUSH-MAIN-EXIT` already in
  the log).

## Root cause

`grep -m1` exits on the match. `tail -f` learns that its reader is gone (SIGPIPE) only when it next WRITES. A verdict
line is normally the log's last, so `tail` never writes again and the pipeline lives until `timeout` kills it.
- Measured on a two-line log: the `tail -f` form returned at the 8 s timeout (exit 124); `until grep -q …; do sleep 5;
  done` returned in 0 s.
- The form was PRESCRIBED: MANDATORY-PRACTICES P2, the workstream skill, the implementer dispatch spec, the lander-train
  brief, and the public agent-fleet skill and brief template. `check_practices.py` even REQUIRED `tail -n \+1 -f` in two
  briefs.
- Every gate wait, `push-main` wait and battery wait therefore idled for up to one timeout (~10 min) after its result.
  Per implementer that is 1–3 waits, and per lander 2 or more.

## Fix

- The form everywhere is now `timeout 580 bash -c 'until grep -q "<verdict>" <log>; do sleep 5; done'`: P2, the
  workstream SKILL, the dispatch spec (which also matches the new `BUILD-LOCAL GATE` verdict), and the lander-train
  brief.
- `check_practices.py` requires `until grep -q` where it required `tail -n \+1 -f`, and REJECTS any brief carrying
  `tail -f … | grep -m1`.
- The public agent-fleet skill §5 and brief template are fixed in claude-skills v1.13.4, with the measurement.

## Landed (2026-09-29)

- The brief edits were re-applied on train 72's M13 versions of the same files and committed after train 72 landed
  (DEVLOG 1780). The main checkout had carried them uncommitted meanwhile, so train 72's lander and wave 73 read the
  fixed wait from disk.
- The owner pushed claude-skills v1.13.4 (SKILL.md and the brief template) and v1.14.1 (the last copy, in the skill's
  README). The project pin is now 1.14.1.
- `check_practices` holds the rule; the reproduction is in the DEVLOG entry.
