**Scenario:** Each verb emitter re-decides whether a receiver takes the encoded image and re-picks the fixed or float encoder: SetEmitter.EmitSetContent 240-242, StoreSetTarget 273-275, AugmentSetTarget 318-324; ArithmeticEmitter.StoreArith 540-542, 586, 590; MoveEmitter.ConvertSource 731, 756, 772; AcceptDisplayEmitter 144-146, 159-161, 210-212; ReportWriterEmitter 557. The predicate already differs by site: most test `Item.StoreAsImage`; AcceptDisplayEmitter:144 and MoveEmitter:184 test `StoreAsImage || target is RedefViewPlace`; MoveEmitter:249 adds `or NumericImagePlace`; PlaceRenderer.IsNativeImageWindow (538) says a RedefViewPlace{Coding: null} over an item whose StoreAsImage is false but which has an image byte form IS a window that needs the encode. So a numeric receiver of that last shape is encoded by ACCEPT and by PlaceRenderer's cell-leaf store but handed the raw carrier by SET, arithmetic and MOVE; whichever predicate is right, the others are wrong for some Place kind, and the next Place kind (the §8.5 Place union) must be taught at 14 sites. The fixes so far landed one site at a time (SetEmitter:272: 'The raw arm against a window was CS1503 in generated code'; ArithmeticEmitter:534-536 records the same CS1503 for the float arm)
