## Landing

Re-probed on the worktree's tree (base 8958152ce) before the change: `audit_grammar_optional_words` and
`audit_evidence_supersession` each read 645 parser rules, `phase_b_batch.grammar_rules` 642 — it missed `methodName`,
`className` (CobolOO.g4) and `displayDirectiveFragment` (CobolExpressions.g4), and 74 lexer rules as well, and quoted
40 more with the wrong extent. The evidence audit's own reader had two further holes the note did not name: it never
found `NAME_BODY` (a name-alone head whose line carries a trailing `// IDENTIFIER` comment) and it ended `PIC_STRING`
at a `;` inside the rule's C# action (line 1281 instead of 1302).

Fixed at the root in ONE module, `scripts/spec/grammar_source.py`: `read()` (cached, population asserted) returns every
rule as `Rule(name, kind, file, first_line, last_line, body, source, lead)`; `blank()` replaces comments and `{…}`
actions with spaces (literals and `[…]` sets kept, newlines kept), a head is a column-0 identifier whose next non-blank
character is `:`, and a rule ends at the first `;` outside a literal or set. The three private readers are deleted
(phase_b's `RULE_HEAD` and line reader, the evidence audit's `grammar_rules`, the optional-word audit's `strip_comments`,
`RULE_HEAD`, `grammar_rules` and `LEXER_RULE`); the consumers are `phase_b_batch.quotable_rules` (the dossier
projection, renamed from `grammar_rules`), `audit_evidence_supersession.rule_spans` and
`audit_grammar_optional_words.parser_rules`/`keyword_spellings` (now over `Rule.rhs`). Neutrality, measured: the
optional-word audit's derived facts (every rule's required tokens, alternatives, references, and the 504 keyword
spellings) are IDENTICAL before and after; the evidence audit's `--check` output is identical (its two corrected spans
change no verdict); the dossier gains the 78 rules and corrects the 40 extents. `--self-test` plants every trap (a `;`
with a trailing comment, a comment between head and `:`, a predicate holding `;`, a set holding a quote, a literal `;`,
a commented-out rule, declarations and modes) and refuses a script that declares one of the retired names again
(proved to fail on the pre-change optional-word audit). Design §8.2 carries the scripts rule.

For the next implementer:
- `scripts/spec/grammar_source.py#read` — THE grammar reader; take `Rule.body`/`Rule.rhs` (blanked) for token logic and
  `Rule.source`/`Rule.lead` (raw) for quoting. Trap: line-based end tests miss `;   // comment` and `;` inside actions.
- `scripts/spec/audit_grammar_optional_words.py#parser_rules` — name → Rule for parser rules; `keyword_spellings()` reads lexer rules from `read()`.
- `scripts/spec/audit_evidence_supersession.py#rule_spans` — name → (file name, first, last) for every rule kind.
- `scripts/spec/phase_b_batch.py#quotable_rules` — the dossier's projection of `read()`; never a reader of its own.
