# PB4807 — the fallback estimate anchors days back and undercounts

**Sites:**
- `scripts/orchestrator/budget.py#current` (merges `live_reading` into the readings only for this call; it never stores it)
- `scripts/orchestrator/budget.py#live_reading` (`LIVE_MAX_AGE` 30 minutes)
- `scripts/orchestrator/budget.py#record` (the only writer of `<coord>/readings.json`)
- `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` (`calibration.tokens_per_point`, fitted 2026-10-07)
- `scripts/orchestrator/test_budget.py` (whole: a module-level script with no members; the anchor and refit cases are added)
- `scripts/spec/ledger_plan.py#measure_pacing` (the ledger's Pacing section: the newest reading in `<coord>/readings.json`, so it shows the last STORED reading, never the live one)
- `scripts/spec/ledger_plan.py#estimate_inputs` (the measured burn, from the same stored readings)

**What the operator saw (account 2, 2026-11-05, PST).** The tap read 82 % at 14:39, with budget.py at 82.06. Once the tap went stale
(about 14:56), the loop's budget.py read 75.86 at 15:52. The tap read 85 % at 17:06, and at 17:49:34 the loop read 77.81. One second
earlier the tap had written 86 % (`measured_at` 2026-11-06T01:49:33Z), so the loop's call came before that write could anchor it. The
loop launched a wave at 16:06 thinking the account was below the day allowance; it was really at about 84 of 85.8.

**Reproduced.** Run `budget.py --account account2 --json --now 2026-11-06T01:49:00Z`, so the live reading is in the future and excluded.
It prints `weekly_est_pct` 77.81, anchored on the readings.json entry of **2026-11-02T19:41Z at 45 %**, plus 32.811 points of telemetry
(41.56 M Opus, 27.25 M Sonnet and 0.32 M Haiku counted tokens). The meter reads 86 % one minute later. The same spend therefore cost
about 41 points, and the calibration counted 32.8 of them: about 20 % low.

**Two causes, both in the fallback.**
1. **The anchor is days old.** The statusline tap (kb/Work PB4505) only replaces the anchor while it is fresh. Each fresh reading
   `current` sees is discarded after the call, so when the tap goes stale the estimate falls back to the newest `record`ed reading.
   That was 3.5 days and 41 points back here, and every calibration error compounds over that span.
2. **The calibration is stale.** `tokens_per_point` was refitted on 2026-10-07 from one bracket of one session. Its source line says
   to refit "whenever two readings of one account bracket a measured spend", and none has been done since. The bracket above
   (45 % to 86 % over 41.56 M / 27.25 M / 0.32 M) is such a pair.

**Fix.**
- Persist the anchor. Whenever `current` takes a fresh live reading newer than the account's stored anchor, append it to
  readings.json, deduplicated on `measured_at`, so the fallback anchors at most `LIVE_MAX_AGE` plus the idle gap back. Drift then
  stays at minutes of spend, not days.
- Refit `tokens_per_point` from brackets the stored live readings now provide, per account, keeping the family ratios unless
  the brackets separate them. Record the fit in `calibration.source`.
- Test: after a fresh live reading is seen and the live file then goes stale, the estimate anchors on that stored reading,
  not on an older `record`ed one. Also add a refit check that a stored bracket reproduces its own meter delta within a tolerance.

**Interim.** Account 2's loop is held in hold-day until 2026-11-06 09:05 PST (operator). There was no overspend on 2026-11-05.

**Second reader (the operator, 2026-11-06 11:31 PST).** Ledger v325's Pacing section showed account 2 at 45 %, noted 2026-11-02 19:41 UTC, with the 52 points left funding about 66 h, while `budget.py` read 88.2 % from the live status-line anchor. `gen_ledger.py` gets its pacing and measured burn from `ledger_plan.py` (`measure_pacing`, `estimate_inputs`), which read `<coord>/readings.json` directly, so they show the last STORED reading. Storing the fresh reading (this note's fix) corrects both readers. The fix's self-test should also cover the ledger's reader.
