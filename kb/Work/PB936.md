# PB936 — OPEN — "shared memoized", nine times over

Source: the `PB560` report, "New defects" item 2 — filed by the implementer who ADDED the ninth site, and
who says in the report that changing the dispatch was outside their mechanism. It is inside this one.

## The measurement

`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/DataDivision/DataEmitter.cs:22` — the class's own doc-comment describes its
`PhysicalModel` as "shared memoized". The memoization is a field on the INSTANCE. The instance is built at
nine call sites:

* `ProgramEmitter` ×3
* `OoEmitter` ×3
* `PtrEmitter`
* `ReportWriterEmitter`
* `SetEmitter` (added by the PB560 change set)

So a compilation unit containing a report and a `SET condition-name TO TRUE` walks the storage forest
three times, and the comment says it walks it once.

**Repro:** any program with both. The honest measurement is a COUNT of `new DataEmitter(` executions per
compile, not a stopwatch — the cost is allocations and repeated tree walks, and a count is deterministic
where a timing is not.

## Why this is one of the four required review dimensions and not a nice-to-have

The owner requires **architecture · full code review · performance · duplication and efficiency** as
continuous criteria (`PROMPT.md` §4). This is the third and fourth at once: a memoization that does not
memoize is a duplication defect whose symptom is a performance one, and a doc-comment that asserts the
opposite of the code is how it survived nine sites.

## The fix

One cached `DataEmitter` on `EmitContext`, and the doc-comment's claim becomes true. ⛔ Then the comment
should be made into an ASSERTION rather than a promise — a drift test that the constructor runs once per
`EmitContext` — because "shared memoized" was believed for nine sites and nothing could contradict it
(`feedback_a_dead_lookup_is_also_unverified`).

`feedback_change_the_dispatch_not_the_callers`: the repair is the dispatch (one instance on the context),
not nine call-site edits, and not a static cache.
