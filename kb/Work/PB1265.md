## Measured

### Finding occurs-clause-p1 — §13.18.38.2 Formats 1-4; §13.18.38.3 SR24 (under-rejects; rows FMT-13.18.38.2)

**Mechanism.** The OCCURS grammar is a SUPERSET parse (bounds, DEPENDING, STEP and the Format 4 phrases are all independently optional and order-free) and the binder never narrows it back to the four general formats outside the report section

**Repro.** WORKING-STORAGE, --std 2023, under out13/probe/occurs-p1/: `05 T PIC X OCCURS 5 DEPENDING ON N.` (fmt_dep_no_to_run: bound as an ODO with minimum 1); `05 T PIC X OCCURS 2 TO 5.` (fmt_to_no_dep_run: silently a FIXED 5 table); `05 T PIC X OCCURS 5 STEP 2.` (fmt_step_ws); `OCCURS DYNAMIC CAPACITY IN C FROM 1 FROM 4` (fmt_f4_dup_run: last wins, C = 4); `OCCURS DYNAMIC TO 5 FROM 1` (fmt_f4_reorder)

**Expected, from the standard.** Each is rejected. §13.18.38.2 Format 2 requires `integer-1 TO integer-2 TIMES DEPENDING ON data-name-1` in full. TO without DEPENDING and STEP exist only in Format 3, the report-writer format, where SR24 still pairs TO with DEPENDING. Format 4 prints each of CAPACITY/FROM/TO/INITIALIZED once, as optional brackets in a fixed order.

**Observed.** All compile clean, with no documented extension in docs/CONFORMANCE.md. The occursStepPhrase grammar comment asserts that 'the binder enforces' STEP's report-only placement; the probe refutes it for a data-division entry.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4#occursClause / #occursDynamicPhrase; src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Odo.cs#OdoBindOccursSpec

## Landed (wave 1015 Y)

**Fix.** The grammar's `occursClause` stays a superset parse, so the refusal can name the rule. Its data-description arm now narrows it to §13.18.38.2's data-division formats: `DataBinder.OdoBindOccursSpec` first calls `NarrowToDataDivisionFormats` (`DataBinder.Odo.cs`), and each of the following draws the new **COBOLNET2789** `occurs-format-not-printed`:
- TO without DEPENDING (it used to bind silently as a FIXED table at the maximum);
- DEPENDING without TO (it used to bind as an ODO with minimum 1);
- a STEP phrase, which belongs to Format 3 only;
- a Format 4 phrase written twice (a repeated FROM used to bind its last value);
- a Format 4 phrase out of the printed CAPACITY IN, FROM, TO, INITIALIZED order (§5.2.1).

The other arm, the report section, was already narrowed to Format 3 by `DataBinder.Reports.cs#ReportOccursOf`, which refuses DYNAMIC, KEY and INDEXED BY with COBOLNET2021 and pairs TO with DEPENDING under SR24. KEY before INDEXED BY is the grammar's own order. The grammar comments that claimed "phrases are order-independent" and "the binder enforces" STEP placement now point at the site that does it.

**Sweep.** I scanned every `.cob`, `.cs` and `.md` under `tests/`, `src/` and `docs/` for out-of-order or repeated Format 4 phrases (scratch `scan_dyn.py`). It found three sources written `OCCURS DYNAMIC FROM n CAPACITY IN c`: two in `OccursDynamicGuardTests` and `negative/pb1260-odo-under-occurs-dynamic`. That order is not legal COBOL, and the gate turned one of them red, so all three were rewritten in the printed order. The scan for `OCCURS n DEPENDING` without TO found none.

**Witnesses.** The positive is `conformance:2014/pb1265_occurs_printed_formats`, with Formats 1, 2 and 4 written in full. The negatives are `conformance:negative/pb1265-occurs-depending-without-to`, `-to-without-depending`, `-step-in-working-storage`, `-dynamic-phrase-repeated` and `-dynamic-phrases-reordered`.
