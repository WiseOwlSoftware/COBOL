### Finding occurs-clause-p1 — §13.18.38.2 Formats 1-4; §13.18.38.3 SR24 (under-rejects; rows FMT-13.18.38.2)

**Mechanism.** The OCCURS grammar is a SUPERSET parse (bounds, DEPENDING, STEP and the Format 4 phrases are all independently optional and order-free) and the binder never narrows it back to the four general formats outside the report section

**Repro.** WORKING-STORAGE, --std 2023, under out13/probe/occurs-p1/: `05 T PIC X OCCURS 5 DEPENDING ON N.` (fmt_dep_no_to_run: bound as an ODO with minimum 1); `05 T PIC X OCCURS 2 TO 5.` (fmt_to_no_dep_run: silently a FIXED 5 table); `05 T PIC X OCCURS 5 STEP 2.` (fmt_step_ws); `OCCURS DYNAMIC CAPACITY IN C FROM 1 FROM 4` (fmt_f4_dup_run: last wins, C = 4); `OCCURS DYNAMIC TO 5 FROM 1` (fmt_f4_reorder)

**Expected, from the standard.** Each is rejected. §13.18.38.2 Format 2 requires `integer-1 TO integer-2 TIMES DEPENDING ON data-name-1` in full. TO without DEPENDING and STEP exist only in Format 3, the report-writer format, where SR24 still pairs TO with DEPENDING. Format 4 prints each of CAPACITY/FROM/TO/INITIALIZED once, as optional brackets in a fixed order.

**Observed.** All compile clean, with no documented extension in docs/CONFORMANCE.md. The occursStepPhrase grammar comment asserts that 'the binder enforces' STEP's report-only placement; the probe refutes it for a data-division entry.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4#occursClause / #occursDynamicPhrase; src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Odo.cs#OdoBindOccursSpec
