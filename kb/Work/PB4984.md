# PB4984 - real-word misprints in the printed 1985 standard

**Sites:** `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (the Errata 1985 section, written by PB4972), the misprint scan behind it (`scripts/spec/verify_publishable.py#check_corrections`; the scan itself is the tool PB4972 used to find its corrections).

**Report.** PB4972 corrected 19 misprints of the printed 1985 standard (each read on the rendered page) and ended the transcription with a section listing them. The scan that located candidates finds only misprints that
make a NON-word (BEFCRE, fopting, thac and the like). A misprint that makes another real word (for example a wrong-but-valid word in a rule) is invisible to it. Finding those needs a reader: a page-by-page
read of the rendered 1985 text against its sense and against the 2002/2023 counterpart clause. Analysis to decide: what is the cheapest reader that covers the normative clauses (the audit_batch map/route machinery, PB4971, already pairs 1985 clauses with 2023 counterparts and could flag wording differences), and what residual risk the Errata section's completeness claim should state.
