# PB4984 - real-word misprints in the printed 1985 standard

**Sites:** `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (the Errata 1985 section, written by PB4972), the misprint scan behind it (`scripts/spec/verify_publishable.py#check_corrections`; the scan itself is the tool PB4972 used to find its corrections).

**Report.** PB4972 corrected 19 misprints of the printed 1985 standard (each read on the rendered page) and ended the transcription with a section listing them. The scan that located candidates finds only misprints that
make a NON-word (BEFCRE, fopting, thac and the like). A misprint that makes another real word (for example a wrong-but-valid word in a rule) is invisible to it. Finding those needs a reader: a page-by-page
read of the rendered 1985 text against its sense and against the 2002/2023 counterpart clause. Analysis to decide: what is the cheapest reader that covers the normative clauses (the audit_batch map/route machinery, PB4971, already pairs 1985 clauses with 2023 counterparts and could flag wording differences), and what residual risk the Errata section's completeness claim should state.

## Decision (wave 20261937 B)

**Re-probe.** The audit state of the 1985 run (`state-k15`: 2,253 rules, 1,251 SAME pairs verified by a reader that saw both texts) carries no verdict that names a misprint of the 1985 text: the only "typo" it contains is a 2023 Addendum note quoted in a source. The verify prompt never asked the question, so the silence is no evidence of absence.

**Cheapest reader.** The map/verify machinery (`scripts/spec/audit_batch.py`, PB4971) already puts each 1985 rule next to its 2023 counterpart in front of a reader, 1,789 pairs in the SAME and DIFFERS arms. Adding one question to that pass ("does a word of the 1985 sentence read as a misprint of the sense the 2023 sentence carries?") costs no new retrieval and no new corpus. It covers the rules (GR, SR and their kin) only; prose, general-format diagrams and the index are outside the catalog and stay uncovered.

**Residual risk, stated.** The Errata section now says its list is not known complete, why (a real-word misprint is invisible to both finders), and what a user does (check a wording that disagrees with 2023 against the rendered page). The statement is the landed part of this note.

**Still open.** The extra question in the verify pass, its run over the 1,789 pairs, and a read of the prose outside the catalog. The note stays open until that read is run and either lists its findings in the Errata or the section's statement is narrowed to the prose.
