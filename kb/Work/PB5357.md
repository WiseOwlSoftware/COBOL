# PB5357 - no borrowed days; each account follows its day line to its own reset

## The decision, in the owner's words

To the Mythos session, 2026-11-11 evening: "Account 2 now is enabled for Mythos though cannot use it without my explicit
approval, Same as here. I also revoked the account 2 quota borrow, so not it must wait until the days catch up. I am
doing the same here. Usage on both accounts should evenly scale to exhaust the quota the late evening before the quota
reset". Then: "Reconcile all instructions across you and operator with the new quota spend guidelines".

To the operator session the same evening (its resume note): "Reduce the borrow to zero", and "This account now has
Mythos 5.2 access, though you are not to use it without my explicit approval."

## What it means (the Mythos session's reading, told to the owner)

1. No `-BorrowDays` and no `--borrow-days` on either account. `scripts/orchestrator/budget.py` at borrow 0 is the line:
   day N of the account's week allows N × `daily_pct` (14.3 %), stepping at the account's reset hour.
2. An account above its line WAITS until the line passes its reading: it starts no worker, fleet, reconcile run or
   landing, and its controller takes owner prompts and mail that needs a decision. It does not stop for the week.
3. Each account spends its remainder by the late evening before ITS reset (account 1 Sunday 02:00 PST, account 2
   Saturday 09:00 PST), up to the dispatch cap (`weekly_cap_pct`, 97).
4. A Mythos or Fable dispatch on either account needs the owner's explicit approval for that dispatch. The account 1
   controller session itself runs Mythos on his instruction of 2026-11-11 ("only you as coordinator and owner of the
   COBOL project, run as Mythos").
5. A new borrow is the owner's to offer.

It withdraws the whole-week borrow of 2026-10-10 ("100 % of the weekly allowance", loop `-BorrowDays 6`) and replaces
the Mythos session's straight target line of 2026-11-10. What stands from 2026-11-10: use as near to 100 % as possible
on each account just before its reset, and delegate specified Opus-or-lower work to the operator account.

## Where the meters stood

Account 1: 87 % on day 4 (line 57.2 %), `budget.py` at 18:15 PST; its line passes 87 % on Saturday 2026-11-14 02:00 PST.
Account 2: 84 % against a 71.5 % day-5 allowance (the operator's resume note), on hold until 2026-11-12 09:05 PST.
