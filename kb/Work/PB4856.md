# PB4856 - consecutive EVALUATE WHEN phrases over one body are OR-ed into one BoundEvaluateWhen.Match, so a later WHEN's operand that short-circuit never evaluated still runs its debug section

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R3-3 LEAD (medium, not blocking at round 3): `EvaluateBinder.cs:84` R2-4 is only partly fixed for EVALUATE: consecutive WHEN phrases over one body are OR-ed into a single `BoundEvaluateWhen.Match`, so `PhraseOperands` and the flag treat them as one phrase, and an operand of a later WHEN that short-circuit never evaluated still runs its section.

**Not probed.** The clerk did not reproduce it; no program is named.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/EvaluateBinder.cs` (`EvaluateBinder (line 84)`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundEvaluate.cs` (`BoundEvaluateWhen.Match`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundEvaluate.cs` (`BoundEvaluate.PhraseOperands`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/EvaluateEmitter.cs` (`EvaluateEmitter.Emit`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/UsageCollectionPass.cs` (`Visit(BoundEvaluate)`).

## Landed (wave 20261887, group E)

Reproduced by reading the emitter: `EvaluateEmitter` marked ONE flag per WHEN arm around the OR-ed `BoundLogical("||")`, so `WHEN 1 WHEN D`
with K = 1 flagged D evaluated though the OR short-circuited. `BoundEvaluateWhen` now carries `Matches` (one condition per WHEN phrase,
ISO §2023:14.9.13.4 GR4 left to right); `BoundEvaluate.PhraseOperands` flattens them across the statement, the emitter marks each inside its own
phrase and ORs them. Also touched: `UsageCollectionPass.cs` (the `Visit(BoundEvaluate)` walk) and `EvaluateBinder.Bind`. Golden
`pb4856-debug-evaluate-consecutive-when` (accept 85) and the 2002 refusal `pb4856-debug-evaluate-phrase-removed`.

Filed from review leads of waves 20261884 and 20261885 by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.
