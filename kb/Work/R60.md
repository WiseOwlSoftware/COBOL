# R60 — unchecked ref-mod overrun

With EC-BOUND-REF-MOD checking off the standard names no outcome for an out-of-range reference modification. The owner rule of 2026-07-28 is a LOUD ABORT wherever the standard names none. **Owner: option C — terminate** (today every overrun pads with spaces and continues, so `CX(1:10)` over a 5-character item would now terminate instead of printing `ABCDE` plus 5 spaces). Part 1 (out-of-range LITERAL reference modifications are compile-time errors, §14.6.13.1.3 8), as GnuCOBOL does) needed no decision under the 2026-08-30 protocol.

## Re-verified against the ISO text (2026-09-30)

The spec is explicit about the violation and the condition: §8.4.3.3.4 5) c) (cite-checked): "The sum of leftmost-position and length minus the value one shall be less than or equal to the number of positions in the data item", and a position outside the area sets EC-BOUND-REF-MOD, a FATAL exception (Table 13 of exception conditions). What the standard does NOT name is the run-time outcome when that checking is not enabled (a violated `shall` leaves the result undefined). The owner's standing rule of 2026-07-28 — LOUD ABORT wherever the standard names no outcome — therefore decides, and the owner confirmed option C (terminate). Part 1 (an out-of-range LITERAL reference modification is a compile-time error) stands.
