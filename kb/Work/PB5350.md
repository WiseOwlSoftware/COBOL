# PB5350 - verify_publishable.ledger_pages reads a page count as pages 1..N and describes LEDGER-1985.json as recording pages, but every ledger (LEDGER-1985.json, LEDGER.json and what merge_reconciliation writes for 2002 and 2014) carries pages_swept; the self-test proves reconciled(1985) against a planted {pages: N} shape no real file has

**Sites:**

- `scripts/spec/verify_publishable.py` (`ledger_pages`, `reconciled`, `self_test`)

**Finding.** The fallback branch of `ledger_pages` (`set(range(1, int(ledger.get("pages", 0)) + 1))`) serves a shape ("a page-keeping batch ledger ... records how many (`pages`)") that no ledger on disk has: all carry `pages_swept`. The self-test exercises `reconciled(1985)` through the planted `{pages: N}` shape, so the real path (`pages_swept`) is the one the fallback hides if it breaks.

**Fix direction.** Delete the fallback (rule 4: no compatibility layer; every caller reads `pages_swept`), make an absent `pages_swept` fail loudly, and point the self-test at a planted `pages_swept` ledger.

**Source.** Review lead R1-4, info, not blocking at round 1.
