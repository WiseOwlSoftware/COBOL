# PB4913 - two 2023 figures drifted from their generator

**What was seen.** The w20261890a implementer (PB4849) ran the 2023 `sweep_figures.py --check` as a baseline. At eb3b2d135c,
on an untouched copy of HEAD's scripts, it reports 2 drifted figures:
- §2023:14.9.9.2 (the CONTINUE statement's general format; folio 604), `specs/ISO_COBOL_2023.md` lines 23196-23198. PB3751
  corrected this figure note and its underline of CONTINUE by hand.
- §2023:14.9.28.2 Format 3 (folio 682), lines 25776-25801.

So the drawn figure in the transcription no longer equals what `render_figure.build` draws from the figure's source. Either
a hand edit was not carried back to the source, or `build` changed after the figure was written. The check is the drift test
that should keep the two in step. Find out why it did not stop the landing that introduced the drift: is
`sweep_figures.py --check` in the gate's audits, or only run by hand?

**The fix.** For each figure: render the PDF page (`scripts/render-spec-page.py <page>`), decide which side is right, and
correct the other (the source, `build`, or the transcription). Then make `sweep_figures.py --check` part of the tree audits
if it is not, so the next drift fails a gate.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/render_figure.py` (`build`), `scripts/spec/sweep_figures.py` (`main`), `specs/ISO_COBOL_2023.md` (§2023:14.9.9.2 and §2023:14.9.28.2 figures).
