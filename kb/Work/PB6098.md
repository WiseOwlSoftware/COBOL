# PB6098 - the inventory schema's one reader is not the only reader, and rule 7 has no enforcement

Three leads of the review of PB3206 (`w20262139b`) share one mechanism: PB3206 and PB3210 made `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py` the one
reader of `tests/version-matrix/inventory-schema.json`'s vocabulary, and wrote that as section 8.2 rule 7 of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-architecture-review.md`. Re-verified on main ae1136f781:

1. **No second parse of the JSON (R1-2).** `scripts/rename_diagnostic_prefix.py:122` reads `RETIRED_MARK` with its own
   `json.loads((REPO / "tests" / "version-matrix" / "inventory-schema.json").read_text(...))["notes"]["retired-witness-mark"]`, while
   `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py:327` already exposes it as `RETIRED_WITNESS_MARK`. Rule 7 forbids exactly "a second parse of the JSON". PB3206's
   sweep grepped only `["verdicts"]`, so it missed this read.
2. **No hand copy of the split rule (R1-3).** `Schema.split` (`inventory_schema.py:1431`: split on the stripped separator, strip each part, drop
   empties) has an inline second copy in `scripts/spec/normalize_batch.py`: the test-ref loop at line 95
   (`.split(schema.test_ref_sep.strip())`) and the code-location loop at line 123 (`.split(schema.code_location_sep.strip())` plus
   `strip` and `continue`). It is the file PB3210 de-duplicated; every other reader calls `schema.split`.
3. **The rule is not a drift assertion and its sentence is false (R1-1).** `DESIGN-architecture-review.md:970` adds rule 7 to the section 8.2 list
   introduced as rules "each a drift assertion, listed with the measure", yet no assertion or measure enforces it, and its sentence "Each
   reader's self-test plants a schema with a new entry and shows it carried through (`scripts/selftests/spec/normalize_batch/`)" holds only
   for `normalize_batch`: 24 production scripts outside the selftests import `load_schema`, and the `backfill_closes_rows` self-test plants none.

**Sites:**
- `scripts/rename_diagnostic_prefix.py#RETIRED_MARK`
- `scripts/spec/normalize_batch.py#normalize` (the test-ref and code-location loops)
- `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py#Schema.split`
- `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-architecture-review.md` §8.2 rule 7

**Next:** point `RETIRED_MARK` at `inventory_schema.RETIRED_WITNESS_MARK`; call `schema.split` in both loops of `normalize_batch.py` (keep its
commentary-stripping); add the drift test rule 7 promises (a script outside `inventory_schema.py` that opens the schema file, or splits on a schema
separator by hand, fails it) and correct the rule's self-test sentence to what the tests hold, in the same change (CLAUDE.md rules 4, 5, 6).
