# PB6129 - umbrella notes are READY to the planner

**Site.** `scripts/spec/work.py` `waiting_on` (line ~1109) and `cluster_order` (~1124) read `blocked_by` only and ignore notes whose
`cluster` list names the note.

**Repro.** On base 223cd4fab5 (before the PB2119 hold), `python scripts/spec/work.py next --cluster PB1754 --json` lists PB2119 with
`ready: true`, though PB2119 is a member of PB1754 with open members of its own. Wave 20262147 group D was dispatched on exactly this case.

**Stopgap, not the fix.** PB2119's `status: blocked` is the per-note hold (`kb/Work/PB2119/2026-11-23-umbrella-held.md`).

**Fix.** In `waiting_on`, an open note that `cluster_members` shows leading open members waits on them; add a selftest in
`scripts/selftests/spec/work/test_work.py`; check the other umbrella notes (PB1754, PB2108-style leads) the same way.
