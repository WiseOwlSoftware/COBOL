# PB4283 — a bare gated verb sentence is named as a misused paragraph name

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs:462` (`CobolParserCoreBase.procedureHeaderHere`: admits a gated word + period as a paragraph header), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs:117` (`CobolErrorStrategy.ReservedWordUse`: names the word at the failed prediction).

## What happens

Group B's R1-1 (blocking) found that a bare ALWAYS-reserved verb sentence in a paragraph (`STOP.`) was named COBOL0901 as a misused paragraph name; its finisher fixed that arm with `CobolParserCoreBase.IsIncompleteStatement`. The reservation-gated arm, already on main since PB4132 / PB4206, still reproduces: at `--std 2023` a bare gated verb sentence (`FREE.`, `VALIDATE.`, `INVOKE.`) inside a paragraph draws COBOL0901 (the word "used as a procedure-name") where the sentence is an incomplete statement and the ordinary statement diagnosis is the correct one. `procedureHeaderHere` admits a gated word followed by a period as a header, and `ReservedWordUse` then names the word at the failed prediction.

Repro: `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261788b/fin/g1.cob` through `g5.cob` (at `--std 2023`). Changing the predicate alone was tried by the finisher and reverted: the gated word is a legal header at editions where it is not reserved, so the decision must read the edition's reservation state, not the word.

## Fix

One decision for both arms: the header reading refuses a word that begins a statement at the current edition (the grammar's own FIRST of `statement`, edition-filtered), with the incomplete-statement test of the ALWAYS arm as its shared path; no word list. Golden per gated verb at 2023 and the legal header at an edition that does not reserve it (spec: 8.3.2.1 and the reserved-word tables).

## Wave 20261826 group A: re-probed, fix tried and reverted (the note's direction conflicts with PB4206's pinned case)

**Re-probe** (built tree 03ec1b251, `--std 2023`): g1-g5 (`FREE.`, `VALIDATE.`, `ALLOCATE.`, `RAISE.`, `INVOKE.` after
`P0.`) each draw COBOL0901 at the verb. Reproduced.

**What was tried.** One decision, a verb (FIRST(`statement`)) between a period and a period is an incomplete statement,
asked by `procedureHeaderHere` (no paragraph-header reading), by `ReservedProcedureName` (in place of
`IsIncompleteStatement`) and by `CobolErrorStrategy.ReservedWordUse` (no COBOL0901). g1-g5 then drew COBOL0001 "cannot
parse construct near 'FREE'" (the prediction fails at the verb, not at the period as `DISPLAY.` does).

**Why it was reverted.** `ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.AReservedVerbWrittenAsAProcedureName_IsNamedByCobol0901`
(kb/Work PB4206) pins the DEFINED case: a paragraph header `RECEIVE.` (or `SEND.`, `VALIDATE.`) that `PERFORM RECEIVE.`
references draws exactly one COBOL0901 for the definition and the reference together. Its header is the same text as
g1's `FREE.`: from 2002 there are no areas (§2023:6.3.1), so no token tells a header from a bare verb sentence, and the only
evidence that the source USES the word as a name (§2023:8.3.2.1 1)) is a procedure reference elsewhere. With the header
reading refused, the defined case loses its COBOL0901 (13 rows red): the parse fails at the header with COBOL0001 and
the reference's COBOL0901, a bind-time funnel report, never runs. The verb reserved at EVERY edition already answers the
other way (`READ.` is COBOL0001 at the period whether or not a reference exists, branch review R1-1 of PB4229), so the two
arms disagree, and making them agree needs that evidence.

**Fix direction (revised).** Decide the bare `VERB.` sentence by whether the program references a procedure of that name
(the binder knows every procedure reference; the parse does not): with a reference, the header reading and one COBOL0901
(PB4206's rule, for both arms); without one, the incomplete statement's report (R1-1's rule, for both arms). That moves
the decision for the always-reserved arm too, so it is a design change in DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md (the reserved
procedure-name paragraph), not a predicate edit.

## Landed (wave 20261842, group A): the revised direction, both arms

**Re-probe** (built tree, `--std 2023`, `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261788b/fin/g1.cob` through `g5.cob`): `FREE.`, `VALIDATE.`,
`ALLOCATE.`, `RAISE.`, `INVOKE.` each drew COBOL0901. Control probes (`p/a1.cob`..`a4.cob`): `DISPLAY.` with no reference drew
COBOL0001 at the period; `PERFORM DISPLAY.` with the header `DISPLAY.` drew COBOL0901 at the reference AND COBOL0001 at the
header (two diagnostics, the second the wrong one); `PERFORM FREE.` with the header `FREE.` drew the one COBOL0901 (PB4206).

**Fix (one decision, both arms).** A verb that is its sentence's only word (`CobolParserCoreBase.IsBareVerbSentence`: a statement
opener read off the ATN, the period before and after it) is a paragraph header only where the program names the word as a
procedure elsewhere (`NamedAsAProcedureElsewhere`: every other token of the word's type is re-read in its own sentence with the
word retyped `IDENTIFIER`, and a reading that puts it under `procedureName` is a reference; no list of procedure-name
positions). Asked in three places that were three copies of the question: `procedureHeaderHere` (the grammar's predicate, for a
gated word: without the evidence the paragraph alternative is out and the sentence is the statement's), `CobolErrorStrategy.ReservedWordUse`
(returns no violation for such a verb, so the prediction failure reports as the statement's own: `cannot parse construct near 'FREE'`),
and `ReservedProcedureName` (the always-reserved arm: the header readings are asked when the evidence exists, where
`IsIncompleteStatement` had vetoed them unconditionally). With the evidence, the word is named once: `CobolErrorStrategy` keeps the
§2023:8.9 messages given for a reserved word named as a procedure (`_procedureWordsNamed`) and recovers the later sentences silently,
the bound-tree funnel's rule for a gated word (it reports the first use only). After: g1-g5 draw COBOL0001 `cannot parse construct near`
the verb, no COBOL0901; `PERFORM DISPLAY.` + header `DISPLAY.` draws the one COBOL0901; `PERFORM FREE.` + header unchanged.
`IsIncompleteStatement` is renamed `IsBareVerbSentence` (it no longer decides alone), its caller changed in the same change.
**Goldens:** `conformance:cases/8.3.2.1-General/pb4283-bare-gated-verb-sentence-is-an-incomplete-statement` (refused at 2002, 2014 and
2023), `pb4283-bare-verb-header-where-the-word-is-not-reserved` (COBOL-85, VALIDATE. is a header, output A), and
`ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.ABareVerbSentence_IsTheHeaderOnlyWhereAProcedureReferenceNamesIt` (twelve rows: FREE, VALIDATE,
ALLOCATE, RAISE, INVOKE, DISPLAY, READ without a reference; FREE, VALIDATE, RAISE, DISPLAY, READ with one). Design: the
`DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md` reserved-procedure-name paragraph is rewritten.
**Remaining difference between the arms (not a defect).** `FREE.` is reported at the verb (`cannot parse construct near`: the
prediction refuses both alternatives there) and `DISPLAY.` at the period (`unexpected '.'`: the prediction reaches it). Both name the
statement and neither names a misused name.
