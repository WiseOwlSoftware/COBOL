# PB4283 — a bare gated verb sentence is named as a misused paragraph name

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs:462` (`CobolParserCoreBase.procedureHeaderHere`: admits a gated word + period as a paragraph header), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs:117` (`CobolErrorStrategy.ReservedWordUse`: names the word at the failed prediction).

## What happens

Group B's R1-1 (blocking) found that a bare ALWAYS-reserved verb sentence in a paragraph (`STOP.`) was named COBOL0901 as a misused paragraph name; its finisher fixed that arm with `CobolParserCoreBase.IsIncompleteStatement`. The reservation-gated arm, already on main since PB4132 / PB4206, still reproduces: at `--std 2023` a bare gated verb sentence (`FREE.`, `VALIDATE.`, `INVOKE.`) inside a paragraph draws COBOL0901 (the word "used as a procedure-name") where the sentence is an incomplete statement and the ordinary statement diagnosis is the correct one. `procedureHeaderHere` admits a gated word followed by a period as a header, and `ReservedWordUse` then names the word at the failed prediction.

Repro: `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261788b/fin/g1.cob` through `g5.cob` (at `--std 2023`). Changing the predicate alone was tried by the finisher and reverted: the gated word is a legal header at editions where it is not reserved, so the decision must read the edition's reservation state, not the word.

## Fix

One decision for both arms: the header reading refuses a word that begins a statement at the current edition (the grammar's own FIRST of `statement`, edition-filtered), with the incomplete-statement test of the ALWAYS arm as its shared path; no word list. Golden per gated verb at 2023 and the legal header at an edition that does not reserve it (spec: 8.3.2.1 and the reserved-word tables).
