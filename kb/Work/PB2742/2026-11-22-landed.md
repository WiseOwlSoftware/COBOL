# PB2742, 2026-11-22: MODULE-NAME keyword is one type (wave 20262139 group D)

`ModuleNameKeyword {Current, Activating, Nested, Stack, TopLevel}` (runtime) is bound once by `IntrinsicBinder.BindModuleName`,
carried as `BoundIntrinsicCall.ModuleNameKeyword` (null for every other function; the renderer throws on a MODULE-NAME call without
one), emitted as `CobolModule.Name(ModuleNameKeyword.X)` by `RuntimeApi.ModuleNameFn`, and switched on by `ModuleStack.Name`, whose
default arm now throws `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` instead of answering a single space. Behavior-neutral: the
`15.65.4-Returned-value-rules/module_name` golden prints its `.out` unchanged on the branch build; the emitted text names the member
instead of the integer.
