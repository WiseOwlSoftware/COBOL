## Why it is unimplemented, and what makes it hard

PB528's determination made character-1 TRANSPARENT to the precedence walk, because applying §13.18.40.6's
sentence literally also rejects `PIC L9999.99F` (Annex D.24) and `PIC LL EDITING "L" IS ":"` (SR12 a) — both of
which the standard admits. So the open question is whether a WEAKER form holds (the 'es' confined to the
row/column of the LEADING or TRAILING currency symbol, but not measured against another 'es'), and SR26's
singular "optionally preceded by character-1" is likewise a reference to the syntactic term rather than a
demonstrated count — no rule of the clause states a bound. Row 26's notes record that reading; this note exists
so the next adjudicator does not re-derive it from scratch and so the question has an owner.
