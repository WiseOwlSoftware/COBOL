# PB5177 - at --std 85 PERFORM VARYING ... BY index-name compiles and augments by the index's occurrence number: the BY slot takes an index-name at every edition

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ControlFlowBinder.PerformVarying.cs` (`BindVaryingOperand`, `Classify`, `CheckVaryingOperandRules`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:VI.6.21.2 prints BY identifier-4 or literal-2 (identifier-7 or literal-4 in AFTER), with no index-name, while VARYING and FROM take an index-name. `ControlFlowBinder.PerformVarying` binds the BY operand through the same classifier as the other slots (`BindVaryingOperand`, `Classify`, `CheckVaryingOperandRules`) and admits an index-name there at every edition, so `PERFORM ... VARYING IX FROM 1 BY IX2` compiles at --std 85 and augments by the index's occurrence number.

**Expected (from the 1985 text, the record's).** A diagnostic at the PERFORM: Format 4 of §1985:VI.6.21.2 prints the BY operand as a brace over identifier-4 and literal-2 only, while its FROM brace lists index-name-2 as a separate alternative, and §1985:IV.2.1.4: "When braces, { } , enclose a portion of a general format, one of the options contained within the braces must be either explicitly specified or implicitly selected." An index-name is not an identifier: §1985:III.2 defines an identifier as "A syntactically correct combination of character-strings and separators that names a data item." and an index-name as "A user-defined word that names an index associated with a specific table."

**Observed (the record's).** exit 0, no diagnostic: the PERFORM compiles at --std 85 (and at --std 2023) and the program displays A1, A3, A5 (the augment is the index's occurrence number, 2).

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Give the classifier the slot it is binding (VARYING, FROM, BY) and the edition, so the BY slot refuses an index-name below the edition that admits it; one table of slot x operand kind x edition, read by CheckVaryingOperandRules.
