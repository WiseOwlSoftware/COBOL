## Landed (wave 20261788, group C)

**Re-probe** on the branch's own build of 11e8949be: `01 AUTO TYPEDEF PIC X(4). 01 PT TYPEDEF USAGE POINTER AUTO.` and
the same with each of the 13 words, and with PROGRAM-POINTER, each drew `COBOL1941: cannot parse construct near 'AUTO'.
'AUTO' is not a clause of the data description entry for 'PT'` (26 of 26). After the fix each POINTER spelling compiles
clean, and each PROGRAM-POINTER spelling draws the COBOL1958 its TO-full twin draws (the probe's operand names a type,
not a program prototype): the word is now read as the operand.

**Fix.** `pointerOperandHere` asks `KeywordContinuesFrom(ruleToStopState[Context.RuleIndex])`: is the lookahead word a
keyword reading here (`IsKeywordReadingHere`, §8.9 status or a §8.10 word) AND can it be matched as a keyword of what
follows the pointer rule in THIS parse, walked through the live rule-invocation stack (`KeywordFollows`). It is the
question `keywordContinuesHere` already asked at an operand list's end, now one method both predicates call; the
context-free `UsageFollowSet` forwarder is deleted with its only product caller (PB4211). The rule's stop state is
the operand slot's exit because the optional operand is each pointer rule's last element, which keeps the answer the
same during prediction (State = the block decision) and in the inline predicate check (State = the predicate's
state); `PointerUsageOperandDriftTests.EveryGuardedRule_EndsInItsOptionalOperand` holds that shape. A declared word
§8.9 leaves free at the edition is now the operand, as in every operand list (the PB805 determination).

**Witnesses.** `conformance-suite/cases/13.18.60-USAGE/pb4235_pointer_operand_screen_word` (accept 2002: POINTER AUTO,
POINTER SECURE and PROGRAM-POINTER BELL each equal to their TO-full twins through §14.9.39.3 SR19/SR22 and a CALL
through the program-pointer) and `pb4235-pointer-operand-screen-word-below-2002` (refused at 85 by the POINTER
introduction gate). `PointerUsageOperandDriftTests` derives each entry's OWN follow of a usage keyword (data, report
group, screen) along its rule chain and asserts, for every word `cobolWord` admits, that the TO-less operand is bound
exactly when the word does not follow (the data entry at all four editions). FMT-13.18.60.2 re-verdicted CONFORMS
with these witnesses (batch `w20261788c-PB4235`); the old per-word theory is retired from the row.
