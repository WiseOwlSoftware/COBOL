## Repro — measured

## The rule
`OK  §10.7.3` — "2. Program-name-1 shall be identical to the program-name declared in a preceding PROGRAM-ID paragraph."
`OK  §10.7.3` — "8. Program-prototype-name-1 shall be identical to the program-prototype-name declared in the corresponding
PROGRAM-ID paragraph." Rules 4–7 and 9 state the same for END CLASS, END METHOD, END INTERFACE, END FUNCTION (user
function and function prototype).

## Code site
The end-marker contexts (`endProgramHeader` and its siblings in the grammar) are parsed and read by nothing in the binder.

## What the fixer owes
ONE check over all seven end-marker kinds (not seven): the nesting walk that pairs an ID paragraph with its end marker
compares the names (case-insensitively — they are COBOL words) and reports the rule of the marker's kind. Rule 5's
second sentence (a PROPERTY method's END METHOD omits the name) is part of the same check. Negative goldens per kind at
the edition that introduced it; rules 1 and 3 (presence and order) are not claimed here.
