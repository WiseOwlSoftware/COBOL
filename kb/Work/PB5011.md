# PB5011 - a job cancelled at its time bound on a slow runner is UNCLASSIFIED, not RUNNER

**Found.** PR #1358 (PB4954's branch) left the merge queue: merge-group run 42517655924 concluded cancelled, and the
landing lane read it UNCLASSIFIED because the cancelled job uploaded no TRX. The finisher found a degraded runner, not
the branch: only job "Windows shard 2/7" (128844217660, runner "GitHub Actions 1000031642") failed, its step "Unit, this
shard" cancelled at 20:18:31 by `timeout-minutes: 20` (`.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml`, windows-shard; PB4324
set the bound). Every step before the tests ran 3 to 5 times slower than shard 5/7 of the same run on the same tree
(checkout 64 s vs 17 s, toolchain 98 s vs 41 s, NuGet restore 53 s vs 16 s, Release build 559 s vs 109 s). The Unit step
had run 6 m 48 s when the bound fired; green runs take 1 m 52 s to 1 m 57 s, so at that slowdown it needed 8 to 10
minutes. The other six Windows shards and every Linux job were green. Class (e), RUNNER (CLAUDE.md, the merge-group
rule); the finisher re-armed the branch at its unchanged head.

**Mechanism.** `land_lane.classify_red` (the PB4271 classifier) returns UNCLASSIFIED when the run's results cannot be
read (`RedFacts.unobserved`), and a job that the bound cancelled uploads no TRX, so a cancelled job reads as unobserved.
A cancelled run is a different fact from an unreadable one, and the job's own step timings are readable.

**Owed.** A job cancelled at its bound whose pre-test steps ran at least twice the median of the same step on its
sibling shards is RUNNER: re-queued once automatically like INFRA, with the runner name and the slowdown recorded toward
PB1957's class-(e) rate. A cancelled job whose pre-test steps ran at the median (a test hang) stays a finisher's. Needs
the job's step timings in `RedFacts` and a self-test arm for each of the two.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` (`RedFacts`, `classify_red`)
