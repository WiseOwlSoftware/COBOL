# PB3956 — PB3867 does not record why the local gates passed a fuzz_ollama self-test that fails about 1 run in 6 on Linux (the CI invariant)

**Sites:** `kb/Work/PB3867.md` (the missing record, as a dated paragraph), `scripts/build-local.ps1` and `scripts/linux-gate.sh` (which legs run the audits' self-tests)

PB3867 fixed the racy stub of `fuzz_ollama.py --self-test` (a shared `threading.Event`; 7 of 40 isolated WSL runs RED
before, 0 of 40 after). CLAUDE.md's CI invariant says a CI red is ALSO a defect in the local gates, to be found and fixed in
the same change, and PB3867 records neither why the local gates (Windows leg 1 plus the targeted Linux half) passed it, nor
the sweep for siblings. Candidate causes the review did not check: the audits' self-tests do not run in leg 1 of a gate that
does not touch the harness; the Windows scheduler hides the race (Windows PASSED); the Linux half runs the self-test once.

## Acceptance
- Find which local leg ran, or did not run, `fuzz_ollama.py --self-test` and why it passed; extend
  `kb/Work/PB1957.md`'s survey (the drift test that maps every CI job to a local leg) if a leg is missing; sweep the other
  `--self-test` threads-and-events harnesses in `scripts/` for the same unsynchronized flag.
