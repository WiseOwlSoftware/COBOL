# PB5340 - two landed MCS arms have no witness: PURGE over a destination named in two DESTINATION TABLE occurrences (PB5288's DestinationKey) and the status-40 error key 5 of ENABLE/DISABLE OUTPUT (the AlreadyKey arm of SetErrorKeys)

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Communication/MessageControlSession.cs` (`Purge`, `SetErrorKeys`)

- **R1-1 (MessageControlSession.cs:162, `Purge`).** `Purge` keys each DESTINATION TABLE occurrence with `DestinationKey(area, i)` (PB5288), but no committed test drives a PURGE over a destination named in two occurrences; the duplicate-destination golden (1985-GR-XIV_3_6_4-6-duplicate-destination) exercises SEND only.
- **R1-2 (MessageControlSession.cs:99, `SetErrorKeys`).** The status-40 error-key path of ENABLE/DISABLE OUTPUT writes 5 for a destination already in the requested state, but the GR22 golden (1985-GR-XIV_2_2_4-22-refused-statement) covers only 0 and 1 (D3 disabled under ENABLE, D4 enabled under DISABLE), so the `AlreadyKey` arm of the lambda has no witness.

**Fix direction.** One golden each: PURGE over a twice-named destination (expected: both occurrences purged by their own key), and ENABLE OUTPUT of an enabled destination with its error key 5 (and DISABLE likewise), expected values from §1985:XIV.2.2.4 GR22 and Table 2 of the same clause, checked with `cite.py` before the golden is written.
