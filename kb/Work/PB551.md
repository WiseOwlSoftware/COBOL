## Mechanism — value-clause-p1

### Expected, from the standard
`01 CR CONSTANT RECORD. 05 X PIC 9 VALUE 1. 88 F VALUE 1.` is nonconforming -- the level-88 carries a format-3 VALUE clause in an entry subordinate to a CONSTANT RECORD entry -- and shall draw a compile-time diagnostic; likewise at any depth below the CONSTANT RECORD entry. The FORMAT-1 VALUEs that give the record its content stay legal (they are what 13.18.15 is for), and formats 4 and 5 are unreachable here (a report group cannot be subordinate to a working-storage / local-storage CONSTANT RECORD, and format 5 belongs to the owner-declined A.4.14 VALIDATE module, kb/Work PB283).
