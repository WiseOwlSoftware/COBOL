still took `CobolContiguousLayout`'s `FixedTotal`, `FixedAt`, `Unit`, `MaxUnits`, `_structure` and `_spaceUnit` as loose

CobolContiguousLayout(...).Decompose(record)`, the same rule with no extents, structure or space units. The comments and
docs that named the old members (GroupImageCodec, FILES_DESIGN, D-FRA) name the layout's.
