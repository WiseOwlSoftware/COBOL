# PB5451 - the 2023 mint (PB479 step 3)

**Sites:**
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog.json` (regenerated)
- `tests/version-matrix/inventory/` (the split re-stamps of 57 parent records, the carved rows' records, the first-pass records)

**What.** On PB5450's branch: regenerate the 2023 catalog with the dispositions; carve each level-0 glued paragraph
out of its parent through `record_verdicts.py split` (57 parents, 121 paragraphs, among them `SR-14.8.2.2-2`'s

words; 293 of the 1,774 paragraphs measured at design time) takes that record's verdict, code location and test as a
candidate, recorded only where the refuter upholds that the test exercises this paragraph. PB5319's two FMT fields are
fixed in the same regeneration, so FMT digests move once (7 rows' text grows).

**Oracle.** The view (`inventory_view.py --check`), the split's own text check, the refuter's answers, `cite.py
--check` on every new row's citation.

record and no STALE change record, kb/Work PB479's two named clauses have rows (`SR-14.8.2.2-p<n>` for the
variable-length sentence, a §2023:14.8.3.2 row) held by a drift test that fires when the lift is reverted, the 2023
GAP is reported before and after with the commands, and kb/Work PB479 and PB284's carried residue are updated.
