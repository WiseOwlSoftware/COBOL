# PB5451 - the 2023 mint (PB479 step 3)

**Sites:**
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog.json` (regenerated)
- `specs/ISO_COBOL_2023.md` (only the transcription repairs PB5450 recorded: an unmarked NOTE, example or format line)
- `tests/version-matrix/inventory/` (the parents' recheck records and re-stamps)
- `tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/` (the `next.digests` re-stamps of the 67 records naming a parent and of the about 42 records naming a PB5319 FMT row; the reasons quoting `§2023:8.8.4.2.1 GR13` and the other moved text re-pointed)
- every stored citation of a parent whose quotation moves (76 designator citations across `src/`, `tests/`, `docs/`, `scripts/` at design time, `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/pb479/review/parents.py`), held by the citation audit
- `scripts/spec/extract_annex_a2.py` (`resolve`: an A.2 item resolves to `p<n>`, `T<t>.<r>` and numbered-item rows through its disposition; item 11)
- `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (`extract`, `FMT_LABEL`; PB5319's format count and the 4,000-character cut)

**Readers held green, changed only if the regeneration breaks them:** `scripts/spec/audit_annex_a1.py`,
`scripts/spec/lineage.py`, `scripts/rendered_views.py`, `DerivedVerdictDriftTests`, `EcRaiseSiteDriftTests` and
`EcRaiseSite` in `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/` (it matches catalog text uniquely, so a raise site whose text
moves into a carved row must still match exactly one row).

**What.** On PB5450's branch: regenerate the 2023 catalog with the dispositions (every new row open, no record); carve
each level-0 glued paragraph that has a disposition out of its parent through `record_verdicts.py split` (121
paragraphs in 57 parents at design time: 55 CONFORMS with a `test-ref`, 1 CONFORMS on the derivation of `RV-15.4.1-4`,
1 DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT). The carved rows get NO record and no derivation: they are adjudicated in PB5455. Each parent
keeps its id; its digest is re-stamped only when a recheck of its REMAINING text (the three questions of section 10.3 of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md`) and a refuter uphold its verdict and `test-ref` (for
`RV-15.4.1-4`, its derivation's argument); a parent that fails is opened and goes to PB5455. With the carve, `rule_book`
reads the new extents, so the "it lies in GR13" and "it lies in SR2" answers go (kb/Work PB222's glued form), and every
stored citation and 1985 reason that quoted moved text is re-pointed to the carved row in this landing. PB5319's two FMT
fields are fixed in the same regeneration: seven FMT rows' digests move (`FMT-14.9.39.2` is exactly 4,000 characters
today), and the 1985 change records naming them (`FMT-10.6.1` 12, `FMT-12.4.5.1` 17, `FMT-12.3.7.2` 3, `FMT-14.9.12.2` 3,
`FMT-14.9.28.2` 4, `FMT-14.9.39.2` 3) are re-stamped, each whose `reason` quotes the restored tail sent to adjudication.

**Oracle.** The view (`inventory_view.py --check`), the split's own text check, the recheck and refuter records,
`cite.py --check` on every new row's citation, `audit_code_citations.py`.

**Done when** the regenerated catalog matches the dispositions; `inventory_view.py --check` passes with no stale record
and no STALE change record; `audit_code_citations.py` passes; `cite.py --check "§2023:8.8.4.2.1" "A relation condition
shall contain at least one reference to an operand that is not a literal"` names the carved row, not GR13; kb/Work
PB479's two named clauses have rows (`SR-14.8.2.2-p<n>` for the variable-length sentence, a §2023:14.8.3.2 row) held by
a drift test that fires when the lift is reverted; the 2023 rule count and GAP are reported before and after with the
commands (every minted row open, so GAP rises by exactly the minted count plus the parents opened); and kb/Work PB479
and PB284's carried residue are updated.
