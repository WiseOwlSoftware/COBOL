# PB5154 - CCVS CM102M times out at 120 s on GitHub's Windows runners: it waits 90 s on its own clock by design, and the one-unit budget left 30 s for load

**Sites:** `tests/_shared/ObservationBudget.cs` (`For`, `OneProcessWaiting`, `OwnWaitMs`, `ToString`: the fix),
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/CompilerUnderTest.cs` (`CutRunner.Run`, `CutRunner.RunExit`: the budget is passed
through), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/CommunicationCcvsTests.cs` (`Run`, `CM102M_ReportsNoFailure`: CM102M states
its wait), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/ProcessObservationDriftTests.cs` (`TheBudget_AddsAStatedOwnWait_OnceOnTopOfTheUnits`).

## The symptom

`CommunicationCcvsTests.CM102M_ReportsNoFailure` was the only red of the merge group twice in one day, both times in
"Windows shard 3/7": `HarnessNonObservationException`, both attempts `TimedOut` at 120 000 ms, 31.5 s and 31.3 s of the
process's own processor time, the 4-processor runner 13-15 % busy (run 42841304077, PR #1465; run 42835167709, PR #1463,
which later merged unchanged). The same test passed at the bounced branch's local gate in 91.6 s
(`000003-20261110T143350Z`, Conformance.trx, `duration="00:01:31.63"`).

## The root cause

It is not a hang. CM102M is specified to wait: `DELAY-FOR-30-SECS` spins on `ACCEPT SYSTEM-TIME FROM TIME` until 30
seconds have passed since the last SEND, `MSG-BEFORE-DELAY-AND-DISABLE` falls into it once and
`DELAY-DISABLE-DELAY-AND-STOP` performs it twice (tests/nist/programs/CM102M.cob lines 729-779), so every run spends 90 s
on its own clock whatever the host does. The harness budget (`ObservationBudget`, kb/Work PB3108) gives one program one
unit of 120 s on the premise that the work is computation and I/O which a load stretches, a multiple of 13 to 32 over
the measured cost. For CM102M the multiple was 120 / 90 = 1.3: the remaining 30 s had to hold .NET start-up and more than
500 durable MCS statements (each SEND a paged-store landing under the store mutex, with its device-file write). The CPU
time is consistent with that: about 30 s is one delay's spin, and the rest of the attempt was not computing, which points
at the runner's storage (not measured on the runner; the run directory was not kept). The MCS directory is the test's own scratch directory (`CutRunner.RunExit` sets
`WISEOWL_COBOL_MCS`), so no other test contends for its store lock.

## The fix

The budget rule gains its missing term: a process that waits on its own clock by design states the wait, which is ADDED
to its units and never multiplied (no load lengthens or shortens it): `ObservationBudget.OneProcessWaiting(wait, work)`,
`For(..., ownWait)`, the report naming the wait. `CutRunner.Run`/`RunExit` take the budget, and CM102M's test states its
3 x 30 s. Its budget is now 210 s, of which 120 s is the unit's load margin over the work that is not waiting. Sweep: the
only CCVS programs with a delay are CM101M, CM102M and CM202M (`grep -liE "DELAY|PAUSE OF" tests/nist/programs/*.cob`),
and CM101M and CM202M are compiled only; no other test runs CM102M. DESIGN-test-build-ci.md's observer row states the
term.
