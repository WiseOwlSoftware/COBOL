## Defect

`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` step 7 reserves "three per group" codes (`per` is the one rules value
`wave.codes_per_group`, read in `plan_main`; its inner `group_json` hands group k the range `first + k·per`). Nothing reads how many codes a group's notes need. The rendered spec
(`templates/dispatch-spec-implementer.md` line 47) then says codes come ONLY from that range.

**Witness.** Wave 20261819 group A (lead PB2103, run wf_0a800ee7-313) returned SPLIT at 296e68ed3, gated GREEN, with
COBOL5914-5916 unused. Its report (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w20261819a-PB2103-report.md`) says PB2103 part 2
and PB2104 need about eleven codes (`MetadataRepositoryProvider`'s REPO-1..-5 and -7..-12, reported together), and
that this is the second wave in a row sent with three. Each such wave spends an Opus implementer's setup on a group
it cannot finish.

## Fix direction

Let a note declare its need (for example a `codes_needed:` frontmatter field, or a count the planner derives from
the design doc the note names) and have `plan_wave.py` reserve the sum for the group, with a drift test that a
group's reserved range is at least what its notes declare. Alternatively, plan the REPO slice as its own group with
its declared count, starting at `Binding/RepositoryResolver.cs#RepositoryResolver`, as the implementer suggested.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`plan_main`)

Both waves sent PB2103 with three codes and got it back unstarted: wave 20261821 group A (the planner lead of
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w20261821a-PB2103-report.md`) and wave 20261823 group B (`w20261823b-PB2103-report.md`,

allocation = max(`codes_per_group`, the sum of its notes' `codes_needed`), a `work.py check` rule for the field's shape, and
a planner test. Sites it names: `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py:1161-1168` (`group_json`) and
`scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json:265` (`codes_per_group`: 3). Repro it gives:
`python scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py --cluster PB2293 --dry-run` on a tree where PB2103 is open and unblocked shows the
group with three codes. Until fixed, hold PB2103 and PB2104 out of automatic planning or dispatch PB2103 by hand with eleven codes.
