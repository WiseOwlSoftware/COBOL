# PB5026 - REMARKS accepted at --std 85

**Found.** Wave 20261935 group C (PB4996). `remarks-removed-2002` accepts the REMARKS paragraph at `--std 85`, but ANSI X3.23-1985
deleted it (section 1985:A, item 8), so the 85 compiler under-rejects. It answers item 2 of PB1802 (landed), which asked whether
1985 still had REMARKS. `scripts/spec/edition_gates.py` carries it as a `HELD_OPEN` entry that names PB4996, the tooling slice,
as owner; no note owned the behaviour change until this one.

**Repro.** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261935c/remarks85.cob` (`REMARKS. ...` after PROGRAM-ID) at `--std 85` compiles and runs
(`RAN`); it should be refused with the edition named.

**Owed.** The row's removal window is set from the 1985 chain, the 85 compiler refuses the paragraph with its edition,
`edition_gates.HELD_OPEN` names this note, and the sibling comment paragraphs (AUTHOR, INSTALLATION, DATE-WRITTEN,
DATE-COMPILED, SECURITY) are checked against the same 1985 rules.

**Sites:** `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (`remarks-removed-85`, formerly `remarks-removed-2002`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ConstructRegistry.g.cs` and `Constructs.g.cs` (regenerated, `Constructs.RemarksRemoved85`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4:425` (`remarksParagraph`, its comment),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`ParseArm.VisitRemarksParagraph`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Cst/ClauseCardinality.cs` (the identification-division row's comment),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/DivisionCursor.cs` (the class comment),
`scripts/spec/edition_gates.py` (`HELD_OPEN`: the entry is deleted, since the audit finds nothing at an empty window).

**Re-probe (wave 20261946 A, base c865ee6dc3).** The repro at `--std 85` printed `RAN`; at `--std 2002` it drew
COBOL0902 "removed in COBOL-2002". Reproduced.

**Verdict.** The 1985 text has no REMARKS paragraph anywhere: §1985:VI.3.2.1 "defines the order of presentation in the
source program", and its general format §1985:VI.3.2.1.1 prints PROGRAM-ID, AUTHOR, INSTALLATION, DATE-WRITTEN,
DATE-COMPILED and SECURITY only; the one mention of REMARKS is the history of COBOL, §1985:A.2.5 item 8, which records
the CODASYL Journal of Development 1968 deleting it (not X3.23-1985, as the finding first said). No NIST CCVS-85
program writes the paragraph (the only matches are a procedure paragraph `REMARKS-TEST` and a literal), so the
"accepted at 85 for CCVS" carve-out protected nothing. The ISO 2002 and 2023 texts carry no REMARKS at all. The five
sibling paragraphs are in the 1985 figure, so `identification-comments-removed-2002` (85 to 2002) is right.

**Fix.** The row is `remarks-removed-85`: `introducedIn` 85 and `removedIn` 85, an empty window that refuses the
paragraph at every edition with COBOL0902 ("removed in COBOL-85") and warns under `--permissive`, where a migrated
program keeps compiling. The grammar keeps recognizing the paragraph so the refusal names it rather than a parse error.
`edition_gates.HELD_OPEN` loses its entry: a window that starts and ends at the oldest held edition has no edge inside
any pair, and a held entry that finds nothing is itself a finding. Goldens: `remarks-paragraph` moved to
`cases/11.2-Identification-division-structure/` and refused at all four editions with the message;
`pb1758-fixed-remarks-at-2023` refused at 85 too. The version matrix's `remarks-removed-85` row runs the refusal and the
permissive warning at every edition. Two goldens wrote an IDENTIFICATION DIVISION REMARKS at 85:
`wiseowl/.../pb3245_free_comment_entry_is_commentary` (its no-period leg moved to SECURITY) and
`pb4487_library_text_copied_twice_is_decided_per_inclusion`, now a refusal at all four editions (one COBOL0902, at the
first inclusion); its per-inclusion mechanism stays held by `CommentEntryResolverTests`' PB4487 facts.

  window (`introducedIn` = `removedIn` = 85), so `ConstructRegistry.Check` refuses it everywhere and `--permissive`
  warns. Regenerate with `scripts/gen-constructs.ps1`; never hand-edit the `.g.cs`.
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs#ParseArm.VisitRemarksParagraph` is the one gate site.
- `scripts/spec/edition_gates.py#HELD_OPEN`: an empty window has no edge inside a pair, so its held entry goes stale
  and must be deleted.
- Trap: a grep for `REMARKS.` misses goldens that write `REMARKS` with no period or through a library text.
