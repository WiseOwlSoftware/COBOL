# PB5531 - the 1985 rule catalog files a list's closing paragraph under its last item: 1985-GR-IV.4.4.2-L2.5 absorbs the 'program flow of control is undefined' sentence, so GRL2.1 and GRL2.2 cannot close on undefined-in-rule

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (`extract`, `ordinal_shape`: where a list item's text ends), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json` (regenerated)

**Mechanism.** The 1985 extraction does not end a list at the paragraph that closes it. `1985-GR-IV.4.4.2-L2.5` ("The end program header.") carries "When there is no next executable statement and control is not transferred outside the COBOL program, the program flow of control is undefined unless ...": `cite.py --check "§1985:IV.4.4.2" "When there is no next executable statement and control is not transferred outside the COBOL program"` finds the text and names the designator GRL2.5, so the sentence is filed under item 5. `1985-GR-IV.4.4.2-3` likewise absorbs "An explicit transfer of control consists of ...". Effect: L2.1 and L2.2 cannot close on the `undefined-in-rule` arm (it would have to quote another row's text, which the checker refuses), and L2.5's row and digest cover a rule that is not item 5. PB5373's dated file `kb/Work/PB5373/2026-11-14-class-1-three-rows.md` records the two rows moved out of class 1 and calls this "a defect of the 1985 extraction, reported by this wave as a lead"; no note held it.

**Fix direction.** End the list item at the closing paragraph (its own unnumbered rule or a paragraph row), regenerate the 1985 catalog, and re-record L2.1 and L2.2 through `record_verdicts.py --edition 1985`; a catalog self-test that no list item's text contains a sentence beginning at a lower indent keeps the next case automatic.
