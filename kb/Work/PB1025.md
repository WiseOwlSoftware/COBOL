# PB1025 — LANDED

## What the fixer owes
One per-record key-window computation (offset = sum of the preceding members' CURRENT lengths) shared by the SORT/MERGE
key extractor and the indexed connector's key slicer; then lift SortBinder's by-name refusal. Goldens: a variable-length
record whose key follows a DYNAMIC LENGTH item, sorted and written to an indexed file, with records of two different
dynamic lengths.

## Landed (2026-09-22, wave 55 finisher, worktree wf_aaa1a00f-895-3)
Re-probed on this worktree's build before the fix: `w55b/s1.cob` (SD, key after `PIC X DYNAMIC LENGTH LIMIT 10`)
was refused COBOLNET1757 by name; `w55b/x1.cob` (INDEXED, RECORD KEY after the same item) compiled and

minimum is 2. Neither indexed rule was enforced anywhere, and SR6 g) fired only when a RECORD clause wrote
integer-2 and measured the FIXED-run offset.

  now on every variable-length file, `FileModel.VaryMin`), two new `FileControlKeyRules` rows for SR4 / SR5
  (COBOLNET0863) and `KeyedIoEmitter.KeyWindow`. The by-name refusal and `FirstVariableOffset` are deleted.
