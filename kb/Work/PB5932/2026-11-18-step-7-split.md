## Decision: step 7 is split; the grammar's name slots land as step 7a, the occurrences wait for step 6b as step 7b

Step 7 of `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-listing.md` was dispatched (w20262104h, branch `listing-step-7`) while step 6b, which this
note orders before step 7, had not been built. Step 7 holds two bodies of work:

- (a) the grammar half: `GrammarNameSlotDriftTests`, written first and red, the `NameSlotFixture.g4` self-test, and a
  classifying label on every name slot of the grammar. It reads the generated parser and the grammar only; it changes no
  parse and touches nothing step 6b restructures (the binder's name tables and lookups).
- (b) the occurrences: `Identify` appends each `SymbolOccurrence` to its entry, `PlaceBuilder` takes `IdentifiedName`,
  `StatementBinder.BindStatement` classifies through `BoundStores.StorePositionsOf`, `ReferenceOccurrenceDriftTests` (the
  completeness oracle and the pairing test), then `CrossReferenceBuilder`, the PERFORM and CALL graphs, `ListingLine.Names`
  and the statement-line margin. "`Identify` appends" is the one `SymbolTable.Identify` step 6b makes the only way from a
  written name to a symbol; recording at today's many resolve paths instead is what rounds 1 and 2 of the design review
  rejected (a list of paths was incomplete twice), and it would be undone by 6b.

**Decided:** (a) is step 7a, built now on `listing-step-7`; (b) is step 7b, built after step 6b lands. Building (a) first costs
nothing later: 6b does not touch the grammar, and 7b's oracle reads the labels 7a writes.

**Why not build 6b first on this branch:** 6b is the binder-wide restructuring this note measures (65 tables, 194 members,
294 comparison sites) with its own differential; folding it into a step dispatched as "the cross-reference" would hide a
landing of its own inside another (rule 5 asks for the restructuring, in its own step).

**Why not wait:** the grammar half is independent, it is the part of step 7 the design orders first, and the owner's order of
2026-11-18 ("build the full listing support") asks for every step now.

The design's section 11 records the split ("Step 7a", "Step 7b").
