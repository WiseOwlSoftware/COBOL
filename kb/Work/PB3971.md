# PB3971 — rearm_hold and drop_stale_rearms disagree for a worktree-less LANDED row

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` (`rearm_hold`, `rearm_effect`, `drop_stale_rearms`). The LANDED arm of
`rearm_hold` and `drop_stale_rearms`' LANDED drop land with PB3609 (PR #703); at the PB3609 tip the cited line is about
1223. On origin/main `rearm_hold` is at line 1203 and has no LANDED drop yet.

Filed 2026-10-27 by the enqueue unit's clerk. Checked first: PB3772 (open) owns the queue watch ending too early and lists
`rearm_hold` only as a site; PB3628 (landed) introduced the one predicate. Neither owns this ordering, so this is a new note.

## The defect

`rearm_hold` tests the new LANDED arm AFTER the no-worktree arm, but `drop_stale_rearms` drops a LANDED re-arm whatever
its worktree. For a worktree-less LANDED row, `rearm_effect` (the status line) says the survey names no worktree and the
re-arm waits for one, while the next tick drops the re-arm: the opposite promise. This is the drift `rearm_hold`'s
docstring says the one predicate prevents (PB3628 R1-1: a hand-kept copy dropped the worktree arm).

## To do

One predicate: order the arms so `rearm_hold` answers exactly what `drop_stale_rearms` does (or have `drop_stale_rearms`
ask `rearm_hold`), with a self-test arm for a worktree-less LANDED row seen RED first.
