# PB5913 - a large PICTURE costs about 30 seconds of compile time

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs` (`TryExpandRepeats` builds the expanded string; what walks it after is the lead, not yet located)

**Measured (2026-11-17, Debug build, `cobol check-batch`, one entry each, at --std 85).**

    01 U.
       05 F PIC X(67108864) OCCURS 2.      main 29.6 s   branch 29.6 s
    01 T.
       05 E PIC X OCCURS 134217728.        main  0.5 s   branch  0.6 s

Both items are 134,217,728 character positions (the limit of D-ITEM-SIZE, so both are legal). The cost follows the PICTURE's
expansion, not the item's size: building a 64 M-character string is well under a second, so something walks the expanded
character-string (or a per-position structure) many times. A legal elementary item at the limit costs a compile half a minute.

**Expected.** Check time independent of a repetition factor's magnitude (the character-string's symbols are few; a factor is
a count). Measure first (a stack sample of the check), then fix the mechanism.

**Probes.** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/my-20261115/w20262102/b/p/edge-pic.cob`, `edge-occ.cob`.
