# PB931 — OPEN — an audit whose granularity is coarser than the defect it hunts

Source: the `PB412` report, "New defects" item 1. This IS PB402 §4's prescription, correctly sized as its
own design item.

## The measurement

`scripts/spec/audit_grammar_optional_words.py`'s own docstring states the limitation: it matches at
CONSTRUCT granularity and "drops any word that is underlined ANYWHERE in the construct's formats".
`EXCEPTION` is underlined in the `EXCEPTION exception-name-1` arm of the very brace whose `LAST EXCEPTION`
arm leaves it plain — so the word is dropped from the audit for the whole construct, and the `LAST
EXCEPTION` arm can never be examined. **PB402** and its SEND sibling were both invisible to it, and the
defect RECURRED BY COPY after **PB407** fixed one site.

## The rule

```
$ python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 5.2.3 "They are shown in uppercase and not underlined in general formats"
OK  §5.2.3   (Optional words)
    Optional words are reserved words or context-sensitive words. They are shown in uppercase and not
    underlined in general formats. They may be written to add clarity …
```

§5.2.3 makes underlining a PER-OCCURRENCE property of a printed word. The audit reads it as a per-word,
per-construct property, and the gap between those two readings is the defect class.

## Why this is a design item and not a patch

Per-OCCURRENCE granularity needs printed-word-position → grammar-token-position alignment, which the
script's docstring says it cannot do, and it needs the `specs-private` PDF, which is ABSENT in fresh
worktrees (so any such audit must degrade to a recorded SKIP rather than a false green —
`feedback_verdict_evidence_invariant`: a missing observation is not a negative one). That is a real design,
and sizing it as one is the point of this note.

Interim cover, already landed by the PB412 group: the `LAST EXCEPTION` drift fact. It covers one figure.
