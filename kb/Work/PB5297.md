# PB5297 - at --std 85 DEBUG-SUB-n written as a subscript or reference-modifier position outside a debugging section compiles: ReferencesDebugItem walks the statement's operand places and not their subscript and reference-modifier expressions

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/StatementBinder.cs` (`ReferencesDebugItem`, `BindStatement`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:XV.3.2.3 SR11: "References to the special register DEBUG-ITEM are restricted to references from within a debugging section." `BindStatement` refuses (COBOL6442) a statement outside a debugging section whose operand places reference DEBUG-ITEM, but `ReferencesDebugItem` does not look inside subscripts or reference modifiers, so `MOVE "A" TO TE (DEBUG-SUB-1)` compiles, and `W (DEBUG-SUB-1:1)` compiles and aborts EC-BOUND-REF-MOD at run time. kb/Work PB4858's landing paragraph says the subscript form is refused; its witness covers only a direct operand.
