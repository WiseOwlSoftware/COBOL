# PB2769 — medium effort for the mechanical inventory roles

**Owner decision, 2026-10-08 (kb/Work PB468 Q6):** agents doing mechanical inventory work run at medium reasoning
effort; the judgment-bearing roles keep full effort, and the overturn rate of the medium-effort roles is measured. The
saving and the quality cost were unmeasured when the question was answered (the learnings ledger's C86 is UNPROVEN).

## What closes it

1. Find where each mechanical role is dispatched (agent definitions under `.claude/agents/`, Workflow `agent()` options,
   the workstream templates) and set `effort: medium` there for: evidence-dossier assembly, the link-checking pass,
   validation-output triage, and the FMT/DOC verdict routes. Verdict derivation, witness authoring and the independent
   blind replicate keep full effort; say so next to each setting.
2. Measure: the overturn rate of each medium-effort role's outputs (a later refuter, validator or replicate reversing
   them) against its rate before the change, recorded per run (telemetry or the learnings ledger), so a quality drop is
   seen, and the role returns to full effort if its overturn rate rises.
3. Record the policy in `.claude/skills/workstream/SKILL.md` where the effort rules live.
