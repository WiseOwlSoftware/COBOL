# PB5382 - lines a REPLACE or COPY REPLACING introduces on a debugging line do not stay debugging lines: only the first introduced line is hidden without WITH DEBUGGING MODE, and a literal continued onto a debugging line compiles instead of being an error

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ApplyReplaceStatements` and `#ApplyReplacements` (the replacement takes the origin of the line its match began on)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/DebuggingLineRewriter.cs#DebugLines` (hides or keeps only the tokens on the D carrier's own line)

**Mechanism.** Replacement works on logical text, and each replacement token takes the origin of the line where its match began. `DebuggingLineRewriter.DebugLines` decides by the carrier line, so when pseudo-text-2 spans several lines, only the tokens that land on the D line are treated as debugging text. The 1985 rule gives every introduced line the indicator of the line where the replaced text begins, and makes a literal that would have to continue onto a debugging line an error. Neither holds. kb/Work PB5214 (open) is a COPY statement written on a debugging line; PB5212 (open) is debugging lines in the wrong place; neither covers introduced lines.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids below. Its row is recorded PARTIAL naming this note.

**Rules (1 claimed; every citation checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `1985-GR-XII.3.4-11` - `§1985:XII.3.4 GR11` (read: PARTIAL, harm: wrong answer and under-rejects): "the indicator area of the introduced lines contains the same character as the line on which the text being replaced begins", and "If replacement requires the continued literal to be continued on a debugging line, the program is in error." The COPY sibling, outside this row: `§1985:XII.2.4 GR11` ("the indicator area of the introduced line contains the same character as the line on which the text being replaced begins") and `§1985:XII.2.4 GR9` ("If replacement requires that the continued literal be continued on a debugging line, the program is in error").

**Repro.** In `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/out-reread2/work-rr-04/`: `p11b.cob` (a 100-character continued literal replacing XLIT on a D line, under WITH DEBUGGING MODE), `p11g.cob` (a multi-line pseudo-text-2 of DISPLAY statements replacing a word on a D line, without the mode), `p11h.cob` (`ADD 1 TO WS-N` / `ADD 2 TO WS-N` replacing a word on a D line, without the mode), and the COPY REPLACING siblings `p11f.cob` (with `CPYD.cpy`) and `p11i.cob` (with `CPYE`, `CPYD` and `CPYR`); `cobol.exe --std 85 --source-format fixed --run <file>` beside its copybooks.

**Observed (the pinned compiler of `E:/COBOL-wt/coderead85-tree-b` (origin/main 8147f008b3; `git diff --stat 8147f008b3 origin/main -- src` is empty at 5237495315), rerun 2026-11-12; `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/reprobe-85f/log.txt` and `log2.txt`).** p11b compiles and prints the 100-character literal. p11g prints `THREE` through `SEVEN` and `END`. p11h prints `3`. p11f compiles and prints the literal; p11i prints `3`.

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** p11b and p11f are refused (the continued literal would continue on a debugging line). p11g prints only `END`. p11h and p11i print `1`.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Carry the debugging-line indicator on every token a replacement introduces (the indicator of the line where the replaced text began), so `DebuggingLineRewriter` sees introduced lines as debugging lines; report the continued-literal error where the replacement is made. One mechanism serves REPLACE and COPY REPLACING (R56 keeps the rewriter as the one decider).
