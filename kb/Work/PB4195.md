samples show it alone (most slots held: 1). A and B2 rebuilt at `f49f3e7e4` in a detached worktree (B2: the suite
collapsed to one class, Meziantou.Xunit.ParallelTestFramework 2.3.0, `parallelAlgorithm: aggressive`), cache off,

## Determination (2026-10-31): keep the partition classes

B2's gain on a quiet host is 2.6 %, inside the noise, so the bar is not met and nothing is adopted; the shared host's
12 % was contention. The quiet leg's 133 s against about 200 s on the shared host says the gates beside a leg cost it far
more than its class structure, which is PB4181's question. DESIGN-test-build-ci.md section 3.11.1 carries the series and
the determination; PB4150's record points here. No package lands.
