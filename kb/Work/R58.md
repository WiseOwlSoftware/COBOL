# R58 — END-INVOKE

ISO §14.9.23.2's INVOKE format ends at `[RETURNING identifier-4]`; Table 12 gives INVOKE no conditional phrase and no explicit scope terminator; `END-INVOKE` is not in §8.9 (PB758 measured zero occurrences in the standard and the PDF). GnuCOBOL 3.2 has no END-INVOKE. **Owner: remove it as non-COBOL.** The optional `END_INVOKE?` in the `invokeStatement` rule and the lexer token are deleted (END-INVOKE is already a user word since PB1689); the three goldens that pin the terminator (`pb1689_vendor_invoke_terminator_still_parses`, `pb1689_vendor_invoke_using_returning_terminator_still_parses`) are changed to negatives.

## Re-verified against the ISO text (2026-09-30)

§14.9.23.2's INVOKE general format ends at `[ RETURNING identifier-4 ]` and §14.5.3.2 / Table 12 give INVOKE no explicit scope terminator; END-INVOKE is in no ISO word list (§8.9) and appears nowhere in the standard (PB758 measured 0 hits in the transcription and the PDF). §4.2.10 permits nonstandard extensions, which is the only thing that made "keep it" an option; under the project rule "follow the ISO specification where it explicitly addresses the issue" the terminator is removed. This was presented to the owner as a question in error.
