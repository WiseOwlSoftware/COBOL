# PB4285 — one family: a reading that ends at a context-free FOLLOW token is taken for a reading of the sentence

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs` (`ReadingParses`, `ReadStatementAt`, `StatementReading`, `FollowSet`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`NextStatementBegins`, `NameReach`, `BeginsTheNextStatement`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/ReadingParsesFollowDriftTests.cs`.

## What happens

Three leads, one mechanism: `ReadingParses` accepts a speculative reading that ends at any token in the context-free FOLLOW of the rule. For `statement` that is every phrase or terminator of any statement, the same context-free-union shape PB4235 fixed for usage.

1. R3-1 (medium, from the review of PB4208's branch at round 3). R2-1 lets a CutShort reading end mid-sentence, at any `FollowSet(statement)` token. `NextStatementBegins` still weighs it only against `NameCarriesTheSentence`, whose stated premise (a CutShort statement ran to the end of the sentence, so a name reading that breaks inside it went less far) no longer holds. `MOVE W-X TO RECEIVE W-Y` / `DISPLAY W-Z UPON.` now names the shorter statement reading and loses the DISPLAY's fault (it was COBOL0901 plus `unexpected '.'`). The doc comments of `NextStatementBegins` ("cut short by the sentence end") and `NameCarriesTheSentence` are stale against the new rule (CLAUDE.md rule 6), and PB4210 (DESIGN-frontend-grammar prose) describes the older semantics.
2. PB4235 lead (low, group C; no failing repro built yet). `ReadingParses` is used by `LookaheadBeginsAStatementThatParses` in reserved-word error recovery; a reading that ends at a phrase token of another statement (`TO`, `UPON`, `GIVING`) is a context-free accept. Build the repro first (a statement refused at a phrase word that only a different verb owns).
3. PB4208 lead 2. `MOVE W-X TO` then `ADD 1 FROM W-Y.` stays one COBOL0901 ADD: `ADD 1` is a complete prefix, so `ReadStatementAt` reads None (the refusal at `FROM` is not in FOLLOW), and the misused name is kept. Same root as R1-1 and R2-1 of PB4208.

## Fix

Re-derive what "reading carries the sentence" means from the live context, not the rule's context-free FOLLOW: the accepted end token must be one the grammar allows after THIS statement in the enclosing production, or the reading is None/prefix. Restructure `StatementReading` and `NextStatementBegins` so the next case is automatic, and pin it with a drift test over the FOLLOW tokens (PB4208's `ReadingParsesFollowDriftTests` is the starting point). Correct the stale docs of both members in the same change. Re-probe the repros in `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261788d/` and the three above after the fix. Coordinate with PB4208 (open) and take its branch first.

## Item 1 landed (wave 20261815 A, 2026-11-01); items 2 and 3 stay open

Item 1 (R3-1, `MOVE W-X TO RECEIVE W-Y` / `DISPLAY W-Z UPON.`) is fixed on the PB4208/PB4207 branch's review finisher branch, not by the premise this note
proposed (re-deriving the accepted end token from the live context) but by the rule the design doc already stated: of two readings of the word the one
that goes further is the one the report names. `ReadStatementAt` returns how many tokens a CutShort reading took, `OffendingPosition.NameReach` how many the
name reading takes, and the statement reading wins only when strictly further (PB4207's Landed section, round 3; golden
`pb4207-name-carries-the-sentence-past-the-next-verb`). The stale doc comments of `NextStatementBegins` and `NameCarriesTheSentence` (the member is deleted)
and the PB4210 prose are corrected in the same change. Item 3 is the same function: the statement reading of `ADD 1 FROM W-Y` is refused at `FROM`, a token
no statement is followed by, so it reads None and its two tokens are not weighed against the name reading's one; weighing every refusal with two tokens or
more the same way is the shape to try, probed against the `GOBACK`/`VALIDATE` prefix rows of `ReadingParsesFollowDriftTests` first. Item 2 still has no
repro. Take `status` to landed when both are closed or discharged.

## Item 3 lead (wave 20261815 A review, 2026-11-02)

The PB4208 finisher re-confirmed item 3 on its build: `MOVE W-X TO` / `ADD 1 FROM W-Y.` stays one COBOL0901 at the ADD. The statement reading `ADD 1` is refused at `FROM` (no statement is followed by it), reads None, and its two tokens are never weighed against the name reading's one. Shape to try: weigh every refusal of two tokens or more as `OffendingPosition.NameReach` weighs a CutShort reading; probe the `GOBACK` / `VALIDATE` prefix rows of `ReadingParsesFollowDriftTests` first. Site `CobolParserCoreBase.ReadStatementAt` / `ReadingParses`. Item 3 is not started.
