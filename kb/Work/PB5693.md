# PB5693 - the `examined` field and the tool-written difference trigger

**Sites:** none yet (the analysis names the sites: today `scripts/spec/lineage_reads.py#normal_read`,
`scripts/spec/audit_batch.py#import_reads` and the screen's trigger table `scripts/spec/data/lineage-screen-triggers-2002.json`
are where a read is normalised, imported and flagged).

**The failure.** Rule A is word-identical in 2002 and 2023 and its relation was decided as is by a read. A later read of
rule B finds that a term A also uses, or the clause that governs A, differs between the editions. Nothing today records
that finding as a trigger of the lineage screen, so A's relation stays green and its row keeps a verdict shared across
the difference (review R2-6). The screen flags only what a trigger row says, and a trigger row is written by hand.

**What exists.** A read record (`tests/version-matrix/lineage-reads-2002/<slice>.json`) holds `clause-2002`,
`clause-2023`, `checked` (categories such as `terms`, `references`), `citations`, `flags-answered` and
`answer-citations`; no field names the definitions and governing clauses the reader relied on. 69 slices; the 704 stored
2002 KEPT records rest on them.

**To decide.**
1. The writer step: the tool that records a read's finding of a definition or governing-clause difference writes the
   screen trigger in the same call, so every relation the trigger flags turns red until re-read (PB5679 Q5).
2. The `examined` field of a read record (per edition, checked citations of each definition and clause relied on), and
   which relations a difference trigger reaches through it.
3. The 704 existing reads: back-fill `examined` (by a re-read or by extraction from their `citations` and
   `flags-answered`), or treat a read with no `examined` as reached by every difference trigger of its pair until it has
   one. The choice states its cost and what it protects.

**Rows.** None claimed.
