# PB1231 — OPEN

## Measured

**constant-entry** — F-FORWARD-REF — the constant table is filled in declaration order, so every reference that PRECEDES an entry (to a constant, or from LENGTH OF to a later data-name) is rejected

- **Repro:** (a) 01 K CONSTANT AS LENGTH OF W. 01 W PIC X(7). DISPLAY K.  (b) 01 W PIC X(K). 01 K CONSTANT AS 3.  (c) 01 K CONSTANT AS K2 + 1. 01 K2 CONSTANT AS 3.
- **Expected (spec):** (a) K = 7; (b) W is X(3); (c) K = 4. GR1/GR3 state no ordering condition, and SR4 ('The length of data-name-1 or data-name-2 shall not be dependent, directly or indirectly, upon the value of constant-name-1') and SR5 ('... directly or indirectly ...') are only violable if an entry may reference a LATER declaration — their existence presumes forward references are otherwise legal. Only the circular shapes are to be rejected, naming SR4/SR5. (Reasoning-based: no rule states the order explicitly; flag for owner confirmation if contested.)
- **Observed:** (a) COBOLNET1547 'the data-name is not defined at this point (a constant entry reads only PRECEDING declarations; ISO §13.10.3 SR4 rules out the reverse dependence)'; (b) COBOLNET2147 repetition factor '(K)' not an unsigned integer; (c) COBOLNET1547 'previously defined numeric constant-names'. The diagnostic in (a) cites SR4 for a program that has no dependence at all.
- **Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Constants.cs#BindConstantLength; #ResolveConstantName; #ExpandPicConstants
- **Clause (adjudicator):** §13.10.4 GR1/GR3/GR6; §13.10.3 SR4/SR5

## Progress (wave 1006 C, 2026-10-03) — re-probed, not taken; the design for the next slice

**Re-probed on the w1006c build (after PB1227/PB1232 landed beside it), all three still reproduce:** (a) `01 K CONSTANT AS
LENGTH OF W. 01 W PIC X(7).` → COBOLNET1547 "not defined at this point"; (b) `01 W PIC X(K). 01 K CONSTANT AS 3.` →
COBOLNET2147 "(K) … not an unsigned nonzero integer"; (c) `01 K CONSTANT AS K2 + 1. 01 K2 CONSTANT AS 3.` → COBOLNET1547
"previously defined numeric constant-names". The same order rule now also refuses `BYTE-LENGTH OF` a later item and a
later constant as a LENGTH OF subscript (PB1227 and PB1232 route both phrases through `BindConstantLength`).

**Why it was not taken in group C.** It is a second mechanism (the binder's ORDER of constant evaluation), not part of
the FROM/BYTE-LENGTH/subscript fixes, and the dispatch's sizing rule says SPLIT at the note boundary. The spec reading
stands: §13.10.3 SR4 and SR5 forbid only dependence on constant-name-1 "directly or indirectly". Both rules can only be
broken if an entry may refer to a later declaration, and no clause of §13.10 or §8.4 orders the references (a search of
`specs/ISO_COBOL.md` for constant-name with previous/preced/before/follow finds only §13.18.40.3 SR6's
repetition-length sentence).

**The design (one mechanism, every reader).**
1. *Declare before binding.* At the top of `DataBinder.Bind`, collect EVERY constant entry of the unit into a
   pending table, name → entries: the `constantEntryBody` of each `dataDescriptionEntry` in every section, plus the
   report section's `constantEntry` (`DataBinder.Reports.cs:1106`). Keep the entry's own position too, because SR9's
   duplicate check compares specifications.
2. *One lookup, which binds on demand.* Every read of `_constants` goes through ONE `ResolveConstant(name)`:
   `FindConstant`, `ConstantOf`, `IsIntegerConstant`, `ResolveConstantName`, `OccursBoundValue`, `ExpandPicConstants`
   and `LiteralEnvironment.Constant`. Today each of them reads the dictionary itself. A name that is declared but not
   yet bound is bound THEN, through `BindConstantEntry`. An in-progress stack makes a re-entry a cycle, reported as
   §13.10.3 SR5 (literal or expression dependence) or SR4 (a length that depends on the constant) with the cycle
   named. The entry's own in-order bind then finds it already bound and does nothing; record the bound entry
   contexts so SR9 does not compare an entry with itself.
3. *LENGTH OF / BYTE-LENGTH OF a later data item.* This is the hard leg: the item has no `DataItem` until its record
   binds. Two shapes were weighed:
   (i) DEFER: a length constant whose operand is declared later (a pre-scan of declared data-names answers that)
   stays pending, and binds when its operand's level-01 record completes. A reader that asks before that point can
   only be another entry that precedes the operand. If the operand's record is the one being bound now, that is the
   SR4 cycle (`01 W PIC X(K). 01 K CONSTANT AS LENGTH OF W.`). Otherwise it is a legal order this shape cannot
   serve: `01 A PIC X(K). 01 K CONSTANT AS LENGTH OF W. 01 W PIC X(7).`
   (ii) ORDER the level-01 records of each section by their constant dependencies, bind them in that order, and
   restore source order in `_roots`. This serves every legal program, but it reorders the binder's walk
   (REDEFINES "immediately preceding", the level stack, uid seeding), and it needs its own drift test that the
   emitted order is the source order.
   Recommendation: (ii) is the complete feature (CLAUDE.md rule 3). (i) is acceptable only as the first half of it,
   and only with the residual case reported loudly by name, never as "not defined".
4. *Goldens.* (a)/(b)/(c) above as one 2002 positive (K = 7, W is X(3), K = 4). Add the cross-record case
   `01 A PIC X(K). 01 K CONSTANT AS LENGTH OF W. 01 W PIC X(7).` (A is X(7)). Add negatives for the direct and the
   indirect SR5 cycle and for the SR4 cycle.
5. *Sweep.* `LiteralEnvironment.Constant` (ConcatFolder's constant arms) also serves SPECIAL-NAMES literal positions. Whether
   those bind before the data division, which would make every constant a forward reference there, was NOT probed in
   this slice: probe it (`CURRENCY SIGN IS K`, `SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS`, `CLASS … IS K` with K a constant) and route it
   through the same lookup. PB780 owns whether SR2 admits each of those positions; this note owns the order.
