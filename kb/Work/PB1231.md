# PB1231 — OPEN

## Measured

**constant-entry** — F-FORWARD-REF — the constant table is filled in declaration order, so every reference that PRECEDES an entry (to a constant, or from LENGTH OF to a later data-name) is rejected

- **Repro:** (a) 01 K CONSTANT AS LENGTH OF W. 01 W PIC X(7). DISPLAY K.  (b) 01 W PIC X(K). 01 K CONSTANT AS 3.  (c) 01 K CONSTANT AS K2 + 1. 01 K2 CONSTANT AS 3.
- **Expected (spec):** (a) K = 7; (b) W is X(3); (c) K = 4. GR1/GR3 state no ordering condition, and SR4 ('The length of data-name-1 or data-name-2 shall not be dependent, directly or indirectly, upon the value of constant-name-1') and SR5 ('... directly or indirectly ...') are only violable if an entry may reference a LATER declaration — their existence presumes forward references are otherwise legal. Only the circular shapes are to be rejected, naming SR4/SR5. (Reasoning-based: no rule states the order explicitly; flag for owner confirmation if contested.)
- **Observed:** (a) COBOLNET1547 'the data-name is not defined at this point (a constant entry reads only PRECEDING declarations; ISO §13.10.3 SR4 rules out the reverse dependence)'; (b) COBOLNET2147 repetition factor '(K)' not an unsigned integer; (c) COBOLNET1547 'previously defined numeric constant-names'. The diagnostic in (a) cites SR4 for a program that has no dependence at all.
- **Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Constants.cs#BindConstantLength; #ResolveConstantName; #ExpandPicConstants
- **Clause (adjudicator):** §13.10.4 GR1/GR3/GR6; §13.10.3 SR4/SR5
