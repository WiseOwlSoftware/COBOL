# PB5568 - a section with no paragraphs is never a debugging subject: USE FOR DEBUGGING ON an empty section generates C# that does not compile (CS0103 __dbgItem), and ALL PROCEDURES skips it

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs` (`ProcedureTableBuilder.AddDebugSectionSubject`, `ProcedureTableBuilder.AddNamedDebugSubject`, `ProcedureTableBuilder.AddAllProceduresSubjects`, `ProcedureTableBuilder.FinalizeDebug`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/DispatchEmitter.cs` (`DispatchEmitter.EmitDebugRunner`)

**Mechanism.** A section may hold no paragraph: "A section consists of a section header followed by zero, one, or more successive paragraphs". `ProcedureTableBuilder.AddDebugSectionSubject` records a section subject at the section's first pc; `AddAllProceduresSubjects` calls it only for a section whose `StartPc` is the pc of a paragraph it walks, and `AddNamedDebugSubject` returns early on `named.IsSection && named.Range.IsEmpty` with the comment "a section of no paragraphs is never executed: no trigger point". That premise is false: PERFORM S2 and the fall-through into S2 execute the (empty) procedure S2, and S2 is a procedure-name, so the empty section is never recorded as a subject. With the section named in USE FOR DEBUGGING ON, the declarative is still emitted with its DEBUG-ITEM references, and `DispatchEmitter.EmitDebugRunner` never declares the `__dbgItem` they name: the backend refuses the generated C# (a crash on legal source, no COBOL diagnostic). Under ALL PROCEDURES the empty section silently runs nothing: neither PERFORM of it nor the fall-through into it runs the debugging section (a wrong result). An empty PARAGRAPH subject works (`d3_emptypara.cob`), so the gap is the section with no paragraph alone. One mechanism for both symptoms: an empty section needs its own pc as a subject.

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** `d16_empty.cob` compiles and runs the debugging section for S0 at the start of the program, then prints `P1`. `d3_emptymid.cob` compiles and runs S2's debugging section for the PERFORM and again for the fall-through. `d4_emptymid.cob` adds a line for S2 at the PERFORM S2 (line 17) and one for the fall-through from P0 into S2 (line 18), before S3's.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Give an empty section a pc of its own in the procedure table (where control lands when it is performed or entered), so `AddDebugSectionSubject` records it like any section and the runner declares its DEBUG-ITEM; sweep the other consumers of a section's first-paragraph pc (PERFORM, GO TO, ALTER, the segment-entry machinery) for the same empty-section gap.
