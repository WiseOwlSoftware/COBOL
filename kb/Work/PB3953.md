# PB3953 — an arithmetic expression wider than 38 significant digits inside a function argument keeps about 38 digits where GnuCOBOL's GMP intermediate is exact

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/FunctionValueField.cs:65` (`FunctionValueField.OfValue`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs` (`IntrinsicRenderer.RawArg`, the intake that receives the lowered expression), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs` (`NumericRenderer.Settle`, `NumericRenderer.LowerWide`, where the exact form is lowered; read, not changed), `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (DOC-A.1-123), `conformance-suite/cases/15.4.1-Numeric-and-integer-functions/pb3953_function_argument_past_int128.cob`

`ABS(BIG * BIG * BIG)` with 18-digit items differs from cobc in its DIGITS: ours ends `...100000000000000000000`, cobc's
exact result ends `...2999999999999999999`. The standard-decimal intermediate keeps 34 digits and the value 38, where
cobc's GMP is exact. The report calls it intermediate-precision latitude (a nested product past 77 digits is already a
documented determination, `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` DOC-A.1-123); it is filed because the PB3514 review found it recorded in
prose only. Probe: `scratchpad/q` `e.cob` of the PB3514 implementer.

## Acceptance
- Adjudicate whether the determination in DOC-A.1-123 covers an expression inside a function argument; either record the
  case in that row or make the argument exact to the 77-digit lane the non-argument arm already has.

## Resolution (adjudicated, 2026-10-27)
Re-probed on a fresh build: `FUNCTION ABS (BIG * BIG * BIG)` of three 18-digit items still shows
`999999999999999997000000000000000100000000000000000000` (the exact product ends `...0002999999999999999999`). The
boundary is the `Int128` window, not 34 digits: a 36-digit or a 38-digit argument expression is exact
(`ABS (BIG * BIG * K2)`, `K2 PIC 99`), a 39-digit one is the SDIDI image (`ABS (BIG * BIG * K3)` ends `...100000` for
`...000999`), and `FUNCTION MOD (BIG * BIG * K3, 1000)` answers 0 from the image where the exact residue is 999.

The determination: an argument expression is a receiver-less operand. `NumericRenderer.Settle` lowers the exact 256-bit
form to the SDIDI by round-to-odd, and `ExactWideSettlementDriftTests` closes the list of consumers that take it exact
at the arithmetic statements and the relation. The standard fixes no width under native arithmetic (§8.8.1.3, §15.4.1),
and under STANDARD-DECIMAL the 34-digit SDIDI is the value. DOC-A.1-123 now records it, names its departure from the
precedence order (GnuCOBOL is exact), and cites the witness `pb3953_function_argument_past_int128` (its leg 5 shows the
same product exact in a COMPUTE) with the negative `pb3953_function_argument_below_2014`.

Making the argument exact is a change to the function family (a wide carrier for every body, and for the returned value
of ABS, MAX, MIN and MEDIAN), not to one expression, so it is not taken here. A successor reopens it by adding the
functions to the closed list in `ExactWideSettlementDriftTests` and giving each body a `CobolWide` form.
