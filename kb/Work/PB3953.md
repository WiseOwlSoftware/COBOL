`ABS(BIG * BIG * BIG)` with 18-digit items differs from cobc in its DIGITS: ours ends `...100000000000000000000`, cobc's
exact result ends `...2999999999999999999`. The standard-decimal intermediate keeps 34 digits and the value 38, where
cobc's GMP is exact. The report calls it intermediate-precision latitude (a nested product past 77 digits is already a
documented determination, `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` DOC-A.1-123); it is filed because the PB3514 review found it recorded in
prose only. Probe: `scratchpad/q` `e.cob` of the PB3514 implementer.

## Acceptance
- Under native arithmetic an arithmetic expression used as a function argument is exact to the 77-digit lane the non-argument
  arm already has (GnuCOBOL's argument temporary is arbitrary-precision, CLAUDE.md rule 1 for an implementor latitude,
  §8.8.1.3 and §15.4.1): `FUNCTION ABS (BIG * BIG * K3)` ends `...000999` and `FUNCTION MOD (BIG * BIG * K3, 1000)` is 999.
  The family added to the closed list in `ExactWideSettlementDriftTests`, each body given a `CobolWide` form, and ABS, MAX,
  MIN and MEDIAN a wide returned value (`FunctionValueField` carries a `CobolDec` today). Its golden legs are the 39-digit
  ones, added to `pb3953_function_argument_int128_window` (the window's own legs are in it already). STANDARD-DECIMAL is
  already right (the 34-digit SDIDI is the value, §8.8.1.5.2).

## Findings so far (2026-10-27, NOT a resolution; the note stays open)
Re-probed on a fresh build: `FUNCTION ABS (BIG * BIG * BIG)` of three 18-digit items still shows
`999999999999999997000000000000000100000000000000000000` (the exact product ends `...0002999999999999999999`). The
boundary is the `Int128` window, not 34 digits: a 36-digit or a 38-digit argument expression is exact
(`ABS (BIG * BIG * K2)`, `K2 PIC 99`), a 39-digit one is the SDIDI image (`ABS (BIG * BIG * K3)` ends `...100000` for
`...000999`), and `FUNCTION MOD (BIG * BIG * K3, 1000)` answers 0 from the image where the exact residue is 999.

Mechanism: an argument expression is a receiver-less operand. `NumericRenderer.Settle` lowers the exact 256-bit form to the
SDIDI by round-to-odd, and `ExactWideSettlementDriftTests` closes the list of consumers that take it exact at the
arithmetic statements and the relation. No function body has a wide carrier, and ABS, MAX, MIN and MEDIAN would also need a
wide returned value. The standard fixes no width under native arithmetic (§8.8.1.3, §15.4.1), so no clause is violated, but
for a latitude CLAUDE.md rule 1 makes GnuCOBOL's behaviour the answer and GnuCOBOL is exact. A first adjudication recorded
the limit as a determination and closed this note; review round 1 refused that (R1-1: no owner decision departs from the
precedence order, and closing the note dropped a silent wrong answer from `work.py next`). It is open again, and
DOC-A.1-123 now states the limit as a known departure that this note tracks. The review finisher weighed the exact carrier
(a new wide form for about fifteen bodies and a wide returned value, with the `ExactWideSettlementDriftTests` list and the
`Intrinsic*DriftTests` contracts) as a redesign beyond a review round, which is why it is a note and not a fix (I9).

Landed with it: the exact-window golden `pb3953_function_argument_int128_window` (38-digit ABS and MOD, and the COMPUTE that
keeps every digit of the 39-digit product) with the negative `pb3953_function_argument_below_2014` (ABS is a 2014 function).
No golden pins the 39-digit image as conforming.

## Landed (wave 20261758, group A)
Re-probed on the branch's own build before the fix: `ABS (BIG * BIG * K3)` showed `998999999999999998002000000000000100000`,
`MOD (BIG * BIG * K3, 1000)` 000000000, `ABS (BIG * BIG * BIG)` ended `…100000000000000000000`, and `MOVE FUNCTION INTEGER
(BIG * BIG * K3) TO R` stored `…0000000100000`. After it: `…000999`, 000000999, `…0002999999999999999999` (GnuCOBOL's), and
`+9999999998002000000000000000999`.

The fix is the shape the acceptance named, built so the next exact function is automatic. `IntrinsicRenderer.RawWideArg` keeps
the exact wide form at the argument intake (a §15.3 type-6 position takes its integer on that form, `CobolIntrinsics.IntegerArgWide`);
`RawArg` lowers it for every consumer with no wide form. `IntrinsicRenderer.RenderWide`, consulted under native arithmetic before
the SDIDI arm, evaluates ABS, SIGN, INTEGER, INTEGER-PART, FRACTION-PART, MOD, REM, MAX, MIN, ORD-MAX, ORD-MIN, SUM, RANGE,
MEDIAN and MIDRANGE on `CobolWide` (`CobolIntrinsics.Wide.cs`) when the arguments' digit bounds prove the work fits 77 digits,
and returns a `NumX.Wide` that `NumericRenderer` settles like a nested product. Three consumers of a function's value that used to
lower it now take it exact too: DISPLAY (`FunctionDeviceText`, `FunctionValueField.OfWide`, whose MAX/MIN/MEDIAN refine the
34-digit images' order by the exact value), MOVE (`NumericRenderer.TransferWide`, the transfer `Settle` itself takes) and the
string-image channel (`OperandText.NumericExprText`, `CobolNum.FormatWideFunctionText`: a MOVE to an alphanumeric item, STRING).
`ExactWideSettlementDriftTests` registers the four new settling sites and holds the family closed
(`EveryExactArmTakingANumericArgument_HasAWideBody`). Golden legs: the 39-digit ABS, MOD, MAX and MOVE added to
`pb3953_function_argument_int128_window`; unit witness `CobolWideIntrinsicsTests`. Still on the image, and OPEN WRONG ANSWERS
rather than determinations: MEAN (it double-rounds: the image, then the SDIDI quotient) and the binary64 family (SIN of a 39-digit
product reads an argument about 999 radians away). Both share this note's root, an argument lowered at intake, and are filed from
the report (w20261758a-PB3953-report.md, "New defects").

Branch review round 1 (R1-1) found `FunctionValueField.Compare` ordering a wide temporary against a NON-wide one by the wide value's
34-digit image whenever the images differed. A non-wide temporary holds up to 38-39 EXACT digits (an Int128 value `OfWide` hands to
`OfValue`), off the 34-digit grid, so with `A = X * Y + C` just under `Int128.MaxValue` and `A + 1000` just past it,
`MAX (A, A + 1000)` showed A, `MIN` showed A + 1000 and `MEDIAN (A + 1000, A, A + 2000)` showed A + 2000. `Compare` now orders any
pair holding a wide value by the exact values (`CobolWide.Compare`); an SDIDI value past the 256-bit range, the one temporary with
no exact wide form, is larger than every wide value and its sign decides. Witnesses: golden legs 8-11 and
`CobolWideIntrinsicsTests.AWideTemporary_OrdersExactlyAgainstANarrowOneOffTheImageGrid`.

## Landing (for the next implementer)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs#RawWideArg` — the ONE numeric-argument intake (type-6 integrality on every carrier, the wide form kept); `RawArg` is its lowering. Read an argument through one of the two, never `num.AsNum` directly, or a wide argument loses its digits.
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs#RenderWide` — a new exact-family arm adds its `"Name" =>` body here (`ExactWideSettlementDriftTests.EveryExactArmTakingANumericArgument_HasAWideBody` fails until it does); a table(ALL) element is rendered by `Element`, a written argument reuses the kept `xs[i]`.
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs#TransferWide` — the one spelling of "a wide value into one fixed-point receiver"; `Settle` uses it, so does MOVE. Reuse it for any new store of a wide value.
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/FunctionValueField.cs#Compare` — the one ordering of two temporaries (SDIDI values when neither is wide; the EXACT values whenever either is, never the wide value's image); `CobolNum.ReturnedValueProfile`'s private digit-string core is the one description rule for both carriers.
- Trap: the public-API analyzer refuses two overloads with optional parameters (RS0026), so a wide twin of a runtime method with defaults needs its own NAME (`FormatWideFunctionText`); and the doc-citation audit attributes a quotation to the last § before it on the line.
