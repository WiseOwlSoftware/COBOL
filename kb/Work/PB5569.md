# PB5569 - at --std 85 the temporary of a mixed comparison omits a SIGN SEPARATE item's sign position: S99PP SIGN SEPARATE -1200 compares as "12", not "120", and ALL "12" opposite S9(3) SIGN LEADING SEPARATE is sized to 3, not 4

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/OperandText.cs` (`OperandText.AsItemCharacters`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/ConditionRenderer.cs` (`ConditionRenderer.ComparisonText`, `ConditionRenderer.RenderFigurativeRelational`)

**Mechanism.** At --std 85 a numeric operand compared with a nonnumeric one is "treated as though it were moved to an elementary alphanumeric data item of the same size as the numeric data item (in terms of standard data format characters)". The size of a SIGN SEPARATE item counts the sign's character position (§1985:VI.5.12.4 GR6: the letter S "is counted in determining the size of the item"). `ConditionRenderer.ComparisonText` asks `OperandText.AsItemCharacters`, which builds the image from the de-signed sending image and, for P scaling, cuts it to `pic.Digits` (kb/Work PB5187): so the temporary of a SIGN SEPARATE item is its digits, one position short. Two symptoms of the one size: (a) with P scaling, `PIC S99PP SIGN TRAILING SEPARATE VALUE -1200` (size 3; the MOVE sends 1200, P positions counted, §1985:VI.6.19.4 GR4a) must compare as "120" and compares as "12"; (b) an ALL literal opposite the item is repeated to the associated operand's rendered length (`RenderFigurativeRelational` sizes the figurative to `ComparisonText`'s text), so ALL "12" opposite `S9(3) SIGN LEADING SEPARATE` (4 character positions) becomes "121", not "1212". The MOVE arm is right (`MOVE N1 TO X3` gives "120"; kb/Work PB5176 fixed the MOVE of ALL "12" to a SIGN SEPARATE item), and PIC 99PP with no sign compares right. From 2002 the temporary is the digits of the integer (§2023:8.8.4.2.5), so the defect is the 85 arm alone.
