# PB5185 - MOVE CORRESPONDING identifies its group operands once, so a pair that stores into a subscript of a group operand does not move the later pairs' element: the result equals no order of the separate MOVE statements GR1 makes it equal to

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CorrespondingEmitter.cs` (`Emit`, `EmitHoists`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CorrespondingBinder.cs` (`Bind`, `CorrAccess.Create`, `CorrAccess.ChildPlace`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:VI.6.19.4 GR1 makes MOVE CORRESPONDING's results "the same as if the user had referred to each pair of corresponding identifiers in separate MOVE statements", and each separate MOVE evaluates its own subscripts (§1985:VI.6.19.4 GR2). `CorrespondingEmitter.EmitHoists` identifies the group operands once, when the statement begins, for every pair; with one pair that stores into a subscript of the group operand the frozen result equals one order of the separate statements, with two it equals none (the repro prints 22 where the separate MOVEs print 27, or 82 in the other order). No edition arm exists.
