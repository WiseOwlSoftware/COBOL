# PB3530 — render_figure.py mis-draws the LINE clause's three-way brace

## Lead

`python scripts/spec/sweep_figures.py --check` is RED on main: 1 figure has drifted. `render_figure.py` draws §13.18.35.2
Format 1 (the report-writer LINE clause) with `LINE NUMBER IS` outside the three-way brace. PB1221 (commit 241bd0d0c)
re-drew that figure by hand from the rendered PDF page (page 420), and the hand drawing in `specs/ISO_COBOL.md` is the
correct one. `--apply` would revert PB1221's fix.

Repro: run `python scripts/spec/sweep_figures.py --check` in a checkout with the `specs-private` submodule.

Code site: `scripts/spec/render_figure.py`, the brace-group layout.

## Fix shape (for the implementer)

Fix the renderer's brace-group layout, not an exclusion list for this figure.

## Landed — 2026-10-22 (wave 1150 group C)

**Four mechanisms of one root: the drawing put a word or a delimiter outside its own enclosure.** Re-probed on the
worktree: `sweep_figures.py --check` RED, 1 drifted (§13.18.35.2 Format 1). The PDF page (folio 420) prints
`{ LINE NUMBER IS / LINE NUMBERS ARE / LINES ARE }` as one three-way brace.

1. **Label snapping** (`outer_label_rows`). A row was snapped as an "outer enclosure's label" whenever its words sat
   left of a brace and inside its height. `LINE NUMBER IS` is an alternative of the sibling brace beside the tall
   `{ integer-1 … }` brace, so it was lifted above its own top hook. A label is now known by what it annotates: the
   point row of a brace further out that spans the inner brace from hook to hook (ASSIGN keeps snapping). The same
   rule had pulled `PICTURE` out of `{ PICTURE / PIC }`, `IS EQUAL TO` out of SEARCH ALL's brace (twice), set SET's
   and USE's labels on the wrong row, and fused INVOKE's `object-class-name-1` and `identifier-1` onto one row.
2. **Row clustering across a hook** (`cluster`, `enclosures`, `place_rows`). A clustered row's average lay above
   the hook of a brace holding one of its words, so the brace drew that word outside itself (`FOR ALPHANUMERIC`
   beside `CODE-SET`, `THROUGH` in §14.9.13.2 and §13.18.63.2 Format 5). Delimiters are paired like parentheses; a
   word between a pair constrains its row to that side of the boundary, a row whose words agree stays WHOLE and is
   seated at the nearest height of its own words that satisfies them, and it is split only on a conflict. The first
   cut split every word of the row by the boundary, so the review (R1-1) found `CODE-SET` and `IS
   character-string-1` torn off the printed line they share with the held words. And a row is one printed line:
   within 4.0 pt of its first word and with no step wider than 3.5 pt, measured over every figure (level words sit
   up to 3.2 pt apart, the widest one-line step is 3.5 pt, and every step from 3.6 to 4.0 pt is the next line:
   `THROUGH` 3.9 pt below `identifier-3`, and the `…` of folio 133 that belongs inside the parentheses).
3. **Stem spans by height alone** (`covers`). WRITE's `| BEFORE / AFTER |` choice bars drew down through a row of the
   brace beside them. A paired stem now spans only the rows that hold its words.
4. **A bar read as a bracket** (`figure_geometry._foot_side`, the sibling found by the pairing survey: 7 of 1,733
   delimiters were unpaired). SEND's choice bars (§14.9.38.2, folio 726) stand 0.70 pt clear of the bracket's foot,
   whose end fell within the 1.2 pt tolerance of the bar's far edge, so both formats drew `[ ] ON EXCEPTION … [ ]`.
   A foot now belongs to a stem only when its near end lies inside that stem's stroke; over the whole standard
   that changes those four bars and no other general-format stem.

`sweep_figures.py --check` names every drifted figure with its diff. 24 figures regenerated, then 9 more by the
review finisher, each checked against the printed page; `--check` is GREEN with no exceptions. Twenty-five FMT rows'
rule text changed (layout only), so their verdicts were re-affirmed through batches
`w1150c-pb3530-figure-regeneration`, `w1150c-pb3530-send-choice-bars` and
`w1150c-pb3530-review-r1-figure-regeneration` (GAP 66 → 66). Witnesses: `scripts/spec/test_render_figure.py` (eight
measured shapes plus ten unit checks; the new-mechanism shapes fail on main's renderer, the guard shapes pass on
both, and the two the review added fail on the first cut) and `scripts/spec/test_figure_geometry.py` (SEND's four
stems; both bars fail on main).

**For the next implementer.**
- `scripts/spec/render_figure.py#build` owns the row layout. Spans are decided against rows, so a rule about which
  words a delimiter holds belongs in `enclosures` (the pairing; reuse it, never re-pair) and `place_rows`; what one
  printed line is belongs in `cluster` (`ROW_TOL`, `LINE_GAP`); a label rule belongs in `outer_label_rows`. Trap: a
  tolerance cluster's average can sit on the wrong side of a hook while its words do not, and a word beside an
  enclosure is on the same printed line as the words it holds.
- `scripts/spec/figure_geometry.py#_foot_side` decides bracket versus bar. A foot belongs to a stem only when its
  near end lies inside the stem's stroke (`STROKE_EPS`); a distance tolerance against the stem's edge reaches across
  to a bar 0.7 pt away.
- `scripts/spec/sweep_figures.py --check` names each drifted figure with its diff; regenerating figures changes FMT
  rule text, so regenerate the catalog and the inventory and re-affirm the verdicts through a `record_verdicts`
  batch in the same change. `scripts/spec/test_render_figure.py` and `test_figure_geometry.py` replay measured
  figures without the PDF: add a case there for each new shape.
