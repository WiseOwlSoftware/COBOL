# PB3530 — render_figure.py mis-draws the LINE clause's three-way brace

## Lead

`python scripts/spec/sweep_figures.py --check` is RED on main: 1 figure has drifted. `render_figure.py` draws §13.18.35.2
Format 1 (the report-writer LINE clause) with `LINE NUMBER IS` outside the three-way brace. PB1221 (commit 241bd0d0c)
re-drew that figure by hand from the rendered PDF page (page 420), and the hand drawing in `specs/ISO_COBOL.md` is the
correct one. `--apply` would revert PB1221's fix.

Repro: run `python scripts/spec/sweep_figures.py --check` in a checkout with the `specs-private` submodule.

Code site: `scripts/spec/render_figure.py`, the brace-group layout.

## Fix shape (for the implementer)

Fix the renderer's brace-group layout, not an exclusion list for this figure.

## Landed — 2026-10-22 (wave 1150 group C)

**Four mechanisms of one root: the drawing put a word or a delimiter outside its own enclosure.** Re-probed on the
worktree: `sweep_figures.py --check` RED, 1 drifted (§13.18.35.2 Format 1). The PDF page (folio 420) prints
`{ LINE NUMBER IS / LINE NUMBERS ARE / LINES ARE }` as one three-way brace.

1. **Label snapping** (`outer_label_rows`). A row was snapped as an "outer enclosure's label" whenever its words sat
   left of a brace and inside its height. `LINE NUMBER IS` is an alternative of the sibling brace beside the tall
   `{ integer-1 … }` brace, so it was lifted above its own top hook. A label is now known by what it annotates: the
   point row of a brace further out that spans the inner brace from hook to hook (ASSIGN keeps snapping). The same
   rule had pulled `PICTURE` out of `{ PICTURE / PIC }`, `IS EQUAL TO` out of SEARCH ALL's brace (twice), set SET's
   and USE's labels on the wrong row, and fused INVOKE's `object-class-name-1` and `identifier-1` onto one row.
2. **Row clustering across a hook** (`enclosures`, `split_rows`). `THROUGH` sat 3.9 pt below `identifier-3` and
   0.2 pt below its own brace's hook, and the clustered row's average lay above the hook, so the brace drew it
   outside itself (§14.9.13.2, §13.18.63.2 Format 5; CODE-SET's inner brace collapsed the same way). Delimiters are
   paired like parentheses, and a row is split where a held word and the row's average disagree.
3. **Stem spans by height alone** (`covers`). WRITE's `| BEFORE / AFTER |` choice bars drew down through a row of the
   brace beside them. A paired stem now spans only the rows that hold its words.
4. **A bar read as a bracket** (`figure_geometry._foot_side`, the sibling found by the pairing survey: 7 of 1,733
   delimiters were unpaired). SEND's choice bars (§14.9.38.2, folio 726) stand 0.70 pt clear of the bracket's foot,
   whose end fell within the 1.2 pt tolerance of the bar's far edge, so both formats drew `[ ] ON EXCEPTION … [ ]`.
   A foot now belongs to a stem only when its near end lies inside that stem's stroke; over the whole standard
   that changes those four bars and no other general-format stem.

`sweep_figures.py --check` names every drifted figure with its diff. 24 figures regenerated, each checked against
the printed page; `--check` is GREEN with no exceptions. Twenty-one FMT rows' rule text changed (layout only), so
their verdicts were re-affirmed through batches `w1150c-pb3530-figure-regeneration` and

shapes plus six unit checks; the three new-mechanism shapes fail on main's renderer, the three guard shapes pass on
both) and `scripts/spec/test_figure_geometry.py` (SEND's four stems; both bars fail on main).
