# PB5712 - at --std 2002 a floating-point edited PICTURE with a four-digit exponent (+9.9E+9999) or a 32-to-36-digit significand (+9(32)E+99) is accepted

**Sites:**
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs#AnalyzeFloatEdited
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DialectBehavior.cs` (`DialectBehavior.FloatEditedPictureWideBounds`, VCR row 7.118: the edition edge the analyzer asks); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/SendingValueTemp.cs` (`SendingValueTemp.WidestFloatEditedPic`: the implementor's own 36-digit intermediate is analyzed at the newest edition)

**Mechanism.** PictureAnalyzer.AnalyzeFloatEdited applies the later editions' bounds (36 significand digits, four exponent digits) at every edition; 2002 allows 31 digit positions and an exponent of +9, +99, +999 or +9(n), n of 1 to 3.
