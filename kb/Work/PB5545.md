# PB5545 - EvaluateOperandCombinations carries two quotations attributed to the 1985 standard that are not in it

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/EvaluateOperandCombinations.cs#IsPermitted` (the doc comment above the edition-permits method, about lines 68-76, written by kb/Work PB5481).

**Mechanism.** CLAUDE.md rule 1: a citation is validated mechanically, and a quotation of four or more words must lie inside the cited rule. The comment attributes to the 1985 standard (a) "a valid operand for comparison to the corresponding operand in the set of selection subjects" at §1985:VI.6.13.3 SR6 a, and (b) "the value and class of the specified literal" at §1985:VI.6.13.4 GR1. The reviewer reports that (a) the rule reads "must be valid operands for comparison ..." and (b) GR1b reads "are assigned ..." (the "is assigned" wording being the 2002/2023 GR text). The comment is wrong at the quotation level, not at the behavior level (the `--std 85` decision itself is PB5481's, settled by GnuCOBOL latitude).

**Fix.** Run `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check "§1985:VI.6.13.3 SR6a" "<text>"` and `... "§1985:VI.6.13.4 GR1b" "<text>"` for the real wording, replace both quotations with the checked text (or paraphrase without quotation marks), and sweep the other PB5481 files (the conformance entry under `docs/conformance/`, golden comments, DEVLOG) for the same two strings.
