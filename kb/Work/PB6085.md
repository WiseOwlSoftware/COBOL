1. **Live register.** `case_107` and `case_108` read `work.load()` and PB3330's real note text. They turn red when the
   PB3330 campaign's last member lands, or when its note is reworded, on a commit that changed no code. Fix: a synthetic
   campaign note with keyed requirements, built in the case's own fixture.

   planner's POPULATION declaration covers that read today; moving the case to `scripts/selftests/spec/work/test_work.py`
   needs a TOOLING_READS entry for the .cs file.
