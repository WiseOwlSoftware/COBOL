# PB55 — OPEN — whether a nested numbered item is a catalog ROW depends on how the transcription indented it

> ⚙ **flags:** process · found 2026-08-08 · owner rule taken 2026-08-08 · **scheduling answered 2026-08-30**

> **Found by PB39's segmentation fix, and deliberately NOT ridden in on it** (PB39's contract was
> count-preserving identity repair; this changes WHAT COUNTS, which is a denominator question — the class
> D13 makes an owner decision).
>
> **THE DEFECT.** The standard nests numbered lists inside rules (GR 4 a) 1. 2. 3. …). The transcription
> sometimes indents those items (they are absorbed into the parent rule's catalog text) and sometimes
> flattens them to column 0 (they become their own `L<k>.<n>` catalog rows) — **and the same list can be
> split between the two treatments.** §14.9.13.4 GR4a's six-item list is transcribed items 1–3 indented,
> items 4–6 at column 0: the catalog holds rows `GR-14.9.13.4-L2.4/.5/.6` while items 1–3 are invisible
> inside GR4's text. §12.3.7.4 GR7's list likewise contributes `L3.2–L3.6` with its item 1 absorbed.
> So the denominator counts SOME nested sub-rules as rows and hides others, on no principle at all —
> the deciding input is the transcriber's indentation.
>
> **THE QUESTION AN OWNER DECISION MUST SETTLE:** are nested numbered items inventory rows in their own
> right, or body text of their parent rule? The status quo mis-states the denominator in BOTH directions at
> once: it counts tails without heads and hides whole normative sub-lists (§14.9.13.4 GR4a items 1–3 state how
> EVALUATE pairs selection subjects with objects — semantics PB45 shipped a defect in).

### ⚖ OWNER DECISION 2026-08-08 23:20 — THE RULE IS THE UNIT

> Nested numbered items are the parent rule's BODY, not rows. The implementation this authorizes:
> 1. **Re-indent every flattened nested item in the transcription** (tails AND fully-flattened lists), each
>    repair verified against the printed page (`scripts/render-spec-page.py`), so the transcription's
>    indentation matches the print and the extractor's indented-run evidence sees the whole list.
> 2. **Absorb the 234 L-rows into their parent rules** — the parent's text carries the items; ids return to
>    the standard's primary numbering only.
> 3. **Migrate the adjudicated L-rows** to their parent rows — the adjudication record moves into the parent
>    row's notes/test-refs; nothing is dropped. **(27 rows, not 9 — see the measurement above.)**
> 4. Denominator shrinks by 234; the count/id churn is measured by PB39's text-keyed diff before the inventory
>    rebuild, and the extractor's drift guards stay the proof that nothing silently left.
> Verifying a rule still means verifying EVERY sub-item — recorded in that rule's single row.

### ⚖ OWNER DECISION 2026-08-30 — SCHEDULING: **AFTER the defect burn-down**
