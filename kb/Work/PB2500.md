# PB2500

Filed by the w1033 clerk from the PB780 adjudication (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1033-adj\PB780-decision.md` section 4; refuter
`PB780-refute.md` section 4). Both files mark it PLAUSIBLE and UNDECIDED (latitude), so it is an analysis, not a confirmed defect. If
decided as substitution, the harm is wrong-answer, silent.

**Lead A: ASSIGN TO.** `p12assign.cob` (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1033-adj\PB780\src\`): `SELECT F ASSIGN TO KN ...` with
`01 KN CONSTANT AS 'p12out.txt'.` compiles clean and writes `kn.txt` (the device-name-1 spelling mapped by `CobolFile.ResolveHostPath`),
not `p12out.txt`. GnuCOBOL 3.2 (`PB780\gc\run.log`) substitutes the constant and writes `p12out.txt`.

**Lead B: LOCALE.** Refuter `n03locale`: `LOCALE LC IS FR.` with `01 FR CONSTANT AS 'de-DE'.` and
`DISPLAY FUNCTION LOCALE-DATE('20260107' LC)` prints `07/01/2026`, the same as `n03localeword` (no constant; FR read as the
external-locale-name). The literal `'de-DE'` (`n03localelit`) prints `07.01.2026`. Site: `DataBinder.Switches.cs` `LocaleBind` (about line
1033, `external = words[1].GetText()`) never consults the constant table. GnuCOBOL gives no guidance (it refuses the forward reference).

**What the spec says.** `§13.10.3 2)` admits a constant-name wherever a format specifies a literal; `§12.4.5.2 5)` leaves "the meaning and
rules for the allowable specification of device-name-1 and the value of literal-1" to the implementor; `§12.3.7.4 5)` leaves the allowable
external-locale-names to the implementor; `§8.3.2.1 4)` lets one word be both a system-name and a user-defined word. The spec does not
settle which reading a word that is both gets. `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` DOC-A.1-71 (device-name-1's spelling) and DOC-A.1-181 (any BCP-47 tag
spelled as a word) do not address a declared constant-name, so the gap is in those determinations.

**Precedence (CLAUDE.md rule 1).** ISO does not control; GnuCOBOL substitutes for ASSIGN TO, so the expected result for Lead A is
substitution; Lead B has no GnuCOBOL guidance. Owed: an adjudication that picks the reading, documents it in `docs/CONFORMANCE.md`, and
sweeps other slots where a system-name and a literal are both admitted. Related defect set: `PB780`.

Owner 2026-10-17: addressed in the SORT and file I/O campaign (kb/Work PB3353).

## Landed (wave 1129, group G)

**Decision (latitude, CLAUDE.md rule 1: ISO leaves it open, GnuCOBOL substitutes): the program's own declaration of the word
wins over the implementor's system-name of the same spelling.** One answer for every position that admits both readings.
`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 8.3.2.1 "A given word may be used as a system-name and as a user-defined word"` -> OK
8.3.2.1 4); the rest of the spec text is in the body above.

- **Lead B (LOCALE) no longer reproduced on this tree.** `LOCALE LC IS FR.` with `01 FR CONSTANT AS 'de-DE'.` prints
  `07.01.2026` (the literal form's answer): `DataBinder.LocaleBind` already asks `NamesConstant` first (kb/Work PB1942), as does
  the ALPHABET code-name (`AlphabetCodeName`). Their determination was documented (CONFORMANCE.md items 181 and 184).
  Witness: `12.3.7-SPECIAL-NAMES/pb2500_locale_constant_name` (a constant `FR_FR` standing for "ja-JP" dates in the ja-JP
  form; the literal "fr-FR" shows the reading it beat).
- **The generated check.** `ConstantNameLiteralPositionDriftTests.Unwitnessed` argued `assignTarget` and `localeClause` as
  this note's open latitude; with the decision made and both slots witnessed by the two goldens, both entries are gone.
- **Lead A (ASSIGN TO) fixed.** `AssignTargetRule.WordText` reads a word through `LiteralEnvironment.ReadConstant` (the lookup
  the AS phrases use): a constant is literal-1 and meets SR4 (alphanumeric, not zero-length; COBOL2645 otherwise), a refused
  constant entry recovers silently, any other word is device-name-1. A two-operand list's leading word stays the device class.
  DOC-A.1-71 documents it. Witnesses: `pb2500_assign_constant_name` (success, 05, AT END, 35, USE declarative),
  `pb2500-assign-constant-numeric`, `pb2500-assign-constant-below-2002`, `AssignConstantNameTests`.
- **Sweep.** The other slots admitting both a system-name and a literal are exactly these three (grammar `cobolWord | literal`
  positions: ASSIGN, LOCALE, ALPHABET literal phrase); the rest are constant-only (`specialNamesLiteral`, AS phrases) or inert
  (`VALUE OF`).
