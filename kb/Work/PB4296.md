# PB4296 - FUNCTION ABS is windowed 2014 in IntrinsicCatalog but is a 2002 function; `--std 2002` refuses it with COBOL1502, and the wrong window has spread to the inventory bands and two goldens

Found by the Mythos front-end review, gap pass, 2026-10-31 (GAP-REVIEW.md, G2-1, G5-3; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/KaxkLe85tfZBZ3nZ1tDGTs). Pin d7daee1cf.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/IntrinsicCatalog.cs:338` (the ABS row, IntroducedIn 2014; the "COBOL-2014 additions - windows CONFIRMED (R28)" block :333-337); `IntrinsicSig.IsDefinedAt`; COBOL1502 (names 2014 at 85 too); inventory rows AR-15.7.3-1 / RV-15.7.4-1 (PB3173 R1-3) under `SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests.EveryFunctionRule_StartsNoEarlierThanItsFunction`; goldens `pb3538_zero_operand_precision`, `pb3953_function_argument_below_2014` (now `pb3953_function_argument_below_2002`); as landed also `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Exact.cs` (`AbsScaled`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/IntrinsicIntroductionDriftTests.cs` (`IntrinsicIntroductionDriftTests`), `tests/version-matrix/intrinsic-introductions.json`, `tests/version-matrix/inventory/AR-15.7.3-1.json`, `tests/version-matrix/inventory/RV-15.7.4-1.json`, `tests/version-matrix/inventory/FMT-15.7.2.json`.

**Finding.** `FUNCTION ABS` is windowed IntroducedIn 2014 in IntrinsicCatalog.cs:338 but is a 2002 function: t2002.txt:8821 (the section 8.11 list heads with ABS), :30815 "15.6 ABS function", Annex F.2 item 78 :44116 "New intrinsic functions are: ABS ..."; the 2009 CD D.2 item 4 names eleven 2014 functions, not ABS. `G2-refute/r1-abs.cob` and `G5/f02-abs.cob` at `--std 2002`: COBOL1502 "FUNCTION ABS was introduced by ISO/IEC 1989:2014", rc 65; 2014 and 2023 rc 0; `cobc -std=cobol2002` rc 0. At 85 the refusal is right but names 2014 instead of 2002. Sibling sweep: ABS is the only mismatched row of 94. The misdating has spread: PB3173 R1-3 moved inventory rows AR-15.7.3-1 and RV-15.7.4-1 to 2014 to match the catalog (via the drift test above); PB3953's negative `pb3953_function_argument_below_2014` (:64) and PB3538's `pb3538_zero_operand_precision` (:215) assume "ABS is a 2014 function". PB2051:27 says "ABS is a 2002 intrinsic".

**Fix direction.** Both refuters: the drift test must read a COMMITTED inventory of intrinsic introductions derived from the drafts (which are not tracked in git), covering each edition's section 8.11 AND the change-annex new-function lists (2002 F.2 item 78, CD D.2 item 4). This is change set GCS-1 (b) of the gap review. Move the ABS row to 2002, re-band the two inventory rows, rework both goldens, and make COBOL1502 name the true edition.

**Resolution (landed).** Re-probed on the branch build: `cobol abs.cob --std 2002 --run` printed `error COBOL1502: FUNCTION ABS was introduced by ISO/IEC 1989:2014 ... (targeting COBOL-2002)`, rc 65; at 2014 it ran. The ABS row moved from the 2014 block to the 2002 block of `IntrinsicCatalog.Build` (IntroducedIn 2002), so `--std 2002` accepts it and COBOL1502 at 85 names 2002 (the message reads `sig.IntroducedIn`, so no binder change was needed). The 85 set is independently confirmed: the 2002 edition's section 8.11 (76 names) less its Annex F.2 item 78 (34 names) is exactly the catalog's 42 rows windowed at 85.

The guard the refuters asked for: `tests/version-matrix/intrinsic-introductions.json` commits each edition's new-function list with its source passage (the 2002 and 2009 drafts are not tracked in git), and `IntrinsicIntroductionDriftTests` holds `IntroducedIn` equal to it for all 94 clause-15 functions in both directions and re-derives the 2023 list from Annex E.2 item 13 of `specs/ISO_COBOL.md` on every run. With ABS temporarily back at 2014 it reports `ABS: IntrinsicCatalog says IntroducedIn 2014, the committed list of the standard says 2002`.

Inventory rows AR-15.7.3-1, RV-15.7.4-1 and FMT-15.7.2 were re-banded `2014,2023` to `2002,2014,2023` through `record_verdicts` (verdicts unchanged, GAP 19 to 19). Goldens `pb3953_function_argument_int128_window` and `pb3951_length_of_numeric_function_result` now accept from 2002 (output byte-identical at 2002, measured); `pb3538_zero_operand_precision` keeps 2014 (its other rule GR-11.9.11.2-1 is a 2014 rule, which the suite generator enforces) though it runs identically at 2002; the two negatives were renamed `pb3953_function_argument_below_2002` and `pb3951_length_of_abs_below_2002` and refuse at 85 only. Also touched: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Exact.cs` (`AbsScaled` summary said COBOL-2014+), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/IntrinsicIntroductionDriftTests.cs` (`IntrinsicIntroductionDriftTests`), `tests/version-matrix/inventory/AR-15.7.3-1.json`, `RV-15.7.4-1.json`, `FMT-15.7.2.json`.
