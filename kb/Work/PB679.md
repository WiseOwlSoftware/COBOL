# PB679 — OPEN — a pad that knows one space

Found 2026-09-05 by the `PB327` implementer (report §"New defects" B) while making a national record key a byte
window. The same shape it fixed in the connector's record-area fit (`FitRecord` pads `00 20` on the area's even
boundary, because a byte-level `0x20` pad manufactures U+2020) survives in two key-side pads.

## The rule

§8.8.4.2.10 (`cite.py --check 8.8.4.2.10 "national"` → `OK 1)`): a national comparison proceeds *"by comparing
national characters in corresponding national character positions"*; its unequal-length item extends the shorter
operand with NATIONAL spaces (re-run `--check` on that item's wording before citing it in code). The START
statement's key comparison rules (§14.9.41.4 — the implementer named GR17 a/b; verify the number, the clause does
not contain the word "shorter") lean on the same comparison.

## The mechanism

`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/IndexedConnector.cs`, the two sites that pad a key operand to the registered key width
before comparing, use the alphanumeric space. A national key registered in `ByteWidth` (PB327) padded with `0x20`
bytes compares against stored `00 20` pairs as U+2020 — never equal to a space-padded key. Latent: after PB327 the
START operand goes through the storage channel at full width and GR13's LENGTH is scaled to bytes, so today these
are truncation paths. Sibling to sweep: any other place a key or record window is padded or trimmed by a byte
count without asking the window's category (`TrimRecordEnd` already sheds national spaces in pairs; make the pad
and the trim one pair of functions keyed on the window's category, not two literals).

## Fix

One `FitKey` beside `FitRecord`, keyed on the key's category, used at both sites; a unit test that hands each site
a short national operand and asserts the pad bytes are `00 20`. Dispatchable after train 11 lands (PB327 owns the
key registration; PB325 rewrote the keyed connectors' base).
