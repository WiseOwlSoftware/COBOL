# PB4035 — a CI row CI cannot install passes every local check

**Sites:** `scripts/toolchain.py:101` (`Tool.scopes`, `ci_installs`, `TOOLS`).

**What.** `Tool.scopes` places every `runs=CI` row in the `ci` and `gate` scopes whatever its `source`. `ci_installs` installs
only `INSTALL_TOOL` rows. A `runs=CI` row with source `UV_TOOL`, or a second `SETUP_PYTHON`, is therefore in the gate's scope
(and green on every local gate) yet never installed by CI, so every CI job fails on it: the CI invariant (a CI red the local
process could not see) in miniature.

**Fix shape.** `check` (and a drift test) refuses a `runs=CI` row whose source is not one CI installs, with a self-test arm
for each such source.
