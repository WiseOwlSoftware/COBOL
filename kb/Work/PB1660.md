# PB1660 — OPEN

Filed 2026-09-27 by the train-67 lander from the wave-67 M implementer report (`w67m-PB1352-report.md`, new
defect 2). Not probed: the implementer read the sites while landing PB1543 and reported them unprobed.

**Observed (code reading).** PB1543 put the one separator predicate, `TextWordScanner.IsSeparatorSpace` /
`SeparatorSpaces` (the lexer's space set), at the four text-manipulation sites and the pseudo-text Trim. The
compiler-directive operand stages kept their own splitting, on .NET's Unicode White_Space set:

- `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ConditionalCompilationProcessor.cs` — 16 sites;
- `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs` and `ReferenceFormatProcessor.LogicalConversion.cs` — 7;
- `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CobolWordsDirectiveProcessor.cs` — 3;
- `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/FlagDirectiveProcessor.cs` — 1;
- `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/LeapSecondDirectiveProcessor.cs` — 1.

**Rule.** §8.3.5 1): "The COBOL character space is a separator" (`cite.py --check 8.3.5` OK). A U+00A0 inside a
directive operand is a character of the operand, not a separator; the blank-line test is the same question.

**Repro to write.** A `>>DEFINE` / `>>IF` whose operand carries U+00A0 between two words (write it with
`(char)0x00A0` in C#, never an escape the Edit tool decodes): today the two words are two operands.

**Fix shape (rule 4/5).** Every stage asks `TextWordScanner.IsSeparatorSpace`; add a drift test that finds any
`char.IsWhiteSpace` / parameterless `Trim()` / `Split()` under `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/` so the next
stage cannot re-grow a second separator set.
