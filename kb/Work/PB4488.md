# PB4488 - COBOL-WORDS and the word COPY (adjudication)

**Observed.** `>>COBOL-WORDS EQUATE "COPY" WITH "CPY"` followed by `CPY BOOK.` draws COBOL0307 (no COPY statement found): `CopyProcessor` finds a COPY statement by
the word as written, before the COBOL-WORDS stage respells words (the stage order in `Frontend.Preprocess`). PB4281's landed note registers this as a sibling and leaves
open whether GR2 of 7.3.10.4 applies to text manipulation at all (the owner question).

**To adjudicate.** (1) Run `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 7.3.10.4 "<GR2 text>"` and read 7.3.10.4 against 7.3.1 (the order of the text-manipulation stages) and 7.3.10
(what a COBOL-WORDS directive may equate): does an EQUATE of a reserved word that begins a text-manipulation statement take effect for that statement? (2) If yes, the COPY
and REPLACE finders must read the finished word map, which touches `CopyProcessor` (~743 library text too) and the comment-entry cursor of PB4281; (3) if no, the directive
must refuse the EQUATE of COPY and REPLACE (the refusal is then a rule to cite) rather than accept and fail later with COBOL0307. Either answer needs a golden. GnuCOBOL's
behavior is the tie-break only if the spec is silent.
