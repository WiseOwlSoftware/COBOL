# PB4488 - a COBOL-WORDS synonym of a reserved word with no lexer token is accepted as a user-defined word

**Observed.** `>>COBOL-WORDS EQUATE "COPY" WITH "CPY"` followed by `CPY BOOK.` draws COBOL0307 (no COPY statement found) with the hint "A period may be
missing at the end of the previous sentence". PB4281's landed note registered this as a sibling and left open whether GR2 of 7.3.10.4 applies to text manipulation.

**Adjudicated (wave 20261857, E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-m4/b/determination.md).** The standard answers the question, and
neither branch the note offered is right. GR6 of 7.3.10.4 (`cite.py --check 7.3.10.4 "A COBOL-WORDS directive does not
affect any Compiler directing statements or Compiler directives."`, result `OK  §2023:7.3.10.4 6)  (General rules)`) excludes COPY and REPLACE (7.2.2.2), and the stage
order of 7.2.1 agrees: COPY is found in Step 1, REPLACE applies in Step 3, and the COBOL-WORDS actions come last, in
Step 4. So `CPY BOOK.` is no COPY statement, and the finders in `CopyProcessor` rightly read the word as written. The
directive is legal too: SR3 admits any reserved word as literal-1, so refusing `EQUATE "COPY"` would reject legal source.
The repro is not legal source, and refusing it is correct. GnuCOBOL 3.2 says `unexpected COPY`. `DivisionCursor` already
leaves COPY and REPLACE out of the words a directive can respell, so the comment-entry cursor of PB4281 needs nothing.

**What is wrong instead (two defects, one root).** Under GR2 and GR4 a synonym of a reserved word is that reserved word,
and 8.3.2.1 rule 1 forbids it as a user-defined word. But `CobolWordsRewriter.Plan.For` installs a synonym only where the
original word has a lexer token, and `ReservedWordSet.Compose` reserves only RESERVE's literal-6. So when the original
word has no lexer token, the synonym stays a plain IDENTIFIER:
- (A) `01 CPY PIC X.` compiles and runs after `EQUATE "COPY" WITH "CPY"`, as do REPLACE, LOCALE, NESTED, ANYCASE,
  SYSTEM-DEFAULT, USER-DEFAULT, EXCEPTION-OBJECT, COMMA and MINUS, and the SUBSTITUTE form (probes p21, p27-p38, p40;
  GnuCOBOL rejects every one).
- (B) `CPY BOOK.` is read as a paragraph-name, and the error at BOOK carries COBOL0307's "A period may be missing" when the
  previous sentence has its period. The same happens in library text (p16) and for `RPL OFF.` after an EQUATE of REPLACE
  (p26, a bare COBOL0001).

**Siblings (same mechanism, same fix A).** The ten tokenless original words, each probed, each printing `C`: COPY (p21), REPLACE (p30), LOCALE (p27), NESTED (p28),
ANYCASE (p31), SYSTEM-DEFAULT (p32), USER-DEFAULT (p38), EXCEPTION-OBJECT (p33), COMMA (p34), MINUS (p37); the SUBSTITUTE form fails too (p35 COPY to CPY, p40 LOCALE to LOC
printing `CD`; in p40 `01 LOCALE` stays legal, only `01 LOC` errs). **Same fix B, three more shapes:** library text `CPY BK.` (p16), SUBSTITUTE COPY BY CPY then `CPY BK.` (p23),
and `RPL OFF.` drawing a bare COBOL0001 (p26). Checked clean: p04-p10, p17, p19, p20 (COPY and REPLACE survive SUBSTITUTE and UNDEFINE of COPY, REPLACE, REPLACING, BY, LEADING, OFF,
SUPPRESS, TRAILING), p12-p14 (Step 4 applies to library text and replaced text), p15 (RESERVE of a text-name), p24 (INSPECT synonyms). The anonymous COBOL0001 for `>>SRC FORMAT` after
`EQUATE "SOURCE" WITH "SRC"` (p18 / p41) is kb/Work PB1575's, not new.

**Fix.**
- `CobolWordsMap` exposes the synonyms whose original word §2023:8.9 reserves (a synonym of a context-sensitive word or an intrinsic function name stays what it was and is not in the set).
- `ReservedWordSet.Compose`/`RejectsAt` reserve them (verdict `Removed`, as RESERVE), so `FlagReservedUserWord` reports
  COBOL0901, naming the synonym and its original word.
- `CobolErrorListener` gains a sibling of `NoteWithdrawnWord` that names the directive and GR6 when the word at a syntax
  error is a synonym of COPY or REPLACE (a warning once per directive, a new `DiagnosticCatalog` code, the pair COPY and REPLACE taken from one owner).
- `CobolErrorStrategy` drops the period hint there.
- `DESIGN-cobol-words-directive.md` states both rules; `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` needs nothing, no latitude is chosen.
- Drift test: every word in `CobolWordsMap.Synonyms` whose original word is reserved under §2023:8.9 must reject as a user word through `ReservedWordSet.Compose(map)`,
  once for a word the lexer retypes and once for one it does not.
- Goldens (`cases/7.3.10-COBOL-WORDS/`): a negative for the repro with the GR6 note and no period hint; positives for COPY surviving UNDEFINE and SUBSTITUTE of COPY (also in library text),
  REPLACE matching `DISPLAY` and not its synonym `SHOW`, Step 4 reaching library and replaced text, INSPECT through REPLACING/BY synonyms, `RESERVE "BK"` with `COPY BK.`;
  negatives for a COPY phrase word and a REPLACE word respelled, and for the synonym data-name refusals.
- The PB4281 note's parenthesis "(DIVISION, IDENTIFICATION, ID, COPY, REPLACE, ...)" over-lists COPY and REPLACE: by GR6 a respelling of them changes nothing the cursor reads.
