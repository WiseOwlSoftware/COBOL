**Fix.** `ItemCategory.IsElementaryReference(Place)` is the one reader of "does this reference denote an elementary item" (a reference-modified place does, whatever its base item; §1985:IV.4.3.8.4.4 GR5). `StatementValidation.IsInspectElementaryCharacterItem` and `IsInspectIdentifier1` now take the `Place`, so `CheckInspectOperandUsage` (identifier-3 ... identifier-n) and the identifier-1 screen read the reference. `InspectBinder.Sr4Entry` asked `Place.Item.IsGroup` for whether an operand can trigger SR4 and now asks the same reader.
