# PB1910 — the overlapping-MOVE corners D-OVL2 did not measure

Found by wave 1002 group D (PB1907's overlapping-MOVE part) and filed at the train-1002 landing.

## What is open

§14.6.10 1) (Annex A.2 item 36; §4.4 2)) leaves an overlapping MOVE's result undefined. Group D measured GnuCOBOL 3.2
and adopted its two DISPLAY corners (D-OVL2: JUSTIFIED RIGHT leading pad and numeric DISPLAY zero-fill, both written
before the sender is read). Two more libcob behaviors were seen in the source but not measured or adopted:

1. **PACKED and numeric-edited receivers** are also prepared before the sender is read
   (`cob_move_display_to_packed` memsets the receiver). An overlapping DISPLAY sender into a PACKED receiver would
   then read the cleared bytes. Code site: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/MoveOverlap.cs` `Classify` (add a
   classifier arm, an `OverlapPrefillKind` and an `MoveEmitter.EmitOverlapPrefill` arm; `MoveOverlapDriftTests`
   holds the shape). Probe: run a PACKED receiver overlapping a DISPLAY sender through cobc under WSL (recipe in the
   wave-1002 scratch `w1002d/run_cobc1.sh`).
2. **A signed sender's sign is re-punched after the move**, so a fully overlapped result can be a negative zero in
   libcob (`100p`) where ours stores a positive zero. Documented in D-OVL2 as not adopted.

The owner's choice for PB1907 is "Follow GnuCOBOL, except WRITE length", so each measured difference is either
adopted or recorded in D-OVL2 with the reason it is not; item 2 needs that reason checked against the choice.

## Re-filed as a defect (analyses sweep, 2026-10-23)

Adjudicated read-only by an Opus cobol-adjudicator on main e7cf38ce4; accepted by the Mythos session.

Item 1 is a measured difference: an overlapping MOVE from a numeric DISPLAY item into a PACKED-DECIMAL receiver that redefines part of it. libcob prepares (clears) the packed receiver before it reads the sender, so the sender reads the cleared bytes: with AD PIC 9(6) VALUE 123456 and AP PIC 9(5) PACKED-DECIMAL redefining its first 3 bytes, MOVE AD TO AP gives 00456 in GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 and 23456 here; with BP redefining its last 3 bytes, 23300 versus 23456 (the non-overlapping MOVE gives 23456 on both). §14.6.10 1) makes the result undefined, so the owner's PB1907 latitude decision (DEVLOG 1836: follow GnuCOBOL wherever it measurably differs) sets the expected result as libcob's. Fix: a PACKED arm in MoveOverlap.Classify (today line 75 returns None for any non-DISPLAY receiver) with an OverlapPrefillKind for the packed pre-clear and its MoveEmitter.EmitOverlapPrefill rendering, MoveOverlapDriftTests extended, the pb1907_overlap_move golden gaining PK lines measured against cobc, and D-OVL2 rewritten. Numeric-edited receivers were measured in both head and tail shapes and agree with libcob: no change. Item 2 is mis-attributed and needs only a doc correction in the same change: libcob stores a negative-signed zero for MOVE -1000 TO S9(3) with or without overlap (a sign-of-zero representation difference, invisible to DISPLAY and to comparison), so D-OVL2's 'a signed sender's sign is re-punched after the move' sentence should say that libcob keeps the sender's sign on a zero result in every MOVE and that WiseOwl COBOL stores a positive zero; whether that general representation should follow libcob belongs with the SIGN encoding documentation, not with overlap. Flag set wrong_answer to match the register's classification of rule-1 latitude deviations (PB1540, PB1664: bytes wrong against the latitude determination, not an ISO violation).

Evidence: Measured 2026-10-23, e7cf38ce4 vs GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 (WSL cobc -std=cobol2014), probes/pb1910/ovl.cob + ctl.cob: a PACKED-DECIMAL receiver REDEFINING the head of a 9(6) DISPLAY sender holding 123456 gives ours 23456, libcob 00456; over its tail ours 23456, libcob 23300; non-overlapping control 23456 on both. Numeric-edited receivers (head and tail) agree (3456). The negative-zero item is NOT an overlap effect: MOVE -1000 TO a separate S9(3) stores a negative-signed zero in libcob (00p) and a positive one here (00{), DISPLAY +000 and IF < 0 false on both.

## Landed (wave 20261027 H): the overlapping MOVE converts IN PLACE, the way libcob does

**Re-probe (build of f1e3931e9, `cobol --std 2014`, GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 `cobc -std=cobol2014`).** The note's item 1 reproduced
(`AD 9(6)` holding 123456, `AP 9(5) PACKED-DECIMAL` over its first three bytes: libcob `00456`, ours `23456`; over the
last three: libcob `23300`, ours `23456`). **The adjudication's fix was wrong, and the premise behind it too:**
"libcob clears the packed receiver before it reads the sender" gives `00456` for the head, but `23000` (not `23300`)
for the tail. `cob_move_display_to_packed` clears the receiver and then writes it one digit PAIR at a time while it
reads the sender from live storage, so the second pair is formed from the byte the first pair has just written
(`0x23 << 4 = 0x30`). D-OVL2(b)'s zero-fill had the same flaw for the numeric DISPLAY receiver: it is right only where
the receiver lies at or left of the sender's digits (`PIC 9(4)` over bytes 4 to 7 of `123456` gives `3333` in libcob and
`0000` here). The re-probe was a generated matrix (`gen.py`: 9 senders x 20 receivers x 7 byte offsets = 1,260 cases,
each dumped byte by byte, run through cobc in both sign conventions and through ours): scratch
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w20261027h`.

**Fix (the root, not a corner).** The treatment is no longer a "prefill" plus the ordinary store: for the four families
where libcob writes the receiver while it reads the sender, the store IS the library's algorithm. `OverlappingMove`
(`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/OverlappingMove.cs`) has one method per family (`DisplayToDisplay`,
`AlphanumericToDisplay`, `DisplayToPacked`, `PackedToDisplay`) working on both operands' storage images laid over each
other; `MoveOverlap.Classify` chooses the family at bind time (`OverlapStore`, renamed from `OverlapPrefill`; the
`ZeroDigits` kind is gone); `MoveEmitter.EmitInPlaceStore` reads the two images, calls it and writes the receiver
back. The JUSTIFIED RIGHT leading-space prefill (D-OVL2(a)) is unchanged. After the change the whole matrix agrees with
libcob for every numeric DISPLAY and PACKED-DECIMAL receiver from a numeric DISPLAY, alphanumeric or (DISPLAY
receiver) PACKED-DECIMAL sender, ASCII sign convention and IBM (`-fsign=EBCDIC`) alike; the BINARY receivers agree in
the ASCII convention.

**Sign of zero (item 2): mis-attributed, doc only.** libcob keeps the sender's sign on a zero result in every MOVE
(`MOVE -1000 TO S9(3)` stores `00p`, overlapping or not; DISPLAY `+000` and `IF < 0` false on both). D-OVL2 now says
so and points at the SIGN-encoding determinations (Annex A.1 items 177/178); WiseOwl COBOL stores a positive zero in
an ordinary MOVE.

**Measured, NOT adopted (the three reasons are in D-OVL2 (iii) to (v)); repro in scratch `m` / `mi` with `cmp2.py`:**
- NUMERIC-EDITED receivers from a numeric DISPLAY sender, shapes where the receiver lies right of the sender's
  digits (e.g. `d6u` into `ZZZ9` at byte offset 3: libcob smears, ours keeps the snapshot). The head and tail shapes
  the adjudication measured agree. `cob_move_display_to_edited` scans the picture while it reads the sender and
  back-patches earlier positions; porting it means a second implementation of the editing recipe that reads lazily.
  Code site `MoveOverlap.Classify` (no numeric-edited arm) and `OverlappingMove` (no `DisplayToEdited`).
- PACKED-DECIMAL sender into PACKED-DECIMAL receiver (`cob_move_bcd`: `memset` then `memcpy` over overlapping storage —
  C-undefined, glibc-dependent). Repro `m/m_p7s.cob` offsets 0-3, receivers `pk5u`, `pk5s`, `pk32`.
- A signed overpunched DISPLAY sender into a BINARY receiver that covers the sender's sign byte, IBM convention only
  (`-fsign=EBCDIC`): libcob re-punches the sender's last byte after the binary store. Repro `mi/m_d4s.cob`, `bn4`,
  `bn5s`, `bn9`, `bnt`, offsets 2-3. The ASCII convention agrees with the snapshot.

**Found and fixed in passing (I9): an `ALL literal` VALUE under a REDEFINES base seeded the parse text.** `01 A. 05 F
PIC X(6) VALUE ALL ".".` with any `01 B REDEFINES A` came out `ALL"."` where a plain record gave `......`:
`GroupImageCodec`'s character-image seed was a second copy of `ValueInitializer`'s recipe and lacked the Format-6
arm. Both now ask `ValueInitializer.AllLiteralInitializer` (`BooleanCarrierOf` too). Golden
`8.3.3.6-Figurative-constant-values/pb1910_value_all_literal_redefined_record`.

**Goldens and tests.** `14.6.10-Overlapping-operands/pb1910_overlap_in_place_move` (PK-HEAD, PK-TAIL, SMEAR-N, SMEAR-X,
SMEAR-X4, P-SIGN; every line identical on cobc 3.2.0 and here), `OverlappingMoveTests` (9 cases, every expected image
measured with cobc), `MoveOverlapDriftTests` (the emitter renders every kind; every in-place kind is a runtime
algorithm of the same name with its `RuntimeApi` spelling). Inventory row `GR-14.6.10-1` restated and its
`EmitOverlapPrefill` witness retired to `EmitOverlapStore` (batch `w20261027h-pb1910`).
