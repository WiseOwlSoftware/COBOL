# PB650 — OPEN — a guard that filters its own population is evidence about what it kept

Found 2026-09-05 by the `PB635` implementer (report §"For the orchestrator" (A)), which replaced both filters in
`tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/IntrinsicRealArgDriftTests.cs` (`:105`–`:110` on main) with the same derived predicate
the renderer now dispatches on, and recorded that this file's population had been silently under-counted four
times across the campaign. [[measure_the_selectors_complement]] is the memory: a filter is evidence about what it
RETURNED, never about what it dropped.

## The two shapes

1. **A reason that is true in one lane and false in another.** "Those reject a float at bind time" held under
   strict and not under `--permissive`, where `CheckArgumentClasses` warns and binds; the exemption therefore hid
   every `--permissive`-reachable crash the guard existed to catch ([[PB651]] is the lane-level sibling).
2. **A filter with no reason at all.** `.Where(m => !m.EndsWith("String"))` removed exactly one method — FIND-STRING
   — and no comment said why; PB635's repro was that method.

## The work

1. Sweep: `grep -rn "\.Where(" tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/*DriftTests.cs tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance/*DriftTests.cs`
   — every hit inside a population builder (the expression that decides WHICH rows/functions/files the test covers)
   either carries a comment naming the rule that licenses the exclusion (with `cite.py --check` where the rule is
   the standard's) or is deleted and the excluded members made to pass on their own terms.
2. Every builder asserts a population FLOOR (`Assert.True(population.Count >= N, …)` with N the count measured at
   the time and the reason written beside it), so a filter that silently drops members turns the test red the way
   PB252's `≥ 7` floor caught two scan bugs during development.
3. Where a filter's reason is lane-dependent (strict vs `--permissive`), the builder runs the population in BOTH
   lanes or states which lane it covers in the test's name.
