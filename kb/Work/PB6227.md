**Scenario:** Every listing column (ListingLine.Text positions, ListingDiagnostic.Column, ListingSpan.Column, ListingNameUse.Column) is a character position the front end decided through CharacterPositions. The renderers turn those columns back into string indexes with their own copies of the step `char.IsHighSurrogate(text[at]) && at + 1 < text.Length && char.IsLowSurrogate(text[at + 1]) ? 2 : 1`: TextListingRenderer.IndexAfterPositions (the row wrap of Indented), TextListingRenderer.Positions (the caret row bound), TextListingRenderer.Wrap (the 80-position source cut) and the HTML renderer span/mark walk; SyntaxSpanBuilder.Position counts pairs backwards its own way. A change to the implementor definition (PB1966 refined, e.g. a lone surrogate or a grapheme counted differently) lands in CharacterPositions, PhysicalLinesDriftTests stays green, and the text caret, the wrap and the HTML mark/link positions drift from the diagnostic and span columns by one per affected character on every such line, silently. CaretRow via Positions also lacks the HasSurrogate fast path, so every caret row walks its line char by char.
