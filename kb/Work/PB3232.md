**Rule it breaks:** ISO 8.4.2.2.1 (uniqueness shall be established through qualification for each user-defined name explicitly referenced) with 13.18.54.4 GR5 (the data-name of a SUM entry is the name of the sum counter); the resolver own ResolveUnqualified refuses the same collision between two ordinary items (kb/Work R33), so the two arms of one rule disagree

**Scenario:** Verified on the pin build (cobol.dll at 8be230068): WORKING-STORAGE 01 WS-G. 02 TOT-N PIC 99 VALUE 7, REPORT SECTION 02 TOT-N COLUMN 1 PIC 99 SUM WS-T, PROCEDURE MOVE 3 TO WS-T, GENERATE DET-1, DISPLAY TOT-N compiles with no diagnostic and prints 03 (the sum counter). Two data-names share the spelling and none is qualified, so the reference shall be refused (COBOLNET1639, as for two WORKING-STORAGE TOT-N); instead ResolveImplCore asks SumCounterFor BEFORE ordinary lookup and SumCounterFamilyFor consults ordinary items only when the written qualifiers reach no counter (line 2025), so with no qualifiers the counter always wins. A MOVE to TOT-N alters the report total where the programmer may have meant the WORKING-STORAGE item: a silent wrong answer on source the standard requires to be rejected
