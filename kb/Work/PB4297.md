# PB4297 - 19 words are reserved at `--std 2002` and 12 at `--std 2014` that the printed ISO 2002 section 8.9 and the 2009 CD section 8.9 do not list; the compiler refuses them as data-names with COBOL0901 citing "(ISO section 8.9)"

Found by the Mythos front-end review, gap pass, 2026-10-31 (GAP-REVIEW.md, G5-1; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/KaxkLe85tfZBZ3nZ1tDGTs). Pin e62fbdc35.

**Sites:** `tests/version-matrix/reserved-words.json` (generated); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ReservedWords.Table.cs:328` (POSITION), :335 (PROCEED), :468 (WORDS) and 16 sibling rows; `scripts/gen-reserved-words.ps1` step 5 (:182-235); `ReservedWordSet.RejectsAt` -> COBOL0901 via `CobolParserCoreBase.cs:696/709`; `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` D-RW3 (:1858-1876); `negative/pb656-cobol-reserved-through-2014`.

**Finding.** 19 words carry r2002=true that the printed ISO 2002 section 8.9 (cobolv200112.pdf pp.162-164, rendered by the refuter) does not list: CLOCK-UNITS, COBOL, DEBUG-CONTENTS, DEBUG-ITEM, DEBUG-LINE, DEBUG-NAME, DEBUG-SUB-1/2/3, EVERY, MODULES, NEGATIVE-INFINITY, POSITION, POSITIVE-INFINITY, PROCEDURES, PROCEED, REFERENCES, SEGMENT-LIMIT, WORDS. 12 carry r2014=true that the 2009 CD section 8.9 (STD.BK.pdf pp.175-176) does not list: CLOCK-UNITS, COBOL, EVERY, MODULES, POSITION, PROCEDURES, PROCEED, REFERENCES, WORDS, COMMUNICATION, NEGATIVE-INFINITY, POSITIVE-INFINITY. The compiler refuses them as data-names with COBOL0901 "... is a reserved word in COBOL-2002 and cannot be used as a user-defined word (ISO section 8.9)" (`G5/rw-position.cob` etc., rc 65 at 2002/2014, rc 0 at 2023). The json's source is GnuCOBOL's cobol2002.words, which also reserves implementor words; the json contradicts itself (ALTER, ENTER, RERUN r2002 false while PROCEED, EVERY, CLOCK-UNITS, WORDS, MODULES - words of the same removed constructs - read true). Section 4.2.10 3) permits extension words only when documented as such; these back no extension and the diagnostic attributes them to ISO. ALIGNED is correctly excluded (2002 F.1 item 6, section 13.16.1). PB656 (landed) decided the flags when "the repository holds no 2002/2014 text" and D-RW3 says a text overturns them in one generator run.

**Fix direction.** CORRECTED form: r2014 must NOT be derived from the CD list wholesale (that would newly reserve FLOAT-BINARY-7/16/34, INFINITY, REPRESENTS-NOT-A-NUMBER, names the 2014 final renamed and 2023 lacks, and do NOT import the CD's five renamed names). Clear only the 12 words both the CD section 8.9 and 2023 section 8.9 omit, labelled "per the 2009 CD"; keep GnuCOBOL's 2014 list for the 2014-final-only words (FLOAT-BINARY-32/64/128, FLOAT-INFINITY, FLOAT-NOT-A-NUMBER[-QUIET/-SIGNALING], FARTHEST-FROM-ZERO, IN-ARITHMETIC-RANGE, NEAREST-TO-ZERO, PHYSICAL). Retire `negative/pb656-cobol-reserved-through-2014`; rewrite D-RW3 and PB656's landing paragraph. Change set GCS-1.

**Citations checked** (`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`): section 8.3.2.4.1 "Reserved words shall not be used as system-names or user-defined words"; section 4.2.10 "additional reserved words as nonstandard extensions".

**Evidence.** E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/GAP-REVIEW.md (sections 2 and 5.1); G5/rw-*.cob; rendered 2002 pp.162-164 and CD pp.175-176; review-G5.md.
