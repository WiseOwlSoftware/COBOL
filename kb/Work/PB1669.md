# PB1669 — OPEN

Filed 2026-09-27 by the train-68 lander from the wave-68 T implementer report (`w68t-PB1401-report.md`, new
defect 2).

**Repro.** `scratchpad/w68t/p/c1.cob` with `--std 2023 --permissive`: `IF BZ B-OR (BW)` is COBOL0001. The same
source compiles strict (PB1465 narrowed the lexer's SUBSCRIPT trigger to words the compile admits as user words).

**Rule.** §8.8.2 6) (`cite.py --check 8.8.2` OK): "The permissible combinations of operands, operators, and
parentheses in a boolean expression are specified in Table 4" — a left parenthesis may follow B-AND / B-OR / B-XOR.
§8.3.2.1 1) (`cite.py --check 8.3.2.1` OK §8.3.2.1 1)): "Reserved words shall not be used as user-defined words".
Migration mode admits the operators as legacy data-names, but it may not thereby reject the standard spelling.

**Code sites.** `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Parsing/TokenRetypes.cs#ReservedNonDataNames` / `#PrimeLexer` and
`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4#OnDefaultLParen`: at lex time the word cannot be told from a
data-name, so the decision has to move to where it can be (the parser sees the operator position).

**What the fixer owes.** Decide `(`-after-operator by position, not by the word's reservation status, in migration
mode too; a permissive-mode golden for the c1 shape; confirm a legacy data-name `B-OR (1)` (subscripted) still
parses under --permissive.
