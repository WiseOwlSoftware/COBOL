# PB6069 - two sibling arms of the PB5575 edition edge for integer arguments under STANDARD arithmetic

PB5575 made a written `FUNCTION INTEGER` argument round to 31 digits at `--std 2014` under `ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD`
(`DialectBehavior.IntegerArgumentRoundedUnderStandardArithmetic`). Two sibling arms were not swept (both need a probe and the
governing clause cited with `cite.py --check` before a fix; the clause numbers below are the reports', not re-derived here):

1. `CobolIntrinsics.Dec.cs#ModDec` evaluates MOD's equivalent arithmetic expression
   `(argument-1) - ((argument-2) * FUNCTION INTEGER ((argument-1) / (argument-2)))` (§2014:15.60.3 RV1, per the review) with
   `FloorDec` of the unrounded 32-digit quotient, so MOD disagrees with its own expression written out.
2. `FACTORIAL (2 + 1 / K * K)` under STANDARD arithmetic returns 2 with no EC-ARGUMENT-FUNCTION made visible; the argument is
   `2 + 1/K*K`, not an integer in the exact arithmetic. Sites `IntrinsicRenderer.RawWideArg` and
   `CobolIntrinsics.IntegerArgUnboundedDec`.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Dec.cs#ModDec`, `IntrinsicRenderer.RawWideArg`,
`CobolIntrinsics.IntegerArgUnboundedDec`. Look for every other intrinsic whose definition is an expression over INTEGER.
