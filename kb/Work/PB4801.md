# PB4801 - a numeric literal beside a national argument is accepted by CONCAT

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R2-1 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 2): `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/IntrinsicBinder.cs:2110` Pre-existing sibling of R1-3, one arm below its fix: the numeric-literal arm of `CheckConcatArgs` still continues before the r2 anyNational test, so a numeric literal beside a national argument is not refused (ISO 15.18.3 r2). The numeric item now falls through; the literal does not.

**Citation (`cite.py --check`).** 15.18.3 2) OK ("If any argument is usage national, all arguments shall be usage national, otherwise all arguments shall be usage display.").

**R1.** A grep of kb/Work for `CheckConcatArgs` finds PB4766 (landed; its R1-3 fixed the numeric-item arm) and landed PB1420, PB306, PB58. No open note covers the literal arm.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/IntrinsicBinder.cs#CheckConcatArgs`.

**Landed.** Re-probed on this build: `FUNCTION CONCAT(7 NN)` with NN national compiled and printed `7AB`. The numeric-literal arm of `CheckConcatArgs` now
`continue`s only for a signed or fractional literal (r3); an unsigned integer literal goes on to r2 with its implied usage DISPLAY (ISO 9.3.6 SR3,
`cite.py --check 9.3.6 "the implied usage is DISPLAY"` OK) and is refused COBOL1627 beside a national argument. Witness:
`pb4801-concat-numeric-literal-beside-national` (refuse-at 2023). The sibling arms (numeric item, numeric function, counter register) already reach r2 after PB4766.
