# PB4069 — a hand-bounded job reader outside the PB3819 sweep

Code on the branch `worktree-wf_484eb705-564-18` (PR #777, tip 9c0c240a0, PB3819); `tests/_shared/CiWorkflow.cs` is NOT on origin/main yet. Two halves of one mechanism: the private reader, and the sweep's blind spot for readers bounded by `IndexOf`.

## Lead (verbatim, same report)

> R1-3 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 1): tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/SparseCheckoutDriftTests.cs:100
> TheRealSupersedeJob_WithoutStandardStreams_IsFlagged bounds the supersede job by two hand-found job keys (wf.IndexOf of a newline plus
> `  supersede:` and of a newline plus `  audits:`): a job reader of its own, the sibling PB3819 forbids, which the CiWorkflowDriftTests
> sweep cannot see because OwnSplitter matches only job-key regexes and `jobs:`/`steps:` anchors.

## Fix shape

Take the supersede job from `CiWorkflow.Job(wf, "supersede")` (the shared reader) and locate the list entry inside its text. Widen the
sweep in `CiWorkflowDriftTests` (`OwnSplitter`) to flag an `IndexOf`/`Contains` of a newline plus a two-space job key, so the next private
reader is caught; add an arm for it.
