# PB1933 — OPEN

Filed by the train-1005 lander from wave 1005 group E's report (`w1005e-PB1271-report.md`, lead 2; the remainder
PB1279 records). Not re-probed by the lander.

## The defect

§13.18.44.3 SR5 sentence 2 lets data-name-2 be subordinate to an item with an OCCURS clause, and §13.18.44.4 GR1
gives each occurrence its own redefinition. PB1279 (landed in train 1005) reaches the class's backing through the
subscripted parent path; `ReferenceResolver.BuildBackingPath` refuses a canonical with an OCCURS DYNAMIC ancestor,
because a dynamic table's element is reached through receiving / sending accessors that a `RedefViewPlace` does not
carry, so the reference stays the `NestedClassBacking` deferral.

**Repro.** `05 T OCCURS DYNAMIC CAPACITY IN C FROM 2 TO 5. 10 A PIC X(4). 10 B REDEFINES A. 15 B1 PIC X(2).
15 B2 PIC 99.` then `MOVE 'ab12' TO A(2)` and `DISPLAY B1(2) B2(2)` — COBOLNET0899 on the receiver, COBOLNET1756 on the
sender.

**Sites.** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs#BuildBackingPath` (the dynamic-ancestor refusal) and
`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/PlaceRenderer.cs` ~220-243 (the write arms render the backing with
`AccessDir.Sending`).
