# PB2597 — a context policy that never throws away a fleet

## Facts (verified by the operator 2026-10-08)

- `E:\COBOL-coord\units.jsonl` `peak_context` of the last successful wave units: 117,318 (2026-10-07 11:48),
  118,732 (14:57), 137,718 (20:30), against `-MaxContextTokens` 150,000.
- Wave 1038's unit (started 23:58:59) did an inline planner fix (PB2575) before planning and passed 150k (151,282) at
  00:06:39. The supervisor set `STOP-UNIT` and `scratch\STOP-loop`. The unit's own milestone says its Workflow
  (`wf_dc000831-c97`, 8 implementers) STARTED at 00:06:56, AFTER the wind-down signal, and all 8 returned SPLIT before
  any work (00:10:03).
- The operator restarted the loop with `-MaxContextTokens 220000` at 00:12:54 as a stopgap.

## Harm

A paid-for fleet launch (eight Opus implementers, each loading its brief and the mandatory practices) produced nothing,
and the wave had to be planned and launched again by a fresh unit. Nothing stops a unit from launching a fleet after
its wind-down is signalled, and the cap itself fires on a unit whose context is mostly a running fleet's supervision.

## Done

A context policy that can never throw away a fleet it just paid to launch and never lets a unit launch a fleet after
its wind-down is signalled: the threshold set from the measured distribution with headroom, a unit supervising a running
Workflow treated differently from one doing inline work, `units/wave.md` and `units/common.md` requiring the fleet
launch BEFORE any side work (side work becomes a note), and a `STOP-UNIT` check immediately before any Workflow launch.
Designed (CLAUDE.md rule 5), with the measurements and the chosen numbers recorded here and in the design doc, and
tested in `test_orchestrate.ps1`.

## Measurements (2026-10-08, `units.jsonl` and the unit stream logs, the unit's own messages only)

- Context when the fleet launched (first `local_workflow`/`local_agent` background task), 21 wave, campaign and land
  units since 2026-10-04: 70,337 to 113,385. Wave 1038: 151,282, after its inline side work.
- Final peak of units that completed normally on 2026-10-07: wave at most 137,718, land at most 120,303; two wave units
  of 2026-10-04/05 reached 152,067 and 164,175 and still completed their fleets.
- Growth while and after supervising a fleet: up to about 94,000 (wave 2026-10-05 02:12: 70,337 to 164,175).
- A fresh unit starts at 55,000 to 66,000 (the meter units' peaks); the model's context window is 1,000,000
  (`modelUsage.contextWindow` in the `result` event of every unit log).

## Landed (branch w1038t-loop-control, wave 1038 group T)

The chosen policy (design section 4.3), two levels with the fleet stop at the higher one only:
- SOFT cap `-MaxContextTokens` 200,000 (about 1.45 times the largest normal peak): `STOP-UNIT` only, no fleet stop and no
  kill timer; the unit starts no new step and launches no fleet, and a fleet already running finishes and lands.
- HARD cap `-HardContextTokens` 500,000 (three times the largest peak ever measured, half the window): `STOP-UNIT` plus
  the loop's fleet stop, as for the owner's `STOP`, then the grace-period kill. A fleet launched just under the soft cap
  has more than three times the largest measured supervision growth before it.
- No launch after the signal: `dispatch_guard.py` rule 0 refuses a `Workflow` call and an implementer or lander `Agent`
  call while `<coord>\STOP-UNIT` exists in a LOOP unit (the supervisor exports `COBOL_LOOP_UNIT`; `coord.py` names
  `LOOP_UNIT_ENV` and `stop_unit()` once); an attended session sharing the directory is never refused for it.
- Prompts: `units/common.md` checks `STOP-UNIT` immediately before any fleet launch and leaves the fleet stop to the
  supervisor (the unit no longer creates it); `units/wave.md` launches the fleet before any side work, a defect found on
  the way becomes a note, and one that makes the plan unusable is fixed on its own branch and handed off without a launch.
- `units.jsonl` records `wind_down` (context-soft, context-hard, stop), `fleet_stop_sent` and `fleet_at_wind_down`.
- Tests: `test_orchestrate.ps1` 7 (the hard cap sends the fleet stop) and 7c (the soft cap with a Workflow running: no
  fleet stop, not killed, handed off done, the session marked as a loop unit, a second launch refused by the hook, the
  shipped numbers); `test_dispatch_guard.py` 6 new cases. The `-MaxContextTokens 220000` stopgap is superseded.
- For the next implementer: the policy lives in ONE block of `orchestrate.ps1#Invoke-Unit` (the `THE CONTEXT POLICY`
  comment); `coord.py#stop_unit` and `coord.LOOP_UNIT_ENV` are the names the supervisor and `dispatch_guard.py` share.
  Trap: the fake's `softcap` mode must emit the `background_tasks_changed` event BEFORE the call that crosses the cap,
  or the wind-down sees no fleet.
