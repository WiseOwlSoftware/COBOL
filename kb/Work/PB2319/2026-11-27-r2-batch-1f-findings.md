## R2 batch 1f (pin 9e65164eb3a9): 2 findings on this note

### `codegen-6--architecture--f2#1` (finding, extract, votes upheld/upheld/upheld)

**Finding:** CallEmitter is also the activation boundary storage codec, the callee-side formal registration and the program-exit verbs; DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3 row R0-0024 splits it only into CallArgumentRenderer and CallSiteEmitter

**Rule:** single responsibility per type and one mechanism per job (DESIGN-architecture-review §5.1, DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3: a decomposition names every concern the type holds); layout and naming (DESIGN-architecture-review §8.2 rule 5: the name says the responsibility)

**Scenario:** Outside the CALL statement, OoEmitter makes about 40 calls into CallEmitter statics (CallStringRead 9, CallPlaceIsVarGroup 7, BoundaryCopyWrite 6, CallStringWrite 5, ElementaryFormalWindow 4, IsMovedFormal 3 and others). ProgramEmitter makes 17 (CrossingOf, CallStringRead, BoundaryCopyWrite, BoundaryAtoms, AreaOf, CallBool), HostEntryEmitter 10 (ArgumentLength, Described, AreaOf, BoundaryProfile), and ReportWriterEmitter 6 for its CONTROL restore, with PlaceRenderer and ConditionRenderer also calling in. These are the ISO/IEC 1989:2023 14.2.3 GR8/GR9 crossing classification and image read/write that the INVOKE, callee formal, host-entry and report-control lanes share. The R0-0024 split moves ArgText to CallArgumentRenderer and the statement to CallSiteEmitter, but it gives these 20-odd boundary members no home. So the extract either leaves them on a CallSiteEmitter that the INVOKE and callee lanes then depend on as a peer verb emitter, or splits them between the two new types. Either way a change to the crossing (for example, the next PB1166-style alphabet fix) still edits a type named for one verb. The same row also misses the callee-side RegisteredFormal / RegisteredReturning that ProgramEmitter calls, the GOBACK / EXIT PROGRAM / CANCEL / RAISING-stage verbs, and EmitActivationReturned, the activation tail the INVOKE lane shares

**Target:** Amend DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3 row R0-0024 and PB2319 to four or five types. (1) ActivationBoundaryCodec, a static type holding CallCrossing, CrossingOf, CallPlaceIs*, CallStringRead/Write, BoundaryCopyWrite, CallContentRead, BoundaryImageWidth, ElementaryFormalWindow, BoundaryLength, ArgumentLength, BoundaryProfile, BoundaryAtoms, AreaOf, Described and IsMovedFormal. The CALL, INVOKE, callee, host-entry and report lanes all call it by that name. (2) CallArgumentRenderer (ArgText). (3) CallSiteEmitter (EmitCall, EmitCancel, the EC catch arms). (4) FormalRegistration (RegisteredFormal, RegisteredReturning, FormalLanding), called by ProgramEmitter. (5) ProgramExitEmitter (EmitGoback, EmitExitProgram, EmitRaisingStage, EmitFlowGlobalExit). ActivationTail (EmitActivationReturned plus the propagation pickup) is shared by CallSiteEmitter and the OO MethodEmitter. Every caller changes in the same wave

**Finding sites (the pin's):** src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:1432-1436 (CallEmitter.CrossingOf); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:1468-1610 (CallEmitter.CallStringRead, CallContentRead, BoundaryImageWidth, ElementaryFormalWindow, BoundaryCopyWrite, CallStringWrite); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:601-859 (CallEmitter.PlaceDescription, BoundaryProfile, RegisteredReturning, RegisteredFormal, ArgumentLength, BoundaryAtoms, AreaOf, BoundaryLength); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:359-438 (CallEmitter.EmitActivationReturned, EmitPropagationPickup); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:1615-1789 (CallEmitter.EmitCancel, EmitGoback, EmitExitProgram, EmitRaisingStage)

### `codegen-6--duplication--f1#3` (finding, unify, votes upheld/refuted/upheld)

**Finding:** What an operand states across the activation boundary (length unless unstated, ActivationDescription, area) is composed by three renderers in CallEmitter, one of which documents itself as THE ONE

**Rule:** one rule in one place (DESIGN-architecture-review section 5.2; dimensions.md dimension 4), the rule being section 2023:14.9.4.4 GR3d's statement of each argument and RETURNING item for the section 2023:14.8.2 / 14.8.3 comparison

**Scenario:** Described(built, length, description) at CallEmitter.cs:706-712 says it is THE ONE RENDERER OF WHAT AN ARGUMENT STATES and renders Length/Description as a with-expression; ReturningArgText at :830-837 states the same two facts for the RETURNING item itself, as named constructor arguments, with its own UnstatedBoundaryLength test and its own ActivationDescriptionNew call, and adds the Area through AreaArgument; RegisteredBoundary at :661-670 composes the same profile/length/description triple a third time for the activated side BoundaryItem. Three spellings of one statement means a boundary fact added for GR3d (Area was the last one, kb/Work PB1940, and it reached only ReturningArgText and ArgCarrierText) is taught to one renderer and silently absent from the others, which is exactly the two-sides-disagree defect the Described doc says the single renderer prevents (the HostEntryEmitter lane depends on Described alone).

**Target:** One boundary-statement renderer in the CallArgumentRenderer that section 8.3 extracts from CallEmitter: it takes (carrier text, BoundaryFacts { Length, Description, Area }) and renders the CobolArg statement for an argument and the RETURNING item alike; RegisteredBoundary renders the BoundaryItem from the same BoundaryFacts record, so the activating and activated sides build their facts from one type.

**Finding sites (the pin's):** src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:689-712 (CallEmitter.Described); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:830-837 (CallEmitter.ReturningArgText); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:661-670 (CallEmitter.RegisteredBoundary); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/CallEmitter.cs:805-806 (CallEmitter.AreaArgument)

The first finding is about this note's own target: PB2319 splits CallEmitter into CallArgumentRenderer and CallSiteEmitter, and the activation-boundary storage codec (CallCrossing, CrossingOf, CallStringRead and Write, BoundaryCopyWrite and the rest), the callee-side formal registration and the program-exit verbs have no target, although OoEmitter, ProgramEmitter, HostEntryEmitter and ReportWriterEmitter call them about 70 times. The second is the duplicate renderer inside CallArgumentRenderer's own target (Described, ReturningArgText and RegisteredBoundary state the same boundary facts three ways), so the one wave that extracts it also unifies it. Both are recorded here and not filed apart. The members they name are still declared in CallEmitter.cs on the tree after the pin. The sites are the pin's.
