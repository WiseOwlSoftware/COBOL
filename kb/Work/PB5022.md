# PB5022 - a 2023 SUM refusal of 1985-legal source, to probe at --std=85

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs#ReportSumDataNameRule`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReportSectionBinder.cs#BindSumClause`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReportSectionBinder.cs#RolledRepetitionFault`

**Report (a `cobol-adjudicator` deciding PB4995's contested records, slice 03; the branch review's sample confirmed the
record).** `§2023:13.18.54.3 SR4` b) refuses a same-group SUM crossfoot of non-repeating sum counters, which
`§1985:XIII.3.19.3 SR1` admits; the 1985 change record is CHANGED. The refusal is right at `--std=2023` and a
rejects-legal defect at `--std=85` if the data-name-1 checks refuse it there (PB4950: a diagnostic cites the rule of the compilation's
edition). (The fleet also raised the SPECIAL-NAMES clause order; PB1578 already owns it: any order compiles today.)

**Expected:** a 1985 report with such a crossfoot compiled at `--std=85` (accepted) and `--std=2023` (refused); a refusal
at 85 is fixed at its root (the rule edition-gated through its lineage), with conformance cases at both editions.

**Landed.** Re-probe on the build: a 1985 control footing whose entry TOT writes `SUM SA SB` over its own group's sum
counters was refused at `--std 85` with COBOL2730 (SR4 b) of the 2023 text), so the defect was real. The rule is
COBOL-2002's (`§2002:13.16.52.2 SR3 b)`); COBOL-85's SUM clause has no repetition and admits the same-group sum counter
(`§1985:XIII.3.19.3 SR1`, the crossfoot of `§1985:XIII.3.19.4 GR9 a)`). `RolledRepetitionFault` now returns no fault for
the same-group arm below 2002, and the program prints `04  06  010` at 85. Goldens: conformance
`13.18.54-SUM/pb5022_report_sum_crossfoot_same_group` (accept 85) and negative `pb5022-sum-crossfoot-same-group`
(refused at 2002, 2014, 2023). The older negative `pb1294-sum-data-name-same-group-not-repeating` had been refused at 85
only because of this defect; its `refuse-at` drops 85.
