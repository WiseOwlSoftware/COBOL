# PB3690 — PB3664 cites §14.6.8.2 4) where 5) governs

**Sites:** `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolEdit.cs` (the sign comment near line 247), the golden
`pb3664_edited_sign_stored_value` header, `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/CobolEditStoredSignTests.cs` (its summary),
`docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (the PB3664 determination).

PB3664's branch reviewer (R1-1, low) found that the new sign comment and the same reasoning in three other places cite
§14.6.8.2 4) for a numeric-edited receiver. Rule 4 is "If the receiving operand is a fixed-point numeric item, the data is
aligned by decimal point…"; the edited receiver is rule 5): "If the receiving operand is a fixed-point numeric-edited
item, further alignment, zero fill or truncation, and transfer of digits take place as described in the general rules
and editing rules in 13.18.40". The quoted text passes `cite.py --check` but answers a different question (CLAUDE.md
rule 1, the inherited-citation failure). The operator corrects all four sites after PB3664 lands; no behavior changes.

## Landed (2026-10-24)

All four sites cite §14.6.8.2 5) with its text; §8.8.4.2.4 is named as what it says of comparisons, corroboration only. A sweep of every §14.6.8.2 4) citation in src, tests, docs, scripts and conformance-suite found no other numeric-edited receiver.
