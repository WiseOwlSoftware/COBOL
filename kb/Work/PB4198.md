# PB4198 — the session gate outlives the session limit on account 1

**Sites:**
- `scripts/orchestrator/budget.py#estimate` (the session-window arm: `session_est_pct` from the anchor's `session_pct`
  plus `spent * session_pct_per_weekly_pct`, and the `hold-session` decision at `session_soft_stop_pct`)
- `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` (`quota.session_soft_stop_pct` 97, `session_hard_stop_pct` 99,
  `session_window_hours`, `calibration.session_pct_per_weekly_pct`; the `accounts` rows)
- `scripts/orchestrator/budget.py#record` (a reading's `session` and `session_reset` arguments)
- `scripts/orchestrator/units/meter.md` (step 1 reads "the weekly and session usage")
- `scripts/orchestrator/test_budget.py` (whole: a module-level script with no members; its hold-session cases change
  and per-account cases are added)

**What changed.** Anthropic renamed Max 20x "Ultra" and removes the 5-hour session limit per account, once the account
is verified; the weekly limit is unchanged (the announcement the owner pasted 2026-10-30 ~17:10 PDT). The owner said on
2026-10-31 at about 08:35 PDT that account 1 (the Mythos lane) has been switched and account 2 (the operator) has not.

**The defect.** `budget.py` treats the session window as a limit on every account. For account 1 it still estimates
`session_est_pct` from telemetry and returns `hold-session` once that estimate passes 97 %. That holds the loop on a
limit the platform no longer enforces. If account 1's /usage panel stops showing a "Current session" row, a reading has
no session value, and the meter unit must not record that as an unreadable meter or as 0 %. The week's per-day
allowance (`daily_pct`) is the project's own pacing choice and is unaffected.

**The fix shape.** Make "has a session limit" a property of the account, never a global switch: an `accounts` row key
(for example `quota.session_limit: false` on account1), read through `Account` like the other per-account quota keys.
With it off, `estimate` reports no session estimate (null, never a fake 0), never decides `hold-session`, and `record`
and the meter unit accept a reading with no session row. Account 2 keeps the gate until the owner reports its own
removal, which is then a one-key change in its row. Tests: one per account state (limit on and over 97 % holds; limit
off at any spend goes; a reading with no session value parses), plus a drift test that every `accounts` row states the
key.
