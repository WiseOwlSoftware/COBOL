# PB4470 - PatchEvery duplicates the patch loop, and MakeStale's docstring is wrong

**Sites (origin/main, confirmed):** `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/ExternalRepositoryResolutionTests.cs:352` (`PatchEvery`, used at
383 and 390 by `RenameNamedArgument` and `RedirectRedefinition`), `tests/_shared/RuntimeVersionSkew.cs:35` (`Patch`),
`ExternalRepositoryResolutionTests.cs:343` (the `MakeStale` docstring) and `:415` (the REPO-7 Fact comment).

**Duplication.** PB4460 collapsed three copies of "IndexOf from the start, CopyTo, count, assert non-zero, write back" into
`RuntimeVersionSkew.Patch`. `PatchEvery` is a fourth, because `Patch` takes an encoding and a fixed from/to (the runtime version) where
`PatchEvery` takes arbitrary same-length byte strings. The shape that removes it: a general `ByteImage.ReplaceEvery(byte[] image, byte[] from,
byte[] to, string absent)` in `tests/_shared`, with `RuntimeVersionSkew.Patch` calling it, so one loop remains (rule 4, no second copy).

**Comment.** `MakeStale` now writes `RuntimeVersionSkew.OtherMajor`, which is the NEXT major except at an all-nines major
(`RuntimeVersionSkew.OtherMajorOf`); the docstring at 343 says "an older runtime major" and the Fact comment at 415 repeats it. REPO-7 and
REPO-12 fire on any major that differs, so only the words change.
