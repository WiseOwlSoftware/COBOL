**Finding:** CobolParserCoreBase doubled since its §8.3 row was written, and the new half, a diagnosis-time speculative re-parse engine serving CobolErrorStrategy, is in none of PB2333s three target types

**Rule:** single responsibility per type and one mechanism per job (DESIGN-architecture-review §5.1, §8.3: a god class is split along its named concerns); §8.3 row R0-0038 / PB2333 names only LookaheadPredicates, LocalePredicates and EditionWordPredicates, measured at 79a4b6c58 as 1028 lines and 64 members

**Scenario:** The pin census R0-0023 measures 1896 lines and 126 members. Roughly 700 of those lines are not grammar predicates at all: ReadStatementAt, ReadingParses, Reading (a ParserInterpreter over the ATN on copied tokens), ReservedProcedureName, ReservedEntryName, ReadsWithTheWordAsAProcedureName, NamedAsAProcedureElsewhere/ProcedureReferencesToTheWord, the bare-verb family (IsBareVerbSentence ... Their docs say Diagnosis only (CobolErrorStrategy); never called while parsing, and their callers are CobolErrorStrategy (4042 lines), AdmittedKinds and ReservationGateRewriter. The superclass in turn calls CobolErrorStrategy.IsReservedWord (CobolParserCoreBase.cs:1252,1286), so the parser base and its error strategy depend on each other. Executing PB2333 as written leaves this half behind in the superclass: the wave moves the predicates out and the type stays a ~900-line god class whose job is error diagnosis, and the drift tests that pin it (ReadingParsesFollowDriftTests, ReservedEntryNameDriftTests, EditionReadDriftTests) keep naming the superclass.

**Target:** Amend the §8.3 R0-0038 row (and PB2333) with a fourth target: an internal SpeculativeReadings type in WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend.Parsing, constructed over (the parser ATN, vocabulary, rule names, the token stream, the live context), owning ReadStatementAt, ReadingParses, Reading, the reserved-procedure-name and reserved-entry-name re-reads, the bare-verb decisions and the reserved-word messages; CobolErrorStrategy, AdmittedKinds and ReservationGateRewriter call it directly (no forwarder on the superclass, CLAUDE.md rule 4). The ATN facts (StatementOpeners, FollowSet, KeywordFollows) become one static GrammarAtnFacts type both the predicates and the readings read. IsReservedWord moves out of CobolErrorStrategy to that shared type so the superclass no longer names its error strategy. The predicates the grammar calls (entryNameHere, procedureHeaderHere, keywordContinuesHere, pointerOperandHere) keep calling the readings type through the superclass only where ANTLR requires a superclass member.
