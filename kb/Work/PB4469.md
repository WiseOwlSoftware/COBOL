# PB4469 - a placement-(a) expansion's warning is reported twice, and arm (a) and arm (b) columns differ

**Sites (origin/main, confirmed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/ExpansionAssemblies.cs` (`Write`, the `Written` record at line 29,
`AtTheEntry`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/ExpansionPlacements.cs:45-46` (`ExpansionToWrite.RequestedAt`), the in-group bind
of an `OwnAssembly` expansion (`ExpansionPlacementPass`), and `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-external-repository.md:1879`.

**Defect 1 (duplicate warning).** An expansion placed in an assembly of its own (placement (a)) has its text bound twice: once by the
group, which binds it so the group can type its callers, and once by the sub-compilation `ExpansionAssemblies.Write` runs. A WARNING
that the text raises is therefore raised by both. PB4457 made the sub-compilation's warnings reach the user at the EXPANDS entry; the
group's bind already reported its own. The probe in the PB4456/PB4457 report printed COBOL3146 at `CLIENT1.cob(8,438)` and again at
`CLIENT1.cob(8,5)`. An error cannot double (the group binds first and stops), which is why the report found no natural repro for an
error that only the sub-compilation raises. Root cause as the implementer named it: `CompilerDriver.Result.Errors` and `Warnings` are
strings, so the driver cannot recognise one diagnostic arriving by two routes; a structured diagnostic list through the driver
would let it de-duplicate on (code, entry position, text), and the relabel in `AtTheEntry` (string based) would go.

**Defect 2 (the claim).** The `Write` doc comment (`ExpansionAssemblies.cs:34`), the `RequestedAt` parameter doc
(`ExpansionPlacements.cs:45`) and DESIGN-external-repository 1879 say a placement-(a) diagnostic is positioned at the EXPANDS entry's file,
line and column "exactly as an expansion made in the group (arm b) is positioned". Arm (b) positions at the token column of the expansion
text on the entry line (the 438 above); arm (a) uses the entry's own column (the 5). File and line agree, the column does not.

**Fix direction.** Do the structural fix (Defect 1), then choose ONE column rule for both arms (the entry's column is the one a user can
find) and make the three comments say what the code does. Golden: a multi-unit `build` case whose skeleton raises a warning, asserting
the diagnostics list holds it once, at the entry's column. Sweep: any other path that compiles a text the group has already bound
(`grep -n "Written(" src`, the CALL-lane text crossing).
