# PB4118 — DeclinedFacilityBinder.ClassifySend treats every TO operand that is no message-tag item as message-server-name

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/DeclinedFacilityBinder.cs:90` (`DeclinedFacilityBinder.ClassifySend`).

Review lead R1-1 (low, round 1) of PB4090. In `ClassifySend` (DeclinedFacilityBinder.cs:101 on the reviewed branch) every operand after TO
that is not a message-tag item is classified as message-server-name-1 with no test of its written shape. A message-server-name-1
(§14.9.38.3 SR2) is a bare name, and data-name-2 is a data-name, so no format prints a subscripted, reference-modified or qualified
reference, or a special register, after TO; each is accepted today as a server name. An under-reject on illegal source.

**Fix:** ask the written shape (the operand's text/segment form, as the other binders do through `WrittenSource.Of`) before treating it
as a server name; refuse the shapes no format prints. Pin with negatives for each shape (`SEND TO A(1) FROM x`, `SEND TO A(1:2) ...`,
`SEND TO A OF B ...`, a special register).
