**Fix.** `SequentialConnector.LineDelimiterOnlyFrames` (`_lineSequential && !LineSequentialFileRules`) is the one answer to
"does the delimiter only frame this file's records". When it holds, `Present` writes the record and its delimiter
(`EmitRecordLine`) and nothing else, and the two travel primitives `Advance` and `AdvanceLines` write nothing, so
`PositionOnLogicalPage` still moves LINAGE-COUNTER and raises the end-of-page conditions while no blank line, form feed or
carriage return reaches the medium. The plain WRITE rerouted to `WriteAdvancingRecord` (§1985:VII.4.7.4 GR16) writes the
same file as the spelled-out AFTER ADVANCING 1 LINE. Determination written into `docs/conformance/7/DOC-A.1-150.md`
(§1985:VII.4.7.4 GR12; §1985:C.3, its item 19; the GnuCOBOL contrast), VCR row 7.88 and `docs/FILES_DESIGN.md`.
