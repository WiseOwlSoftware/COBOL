## Landing (wave 20262154 group G)

Re-probe at 27faa4c114: the three rules were still written twice. `GroupImageCodec.EmitVarImageMethods` emitted, per
declared group, `if (__v.HasDyn(k)) T.FromCurrentImage(...) else T.SpaceFillElements(<spaces>, ...)` for a table,
`if (__v.HasDyn(k)) T.Recreate(__v.ElementCarriersAt(k), CobolVarGroup.Empty, ...) else T.SpaceFillElements(...)` for a
table of variable-length group elements, and `X = __storage ? c : CobolDynString.Store(c, max)` for a dynamic-length item;
`StorageCell.StoreComponentAt` wrote the same three branches; `GroupImageCodec.ContiguousLayout` and `StorageCell.LayoutOf`
each computed every component's unit and maximum and the OCCURS DEPENDING tail component. A probe of both lanes on the
built tree (one MOVE into a WORKING-STORAGE group and into an EXTERNAL group: the excess part and a carried pair) printed
the same four lines either way, now the golden `pb2746_carrier_store_both_lanes`.

Fix: each rule is one runtime operation both lanes call.
- `CobolVarGroup.ReceivedDyn(i, maxSize, storage, national)`: §2023:8.5.1.10.4's truncation, the formal copy-in's whole
  content (PB1937), a national component decoded first (PB3441).
- `CobolVarGroup.StoreTableComponent(i, table, elementWidth, unit, store)`: §2023:14.6.9.2's recreation when carried,
  §2023:14.6.9.4's space fill in the element's own unit when not (PB393).
- `CobolVarGroup.StoreGroupTableComponent(i, table, store)`: the same pair over occurrence carriers (PB2496).
- `CobolContiguousLayout.Of(runWidth, fixedAt, maxSize, layoutCodes, tableWidth, odo)`: each component's unit and maximum
  and the OCCURS DEPENDING table of fixed-image elements as the trailing component. The emitted layout field is
  `CobolContiguousLayout.Of(...)` over the walk's component list (a dynamic-length item's maximum is now
  `DataItem.SlotMaxSize`, image characters, as the cell's is); `StorageCell.LayoutOf` delegates to it. The walk no longer
  lists the tail itself: it records where it met it and the emitter throws when that is not the tail's place, so the two
  cannot disagree silently.
- The cell lane's formal copy-in passes `storage: true` at every level instead of `int.MaxValue` maxima at the top level
  (`PlaceRenderer.WriteVarGroupImageAt`, `RuntimeApi.CellVarStoreCarrier`); `StorageCell.Unlimited` is deleted.

Not unified, by decision: the OCCURS DEPENDING iteration bound. The emitted `FromVarImage` loops a declared table's typed
occurrences to `Math.Min(Occurs, __odo)`, while the cell cuts its flattened component list (`OdoTail.CutComponents`);
they are two walks over two storage shapes that share only the clamp, not a second copy of a rule.

mechanical check rewrote every baseline file by the four expected forms and matched 123 of the 151 exactly; the other 28

All 359 changed layout fields were recomputed from the factory's rule and equal the old constructor arguments (the 14
counted in bits checked by hand).
