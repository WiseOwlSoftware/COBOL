# PB4227 — a hung CI step has no time box

**Sites:** `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml` (whole: every job gains a `timeout-minutes`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CiWorkflowDriftTests.cs` (whole: the PB1957 drift test that reads the workflow's jobs gains the every-job-states-a-timeout arm).

**What happened (2026-10-31).** Merge-group run 41226218068 for PR #893 failed. The job "Greenfield conformance (Linux, sharded) (matrix-cont-2023 ...)" (job id 124610924299) started at 17:47:57Z, never finished its `Run actions/checkout@v7` step (no later step started), and ended "failure" at 18:32:57Z, 45 minutes later. The queue removed a branch whose code was fine; it was re-queued unchanged.

**Mechanism.** The workflow sets no `timeout-minutes` on any job or step (PB1957 survey row 31 records this), so a hung step holds a queue slot until GitHub's limit and fails with no diagnosis.

**Fix shape.** A `timeout-minutes` on every job and on the checkout and toolchain steps, sized from measured durations; a retry of checkout on a transient failure; a drift test that every job states a timeout, so a new job without one fails the day it is added.

## Pipeline redesign pointer 2026-10-31 (PB4323)

The pipeline redesign PB4323 closes this note in step 1 (PB4324), which adds timeout-minutes to every job, the checkout timeout and retry, and the drift-test arm. Status unchanged until that step lands.
