# PB4646 - lead from a landed branch's report

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs:716` (`CobolErrorStrategy.ReservedWordUse`)

ClosedFormatResidue keeps HeaderWords and AfterPeriod for exactly that reading; the atAName path bypasses them. Repro not named in the report (a dangling level-number before a section header). Misleading diagnostic. Filed from the reviewer's/implementer's report; the site was re-read on origin/main 2026-11-04 and the named text is still there. Repro as reported, not re-run.

## Landed (wave 20261868 group E)

Re-probed on a fresh build: `01` (period forgotten) then `LINKAGE SECTION.` drew COBOL0901 "'LINKAGE' is a reserved word" (also REPORT, END, IDENTIFICATION); before PB4604 it was the missing-period report COBOL0307. Root cause: `OffendingPosition.EntryNameIsDue` marked the slot from the previous token alone, so a word that begins what follows the entry was read as a name. Fix: `EntryNameIsDue` is false when the word and the one after it are taken by a walk from the entry's end in its real context (`BeginsWhatFollows` over `ResimulationWalk.After(..., skipsErrorProductions: true)`), so `ReservedWordUse`, `ClosedFormatResidue` and the extension-word hint all stand down together and hint 14 reports COBOL0307. Two tokens, because `01 LINKAGE PIC X.` and `01 LINKAGE.` are no header and keep COBOL0901. `ClosedFormats.EntryNameDueAfter` became `ClosedFormats.EntryAwaitingItsName` (it returns the entry; every caller changed). Goldens: `conformance-suite/cases/13.16-Data-description/pb4646-dangling-level-number-before-section-header` (refused at all four editions); unit `ClosedFormatReservedWordTests.DanglingLevelNumberBeforeASectionHeader_IsTheMissingPeriod`. Design: `DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md`, "The entry-name slot".

**Sites (as landed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`OffendingPosition.EntryNameIsDue`, `OffendingPosition.BeginsWhatFollows`, `CobolErrorStrategy.ReservedWordUse`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Cst/ClosedFormats.cs` (`ClosedFormats.EntryAwaitingItsName`).
