**Merge-group bounce of #1530 (run 43116013863, 2026-11-11): two causes, both fixed on the branch.**

1. **The shards downloaded the generated suite by a pattern.** `.github/actions/download-results` was given
   `pattern: generated-linux` and `path: TestResults/handed`, and every host was handed
   `TestResults/handed/generated-linux/conformance-suite`. actions/download-artifact@v8 extracts a pattern that matches
   ONE artifact flat into the path (`artifacts.length === 1` in its `download-artifact.ts`; the step logged "Starting
   download of artifact to: .../TestResults/handed"), so the suite sat at `TestResults/handed/conformance-suite` and every
   Conformance host on all 12 shards refused with "holds no wiseowl/manifest.json" (64 red cases, then `ci_plan.py
   handed` red). The local gate cannot see this: it hands its own directory. Fix: the shards download the leg's one
   artifact BY NAME into `Leg.handed_dir` (`TestResults/handed/generated-<leg>`), which download-artifact extracts
   directly into that path; the action takes exactly one of `name` and `pattern` and states the layout; `ci_wiring`
   refuses a pattern download of the leg's artifact, and a new negative of its self-test arm plants one.
2. **ci_plan.py's self-test was refused as an UNKEYED READ** on the merged tree: main's PB5227 tracer declares a grammar
   per git subcommand, and the self-test's `--no-records` arm plans the real targets file with
   `git ls-tree -r -z --full-tree HEAD^{tree}`, which had none. At the branch's gate the older tracer keyed it on the
   whole tree. Fix: `ls-tree` is declared in `GIT_GRAMMAR` as a history read keyed by the one revision it names (its
   tree is immutable, as `rev-parse`'s object is), its paths parsed but keying nothing; RESOLVED and UNKEYED rows of
   `self_test_inputs.py --self-test` cover it.

Sibling noted, not changed: `ci-gate`'s `pattern: test-results-*-shard-*` relies on matching several artifacts; if only
one shard artifact survived, the layout would be flat and the population check would fail red on the missing
`shards/test-results-<os>-shard-1/` paths, a loud red over a real loss, so it stays.
