# PB5448 - the row shape of an unnumbered obligation

**Sites:** none (a decision; the tooling it asks for is kb/Work PB5449, the rows PB5451, PB5452 and PB5456)

## What is decided

kb/Work PB479 step 2 left the row shape of an unnumbered obligation as "a bare question" for the owner. The codebase is
Claude's to decide (CLAUDE.md, Ownership; owner 2026-11-10), so it is decided here. The full design, with every figure
beside the command that produced it, is "Phase A, continued" of section 3 of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md`.

1. **The margin paragraph is the unit**, as the numbered rule is (kb/Work PB55). It carries its NOTEs, the list a
   colon-ended paragraph introduces, and its tail across a page break. It is a prose clause's paragraph, a rule block's
   lead-in, or a paragraph after a numbered rule that the PDF prints at the block's own margin (measured, committed as
   `scripts/spec/data/margin-levels-<year>.json`).
2. **Ids** `<kind>-<clause>-p<n>` (2023, PB284's form) and `<year>-<kind>-<locator>-p<n>` (1985, 2002), `n` counting
   every margin paragraph of the clause whether or not it has a row; a table row `-T<t>.<r>`, a whole table `-T<t>`, a
   data clause admitted whole `-C`. Kind SR or GR by §2023:5.3.2 and §2023:5.3.3, AR or RV in an intrinsic function's
   clause by §2023:5.3.1. Citations `§2023:14.8.2.2 p3` and `§2023:14.5.1 Table 12 row 2`, validated by `cite.py --check`.
3. **Rows are adjudicated, recognition is mechanical.** Every margin paragraph and table of the normative region gets a
   disposition in the content-keyed manifest's `paragraph-blocks`, keyed by its text key (clause plus the first 100
   normalized characters): a kind (a row), `lead`, `restates` with existing rule ids, or `not-rule` with a reason from a
   closed list; `pending` until decided, outside the denominator and printed beside the rule count as a lower bound.
   The RULE CATALOGS audit fails on a margin paragraph without an entry, on a stale entry, and on a deontic paragraph
   disposed `not-rule`.
4. **Each edition's denominator is corrected once**, in one landing with all its dispositions, the carve of glued
   margin text out of 57 numbered 2023 rows (`record_verdicts.py split`, ids unchanged, records re-stamped) and the
   mechanical first pass.

## Why

- **No sentence-level recogniser can define the rows.** Measured (kb/Work PB479's dated file of 2026-11-12): the verb
  criterion's hits are obligations 22 times in 30 (2023) and 23 in 30 (1985), and of the sentences it does not flag,
  15 in 30 (2023) and 9 in 30 (1985) are obligations: general rules are written in the indicative ("A general rule
  defines or clarifies the meaning or relationship of meanings of an element or set of elements", §2023:5.3.3). Of
  the 2023 text that carries the fourteen held 1985 rules, §2023:14.5.1's definitions, Table 12 and §2023:14.5.2 carry
  no deontic form at all, and §2023:14.6.2.2's active-state rules are indicative. So the decision about a paragraph is a
  person's, and the mechanism's job is that no paragraph escapes the question: every margin paragraph must be disposed,
  and the next one the text grows is refused until it is (rule 5: the next case automatic, held by a drift test).
- **The paragraph, not the sentence**, because a sentence-level row would cite, verdict and test a fragment whose
  subject lives in the sentence before it (the fragment class PB55 removed for sub-items), and a paragraph is what the
  page prints as a unit.
- **`n` is the text's**, so changing one disposition never moves another id; dispositions are keyed by text, so a
  repair that renumbers paragraphs moves ids through a generated move batch (the renumbering rule of section 10.2 of
  the design) without re-typing the wrong paragraph.
- **Once per edition**, because a denominator change moves every clause total and the session probe's number; doing it
  row by row would make each landing a reconciliation (kb/Work PB284's warning).

## What the owner is told
