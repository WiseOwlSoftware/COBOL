# PB5448 - the row shape of an unnumbered obligation

**Sites:** none (a decision; the tooling it asks for is kb/Work PB5449, the rows PB5451, PB5452 and PB5456)

## What is decided

kb/Work PB479 step 2 left the row shape of an unnumbered obligation as "a bare question" for the owner. The codebase is
Claude's to decide (CLAUDE.md, Ownership; owner 2026-11-10), so it is decided here. The full design, with every figure
beside the command that produced it, is "Phase A, continued" of section 3 of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md`. An adversarial review of the design found six blocking
defects, all in how rows would be closed or removed cheaply, in the segmentation and key, and in step 1's
done-condition; this note states the design as revised (kb/Work PB479's dated file `2026-11-12-design-review.md` lists
each finding and where it was answered).

1. **The margin paragraph is the unit**, as the numbered rule is (kb/Work PB55): a prose clause's paragraph, a rule
   block's lead-in, or a paragraph after a numbered rule that the PDF prints at the block's own margin (measured,
   committed as `scripts/spec/data/margin-levels-<year>.json`). A paragraph continues across a blank line when the
   text before it ends no sentence and it opens in lowercase or with a continuation word (the 2023 text has no page
   furniture to key a join on, and breaks 42 sentences that way). A colon unit owns its list and the list's closing
   tail. A numbered item of a prose clause (Annex A.2 to A.4) is a unit keyed by its printed number. A table's unit
   follows its kind: a body row of an entries table, a row line of a matrix, a data table whole; a table already held
   by a row's text is never minted again.
2. **Ids** `<kind>-<clause>-p<n>` (2023, PB284's form) and `<year>-<kind>-<locator>-p<n>` (1985, 2002), `n` counting
   every margin paragraph of the clause whether or not it has a row; a table unit `-T<t>.<r>`, a whole table `-T<t>`,
   a data clause `-C`, a prose clause's numbered item `-<n>`. Kind SR or GR by §2023:5.3.2 and §2023:5.3.3, AR or RV in
   an intrinsic function's clause by §2023:5.3.1, DOC for an Annex A documentation item.
3. **Rows are adjudicated, recognition is mechanical.** Every unit of the normative region gets a disposition in the
   content-keyed manifest's `paragraph-blocks` (still keyed by clause; the positional `kinds` map goes), keyed by its
   clause, the digest of its whole normalized text and, among equal digests, its order. A disposition is a kind (a
   row), `restates` with rule ids, `not-a-rule` with a class, or `pending` (outside the denominator, printed as a lower
   bound). A changed text matches no disposition and the audit fails loudly until it is decided again.
   - A unit that states or defines anything normative is a ROW; one that obliges the implementation to nothing closes
     through the `definitional` arm of section 1.1 of the design document, signed and refuter-upheld under kb/Work
     PB5373, never by leaving the denominator.
   - `not-a-rule` has three classes, each with a mechanical test: `lead-in`, `cross-reference`, `data` (a NOTE, an
     example or a format line the transcription failed to mark is repaired in the transcription, not disposed).
   - Every `restates` and `not-a-rule` names an UPHELD refuter record (the form kb/Work PB5374 builds), and the audit
     counts them per class. The verbal forms, widened by the review's misses, and a SHAPE guard derived from every
     row's sentence openings refuse a `not-a-rule` on text shaped like a rule.
4. **A carved row inherits nothing.** A glued margin paragraph carved out of a numbered row is minted open and closes
   only by its own adjudication, refuter and test; a derivation is never copied. The parent keeps its record only
   through a recheck of its remaining text. Stored citations and 1985 change records follow the moved text in the
   same landing.
5. **Each edition's denominator is corrected once**, in one landing with all its dispositions, and nothing closes in
   that landing. Quotations of a new row's text in existing records order the adjudication that follows (step
   PB5455); they close nothing.

## Why

- **No sentence-level recogniser can define the rows.** Measured (kb/Work PB479's dated files of 2026-11-12): the verb
  criterion's hits are obligations 22 times in 30 (2023) and 23 in 30 (1985), and of the sentences it does not flag,
  15 in 30 (2023) and 9 in 30 (1985) are obligations: general rules are written in the indicative ("A general rule
  defines or clarifies the meaning or relationship of meanings of an element or set of elements", §2023:5.3.3). So the
  decision about a unit is a person's, and the mechanism's job is that no unit escapes the question: every unit must be
  disposed, and the next one the text grows is refused until it is (rule 5: the next case automatic, held by a drift
  test).
- **The paragraph, not the sentence**, because a sentence-level row would cite, verdict and test a fragment whose
  subject lives in the sentence before it (the fragment class PB55 removed for sub-items), and a paragraph is what the
  page prints as a unit.
- **`n` is the text's and the key is the whole text's digest**, so changing one disposition never moves another id, a
  repair that renumbers paragraphs moves ids through a generated move batch (section 10.2 of the design document), and
  a repair anywhere in a unit's text sends it back to a person.
- **One way out of the denominator, and it costs what a derivation costs.** A free "definition" or "overview" reason
  would remove a row more cheaply than the derivation it competes with (PB386's warning: the GAP metric must not become
  cheaper to move than the work it stands for).
- **Nothing closes at minting**, because a record decided against text that merely contained a paragraph, or a record
  that quotes a few of its words, does not witness it: of the 293 paragraphs a CONFORMS record with a test quotes, 19
  are quoted by a record of their own clause and 23 at 90 % or more.
- **Once per edition**, because a denominator change moves every clause total and the session probe's number; doing it
  row by row would make each landing a reconciliation (kb/Work PB284's warning).

## What the owner is told

Every row minted is OPEN the day it is minted; nothing closes in that landing. Estimates from paragraph samples (95 %
intervals); the exact count is the output of the classification steps (PB5450 for 2023):

| | rows today | GAP today | rows minted (estimate) | closed at minting | open the day they are minted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 4,214 | 0 | about 500 to 1,850: paragraphs about 705 (375 to 1,055), carved margin paragraphs up to 121, table rows unmeasured (about 250 to 450), at most 5 data clauses | 0 | about 500 to 1,850, plus any of the 57 parents whose recheck fails |
| 1985 | 2,274 | 318 | paragraphs about 350 (195 to 500); tables and glued margin text unmeasured | 0 | about 670 (515 to 820), plus the unmeasured |
| 2002 | 3,298 | 3,298 | measured when its 89 pending clauses are adjudicated | 0 | every row, as today |

The 2023 GAP then comes down through ordinary adjudication (PB5455), the 1985 GAP through change records (PB5453).
