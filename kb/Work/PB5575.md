# PB5575 - Standard arithmetic at --std 2002 uses 2014's 34-digit model instead of 2002's 32 significant digits truncated per operation

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Expressions/CompileTimeArithmetic.cs:16-20`; the run-time ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD engine.

**Citations (each run through `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`, verdict OK):**
- §2002:8.8.1.3.2: "The result shall be the exact sum truncated to 32 significant digits";
- §2002:8.8.1.3.4: "The result shall be the exact product truncated to 32 significant digits";
- §2002:8.8.1.3.1.1: "with a precision of 32 decimal digits";
- §2002:7.2.5.2 GR1: "Standard arithmetic shall be used for all arithmetic operations".

**Repro.** The pinned compiler is `E:/COBOL-wt/coderead85-tree-d/src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Cli/bin/Debug/net10.0/cobol.exe`.
The programs were `pk.cbl` and `pa.cbl` in the filing session's scratch directory (rv/p/); their essential lines:
- `pk.cbl`: `01 K1 CONSTANT AS 1000000000000000000000000000000 * 100 + 9 - 1000000000000000000000000000000 * 100.` then
  `01 A PIC 9(5) VALUE K1.` and `DISPLAY A`.
- `pa.cbl`: `OPTIONS. ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD.`, `01 B PIC 9(31) VALUE 1000000000000000000000000000000.`, then
  `COMPUTE A = B * 100 + 9 - B * 100` and `DISPLAY A`.
- Run: `cobol --std 2002 --source-format fixed --run pk.cbl` prints `00009`. The same command on `pa.cbl` prints `00009`.
- Expected at 2002: `00000`. `10^32 + 9` has 33 significant digits; truncated to 32 it is `10^32`, and minus `10^32` is 0.
- At 2014, 9 is right (34 digits). At 2023 pk is refused (COBOL1547) and pa is refused (COBOL0807).
- The two programs were written from the plan's description (REVIEW.md of the plan folder quotes them); whoever takes this note must re-run them before relying on the numbers above.

**Cause.** `CompileTimeArithmetic.cs:16-20` says "at 2002 and 2014 the mode is PRESCRIBED - Standard, the SDIDI decimal
engine ... (34 digits, decimal128 range)". The run-time ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD engine is the same.

**2002's model** (§2002:8.8.1.3.1 to 8.8.1.3.8):
- a standard intermediate data item of 32 digits, range 10^-999 to 10^1000 - 10^968;
- each operation's result truncated to 32 significant digits;
- rounded to 31 digits on comparison, function argument or a move without ROUNDED (§2002:8.8.1.3.1.3).

**Sweep:** the intrinsic functions under standard arithmetic (§2002:15.3.1), SUM, and the arithmetic statements.

**Rows.** The 271 arithmetic-dependent paired rules of P0 (`p0-dependent-prototype.json` in the plan folder) are held out
from the reads that decide whether existing evidence covers a 2002 rule, pending this note (PB5574).
