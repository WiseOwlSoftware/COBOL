# PB4901 - the X3.23-1985 DEBUG-ITEM register is refused as an operand of CALL and of SET

**What the report says (the implementer's words).** (1) `CALL ... USING DEBUG-NAME` / `RETURNING DEBUG-LINE` is refused COBOL1677
("no DATA DIVISION entry describes" the register, citing ISO 2023 §14.9.4.3), a 2023 rule applied to the 1985 register;
GnuCOBOL compiles it. Repro `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261887a/sw/r7.cob`, site `SectionDataItem.cs:37` and the CALL binder.
Outside that group. (2) `SET DEBUG-SUB-1 UP BY 1` is refused COBOL2112 (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261887a/sw/r1.cob`);
GnuCOBOL's behavior unchecked. Same root as (1) by the report's word: the register as an operand of other statements.
Flags: rejects_legal_source (a candidate: neither refusal is adjudicated).

**Citations re-checked** (`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`): `§2023:14.9.4.3 SR3` and `§2023:14.9.4.3 SR7` OK (identifier-2 and
identifier-3 "defined in the file, working-storage, local-storage, or linkage section"). The report's quoted words "no DATA DIVISION
entry describes" are not in the standard (`--find` finds no clause); they are `SectionDataItem.NonSectionKind`'s own text for the register.

**Read on main (2026-11-06).** `SectionDataItem.NonSectionKind` answers `DebugRegisterPlace => "the DEBUG-ITEM special register,
which no DATA DIVISION entry describes"`; `CallBinder` (lines 340, 748, 1436, 1462) and `ParameterConformance.cs:324` reach it for
the COBOL1677 screen. SET's refusal is `SetFormatSelection` (lines 365, 383, 404, COBOL2112). The clerk ran nothing; the repros are
the witnesses. Neither case is held by PB4858 (DEBUG-CONTENTS reference modification, special-register targets, DB205A).
