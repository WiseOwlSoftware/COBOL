## Finding — copy-statement-p1 (rejects-legal-source)

The floating literal continuation indicator ("- / '-) of §6.2.3 is not implemented in ReferenceFormatProcessor. A literal continued that way is rejected in ordinary source, and inside COPY REPLACING pseudo-text it becomes three text-words that never match. The fixed continuation form works, but it is obsolete in 2023.

**Rows:** SR-7.2.3.3-8

**Repro:**

```
Free form: '01 T PIC X(5) VALUE "AB"-' / '   "CDE".' -> COBOL0001 cannot parse near '"CDE"'. And: 'COPY BK1 REPLACING =="HEL"-' / '  "LO"== BY =="WOR"-' / '      "LD"==.' then 'DISPLAY AA-X.' -> HELLO.
```

**Expected from the spec:** §6.2.3.1 names '"- '-' as the literal continuation indicator 'when specified in an unterminated literal'. §6.2.3.2 SR6: the first nonblank character of each continuation line is the matching quotation symbol. So T = "ABCDE". §7.2.3.3 SR8 lets pseudo-text literals continue by those rules, so pseudo-text-1 is the literal "HELLO", it matches, and DISPLAY AA-X prints WORLD.

**Observed:** s11 rejected and s10 printed HELLO. No floating-continuation handling exists in src/Cobol.Net.Frontend (the only mention is the COBOLNET0903 message that ADVISES using it). The fixed-indicator path works: r01 -> WORLD at 2023, r02 word continuation at 2014. No kb/Work note covers this. It is a §6 defect with a much wider blast radius than COPY.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#NormalizeToFreeFormMapped (continuation handling near lines 590-830)

## Finding — replace-statement-p1 (rejects-legal-source)

The floating literal continuation indicator ("- / '-) of 6.2.3.1 is not implemented by the reference-format processor in either fixed or free form, so a literal continued that way -- inside REPLACE pseudo-text or anywhere else -- fails to parse. Outside this subject's clause (a 6.2.3 defect) but it breaks 7.2.4.3 8) at 2002+; no kb/Work note owns it.

**Rows:** SR-7.2.4.3-8

**Repro:**

```
01 BBB PIC X(12) VALUE "HELLO-WO"-
       "RLD".   and   REPLACE ==XX1== BY ==MOVE "HELLO-WO"-
       "RLD" TO AAA==.  (probes sr8d.cob fixed form, sr8e.cob free form, --std 2023)
```

**Expected from the spec:** Compiles; BBB and AAA both hold HELLO-WORLD. 6.2.3.1: "- is the literal continuation indicator 'when specified in an unterminated literal'; 6.2.3.2 6) the continuation line starts with the quotation symbol; 7.2.4.3 8) pseudo-text character-strings may be continued per reference format.

**Observed:** COBOL0001 cannot parse construct near '"RLD"' (VALUE clause) and COBOL0001 unexpected '-' (pseudo-text), both forms; grep finds no floating-continuation handling in ReferenceFormatProcessor.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs (no floating literal continuation arm)

### The rule (every citation re-run through `cite.py --check`)

> 4) A floating literal continuation indicator shall be specified only for an alphanumeric, boolean, or national literal. A given literal shall not be continued with more than one form of continuation.

`OK  §6.2.3.2 4)  (Syntax rules)`

> 5) A floating literal continuation indicator shall not be specified on a line that contains a fixed literal continuation indicator.

`OK  §6.2.3.2 5)  (Syntax rules)`

> National literals may be continued only with a floating literal continuation indicator.

`OK  §6.3.5 2)  (Continuation of lines)`

> 4) If the line is a fixed-form or free-form line that contains a floating literal continuation indicator, the end of the program text area is set to immediately follow the character preceding the continuation indicator. …

`OK  §6.5 4)  (Logical conversion)`

> 8) … the content of the program-text area, beginning with the first character after the initial quotation symbol, is appended immediately to the right of the last character in the latest logical line of the resultant compilation group.

`OK  §6.5 6) a)  (Logical conversion) — the same sentence closes item 8)`


Three subjects (fixed-form-reference-format, floating-indicators, reference-format) reported the same missing stage independently; they are ONE mechanism — the §6.5 logical-conversion pass has no rule-4/rule-8 arm.

### Finding — `fixed-form-reference-format` (rejects-legal-source; rows `SR-6.3.4-2`, `SR-6.3.5-2`)

**Mechanism.** F3 - the floating literal continuation indicator ("- / '-) is not implemented at all: STR_BODY admits no continuation form and no preprocessor stage joins a line ending in an unterminated literal plus '-', so every alphanumeric, boolean or national literal continued the §6.3.5 item 2 way (the only legal way for a national literal, and the replacement the 2023 standard recommends for the obsolete column-7 hyphen) is a parse error

**Repro.** Fixed form: `000100 01 X PIC X(20) VALUE "ABC"-` then `000100     "DEF".` then `DISPLAY "[" X "]"` (probe ffp5). Variants: an interspersed '*' comment line (ffp5b); a national literal `PIC N(10) VALUE N"ABC"-` (ffp5n).

**Expected from the spec.** §6.3.5 item 2 and §6.2.3.1 ("- '- literal continuation indicator; 'Floating indicators may be used in fixed-form or free-form reference format'). X = "ABCDEF" followed by spaces, output [ABCDEF              ]. The same applies in free form (§6.4.2).

**Observed / reasoning.** ffp5, ffp5b and ffp5n all give COBOL0001 'cannot parse construct near '"DEF"'' at --std 2002 and 2023. CobolLexer.g4 has `fragment STR_BODY : '"' (~["\r\n] | '""')* '"' | ...`, which has no continuation form. A grep of src for floating/literal continuation finds only the COBOLNET0903 message that tells users to 'use the floating continuation indicator'. The compiler recommends a replacement it rejects. §6.2.3.2 SR4/SR5/SR6 (one form per literal, no floating indicator on a fixed-continued line, first nonblank of each continuation line = the quote) and the §6.3.7.3 ban on an inline comment after a floating continuation must come with the feature. No kb/Work note owns this.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4#STR_BODY (l.872), NAT_BODY, BOOL_BODY; src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ConvertFixedToFree (no floating-continuation arm)

### Finding — `floating-indicators` (rejects-legal-source; rows `SR-6.2.3.2-4`, `SR-6.2.3.2-5`, `SR-6.2.3.2-6`)

**Mechanism.** The floating literal continuation indicator ("- / '-) is not implemented anywhere: the lexer's STR_BODY closes a literal at its quote and lexes the following '-' as MINUS, and ReferenceFormatProcessor knows only the col-7 '-' continuation, so there is no §6.5 rule-4/rule-8 logical-conversion step for a floating continuation in either reference format.

**Repro.** >>SOURCE FORMAT FREE ... 01 X PIC X(12). PROCEDURE DIVISION.
    MOVE "ABC"-
         "DEF" TO X.
    DISPLAY "[" X "]".  (probe E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2b\out\probe\floating-indicators\p01_free_cont; also p02 apostrophe, p04 X\"4142\"-, p05 N\"AB\"-, p06 B\"0101\"-, p07 fixed form, p20 --std 2002)

**Expected from the spec.** Compiles and DISPLAYs [ABCDEF      ]: §6.2.3.1 defines '"-' as the literal continuation indicator in an unterminated literal; §6.4.2 requires exactly this spelling for a free-form continued literal; §6.5 rule 4 ends the program-text area before the indicator and rule 8 appends the continuation line's content after its opening quote. X"4142"- / "43" -> ABC; N"AB"- / "CD" -> ABCD (national literals may be continued ONLY this way, §6.3.5); B"0101"- / "1111" -> 01011111. SR4-SR6's negative cases then need their own diagnostics (numeric literal, mixed fixed+floating continuation, floating indicator on a col-7 continuation line, continuation line not starting with the opening quote).

**Observed / reasoning.** Every legal form fails with error COBOL0001: unexpected '-' at the indicator (rc 65) at --std 2023 and 2002; in fixed form too. Free-form source has NO way to continue a literal, and a national literal cannot be continued in either format. The SR4/SR5/SR6 negatives are rejected only incidentally by the same parse failure.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4 line 872 (fragment STR_BODY) and the literal token rules built on it; src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ConvertFixedToFree (only the col-7 '-' arm) - no free-form continuation step exists

### Finding — `reference-format` (rejects-legal-source; rows `GR-6.5-4`, `GR-6.5-8`, `GR-6.1-1`)

**Mechanism.** The floating literal continuation indicator ("- / '-) is not implemented anywhere: the lexer's STR_BODY cannot span a line and ReferenceFormatProcessor joins only on a col-7 hyphen, so no alphanumeric, boolean or national literal can be continued the way §6.4.2 requires in free form and 2023 recommends in fixed form

**Repro.** E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2b\out\probe\reference-format\rf1.cob (free form): `01 X PIC X(12) VALUE \"ABC\"-` then `     \"DEF\".`; `cobol rf1.cob --run` -> rf1.cob(7,6): error COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near '\"DEF\"'. Same for rf2.cob (fixed form), rf10.cob (apostrophes + an interspersed comment and blank line), rf12.cob (national N\"AB\"-).

**Expected from the spec.** §6.5 4): the floating indicator and what follows are discarded and the program-text area ends before it; §6.5 8): the continuation line's text after its opening quotation symbol is appended to the latest logical line. rf1 displays [ABCDEF      ]; rf12 [ABCD..] (national literals may be continued ONLY this way, §6.3.5).

**Observed / reasoning.** COBOL0001 at the continuation line in every probe. Searched every Preprocessor/*.cs and Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4: no recognition of the indicator. COBOLNET0903's message tells a 2023 user to 'use the floating continuation indicator', which is then rejected.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ConvertFixedToFree (fixed arm); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#NormalizeToFreeFormMapped (free arm copies as-is); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4#STR_BODY

### What the fixer owes

Add the §6.5 rule-4 / rule-8 arm to the ONE logical-conversion pass for BOTH reference formats (ReferenceFormatProcessor's fixed arm and the free-form normalizer): a line whose program text ends in an unterminated alphanumeric/boolean/national literal followed by `-` ends before the indicator, and the next line that is not a comment or blank line must begin (first non-space) with the opening quotation symbol, whose following text is appended to the latest logical line. Enforce §6.2.3.2 SR4 (only these three literal kinds; one form of continuation per literal) and SR5 (not on a line carrying the column-7 hyphen) and §6.3.5's national-only-by-floating rule (the column-7 hyphen continuing an N"…" literal is then an error — PB1492's F4 national arm). Edition-gate the indicator at its introducing edition (check `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md`; add the constructs.json row). Goldens: free form, fixed form, a national literal, a boolean literal, an interspersed comment line between the parts; negatives for SR4/SR5.
