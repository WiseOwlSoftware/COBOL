# PB925 — OPEN — the second hand-maintained list in the same file

Source: the `PB897` report, "New defects" item 1.

## The measurement

`scripts/guard.sh:138` carries a `NIST_TESTS="…"` block — a literal list of program names — and
`scripts/guard-fast.sh:80` `sed`s it out, which is exactly what it used to do for `LEGACY_DIVERGENT`
before **PB898** (landed) replaced that list with a derivation from `tests/nist/corpus.tsv`.

The gate that is supposed to prevent the drift does not:
`CorpusManifestTests.GuardNistPopulation_RunsEveryProgramTheGoldenSuiteAsserts` and its two siblings assert
CONTAINMENT (every asserted program is run) plus a surplus restricted to `pending` rows. Neither asserts
EQUALITY with the manifest. **Repro:** add a `green` row to `tests/nist/corpus.tsv` with a program under
`tests/nist/programs/` — the containment fact stays green until the golden suite adopts it, and until then
nothing runs it and nothing says so.

This is `feedback_green_gates_arent_evidence`: a check that passes without looking at what changed.

## The rule

None — there is no ISO clause here. The obligation is the project's own single-source-of-truth rule, and
`kb/Work/PB750` and `kb/Work/PB898` (both landed) are the two precedents that already moved a guard
population out of a shell literal.

## The fix, and why it is registered rather than done

The shape is the one just landed: extend `scripts/guard-population.sh` with a `guard_nist_population()`
that derives the list from `corpus.tsv`, and tighten the three `CorpusManifestTests` facts from containment
to equality (with `pending` as the one declared exception, named in the test). ⛔ It CHANGES WHAT THE GUARD
RUNS, so it needs its own battery — a guard whose population silently grows is how a red leg appears on
someone else's landing — which is why the PB897 implementer registered it instead of folding it into their
change set.
