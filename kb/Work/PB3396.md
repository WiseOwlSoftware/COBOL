# PB3396 — Retire reserves WriteBound's upper bound, which can hit the 2^32-page boundary before the real need does

A lead carried from round 1 of the PB3334 review (`mythos-a4-review`, report
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1118a4-PB3334-report.md`, `## New defects`: "the OPEN I-O/EXTEND fence reserves
`Ledger.WriteBound` near the 2^32-page boundary (`PagedFile.cs#Retire`)"). The report gives no repro and no measurement;
this note records the mechanism as the code shows it, UNMEASURED.

**Code sites** (as of `worktree-wf_14a1e62b-326-31`, PR #268, not yet on main; verify on main once it lands):
- `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/Store/PagedFile.cs:539` `Retire(MetaPage promoted, ulong claim, Rejected? rejected)`: builds the
  FREE-tree edit, then `Reserve(default, ledger, free, promoted.PageCount, out var reservation)` (line ~560) and answers
  `StoreStatus.PermanentError` ('30') when it is not `Ok`. The comment above it reads "a retirement that would grow past
  the format's last page is '30'".
- `PagedFile.cs:626` `Reserve`: sizes the reservation as `bound.Pages + free.PagesToWrite + ledger.WriteBound(need, releases)`
  and refuses with `StoreStatus.Boundary` when `pageCount + (need - reserved.Count) > FormatPages` (line 636; `FormatPages =
  1ul << 32`, line 74).
- `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/Store/Ledger.cs:96` `WriteBound`: documented as "an upper bound of the pages Write takes".

**Mechanism (to measure).** `Reserve` checks the boundary against the BOUND, not against the pages the ledger write really
takes. For a statement far from the boundary the slack is harmless. For `Retire`, which runs at an OPEN I-O/EXTEND fence and
at every promotion, a store whose page count is within `WriteBound` pages of 2^32 can be refused although the retirement
would have fitted, and the refusal is '30' (a permanent error) where a statement-time boundary would be '24'/'34'.
Whether any reachable store gets there (2^32 pages is 16 TiB at 4 KiB) is part of the measurement; if the boundary cannot be
reached on any supported medium, close this note with that finding.

**Repro.** None given by the report. A unit case on `PagedFile` with a `PageCount` set within `WriteBound` of the format
limit (through a hand-built meta page, or a test seam on `FormatPages` if one exists) that opens the store I-O so the fence
runs, and asserts the status.

**Source.** `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1118a4-PB3334-report.md`, `## New defects`, last bullet.

## Landed

**Measured (worktree-wf_6b86238b-6eb-4, on main 9ea0899ea).** The mechanism reproduced, and was wider than the note's "OPEN
I-O/EXTEND fence": every RETIREMENT (the fence, a promotion at a CLOSE, at the generation ceiling or at the ledger
checkpoint) sized its reservation to `Ledger.WriteBound` and refused it '30' when the BOUND passed 2^32 pages, whatever it
really wrote. Three `PagedFileBoundaryTests` cases were RED on the old code: a store whose recorded count left 3 pages
refused its fence '30' and the first headroom that let it complete left the file 3 pages short of the format's end (the
bound's slack, not a page written); a CLOSEd store at exactly 2^32 recorded pages, whose fence writes no page past the page
count, answered '30'.

**Fix (one place, `PagedFile.Reserve`).** `Reserve` takes a `BoundaryAt`. A changing statement's boundary stays its
RESERVATION (`BoundaryAt.Reservation`: refused before the claim, '24' for a WRITE or REWRITE, the implementor's definition
that §9.1.13.5 item 4 leaves to it). A retirement's is the PAGE (`BoundaryAt.Take`): the pages of its reservation past the
format's last are `PageOrigin.Beyond` (no page number; `Reservation.TryTake` answers false) and the retirement is refused
('30': a promotion has no '24') only if it writes one. `Retire` is the only `Take` caller and `Begin` the only
`Reservation` one; no other site compares with `FormatPages`. Design 2.2.1.9's "Measured" paragraph is rewritten as the
two arms.

**Witnesses (I11, the abnormal paths).** `PagedFileBoundaryTests`: the fence refused '30' (never the boundary) at each
headroom below what it writes, committed records readable and R1 intact, completing at exactly the headroom it needs with
the store filling the format's last page; a fence that writes nothing past the page count completes at the last page,
coherent and NO OTHER. `KeyedFenceBoundaryTests`: INDEXED and RELATIVE OPEN I-O '30' on a real host file with OPEN INPUT
still reading it. `IndexedEnginePermanentErrorWitnessTests.FenceRefused*`: the compiled program's five USE and FILE
STATUS combinations, checking on and off, after the OPEN's '30'.
