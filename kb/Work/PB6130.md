# PB6130 - one reading for the A.1 exemptions that disagree with their governing rule's band

PB3320's mechanical check ties a DOC-A.1 item's `editions` band to the earliest rule its A.1 text names. DOC-A.1-77 and -131 sit at the 85
band, while rule GR-14.9.27.4-23 is banded 2002; two 85 goldens and the PB670 determination keep them at 85. PB3320 recorded the exemptions
(-43, -64, -65, -131, with -65 restored to 85,2002,2014,2023 at its review) in an Exempt table with the source of each.

**Question for the adjudicator.** Which reading holds for the whole set: the rule's band (re-band the A.1 items, move or retire the 85
goldens) or the determination's (keep the exemptions and say why in each row). Decide from the 1985 and 2002 texts, each on its own text,
record the verdict through `record_verdicts`, and close the Exempt table entries the verdict removes.

## Resolution (2026-11-24, kb/Work PB6161's branch)

The adjudication (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/pb6130/adjudication.md`) and its refuter (`refute.md`) agree on one reading: an A.1
item takes the band of the rule whose own edition's text leaves the latitude, and a determination of the behaviour at an edition
creates no latitude that edition's text lacks (docs/VERSION_TEST_MATRIX_DESIGN.md P2.10, now stated there).
- DOC-A.1-43 → 2014,2023: the latitude is §2014:12.3.6.2 SR20; 2002 has no such sentence, and 1985 says "All other lowercase
  letters are not equivalent to their corresponding uppercase representations" (§1985:VI.5.9.3 SR3). Its Exempt entry closed.
- DOC-A.1-77 and -131 → 2002,2014,2023 (§2002:14.8.26.3 GR22). The -131 Exempt entry closed.
- DOC-A.1-64 stays 2002,2014,2023, reason amended by the refuter: §2023:11.9.7.4 GR2 carries the default-convention half, which
  2002 had in §2002:11.8.5.3 GR2's second paragraph; GR3 carries the names (band 2002). Not a standard defect.
- DOC-A.1-65 stays 85,2002,2014,2023: the cross-reference to GR3 of 14.9.18 is stale; the latitude is §2023:14.9.18.4 GR1a and,
  for EXIT PROGRAM, §2023:14.9.14.4 GR3 and §1985:II.6.1.

Recorded through `record_verdicts.py` (dry run, then real): `rows changed: 9` (the three re-banded items, the witness moves of
SR-13.18.40.3-3 and SR-12.3.7.3-20, and the four OPEN rows given PB6161's goldens), 7 witnesses retired, `GAP 1 → 1`. The golden
`l1c06_currency_non_cobol_equivalence_85` lost its É lines and the DOC-A.1-43 tag and moved to `cases/13.18.40-PICTURE/` as the 85
witness of SR-13.18.40.3-3 (output `LO=[é1.50]`). The gate then found a sibling the adjudication had not listed:
`l1c06_currency_non_cobol_symbols` was accepted at 2002 with DOC-A.1-43 in its header. It is now accepted at 2014 and 2023, and its
2002 twin `l1c06_currency_non_cobol_symbols_2002` writes each symbol as its clause gives it (a second batch of 3 witness-only
records: DOC-A.1-44, SR-12.3.7.3-20, SR-13.18.40.3-3; GAP 1 → 1); the refusal of `l1c06-currency-non-cobol-equivalent-rebound`
names 2014 and 2023 only. The PB670 golden became PB6161's 85 witness. The adjudication's leads are
PB6161 (L1, owner decision), PB6162 (L2), PB6163 (L3) and PB6164 (L4, item 65 only).
