## Why

The owner does not use Obsidian (2026-10-25, above) and no agent opens it: the 105 transcripts of the skills
measurement (docs/rearchitecture/evidence/fleet-optimization/2026-10-25-skills-token-optimization.md) read the register
only through `work.py next`. The Bases files are read by Obsidian alone, yet they cost work: `work.py check` carries
`check_base_agrees` to keep `Work.base`'s filters equal to `HARM_FLAGS`, PB3389 had to add a `blocked` formula, PB3789
filed that the formula was never run, and that note's implementer opened Obsidian on the owner's desktop (PB3798).
The rest of `kb/` (the spec lookup pages, the MOCs, the diagrams) is plain Markdown that agents and skills read without
Obsidian and stays; whether each of those pages still earns its upkeep is a separate question this note does not decide.

## Acceptance

- Each Work.base view a reader needs has a `work.py` subcommand first: `next` is "Fix next"; add `blocked`, `unharmed`
  (open, no harm flag), `analyses` and `landed` (rows closed) with `--json`, each with a self-test; "Everything" is
  `stats` or dropped. Then the two `.base` files and `.obsidian/` are deleted, `check_base_agrees` with them (no shim,
  CLAUDE.md rule 4).
- Every sentence naming `kb/Work.base` or the Obsidian vault in the Sites above names the work.py subcommand instead,
  in the same change (rule 6); a grep of the tree outside DEVLOG/ and kb/Work/ finds none.
- PB3789 closes as superseded by this note (its subject, the Bases formula, is deleted) with `closes_rows: []` and a reason.
