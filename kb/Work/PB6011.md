# PB6011 - Weak diagnostics at --std 85: a missing separator period after a data entry is reported as COBOL3735 plus five cascades; SOURCE-COMPUTER. MERGE. is reported by COBOL1971 as a misplaced paragraph

`01 A PIC X VALUE "A"` followed by `01 B` with no period: the first diagnostic is COBOL3735 "'01' begins in area A" and five
cascades follow, where the cause is the missing separator period. `SOURCE-COMPUTER. MERGE.` is reported by COBOL1971 as a
misplaced paragraph rather than as a reserved word written where a computer-name stands (§1985:IV.4.2.2.1.3, unchecked lead).
No wrong verdict. Not fixed in the one-pass branch.

**Sites:** `AreaPlacementPass.cs` (COBOL3735), the COBOL1971 emitter.
