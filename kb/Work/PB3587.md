# PB3587 - the Format-1 CALL cell and the INVOKE literal arm cross a group formal without the sender codec

PB3562 made `OperandText.NonElementaryMoveSender` the one place that decides which representation a group-move sender copies
(section 14.9.25.4 4, "there is no conversion of data from one form of internal representation to another", `cite.py --check` OK).
It fixed MOVE and the Format-2 CALL / INVOKE group-formal arms for a KNOWN formal (`CallEmitter.IsPlainGroupFormal`,
`OperandText.CarrierDiffersFromStorage`). Two channels still skip it.

## One note, not three

The two defects and the PB3562 review lead R1-1 share ONE mechanism: an argument cell is built in the elementary alphabet (operand
text or a bare string) and the plain-group formal reads it as a storage image, without asking the codec. The stale doc sentence in
R1-1 is the description of exactly these two bypasses, so it is corrected in this note's fix (docs in the same change set), not
filed apart.

1. **Format-1 CALL.** `CALL "SUBG" USING BY CONTENT E8` where E8 is `PIC 1(8) USAGE BIT` and SUBG's formal is a plain group of eight
   bit items: the group arrives as `[00110001]` (the text '1' as byte 0x31), and a USAGE NATIONAL argument arrives as mojibake. BY
   REFERENCE is right. The probe (`call.cob`) lived in the PB3562 implementer's scratchpad and was not kept; rebuild it from this
   description. Rule: a BY CONTENT argument is moved to the allocated record without conversion (section 14.2.3 9, "moved to this
   allocated record without conversion", `cite.py --check` OK), a record of the argument's length. Cause: `BoundCallArg.Formal` is
   null for a Format-1 CALL (the formal is not known at bind), so `CallEmitter.WithMoveSending -> ElementarySending ->
   OperandText.AsString` builds elementary text; the cell must carry both alphabets (elementary formals read text, a group formal
   reads the storage image). That is an ABI change in `Runtime/Control/CallAbi.cs` (`MoveSending` / `MoveLanding`), which is why the
   PB3562 slice stopped.
2. **INVOKE BY CONTENT of a national or boolean literal** into a group formal: `OoEmitter` builds `new BoundStringLiteral(slit)` from
   a bare string (`OoEmitter.cs` around line 1757, `CsLiteral(a.StringLiteral)`), so the literal's category is lost at bind and the
   national or boolean literal reaches the group as its alphanumeric characters. Same cell-alphabet family.
3. **Doc (review lead R1-1, low).** `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` D-B3 (landing with PB3562) says the rule is written once in
   `OperandText.NonElementaryMoveSender`, "which every group-move sender channel (MOVE, an implicit MOVE such as WRITE FROM, a CALL /
   INVOKE argument into a group formal) reads". The two channels above do not. Until they do, the sentence is false; after the fix
   it must read true, and a drift test should pin it so the next channel is automatic, not a hand-kept list.

## Fix shape (for the implementer)

Give the call cell one representation that carries both alphabets, or make the Format-1 CALL decide at the callee (the formal is
known there), and route the INVOKE literal through a bound literal that keeps its category. Reuse `StaticUsageOf` and
`CarrierDiffersFromStorage`; add goldens for both channels (national and usage-bit argument, national and boolean literal), editions
2002 and up, plus a drift test over every `CallAbi.MoveSending` producer. No I/O path is reached.

`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/OoEmitter.cs`, `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Control/CallAbi.cs`, `docs/CONFORMANCE.md`.
