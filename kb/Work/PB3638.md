## Defects

1. **A same-file chain ignores tier.** `plan_wave.py` chained PB3112 (untiered) → PB2878 (untiered) → PB3612 (tier 1)
   in id order; the owner's first priority ran last. The chain's ORDER must follow the campaign's tier (the
   effective-tier rule `cluster_order` already computes), so the highest-tier note heads the chain.
2. **One unit holds the loop for hours.** A wave with a chain runs until its last link returns; the enqueue unit
   (reviews, finishers, re-arms) and the other campaigns wait behind it. Encode the owner's 10-23 rule: a wave plans
   ONE link per chain (the successor goes to the next wave, planned from the reviewed branch), and a unit hands off
   at about 60 minutes; the review lane beside the landing lane (PB3608) takes the reviews out of the serial units.
3. **No progress reports.** The supervisor and the wave log a progress line every ~10 minutes (groups running, done,
   reviewed, landing, landed; the oldest item waiting and on what) to the supervisor log AND the operator's mailbox,
   so a stall is visible in minutes.
4. **The post-DONE wait.** DONE to enqueued median 34 min (p90 2.2 h) is review queueing and stranded stops (PB3628,
   PB3636's class survey): every stop a script can resolve is resolved by the loop at once (O11).

## The bar, and how it is measured

A simple fix (one mechanism, one subsystem) reaches the merge queue within about 30 minutes of dispatch (10-15 min of
work, the targeted gate, a review in parallel), and merges about 15 minutes later. `landing_measure.py summary` reports
dispatch→DONE, DONE→review, review→enqueued and enqueued→merged per landing, with median and p90, and the operator
reports them at each check-in. A stage whose median exceeds its budget is a defect filed against this note.

## Landed

Each defect as its own mechanism, each re-probed first:

1. **The placement order is the tier.** Re-probe: wave 20261029's specs show A and A2 were FINISHERS (PB3112, PB2878)
   and A3 the tier-1 cluster PB3612; `plan_wave.plan` placed every finisher before every cluster whatever its tier.
   Now `plan(tiers=...)` (the campaign's effective tiers from `work.cluster_order`, passed by `main`) gives every group a
   tier (its notes' lowest, and its clusters'; `lift_tiers` lowers a blocker's group to its dependent's) and places the
   groups in tier order, finishers first inside a tier. `test_plan_wave.py` 9c: a tier-1 group heads its file's chain
   ahead of every untiered finisher; the fix lane's order is unchanged.
2. **One chain link per wave.** `model_rules.json` `wave.links_per_chain` (1, the owner's 2026-10-23 rule): a link past
   it is not dispatched; its notes wait, named, and the next wave plans them from the earlier link's branch (the
   partition's continuation). The unit's hand-off: `orchestrate.ps1 -UnitSoftMinutes 60` writes STOP-UNIT, recorded as
   the wind-down `time-soft`, the context soft cap's semantics (no fleet stop: a running fleet finishes and lands).
   The review lane beside the landing lane stays PB3608's own note.
3. **Progress reports.** `scripts/orchestrator/progress.py` (self-test) reads the dispatch ledger, the reports and the
   landing records: one `PROGRESS` line (groups by state, landings since the unit started, the oldest waiting item)
   and a `STALL` line per group running past 60 minutes or DONE and unreviewed past 15. `orchestrate.ps1` runs it every
   `-ProgressSeconds` (600) in the background through its one background starter (`Start-PyBackground`, which the
   checkpoint and the lane tick now use too), says the lines in its log and posts each stalled item to the operator's
   mailbox once (`--post`, `--unless-pending`, again only an hour later). `test_orchestrate_stop.ps1` 7d.
4. **The post-DONE wait, measured per stage.** `landing_measure.stage_times` records `dispatched` (the dispatch
   ledger) and `review_clean` (the CLEAN verdict's findings record) at enqueue; `review_clean` had been recorded for 0
   of 540 landings. `summary` prints dispatch → DONE → review clean → enqueued → merged (median, p90) and the two spans
   of the bar (dispatch → enqueued 30 min, enqueued → merged 15) marked OVER when the median exceeds the budget. Live on
   2026-10-24: enqueued → merged median 17, p90 21 over 524 landings, OVER. The stops a script can resolve stay with
   PB3628 (landed) and PB3636.
