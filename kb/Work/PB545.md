### Observed
It compiles and runs clean (u_sr15_typeclone). MECHANISM, confirmed by reading both sites: the clone that lands in the FILE SECTION is pruned out of ConformanceForest by design (so that a template's defects are reported once, at the template), while the TEMPLATE itself is a perfectly legal level-1 WORKING-STORAGE entry -- so neither node is ever asked the file-section question. SR15's own arm is complete and was measured on both file-section entry kinds and both levels: `FD F. 01 R USAGE OBJECT REFERENCE.` (the negative golden), a subordinate member of an FD record (u_sr15_sub) and an `SD S. 01 SR USAGE OBJECT REFERENCE.` sort-merge record (u_sr15_sd) are all COBOLNET1725. This is the two_arm_dispatch shape at THREE arms: the standard writes the same prohibition once per acquisition route, the compiler wrote two of the three, and the missing one is the route whose subject the conformance forest deliberately hides. The fix is not a fourth hand-written walk but a per-ROUTE question asked where the route is expanded (DataBinder.ExpandTypes already visits the reference site and knows its section).
