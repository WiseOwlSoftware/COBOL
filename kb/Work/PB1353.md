## Finding — copy-statement-p1 (under-rejects)

## Finding — replace-statement-p1 (under-rejects)

ParseReplacements/ReadReplaceOperand perform no validation of the CONTENT of an ==operand==: the syntax-rule constraints on operand word counts, literal content, text-word length, directive lines and the required BY are never checked, so illegal operands are silently dropped, truncated, or spliced into text that fails later with an unrelated error.

**Rows:** SR-7.2.4.3-3, SR-7.2.4.3-5, SR-7.2.4.3-6, SR-7.2.4.3-7, SR-7.2.4.3-9, SR-7.2.4.3-10, FMT-7.2.4.2

**Repro:**

```
Each compiles with no REPLACE diagnostic: 'REPLACE ==,== BY ==AAA==.' and 'REPLACE ==== BY ==AAA==.' (sr3/sr3b, operand silently discarded); 'REPLACE LEADING ==OLD- XYZ== BY ==NEW-==.' (sr5, first word used); 'REPLACE LEADING ==OLD-== BY ==NEW- X==.' (sr6, downstream COBOL0309 'unexpected XAAA'); 'REPLACE LEADING =="AB"== BY =="XY"==.' (sr7); a 70,000-character text-word in pseudo-text-1 (sr9); a '>>DEFINE ZZ AS 1' line inside ==XX1== ... (sr10); 'REPLACE ==XX1== ==AAA==.' with no BY (noby).
```

**Expected from the spec:** Each is a syntax-rule violation to be diagnosed at the REPLACE statement: 7.2.4.3 3) pseudo-text-1 needs a text-word other than separator comma/semicolon; 5) partial-word-1 is one text-word; 6) partial-word-2 is zero or one; 7) no alphanumeric/boolean/national literal as a partial-word; 9) text-word length 1..65,535; 10) no compiler directive lines inside the operands; 7.2.4.2 BY is a required word.

**Observed:** No diagnostic; ApplyReplacements filters empty operands (.Where(tokens.Count > 0)) and uses tokens[0] for partial words; BY is consumed only if present; COBOLNET1641 fires only for operands NOT delimited by == (the R39 landed fix covers that half of SR7 only).

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ParseReplacements (lines 671-700); #ReadReplaceOperand (713-724); #ApplyReplacements (189-192, 226)

### Finding — `lexical-elements` (wrong-answer; rows `SR-8.3.5-6`, `SR-8.3.5-8`)

**Mechanism.** Pseudo-text delimiters: ReadReplaceOperand accepts '==' with no preceding-space / following-separator check and, when the closing '==' is missing, silently consumes the rest of the source as the operand

**Repro.** PROCEDURE DIVISION. REPLACE ==XX== BY ==DISPLAY.  XX "OK".  STOP RUN.  — compiles rc=0 and the program prints nothing. Also accepted: REPLACE ==XX==BY ==DISPLAY==. ; REPLACE ==XX== BY==DISPLAY==. ; COPY LXC REPLACING ==XX==BY ==DISPLAY==.

**Expected from the spec.** §8.3.5 6): 'An opening pseudo-text delimiter shall be immediately preceded by a space; a closing pseudo-text delimiter shall be immediately followed by one of the separators space, comma, semicolon, or period. Pseudo-text delimiters may appear only in balanced pairs delimiting pseudo-text.' §8.3.5 8) c): the space before the opening pseudo-text delimiter is required. All four are compile-time errors; the unbalanced one must not compile to a program with its procedure text deleted.

**Observed / reasoning.** CopyProcessor.ReadReplaceOperand: `while (pos < text.Length - 1 && !(text[pos]=='=' && text[pos+1]=='=')) pos++;` then returns text[start..pos] with no diagnostic when the loop hits end of text; no check of text[pos-1] before the opener or text[pos+2] after the closer. The same reader serves COPY REPLACING and REPLACE.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ReadReplaceOperand (line ~713), #ParseReplacements, #ApplyReplaceStatements
