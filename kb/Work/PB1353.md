### Finding — `lexical-elements` (wrong-answer; rows `SR-8.3.5-6`, `SR-8.3.5-8`)

**Mechanism.** Pseudo-text delimiters: ReadReplaceOperand accepts '==' with no preceding-space / following-separator check and, when the closing '==' is missing, silently consumes the rest of the source as the operand

**Repro.** PROCEDURE DIVISION. REPLACE ==XX== BY ==DISPLAY.  XX "OK".  STOP RUN.  — compiles rc=0 and the program prints nothing. Also accepted: REPLACE ==XX==BY ==DISPLAY==. ; REPLACE ==XX== BY==DISPLAY==. ; COPY LXC REPLACING ==XX==BY ==DISPLAY==.

**Expected from the spec.** §8.3.5 6): 'An opening pseudo-text delimiter shall be immediately preceded by a space; a closing pseudo-text delimiter shall be immediately followed by one of the separators space, comma, semicolon, or period. Pseudo-text delimiters may appear only in balanced pairs delimiting pseudo-text.' §8.3.5 8) c): the space before the opening pseudo-text delimiter is required. All four are compile-time errors; the unbalanced one must not compile to a program with its procedure text deleted.

**Observed / reasoning.** CopyProcessor.ReadReplaceOperand: `while (pos < text.Length - 1 && !(text[pos]=='=' && text[pos+1]=='=')) pos++;` then returns text[start..pos] with no diagnostic when the loop hits end of text; no check of text[pos-1] before the opener or text[pos+2] after the closer. The same reader serves COPY REPLACING and REPLACE.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ReadReplaceOperand (line ~713), #ParseReplacements, #ApplyReplaceStatements
