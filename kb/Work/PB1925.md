# PB1925 — LANDED

Filed by the train-1004 lander from wave 1004 group C's report (`w1004c-PB1175-report.md`).

## Finding

**Site.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs#GateSortMergeProcedures` — the placement rule's
procedure leg (§14.9.40.3 SR3, §14.9.24.3 SR1) raises its `EditionDiagnostic` from bound statements, and a bound node
carries no source span, so the diagnostic prints without a line and column. The declarative leg
(`SortBinder.ScreenDeclarativePlacement`) is raised at bind time with a position.

**Repro.** Any negative of the `pb1139-sort-in-sort-procedure` / `pb1139-merge-in-sort-procedure` family: the `.err`
line has no position.

**Fix shape.** Carry the statement's source location on the bound SORT/MERGE node (or record it in the procedure
table the pass walks) and raise the diagnostic at it; `DiagnosticPositionTests` then covers the code.

Owner 2026-10-17: addressed in the SORT and file I/O campaign (kb/Work PB3353).

## Landed (wave 1115 group H)

**Re-probe.** The note's own site was already fixed: kb/Work PB812 (wave 1031 H) gave `BoundSort`, `BoundMerge` and
`BoundCommitRollback` an `At` cursor, so `COBOL2700`, `COBOL1572` and `COBOL1690` printed `file(line,col)`
on this build. But the note named the ROOT correctly ("bound nodes have no span") and PB812 fixed three nodes, not the
root. A corpus sweep (every `diagnostics.json` negative at its first refused edition: an error or warning line without
a `(line,col)`) and direct probes showed the rest of the pass still printing bare: the bound-arm statement gates
(`SET p UP BY`, `SET fp TO fp`, `MOVE SPACE TO numeric`, INITIALIZE REPLACING), the USAGE / PICTURE data gates and the
`>>COBOL-WORDS` SR3/SR4 checks.

**Fix (the shape that makes the next case automatic).** `BoundStatement.At` is a property of the base node, stamped
once by the one funnel every statement passes through (`StatementBinder.BindStatement`, which already positions the
diagnostic cursor); the three per-node `At` properties and their three construction-site assignments are deleted.
`VersionConformancePass.WalkStatement` and `GateSortMergeProcedures.Flag` position the sink from it, `GateDataItem` from
`DataItem.DeclaredAt`, and `ValidateCobolWords` from the new `CobolWordsOp.At` (set by the directive stage, which holds
the directive's resultant line and indicator column). A new bound statement, or a new gate over one, inherits the
position.

**Witnesses.** `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/BoundArmDiagnosticPositionTests.cs` (31 cases): every placement
refusal at its statement, nested under an IF, at each edition, with the legal twin (outside a procedure; MERGE below
2023; MERGE under `--permissive` as a positioned warning), and the SET / MOVE / USAGE / COBOL-WORDS arms.

## Landing

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/StatementBinder.cs#BindStatement` — stamps `BoundStatement.At` once, right after `BindStatementCore`; REUSE `BoundStatement.At` for any pass that runs over the bound tree (never add a per-node `At`). Trap: a record's `init` cannot be assigned after construction, so the base property has an `internal set`.
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs#WalkStatement` — positions the sink at `s.At` before every bound-arm gate; `GateDataItem` at `DataItem.DeclaredAt`; `ValidateCobolWords` at `CobolWordsOp.At`. Trap: `sink.At(unsetCursor)` leaves the enclosing position standing, which is what an implicit-series member needs.
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CobolWordsDirectiveProcessor.cs` — fills `CobolWordsOp.At` with `(directiveLine, indicator column)`; the cursor is built where the directive's line and indicator column are known and is passed into `TryParseOption`/`TryBinary`/`TryUnary`, which construct the op with it (`At` is a required positional member of `CobolWordsOp`; the unread 0-based `Line` was deleted). The syntax-only `CheckOperand` parse passes `default`.
- `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/BoundArmDiagnosticPositionTests.cs` — the fixed-form helper `Build(outer, inner, edition)` finds the inner statement's line itself; a fixed-form line past column 72 is silently truncated (a long section name made `OUTPUT PROCEDURE IS` lose its name).

**Not fixed here (other passes and files; reported as leads).** The sweep's remaining 23 position-less negatives are
report-section (RD) diagnostics, external-file consistency (COBOL1573/1575/1624), OO method-header rules
(COBOL2648/1542/1643) and OptionsBinder's COBOL0806.
