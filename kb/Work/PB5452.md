# PB5452 - the 1985 rows of unnumbered text (PB479 step 6)

**Sites:**
- `scripts/spec/data/margin-levels-1985.json` (new data, measured on the 1985 PDF)
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-unharvested-rule-blocks-1985.json` (`paragraph-blocks`, keyed by citation as its `blocks` are)
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json` (regenerated)

**What.** Measure every unlabelled 1985 paragraph after a numbered rule on the 1985 PDF (`measure_margin_levels.py
--edition 1985`); the 1985 reader then separates margin text from rule continuation, as the 2023 reader does. Adjudicate
the dispositions of Sections IV, VI to XVI and Amendment 1's Section A (design figures: 238 deontic sentences in 186
paragraphs and 1,296 indicative sentences in no row; tables and glued margin text unmeasured), by §1985:IV.3.1 and
IV.3.2, refuted as in PB5450. Mint in one landing: ids `<year>-<kind>-<locator>-p<n>` / `-T<t>.<r>`, each with its
`box`, `level` and citation `§1985:<locator> p<n>`. Section II stays outside (explanatory, as §1985:II.1 says).

**Oracle.** The 1985 PDF for levels and boxes; the 1985 text for dispositions; `cite.py --check` on every citation.

**Done when** no 1985 margin paragraph is pending, `extract_rule_catalog.py --edition 1985 --check` passes, the 1985
GAP is reported before (318 at design time) and after with the command, and every 1985 change record whose rule's text
moved is either re-stamped by `split` or listed for re-adjudication.
