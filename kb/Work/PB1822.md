# PB1822 - OPEN

Found 2026-09-30 by the R66 batch while repairing PB309 and PB1635. `specs/ISO_COBOL.md` has 569 lines `x) ...` at column 0 and 797 at three spaces. Many column-0 ones are legitimate (a clause's own lettered list), but in a rule block a lettered item after an unnumbered paragraph is a sub-item of the rule and is PRINTED INDENTED (PDF p637 14.9.10.4 GR15 a) b) c); p698 14.9.30.4 GR21 a)-g); §9.1.13.2 rule 4 a) b)). `cite.py` (SUB needs two or more leading spaces) then reports the bare rule. This is PB309's class and PB698's (page-break outdents) at larger scale.

## To do
Measure each column-0 `x)` line against the PDF with the method of `scripts/spec/repairs/rule_label_delimiter.py` (match the printed line, compare the marker's x with the rule-number and text columns of the SAME page - margins alternate 80.0/81.0 by page parity and some continuation pages lose a whole level, so compare within a page), then indent the ones printed at the sub-item level. Indenting only moves `cite.py` paths and the extractor's fold of a lettered item into its rule (already the rule's text), so it should change no catalog row; verify with `extract_rule_catalog.py` and `catdiff` (ids and texts unchanged). The 152 printed-`N.` third-level items of PB1635 belong to the same pass but DO move catalog rows (PB55 wave).
