# PB3427 — keep the harness observation log with a Linux red

Filed 2026-10-19 by the enqueue clerk. Grep of kb/Work for `observations.log`: PB3243 (a fixed shared log path) and PB3388
(landed; its trap line tells a person to copy the log by hand) only; neither makes the gate do it.

**Mechanism.** A test process records every retry it makes in `cobolnet-harness-observations.log` in the machine temp
directory (`tests/_shared/ProcessObservation.cs`). `scripts/linux-gate.sh` keeps a red's log but not this file, so after a red
whether the harness retried (PB3388's IX216A: a killed program retried over its own leftovers) is inferred from the
outcome.

**Fix shape.** The Linux gate copies the observation log beside a red's log (evidence directory) in the same step that keeps
the red's log, and a drift test pins that the script names the file. Source: `w1129a-PB3388-report.md` `## Review
findings`, the LEAD line.
