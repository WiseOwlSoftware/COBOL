# PB3435 — the leading-comma residue opening does not check that the comma begins a character-string

Filed 2026-10-19 by the enqueue clerk from `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1129b-PB3390-report.md` (PB3390 is PR #295 in the
merge queue; the sites are on its branch, so they are not on main yet). Register check: a grep of kb/Work by `residueOpening`,
`CobolErrorStrategy`, `ResidueRunTokens`, `level-number` and `missing-period` found PB3390 (the branch itself), PB3391,
PB3428 and PB3430; none records this. PB3390 is held by an in-flight implementer and was not edited.

**Mechanism.** `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolExpressions.g4:92`: the new leading-comma opening of
`residueOpening` is `{tokensAreContiguous(2)}? COMMA INTEGERLIT`. It checks only that the comma abuts the integer after it,
not that the comma begins a character-string. It therefore opens the error production in the middle of a comma-decimal
literal whose integer head a clause already took, and the refusal quotes half a literal. That contradicts the branch's
premise that `1,5` is ONE character-string (ISO 8.3.5 2), to be quoted whole.

**Fix shape.** The opening must see that the comma is not preceded by a contiguous token that already forms the literal's
head, the same shape `numericLiteralCore` uses. Keep one spelling of the shape (the PB3390 note names the drift tests that
follow it) and add a case whose clause takes `1` and leaves `,5`.
