# PB4259 — under --cluster a declined enqueue handoff can fall through to a wave-type choice, where campaign_choice replaces its reason wholesale, so the decline that design section 3.2 rule 2 and the module docstring promise never reaches the supervisor log or units.jsonl

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/next_unit.py:376` (`campaign_choice`, `base_choice`).

Under --cluster (the live loop runs -Cluster PB4147), a declined handoff `enqueue` can fall through to a wave-type choice.
There `campaign_choice` replaces its reason wholesale, so the decline that design section 3.2 rule 2 and the module
docstring promise (a reason saying the handoff was declined) never reaches the supervisor log or units.jsonl, in exactly
the case PB4180 targets.
