# PB3676 — the waiting hold's one retry is spent once per call, not once per exhausted round

**Sites:** `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/Sharing/SidecarRegionLocks.cs` (`Hold`, `lastRoundWasTheExtraOne` / `extraRoundSpent`,
the extra round in the lost-round arm; `Backoff` and `BackoffObserver`, the test seam), and the witness
`tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/RegionLocksDriftTests.cs`
(`AWaitingHoldThatSawALiveHolderBetweenTwoLostRounds_IsOwedAnotherRound`; the note first named
`SidecarColumnLockHostDriftTests.cs`, which tests the column host and holds no waiting region hold).

Filed 2026-10-24 by the enqueue unit's clerk from the group G reviewer's R2-1 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 2) on
PB3632, branch `worktree-wf_6ffe8083-b46-29` (PR #536, merged), report

## What happens

The R1-1 fix of PB3632 grants a waiting hold one extra publication round so that an exhausted publication beside a
holder that has since left is absorbed rather than answered `Unavailable`. `roundRetried` (line 92) is set once per
`Hold` call and never reset. A long wait that sees a Live holder between two exhausted rounds spends its single retry
on the first holder departure and answers `Unavailable` at the second, which is the same interleaving the fix exists
to absorb.

## Fix shape

Reset the retry allowance whenever a round observes a Live holder (progress), or make the allowance per exhausted round
and bound it by the wait's own deadline; add a driver test with two exhausted rounds separated by a holder departure.

## Landing

Re-probe on a fresh build: the note's reading was right. A one-thread driver (`RaceInjector` loses rounds 1 to 3 and releases the
first holder as round 1 begins; a second endpoint takes the byte as round 2 begins; the new `SidecarRegionLocks.BackoffObserver`
lets it go as the wait pauses) answered `Unavailable` at round 3 before the fix and `Ok` at round 4 after it.

Fix: the extra round is not a per-call flag but a property of the PREVIOUS iteration (`lastRoundWasTheExtraOne`): a lost round with
nothing in the way earns one more round unless the round just before it was itself the extra one. Any iteration in between (a
live holder seen and waited for) clears it, so progress earns a new extra round, and two lost rounds in a row with nothing in the
way stay `Unavailable` (the wait stays bounded; the R1-1 test's bounded half still holds). `Backoff` reports to `BackoffObserver`
instead of sleeping when a test sets it (the precedent of `SidecarLockFiles.PauseObserver`), so the departure between two attempts
needs no thread and no timed sleep. Sibling sweep: no other waiting hold keeps a once-per-call allowance (`SidecarColumnLockHost`
and `HandleColumnLockHost` publish without waiting; `grep "roundRetried|one more round"` finds only this site).
