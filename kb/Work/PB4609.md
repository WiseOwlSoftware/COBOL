# PB4609 — the SEARCH identifier-1 subscript is admitted from 2014; the 2014 FCD forbids it

**What is wrong.** PB4577 landed (wave 20261863, commit a89c49021 and its round) a construct row,
`search-identifier-1-enclosing-subscript-2014` in `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json`, asked in
`StatementValidation.ResolveSearchTable` for both SEARCH formats: COBOL0900 refuses `SEARCH H (GI)` (H nested in G, GI
subscripting G) at 85 and 2002 and accepts it from 2014. That edge rested on 2023 Annex E listing no SEARCH change. The
2014 cycle's FCD 1.0 (WG4, 2010-07-01, the final committee draft that became ISO/IEC 1989:2014;
`E:/COBOL-coord/specs-drafts/t2014.txt` line 32933) still says "Identifier-1 shall not be subscripted or reference
modified", as 2002 and the 2009 CD 1.2 do. Only 2023's §14.9.37.3 2) ("identifier-1 shall not be subscripted at the level
for which the SEARCH is applicable") and 3) ("Identifier-1 may be contained within one or more other tables, for which the
subscripting is still required") allow it. So `--std 2014` accepts source its standard forbids. The owner decided
2026-11-04 that the relaxation is 2023-only (PB4547, final decision).

**Expected.** COBOL0900 refuses the subscripted identifier-1 at 85, 2002 and 2014; at 2023 it compiles as now. The bare
spelling (`SEARCH H` with the enclosing subscript set in the WHEN phrase) stays legal at every edition.

**Fix shape.** Move the row's edition to 2023 and rename it `search-identifier-1-enclosing-subscript-2023` (every caller and
the regenerated `Constructs.g.cs` / `ConstructRegistry.g.cs`, with no alias, CLAUDE.md rule 4); its `citation` adds the 2014
FCD sentence; VCR Table 7 row 7.49 says "from 2023" and cites the FCD; the goldens PB4577 moved to `accept: [2014]` move to
`accept: [2023]`, the `pb4577_*` positives to 2023, and the two negatives refuse at 2014 too (rename `...-below-2014` to
`...-below-2023`). `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` D-SRCH-DYN and `docs/VERSION_TEST_MATRIX_DESIGN.md` follow in the same change.

**Re-probe (wave 20261867 B).** On the pre-fix build `SEARCH H (GI)` and `SEARCH ALL H (GI)` compiled at `--std 2014` (the PB4577 positive
`pb4577_search_enclosing_subscript_2014` printed FOUND 8 / NOT FOUND / FOUND 9 there). After the fix `cobol <case> --std 85|2002|2014` prints
COBOL0900 "a subscript on SEARCH identifier-1 requires COBOL-2023" at each subscripted identifier-1 (three errors for the positive: both SEARCH
statements and the SEARCH ALL), and `--std 2023` compiles clean. The FCD sentence: `t2014.txt:32933`, 14.9.34.2 ALL FORMATS rule 1
(outside the tracked tree, so not `cite.py`-checkable); `cite.py --check 14.9.37.3` is OK on SR2 and SR3 (2023).

**Fix.** The construct row is `search-identifier-1-enclosing-subscript-2023` (introducedIn 2023; regenerated `Constructs.g.cs`/`ConstructRegistry.g.cs`;
the one caller `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.GateSearchIdentifier1` asks the renamed constant; no alias). VCR row 7.49, `docs/CONFORMANCE.md`
D-SRCH-DYN, the `SearchIdentifier1Operand` catalog description and the code comments say 2023 and cite the FCD. Goldens: the PB4577 positive is
`pb4577_search_enclosing_subscript_2023` (accept 2023), the negatives are `pb4577-search-enclosing-subscript-below-2023` and
`pb4577-search-all-enclosing-subscript-below-2023` (refuse 85, 2002, 2014). Sibling sweep (`SEARCH ... (` over `conformance-suite` and `tests`): the
four goldens that write the enclosing subscript move to `accept: [2023]` (`pb443_search_identifier_1`, `pb877_subscripted_subscript`,
`pb1263_occurs_key_is_table_in_outer_table`, and `pb3365_search_nested_dynamic_table`, which writes `SEARCH EP(I)` and PB4577 had missed);
`l1_search_all_gr5_preconditions` was already 2023. Unit `SearchIdentifier1GateTests` is now a theory over 85, 2002 and 2014.
SR-14.9.37.3-2/-3 test-refs re-pointed by `record_verdicts` (batch `pb4609-search-identifier-1-2023`, 2 rows, GAP 1 -> 1).
