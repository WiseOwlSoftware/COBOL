# PB2283 — a theory's cases reported NEVER RAN in one gate

Found by wave 1031 group E (kb/Work PB397, report `w1031e-PB397-report.md`), filed by the train-1031 lander.

## Observation

In implementer gate 1 of group E (run `20261007T125202Z-9e20ed`, worktree `wf_12f44c89-8ac-5`), the population check
reported 8 cases of `UniversalCrossingShapeDriftTests.UniversalInvoke_CrossesTheShapeAsTheTypedInvokeDoes`
(`UCS01`-`UCS03`, `UCS05`-`UCS09`) as NEVER RAN: the leg's trx held 1 of the 9. All 9 pass when run alone, and gates
2 and 3 of the same branch ran all 9. Group E's diff did not touch that test or its subject.

## Where to look

`tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance/UniversalCrossingShapeDriftTests.cs` (the theory) and the trx-against-listing
population comparison in `scripts/run_gate_legs.py`. Candidates: theory cases whose display names collide after the
trx writer's normalization (so 9 results fold into 1), or a test host that died mid-theory with the leg still
reporting. Get the run directory's trx and `--list-tests` output before forming a hypothesis. A gate that calls a
real run a red costs a re-gate; one that folds cases could equally hide a red, which matters more.
