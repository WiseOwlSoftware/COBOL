**Why.** 2002 had a transcription (PB4813) and nothing after it: `extract_rule_catalog.py --edition 2002 --check` exited
"no rule catalog arm for the 2002 edition", and the schema held no 2002 store, so 2002 had no GAP count and no
conformance claim could be made for it.

**Decision 1: one ISO extraction, a table of edition facts (rule 5).** Measured first: the 2023 arm read the 2002 text
unchanged into 2,990 rows and 13 findings, every one a structure fact or a transcription defect. So the 2023 arm became
THE ISO extraction (`extract`, pure but for the standing lists and citations it reads) over `ISO`, one `IsoEdition` row
per ISO edition: its rule-bearing annex (2002's Annex A, the communications facility), its implementor-defined list (A.1 /
B.1), its excluded and known-empty blocks, its conventions clause, its table of contents' form, its row form (2002's rows
carry the year-prefixed id, `citation` and `page`, as 1985's do; 2023's keep the shape its 4,214-file store is keyed by)
and whether its transcription is reconciled. `rule_catalog_1985.py` stays its own reader: 1985 is not ISO-structured, and
it already shares the kind table, the heading rules, the content-keyed scan and the standing lists. The 2023 and 1985
catalogs regenerate byte-identical (sha256 6d785fcf… and 88a78bc4…, unchanged).

**Decision 2: a finding, never a guess.** What a text cannot be read as is a `Finding` (kind, clause, line, PDF page),
counted by kind. A reconciled edition fails `--check` on any (2023: zero). An unreconciled one records them in its catalog
(`findings`) for its reconciliation and fails `--check` when they change: the six of 2002 are a wrapped table cell of
§2002:15.16.2 read as ordinal 99, a nested item of §2002:14.8.1.3 GR25 at column 0 after a page break, and four
rule-shaped headings no kind types (§2002:8.8.1.3.1.3 and three "Rules" under §2002:8.8.4.1), which wait for the manifest's
adjudication. An ordinal no list explains is no longer filed as a nest with a guessed id, in any edition: it stays in the
rule it interrupts and is reported.

**Decision 3: held is not linked.** Making 2002 HELD would have moved 1985's `next_held` to 2002 and refused all 1,937 of
1985's KEPT and CHANGED records, which name 2023 rules. So the schema says `linked: false` on 2002: the edition has its
catalog, store, rows and GAP count, and the lineage bridges over it as over an edition not held (Python `editions_of`, C#
`TraceabilityInventory.Editions`, `edition_gates.repository_inputs`). The adjudication of the 1985 to 2002 records sets it.

**Decision 4: the manifest carries no disposition it cannot prove.** None of the 92 clauses the 2002 content-keyed scan
measures is word for word a clause of the 2023 manifest, so all are `pending`, each with the nearest same-titled 2023
clause as `candidate` (71). Their rules (an upper bound of 395 ordinals) are outside the 2002 denominator until adjudicated.

**Two defects found on the way, fixed at the root.** `citation.iso_regions` read an annex's text before its first clause as
the last body clause's (2002's Annex A preamble as §2002:16.1.2.1): an annex title now opens the annex's region (`§2002:A`).
`cite.norm` kept a markup tag's letter as a word, so no quotation of two underlined words could be found.

**Counts (2026-11-11).** 2002: 3,290 rows from 548 blocks (SR 1,168, GR 1,361, AR 142, RV 142, DOC 184, FMT 293; Annex A
117), 11 archaic and 129 obsolete; findings 6 (unrecognised-heading 4, unexplained-ordinal 1, flattened-tail 1); the
inventory 3,290 rules, 3,290 GAP; candidate pairs 1,955 of 3,290 (1,732 exact, 223 normalized, 2 with two candidates),
1,335 unpaired. Citation sample: 284 of 284 pass `cite.py --check` (235 numbered rules with their ordinal, 30 formats,
19 implementor-defined items) over 236 clauses.

**What the adjudication fleet needs next.** (1) The 92 pending manifest clauses, adjudicated by §2002:5.2.1 and 5.2.2 from
their candidates (they also resolve the four unrecognised headings). (2) The 2002 change records against 2023, from
`spec-lineage-candidates-2002.json`, through `record_verdicts.py --edition 2002`. (3) Then the 1985 to 2002 records, which
set `linked: true` in the same change. The transcription's findings go to PB4813's reconciliation. The gate audit
`extract_rule_catalog.py --edition 2002 --check` and the conformance target's input `spec-rule-catalog-2002.json` are to be
declared by the branches that own `scripts/gate.py` and `scripts/build_targets.json`.
