# PB6057 - 1985 headings from the Table of Contents

**Observed.** The second pass (PB6051) confirmed 32 heading defects in pages 227-466 alone (a clause one level too deep
or too shallow, a clause heading written as a paragraph, a heading on the wrong page). A scan of the whole of
`specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` finds 1,342 numbered headings: 942 at their numbering's depth, 289 one level deeper, 94 one
shallower, 15 more off, and 158 short numbered plain lines that are heading candidates. The PDF is a scan with no outline.

**The owner, 2026-11-22:** "Shouldn't the PDF Table of Contents provide the correct heading structure?" It does: the
printed contents (PDF pages 9-15, transcribed) list every Section, chapter (`6.`) and clause (`6.18 The INSPECT
Statement ... VI-94`) with its folio, 319 entries; below the clause the number fixes the depth.

**The rule:** a Section is level 1, a chapter `n.` level 2, a clause `n.n` level 3, and each further number one
level deeper. **Build:** (1) a lint in `verify_publishable.py --edition 1985` (a gate audit): every contents entry is a
heading of its level on the page its folio names, and every numbered heading in the body sits at its numbering's depth
under a contents entry; (2) a one-time normalizer applying it, every change listed; a numbered plain line becomes a
heading only when the rendered page prints it as one (read on the page, `render-spec-page.py --edition 1985`); (3) a
drift check: the 1985 rule catalog and `cite.py` locators regenerate identical after the change (a heading level is
markup; a locator is the number), and the lint flags every heading defect PB6051's verified claims name (its expected
set). Ledger entries for every repair (`LEDGER-1985.json`, README "A new repair adds its ledger entry").

**Sites:** scripts/spec/heading_levels.py (whole: the rule, `level_of`, `canonical`, `normalize` and the audit `check`); scripts/spec/verify_publishable.py:main (`main`, the audit's call beside `check_ledger`; its self-test's planted Section I marker); scripts/spec/spec_pdf.py:66 (`EDITIONS`, the 1985 body marker, now the level-1 `# <u>SECTION I: INTRODUCTORY INFORMATION</u>`); scripts/spec/reserved_word_order.py:72 (`_columns_1985`, the heading of Section V that closes the list); scripts/selftests/spec/heading_levels/test_heading_levels.py (whole); specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md; docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/LEDGER-1985.json; docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/TRANSCRIPTION-STATE.md (its 1985 section states the heading rule); docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json (regenerated: `§1985:VII.1.3.7` paragraph 1)
