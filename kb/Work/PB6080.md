# PB6080 - one integrality decision, three authors (CLAUDE.md rules 4, 5; DESIGN-architecture-review §5.1 one mechanism per job)

**Found by:** the review of wave 20262135 group B (PB2645 landing), round 1, R1-1.

**Mechanism.** The question "is this numeric value an integer" is decided in `SetAmount.Land` (carrier-neutral), in
`CobolNum`/`CobolDec.HasFraction`, and again in `CobolReport.VaryingInteger` (`runtime-io-3--duplication--f1#1`). The
`VaryingInteger` copies drop the carrier guard and re-spell the decimal test, so a carrier added to one author is not
picked up by the third.

**Target.** `CobolReport.VaryingInteger` asks the one decision (the carrier-neutral one PB2645 left in `SetAmount`) and
holds no test of its own; a drift test pins that no other site re-spells the decimal-integrality test.

**Not tracked elsewhere.** `kb/Work/PB2645/2026-11-22-landing.md` names it and defers it; PB1305, PB2647 and PB2841 are
landed and mention `VaryingInteger` only in passing.
