# PB4994 - lineage slice 3: chain state and the GAP count per edition

docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md §10.2, slice 3. `Schema.state_for` resolves a KEPT row through its chain to the newest row's verdict and evidence, and
requires the chain's test to be placed at the older row's edition; CHANGED, REMOVED and NEW rows are judged on their
own fields. The session probe's `invent` line and the ledger report each edition's rows and GAP count side by side.

**State after PB4992 (wave 20261926, group A).** In place in Python: the view computes an older row's state through its
change record (`inventory_view.state_of` -> `lineage.state_of`: a KEPT row is OK when every `to` row is OK and its
`editions` band places the chain's test at the older edition; every other row by `Schema.state_for`), against the next
held edition's view; and the session probe's `invent` line is the view's `--summary`, each held edition's rules and GAP
side by side (`invent : 2023 4214 rules · 0 GAP | 1985 2253 rules · 2253 GAP`). Still to do: the ledger's per-edition
counts (`gen_ledger.measure_inventory` and its trend read 2023 only), and the chain state in the C# view (today
`TraceabilityInventory` is the 2023 view only, so no C# engine computes a 1985 state). `build_inventory.py` no longer
exists (PB4992).

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/lineage.py` (`state_of`), `scripts/spec/inventory_view.py` (`state_of`, `summary`),
`scripts/spec/gen_ledger.py` (`measure_inventory`, `measure_commits`: the per-edition counts),
`scripts/session-probe.ps1` (whole: the `invent` line, from the view), `tests/_shared/TraceabilityInventory.cs` (`Row`, `Load`: the 1985 view and its
chain state).
