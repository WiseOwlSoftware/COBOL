# PB4994 - lineage slice 3: chain state and the GAP count per edition

docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md §10.2, slice 3. `Schema.state_for` resolves a KEPT row through its chain to the newest row's verdict and evidence, and
requires the chain's test to be placed at the older row's edition; CHANGED, REMOVED and NEW rows are judged on their
own fields. The session probe's `invent` line and the ledger report each edition's rows and GAP count side by side.

**Landed (wave 20261935, group B).** The Python view's chain state (`lineage.state_of`) and the probe's `invent` line were
in place from PB4992. This slice adds the other half. The C# view reads every held edition (`TraceabilityInventory.Editions`,
`RowsOf`, `Row.Next`) and its test-side state rule `StateAt`/`StatesOf` mirrors `lineage.state_of`, held to the Python view's
`--states --edition` by `SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests.EveryOlderEditionsRuleState_AgreesWithThePythonView` (1985: 2,253
rules, the same 1,087 GAP on both sides). The ledger reports every held edition's rules and GAP: `inventory_view.edition_views`
and `gaps` are the one read (the probe's `summary` uses them too), `gen_ledger.measure_inventory` carries `editions`, and the
page's GAP tile names each older edition's. The ledger's trend series stays the newest edition's GAP (v1.0's metric); no per-edition series is recorded, because
it would need each commit's older store joined under that commit's own schema, and the points before the 1985 store existed
have none.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/lineage.py` (`state_of`), `scripts/spec/inventory_view.py` (`state_of`, `summary`, `edition_views`, `gaps`),
`scripts/spec/gen_ledger.py` (`measure_inventory`, `editions_text`: the per-edition counts),
`scripts/session-probe.ps1` (whole: the `invent` line, from the view), `tests/_shared/TraceabilityInventory.cs` (`Row`, `Edition`, `RowsOf`, `Load`: the 1985 view),
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests.cs` (`StateAt`, `StatesOf`: its chain state).
