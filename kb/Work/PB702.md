# PB702 — OPEN — a row that became true and was not claimed

Found 2026-09-05 by the `PB341`/`PB342` implementer (report §"New defects" 2): after the per-key release ordinals,
`IndexedConnector.Rewrite` implements the whole of §14.9.35.4 GR24 (`cite.py --check 14.9.35.4 "When the value of
a specific alternate record key is not changed, the order of retrieval"` → OK 24)) — a), b), both SUPPRESS WHEN
sub-rules, the "comparison … based on the collating sequence for the file" sentence (the `KeyEq` change), and
"suppressed entries never cause a duplicate key condition". The row is `state: GAP` with no verdict. The
implementer did not claim it: its dispatch scoped it to the four rows the notes own, the edition band needs dating,
and a half-right row is the worst outcome `record_verdicts.py`'s doctrine names.

## What closes it

Read `tests/conformance/2023/altkey_suppress_when.cob` for whether the REWRITE suppression transitions (a key that
becomes suppressed, a key that stops being suppressed) are covered; if not, extend `l1_rewrite_alt_key_order` or
add a sibling with those two transitions, expected values derived from GR24's sub-rules; date the row's editions
(the a)/b) halves are 2002+, the SUPPRESS WHEN halves 2023 — a row whose sub-rules straddle editions needs the
`EDITIONS AS ADJUDICATED` sentence to say so, PB659/PB672's convention); then a batch: CONFORMS, test-ref = the
goldens, code-location = `IndexedConnector.cs#Rewrite`. A registrar-lane item, small.
