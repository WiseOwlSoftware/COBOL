**Rule it breaks:** §2023:13.18.51.3 SR1 admits SELECT WHEN at the 01 level in the local-storage and linkage sections, GR2 gives it no effect there; §2023:11.7.4 GR5 makes a method own word the one the method uses. The table rule that every lookup reads the method tier first (SymbolTable.cs TryResolveCondition, GlobalNameClassDriftTests) is broken by this one reader.

**Scenario:** CLASS-ID C. ... METHOD-ID M. DATA DIVISION. LOCAL-STORAGE SECTION. 01 R SELECT WHEN K. 05 X PIC X. 88 K VALUE "A". The 88 K registers in MethodNames.ConditionNames (DataBinder.RegisterCondition -> DeclareConditionName(cond, NameScopeOf(cond.Parent))); the method roots are in State.Forest.Roots (DataBinder.BindEntries AddRoot, OoDataBinder.Roots filters MethodScopedRoots) and OoDriver.BindClassData runs BindMethodData before BindResolve, so ResolveRecordFormatPass.Run line 50 asks SelectWhenConditionsOf(R), which reads _conditionNames only, gets 0, and reports SelectWhenClauseViolation "K is not the name of a level-88 entry" on legal source. The sibling arm: an OBJECT-level 88 K that the method K hides is accepted instead.
