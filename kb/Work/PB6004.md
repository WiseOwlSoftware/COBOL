# PB6004 - At --std 85 a debugging line of spaces before OBJECT-COMPUTER is refused COBOL7060, though §1985:VI.7.3 p1 makes it a blank line, which may stand anywhere

§1985:VI.7.3 p1: "Any debugging line that consists solely of spaces from margin A to margin R is considered the same as a
blank line" (cite.py OK), and §1985:IV.7.2.3 p1: "A blank line can appear anywhere in the source program" (cite.py OK). The
compiler refused both a bare `      D` line and one padded to margin R, standing before OBJECT-COMPUTER, with COBOL7060 (probes
E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/pb5453/probes/s-12/p04_blank_debug_line_before_objcomp.cob and p04b_blank_debug_line_padded.cob, run
before the fix: COBOL7060 at (6,8); control p04c, a true blank line, compiles).

Root: `DebuggingLineRewriter.Resolve` placed every debugging line by position, blank or not. The fix gives `Extent` a `Blank`
flag (every token after the marker on its line is spaces; a region line is never blank) and never counts a blank line as
misplaced. Sibling sweep: the other consumers of the extents (source/hidden lines for COBOL7061, `WithCarriersBlanked`, comment
lines) treat a blank line harmlessly; it holds no text either way. Witness:
`conformance-suite/cases/VI.4.3@1985-SOURCE-COMPUTER-PARAGRAPH/pb5453-blank-debugging-line-before-object-computer.cob` (accept 85).

**Sites (as changed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/DebuggingLineRewriter.cs` (`Extent`, `DebugLines`, `Resolve`).
