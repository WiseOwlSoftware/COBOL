**Fix.** `MoveEmitter.AllLiteralNumber` (replacing `AllDigitFill`) is the one place a digit-only ALL literal becomes a number for a numeric or numeric-edited receiver. At --std 85 the string is repeated to the receiver's size in standard data format characters (§1985:IV.4.2.2.2.3: digits plus a separate sign position, or the whole picture of an edited item) and the digits are an unsigned INTEGER (§1985:VI.6.19.4 GR4 b 3), so alignment and editing are the receiver's. From 2002 the digits keep the positional reading §2023:14.9.25.4 GR6 d 3 b and kb/Work PB426 give (`ALL "12"` into 9(3)V99 holds 121.21). `ConvertSource` sends a numeric-edited receiver's digit-only ALL through the numeric edit arm at every edition (it stored the repeated characters as the edited image before); `IsNumericOperand` names it. `FigurativeToNumericImageStore` stores the byte-fill value into a fixed-point receiver with a native carrier (the item's own byte form decodes the fill characters, `NumericRenderer.CarrierOfImage`, no digit truncation); a floating-point receiver stays loud.

**Open reading.** §2023:14.9.25.4 GR6 d 3 b says the figurative constant is "treated as if it were an unsigned integer" of N digits where N counts the digits right of the decimal point, which can be read as 121.00 as well as 121.21; the compiler keeps the positional reading from 2002 on (reachable only under --permissive, COBOL0902).
