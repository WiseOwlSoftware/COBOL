# PB5703 - at --std 2002 exception-names 2002 does not list (EC-FUNCTION and its level-3 names, EC-BOUND-FUNC-RET-VALUE, EC-BOUND-SET, EC-BOUND-TABLE-LIMIT, EC-DATA-NOT-FINITE, EC-DATA-OVERFLOW, EC-FLOW-SEARCH) are accepted in >>TURN, RAISE, USE and RAISING

**Sites:**
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Exceptions/ExceptionCatalog.cs#Build (the L3 local function's introduced = 2002 default)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ExceptionNameRule.cs#Window` (whole: the report names no member; unchanged, the window it applies is the catalog's)
- `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/ExceptionCatalogEditionDriftTests.cs#EveryCatalogName_IsIntroducedByTheFirstEditionThatNamesIt` (the drift test that holds `EcInfo.IntroducedIn` to the three transcriptions)

**Mechanism.** ExceptionCatalog.Build registers each level-3 name through L3(name, fatality, introduced = 2002): an entry without an explicit later edition defaults to 2002, so ExceptionNameRule (IntroducedIn > dialectLevel) passes it at --std 2002. Main has since banded many 2023 names (the probe now draws COBOL0878 for 17 of 27), but the defaulted entries remain.
