**Target:** One span-based literal/comment lexeme reader in CobolNet.Editions beside CobolSpace (the lowest layer, so CompilerDirectiveLine can use it; e.g. a CobolSourceText static class): OpeningDelimiterAt(span, pos) -> (prefix length, LiteralKind) per §8.3.5 5), LiteralEnd(span, quotePos, ref state) honouring the doubled quotation symbol with an explicit carry-across-line state, and CommentIndicatorAt(span, pos).
