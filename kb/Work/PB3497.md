# PB3497 — the not-retaken arm of RetakeKeptHandle is unpinned

Lead R3-1 (low) of the branch review of PB3484 (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w1140b-PB3484-report.md`).

`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/KeyedConnector.cs` about line 341: `RetakeKeptHandle`'s not-retaken arm, taken with record locks in hand (the unpublished ids non-empty, `Republish` skipped, `Kept.Endpoint` left on the given-back endpoint), has no test. The report says the refused retake needs a host race, but the other cause, the retake's `Attach` failing, is forceable: the R1-2 witness's `RefusingPageMedium`, kept armed through `AbandonOpen`, refuses the retake's root read the way it refused the OPEN's (PB3483's swap test uses `refuseOnce` to avoid exactly that).

**Fix shape (MANDATORY-PRACTICES I11, abnormal path).** Add the witness: arm the medium through `AbandonOpen`, assert the status and what another run unit reads of a record the CLOSE locked (§14.9.6.4 GR9; `cite.py --check` it first). Decide from the result whether the arm needs a code change (a record lock left unpublished would be a wrong answer) or only the pin.

**Sites:**
- `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/KeyedConnector.cs`
- `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/Store/CommitRollbackRuntimeTests.cs`

## Landed

Re-probed on a fresh build: the arm is reachable without a host race. A refused retake is forced by a medium that refuses reads
after the OPEN's own refusal: the give-back of the kept handle detaches the store's last handle, so the store has no file, and
the retake's `Attach` reads the store's pages again. Measured before the fix (Windows, both keyed organizations; A under APPLY
COMMIT and SHARING WITH ALL OTHER READs record 1, REWRITEs record 7, is CLOSEd; re-OPEN I-O under SHARING WITH NO OTHER, the
medium refusing reads): the OPEN answers '30', and then another run unit's READ of record 1 answers '00' (the record lock the
CLOSE kept is unpublished, §14.9.6.4 GR9, §9.1.16), its writable OPEN's dead-owner step finds the transaction's liveness byte
free and rolls A's live transaction back (READ of record 7 answers '23'). A wrong answer, not only a missing pin.

Fix (`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/KeyedConnector.cs`): the attach's two host-level steps (the liveness byte through the new
handle, the record locks rehomed to its endpoint) are one method, `CarryKeptHoldings`, which reads no page; `RetakeKeptHandle`
runs it whether or not the attach's adoption of the store succeeded, and keeps the handle in the kept state either way. Only a
retake the HOST refuses (the window the design names) still leaves the kept state without its handle. Both callers of the retake
are covered: `AbandonOpen` (every unsuccessful ending after the swap) and `HeldByAnotherHandle` (the OPEN OUTPUT probe).

- `CommitRollbackRuntimeTests.A_reopen_whose_kept_handle_cannot_adopt_the_store_again_leaves_what_the_close_kept_standing`
  (both organizations): '30', the transaction stands, another run unit meets '51' on the locked record and on the changed one
  and its REWRITE answers '51', an OPEN NO OTHER is refused '61', the re-OPEN is the transaction's, the ROLLBACK restores.
  Red before the fix ('00' where '51'), green after.
- `CommitRollbackRuntimeTests.A_reopen_output_refused_by_another_run_unit_with_the_medium_refusing_the_retake_keeps_the_kept_state`
  (the `HeldByAnotherHandle` caller): red before the fix, green after.
- Sibling sweep: `RetakeKeptHandle` has two callers, both covered; the sequential connector keeps its handle by another
  route (no give-back, no retake).

Found and NOT fixed (a separate mechanism, reported as a lead): the kept state of the unsuccessful OPEN has a handle but its
store has no file, so a ROLLBACK or COMMIT before the connector's next OPEN is refused ('30', the file named) although the medium
answers again (`KeptFile.CommitTransaction`/`RollbackTransaction` -> `KeyedStore.Commit`/`Rollback`, "no writable handle left").
The transaction is safe (its liveness byte is held, the dead-owner step settles it at the run unit's end); the program's own
ROLLBACK is what fails.

For the next implementer: `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/KeyedConnector.cs#CarryKeptHoldings` owns the two host-level steps a CLOSE under commit control kept (the liveness byte through the new handle, the record locks rehomed to its endpoint), and `#RetakeKeptHandle` runs it whether or not `Attach` adopted the store; reuse it for any new path that puts a handle back on a kept state. Trap: a refusing medium must stay armed through `AbandonOpen` and the file must be `DropCache`d AND the kept handle given back (SHARING WITH NO OTHER on the re-OPEN, or an OPEN OUTPUT beside another run unit) before the retake reads a page at all; the give-back detaches the store's last handle, which drops its file. `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/Store/CommitRollbackRuntimeTests.cs#A_reopen_whose_kept_handle_cannot_adopt_the_store_again_leaves_what_the_close_kept_standing` is the model.
