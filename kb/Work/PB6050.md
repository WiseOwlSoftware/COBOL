# PB6050 - suite 85 Stage 0: the coverage-point catalog of the 213 1985 FMT rows from the decided figures, and each point's credit from WiseOwl's parse of the tests (design §3.4, S14 and S15 cut to 85)

Revision 4 of `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-conformance-suite.md` (§8 Stage 0, §13), decided in kb/Work PB6046.

Read every alternative (bracket, brace, choice indicator, ellipsis, optional word, format) of the 1985 general formats from
the decided figures of `figures-1985` (the OCR'd diagrams are lossy: CLAUDE.md rule 1), and credit a point when WiseOwl's
parse of a test accepted at 85 contains it (decision 8; no verdict depends on it). Output: points per FMT row, points
credited, points owed; feeds PB6047's points term and Stage 3's obligations.

**Sites:** conformance-suite/harness/formats.py (`read`, `simplify`, `agrees`, `DECIDED_READINGS`); conformance-suite/harness/points.py
(`figures`, `points_of`, `build`, `load`, `dump`, `main`); conformance-suite/harness/coverage.py (`Matcher`, `Catalog`, `frames_of`,
`operand_ok`, `tokens_of`, `record`, `inputs_of`, `reading_of`, `unmet`, `report`); src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/ParseNodeReader.cs (`Read`, `Collect`);
src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Cli/Program.cs (`CheckBatchName`, `PointsBatchName`, `BuildPointsBatchParser`, `RunPointsBatch`, `PointsBatchLine`, `Parse`);

(read only)

**Landed.** The catalog is 3,846 points in 252 formats of the 213 FMT rows (design section 3.4, "The catalog at 85"), read from the
transcription's decided figures with every tree held to the words of its drawing; `coverage.py` credits them from WiseOwl's parse of the
2,447 tests accepted at 85 (`cobol points-batch`, the one new compiler surface: it reads the parse and decides nothing). At this
landing `python conformance-suite/harness/suite_status.py --edition 85` prints `points 994 owed` (2,852 credited). The rows no parse
node carries (COPY, REPLACE, a section header, a nonnumeric literal, the four division-structure rows) keep their points owed and
`coverage.py --edition 85` names them. Leads carried in the landing's report: the operand-kind map, a nightly record re-run,
the two figures read by hand, and the cross-module credit of a statement two modules print.
