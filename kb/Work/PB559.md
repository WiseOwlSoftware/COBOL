# PB559

## Mechanism — value-clause-p2
THE GRAMMAR TAKES THE CROSS-PRODUCT OF A TWO-LINE REQUIRED CHOICE. src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4:620-621 writes both valueClause alternatives as `(VALUE | VALUES) (IS | ARE)?`, which admits all four pairings. The printed general formats admit two: Format 1 is `VALUE IS literal-1` (no ARE at all) and Formats 2, 3 and 4 print `{ VALUE IS / VALUES ARE }` as a two-line required choice, i.e. VALUE pairs with IS and VALUES with ARE. Only Format 5 detaches the connective, and SR39 exists precisely to say which word pairs with which there.

### Expected, from the standard
13.18.63.2 Format 1 as printed (PDF p.546 = printed 516, rendered at 200dpi): `VALUE IS literal-1` -- VALUE underlined, IS not, and no ARE anywhere. Formats 2, 3 and 4 print `{ VALUE IS / VALUES ARE }` as a plain required choice between the two rows. SR39 (13.18.63.3) states the pairing for the one format that detaches the connective: "If the word VALUE is specified, the word IS may be specified. If the word VALUES is specified, the word ARE may be specified." (cite --check OK) `VALUE ARE 1` and `VALUES IS 1` are therefore nonconforming source at every edition and shall be diagnosed.


⛔ **THE SCREEN IS IN THE BINDER, NOT THE GRAMMAR, AND THAT IS THE RULE'S SHAPE, NOT A CONVENIENCE.** The
constraint is about which FORMAT is being written, and the format is a fact only the caller has: Format 1 and
Format 3 are the SAME token sequence and differ by the entry's level-number (SR33), Format 4 by the section.
A parse rule cannot see a level-number, so the grammar deliberately keeps the `(VALUE|VALUES) (IS|ARE)?`
superset and `DataBinder.CheckValueConnective` takes `pairedConnective` from its caller. **Which arm did you
fix? All three live ones**: the condition-name arm (Format 3) and the data-item/table arm (Formats 1 and 2)
in `DataBinder.cs`, and the report-section arm (Format 4) in `DataBinder.Reports.cs`. The screen-section
`valueClause` (`CobolScreen.g4`) is not a fourth arm — it is refused as a declined facility by
`ScreenFacility`. Format 5 is skipped by name in the Format-3 arm, because it detaches the connective
entirely and is already refused whole with COBOLNET1708.

**Goldens:** `tests/conformance/negative/pb559-value-format1-values` (the Format-1 half) and
