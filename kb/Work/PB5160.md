# PB5160 - at --std 85 an apostrophe-delimited nonnumeric literal compiles: STR_BODY takes the apostrophe at every edition and LiteralScreen.GateFormat has no arm for the delimiter

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4` (`STR_BODY`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/LiteralScreen.cs` (`GateFormat`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Common/CobolLiteral.cs` (`IsApostropheDelimited`, new)
- `tests/version-matrix/constructs/apostrophe-quotation-symbol-2002.json` (the construct row; `Constructs.g.cs`, `ConstructRegistry.g.cs` regenerated)
- `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` (Table 7 row 7.72)

**Mechanism.** 1985 delimits a nonnumeric literal by the quotation mark only (§1985:IV.4.2.2.2.1: "delimited at the beginning and at the end by the separator quotation mark"; §1985:IV.4.2.1 SR5 makes the quotation mark the separator). The lexer fragment `STR_BODY` takes a quotation mark or an apostrophe as the delimiter at every edition, and `LiteralScreen.GateFormat`, the one edition screen of a literal's written form, refuses the X literal and the zero-length literal at 85 but has no arm for the apostrophe. So `DISPLAY 'HI'` compiles and runs at --std 85 in every position probed (DISPLAY, MOVE, VALUE, a level-88 VALUE, an ALL literal), with no diagnostic even under --flag-extensions.

**Found by.** The 1985 code read (2026-11-10; its records are `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/for-register.json`): each rule below was read against the code sites it names and probed at `--std 85`, and found non-conforming. Verdict, harm, evidence, repro and observed text are the records'; the rows' verdicts are recorded on another branch.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids of the list below.

Code read: §1985:IV.4.2.1 SR5 reads "The punctuation character quotation mark is a separator", requires the opening one to be "immediately preceded by a space, left parenthesis, or opening pseudo-text delimiter" and the closing one to be "immediately followed by one of the separators space, comma, semicolon, period, right parenthesis, or closing pseudo-text delimiter". The adjacency half conforms at 85: SeparatorRule.Violations (run over every token by Frontend.LexAndParseOnce, edition-invariant) asks OpeningDelimiterSeparated and ClosingDelimiterSeparated of each literal token and reports COBOL2633; probes refused DISPLAY"HI", S"A", ="ABCDEF", "A"S, "ABCDEF"OR and "A","B" (with COBOL2631 for the comma) and accepted a literal after a left parenthesis, before a right parenthesis, comma-space, semicolon-space and period, and inside ==...== of a REPLACE. The delimiter half does not: the row's change record names the 2023 difference as §2023:8.3.5 5), "Either an apostrophe or a quotation mark may be used as the quotation symbol character in opening and closing delimiters", and the compiler applies that sentence at every edition. The lexer fragment STR_BODY takes an apostrophe as an opening and closing delimiter, and LiteralScreen.GateFormat, the one edition screen of a literal's written form, gates X"..." (COBOL0900 at 85, probed; N"..." and B"..." are refused with their classes) and the zero-length literal and has no arm for the apostrophe, so at --std 85 an apostrophe-delimited literal is a separator-delimited literal. The witness is DISPLAY"HI", refused at 85. Same mechanism and one fix with row 1985-FMT-IV.4.2.2.2.1.1 (the nonnumeric literal's format); no kb/Work note owns it.

**Repro (`1985-SR-IV.4.2.1-5`).** `cobol.exe <file> --std 85 --source-format fixed --run`

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. APOS85.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       P1.
           DISPLAY 'HI'.
           STOP RUN.
```

**Expected (from the 1985 text, the record's).** A diagnostic at 'HI' (line 5) and no object program. §1985:IV.4.2.1 opens "A separator is a character or two contiguous characters formed according to the following rules" and its rule 5 names one literal delimiter: "The punctuation character quotation mark is a separator." §1985:IV.4.2.2.2.1: "A nonnumeric literal is a character-string delimited at the beginning and at the end by the separator quotation mark." The apostrophe is no separator under any of the nine rules and is not in the 1985 COBOL character set (glossary, page III-3), so 'HI' is neither a literal nor any other character-string.

**Observed (the record's).** exit 0, no diagnostic (none with --flag-extensions either); the program displays HI. The same statement written DISPLAY X"4849" is refused at 85 with COBOL0900 (the hexadecimal alphanumeric literal requires COBOL-2002).

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Have the lexer record the delimiter on the literal token (it already distinguishes the X, N and B prefixes) and give `GateFormat` an arm that refuses an apostrophe delimiter below the edition that introduced it, as one registry construct. The token kind, not a re-scan of the text, carries the fact, so every consumer of a literal sees one answer.
