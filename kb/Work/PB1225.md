## Landed (wave 1007 C, 2026-10-03)

Re-probed on the w1007c build first: `01 K CONSTANT 8.` / `01 K2 CONSTANT "AB".` -> COBOL0001 'cannot parse construct
near 8', and so did `CONSTANT LENGTH W` and `CONSTANT BYTE-LENGTH W`. PDF p.355 (anchor page-385) RENDERED: only 1/01,
CONSTANT, GLOBAL, BYTE-LENGTH, LENGTH and FROM are underlined, so IS, AS and OF are optional words
(`cite.py --check 5.2.3 "Optional words are reserved words or context-sensitive words. They are shown in uppercase and
not underlined in general formats"` -> OK).

- **Grammar** (`CobolData.g4`): `constantEntryBody : CONSTANT (IS? GLOBAL)? (AS? constantValue | FROM cobolWord)` and
  `constantValue : LENGTH OF? dataReference | constantByteLengthWord OF? dataReference | ...`. The sweep this note
  asked for ("recheck every optional word") found OF required in both length phrases, the same defect: fixed together.
- **SR9 specification** (`DataBinder.Constants.cs#WrittenSpecification`): a length phrase's specification is its
  keyword and its operand, never the optional OF, so `LENGTH W` and `LENGTH OF W` are one specification of
  data-name-2 (§13.10.3 SR9 compares "the specification of ... data-name-2"), while BYTE-LENGTH OF stays distinct.
- Goldens: `2002/pb1225_constant_optional_words` (K=8 K2=AB K3=7 K4=7 K5=7 K6=5, with the SR9 duplicate) and
