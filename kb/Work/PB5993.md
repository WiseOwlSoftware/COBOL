# PB5993 - at `--std 85` at least 25 diagnostic codes cite only a later edition's rule (§2023/§2002/§2014) where 1985 has its own text: COBOL0880, 0804, 1560, 2495, 4733, 2691, 0865, 0877, 0876, 5434, 5962, 1505, 1641, 2496, 1565, 0831, 0803, 0898, 1507, 0827 (measured), and 2403, 0854, 1927, 2422, 2198, 1858, 2894 (found by hand)

Measured on 2026-11-20: every conformance case the suite expects refused at `--std 85` was compiled (2439 of 2482; 43 case
files not found by id) and each diagnostic whose citation names §2023/§2002/§2014 and no §1985/§1989 counted; the edition-gate
codes COBOL0900/3734/3735 (which name the edition that adds a feature) excluded by design: 20 codes, 42 occurrences
(E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/cite85-class.json; script: the session scratchpad's cite85_run.py, to move into the repo as a drift
check). Seven more found by agents probing other questions: COBOL2403 and 0854 (cite 2023 where 1985 has a rule), COBOL1927
(says BINARY for COMP), COBOL2422 (report site cites the general SIGN rule), COBOL2198 ("DISPLAY or NATIONAL" at 85), COBOL1858
(ORGANIZATION default, cites §2023:12.4.5.10.3 GR6 for §1985:VII.2.6.3 GR3), COBOL2894 (cites §2023:4.2.10 for
§1985:I.1.5.2.5.2). The measurement sees only diagnostics the suite exercises at 85, so the class is larger. Same family as
PB5978 and PB5933; the fix is PB5978's shape: one citation table keyed by edition, every message's citation chosen from the
edition in force, and a drift test that compiles the 85 refusal corpus and fails on a non-1985 citation.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (whole: the per-edition citation of every descriptor)
