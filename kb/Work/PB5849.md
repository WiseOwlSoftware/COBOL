# PB5849 - OPEN: is "the same usage" a group and its subordinate must specify the alternative as written, or the manner of representation?

**Ruling: NOT DECIDED (an open reading).** A group declared USAGE BINARY accepts a COMP subordinate. Is "the same usage" of SR2 the alternative as written, or the manner of representation? The data-model design chose the second on purpose. The row `1985-SR-VI.5.14.3-2` stays `undecided`. Harm: under_rejects under one reading (not set while the row is open). The checker's confidence: medium (the reading is the question). Every other pair is refused (control: BINARY over PACKED-DECIMAL, COBOL1927).

**The checker's evidence (rulings.json, copied).** `§1985:VI.5.14.3 SR2` says "the same usage must be specified in both entries".
- READING A (the usage specified is the clause's alternative as written): BINARY and COMPUTATIONAL are distinct alternatives of the general format with distinct general rules (`§1985:VI.5.14.4 GR3` radix 2; GR4 "a radix and format specified by the implementor"), so the pair is two usages and must be refused; the implementor's choice to represent both alike does not change what was specified. Under A the row is UPHELD-b, harm under_rejects (a program the next implementation, whose COMP is not binary, gives a meaning the standard forbids). Cost: COBOL1927 compares the written usage words (COMP and COMPUTATIONAL still one, being one word's abbreviation), a one-site change in UsageInheritancePass plus a design-doc correction to `DESIGN-data-model.md` 2.7.1 item 3.
- READING B (a usage is a manner of representation): `§1985:VI.5.14.4 GR2` says the USAGE clause specifies the manner in which a data item is represented in the storage of a computer, and the implementor, who must specify COMPUTATIONAL's representation (`§1985:C.2` item 24), has specified it as BINARY's (`docs/conformance/7/DOC-A.1-208.md`: "COMPUTATIONAL ... is identical to USAGE BINARY"); then the same manner is specified and the row CONFORMS. Cost: a 1985 register sentence (DOC-A.1-208 cites only the 2023 text) stating the determination and that SR2 compares the manner.
- What would settle it: whether SR2's "usage" is the clause's specified alternative or GR2's "manner". The word "specified" and the five-way list lean to A, but GR2 defines the clause by its manner, so the text admits both. `DESIGN-data-model.md` 2.7.1 item 3 records the folding as "this implementation's BINARY identification" with no 1985 (or 2023) text for treating it as the SAME usage; DOC-A.1-208 documents representation, not SR2.
