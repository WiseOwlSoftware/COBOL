timer's limit) and creates one timer with `Timeout.InfiniteTimeSpan` as the period, so `CONTINUE AFTER 5000000 SECONDS` (about 58 days) stops
waiting after 49.7 days: the deadline queue entry fires at the cap and the program continues early. Expected: the CONTINUE AFTER statement

drives it. Observed: one timer, capped. PB4744 (open, the exchange slice that owns CONTINUE AFTER) may take this with its other work; the
clerk filed it separately because the site is landed code in `MessagingPeer` and the note does not name `Arm`.
