## Measured

### Finding type-clause-p1 — ISO/IEC 1989:2023 §13.18.57.2 Format 2; §13.18.57.3 SR12; §13.18.57.4 GR6c, GR7d; §5.2.3 (rejects-legal-source; rows FMT-13.18.57.2, SR-13.18.57.3-12)

**Mechanism.** The report-group TYPE clause grammar (CobolReportWriter.g4 reportGroupType) omits the optional words ON/FOR and the whole [OR PAGE] phrase of §13.18.57.2 Format 2, so every CONTROL HEADING / CONTROL FOOTING spelled with them is a parse error, and the OR PAGE semantics (§13.18.57.4 GR6c/GR7d) and SR12's OR PAGE leg have no model to hang on.

**Repro.** out13/probe/type-clause-p1/fmt_on_or_page.cob: `RD R1 CONTROL IS CX PAGE LIMIT 20 LINES.` / `01 G1 TYPE IS CONTROL HEADING ON CX OR PAGE LINE PLUS 1. 03 COLUMN 1 PIC X(3) VALUE "HHH".` / a TYPE DE detail; also fmt_for_final.cob (`TYPE CH FOR FINAL`, `TYPE CF ON CX`), fmt_or_page_nodata.cob (`TYPE CH OR PAGE`), sr12_orpage_nopage.cob.

**Expected, from the standard.** Rendered PDF p524 (folio 494): `{CONTROL HEADING|CH} [[ON|FOR] {data-name-1|FINAL} [OR PAGE]]` and `{CONTROL FOOTING|CF} [[ON|FOR] {data-name-2|FINAL}]`, ON/FOR not underlined (§5.2.3 optional words), OR and PAGE underlined. All four probes are conforming and must compile; the CH with OR PAGE must additionally print after each page advance following any page heading (§13.18.57.4 GR6c) and move the body-group upper limit (GR7d). In an unpaged report, OR PAGE must be rejected under §13.18.57.3 SR12, not by the parser.

**Observed.** Pinned adj-d1 cobol.exe, --std 2023: COBOL0307 unexpected 'ON' / unexpected 'FOR' / unexpected 'OR'. reportGroupType reads `(CONTROL HEADING | CH) (FINAL | dataReference)?` and `(CONTROL FOOTING | CF) (FINAL | dataReference)?` — no ON, FOR, OR PAGE. BindGroupType has no OR PAGE flag, the runtime ReportWriter has no per-CH page-advance re-presentation. The PH/PF legs of SR12 are enforced (COBOLNET0899 report-page-type-requires-page). Separately, specs/ISO_COBOL.md §13.18.57.2 lost BOTH format diagrams in transcription (heading, 'Format 1 (type-name):', then the SR anchor) — a transcription defect to repair alongside.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolReportWriter.g4#reportGroupType; src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs#BindGroupType; src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/ReportWriter.cs
