# PB3718 — size groups by predicted turns

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (group formation through `fix_clusters.py` and `campaign_clusters`), `scripts/orchestrator/turns_by_group_size.py` (the per-note cost, by area), `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` (the turn budget beside the count cap).

The owner, 2026-10-25 ~00:05 PDT, answering the operator's explanation (keep 8, cut to 4-5, or size by predicted turns; recommended: cut to 4-5 now, predicted-turns sizing as the follow-on, and the 400 cap applied regardless): "Both your recommendations". PB3704 sets a count cap of 5 now (groups of 4-5; the owner: "Plan predicted turns later"); this note is the follow-on the owner chose. `turns_by_group_size.py` fits a fixed group cost plus a per-note cost, and the per-note cost differs by area. The planner should predict each candidate group's turns from that fit (per area when the data supports it) and fill a group up to a turn budget under the implementer cap (PB3701), keeping the count cap only as a ceiling. Measure: the split rate and the turns-per-group error of the prediction over a day of waves. Self-tests: a group of cheap notes holds more than a group of expensive ones; no group's prediction exceeds the budget.
