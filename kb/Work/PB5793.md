# PB5793 - a comma or semicolon separator inside a subscript list is refused where a space may stand (every edition)

**What is wrong.** A comma or semicolon followed by a space (`, ` or `; `) in a position where the separator space may stand, inside a subscript list, is a parse error at every edition: `W-E (I +, 1)`, `W-E (, I)` and `W-E (I, )` each stop with `COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near ', '` (the third near `')'`), exit 65, at `--std 85` and at `--std 2023`. `§1985:IV.4.2.1 SR2` lets the comma and semicolon followed by a space stand anywhere the separator space is used; SR8 and SR9 let a space optionally follow `(` and precede `)`, and a space is required between `+` and its operand in relative subscripting, so all three are legal 1985 source. The ordinary forms (`W-E (I + 1)`, `W-T (2 : 2)`, and `IF I = 1, DISPLAY`) compile (sep4). I ran sep1 at `--std 85` myself: refused, `COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near ', '`. The 2023 text says the same (`§2023:8.3.5 SR2`), so the 2023 row `SR-8.3.5-2` is wrong the same way (cause b).

**Mechanism (the checker's reading).** Inside a list region the lexer keeps `, ` as `FNARG_SEPARATOR` (kept, not skipped) and the subscript rule admits it only between two subscript items. The same shape is in the function-argument list.

**Sites:**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4#FNARG_SEPARATOR` (kept, not skipped, `InListRegion()`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#subscriptItem` (the subscript list rule, line about 819: `subscriptItem ((FNARG_SEPARATOR | COMMA)? subscriptItem)*`)
