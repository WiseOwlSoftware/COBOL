# PB4425 — a literal at a sentence start after a completed sentence draws the bare COBOL0001

**Found:** by the PB4224/PB4225 finisher (lead 1). Same family as PB4224 (a literal where a sentence must begin) but a different arm: PB4224's fix is the statement arm of `AdmittedKinds.Refusal`; here the position follows a period inside a paragraph, where the parse admits the next-program start, so the open path of `AdmittedKinds.Refusal` is taken and the written-kind sentence is not produced.

**Repro** (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261819b/r1.cob`):
```
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           DISPLAY "A". "B".
           STOP RUN.
```
draws only `COBOL0001: unexpected '"B"'`. The same literal after `THEN`, `WHEN` or an operand draws the PB4224 sentence.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs#Refusal` (open path) and `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (the position's admitted set at the end of the procedure division's last sentence).

**Fix direction.** At a sentence start inside a paragraph the statements are legal (the next sentence), so the refusal must state that a statement may begin here and the literal cannot begin one, derived from the sentence boundary rather than from the parser's lookahead set; give it a golden at each edition. Sweep: a literal after a paragraph header, after `END-IF.`, and after the last sentence of a section.

## Landed (wave 20261822 A)

Re-probed on 83a1e47f4: `DISPLAY "A". "B".` drew `COBOL0001: unexpected '"B"'`, and so did every sibling the note asked
for (after a paragraph header, after `END-IF.`, after a section header, after the division header, a figurative).

**Fix.** Hint 0i, when the parser's own admitted set (the program's end, where every procedure-division list has let
the literal pass) names nothing, asks `OffendingPosition.SentenceMayBeginAt`: the rule whose last token is the separator
period before the token, re-simulated over its own tokens through its periods (`ResimulationWalk.Over` with
`throughItsPeriods`), takes every statement start. The refusal is then `AdmittedKinds.Refusal` over FIRST(statementBlock):
the PB4224 statement arm, at every edition. A user-defined word or an integer (a paragraph-name need not contain a
letter, ISO 8.3.2.2 3)) is left to the missing-period guess (COBOL0307).
Witnesses: `WrittenKindRefusalTests` rows literal-after-a-sentence (2023, 85), literal-after-end-if,
figurative-after-a-paragraph-header (2014), literal-after-a-section-header (2002), literal-after-the-division-header;
the refusal `pb4425-literal-where-the-next-sentence-begins` at every edition.

**Sites (as landed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`GuessCobolIntent` hint 0i,
`OffendingPosition.SentenceMayBeginAt`, `ResimulationWalk.Over`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs`
(`Refusal`, unchanged, now also called over the sentence boundary's set), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md`
section 3.9, `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/WrittenKindRefusalTests.cs`,
