# PB3611 — raise the cluster cap for same-file groups

## Symptom

The fix lane's cluster cap of 5 notes per group (`fix_clusters.py`) makes more branches than the landing rate carries: 1.5 landings per hour as measured by `landing_measure.py`; wave 1157 made 11 branches for 15 notes.

**Sites:** `scripts/fix_clusters.py`, `model_rules.json`.

## Fix shape

Raise the cap for same-file groups to 8 to 10, with the cost constants re-measured (`model_rules.json` `wave`), keeping one mechanism per group. The owner's question, whether a bigger cap costs review depth, is answered by measurement on one wave: findings per note before and after.

## Register

Member of campaign PB1957 at tier 1. `inventory_rows: []`, `closes_rows: []`.

## Landed

`scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` `wave.max_notes_per_group` is 8, from 5 (`max_notes_per_group_source` carries the
measurement; the cap lives there, since `fix_clusters.py` is the public submodule's and takes it as `--max`). Re-probe:
`plan_wave.py --budget-points 3 --dry-run` grouped 5 notes into the DataBinder.cs group at cap 5 and 7 at cap 8 (PB1319
and PB3162 name DataBinder.cs as a site and joined it instead of each becoming a group and a same-file successor);
nothing else in that plan moved, because most open files carry fewer than 5 notes. Why 8 and not 10: over 314 reports
with a turns figure, turns grow about 66 + 13 per note (1 note 79 turns, 3 notes 108, 5 notes 130, 6 notes 150), so 8
notes is about 170 turns and 10 about 196, past the 190-turn point where reports begin to SPLIT at the 220-turn cap. The
cost constants are unchanged: `group_cost` is linear in the notes. Every doc that restated "five" now names the key
(`plan_wave.py`, `DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md`, the workstream `SKILL.md`, `DESIGN-architecture-review.md`).
Not measured here: the owner's question whether a bigger cap costs review depth (findings per note before and after);
the reports of the first wave planned at cap 8 carry the figure.
