# PB4608 — Annex E is not complete; re-check the 2014 edges against the 2014 FCD

**Finding.** `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` said the 2023 spec documents the 2014→2023 delta "completely" through
Annex E, and constructs.json rows take a 2014 edge from Annex E's silence ("ratified decision #1"). The 2014 cycle's FCD 1.0
(WG4, 2010-07-01, 926 pages, the final committee draft that became ISO/IEC 1989:2014), downloaded 2026-11-04 to
`E:/COBOL-coord/specs-drafts/COBOL ISO_IEC 1989_2014 latest_draft.pdf` with its text in `t2014.txt`, shows one omission:
it still says "Identifier-1 shall not be subscripted or reference modified" for SEARCH (line 32933), while 2023's
§2023:14.9.37.3 rules 2) and 3) allow the enclosing tables' subscripts, and Annex E lists no SEARCH change. PB4577 landed a
2014 edge from that silence; PB4609 moves it to 2023.

**Why it matters.** A wrong 2014 edge either accepts illegal source at `--std 2014` (an edge too early) or rejects legal
source there (too late). PB3602 re-derived 45 provisional edges from the 2002 text and the 2009 CD 1.2, rating a 2014
verdict from the CD medium-high; the FCD is a year later and closer to the published text.

**Work.** (1) Every constructs.json row whose 2014 edge rests on Annex E's silence or on the 2009 CD: read the governing
rule in `t2014.txt` and record a verdict with the quoted FCD sentence (page and line); an edge that moves becomes a defect
note. (2) Every VCR row that states a 2014 rule from the CD: the same. (3) The VCR's Annex E method (its admission test,
kb/Work PB344) gains the rule that the FCD decides a 2014 edge. Fixed already: the VCR scope note no longer says "completely".

## The FCD's limits

The FCD (2010) is not the published 2014 text: the floating-point usages were reworked after it (FLOAT-BINARY-7/16/34 in the
FCD, 32/64/128 in 2023 with no Annex E entry), and DYNAMIC LENGTH is absent from it though 2023 E.3.3 item 17 presumes it predates
2023. So an FCD ABSENCE alone never proves a 2023 edge, while an FCD sentence that matches another text (2002, or a later CD's
change bars) does.
