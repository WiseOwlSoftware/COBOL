# PB6120 - the PB5650 determination is unrecorded and one witness is missing

1. **Determination not recorded (CLAUDE.md rules 1 and 6).** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Globalization/MonetaryPlacement.cs:47`
   `ForPicture` is an implementor determination: POSIX `sep_by_space` is defined relative to the currency symbol, and dropping every space
   slot for a picture without a currency symbol (including the sign-to-value space of `sep_by_space` 1 with `sign_posn` 4, .NET pattern 16
   `"$- n"`) is a choice. `docs/conformance/4/LOCALE.md` determinations and `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-locale-facility.md` §8 do not say so.
2. **Witness missing.** `conformance-suite/cases/13.16.38@2002-PICTURE-CLAUSE/pb5650-locale-no-currency-symbol-no-space.cob` carries de-DE,
   pt-BR and nl-NL legs. `kb/Work/PB5650.md` prescribed a U+2212 (hr-HR) leg and the repro-1 legs for rows -13 and -14, so row -13 (the sign
   represented as the locale specifies) is closed without the discriminating non-ASCII sign witness. The PB5650 report acknowledged the omission.

**Fix.** Record the determination in both documents in one change and add the hr-HR leg to the golden.
