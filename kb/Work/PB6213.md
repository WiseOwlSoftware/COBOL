# PB6213 — a re-read's edition-consulted entry flattens GateSr5's three rows

**Mechanism.** PB2338's finisher re-read the nine 1985 code reads whose members the binder split moved. The re-read of
`tests/version-matrix/code-reads-1985/1985-SR-VI.6.17.3-2.json` (line 24, the edition-consulted entry) says
`FigurativeAssociation.GateSr5` permits "a figurative constant to a numeric receiver through 2014 and refuses it at 2023". GateSr5
has three distinct rows: a multi-character ALL literal (GateSr3) is refused from 2002; a digit-only ALL literal to an integer
receiver stays valid at 2023 (obsolete flag COBOL0903); only QUOTE, HIGH-VALUE and LOW-VALUE are removed at 2023 (COBOL0902). The
record main held before the re-read stated these rows correctly.

**Fix shape.** Restore the three-row statement in the record's edition-consulted entry (a re-read at the same member, imported with
`code_reads.py import --replace`; `code_reads.py --check 1985` stays GREEN). Then look for the same flattening in the other eight
re-read records of that branch.

**Sites:** `tests/version-matrix/code-reads-1985/1985-SR-VI.6.17.3-2.json:24`, `FigurativeAssociation.GateSr5`
