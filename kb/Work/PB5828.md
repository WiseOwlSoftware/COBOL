# PB5828 - a tooling-lane gate cannot finish while the gate pool is full

Source: the report of wave 20262056a (kb/Work PB5764), `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w20262056a-PB5764-report.md`, its gate section and its LEAD; measured on this host on Sunday 2026-11-15 between 18:53 and 20:01 PST, while three whole-population gates held the pool's three slots. kind: defect. process_only: true. No harm flag. Severity: the filer's choice is MINOR unless the register's own rule says otherwise. area: tooling/gate. related: PB5764, PB5697, PB4395, PB1590.

**Sites:** none (a pipeline finding: the changes it asks for are decisions listed below, not yet made; the files it names are in the sections and the Lead).

## What happened (the implementer's three gate runs of one tooling-lane branch)
- Run 000001-20261116T025348Z ended `=== BUILD-LOCAL GATE: RED — SELF-TESTS RED`. Its self-tests took, on the saturated host: `enqueue.py --self-test` 3385.5 s, `prune_worktrees.py --self-test` 1812.6 s, `buildgraph.py --self-test` 914.7 s, and the hang detector stopped `scripts/guard-verify.sh --self-test` at 3602.7 s. The one real red was `test_forbidden_commands.py`: one hook call ran past the test's fixed 30 s `subprocess.run` timeout and raised TimeoutExpired; the same file passed 311 of 311 standalone.
- Run 000002 was NOT RUN: `gh` gave no version within 60 s.
- Run 000003-20261116T040111Z was GREEN (the tooling lane), after the pool had drained.

## The three mechanisms
1. **A tooling gate takes no slot and so competes, unadmitted, with every gate that holds one.** `scripts/gate_slot.py` admits whole-population gates by slot and by committed memory (kb/Work PB5697); a tooling-lane gate runs its self-tests outside that admission, on whatever CPU three test hosts leave. Under a full pool a self-test that takes seconds takes most of an hour, and the hang detector's fixed hour (`scripts/self_tests.py`) then reports a slow run as a hang.
2. **A tool-version probe with a fixed 60 s limit turns load into NOT RUN** (the `gh` version read of the toolchain check at the head of the gate).
3. **Fixed per-call time limits in hook self-tests.** PB5764 changed `test_forbidden_commands.py`'s 30 s to a 300 s hang guard. Two siblings still carry `timeout=30` on main: `scripts/hooks/test_worktree_rm_allow.py` lines 93 and 111. The review skill's rule (no fixed time limit in a test; kb/Work PB1590) names the class: a limit measures the host, not the code.

## What a fix must decide (not decided here)
- Whether a tooling gate is admitted through the pool as a light class, or its self-tests run at a priority that three test hosts cannot starve, and what the hang detector measures instead of wall time (progress: output, or CPU time consumed).
- The version probe's limit under load: a retry with the reason printed, never a silent NOT RUN.
- The 300 s guard PB5764 introduced deserves the same question as the 30 s it replaced: it is longer, not different in kind. Nobody has reviewed it.

## Lead nobody has checked
`guard-verify.sh --self-test` re-ran in run 000001 because a new file under `scripts/selftests/` changed one of its declared inputs (`content:scripts/selftests...`). Whether that declared input is wider than what the script reads was not examined.
