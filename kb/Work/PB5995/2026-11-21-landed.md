## 2026-11-21 - the structural fix, points 1 to 4 (wave 20262131 group f, train B)

`scripts/spec/work_editions.py` holds what `editions:` means, its check, the seed and the recorded narrowing.
`work.py check` refuses a non-terminal note without the field, a value outside `work.EDITIONS`, and an open note that
cites an edition's text (`§1985:`, `§1989:`, `§1993:`, `§2002:`, `§2014:`, `§2023:`) without declaring it unless its
newest `set-editions` judgment holds the declared value. `work.declared_editions` is the field's one reader;
`edition_status.py` part 3 and `gen_ledger.py` read it and nothing else, and `EDITION_PATTERNS` and `touches` are
gone from `edition_status.py` (the text match survives only as `work_editions.SEED_PATTERNS`, the seed of
`backfill-editions` and of the note-filing tools, never a measure). The backfill runs once on the train branch after
the merges (`python scripts/spec/work.py backfill-editions --write`), which lands the check and the field together;
on a scratch copy of the tree it wrote 1560 notes and left the per-edition open-note counts unchanged (1985 133,
2002 319, 2014 254, 2023 505). Narrowing the over-counted notes is a reader's and a refuter's judgment fed to
`work.py set-editions --from <file.json> --write`.
