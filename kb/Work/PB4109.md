# PB4109 — a level-01 multiple LINE clause reports the group twice in one RD

## What happens

A multiple LINE clause on the 01 entry of a report group compiles while the group is never referenced. When the group is
GENERATEd, every spelling fails, at 2023, 2014 and 2002 alike (probes KL01 to KL05, KL12, KL14, KL15, LE2014a/b,
LE2002a/b; at 85 the clause's edition gate fires first):

    rc=65 COBOL1920 report group 'DET-A' is described in more than one report description entry ('R-1', 'R-1')

The same clause at level 03 (its Annex D placement) prints lines 3 and 5 for every spelling (ML01 to ML10).

## The rules (each checked with `cite.py --check`, OK)

- §13.18.35.3 10): "If more than one integer-1 or integer-2 operand is specified, the clause is referred to as a multiple
  LINE clause." No level restriction is stated.
- §13.18.35.4 9): "A multiple LINE clause is functionally equivalent to a LINE clause with a single operand, together with
  a simple OCCURS clause".
- §13.18.38.3 1) a) (OCCURS) bars an entry that "Has a level-number of 01, 66, 77, or 88".

Whether a multiple LINE clause is legal at level 01 is the fix's first question (an adjudicator, from those rules). Either
answer makes today's behavior wrong: if it is legal, the compiler rejects legal source; if it is not, the compiler
accepts it until the group is referenced and then gives a false diagnostic that names one RD twice instead of the
rule broken. The diagnostic is false in both cases, hence `misleads_diagnosis`; the fix sets `rejects_legal_source` or
`under_rejects` once the legality is ruled.

## Mechanism and site

`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs#BindReportEntry` (about lines 2866-2869) replays a repeating
entry once per operand (§13.18.35.4 9)); at level 01 every replay adds a new `ReportGroupModel` named DET-A, and
`ReportGroupResolution.Resolve` (about lines 72-90) then finds DET-A twice in R-1.

## Landed (wave 20261780 group D)

**Re-probe.** `KL01` (`01 DET-A TYPE DE LINE NUMBERS ARE 3 5.` with a `02 COLUMN 1 PIC X VALUE "A".` child, GENERATEd) still drew `COBOL1920 … ('R-1', 'R-1')` on the PB4109 build, as did the spellings `LINE ARE`, `LINE IS`, `LINE NUMBER`, `LINES`, `LINE NUMBERS + 1 + 2`, `LINES ARE 3, 5` and `LINE NUMBERS ARE 3, + 2`.

**Ruling (the note's first question): legal at level 01.** §13.18.35.3 SR10 states the multiple LINE clause's additional rules a) to d) with no level restriction (`cite.py --check 13.18.35.3 "If more than one integer-1 or integer-2 operand is specified"` OK); §13.18.35.4 GR9 makes it "functionally equivalent" to LINE plus a simple OCCURS, an equivalence of FUNCTION and not of syntax: the OCCURS clause's own syntax rules do not carry over (SR25 would demand a STEP phrase of every absolute multiple LINE clause, and none exists), so §13.18.38.3 SR1 a) (`cite.py --check 13.18.38.3 "Has a level-number of 01, 66, 77, or 88"` OK) bars an OCCURS clause the source does not contain. Refusing the clause at level 01 would be rejecting legal source (CLAUDE.md rule 3). So `rejects_legal_source` is set: the compiler rejected the legal source the moment the group was GENERATEd.

**Fix.** `BindReportEntry` opens a `ReportGroupModel` (and reads the TYPE and NEXT GROUP clauses) only on a level-01 entry's FIRST repetition (`opensGroup = level == 1 && ordinal == 0`); the replays multiply the group's report lines, as they do at every other level. The replay's `VerticalCursor` and `st.Line` are no longer reset per repetition, so the §13.18.35.4 GR4c trial sum still measures the whole group.

**Witnesses.** Conformance `13.18.35-LINE/pb4109-level-01-multiple-line` (2002; absolute `LINE NUMBERS ARE 3 5` then relative `LINE PLUS 2 PLUS 3`, successive VALUE operands per repetition, expected lines derived from GR4c, GR5a, GR5b3, GR7, GR9 and GR23) and the refusal `pb4109-level-01-multiple-line-at-85` (COBOL0900, the multiple LINE clause entered at 2002).
