# PB5613 - at --std 2002 floating-point literals with a 32-36 digit significand or a four-digit exponent are accepted

**Sites:**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol` .Frontend/Common/NumericLiteral.cs#CheckFloatingPointForm

**Mechanism.** NumericLiteral.CheckFloatingPointForm checks the 2023 limits (significand 1..36 digits, exponent up to 4 digits) at every edition; 2002 allows 1..31 and 3.

**Inventory.** Rows claimed: `2002-SR-14.8.25.2-3`.

**Rules (each citation checked with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`, verdict OK).**

- `§2002:8.3.1.2.2.2`: "The significand shall be 1 through 31 digits in length."
- `§2002:8.3.1.2.2.2`: "shall have a maximum of three digits and no decimal point"

**Repro.** Scratch originals under `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-20261114/read-2002/defects-work/` (p/mul-fl-sig33.cob); the text is inline. Command: `cobol.exe p/mul-fl-sig33.cob --std 2002 --run`

`p/mul-fl-sig33.cob`:

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. MULFLSIG33.
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01 W-N  PIC 9(4) VALUE 2.
       01 W-G  PIC 9(6) VALUE 0.
       01 I    PIC 9(4) VALUE 0.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       MAIN.
           MULTIPLY 1.00000000000000000000000000000000E0 BY W-N.
           DISPLAY "W-N=" W-N " W-G=" W-G " I=" I.
           STOP RUN.
```

**Observed.** MULTIPLY 1.00000000000000000000000000000000E0 BY W-N (33-digit significand): compiles, prints W-N=0002 ..., rc=0.

**Expected (from the 2002 text).** refused: the literal is not a 2002 numeric literal.

**Sibling sweep.** every 8.3.1.2.2.2 formation row at 2002; fixed-point literal digit limits (2002: 31) and the 2014 limits.

**Other editions.** 85: n/a (no floating-point literal). 2014: unknown (read 2014 8.3.3.3).
