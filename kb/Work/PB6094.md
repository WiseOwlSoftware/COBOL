# PB6094 - the loop writes to its own account's lane

**Observed** (2026-11-22): account 1 ran `orchestrate.ps1 -Cluster PB1754`. Its supervisor posted "Ledger publish owed at
87ca4265c8 (account1)" and its land lane posted "Landing stalled: PR #1952", both to `to-operator`, the inbox of account
2's attended session. An artifact belongs to the account that published it (PB2481), so account 2 could only decline the
publish and relay it, and the stall alert reached the session that had not dispatched the branch.

**Mechanism.** `orchestrate.ps1` sent `--to operator` and `land_lane.notify_operator` sent `to="operator"`. Both were
written when only account 2 ran the loop. `mailbox.account_lane`, the one rule for which lane a process belongs to, which
the dispatch ledger already uses (PB4190), was not consulted.

**Fix.** `mailbox.send --to account` (`TO_ACCOUNT`, `mailbox.recipient`) resolves to `account_lane` of the sending
process. The supervisor's publish message and the land lane's alerts (renamed `notify_account`; the review lane's hold
alert calls it) use it. `progress.py` already posted stalls to the dispatching lane. Self-tests: the mailbox self-test
covers `recipient` for both accounts and for a loop unit; `land_lane/test_tick.py` case 22 checks that the HALT alert
reaches the running account's lane and not the other one. Docs: `mailbox.py`'s header, landing.md and
DESIGN-orchestrator-loop (the HALT paragraph and the split plan's `notify_account`).
