# R68 — dispatch guard, fix-what-you-find, Sonnet bundles, quota override

**Observed 2026-09-30:** the orchestrating session hand-wrote three implementer briefs and dispatched them through the Agent
tool without loading the `workstream` skill. The briefs dropped P2 (an agent that ends its turn kills its own gate), P3, P4,
P6 to P11 and I7; the session also skipped O1, O2, O3 and O8. The owner: "I wrote all those so you would act correctly",
then "Reconfigure such that all future sessions cannot skip learning this".

**Decisions (owner):**
1. **No dispatch without the skill.** `scripts/hooks/dispatch_guard.py` (PreToolUse on `Agent|Workflow`) refuses any Workflow call
   and any `cobol-implementer` or `cobol-lander` Agent call until the transcript shows the `workstream` skill was loaded, and
   refuses an implementer or lander call whose prompt does not point at a rendered spec or a template that `check_practices.py`
   passes. MANDATORY-PRACTICES O9; CLAUDE.md rule 9; self-test `scripts/hooks/test_dispatch_guard.py` in the gate and CI `audits`.
2. **Fix what you find (MANDATORY-PRACTICES I9).** An agent that has already read and understood a file and finds new issues
   there fixes them in the same change, with golden and sibling sweep, unless the effort is so excessive (a redesign beyond its
   slice, or its turn budget) that the issue goes to `kb/Work/` as a note instead. Written into the three implementer templates;
   `check_practices.py` refuses a brief without `FIX WHAT YOU FIND`.
3. **Sonnet implementers and bundles.** Implementers may run Sonnet (newer than P1's Opus default for this wave), and a bundle of
   5 to 10 bugs lands as ONE commit after each bug is tested individually (WIP checkpoints are squashed before the single gate).
4. **Quota.** The day-4 cap was overridden and day 5's allowance (71.4 %) may be used now; the standing daily cap otherwise holds.

**Not decided here:** whether P1's role-to-model table should change for good. Measure the Sonnet bundles' overturn and red rates
first (kb/Work PB1721 telemetry), then decide.
