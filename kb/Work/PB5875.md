# PB5875 - at --std 85 the decline of RECORD DELIMITER IS STANDARD-1 (COBOL1778) cites only 2023 text, where the 1985 ground is the 1985 standard's own

Found by the implementer of kb/Work PB5844 (report `w20262084a-PB5844-report.md`). Its words, unchanged:

"COBOL1778 at --std 85 cites only 2023 text (§2023:4.2.6, A.3 item 26) though §1985:C.3 / §1985:I.1.5.2.5.1 are the 85 ground; `EditionContext.Declined` composes the descriptor's one message, so a per-edition posture citation needs the Declined mechanism to pick by edition (`DiagnosticCatalog.RecordDelimiterUnsupported`, `EditionContext.Declined`). misleads_diagnosis only."

The fix chooses the citation by edition in the one mechanism that composes a decline's message, `EditionContext.Declined`, so that every declined facility can carry an edition's own ground and none carries another edition's.

**Where:** `DiagnosticCatalog.RecordDelimiterUnsupported` holds the one message that names only 2023 text; `EditionContext.Declined` composes it at every site.

**Sites:**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs#RecordDelimiterUnsupported`
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/EditionContext.cs#Declined`
