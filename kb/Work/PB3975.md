# PB3975 — DIVIDE ... REMAINDER stores the remainder after a quotient size error

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs` (`ArithmeticEmitter.EmitDivideRemainder`, the
`else` arm that runs when the statement has no size-error phrase or checking is off).

Filed 2026-10-28 by the enqueue unit's clerk from the PB3869 implementer's lead, carried unchanged. Not re-probed.

## Rule

ISO/IEC 1989:2023 14.9.12.4 General rule 6 c) (checked with `cite.py --check`): "If the size error condition is not
raised, a subsidiary quotient is developed ... The remainder is calculated as indicated in General rule 7 and is stored
in the data item referenced by identifier-4 unless storing the value would cause a size error condition, in which case
the content of identifier-4 is unchanged". The remainder step is conditioned on the quotient raising no size error.

## Repro (measured by the PB3869 implementer)

`DIVIDE 1000 BY 4 GIVING A REMAINDER R` with `A PIC 99` and `R PIC 9`, no `ON SIZE ERROR` phrase (or checking off):
the quotient 250 overflows `A`, and the program still stores the remainder, printing `50 0`.

## Scope and disposition

Under a size-error phrase the clause leaves identifier-4 unchanged and the emitter honors it. When the statement has no
phrase, or checking is off, the quotient's result is implementor-defined (14.7.5), and the report records that
DOC-A.1-70 should decide whether the remainder is stored then. Fix at the root with that decision recorded in
`docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (DOC-A.1-70), a golden per arm, and a sweep of the sibling lanes (`NativeSubsidiaryQuotient`,
`CarriedSubsidiaryQuotient`).
