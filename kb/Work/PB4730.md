# PB4730 - a mixed selection list holds a float item's content to 34 digits

**Witness.** `DP FLOAT-LONG 2.5E+40`, `ONE PIC 9 VALUE 1`, `X5 PIC 9(5)`. `MOVE FUNCTION MAX (DP ONE) TO X5` and `MEDIAN (DP ONE DP)` store 00000,
where `MOVE DP TO X5` stores 73792. DP's binary64 value is 25000000000000000155002161260194579873792 exactly (41 digits). MAX returns "the
content of the argument-1 having the greatest value" (§2023:15.59.4 1)), MEDIAN of an odd count "the content of the argument" (§2023:15.61.1), MIN
the content of the least (§2023:15.63.4 1)), so the stored low digits are those of the 41-digit expansion.

**Mechanism.** A selection list that mixes a float item with a fixed-point item rides the SDIDI (the `RenderDec` selection arm), which holds the
float's content only to 34 significant digits (§2023:8.8.1.5.1 leaves the conversion to the implementor, and PB65 / PB4568's `CobolExact` intake takes
a binary64 "through its §2023:8.8.1.5.1 conversion"). Within 34 digits the value is exact (`MIN (A1 ONE)` = .1000000000000000055, p5 S04), so the
defect shows only past the 34th significant digit. Different carrier from PB4622's classification fix, hence a separate note.

**Owed.** Take a binary64's exact expansion onto the exact form where it fits 77 digits. Probes and the all-float comparison:
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-w20261870/a/determination.md` (section 5, S5).

**Sites:** the `RenderDec` selection arm of `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs`, and the binary64 intake of `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolExact.cs`.

**Landed (wave 20261872, group A).** Re-probed on the build (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261872a/pa/p5.cob`, golden program): `MOVE FUNCTION MAX (DP ONE) TO X5`, `MAX (ONE DP)`, `MEDIAN (DP ONE DP)` and the two-receiver MOVE store 73792 (as `MOVE DP TO X5`); the main build stored 00000.

Fix, two halves: (1) `CobolExact.FromBinary64` / `CobolBig.FromBinary64` take a float ITEM's content as its exact expansion (`m x 5^k` at scale `k`, every digit; 0.1 is its 55 places, DP the 41-digit integer), and `NumericRenderer.ExactOperand` uses it for a `Real` operand that is not `Approximate` (an engine approximation keeps its shortest decimal); (2) the selections return the selected argument whole: `CobolIntrinsics.MaxExact`, `MinExact` and `MedianExact` return the `CobolExact` itself (an even MEDIAN, the mean of the two middle values, its SDIDI quotient), `RenderDec`'s MAX, MIN and MEDIAN native arms are `NumX.Exact`, and `FunctionValueLane.NativeSdidiBodiesWithExactResult` names the three (with the drift test's derivation corrected: a `when !num.StandardDecimal` arm is the NATIVE one).

**Goldens:** `conformance-suite/cases/15.59.4-Returned-value-rules/pb4730_mixed_selection_keeps_a_float_items_content` (2002, 2014, 2023), refused at 85 by `wiseowl/diagnostics/pb4730_mixed_selection_below_2002.json`; unit `CobolBigExactFormTests.FromBinary64_IsTheExactExpansion` and the ported `CobolExactIntrinsicsTests` selections.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs#RenderDec` (the MAX/MIN/MEDIAN native arms); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs#ExactOperand`; `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Roslyn/RuntimeApi.cs#ExactFromBinary64` (new); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/FunctionValueLane.cs#NativeSdidiBodiesWithExactResult`; `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.ExactValue.cs#MaxExact`, `#MinExact`, `#MedianExact`; `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolExact.cs#FromBinary64` (new); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolBig.cs#FromBinary64` (new); `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/FunctionValueLaneDriftTests.cs`.

**Review round 1 (finisher, wave 20261872 group A).** R1-3: a float RECEIVER got the selected float item's content through the SDIDI's 34-digit image,
truncated one binary64 below the item (`COMPUTE AF = FUNCTION MAX (DP ONE)` then `IF AF = DP` was NE). An exact native value now lands in a float
receiver from all its digits, once: `FloatResultant.FromExact` over the `IBinary64Magnitude` questions (the 128-bit carrier's `ExactMagnitude` and
`CobolBig.Binary64Magnitude`), handed the final transfer by `NumericRenderer.Settle` when `ReceiverContext.LandsAsBinary64`; an approximation that entered the exact lane
remembers its binary64 (`CobolExact.FromApproximation`). Goldens as PB4622 (legs D to F); unit `CobolBigExactFormTests.FromExact_*`. R1-4: a float item's
content is formed on the 256-bit form first (`CobolExact.FromBinary64`, `CobolBig.SplitBinary64`).
