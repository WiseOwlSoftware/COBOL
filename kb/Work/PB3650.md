# PB3650 — a docstring misstates the report-order tie

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`load_reports`, `report_order`).

## What happens

The docstring says two reports written within one file-system tick tie on the first two keys (write time, wave). They tie on both only when they also share a wave number; reports of different waves are ordered by the second key. The sentence overstates when the name tie-break (the third key) is needed.

## Fix direction

Reword the sentence: the name breaks a tie only between reports of one wave written in one tick.
