## Landed

Built from DESIGN-message-control.md section 5 with no socket and no `RunUnit` change. The protocol is a pure state machine (statements and `ProtocolEvent`s in, `ProtocolAction`s out); `IPeerTransport` is the medium the peer (S2b) executes the actions on; `InMemoryTransport` is the scripted medium of `unit:MessagingProtocolTests`. The design text is corrected where building it proved it wrong: a requestor's HELLO carries no served name, so the name check lives at the requestor; a CLAIM crossing a WITHDRAW is ignored, not a violation; a link the protocol closes after a bad HELLO is burned for the current SEND (the refusal rule alone cannot bound that loop).
