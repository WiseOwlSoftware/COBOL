**Fix:** decide, per gated word, whether a statement-position occurrence is the keyword's misuse (so the scope-terminator or statement
refusal is the true diagnosis) rather than a user word, and derive the set from the grammar, not a hand list.

## Landed (wave 20261785 group C)

**Re-probe on the build (reproduced):** at --std 2023 `MOVE 1 TO W-N END-DISPLAY.` drew `COBOL0901: 'END-DISPLAY' is a reserved
word in COBOL-2023 and cannot be used as a user-defined word`; END-ACCEPT, END-SEND and END-RECEIVE the same (END-RECEIVE at 85
too); `READ F ADVANCING ON LOCK RETRY 3 TIMES.` drew COBOL0901 at RETRY. A probe that printed the strategy's own message under
the re-code showed `[COBOL0306] … END-DISPLAY appears without a matching DISPLAY statement` and `[COBOL2269] … The READ statement
ends before this token`.

**Root (the filed one was wrong).** Nothing parsed the word as a data-name: the parse failed at it, the error strategy named the
true fault, and `CobolErrorListener.SyntaxError` then re-coded EVERY syntax error whose offending token was a reserved
reservation-gated word to COBOL0901, after the strategy had ranked its hints. ISO §8.3.2.1 1) ("Reserved words shall not be used as
user-defined words or system-names") is broken only by a USE as a user-defined word, and where §8.9 reserves the word the token IS
its keyword. The §8.9 arm moved into the strategy's one report path (`CobolErrorStrategy.ReservedWordUse`): it is the report only
where a user-defined word could stand at the token (`OffendingPosition.TakesAUserWord`: the parse there takes an IDENTIFIER, the
construct closed just before it would, or a period would let a paragraph header begin there), and not where the token is a scope
terminator no open statement owns written where a statement may end (`AStatementMayEndBefore`; ISO §14.5.3.2). The recovery
(`IsReportedReservedOperand`) reads the same decision, and so does the single-token deletion, which asks it of the token's own position before its report is made (`IsReservedOperand`, main's PB3248 x PB4133 review R3-1; hint 13's test is `UnownedScopeTerminator`, so the decision reads only the position); the listener only drops a second COBOL0901 at one token. Derived from the
ATN (expected sets, the terminator map), no word list. `DISPLAY END-RECEIVE.` (DISPLAY still needs its operand) keeps COBOL0901,
as `user-word-end-receive` pins.

**Witnesses.** `conformance-suite/cases/14.5.3.2-Explicit-scope-termination/pb4132-stray-end-display` (COBOL0306 at 2002/2014/2023),
`conformance-suite/cases/14.9.30-READ/pb4132-read-retry-after-advancing-on-lock` (COBOL2269 at 2002/2014/2023);
`conformance-suite/cases/14.5.3.2-Explicit-scope-termination/pb4132-stray-end-display-sentence-start` (COBOL0306 at 2002/2014/2023,
review R1-1); `ParseRecoveryTailTests.AStrayTerminatorBeginningASentence_DrawsExactlyOneDiagnostic` (every terminator, read from
the ATN, beginning a sentence after another and beginning the paragraph's first) and
`AStrayGatedTerminator_IsTheMisplacedTerminator_AtEveryOtherEditionThatReservesIt` (every gated terminator in all three stray
positions at every edition before 2023 that reserves it; 2023 is the all-terminator sweeps'); the old IsGatedWord carve-out that
pinned COBOL0901 is deleted; `ReservedWordPositionConformanceTests.ARetryPhraseAfterAdvancingOnLock_IsTheReadsRefusal_Never0901`
and `AReservedWordWhereAUserWordStands_Draws0901` (the other arm, now with the written header `END-DISPLAY.` and the sentence-start
`CONSTANT MOVE 1 TO W-N.`).

## Review finisher (round 1 of the branch review)

**R1-1 (blocking) reproduced and fixed.** On the merged build, at --std 2023, `MOVE 1 TO W-N.` then `END-DISPLAY MOVE 2 TO W-K.`
drew `COBOL0307: cannot parse construct near 'MOVE'. A period may be missing at the end of the previous sentence.` at MOVE (END-ACCEPT,
END-SEND, END-RECEIVE the same; also as the paragraph's first sentence, the procedure division's first, and after a SECTION header),
where END-ADD drew COBOL0306 at the word. Root: `paragraphName` and `sectionName` reach `reservedGatedWord` through `procedureName`
(kb/Work PB4206), and at the start of a sentence that header reading was the only one a gated word had, so the prediction took it and
the parse died at the next token. Fix in the grammar: `{procedureHeaderHere(DOT)}?` on `paragraphName` and
`{procedureHeaderHere(SECTION)}?` on `sectionName` take a word §8.9 reserves here as a header's name only where the header follows it
(ISO §14.4.3 "A paragraph consists of a paragraph-name followed by a separator period"). Elsewhere it fails AT the word, and
`ReservedWordUse` decides: COBOL0306 for a stray terminator, COBOL0901 for a word whose header's user word stands there
(`CONSTANT MOVE 1 TO W-N.` at 2002). A WRITTEN header (`END-DISPLAY.`) still parses and draws the funnel's COBOL0901 for its name: the
text matches the paragraph format and no other, and a COBOL-85 paragraph so named is refused for its name where 2002 reserved it.
Sibling sweep: `reservedGatedWord` is reached in the procedure division only through `procedureName`, whose reference slots (PERFORM,
GO TO, ALTER, the SORT/MERGE procedures) are where a user-defined word stands, so the two header slots are the only arm.
