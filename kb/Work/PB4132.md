# PB4132 — the reservation gate hijacks a keyword in statement position

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/ReservationGateRewriter.cs:49` (`ReservationGateRewriter.ParseToFixpoint`, `ReservationGateRewriter.Retype`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4` (`reservedGatedWord`).

New defect 1 of PB3843's implementer ("note-ready, none filed"; also in PB3843's landed note under "Not fixed here (leads)"). The
gated-word alternative `reservedGatedWord` (CobolParserCore.g4:185) holds END-ACCEPT, END-DISPLAY, END-RECEIVE and END-SEND, so a stray
one in statement position parses as a data-name and the §8.9 funnel answers COBOL0901 where hint 13 (the scope-terminator hint) is never
asked, instead of COBOL0306. Likewise a misplaced RETRY after `ADVANCING ON LOCK` reads as a user word (COBOL0901) where the READ
statement's refusal (COBOL2269) would name the fault.

**Repro:** `MOVE 1 TO W-N END-DISPLAY.` (probe of the PB3843 implementer); the RETRY variant is the source of
`conformance-suite/cases/14.9.30-READ/pb331-read-two-from-one-lock-bracket` (re-pinned by PB3843 to `ADVANCING ON LOCK` with
`IGNORING LOCK`, whose old RETRY-bearing source drew COBOL0001 only as the cascade).

**Fix:** decide, per gated word, whether a statement-position occurrence is the keyword's misuse (so the scope-terminator or statement
refusal is the true diagnosis) rather than a user word, and derive the set from the grammar, not a hand list.
