# PB5449 - the mechanism for rows of unnumbered text (PB479 step 1)

**Sites:**
- `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (`prose_paragraphs`, `SENTENCE_END`, `PARAGRAPH_CONTINUATION`, `extract`, `manifest_of`, `IsoEdition`, `PARAGRAPH_BLOCKS`)
- `scripts/spec/rule_catalog_1985.py` (`parse`)
- `scripts/spec/cite.py` (`DESIGNATOR`, `TABLE_DESIGNATOR`, `rule_book`, `check`)
- `scripts/spec/citation.py` (`RULE`, `rule_designator`, `validate`)
- `scripts/spec/record_verdicts.py` (`validate`, `main`)
- `scripts/spec/inventory_view.py` (`lower_bound`, `check`)
- `scripts/spec/measure_margin_levels.py` (a new tool, `measure_sublist_levels.py`'s pattern)
- `scripts/spec/data/margin-levels-2023.json` (new data)
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-unharvested-rule-blocks.json` (`paragraph-blocks` takes the disposition schema; every margin paragraph entered `pending`)
- `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CatalogCoverageDriftTests.cs` (`ProseClauses_AreHeldSentenceForSentence`)

**What.** Build "Phase A, continued" points 1 to 5 of section 3 of `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md`
for 2023 and 2002 (the ISO reader) and 1985 (its reader), with every margin paragraph `pending`, so the catalogs do not
change:

1. one margin-paragraph segmentation (rule blocks included), repairing the three defects the design's self-attack
   found in `prose_paragraphs`: a colon paragraph owns its dash or lettered list (§2023:14.6.2.2's fifth paragraph is
   today "— a STOP statement — ..." alone), a table is never paragraph text (§2023:14.5.1's ninth paragraph is today
   Table 12), and a sentence may end in a closing bracket or quote after its stop while a tail joins only across page
   furniture (§2023:8.4.2.1 reads as two paragraphs today, the uniqueness sentences joined to "(See 8.4.6, Scope of
   names.)"; 64 lines of the 2023 text end ".)");
2. the ids `p<n>` (n over all margin paragraphs), `T<t>.<r>`, `T<t>`, `C`, and the kind set by §2023:5.3.1 (AR or RV in
   an intrinsic function's clause);
3. `measure_margin_levels.py` measuring every unlabelled paragraph after a numbered rule on the 2023 PDF (design: 122
   at level 0, 279 at the rule's indent or deeper, 16 ambiguous, 85 not found, each of the last two read on the
   rendered page and recorded with it), `--check` holding the transcription to the data;
4. the dispositions (kind, `lead`, `restates`, `not-rule` with a closed reason, table `rows`/`whole`, clause `whole`,
   `pending`) keyed by text key; the RULE CATALOGS audit's three refusals; the verbal forms per edition as data;
5. `cite.py --check` designators `p<n>` and `Table <t> row <r>`, and `OK §<clause> (unnumbered prose, pending)` for a
   quotation in a margin paragraph with no row (kb/Work PB222's repair: the two commands in PB222 print different
   paths);
6. the move batch a regeneration writes when an adjudicated row's id moves, and `record_verdicts.py`'s `split`
   operation (old text = new text + carved paragraphs, in order, checked; parent digest re-stamped; carved rows'
   records copied; `next.digests` re-stamped unless the record's reason quotes the carved words);
7. `inventory_view.lower_bound` counting pending margin paragraphs and tables for every held edition.

**Oracle.** The printed PDF for the levels; `cite.py --check` for every new designator; the extractor's `--self-test`
on planted text for the segmentation (a dash list, a table, a ".)" ending, a page-break tail, a paragraph split by a
planted repair moving `p` ids and producing the move batch).

**Done when** `extract_rule_catalog.py --held --check` passes and the three committed catalogs regenerate
byte-identical (the 18 `shape: paragraph` rows included); `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check "§2023:8.8.4.2.1"
"A relation condition shall contain at least one reference to an operand that is not a literal"` no longer names
GR13; the session probe prints 2023's rule count as a lower bound with the pending count; and each new refusal was
seen to fire once on a planted failure.
