# PB5449 - the mechanism for rows of unnumbered text (PB479 step 1)

**Sites:**
- `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (`prose_paragraphs`, `SENTENCE_END`, `PARAGRAPH_CONTINUATION`, `extract`, `manifest_of`, `IsoEdition`, `PARAGRAPH_BLOCKS`, `catalog_document`, `OUTSIDE`)
- `scripts/spec/rule_catalog_1985.py` (`parse`, its catalog document's outside block)
- `scripts/spec/cite.py` (`DESIGNATOR`, `TABLE_DESIGNATOR`, `check`; `rule_book` unchanged)
- `scripts/spec/citation.py` (`RULE`, `rule_designator`, `validate`)
- `scripts/spec/record_verdicts.py` (`validate`, `main`; the new split operation and the parent recheck record)
- `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py` (`Edition.outside`) and `scripts/spec/inventory_view.py` (`lower_bound`, `check`)
- `scripts/spec/gen_ledger.py` (`measure_inventory`, which prints the lower bound)
- `scripts/spec/audit_catalog_coverage.py` (`audit`, which reads the keys of the paragraph blocks as clause labels)
- `scripts/inert_lane.py` (`CLASSIFICATION`: the lane of the new data files)
- `scripts/spec/measure_margin_levels.py` (a new tool, `measure_sublist_levels.py`'s pattern)
- `scripts/spec/data/margin-levels-2023.json` (new data), `scripts/spec/data/verbal-forms.json` (new data)
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-unharvested-rule-blocks.json`, `-1985.json`, `-2002.json` (`paragraph-blocks` takes the disposition schema; every unit entered `pending`; the 18 PB284 rows' kinds as per-unit dispositions; `kinds` removed)
- `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog.json`, `-1985.json`, `-2002.json` (ONLY the `outside-denominator` block)
- `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CatalogCoverageDriftTests.cs` (`ProseClauses_AreHeldSentenceForSentence`)

**What.** Build points 1 to 6 of "Phase A, continued" of section 3 of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md`, and point 7's tooling, for 2023 and 2002 (the ISO reader) and
1985 (its reader), with every unit `pending`:

1. the segmentation of point 1: a sentence may end in closing brackets or quotes after its stop (§2023:8.4.2.1 reads
   as three paragraphs, not two); a unit continues across blank lines (and 1985's page furniture) only when the text
   before it ends no sentence and it opens in lowercase or with a continuation word (the 42 lowercase continuations of
   `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/pb479/review/tails.py`, §2023:14.9.35.4's among them, as `--self-test` data); a colon unit
   owns its list and the list's closing tail (§2023:14.6.2.2's fifth paragraph is today "— a STOP statement — ..."
   alone; §2023:14.7.5's "and processing proceeds as specified in 14.6.13.1.3" belongs to the lead, not to `GRL3.5`);
   a numbered item of a prose clause is a unit by its printed number; NOTE, EXAMPLE, general format and code are never
   units; a table is never paragraph text, its kind (entries, matrix, data) is proposed by the shape test, and a table
   a row's text holds or names is recorded `held-by` that row (`SR-14.9.25.3-10` holds Table 16);
2. the ids `p<n>` (n over all margin paragraphs), `-<n>` for a prose clause's numbered item, `T<t>.<r>`, `T<t>`, `C`,
   and the kinds by §2023:5.3.1 (AR or RV in an intrinsic function's clause, DOC for an Annex A documentation item);
3. `measure_margin_levels.py` measuring every unlabelled paragraph after a numbered rule or a list item on the 2023 PDF
   (at design time, after a rule: 122 at level 0, 279 deeper, 16 ambiguous, 85 not found, each of the last two read on
   the rendered page and recorded with it), keyed by the unit key, `--check` holding the transcription to the data;
4. the dispositions keyed by clause + whole-text digest + order among equal digests; the kinds, `restates`,
   `not-a-rule` with its three classes (`lead-in`, `cross-reference`, `data`) and each class's mechanical test;
   the refuter record each `restates` and `not-a-rule` names (kb/Work PB5374's form; if PB5374 has not landed, built
   here and PB5374 uses it); the RULE CATALOGS audit's refusals (a unit without an entry, an entry no unit has, a
   disposition without an UPHELD refuter record of its digest, a class test failing, the verbal-form and shape guards,
   per-class counts of dispositions and records unequal, a table held in two places), and the same record required of
   the manifests' existing block disposition `not-rules` (4 entries in 2023, 37 in 1985; the audit's refusal of a
   missing record is enabled in the step that refutes them, PB5450 and PB5452, so this landing stays green);
5. `cite.py --check` and `citation.py`: the designators `p<n>`, `Table <t> row <r>` and a prose clause's item number,
   and the answer `OK §<clause> (unnumbered prose, pending)` for a quotation in a unit that lies in no row's text (a
   prose clause's paragraph, a dropped lead-in). `rule_book` keeps reading extents from the catalog, so a quotation
   inside a row's text answers as it does today: the glued form of kb/Work PB222 is PB5451's;
6. the move batch a regeneration writes when an adjudicated row's id moves; `record_verdicts.py split` (old text = new
   text + carved paragraphs, in order, checked; the carved rows written with NO record; the parent's digest re-stamped
   only with an upheld recheck record; `next.digests` of the 1985 change records naming the parent re-stamped unless
   the record's reason quotes the carved words), built and self-tested on planted data, not applied;
7. the catalogs' `outside-denominator` gains the pending units (`pending-units`, beside 2002's `pending-clauses`), and
   `lower_bound` and every reader that prints it count them for every held edition.

**Which files change bytes.** The scripts and tests above; the three content-keyed manifests (`paragraph-blocks` only);
the new data files; and in each of the three catalogs ONLY the `outside-denominator` block (2023 gains one; it has none
today). NOT changed: every catalog's `rules` array (every id, text and `rule-digest`), every store record of
`tests/version-matrix/inventory/` and `inventory-1985/`, every 1985 change record, and the answer of `cite.py --check`
for every quotation that lies in a row's text, so every stored citation resolves as before.

**Oracle.** The printed PDF for the levels; `cite.py --check` for every new designator; the extractor's `--self-test`
on planted text for the segmentation (a dash list, a closing tail, a table of each kind, a ".)" ending, the 42 blank-line
continuations, a numbered prose item, a paragraph split by a planted repair moving `p` ids and producing the move
batch, a planted repair after character 100 sending its unit back to undecided).

**Done when** `extract_rule_catalog.py --held --check` passes and every catalog's `rules` array regenerates
byte-identical (the 18 `shape: paragraph` rows included); `audit_code_citations.py` passes unchanged and
`cite.py --check "§2023:8.8.4.2.1 GR13" "For comparison, an alphanumeric group item shall be treated as an elementary
alphanumeric data item"` still answers OK (the carve is PB5451's); `cite.py --check "§2023:8.4.2.1" "The same name
shall not be used both as the name of an external record"` answers `(unnumbered prose, pending)` and names no rule;
the session probe prints 2023's rule count as a lower bound with the pending count; and each new refusal was seen to
fire once on a planted failure.
