# PB5525 - the NOT AT END arm of CheckReadDynamicNextWritten has no refusal golden, and its doc comment wrongly calls NOT AT END a later edition's phrase (the 1985 READ prints it in format 1)

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs` (`StatementValidation.CheckReadDynamicNextWritten`: the doc comment at line 1536 and the guard `!(atEnd || notAtEnd)` at line 1542), `conformance-suite/cases/` (new: a refusal golden beside PB5102's two)

**Mechanism (one site, two faults).** (1) PB5102's two refusal goldens write AT END; none writes NOT AT END alone. Dropping `|| notAtEnd` from the guard at line 1542 leaves every test green, against the owner rule that each illegal form a refusal covers has its diagnostic tested (I11). (2) The doc comment says AT END's "NOT twin ... a later edition adds", but the 1985 READ prints NOT AT END in format 1 of both the relative and the indexed module (the review cites §1985:VIII.4.5.2 and §1985:IX.4.5.2; the 1985 feature table lists it 1 REL, 1 INX), which is why the check tests `notAtEnd` at 85 at all. Those two citations are not quoted here: a 3-word quotation fails `cite.py --check` (quote-too-short) and the full format text was not re-derived. The refused rule itself, `§1985:VIII.4.5.3 SR3`, was checked ("The NEXT phrase must be specified for files in dynamic access mode").

**Fix direction.** Correct the comment; add the `READ f NOT AT END ... ` (no AT END, no NEXT) dynamic-access refusal golden at 85 for both a relative and an indexed file, and the accept-at-2002 witness.
