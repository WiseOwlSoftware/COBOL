# PB4993 - lineage slice 2: the change record and its recording path

docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md §10.2, slice 2. `inventory-schema.json` gains the `next` field of an older edition's row (`kind`, `to`, `edition`,
`bridge`, and `evidence`: the citation of the row and of each `to` rule, each `cite.py --check`ed) and `status`
(normal, obsolete, archaic, read from the edition's text). A KEPT row may carry no verdict fields of its own (its chain's
newest row holds them). `record_verdicts.py --edition 1985` is the one writer. A derived batch turns the audit run's
upheld SAME answers (state `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/audit-1985/state-route`, `results.json` CARRIES: 759 rules) into KEPT
records, the way the 2023 derived batches record (`derive_verdict_batch.py`).

**Sites:** `tests/version-matrix/inventory-schema.json` (whole: the new fields), `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py` (`load_schema`,
`Schema`), `scripts/spec/record_verdicts.py` (`main`), `scripts/spec/derive_verdict_batch.py` (`main`),
`scripts/spec/audit_batch.py` (`dispositions`).
