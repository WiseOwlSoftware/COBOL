# PB638 — OPEN — one arm short of its sibling

Found 2026-09-05 by the `PB254` implementer (report §"New defects" (D)), from the RV-15.91.4-1 refuter's list of
un-swept carriers that a read-only agent could not measure. PB254 moved TEST-DATE-YYYYMMDD, TEST-DAY-YYYYDDD and
FIND-STRING's argument-3 to `AsIntWide`, which HAS the arm, and left every function that correctly keeps the
narrow landing exposed.

## The site

`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs`, `AsInt(NumX a)` (`:884` on main after train 5): its
arms are Real / Dec / `Scale == 0` / rescale, with no `a.U ? RuntimeApi.NumWiden(a.Expr) …` arm; compare
`AsIntWide` a few lines below, which has one. A UInt128-carried operand (the `U` carrier: `USAGE COMP-5` with an
unsigned PICTURE of 19 or more digits) therefore reaches the `long` landing as a `UInt128` expression and Roslyn
rejects the generated C# with CS1503 — a compile-time crash on conforming source, at every bounded
integer-argument position.

## The rule

§15.3's integer-argument definition admits any integer data item; the compiler's carrier for the item is its own
business (`OK §15.3 14)` is the only exception path, and it is about VALUE, not carrier). PB22's bounded landing is
right to narrow; it must narrow from every carrier the binder can produce.

## Fix shape

One arm in `AsInt` mirroring `AsIntWide`'s `U` arm (widen, then the existing range-checked narrowing), plus the
probe the refuter could not run: `USAGE COMP-5 PIC 9(19)` and `PIC 9(31)` operands at INTEGER-OF-DATE's and CHAR's
argument positions, expecting the bounded verdict (a §15.3 r14 EC-ARGUMENT-FUNCTION for the out-of-range value,
the correct result for an in-range one) at 2002+. Drift: `IntrinsicCarrierAgreementDriftTests` already pairs
intake width with the body's carrier; add the assertion that BOTH intakes accept every `NumX` carrier the binder
enumerates (`Real`, `Dec`, `U`, the scaled forms) — derive the carrier list from the `NumX` type, not from a list
in the test.
