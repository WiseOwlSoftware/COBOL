# PB2108 — Cut 1a of the legacy retirement (R69)

Implement FROM `docs/COBOLNET_REARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md` Part II §PHASE-15, CUT 1 Step 1, as amended by R69 (the
cut is decoupled from v1.0). This note is the register entry; the lead of the `PB2108` cluster.

**Prerequisites:** none. **Model:** Opus (the CI invariant: a change to CI or gate wiring is judgment work).

## Scope (one atomic change)

1. `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance.csproj` lines 34–35: delete the two
   `ProjectReference`s to `CobolSharp.Compiler` and `CobolSharp.Runtime`.
2. `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance/CompilerUnderTest.cs`: delete `using LegacyCompilation = …`,
   `using LegacyState = …`, the `LegacyCompiler : ICompilerUnderTest` class and every `Legacy*` member or branch of
   `ICompilerUnderTest`; keep `CobolNetCompiler` and `CutRunner`.
3. `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance/DifferentialGolden.cs`: the `golden` mode becomes the only mode. Delete the
   `bake` and `verify` arms, the `Legacy` field and the `COBOLNET_DIFF_MODE` switch. A new differential golden is
   spec-derived and written by the golden lane (owner doctrine since DEVLOG 997), never baked from an oracle.
   `DifferentialGoldenDriftTests.cs`, `CorpusManifest.cs`, `CorpusManifestTests.cs` (the string assertion on
   `COBOLSHARP_LEGACY_DIFFERENTIAL`), `NistDifferentialTests.cs`, `SpecPinnedNistTests.cs` and
   `scripts/hooks/golden_registered.py` lose their legacy-mode references in the same change.
4. `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml`: delete the `legacy-oracle` job ("Legacy-oracle bake-verify cross-check")
   and its comment block; nothing else in the workflow changes here (the Guard job's legacy legs are PB2109).
5. `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance` must build and run with no `CobolSharp` symbol in scope:
   `grep -rn "CobolSharp" tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance/` is empty after the change.

## Rules

- No wrapper, shim, alias or compatibility switch (CLAUDE.md rule 4); the environment variables die with their arms.
- `tests/differential/**/*.out` (364 files) are kept: they are the goldens the default mode compares against.
- Sweep for siblings: every other `COBOLNET_DIFF_MODE` or `COBOLSHARP_LEGACY_DIFFERENTIAL` reader in `tests/`,
  `scripts/` and `.github/` (the measured set is the 14 files + 6 scripts listed in R69; the guard scripts are
  PB2109's).

## Verification

The whole-population gate (`build-local.ps1 -Mode implementer`), the Linux gate and CI through `push-main.sh`.
Neutrality: no `src/Cobol.Net.*` file changes, so emitted C# and diagnostics are unchanged by construction; the
differential tests still compare every case against its committed golden (case counts unchanged: assert the
population with `scripts/filter_population.py`).
