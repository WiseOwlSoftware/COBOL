# PB5997 - `§1985:C.1 item <n>` outside the PB5986 train

The PB5986 train re-pointed every citation of the form `§1985:C.1 item <n>` it introduced to the printed page of the obsolete
element list (item 1 on p.XVII-81, items 2-4 on p.XVII-82, 5-8 on p.XVII-83, 9-13 on p.XVII-84, 14-17 on p.XVII-85, item 18 on
p.XVII-86), each run through `scripts/spec/cite.py --check` with a quotation of the item. Three sites it did not touch keep the
old form. Fix: the same re-pointing at each. The alternative is the one kb/Work PB5449 left open: make `item <n>` a designator
of the list-entry locator `C.1` in `scripts/spec/citation.py`, after which the PB5986 form still validates.

**Sites:**
- `docs/conformance/3.11/index.md` line 13 (`§1985:C.1 item 5`, the RERUN clause; p.XVII-83)
- `scripts/spec/conformance_1985.py` (`render`; line 197 the table header `§1985:C.1 item`; the rendered docs/conformance/9/index.md table)
- `kb/Work/PB4997/2026-11-20-landing.md` line 7 (`§1985:C.1 item by item`, prose)

## Landed (branch `cleanup-1985-leads`)
- `docs/conformance/3.11/index.md` line 13: `§1985:C.1 item 5` is now `§1985:p.XVII-83` (cite.py --check with "the syntax and
  semantics for restart are not specified": OK).
- `scripts/spec/conformance_1985.py` line 197: the table header `§1985:C.1 item` named no item (its cells ARE the item
  numbers, which span pages), so the page form does not fit it; the header now reads `Item of §1985:C.1`, a locator
  cite.py resolves.
- `kb/Work/PB4997/2026-11-20-landing.md` line 7 is left as written: it is the body of a dated addendum, which only its own
  frontmatter may change (CLAUDE.md rule 8), and its `§1985:C.1 item by item` is prose naming the list, no item.
