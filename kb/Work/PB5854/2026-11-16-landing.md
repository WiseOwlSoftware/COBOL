# PB5854 landing, 2026-11-16: what was built

`scripts/spec/code_reads.py` gains `fixed_since(record, ctx)`: a contradicted record whose `work` note is `landed` in the
tree and was absent or not `landed` at the record's own pin. The note's status is read from its text by the register's own
`work.read_note`, in the tree and at the pin (`git show <pin>:kb/Work/<id>.md`) alike.
`plan --edition YEAR` lists those rows in a re-read group after the rows owed a read (text, and `--json` as
`{"owed": [...], "re-read": [...]}`); `--check` prints how many are fixed since, as information. A note already `landed` at
the record's pin cannot be the mechanism of a contradiction read after it: `work` and `adjudicate` refuse to name one and
`--check` is red on a record that holds one. `code_read_store.py` is untouched (generator import closure; inert lane).
