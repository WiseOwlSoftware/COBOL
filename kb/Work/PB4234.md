# PB4234 — A COBOL word with a leading or trailing underscore is split at the `_` by the lexer, so §8.3.2.1's 'shall not appear as the first or last character' is never named: COBOL0001 near `_`

Found by the Mythos front-end review 2026-10-31 (GRAMMAR-REVIEW.md, N5; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/VpxTVcaFUsArrq59EvUe5r). Pin 84cdb410f.

**Sites** (file:line at pin 84cdb410f): `CobolLexer.g4:1166-1172 (NAME_TAIL/NAME_BODY), :1292 (ANY_CHAR)`; `WordCharacterScreen.cs:91-97, :99-125`; `CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation / CobolCharacterRepertoire.Violation (position classes)`.

**Carried shard findings:** S1-2.

**Evidence paths:** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr1/GRAMMAR-REVIEW.md`, `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr1/review-S1.md`, `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr1/S1-refute/`.

**Evidence.** Probes r10-r13: the contrast `A€` IS named while `_A` / `A_` are not.

**Direction.** The position report needs its own message. Sweep `CobolWordRule.DirectiveWordViolations`. At `--std 85`, COBOL0900 also applies.

**Landed.** The lexer takes a word that starts or ends on an underscore whole, as an ill-formed IDENTIFIER (the last two `NAME_BODY` alternatives, with the new `WORD_CHAR` fragment), and one rule names it: `CobolCharacterRepertoire.SeparatorPositionViolation` (edition-free, COBOL2773 at every edition; at COBOL-85 the underscore gate COBOL0900 also reports). `CobolWordRule.CharacterViolation` asks it first, so the screen and the directive stages share it; `RemovedAt2023` asks the whole character rule at 2014, so an edge underscore is no 2023 removal under `--permissive`. The directive stages (`>>DEFINE`, `>>COBOL-WORDS`) refused an edge word through `IsWordShape` already and now say why. Not fixed, a sibling with another mechanism: a hyphen at a word edge (`A-`, `-A`) is also the arithmetic minus and a sign, so the lexer cannot take it whole; it still reads COBOL1941 / COBOL5506 (the report's lead).
