# PB4174 — COBOL5506 still names the §8.10 context-sensitive words at every edition

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs:136` (`AdmittedKinds.Name`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs#Refusal`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs#PrintsKeyword`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ReservedWords.cs#AdmitsAsKeyword`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs#GuessCobolIntent` (hint 0i), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolLexerWordSet.g.cs#IsKeywordWhereFree` (generated by `scripts/gen-cobol-words.ps1`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Diagnostics/DiagnosticDescriptors.cs#COBOL5506`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs#WrittenKindNotAdmitted`, `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/EditionReadDriftTests.cs` (`EditionReadDriftTests.EditionReads_AreExactlyTheAdjudicatedSet`, the recorded read).

**The lead** (PB3428 report, 'New defects' items 2-4): "COBOL5506's enumeration is edition-blind (`AdmittedKinds.cs#Name`)". The repro was not recorded. On the wave-20261783 build the probe is `COMPUTE W-N = (W-N + 1.` (conformance `8.3.5-Separators/l1c17-paren-unclosed`) at each `--std`: it named `B-AND, B-OR, B-SHIFT-L, B-SHIFT-LC, B-SHIFT-R, B-SHIFT-RC, B-XOR` at COBOL-85, 2002 and 2014 alike, although §8.9 reserves the boolean operators from 2002 and the shift operators from 2023 (ISO §8.3.2.1 1: "Reserved words shall not be used as user-defined words or system-names", so an unreserved word is a user-defined one). The same blindness named `PROTOTYPE` after `PROGRAM-ID. X IS GLOBAL.` and `EC, EO` after `USE` at COBOL-85.

**Landed (the §8.9 half).** `AdmittedKinds.Refusal` takes a `theEditionPrints` test, `CobolParserCoreBase.PrintsKeyword`, and names a keyword unless the §8.9 table (`ReservedWords.AdmitsAsKeyword`) proves a LATER edition introduced it. A word an older edition reserved and this one dropped (WORDS at 2023, `COBOL` and `PHYSICAL`, which 2023 formats underline although its §8.9 does not list them), a `keywordWhereFree` word (ORDER in the 1985 SORT; `CobolLexer.IsKeywordWhereFree`, generated from the rows that carry the determination) and a §8.10 context-sensitive word are named whatever the table says. Witnesses: `conformance:cases/8.3.2-COBOL-words/pb4174-expected-words-are-the-editions-85|2002|2023`, `unit:WrittenKindRefusalTests`, `unit:CobolWordsDriftTests`. CONFORMANCE.md D-EXPECT and DESIGN-frontend-grammar carry the rule.

**What stays open (the §8.10 half).** `ADD 1 TO W ROUNDED MODE IS FOO.` at COBOL-85 still names `AWAY-FROM-ZERO, NEAREST-AWAY-FROM-ZERO, NEAREST-EVEN, NEAREST-TOWARD-ZERO, PROHIBITED, TOWARD-GREATER, TOWARD-LESSER or TRUNCATION`, although the phrase is a 2014 introduction (`rounded-mode-is-2014`; a well-formed `ROUNDED MODE IS TRUNCATION` is refused by COBOL0803). These are §8.10 context-sensitive words, which are no §8.9 row and carry no per-edition flag (`ContextSensitiveWords` is a 2023 transcription: "the only edition at which SR3/SR4 are ever asked"). The repository holds no text of the 1985, 2002 or 2014 standards, and the GnuCOBOL per-standard word lists that fed §8.9 are NOT a safe substitute for them: measured on this branch, 91 §8.10 words against `cobol85|2002|2014.words` give 19 first listed at 2023, 21 at 2014, 50 at 2002 and one at 85, but the 2014 list omits `NEAREST-EVEN` (it lists `NEAREST-EVEN-INTERMEDIATE`) and the 2002 and 2014 lists omit the `LC_` words, so a flag derived from them would hide a word an edition really prints. **To close:** an authoritative per-edition source for the §8.10 population (the 2002 and 2014 §8.10 tables, or the construct registry rows joined to the words their phrase prints), then a per-edition flag on `ContextSensitiveWordEntry` generated beside the §8.9 table and `ReservedWords.AdmitsAsKeyword` asking it; the two exemptions in `PrintsKeyword` (`ContextSensitiveWords.Contains`) then go.

## 2026-10-31 landed: the §8.10 half (wave 20261785)

**The premise was wrong, as R1-1 said.** The register holds the 2002 post-ballot text (PB3602 source P2) and the 2009 CD 1.2 of
the 2014 line (P3), and the 2023 standard's own Annex E.3.3 item 13 lists the context-sensitive words 2023 added (checked with
`cite.py --check E.3.3 "the following words have either been added to the list of context-sensitive words"`: OK). Reading them:

- P2's §8.10 lists 58 words (every one in the 2023 table); the FCD of 2001 (P1) lists those and three more that left the
  standard (ALIGNED, LOCALIZE, UCS-2). Recorded as `tests/version-matrix/context-sensitive-words-2002.json`.
- P3 (CD 1.2, 2009) lists 76 words: the 58, 14 more that the 2023 table has, and four that left (INDIRECT, NEGATIVE-INFINITY,
  NOT-A-NUMBER, POSITIVE-INFINITY). **P3 is NOT the 2014 standard**: it lacks BINARY-ENCODING, DECIMAL-ENCODING, FLOAT-BINARY,
  FLOAT-DECIMAL, HIGH-ORDER-LEFT, HIGH-ORDER-RIGHT, SHORT and STRUCTURE, which the construct registry dates 2014
  (`usage-float-format-phrase-2014`, `options-float-binary-2014`, `options-float-decimal-2014`, `dynamic-length-structure-2014`).
  So the CD only corroborates and the 2014 edge rests on the 2023 standard instead (E.1: the annex lists the changes since the
  previous standard; E.3.3 item 13 names the 2023 additions, ACTIVATING, ANUM, APPLY, BACKWARD, BYTE, BYTES, CURRENT, HEX, NAT,
  SECONDS, STACK and TOP-LEVEL; SECONDS is also in the 2002 list, the "expanded" arm).
- No 1985 text is held. The 85 edge needs none: 85 had no context-sensitive words, every word of a format was reserved, so a
  word first listed in 2002 is not a keyword at 85 unless §8.9's 85 column reserves it (`MULTIPLE`, the one §8.10 word the
  GnuCOBOL 85 list reserves, is answered by the §8.9 row first).

**The fix.** `ContextSensitiveWordEntry` gained `Introduced` (2002 | 2014 | 2023), generated by `scripts/gen-reserved-words.ps1`
step 7c (22 words 2014, 11 words 2023, 58 words 2002) and emitted to `ContextSensitiveWords.Table.cs` and
`context-sensitive-words.json`; `ReservedWords.AdmitsAsKeyword` asks the §8.9 flags and then the row; the two exemptions in
`CobolParserCoreBase.PrintsKeyword` (`ContextSensitiveWords.Contains`) are gone. `ADD 1 TO W ROUNDED MODE IS FOO.` at 85 and
2002 draws the bare COBOL0001 (no keyword exists to name); 2014 and 2023 name the eight modes. `PROGRAM-ID. X IS GLOBAL.` at 85
no longer names RECURSIVE (a 2002 word), `OPTIONS. ARITHMETIC IS FOO.` names STANDARD-BINARY and STANDARD-DECIMAL from 2014.
Witnesses: `unit:ContextSensitiveWordsDriftTests` (`Introduced_IsDerivedFromThe2002ListAndAnnexE33`, which re-derives each
flag from the file and the spec), `unit:WrittenKindRefusalTests`, `conformance:cases/8.3.2-COBOL-words/pb4174-context-words-arithmetic-2002|2014`
and `pb4174-context-words-rounded-mode-85|2014`. R1-2: the recorded-read comment in `EditionReadDriftTests` now says what
`PrintsKeyword` does.
