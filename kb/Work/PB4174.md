# PB4174 — COBOL5506 still names the §8.10 context-sensitive words at every edition

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs:136` (`AdmittedKinds.Name`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs#Refusal`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs#PrintsKeyword`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ReservedWords.cs#AdmitsAsKeyword`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs#GuessCobolIntent` (hint 0i), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolLexerWordSet.g.cs#IsKeywordWhereFree` (generated by `scripts/gen-cobol-words.ps1`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Diagnostics/DiagnosticDescriptors.cs#COBOL5506`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs#WrittenKindNotAdmitted`, `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/EditionReadDriftTests.cs` (the recorded read).

**The lead** (PB3428 report, 'New defects' items 2-4): "COBOL5506's enumeration is edition-blind (`AdmittedKinds.cs#Name`)". The repro was not recorded. On the wave-20261783 build the probe is `COMPUTE W-N = (W-N + 1.` (conformance `8.3.5-Separators/l1c17-paren-unclosed`) at each `--std`: it named `B-AND, B-OR, B-SHIFT-L, B-SHIFT-LC, B-SHIFT-R, B-SHIFT-RC, B-XOR` at COBOL-85, 2002 and 2014 alike, although §8.9 reserves the boolean operators from 2002 and the shift operators from 2023 (ISO §8.3.2.1 1: "Reserved words shall not be used as user-defined words or system-names", so an unreserved word is a user-defined one). The same blindness named `PROTOTYPE` after `PROGRAM-ID. X IS GLOBAL.` and `EC, EO` after `USE` at COBOL-85.

**Landed (the §8.9 half).** `AdmittedKinds.Refusal` takes a `theEditionPrints` test, `CobolParserCoreBase.PrintsKeyword`, and names a keyword unless the §8.9 table (`ReservedWords.AdmitsAsKeyword`) proves a LATER edition introduced it. A word an older edition reserved and this one dropped (WORDS at 2023, `COBOL` and `PHYSICAL`, which 2023 formats underline although its §8.9 does not list them), a `keywordWhereFree` word (ORDER in the 1985 SORT; `CobolLexer.IsKeywordWhereFree`, generated from the rows that carry the determination) and a §8.10 context-sensitive word are named whatever the table says. Witnesses: `conformance:cases/8.3.2-COBOL-words/pb4174-expected-words-are-the-editions-85|2002|2023`, `unit:WrittenKindRefusalTests`, `unit:CobolWordsDriftTests`. CONFORMANCE.md D-EXPECT and DESIGN-frontend-grammar carry the rule.

**What stays open (the §8.10 half).** `ADD 1 TO W ROUNDED MODE IS FOO.` at COBOL-85 still names `AWAY-FROM-ZERO, NEAREST-AWAY-FROM-ZERO, NEAREST-EVEN, NEAREST-TOWARD-ZERO, PROHIBITED, TOWARD-GREATER, TOWARD-LESSER or TRUNCATION`, although the phrase is a 2014 introduction (`rounded-mode-is-2014`; a well-formed `ROUNDED MODE IS TRUNCATION` is refused by COBOL0803). These are §8.10 context-sensitive words, which are no §8.9 row and carry no per-edition flag (`ContextSensitiveWords` is a 2023 transcription: "the only edition at which SR3/SR4 are ever asked"). The repository holds no text of the 1985, 2002 or 2014 standards, and the GnuCOBOL per-standard word lists that fed §8.9 are NOT a safe substitute for them: measured on this branch, 91 §8.10 words against `cobol85|2002|2014.words` give 19 first listed at 2023, 21 at 2014, 50 at 2002 and one at 85, but the 2014 list omits `NEAREST-EVEN` (it lists `NEAREST-EVEN-INTERMEDIATE`) and the 2002 and 2014 lists omit the `LC_` words, so a flag derived from them would hide a word an edition really prints. **To close:** an authoritative per-edition source for the §8.10 population (the 2002 and 2014 §8.10 tables, or the construct registry rows joined to the words their phrase prints), then a per-edition flag on `ContextSensitiveWordEntry` generated beside the §8.9 table and `ReservedWords.AdmitsAsKeyword` asking it; the two exemptions in `PrintsKeyword` (`ContextSensitiveWords.Contains`) then go.
