# PB2113 — D10, the SUBSCRIPT lexer-mode removal (R69 unblocks it)

The owner's D10 ruling (plan §6 D10): FULLY remove the lexer `SUBSCRIPT` mode (`CobolLexer.g4` line 1275, `SUB_LPAREN`
at line 1338, 35 grammar lines) and the binder's subscript re-parse (about 250 lines of hand-rolled C#), replacing
them with interpreted grammar rules. It could not land while the legacy compiler consumed `SUB_*` /
`SubscriptEntryContext`; PB2110 deleted that consumer.

Implement FROM `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md` §9, including the §9.4 ISO §8.3.5 space-separator
decision the executing implementer must resolve first (from the spec, cited and checked with `cite.py --check`).
**Prerequisite:** PB2110 landed. **Model:** Opus (grammar is design-heavy; `model_rules.json` routes it).

## Rules

- The token-stream differential (the PB1715 form) and the parse-tree shape differential over every lexer input the
  suites use are the neutrality proof for a grammar change (DESIGN-architecture-review §4 item 3), beside the
  whole-population gate.
- `LexerDfaCacheDriftTests`: no left-edge semantic predicate in a lexer mode.
- The §9.5 D10.1 corpus (subscripts, reference modification, space-separated arguments, nested FUNCTION) ships with
  the change as goldens.
- Every citation mechanically checked; the design doc corrected in the same change where implementation proves it
  wrong (rule 5).
