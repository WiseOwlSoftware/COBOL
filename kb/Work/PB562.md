# PB562

## Mechanism — value-clause-p4
A report-section (Format 4) VALUE is composed through the SOURCE clause's implicit-MOVE conversion path, so the three transforms 13.18.63.4 GR21's imports explicitly exclude are all applied. ReportWriterEmitter.FieldImage builds a BoundOperand from the field's FieldValueSource and then returns `move.ConvertSource(source, f.PrintItem)` unconditionally -- the same call the FieldDataSource (SOURCE clause) arm uses, where the implicit MOVE of 13.18.53.4 GR1 is correct. The printable item carries Justified and BlankWhenZero (bound at DataBinder.Reports.cs:535-536) and its PICTURE's edit mask, and ConvertSource honours all three. GR21 imports GR7 ('Each literal is aligned ... except that initialization is not affected by a JUSTIFIED clause and no editing takes place') and GR8 ('the BLANK WHEN ZERO clause has no effect on initialization'), and 13.18.63.3 SR34 makes SR11 -- 'Editing characters in a picture character-string for an alphanumeric-edited or national-edited data item do not cause editing of the initial value' -- apply to Format 4 by name. ONE dispatch decision (VALUE vs SOURCE) is missing at ONE site, and it produces three distinct wrong answers; the WORKING-STORAGE arm of the same rules is correct, so this is the second arm of a two-arm dispatch. The fix is to give a Format-4 VALUE its own placement (the GR7 placement the Format-1 ValueInitializer already performs) rather than to special-case Justified/BlankWhenZero/EditMask at the MOVE call, so a future printable-item attribute does not have to be remembered here. GR21's fourth import, GR9 (every occurrence of an OCCURS entry takes the value), cannot be reached at all -- see the separate report-group OCCURS finding.
