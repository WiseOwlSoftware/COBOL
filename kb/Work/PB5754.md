# PB5754 - a witness at an edition is source text of that edition: free form witnesses nothing before 2002

**The question.** The branch review of kb/Work PB5730 found that `generate.check_editions` refuses a free-form case
of the `cases/` tree that is ACCEPTED or flagged before 2002 ("the free-form reference format is a 2002 feature") and
never looks at a refusal entry's `refuse-at`. So PB5730's widening discarded 33 free-form accepted cases as not 1985
programs while it placed 34 free-form refusal tests at `refuse-at` 85, and such tests close 1985 rows as own-edition
witnesses. Must a refusal witness at 85 be 1985 source text?

**The decision (Mythos lane, 2026-11-15; CLAUDE.md Ownership).** Yes, and the rule is one rule with no exception:
**a conformance-suite case witnesses a row at an edition only when its text is written in a reference format that
edition defines.** Before 2002 that is fixed form alone. It holds for an accepted case and for a refusal test, for the
`cases/` tree and the `wiseowl/` tree, for a KEPT row's own witness and for an own verdict's witness.

**Why.**
1. The 1985 text defines one reference format and requires it of every source program: "Each division must be written
   according to the rules for the reference format." and "each compiler accepts source programs written in reference
   format" (both `cite.py --check "§1985:IV.7.1"` OK, 2026-11-15). A free-form text is not a 1985 source program.
2. The compiler reads free form at `--std 85` only through WiseOwl's own option (`--source-format free` at every
   edition, `docs/conformance/7/DOC-A.1-158.md`). A free-form test at 85 is therefore evidence about that extension's
   path. That it also shows what the compiler does with 1985 text rests on an assumption no test holds: that nothing
   after reference-format processing depends on the format.
3. The stronger evidence is cheap. Fixed form is valid at every edition, so a free-form case is converted IN PLACE
   (no twin, no duplicate) and then runs in fixed form at every edition it is placed at, with its expected output or
   diagnostic unchanged; the gate proves each conversion.
4. A rule with no exception is one predicate the audit and the writer can both ask. "Refusals are exempt because a
   diagnosis does not depend on the format" would be an exception resting on point 2's assumption.

**What it does not change.** A free-form refusal test may still be refused at 85 as a regression test of WiseOwl's
extension and of the edition gates (main carries 1,256 of them in `cases/` and 24 in `wiseowl/cases/`); it only stops
counting as a witness of a 1985 row. `generate.check_editions` keeps refusing a free-form `cases/` case accepted or
flagged before 2002. From 2002 on both formats exist and nothing changes.

**Measured on main 5e49d46678** (1985 rows that carry a test-ref: 1,444; each row classed by its strongest reference
at 85):

| strongest evidence at 85 | rows |
|---|---|
| accepted, fixed form | 1,068 |
| refused, fixed form | 264 |
| refused, free form | 100 (91 SR and 9 FMT, all KEPT) |
| accepted, free form (`wiseowl/` tree) | 6 (5 GR and 1 FMT, all KEPT) |
| a reference of another form only | 6 |

References: 176 name a free-form refusal test and 15 a free-form accepted WiseOwl test; the distinct cases are 128 in
`cases/` and 4 in `wiseowl/`. PB5730 adds 4 such references; one of its 64 rows (`1985-SR-VI.6.8.3-1`, case
`cases/14.9.8-COMPUTE/pb128-compute-alphanumeric-receiver`) has nothing stronger.

**The open rows it reaches.** Of PB5730's 291 widenings that did not hold, 33 cases are refused at 85 for free form
ALONE (the generator's refusal, and the compile at `--std 85` gave no diagnostic); 147 more are free form and also use
a later construct. Converted, the 33 give every lacking target of 45 open rows a case, and some targets of 11 more
(`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/my-20261115/w20262054/free-only-1985.json`). Of the 33, none has an inline comment and 2
have a code line that does not fit columns 12 to 72 unwrapped.

**The work.**
1. ONE predicate, asked by the audit and by the writer (and by the planner, so `widen` and `witness-batch` never
   propose such a case): before the first edition that has free form, a free-form case is no witness.
2. ONE converter, free form to fixed form in place, that refuses what it cannot convert with certainty.
3. The data: every free-form case that witnesses a 1985 row is converted, or its reference leaves the row and the row
   is open again, said aloud. The 33 are converted and widened, and their rows recorded.
