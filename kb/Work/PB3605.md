# PB3605 — MOVE SPACE to a numeric item is illegal before 2023, not only removed at 2023

## Scenario

The row models the whole figurative-constant move family as "removed in 2023". The SPACE arm is wrong. "The figurative
constant SPACE shall not be moved to a numeric or numeric-edited item" is syntax rule 5 of clause 14.8.24.2 of P2,
identical in P1 and in clause 14.9.24.2 of P3 (syntax rule 5). So `MOVE SPACE TO N` (N PIC 9(3)) is illegal at 2002
and 2014 already, not only at 2023. The HIGH-VALUE, LOW-VALUE and non-digit ALL arms are right: Annex G.1 item 2 of P2
(archaic), Annex E.1 item 2 of P3, and the 2023 removal in Annex E.2 item 1 (checked).

Repro: `E:\Temp\claude\E--COBOL\8bd1c3d3-6c48-4db9-ada1-5e4edea40828\scratchpad\edges\E2\probe\MVSPC.cob` compiles
clean at 85, 2002 and 2014 and raises COBOL0902 only at 2023. Expected: refused at 2002 and 2014 by the SPACE
syntax-rule gate.

Code site: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs`, `GateMove` / `Sr5FigurativeText` / `GateSr5`.

The 1985 edge is unsettled: there is no 1985 text in the archive.

## Fix (for the fix lane)

1. SPACE to a numeric or numeric-edited item is refused at 2002 and 2014 by its own syntax-rule-5 gate, separate from
   the 2023 removal arm.
2. Goldens per edition (85 decision, 2002, 2014, 2023) and the VCR row.
3. The edition-gate sweep and sibling sweep of the other figurative arms in `GateMove`.

## Landed

Re-probed on the worktree build (main 5a8125574): `MOVE SPACE TO N` (PIC 9(3)) and `MOVE SPACES TO E` (PIC ZZ9) compiled
clean at 85, 2002 and 2014 and were COBOL0902 only at 2023. GnuCOBOL 3.2 refuses both at `-std=cobol85`, `cobol2002`,
`cobol2014`, `default` and `ibm` ("MOVE of figurative constant SPACE to numeric item"); only `mf` warns.

The edge. 2002 and 2014 are quoted (clause 14.8.24.2 SR5 of the 2001-12-04 J4 text and the same SR5 in the 2009 committee
draft). The 1985 edge is derived (VCR row 7.41): the 2002 Annex F.1 list of changes that may affect existing programs has
no SPACE item, its Annex G.1 item 2 names "any figurative constant except SPACE", and GnuCOBOL refuses the move at
`-std=cobol85`. So the SPACE arm is no edition row at all: the move is prohibited at EVERY edition, and `--permissive`
does not admit it.

The fix puts the rule where the edition-invariant figurative rules already live. `MoveValidity.FigurativeRefusal` (the
one copy of SR6 / SR7, asked by the compiler's MOVE chain and by the run-time activation relations) gained SR5's SPACE arm,
and its two booleans became one `FigurativeSender` value (Other, Zero, Space, BooleanCharacters) read by ONE reader,
`MoveTable16.FigurativeSenderOf`. Every MOVE-validity asker now refuses SPACE into a numeric or numeric-edited item with
no edit of its own: the written MOVE and each implicit FROM/INTO move (COBOL0819), INITIALIZE REPLACING (COBOL2031),
and a BY CONTENT argument, at bind and at run time (COBOL1688), through the new `ActivationCategory.FigurativeSpace`,
which one category table maps to and from the sender. `VersionConformancePass.Sr5FigurativeText` no longer lists SPACE,
so GateSr5's 2023 removal row covers HIGH-VALUE, LOW-VALUE, QUOTE and the non-digit ALL literal only (row text and the
VCR row 1 corrected). A zero-length literal (GR2's substituted SPACE) stays legal: SR5 names written figuratives only.

Goldens: positive `85/pb3605_move_space_nonnumeric` (alphanumeric, ref-mod and group receivers stay legal); negatives
`pb3605-move-space-numeric` (85, 2002, 2014, 2023), `pb3605-initialize-replacing-numeric-by-space` (all four),
`pb3605-content-space-into-numeric-edited` (2002, 2014, 2023). Witnesses that wrote MOVE SPACE into a numeric item
to show a pre-2023 fill were moved to a shape that has one: `MoveEditionDifferentialTests` (non-digit ALL literal and
HIGH-VALUE), `pb880_initialize_replacing_figurative_numeric` (BY ALL "X"), `pb879-initialize-replacing-high-value-numeric-2023`
(renamed from `...-space-numeric-2023`), and the SPACE moves of `pb2553_sum_counter_character_cell`,
`pb425_zero_length_literal_move` and `ZeroLengthLiteralMoveTests.Gr2_AndMoveSpace_AreTheSameStatement` now go through the
item's full-width reference-modified view. Measured: all 459 NIST programs compile at `--nist` with no SR5 refusal, and
the conformance suite's cases at their accept editions hit it only where listed above.
