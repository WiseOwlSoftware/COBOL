## Landed (wave 20262068 group C, 2026-11-16)

**Re-probe (this tree, before the fix).** `m5str.cob` at `--std 85` and `--std 2023`: `OVF`, then `H=[5a    ] PTR=03`. Reproduced.

**Root cause.** `StringEmitter.EmitString` read identifier-3 through the SENDING reader (`OperandText.AsString` -> `PlaceRenderer.SendingGroupValue`),
whose occurs-depending arm slices to the current extent, and stored the image back through `PlaceRenderer.WriteGroupValue` in the
RECEIVING direction, which keeps the maximum for a group whose data-name-1 lies inside it (`UsesCurrentExtent`). The working image was
therefore shorter than the receiver, so GR8's overflow test fired at the current extent and the store blanked the rest.

**Fix.** `PlaceRenderer.ReceivingGroupValue` is the read twin of `WriteGroupValue` (same `UsesCurrentExtent` law, alphanumeric, bit and
national arms); `StringEmitter.ReadReceiverImage` asks it for an `OdoGroupPlace` identifier-3. Probe after: `H=[5ab***] PTR=04` at 85 and 2023.
Witnesses: `conformance:cases/VI.5.8@1985-OCCURS-CLAUSE/pb5776_string_odo_group_receiver_maximum_length` (85) and
`conformance:cases/13.18.38-OCCURS/pb5776_string_odo_group_receiver_maximum_length_2023` (2023).
