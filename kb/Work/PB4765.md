# PB4765 - unprobed tree-shape function-identifier reads

**What the report says (unprobed).** `MoveBinder.cs:624` (`senderIsFunction`), `ConditionBinder.cs:965`, `OoBinder.cs:1370` and
`RelationComparability.cs:119` read `{ Expr: BoundIntrinsicCall }` as a function-identifier. PB4712 found that the binder drops a
parenthesis, so a tree-shape read cannot tell a function-identifier from a parenthesized function. The report did not probe these
four sites; each needs a probe before it is called a defect or cleared.

**R1.** No open note covers it. PB4712 (landed) replaced the tree-shape read at its four sites.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/MoveBinder.cs:624` (`senderIsFunction`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ConditionBinder.cs:965`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.cs:1370`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/RelationComparability.cs:119`.

**Audit 2026-11-07** (the operator's audit of the 37 open analyses that set no harm flag (kb/Work PB4949; four read-only adjudicators, evidence in E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/analyses-audit/)). HIDDEN DEFECT, under-rejects, probed: `IF IX = (FUNCTION ABS (N))` compiles while `IF IX = (N)` is refused with COBOL2533 (RelationComparability.cs:119; §2023:8.8.4.2.13 GR2). The class-condition site was fixed by PB4763; the relation site was not.


**Landed (wave 20261930 B).** Re-probed on the built compiler: `IF IX = (FUNCTION ABS (N))` and `IF IX = (LINAGE-COUNTER)` compiled while
`IF IX = (N)` drew COBOL2533, and `IF A = (LINAGE-COUNTER)` over a PIC X item compiled (the register is a usage-display integer). The
other three sites are clear: `MoveBinder.Table16Framing`'s `senderIsFunction` cannot see a parenthesized MOVE source (the sending
operand's grammar prints no parenthesized form: `MOVE (FUNCTION ABS (N)) TO X` is COBOL0001), `OoBinder`'s test at the BY CONTENT
fold sits behind `ConditionBinder.SoleFunctionCall` (the written shape), and `ConditionBinder.CheckClassConditionOperand`'s reads
come after PB4763's `ExpressionAsWritten` refusal. The fix: `RelationComparability.IndexNameAgainstNumeric` and
`RelationComparability.IsNumericIntegerOperand` ask `ExpressionAsWritten` first (§2023:8.8.1.1; §2023:8.8.4.2.13 row 2; §2023:8.8.4.2.5).
Witnesses: `pb4765-index-name-paren-function`, `pb4765-index-name-paren-counter`, `pb4765-alphanumeric-paren-counter` (refused), `pb4765-index-name-sole-function` (control).

**Sites (landed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/RelationComparability.cs#IndexNameAgainstNumeric`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/RelationComparability.cs#IsNumericIntegerOperand`.
