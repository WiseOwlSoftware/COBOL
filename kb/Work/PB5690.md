# PB5690 - a hand group that commits reads as stalled

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/progress.py` (`group_activity`, `STALL_IDLE_MIN`, `stall_lines`),
`scripts/orchestrator/orchestrate.ps1` (whole: top-level script body that calls the PROGRESS and STALL functions).

**What happened.** Three STALL mails reached the Mythos inbox about hand groups (`E:/COBOL-coord/mailbox/done/`); each was
answered in its `result.report` field, and each answer found the group working.
- `20261115-104026-loop-info-stall-w20262047a-pb5675`: "no progress for 30 min on running (30 min since it began)". Its
  answer: not stalled; a two-phase hand job whose phase 1 ended in one commit by design, `edafa17478` at 10:28; phase 2 began
  10:32 and its worktree showed edits at 10:37 to 10:39.
- `20261115-112027-loop-info-stall-w20262046a-pb5673`: "no progress for 28 min on running (103 min since it began)". Its
  answer: its gate had ended RED at 10:51 on a self-test hang; the worker was re-running that self-test (bash started 11:25:29,
  PID 481980).
- `20261115-114033-loop-info-stall-w20262047a-pb5675`: "no progress for 90 min on running (90 min since it began)". Its
  answer: in `E:/COBOL/.claude/worktrees/agent-a06f7c99d65e2658f` commits `edafa17478` (10:28), `51a6e4d97d` (11:04) and
  `ff6ba9623e` (11:12) and a gate running since 11:13 (`gate.py` PID 492820, Conformance testhost started 11:43); "the stall
  measure does not see this hand group's commits".

**Evidence.** `scripts/orchestrator/progress.py` `group_activity` reads the group's scratch directory and files named for its
slug, its review's scratch and its reports in the coordination directory; its docstring says "The loop reads no git, so a
commit shows only as the STATUS.md and gate log the checkpoint rewrites". The third mail's "90 min" window holds two commits.
The notes the report named as covering stall mail for hand groups (PB3742, PB3804, PB3859, PB4396) were read: their titles
concern `check_practices` turn caps, docstring drift, file-set partitioning and group sizing, none the stall measure. The
notes that do concern the stall measure are PB4944 (landed; stopped-moving versus over-size) and PB5097 (landed; a queued
group is not a stalled group); neither mentions a hand group that commits without writing to the coordination directory.

**The question this note asks.** What a hand group's activity is, for the stall measure, when it commits and gates in its own
worktree and writes no file in the coordination directory.
