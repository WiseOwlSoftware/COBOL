# PB3712 — a deferred link names a head that may not run

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`plan`), `scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py`.

## What happens

The `links_per_chain` deferral runs before the budget loop looks at the chain head. A link whose head was skipped (budget or `max_groups`)
still lands in `waiting` with the message that its predecessor is a group "which this wave runs" and that "the next wave plans it from that
group's branch". Scenario: a campaign wave whose budget fits no group: head A goes to `skipped`, while its successor A2's notes say A is
run by this wave. The operator reading the plan is told work is dispatched that is not.

## Fix

Word the message by whether `g.after` is among the chosen letters ("which this wave runs" against "which waits too"), or test the link
after the head's fate is known. Add a planner self-test arm: a chain whose head is cut by the budget.

## Landed (2026-10-25, fix-lane wave 20261107 group C)

**Re-probe (live `links_per_chain` 1).** Three notes chained on one file (A, A2, A3): with the budget fitting no group, or
`max_groups` 0, A went to `skipped` while A2 and A3 were told they followed a group "which this wave runs"; and even with A
running, A3 was told A2 "runs" though A2 is deferred itself.

**Fix.** The deferral is decided in the budget loop, where the predecessor's fate is known (groups are placed in order):
the reason says "which this wave runs" only when `g.after` is among the chosen letters, otherwise "which this wave does not
run either (the group cap, the budget or its own link cap); the next wave plans the chain again". **Tests.** The three
cases (head runs, budget cuts it, group cap cuts it) under the live rules.
