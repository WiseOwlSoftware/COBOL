# PB3276 — D7: parse-time edition gates beside VersionConformancePass

Sources: "New defects" of `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1092e-PB2291-report.md` and the paragraph in `DEVLOG/pending-pb2291-edition-read-verdict.md` on branch `worktree-wf_9c1febe0-ebf-13` (NOT yet landed; read it with `git -C E:\COBOL\.claude\worktrees\wf_9c1febe0-ebf-13 show HEAD:DEVLOG/pending-pb2291-edition-read-verdict.md`). PB2291 asked whether each front-end edition read is word status (sanctioned, §8.9) or refuses a construct by edition (a D7 defect: `VersionConformancePass` is the one edition gate).

**The three gates**, all on the declined Annex A.4 surface:
- `{is2023()}? applyCommitClause` (`CobolIO.g4`): APPLY is §8.10 context-sensitive and no earlier edition has an APPLY clause.
- `{is2002()}? validationClause` (`CobolData.g4`): CLASS, DESTINATION, INVALID and VARYING are reserved at every edition.
- The INVALID arm of `({is2002()}? validateValidPhrase)?` (`CobolData.g4`): VALID itself is a §8.9 user word below 2002 and, per §8.3.3.6.3 SR1, a legal VALUE operand as a symbolic-character, so that arm keeps word status.
Also `DeclinedFacilityPass.cs:64` (carried by the report).

**Repro (the paragraph's).** Re-probed on the branch's build: below the introducing edition each gate gives a bare COBOL0001 where COBOLNET0900 belongs.

**Fix shape (the paragraph's).** Move them to `VersionConformancePass`. That needs a `constructs.json` row form the version matrix lacks (a construct refused by name at its introduction). `ConstructGates_AreOnlyTheOnesPb2291Found` on that branch pins exactly these three until fixed and refuses a fourth; this note's fix removes the pin entries.
