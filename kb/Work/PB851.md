## What closing it requires (the reason it is not closable in one golden)

One golden per clause per format band, each asserting the clause's effect on a NON-FIRST occurrence: PICTURE
(width and category), USAGE (storage and DISPLAY form), SIGN (LEADING SEPARATE on occurrence 3), JUSTIFIED
(right-justified store into occurrence 2), BLANK WHEN ZERO (occurrence 2 blanks while occurrence 1 does not),
and the SYNCHRONIZED alignment case. The dynamic-capacity band is where the replication is most likely to be
absent, because its element is materialized differently.
