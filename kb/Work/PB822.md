# PB822 — LANDED — a deleted member still carried three determinations

Filed 2026-09-09 by the registrar (second pass) from the **PB803 implementer's report**, "New defects" (a) —
`scratchpad/reports/PB803-report.md`, which found it while landing the `--sign-encoding` option and explicitly
declined to adjudicate it. **Re-measured on this worktree at `d3ba5795`.**

Two further hits are in genuinely historical records — `docs/rearchitecture/PHASE-11-scout-notes.md:1170` and
`docs/rearchitecture/SURVEY-xcut-data-model-coherence.md:92` — and are **correctly left alone**: point-in-time
artifacts are exempt from the current-state rule (kb-sync skill, "Leave genuinely historical artifacts alone").

## Why it is not a one-line delete

CLAUDE.md rule 6 says every doc except `DEVLOG.md` describes the CURRENT compiler, so three of the five sites
are false statements today. But **the determinations they carry are not obviously false** — they are about
where a signed binary leaf's sign lives when the leaf is imaged:

- **D7** (`COBOLNET_FILES_DESIGN.md:57-59`) is the record-image determination for a mixed-usage record, and it
  calls the SORT/MERGE consequence *load-bearing*: `BoundSortMergeKey.SignKind` shall carry the IMAGE sign,
  "never the leaf's stored `BinaryMinus`", and it names the failure mode as SILENT (everything sorts; negatives
  order as positives). That determination may still be exactly right while the member that used to express it
  is gone — in which case the repair is to re-express it in terms of whatever carries it now.
- `COBOLNET_DESIGN.md:1124` states the deletion and its reason ("a binary leaf's sign lives in its bytes").
- `COBOLNET_DESIGN.md:787` and `COBOLNET_OO_DESIGN.md:151` each rest a rule on the member by name.

**This note deliberately does not adjudicate which statement is current.** That needs the D7 / §14.4
record-image owner, not a registrar and not the PB803 mechanism. Filing it is rule 6; deciding it is
someone's design work.

## What the fix owes

1. Establish what carries the image sign today (start at `BoundSortMergeKey.SignKind`, `ImageProfileOf`, and
   `CobolSort.NumericKey`) and whether D7's "never `BinaryMinus`" property still holds — **measure it**, because
   its failure mode is silent by D7's own account.
2. Re-express all four current-state sites in those terms, in one change set (`feedback_doc_sweep_on_discovery`
   — fix on discovery, then sweep every occurrence).
3. If the four sites would end up repeating one determination, add ONE authoritative statement and point the
   others at it rather than re-drifting three copies (kb-sync, "do not repoint each site").

Owner 2026-10-17: addressed in the SORT and file I/O campaign (kb/Work PB3353).

## Landed

Re-probed on the PB3353 wave-1119 tree (main `949670467`): `grep -rn ImageSignKind src/` is empty; the sites had MOVED
(`COBOLNET_DESIGN.md` 1007 and 1430 by then, D7 at `COBOLNET_FILES_DESIGN.md:211-215`, `COBOLNET_OO_DESIGN.md:307`).

**D7's determination is still right; its mechanism and most of its premises were not.** Measured: a SORT on a signed
`COMP` key and on a signed `COMP-3` key ordered -300, -3, -1, 0, 5, 300 (and 99999 down to -99999) correctly, because
since the V59 re-base (`COBOLNET_DESIGN.md` §14.4) the image IS the leaf's bytes and `SortBinder` binds the key LEAF
(`BoundSortMergeKey.Item`), which `SortKeyColumns.NumericKey` decodes with `CobolNum.ParseImage` on the leaf's own
profile. No image sign form exists to carry, so "`SignKind` carries the IMAGE sign, never `BinaryMinus`" had no
referent; the zoned `Pic.Digits`-wide image, the "80 chars, not 72+4" width, the "digit-image coordinates" key geometry
and "the legacy raw-byte form is the rejected alternative" were all the PRE-V59 design (§14.4 names it a retired
loser), and §8.2's "float, COMP-5, INDEX stay outside the codec" had been superseded by PB164/R40.

Repair, one change set: the ONE authoritative statement of the SORT/MERGE key consequence now lives in §14.4's total
rule ("THE SORT/MERGE KEY CONSEQUENCE": leaf-profile decode, algebraic order, the MERGE sequence check on the same
order, the silent failure mode); §8.2 and D7 point at it and keep only what they own (the file-side window geometry);
D7's title, rationale and rejected alternatives are re-expressed; the OO design's SIGN representation names
`PicInfo.SignKind`; §14.4's own "deleted member" sentence no longer names it. The measurement the note owed (item 1) is
pinned by three new end-to-end witnesses in `MixedUsageRecordImageDifferentialTests`: MERGE over signed BINARY keys
(ascending), over signed PACKED keys (descending), and the EC-SORT-MERGE-SEQUENCE path in both verdicts (a file
ordered by VALUE whose bytes descend raises nothing; one out of order by value raises the condition). PB868 item 1 is
the same defect and is repaired by this change; its items 2-3 and the symbol-existence audit stay open there.
