# PB5032 - PB4984's design text and change-record count disagree with what ran

**Sites:** `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md` (section 10.3), `tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/` (the change record of 1985-GR-VI.6.4.3-4). The files PB4984 adds (`kb/Work/PB4984.md` and its DEVLOG entry) arrive on main with PR 1401.

**Found (review R1-1 and R1-2 of PB4984).** Two statements in the PB4984 change set disagree with what ran.
1. Design 10.3 (`DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md`, line 1022 on the branch) says the misprints pass leaves the general
   formats unread. `plan_misprints` plans every catalog rule including the 210 FMT rows, and the Errata and PB4984 both say the
   210 general formats were read.
2. The DEVLOG entry, PB4984 and the fix-lane report say the 1985 catalog was regenerated with ten rule texts changed. Eleven
   changed: `1985-GR-VI.6.4.3-4` (C22, CORRESPONDING rule 4) also changed and has no change record, which is why only ten records
   were restated through `record_verdicts --edition 1985`.

**Owed.** Correct design 10.3 to say the general formats were read. Find whether 1985-GR-VI.6.4.3-4 has a disposition that
rests on the old text; if so restate its record through `record_verdicts --edition 1985`. Then correct the count in
`kb/Work/PB4984.md` (the DEVLOG entry is history and stays as written). Wait for PR 1401 to land first; the files are not on
main yet.

**Resolved.** Design 10.3 now says the 210 general-format rows are read. 1985-GR-VI.6.4.3-4 has no record under tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/ (2,104 records, none for it), so no disposition rests on its old text and nothing needed restating. kb/Work/PB4984.md now says eleven rule texts changed, ten with change records.
