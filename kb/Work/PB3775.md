# PB3775 — review_findings.py start accepts a head that is no commit

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/review_findings.py#start`, `scripts/orchestrator/review_findings.py#SCHEMA`

## What happened

The round-2 reviewer of `worktree-wf_ba46c1d4-922-3` (w20261500c, PB3677) mistyped the head when it opened round 2:
`review_findings.py start` recorded `b4b38be60fd1...`, a sha that does not exist, in
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\mythos\review-w20261500-c.jsonl`. The tool accepted it, so round 2 named a head no commit has.
The reviewer noticed, closed round 2 with an empty verdict and ran round 3 at the real tip `b4b38be60b6b`.

## The defect

`start` checks the head's FORM (the schema's `COMMIT` is a hex string) but not that it is a commit of the branch under
review. A round opened at a head that is not in the branch can never be covered by `prune_worktrees.stamp_covers`, so
a CLEAN verdict on it would read as no review at all, silently, and a FINDINGS verdict would hold a branch for a head
it never had.

## What a fix must give

`start` (and every writer that records a head) resolves the head with `git rev-parse --verify <head>^{commit}` in the
branch's worktree and refuses, before writing, a head that is not a commit or not reachable from the branch's tip
(`git merge-base --is-ancestor <head> <branch>`), naming both; a self-test arm for each refusal and for the accepted
case.
