## Landing (wave 20261827 group A, with PB2299 and PB2371-PB2374)

**What landed.** `PlaceBuilder` (`Binding/Model/PlaceBuilder.cs`, namespace `WiseOwl.Cobol.Binding.Model`) is the one item-to-Place
builder, moved out of the resolver with `PlaceForItem`, `PlaceForRenames`, `SpanLeafPlace`, `WrapIfOdoGroup`, the access- and
table-path builders, the window scope walk and `CurrentOccurrenceCount`. It reads the binding through `IPlaceFacts`
(`OdoReferenceChecking`, `IsImageBackedEarly`, which `DataBinder` implements), so the model layer names no binder type;
`DeferredShape` (the census enum `PlaceGap` carries), `OccurrenceCounts` and `DataItem.HasVariableLengthSubordinate` moved to
the model with it. `PlaceTablePass` (a group pass after the storage-form pass, phase `PlacesBuilt`) fills a `PlaceTable` for
every unit (`BoundUnit.Places`, with its containers' GLOBAL items, because a contained program's places are built over its own facts)
and every OO class half (`OoClassUnit.Places`, `.FactoryPlaces`), and sets `ReportControlModel.Place` for each CONTROL operand
(the reference-modified slice of 13.18.16.3 SR4 included). The emitters read them: `UnitEmitters.Refs` is gone, the emit-time
`ResolveItem`/`RecordArea`/`ResolveItemRefMod` calls are `PlaceTable.Of`/`RecordArea` or the control's `Place`. The funnel
composes the builder for its own resolutions (`ReferenceResolver.Places`). Proof: `compare_oracle.py` IDENTICAL, 16113 cases.
**Edges.** The CodeGen and CodeGen.Emit pairs onto `ReferenceResolver`, `RefModBinder`, `SubscriptBinder` and the other parts,
and `Binding.Model` pairs for `OdoModel`, `DataItem`, `FileModel`, `RecordLayout`, are gone from the tolerated rows (ratcheted);
PB2375 left every `removedBy`. `BoundUnit` and `OoClassUnit` still hold the binder-side `Refs` (the PB2361 `DataBindResult` wave
takes them off the model) so those two pairs now name PB2361.
**Not removed here (reported as a lead).** The Format-2 table SORT of a shared-storage area windows its elements and keys at the
run-time element numbers of its own comparator (`SortEmitter.EmitSharedAreaTableSort` and the KeyWindowOuter arm): three
`PlaceTable.Window` / `.CurrentOccurrenceCount` calls whose positions are the statement's emit-time locals, so they cannot be
built when binding finishes. They go through the model-layer table over the frozen facts, never the binder; making them
bound-tree data means SortBinder building the windows over `StatementLocal` positions (kb/Work PB4321's pattern).

**Review round 1 (R1-1, R1-3).** The boundary's drift test is `PlaceTableBoundaryDriftTests` (tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit): no
file under `CodeGen` names `PlaceBuilder`, `IPlaceFacts`, `ReferenceResolver` or a by-item entry; the windowed answers are asked
only at the three `SortEmitter` sites (two `Window`, one `CurrentOccurrenceCount`; the count can drop, never rise); and
`PlaceTable.Build` is called only from `PlaceTablePass`. The architecture-edge ratchet cannot see this, because `CodeGen` to
`Binding.Model` is an allowed pair. A contained program's `PlaceTable` takes only its containers' GLOBAL items
(`DataBinder.GlobalPlaceableItems`, ISO 8.4.6.2.2), since it can name no others.
