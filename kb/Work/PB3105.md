# PB3105 — OCCURS has two mechanisms for its defining slot

Source: w1069h-PB2721-report.md, "New defects" 2 (wave 1069, branch db5-18).

After PB2732, index-names in `INDEXED BY` are refused by the grammar. `CAPACITY IN data-name-3` is still `dataReference` in
the grammar and is screened at bind time instead: `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Odo.cs:204`
(`CapacityRegisterName`, called at line 66) raises `ClauseOperandNotADataName` (also `DataBinder.cs:1822,1845,1875`).

Two mechanisms now do one job (what an OCCURS-clause operand may be). The implementer decides on one shape (grammar-level or
bind-level) and applies it to both. Shares its root with PB2732. No wrong answer is recorded; this is a structural defect.
