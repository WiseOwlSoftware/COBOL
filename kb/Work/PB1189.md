# PB1189 — OPEN

## Finding — `write-statement-p1` (under-rejects; rows `FMT-14.9.51.2`)

**Mechanism.** writeBeforeAfter grammar attaches the optional LINE/LINES to a dataReference without distinguishing identifier-2 from mnemonic-name-1, and BindAdvancing takes the mnemonic arm and drops the trailing word

**Repro.** SPECIAL-NAMES. C01 IS TOP-OF-FORM. ... WRITE R AFTER ADVANCING TOP-OF-FORM LINES (probe ws1mnlines.cob) and WRITE R BEFORE TOP-OF-FORM LINE (ws2mnl.cob), --std 2023

**Expected from the spec.** §14.9.51.2 Format 1 prints `{identifier-2 | integer-1} [LINE | LINES]` as one alternative and `{mnemonic-name-1 | PAGE}` as the other; LINE/LINES is not part of the mnemonic-name alternative, so `mnemonic-name-1 LINES` is not a WRITE statement and shall be rejected (as `AFTER ADVANCING PAGE LINES` already is, COBOL0307).

**Observed / reasoning.** Both compile rc=0 with no diagnostic; BindAdvancing resolves the dataReference through ctx.Mnemonics and returns FeatureAdvancing without looking at LINE()/LINES(). Semantics are unaffected (the word is ignored), hence under-rejects rather than wrong-answer. Fix belongs at bind (the grammar cannot tell a mnemonic from an identifier): reject when the mnemonic arm is taken and LINE()/LINES() is present.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/SequentialIoBinder.cs#BindAdvancing; src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolIO.g4#writeBeforeAfter

## What the fixer owes

SequentialIoBinder.BindAdvancing: reject when the mnemonic arm is taken and LINE()/LINES() is present (the grammar cannot tell a mnemonic from an identifier). Negatives ws1mnlines.cob, ws2mnl.cob.
