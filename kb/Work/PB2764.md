## Landed (wave 1052, group F)

**Reproduced on the base build** (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1052f\p1.cob`): `LOCALE-DATE("20261008" ar-EG)` returned `8 U+200F / 10 U+200F / 2026`
(11 positions), `LOCALE-TIME("093000" es-US)` returned `9:30:00 a. U+00A0 m.`, and the time-from-seconds arm the same designator.
Found beside it (same root, the culture's DEFAULT CALENDAR read where the Gregorian argument was formatted):
`LOCALE-DATE("20261008" fa-IR)` returned `1405/7/16` and `th-TH` `8/10/2569` (15 cultures: fa-*, ps-*, ckb-IR, lrc, mzn,
uz-Arab, th-*, ar-SA).

**Root cause.** Only the PATTERN was normalized (`NormalizeSpacing`); the renderer then substituted the raw
`DateTimeFormatInfo.DateSeparator` / `TimeSeparator` / `AMDesignator` / `PMDesignator` / `NumberDecimalSeparator` for the
pattern's `/`, `:`, `tt` and the fraction, and let `DateTime.ToString` format the date through the culture's calendar.

**Fix.** `Globalization/TimeFacts.cs`, the LC_TIME snapshot beside `MonetaryFacts` (internal, cached per `LocaleFacts`):
it owns the normalized separators, designators, era, day and month names and decimal point, and is THE renderer of
`d_fmt` (`FormatDate`) and `t_fmt` (`FormatTime`, moved out of `CobolLocale`). The date's year, month and day come from the
Gregorian date directly and the names from a Gregorian clone of the culture's format data (DETERMINATION L13).
`CobolLocale.Date/Time/TimeFromSeconds` format only through it. The shipped public `LocaleFacts.DateTimeFormat` /
`NumberFormat` / `DateFormat` / `TimeFormat` stay (removing a shipped member is a runtime-major change that re-baselines
every emitted module, `RuntimeAbiPinDriftTests`; outside this slice) and are documented as raw; `LocaleTextNormalizationDriftTests`
(Unit) holds it: no Cf/NBSP/U+202F/U+2009 in any of the 889 predefined cultures' date or time results, every pattern letter
a token the renderer handles, and no source outside the four snapshot classes reading a raw `DateTimeFormat`/`NumberFormat`.
Golden `2014/pb2764_locale_text_normalized`; CONFORMANCE.md section 4 item 5 (L12 extended, L13) and DESIGN-locale-facility 4.7 updated.
