Re-verdict the row on that residue, not on the two dead costs.

WHY THIS IS THE MECHANISM AND NOT FOURTEEN COINCIDENCES: the inventory row is the only place these defects are written down, and nothing reads it back. A kb/Work note lands and flips its own `status`; `record_verdicts.py` writes verdicts a batch supplies; no step asks 'which rows cited the code this change just replaced?'. The consequence is measurable and directional — the GAP denominator counts closed work as open, and `work.py next` ranks the campaign on it.

FIX SHAPE: a `closes-rows:` field in the kb/Work frontmatter (or a mechanical `code-location` → landing cross-check), plus a CI check that a note flipping to `status: landed` either names the inventory rows it closes or asserts it closes none — so the back-link exists as DATA rather than as a habit. That is the same 'selected, not listed' discipline PB198 used for the standard-binary predicate.

⚠ SCOPE HONESTY: this note measures ONE clause family (the §15 rows in this reviewer's slice). The same batch adjudicated §8.8.1 and §14.9.1-14.9.12; the population elsewhere is unmeasured and the sweep is part of the work.

## Mechanism

tests/version-matrix/traceability-inventory.json is the ONLY record of each of these defects, and the landing loop (a kb/Work note flipped to `status: landed` + a DEVLOG entry + scripts/spec/record_verdicts.py) has NO step that re-verdicts the rows a fix closes. The rows' notes carry located file:line evidence, which is exactly what goes stale silently: every one of the fourteen was re-measured at HEAD against its OWN located claim and the claim is now false.
