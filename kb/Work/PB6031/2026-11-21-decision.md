**Decision** (the codebase owner's, CLAUDE.md "Ownership"). An edition difference of the compiler is one of two kinds
and each has one register. A CONSTRUCT an edition lacks is a `tests/version-matrix/constructs/` row (COBOL0900, the
version-conformance pass). A RULE that every edition has but states differently, a syntax screen included, is a
`DialectBehavior` member asked through `DialectBehaviors.IsActive`, with its VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE row (both edges
quoted, `cite.py --check`). No site compares an edition year inline. A window that is not one edge (ExternalFileConnector

in DESIGN-version-conformance-pipeline.md section 2.4.3.
