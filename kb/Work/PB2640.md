**Rule it breaks:** ISO §14.7.4.3 GR4/GR5/GR6: under NEAREST-AWAY-FROM-ZERO (the bare ROUNDED), NEAREST-EVEN and NEAREST-TOWARD-ZERO the value is rounded to the NEAREST representable value; RoundDiv's own contract (CobolNum.cs:1101-1109) is the ONE rounding kernel for every carrier, so it must be exact over every divisor its callers pass

**Scenario:** RoundDiv computes twiceRem = T.Abs(rem) * 2 (CobolNum.cs:1117) with the unchecked generic operator. On Int128 any remainder above 2^126 overflows (verified on the pin's runtime: Int128 9.025e37 * 2 = -1.5978e38). CobolNum.Divide (1095-1098) passes den = b (or b*10^-exp) straight through, and NumericRenderer.Divide sends the OUTERMOST quotient to it at the receiver scale with the receiver's mode (NumericRenderer.cs:759-797); NumericRenderer.Multiply emits a bare Int128 product whenever the digit bounds sum to <= 38 (735-751). Repro: A, B PIC 9(19) VALUE 9500000000000000000; C, D PIC 9(19) VALUE 9700000000000000000; Q PIC 9; COMPUTE Q ROUNDED = (A * B) / (C * D). The quotient is 9.025e37 / 9.409e37 = 0.959, so GR4 stores 1; RoundDiv gets q = 0, rem = 9.025e37, twiceRem wraps negative, 'twiceRem >= divisor' is false, and Q is 0, silently, with no size error. The same happens for any binary-capacity 16-byte COMP-5 dividend and divisor above 8.5e37, and for NEAREST-EVEN / NEAREST-TOWARD-ZERO. The UInt128 lane is safe only because its divisors are powers of ten <= 10^38, and the CobolFloat caller only because its divisor is <= 2^126.

## Landed - 2026-10-08 (wave 1042 group F)

Re-probed (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1042f\p2640.cob`, the note's repro verbatim): `Q` was `0`; it is `1` after the fix. Fixed at the root the note named: `CobolNum.RoundDiv`
compares `|rem|` with its COMPLEMENT `divisor - |rem|` (above half <=> `|rem| > rest`, tie <=> `==`), which cannot overflow on any carrier, instead of doubling the remainder. One kernel, three carriers
(Int128, UInt128, BigInteger), so all three are exact; `RoundDivBoundaryTests` runs a sweep of remainders around the half on a divisor above 2^126 and checks the UInt128 and BigInteger lanes
agree with it. Golden `2023/pb2640_nearest_quotient_past_2_126` (the three NEAREST modes, the negative twin, the below-half control, TRUNCATION and AWAY-FROM-ZERO as the unaffected arms).

**Sibling sweep.** Every `* 2` / `<< 1` / "half" comparison in `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime`: `CobolDec.RoundFromRemainder` already used the complement form (its comment says so; it adds a sticky tail, so it stays its
own function -- the duplication of the decision is `PB2644`'s subject); `CobolDec.Round34*` compare a single digit; `FloatResultant` compares by exact decimal expansion. No other site doubles a remainder.
**Found in the same chain and FIXED (I9):** `Int128.MinValue` (the signed 16-byte COMP-5 minimum, the PB2641 family) has no signed negation, so `CobolNum.Divide`'s `if (den < 0) { num = -num; den = -den; }` left
`Min / -3` NEGATIVE and `Min / -1` silently `Min`, a divisor of exactly `Min` reached `RoundDiv` negative (the directed modes took the wrong sign), and `CobolDec.QuotientAtScale` refused `den > Int128.MaxValue`
(so `Min * 10 / Min` raised EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW). The rounding decision is now ONE function, `CobolNum.BumpsMagnitude` (sign and parity in, "move the magnitude one unit?" out), asked by `RoundDiv` and by
`CobolNum.LandQuotient`, which rounds a quotient formed on UNSIGNED magnitudes and signs it afterwards (2^127 is a legal result only when negative; anything else past `Int128.MaxValue` is EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW).
`Divide` sends a `Min` operand through `DivideByMagnitude`; `QuotientAtScale` ends in `LandQuotient`, which also deleted its private copy of the round-then-check dance and the `den > MaxValue` refusal. Pinned by
`RoundDivBoundaryTests` (`Min / ±3`, `Min / ±1`, `Min / Min`, the tie `2^126 / Min` in every mode, the wide alignment). `CobolDec.RoundFromRemainder` (the SDIDI rounding, with a sticky tail) keeps its own switch: it is `PB2644`'s subject.
