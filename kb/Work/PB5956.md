# the special registers LINAGE-COUNTER, LINE-COUNTER, PAGE-COUNTER and EXCEPTION-OBJECT have no symbol-table entry, so a reference to one is recorded by no occurrence and the cross-reference lists none of them

**Found by** listing step 7b (branch `listing-step-7b-2`, kb/Work PB5925's dated file `2026-11-20-step-7b-2-built`, lead 2 of its list), filed by step 9b so no lead of the listing's design stays in a dated paragraph alone.


`ReferenceResolver.Identify`'s register arms identify them, and no declaration enters them (design section 6.7.1's "Identify's arms all
record" holds for the CAPACITY, DEBUG-ITEM and sum-counter arms only). Each needs an entry where the standard defines it implicitly (a
LINAGE file's counter at its LINAGE clause, a report's counters at its RD), then the arm records like the others. The occurrence
oracle's `NamesNothing` exemption for the three counter tokens is removed with it.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs` (`ReferenceResolver.Identify`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/Listing/ReferenceOccurrenceDriftTests.cs` (`ReferenceOccurrenceDriftTests.EveryWrittenReferenceIsRecorded_OnceAtItsPosition_AndEveryStorePairs`).
