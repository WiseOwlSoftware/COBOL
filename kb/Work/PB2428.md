**Found by:** the PB2118 Draft 10 reviser, recorded as the first thing a tenth refuter should try (PB2118 "Draft 10",
"What a tenth refuter should try first" 1). No tenth refuter ran; the owner approved §8 Draft 10. In
`scripts/orchestrator/landing_check.py` an in-flight branch is R3 work when it can be bound to a dispatch-ledger entry
or train manifest; the binding uses declared-set overlap of the branch's changed files.

**Harm:** an R3 wave whose first commits edit only files outside its declared set (exactly the case check (1) exists
to stop) is read as fix-lane work until it touches a declared file. Its own landing is still stopped by check (1), but a
fix-lane train that lands in that window on one of those files is not stopped, and the R3 wave later meets a moved base.

**Fix direction (rule 5):** bind a worktree to its dispatch by the dispatch record itself (the ledger entry names the
branch or worktree at dispatch time), never by what it has edited; extend the self-test with the planted case.
