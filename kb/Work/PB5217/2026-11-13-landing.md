**Mechanism.** Every one of these rendered the record AREA through `PlaceTable.RecordArea`, the widest record's place, which for an OCCURS DEPENDING record is an `OdoGroupPlace` whose sending image is cut to the DEPENDING item's current extent. That extent describes that record only (§2023:13.18.43.4 GR13c), not the area every level-1 entry redefines (§2023:13.18.33.4 GR3).

**Fix.** `OperandText.RecordSendImage` takes the `AreaUse` its `RecordAreaImage` already had (PB5309); `SequentialIoEmitter.RecordAreaSendImage(file)` is the one image of the whole area (`AreaUse.Whole`), used by `SentRecordImage` (WRITE, REWRITE and RELEASE in every organization) and by the keyed READ, DELETE and START key images; the debug FileRecord contents read the area whole. `FileModel.TransfersPastRecord` also holds for a record-name-1 that holds an OCCURS DEPENDING table (its current size can be below the area's), so such a record in a fixed-length file, or a VARYING DEPENDING file, is followed by the area's positions. D-WRT1 in docs/CONFORMANCE.md says so.
