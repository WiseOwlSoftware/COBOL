# PB5093 - landed (train 1985 b, group t, 2026-11-21)

**Root cause.** `resolve_change` read a change only by its DESIGNATOR ("general rule 4"), inside `parse`, before the mint
made the rows of unnumbered text. So (a) a change written against an earlier numbering landed on the wrong rule (Amendment
1's INSPECT "general rule 4" is GR5 once Amendment 2 inserts a new GR1: the X3.23a-1989 sat on GR4); (b) a change that
names an ITEM ("last paragraph, item 2", IV-27; "item 3b for I-O status 22", IX-4; "second occurrence of a paragraph
numbered (3)", X3.23a-1989 IX-4 (T)) or a paragraph of prose ("Paragraph 6.4.7, change in part to read") named nothing;
(c) a change-table row with no page cell, which continues a change on the next table page (X3.23b-1993 page 918, the rest
of 4.2.2.1's new text), was dropped.

**Fix.** `rule_catalog_1985.attribute` (called by `extract_rule_catalog.main_1985` after `mint`) resolves each change row in
order: a whole added paragraph (PB5063); the change's NEW TEXT located in one row of the paragraph it names (`_keyed`,
a printed place such as "second paragraph" settling a text two paragraphs hold); else the designator (rule, ITEM with
occurrence and lettered sub-item, general format). A continuation row joins its change. The design is
DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md section 10.2, "How a change row names its rows".

**The drift test** is THE ATTRIBUTION CROSS-CHECK (`attribution_drift`, a finding `--check` fails on): every Errata
correction of kind amendment-1/amendment-2 whose text as applied lies in exactly one row of the paragraph its `where`
names must find that row carrying the amendment. Before the fix (prototype over the committed catalog) 27 such rows
lacked it; after: `located, the row carries its amendment 140`, 0 findings (`extract_rule_catalog.py --edition 1985
--check`, exit 0, "✓ no findings"). 161 corrections are not located (94 name no paragraph or quote no text, 58 hold a
text in no row, 9 in several), counted in the summary.

**The three rows of this note:** `1985-GR-IV.4.4.4-2` (was 1985-SR-IV.4.4.4-2; now `[X3.23b-1993]`),
`1985-GR-IX.1.3.4-L2.3` (now `[X3.23a-1989, X3.23b-1993]`), `1985-GR-VI.6.18.4-4` (now `[X3.23b-1993]`) and
`1985-GR-VI.6.18.4-5` (now `[X3.23a-1989]`). 47 rows' `amended_by` changed in all; no other field of any row changed.
Self-test cases (planted) fail on the base and pass at the head: text over number, occurrence and lettered item,
continuation row, the cross-check.
