# PB5497 - `X. Y.` with Y in column 11 is refused (1985 fixed form, paragraph-name after another paragraph header in the same line)

UNVERIFIED LEAD: re-probe before fixing.

**Lead, verbatim (reader rd-06).** "`X. Y.` with Y in column 11 is refused (no note)."

**The reader's text** (`rd-06.json`, rule `1985-GR-XII.2.4-9`, notes): "Also observed, root outside COPY: p9d2 (no COPY) refuses a paragraph-name beginning in column 11 after X. ("follows other text of its line"), so GR9's second sentence's case of two words in area A of one line cannot arise." The record's `known_note`: "The within-line area-A refusal (p9d2): none found in kb/Work."

The probe is `p9d2.cob` in `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/out-read4/work-rd-06/p`. This is an analysis because nobody has recorded whether the refusal is what the standard requires; the clerk judges nothing.

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/AreaPlacement.cs` (`AreaPlacement`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatMap.cs` (`PlacementAt`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/AreaPlacementPass.cs` (`Screen`)

**Verdict (wave 20262033, group C).** Re-probe on a Debug build of 6cc1376ab5: p9d2 exit 65, `COBOL3734 ... the paragraph header Y follows other text of its line`. The refusal was wrong: §1985:IV.7.3.3 (checked with `cite.py --check`) says "The paragraph header or paragraph-name starts in area A of any line following the first line of a division or a section", and area A is positions 8-11 (§1985 IV reference format), so Y in position 11 starts in area A whether or not it begins the line. The pass judged where an area-A item BEGINS ITS LINE, not where it starts. Fix: `AreaPlacement.WithinLine` is split into `WithinLineInAreaA` and `WithinLineInAreaB`; `ReferenceFormatMap.PlacementAt` classifies by the expanded program-text offset; `AreaPlacementPass.Screen` accepts a head in area A at line start or within the line. The text-in-area-A arm (COBOL3735) is unchanged: it asks only about text that begins a line. Goldens: `pb5497-paragraph-name-in-area-a-after-text-85` (accept 85, runs, output `IN Y`) and the refusal `pb5497-paragraph-name-in-area-b-after-text-85` (Y in position 12, COBOL3734).
