# PB5740 - a general format is witnessed by refusals alone

**Finding.** The evidence table `scripts/spec/data/lineage-evidence-kinds.json` lists `FMT` beside `SR` under
`diagnosed-kinds` ("a general format states the syntax a program may write: a program outside it is diagnosed"). Through
it, `lineage_reads.EvidenceTable.diagnosable` lets a diagnosis at the edition (a refusal test's `refuse-at`, a flagged
case's `at`) witness an older FMT row. The design states the same in DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md section 10.6
item 4: a diagnosis "witnesses a rule only when the catalog's `kind` of the older rule is one the evidence table ...
names as diagnosed", and the table's own caveat, "it misses a grant no sentence of the rule states (a form only its
general format shows)". The `needs-accepting-case` patterns read a rule's sentences, and a general format has none: so
no FMT row is ever sent to an accepted case.

**Why it is wrong.** A general format GRANTS forms. A refusal of a malformed one shows the compiler diagnoses what lies
outside the format; it does not show that any form the format grants compiles at the edition. Example (the reviewer's):
gate `FUNCTION PI` to 2014 and later by mistake, so `--std 2002` refuses it. `l1-pi-argument-list` still refuses
`FUNCTION PI(1)` at 2002, so a 2002-FMT-15.60.1 record witnessed by it alone stays CONFORMS and closed.

**Measured (2026-11-15, `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/records-2002-u2/finish/measure_fmt.py`, every stored record of each
older held edition whose rule's kind is FMT, its own conformance test-refs read through `lineage_reads.Suite`).** A row
counts when at least one case it cites runs at the edition and none is ACCEPTED there:
- **1985:** 213 FMT rows hold a record (72 KEPT, 141 own verdicts). **12 KEPT rows** rest on refusals alone:
  1985-FMT-VI.2.4.2, -VI.5.12.2, -VI.6.16.2, -VII.2.13.2, -VIII.2.5.2, -X.2.5.2, -X.5.6.2, -XIII.4.9.2,
  1989-FMT-A.2.16.2, -A.2.21.2, -A.2.39.2, -A.2.43.2. No own verdict does.
- **2002:** 0 KEPT FMT rows (the word-identical pairing kept none); 112 own verdicts. **19 rest on refusals alone, every
  one DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT** (the refusal there is the decline, under the read's checked `licence-2002`). The 11
  CONFORMS FMT own verdicts of the same shape that branch records-2002-reads-2 had written (2002-FMT-12.1.1, -7.2.18.1,
  -12.3.4.14.1, -12.3.4.9.1, -8.7.5.1, -14.8.44.1, -15.44.1, -15.60.1, -15.67.1, -15.72.1, -15.78.1) were dropped
  before it landed (commit 2a8f7d0f85) and are on its remaining list as `refused-needs-accepting-case`.

**The proposed rule.** An FMT row needs a case ACCEPTED at its edition, unless its format grants no form (a declined
facility, DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT with its licence, which a refusal does witness). In the table: move `FMT` out of
`diagnosed-kinds` (or give it a kind-wide `needs-accepting-case`), so the one judgment (`Witnesses.search` for the
writer, `Witnesses.own_problems` for the audit) asks an accepted case of every FMT row whose target is not declined.

**Why not in the landing that found it.** kb/Work PB5675 (landed e870f98665) and PB5730 (in flight) change how stored
records are evidenced; the tightening reopens stored rows, so it lands on its own with a before/after
`inventory_view.py --summary` and the list of reopened rows (by the measurement above: the 12 1985 KEPT rows, and any
2002 or 1985 FMT row recorded between now and then).
