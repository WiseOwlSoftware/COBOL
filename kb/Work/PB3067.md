# PB3067 — a FINDINGS branch can land through a carrier that never answered its findings

**Found 2026-10-11 (train 1063 lander's review pass, a silent-failure-hunter shard, over PB3027's landing diff).**

**Code site:** `scripts/prune_worktrees.py#contained` (the `findings` arm: a `DECISION` row whose report is stamped
`Review: FINDINGS` is made `IN FLIGHT` when its tip is an ancestor of any carrier's tip) and
`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (the FINDINGS arm of the finisher planning reads only branches that are not on main).

**Scenario.** Branch `pred` is reviewed and stamped `FINDINGS <n>`. A branch `succ` that contains `pred` (a same-file
successor cut from it, or a hand-cut continuation like train 1063's PB3061 branch cut from PB3022's) is reviewed CLEAN
and reaches a train. `contained()` reads `pred` IN FLIGHT ("its work lands through that branch"); once `succ` lands,
`pred`'s content is on main, the planner plans no finisher for it, and `--apply` deletes it. Nothing ever answers
`pred`'s findings by id. The design intends the absorbing carrier to be the REVIEW FINISHER, whose report carries a
`## Review findings` section answering each id (`wf_rolling_wave.js#fixFindings`); any other carrier's CLEAN review is
an independent review that may simply not have seen the findings.

**Fix shape (one mechanism).** In `contained()`, let a carrier absorb a FINDINGS row only when the carrier's report
answers every finding id of the row's findings file (`review_findings.py show <file> --round <n>` ids against the
carrier report's `## Review findings` lines); otherwise the row stays a DECISION naming the unanswered ids. A
`prune_worktrees.py --self-test` arm: a FINDINGS predecessor under a CLEAN non-finisher carrier stays DECISION.

**Repro.** `python scripts/prune_worktrees.py --self-test` has the finisher-carrier arm; plant a second carrier whose
report has no `## Review findings` section and observe the predecessor read IN FLIGHT.
