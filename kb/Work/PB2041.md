# PB2041 — OPEN — COBOLNET2024 quotes a rule the operand's clause does not have

Filed 2026-10-05 by the train-1020b lander, from its review of wave 1020 group H (PB1281).

**What happens.** PB1281 gave `DataBinder.ScreenDataNameShape` (the one data-name-n screen, COBOLNET2024) a required
`subscriptRule`, because its fixed closing sentence, *"the operand shall not be subject to any OCCURS clauses"*, was
false for a REDEFINES data-name-2 and for a SORT table key. Those two callers now pass their own rule. Every operand
that reaches the screen through `ScreenClauseOperandShape` / `ClauseDataName`
(`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.cs#ScreenClauseOperandShape`, which passes `NotSubjectToOccurs` for all
of them) still gets the fixed sentence, and the constant's own summary claims it is right for "the file clauses and the
SORT / MERGE file keys" only. It is wrong for at least:
- `OCCURS … KEY IS data-name-2` (`DataBinder.Odo.cs:48`): the key is the table entry or subordinate to it, so it IS
  subject to that OCCURS; its rule is `cite.py --check 13.18.38.3 "Data-name-1 and data-name-2 shall not be
  subscripted"` → OK §13.18.38.3 2).
- `OCCURS … DEPENDING ON data-name-1` (`DataBinder.Odo.cs:105`, `DataBinder.Reports.cs:2668`): the same SR2.
- `OCCURS DYNAMIC CAPACITY IN data-name-3` (`DataBinder.Odo.cs:206`): §13.18.38.3 31) "Data-name-3 shall not be
  subscripted" (`cite.py` OK).
- `RENAMES data-name-2 [THRU data-name-3]` (`DataBinder.cs:4295,4297`): §13.18.45.3 7) "Data-name-2 and data-name-3
  shall not be subscripted" (`cite.py` OK).

**Repro.** `01 T. 05 E OCCURS 3 ASCENDING KEY K(1) INDEXED BY I. 10 K PIC X.` — COBOLNET2024's message ends "the
operand shall not be subject to any OCCURS clauses", which is false for K. The source is illegal either way; only the
quoted rule is wrong.

**Fix shape.** `ClauseDataName(dref, clauseFace, subscriptRule)`: every caller passes its own clause's sentence (the
file-control and file-description clauses keep `NotSubjectToOccurs` only where their rule says that), and
`ScreenClauseOperandShape` disappears into it. Re-derive each caller's rule with `cite.py --check`; the
ASSIGN USING, FILE STATUS, COLLATING SEQUENCE OF, RECORD DEPENDING and LINAGE operands need the same check.
