# PB3512 — signed numeric MOVE into a reference-modified receiver moves the sign

## Scenario

Fuzz programs: fz04443 (`OUTPUT_DIFFERS:8.5.3`) and fz00248 (`OUTPUT_DIFFERS:8.8.4.12`, displays `001{002{` where the rule
requires `00100020`). Confirmed on the build of origin/main at fd407980f. Related: PB430 (the receiving half of GR4's one
test in MoveClassifier), PB803 (the overpunch letters).

### Reduced repro (the adjudicator's probe, verbatim)

```cobol
IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
PROGRAM-ID. D1RMSIGN.
DATA DIVISION.
WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
01 S4 PIC S9(4) VALUE 2468.
01 P4 PIC S9(4) PACKED-DECIMAL VALUE -1234.
01 L4 PIC S9(4) SIGN LEADING SEPARATE VALUE -1357.
01 X5 PIC X(5).
PROCEDURE DIVISION.
    MOVE S4 TO X5
    DISPLAY "A [" X5 "]"
    MOVE S4 TO X5(1:5)
    DISPLAY "B [" X5 "]"
    MOVE P4 TO X5(1:5)
    DISPLAY "C [" X5 "]"
    MOVE L4 TO X5(1:5)
    DISPLAY "D [" X5 "]"
    MOVE -42 TO X5(1:5)
    DISPLAY "E [" X5 "]"
    STOP RUN.
```

Expected (derived from the rules): `A [2468 ]`, `B [2468 ]`, `C [1234 ]`, `D [1357 ]`, `E [42   ]`.
Actual on main fd407980f (compile rc 0, run rc 0): `A [2468 ]`, `B [246H ]`, `C [-1234]`, `D [-1357]`, `E [-42  ]`.
Line A (the same MOVE into the un-modified receiver) is right; every reference-modified receiver is wrong.

## Expected (spec)

Each citation was re-run through `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check` from the worktree on 2026-10-22.

- §14.9.25.4 GR6 a) (OK; the check prints the item's lead sentence, the quoted sentence passes inside it): "If the sending
  operand is described as being signed numeric, the operational sign is not moved; if the operational sign occupies a
  separate character position, that character is not moved and the size of the sending operand is considered to be one
  less than its actual size."
- §8.4.3.3.4 GR6 (OK): "The unique data item is considered to be an elementary data item without the JUSTIFIED clause."
  A reference-modified receiver is an ELEMENTARY alphanumeric item, so the move is the elementary numeric-to-alphanumeric
  move of §14.9.25.4 GR6, not a byte copy.
- §14.6.8 "Alignment and transfer of data into data items" (OK as a heading; the GR6 a) text names it for the receiving
  alignment and space fill).

## Mechanism (read on main)

- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Bound/MoveClassifier.cs:300`: `Kind(BoundOperand source, Place target)` begins with
  `if (target is RefModPlace) return MoveKind.RefModSlice;`, BEFORE the elementary-move classification, so every
  reference-modified receiver takes one arm regardless of the sender's category.
- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/MoveEmitter.cs:152`: the `MoveKind.RefModSlice` arm's default case writes
  `OperandText.AsString(source, num)`, the sender's RAW STORED FORM (digits with the overpunched sign, a packed item's
  rendering with its '-', the SIGN SEPARATE character), into the slice.

## Fix shape (for the implementer)

Root-cause shape to require (CLAUDE.md rules 4 and 5): the reference-modified receiver must classify as what §8.4.3.3.4
GR6 says it is, an elementary alphanumeric (or national) receiver, and go through the SAME sender-category conversion
every elementary move uses (GR6 for a signed numeric sender, the figurative and literal rules for the others), the slice
being only the PLACE written. A sign-stripping patch inside the RefModSlice arm is a workaround (a second place where GR6
is written) and is not acceptable. Sibling sweep: every other receiver kind that is routed by place shape before sender
category (a slice of a numeric-edited? a national slice with an alphanumeric sender: §14.9.25.4 GR6 lead paragraph conversion (GR6 b) is the same-item rule, corrected on landing)), and the
other verbs that write a reference-modified receiver from a numeric sender through the same arm (STRING/UNSTRING/INSPECT do
not convert; MOVE CORRESPONDING cannot name a slice; INITIALIZE? check).

## Golden to require

The repro above as a conformance case with the derived output; a second case with a national receiver slice and a numeric
sender (§14.9.25.4 GR6 lead paragraph)); a drift test that MoveClassifier never returns a kind for a RefModPlace receiver before the sender's
category is classified.

## Why the fuzz saw it

The differential (PB3463/PB3488) classified fz04443 and fz00248 `OUTPUT_DIFFERS`: GnuCOBOL moved the unsigned digits,
WiseOwl moved the sign. The evidence record and the DEVLOG entry are added by a later pass.

## Landed (wave 1147, group C)

Re-probed on the worktree build of aef934867 first: the reduced repro printed exactly the note's "Actual" lines
(`B [246H ]`, `C [-1234]`, `D [-1357]`, `E [-42  ]`), and three more shapes failed the same way — a JUSTIFIED RIGHT
item's slice (`*246Q*`), a national slice (`246Q *`) and a slice of a numeric item (`246Q 0`). After the fix every line
is the derived one (`2468 `, `1234 `, `1357 `, `42   `, `*2468*`, `2468 *`, `2468 0`).

**Fix.** `MoveKind.RefModSlice` is deleted: no kind names a receiver's place shape. `MoveClassifier.Kind` classifies a
slice like any elementary receiver (group receiver → group sender → figurative-into-numeric → convert), the numeric test
reading the new `ReceivingCategory`, which answers a slice through `RefModPlace.Category` (§8.4.3.3.4 GR6, never
numeric). The emitter's receiving half chooses by place: `MoveEmitter.ElementaryStore` sends a slice to `SliceStore`
(the splice, and §8.3.3.6.4 GR2's whole-slice figurative / ALL fill), whose sender is `SendingCharacters` — THE
§14.9.25.4 GR6 a) characters `ConvertSource`'s three character arms now also call, so the sign drop is written once.
A group sender into a slice is GR4's group move (`EmitGroupToElementaryMove`'s slice arm →
`ReceivingStore.SliceStorageArea`), which also fixed a sibling found on the way: a group into a NATIONAL slice took the
group's bytes as its characters (`*AB1K*`) where the whole national receiver decodes them (design D-N8); it now
decodes alike. `ReceivingStore.StorageBytes` is the one byte-width spelling both group arms use.

**Sweep.** The implicit MOVEs (READ / RETURN INTO, INITIALIZE, CORRESPONDING, function RETURNING) are `BoundMove`s
classified by the same `MoveClassifier.Classify`, so they inherit the fix (INITIALIZE of a slice and a zero-length
literal into a slice probed right). STRING / UNSTRING / INSPECT / ACCEPT write slices from character senders and convert
nothing. CALL / INVOKE RETURNING into a slice of a numeric returning item is §14.8.3.3 nonconforming (the PICTUREs must
match), not a MOVE. ⚠ Citation correction: this note's "GR6 b)" for the alphanumeric-to-national conversion was wrong —
`cite.py` places that text in GR6's lead paragraph; GR6 b) is the sending-and-receiving-the-same-item rule.

**Goldens.** `conformance-suite/cases/14.9.25-MOVE/pb3512_signed_sender_refmod_receiver` (85-2023) and
`pb3512_numeric_sender_national_slice` (2002-2023); drift test `RefModMoveClassificationDriftTests`.

## Landing

- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Bound/MoveClassifier.cs#Kind` — classifies every receiver by the sender's category;
  `ReceivingCategory` is the receiver-category reader (a slice through `RefModPlace.Category`). Trap: `target.Item` of a
  `RefModPlace` is identifier-1, never the slice — ask the category reader.
- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/MoveEmitter.cs#SliceStore` / `#SendingCharacters` — the slice receiving half and
  the ONE GR6 a) sending characters; reuse `SendingCharacters`, never `OperandText.AsString(…, deSign: true)` again.
- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ReceivingStore.cs#SliceStorageArea` / `#StorageBytes` — GR4 group bytes into a
  slice, decoded by usage; `PlaceRenderer.SliceLength` is the run-time slice length.
