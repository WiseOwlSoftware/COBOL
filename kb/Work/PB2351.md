# PB2351 — the census computes DESIGN-architecture-review §8.2 rules 1-3

**Wave kind:** census tooling (then the generator files the findings). **Model:** Sonnet.

**What §8.2 rule 6 says:** the census computes rules 1-3 with three new per-type columns (`files`, `publicTypesInFile`, `fileNameMatches`), so the counts stop being text scans; `scripts/arch/file_census_notes.py` files their findings like the others (rules 2 and 3 have no notes until it does); the drift assertions read the newest record, as `ArchCensusDriftTests` does.

**Sites:** `tools/ArchCensus` (the measurement), `scripts/arch/census.py`'s record (the columns), `scripts/arch/file_census_notes.py` (the filing) and `ArchCensusDriftTests`.

**What it makes into findings:** the 178 multi-type files, the 22 misnamed files (`EmitCore.cs` and `ExceptionState.cs` are the measured offenders among them) and the `BoundTree.cs` family split. §8.7's "Not yet notes" row lists exactly these ("§8.2 rules 2-3 (178 multi-type files, 22 misnamed, `BoundTree.cs`) when the census computes them (§8.2 rule 6)"). The notes the generator then files are Move and rename waves (Sonnet for rewriter-driven moves, Opus for judgments).

**Already claimed (R1 Draft 3):** `BoundTree.cs`'s family split is filed as designed notes PB2393 and PB2394 (steps 4 and 5 of §8.3 (8)); their targets record claims that file for the finding kinds `multi-type-file` and `misnamed-file`, so when this note makes the census report those kinds under exactly those names, `file_census_notes.py` skips the `BoundTree.cs` findings as `claimed-by-design` instead of filing a second claim on one extraction.

**Blocked by:** PB2118 (§8 binds nothing until the owner approves it).
