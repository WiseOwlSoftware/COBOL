# PB84 — LANDED 2026-08-18 — `IF 9 ** TWO + (180 - 90) IS NOT POSITIVE` was `error CS0019` (NIST NC250A red in battery #5)

> ⚙ **flags:** crashes (backend compilation failure on legal source), rejects-legal-source · found 2026-08-18 by the
> PB68+PB69 battery (`NistDifferentialTests.NistProgram_MatchesGolden(NC250A)` — the only red of 4633) · fixed the
> same session (DEVLOG 1302).

**Measured** (`--std 2023`, native arithmetic, A = 12): `IF A ** 2 IS POSITIVE` → `CS0019: Operator '>' cannot be
applied to operands of type 'CobolDec' and 'int'`; `SET P UP BY A ** 2` and `ALLOCATE A ** 2 CHARACTERS` →
`CS0030: Cannot convert type 'CobolDec' to 'long'`; `CALL … USING BY VALUE A ** 2` → CS0030; `INVOKE O "M" USING
BY CONTENT A ** 2` → CS1503. **The same five consumers were ALREADY broken for every arithmetic expression under
`ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD-DECIMAL`** (`IF A * B - 60 IS ZERO`, `SET P UP BY A + 1`, `CALL … BY VALUE A / 8` — 14
Roslyn errors on the twin golden before the fix, measured by stashing the fix and rebuilding).

**Mechanism:** PB69 made every native integer power an SDIDI (`Dec`) intermediate; each of these consumers read a
rendered `NumX` as if it were the native Int128 carrier — `{v.Expr} > 0`, `(long)({x.Expr})`,
`CobolNum.Store(ex.Expr, ex.Scale, profile)` — with no `Dec` arm (the two-arm-dispatch shape: the relation renderer,
the arithmetic store and the MOVE store each had the arm; these five did not).

**Fix (one landing, one store):** `NumericRenderer.Landed(x, rcv)` is now THE landing of an intermediate into the
Int128 lane at the receiver's working scale (moved out of `IntrinsicRenderer`, which delegates); the CALL BY VALUE,
SET UP/DOWN BY and ALLOCATE emitters funnel through it. `NumericRenderer.StoreArgs`/`StoreExpr` are THE carrier
switch at a fixed-point store — the arithmetic store, the numeric MOVE and both INVOKE BY CONTENT arms now spell it
once (`RuntimeApi.NumStoreDec` deleted as dead). The sign condition gained its `Dec` arm (`RuntimeApi.DecSign` —
`Int128.Sign(dec.Sig)`, exact at every exponent). Goldens `pb84_sdidi_intermediate_consumers` (native — the NC250A
shapes, pointer round-trip, ALLOCATE, CALL BY VALUE 144 / 1.25, INVOKE 000144 / 125) and
`pb84_standard_decimal_intermediate_consumers` (the twin). Sibling found by the same probe: [[PB85]] (DIVIDE …
REMAINDER over a float sender), landed in the same commit; [[PB86]] (PERFORM … TIMES with a function-identifier
count runs once) registered.
