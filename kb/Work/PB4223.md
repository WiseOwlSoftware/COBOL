# PB4223 — numericOrdering past 254 digits: ICU parity or full numeric value

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Collation/CollationElementIterator.cs:94` (`CollationElementIterator.StartNumber`) and
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/Collation/CldrCollationSettingsTests.cs:256`
(`NumericOrdering_AVeryLongRunIsOneNumber`). These lines are on branch `worktree-wf_94e81f77-f38-7`, queued as PR #894, and may
not be on origin/main yet; the lines were read there.

**The facts.** ICU splits a digit run longer than 254 into 254-digit numbers. This engine orders by the run's full numeric
value, deliberately, and the test `NumericOrdering_AVeryLongRunIsOneNumber` documents and pins it.

**Question for the owner.** Keep ordering a very long digit run by its full numeric value, or switch to ICU's split at 254
digits for parity?
