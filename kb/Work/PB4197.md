# PB4197 — finishers of one session share a gate log name

**Sites:**
- `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/finisher-brief.md` (THEN step 2, "GATE": "in the background to a log")
- `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/dispatch-spec-implementer.md` (check for the same unnamed log)

**What happened.** On 2026-10-31 the Mythos session dispatched three finishers at once (branches c21-35, 6b1-4, 6b1-6).
Subagents of one session share its scratchpad directory, and two of them chose the same name, `gate.log`, for their gate's
background output. The PB4174 finisher's `until grep -q "=== BUILD-LOCAL GATE: "` loop matched the 6b1-4 finisher's GREEN line
first. It noticed (the run directory named in the line was not its own) and read its own run's `verdict.json` through
`run_gate_legs.py --await`, so no wrong verdict was used; a less careful agent would have reported another branch's GREEN.

**To fix.** The brief names the log, unique per branch: `<worktree>\TestResults\finisher-gate-<n>.log` (inside the worktree,
so two branches cannot meet), and says the verdict is read from the run's own `verdict.json` (`run_gate_legs.py --await`),
whose head is checked against the branch's HEAD. Sweep every brief and template that says "to a log" for the same gap, and
make `check_practices.py` require the named path.
