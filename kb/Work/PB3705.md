# PB3705 — the by-content MOVE golden cites a clause arm that answers a different receiver class

**Sites:** `conformance-suite/cases/_unassigned/pb53_by_content_move_rules.cob:41` (the header comment naming the governing clause).

## What is wrong

The header says pairs 1, 4 and 6 "move boolean values (14.9.25.4 GR6a)". `cite.py --check` passes because the quoted sentence ("If the
sending item is of class boolean, its boolean value shall be moved") is genuinely in the standard. It sits in arm a), which covers an
alphanumeric, alphanumeric-edited, national or national-edited RECEIVING operand. That fits pair 1 (B1 into `RN PIC N(4)`). Pairs 4 and 6
move into `Q`/`RB PIC 1(4) USAGE BIT`, a boolean receiver: pair 6 (same usage) is GR6 c) ("transferred to the receiving data item
without change"), and pair 4's truncation is §14.6.8.6, which line 75 already cites. The expected values (`1100`) are correct either way.

This is the inherited-citation pattern of CLAUDE.md rule 1: the text is real, the clause answers a different question. A maintainer who
adjudicates a boolean-to-boolean MOVE and starts from this header reads GR6 a) as governing it, and may take alignment from
§14.6.8 "any necessary space filling" instead of the zero fill of §14.6.8.6.

## Fix

Cite GR6 a) for pair 1 only, GR6 c) for pair 6 and §14.6.8.6 for pair 4, each through `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check <clause> "<text>"`
(rule 1). Citation only: no expected-output change. Sweep the neighbouring `pb53_*` goldens for the same pairing of a GR6 arm with a
receiver class.
