# PB3550 — the two-runner drift test reads a non-observation as a verdict difference

## Lead (the implementer's, verbatim from the PB3108 report; found by reading, not by a run)

Two-runner drift test reads a non-observation as a verdict difference (process-only). In-process `ConformanceSuiteRunner.Judge` turns a budget kill into `NO VERDICT` (tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/ConformanceSuiteRunner.cs:58-60) and run.py gives `NO VERDICT` only past its 600 s hang guard (conformance-suite/harness/run.py:76, 245); the drift test's `differing` compare (ConformanceSuiteRunnerDriftTests.cs, `differing`) then reports load as drift, and the two runners judge one program under different per-process budgets. Repro: any suite program that runs past one unit in-process (killed twice, NO VERDICT) and under 600 s in run.py (PASS) is reported as differing verdicts, not as a non-observation; shown by reading the two code sites, not yet by a run. Same root as PB3108's budget family.
