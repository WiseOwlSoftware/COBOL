# PB2921 — the factory suffix is not injective

Filed 2026-10-10 by the train 1052 lander (phase 1b) from the wave 1051 B implementer's report
(`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1051b-PB2843-report.md`, "New defects" item 2).

**Rule.** §8.3.2.1: a COBOL word may contain hyphens anywhere but first or last ("The hyphen or underscore shall not
appear as the first or last character in such words"), so `X--FACTORY` is a legal class-name.

**Where.** PB2839 (landing in train 1052) made the word-to-identifier mapping injective
(`DataItem.IdentifierCharacters`, `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/DataItem.cs`): `-` becomes `_`, so
`X--FACTORY` becomes `X__FACTORY`. The factory class of a class `X` is named `X` + `NamingConvention.FactorySuffix`
(`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Oo/NamingConvention.cs`, line 21: `"__FACTORY"`), also `X__FACTORY`.
`OoClassTable.Build` adds the factory name to `usedCsNames` and ignores the result
(`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Oo/OoClassTable.cs`, about line 355, commented "belt-and-braces (a `__` name cannot collide
with COBOL-derived names)"), and `docs/COBOLNET_OO_SLICE_BRIEFS.md` (Naming safety, about line 39) states the same false
premise.

**Repro (to write).** A compilation group with `CLASS-ID. X.` (with a FACTORY paragraph) and `CLASS-ID. X--FACTORY.`:
expected to compile and run; observed CS0101 (the namespace already contains a definition for `X__FACTORY`).

**Fix shape.** The tagged families (`__FACTORY`, `__Instance`, `__New`, ...) must be outside the image of the word
mapping: either the mapping escapes a doubled `-` (so no word yields `__`), or the suffix carries a character the
mapping never emits; then `usedCsNames.Add` failing is an internal error, not ignored. Correct the slice brief's
"Naming safety" paragraph in the same change, and add the pair to `IdentifierInjectivityTests`.
