**Landed (2026-11-13).** Wave 20262020, group F. Re-probe on the base build (45199b9c36): `GENERATE R1` of a one-detail report with a USE BEFORE REPORTING DE-1 procedure displayed no USE line and left the hit count 0 at `--std 85` and `--std 2023` alike. The fix: `CobolReport.GenerateCore`'s summary arm now PROCESSES the sole detail through the one group prologue, `BeginGroup(detail, print: false)`: its own sum additions, its USE procedure (after the subtotalling, §1985:XIII.3.20.4 GR11 a) then b)), its end-of-group reset, and no presentation (GR11 d)). A SUPPRESS the procedure executes has nothing left to inhibit and does not carry into the next GENERATE (the hook scopes it).

**No edition arm.** The note's fix direction proposed a dialect behavior on the premise that 2023 produces no group. The 2023 text does not support that: §2023:14.9.16.4 GR2 makes summary reporting "the same processing as for a GENERATE data-name statement for the same report, except that no detail is printed", and §2023:14.9.49.4 GR9 performs the procedure "on each occasion that the named report group is processed"; the 1985 inventory record of the rule (`tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/1985-GR-XIII.3.20.4-11.json`, KEPT) says "Same processing"; GnuCOBOL 3.x runs the procedure as well (it runs it before the subtotal, and for a report of several details it runs the first detail's, both of which the standard's order and the PB5249 pinned choice rule out). So the behavior is every edition's, recorded in docs/CONFORMANCE.md beside PB5249's (d).
