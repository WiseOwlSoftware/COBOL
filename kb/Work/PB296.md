# PB296

Raised by [[PB183]]'s landing (2026-09-01), which enforces §13.18.60.3 SR14 / SR15 / SR4 at every edition that
can express their subjects (2002+).

**THE QUESTION.** Were these three rules present, and worded this way, in COBOL 2002 and COBOL 2014? The mission
is four per-edition compilers, and a rule introduced or tightened in 2023 must NOT be enforced below 2023 — that
is the [[four_editions_one_compiler]] obligation, and gating it later is an edition-gate sweep, not a one-liner.

**WHAT IS SETTLED.** The MESSAGE-TAG phrase is a 2023 addition (`ReservedWords.Table.cs`: "added 2023 (ISO Annex
E.2 item 25 = VCR row 32)"), so SR14's and SR4's message-tag arms are ≥2023 by construction. That arm is
unreachable today anyway — there is no `Usage.MessageTag` member.

**WHAT IS NOT, AND WHY THE OBVIOUS CHECK DOES NOT SETTLE IT.** For POINTER / OBJECT REFERENCE / PROGRAM-POINTER /
FUNCTION-POINTER, a sweep of Annex E finds NO note recording a change to §13.18.60.3. That is tempting to read as
"unchanged since 2002" and it is not evidence: Annex E's scope is the 2014→2023 delta only —
`VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` row 7.15 states this explicitly for RERUN ("no Annex E note exists — the deletion
predates its 2014→2023 scope"). A missing observation is not a negative one
([[verdict_evidence_invariant]]). Annex E can rule out a 2014→2023 change; it cannot speak to 2002→2014 or to
1985→2002 at all.

**CURRENT POSTURE, chosen deliberately.** The arms ship UNGATED below 2023. That enforces the rule the compiler
can actually cite, and it is the direction that does not invent an edition boundary from silence. If SR14 turns
out to be a 2023 tightening, the pre-2023 compilers are over-rejecting a shape those editions allowed — which is
`rejects_legal_source` for three of the four compilers and would raise this note's severity.

**WHAT SETTLING IT NEEDS.** The ISO/IEC 1989:2002 and ISO/IEC 1989:2014 texts, which are not in this tree
(`specs-private/` holds the 2023 PDF only). This is the `blocked: true` reason — it is blocked on a document,
not on analysis or on another note.

**IF IT TURNS OUT TO BE A 2023 TIGHTENING**, the change is confined by design: the level/strong test is one named
predicate (`Sr14PermittedLevel` / `Sr14UnderStrongTypeDeclaration`) and the pass is one declared entry in
`BindPipeline`, so the gate is an edition guard at the head of `CheckUsageDeclarations` plus the mandatory
corpus re-run at 85/2002/2014/2023 ([[edition_gate_sweep]]) and a VCR row.

**COLLATERAL WORTH RECORDING.** This is not unique to §13.18.60.3. Any rule whose per-edition history is asserted
from Annex E's silence carries the same defect, and the derivation "Annex E is silent, therefore unchanged" reads
as sound at every one of them.
