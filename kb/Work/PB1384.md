## Finding — compiler-directives (silent)

directive-inside-text-manipulation-statement: the merged CC+COPY driver (ConditionalCompilationProcessor.Render) flushes the pending COPY block at every '>>' line on the stated premise 'a COPY can never span a directive line', but nothing diagnoses the violation. A COPY statement split by a directive is then resolved from a truncated block: no text-name is read, the non-strict Frontend path (CopyProcessor strict:false) turns the not-found result into a comment with no diagnostic, and the COPY's remaining words ('cb3.') are left for the parser, where they read as a paragraph-name. A directive inside a REPLACE statement is likewise accepted.

**Rows:** SR-7.3.3-8

**Repro:**

```
Copybook cb3.cpy: '           DISPLAY "CB3 AA".' Program: PROCEDURE DIVISION. '           COPY' / '       >>DEFINE VV AS 1' / '               cb3.' / '           STOP RUN.' (E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2\out\probe\cd\W4C.cob; W4.cob uses >>TURN, W4D names a nonexistent copybook, W2.cob puts a >>DEFINE inside 'REPLACE ==AA== BY' ... '==BB==.').
```

**Expected from the spec:** §7.3.3 SR8: a compiler directive may be specified anywhere 'except ... b) within a source text manipulation statement' (cite.py --check 7.3.3 OK). W4C, W4, W4D and W2 must each be rejected with a diagnostic naming the directive inside the COPY/REPLACE statement.

**Observed:** W4C and W4 compile clean, run, and print NOTHING - the copybook is silently not incorporated; W4D (copybook does not exist) is equally silent and prints 'W4D'; W2 compiles clean. W1 and W3 are rejected only incidentally (COBOL0001 / COBOLNET1641). Aggravating sibling, separately worth a note: a plain 'COPY nosuch.' with a nonexistent copybook compiles and runs with NO diagnostic in the default (non-NIST) mode (W4E prints 'W4E'), because Frontend.Preprocess constructs CopyProcessor with strict:false - §7.2.3.4 GR1 'Text-name-1 or literal-1 identifies the library text to be processed by the COPY statement.'

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ConditionalCompilationProcessor.cs#Render (the Flush at a directive line); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ResolveOneCopy (NotFound fallback, reported only when _strict); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Pipeline/Frontend.cs#Preprocess (strict: false)

## What the fixer owes (and the sibling sweep)

Diagnose a directive line while a COPY/REPLACE statement is open (the driver knows — it flushes there); with PB1355 the truncated COPY can no longer degrade to a comment. Negative golden: `COPY` / `>>DEFINE X AS 1` / `cb3.`.
