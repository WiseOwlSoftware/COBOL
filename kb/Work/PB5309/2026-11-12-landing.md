Landed (wave 20262009, group A). Re-probe on this build: `p08.cob` printed `INTO: [        AB          ] LEN=12`, as the note says.

**Cause, corrected.** The area of a classic OCCURS DEPENDING ON record is not a "variable-length" record in the compiler's sense (`FileModel.IsVariableLengthRecord` is the 2023 dynamic-length and dynamic-table notion), so the record area was read by `OperandText.AsStorageImage` through `PlaceRenderer.SendingGroupImage`, which cuts an `OdoGroupPlace` to the table's current count (`SendingImage`, `Substring(0, OdoExtentChars(...))`), the sending reading of §1985:VI.5.8.4 GR3. The sender of an INTO move is not an operation on the group: GR12 fixes its positions alone.

**Fix.** `OperandText.RecordAreaImage` takes an `AreaUse` (`Sending`, `Receiving`, `Whole`), the one vocabulary of how much of an OCCURS DEPENDING group an operation uses of its record area; `CurrentRecordImage` reads `AreaUse.Whole`. Golden `cases/VII.3.8@1985-RECORD-CLAUSE/pb5309-read-into-odo-first-record` (the table in the first record description of one file and the second of another). Drift: `RecordAreaUseDriftTests`.

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Roslyn/PlaceRenderer.cs` (`UsesCurrentExtent`, now `internal`)
