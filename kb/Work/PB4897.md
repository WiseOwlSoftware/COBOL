# PB4897 - a bracket read as a brace on the bold face

**What the reports say.** The PB4816 finisher's review lists as a standing lead: "Amendment 1 bold face (PDF pages 841, 844)
54 pt bracket read as a brace, `figure_raster._straight`/`_classify_curly`." PB4848 (landed) states the same in prose, "Not right
yet (registered, not fixed)": the 54 pt closing bracket with 1.3 pt feet reads as a brace and one five-line shape stays
unclassified; `BRACKET_STRAIGHT` cannot separate it from the brace without losing page 693's.

**Why a note.** PB4848 is landed and holds the item only as prose; no open note carries it (grep of `kb/Work` for
`figure_raster`: PB4815, PB4817, PB4848 landed, PB4849 open and silent on it).

**Re-probe (2026-11-06, this tree).** The format is the subscript format, §1985:IV.4.3.8.2.2 as Amendment 1 (PDF page 841) replaces it:
`data-name-2 [{+|-} integer-2]` over `index-name-1 [{+|-} integer-3]`, so each side holds TWO brackets and the inner
`{+|-}` are FOUR braces. The note's "54 pt closing bracket" is the two closing brackets of those two lines, stood one on
another and joined foot to foot. Measured on a crop of each page at its own skew (base tree):

| page | brackets L/R | braces L/R | what happened |
|---|---|---|---|
| 841 | 1/1 (want 2/2) | 3/3 (want 4/4) | each bracket pair one 53 pt stem; each `{+/-}` pair, joined hook to hook, one brace pointing at the joint |
| 844 | 1/0 (want 2/2) | 4/5 (want 4/4) | the closing pair's stem was DROPPED (`find_stems` took the component from the stem's middle pixel, which is paper between the joined feet), so its ink was read as a brace pointing at the joint |

The "five-line shape unclassified" (the 80 pt brace) does not reproduce in the format band at any skew from -0.4 to
+0.3 degrees after the fix, nor at the page's own skew on the base tree.

**Landed.** One mechanism, both arms: delimiters of consecutive lines JOINED END TO END are read as one.
- Brackets: `split_stacked_stems` cuts a stem at each band of feet INSIDE it (feet turning as its end feet do, at most
  `STACK_JOINT_H` 3.0 thick: 2.0 to 2.3 measured on both pages; a glyph fused to a stem is 5 or more), each piece at least
  MIN_VERT_H tall and a bracket of the same hand by itself. `_is_foot` is the one foot test `_foot_side` and the cut share.
- `find_stems` takes a stem's component as the label holding most of its pixels, not its middle pixel.
- Braces: `_curlies` cuts a shape at a bend of its centre line TOWARD the hooks' side (`BRACE_JOINT` 0.6: 1.1 at page
  841's joints; the 80 pt brace's middle 0.3 at most) when both pieces hold braces of that hand; `_edges` is the one edge
  reader `_classify_curly` and the cut share.
- Self-test: planted 8h (bold brackets joined foot to foot, paper at the middle pixel) and 8i (braces joined hook to
  hook, and a single brace as tall that must not be cut), and crops of PDF pages 841 and 844 in `figure_raster_pages/`
  (2/2 brackets, 4/4 braces each, counted by eye). Each new case fails on the base tree for the reason above.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/figure_raster.py` (`find_stems`, `_foot_side`, `_is_foot`, `split_stacked_stems`, `_edges`,
`_classify_curly`, `_curlies`, `find_braces`, `measure_image`, `STACK_JOINT_H`, `BRACE_JOINT`, module docstring),
