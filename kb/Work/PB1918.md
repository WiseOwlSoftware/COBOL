## Finding

**Site.** `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Cobol.Net.Frontend.csproj:23` — the `InternalsVisibleTo` item names `CobolSharp.Tests.Unit`,
an assembly name that no longer exists; the unit test assembly is `Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit` (`Cobol.Net.Editions` grants
the right name). Wave 1004 group B had to test `CopyProcessor.OpenStatementAt` and the DISPLAY directive's helpers
through public entry points for that reason.

**Fix shape.** Rename the grant to `Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit` (no alias kept, CLAUDE.md rule 4), then let the tests that
reached internals indirectly call them directly where that makes them sharper (`OpenStatementAt` first).
