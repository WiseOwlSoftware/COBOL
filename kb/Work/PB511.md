# PB511

## Mechanism — data-description-entry-p1
The EXTERNAL clause's `AS literal-1` phrase has no grammar: `externalClause : IS? EXTERNAL` is the whole rule, so the externalized-name phrase printed in the data-description Format 1 (and in the EXTERNAL clause's own general format) is an ANTLR parse error at every edition that has it.

### Expected, from the standard
ISO 13.16.2 Format 1 prints the slot as `[ IS EXTERNAL [ AS literal-1 ] ]`, and 13.18.22.2 gives the clause's whole general format as `IS EXTERNAL [ AS literal-1 ]`; 13.18.22.3 SR3 constrains literal-1 (alphanumeric or national, not a figurative constant, not zero-length) and 13.18.22.4 GR5/GR6 make it the externalized name. The entry shall COMPILE, and A's externalized name shall be 'EXTNAME'.

## Landed

**Both arms of one clause, because the figure prints the slot twice.** 13.18.22.2's general format is
`IS EXTERNAL [ AS literal-1 ]` -- RENDERED from the printed page (PDF p430 / folio 400: `IS` bare, `EXTERNAL` and
`AS` underlined) -- and the data-description Format 1 (13.16.2) and the file-description Formats 1/2/3 (13.4.5.2)
both print the slot as `[ IS EXTERNAL [ AS literal-1 ] ]`. The grammar had `externalClause : IS? EXTERNAL` and
`fileGlobalExternalClause : IS? (GLOBAL | EXTERNAL)`; the second spelling is what HID the asymmetry, since the AS
phrase rides EXTERNAL only (13.18.30's GLOBAL clause has no such phrase). Fixing one arm and not the other would
have been this repo's most reproducible defect shape, so both are spelt now:

```
externalClause           : IS? EXTERNAL externalizedNamePhrase? ;
fileGlobalExternalClause : IS? GLOBAL | IS? EXTERNAL externalizedNamePhrase? ;
```

**No new gate and no new screen.** kb/Work PB303's landing states that a new AS site is gated by writing
`externalizedNamePhrase?` and nothing else: the shared rule carries the ONE COBOL-2002 introduction gate
(`VersionConformancePass ParseArm.VisitExternalizedNamePhrase` -> constructs.json `externalized-name-as-2002`,
whose description, display and citation are widened here to name this site) and the ONE literal screen. So
13.18.22.3 SR3 -- "Literal-1 shall be an alphanumeric or national literal and shall be neither a figurative
constant nor a zero-length literal" -- is the SEVENTH restatement of one sentence and goes through
`ExternalizedName.Screen` on COBOLNET2156, not a seventh copy of the test.

**13.18.22.4 GR5 is applied at ONE site.** The rule has two sentences -- literal-1 when written, the subject's own
name otherwise -- and they are now the two halves of one expression at the one cell-keying site
(`CallMakeExternal`: `externalName ?? item.ExternalizedAs ?? item.CobolName!.ToUpperInvariant()`), with the FD
path resolving its own connector name at the FD entry. The literal is taken VERBATIM and the default is
uppercased, because a literal is a character-string and a COBOL word is case-insensitive.

**SR2 came with it, and it is not a diagnostic-quality rule.** "In the same source element, the externalized name
of the subject of the entry that includes the EXTERNAL clause shall not be the same as the externalized name of
any other entry that includes the EXTERNAL clause" was UNVIOLABLE before this change -- every externalized name was
GR5's default, and 8.4.2.2 already keeps those unique. With literal-1 writable, two subjects can claim one
run-unit `ExternalStore` cell, and the consequence is a SILENT ALIAS of differently-shaped storage rather than a
rejection. `CheckExternalizedNameUniqueness` screens it on COBOLNET2159 over the entries GR5 gives an externalized
name to -- file connectors and records; a type declaration is neither, and GR3 externalizes the records that
reference such a type under their own names.

Goldens: `tests/conformance/2002/pb511_external_as_name` exercises BOTH arms across two programs whose data-names
and file-names differ and whose AS literals match, which is the only way GR5's first sentence is observable;
negatives `pb511-external-as-below-2002` (85 -> COBOLNET0900, both arms written), `pb511-external-as-figurative`
(2156) and `pb511-externalized-name-not-unique` (2159). Row `FMT-13.16.2` re-verdicted -- it was already CONFORMS,
and a closed-format row is a claim about every slot being SPELT as well as LISTED.
