# PB1454 — OPEN

## The rule (every citation re-run by the registrar through `cite.py --check`)

> Each data-name-2 shall be the name associated with a level number to which the item being qualified is subordinate

`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 8.4.2.2.3 "…"` → `OK  §8.4.2.2.3 4)  (Syntax rules)`

## Finding — `qualification` (rejects-legal-source; rows `SR-8.4.2.2.3-2`, `SR-8.4.2.2.3-4`, `FMT-8.4.2.2.2`)

**Mechanism.** A report-section sum counter accepts only one qualifier, a report-name. ReferenceResolver.SumCounterFor treats a sum counter as having no hierarchy: more than one qualifier returns null, so ordinary resolution says 'not defined'; a single qualifier that is not a report-name is diagnosed COBOLNET2145 ('a sum counter may be qualified only by the report-name'). The data-qualifier that §8.4.2.2.2 Format 1 and §8.4.2.2.3 SR4 allow (the data-name of the report group the SUM entry is subordinate to) is therefore refused. Two same-named counters in different groups of ONE report cannot be referenced at all.

**Repro.** probe/qual/f3.cob (--std 2023): RD R1 CONTROL FINAL ... 01 CF1 TYPE CF FINAL. 02 LINE PLUS 1. 03 TOT1 COLUMN 1 PIC 99 SUM WX UPON D1.  Procedure division after two GENERATE D1 (WX = 7): DISPLAY TOT1 " " TOT1 IN R1 " " TOT1 OF CF1. Observed: error COBOLNET2145 "'TOT1 OF CF1': ... no report description entry named 'CF1' contains one - a sum counter may be qualified only by the report-name of the report that defines it (ISO §8.4.2.2.2 Format 1)". Without the third operand (f4) it prints F3=14 14. probe/qual/f1.cob: `TOT OF CF2 OF R2` -> COBOLNET1639 'not defined'.

**Expected from the spec.** Compiles and prints F3=14 14 14. Format 1 is `data-name-1 [ data-qualifier ] … [ file-report-qualifier ]`. SR4 (--check OK §8.4.2.2.3 4): "Each data-name-2 shall be the name associated with a level number to which the item being qualified is subordinate". The SUM entry TOT1 (level 03) is subordinate to 01 CF1, and CF1 is a data-name: §13.18.20.3 SR3 (--check OK): "If the entry-name clause is specified in a data description entry or a report group description entry, either data-name-1 or FILLER shall be specified". §13.18.54.4 GR5 (--check OK) makes that data-name "the name of the sum counter". SR2 (--check OK §8.4.2.2.3 2): "if there is more than one combination of qualifiers that ensures uniqueness, then any such set may be used". So `TOT1 OF CF1`, `TOT1 OF CF1 OF R1` and `TOT OF CF2 OF R2` are all legal, unique references.

**Observed / reasoning.** Measured with the pinned cobol.exe (probes f1, f3, f4). The design comment on SumCounterFor says a sum counter is "a level-01-free report-section item whose only available qualifier is its report", and DIAGNOSTICS.md COBOLNET2145 repeats the claim. Both are false: a SUM entry is always subordinate to its 01 report group. kb/Work PB840 (landed) introduced the one-qualifier model and PB882 (landed) made same-named counters legal to declare, so this gap is unowned.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs#SumCounterFor (~line 1726-1760: `if (suffixes.Length > 1) return null;` and the report-name-only filter `homonyms.Where(h => h.Report.Name.Equals(qualifier, ...))`); docs/DIAGNOSTICS.md COBOLNET2145 row

## What the fixer owes

Give a sum counter its real hierarchy (report → 01 group → … → SUM entry) in the qualification resolver instead of the one-qualifier model PB840 introduced; correct the SumCounterFor design comment and the DIAGNOSTICS.md COBOLNET2145 row, both of which state the false premise. Goldens f1/f3.
