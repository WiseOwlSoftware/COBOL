# PB6097 - staled_by selects on "decided at base, owed at head", refusal() says "rule text changed"

**Lead** (review R1-1 of PB6090, `w20262138f`), re-verified on main ae1136f781.

**Mechanism.** `scripts/spec/code_reads.py` `staled_by` (line 781) returns each row that is `owed` at the head and whose stored read
`outcome(record, base)` is confirmed or contradicted. It never compares the record's `rule-digest` with the row's. `scripts/spec/code_read_landing.py`
`refusal` (line 95) words every such row as `the read covers rule-digest {was}, the row's text now digests to {now}` (line 106), and the
heading line says the change "changes the rule text". But `code_read_store.outcome` (line 85) also depends on `shared`, the chain verdict
at the next held edition: a non-CONFORMS resolving read is CONFIRMED only through `shared.get(rule-id)`, and UNDECIDED when that is None. A branch that
re-verdicts or re-chains the target row without touching the row's text turns such a read UNDECIDED, so it is owed at the head, `staled_by` names it, and the
refusal prints two equal digests as a rule-text change.

**Failure scenario.** A branch changes the chain verdict of a row at the next edition (no rule text edit). The landing is refused with a message that
claims a text change; the digests it prints are equal, and a finisher looks for a text edit that is not there.

**Sites:**
- `scripts/spec/code_reads.py#staled_by`
- `scripts/spec/code_read_landing.py#refusal`
- `scripts/selftests/spec/code_reads/test_code_reads.py#case_23_staled_by_names_the_reads_a_changed_rule_text_made_stale_and_no_other` (case 23 pins only the text-change arm)

**Next:** decide whether `staled_by` selects on the digest (`rule-digest` differs) and a separate predicate names the reads a moved chain verdict
left undecided, or whether `refusal` words the two causes apart; add a self-test case for the shared-verdict arm.
