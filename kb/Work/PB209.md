# PB209

Found by COMPREHENSIVE BATTERY #39 (tree `3bc4e4db`, 2026-08-31, artifacts `scratchpad/battery-39`). Four legs
green; the GnuCOBOL differential returned **2 per-case flips**, and the flips are the reason this note exists —
not because the compiler is wrong, but because the evidence the two landing waves shipped on is.

## What the battery measured

```
=== DIFFERENTIAL: 2 PER-CASE FLIP(S) ===
  FLIP  run_subscripts:351   AGREE_ACCEPT -> WE_REJECT_THEY_ACCEPT   error COBOLNET1702
  FLIP  syn_redefines:172    AGREE_ACCEPT -> WE_REJECT_THEY_ACCEPT   error COBOLNET1701
```

Totals moved 571/475/206/71 → 573/473/206/71 — consistent with exactly these two and no offsetting pair. No
NEW/REMOVED cases: the same 1323-case population as battery #38.

## The compiler is RIGHT on both — adjudicated against the standard, citations `--check`ed

- **`syn_redefines:172`** is an `02` elementary with `OCCURS 2` immediately redefined by a sibling `02 … REDEFINES
  … PIC XX`. **§13.18.44.3 SR5** sentence 1: *"The data description entry for data-name-2 shall not contain an
  OCCURS clause."* Squarely named. COBOLNET1701 is correct. GnuCOBOL itself only WARNS here, and its own testsuite
  carries the comment that this "should be a dialect option, currently it is _always_ a warning" — so the
  `WE_REJECT_THEY_ACCEPT` verdict is the CONFORMING one, and their acceptance is documented latitude on their side.
- **`run_subscripts:351`** is an `01` group carrying a VALUE literal with a subordinate elementary that carries
  its own VALUE. **§13.18.63.3 SR13** sentence 2: *"The VALUE clause shall not be specified at subordinate levels
  within this group."* COBOLNET1702 is correct.

Both clause numbers were re-derived and validated mechanically (`scripts/spec/cite.py --check`) rather than
inherited from the diagnostic text — CLAUDE.md rule 1.

## The defect: the sweep that reported ZERO could not see the corpus it claimed to have read

Plan §0 records, as the standing reachability evidence for COBOLNET1701's new rejection:

> A corpus-wide mechanical scan found ZERO programs with a REDEFINES whose target entry carries an OCCURS clause
> (conformance + NIST + characterization **+ the GnuCOBOL testsuite sources**, 1,448 files)

**Measured now, on the same tree:**

| population | files the sweep could have opened |
|---|---|
| `tests/conformance/**/*.cob` | 959 |
| `tests/nist` COBOL sources | 459 |
| `tests/external/gnucobol/**` — *whole tree* | **75 files, of which exactly 2 are `.cob`/`.cbl`** |

959 + 459 + 2 = 1,420. That is where "1,448 files" came from: **the GnuCOBOL contribution to it was two files, not
a corpus.** The 1323 differential cases' COBOL sources do not exist as files at all — they live as `AT_DATA`
heredocs inside **38 `.at` autotest wrappers**, which `gnucobol_differential.py` parses and materialises into a
temp dir at run time. A `find … -name '*.cob'` sweep walks straight past them.

⛔ **The refutation is not subtle, and that is the point.** The case that fired COBOLNET1701 is
`syn_redefines:172`, whose `AT_SETUP` title is literally **"REDEFINES: with OCCURS"**. A sweep that had reached
the GnuCOBOL sources at all would have hit it on the keyword. The scan returned a clean zero, the zero read as
evidence, and two new bind-time rejections landed with a blast radius stated as empty that was in fact non-empty.
This is [[feedback_measure_the_selectors_complement]] and [[feedback_reachability_is_measured_not_deduced]] in the
same instrument: the sweep is evidence about the files it OPENED, never about the corpus it was named after.

The other four codes from the same batch — COBOLNET1698, 1699, 1700, 1703 — fired on **no** differential case, so
their zero-candidate claims survive this run's measurement. Only the two above were contradicted.

## Why the battery stopped rather than rebaselined

Standing order for battery #39: an unexpected `WE_REJECT` flip out of the 1698–1703 family is a STOP, precisely
because it contradicts the evidence the wave shipped on. Both flips are conformance IMPROVEMENTS and the baseline
almost certainly should be regenerated to encode them — but rewriting the baseline in the same breath that
discovers the measurement was broken would destroy the only record that this happened, and would leave the
false 1,448-file claim standing in §0 as though it had been confirmed. The baseline TSV is therefore
**UNCHANGED** at 571/475/206/71 and battery #39 is **not** recorded as a green battery reference.

## The fix shape (structural, not a bigger `find`)

⛔ Not "add `.at` to the scan's glob". The real defect is that **the reachability question and the differential
gate measure different populations from different extractors**, so a wave can prove a shape absent from a corpus
the gate then finds it in. One extraction, two readers:

1. Lift the `AT_DATA` extraction out of `scripts/gnucobol_differential.py` into a shared module that yields
   `(case_id, filename, text)` for the whole external corpus.
2. Give the reachability sweep that module as its source for the GnuCOBOL half, so "does any corpus program have
   this shape" is asked of the SAME 1323 programs the differential compiles — and have the sweep PRINT its
   per-population file counts, so a population that contributes 2 files cannot be reported as a corpus again.
3. A drift test that asserts the sweep's GnuCOBOL population size is within one case of the differential's
   `cases run:` — the "automatic" that keeps step 2 true (CLAUDE.md rule 5).

Then re-run the differential, attribute both flips as intended, and regenerate the baseline in that commit.

## ✅ LANDED 2026-08-31 — one extractor, two readers, and a drift test that was made to fail first

### The extractor: nothing new was written, the DEFINITION was moved

The fix shape above said "lift the `AT_DATA` extraction out of `gnucobol_differential.py`". Measured at
implementation time, it was already out: `scripts/gnucobol_extract.py` has parsed the `.at` wrappers since the
corpus landed, and the differential has always imported `parse_file` from it. **The duplicated thing was never
the parser — it was the POPULATION PREDICATE**, and that is a sharper defect than the note guessed. The
differential's worker re-derived "a case is a group with a COBOL member AND a compile check" inline in
`run_case`, so the only executable definition of *the corpus* lived inside a `ProcessPoolExecutor` payload
function that nothing else could call. Every sweep therefore had to invent its own definition, and the one it
invented was `find … -name '*.cob'`.

So `gnucobol_extract.py` now owns the population as an API, and both readers call it:

- `primary_source(g)` — THE predicate, returning the `(member, compile-check)` pair rather than a bool, because
  that pair is what `run_case` needs and re-deriving it is how the second spelling appeared.
- `differential_cases(src)` — the population; `len()` of it IS the differential's `cases run`.
- `iter_programs(src)` → `CorpusProgram(case_id, at_file, filename, title, text)` — every COBOL member of every
  case (1,611 programs across 1,323 cases; a case may carry a CALLed subprogram), which is what a sweep must
  read and what a per-case list would under-count.

`gnucobol_differential.py` now filters with `gx.differential_cases()` BEFORE dispatch, so `len(payload)` is the
case count and the progress counter stops counting groups it will silently drop. Its `import gnucobol_extract`
is deliberately module-level: `run_case` is shipped to a `ProcessPoolExecutor`, and a Windows spawn re-imports
the module rather than inheriting `main()`'s locals — an import inside `main()` would leave every worker with no
`gx` and produce 1,323 identical `RUNNER_ERROR`s.

### The sweep: `scripts/corpus_sweep.py`, the reachability question as an INSTRUMENT

There was no sweep script to fix. "A corpus-wide mechanical scan" was a shell one-liner typed per wave and
never kept, which is why its population could be wrong twice without anyone noticing between. It is now a
committed instrument with the three properties the defect demands:

1. **The external half is EXTRACTED, never globbed** — `gx.iter_programs`, the same call the gate makes.
2. **Every population prints its program count, always.** The measured census is **conformance 966 ·
   nist-programs 459 · nist-copylib 53 · characterization 17 · version-matrix 0 · differential 0 ·
   gnucobol-external 1,611 = 3,106 programs.** Compare the withdrawn claim: *1,448 files*. A population
   contributing two files can no longer be invisible, because the line is always printed.
3. **The instrument proves itself before it is believed.** When the population check fails, a `--pattern` sweep
   **refuses to report hit counts at all** rather than returning the clean zero that is the entire defect.

It also carries `--codes COBOLNETnnnn`, which answers the *other* half of the same question — "does this new
screen FIRE on the corpus" is a compile question that no grep can answer — from the differential's own per-case
report. That is the rule-5 shape: the next new diagnostic's reachability is one command, not a new one-liner.

### The drift test, and the red it was made to show

`tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/ExternalCorpusPopulationDriftTests` — the sweep's live external population, recomputed
from the `.at` wrappers, must equal the differential's **committed** per-case baseline (`1323 == 1323`). The two
sides are produced independently, so agreement is evidence rather than a tautology. It runs the sweep through
`ProcessObserver.ObserveOrThrow` (no seventh private process launcher) and treats a missing interpreter or an
absent corpus as a LOUD failure, never a skip — a population that cannot be measured is not a population of zero.

⛔ **Proved failing before it was trusted** ([[feedback_green_gates_arent_evidence]]). `verify_population` was
temporarily repointed at the OLD reader — a `*.cob` glob over `tests/external/gnucobol` — and the instrument
reported `{"baseline": 1323, "external": 2, "state": "drift"}`, exit 1, with
`SweepExternalPopulation_EqualsTheDifferentialsCommittedCaseCount` **FAILED**. That `external: 2` is the literal
PB209 number, produced by the literal PB209 reader. Restored, 3/3 green.
`TheDriftCheck_ActuallyFails_WhenTheExtractionIsEmptied` keeps the red reachable permanently, and additionally
asserts the pattern sweep REFUSES rather than reporting zero.

### The re-measurement — every one of the six codes, against the TRUE population

`python scripts/corpus_sweep.py --codes COBOLNET1698,…,COBOLNET1703`, over the 1,323 cases the differential
actually compiles:

|---|---|---|
| COBOLNET1698 (redefines-variable-length) | **0 cases** | zero-candidate claim SURVIVES measurement |
| COBOLNET1699 (control-operand-shape) | **0 cases** | survives |
| COBOLNET1700 (report-control-operand-index) | **0 cases** | survives |
| **COBOLNET1701** (redefines-target-occurs) | **1 — `syn_redefines:172`** | ⛔ claim REFUTED |
| **COBOLNET1702** (group-value-subordinate) | **1 — `run_subscripts:351`** | ⛔ claim REFUTED |
| COBOLNET1703 (group-value-subject-shape) | **0 cases** | survives |

The sweep-side confirmation is the same shape from the other direction: the SR5 sentence-1 pattern (an `OCCURS`
entry immediately REDEFINEd) now returns **5 hits over 3,106 programs — 3 conformance fixtures and
`syn_redefines:172` + `syn_redefines:219` in the external corpus**, where the old instrument returned zero for
the external half. `syn_redefines:219` ("REDEFINES: with variable occurrence") is `AGREE_REJECT` on COBOLNET0855
and never flipped — it was already being caught, by a different screen, in a corpus the sweep could not see.

Both refutations were the flips battery #39 measured; nothing new was found, which is the point. **The
compiler's behaviour was never in question — the EVIDENCE was**, and it is the evidence that was repaired.
