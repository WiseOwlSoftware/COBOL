# PB4792 - AlignmentMayLeaveCarrier keeps the alignment overflow for an operand of unknown bound

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R1-2 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 1): `IntrinsicCarrierRules.cs:190` The function arm of the PB4752 mechanism is not swept: `AlignmentMayLeaveCarrier` still answers false for an operand of unknown bound, so SUM, RANGE and MEDIAN keep the alignment overflow that `CombineAdditive` no longer has, and the report's sweep names only the Wide, Dec and Real additive arms.

**Harm flag.** The lead names an overflow and does not say whether the result is a wrong answer or a run-time stop. This note takes the less specific flag, `crashes`, and does not claim a wrong answer.

**R1.** A grep of kb/Work for `AlignmentMayLeaveCarrier` finds PB4370 (landed: made the unbounded-argument case answer true), PB4417 (open: questions that early return for RANGE), and landed notes PB1143, PB3268, PB3860, PB621. PB4417 names the same member from the other side (an unbounded argument answers true there); neither it nor any other note covers the PB4752 lead as stated, so this is filed as its own note and linked, not merged. Whether the two leads agree is for whoever fixes them.

**Fix (landed, wave 20261881).** The overflow is a RUN-UNIT STOP, not a wrong answer: `COMPUTE R = FUNCTION SUM (FUNCTION INTEGER (D) F)` (D 1000000000000000001, F `9V9(30)` 1.01, R `9(19)V99`) ended with EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW ("scale alignment to 30 fraction digits exceeds the Int128 intermediate"); RANGE, MEDIAN and MIDRANGE the same. `AlignmentMayLeaveCarrier` received `null` for an operand of unknown bound (`NumX.Digits` 0), the same answer as for an SDIDI or binary64 operand. The renderer now states such an operand with its scale (`AlignedOperand.Unbounded`), and the rule answers true when any argument is unbounded and the alignment shifts any argument, the function arm of `CombineAdditive`'s rule (an unbounded operand is its carrier's 39 digits, past the Int128's 38); with no shift the native arm keeps the list (the documented size error, DOC-A.1-179 (1)). The bind-time lane prediction (`FunctionValueLane`) still reads such an operand as unproven: the two answers differ only between the two non-binary64 carriers, which the prediction does not distinguish. After: SUM 1000000000000000002.01, RANGE 999999999999999999.99, even MEDIAN and MIDRANGE 500000000000000001.005, odd MEDIAN 1.01. Swept: MAX, MIN, ORD-MAX, MEAN and MOD over the same operands answered before and after. Golden `pb4792_alignment_with_an_unbounded_argument_leaves_the_carrier` (and its `_85` refusal). PB4417 still questions the early return for RANGE.
