# PB6095 - the format-page title is one level-2 heading

**Survey.** Every page of general formats (PDF pages 194 to 232, Section V) prints one centred, bold, underlined title,
`GENERAL FORMAT FOR COBOL VERBS` and the like (rendered pages 197, 212, 224, 231 read). The transcription set the 41 of them in
five ways: `## <u>TITLE</u>` (30), a bare `<u>TITLE</u>` line (pages 197, 208, 229), bold underlined (224, 227, 231 twice, 232),
underlined bold (225) and a centred line inside a `<pre>` figure (200, 202).

**Decision.** The one form is the majority one, `## <u>GENERAL FORMAT FOR ...</u>` on a line of its own: a heading, so a
heading-based outline finds every format page, underlined as printed, never inside a figure, never bold (the heading's own
weight is bold). Level 2 follows PB6057's rule for an unnumbered title that no contents entry lists: it sits directly under the
Section heading beside the chapters. Amendment 1's addendum (pages 846 and 847) prints its own title, bold and not underlined,
under that part's own structure, and stays out of scope as the heading audit already leaves it.

**Enforcement.** `heading_levels.check_format_titles` (run by `check`, so by `verify_publishable.py --edition 1985`) refuses any
line that opens with `GENERAL FORMAT FOR`, found anywhere including inside a `<pre>`, that is not exactly that form; quoting
notes and tables are skipped. Its test is `case_06` of `scripts/selftests/spec/heading_levels/test_heading_levels.py`. Run on
the text before this change it names the 11 titles repaired.

**Consequences.** Pages 200 and 202 lose a `<pre>` block that held the title, so page 202's later figure verdicts renumber
(`figures-1985/p202-*.json`) and page 200's first block is the running head alone.
