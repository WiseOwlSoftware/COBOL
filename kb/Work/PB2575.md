# PB2575 — a SPLIT branch was stranded between the planner and the land unit

## Defect

`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` `plan()` classified each open note by its newest report. The first arm,
`if cls not in LANDED_CLASSES: awaiting…`, took every report whose branch was not on main, whatever its status; only
a report on a LANDED, MERGED or ABSENT branch could reach the finisher arm. But `scripts/orchestrator/units/land.md`
lands only `branches_pending` with status DONE, and `units/resume.md` step 2 says a SPLIT branch is one that
"plan_wave.py picks up as a finisher". So a SPLIT branch that was not landed was owned by neither unit. The planner
excluded it on every wave ("awaiting landing (excluded; a `land` unit first)"), and no land unit could take it. The
UNLANDED arm of `finisher_pred` ("cherry-pick its unlanded commits onto current main") was dead for every report.

Seen 2026-10-08 00:00 PDT, wave 1038's first plan: `worktree-wf_19837670-9d4-1` (PB667, PB1722, PB1941; report
`w1032a-PB667-report.md`, SPLIT before its gate) and `worktree-wf_b3526bdd-e0d-4` (PB1544, PB1470; report
`w1020d-PB1544-report.md`, SPLIT at the turn cap since 2026-10-05) were listed as awaiting landing, although the
operator's handoff had asked for their finishers.

## Fix

A report that says SPLIT or NOT STARTED makes a finisher whatever its branch's state; its pred keeps the existing
UNLANDED/CHECK instruction to cherry-pick the branch's commits onto current main. A DONE report that names a note NOT
started still waits for its landing, because its finished work belongs to the land unit. Self-test check 6b
(`test_plan_wave.py`) plants both shapes in its own reports directory, so check 8's golden of the default plan is
unchanged.
