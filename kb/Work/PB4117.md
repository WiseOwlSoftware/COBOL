# PB4117 — SEND RETURNING/TO and RECEIVE operands

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/DeclinedFacilityBinder.cs:41` (`DeclinedFacilityBinder.BindReceive`, `DeclinedFacilityBinder.BindSend`).

**The question for the owner.** The messaging facility is declined (DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT), and `USAGE MESSAGE-TAG` is refused by name
(COBOL1943). §14.9.38.3 SR4 (SEND) and §14.9.31.3 (RECEIVE) say data-name-1 / data-name-2 shall be a message-tag data item (a paraphrase of both clauses; quote through `cite.py --find` before it goes in a doc). No
compiled program can hold such an item, so a screen that enforced SR4 literally would reject every SEND with RETURNING and every
RECEIVE operand: the facility would be unwritable rather than declined at its USAGE. PB4090's landed note records the choice made
without an owner decision: "SR4's 'shall be a message-tag data item' is NOT screened (the usage is refused by name, COBOL1943, so no
compiled program can hold the item)".

Options to put to the owner, one decision: (a) keep it unscreened, record that as the DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT of the facility in
docs/CONFORMANCE.md (the refusal at the USAGE is the facility's one screen); (b) screen SR4 and accept that the declined facility's
statements refuse everything; (c) accept USAGE MESSAGE-TAG in the declined facility's items only. Run `cite.py --check 14.9.38.3` /
`14.9.31.3` on the quoted SR text before it goes into a doc.

Shares its root with PB3594 (operand goldens of the declined facility). The PB4090 report calls it "needs an owner decision".
