# PB902 — OPEN — `feedback_probe_the_shape_the_subject_hides`, occurring inside the burn-down itself

Source: the `PB877` report, "New defects", and the train-pb877 lander report, which carried it up as a
registrar lead and asked for a SWEEP rather than a fix.

## What happened

`GR-8.4.2.3.4-1` was recorded CONFORMS. Its b) arm — `arithmetic-expression-1` as a subscript — was
evidenced by five goldens: pb136, pb41, pb201, pb170, pb50. **Every one of them varies the VALUE of the
subscript operand or its CARRIER, and every one holds its SHAPE fixed** at a bare data-name or a literal.
`MOVE 1 TO Y2 OF Y-REC (X(1), 2)` — an identifier carrying its OWN subscripts — was refused with
COBOLNET0899 on the receiving side and aborted at run time on the sending side, on a row that said the rule
conformed. PB877 landed the fix; the ROW's evidence shape is what this note is about.

## The sweep this note asks for

A row's `test-ref` list is a SET OF WITNESSES, and a set of witnesses that all vary one axis is not coverage
— it is one measurement repeated. The sweep is mechanical enough to be scripted against
`tests/version-matrix/traceability-inventory.json`:

1. take every CONFORMS row with two or more `test-ref` entries;
2. for each, read the cited rule and enumerate the AXES its operands can vary on (value, carrier/usage,
   SHAPE — literal / bare name / qualified / subscripted / reference-modified / function result — edition,
   and the receiving-vs-sending side);
3. flag the row when its witnesses collapse onto one axis.

Step 2 cannot be fully mechanical, but the SHAPE axis can: for an operand-taking rule, "does any witness
write the operand as something other than a bare name or a literal" is decidable by reading the goldens.
**Start there** — that is the exact axis that hid PB877 — and treat it as a burn-down input set, one flagged
row per verification item, rather than as a single large task.

## Why `kind: analysis`

The output is a list of rows whose verdicts are unearned. Each of those becomes either a new golden (the row
was right and under-witnessed) or a defect (the row was wrong). Filing it as a defect would imply a single
fix, and there is not one.
