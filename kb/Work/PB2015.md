# PB2015 — LANDED: the supervisor checkpoints a running unit, and writes the handoff the unit never did

Owner 2026-10-04 ("In the future, we need more frequent handoffs written"), two hours into the third `wave` unit. The
loop's only record of a unit was the handoff its model wrote at the very end. Wave 1017's unit died at minute 11 with
none: the next unit read stderr, git and the worktrees to rebuild the state, and `next_unit.py` chose by heuristics.
A longer unit loses proportionally more, and a supervisor that dies mid-unit (a reboot) left no record at all.

Mechanism (`scripts/orchestrator/checkpoint.py`, `orchestrate.ps1`, design doc section 5.1):
1. `checkpoint.json`, written by the supervisor with no model involved: at unit start, every `-CheckpointSeconds` (300),
   when the set of background tasks changes, and at the end. Counters plus every linked worktree's head, commits ahead
   of `origin/main`, uncommitted-file count and `STATUS.md` headline.
2. `milestones.jsonl`, appended by the unit's model at each milestone (`units/common.md`): the decisions and reasons
   the supervisor cannot see.
3. A synthesized handoff (`outcome: split`, `next_unit: resume`, `synthesized: true`) whenever a unit ends without a
   valid one, built from the checkpoint, the last milestones and the worktrees as they are then. A checkpoint that
   survives into the next supervisor start (the supervisor died) is turned into the missing handoff first.

The unit still counts as failed for the circuit breaker; `units.jsonl` records `synthesized (missing)` or
`synthesized (invalid)`. Tests: `test_checkpoint.py` (real linked worktrees; the synthesized handoff against the schema's
required and permitted keys) and `test_orchestrate.ps1` 4h (a crash with a milestone, the periodic and final checkpoint,
a supervisor that died mid-unit). `handoff.schema.json` gained `synthesized` and `checkpoint`.

Not covered: a model that writes its handoff but writes it WRONG about the state is still trusted (the schema checks
shape, not truth); `plan_wave.py` re-derives state from disk, which is the check.
