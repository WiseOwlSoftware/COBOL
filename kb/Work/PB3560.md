# PB3560 — the STATUS-AT guard blocks a read-only reviewer

## Lead (verbatim from the round-4 reviewer of wave 1152 group A, PB3536)

scripts/hooks/status_guard.py (the STATUS-AT stamp guard, kb/Work PB1701) also blocks a READ-ONLY cobol-reviewer that runs its commands inside the worktree it reviews: when the branch's head moved past the STATUS.md stamp (the orchestrator's own docstring commit afee90ee5 on worktree-wf_c19bd7b9-9a1-4 had not restamped it), the reviewer's turn end was refused with the restamp demand although the reviewer may write nothing in that worktree; the hook let the stop through only on the second try.

## Fix shape

- The guard applies to roles that write the worktree (implementer, finisher) and never to a read-only role (reviewer,
  adjudicator, refuter, locator), recognized the way the write-refusing hooks already recognize those roles.
- The orchestrator's own commits in a worktree restamp STATUS.md in the same step. Recorded here as the practice: a
  Mythos or operator commit in an agent's worktree rewrites STATUS-AT at once.

**Sites:**
- `scripts/hooks/status_guard.py`
