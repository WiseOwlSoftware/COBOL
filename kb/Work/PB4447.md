# PB4447 — false COBOL0306 at END-EVALUATE after a left IF's END-IF

**Repro (report text, verbatim).**
`IF W-N = 1 EVALUATE W-N WHEN 1 IF W-K = 1 ELSE DISPLAY W-A END-IF DISPLAY "X" END-EVALUATE END-IF.` draws a false
COBOL0306 at END-EVALUATE; identical on main, not a regression. The inner `IF W-K = 1 ELSE` has no statement before
its ELSE, so the source is itself faulty; the harm is the extra, false COBOL0306 at a legitimate END-EVALUATE (the
dispatching brief flagged it rejects-legal-source; the END-EVALUATE is legal, the program as a whole is not).

**Root (report).** The outer IF's optional END-IF matches the left IF's terminator without any failure, so no recovery
sees it, and `EndedWithItsTerminator` kills the EVALUATE record. Fix direction in the report: hook the terminator pairing
at the match, or have the empty-block recovery keep the construct open. It needs more than the PB4436 slice.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`CobolErrorStrategy.StillOpen`); `EndedWithItsTerminator` and `ContinuesAStatementTheRecoveryLeft` exist only on the PB4436 branch (`worktree-wf_a56965c4-16c-9`, head 797d1e17d) and become members of this file when it lands.

## Resolution

**Re-probed on the wave 20261828 build**: the repro drew COBOL2072 and the false COBOL0306 at END-EVALUATE, and the shape
is wider than the note says: the outer statement may be any IF (terminated or not), the inner construct any statement
with an optional END-x or a conditional phrase (`END-SEARCH`, `END-ADD` after `ON SIZE ERROR`). Traced with the recovery
logged (Recover, NoteLeft, the tokens consumed): the root is not `EndedWithItsTerminator` killing a record, the EVALUATE
never had one. After the inner IF faults at its ELSE the resynchronization stops AT the ELSE (the outer IF's ELSE is in
the recovery set), the outer IF takes `ELSE DISPLAY W-A` as its else branch, the WHEN group's block cannot take the END-IF,
the EVALUATE (optional END-EVALUATE) returns, and the outer IF's optional END-IF matches the left IF's END-IF without any
failure. Without an enclosing IF, `Recover` of the WHEN group's block records the EVALUATE (PB4437); with one, the
prediction accepts the END-IF (the outer IF may follow) and no recovery runs.
**Fix, three places, each the nearest unterminated statement winning (ISO §14.5.3.2 1), §14.5.3.3 4)).**
`Recover` consumes a fault's own phrase word with it (the ELSE the failed IF faulted at), as it consumes the terminator
the fault stood at. `Sync` (which the generated parser calls before every optional END-x and loop decision) consumes a
scope terminator as a left statement's own where a statement of its kind is open on the parser's stack and a record due
began AFTER it (`ConsumesALeftStatementsTerminator`), so the older open statement never sees it. And, found on the way
(same family, same bookkeeping): `NoteLeftBefore` climbs the enclosing statements, not only the nearest, because the
prediction that dies inside the innermost malformed statement ends the preceding statement of EVERY enclosing list early
(`IF A DISPLAY "Z" IF B IF C ELSE … END-IF END-IF ELSE DISPLAY "Y" END-IF` drew a COBOL0001 at the ELSE and a false
COBOL0306 at the END-IF on main).

**Sites (as landed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`Recover`, `Sync`,
`ConsumesALeftStatementsTerminator`, `TakenByALeftStatement`, `LeftStatement`, `NoteLeftBefore`, `StatementAround`),
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md` (the PB4382 paragraph of 3.9),
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/ParseRecoveryCascadeTests.cs` (`ATerminatorAnOlderOpenStatementCouldTake_IsTheLeftStatementsOwn`,
`AFaultDeepInsideAStatement_LeavesEveryStatementItEndedEarly`),
`conformance-suite/cases/14.5.3.2-Explicit-scope-termination/pb4447-stray-end-if-after-an-evaluate-in-an-if-holding-an-empty-if.cob`,
`conformance-suite/cases/14.5.3.2-Explicit-scope-termination/pb4447-stray-end-perform-after-an-if-ended-early-by-a-fault-inside-it.cob`.

**Open-note overlap.** PB4437 (same family: a legitimate terminator drawing a false COBOL0306 after a left IF) and PB4436
own the neighbouring shapes; this one is the EVALUATE terminator case and is filed apart because its stated mechanism
(a terminator matched without a failure) differs from theirs.
