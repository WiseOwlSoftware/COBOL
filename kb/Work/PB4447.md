# PB4447 — false COBOL0306 at END-EVALUATE after a left IF's END-IF

**Repro (report text, verbatim).**
`IF W-N = 1 EVALUATE W-N WHEN 1 IF W-K = 1 ELSE DISPLAY W-A END-IF DISPLAY "X" END-EVALUATE END-IF.` draws a false
COBOL0306 at END-EVALUATE; identical on main, not a regression. The inner `IF W-K = 1 ELSE` has no statement before
its ELSE, so the source is itself faulty; the harm is the extra, false COBOL0306 at a legitimate END-EVALUATE (the
dispatching brief flagged it rejects-legal-source; the END-EVALUATE is legal, the program as a whole is not).

**Root (report).** The outer IF's optional END-IF matches the left IF's terminator without any failure, so no recovery
sees it, and `EndedWithItsTerminator` kills the EVALUATE record. Fix direction in the report: hook the terminator pairing
at the match, or have the empty-block recovery keep the construct open. It needs more than the PB4436 slice.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`CobolErrorStrategy.StillOpen`); `EndedWithItsTerminator` and `ContinuesAStatementTheRecoveryLeft` exist only on the PB4436 branch (`worktree-wf_a56965c4-16c-9`, head 797d1e17d) and become members of this file when it lands.

**Open-note overlap.** PB4437 (same family: a legitimate terminator drawing a false COBOL0306 after a left IF) and PB4436
own the neighbouring shapes; this one is the EVALUATE terminator case and is filed apart because its stated mechanism
(a terminator matched without a failure) differs from theirs.
