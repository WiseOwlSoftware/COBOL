# PB2144 — two arms of one arithmetic rule give two kinds of diagnostic

Found by the PB2114 implementer (wave 1028 group D), filed by the train-1028 lander. Not re-probed by the lander.

Format 2 of ADD, SUBTRACT, MULTIPLY and DIVIDE prints ONE `{identifier-2 | literal-2}` sending operand before
GIVING. A second operand is an error in every verb, and the verbs report it differently:

- `MULTIPLY A BY 2 3 GIVING C`: `multiplyByOperand+` parses the list and `ArithmeticBinder` reports the named
  COBOL1689 (the Format-2 operand rule, §2023:14.9.26.2).
- `ADD A TO 2 3 GIVING C`: `receiversOrSendingOperand` admits `receivingArithmeticOperand+` or ONE literal, so `2 3`
  fails to parse and the user gets a raw COBOL0001 parse error. SUBTRACT FROM and DIVIDE INTO share the rule and
  behave the same way.

This is the two-arm dispatch shape (`feedback_two_arm_dispatch`): one rule, one diagnostic, written per arm.

**Repro:** compile the two statements above in one program at the default edition and compare the diagnostics.

**Code sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#multiplyByOperand` against
`#receiversOrSendingOperand`; `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ArithmeticBinder.cs:105` (MULTIPLY's
named refusal).

**Fix shape:** parse the TO / FROM / INTO operand as wide as MULTIPLY's BY (a repeated mixed operand) and let
`ArithmeticBinder` name the Format-2 violation for all four verbs from one check, or narrow MULTIPLY to match and
accept the raw parse error. The first is the better diagnostic. PB2114 measured that folding `multiplyByOperand`
into `receiversOrSendingOperand` changes what parses, so the change needs goldens for all four verbs' error forms.

**Was blocked by** PB2350, the token-stream and parse-tree differential (DESIGN-architecture-review §4 item 3), because DESIGN-architecture-review §8.4 makes that differential the oracle of every grammar wave (R1 Draft 3, refuter 2 D13). **The owner released it on 2026-11-03** in the attended operator session ("yes, do all four steps", answering the recommendation to take PB2144 off PB2350): PB2350 belongs to the PB2119 Delete program, which PB2911 holds until zero GAP and zero known defects, so the wait was circular. PB2144 lands without the differential; its oracle is the whole gate plus the goldens for all four verbs' error forms that this note already requires, and DESIGN-architecture-review §8.4 records the exemption.

## Landed (wave 20261851 group C, 2026-11-03)

**Re-probe on the base (c9bfddd3f).** The parse error is now COBOL2269 ("unexpected '3'. The ADD statement ends
before this token") rather than COBOL0001, and the split was wider than the note said: `ADD A TO 2 3 GIVING C`,
`SUBTRACT A FROM 2 3 GIVING C`, `DIVIDE A INTO 2 3 GIVING C`, `DIVIDE A BY 2 3 GIVING C`, `ADD A TO 2 ROUNDED GIVING
C`, `ADD A TO B 3`, `DIVIDE A BY B ROUNDED GIVING C` and `MULTIPLY A BY FUNCTION ABS(B) ROUNDED GIVING C` were
COBOL2269 while their MULTIPLY twins were COBOL1689. The sibling sweep found an UNDER-REJECTION in the same file:
the DIVIDE REMAINDER arm (Formats 4-5) bound the INTO phrase whole and never asked the Format-2 discipline, so
`DIVIDE A INTO B C GIVING D REMAINDER E` and `DIVIDE A INTO B ROUNDED GIVING D REMAINDER E` compiled clean, the
second operand and the ROUNDED silently dropped.

**Fix (the note's first shape, widened to the fifth position).** ONE grammar rule, `arithmeticPhraseOperand :
(receivingIdentifier | literal | functionCall | inlineMethodInvocation) roundedPhrase?`, written `+` after TO, FROM,
MULTIPLY's BY, INTO and DIVIDE's BY; `receiversOrSendingOperand`, `multiplyByOperand` and `receivingOperand` are
deleted. ArithmeticBinder names every violation from two checks: `GivingSendingOperand` (every GIVING form, DIVIDE
Formats 2-5 included: one operand, no ROUNDED, each message naming the phrase, the count and the format) and
`Format1Receivers`; `ArithmeticOperandRole` classifies the one mixed rule (receiver = `receivingIdentifier`), and
ExpressionBinder's three arithmetic `Receivers` overloads share one `ResolvedReceiver`.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4` (`arithmeticPhraseOperand`, `addToPhrase`,
`subtractFromPhrase`, `multiplyStatement`, `divideIntoPhrase`, `divideByPhrase`, `sendingOperand`'s comment);
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ArithmeticBinder.cs` (`BindAdd`, `BindSubtract`, `BindMultiply`,
`BindDivide`, `GivingSendingOperand`, `Written`, `Format1Receivers`);
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ArithmeticOperandRole.cs` (`MixedRules`, `ReceiverRules`,
`IsReceiver`, `Describe`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ExpressionBinder.cs` (`Receivers` x3,
`ResolvedReceiver`).

**Goldens.** Eleven refusals, one per file (`conformance-suite/cases/14.9.{2,12,26,44}-*/pb2144-*.cob`, COBOL1689 at
85-2023; the MULTIPLY function one at 2002+), and `cases/14.9.12-DIVIDE/pb2144_divide_phrase_sending_operands`
(accept 85), the first golden that writes DIVIDE Format 4 (it closes GR-14.9.12.4-2's Format-4 residue).
