# PB1185 — LANDED 2026-09-29

## The rule (every citation re-run by the registrar through `cite.py --check`)

> If the context of the figurative constant requires national characters, the national program collating sequence is used; otherwise, the alphanumeric program collating sequence is used.

`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 8.3.3.6.4 "…"` → `OK  §8.3.3.6.4 6)  (General rules)`

## Refuter evidence — row `GR-14.9.48.4-7` (DIVERGES)

DIVERGES — Half 1 is violated: a figurative delimiter with a NATIONAL identifier-1 must be a single-character NATIONAL literal (GR7), whose HIGH-/LOW-VALUE is taken from the NATIONAL program collating sequence (8.3.3.6.4 GR6 'If the context of the figurative constant requires national characters, the national program collating sequence is used' - cite.py OK). StringEmitter.EmitUnstring renders every delimiter through OperandText.AsString, whose BoundFigurative arm calls FigurativeConstants.Fill(kind, num.Collating, null, ...) with cat=null, i.e. always the ALPHANUMERIC program collating sequence. Under an alphanumeric PCS the national sender's HIGH-VALUE is not recognized. The adjudicator's p3 probe could not see this: it had no PCS and moved the ALPHANUMERIC HIGH-VALUE into the sender. Half 2 (ALL) still conforms (q3 T1/T3/T4).

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/StringUnstringBinder.cs#BindUnstring; src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/StringUnstringBinder.cs#StrUnstrOperand; src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Verbs/CobolStringOps.cs#UnstringExtract

## What the fixer owes

Pass the context category (national when identifier-1 is national, §14.9.48.4 GR7) into OperandText.AsString's figurative arm for every UNSTRING delimiter; sweep the other OperandText.AsString callers that render a figurative in a national context (STRING delimiters, INSPECT operands). Golden with a PROGRAM COLLATING SEQUENCE so the alphanumeric and national HIGH-VALUE differ.

## Landing 2026-09-29 (wave 73, group C)

**For the next implementer.**
- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/OperandText.cs#AsString` — `characterCategory:` picks the PCS of a figurative (§8.3.3.6.4 GR6); pass it, never re-call FigurativeConstants.Fill.
- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundStringUnstring.cs` — `BoundStringStmt/BoundUnstringStmt.CharacterCategory` is the statement's ONE national-or-not fact (UNSTRING: also the GR11 conceptual items).
- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/StringEmitter.cs#EmitString/EmitUnstring` — every sender/delimiter AsString passes it. Trap: INSPECT binds figuratives to native pins in InspectBinder (PB1414).


**Root.** §8.3.3.6.4 GR6 makes the program collating sequence of a figurative HIGH-/LOW-VALUE a property of its
CONTEXT, and STRING/UNSTRING state that context as a statement rule — §14.9.48.4 GR7 (`cite.py --check` OK 7):
a figurative delimiter "stands for a single-character national literal if identifier-1 is a national data item";
§14.9.43.4 GR2 (OK 2): a figurative literal-1 / literal-2 takes "the usage of identifier-3, either display or
national". `OperandText.AsString`'s figurative arm had no way to be told, and passed `cat: null` (the alphanumeric
sequence) for every caller.

**Fix.** The binder records the fact once — `BoundUnstringStmt.CharacterCategory` (the same category GR11 c)/d) mint
the conceptual items from) and `BoundStringStmt.CharacterCategory` (identifier-3's class) — and `StringEmitter`
passes it to `OperandText.AsString(characterCategory:)` for every UNSTRING delimiter and every STRING sender and
delimiter, which hands it to `FigurativeConstants.Fill`. DISPLAY, STOP and the intrinsic-argument channel keep the
alphanumeric sequence (null), which is what their contexts require.

**Witnesses.** `conformance:2002/pb1185_figurative_delimiter_national_pcs` (PCS FOR ALPHANUMERIC `"ZY"` + FOR
NATIONAL `N"CBA"`: a national sender splits at the national LOW-VALUE `C`, an alphanumeric one at `Z`, a national
HIGH-VALUE delimiter matches the HIGH-VALUE MOVE stores, STRING into a national group takes the national LOW-VALUE
as delimiter and as sender); `conformance:negative/pb1185-national-unstring-delimiter-at-85` (COBOLNET0900).

**Sibling left to its own note.** INSPECT resolves a figurative to a native pin at BIND time
(`InspectBinder.InspectFigurativeChar`) and ignores the program collating sequence in both categories — that is
kb/Work PB1414 (open). `OperandText.AsString(characterCategory:)` is the seam it can route through.
