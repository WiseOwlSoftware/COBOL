# PB4277 — PB4238's "one reader of a procedureName node" still has three GetText() readers

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs:251` (`ProcedureTableBuilder.ResolveProcedureOperand`: passes `pn.GetText()` to `CheckDeclarativesBoundary`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs:191` (`ProcedureTableBuilder.HeaderName`: inlines `pn.GetChild(0).GetText()`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/EcBinder.cs:175` (RESUME AT message, `pn.GetText()`; the member is the binder method holding the COBOL0714 report).

## What happens

PB4238 (a qualified paragraph or section header) added `ProcedureNameShape` whose doc says a procedureName node is "read here ... and nowhere else", and its DEVLOG says no consumer of the node calls `GetText()`. Three do. `pn.GetText()` on a multi-token context glues the tokens (`P1 OF S1` prints as `P1OFS1`), so a COBOL0714 declaratives-boundary message and the RESUME AT message quote a qualified procedure name glued, the class PB4252 and PB4267 record. `HeaderName` reads the head by `GetChild(0).GetText()` where `ProcedureNameShape.Head` is the one reader.

Repro (to be confirmed by the implementer; no probe was run by the reviewer): a qualified procedure-name operand that draws the declaratives-boundary diagnostic or COBOL0714, e.g. `RESUME AT P1 OF S1` with S1 a declarative section; the message quotes `P1OFS1`.

**Sites (landed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureNameShape.cs` (`ProcedureNameShape.AsWritten`, new), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs` (`ProcedureTableBuilder.HeaderName`, `ProcedureTableBuilder.ResolveProcedureOperand`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/EcBinder.cs` (the RESUME AT COBOL0714 report), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/ProcedureNameReaderDriftTests.cs` (the syntax-scan drift test), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/ProcedureNameMessageTests.cs`.

## Landed

Re-probed on the build: `GO TO DP1 OF D1` (SR4, COBOL2376), `GO TO MAIN-P OF S1` from a declarative (SR3, COBOL2375) and `RESUME AT DP2 OF D1` (COBOL0714) quoted `DP1OFD1`, `MAIN-POFS1`, `DP2OFD1`. All three readers now call `ProcedureNameShape.AsWritten` (`DP1 OF D1`), `HeaderName` and the COBOL1639 report use it too, and `ProcedureNameReaderDriftTests` refuses `GetText()` on a procedureName node anywhere in the compiler outside `ProcedureNameShape.cs` (a Roslyn syntax scan with a planted self-test), so the one-reader claim in `ProcedureNameShape`'s doc is now enforced. PB4252's general class (other contexts) is not closed by this.

## Fix

Route all three through `ProcedureNameShape` (the head and `QualifierAsWritten`), then delete the false claim or make it true with the Roslyn drift test PB4252 asks for. Sweep `GetText()` on `ProcedureNameContext` in the binder for further readers.
