# PB4818 - a report-group SCREEN SECTION after an RD takes SCREEN as the entry's name, so COBOL0307 stands at SECTION, and the same fault is worded differently by the screen and data slots

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** New defects 1 and 2 (one family). (1) A report-group `01 SCREEN SECTION.` (after an RD) takes SCREEN as the entry's name (a free cobolWord at that slot), so COBOL0307 stands at SECTION, not SCREEN as in WORKING-STORAGE. Site: CobolData.g4 dataName / reportGroupEntry. Probe: `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-mythos-1882/w20261882b/p13.cob`. Different mechanism from PB4788 (a name predicate). (2) The same fault is worded differently: the screen slot says "cannot parse construct near 'LINKAGE'", the data slot "unexpected 'FILE'" (both COBOL0001). Cosmetic; same family.

**R1.** kb/Work PB4788 (landed) fixed the screen entry's slot; PB1272 also mentions a report-group/SCREEN shape and was not checked beyond a grep hit.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4` (`dataName`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolReportWriter.g4` (`reportDescriptionEntry`, `reportGroupEntry`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`CobolErrorStrategy.Report`, `CobolErrorStrategy.HeaderAnEntryLoopReadAsAName`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Cst/ClosedFormats.cs` (`ClosedFormats.EntryAwaitingItsName`, doc comment's clauses).

Filed from a review lead of a merged fix-lane branch (wave 20261879 to 20261882) by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

## Landing (wave 20261887 group H)

Re-probed on a fresh build: `RD R.` then `01 SCREEN SECTION.` drew COBOL0307 at (6,18), SECTION, where the working-storage section draws it at SCREEN; `01 LOCAL-STORAGE SECTION.` after an RD drew COBOL0001 at SECTION where the screen and data slots stop at the word; at 85 the same line named a missing period for a section that edition does not print. And the slot had two wordings for one fault, `unexpected 'FILE'` and `cannot parse construct near 'LINKAGE'`, picked by which decision met the word.

**The filed mechanism was half right.** No name rule of the grammar is at fault: `dataName` already refuses the word (`noHeaderWrittenHere`, PB4706). The report section's loop decides between `constantEntry` and `reportGroupEntry` BEFORE the level-number, adaptive prediction evaluates a predicate only at its decision's left edge, so the predicate is skipped and the prediction runs on to SECTION with both kinds alive. The screen section's loop stops earlier only because `screenName` admits no gated word, which leaves `constantEntry` the unique survivor.

Fix shape: `CobolErrorStrategy.HeaderAnEntryLoopReadAsAName` moves such a report to the header's first word, where the predicate would have stopped the parse (a gated word before SECTION or DIVISION, after an integer the parser has not passed). Every later question follows from that position: the slot, the period (§2023:13.2.1), and §2023:8.9's freedom of the word, because `NoteGatedOffender` reads the offender, so at 85 the word is retyped, the re-parse takes it as the name, and the report is the plain one at SECTION, as in the working-storage section. `Report` words a slot that yields as `unexpected 'WORD'`. Also corrected the clause numbers in `ClosedFormats.EntryAwaitingItsName`'s comment (PB4788's review lead R1-1: the entries' formats are §2023:13.17.2 and §2023:13.15.2).

Goldens: `conformance-suite/cases/13.8-Report-section/pb4818-*` (three). Unit: `ClosedFormatReservedWordTests.AHeaderAfterAReportGroupsLevelNumber_IsReportedAtTheDataEntrysWord`. Design: `DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md`, "The name predicate past a loop's level-number, and one wording".

**For the next implementer.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs#HeaderAnEntryLoopReadAsAName`: the report path's answer to a predicate a loop's prediction cannot ask past the level-number; move a report there rather than add an edition test, because `NoteGatedOffender` reads the offender. `CobolErrorStrategy.cs#Report`: `EntrySlotYields` picks the wording, so a slot's goldens read `unexpected 'WORD'` whichever decision met the word; a report at SECTION after a free word is not the slot and keeps ANTLR's kind. `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Cst/ClosedFormats.cs#EntryAwaitingItsName`: the screen section's loop reaches the entry only because `screenName` holds no gated word, so `constantEntry` survives alone; adding one to `screenName` makes that loop fail as the report loop did, and this report path then covers it.
