# PB2281 — one correspondence walk, not two

Found by wave 1031 group B (kb/Work PB480, report `w1031b-PB480-report.md`, new defect 2), filed by the train-1031
lander.

## Finding

PB480 made `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Values/GroupCompatibility.cs#Walk` the ONE §8.5.1.12 walk the compiler and the
run time share, and `CobolVarGroup.Reshape` / `Overlay` convert a carrier between two compatible shapes by its pairs.
A second walk remains for the fixed ⇄ variable-length pair: `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Values/CobolVarGroup.cs#CorrespondingSpans`
pairs a FIXED group's `int[]` character layout with a variable-length group's, and `FromFixedImage` / `ToFixedImage` /
`OverlayFixedImage` convert through those spans. Its callers include `UniversalGroupCarrier.Spans`
(`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Control/CobolInvokeArg.cs`), `ActivationRelations.VariableLengthViolation`, the typed INVOKE
lane (`OoEmitter.FixedArgumentSpans`) and the CALL ABI.

A fixed group is the carrier `(image, [])` with its own atoms, so `Reshape` / `Overlay` over the pair's atoms already
answer every question the span walk answers. Two walks over the same rule can disagree, and PB204 / PB480 each found
exactly that kind of split.

## Fix shape

Do this together with kb/Work PB2280: once the CALL ABI carries atoms, route every fixed ⇄ variable conversion through
`Reshape` / `Overlay`, delete `CorrespondingSpans` and the three `*FixedImage` converters, and re-site any inventory
anchor that names them (the traceability inventory resolves an anchor by its text; PB480's gate went red on a stale
`#FlatTableSpans` anchor).

## Extended 2026-10-07 (wave 1033 group B, landing PB2280)

The precondition is met: the CALL ABI carries atoms (`CobolArg.Atoms`, every CALL adapter takes `GroupAtom[]`), and the
variable-⇄-variable pair converts through `Reshape` / `Overlay` on every lane. What remains is exactly this note: the
fixed-⇄-variable pair still converts through `CorrespondingSpans` over `GroupCompatibility.Layout(atoms)` in
`CobolArgAdapt.Text` / `TextValue` / `VarGroup` / `VarGroupValue` / `StoreReturnGroup` / `StoreReturn`
(`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Control/CallAbi.cs`), `UniversalGroupCarrier`, `OoEmitter` and `MoveEmitter`. PB2280 left it
because it is a behavior-neutral refactor across MOVE, INVOKE and CALL, outside that defect's slice.

## Landed (wave 1051 F, 2026-10-10; with kb/Work PB2690, which this note's mechanism is the whole of)

The second walk is gone. `CobolVarGroup.CorrespondingSpans`, `FromFixedImage`, `ToFixedImage`, `OverlayFixedImage` and the
`int[]` `FixedRun` are deleted, and so are the compile-time `VariableLengthCompatibility.CorrespondingSpans` and
`OoEmitter.FixedArgumentSpans` / `VarPlaceSpans` and the run-time `UniversalGroupCarrier.Spans`,
`CallAbi.FixedLayoutOf` / `VarGroupSpans`. A fixed-length group is the carrier `CobolVarGroup.OfImage(image)` in its own
shape, and every activation boundary converts it through four members over the pair's two atom arrays, each a composition
of `Reshape` / `Overlay`: `FromImage`, `ToImage`, `OverlayImage`, `OverlaidImage`. `GroupCompatibility.Walk` is the only
correspondence question, so `ActivationRelations.VariableLengthViolation` lost its extra span check. The inventory
anchors that named the deleted symbols were retired and re-sited by a `record_verdicts` batch (GR-14.2.3-8, SR-14.8.2.2-2,
GR-14.6.9.2-1, GR-14.9.25.4-9, GR-14.9.25.4-L2.3). The runtime major is raised to 2 because the shipped surface lost five
public members (`RuntimeAbiPinDriftTests`).

## Landing

- `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Values/CobolVarGroup.cs#FromImage` / `#ToImage` / `#OverlayImage` / `#OverlaidImage` — the four fixed ⇄
  variable-length conversions, each a composition of `Reshape` / `Overlay` over `OfImage(image)`; REUSE them, never a span walk.
  `OverlaidImage` / `OverlayImage` are the BY REFERENCE write-backs (14.2.3 GR8) and keep what the formal does not describe.
- `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Control/CallAbi.cs#FixedToVariableShape` / `#VariableToFixedPair` — the one place the CALL adapters
  ask `GroupCompatibility.Walk` for a fixed ⇄ variable pair; a fixed group that states no atoms is `GroupCompatibility.FixedRun(width)`.
- `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Control/CobolInvokeArg.cs#UniversalGroupCarrier` — the universal INVOKE lane, the same four calls over `ActivationRelations.AtomsOf`.
- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/OoEmitter.cs#FixedArgumentShapes` / `#VarPlaceShapes` — the typed INVOKE lane states the two shapes.
- The trap: the runtime major is a contract (`RuntimeAbiPinDriftTests`): deleting a Shipped member raises it (now 2), and a
  merge with a sibling that also edits `PublicAPI.Shipped.txt` or `Directory.Build.props <Version>` must keep one bump.
