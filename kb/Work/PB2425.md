**Found by:** the R1 (PB2118) landing brief, from the ledger work of Drafts 6–10. `scripts/arch/compare_oracle.py`
compares a fresh oracle capture with the recorded baseline (`docs/rearchitecture/evidence/arch-oracle/*.manifest.json`)
and prints IDENTICAL or the ADDED/REMOVED/CHANGED cases, then exits. Nothing is written. `scripts/spec/ledger_plan.py`
can therefore only show the row "The oracle's differences explained" as `("note", "PB2152")`, the status of the note
that classified the last batch of differences.

**Harm:** after PB2152 landed, the ledger reads "done" whatever the oracle says now. A restructuring wave that changes
emitted C# shows as green on the owner's status page until someone runs the script by hand. The ledger stops being
computed from the tree it names, which is its stated rule (`gen_ledger.py` footer).

**Fix direction (rule 5):** `compare_oracle.py` writes a small result record (baseline sha, candidate sha, counts by
class, the verdict) under `docs/rearchitecture/evidence/arch-oracle/` or the coordination directory, and the ledger row
reads the newest record (`("record", ...)`) instead of a note. A drift test keeps the row's source a record.
