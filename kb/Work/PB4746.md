# PB4746 — account 2's weekly reset is a fixed instant, kept in UTC

**What was wrong.** `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` gave account 2 `weekly_reset {weekday 5, hour 10, tz
America/Los_Angeles}`, from the owner's 2026-10-07 statement "your usage resets on Saturday at 10 AM", made in daylight
saving time. The reset is a fixed instant. After daylight saving time ended on 2026-11-01, /usage read "Resets Nov 7,
8:59am", and the owner said on 2026-11-05: "The reset moved to 09:00 AM due to daylight saving time". `budget.py` still
counted the week and each pacing day from 10:00 Pacific, an hour late. On 2026-11-05 the loop held "until 10:05" when
the day's allowance actually stepped at 09:05.

**As landed.** The row is `{weekday 5, hour 17, minute 0, tz UTC}`, Saturday 17:00 UTC, which is 10:00 Pacific in
daylight saving time and 09:00 outside it. Its `_source` records both owner statements. `account.py`'s self-test pins
`(5, 17, "UTC")`. `test_budget.py` compares the week start as an instant, and a new check pins 2026-11-07 09:00 Pacific
as the start of the week after daylight saving time ends. `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md` (the One
account bullet) states the instant.

**Not changed.** Account 1's row (Sunday 03:00 America/Los_Angeles) may have the same flaw. No reading since
2026-11-01 shows whether its reset moved to 02:00, so it is left as it is until its /usage is read.
