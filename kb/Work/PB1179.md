## Finding — `string-statement` (crashes; rows `GR-14.9.43.4-3`, `GR-14.9.43.4-2`, `GR-14.9.43.4-1`, `GR-14.9.43.4-6`, `GR-14.9.43.4-7`, `GR-14.9.43.4-8`, `GR-14.9.43.4-9`)

**Mechanism.** M1 - StringEmitter.WriteImage has no national arm: every STRING INTO an elementary class-national receiver is a run-time NotImplemented stage mislabelled as an SR1 violation

**Repro.** IDENTIFICATION DIVISION. PROGRAM-ID. STNAT2. DATA DIVISION. WORKING-STORAGE SECTION. 01 NS PIC N(4) VALUE N"AB-C". 01 NOUT PIC N(8) VALUE N"........". PROCEDURE DIVISION. STRING NS DELIMITED BY SIZE INTO NOUT DISPLAY "A[" NOUT "]" STOP RUN.  (probe E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d1\out\probe\string-statement\nat2.cob; pinned cobol.exe --run at --std 2002, 2014 and 2023)

**Expected from the spec.** §14.9.43.3 SR1 allows identifier-3 to be usage national ("all identifiers, except identifier-4, shall be described implicitly or explicitly as usage display or national"). §14.9.43.4 GR3 a) moves the characters "national-to-national" when identifier-3 is of class national, with no space filling, and GR7 keeps every position that is not written. Expected output: A[AB-C....].

**Observed / reasoning.** The program compiles without errors. At run time it aborts with an unhandled NotImplementedCobolFeatureException: "STRING INTO receiver 'NOUT' (usage display required, ISO §14.9.43.3 SR1)". In the generated nat2.g.cs, line 47 is the NotImplemented.Run call. StringEmitter.WriteImage sends every receiver that is not a RefModPlace, not a group, not character-form numeric and not Category Alphanumeric, and that is not StoreAsImage, to LoudStmt. A national elementary item (Category National, stored as a string) therefore hits the loud stage. The binder has already admitted national (Sr1Offence admits Usage.National, and AllOrNothingClass enforces the all-national rule), so the loud stage fires only on LEGAL source, and the SR1 citation in its message is wrong. The same failure hits national figuratives (GR2, probe nat.cob) and every GR6-GR9 path on a national receiver. The comments in BoundStringUnstring.cs and StringUnstringBinder.cs still say that class-national operands "concern data shapes the current data model cannot describe", but national items are modeled now (PB646/PB980 landed). Fix at the root: give WriteImage a national-class store (PlaceRenderer.Write of the image, the way the alphanumeric arm does it, with sizes counted in national character positions per GR6/GR8). Also check the siblings: the group-national receiver and a RedefViewPlace national.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/StringEmitter.cs#StringEmitter.WriteImage (the `p.Item.Pic is not { Category: PicCategory.Alphanumeric }` -> LoudStmt arm)

## What the fixer owes

Give WriteImage a national-class store (PlaceRenderer.Write of the image, as the alphanumeric arm does, sizes counted in national character positions per GR6/GR8) and delete the LoudStmt arm for legal national receivers; fix the stale comments in BoundStringUnstring.cs and StringUnstringBinder.cs ('data shapes the current data model cannot describe'). Siblings: national figuratives (GR2, nat.cob), the group-national receiver, a RedefViewPlace national. Golden nat2.cob at 2002: A[AB-C....].
