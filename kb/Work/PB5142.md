# PB5142 - INSPECT's level-1 size rule at --std 85

**Found.** §1985:VI.6.18.3 SR5 ("In level 1, literal-1, literal-2, and literal-3, and the data items referenced by identifier-3,
identifier-4, and identifier-5 must be one character in length", the text checked with `cite.py --check`) is not enforced. The
PB5089/PB5103 implementer noted it while landing the size rules (SR6-SR10) and did not touch it because the compiler has no
language-level (level 1 or 2) axis.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs` (`StatementValidation.CheckInspectLiteralSizes`), `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (§3.4).

**What is not known.** Whether this compiler claims level 1 at all. At level 2 and above the rule is lifted ("Except as specifically
noted in syntax and general rules ..."), so a refusal at all of --std 85 would reject legal level-2 source. This needs a decision
on the language-level axis (CONFORMANCE.md) before any code: if only the full level is claimed, record that decision here and in
`docs/CONFORMANCE.md` rather than leave a silent gap.

**Owed.** Decide the level posture; then either enforce at a level-1 mode or record the null facility.
