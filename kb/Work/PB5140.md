# PB5140 - one field, two meanings, in the dialect-behavior table

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DialectBehavior.cs:96` (`DialectBehaviors.Row`, `DialectBehaviors.Table`); the new PB5101 row, near line 126, puts a normative COBOL-85 clause in the `AnnexRef` slot.

**Found.** `AnnexRef` is documented as "the informative annex item that records the change: an Annex E item for a 2023 change, ...".
The PB5101 row (`perform-varying-after-init-inner-first`, ChangedIn 2002) stores a normative 1985 general rule there, and the
param doc was not widened. A reader of the registry cannot tell an annex item from a normative clause by the slot.

**Owed.** Decide the shape: widen the slot's definition (and its drift test) or give a normative reference its own member so each
column has one meaning. Record the rule-1 citation with the column that carries it (run `cite.py --check` before writing one).
