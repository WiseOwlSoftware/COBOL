# R20 — FIND-STRING imposes no order or multiplicity on its phrase words, and a dangling START AFTER silently discards source

> R00 ledger-audit orphan (2026-08-08): F28 of `PHASE-B-15.32-15.44-findings.md`, probe-verified OPEN
> against the R10-landed tree (`5312996a`). The probe programs are reproducible from the finding text.

PROBE (2026-08-08): `FUNCTION FIND-STRING(H N START AFTER)` — the argument-3 introducer with NO
argument-3 — compiles clean and prints P=1: the two written words are discarded and the call degrades to the
plain two-argument form. The §15.37.2 general format fixes phrase order and multiplicity; the class/type
screens landed (PB12 — a wrong-class argument now draws COBOLNET1627 with the r1/r2/r3 citation), but
BindFindString still walks phrase words with an order-free switch. FIX (ledger): a positional walk over the
format (two operands, optional LAST, optional [START AFTER] argument-3, optional ANYCASE), rejecting
out-of-order, repeated, and dangling phrases — BindSubstitute/BindConvert already validate positionally.

## LANDED 2026-08-08

`BindFindString`'s order-free switch is replaced by a POSITIONAL slot walk over §15.37.2's format —
`argument-1 argument-2 [LAST] [[START AFTER] argument-3] [ANYCASE]` — the last of the phrase-keyword arms
still walking order-free (BindSubstitute/BindConvert already did it positionally). Eleven probe shapes:
five legal forms compute their §15.37.4-derived positions (plain=1 · LAST=7 · START AFTER 1 = 4 · bare
argument-3 = 4 · LAST ANYCASE = 7) and six malformed forms draw COBOLNET1504 with a per-shape reason —
including the case that mattered, the dangling `START AFTER` whose two written words used to be silently
DISCARDED (the call degraded to the plain two-argument form, hiding a real coding error).

Golden `find_string_phrase_order` (the five legal shapes) + negative
`find-string-dangling-start-after` (reject-at 2023). Gate: Intrinsic|Corpus 648/648 · characterization 33/33.
