# PB5930 - a 2002 DOC row cannot reach CONFORMS

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/audit_annex_a1.py` (`evaluate`, `ITEM_KEY_RX`), `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py` (`Schema.anchor_for`, `Schema.anchor_obliged`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.RepoDrift/SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests.cs` (`SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests.AnchorFor`), docs/conformance/7 (§7)

**Found by** listing step 4b (kb/Work PB5925, branch `listing-step-4b`), adjudicating the twelve 2002 listing rows of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-listing.md` section 2.2. Ten closed; the two DOC rows could not.

## What happens

- `tests/version-matrix/inventory-schema.json` `kinds.DOC.anchor-template` is `docs/CONFORMANCE.md#{rule-id}`, so a 2002 row
  `2002-DOC-B.1-90` with verdict CONFORMS owes the anchor `docs/CONFORMANCE.md#2002-DOC-B.1-90`
  (`inventory_schema.Schema.anchor_for`, `anchor_obliged`; `record_verdicts.py` refuses the record without it:
  "kind DOC requires the register anchor 'docs/CONFORMANCE.md#2002-DOC-B.1-90'").
- `lineage_reads.own_verdict_problems` already knows the case (`NO_REGISTER_ENTRY`: "owes its own determination ... which
  the register does not hold: the pointer's is another edition's"), so the design intends a section 7 row of the 2002 item's own.
- But section 7 of the conformance statement is the 2023 Annex A.1 register: `scripts/spec/audit_annex_a1.py` keys every row
  by `ITEM_KEY_RX` (`^DOC-A\.1-(\d+)([a-z]?)$`) and refuses any other key ("N section 7 row(s) carry no A.1 item number"),
  measured on this branch by adding `docs/conformance/7/2002-DOC-B.1-90.md` (then removed).
- So no 2002 DOC row can be CONFORMS, PARTIAL or DIVERGES. 157 of the 184 2002 DOC rows are GAP today (the other 27 are
  DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT, which the template exempts when the register holds no row, or KEPT).

## What the fix must decide

Where an older edition's own determination of an implementor-defined item lives, and how the A.1 audit checks it against
THAT edition's annex (B.1 for 2002, A.1 for 2014), not 2023's: a section of the statement per edition, or section 7 rows
keyed by the edition's rule-id with `audit_annex_a1.py` (and its self-test, and the C# `AnchorFor` reader in
`SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests`) taught each edition's item catalog. Either way the determination text may point at
the 2023 row it shares (items 117 and 200 already document B.1 items 90 and 165 and 2014's A.1 items 106 and 182 in their
prose, step 4b), but the anchor must be the row's own.

## The rows this holds

- `2002-DOC-B.1-90` (listings: whether and when produced, effect of logical conversion): the determination is documented
  in `docs/conformance/7/DOC-A.1-117.md`; witnesses `Listing2002Tests` (all seven cases).
- `2002-DOC-B.1-165` (the stage of LISTING, PAGE and SUPPRESS): documented in `docs/conformance/7/DOC-A.1-200.md`; witnesses
  `Listing2002Tests.ListingOff_...`, `APageDirective_...`, `CopySuppress_...`.
Both close in the change that gives the 2002 row its own anchor, with a record batch carrying those witnesses.
