# PB2841 — OPEN — CONTINUE AFTER screens every non-finite double as EC-DATA-NOT-FINITE

Found by the train 1047 lander's review (step 5b) and filed by it, 2026-10-08 20:54 PDT. Reviewer finding on cluster 45G (PB2697+PB2647), PLAUSIBLE (traced in code, not run); outside 45G's diff.

## Mechanism
`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Control/CobolTiming.cs:42-46` sends every non-finite double to `FloatNotFiniteError`. Table 12
and §14.6.13.2 item 3 limit EC-DATA-NOT-FINITE to a STANDARD floating-point usage; PB2647's determination (an
infinity is a number in ISO/IEC 60559, a NaN is not) sends −∞ to a statement's less-than-zero leg.

## Repro to build
`CONTINUE AFTER F SECONDS` with F FLOAT-LONG holding −∞ or NaN through a REDEFINES (the shape of the golden
`2023/pb2647_set_size_not_finite`), `>>TURN EC-DATA-NOT-FINITE CHECKING ON`. Observed (by trace): fatal
EC-DATA-NOT-FINITE. Expected: −∞ takes CONTINUE's less-than-zero rule (`checkLessThanZero`); NaN takes the rule the
statement gives a value that is not a number.

## Fix shape
An `IsNaN` / `< 0` / `> max` branch mirroring `CobolDynString.SetSize`, plus one golden.
