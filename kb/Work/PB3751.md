# PB3751 — the grammar demands an optional word that sits inside an optional group

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolReportWriter.g4#reportPageClause`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4#valueClause`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#mcsReceiveStatement`, `scripts/spec/audit_grammar_optional_words.py#demanded` (`_flat_spellings`, `decisions`, `member_declared`, `main`, `self_test`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/OptionalWordSubsetDriftTests.cs#Formats` (`Partitions`, `OptionalWordSubsetDriftTests_P8`).

## Landed (wave 20261505 C, 2026-10-25)

Re-probed on the worktree's own build: `RD RP PAGE IS 10 LINES.` → `error COBOL0001: unexpected 'IS'`;
`RECEIVE T GIVING B L CONTINUE 5 SECONDS` → `COBOL0307: unexpected '5'`; `88 CN VALUE "A" THRU "Z" AL1.` compiled
and ran (`CN`) — site 2 did NOT reproduce. Rules re-run through `cite.py --check`: §8.3.2.4.3 OK, §5.2.6.2 OK,
§14.9.31.4 2) OK; pages 468, 546, 634 and 732 rendered.

1. **PAGE (§13.18.39.2).** Only PAGE is ruled. `reportPageClause` is now `PAGE (LIMIT IS? | IS | LIMITS ARE? | ARE)?`:
   either word of the chosen alternative may go, a word of each (`PAGE LIMIT ARE`) stays refused (§5.2.6.2).
   ⚠ DETERMINATION over GnuCOBOL, which demands LIMIT: the printed page controls (CLAUDE.md rule 1).
2. **VALUE `[ IN alphabet-name-1 ]` (§13.18.63.2).** DISCHARGED as a refusal: the IN-less spelling is one more
   literal operand by position and the binder decides it by symbol (`DataBinder.RangeAlphabetPhraseOf`, kb/Work
   PB983; pinned by `pb983_range_alphabet_in_optional` V5 and `TrailingInPhraseDriftTests`). The grammar keeps
   `(IN cobolWord)?` and now says so to the audit in a checked marker line,
   `audit:optional-word IN decided-by DataBinder.RangeAlphabetPhraseOf`.
3. **RECEIVE's CONTINUE phrase (§14.9.31.2).** ⚠ DETERMINATION reversing the earlier reading that page 634 (the
   CONTINUE STATEMENT's format, AFTER and SECONDS ruled) governs this phrase: §8.3.2.4.3 decides optional words
   "within each format", and §14.9.31.4 GR2 gives RECEIVE's "CONTINUE phrase" its own semantics. Now
   `CONTINUE AFTER? (arithmeticExpression SECONDS? | MESSAGE cobolWord)`; the CONTINUE statement keeps its own.
   `specs/ISO_COBOL.md`'s §14.9.9.2 figure note is corrected to match (and its figure now underlines CONTINUE, as
   the C2 correction it cites says).

**Goldens.** `13.18.39-PAGE/pb3751_page_limit_optional_words` (accept 85: seven spellings, one page limit, 1/01 1/02

## The three sites
1. **PAGE clause**, §13.18.39.2 (page 468 of the PDF): `[ LIMIT IS | LIMITS ARE ]`, all four words plain. `reportPageClause` has
   `(LIMIT IS? | LIMITS ARE?)?`, so LIMIT is demanded wherever IS is written and `PAGE IS 60` is refused.
   **Needs adjudication before a fix:** GnuCOBOL also requires LIMIT before IS. The spec reading (render page 468 first,
   CLAUDE.md rule 1) controls over GnuCOBOL (rule 1 precedence); an adjudicator confirms the reading.
2. **VALUE clause formats 3 and 5**, §13.18.63.2 (page 546): `[ IN alphabet-name-1 ]`, IN plain. `valueClause` has `(IN cobolWord)?`,
   which refuses the IN-less form.
3. **RECEIVE**, §14.9.31.2 (page 732): `CONTINUE AFTER { arithmetic-expression-1 SECONDS | MESSAGE RECEIVED }`, AFTER and SECONDS plain.
   `mcsReceiveStatement` demands both.

out) is refused as a syntax error.

adjudication of site 1. Then re-run the audit: it must report none of the three.
