# PB3685 — the over-2-GiB journal test does not reach an offset past 2 GiB

**Sites:** `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/SequentialJournalTests.cs` (the over-2-GiB test at about line 469 and
`JournalWithTornImage`).

Filed 2026-10-24 by the enqueue unit's clerk from the R1-1 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 1) of the PB3614 review,
branch `worktree-wf_6ffe8083-b46-17` (PR #534, merged; PB3614 is landed), report
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w20261032h-PB3614-report.md`. Not re-probed.

## What happens

The test says the image record checksum is zeros and that the records end at the first zero byte after them.
`JournalWithTornImage` fills all 2+4 payload bytes with `x`, so the checksum is `xxxx`, and the reader stops at that
`R` record (its CRC fails) at about byte 70. The test never reaches the zeros or any offset past 2 GiB, so it does not
witness PB3614's 64-bit sizing.

## Fix shape

Build the journal so a valid frame (correct CRC) precedes the torn tail and the tail's length word lies beyond
`int.MaxValue` (a sparse or virtual stream, no 2 GiB allocation), assert the reader's outcome from the recorded
record format, and correct the comment. A test that passes with the 32-bit arithmetic restored is no witness.
