# PB6031 - edition differences written as inline DialectLevel comparisons

**Found.** PB5048 moved the three SUM-operand arms of `ReportSectionBinder` (`ResolveRolledAddend`'s sum counter and
other-report arms, `RolledRepetitionFault`'s same-group arm) from `Edition.DialectLevel < 2002` to
`DialectBehaviors.IsActive(DialectBehavior.SumDataNameRules, ...)` with VCR row 7.89, because `DialectBehaviors.IsActive`'s
contract reads "Every per-edition behaviour gate — emitter-side or runtime-side — asks this and never an inline year
comparison, so the edition a change landed in is written down exactly once", and `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md`
Table 7 says "Any pre-2023 delta belongs HERE". The sweep for the same shape finds, on 26e6da8f73 after PB5048,
`git grep -n -E "DialectLevel\s*(<|>=|>|<=)\s*(2002|2014|2023|85)" -- src` = 69 sites in 34 files: 18 more in
`ReportSectionBinder.cs` (lines near 185, 331, 397, 452, 525, 550, 573, 926, 1381, 1489, 1675, 1703, 1708, 1712, 1717, 1721, 1741, 4522: the
1985 Report Writer syntax rules of PB5046, PB5078, PB5079 and their 2002 replacements), 7 in
`Validation/StatementValidation.cs`, 4 each in `ExternalFileConnectorRules.cs` and `CodeGen/Emit/ConditionRenderer.cs`,
and the rest one to three per file. Other spellings (`Edition.Edition >= ...`, `is2023` locals) were not counted.

**The question this note owns.** Each site is an edition difference whose edge is written where it is used and nowhere
else: no VCR row names most of them, no registry row carries their two citations, and nothing fails when a second site
writes the same edge differently. Two registers already exist for the two kinds: `tests/version-matrix/constructs/`
(a construct an edition lacks, refused with COBOL0900) and `DialectBehaviors` (a rule whose answer or screen differs, the
VCR `behavior:` anchor; rows 7.75 and 7.89 are syntax screens there). Decide, per site, which register owns it, move it
there with its VCR row (both edges quoted, `cite.py --check`), and add a drift test that refuses a `DialectLevel`
comparison against an edition year outside the registers' own predicates (an expected list that ratchets down only,
as `EditionCitationCatalogDriftTests` does). The note's estimate is a decision plus a mechanical move of 69 sites; the
report binder's 18 are the first slice, since they are one subsystem and PB5048's row shows the shape.
