# PB3955 — PB3860 residue: docs/CONFORMANCE.md DOC-A.1-123 still lists MEAN among the Int128-aligned functions, and the pb3860_mean_one_value golden covers neither 85 / 2002 nor states the right -0.01

**Sites:** `docs/CONFORMANCE.md:3604` (DOC-A.1-123), `conformance-suite/cases/15.60.4-Returned-value-rule/pb3860_mean_one_value.cob:3,28` and a new companion case

Two follow-ups of PB3860 (MEAN has one value whatever receives it), one note because both are the post-landing
text of the same change.

1. `docs/CONFORMANCE.md:3604` row DOC-A.1-123 (native arithmetic techniques, an A.1 implementor-defined row) still says
   SUM / RANGE / MEAN / MEDIAN / MIDRANGE align their arguments to a common scale on the Int128 carrier and go to the SDIDI
   only when alignment can leave it. After PB3860 MEAN is the SDIDI quotient in every mode
   (`IntrinsicCarrierRules.SdidiQuotientFunctions`). The owner override barred the implementer from editing the row.
2. `conformance-suite/cases/15.60.4-Returned-value-rule/pb3860_mean_one_value.cob`: (a) the positive golden accepts only
   2014 and 2023 because most legs use ROUNDED MODE, but the fix is edition-independent and the defect also lived at 85 and
   2002 (the relation leg F2 and the bare-ROUNDED nested leg); the 2002 negative refuses only the ROUNDED MODE phrase and
   pins nothing about MEAN; no golden runs MEAN with one value at 85 or 2002. (b) Line 25-28, the header derivation for leg
   group B reads "the negative mean -5E-10: TOWARD-LESSER 0.01", but the owed and expected value (`.out` B1) is -0.01.

## Acceptance
- DOC-A.1-123 names MEAN as the SDIDI quotient (and the PB3860 witness); a companion golden at 85 and 2002 that runs the
  edition-independent legs (F2 and the bare-ROUNDED nested leg); the header says -0.01.
