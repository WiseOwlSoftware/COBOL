# PB5344 - at --std 85 NUMVAL-C trims the edge spaces of argument-2 (NvScan, currency.Trim()); §1989:A.2.30.3 AR1 makes cs "the string of one or more characters specified by argument-2" and only 2002 says edge spaces are ignored: adjudicate whether a PIC X(3) "$  " is cs "$" at 85

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Exact.cs` (`CobolIntrinsics.NvScan`)

**Question (a determination, "not a defect yet").** `NvScan` trims `currency.Trim()` at every edition. §1989:A.2.30.3 AR1 (`cite.py --check "§1989:A.2.30.3 AR1" "cs is the string of one or more characters specified by argument-2"` prints OK) defines cs as that string, and the lead says only 2002 states that edge spaces are ignored. Whether a PIC X(3) argument-2 `"$  "` is cs `"$"` or the three-character string at 85 is open. Shares PB5161's mechanism (`NumvalCCurrencyContentRules`).

**Fix direction.** Adjudicate against §1989:A.2.30.3 and the 1985 intrinsic-function text (rule 1); if edge spaces are significant at 85, add an edition arm (a `DialectBehavior` row) and a golden at 85.
