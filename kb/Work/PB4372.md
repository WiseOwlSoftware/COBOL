# PB4372 - The margin-R note has two uncovered shapes

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorListener.cs` (`CobolErrorListener.NoteMarginRCut`, line 112 at the filing). PB2555 is OPEN and BOUNCED (PR #961); the file lands with it, not on main at filing.

The note's owed shape is "the parse fails on a line whose predecessor carried non-blank text past margin R". `NoteMarginRCut` looks only at the physical line of the token before the offending one. Two shapes escape or misfire:

1. A statement written only past margin R, on a line whose program-text area is blank, has no token to anchor, so no note is given. The implementer recorded it in PB2555 as "Not covered, deliberately" with no owner decision, which CLAUDE.md rule 3 forbids (the complete feature, deferral only by explicit owner decision).
2. A lone tag-shaped word (identification area) on a minority of lines is indistinguishable from a cut word and fires the note.

Fix: anchor on the preceding non-blank physical line (not the preceding token's line), and tell a tag column from a cut by the same line-population rule `ReferenceFormatMap` uses. If the owner wants shape 1 left out, that is an owner decision and gets recorded as one.
