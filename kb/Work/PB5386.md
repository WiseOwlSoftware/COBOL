**Mechanism.** The INTO move is emitted after the returned record is stored in the record area, yet the receiver's subscript has the value it had before the RETURN, so the receiver's place is identified before the statement runs. The point where it is frozen is not yet located: this note records the observed behaviour and the two sites that build and emit the move. The refuter expects READ ... INTO to share the mechanism (its general rule has the same sentence); that was not probed here.

**Inventory.** This rule is outside the 92 rules of the re-read; no row is claimed or recorded here. The 1985 row of `§1985:XI.4.3.4 GR6` should be re-read with this repro.

**Rules (every citation checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `§1985:XI.4.3.4 GR6b`: "Any subscripting associated with identifier-1 is evaluated after the record has been read and immediately before it is moved to the data item".
