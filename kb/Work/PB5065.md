# PB5065 - the haiku rate's prompt tier is assumed

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` (whole: the calibration constants).

**Found (PB5058's reviewer).** Haiku 5.5 bills a prompt of 100K tokens or less at $0.10/$0.50 (1/40 of Opus, 64,000,000
tokens per point) and only a larger prompt at $0.50/$2.50 (1/8, the 12,800,000 used). Nothing measures which tier our Haiku
turns are in, so a short turn is overstated about fivefold. It only overstates, so no wave is over-dispatched; measure the
prompt sizes from telemetry before refining, together with PB5066's refit.
