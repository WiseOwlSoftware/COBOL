**Landed (2026-11-14, wave 20262027 group C).** Re-probed on a fresh build: a pseudo-text-2 literal written after a tab, on a debugging line (`REPLACE ==XLIT== BY` then a tab, `=="` and a literal continued onto a second line; `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20262027c/p/t1.cob`), was refused with COBOL7039 although its quote stands in area A (65 positions), while the same text written with spaces ran (`t1s.cob`). Its mirror, a literal that a tab pushes into area B, was accepted though only 61 positions remain. The cause is as the note says: `IntroducedLiterals` and `ContinuedLiterals` read `Column - SourceAreaStart` from the source map, whose column counts a tab as one position, where the line-entry stage expands a tab to the next stop (§1985 area A is positions 8-11 of the fixed line as laid out).

**Fix.** `ReferenceFormatMap.ProgramTextOffsetAt` (with `ColumnMap.ToExpanded`, the inverse of `ToPhysical`) gives the offset into the program-text area once every tab is expanded; the formats of every text read now reach `ApplyReplaceStatements`, `ApplyReplacements` and the `StatementCursor` (a COPY statement's `_referenceFormats`, the REPLACE path's `CopyProcessor.ReferenceFormats` through `ConditionalCompilationProcessor.Manipulate`), and `CopyProcessor.ProgramTextOffsetAt` is the one reading of it, used by both COBOL7039 sites. The other consumer of the physical column, `ReferenceFormatMap.PlacementAt` through `AreaPlacementPass`, compares physical columns only (a token against its line's first text), so it is not affected by tabs; the REPLACE-inserted-token arm of the same family is PB5313 and is not changed here.

**Goldens.** `pb5503-replace-literal-tab-area-a` (accept, runs at 85; failed before) and the refusal `pb5503-replace-literal-tab-area-b` (COBOL7039; accepted before), plus `PhysicalLinesTests.ProgramTextOffsetAt_CountsATabAsTheStopItFillsTo` and `ColumnMap_ToExpanded_InvertsToPhysical`.
