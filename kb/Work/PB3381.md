# PB3381 — finished work is postponed by the rolling wave's hold and by a review loop that does not converge

## The owner's decision

The repository owner, 2026-10-18 ~12:10 PDT, verbatim: **"We should not be holding finished work this long,
continually postponing it. Something in our process is flawed"**, and, on the Mythos lane's proposed changes (delete the
predecessor hold so a branch reviewed CLEAN lands at once; review a group when it is DONE and let its successor merge the
REVIEWED head; round 2 and later review only the diff, with severity thresholds; the targeted Linux leg at a host-I-O
group's own gate), verbatim: **"Make the changes now and land the clean links as they come."**

Related, on the operator's unlanded branch `notes-pb-loop-drain-ownership-20261018` and not on this branch: PB3379 (the
loop reviews foreign branches) and PB3380 (the successor chain never carries review fixes forward).

## The evidence (wave 1118, the PB3330 engine core)

Workflow `wf_14a1e62b-326`; reports `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w1118*`. Seven hours, six groups DONE, **zero
landed**.

- PB3331: DONE 06:22, three whole-branch review rounds (5, 2, 1 findings; 2.3 h), then HELD from 08:43 to land
  "through" its successor. The round cap turned one LOW finding (a test theory's length class) into a DECISION row.
- PB3332: DONE 07:39 on its predecessor's UNFIXED head (the successor started the moment the predecessor's implementer
  returned, while its review finisher was still working), so its reviewer restated the predecessor's findings.
- PB3333: DONE 08:50, reviewed only at 10:10 after its successor returned, CLEAN 11:07, refused at the landing 11:26 by
  the Linux legs (31 NIST RL programs differ on Linux), which no earlier gate had run.
- Under the hold rule nothing in a six-link chain can land until the LAST link is clean.

## The root cause

The hold ("the predecessor's branch is HELD and lands THROUGH the successor, which contains it") is from the train era,
when two same-file branches had to ride one train. Since PB3023/PB3024 the landing is one pull request per branch through
GitHub's merge queue, so the hold saves nothing and only postpones. Three more rules compounded it:

1. a held predecessor was reviewed only after its successor returned (`wf_rolling_wave.js` `worker`: `if (r.finished &&
   !hasSuccessorQueued(g.letter)) r = await reviewed(g, r)`; `onResult`'s HELD arm; `landAlone`);
2. every review round re-reviewed the whole branch (`reviewBranch`: round > 1 still `origin/main...HEAD`), so each round
   found something new and the cap ended in a DECISION instead of a landing; every recorded finding blocked
   (`review_findings.verdict`: CLEAN only when the round recorded none);
3. the Linux legs ran first at the landing (`dispatch-spec-implementer.md`'s LINUX GATE paragraph: skip it under batched
   gating), for every group, the host file I/O layer's included.

A fourth, found on the way (the sibling arm of rule 1): the branch survey held the predecessor too.
`prune_worktrees.contained()` read every WAITING TO LAND, WAITING FOR REVIEW, STOPPED, ENQUEUED or BOUNCED branch whose
tip a successor contained as IN FLIGHT ("it lands through that branch"), so the landing lane would not have landed a
CLEAN predecessor even with the wave's hold gone.

## Landed

Fixed at each root, in one change set:

- **The hold is deleted** (`wf_rolling_wave.js`: `held`, `landAlone`, `hasSuccessorQueued`, the HELD arm of `onResult` and
  the final `held` loop). Every finished result is reviewed in its own slot the moment its implementer returns and is
  `ready` when CLEAN, predecessor or not. A successor starts once its predecessor is `settled()` (its review loop ended:
  CLEAN, FINDINGS n, STOPPED, NO-RESULT or STALE; or it returned unfinished); `byLetter` holds the REVIEWED result, so the
  successor's note names the FINAL branch, worktree, head, report and verdict (the review finisher's when one ran), and a
  predecessor that ended FINDINGS at the cap keeps those findings for its next-wave finisher.
- **The survey keeps a contained branch's own lifecycle** (`prune_worktrees.contained()` now carries only a DECISION row,
  no report or a FINDINGS stamp), and its safety arm is `carrying_findings()`: a WAITING TO LAND branch that contains an
  unlanded branch of ANOTHER note stamped FINDINGS at its tip reads IN FLIGHT (`waits_on`) until that note's finisher has
  landed it, since the successor's reviewer was told not to record those findings again; a finisher of the same note
  carries them to answer them and lands.
- **Round 2 and later review only the finisher's diff** (`reviewBranch` passes `reviewed_head`, the head the last round
  reviewed: `git diff <it>..HEAD` plus the report's answers); round 1 stays the whole branch, and a successor's round 1
  leaves its CLEAN predecessor's reviewed content alone.
- **Severity thresholds** (`review_findings.py`): a finding requires a `severity` (critical, high, medium, low, info);
  `verdict --blocking` (default `critical,high,medium`) is CLEAN when no blocking finding was recorded; the rest are LEADS,
  listed by the stamp under the report's `## Review findings`, printed by `verdict`, returned by the reviewer and carried in
  the wave's `leads` for the enqueue unit's clerk. The wave passes `critical,high` for the round after its last finisher.
  A legacy finding with no severity blocks. `review_fix_rounds` stays 2.
- **The Linux leg at a host-I-O group's gate** (`make_dispatch_specs.host_io`, `group_files`, `LINUX_GATE`): a group whose
  file set holds a file under `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/` gets the paragraph that runs `bash scripts/linux-gate.sh --legs
  unit,conformance --nice` after its Windows gate; every other group keeps the batched-gating paragraph.
  `check_practices.py` learns the placeholder and refuses a spec whose paragraph is not its group's.
- **A harness for the wave script** (`scripts/orchestrator/test_rolling_wave.py`): the script had none (its rules were
  pinned as source text only); it now runs under node with stub agents, and the case "a predecessor reviewed CLEAN is in
  `ready` before its successor returns" is checked by the order of events. Run against the pre-change script it fails 17
  of 18 checks.
- The docs, current in the same change: workstream `SKILL.md` §2, `MANDATORY-PRACTICES.md` I8, I10, O2, O10, the review
  skill's branch form, `cobol-reviewer.md`, `units/wave.md`, `units/enqueue.md`, `DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` §3.17.2 steps
  2 and 3, `DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md` (dependency successors).

Out of scope: the running waves 1118 and 1123 keep the script they loaded; the loop's units take the new rules at their
next start from main. The public base skill (`tools/claude-skills`, brent-tools 1.21.0) carries the same hold in its
`references/rolling-wave.js` (`held`, `hasSuccessorQueued`, lines 115-132), and its `agent-fleet/SKILL.md` line 124
still offers "review a held chain once at its tail"; it needs the same change and a pin move (PB1699's pattern),
recorded as a lead of this landing (the submodule is not edited here).
