# PB4299 - Recovery after a fault whose sentence tail holds a user word not followed by a period swallows every sentence up to the next `word.` and parses that word as a paragraph header; one false COBOL0306 cascade (R10)

Found by the Mythos front-end review, gap pass, 2026-10-31 (GAP-REVIEW.md, G4-1; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/KaxkLe85tfZBZ3nZ1tDGTs). Pin d7daee1cf.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`Recover` :173-185, `ConsumeUntil` :405-433 with GetErrorRecoverySet, `OrphansItsTail` :138-141, `BeginUnitTailDiscard` :206-214, `UnitTailDiscard` :299-356); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4` (`sentence` :854-856, `paragraphDefinition` :879-881, `paragraphName` :892-894 - `procedureHeaderHere` is a predicate the token-type set never evaluates - comment :883-891); `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md` section 3.9 last bullet; `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/ParseRecoveryTailTests.cs:191`.

**Finding.** After a prediction failure whose sentence tail holds a user-defined word NOT directly followed by a period (`ADD TO A END-IF.`, `ADD TO A ELSE ...`, `ADD TO A DISPLAY "X".`; no terminator needed - the trigger the first report named was wrong), recovery stops at that word because IDENTIFIER is in the recovery set as a possible paragraph header, the sentence cannot resume there, and the outer recoveries silently consume every token up to the first user word that IS followed by a period, parsing it as a phantom paragraph header; faults in between are never reported (`G4/V2`, V12, V18 loses three sentences, V19; `G4-refute/R3` `DISPLAY B "X".` does not stop it, R14 `ADD TO A B.` makes `B.` the header). A tail with no such word recovers at the next verb or the period (R1, R2, R11, R15). cobc reports every planted fault. Also found: R10 `IF A = 1 THEN ADD TO "X" ELSE MOVE 1 A END-IF.` draws a FALSE COBOL0306 "END-IF appears without a matching IF statement", one cascade the first report said never happens. MEDIUM, not high: section 4.2.2 asks only that violations be indicated and the unit is rejected either way.

**Fix direction.** Inside a sentence an IDENTIFIER is a resync point only where a paragraph header can stand (followed by its period). Do NOT discard the whole sentence as first proposed: that loses in-sentence second faults reported today (R11 `ADD TO "X" MOVE 1 A.` reports both, as cobc does; R10 reports the ELSE fault).

**Citations checked** (`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`): section 4.2.2 "violations of the general formats and the explicit syntax rules of standard COBOL"; section 14.4.3 "A paragraph consists of a paragraph-name followed by a separator period".
