# PB5796 - EVALUATE accepts a THRU range between a figurative constant and an operand of another class (every edition)

**What is wrong.** `EVALUATE N WHEN SPACE THRU 9` and `WHEN 1 THRU HIGH-VALUE` (N is `PIC 9(3)`) compile and print `IN` at `--std 85` and at `--std 2023`, while `WHEN "A" THRU 9` is refused (`COBOL2107`, the control e3). A figurative constant is a literal, and every literal is nonnumeric or numeric; SPACE, QUOTE, HIGH-VALUE and LOW-VALUE represent characters, not a numeric value, so they are nonnumeric literals and of category (class) alphanumeric (only ZERO takes the subject's class, `§1985:VI.6.13.4 GR1b`). `SPACE THRU 9` and `1 THRU HIGH-VALUE` therefore join operands of two classes, which `§1985:VI.6.13.3 SR4` forbids. 2023 is wrong the same way (cause b): a figurative constant represents an alphanumeric (or, where national is required, national) character value, never numeric; the 2023 row `SR-14.9.13.3-4` is re-recorded non-conforming by a registrar. The reader's ground (1985 gives figurative constants a category only for MOVE) misses `IV.4.2.2.2` and its GR3.

**Mechanism (the checker's reading).** `IntrinsicArgumentRules.ClassOf` answers null for a figurative with two candidate classes (Alphanumeric, National), and `ScreenRangeOperands` skips the same-class test when either end is null, though no candidate of the figurative is numeric.

**Sites:**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/EvaluateBinder.cs#ScreenRangeOperands` (the same-class test runs only when both `TableTwoClassOf` ends are non-null)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/IntrinsicArgumentRules.cs#ClassOf` (null for a figurative with two candidates)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/IntrinsicArgumentRules.cs#CandidateClasses`
