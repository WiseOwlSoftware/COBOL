## Landed (wave 1039 A, 2026-10-08)

**A WARNING, not an error — the determination changed, and that is why the 21 corpus programs no longer need
rewriting.** §8.8.4.2.1's sentence is neither a general format nor a syntax rule, so §4.2.2 settles only that "This
warning mechanism shall indicate violations of such rules"; the severity is implementor latitude and the owner's
precedence decides it. GnuCOBOL 3.2, measured under WSL (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1039a\p\lit.cob`), compiles
`IF 1 = 1`, `IF "A" = "A"` and `PERFORM UNTIL 1 = 1` and warns under `-Wall` (`-Wconstant-numlit-expression`,
`-Wconstant-expression`; silent by default; `IF "A" = SPACE` and `IF ZERO = ZERO` draw nothing). So each relation of
two literals is **WARNING COBOLNET3146** at every edition and strictness and evaluates as written (docs/CONFORMANCE.md
**D-RELLITERAL**, the D-DECLREF SR3 precedent). Wave 1020 D's error design (COBOLNET2903, never landed; that code stays
unused) had turned 21 collation and figurative-sizing goldens red; under the warning they compile unchanged.

**Mechanism.** `ConditionBinder.WrittenRelational` is the one seam every WRITTEN relation condition passes (IF,
PERFORM UNTIL, SEARCH WHEN, a boolean relation, an abbreviated relation's inserted subject — `IF 1 = N OR 2` holds
`1 = 2` — and an EVALUATE partial-expression, §14.9.13.3 SR8 "treated as though it were specified as condition-2");
it asks `StatementValidation.CheckRelationHasNonLiteralOperand` of the operands as written (an EVALUATE subject's
intermediate reads through) and then builds through `CheckedRelational`. EVALUATE subject/object pairs and ranges call
`CheckedRelational` directly: §14.9.13.3 SR10's Table 15 governs them (it refuses literal/literal and permits a
range-expression against a literal subject). Literals: every bound literal and figurative shape, a symbolic-character
(§8.3.2.2.29 "a user-defined figurative constant") and a constant-name (§13.10.4 GR1 "as if literal-1 … were written
where constant-name-1 is written"); NOT a data item, an arithmetic expression (`IF 1 + 1 = 2`) or a function-identifier,
folded (`BoundNumericLiteral.FunctionValue`) or not (§8.4.3.2.1).

**Witnesses.** `conformance:RelationLiteralOperandTests` — every ordered pair of 16 operand kinds, expected verdicts
computed from the kinds' one property; every surface with the complement; a warning at all four editions — and the
golden `conformance:85/pb1470_relation_of_two_literals_runs` (spec-computed values: §8.8.4.2.7 rule 2 padding,

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs#CheckRelationalOperands (no both-literal arm); src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ExpressionBinder.cs#NonNumericLiteralOperand (comment ~line 213)
