# PB3640 — what the partition can and cannot prevent

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`collision`, the in-wave `unpart` rule), `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` (exit 4 handling), `scripts/spec/work.py` (`SITES_REQUIRED_FROM`), `scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py`.

## The incident (2026-10-24)

Wave 20261030 (campaign PB1957) ran PB3628 (`tooling/orchestrator`, site block: `land_lane.py`), PB3275
(`tooling/landing`, no site block at plan time) and PB3310 (`tooling/gate`, no site block at plan time) in parallel; the
planner printed UNPARTITIONED for the unsited groups and the operator launched anyway. PB3628 and PB3275 both rewrote the
NOT QUEUED rule in `prune_worktrees.lifecycle`; after PB3275 merged (PR #504), PR #505 read DIRTY and needed a finisher,
and PB3267, built on PB3628's old head, carried the same conflict. Separately PB3612 (a 3-hour chain link) conflicted
with PB3310 in `enqueue.py`. The owner: "<sigh> Even more and more cases of wasting time and tokens"; "I don't understand
why github merge doesn't handle the conflicts".

## Two fixes tried and ABANDONED (branch `fix-pb3263-unknown-subsystem-prefix`)

1. Partition an unknown set by the area's TOP segment. The branch review (w20261024-o, R1-1, medium) refuted it: the
   orchestrator's and landing's scripts are filed under five top segments (tooling/, process/, orchestrator/, landing/,
   scripts/); open unsited pairs PB3157 (`orchestrator/landing`) / PB3367 (`process/landing`) on `enqueue.py` and PB3409
   (`tooling/landing`) / PB3386 (`scripts/orchestrator`) on `prune_worktrees.py` still met nowhere.
2. An unknown set meets EVERYTHING (in the wave, or also in flight). `test_plan_wave.py` went 336/341: finishers of
   FINDINGS branches (PB3311, R1-1, R1-2 cases) whose sets read unknown were held a wave, the opposite of finishing first.

fix-pb3263-unknown-subsystem-prefix ABANDONED 2026-10-24: neither rule is right (above), and nothing of it landed.

## Why no partition rule closes it

Even a fully sited wave would have run PB3628 beside PB3275: PB3628's own site block named `land_lane.py` and its
change also edited `prune_worktrees.py`. A declared file set is a prediction. The partition reduces conflicts; it cannot
remove them.

## What does reduce the cost (fix direction)

- **Resolve a conflict at once.** An exit 4 or a DIRTY bounce today waits for the next serial enqueue unit to dispatch a
  finisher (PB3612's waited from 13:55). The landing lane, or the review lane PB3608 adds, dispatches the conflict
  finisher the moment the stop is recorded, with both sides' notes and reports in its brief.
- **Small, fast branches** (PB3638): one link per wave, so a branch lives minutes, not hours, before it lands.
- **Complete site blocks**: `work.py check` requires a site block only from PB3443 on (`SITES_REQUIRED_FROM`); a note a
  running campaign names should need one whatever its number, and an implementer whose diff leaves its declared sites
  adds them to the note in the same change (so the ledger's file set matches what landed).
