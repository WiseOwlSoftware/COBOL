# PB5470 - at --std 85 a COPY statement whose pseudo-text holds the text word *> is refused (COBOL0900, COBOL2449): the 2002 floating comment indicator is applied to COBOL-85 pseudo-text and swallows the rest of the line

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.LogicalConversion.cs` (`ReferenceFormatProcessor.FixedFormConverter.ConvertLine`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/TextWord.cs` (`TextWordScanner.CommentStartsAt`, `TextWordScanner.TryNext`)

**Mechanism.** COBOL-85 has no floating comment indicator: `*>` is "Any other sequence of contiguous COBOL characters except comment lines and the word 'COPY'" (§1985:III.2), an ordinary text word, and pseudo-text is a sequence of text words. The logical conversion and the text-word scanner read `*>` as the 2002 comment at every edition, and at --std 85 add the edition error COBOL0900, so a pseudo-text-1 holding `*>` loses its closing delimiter and BY phrase (COBOL2449). kb/Work PB1459 and PB1493 (landed) gate `*>` at 85 and teach the directive scanner to see it; neither reads it as a COBOL-85 text word inside pseudo-text.

**Found by.** The 1985 code read, read 4 (`kb/Work/PB4996/2026-11-13-read-4.md`): the refuter of `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/out-read4/rd-06-refute.json` OVERTURNED the read's CONFORMS of 1985-GR-XII.2.4-5 and 1985-GR-XII.2.4-6 on this one root.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids below. Its rows are recorded naming this note (records-rd4).
