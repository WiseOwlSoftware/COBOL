# PB4743 — Messaging slice S3: binding SEND and RECEIVE (MessagingBinder, the screens, RECEIVED)

A slice of `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-message-control.md` (§4.3, §9 S3), which the owner approved on 2026-11-04 (the owner, 2026-11-04: "yes, both approved" (the transports) and "Yes" (both documents and Q1-Q9 as recommended)). Build exactly what that section and the slice's entry say, with its goldens, tests and doc changes in the same landing.

**Prerequisites:** PB4740.

## Landed (2026-11-05)

Slice S3 is built as §2023:4.1 and §2023:4.3 of the design say, except for two corrections the design now records. The re-probe on the build before the change:
`RECEIVE WS-X GIVING ...` with a `PIC X` data-name-1, `SEND TO 5 FROM ... RETURNING ...`, a `RETURNING` item of class pointer or numeric, an identifier-1
with a `POINTER`, `BASED`, `DYNAMIC LENGTH` or outside-`DEPENDING ON` description, and `CONTINUE AFTER MESSAGE FOO` all compiled clean; after it each draws its
refusal.

- **`MessagingBinder`** (`Binding/Procedure/Verbs/MessagingBinder.cs`) replaces `DeclinedFacilityBinder`'s SEND and RECEIVE members; it builds
  `BoundSendToServer`, `BoundSendResponse` and `BoundReceiveMessage` (`Binding/Bound/BoundMessaging.cs`), each with its exception phrases. The screens run in the
  design's order: name shape (COBOL2024), literal-1 class, message-tag items, RECEIVE's integer item (COBOL6265), the message-area walk (COBOL6266), RAISING's
  exception-name through `EcNameResolution`. `StatementBinder` routes both arms through `BindDeclinedStatement` still (COBOL1578 stands until S4), whose operand action
  calls `MessagingBinder` and drops the node; `BindDeclinedStatement` takes the deferred-shape operands off the unbuilt ledger for every declined statement.
- **Grammar.** The rules are `messagingReceiveStatement` and `messagingSendStatement`; the word after `MESSAGE` is `RECEIVED` (a predicate; `FOO` is COBOL0001).
- **Correction to the design.** RECEIVE's CONSTANT RECORD arm is unreachable in the message-area walk: `ResolveReceiving` refuses every item of a constant record first
  (COBOL1548), so the arm is the receiving funnel's. SEND accepts a constant record. The design's §4.3 says so.
- **Visitors.** `BoundStores` and `UsageCollectionPass` answer the three nodes; `StatementEmitter` throws for them (no node reaches it before S4).
- **Goldens.** Positive: `pb4743-receive-syntax-rules-accepted`, `pb4743-send-syntax-rules-accepted`. Refusals (`diagnostics/pb4743-*.json`): nine, one per code arm
  and statement. `MessagingSyntaxRuleTests` pins every forbidden-clause arm for both statements, the legal neighbours and the below-2023 edge. Nine existing goldens and
  five test sources that used a `PIC X` or `POINTER` item as a message tag now declare `USAGE MESSAGE-TAG`.
- **Registry.** Nine rows are CONFORMS (the formats and syntax rules); `mcs-only` now selects the general rules alone (14 rows).
