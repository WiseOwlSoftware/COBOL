# PB5138 - the 85 reserved-word source is GnuCOBOL, not the standard

**Sites:** `scripts/gen-reserved-words.ps1` (`Parse-GcWords`, `Read-WordList`: the 85 source), `tests/version-matrix/reserved-words.json` (the `r85` flag),
`specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (the page-191 table, Section IV paragraph 8).

**Found.** PB5060 measured: the 350 words the printed 1985 list holds equal the 350 words that `reserved-words.json` flags `r85`
(GnuCOBOL's list), so there is no harm today. CLAUDE.md rule 1 puts an ISO or ANSI text above GnuCOBOL; the generator reads the
2023 list from §8.9 but the 1985 flags from GnuCOBOL.

**Owed.** Read the 85 flags from the transcribed list as the 2023 flags are read, keep GnuCOBOL's list as the net (a drift check
that the two sets agree, reporting a difference rather than choosing), and regenerate with no change in the output.
