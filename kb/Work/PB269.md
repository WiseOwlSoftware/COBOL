# PB269 — the near-unit logarithm band, and an 'inherent' that was not

Found by the arithmetic-wave review fleet over the D-B/D-C/PB194/PB195 working tree, 2026-08-31, and fixed in the change set that landed the wave. Every number below was re-derived independently by the implementer (a 34-digit emulation of `CobolDec.cs` validated against the shipped `pb167` goldens, and 80–120-digit `Decimal` truth) BEFORE the confirming run, and then confirmed by it.

## The mechanism

`LnReduced(m)` computed u = m^(1/8) by three 34-digit-rounded square roots, then z = (u−1)/(u+1). For m = 1 + δ
the roots put an absolute error of ~1.5×10⁻³⁴ into u while u − 1 is only ≈ δ/8, so `Sub(u, One)` keeps a relative
error of ~10⁻³³/δ and ln m loses log₁₀(1/δ) digits. At δ = 10⁻³³ — the closest an SDIDI value can get to one —
the first root rounds straight back to 1 and ln m comes out EXACTLY ZERO.

## Measured (an independent emulation of this file against 120-digit truth)

| ln m, correct significant digits | three-root reduction | series over δ |
|---|---|---|
| worst over \|m−1\| ≤ 0.1 | 17.05 | 32.98 |
| worst over \|m−1\| ≤ 0.25 | 17.05 | 32.74 |
| m = 1 + 10⁻³³ | **0.00** | 34.00 |
| m = 1 + 10⁻¹⁶ | 16.44 | 33.88 |

…and end to end:

| `b ** e` | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `1.00001 ** 1000000` | 26.60 digits | 32.39 |
| `1.0000000000000001 ** 1000000` | 26.44 | 33.48 |
| `(1 + 10⁻³³) ** 1.0E+20` | 13.00 | 39.78 |
| `(1 + 10⁻³³) ** 1.0E+30` | **3.00** (answered a flat 1) | 33.16 |
| a 31-digit near-unit base ** 10¹⁴ | 18.46 | 29.08 |

## The fix

ln(1 + δ) = 2·atanh(δ/(2+δ)), taken over δ = m − 1 directly inside |m − 1| ≤ ¼. There is no cancellation to lose:
`Sub(m, One)` is EXACT in that band (both operands within a factor of two). It is also CHEAPER — no square root
at all, ≤20 series terms against the reduction's three BigInteger roots plus ~10 — so the band was chosen where
the direct arm is both more accurate and less work, measured, not reasoned. Outside it the reduction keeps
everything (the series over δ would need up to ~58 terms there, and u − 1 is large enough that it loses nothing).
`AtanhSeries` is now one series serving both reductions.

## The determination, restated honestly

The published law — CobolDec.cs's r2e banner, CONFORMANCE.md §7, PB167.md and the GR-8.8.1.5.4-2 inventory note —
said "roughly 31–33 correct significant digits for a moderate exponent, degrading as |operand-2 × ln operand-1|
grows (… inherent to the equivalent expression, not to this coding of it)". Two things were wrong. It named ONE
driver where there were two, and it called the degradation inherent — true of the |p·ln b| driver, false of the
near-unit one, which was manufactured in `LnReduced` and is now gone. The digits were also understated. All four
copies now publish the same measured law:

* **≈33 correct significant digits** for a moderate exponent (33.00–33.58 over the six
  `CobolDecPowDevelopmentTests` rows; 30.87 the floor over a 400-case randomized in-range sweep);
* degrading as **≈ 33 − log₁₀|operand-2 × ln operand-1|** once that product passes 1 — 32.15 digits at 7.1,
  31.12 at 69, 30.11 at 693, 28.78 at 13 863. THIS driver is inherent: an exp development turns absolute error in
  its argument into relative error in its result.
