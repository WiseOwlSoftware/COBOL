# PB5691 - the practices check can only go GREEN over an implementer's brief

**Sites:** `.claude/skills/workstream/check_practices.py` (`BRIEFS`, `main`; the argument handling at lines 470 to 501),
`.claude/skills/workstream/SKILL.md` (lines 25 and 80).

**What happened.** `python .claude/skills/workstream/check_practices.py E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-20261114/read-2002/BRIEF-reader.md
E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-20261114/read-2002/BRIEF-refuter.md` printed `FAIL BRIEF-reader.md: missing ['claude-skills',
'MANDATORY-PRACTICES.md', 'BelowNormal', ... 'linux-gate\.sh']` (36 implementer markers) and `=== PRACTICES CHECK: RED (2) ===`.
The two briefs are those of a read-only reading and refuting fleet for the 2002 edition. The fleet was launched on the two `ok`
lines the check prints when run with no arguments.

**Evidence.** The workstream skill (`SKILL.md`) says the check "must print GREEN over the briefs and over every rendered spec
before a Workflow call". `check_practices.py` takes every non-`.json` argument as a rendered spec (line 486) and holds it to the
implementer's patterns (`SPEC`, line 152, from `BRIEFS['dispatch-spec-implementer.md']`); no argument form names another
profile. The brief files are under `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-20261114/read-2002/` (`BRIEF-reader.md`, `BRIEF-refuter.md`).
PB3742 concerns the same file's turn-cap check, not this.

**The question this note asks.** Which profile of `check_practices.py` a read-only reader or refuter brief is held to, given
the skill's statement that the check must print GREEN over the briefs of a Workflow.
