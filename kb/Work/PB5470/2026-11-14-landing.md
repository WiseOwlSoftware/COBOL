**Mechanism and fix.** Comments are removed by the §2023:6.5 logical conversion alone, before text manipulation; below COBOL-2002 fixed form has no floating comment, so `*>` is an ordinary text-word (§1985:III). One edition predicate, `ReferenceFormatDiagnostics.FixedFormReadsFloatingComments`, tells the conversion (`LogicalLineBuilder.CommentAt`, `TryFirstText`, `ScanProgramText`) to leave `*>` in the text there. The text-word scanner no longer reads a comment of its own: it skips only a carried comment-entry line (`TextWordScanner.IsCommentEntryCarrierAt`, the old `CommentStartsAt`), and `HoldsComment` asks for a character-string holding the indicator. The 2002 introduction gate (COBOL0900, COBOL2495) is asked once of the finished text by `SurvivingFloatingComments.Report`, run in `Frontend.Preprocess` after the comment-entry resolver: only a `*>` that survives COPY and REPLACE is refused, and the lexer still skips it as before. Free form always reads `*>` as a comment (the extension's one comment). `ForbiddenIn` (a replacing action introducing a comment) stays edition-neutral: at 85 an introduced `*>` is a text word that the gate then refuses, still an error.
