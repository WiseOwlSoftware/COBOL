# PB5092 - 1985 change records the PB5062 re-adjudication left out disagree with their re-decided siblings

**Sites:** `tests/version-matrix/inventory-1985/` (whole: the change records named below).

**Found (PB5062, 2026-11-09).** The adjudicators re-decided only the 95 records PB5045 withdrew, and three of them reported
that sibling records outside their slices now rest on reasoning the re-decision refuted. Each pair should take ONE ruling:

- **INVALID KEY not required when a USE procedure applies** (slices 09 and 10): `1985-SR-IX.4.6.3-3` and `1985-SR-VIII.4.6.3-4`
  were re-decided CHANGED (a lifted refusal whose case 2023 defines; the deciding words and checked citations are in the
  records of `readj-85/out/slice-09.json` and `slice-10.json`), as the WRITE records
  (`1985-SR-VIII.4.9.3-3`, `1985-SR-IX.4.9.3-3`) already are; the store still holds the DELETE, READ and START INVALID KEY
  records of sections VIII and IX as REMOVED.
- **READ GR11 (record-operation conflict)** (slice 02): `1985-GR-IX.4.5.4-11` stays CHANGED on the GR13a proviso alone; the
  withdrawn record's fatal-condition ground is refuted by the 1985 text (the adjudicator's record in `slice-02.json`). Its siblings `1985-GR-VII.4.4.4-10`,
  `1985-GR-VIII.4.5.4-10` and `-11` still carry the refuted ground.
- **INSPECT GR12 against GR15** (slices 03 and 04): GR12 was decided KEPT ("leftmost" against "first encountered" differs
  only under BACKWARD) while GR2 was decided CHANGED for BACKWARD; GR15 was decided on the same ground. One reading must
  hold for all three.

**Fix.** Re-adjudicate the named siblings against the ruling of their re-decided pair (the design's change kinds quoted
verbatim, DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md section 10.2), and record them through `record_verdicts.py --edition 1985`.
