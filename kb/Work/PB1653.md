# PB1653 — OPEN

Filed 2026-09-27 by the train-66 lander from the wave-66 K implementer report (PB1128 + PB1126 + PB908). Same
alphabet root as PB1128's national-group store fix (`PlaceRenderer.WriteGroupValue`), which deliberately serves a
REDEFINES view as its character window so the reader and writer agree.

**Observed.** A Tier-B REDEFINES view whose item is a national group is read through the view's character window,
not its national positions. The generated read for a 5-position national group is
`CobolString.RefMod(REC._redef_BASE, 1, 10)`: ten characters for five national positions. ASCII content only
appears to match because each UTF-16 code unit's low byte coincides.

**Repro.** `E:\Temp\claude\E--COBOL\eb162d4b-e0bb-4027-b8a0-86fd190d20be\scratchpad\w66k\p1128r.cob` (copy it into
the tests tree when fixing; the scratchpad is not durable).

**Rule.** §13.18.29.4 2) b): "a national group is treated as though it were an elementary data item of usage
national and class and category national described with PICTURE N(m), where m is the length of the group"
(`cite.py --check 13.18.29.4` OK 2) b)).

**Code sites.** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/OperandText.cs:415` (the `RedefViewPlace` arm, which precedes the
group dispatch), the view's identity coding in `ReferenceResolver`, and the matching `RedefViewPlace` arms in
`PlaceRenderer.WriteGroupValue` / `WriteGroupImage`. Second site, not yet probed:
`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/AcceptDisplayEmitter.cs:107` stores device characters into any group through
`WriteGroupImage`, so a national group receiver of ACCEPT probably gets bytes as characters too. Route it through
`WriteGroupValue`.

## Landed (wave 1001 B)

Re-probed on this wave's Debug build (`n1.cob`: `01 A PIC X(10). 01 NG REDEFINES A GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL. 05 N1 PIC N(2).
05 N2 PIC N(3).`): after `MOVE N"ABCDE" TO NG`, `N1`/`N2` printed `䅂䍄|䔠††` — the five national positions had been stored
as five BYTES and read back as UTF-16BE pairs. §13.18.29.4 GR2 b) re-checked (`cite.py --check`, OK).

**Mechanism.** `RedefViewPlace.For` gave a bit group a `BitWindow` (its value alphabet is its boolean positions) but a
national group NO coding, because `NationalWindow.PositionsOf` is the ELEMENTARY test. Every verb then read/wrote the
group's value through the 2m-byte window as characters — and each verb did it its own way: MOVE stored national
characters as bytes, ACCEPT poured m device characters through the storage image (`FromImage`), a CALL crossed 2m bytes
against a formal counting m positions (`IsAsIfGroupCrossing` excluded views). ACCEPT into a national or bit group was
wrong for the PLAIN group too (the same `WriteGroupImage` on an as-if group).

**Fix (one rule: a group view's value channel is its window's coding).**
- `NationalWindow.WindowPositionsOf` (elementary national → `PositionsOf`; national GROUP → its as-if N(m) length) is
  what `RedefViewPlace.For` asks, so a national group's window is a `NationalWindow` like a bit group's is a `BitWindow`.
- `PlaceRenderer`: `ByteWindowRead/Write` are the byte window (storage channel); `GroupImage`/`WriteGroupImage` serve a
  national GROUP view through them (file records, group MOVE, status groups deposit storage), while `Read`/`Write` and
  `WriteGroupValue` are the value channel. `BitValue`/`NatValue`/`BitStore`/`NatStore` serve a view with its own
  read/write — the old "a Tier-B view never reaches here" premise of `SendingBits` is gone, and `CallEmitter.
  IsAsIfGroupCrossing` no longer excludes views.
- `AcceptDisplayEmitter`: a group receiver takes `AcceptSource.Device(size)` / `DeviceBoolean(size)` (size = the one
  receiver-size reader) through `WriteGroupValue` — national and bit groups, view or plain.

Sweep (variant-analysis): dispatch = the `RedefViewPlace.Coding` arms + the two channels of every group reader/writer;
arms: identity (probed OK), bit group (probed OK: MOVE, ref-mod, compare, CALL BY REFERENCE/CONTENT view vs plain —
`b2.cob`), national group (fixed), SlotWindow/DynSlotWindow/VarGroupWindow (untouched). Probed OK after the fix, view =
plain: MOVE/INITIALIZE/INSPECT/STRING/UNSTRING/EVALUATE/relation/ref-mod, CALL BY REFERENCE and BY CONTENT with a
national group formal, a table of national groups under REDEFINES, an EXTERNAL and a BASED national group, an FD record
that is a national group (WRITE then READ back).

Witnesses: `2002/pb1653_national_group_view` (+ negative `pb1653-national-group-view-below-2002`),
`GroupViewAliasDriftTests` (view vs plain over ten statement shapes, each pinned to the ISO-derived value; GVA01/GVA02 fail
on the pre-fix tree), `AcceptDifferentialTests.Device_NationalGroupReceiver_TransfersItsPositions` and
`Device_BitGroupReceiver_TransfersItsPositions` (both fail on the pre-fix tree). The two drift tests that pinned the old
spelling were re-stated: `GroupStoreTargetDriftTests` now asserts each KIND of generated group store appears (the bit and
national writers share one spelling), `ReceivingStoreDriftTests` no longer exempts a group-image line.
