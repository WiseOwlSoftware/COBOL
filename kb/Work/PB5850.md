# PB5850 - at --std 85 JUSTIFIED, and BLANK WHEN ZERO, on a report group entry that has subordinates compiles

**Ruling: UPHELD (a), harm under_rejects.** The row `1985-SR-VI.5.6.3-1` does not close on its inherited verdict. JUSTIFIED, and BLANK WHEN ZERO, on a report group entry that has subordinates compiles at 85; 2023 refuses it. The checker's confidence: high. Cause (a): wrong at 85 only.

**The checker's evidence (rulings.json, copied).** `§1985:XIII.3.9.4 GR2` makes the Report Writer's BLANK WHEN ZERO and JUSTIFIED clauses the same as the Nucleus clauses, so `§1985:VI.5.6.3 SR1` and `§1985:VI.5.4.3 SR1` (BLANK WHEN ZERO can be specified only for an elementary item) reach a report group entry; and the 1985 report group general formats carry JUSTIFIED and BLANK WHEN ZERO only in Format 3 (the elementary entry with PICTURE), not in Format 2 (the LINE group) (`specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` lines about 22756 to 22790, transcription; page not rendered: the question is the presence of the clause in a format, not a bracket). A `05 LINE 1 JUSTIFIED RIGHT.` with a COLUMN entry under it compiles and runs at 85 and writes the line `ABCD`; the same with BLANK WHEN ZERO likewise. At 2023 both are refused (COBOL2247, `§2023:13.15.3 SR15`), so the 85 arm is the gap. The data-division arm (`CheckPictureRequiredPass.CheckElementaryOnlyClauses`) is right, as the reader found; it never sees report entries.
