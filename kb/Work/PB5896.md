# PB5896 - analysis: at --std 85 a page heading whose absolute line lies above GR1's upper limit compiles with no diagnostic, where the lower-limit violation is refused (COBOL2199)

Filed from the Mythos lane's rulings on the newly printed 1985 folios (`kb/Work/PB5683/2026-11-17-rulings-pb5789-folios.md`). The ruling's words, unchanged:

> Lead (not a defect): the compiler refuses the lower-limit violation at compile time (COBOL2199) but not the
> upper-limit one. A diagnostic for the upper limit would be consistent.

The ruling's reasoning, from the same section: GR1 sets the first line on which a page heading CAN be presented. A program whose absolute page-heading line lies above that limit violates a general rule, and `§1985:I.1.6` makes "the results of violating the formats or rules" undefined. The compiler's placement on that line (by GR3a) is therefore not a defect, and this is consistency, not conformance.
- `§1985:I.1.6`: "the results of violating the formats or rules"
