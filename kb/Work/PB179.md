# PB179

Found by the Step D design scout (2026-08-30; the classify + spec readers, independently). DataBinder.ComputeTier
screens float/COMP-5/BINARY-*/INDEX (dissolving with Step D) and NATIONAL, but nothing for
PicCategory.Pointer/ProgramPointer/ObjectReference — its comment at ~3618 says "No pointer/object/strongly-typed
items exist in the bound model yet", which is stale (they are live). Such a leaf reaches Tier B with
ElementaryImageWidth 0 → a zero-width window over the backing. §13.18.44.3 SR12/SR14 bar object/pointer/
strongly-typed items from BOTH sides of a REDEFINES — the rejection is SPEC-REQUIRED (a bind diagnostic, not the
Tier-C stage). FIX: the Tier-D arm in ComputeTier citing SR12/SR14, a negative fixture per side, and
RedefinesTier.Rejected's doc corrected. Lands naturally WITH the Step D widening (same function).
