**Repro:** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20262131q/work-q-02/g03.cob` (`MOVE DA TO W`, DA the 01 TYPE DETAIL entry) at `--std 85`
prints `§1985:XIII.3.9.3 SR10d: "Data-name-1 may be referenced only if the entry defines a sum counter"`. SR10d sits under "(10)
In format 3:"; a 01 entry's rule is SR5 ("If specified, data-name-1 may be referenced only by a GENERATE statement, the UPON
phrase of a SUM clause, a USE BEFORE REPORTING sentence, or as a sum counter qualifier") and a format-2 entry's is SR7.

**Code site:** the refusal of `ReportItemReference` (see Sites); the declaration fact it
reads carries no entry format, so the fix gives the fact the format and the message the matching rule. The descriptor's Rule1985
then becomes `§1985:XIII.3.9.3 SR5 / §1985:XIII.3.9.3 SR7 / §1985:XIII.3.9.3 SR10d`.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReportSectionBinder.cs:4823` (`ReportSectionBinder.ReportEntryReferenceRefused`)
