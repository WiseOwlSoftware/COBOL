# PB5870 - two 1985 choices settled by the precedence have no sentence in the conformance register

Noticed on Monday 2026-11-16 by the Mythos lane, ruling re-reads of the 1985 code reads (kb/Work PB5683; the rulings are tracked beside it: batch 14 and batch 15).

kind: analysis. No harm flag. Severity: MINOR. area: docs/conformance. related: PB5683, R13.

## What is owed
CLAUDE.md rule 1: where the standard leaves an implementor a choice, the precedence settles it (the ISO text if it controls, otherwise GnuCOBOL) "and the choice is documented in `docs/conformance/`". Two rulings of 2026-11-16 settled a 1985 row that way and found no entry that states the choice:

1. **A group item is of the category alphanumeric.** Row `1985-SR-VII.2.5.3-2` (the FILE STATUS item "must be defined in the Data Division as a two-character data item of the category alphanumeric"; `§1985:VII.2.5.3 SR2`, cite.py OK). The 1985 text gives a group item the class alphanumeric and names no category for it. This compiler accepts a FILE STATUS item that is a group, a group of two numeric items included, as GnuCOBOL does (`cobc/tree.c#cb_tree_category` gives every field that has subordinate items the category alphanumeric; `cobc/typeck.c#validate_file_status` accepts a two-character item of that category; 3.3-dev, source read, not run).
2. **"The same data-name" in RENAMES is the same item, not the same word.** Row `1985-SR-VI.5.11.3-4` (data-name-2 and data-name-3 "cannot be the same data-name"; `§1985:VI.5.11.3 SR4`, cite.py OK). This compiler refuses `RENAMES A THRU A` and accepts `RENAMES A OF G1 THRU A OF G2`, two different items that share a word, as GnuCOBOL does (`cobc/field.c#cb_validate_renames_item` compares the two resolved items; 3.3-dev, source read, not run).

## What closes it
An entry (or a sentence in the entry that already speaks of the clause) for each, in `docs/conformance/`, saying what the 1985 text leaves open, what this implementation does and on what evidence, then `python scripts/doc_audits.py --write-register`. Whether the 2002 and 2023 texts leave the same two points open is to be read from their own words when the entries are written: neither ruling read them.

- `§1985:VI.5.11.3 SR4`: "cannot be the same data-name": `OK  §1985:VI.5.11.3 SR4  (║5.11.3 Syntax Rules║)  the text is in SR4`
