# PB4731 - D16's binary64 evaluation of float-item arithmetic against the exactness ruling

**Witness.** `A1 FLOAT-LONG 0.1`, `A2 FLOAT-LONG 0.2`, `R PIC S9V9(19)`. `COMPUTE R = A1 + A2` stores +0.3000000000000000444 (the binary64 sum, NUMERIC_DESIGN D16
and DOC-A.1-123). The exact sum of the two items' binary64 values is +0.3000000000000000166. GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 evaluates the expression in decimal over its
own lossy conversion of each double and stores .2999999999999999750 (p2 G01, p4 T07r).

**Question, not decided here.** The owner's ruling of 2026-11-04 on PB4569 (rule 1 supersedes NUMERIC_DESIGN D19 (a); results are exact where the standard
defines an exact value) concerned the exact form. Does it also reach D16's binary64 evaluation of an expression with a float-item operand? Native evaluation
is implementor-defined (§8.8.1.3), and the conversion of a float item into decimal is the implementor's (§8.8.1.5.1), so the standard fixes no value, and
GnuCOBOL's own value comes from a lossy intake that is not followed (see PB4732). The answer may need an owner decision.

**Dependency.** PB4622's fix keeps SUM, MEAN, RANGE and MIDRANGE over float items equal to this evaluation whatever it becomes, because they share the
`CarrierKind.Real` kind with `A1 + A2`.
