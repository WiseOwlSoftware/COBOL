So the accepted U+0130 `>>IF`/`>>END-IF` are the OUTLIERS: the fix is to stop folding directive keywords, not to fold DEFINED. **Owner decision, 2026-10-31 (Mythos session, answering GAP-REVIEW.md section 5.3 question 2):** the section 8.1.3.2 GR4 fold (Annex C, including 0130 to i and 212A to k) governs user-defined words only. `DİSPLAY` is neither the DISPLAY verb nor a legal data-name, and directive keywords match on basic letters alone: `>>IF` and `>>END-IF` are brought in line.
