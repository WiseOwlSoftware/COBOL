## Finding — constant-conditional-expression (rejects-legal-source)

The constant-conditional-expression grammar tier (cceOr/cceAnd/cceNot) is a hand-written second copy of the §8.8.4.9 logical-operator tier that never received the 2023 EXCLUSIVE-OR/XOR connector and never received the Table 5 'NOT NOT' exclusion the runtime tier (logicalXorExpression / unaryLogicalExpression) carries — a two-arm dispatch where only the runtime arm was updated.

**Rows:** GR-7.3.8.3-1, SR-7.3.8.2-1

**Repro:**

```
Fixed-form program, --std 2023: '>>IF 1 = 2 XOR 1 = 1' / DISPLAY "TRUE". / >>ELSE / DISPLAY "FALSE". / >>END-IF. Also '>>IF 1 = 1 EXCLUSIVE-OR 1 = 1' and '>>IF NOT NOT 1 = 1' (any edition).
```

**Expected from the spec:** At 2023 the XOR program compiles and displays TRUE; the EXCLUSIVE-OR case displays FALSE; XOR binds tighter than OR and looser than AND (§8.8.4.11.3). At 2002/2014 XOR is not a connector (Annex E.3.2 item 4). 'NOT NOT 1 = 1' is rejected at every edition (§8.8.4.11.3 Table 5: NOT may be followed only by simple-condition or '(').

**Observed:** Pinned cobol.exe: XOR and EXCLUSIVE-OR both -> COBOLNET1619 'malformed compile-time expression'; 'NOT NOT 1 = 1' compiles and displays TRUE at 2023 and 2002 (under-reject).

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolExpressions.g4 (cceOr/cceAnd/cceNot ~587-591); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Expressions/CompileTimeExpressionEvaluator.cs EvalCceOr/EvalCceAnd/EvalCceNot

## What the fixer owes (and the sibling sweep)

Make the CCE tier REUSE the runtime logical-connective rules (one grammar tier parameterized by operand, or a shared rule) rather than re-copy them, and add a drift test that the two tiers accept the same connective set. Goldens: XOR in >>IF at 2023; NOT NOT rejected.

**Rows claimed:** GR-7.3.8.3-1, SR-7.3.8.2-1
