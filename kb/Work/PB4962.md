# PB4962 - a second copy of the literal-to-operand mapping

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ExpressionBinder.cs` (line 260, `PeekNonNumericLiteralOperand`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs` (`CalleeSignatureOf`, `BindCall`, `Literal1Of`, `LiteralAliasOf`, `AliasOperand`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/MessageTagIntroductionGateTests.cs` (where the drift test sits)

**Report (round-1 review LEAD, low).** `PeekNonNumericLiteralOperand` is a second hand-maintained copy of the nonNumericLiteral-to-operand mapping that `NonNumericLiteralOperand` documents as THE ONE mapping; the DA3 defect was exactly such a copy. Separately, `CallBinder.CalleeSignatureOf` re-derives literal-1 (`LiteralAliasOf`, the non-numeric filter and `AliasOperand`) and the three callee producers beside `BindCall`/`Literal1Of`; its doc claim that the two cannot name different callees is enforced by no drift test.

Expected (CLAUDE.md rule 5, the shape that makes the next case automatic): one mapping that both the peek and the bind call, and a drift test that binds a CALL with each literal shape and asserts `CalleeSignatureOf` and `BindCall` name the same callee. Sweep the tree for other `Peek*` copies of a binding mapping.
