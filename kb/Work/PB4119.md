# PB4119 — ReferenceResolver.ProbeDeclaration is a second copy of FlagConformancePass.ResolveItem (the silent declaration

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs:161` (`ReferenceResolver.ProbeDeclaration`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/FlagConformancePass.cs:605` (`FlagConformancePass.ResolveItem`).

Review lead R1-2 (low, round 1) of PB4090. `ReferenceResolver.ProbeDeclaration` (ReferenceResolver.cs:170 on the reviewed branch;
161-178 on main) resolves a written reference to its declaration silently (register check, `BaseName`, written qualifiers, `FindItem`).
`FlagConformancePass.ResolveItem` (FlagConformancePass.cs:609 on the branch; 605-618 on main) is the existing silent lookup of the same
thing. The two already differ in one observable way: `ProbeDeclaration` sets `_probing`, `ResolveItem` does not, so a diagnostic path
that reaches one and not the other disagrees.

**Fix:** one silent declaration lookup, both callers ask it (CLAUDE.md rules 4 and 5: one rule in one place); decide which side of the
`_probing` difference is right first (read what `_probing` guards in `ReferenceResolver`), pin it with a test.
