# PB5583 - Make "every older edition chains straight to 2023" the stated design

**Sites:** `lineage.py:76-78`, `inventory_schema.py:195-205`, the C# twin's reading of `linked`,
DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md §10.2 and §10.4.

**Work.**
- rewrite DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md §10.2 ("A missing edition is bridged, provisionally ...") and §10.4
  ("the 1985 to 2002 records after it link 2002");
- delete `Edition.linked`, the schema's `linked` key, `link_errors`' linking clause and the C# twin's reading of it;
- make `next_held` of every older edition the newest held edition;
- make `lineage.spanned` / `chain_bands` prove a band at the chain's own edition only;

from the linked editions.

it is owed. The decision is PB5574, decision 1.
