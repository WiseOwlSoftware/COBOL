# PB4857 - RAISE and RESUME unwind past an enclosing statement's after-statement debugging section: EmitRaise and EmitResume are not marked IBoundTransfersControl and do not land the owed section

**What the report says.** New defect 2: RAISE and RESUME (`EcEmitter.EmitRaise`, `EmitResume`) unwind past an enclosing statement's after-statement section: the same shape as R2-1 for an exception unwind (R2-1 fixed GO TO DEPENDING and EXIT PROGRAM on the branch: the owed sections land on those transfers). The report says it shares root with the DataItem-only lead only by the debug facility.

**Not probed.** The report names no program; the clerk did not reproduce it. `IBoundTransfersControl` marking is the branch's mechanism for a transfer that lands the owed sections.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/EcEmitter.cs` (`EcEmitter.EmitRaise`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/EcEmitter.cs` (`EcEmitter.EmitResume`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundTree.cs` (`IBoundTransfersControl`).

Filed from review leads of waves 20261884 and 20261885 by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

**Landed (wave 20261887 A).** Re-probed (2002 compile with `--permissive`, the one compile that has RAISE and the removed facility): `IF D = 1 RAISE
EXCEPTION EC-USER-RZ` whose declarative executed `RESUME AT M3` ran no section for ALL REFERENCES OF D, the unwind leaving the IF before its
after-statement fire. The root is wider than the two emitters the lead names: EVERY raise site lands a RESUME AT procedure-name through
`DispatchState.ResumeTransfer`, so the owed sections land there (and on `EcEmitter.EmitResume`'s unwinding arm for the RESUME's own enclosing
statements) through the one edge `DispatchState.OwedBeforeTransfer` to `DebugTriggerEmitter.OwedBeforeTransferText`; marking RAISE and RESUME
`IBoundTransfersControl` would have landed them on the arm that continues too, and twice for the global-declarative CONTINUE case. RESUME NEXT
STATEMENT owes none (docs/CONTROL_FLOW_DESIGN.md D20). Witness: `DebugFacilityTests.RaiseAndResume_LandTheSectionsTheAbandonedStatementsOwe`.
