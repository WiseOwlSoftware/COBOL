# PB6002 - a split batch reaches the tree only by a hand copy

**Sites:**
- `scripts/spec/record_verdicts.py#main` (the apply of a move/split batch writes no tracked copy)
- `scripts/spec/rule_moves.py#SPLIT_DIR`
- `scripts/spec/audit_witness_loss.py#recorded_splits`

**What.** PB5452's mint tied the witness-loss audit's WITHDRAWN excuse to a split batch recorded in the same change
(`audit_witness_loss.recorded_splits`, `rule_moves.explains`), and committed the mint's 29-split batch to
`docs/rearchitecture/spec-splits-1985/split-batch.json` by hand. `record_verdicts.py` applies a split batch but never
writes it to `rule_moves.SPLIT_DIR`, so the writer and the audit's reader do not share one step: a future split applied
from a scratch batch leaves its parents WITHDRAWN with no tracked batch changed, and the audit goes RED until someone
copies the file. Loud, not silent; PB5451's 2023 mint will be the next split.

**Fix.** `record_verdicts.py` writes each applied split batch to `SPLIT_DIR.format(year=edition)/<batch>.json` in the
same operation as the apply, with a self-test that an apply leaves the file.

**Done when** an apply of a split batch leaves its tracked copy, the self-test can fail, and the hand-copy step is gone.
