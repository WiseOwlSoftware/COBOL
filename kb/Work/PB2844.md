# PB2844 — OPEN — a constant's LENGTH OF cannot see a subordinate that a TYPE clause supplies

Found by the train 1047 lander's review (step 5b; `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\train1047-report.md`, finding (a)) on cluster 47A (PB1941 group: PB2465's constant length phrase), CONFIRMED by probe on both main and the train build. The lander had no ids left; the land unit reserved this id and the resume unit of 2026-10-08 21:46 PDT filed it.

## Repro
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\t1047\p47a\b.cob`:

```
       01 T TYPEDEF.
          05 M PIC X(5).
       01 V TYPE T.
       01 K CONSTANT AS LENGTH OF M OF V.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           DISPLAY K.
```

Observed on main (`495e8fb6f`) and on the train 1047 build: COBOLNET1547 (constant-entry-rule). Expected: the program
compiles and displays 5.

## Rule
- OK §13.18.57.4 2) a): "the subject of the entry is a group whose subordinate elements have the same names,
  descriptions, and hierarchy as the subordinate elements of type-name-1" — so `M OF V` is a data-name with T's
  description of M, a PIC X(5) elementary item.
- §13.10.3 (constant entry) SR10 and SR12 refuse only an ANY LENGTH operand and a dynamic-length elementary item or
  variable-length group; M is neither.

## Where to look
`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Constants.cs` evaluates the length phrase. PB2465 made it measure the
completed description; the TYPE-expanded subordinates of V apparently do not exist yet (or are not resolvable by
qualification) when the constant is evaluated. Check whether this shares PB1941 arm (a)'s root
(`DataBinder.cs#BindEntry`'s create-item/apply-clauses split), which wave 1048's PB1941 finisher works on.

## Done when
A 2023 golden from §13.18.57.4 2) a) (`LENGTH OF M OF V` and `BYTE-LENGTH OF M OF V` through a TYPE, including a
nested TYPE) and the sibling sweep: every other place a constant or a compile-time length names a TYPE-supplied
subordinate.
