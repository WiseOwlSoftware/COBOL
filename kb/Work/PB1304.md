## Landed 2026-10-04 (wave-1011 implementer B)

**Re-probe (own build of 28fc8a7f9).** `01 T TYPEDEF. 05 A PIC X. 05 B PIC X. 66 AB RENAMES A THRU B. 01 R TYPE T.` drew
COBOLNET1535 `typedef-renames-staged` (plus COBOLNET1639 on `AB OF R`); the level-88 arm of the same rule worked.

**The two-arm dispatch.** §13.18.58.4 GR1 names three things that are "part of the type declaration": subordinate
entries, condition-name entries and RENAMES clauses. The first two were cloned by `ExpandType`/`CloneItem`
(`CloneConditionOnto` for the 88s); the third was a staged refusal. Fixed at the root, by making the third arm the same
shape as the second rather than by teaching `CloneItem` a special case:
- `BindEntries` tracks the template's root; `BindRenames(entry, owner)` hangs a template's level-66 entry on it
  (`DataItem.Renames66`, the place a record's own ride) and keeps it off the by-name index (a template's names are not
  referenceable, GR1);
- `ExpandType` calls the new `CloneRenamesOnto` after the 88s, which gives the TYPE's subject an alias of its own with the
  same operand names (a fresh `RenamesInfo`: the resolved `From`/`Thru`/`Span` are per clone, like `OccursSpec`'s);
- the subject may be a group INSIDE a record (`05 G1 TYPE T`; nested types: `05 P TYPE U` in V), so the alias owner is any
  item, not only a root: the two post-build RENAMES passes (the §13.18.45 resolve+tile pass and the §13.18.57.3 SR3
  strong-type screen) walk `RenamesOwners()` — every item that owns level-66 entries — instead of `Roots`, with storage
  offsets still measured from the record's root. A new alias owner therefore gets every screen by construction;
- the staged descriptor, its emit site and its doc mentions are deleted; COBOLNET1535 keeps one meaning (this also ends
  kb/Work PB608).

**Sibling sweep.** Every `Renames66` reader: `DataBinder.Oo.cs` (per item), `DataBinder.Linkage.cs` InheritGlobalSubtree
(per item), `MoveEmitter` (per item), `CorrespondingBinder` (per group) — all item-level already; the two `Roots` loops
were the only root-only ones and are fixed. `SAME AS` copies a description "as though coded in place" and has no RENAMES
arm to clone; it is outside this rule (GR1 of §13.18.49 is not the TYPEDEF's).

**Witnesses.** `conformance:2002/pb1304_typedef_renames` (two records and two groups of one type each with their own
`AB`, a no-THRU alias, a nested type's alias inside a record and V's own alias spanning it; expected values read off the
stores), negatives `pb1304-typedef-renames-unresolved-operand` (COBOLNET1655 at the group built from the type) and
`pb1304-typedef-strong-renames` (COBOLNET1532, §13.18.57.3 SR3: a STRONG type's own RENAMES). Row GR-13.18.58.4-1 DIVERGES
-> CONFORMS (GAP 497 -> 496).
