# PB5962 - the `All` row is over its budget in some runs after the cross-reference was fixed

**Measured** by listing step 9b (branch `listing-step-9b`, head `7ffd4882ba`), Release, the benchmark README's step-9b table: `All` over
`None` on a full compile, two runs: IX113A +19.2, +19.4 %; NC105A +23.3, +22.2 %; NC218A +21.8, +29.1 %; SM201A +42.6, +32.1 %. The parent's
rows at `0c29eebc42` (kb/Work PB5941) were +16.8 to +23.5 %, so the step-7b-2 review round (which moved the entry lookups of a data item into
the listing's path and records the CALL targets) and the run-to-run spread of the denominator (`None` swings up to 7 MB between runs of
identical code) both show here.

**Thread-level probe** (`GC.GetAllocatedBytesForCurrentThread`, minimum of twelve compiles after ten warm-ups, SM201A, Debug): `None` 29.73 MB;
`All` without the cross-reference +8.74 MB (29 %); the cross-reference +0.25 MB. By section on SM201A over the source-only listing: DataMap
2.11, ObjectMap 2.28, ObjectCode 4.91, Statistics 0.85, and 0.5 to 0.6 MB each for Programs, Diagnostics and Options (a floor per section that
the three formats each pay). A process-wide run adds 4 to 8 MB over the thread-level figure for the same compile, which the probe does not
explain.

**To do, in this order:** (1) find the process-wide excess (an allocation on another thread, or the benchmark's own GC accounting) by
running the same compile under an allocation-sampling profiler with stacks; (2) the per-section floor of 0.5 MB (the three renderers' per-page
set-up: `PageComposer`, the page's `HashSet`s and dictionaries over every line, `ListingSections` lookups); (3) the object code's 4.9 MB:
`ListingInstruction` strings and the text rows. A drift test pins the result as a structural guard, never a wall-clock limit.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Listing/Html/HtmlListingRenderer.cs` (`HtmlListingRenderer.Sections`, `HtmlListingRenderer.SourceWriter`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Listing/Text/PageComposer.cs` (`PageComposer.Compose`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Listing/ObjectCodeMapReader.cs` (whole: the
object code's decoding is read once per body and every instruction of it is made here).

**Budget**: section 9's `All` row, at most 25 % over `None` on a full compile.
