# PB524

## Mechanism — linage-clause
linage-syntax-rules-unimplemented

### Expected, from the standard
SR1: 'Data-name-1, data-name-2, data-name-3, and data-name-4 shall not be subject to any OCCURS clauses.' SR2: 'Data-name-1, data-name-2, data-name-3, and data-name-4 shall reference elementary unsigned numeric integer data items.' SR3: 'Integer-2 shall not be greater than integer-1.' All three are SYNTAX rules, so every one of the eight programs above shall be REJECTED AT COMPILE TIME with a diagnostic naming the rule. SR3 in particular needs no analysis at all: both operands are integer literals and BindLinageClause has them both in hand at DataBinder.cs:1106-1110.

```
$ python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 13.18.34.3 "Data-name-1, data-name-2, data-name-3, and data-name-4 shall reference elementary unsigned numeric integer data items"
  OK  §13.18.34.3 2)  (Syntax rules)
```

### Observed
Not one of the three rules has an implementation. Every one of the eight programs compiles clean; what happens next splits three ways and none of them is a diagnostic. (a) The two OCCURS shapes reach run time and die with an unhandled CobolNet.Runtime.NotImplementedCobolFeatureException out of the OPEN - 'LINAGE operand \'T-LINES\' is not resolvable to storage (ISO 13.18.34 SR2)' - which is a CRASH where a rejection was owed, and cites SR2 for what is SR1's obligation. (b) The group, alphanumeric and non-integer shapes run to completion on a silently reinterpreted value: p19_alphapage.cob reads page size 7 out of a PIC X(3) holding "007" and the AFTER ADVANCING 6 write reports NO-EOP with LINAGE-COUNTER 7; p4_nonint.cob's PIC 9V9 5.5 is rescaled to 5 by LinageOpExpr with CobolRounding.Truncation. (c) The signed and SR3 shapes reach EvaluateLinage and throw System.InvalidOperationException (see linage-ec-is-a-clr-throw). THE SHAPE IS A RULE ASSUMED BY ITS CONSUMER AND ENFORCED BY NOBODY: SequentialIoEmitter.cs:146-148 says in terms 'scale 0 by SR2\'s elementary-unsigned-integer rule, with a defensive rescale' - the emitter relies on SR2 while nothing establishes it, which is why the defensive rescale silently launders an illegal operand instead of failing. The fix belongs in ONE place, a LINAGE operand screen run over LinageInfo.Operands at the point the operands resolve (DataBinder.ResolveFiles:1181-1184), because that is the single site every one of the four operands already passes through - and it must take SR1's 'subject to any OCCURS clauses' to mean the ancestry, not just a direct OCCURS, and reject the subscripted spelling rather than discarding the subscript. SR3 is a separate two-line literal comparison in BindLinageClause. RELATED, NOT DUPLICATE: kb/Work PB318 owns four OPEN syntax rules with the same 'nowhere to live' shape and notes that the version-conformance pass already holds CheckWriteEopLinage for 14.9.51 SR19 - that pass is the obvious host for SR3, and PB318's own note proposes exactly this staging for its LINAGE conjunct.
