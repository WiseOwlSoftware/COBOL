# PB5331 - repair the 2014 transcription's confirmed discrepancies

**Sites:** none (the repairs edit `specs/ISO_COBOL_2014.md` and the reconciliation agent files under docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/2014; no code file changes).

Every page of the 2014 transcription (PB5268, landed) was compared with the PDF: fourteen `spec-reconcile.js` runs on
account 2, #1526 and #1527, merged into `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/LEDGER-2014.json` and
`2014/REPORT.md`. 134 claims, 74 confirmed (13 normative, 57 structural, 4 cosmetic), 60 refuted, each claim with its
own adversarial verdict. The ledger's confirmed entries are this note's work; it holds no second list (rule 8).

The normative ones misstate the standard to anyone citing the 2014 text (hence `misleads_diagnosis`): the OCCURS
Formats 2 and 4 general formats with braces spanning the wrong lines (page 370), the 13.10.1 level-number `1` without
its underline (page 306), Table 18's merged numeric-edited sub-rows (page 559), operand subscripts flattened into
other names (ADD, SUBTRACT 14.9.40.3, intrinsic functions 15.56.3, 15.69.3, 15.81.3, 15.91.3), the 8.8.1.3.1.1 bounds
without their superscripts (page 166), and the reserved-word list splitting `FLOAT-NOT-A-NUMBER-SIGNALING` (page 192).
Page 219 also holds a Greek capital iota for the Latin `I` of "If", which defeats `cite.py --check` on that rule.

**Fix direction:** repair each confirmed entry from its rendered page (`scripts/render-spec-page.py`), then re-run its
batch through the workflow (README "Re-running a batch") so the ledger records the repair; the 2002 repairs (PB4813)
are the same patterns and share the method.
