# PB4640 - lead from a landed branch's report

Repro `DIVIDE FUNCTION ABS (A * A * A + 7) BY 1000 GIVING R REMAINDER R2` (probe `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261865a/rem2.cob`, as reported, not re-run): the quotient passes 34 digits (a size error at R with the remainder still stored, DOC-A.1-70), the subsidiary quotient is an SDIDI of 34 digits, so the remainder is the dividend. Needs the wide form (a redesign). Unchanged from PB4599's round 0; PB4599 landed the wide QuotientDec but its note records this as not fixed.

**Landed (wave 20261868, group B).** Re-probed on the build (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261868b/rem2.cob`, REMD): `DIVIDE FUNCTION ABS (A * A * A + 7) BY 1000 GIVING R REMAINDER R2` stored R2 = 99999999994 where the remainder is 6 (a 42-digit quotient, a size error at `R PIC 9(18)`, the remainder still stored, DOC-A.1-70). Cause: the carried arm took the subsidiary quotient from `FinalQuotient`, which is held to the SDIDI's 34 digits (`CobolExact.DivToOdd`), so truncating it at identifier-3's scale kept 34 digits of a 42-digit quotient and `dividend - subsidiary * divisor` was the dividend less a quotient eight digits short. ISO 14.9.12.4 GR7: "When native arithmetic is in effect, the remainder is the result of multiplying the subsidiary quotient and the divisor and subtracting the product from the dividend", GR6 c) derives the subsidiary quotient by truncation of digits at the least significant end; native arithmetic is the implementor's (8.8.1.3) and CLAUDE.md rule 1 sends the latitude to GnuCOBOL, whose `cob_div_quotient` / `cob_div_remainder` work on the exact decimal.

Fix: no redesign of the SDIDI was needed, only the exact wide form that PB4599 landed. `CobolExact.SubsidiaryQuotient` (new) is the exact quotient truncated toward zero at identifier-3's scale, every integer digit, through the same `CobolExact.TruncatedQuotient` the nested quotient uses (`DivTruncated` now calls it; exact on `CobolWide` while the dividend's alignment fits 256 bits, the 34-digit round-to-odd image past it, DOC-A.1-123); `NumericRenderer.ExactSubsidiaryQuotient` renders it and `ArithmeticEmitter.CarriedSubsidiaryQuotient` takes it for a fixed-point identifier-3 when `TakesExactSubsidiary` (a native sender on the wide or run-time-scaled exact form, no binary64). Its value stays an exact value (`NumX.Exact`), so GR7's back-multiply and subtraction keep every digit. The standard-decimal lane (ISO GR1 names the SDIDI expression) and an SDIDI-only native pair (DOC-A.1-82) keep the 34-digit SDIDI subsidiary quotient: `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261868b/sib.cob` (`DIVIDE 1.0E40 BY 3 GIVING R REMAINDER R2`) is that lane by determination, unchanged. After the fix REMD stores R2 = 6; the siblings in `sib.cob` (a scaled receiver, a fractional divisor, DIVIDE INTO, ROUNDED, a negative divisor, a negative dividend) agree with Python integer arithmetic.

**Not fixed, a lead:** the STORED quotient of a size error (checking off, no phrase) is the 34-digit image's low-order digits: `R` of REMD is 000003000100000000 where GnuCOBOL's exact decimal keeps 000003000000000000. It is the final transfer's quotient (`CobolExact.DivToOdd`), the same in `COMPUTE R = FUNCTION ABS (A * A * A + 7) / 1000` and DIVIDE GIVING without REMAINDER, and is item 8 latitude of a value the receiver cannot hold; making it exact means landing the final transfer from the exact wide quotient and its remainder rather than the SDIDI.
