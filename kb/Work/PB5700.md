# PB5700 - at --std 2002 the COPY and REPLACE text-word and operand screens are 2023's: a 323-character text-word is accepted (2002 caps it at 322), and a COPY REPLACING identifier operand with a subscript expression such as T(I * 2) is accepted

**Sites:**
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#MaxTextWordLength
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ScreenOperandPair

**Mechanism.** CopyProcessor holds one MaxTextWordLength = 65_535 (§2023:7.2.3.3 SR9 / 7.2.4.3 SR9) applied at every edition (library text at line ~742, pseudo-text at ~1408); 2002's limit is 322 in both statements. The text-1/text-2 identifier-format screen of §2002:7.1.2.2 SR9 (a subscript is only a literal, an identifier, or an identifier plus or minus an integer) has no 2002 arm in ScreenOperandPair: at 2023 the non-pseudo-text operand is removed outright, so no screen of its shape exists.

**Inventory.** Rows claimed: `2002-SR-7.1.2.2-11`, `2002-SR-7.1.2.2-9`, `2002-SR-7.1.3.2-9`.

**Rules (each citation checked with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `§2002:7.1.2.2 SR11`: "The length of a text-word within pseudo-text and within library text shall be from 1 through 322 character positions" (`cite.py --check`: OK  §2002:7.1.2.2 SR11  (Syntax rules)  the text is in SR11)
- `§2002:7.1.3.2 SR9`: "The length of a text-word within pseudo-text shall be from 1 through 322 characters" (`cite.py --check`: OK  §2002:7.1.3.2 SR9  (Syntax rules)  the text is in SR9)
