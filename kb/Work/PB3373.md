# PB3373 — a literal after the computer-name misattributes the computer-name

Filed 2026-10-18 from wave 1119 group A's report (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1119a-PB3370-report.md`, leads) and the
"Not closed here" paragraph of `kb/Work/PB3370.md` (landed). Pre-grep: only PB3370 mentions it.

## What happens

`SOURCE-COMPUTER. XYZ "LIT".` and `OBJECT-COMPUTER. XYZ "LIT".`: `genericClause` opens only at an IDENTIFIER, so the
name-less reading is the only viable parse and XYZ is reported as a configuration paragraph or as an OBJECT-COMPUTER
clause, with a false COBOLNET0900 or COBOLNET3734 at 85. Harm: a misattributed diagnostic (the source is refused).
Whether the form is legal COBOL is not judged here; the implementer reads §12.3.5 and §12.3.6 before any fix.

## Repro

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. P09.
       ENVIRONMENT DIVISION.
       CONFIGURATION SECTION.
       SOURCE-COMPUTER. XYZ "LIT".
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           STOP RUN.
```
