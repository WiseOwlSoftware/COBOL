# PB5944 - orphaned XML doc comments: two summaries on one member

**Found** by review round 1 of listing step 6b slice 4 (PB5932, finding R1-2): the branch inserted
`IntrinsicBinder.KeywordWordContext` between `KeywordWordOf`'s doc comment and `KeywordWordOf`, so the long ">>COBOL-WORDS funnel"
summary documented the new member and the funnel had none. The C# compiler accepts two `<summary>` elements on one member
without a warning, so nothing caught it.

**The sweep** (regular expression `</summary>\r?\n[ \t]*///[ \t]*<summary>` over `src/**/*.cs`, 2026-11-19, branch
`listing-step-6b-4`): 46 sites before the round, the finding's own included. The round fixed the 10 in files the branch already
had open (rule 4):
`BinderDriver.cs` (BindUnitData's doc moved to it), `DataBinder.cs` (a stale summary of a check folded into ScreenValueLiteral,
deleted), `ConditionBinder.cs` (IsFormat2FloatSign's), `IntrinsicBinder.cs` (KeywordWordOf's, IsStringOperand's,
BindByteLengthFold's, BindAlgebraicFold's, HasRuntimeLength's, and IsVariableLengthGroup's two summaries merged),
`SpecialNamesBinder.Locales.cs` (OrderTableBind's, moved to SpecialNamesBinder.cs). **36 remain, in 33 files**: under
`Binding/` FileControlKeyRules, PictureAnalyzer, RecordClauseRules, SubscriptBinder, Bound/FunctionValueLane, Model/DataItem,
Model/PicInfo, Model/RecordLayout, Procedure/Verbs/EcBinder, InitializeBinder, MoveBinder (2); under `CodeGen/` EcEmitter,
StatementEmitter (2), DataDivision/GroupImageCodec, InitialStateBackground, PhysicalModel, ValueInitializer,
Emit/IntrinsicRenderer, NumericRenderer, OperandText, Roslyn/PlaceRenderer, RuntimeApi; under `Validation/` IntegerOperandPass,
VersionConformancePass; in `WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend` Common/CobolLiteral (2), Expressions/CompileTimeExpressionEvaluator,
Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase; in `WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime` Control/CallAbi, Intrinsics/CobolDate, CobolIntrinsics,
CobolIntrinsics.Exact, CobolLocale, Values/Text/CobolBits.

**The fix**: each orphan moves to the member it describes, merges into the member's own summary as a `<para>`, or is deleted when
its member is gone; then a RepoDrift test refuses two `<summary>` elements on one member (the shape that makes the next case
automatic), so a member inserted between a doc comment and its member fails the gate rather than a review.
