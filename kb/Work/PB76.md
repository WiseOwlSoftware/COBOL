# PB76 — OPEN — `COMPUTE R9 ROUNDED = 10 ** -20` under STANDARD-DECIMAL stores 0.000000001

> ⚙ **flags:** wrong-answer, silent · found 2026-08-17 while writing PB60's NUMVAL-F Dec range-check
> tests (`NUMVAL-F("1E-50")` ROUNDED into `S9(9)V9(9)` also stores 0.000000001).

**Measured repro** (`--std 2023`, `OPTIONS. ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD-DECIMAL.`, `01 R9 PIC S9(9)V9(9).`):
`COMPUTE R9 ROUNDED = 10 ** -50` → `0.000000001`; `10 ** -40` → `0.000000001`; `10 ** -20` →
`0.000000001`; `FUNCTION NUMVAL-F("1E-50")` → `0.000000001`. Control: `NUMVAL-F("4E-10")` ROUNDED →
`0.000000000` (correct), `"5E-10"` → `0.000000001` (correct tie, away from zero).

**The charge:** §14.7.4 ROUNDED — the default NEAREST-AWAY-FROM-ZERO (§14.7.4.3 r3 / §11.9.6) rounds to
the nearest representable value, ties away from zero; 10⁻²⁰ at nine fraction digits is nowhere near a
tie and its nearest value is 0. §8.8.1.5.2 keeps 10⁻²⁰ well inside the SDIDI (the intermediate is right);
the defect is the FINAL TRANSFER's rounding decision.

**Mechanism:** `CobolDec.DivRemPow10(v, n)` returns the marker `(0, v == 0 ? 0 : 1, 2)` when `n > 38`
("far below precision: quotient 0, inexact marker") — a remainder of 1 over a denominator of 2 is EXACTLY
HALF, so `RoundFromRemainder`'s NearestAwayFromZero arm (`absRem2 >= den`) and NearestEven's tie arm both
lift the quotient. It is reached whenever the SDIDI's exponent sits more than 38 places below the target
scale — which the 34-digit significand makes common: `1 / 10²⁰` is `10³³ × 10⁻⁵³`, 44 places below scale
9. The marker's sign is also wrong for a negative value (`rem = +1` ⇒ AwayFromZero / TowardGreater round
a negative toward +∞). Both `ToUnscaled` (every SDIDI store, MOVE and COMPUTE) and `Clamp`'s subnormal
re-round ride it. **Fix:** the marker must be a below-half inexact remainder with the value's sign —
`(0, sign(v), 4)` — so NEAREST modes fall to 0, AWAY-FROM-ZERO / TOWARD-* keep their one-unit step in the
right direction, TRUNCATION keeps 0, PROHIBITED still throws. Golden: the four ROUNDED legs above plus a
negative-value AWAY-FROM-ZERO leg.
