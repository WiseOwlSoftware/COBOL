# PB4300 - Hint 0b emits COBOL2073 ("WHEN OTHER at most once, after every WHEN") at a program's only WHEN OTHER when the preceding statement lacks a required operand; a true repeat's diagnostic depends on line layout

Found by the Mythos front-end review, gap pass, 2026-10-31 (GAP-REVIEW.md, G4-2; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/KaxkLe85tfZBZ3nZ1tDGTs). Pin d7daee1cf.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (hint 0b :735-770, `EndsAConstructEarly` :150-154); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Diagnostics/DiagnosticDescriptors.cs:84` (COBOL2073); `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/ParseRecoveryHintTruthTests.cs` (no 2073 row).

**Finding.** Hint 0b emits COBOL2073 "A WHEN OTHER phrase may be specified at most once, and only after every WHEN phrase" at the program's ONLY WHEN OTHER when the statement before it lacks a required operand (`i14` `DISPLAY WHEN OTHER`, `S10` `MOVE 1 TO WHEN OTHER`, W5): false of the program; DISPLAY's brace is required (rendered p640, section 14.9.11.2). The missing operand is named by no arm; before a plain WHEN it is a bare COBOL0001 (i16, W7). The first report's second half was wrong: a TRUE repeat draws the correct COBOL2073 when it starts a new line (W1, the PB396 golden) and a true-but-less-specific COBOL2269 when both phrases share a line (S11, W2, W3) - a layout dependence worth its own truth row. EndsAConstructEarly :150-154 lists COBOL2073, so the false hint also engages the tail discard. Not PB4133 (missing selection object).

**Fix direction.** Decide 0b from whether the open EVALUATE/PERFORM context already holds a whenOther child. Truth rows i14, S10, W1, W2 in ParseRecoveryHintTruthTests.

**Citations checked** (`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`): section 14.9.13.2 "imperative-statement-2 ]"; section 14.9.11.2 "mnemonic-name-1 ] [ WITH".

## Landed (wave 20261822 A)

Re-probed on 83a1e47f4: i14 and S10 drew COBOL2073 at the program's only WHEN OTHER. The "layout dependence" of S11/W2
(COBOL2269 where the repeat shares a line) is the probes', not the compiler's: those lines run past column 72 of fixed
form, where `WHEN OTHER` is cut to `WHEN OTH`; in free form the same-line repeat draws COBOL2073
(`ParseRecoveryCascadeTests.ARepeatedOrLeadingWhenOther_IsCOBOL2073`).

**Fix.** Hint 0b decides from the construct around the parser (`WhenOtherPlacement`, `WhenOtherRules` read from the
ATN): COBOL2073 only where the construct already holds a WHEN OTHER or holds no WHEN group before it; in place after an
incomplete statement it says nothing (i14 draws COBOL0001 at the WHEN), and where the parse died at the OTHER it names
the WHEN group's missing statement (COBOL2072). A prediction that failed ahead through an EVALUATE or PERFORM no
context holds yet leaves the placement unread (`PhrasePlacement.Unread`, `WhenOtherOwnerOpeners`).
