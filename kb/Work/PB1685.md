# PB1685 — OPEN

Filed by the train-70 lander from wave 70 V3's report (implementer of PB1509; `w70v3-PB1509-report.md`, lead 1).

## Finding

**Repro.** `E:\Temp\claude\E--COBOL\73cac64c-391d-42c0-bcdf-c880e3e10305\scratchpad\w70v3\p\fsumq.cob`: a report
entry `PIC 99.99 SUM WS-F`, with WS-F = 2.75 at two GENERATEs, prints `04.00`; the counter owes `05.50`.

**Rule** (`cite.py --check` OK). §13.18.54.4 1): "Each entry containing a SUM clause establishes an independent sum
counter and size error indicator. … The number of decimal …" — the counter's digits, integral and fractional, are
the entry PICTURE's, "excluding insertion editing characters".

**Code site.** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs:2499` — `Scale = pic?.Scale ?? 0` reads the
scale of an EDITED PicInfo, which carries no fixed-point scale, so the counter is registered at scale 0 and each
addend is aligned (truncated) to an integer.

**Mechanism.** GR1's digit derivation (`DataBinder.SumCounterDigits`, PB1296) counts digit positions but the SCALE
half was never derived from the edited picture's decimal-point position. One derivation of the counter profile for
numeric and numeric-edited entries closes it; the golden needs a numeric-edited SUM entry with a fraction, at the
NATIVE and STANDARD-DECIMAL arms.
