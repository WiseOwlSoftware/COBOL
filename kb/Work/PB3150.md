# PB3150 — the wave planner could not drain

> flags: process-only · silent

Source: wave 1075's unit (2026-10-14, the owner's drain of 2026-10-13 21:35 PDT: "do not schedule new work items but
continue all necessary work to get in process work to main"). The last handoff asked for "exactly the 2 review
finishers (plan_wave.py --max-groups 2): e99-4 R4-1 and license-product-name R1-1". The planner gave:

- **A** = the finisher of `w1074a-PB546-report.md` (SPLIT on `worktree-wf_619cbe77-e99-1`, notes PB546, PB3030,
  PB3101). Its finisher would have STARTED PB3101, which the report says was never started (new work, which the drain
  forbids), and cherry-picked e99-1's 18 commits onto main although `worktree-wf_619cbe77-e99-4` (A2 of the same wave)
  had merged e99-1 and carries them, so they would have landed twice.
- **A2 after A** = the e99-4 review finisher: the one group the drain wanted, held behind A.
- **license-product-name** listed only as "UNLANDED branches naming no open note (judgment)": its lead PB3146 is
  `process_only` (fix_clusters.py skips it) and existed only on its branch, so `newest` never saw its FINDINGS report.
  MANDATORY-PRACTICES O10 and kb/Work PB3027 promise that the next wave plans every FINDINGS branch's finisher; for such
  a branch nobody did, and it sat as a DECISION row.

## Fix (scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py)

1. `branch_carrier`: branch → the branch of a DONE report, not on main, that contains it (`git merge-base
   --is-ancestor`). A carried SPLIT's finisher merges the carrier instead of cherry-picking (every mode); a carried
   FINDINGS branch whose lead the fix lane cannot see gets no finisher of its own.
2. `branch_lead_notes`: a FINDINGS report's lead note that main lacks is read from the report's own branch; such a
   report gets its finisher from the newest report of its branch, in every mode.
3. `--drain`: only the finishers of STARTED work. A review-FINDINGS finisher; a SPLIT/NOT STARTED finisher only while
   its branch holds unlanded work no carrier holds; an unlanded branch's finisher on the same terms. Never a cluster,
   never absorption, never a half note's open half. Every finisher's pred says to finish only what its branch started.
   The waiting notes are listed with the reason, and the clusters' notes are counted.

Wave 1075's drain plan with the fix: A = e99-4's review finisher (PB2921, PB2988), B = license-product-name's (PB3146).
PB546, PB3030 and PB3101 wait ("contained in worktree-wf_619cbe77-e99-4"), and 8 open notes wait for the drain to lift.
