**Found by** the PB2182/PB2361 landing (PR #1101), report w20261856a, which left it unfixed as outside its files.

**The defect.** Since PB2361 the emitter reads binding facts from `DataBindResult` (`ctx.Data`), but a comment still names the binder.

**Verified at origin/main 047025e55** (the report's line numbers had drifted, and two of its three siblings are not stale):
- STALE: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs#LeapSecondFlag` (summary, line 1819) says `DataBinder.LeapSecond`; the property it reads is `DataBindResult.LeapSecond` (`Binding/Model/DataBindResult.cs:61`). `DataBinder.LeapSecond` (`DataBinder.cs:85`) remains only as the binder's init input.
- NOT stale (the named member still lives on `DataBinder`): `CodeGen/DataDivision/GroupImageCodec.cs` lines 160 and 234 (`DataBinder.AssignClassOffsets`, `DataBinder.cs:5527`); `CodeGen/DataDivision/PhysicalModel.cs` line 69 (`DataBinder.ByteWindowResidueOf`, `DataBinder.cs:5592`).
- Sweep of `grep -rn "DataBinder\." src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen` found, all naming members that still exist: `CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs` line 906 (`BindDeclarations`), `CodeGen/ProgramEmitter.cs` line 660 and `CodeGen/Verbs/OoEmitter.cs` line 1302 (`CellCanCarry`, `DataBinder.Ptr.cs:198`), `CodeGen/Verbs/OoEmitter.cs` lines 52 and 57 (`OoDataBinder.BindMethodData`).

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs` (`IntrinsicRenderer.LeapSecondFlag`)

**Fix.** Change `IntrinsicRenderer.cs#LeapSecondFlag`'s comment to `DataBindResult.LeapSecond`. Re-run the sweep when a fact moves from `DataBinder` onto `DataBindResult`.
