## Finding — replace-statement-p1 (rejects-legal-source)

The REPLACE pass models only one flat active replacement set: ApplyReplaceStatements recognizes REPLACE OFF and replacing pairs but never the ALSO or LAST keywords, and has no LIFO queue of inactive REPLACE statements, so both stacking forms are rejected as non-pseudo-text operands (COBOLNET1641).

**Rows:** FMT-7.2.4.2

**Repro:**

```
PROCEDURE DIVISION. / REPLACE ==XX1== BY ==AAA==. / DISPLAY XX1. / REPLACE ALSO ==XX2== BY ==CCC==. / DISPLAY XX1 " " XX2. / REPLACE LAST OFF. / DISPLAY XX1. / REPLACE OFF. (probe also.cob); also 'REPLACE LAST OFF.' with nothing active (also2.cob)
```

**Expected from the spec:** Compiles; prints AAA / AAA CCC / AAA. 7.2.4.4 7) a): the active statement is pushed and the ALSO statement is expanded to its own operands followed by the pushed one's; 7) c): REPLACE LAST OFF cancels the active one and pops the prior one; 6) b): a format 2 REPLACE with nothing active has no effect.

**Observed:** COBOLNET1641 'a REPLACE statement operand is not pseudo-text' at the ALSO line and at the LAST OFF line; the lone REPLACE LAST OFF also draws COBOLNET1641. activeReplacements is Clear()ed at every REPLACE.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ApplyReplaceStatements (lines 133-154); #ParseReplacements

## Finding — replace-statement-p2 (rejects-legal-source)

replace-also-last-state-machine: ApplyReplaceStatements models a single active operand list (Clear on every REPLACE) with no inactive LIFO queue, and ParseReplacements has no ALSO/LAST arm, so both phrases are read as operands and rejected

**Rows:** GR-7.2.4.4-4, GR-7.2.4.4-5, GR-7.2.4.4-6, GR-7.2.4.4-7

**Repro:**

```
PROCEDURE DIVISION. REPLACE ==XX1== BY ==AA==. DISPLAY XX1 / REPLACE ALSO ==XX2== BY ==BB==. DISPLAY XX1 XX2 / REPLACE LAST OFF. DISPLAY XX1 / REPLACE OFF. (AA, BB PIC X(2) VALUE 'AA','BB'); also REPLACE LAST OFF. and REPLACE ALSO ==..== BY ==..==. with nothing active
```

**Expected from the spec:** Output 'AA' / 'AABB' / 'AA'. §7.2.4.4 GR7 a) ALSO pushes the active statement and activates current operands followed by the pushed ones; GR7 c) LAST OFF pops it back; GR6 a) ALSO with nothing active has no effect, GR6 b) LAST OFF with nothing active is a no-op.

**Observed:** Pinned cobol.exe: COBOLNET1641 'a REPLACE statement operand is not pseudo-text' at each ALSO and LAST statement (also.cob lines 12,14; alsonone.cob lines 7,8); no program is produced. The GR4 inactive state is unrepresentable.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ApplyReplaceStatements (lines 116-160); #ParseReplacements (671-700)

## What the fixer owes (and the sibling sweep)

Model the REPLACE states explicitly (active statement + LIFO of inactive statements; ALSO pushes and expands operands current-first; format-1 without ALSO cancels all; LAST pops; OFF cancels all) in `ApplyReplaceStatements`; parse ALSO/LAST in the REPLACE header (edition-gated at the introducing edition per constructs.json). Goldens: ALSO stack, LAST pop, ALSO with nothing active (no effect), OFF clearing the queue.
