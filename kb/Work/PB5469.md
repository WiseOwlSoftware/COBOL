# PB5469 - COPY REPLACING refuses an identifier-1 or identifier-2 that is both subscripted and reference-modified, or a function-identifier (COBOL2449): ReadOperand reads a word, its qualifiers and ONE parenthesized group

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs` (`CopyProcessor.ReadOperand`, `CopyProcessor.ParseReplacingOperands`)

**Mechanism.** `CopyProcessor.ReadOperand` turns a non-pseudo-text REPLACING operand into the text words it spans: the word, its OF/IN qualifiers and at most one parenthesized group. A 1985 identifier may carry qualifiers, subscripts AND a reference modifier, and Amendment 1 makes a function-identifier an identifier, so `T (2) (1:1)` and `FUNCTION UPPER-CASE ("ab")` stop at the second group or at the argument list, and `ParseReplacingOperands` then finds no BY and refuses the statement with COBOL2449 before any matching. The same operands written as ==pseudo-text== are matched and replaced correctly, so only the operand reader is short.

**Found by.** The 1985 code read, read 4 (`kb/Work/PB4996/2026-11-13-read-4.md`): the readers of `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/out-read4/rd-01.json` (1985-FMT-XII.2.2) and `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/out-read4/rd-06.json` (1985-GR-XII.2.4-3), and the refuter of `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-85/out-read4/rd-06-refute.json`, which OVERTURNED the read's CONFORMS of 1985-GR-XII.2.4-4 on the same operands.
