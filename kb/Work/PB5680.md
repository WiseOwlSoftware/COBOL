# PB5680 - the 1985 read sample (PB5679 step 1a)

**Sites:** none (a read of inventory rows; it adds read records under `tests/version-matrix/` and changes no code file).

**Blocked by PB5675** (on branch `worktree-agent-a06f7c99d65e2658f`, not yet on main, so `blocked_by` cannot name it
until it lands): the sample's population is the rows PB5675 leaves closed.

**Why first.** L6 (revised) has every stored 1985 KEPT record read in 1985's own terms and confirmed or re-recorded. Before paying for all
1,373, measure how often a record the inventory now counts as covered is wrong. The 2002 samples found 16 of 100 and 13
of 100 (plus 3 doubtful); PB5596 found 1985 KEPT records whose 2023 targets lost the communication section.

**Population.** The 570 KEPT records PB5675's measurement witnesses at 85 for every target
(`python scripts/spec/lineage_reads.py own-edition --edition 1985`, 2026-11-15: 570 every target, 232 some, 571 none).
After PB5675 lands these are the 1985 rows that stay closed, so their error rate is the rate of rows wrongly closed.

**Work.** A blind random sample of 100 (seed recorded), read whole by the pipeline of section 10.6 aimed at 1985: the
reader states what the 1985 rule governs in the 1985 text (its section, its module and level, its box), what the 2023
targets show of the code, and the evidence at `--std 85`; a refuter attacks every "covered". The read records land as
`tests/version-matrix/lineage-reads-1985-2023/` slices through `audit_batch.py import-reads` (the pair parameter is
PB5683's; until then the edition form `--edition 1985`; this step writes no change record, so the pairing check from the stored record's targets, PB5683's, is not needed here), and a labelled fixture beside the 2002 one.

**Done when.** The sample's rate with its 95 % interval is in this note's landing paragraph, each wrong row is a work
item (`defect` or `needs-own-witness`), and PB5683's estimate is re-measured from it.

**Rows.** None claimed: the reads decide no record here.
