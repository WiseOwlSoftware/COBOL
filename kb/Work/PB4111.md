# PB4111 — VARIANCE, STANDARD-DEVIATION and PRESENT-VALUE lose the value to 34-digit intermediates under native arithmetic

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs#FloatBody` (the `ExactListBodies` arm),
`#RenderDec` (the `Variance`, `StandardDeviation` and `PresentValue` arms, which build their argument list with `DecArgList`),
`#RenderWide` (no arm for them), `#RawArg` (remark), `#ExactListBodies` (remark),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Dec.cs#VarianceDec` / `#StdDevDec` / `#PresentValueDec`,
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Float.cs#Variance` / `#StandardDeviation` / `#PresentValue`,
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Wide.cs` (the new body), `docs/INTRINSICS_DESIGN.md`,
`docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (DOC-A.1-123, DOC-A.1-92b).

**Observed.** PB4075 gave MEAN and the binary64 family the exact wide value of an argument expression of 39 to 77 digits
(`RawWideArg`). These three functions were left on the same intake root PB4075 fixed: `DecArgList` hands each argument to the
body as the 34-digit round-to-odd image. With `P = BIG * BIG * K3` (BIG `9(18)` all nines, K3 `999`),
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261766a/p3.cob` shows `FUNCTION VARIANCE (P, P - 1)` = `00000000010000000000` where the exact value is
`0.25`, and `FUNCTION PRESENT-VALUE (0, P, 1 - P)` = `0` where the exact value is `1`: the low digits that cancel are the
digits the image dropped. Correction to the first filing: that VARIANCE image is 10^10 (SD 10^5), not 10^-10. Both images are
9989999999999999980020000000000001E5; their 35-digit sum rounds to an even ...004E6, so the mean misses each image by 10^5 and
the variance is (10^5)^2 = 10^10.

**Determination (adjudicator, wave 20261857).** Under STANDARD-DECIMAL the 34-digit evaluation is the value: §15.4.1 1) makes the
returned value equal the equivalent arithmetic expression's, every argument is an SDIDI (§8.8.1.5.2 1) and so is the FUNCTION MEAN
inside §15.98.4. VARIANCE (P, P - 1) is 0 there, and the compiler already answers it. Under native arithmetic §8.8.1.3 and §15.4.1
leave "an implementor-defined approximation of the value of that expression", so for that latitude CLAUDE.md rule 1 makes
GnuCOBOL the answer. GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 evaluates the three functions from the exact argument values, with a mean carried past 38
places, and answers 0.25, 0.5 and 1. The native value is therefore the exact value of the expression (exact arguments, exact
mean, exact discount quotients), rounded once into the returned-value carrier. STANDARD-DEVIATION is SQRT of that VARIANCE
(§15.86.4 1)). The 34-digit mean is no approximation of it: a mean off by δ adds exactly δ², and round34(P - 0.5) makes
VARIANCE (P, P - 1) 997002.5. The observed value was misread when the note was filed: `00000000010000000000` is 10^10, not
10^-10.

**The defect is wider than the wide lane.** The same three bodies lose the value at three intakes: a 39-77-digit argument
(`RawArg`'s image), a 35-38-digit Int128 argument (`DecOperand` rounds it: VARIANCE (BIG * BIG, BIG * BIG - 1) = 0.5), and an
exactly lifted argument whose 34-digit mean or term quotient rounds (VARIANCE (A * 1000, A * 1000 + 1) with A = 10^30 is 0.5 for
0.25, PRESENT-VALUE (3, A * 1000 + 1, -4 * A * 1000) is 0.3 for 0.25, and VARIANCE (A, B, B) of `9(31)` items 10^30, 10^30 + 1,
10^30 + 1 is 0.2222223333 for 2/9). Fix: one exact native body per function. VARIANCE is (n·ΣX² - (ΣX)²) / (n²·10^(2s)) over the
aligned integers, on `BigInteger`, divided once as `CobolWide.QuotientDec` divides. PRESENT-VALUE is one numerator over
(1 + rate)^n. All native routes reach it: `FloatBody`'s `ExactListBodies` arm, `RenderDec`'s three arms when not standard-decimal,
and a new `RenderWide` arm, each taking `RawWideArg`, never `DecArgList`. `VarianceDec` / `StdDevDec` / `PresentValueDec` stay as
the STANDARD-DECIMAL bodies. Docs: INTRINSICS_DESIGN (the PB1565 variadic paragraph and "What still takes the image"),
CONFORMANCE DOC-A.1-123 and DOC-A.1-92b. Golden legs and the standard-decimal twin are in
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-m4/a/determination.md` section 3 (pin the W, I and T legs; pin D only after the returned-value carrier
is settled, PB4570).

**Siblings folded into this note.**
- S1 (in this fix): a 35-38-digit argument inside the Int128 window is lifted with rounding by `DecOperand`. VARIANCE (BIG * BIG,
  BIG * BIG - 1) shows 0.5 for 0.25 and PRESENT-VALUE (0, BIG * BIG, 1 - BIG * BIG) shows 0 for 1 (probe `pa.cob`). Same
  intake, same three bodies.
- S2 (in this fix): the 34-digit mean and the 34-digit term quotients inside `VarianceDec` / `PresentValueDec` under native
  arithmetic, with arguments lifted exactly: 0.5 for 0.25 and 0.3 for 0.25 (`pc.cob`), 0.2222223333 for 2/9 and 0.1111 for 1/9
  (`pa.cob`). Wrong in the 7th digit for ordinary 31-digit `PIC` items, not only for wide arguments.
- Filed separately: PB4568 (a wide argument beside an SDIDI or binary64 argument enters as its image), PB4569 (native `2 ** 130`
  keeps 16 significant digits), PB4570 (MEAN's 34-digit value and the binary64 carrier against GnuCOBOL's exact decimal).
- Not siblings: ANNUITY (no cancellation between arguments), EXP / EXP10 / SQRT (documented condition numbers), MEAN of a wide
  list with no SDIDI beside it (already exact-sum, PB4075).
