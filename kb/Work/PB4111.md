# PB4111 — VARIANCE, STANDARD-DEVIATION and PRESENT-VALUE take a wide argument as its 34-digit image

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs#RenderDec` (the `Variance`, `StandardDeviation` and
`PresentValue` arms, which build their argument list with `DecArgList`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Dec.cs#VarianceDec`.

**Observed.** PB4075 gave MEAN and the binary64 family the exact wide value of an argument expression of 39 to 77 digits
(`RawWideArg`). These three functions were left on the same intake root PB4075 fixed: `DecArgList` hands each argument to the
body as the 34-digit round-to-odd image. With `P = BIG * BIG * K3` (BIG `9(18)` all nines, K3 `999`),
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261766a/p3.cob` shows `FUNCTION VARIANCE (P, P - 1)` = `0.0000000001` where the exact value is
`0.25`, and `FUNCTION PRESENT-VALUE (0, P, 1 - P)` = `0` where the exact value is `1`: the low digits that cancel are the
digits the image dropped.

**Question owed (adjudicator role).** What is the function owed? The mean inside VARIANCE is itself a 34-digit SDIDI value
(§15.98.4 and FUNCTION MEAN's resultant), so a variance computed from an exact wide argument list still passes through a
34-digit intermediate unless the equivalent arithmetic expression is evaluated wide end to end. Decide whether §15.98.4 r1,
§15.86.4 r1 and §15.74.4 r1 (each citation to be run through `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check` by the adjudicator) with
§8.8.1.3 and §15.4.1 license the 34-digit mean, or require the exact value, and which of GnuCOBOL's answers governs the latitude
(CLAUDE.md rule 1). The fix follows the determination: the three arms take the wide form at the intake they already have, as
MEAN does (`MeanWide`).
