# PB4481 - two A.3 non-support codes bypass the declined-annex posture

**Where.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/OptionsBinder.cs` (`ArithmeticOf`, `edition?.Error(DiagnosticCatalog.ArithmeticStandardBinaryUnsupported, ...)`, COBOL0806) and
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs` ~1254-1266 (`edition.Error(DiagnosticCatalog.FloatUsageUnsupported, ...)`, COBOL1564). Both descriptors in
`DiagnosticCatalog.cs` are plain `EditionSeverity.Error` with no `Annex: DeclinedAnnex.A3` descriptor argument, while the sibling A.3 elements go through
`edition.Declined(...)` (for example `FloatDecimalClauseUnsupported`, `OptionsBinder.cs` ~195) and a `DeclinedAnnex.A3` descriptor, the permissive-inert posture PB709 designed.

**Effect.** Outside `EditionContext.Refuses`, `--permissive` and the declined-element reporting cannot see these two; the two refusals are the only A.3 non-support
that stay hard errors by a different route. The source stays refused either way (A.3 items are processor-dependent; the compiler claims no support).

**Fix direction.** Give both descriptors `Annex: DeclinedAnnex.A3` and route the call sites through the one declined mechanism, so the posture is one rule; check the
docs/CONFORMANCE.md rows for items 2, 17 and 19 say the same; a drift test that every non-support descriptor naming "Annex A.3" in its citation carries the annex marker
makes the next case automatic.
