**The context.** Every intrinsic function argument, at every position, renders in one context,
`NumericRenderer.ArgumentReceiver(integerPosition)`.
- It is the statement's receiver without its floating-point facts, at the receiver's scale.
- `IntrinsicRenderer.ReceiverFor` applies it at the argument's position; `NumericArgument` applies it to an operand read without its index.
- `IntrinsicArgumentIntakeContractDriftTests.EveryArgument_RendersInTheArgumentReceiver` holds every `num.AsNum(` of the renderer to it.
- An all-fixed-point argument is therefore never taken onto binary64 by the receiver.
- DOC-A.1-92b is unchanged: a floating-point argument still takes binary64.

**Exactness where exact, significance where not** (the coordinator's ruling on review round 1).
- When the receiver is floating-point, an argument at a type 10 (numeric) position keeps significance. "Floating-point" is the one
  predicate `ReceiverContext.IsFloatingPoint`: COMP-1, COMP-2, every FLOAT usage, and a floating-point edited item.
  The rule is `NumericLanes.ArgumentKeepsSignificance`.
- In such an argument, +, - and * stay exact.
- A quotient is the SDIDI's final quotient: 34 significant digits at every magnitude.
- The exception: a quotient whose divisor is a literal that divides exactly within the guard digits stays the exact nested quotient
  (`NumericLanes.QuotientIsFinal`, `TerminatesWithinGuardDigits`).
- `LaneOf` reads the rule through `LaneContext.KeepsSignificance` and the renderer reads it through `NestedQuotient`, so `AssertLane` agrees.
- Every arm of `LaneOf` reads the arguments in the renderer's own argument context, `LaneContext.ArgumentContext`. That includes the
  call arm, which since review R2-1 returns the SDIDI lane when an argument may be on it.
- `ArgumentLaneSweepTests` (a generated compile sweep) holds this. It compiles 270 argument shapes under each of six receiver kinds at
  2023, and under the two fixed-point kinds at 85, and requires every one to compile. With the call arm's fix removed, 56 shapes fail
  under each floating-point receiver.
- Integer positions keep the nested quotient that the type 6 classifier proved exact.
- The first form of this fix, a fixed 17-digit working scale (`NumericLanes.ArgumentScale`), is deleted. It cut `ABS (1 / C / C)` to 0
  and `ABS (1 / T)` to 3.333333E-25.
