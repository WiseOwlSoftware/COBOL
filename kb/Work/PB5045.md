# PB5045 - correct the 1985 transcription to the most accurate text, with attribution

**Owner decision (2026-11-09, verbatim):** "Note that our markdown transcription should always be the most accurate version
of the specification. Therefore we correct obviously types, we update where latest revisions state that corrects/change the
1985 language, etc. We attribute each modification so they remain clearly updates. But we never continue to list incorrect
information just because it was in the original specification that way."

**What the current policy keeps wrong (PB4984's page reads, `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` Errata, the paragraph above C20).**
It keeps as printed "an operand the sense seems to want changed (`index-name-1` where `index-name-2` reads better), a
category list that omits one, a term the editions use differently", and two misprints Amendment 2 corrects in its own
list of changes because "the amendment is the standard's correction". Under the owner's rule:
1. §1985:VI.6.23.4 GR2 (page 360, VI-128; PB5031): `associated with index-name-1` -> `index-name-2`. Evident: the
   sentence's subject is index-name-2; §2023:14.9.39.4 GR2 b) reads "the table element associated with index-name-2".
2. §1985:XI.4.4.4 GR12 (page 934 is Amendment 2's correction): `had been execution` -> Amendment 2's text, applied in the
   body with the CORRECTED marker naming Amendment 2 (ANSI X3.23b-1993) and its page.
3. §1985:XIII.3.10.8 GR4 (`current page group`; Amendment 2, page 936): the same.
4. §1985:XII.3.4 GR7 (REPLACE `is not replaced`, "which Amendment 2 rewrites"): apply Amendment 2's rewrite in the body,
   attributed.
5. Every other change BOTH amendments make to the 1985 base text, in the order Amendment 2's own preamble prescribes (the
   base text updated by Amendment 1 first, then Amendment 2's change): Amendment 1's Addendum 1 (its table of changes to
   the base pages, which exists "to correct typographical errors" among other things; 49 of its rows (38 table rows, 11 bold-labelled) carry the (T) mark
   and about 25 catalog rules are amended by it) and Amendment 2's list (about 135 change rows, pages 913-960 (B-48): 125 on pages 913-939, then XVII-50, -51, -71, -75, -80, -98 and XVIII-1, the index replacement). Apply each
   in the body, attributed to its amendment, row and page, so the base text reads as amended. Decide with the design
   (section 10.2, the catalog's 1993 amendment rows such as §1993:p.B-16) whether an applied amendment change also keeps
   its own catalog row; one fact, one home. Size: a planned wave (`workstream` skill), not an inline fix.
6. Re-judge PB4984's remaining keep-as-printed leads by the new rule, each on its rendered page: `multiplier`
   (§1985:VI.6.20.4 GR1), `for the RECORD IS VARYING` (page 475), and the eight judged without a render. The MOVE category
   list without alphanumeric (§1985:VI.6.19.4 GR3) is already settled by Amendment 1 Addendum 1 row VI-104 (T), which
   inserts "alphanumeric" (`specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md`, the Addendum's VI-104 row): item 5 applies it (review w20261948a
   R4-1). Correct only what is evidently wrong (the sense, the same
   paragraph, or a later revision shows it); a deliberate requirement stays, and the Errata says why.

**Attribution.** Each correction gets an Errata entry and the inline `> ⚠ **CORRECTED — see the Errata (Cnn).**` marker
that states what the page prints and the source of the correction (the sense, Amendment 2 with its page, or a later
edition with a checked `§2002:`/`§2023:` citation). Rewrite the Errata's policy paragraph to the owner's rule, and the
design's misprints-pass rule with it.

**The transcription must SAY so (owner, 2026-11-09: "The markdown doc should clearly explain that it is kept current
with all amendments applied in place").** In the same change set as the corrections, so it is never stated before it
is true, rewrite the 1985 Preface and its "About this transcription" note (`specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md`, the opening
blockquotes). Today they state the opposite: "an amendment is never merged into the text it amends", and "The words,
the punctuation and the spelling are transcribed as printed, with one exception" (a misspelling or a damaged letter). The
new text says plainly that the base text is kept current with every amendment applied in place (Amendment 1, then
Amendment 2), that evident misprints are corrected, that each modification is attributed inline and listed in the Errata
with its source so it reads as an update and can be traced to the printed page, and that the amendment documents stay
transcribed in full under their own headings as the record of what each changed. Sweep the same statement in the 2002
and 2023 transcriptions' "About this transcription" notes (2002's says "the standard's own defects included") and in
every tool or doc that restates the old policy (the acknowledgment and publishable verifiers, TRANSCRIPTION-STATE.md, the
design); the acknowledgment's own wording stays as granted, its slips listed (the grant asks for it "in its entirety").

**Effect on verdicts.** A corrected rule's 1985 change record is restated against the new digest; where the corrected
text changes the decision (item 1 does not: its record rests on the rule's first sentence), the record is re-adjudicated.
