# PB5045 - correct the 1985 transcription to the most accurate text, with attribution

**Owner decision (2026-11-09, verbatim):** "Note that our markdown transcription should always be the most accurate version
of the specification. Therefore we correct obviously types, we update where latest revisions state that corrects/change the
1985 language, etc. We attribute each modification so they remain clearly updates. But we never continue to list incorrect
information just because it was in the original specification that way."

**Sites:** `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (the body text at each item below; the Errata's policy paragraph above C20-C30's
table, and new Errata entries C31 onward), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json` (regenerated, never edited),
the 1985 change records whose rule digests move (re-recorded through `record_verdicts.py --edition 1985`, decisions
unchanged unless the corrected text changes them), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md` section 10.3
(the misprints pass's keep-as-printed rule).

**What the current policy keeps wrong (PB4984's page reads, `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` Errata, the paragraph above C20).**
It keeps as printed "an operand the sense seems to want changed (`index-name-1` where `index-name-2` reads better), a
category list that omits one, a term the editions use differently", and two misprints Amendment 2 corrects in its own
list of changes because "the amendment is the standard's correction". Under the owner's rule:
1. §1985:VI.6.23.4 GR2 (page 360, VI-128; PB5031): `associated with index-name-1` -> `index-name-2`. Evident: the
   sentence's subject is index-name-2; §2023:14.9.39.4 GR2 b) reads "the table element associated with index-name-2".
2. §1985:XI.4.4.4 GR12 (page 934 is Amendment 2's correction): `had been execution` -> Amendment 2's text, applied in the
   body with the CORRECTED marker naming Amendment 2 (ANSI X3.23b-1993) and its page.
3. §1985:XIII.3.10.8 GR4 (`current page group`; Amendment 2, page 936): the same.
4. §1985:XII.3.4 GR7 (REPLACE `is not replaced`, "which Amendment 2 rewrites"): apply Amendment 2's rewrite in the body,
   attributed.
5. Every other change Amendment 2 (the CORRECTION amendment) makes to the 1985 base text: read its list of changes and
   apply each in the body, attributed, so the base text reads as amended. Decide with the design (section 10.2, the
   catalog's `§1993` rows) whether an applied amendment change also keeps its own catalog row; one fact, one home.
6. Re-judge PB4984's remaining keep-as-printed leads by the new rule, each on its rendered page: the MOVE category list
   without alphanumeric (§1985:VI.6.19.4 GR3), `multiplier` (§1985:VI.6.20.4 GR1), `for the RECORD IS VARYING`
   (page 475), and the eight judged without a render. Correct only what is evidently wrong (the sense, the same
   paragraph, or a later revision shows it); a deliberate requirement stays, and the Errata says why.

**Attribution.** Each correction gets an Errata entry and the inline `> ⚠ **CORRECTED — see the Errata (Cnn).**` marker
that states what the page prints and the source of the correction (the sense, Amendment 2 with its page, or a later
edition with a checked `§2002:`/`§2023:` citation). Rewrite the Errata's policy paragraph to the owner's rule, and the
design's misprints-pass rule with it.

**Effect on verdicts.** A corrected rule's 1985 change record is restated against the new digest; where the corrected
text changes the decision (item 1 does not: its record rests on the rule's first sentence), the record is re-adjudicated.
