## Landed — wave 1093 (group E)

Re-probed on the wave's own build, all four scenarios reproduced (COBOLNET1596 on the legal comma, the period accepted,
COBOLNET1658 on two legal grouping periods, two commas accepted and printed `[+1,5,0E+00]`). Fixed at the two sites that
read a literal period: `ValidateEditing`'s SR12 b FOR-form screen and `AnalyzeFloatEdited`'s only-once rule and
significand, which now take `decimalPointIsComma` and ask `PictureComposition.DecimalSeparator` /
`GroupingSeparator` - the new ONE asker of SR13's role swap, which also replaces the four hand-spelled
`decimalPointIsComma ? ',' : '.'` pairs (`FloatingString` / `HasFloatingString` now take the flag, not two characters).
The runtime already swapped (`CobolEdit.FloatMask.Parse` commaMode), so no emit change. The R2 architecture and
duplication variants (route the float-edited string through `PictureComposition.Validate`) are answered by the data-model
design's recorded decision that `AnalyzeFloatEdited` is GR13 b's one place; the agreement is pinned by
`PictureCategoryDriftTests.DecimalPointIsComma_InterchangesTheRolesOfPeriodAndComma_InEveryRule` (SR13 as a closure over
the accepted set) and `PictureSeparatorDriftTests` (no literal separator ternary in the picture files). Goldens
`2023/pb3219_decimal_point_comma_sr12_point_2023`, `negative/pb3219-decimal-comma-for-form-period` (COBOLNET1596),
`negative/pb3219-decimal-comma-float-two-points` (COBOLNET1658).
