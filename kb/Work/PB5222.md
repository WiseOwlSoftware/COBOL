## What happened

On 2026-11-10 the tooling-lane gate of build-stage1 (run `000001-20261111T023340Z`) started at 18:33 PST. Its `gate.log`
gained its last line at 18:34 ("tooling lane … the audits and the script self-tests") and then nothing for over
30 minutes, while `scripts/self_tests.py --reuse` ran (from 18:36; at 19:02 its only child was
`orchestrator/enqueue.py --self-test`). The session could only find progress by walking the process tree. The owner asked
twice, "this is taking a long time", and the session could not say how many self-tests were left.

That run was large because PB5220 changed `toolchain.env`, which voided every recorded self-test pass, and because the
branch touched `scripts/inert_lane.py`, which many self-tests import. Large runs will happen again. A silent one wastes the
owner's time and invites a wrong kill.

## What is owed

**Sites:** `scripts/self_tests.py` (`Runner.run`, `Runner._report`) · `scripts/gate.py` (`Gate._audits`)

`Runner._report` already writes one line per self-test, but only as each one ends, and the gate holds the audits' output
until each audit ends.

`self_tests.py` writes one line per self-test as it starts and as it ends: its name, REUSED / PASS / FAIL, its duration,
and `n of N`. The gate copies those lines into `gate.log` as they arrive, so `gate.py --await` and a `tail` both show
progress. A self-test arm proves the lines appear before the run ends.
