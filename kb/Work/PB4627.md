## Landing

- Re-probe on the branch build (before the fix): `EVALUATE TRUE WHEN > 1 AND S88 AND 5` drew COBOL1634 alone, while the same condition in an IF draws COBOL2318 for the 5. Same for `WHEN NUMERIC AND 5`, `WHEN > 1 AND NOT S88 AND 5`, `WHEN > 1 AND (S88) AND WS-X` and the surplus `EVALUATE N WHEN 1 ALSO > 1 AND S88 AND 5` (COBOL2106 alone).
- Siblings of the one cause, found in the same walk and fixed with it: `WHEN NUMERIC AND > 1` hid COBOL2552 (the walk bound every abbreviated relation as if an insertion were in flight), `WHEN > 1 AND TRUE` hid COBOL2318 (the walk returned on a truth word), and a bare address-identifier after a complete simple condition hid COBOL2318.
- Fix shape: the walk carries `StandaloneInsertion`, the part of `AbbrevCarry` a walk with no subject operand can keep (is an insertion in flight, is its relation boolean), turned on by the leading relational shape and by a stated relation, off by every complete simple condition that is not a relation (ISO 8.8.4.12.4 GR1). Judged by it, a bare value is the object of the carried relation while it lasts and otherwise goes through `RefuseNonCondition`; an abbreviated relation without it reports COBOL2552; after a boolean relation COBOL2723. The three reports are extracted from `BindAbbreviatedRelation`, `BindPrimary` and `BindComparison` so the walk and the bound condition use one wording.
- Pinned by `EvaluateObjectOperandFaultTests` (23 refused-pair rows, 2 surplus rows) and the conformance goldens `pb4627-evaluate-refused-pair-value-after-condition-name` (COBOL2318) and `pb4627-evaluate-refused-pair-abbreviated-relation-after-class-condition` (COBOL2552), refused at 85, 2002, 2014 and 2023.
