# PB2779 — battery launch wrappers linger after the battery ends

Found 2026-10-08 14:43 by the operator while checking that the machine was idle for the owner's Claude Code update.

## Measured

- 00:36 the operator launched `bash -lc "bash scripts/battery.sh E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/battery-ea20c28 >
  ...console.log 2>&1; echo BATTERY-EXIT=$? >> ...console.log"` through Start-Process (Git for Windows: the
  `Git\bin\bash.exe` launcher pid 645580 → `usr\bin\bash.exe -lc` pid 639772 → a child `bash.exe` with no arguments,
  pid 641428).
- 01:01 the console log ends `=== BATTERY: NOT GREEN (rc=1) ===` and `BATTERY-EXIT=1`, so the `-c` string ran to its
  end. Yet all three processes were alive at 14:43; the child had 285 s of CPU, the `-lc` shell 44.6 s. Killed at 14:43
  (command lines re-read in the same call).
- Unknown: what the child bash is (a subshell of battery.sh that never ends — its `tee -a` pipelines, a `wait` on a
  background `dotnet test` — or the login shell's own job) and why the `-lc` shell did not exit after the `echo`.

## What closes it

1. Reproduce: launch the battery the same way on a short run and list the process tree after BATTERY-EXIT is written.
2. Fix the cause in `scripts/battery.sh` or in the launch (the documented way to start a battery detached, plan §9), so
   the battery leaves no process behind; a self-test or the battery's own last line asserts no child survives.
3. Sweep the other detached launchers (gate runs, the GnuCOBOL differential, linux-gate.sh under WSL) for the same.
