# PB3820 — housekeeping that can fail the verdict

**Sites:** `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml` (job `supersede`, its `actions/checkout@v7` step, about line 262; the
`continue-on-error: true` sits only on the "Cancel the superseded merge-group runs (cancel_superseded.py)" step),
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/LandingPipelineDriftTests.cs#TheMergeGroupRun_CancelsTheRunsTheQueueSuperseded_AndNothingElse`
(and `StepsThatCanFailTheJob`, `TheSupersedeStepReader_NamesEveryStepThatCouldFailTheJob`),
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` (3.17.2 step 4, 3.17.8), `scripts/orchestrator/cancel_superseded.py` (module docstring).

## Measured

Re-read on origin/main 33e3dcf21: `supersede` runs on every merge group, is a `needs` of `ci-gate`, and only its script step
is `continue-on-error`. A failed checkout (a runner or network error, a sparse-checkout error) fails the job and so
`ci-gate`, contradicting the stated rule that housekeeping never decides a verdict (the workflow comment,
`cancel_superseded.py`'s docstring and DESIGN-test-build-ci.md 3.17.2 step 4).

## Acceptance

- Either the checkout step is `continue-on-error: true` and the script step is skipped when it failed
  (`if: steps.<id>.outcome == 'success'`), or `ci-gate` does not read the job's result; pick the one the design 3.17.2 step 4
  states and say which.
- The landing-pipeline drift test asserts that every step of the job is non-fatal (not only the script step).

## Landed

Fixed with the first acceptance option, the one design 3.17.2 step 4 states ("housekeeping never decides a verdict"): the
checkout has `id: checkout` and `continue-on-error: true`, the script step runs only when `steps.checkout.outcome ==
'success'`, and a step of its own says a failed checkout as a warning annotation (so a skipped sweep is never silent).
The job stays a `needs` of `ci-gate`, since the ci-gate drift check wires every job. The drift test now reads every step of
the job (`StepsThatCanFailTheJob`, held by its own arms) and fails on one without `continue-on-error: true`; removing the
checkout's line turns it red (probed). A job-level failure (runner never starts) is outside step control and still fails
the verdict.
