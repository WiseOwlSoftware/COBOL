# PB4359 - NUMVAL-C / TEST-NUMVAL-C `ANYCASE` is accepted silently at `--std 85`

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/IntrinsicBinder.cs` (`IntrinsicBinder.BindNumvalCFamily`, the arm that reads the optional `ANYCASE` argument of NUMVAL-C and TEST-NUMVAL-C).

The branch that landed PB4308 (PR #957) added the edition gate for the `PHYSICAL` phrase of `FUNCTION LENGTH` and `BYTE-LENGTH`: below the edition that introduced the keyword it is a user word, and the compiler said nothing. NUMVAL-C and TEST-NUMVAL-C have the same shape with `ANYCASE` (and `LOCALE`), and `BindNumvalCFamily` does not ask the edition. A program compiled with `--std 85` that writes `FUNCTION NUMVAL-C(X, ANYCASE)` therefore compiles as if the phrase were 85 syntax.

**Not verified:** the 1989 intrinsic-function amendment (the text that gives COBOL-85 its intrinsic functions) is not in the repo, so whether NUMVAL-C in the 85 edition has the `ANYCASE` argument at all is unconfirmed. The adjudicator must settle the 85 edge from the edition record in `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` before any fix. `kb/Work/PB619` is the neighbouring defect (the reserved-word funnel exemption for bare function-argument words) and may share the fix.

Fix direction: the same edition predicate PB4308 used, applied in `BindNumvalCFamily`, with an `--std 85` negative golden and a `--std 2002` and later positive.

## Landed (wave 20261808, group E)

**Sites (as landed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/IntrinsicBinder.cs` (`BindNumvalCFamily`, the ANYCASE arm now asks `ConstructRegistry.Check(Constructs.NumvalCAnycaseKeyword2002)`); `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (row `numval-c-anycase-keyword-2002`, introducedIn 2002) with `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ConstructRegistry.g.cs` and `Constructs.g.cs` regenerated; `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-locale-facility.md` (the T6 row names the new construct row).

**The 85 edge, settled.** The note left it unconfirmed because the 1989 amendment is not in the repo. The archived ISO/IEC 1989:2002 text (`E:/COBOL-coord/specs-drafts/t2002.txt`, 15.55.1 line 32393 and 15.76.1 line 33111) already prints `[ ANYCASE ]` in both formats, `reserved-words.json` has ANYCASE r85 false (added 2002), and E.3.3 item 1 of the 2023 text says the keyword was only "clarified" after 2014. So the edge is 2002, the same as the LOCALE keyword's row beside it.

Re-probe on the wave's build: `COMPUTE N = FUNCTION NUMVAL-C("$1,234.56" ANYCASE)` compiled and ran at `--std 85` (`+0001234.56`), and so did the argument-2 form. TEST-NUMVAL-C was already refused at 85 by its own COBOL1502 (a 2002 function), so only NUMVAL-C carried the gap. Now COBOL0900 "the ANYCASE keyword of NUMVAL-C / TEST-NUMVAL-C requires COBOL-2002" at 85, clean at 2002, 2014 and 2023. Goldens: `15.68.3-Argument-rules/pb4359_numval_c_anycase_2002` (2002, 2014, 2023) and refusal `pb4359-numval-c-anycase-below-2002` (85). Inventory: FMT-15.68.2 and FMT-15.94.2 gained witnesses (GAP 19 -> 19). Sweep: FIND-STRING and SUBSTITUTE also carry ANYCASE but are 2023 functions, so their catalog gate covers the keyword; the other phrase words (LEADING, TRAILING, FIRST, LAST, START, AFTER, ANY) sit inside functions introduced at or after them (PB4308's sweep).
