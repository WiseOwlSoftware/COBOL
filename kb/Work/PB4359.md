# PB4359 - NUMVAL-C / TEST-NUMVAL-C `ANYCASE` is accepted silently at `--std 85`

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/IntrinsicBinder.cs` (`IntrinsicBinder.BindNumvalCFamily`, the arm that reads the optional `ANYCASE` argument of NUMVAL-C and TEST-NUMVAL-C).

The branch that landed PB4308 (PR #957) added the edition gate for the `PHYSICAL` phrase of `FUNCTION LENGTH` and `BYTE-LENGTH`: below the edition that introduced the keyword it is a user word, and the compiler said nothing. NUMVAL-C and TEST-NUMVAL-C have the same shape with `ANYCASE` (and `LOCALE`), and `BindNumvalCFamily` does not ask the edition. A program compiled with `--std 85` that writes `FUNCTION NUMVAL-C(X, ANYCASE)` therefore compiles as if the phrase were 85 syntax.

**Not verified:** the 1989 intrinsic-function amendment (the text that gives COBOL-85 its intrinsic functions) is not in the repo, so whether NUMVAL-C in the 85 edition has the `ANYCASE` argument at all is unconfirmed. The adjudicator must settle the 85 edge from the edition record in `docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` before any fix. `kb/Work/PB619` is the neighbouring defect (the reserved-word funnel exemption for bare function-argument words) and may share the fix.

Fix direction: the same edition predicate PB4308 used, applied in `BindNumvalCFamily`, with an `--std 85` negative golden and a `--std 2002` and later positive.
