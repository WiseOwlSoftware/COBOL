## Landed (wave 20262025 group A, 2026-11-13)

**Re-probe (this tree, before the fix).** `probe5.cob` (`COMPUTE R = Z ** 0`, Z = 0) and `probe6.cob` (`COMPUTE R = 5 / Z`)
at `--std 85`: `00`, then `abnormal run-unit termination: EC-SIZE-EXPONENTIATION (fatal)` / `EC-SIZE-ZERO-DIVIDE (fatal)`,
exit 1. Reproduced.

**Rule.** §1985:VI.6.4.2 GR3 (`cite.py --check` OK): with no ON SIZE ERROR phrase "the values of the affected resultant
identifiers are undefined", the others are stored as if no size error had occurred, and "control is transferred to the end
of the arithmetic statement"; §1985:B.2.2 item 45 (OK): "the program will continue and the value of any affected resultant
identifier is undefined"; GR1 and GR2 make the exponentiation violation and the zero divisor size error conditions. 2002 made
it the fatal exception condition (§2002:14.6.4, OK), so the split is at 2002.

**Fix.** One dialect behaviour, `DialectBehavior.UnphrasedSizeErrorIsFatal` (changed in 2002; VCR row 7.71). `ArithmeticEmitter.EmitArith`
gives a COBOL-85 arithmetic statement with no phrase the two-phase shape with NO condition checked (`SizeChecks.None`, so
every render and landing is the unchecked one and an overflowing resultant keeps its low-order digits as before) and EVERY
raise caught (`SizeErrOnlyNames` null): a resultant whose value cannot be formed keeps its content (GnuCOBOL 3.2's
disposition, DOC-A.1-70), the per-receiver `ReceiverStore` try stores the others, a debugging section for a kept item
does not follow it (§1985:XV.3.2.4 GR6, probed), and the next statement runs. `EmitPositionValue` (a reference-modifier
expression, not an arithmetic statement) keeps the termination.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DialectBehavior.cs` (`UnphrasedSizeErrorIsFatal`),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs` (`EmitArith`, `EmitPositionValue`, `WriteSizeCatches` doc),
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolSizeError.cs` (class and `ZeroDivide` docs).

**Witnesses.** Golden `conformance-suite/cases/VI.6.4@1985-COMMON-OPTIONS-AND-RULES-FOR-STATEMENTS/pb5484_no_phrase_size_error_continues`
(the change record's own STATEMENT-1 / STATEMENT-2, a zero base, a no-real-result power, a nested quotient, DIVIDE GIVING
REMAINDER by zero); `SizeErrorDispositionTests.Cobol85_NoPhraseSizeError_KeepsTheResultantsAndContinues` (7 rows) and its
control `Cobol85_NoSizeError_StoresTheResultants`. Row 1985-GR-VI.6.4.2-3 restated (batch
`w20262025a-pb5484-1985-no-phrase-size-error`, GAP 232 -> 232): its notes had claimed the termination unreachable at 85.

**Left as it is.** A size error in a 1985 CONDITION (`IF 5 / Z > 1`) still ends the run unit: GR3 governs arithmetic
statements, and the truth value of a condition whose expression cannot be evaluated is a determination of its own
(reported as a lead).
