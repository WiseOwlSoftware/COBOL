# PB4403 — one self-test keeps its arms outside the register

**Sites:** `scripts/corpus_sweep.py` (`EXTERNAL_CORPUS_ARMS`, `self_test`), `scripts/self_test_arms.py` (`DECLARED`).

`scripts/self_test_arms.py` is the one reader of the arms each pipeline self-test must declare (its DECLARED register, run
by `self_test_arms.py --self-test` over every declared script and the wiring). `scripts/corpus_sweep.py` instead defines
`EXTERNAL_CORPUS_ARMS` itself (line 254) and calls `self_test_arms.findings(declared={'scripts/external_corpus.py':
EXTERNAL_CORPUS_ARMS}, exact=frozenset())` from its own self-test (line 333). So the arms `external_corpus.py` must keep
are stated in a second place, and `self_test_arms.py --self-test` does not check them.

**Fix.** Move `EXTERNAL_CORPUS_ARMS` into `self_test_arms.py`'s DECLARED register under `scripts/external_corpus.py`,
delete the constant and the private `findings` call from `corpus_sweep.py`, and sweep `scripts/` for any other self-test
that calls `self_test_arms.findings` with its own `declared=` map (each is the same defect).
