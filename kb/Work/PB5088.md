# PB5088 - at --std 85 INITIALIZE of a group whose subordinate item has an OCCURS DEPENDING ON clause compiles (the later editions' variable-occurrence INITIALIZE applies at every edition)

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/InitializeBinder.cs` (whole: the triage records name the file and no member).

**Mechanism.** 1985 says the item referenced by INITIALIZE identifier-1, and any item subordinate to it, may not contain the DEPENDING phrase of OCCURS. InitializeBinder implements the later editions' INITIALIZE of a variable-occurrence item with no 85 arm; `INITIALIZE E (1)` for an item whose own description holds DEPENDING is accepted the same way.

**Found by.** The 1985 `--std=85` draft triage (kb/Work PB4995 item 3): each rule below is a conformance-suite draft that did not MATCH at `--std 85`, and its adjudicator decided COMPILER-DEFECT. Harm and observed text are the records'; nothing here was re-derived.

**Inventory.** The traceability inventory carries no 1985 rows yet, so `inventory_rows` is empty; the rows this note covers are the 1985 rule ids listed below (catalog `docs/rearchitecture/spec-rule-catalog-1985.json`).

**Rules (1; every citation checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `1985-SR-VI.6.17.3-4` - `§1985:VI.6.17.3 SR4` (record harm: under-rejects, slice-04): §1985:VI.6.17.3 SR4: "The description of the data item referenced by identifier-1 or any items subordinate to identifier-1 may not contain the DEPENDING phrase of the OCCURS clause." G's subordinate item E is described `OCCURS 1 TO 5 TIMES DEPENDING ON N`, so `INITIALIZE G` (line 11) breaks the rule. The draft reads the rule correctly; its control (`INITIALIZE W-X`) compiles.

**Repro (`1985-SR-VI.6.17.3-4`, slice-04).**

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. INITODO.
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01  N PIC 9 VALUE 3.
       01  W-X PIC X(3).
       01  G.
           05  E PIC X OCCURS 1 TO 5 TIMES DEPENDING ON N.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       P1.
           INITIALIZE G.
           STOP RUN.
```

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** --std 85 refuses INITIALIZE of an item with or containing an OCCURS DEPENDING ON clause, at the statement. The deciding words: "The description of the data item referenced by identifier-1 or any items subordinate to identifier-1 may not contain the DEPENDING phrase of the OCCURS clause." (§1985:VI.6.17.3 SR4).

**Observed (copied from `1985-SR-VI.6.17.3-4`).** Compile exit 0 with no diagnostic; the program runs (no output, exit 0). `INITIALIZE E (1)`, whose own description holds the DEPENDING phrase, also compiles at --std 85 with no diagnostic. The compiler implements the later editions' INITIALIZE of a variable-occurrence item and applies it at every edition: src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/InitializeBinder.cs has no --std 85 check for the DEPENDING phrase. cobol.exe 25.0.0 of E:/COBOL/.claude/worktrees/pin-triage (e8a1b94f50), invoked as the conformance-suite adapter's flag command: cobol t.cob --std 85 --source-format fixed --flag-extensions -o t.dll
