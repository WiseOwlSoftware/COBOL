# PB643 — LANDED (wave 1020 B, 2026-10-05) — a required clause nobody read

Found 2026-09-05 by the `PB292` implementer (report §"New defects" (a)), whose sibling sweep asked which
file-control clauses no binder reads: after its change, `fileReserveClause`, `paddingCharacterClause` (owned by
[[PB300]]/[[PB301]]) and `vendorFileControlClause` (a deliberate non-ISO surface) were the three with zero readers.
This is the one with no owner of any kind.

## The rule

§12.4.5.14.3 GR1: "If the RESERVE clause is specified, the number of input-output areas allocated is equal to the
value of integer-1" (`OK §12.4.5.14.3 1)`) and "If the RESERVE clause is not specified, the number of input-output
areas allocated is specified by the implementor" (`OK §12.4.5.14.3 1)`). Both halves bind: the clause has a
defined effect, and its absence has a determination the implementor must document. The element is required (not
Annex A.3 processor-dependent, not an A.4 optional module), so §4.2.6's accept-and-warn licence — the one PB292
used for RECORD DELIMITER — does not reach it.

## The mechanism

`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolIO.g4` `fileReserveClause : RESERVE integerLiteral (AREA | AREAS)?` is an
alternative of `fileControlClauses` (`:64`), and `grep -rn "\.fileReserveClause()" src/ --include=*.cs` outside
`Generated/` returns nothing. `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` has no row for RESERVE in §2 or §7.

## Disposition, and why it is partly the owner's

The managed I-O model has no notion of a count of input-output areas: buffering is the .NET stream's, and a
program cannot observe how many areas exist (§12.4.5.14.3 GR1 assigns a quantity, not a behaviour a program can
read back). The honest disposition is therefore an implementor determination — "the number of input-output areas
is not a tunable of this implementation; the clause is accepted and has no effect; with the clause absent the
count is one" — documented as a §7 A.1-style row and made OBSERVABLE the way PB292 made RECORD DELIMITER
observable (an accept-inert informational diagnostic through the `EditionContext.Declined` seam, or a documented
no-diagnostic acceptance if the owner reads GR1 as satisfied by any allocation). Whether a required element may
be "accepted with no effect" without a diagnostic is the owner's reading of §4.2.6 versus §12.4.5.14.3; the
implementer records both and asks.

## Fix shape (after the owner's answer)

`DataBinder.BindFileControl` reads the clause beside PB292's arm; the chosen disposition is one descriptor and one

asserting identical I-O behaviour (the determination) and the chosen diagnostic posture; `AnnexA3RegisterDriftTests`'
sibling for §7 holds the new row; inventory rows FMT-12.4.5.14.2 and GR-12.4.5.14.3-1 re-verdicted on the
determination.

## Landed (wave 1020 B, 2026-10-05)

**Re-probed first** (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w1020b/probes/r0.cob`, `r1.cob`, `r4.cob`): a writer under `SHARING WITH
READ ONLY` WRITEs 100 records of 64 bytes and a sibling connector counts what it can READ. Before: no clause, `RESERVE 1
AREA` and `RESERVE 4 AREAS` all read 64 (the 4,096-byte host buffer, whatever the clause said). After: 64, 64, 0.

**The owner question is answered by the spec, not by a determination.** §12.4.5.14.3 GR1's first sentence is
unconditional (`cite.py --check 12.4.5.14.3 "If the RESERVE clause is specified, the number of input-output areas
allocated is equal to the value of integer-1"` -> OK §12.4.5.14.3 1)), so the areas exist in EVERY sharing posture and
the posture decides only WHEN they are emptied. The restructuring that makes that true: the connector keeps ONE buffer
between its record area and the medium, that buffer IS its input-output areas, and it is sized in one place
(`HostFile.InputOutputAreaBuffer` = areas x `HostFile.InputOutputAreaBytes`, 4,096):

- sequential: the `StreamReader` / `StreamWriter` buffers (`SequentialConnector.OpenReader` / `OpenWriter`). The host
  handles beneath (`HostFile.OpenConnectorStream`, `OpenConnectorWriteStream`) are UNBUFFERED in every posture now, so
  PB753's one-buffer rule holds everywhere (before, the exclusive posture had TWO buffers, the reader's default and the
  host's 4,096). A connector that may share with another writer flushes at every release (`ReleaseRecord`, unchanged)
  and discards read-ahead on a sibling's release (`EnsureReaderCoherent`, unchanged);
- relative / indexed: the store handle's buffer, through the new role `HostFile.OpenConnectorStore`
  (`KeyedConnector.TakeFileLock`), buffered in every posture because the store is read once by a fresh handle at OPEN
  and written once at CLOSE;
- the count: `FileModel.ReserveAreas` -> `SequentialIoEmitter.EmitReserveRegistration` (every registering arm, report
  files included: the report arm calls none of `EmitAreaRegistrations`) -> `CobolFile.RegisterReserve` ->
  `FileConnector.InputOutputAreas` (default `HostFile.ImplementorInputOutputAreas` = 1, DOC-A.1-164);
- the limit (`cite.py --check 4.2.15 "A conforming implementation may place such limits"` -> OK): integer-1 <= 524,287
  (`HostFile.MaxInputOutputAreas`), COBOLNET2897 above it in `DataBinder.BindFileControl`; an OPEN that cannot allocate
  the areas answers '30' (`FileConnector.Open`, §9.1.13.6 1)).

DOC-A.1-164 rewritten (the "no input-output area when another writer is admitted" exception is gone) with its witness;
`DESIGN-runtime-library.md` now names four host-path roles. Goldens: `2002/pb643_reserve_areas_visibility` (064 / 064 /
000 / 100, and 100 after every CLOSE), `85/pb643_reserve_areas_all_organizations` (sequential, relative, indexed and a
report file through their areas), `negative/pb643-reserve-beyond-limit` (COBOLNET2897 at every edition). Drift:
`SharedReadCoherenceDriftTests.TheConnectorKeepsOneBuffer_ItsInputOutputAreas_SizedInOnePlace`. Rows (batch
`w1020b-pb643-reserve-areas`): GR-12.4.5.14.3-1 NOT-IMPLEMENTED -> CONFORMS and DOC-A.1-164 GAP -> OK; GAP 232 -> 230.
