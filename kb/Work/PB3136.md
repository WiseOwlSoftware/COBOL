# PB3136 — a negative fixture's header described the tree of the day it was written

> flags: process-only

Source: the operator, 2026-10-13 (seen while landing the PB3107 group; fixed in the branch that files this note).

**Mechanism:** `tests/conformance/negative/pb260-accept-screen-at-phrase.cob` was written on 2026-09-02 with a header
that said "PENDING, NOT ENABLED — the emitted diagnostic was NOT measured", gave the steps to enable it, and said the
refusal was meanwhile asserted by `DocumentedNonSupportWitnessTests`. Its WITNESS paragraph said the AT phrase "has no
grammar surface at all", so the source was refused by a parse error. The same day, DEVLOG 1438 (A.4.2 screen handling
refused by name) enabled the case and recorded `pb260-accept-screen-at-phrase.err` = `COBOL1707: the ACCEPT
statement's screen format 3`. The header was never updated. On 2026-10-13: the case is in the negative manifest's
`enabled` list, `acceptStatement` carries `screenTail?` (the AT phrase parses), `ScreenFacility` refuses Format 3 by
name under COBOL1707, and `DocumentedNonSupportWitnessTests` does not name the case.

**Harm:** none to a user's program. A reader of the fixture is told the opposite of the truth about how the refusal is
made and what asserts it.

**Fix (landed with this note):** the WITNESS and PENDING paragraphs are replaced by the current facts: the AT phrase
parses, the binder refuses Format 3 by name (COBOL1707), the case is enabled and its `.err` holds the measured
substring. The `.err` carries no line number, so the shorter comment changes no expectation.

**Related:** PB260 (the A.4.2 screen-handling refusals), PB3116 (the per-case facts that belong in the fixture's own
header).
