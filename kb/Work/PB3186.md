# PB3186 — the dispatch ledger's hand entries, and a landing check that binds by their union

**Found by:** the Mythos session (c17ef2a9), 2026-10-15 12:50 PDT, measuring why the landing lane held fix-lane
branches with exit 3 all morning (Finding A of `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/mythos-item1/decisions-item1-20261015.md`).
Filed and fixed by wave 1083 group B (with PB2807).

## Mechanism

1. **The scrape.** `scripts/hooks/dispatch_guard.py` `record_hand_dispatch()` / `dispatched_ids()` took EVERY
   `PB<digits>` id from an admitted Agent or Workflow call's prompt and from every `.md`/`.txt`/`.json` file the prompt
   names (one level), and ran `plan_wave.py --record-dispatch <ids>`, which recorded the open ones as ONE `hand` entry
   with the UNION of their computed file sets (`record_dispatch_main` → `plan_wave.record_hand_dispatch`). A brief
   names reference notes (PB1698 status_delta, PB1699 orient, PB1701 the STATUS hook, PB1957 the CI survey ...), so
   each finisher, clerk or Workflow call recorded a few hundred files as "dispatched work" for notes nobody was working.
   Measured in `E:/COBOL-coord/inflight-groups.json` at 12:50: six `whand` entries of 339, 465, 741, 744, 730 and 890
   files (labels `cobol-finisher: Finish 423-17 Linux gate red`, `Workflow: `, `cobol-clerk: Clerk files enqueue
   leads`, ...); at 13:40 five of 470, 343, 303, 377 and 910 files.
2. **The union binding.** `scripts/orchestrator/landing_check.py` `identity()` bound an in-flight worktree to every
   ledger group whose declared files its diff met, and took the UNION of their notes. A fix-lane worktree on
   `DataBinder.*.cs` therefore read as "PB1682, PB1698, PB1699, PB1701 work by its dispatch ledger"; once any bound
   note sat in the R3 cluster (PB2119), the check STOPPED a fix-lane landing against that fix-lane worktree, which its
   own rule says is merged, never stopped. The landing-lane logs `E:/COBOL-coord/logs/land-lane-*.log` of 2026-10-14
   23:23 to 2026-10-15 18:16 carry 23 such stops over six branches (`…3a0-12`, `…3a0-19`, `…3a0-4`, `…423-11`,
   `…423-17`, `…423-5`): 19 "PB1682, PB1698, PB1699, PB1701", 4 "PB1698, PB1699, PB1701, PB1957".
3. **The planner.** `held_by_dispatch` and `inflight_file_sets` honoured hand entries for 24 h (`HAND_TTL_SECONDS`), so
   a campaign plan (`--cluster PB2911`) collided with those sets on nearly every group, and every fix-lane group
   of the 2026-10-15 13:40 dry run met two to five `whand` owners (the PB2807 re-probe).

## Repro

`python scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py --budget-points 40 --dry-run` on main f5fec711b, then
`plan_wave.inflight_file_sets(coord_dir=<coord>, terminal=…)` against each planned group's files: every group meets
`dispatched whand (…)` owners naming notes it has nothing to do with. The lane logs above are the landing-check repro.
