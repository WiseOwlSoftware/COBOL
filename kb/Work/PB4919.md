# PB4919 - the grammar against the 1985 general formats

**What was measured.** PB4849 gave the optional-word audit a scan arm: it reads the decided 1985 drawings
(`figure_options.read_drawn`). Run as `python scripts/spec/audit_grammar_optional_words.py --edition 1985` over the 213 1985
formats, it reports 36 candidates where the grammar and the 1985 format disagree on whether a word is optional. They fall in
two directions:
- **The grammar demands a word that the 1985 format prints as optional, not underlined.** Examples: `memorySizeClause` SIZE,
  `rerunClause` EVERY and OF, `symbolicCharacterEntry` IS/ARE, `readKey` KEY, `valueOfClause` IS. If the grammar reaches
  these rules under `--std=1985`, legal 1985 source is rejected.
- **The grammar lets source omit a word that the 1985 format underlines, so it is required.** Examples: `useStatement` AFTER
  and PROCEDURE, EVALUATE ALSO, PERFORM VARYING BY, SIGN SEPARATE. Under `--std=1985`, illegal source is accepted.
The full list is in the source report.

**Each candidate needs a decision before any grammar change.** Three questions:
1. Does the 1985 format really print it so? Read it from its rendered page, not from the drawing.
2. Does the rule in question apply under `--std=1985`? Some of these rules are 2023-only, or are gated off for 1985.
3. Is the difference a real 1985-versus-2023 language difference? If it is, the edition gate is the fix. If not, the grammar
   is wrong for every edition. CLAUDE.md rule 1's "falsely restrictive" diagrams cut the other way here: the 1985 drawing was
   decided from the page by PB4849, so it is the authority.

This is part of the 1985 audit (PB4886): each confirmed candidate becomes a 1985 inventory row, or a defect note with a
witness compiled under `--std=1985`. It is filed now so the audit starts with it rather than finding it again.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar` (whole: the rules named above), `scripts/spec/audit_grammar_optional_words.py` (`main`).

## Decisions (wave 20261914 group A, 2026-11-07)

Every candidate was read from its rendered 1985 page (`render-spec-page.py --edition 1985`) and probed with a witness
compiled at `--std 85` and `--std 2023` on the branch's build (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-1985-parallel/w20261914a/mkwit.py`).
Five mechanisms were real defects and account for 17 of the 36 candidate lines (the RERUN fix answers the EVERY and OF
lines of p389, p391, p443 and p485 alike); the other 19 are recorded by mechanism.

**Fixed: the grammar rejected legal 1985 source.**
- MEMORY SIZE (p243 4.4.2 and p702 2.2.2): §1985:VI.4.4.2 underlines MEMORY and the unit words, not SIZE. `OBJECT-COMPUTER.
  XYZ MEMORY 8 WORDS.` drew COBOL1970. `memorySizeClause` now writes `SIZE?`, and `memorySizeAhead` accepts MEMORY
  followed by SIZE or by integer-1. Golden `12.3.6-OBJECT-COMPUTER/pb4919_memory_without_size_85`.
- RERUN EVERY and OF (p389 2.10.2, p391 2.12.2): §1985:VII.2.12.2 underlines only RERUN, ON, END, REEL, UNIT, RECORDS and
  CLOCK-UNITS, so EVERY, the OF of END OF and the OF before file-name-2 are optional words. `RERUN ON X 100 RECORDS OF F`,
  `... RECORDS F` and `END REEL F` each drew COBOL5506. The same page shares one `OF file-name-2`, outside brackets,
  between the REEL/UNIT and RECORDS phrases, so the grammar's `integer-1 RECORDS [OF file-name-2]` also ACCEPTED a
  RECORDS phrase with no file: `FileControlBinder.ScreenRerunClauses` now refuses it by name, COBOL6496, at every edition.
  Goldens `12.4.6-I-O-CONTROL/pb4919_rerun_optional_words_85` and `pb4919-rerun-records-without-file`.
- USE FOR DEBUGGING FOR (p693 3.2.2): §1985:XV.3.2.2 underlines USE and DEBUGGING only. `USE DEBUGGING ALL PROCEDURES`
  drew COBOL5506. The rule now writes `FOR?`. Golden `_unassigned/pb4919_use_debugging_without_for_85`.

**Fixed: a real 1985-against-2002 difference, now edition-gated (COBOL0900 at 85, VCR row 7.52).**
- USE AFTER (p424 4.6.2, p465, p509, p550, p654): §1985:VII.4.6.2 underlines AFTER; the 2002 post-ballot text (14.8.45.1
  Format 1, rendered from `x2002/cobolv200112.pdf` p580) does not, and 2002 Annex F.2 names only PROCEDURE (item 141).
  `USE STANDARD ERROR PROCEDURE ON F` compiled clean at 85. New construct `use-without-after-2002`, gated in
  `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitUseStatement` beside `use-without-procedure-2002`. Negative
  `14.9.49-USE/pb4919-use-without-after-at-85`; positive `pb332_use_optional_words` (2002+).
- SET TRUE repeated (p359 6.23.2): §1985:VI.6.23.2 prints Format 4 without an outer ellipsis; the 2002 text (14.8.35.1,
  rendered p547) adds it. `SET A1 TO TRUE B1 TO TRUE` compiled clean at 85. New construct `set-condition-repeated-2002`,
  gated in `VisitSetBooleanStatement`. Negative `14.9.39-SET/pb4919-set-condition-repeated-at-85`; the positive
  `pb450_set_outer_repetition`, which claimed the repetition at 85, now accepts 2002, 2014 and 2023.

**Already right: an edition gate the audit cannot see.** PERFORM VARYING/AFTER BY (p341) is `perform-varying-by-omitted-2002`;
USE PROCEDURE (p424 and its four siblings) is `use-without-procedure-2002`. Both refuse at 85 (witnesses re-run).

**Already right: the audit reads one alternative of a rule whose other alternative allows the omission.** SYMBOLIC
CHARACTERS IS/ARE (p245: `symbolicCharacterEntry`'s first alternative writes `(IS | ARE)?`), VALUE OF ... IS (p407:
`valueOfClause` writes IS inside its repeated operand group), PAGE ... LINE/LINES (p599: `reportPageClause` has a bare
`integerOperand` arm). Witnesses compile at 85.

**Already right: the underlined word sits inside a bracket the grammar also makes optional.** EVALUATE ALSO (p316, both
rules: `(ALSO evaluateSubject)*` demands ALSO whenever the bracket is written; `EVALUATE A B` is refused), SIGN SEPARATE (p629:
`[SEPARATE CHARACTER]`; `SIGN LEADING CHARACTER` is refused), EXIT PROGRAM (p549: the bare EXIT is the Nucleus's other
format), RERUN ON (p443 2.6.2 and p485 2.8.2: the Relative and Indexed I-O modules print a subset with ON underlined, and
§1985:VIII.2.6.3 GR1 points the full clause at the Sequential page, where ON is in brackets). The EVALUATE pair is a
drawing-reading limit: the 1985 transcription draws the WHEN phrase's `[ALSO` as a typed bracket closed by drawn `┐ … ┘`
corners, a pair `figure_reading` does not join (the "6 text delimiters with no partner" line of the run).

**Already right: the drawing anchors the word to the wrong rule.** READ ... KEY (p456 4.5.2): the KEY on that page is
INVALID KEY's optional word, not the Indexed READ's `KEY IS data-name` phrase (`readKey`), whose KEY is underlined.
`READ F INVALID DISPLAY ...` compiles at 85.

After the fixes the run reports 25 candidates, every one decided above; the gated and drawing-limited ones need a marker
the audit does not yet have (an edition-scoped `audit:` line), which the wave report records as a lead.
