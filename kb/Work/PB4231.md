**Mechanism.** Three SET formats reach the NULL literal. The SLL conflict heuristic takes `setToValueStatement`, the parse dies at the next token, and the whole file is re-parsed under LL. 75 of the 78 legal corpus files that bail SLL do so on `SET x TO NULL`. On the LL path N1's quadratic scan applies.

**Evidence.** The review carries the decompiled heuristic (S4-1, S6-3, S8-5).

**Fix direction (as the review states it).** Corrected rule: NULL first; arithmetic before object; the `ADDRESS OF` receiver kept. The binders are re-keyed to the new tree shape (SetBinder, PtrBinder, VersionConformancePass, FlagConformancePass sites above).
