**Fix.** `setToStatement : SET setToReceiver+ TO setToSender` is the one rule for every `SET receivers TO sender`, with
`setToReceiver` Format 7's printed brace (kept: `(ADDRESS OF?)? dataReference | nonReceivableIdentifier`) and
`setToSender : predefinedNull | dataAddressIdentifier | arithmeticExpression` (NULL first; no `objectReference` arm, since
the arithmetic expression derives every object-reference shape but the bare NULL, which keeps PB450's
arithmetic-before-object order by construction). `setToValueStatement`, `setObjectReferenceStatement`, `setAddressStatement`,
`setAddressReceiver` and `setAddressSender` are deleted. Format 7's ADDRESS phrase is the one fact the parse decides,
asked through ONE helper, `SetToStatementCst.SpellsAddressPhrase`, by its three readers: `SetBinder.BindSet` routes it to
`PtrBinder.BindSetAddress`, `VersionConformancePass.VisitSetToStatement` gates it (`set-address-2002`) and
`FlagConformancePass.VisitSetToStatement` excludes it (and a NULL sender) from FLAG-02 e's Format-1 index flag. A NULL
sender binds through `SetBinder.BindSetToNull` (the former object-reference branch, whose SELF / SUPER / computed arms
were unreachable: those senders always parsed as the arithmetic rule), every other through `BindSetTo`.

**A sibling it fixed (rejects legal source).** The former Format-7 rule spelled identifier-6 as a bare data reference, so
`SET ADDRESS OF b TO FUNCTION f(p)` (a function-identifier of category data-pointer: §8.4.3.1.3 SR1, §8.4.3.2.1,
§14.9.39.3 SR17) was `COBOL0001: unexpected 'FUNCTION'` at every edition while the identifier-5 route accepted it (PB1929).
`PtrBinder.BindSetAddress` now takes the sender `SetSenders.Classify` read: a data reference as before, a function-identifier
or inline invocation through its temporary item when that is a data-pointer, and any other sender refused by SR17
(COBOL0869). Golden: `conformance-suite/cases/14.9.39-SET/pb4231_set_address_of_function_sender` (1=ABCD 2=WXYZ 3=WXYZ,
GR12 / GR13).
