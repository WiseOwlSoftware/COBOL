# PB128

Rows: SR-14.9.8.3-1, SR-14.9.2.3-2, SR-14.9.12.3-2, SR-14.9.12.3-1 (receiver half), plus CALL SR-14.9.4.3-5/-9 and COMPUTE's SR-14.9.8.3-2 bypass notes.

ExpressionBinder.ResolveReceiving — the self-declared ONE RECEIVING CHOKEPOINT, already hosting SIX screens from four clauses — has no arithmetic-resultant category screen, so `COMPUTE R = 1` with R PIC X, `ADD 1 TO E` with E numeric-edited (SR4 grants that to identifier-3 only), and `DIVIDE … GIVING X-item` all bind clean and die in StoreArith's LoudStmt at RUN time (§4.2.2 requires a compile-time mechanism). AND four paths bypass the chokepoint wholesale via ctx.Refs.Resolve: DIVIDE's REMAINDER identifier-4, CALL's BY REFERENCE arguments and RETURNING identifier-3, and BuildComputeBoolean's Format-2 receivers — skipping the CONSTANT RECORD (§13.18.15.3 SR2), CAPACITY-register (§13.18.38 SR30–32) and constant-name screens every other receiver gets (a CONSTANT RECORD item is silently overwritable through CALL). Fix: the category screen lands IN ResolveReceiving (one rule, one place — it serves ADD/SUBTRACT/MULTIPLY/DIVIDE/COMPUTE at once), and the four bypasses re-route through it. The CALL bypass also fixes the CapacityRegisterPlace/DebugRegisterPlace COMPILER CRASH (PlaceRenderer.Write throws InvalidOperationException, uncaught in the CLI).
