Leads:

  a reached statement after a GO TO nested in an IF is not asked. A structural reachability over the parse tree (the emitter's
  own rule: a statement after a leaf transfer in its statement list) would ask it.
- The coverage test compiles 374 NIST programs and takes about a minute of one Unit class; if the gate's weights show it as the
  Unit long pole, partition it like the Conformance suite's classes.

  its source path set. Source Link, or an option for full paths in a local debug build, would remove that step.
- The `None` cost is Roslyn's handling of a directive pair per statement (a probe with the directives and not the PDB is
  +14.8 MB on IX113A); generating the syntax tree, or a backend that writes sequence points natively (the CIL backend), removes it.
