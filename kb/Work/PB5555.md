# PB5555 - PB5379's segment-number screen: a constant detached the ScreenSegmentNumber doc comment, and no golden pins a three-digit segment-number on a declarative section header

UNVERIFIED LEAD: re-probe before fixing.

**R1-1.** `VersionConformancePass.cs:901`: the const `MaxSegmentNumberDigits` sits between the `/// <summary>` block of
`ScreenSegmentNumber` (the Segmentation module's ranges, PB5084) and the method, so the XML doc now documents the constant and the
method has none. Fix: move the constant above the summary (or give it its own).

**R1-2 (test gap).** PB5379's form check (a segment-number is one or two digits) is claimed to serve all three callers of
`ScreenSegmentNumber` (the SEGMENT-LIMIT clause, `VisitSectionDefinition`, `VisitDeclarativeSection`), but no golden puts a
three-digit segment-number on a declarative section header; only SEGMENT-LIMIT and the ordinary section header are pinned. Add the
golden so a caller that skipped the form check would fail.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`VersionConformancePass.MaxSegmentNumberDigits`,
`VersionConformancePass.ScreenSegmentNumber`, `VersionConformancePass.VisitDeclarativeSection`).
