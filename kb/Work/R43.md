# R43

Owner, 2026-09-24, when shown the batch of "may we stamp DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT on these rows?" questions:
**"If we do not support a facility, we do not support that facility. We should do all appropriate implementations to
correctly not support the facility. What am I missing?"** — the questions were bookkeeping (D13 reserves the
DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT verdict to the owner), not doubt about the engineering. This decision answers the class.

## The decision

1. **Wholly inside a declined facility** → DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT, applied by derived selectors (the R42 mechanism
   generalized from A.1 items to clause rules), each row witnessed by the test proving the construct is refused or
   warned. Covers today: PB1198 Q1 (RECEIVE/SEND, 22 rows), PB1151 (ACCEPT Format 3 ON EXCEPTION band; MCS implicit
   SEND), PB1517 (STANDARD-BINARY, GR-11.9.5.2-2), PB1518 (I-O status '45'), PB1519 (exact-match polymorphism
   rows), PB1255 (CODE-SET GR4 — jointly declined), PB579 Q1 (FLOAT-BINARY-128 / FLOAT-DECIMAL, refused by name),
   PB1099 Q1 (record-key SOURCE) and Q2 (APPLY COMMIT).
2. **Half inside a declined facility** → the LIVE half takes its normal verdict (CONFORMS when verified); the
   declined half is non-support by (1) and noted in the row. The "one half unverified ⇒ PARTIAL" rule no longer
   applies to a half that is declined. (PB1151 GR-14.7.7-3; PB1519's two PARTIAL rows; PB1255's residue.)
3. **Trigger can only arise through an unsupported capability** (multi-reel files, calls to non-COBOL programs,
   conditionally-required A.1 items whose condition is absent) → same disposition, with the contingency: re-adjudicate
   if the capability is ever added. (PB1198 Q2, PB1536 Q1.)
4. **Rules that define a property of the USER's program and impose no compiler obligation** (§4.4 GR-4.4-1/-2) close
   by a documented derivation (extends PB386; PB468 Q9).
5. **"Correctly not supporting" is itself required work.** A decline that LEAKS — a declined construct accepted,
   silently mis-compiled, or diagnosed wrongly — is a DEFECT under rule 4 of CLAUDE.md and is fixed, never stamped
   (e.g. PB579 Q2: one legal VALIDATE Format-5 spelling is accepted silently).

## Not covered

Genuine choices remain the owner's: whether to IMPLEMENT a facility (PB1099 Q3, the §8.13 external repository),
latitude choices (PB314), transcription policy (PB738), and behavior changes that break a shipped golden (PB935).
