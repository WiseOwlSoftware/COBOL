# PB5113 - the account self-test read the real accounts' live meter

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/test_orchestrate_account.ps1` (whole: case 2b).

**Found (2026-11-10).** Every gate that ran `test_orchestrate_account.ps1` hung for the self-test runner's full hour
(HANG_AFTER_S): the PB5109 gate twice, the PB5098 gate, and on 2026-11-09 the gates of wf_7ae59db5-4ef-5 and
readjudicate-pb5062, whose `acct1` supervisors were still running the next morning. Case 2b runs `orchestrate.ps1
-ConfigDir $HOME\.claude -MaxUnits 1` on a fake claude to prove the account is a parameter. The supervisor asks
`budget.py` before each unit, and `budget.live_reading` reads the status line's meter from `<config dir>/rate-limits.json`
(kb/Work PB4505) and anchors the estimate on it. Account 1 stood at 57% of a 42.9% allowance, so `budget.py` said
`hold-day` until 2026-11-11 10:05Z, and the supervisor waited for that and never ran its one unit. The test's private
coordination and telemetry directories isolated everything except the config dir, which is the one input it varies. The
`acct2` case passed only because account 2 was under its allowance.

**Fix (landed with this note).** Case 2b runs in a temp HOME (`USERPROFILE`) holding empty `.claude` and `.claude-acct2`
dirs, so `~` in model_rules.json's `accounts.list` resolves there and no real meter, sign-in or telemetry id is read. The
cases test the account selection, never its quota (the quota holds have their own planted-reading cases). The account test now passes
21/21 in about a minute whatever either account's meter says.

**Sibling sweep.** The other orchestrate self-tests run without `-ConfigDir`, so their supervisors resolve the running
session's account and its live meter too: a planted `readings.json` (test_orchestrate_campaign.ps1's `$fresh`) is
outranked by a newer live reading. They have not held so far because the operator account was under its allowance. That
is the same leak; PB3032 (the orchestrate self-tests' parallel failures) is where it is chased, and this note's temp-HOME
shape is the fix to apply there.
