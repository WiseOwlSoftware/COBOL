# PB5649 - the grant patterns of the read-record evidence check, and the committed-band reason

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/data/lineage-evidence-kinds.json:12` (whole: the `needs-accepting-case` pattern table),
`scripts/spec/lineage_reads.py` (`kept_witnesses`, `Witnesses.own_problems`; split out of the earlier one-function check by kb/Work PB5675), `scripts/selftests/spec/lineage_reads/test_reads.py` (`case_11_a_rule_that_grants_a_form_needs_an_accepted_case`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md:1819`
(section 10.6, "The band widening"), and for F4 `scripts/spec/inventory_view.py` (whole: the state rule that carries a
target's verdict to an older row, to be shared with `kept_problems`). Filed by the 2002 records registrar (branch `records-2002-reads-1`); not fixed here.

PB5597's branch review raised three leads in round 3 and judged none blocking. They are filed here as they stand
(R3-1..R3-3), together with one finding from the first real recording run (F4).

**R3-1 (low, correctness): a grant worded "need not" is not caught.** The `needs-accepting-case` patterns (`may` other
than `may not`, `is/are optional`, `in any order`, `is/are permitted|allowed`, `can be`) send a diagnosed-kind rule that
GRANTS a form to an accepted case, because a refusal of a forbidden form does not show a permitted one accepted. A grant
worded "need not" escapes them. 2002-SR-8.4.1.1.2-5, "A paragraph-name need not be qualified when referred to from within
the same section", stays diagnosable. So a refusal of a duplicated paragraph-name would witness the permission to leave
it unqualified. Failure scenario: once that row is answered and its target has only a refusal at 2002, it is KEPT,
although no case shows an unqualified paragraph reference accepted at --std 2002. The reviewer's sample: 0 misses in 10
random refusal-only syntax rows, 1 in the 7 rows with a grant cue. Fix: add a pattern for "need not" (and "is not
required"), with its why and a planted case.

**R3-2 (info, cost): the `may` pattern also takes prohibitions.** `\bmay\b(?!\s+not\b)` takes restrictions worded "may
... only" or "No ... may". Four of the five taken rows the reviewer sampled are prohibitions that a refusal does
witness: 2002-SR-14.8.45.2-4, 2002-SR-14.8.1.2-4, 2002-SR-13.16.41.2-2 ("No record description entry for the file may
specify a number of bytes greater than integer-1") and 2002-SR-13.13.2-5. The pattern fails closed: such a row waits
for an accepted case it does not need. It costs closed rows, not correctness. Fix: exempt "may ... only" and a negated
subject ("No ... may") from the grant pattern, with a planted pair.

**R3-3 (info, docs): the design doc gives the wrong reason for trusting a committed band.** Section 10.6 says a committed
band is evidence "because every committed band ran green in the gate before it landed". That premise is false for a band
committed on a branch that has not landed. Failure scenario: a records worker commits a widening that fails at 2002 and
runs `change-batch` on the clean tree. The case counts as an accepted witness and the KEPT records are written. They
cannot land, because the branch's gate runs the widened case at 2002 in the Conformance target and is red, so the guard
holds. But the doc names the wrong reason. Fix: state the invariant as "a committed band is evidence because no record
written from it lands without a gate (`enqueue.py land` refuses a tip no GREEN record covers) that ran the band at the
edition".

**F4 (low, found by the registrar, 2026-11-15): the read-record check accepts a KEPT record that the inventory view
does not close.** Branch `records-2002-reads-1` wrote 704 KEPT records, but `record_verdicts.py` reports
`GAP: 3284 → 2582 (+702 closed of 3298)`. `inventory_view.py --states --edition 2002` keeps two recorded rows GAP:
2002-SR-13.16.33.2-2 (KEPT into SR-13.18.35.3-2) and 2002-SR-13.16.33.2-9 (KEPT into SR-13.18.35.3-9), both from
`lineage-reads-2002/q-15.json`. Both 2023 targets carry `"editions": "2023"`, so their band does not reach 2002, and the
view does not carry the target's verdict to the older row. `lineage_reads.py --check 2002` is GREEN on both
records: `kept_problems` checks the pairing and a witness at the edition, but not the target's `editions` band, which
the view does read. So two checks of one record disagree. Fix: one rule for "a KEPT record closes its row", shared by
`kept_problems` and the view, so that the writer refuses what the view will not close (or the view's band rule
changes). Then decide these two rows: either the target's band is wrong (it lacks 2002) or the records are.
