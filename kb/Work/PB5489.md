# PB5489 - RECEIVE INTO a numeric display item drops non-digit characters: message AB into K-NUM or K-NUM (1:2) of 1234 gives 0034, where the standard puts AB34 (silent wrong answer at --std 85, and at 2002 through a reference-modified slice)

**Landed (wave 20262027 group E).** Re-probed on a fresh build: both repros printed `[0034]`. Root: `UsageCollectionPass` (`Visit(BoundCommunicationStatement)`) collected `Into` and `Area` as whole-GROUP operands only, so an elementary numeric display receiver kept a native carrier whose `StoreImage` parse drops non-digits; the fix records both as character channels (`Channel`), the twin of STRING identifier-3 (PB4089), and the item stores its image: `AB34` at --std 85 and 2002 (slice). Golden `conformance-suite/cases/XIV.3.5@1985-RECEIVE-STATEMENT/pb5489_receive_into_numeric_characters_85`.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/UsageCollectionPass.cs` (`Visitor.Visit(BoundCommunicationStatement)`).
