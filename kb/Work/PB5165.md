# PB5165 - at --std 85 a data-description clause resolves a non-unique name by the 2002 implicit qualifiers: OCCURS DEPENDING ON, KEY IS and RENAMES name a duplicated data-name unqualified and compile

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.cs` (`EntryClauseCandidates`, `SubtreeCandidates`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/OdoResolvePass.cs` (`Run`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/OccursKeyResolvePass.cs` (`Run`)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Passes/ResolveRedefinesPass.cs` (`Run`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:IV.4.3.8.1 SR3 requires every explicitly referenced user-defined name to be made unique by qualification, except in redefinition. The procedure division half conforms. The clauses of a data description entry do not: `EntryClauseCandidates` (OCCURS DEPENDING ON, for `OdoResolvePass.Run`) and `SubtreeCandidates` (KEY IS in `OccursKeyResolvePass.Run`, RENAMES in `ResolveRedefinesPass.Run`) narrow the candidates to the subject's own group or record before counting, which is the 2002 and later rule that the superordinate groups act as implicit qualifiers, and no caller asks the edition. So a DEPENDING ON, KEY IS or RENAMES operand that names a data-name defined twice compiles unqualified at --std 85.

Code read: §1985:IV.4.3.8.1 SR3: "Identical user-defined names may appear in a source program; however, uniqueness must then be established through qualification for each user-defined name explicitly referenced, except in the case of redefinition. All available qualifiers need not be specified so long as uniqueness is established." The procedure division half conforms: QualifiedCandidates returns every declaration whose ancestors carry the written qualifiers in order, gaps allowed and a file-name outermost (QualifierWalk), ResolveUnqualified, ResolveQualified and UniqueOrReportAmbiguous refuse any count but one with COBOL1639, ResolveProcedureWord does the same for a paragraph-name (COBOL2121) and looks only in the referencing program, and ReportOf refuses an unqualified PAGE-COUNTER or LINE-COUNTER under two reports (COBOL2729). None of these has an edition arm. Probed at --std 85: partial, gapped and full qualifier chains with OF and IN, a file-name qualifier, a qualified condition-name, an unqualified REDEFINES of a non-unique name and P OF S all resolve; an unqualified or under-qualified non-unique data-name or condition-name, qualifiers in the wrong order, an ambiguous paragraph-name, a contained program's PERFORM or GO TO of the containing program's procedure-name and a non-unique FILE STATUS data-name are refused. The data division half does not: the clauses of a data description entry take the 2002 exemption at 85. EntryClauseCandidates (OCCURS DEPENDING ON, called by OdoResolvePass.Run) and SubtreeCandidates (OCCURS KEY IS in OccursKeyResolvePass.Run, RENAMES in ResolveRedefinesPass.Run) narrow the candidates to the subject's own group or record before they are counted, which is §2023:8.4.2.2.1 rule 5 ("the names of any group items superordinate to both the data-name and the subject of the data description entry clause are used as implicit qualifiers for the reference"), and no caller asks the edition. 1985 has that exemption for redefinition only (§1985:VI.5.10.3 SR7) and gives the other clauses the means to qualify (§1985:VI.5.8.3 SR2: "Data-name-1 and data-name-2 may be qualified."; §1985:VI.5.11.3 SR5); ISO/IEC 1989:2002 lists the exemption as its change (§2002:F.2 item 67: "Data-names referenced in a data description entry need not be qualified when they are defined in the same group as the subject of the entry"). Probed at --std 85, each accepted with no diagnostic: DEPENDING ON CNT with CNT in the table's record and in another record; ASCENDING KEY IS K with a second K in another record; 66 XY RENAMES X THRU Y with X and Y in another record too. A fourth case is accepted at every edition by a selection of the compiler's own: RECORD KEY IS K with a second K in working-storage (the local function InRecords of ResolveFiles prefers the candidate inside the file's records). The witness references FLD OF REC-A and FLD IN GRP-Y and displays AAA and BBB. No kb/Work note owns the fix.

**Repro (`1985-SR-IV.4.3.8.1-3`).** `cobol.exe <file> --std 85 --source-format fixed --run`

```cobol

       PROGRAM-ID. N4.

       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01  R1.
           05  CNT PIC 9 VALUE 2.
           05  T PIC X OCCURS 1 TO 5 TIMES DEPENDING ON CNT.
       01  R2.
           05  CNT PIC 9 VALUE 4.
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       MAIN-PARA.
           MOVE "AB" TO T (1) T (2).
           DISPLAY "COMPILED".
           STOP RUN.
```

**Expected (from the 1985 text, the record's).** A refusal at the DEPENDING ON phrase: CNT is declared twice and referenced without a qualifier. §1985:IV.4.3.8.1 SR3: "uniqueness must then be established through qualification for each user-defined name explicitly referenced, except in the case of redefinition." §1985:VI.5.8.3 SR2: "Data-name-1 and data-name-2 may be qualified." DEPENDING ON CNT OF R1 is the conforming spelling (accepted at 85, probed). The same program with the first CNT moved to a third record is refused with COBOL1639, so the compiler has the verdict and withholds it only for a candidate in the subject's own group.

**Observed (the record's).** exit 0, no diagnostic; the program displays COMPILED. The same at --std 2002 and --std 2023, where §2023:8.4.2.2.1 rule 5 makes it legal. Also accepted at --std 85 with no diagnostic: OCCURS 3 TIMES ASCENDING KEY IS K with a second K in another record, and 66 XY RENAMES X THRU Y with a second X and Y in another record.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Make the implicit-qualifier narrowing one edition-aware step inside the candidate search (one helper both members call), so at 85 the full candidate set is counted and an ambiguous reference reports COBOL1639 as the procedure division does.
