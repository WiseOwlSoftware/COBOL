# PB4865 - PERFORM VARYING: BindVaryingOperand does not see through BoundUdfEvaluatedExpr, so an integer function-identifier in a BY or AFTER FROM slot whose evaluation activates a user-defined function is refused COBOL2120

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R1-1 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 1): `ControlFlowBinder.PerformVarying.cs:223` `BindVaryingOperand` integer test does not see through the per-evaluation carrier `BoundUdfEvaluatedExpr` that `Classify` treats as transparent, so an integer function-identifier in a BY or AFTER FROM slot whose evaluation activates a user-defined function is refused COBOL2120 under SR4 a). Pre-existing on main at the same line; not a regression of PB4831, and the sum shape PB4831 fixes is not reachable with a carrier (ODO under a subscripted table is refused COBOL0854).

**Not probed.** The clerk compiled no program. Whether the refused source is legal rests on the reviewer's reading of SR4 a) and is not adjudicated here.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ControlFlowBinder.PerformVarying.cs` (`ControlFlowBinder.BindVaryingOperand, line 223`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ControlFlowBinder.PerformVarying.cs` (`Classify`).

Filed from review leads of waves 20261884 and 20261885 by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

**Fix (landed).** Re-probed on the branch build: `PERFORM VARYING IX FROM 1 BY FUNCTION TWO UNTIL IX > 5` (IX an index-name, TWO a user function RETURNING PIC 9) was refused COBOL2120 (SR4 a)). `BindVaryingOperand` now reads the §5.5 integer test through the per-evaluation carrier (`BoundUdfEvaluatedExpr.Inner`), as `Classify` already read the kind through it. Sweep: the other readers of `IsIntegerOperand` bind no carrier (`PERFORM FUNCTION TWO TIMES` already ran). Goldens: `pb4865_perform_varying_user_function_integer_operand` (accept 2002; BY and AFTER FROM slots) and `pb4865-perform-varying-user-function-below-2002` (refused COBOL0900 at 85).

**Review round 1 (landed).** R1-1: a wholly parenthesized operand (`BY (FUNCTION TWO)`, `BY (K)`, `FROM (1)`) is an arithmetic expression (§8.8.1.1) and is now refused COBOL2119 by `BindVaryingOperand` (written shape via `SoleOperand.Unparenthesized`); goldens `pb4865-perform-varying-parenthesized-user-function`, `pb4865-perform-varying-parenthesized-identifier`. R1-2: golden `pb4865-perform-varying-user-function-not-integer` pins the non-integer arm.
