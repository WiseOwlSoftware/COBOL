## Landed (w1069g, 2026-10-12)

Re-probed on the worktree build at ddee217e3: `para1l.cob` (free form, `... STOP RUN. P2. DISPLAY "B".` on one
line) drew `error COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near 'P2'`, and `S1 SECTION. P1. ...` on one line failed at P1
and P3 while the mid-line `S2 SECTION.` parsed. The fix is the Target as written: `paragraphName` carries no
`{IsAtLineStart()}?` predicate on either alternative, and `CobolParserCoreBase.IsAtLineStart` is deleted (it had no
other caller). The phantom-paragraph path it was added for (DEVLOG 0063) is closed by the grammar itself:
`sentence : statement+ DOT` ends every sentence on its separator period, so a header can follow only a period and a
word a statement left unconsumed is a syntax error. A one-word statement written mid-line after a period (`GOBACK.`,
`COMMIT.`) still parses as a statement where the edition reserves the word (the loop decision prefers the sentence,
as it always did at line start), and `COMMIT.` at --std 2014, where COMMIT is a user word, is a paragraph exactly as
it is on a line of its own.

Golden `2002/pb2720_paragraph_header_mid_line` (free form plus a fixed-form segment): section and paragraph headers
mid-line after a period, an empty paragraph followed by a header on the same line, a qualified PERFORM ... THRU; the
expected P1 / P2 / P4 1 / P5 / P6 is the §14.4.3 execution order. No negative: no edition rejects the shape here.
The X3.23-1985 area-A placement is not enforced at --std 85 for ANY header (a header at the start of a line in area B
compiled before this change too); that is a separate reference-format family, reported as a lead by w1069g.
