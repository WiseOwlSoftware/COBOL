## Repro
`scratchpad\w61e\p6.cob` (orchestrator session scratchpad): `ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD-DECIMAL`, FLOAT-LONG F1 = 0.1,
`COMPUTE F2 = F1 + 0` → F2 = 0.09999999999999999.

## Mechanism
`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolDec.cs#FromDouble` (line ~52) converts the binary64 operand to its
shortest-decimal image; `FloatResultant.FromDec` then lands the standard-decimal result into binary64 under the
implied TRUNCATION of §14.7.4.3 10), which rounds toward zero one ulp below 0.1. The w61e implementer found that an
exact 34-digit conversion does not cure it either.

## To decide

standard-decimal form): accept and document (DOC row) or choose a conversion/landing that round-trips. Survey
GnuCOBOL / IBM per CLAUDE.md rule 1 first.
