## R2 batch 1g (pin 9e65164eb3a9): 1 finding on this note

### `codegen-7--architecture--f2#1` (finding, extract, votes upheld/upheld/upheld)

**Finding:** SequentialIoEmitter is the file-I/O common-services hub of six emitters; the DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3 split (PB2326) splits it by verb, names a RELEASE/RETURN emitter for statements it does not emit, and gives the shared services no home

**Rule:** single responsibility per type and one mechanism per job (DESIGN-architecture-review §5.1, dimensions.md 1); DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3 decomposes each god class by the responsibilities read from its members, and its SequentialIoEmitter row (target ReadEmitter, WriteEmitter, OpenCloseEmitter, ReleaseReturnEmitter; seam one Emit per BoundStatement leaf) is the plan PB2326 carries

**Scenario:** On the pin the class emits OPEN, CLOSE, UNLOCK, WRITE, READ and REWRITE only (StatementEmitter.cs:366-371); RELEASE and RETURN are SortEmitter.EmitRelease/EmitReturn (SortEmitter.cs:708,775; StatementEmitter.cs:385-386), so PB2326 as written creates a ReleaseReturnEmitter with nothing to extract into it. The rest of the class (about two thirds of its 1534 lines) is not a verb at all but the shared file-I/O services other emitters call: the USE hook and item-8 ROLLBACK (EmitUseHook, EmitForeignFileIoCheck, ForeignFileCheckedApart, EmitUncoveredFatalTermination; TransactionEmitter, SortEmitter), the FILE STATUS store (EmitStoreFileStatus; SortEmitter x4, TransactionEmitter x2), the record-area landing and released-record stores (EmitRecordAreaStore, EmitImageInto, EmitOutOfLineInto, Begin/EndReleasedRecord, EmitReleasedRecordAlsoAvailable; SortEmitter, CommunicationEmitter x4, MessagingEmitter, TransactionEmitter), the executing-element operands (ExecutingElementArgs, ExecutingAssignment, LinageArg; SortEmitter, KeyedIoEmitter), registration (EmitFileRegistration from DispatchEmitter, static EmitAreaRegistrations and RuntimeRecordLock from KeyedIoEmitter) and the OPEN/CLOSE scope (EmitOpenScope, EmitCloseStatement; SortEmitter). An extract that follows the DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3 row either leaves all of these in a residual SequentialIoEmitter that is still a god class (fan-in 11, census R0-0033) or scatters them into ReadEmitter/WriteEmitter, making SortEmitter, KeyedIoEmitter, TransactionEmitter, CommunicationEmitter and MessagingEmitter depend on verb emitters for services that are not the verb. The class also takes KeyedIoEmitter in its constructor (registration) while KeyedIoEmitter takes it back through the property-wired SeqIo (KeyedIoEmitter.cs:29, UnitEmitters.cs:83), a cycle the verb split keeps.

**Target:** Amend DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3 row R0-0031/R0-0033 and PB2326: target types FileIoStatusHook (EmitUseHook, item 8, DOC-A.1-103 termination, foreign-file check, EmitStoreFileStatus), RecordAreaTransfer (EmitRecordAreaStore, EmitImageInto, EmitOutOfLineInto, the released-record pair, SentRecordImage, RecordAreaSendImage, OdoRecordExceedsMaximum), FileRegistrationEmitter (EmitFileRegistration and the Emit*Registration family, called once by DispatchEmitter, KeyedIoEmitter.EmitRegistration becoming its arm, which ends the KeyedIo ctor edge), FileOperandRenderer (ExecutingElementArgs, ExecutingAssignment, LinageArg, RenderRetry, RuntimeSharing, RuntimeRecordLock, VaryingLengthArg), then the verb emitters OpenCloseEmitter (with EmitOpenScope/EmitCloseStatement/EmitImplicitClose/EmitUnlock), ReadEmitter, WriteEmitter (WRITE, REWRITE); drop ReleaseReturnEmitter (RELEASE/RETURN stay with SortEmitter, DESIGN-architecture-review §8.3 R0-0044). Seam: the services are constructor collaborators of every I-O emitter, never reached through a verb emitter.
