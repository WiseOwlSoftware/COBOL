# PB5809 - at --std 85 STOP with a symbolic-character is refused, though 1985 bars from STOP only a figurative constant that begins with ALL

**What is wrong.** `STOP STAR.`, STAR a symbolic-character (`SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS STAR IS 43`), is refused at `--std 85` with the parse error `COBOL5506` ("expected '&'"). A symbolic-character is a figurative constant (the checker cites `§1985:IV.4.2.2.2.3 GR7` for its value) and a figurative constant "may be used whenever 'literal' appears in a format" (`§1985:IV.4.2.2.2.3 GRL2.3`); STOP's format has literal-1 and `§1985:VI.6.24.3 SR1` bars only a figurative constant that begins with the word ALL, so `STOP STAR` is legal 1985 source and communicates one character (`§1985:IV.4.2.2.2.3 GRL2.2`). Cause (a), wrong at 85 only: STOP literal is removed from 2002, so a refusal at 2023 is the right outcome, and the parse error in place of `COBOL0902` there is a lesser misleads_diagnosis. Keyword figuratives (`STOP QUOTE`) and symbolic-characters in VALUE, DISPLAY, MOVE ALL, IF, STRING and INSPECT all work at 85: the gap is the STOP arm alone. None of the seven files changed between 5e49d46678 and d7d3f99539 is on this path (it is the grammar). The checker's confidence: high. I ran `stopsym.cob` at `--std 85` myself: refused, `COBOL5506 ... expected '&'`, citing a 2023 clause.
