## Audited (wave 20261861 b, 2026-11-04, pinned eafd32083)

The population is 108 sites in 57 files now, not 63 or 95. Every one was read against the rule it implements, and
the per-site verdicts are in `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-w20261861/b/determination.md` §8: 41 model or lowering
sites with no rule, 4 comments, and 62 rule sites where the rule names an elementary class or category only (numeric,
integer, index, numeric-edited, object reference, pointer), so a group correctly fails the PICTURE test. Wherever a rule
names group KINDS, the group arm is asked separately, through `ItemCategory.GroupKindsOf` or a reader of it. The
PICTURE test decides only the elementary half: VALUE format 1 and format 2 (§13.18.63.3 SR13 through SR16),
JUSTIFIED and BLANK WHEN ZERO on a group (§13.16.3 SR11), ACCEPT (§14.9.1.3 SR1, SR3, SR6), RECORD DEPENDING
(§13.18.43.3 SR6) and SIGN (CheckSignClausesPass:45). Nineteen probes of every kind against those rules gave the
spec's verdict, so PB908's pattern has no remaining sibling. The one defect is a face: §13.18.1.3 SR1's diagnostic,
`DescribeAlignedSubject`, writes its own group classification (`GroupUsage is National ? … : "an alphanumeric group
item"`), so `01 T TYPEDEF STRONG ALIGNED.` and a group holding a DYNAMIC LENGTH item are both told they are "an
alphanumeric group item". Its elementary arm prints `PicCategory.ToString().ToLowerInvariant()`, so `PIC ZZ9 ALIGNED`
reads "category numericedited". The fix asks `ItemCategory.Face`, which switches on `GroupKindsOf` and already names
both exclusions, and pins ALNSTR, ALNVAR, ALNNE and ALNOR as negative goldens on the message.
`Model/StorageExtent.cs:53` lets a national GROUP through its USAGE NATIONAL screen, but its only consumer
(MoveOverlap) never admits an as-if-elementary group sender, so it is latent and unreachable.

## Landed (wave 20261863 C)

`DescribeAlignedSubject` keeps no classification of its own: a group's words are `ItemCategory.Face`'s
(`GroupKindsOf`: national, variable-length, strongly-typed, alphanumeric) and an elementary item's are its Table 2
category (`a category-numeric-edited item`, `a category-object-reference item`), never the enum name. Two hints stay
because only this rule needs them: the GROUP-USAGE BIT hint (§13.16.4 GR1), shown only to a plain alphanumeric group,
and the boolean-DISPLAY hint (§13.18.60.4 GR5). A group of a STRONG type declaration is judged at the TEMPLATE (the
conformance forest), where no item is strongly typed yet (§8.5.3.1), so `StrongTypeModel.IsStronglyTyped` is false there
by design; the pass asks `DataItem.DeclaresStrongType` / `DataBinder.UnderStrongTypeDeclaration` (the predicate the
§13.18.60.3 SR14 and §13.16.3 SR24 g) screens already share) and says the group is one that no GROUP-USAGE BIT clause
can make a bit group (§13.18.29.3 SR1), instead of sending the programmer to write one. Goldens:
`pb1654-aligned-strong-typedef`, `pb1654-aligned-variable-length-group`, `pb1654-aligned-numeric-edited`,
`pb1654-aligned-object-reference`. The re-probe on the 2026-11-04 build reproduced the wrong messages before the fix.
Sibling enum-name faces outside the group (`ExpressionBinder.cs:1078`, `SetBinder.cs:378`, `RefModBinder.cs:222`,
`DataBinder.SignClause.cs:94`, `ReportSectionBinder.cs:3591`) are PB3229's. Left, and a lead rather than a fix: an
entry that writes both TYPE and ALIGNED is refused by COBOL2150 (§13.16.3 SR14), which then CLEARS the entry's TYPE clause
(`DataBinder.cs` BindEntry, `typeRefName = null`), so the entry reaches this pass as a PICTURE-less leaf and draws a
second COBOL1942 ("not an elementary or group data item") and a COBOL0881 beside the one real refusal; the cascade is
SR14's recovery, not this pass's face.
