# PB1492 — LANDED

## The rule (every citation re-run through `cite.py --check`)

> 6) For a continued alphanumeric, boolean, or national literal, the first nonblank character of each continuation line shall be the quotation symbol used in the opening delimiter of the literal.

`OK  §6.2.3.2 6)  (Syntax rules)`

> The first non-space character in the program-text area of the continuation line shall be a quotation symbol matching the quotation symbol used in the opening delimiter.

`OK  §6.3.5 2)  (Continuation of lines)`

> All characters composing any multiple-character separator or multiple-character indicator shall be specified on the same line.

`OK  §6.3.5 2)  (Continuation of lines)`


## Finding — `fixed-form-reference-format` (rejects-legal-source; rows `SR-6.3.5-1`, `SR-6.3.5-2`)

**Mechanism.** F2 - the cross-line literal scanner (ScanLiteralState) does not remember WHICH quotation symbol opened the literal and treats ' and " as interchangeable delimiters, so an apostrophe inside a quotation-delimited literal (or a quote inside an apostrophe-delimited one) on a continued line is read as the closing delimiter and legal continuation is rejected

**Repro.** Fixed form: `000100 01 X PIC X(80) VALUE "IT'S A` (padded to col 72), then `000100-    "BC".` (probe ffp3). Mirror case: `VALUE 'SAY "HI` continued by `-    'THERE'.` (probe ffp14).

**Expected from the spec.** §6.3.5: the continued line 'terminates within an alphanumeric ... literal without a closing delimiter'. The closing delimiter of a literal opened by " is " (§8.3.3.2), so ' is literal content. The program compiles and X = "IT'S A", the spaces up to column 72, then "BC".

**Observed / reasoning.** ffp3: COBOL0301 'unexpected '"'. Missing space before string literal.' at 85, 2002 and 2023. ffp14: COBOL0307. ScanLiteralState: `if (c is not ('"' or '\''))`. Any quote character opens, pairs or closes the literal, and the next non-quote character after an unpaired ' ends the literal. inLiteral is therefore false at the end of the line, the default arm trims the literal's trailing spaces, and the '-' line is spliced as non-literal text. StripInlineComment in the same file already tracks the opening quote correctly, which gives two literal scanners with different rules (one rule written down twice).

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#ScanLiteralState (l.699-734); #HandleContinuation l.787-831

## Finding — `fixed-form-reference-format` (under-rejects; rows `SR-6.3.5-2`)

**Mechanism.** F4 - fixed-form continuation lines are never validated: HandleContinuation accepts a continuation line whose first non-space is not a quotation symbol, or is the OTHER quotation symbol, and splices it into the literal; it accepts a national literal continued by the fixed indicator; and its non-literal splice silently glues the halves of a multiple-character indicator or separator split across lines (*> , ==), with no diagnostic in any case

**Repro.** Mismatched quote: `VALUE "ABCDE` (to col 72) then `-    'FGH".` (ffp6). No quote: `-    FGH".` (ffp7). National with the fixed indicator: `01 X PIC N(80) VALUE N"ABCDE` then `-    "FGH".` (ffp8b). Split indicator: `DISPLAY X *` then `-    > text` then `.` (ffp9). Split separator: `REPLACE =` then `-    =ABC== BY ==XYZ==.` (ffp9b).

**Expected from the spec.** §6.3.5: 'The first non-space character in the program-text area of the continuation line shall be a quotation symbol matching the quotation symbol used in the opening delimiter'; 'National literals may be continued only with a floating literal continuation indicator'; 'All characters composing any multiple-character separator or multiple-character indicator shall be specified on the same line'. Each of the five sources violates a 'shall' and must be diagnosed.

**Observed / reasoning.** At --std 2023 ffp6, ffp7 and ffp8b compile and run. For ffp6 and ffp7, X is [ABCDE, spaces, FGH]. ffp9 compiles and prints ABC: the code splices `*` and `>` into an inline comment, so the rest of the line silently becomes comment-text. ffp9b compiles and prints Q. The only diagnostic is COBOLNET0903 (obsolete col-7 hyphen). HandleContinuation's `firstChar is not ('"' or '\'')` arm appends the text as 'non-literal continuation', and its quote arm strips any quote character regardless of the opening one, because the opening quote is not tracked (F2). No NAT_BODY distinction exists at this stage. The non-literal splice (trim and concatenate) has no check against forming a multi-character separator or indicator. COBOLNET0902 flags only word-character splices at 2023.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#HandleContinuation (l.762-796, l.824-831)

## Finding — `floating-indicators` (under-rejects; rows `SR-6.2.3.2-6`)

**Mechanism.** Fixed-form literal continuation does not check the continuation line's first nonblank character: HandleContinuation's in-literal arm appends a non-quote character as literal content, and strips ANY quote character as the resume marker because ScanLiteralState tracks only 'in a literal', never which quotation symbol opened it.

**Repro.** fixed form, X PIC X(80): `           MOVE "ABC` padded to column 72, then `      -    DEF" TO X.` (probe E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2b\out\probe\floating-indicators\n26_fixed_cont_noquote); or opened with an apostrophe and continued `      -    \"DEF' TO X.` (n27_fixed_cont_wrongquote); both at --std 2023 and --std 85

**Expected from the spec.** A diagnostic on the continuation line: §6.2.3.2 SR6 'the first nonblank character of each continuation line shall be the quotation symbol used in the opening delimiter of the literal' (also §6.3.5 'The first non-space character in the program-text area of the continuation line shall be a quotation symbol matching the quotation symbol used in the opening delimiter').

**Observed / reasoning.** Both compile silently (only COBOLNET0903 at 2023) and run; n26's literal becomes 'ABC' + 52 spaces + 'DEF'. The correctly continued p28 is right. Sibling to sweep: ScanLiteralState's quote-kind blindness also means an apostrophe INSIDE a quotation-mark literal toggles its literal state (not probed here).

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#HandleContinuation (the 'Not a quote - non-literal continuation' arm and the unconditional resume-marker strip) and #ScanLiteralState

## What the fixer owes

ScanLiteralState must carry the OPENING quotation symbol (and the literal kind: alphanumeric, boolean, national) across lines; HandleContinuation must require the first non-space character of a continuation line to be THAT symbol (diagnose otherwise, never splice), refuse the column-7 hyphen on a national literal (§6.3.5), and refuse a non-literal splice that joins the halves of `*>`, `==` or another multiple-character separator/indicator. One diagnostic code per rule from the orchestrator's allocation. Goldens: `"IT'S A` continued by `-    "BC"` (and the mirror); negatives for the four under-rejects. Same fill unit as PB1359/PB1491.
