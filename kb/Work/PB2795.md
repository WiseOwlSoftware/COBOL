# PB2795 — a returned wave group stays in the dispatch ledger while any of its notes is open

**Found by:** the resume unit of 2026-10-08 16:13 PDT, re-planning wave 1043's dropped B (PB2163) per kb/Work PB2679.

## What happened

Train 1043's landing check stopped B on, among others, the dispatch-ledger entries `w1039B` (PB667, PB1722, PB1941,
PB1942) and `w1039F` (PB850, PB2496, PB2497). PB2679 records them as "recorded 2026-10-08 00:14, never started". They
were started: both implementers RETURNED that night (`w1039b-PB667-report.md` 00:46 and `w1039f-PB850-report.md` 00:39
PDT, Status SPLIT), and their branches landed in later trains and were pruned. Train 1041's D (PB2169, 06:31 PDT) was
stopped by the same two entries, five hours after they returned.

At 16:13 PDT `E:\COBOL-coord\inflight-groups.json` held nine such entries, every one a group whose implementer had
returned a report and whose branch was gone: `w1035A` and `w1037A` (PB165, PB2097: the same group recorded twice, 68 s
apart, at 20:31 and 20:32 PDT on 2026-10-07; only `w1037a-PB165-report.md` exists, returned SPLIT at 22:08), `w1039B`, `w1039C`, `w1039F`, `w1042A`, `w1042B`, `w1042C` and `w1042E`:
129 declared files. B's re-plan collided with four of them (`DataBinder.cs` with w1039B, w1039F and w1042A;
`NumericRenderer.cs` with w1042E). The resume unit released all nine (`plan_wave.py --release <wave><letter>`; the
ledger before the release is `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\resume1613\inflight-groups.before.json`), and a simulated train
of B, F and the notes branch then passed the landing check with 0 stops.

## Mechanism

`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` `record_dispatch` and `record_hand_dispatch` keep every entry "whose notes are not
all terminal", and `inflight_file_sets` (source 3) and `landing_check.py` (`check`, the ledger-entry arm: "its DECLARED
set, started or not") read every such entry as work in flight. A group that returns SPLIT leaves notes open by design
(its unstarted residue goes to a finisher, which records its own entry), so the entry outlives its agent indefinitely
and its WHOLE declared set (31 files for w1039B) keeps stopping every later landing as "dispatched earlier". The ledger
has no notion of a dispatch ending; only note status ends an entry.

## Fix direction

- End a wave group's entry when its dispatch returns, not when its notes turn terminal: the group's report
  (`<scratch>/reports/w<wave><letter>-<PB>-report.md`, which `plan_wave.py` already reads as `Report`) carries a
  `Status:` line. Once that report exists, the group's actual work is its worktree or branch (sources 1 and 2, by actual
  diff), so the declared set is no longer needed to protect it; a finisher re-plans the residue with a new entry.
- One reader for "is this entry in flight" used by `record_dispatch`, `record_hand_dispatch`, `inflight_file_sets` and
  `landing_check.check`, so the planner and the check cannot disagree.
- Drift test: a plant where a group's report says SPLIT and its branch is gone leaves its declared files free; a plant
  where the report is absent keeps them busy (a running group's not-yet-edited files stay protected).
