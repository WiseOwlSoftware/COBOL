# PB5155 - the orchestrate self-tests read the real account's live meter

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/testdata/orchestrate_test_lib.ps1` (`Run-Orch`), `scripts/orchestrator/test_orchestrate_core.ps1`
(whole: cases 1b and 2).

**Found (2026-11-10).** Five gates over two nights went RED on `SELF-TESTS HANG` with 13 or 14 `test_orchestrate_*.ps1` files
hung (the placement gate's run 000002 of worktree wf_f0711751-f32-1 for 13,869 s, dispatch-guard-rule0's run 000003 for
8,932 s), while the same files passed alone and in dispatch-guard-rule0's run 000001. PB3032's 2026-11-09 paragraph took it
for contention, and PB5113 isolated the account part's case 2b after finding it read account 1's real meter.

**Cause.** Every case's supervisor calls `budget.py --json --telemetry-dir <temp>`, and `budget.live_reading` takes the
account's reading from `<config dir>/rate-limits.json`, the file the status line of the developer's own sessions writes
(kb/Work PB4505); the default config dir is `~/.claude`. The temp telemetry directory does not change that. Measured on
2026-11-10 10:12 with main's library: the core part's supervisor printed `budget (account1): weekly 20 % of 42.9 %`, the
developer's real figure. The reading counts only while its `measured_at` is within `LIVE_MAX_AGE`, so the leak was
intermittent: with the account over its daily allowance (36 % of 28.6 % on 11-09, 57 % of 42.9 % on 11-10) and the reading
fresh, `budget.py` answered `hold-day` and each supervisor waited for the next day (`Wait-Until`); with the reading stale, or
under the allowance after the owner's reset, the same tests passed in seconds.

**Fix.** `Run-Orch`, the one place a case launches its supervisor, runs it in a fixture HOME under the test's scratch root
(`$FxHome`, with an empty `.claude`) and with `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` unset; a case that sets its own HOME (the account part,
PB5113) keeps it. Two checks hold it: the core part's unit case asserts the budget line says no reading anchors the week, and
case 1b plants a reading in the fixture HOME and asserts the budget line reports that one (3 %, while the developer's meter
read 20 %). All 14 files pass on the branch (257 checks).

**Sibling, open.** The hang stop did not stop these at 3,600 s (13,869 s and 8,932 s): those trees predate PB5098's
whole-tree kill (#1460). No further change is owed here once a gate runs with #1460.
