# PB5978 - name-table diagnostics cite another edition's clause

Each edition stands on its own text, and a diagnostic's citation names the clause of the edition the program is compiled at.
Two name-table messages do not:
- `DeclarationFact.ClassPhrase` (the "is declared as ..., not as a data item" family) cites §2023 clauses at `--std 85`.
- `SymbolTable.UserWordCensus.ConflictMessage` (COBOL2692) picks its citation with a two-way `Cite(1985, 2023)`, so at
  `--std 2002` and `--std 2014` it cites §2023:8.3.2.2 rather than that edition's own clause.

One root: the citation is chosen per message with an ad hoc edition switch instead of from a per-edition citation table.
The 1985 half is 1985 work; the 2002 and 2014 halves are recorded here and wait for those editions (2002 is frozen until 1985
is complete). Each citation the fix writes is checked with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check` in its own edition.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SymbolTable/DeclarationFact.cs:87` (`DeclarationFact.ClassPhrase`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SymbolTable/SymbolTable.UserWordCensus.cs:73` (`SymbolTable.UserWordCensus.ConflictMessage`)
