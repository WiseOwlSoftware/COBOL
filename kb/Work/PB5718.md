# PB5718 - at --std 2002 under ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD the NUMVAL, NUMVAL-C and NUMVAL-F family accepts a 32-to-34-digit argument (TEST-NUMVAL* return 0, no EC-ARGUMENT-FUNCTION), where 2002 caps every mode at 31 digits

**Sites:**
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/OptionsModel.cs#NumvalDigitCap

**Mechanism.** OptionsModel.NumvalDigitCap maps ArithmeticMode.Standard to 34 (the 2023 standard-decimal cap, §2023:15.93.4 r1b); 2002 has one cap, 31, in every mode.
