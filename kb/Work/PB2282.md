# PB2282 — are DELIMITED (STRING) and BY (PERFORM VARYING) optional at COBOL-85?

Found by wave 1031 group A (kb/Work PB387, report `w1031a-PB387-report.md`, lead A) while it measured the 42 claims of
the deleted stub suite; filed by the train-1031 lander. Not verified.

## Observation

Two statements compile at `--std 85` with no diagnostic:

- `STRING W-A INTO W-B` (no DELIMITED phrase): repro `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1031a\claims\PBC14.cob`. Grammar site:
  the STRING statement rule (`CobolStrings` / the STRING grammar rule).
- `PERFORM VARYING W-N FROM 1 UNTIL W-N > 2` (no BY phrase): repro `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1031a\claims\PBC11.cob`.
  Grammar site: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolControlFlow.g4#performVarying`, whose comment says BY is
  "optional per COBOL-85 spec" and cites nothing.

## What decides it

The X3.23-1985 general formats for STRING and PERFORM. If 1985 prints DELIMITED and BY as required (unbracketed),
both are 85-only under-rejections and need an edition gate. If 1985 brackets them, the grammar is right and the
comment needs its citation. The ISO 2023 formats (`§14.9.43.2`, `§14.9.28.2`) do not answer an 85 question; the
1985 text is not in this repository (`docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` is the place to record the answer).

## Landed

Re-probed on the worktree build (main 5a8125574): the STRING half stays closed by PB1567 (COBOL0900 at 85). The PERFORM
half reproduced: `PERFORM VARYING N FROM 1 UNTIL N > 2` and an AFTER phrase with no BY compiled and ran at `--std 85`
(N=0001, N=0002) with no diagnostic.

The edge. 2002 is QUOTED, no longer derived: general rule 9 of clause 14.8.27.3 of the 2001-12-04 J4 text reads "For any BY
phrase that is omitted, the augment value is 1", the 2023 sentence (§14.9.28.4 GR12). The 1985 edge is derived (VCR row
7.42, row 7.31's method): no NIST CCVS-85 program omits BY (measured: all 459 compile at `--nist` with the gate active),
and GnuCOBOL 3.2 refuses the omission at `-std=cobol85` ("PERFORM VARYING without BY phrase does not conform to COBOL
85") and accepts it at `cobol2002` and later. Against it, recorded in the row: the 2002 Annex F.2 list does not name the
omission.

The fix is a recognition gate, construct `perform-varying-by-omitted-2002` (COBOL0900), in the ParseArm beside the
STRING gate: `VisitPerformVarying` and `VisitPerformVaryingAfter` each report at the phrase that omits BY, so an AFTER
phrase without BY under a VARYING that writes it is reported too. The grammar comments that said "BY is optional per
COBOL-85 spec" (wrong for 85, and citing nothing) now cite GR12 and the VCR row. `PerformVaryingDifferentialTests.Varying_ThreeLevels_OmittedBy`
ran at the default 85 and now runs at 2002.

Goldens: positive `2002/pb2282_perform_varying_by_omitted` (an inline VARYING and an out-of-line AFTER without BY; output
derived from GR12 and GR13 d) / e)); negative `pb2282-perform-varying-by-omitted-below-2002` (the AFTER site); the
constructs.json row's own source witnesses the VARYING site at 85 in the version matrix.
