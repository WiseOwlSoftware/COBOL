# PB2282 — are DELIMITED (STRING) and BY (PERFORM VARYING) optional at COBOL-85?

Found by wave 1031 group A (kb/Work PB387, report `w1031a-PB387-report.md`, lead A) while it measured the 42 claims of
the deleted stub suite; filed by the train-1031 lander. Not verified.

## Observation

Two statements compile at `--std 85` with no diagnostic:

- `STRING W-A INTO W-B` (no DELIMITED phrase): repro `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1031a\claims\PBC14.cob`. Grammar site:
  the STRING statement rule (`CobolStrings` / the STRING grammar rule).
- `PERFORM VARYING W-N FROM 1 UNTIL W-N > 2` (no BY phrase): repro `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1031a\claims\PBC11.cob`.
  Grammar site: `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolControlFlow.g4#performVarying`, whose comment says BY is
  "optional per COBOL-85 spec" and cites nothing.

## What decides it

The X3.23-1985 general formats for STRING and PERFORM. If 1985 prints DELIMITED and BY as required (unbracketed),
both are 85-only under-rejections and need an edition gate. If 1985 brackets them, the grammar is right and the
comment needs its citation. The ISO 2023 formats (`§14.9.43.2`, `§14.9.28.2`) do not answer an 85 question; the
1985 text is not in this repository (`docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md` is the place to record the answer).
