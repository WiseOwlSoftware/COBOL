# PB1715 — the ANTLR lexer never caches its default mode's start state

Filed by the orchestrator from the PB1708 pivot design (workflow wf_08642598-d3c, reviewed APPROVE_WITH_CHANGES, all eleven findings resolved by the reviser), 2026-09-28.
## Defect

`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4`'s default mode has six rules with a semantic predicate at their
LEFT EDGE: `FN_SIGNED_FLOATLIT`, `FN_SIGNED_COMMA_FLOATLIT`, `FN_SIGNED_DECIMALLIT`, `FN_SIGNED_INTEGERLIT`,
`DEFINED` and `FNARG_SEPARATOR`. With a predicate there, ANTLR 4.13.1's `MatchATN` never caches the mode's start
state (confirmed by decompiling `MatchATN`), so every token recomputes it (`ComputeStartState`) and re-adds it with
`AddDFAState` under `lock(dfa.states)`.

## Harm

This is a PRODUCT speed defect. Every `cobol` compile pays ATN simulation per token, serially. Parallel compiles
(the test suite; any host compiling in parallel) contend on one Monitor.

**Evidence** (`docs/rearchitecture/evidence/impact-map-pb1708/b2-scaling/`):
- the in-process CompileNist check-only probe peaks at 3.5x by 8 threads;
- CPU use falls to 15 % at 24 threads;
- there are about 1,400 contended Monitor acquisitions per compile;
- 28 of 30 single-thread samples and 24 of 24 twelve-thread stacks are in `ComputeStartState`/`AddDFAState`, with
  12 of the 24 blocked in `Monitor.Enter`.
It is the root of the continuity cells' one-process limit (b6).

## Fix (PB1708 pivot M6; `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` section 3.14.5 and the section 3.14.9 row)

After warm-up the lexer performs no ATN simulation: move each predicate off the left edge, or turn it into a mode.
`LexerDfaCacheDriftTests` goes red on a planted left-edge predicate and on one placed after a common prefix's first
character.

**Acceptance:**
- the token streams of every suite source are identical before and after (a one-time differential);
- zero ATN simulation on re-lexing the suite's ASCII sources, with the non-ASCII residual reported;
- b2 at 12 threads is at least 8x today's 1-thread wall, with CPU use of at least 70 %;
- the whole battery is green.

## Harm flag

`process_only: true` because the register has no harm flag for COMPILE-TIME PERFORMANCE: the defect changes no
program's output or diagnostics, only how long the compile takes. It is a product defect all the same (it ships in
the `cobol` CLI).
