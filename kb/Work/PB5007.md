# PB5007 - COBOL-85 validation clauses get a generic diagnostic

**Found.** Wave 20261930 group h. At `--std 85` a data description carrying `DEFAULT IS`, `PRESENT WHEN`,
`VALIDATE-STATUS` or `VAL-STATUS` draws COBOL5506 or COBOL1941, the generic "element not of the kind the parse expects",
with or without a declared item, where the clause is an edition matter: the validation facility is a COBOL-2002 addition.
The cause is the grammar: `dataDescriptionClause` has `| {is2002()}? validationClause`
(`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4`), so below 2002 the alternative is not predicted and the words fall to the
generic error.

**Design-dependent.** `Grammar/Core/CobolDeclined.g4` (the header above `validationClause`) states why the predicate is
there: some of these words are ordinary user-defined words at COBOL-85 (§2023:8.9), so the alternative cannot be
recognized at 85 without turning a legal user word into a clause word. The fix is not to drop the predicate. Owed: the
refusal that fires when the generic error lands on a word the later edition reserves as a clause word, naming the
edition that introduced it (the way `SetFormatSelection` and the construct registry name one), without admitting any
source the edition does not. Decide per word which of the seven clause words are reserved at 85 (the header's two-reason
split) and keep a user word legal.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolData.g4` (`dataDescriptionClause`'s validation alternative, `valueClause`'s `validateValidPhrase` tail), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolDeclined.g4` (the file header, `validationClause`'s hook comment), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`ParseArm.VisitValidationClause`, `ParseArm.VisitValidateValidPhrase`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/DeclinedFacilityPass.cs` (`Run`, `ClauseName`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (`ValidateDataDivisionClauseUnsupported`), `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (row `validation-clauses-2002`, the `declined` field), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ConstructRegistry.g.cs`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Constructs.g.cs` (regenerated), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/VersionMatrixTests.cs` (`VersionMatrixCatalogue.ExpectCompiles`, `ExpectedRejection`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/EditionGateArmDriftTests.cs`, `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/EditionReadDriftTests.cs` (`RecordedSites`, `ConstructGates_AreOnlyTheOnesPb2291Found`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/DeclinedFacilityTests.cs`, `docs/VERSION_TEST_MATRIX_DESIGN.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md` (D7), `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (row A.4.14)

**Re-probe (wave 20261936 group C, 2026-11-08).** Reproduced at `--std 85`: `DEFAULT IS "AB"`, `VALIDATE-STATUS IS "E" WHEN
ERROR FOR A` and `VAL-STATUS ...` drew COBOL5506 at the literal ("expected EXTERNAL or GLOBAL"); `PRESENT WHEN`, `CLASS IS
NUMERIC`, `DESTINATION IS A`, `INVALID WHEN` and `VARYING B` drew COBOL1941 at the word. Sibling arm, same gate: the
VALUE clause's format-5 tail `88 C VALUE "Y" IS VALID` / `IS INVALID` drew COBOL1941 at IS.

**Resolution.** The design question the note left open settles against D7 and the token-level reservation gate: the
{is2002()}? predicates were the "construct gate" PB2291 recorded, and their word-status reason (a) is now the
reservation gate's (PB655, PB4958), which retypes a DECLARED free word to a user-defined word and keeps the keyword
token otherwise. So both predicates are deleted (the union grammar, as for the VALIDATE statement, PB3148), and
VersionConformancePass names the edition on recognition through the new DECLINED row `validation-clauses-2002`
(COBOL0900 below 2002; from 2002 DeclinedFacilityPass's COBOL1708, now skipped below the row's introduction). The
matrix gained the declined-row form PB2291 said it lacked (`declined`: a row that compiles at no edition; its reject
code from the introduction is the decline's), with `VersionMatrixCatalogue.ExpectedRejection` the one statement of
the reject code. Legal COBOL-85 names DEFAULT, PRESENT, VALID (a symbolic-character VALUE operand), VALIDATE-STATUS and
VAL-STATUS in the entry-name slot, a KEY list and an INDEXED BY list still compile and run. Goldens:
`conformance:cases/13.18.17-DEFAULT/pb5007-default-clause-below-2002`,
`conformance:cases/13.18.63-VALUE/pb5007-content-validation-below-2002`; theory
`DeclinedFacilityTests.ValidationClausesAt85_AreRefusedByTheirEdition_NotByTheDecline` (ten arms) and
`ValidationClauseWords_AreLegalNamesAt85`. The third PB2291 construct gate (`{is2023()}? applyCommitClause`) is outside
this group and is reported as a lead.
