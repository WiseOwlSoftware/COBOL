# PB937 — OPEN — one grammar rule where the standard prints two formats, and one figure nobody rendered

Source: the `PB412` report, "New defects" items 2 and 3. One note: both are the §14.9.38 / §14.9.39
figure family and both want the PDF rendered before a line of grammar moves.

```

    > `ON EXCEPTION` / `NOT ON EXCEPTION` mean zero or more of the alternatives, each at most once, in any
    > order — both bra…
```

The inventory row `FMT-14.9.38.2` carries **no verdict and no claimer**, and
`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolParserCore.g4#mcsSendStatement` merges the clause's TWO general
formats into ONE rule with the Format-1 and Format-2 tails both optional. That is exactly the shape the
wave-41 **PB412 + PB421 + PB402** group landed a fix for elsewhere — one grammar alternative per PRINTED
general format — and SEND is the member it did not reach. Its RAISING tail is already correct after that
wave; the format merge is not.

## Arm 2 — `SET LAST EXCEPTION TO OFF`'s underlining has never been measured

`CobolParserCore.g4#setLastExceptionStatement : SET LAST EXCEPTION TO OFF` requires both words. §14.9.39
Format 13 prints them — but **each printing has its own underlining**, and that is the whole lesson of
**PB402**: `EXCEPTION` is underlined in one arm of §14.9.18's brace and plain in another. If this figure
prints the second `EXCEPTION` plain, as the other three printings do, then `SET LAST TO OFF` is conforming
source that is refused outright.

```
$ python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 5.2.3 "They are shown in uppercase and not underlined in general formats"
OK  §5.2.3   (Optional words)
```

⛔ **Render the figure first** — `scripts/render-spec-page.py <page>` — and measure the underline bands, as
PB402 did (it recorded box coordinates and percentages). `feedback_spec_diagrams_render_pdf` and
`feedback_underlining_not_bracketing`: the OCR'd diagrams were systematically lossy toward falsely
restrictive syntax, and a second printing must be checked before a rule is relaxed. The
`rejects_legal_source` flag is left FALSE here deliberately: until the figure is rendered, whether legal
source is refused is unknown, and a flag set on a guess is worse than no flag.

The exclusion is currently made visible by
`…DriftTests.TheSetLastExceptionStatement_IsADistinctFigureAndIsNotSweptHere`, which is honest cover, not
a fix.

## Why the two arms are one note

Both are consequences of **PB931**'s blind audit: an audit that can see a word's underlining per
OCCURRENCE would have produced both findings mechanically. Until it exists, they are found by hand, one
figure at a time — which is the argument for PB931 and the reason these two are clustered with it.
