# PB4749 — retire the loop's meter unit

**What was wrong.** On 2026-11-03 the owner said not to read the quota through the Chrome extension. PB4505 made
`budget.py` anchor on the status line's cached `rate_limits` (`<config dir>/rate-limits.json`). The loop kept its `meter`
unit anyway. `next_unit.py` rule 3 chose it whenever `readings.json` held no reading of the account younger than 3 hours
(`quota.meter_max_age_hours`), and `units/meter.md` told the unit to read the usage page with Claude in Chrome.
`readings.json` is written only by a meter unit or by hand, so the rule fired every cycle. Each failed attempt ran a
Sonnet session that tried Chrome, found the extension disconnected and ended in an ENVIRONMENT FAILURE; the
unreadable-meter gate (PB2665) only spaced the retries 3 hours apart. Logged attempts: 2026-11-04 14:27, 17:36, 17:37,
17:40 and 17:42, and 2026-11-05 01:19 and 10:05. On 2026-11-05 the owner said: "You have been told multiple times not
to use the chrome extension for meter readings. We cache the statusline meter readings."

**As landed.** No rule chooses a unit to read the quota meter.
- `next_unit.py`: rule 3 and its machinery are deleted (`meter_age_hours`, `meter_failure_run`, `meter_unreadable`,
  `meter_due`, `METER_ATTEMPTS`, `attempted_at`). Rules 4 to 7 become 3 to 6, and `choose` no longer takes the meter
  age or the account.
- `orchestrate.ps1`: the `meter` unit and its model are gone, along with the read-only unit handling that only it used
  (`$ReadOnlyUnit`, `Clear-NamedUnit`, `Report-MeterUnreadable`, the `environment_failure` field and its backoff) and its
  `--chrome` launch.
- `steer.ps1`, `handoff.schema.json` (the `meter` unit and next_unit, `meter_reading`) and `model_rules.json`
  (`quota.meter_max_age_hours`) no longer know the meter. The session-start hook drops its meter-unit skills line, and
  `units/meter.md` is deleted.
- The history's existing `meter` lines in `units.jsonl` stay readable: `enqueue_chains` still skips them.
- `budget.py --record` stays, for a reading the owner enters by hand.

**Docs.** `DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md` (the coordination table, the unit table, section 3.2's rules and their
renumbered references, the breaker, the launch, the handoff fields, the failure table, the Anchor bullet, the self-test
list), MANDATORY-PRACTICES O3 (the meter is `budget.py`, never the usage page), `units/enqueue.md` and the account
seed's `_doc` in `model_rules.json` (the Chrome flags stay, for the attended sessions' browser tools, but no unit reads
the quota through them).

**Witnesses.**
- `test_next_unit.py`: no reading, a 54-hour-old reading, and the history's retired meter lines each choose `wave`. The
  rule-5 lane cases and the PB4180 cases are renumbered.
- `test_orchestrate_core.ps1`: no unit is launched with `--chrome`. It also takes over the archived-handoff case from the
  deleted `test_orchestrate_meter.ps1`.
- `test_orchestrate_campaign.ps1`: a coordination directory with no reading chooses `wave`.
- `test_orchestrate_steer.ps1` and `test_checkpoint.py` use `resume` where they used `meter`.
- The fake CLI (`testdata/fake-claude.ps1`) loses its `meterfail` mode, and its handoffs name `resume`.

**Sibling retired.** PB3792 (open: test_orchestrate_meter.ps1 never exercised Report-MeterUnreadable's dedupe) is retired, since both are deleted here.
