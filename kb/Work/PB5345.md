# PB5345 - the PB5208 power arm of NotAlwaysIntegral fails open on a literal base (2 ** M, M signed), on base = exponent item (M ** M), an expression base and a power under a product or negation, and no drift test pins which shapes are witness arms and which are recorded fail-open

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/IntrinsicArgumentRules.cs` (`IntrinsicArgumentRules.PowerWitness`, `IntrinsicArgumentRules.NotAlwaysIntegral`, `IntrinsicArgumentRules.ProvablyIntegral`)

**Mechanism.** PB5208 refuses an argument whose type-6 arithmetic expression is not always an integer when it contains `c ** M` with a signed or fractional exponent (`PowerWitness`, IntrinsicArgumentRules.cs:1786). The arm fails open (accepts) on: a literal base (`2 ** M`, M signed), base = exponent item (`M ** M`), an expression base, and a power under a product or negation. The lead states a sound witness exists for `c ** M` with |c| >= 2 integer. The fail-open shapes are recorded only in prose (the `PowerWitness` doc, INTRINSICS_DESIGN.md).

**Fix direction (as the leads state it).** (1) Add the sound witness for a literal integer base with |c| >= 2. (2) Pair the arm with a drift test that every BoundExpr shape `ProvablyIntegral` rejects is either a witness arm of `NotAlwaysIntegral` or listed as fail-open in a table the test reads (PB5208's own fix direction asked for that test; the branch has none). Citation as the report gives it: `OK §1989:A.2.2` AR4 "An arithmetic expression which will always result in an integer value must be specified." and §2023:15.3 type 6 (not re-run here).

**Source.** PB5130 report lead 2 and review lead R1-3 (info, not blocking at round 1).
