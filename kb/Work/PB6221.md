# PB6221 — the 2014 table is 2023's Annex E reversed

**2014 waits.** The owner's order of 2026-11-15 puts 2014 after the other three editions (memory: 2014-after-the-other-three).
Do not plan this note before the owner lifts that order.

**Mechanism.** `Edition2014` in `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/CobolCharacterRepertoire.cs` derives 2014's table by reversing 2023's
Annex E, so U+00B5 is `NotFirst` at 2014. A word ending in it (`01 Xµ`) is accepted at `--std 2014`. §2014:8.1.2.1 GR4b (checked
with `cite.py --check`: the clause says "Characters identified as special characters in Annex B shall not be written as the first
or last character") refuses it, so the characters are medial, not merely not-first.

**Fix shape.** Derive the 2014 table from 2014's own Annex B (the special row and the combining rows become `Medial`), as PB5702
did for 2002; a test per edition pins `Xµ` and `µX`, both refused at 2014.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/CobolCharacterRepertoire.cs` (`Edition2014`)
