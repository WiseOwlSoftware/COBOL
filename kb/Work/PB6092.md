# PB6092 - a note merged during planning is planned again

**Observed.** A dry run at 15:50 listed `worktree-wf_7b34b83a-2b6-7` (PB3319) as "awaiting landing (excluded)". The
real run minutes later planned PB3319 as group E of wave 20262140 and recorded the dispatch. #1961 had merged at
23:52:20Z (15:52 PST), during that run. `kb/Work/PB3319.md` reads `status: landed` on origin/main and on the branch,
whose tip is on main.

**Mechanism (to confirm in the fix).** The planner reads the open notes from the checkout (`E:\COBOL` at its last
fast-forward, behind origin/main by #1961). The branch survey fetches and reads the branch as LANDED, so the branch
stops excluding its notes, while the checkout still holds them open. `adopt_main.require_current` compares the checkout
with the commit the loop adopted, not with origin/main as fetched by the same run.

**Handled by hand.** `plan_wave.py --release 20262140e`, and group E removed from the wave args before launch.

**Fix.** Read the register at the same commit the survey fetched (origin/main after the fetch), or re-check every
planned group's notes against origin/main just before `record_dispatch` and drop a landed one. Drift test: a fixture
where a branch reads LANDED and the checkout's note reads open must plan nothing for that note.
