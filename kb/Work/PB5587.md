# PB5587 - at --std 85 a sort-merge file control entry's ASSIGN TO SPACE (any figurative constant) or a list of two operands is refused (COBOL2645, COBOL2256) by the 2023 rules §2023:12.4.5.2 SR4 and SR5, where §1985:XI.2.3.3 restricts literal-1 in neither way

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/AssignTargetRule.cs` (`AssignTargetRule.Resolve`, `AssignTargetRule.Text`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/FileControlBinder.cs` (`FileControlBinder.BindFileControl`); `docs/conformance/7/DOC-A.1-71.md` (the determination's 1985 basis)

**Mechanism.** `AssignTargetRule` is the one reader of the ASSIGN TO list for every file control entry, and it applies the 2023 rules at every edition: `Text` refuses a figurative constant as literal-1 (COBOL2645, "ISO §2023:12.4.5.2 SR4"), and `Resolve` refuses a list of two or more operands (COBOL2256) under the DOC-A.1-71 determination of `§2023:12.4.5.2 SR5`. At 1985 the sequential, relative, indexed and report entries each carry their own literal rule, "Literal-1 must be a nonnumeric literal and must not be a figurative constant." (`§1985:VII.2.3.3 SR3`, copied in VIII, IX and XIII), which DOC-A.1-71's 1985 basis names. The sort-merge entry has no such rule: §1985:XI.2.3.2 prints `SELECT file-name-1 ASSIGN TO {implementor-name-1 | literal-1} ...` (the ellipsis printed; the refuter rendered PDF page 554, folio XI-2) and §1985:XI.2.3.3 carries only SR1 and SR2, "only the ASSIGN clause is permitted to follow file-name-1" (`§1985:XI.2.3.3 SR2`). With no restriction, "A figurative constant may be used whenever 'literal' appears in a format" (`§1985:IV.4.2.2.2.3 GRL2.3`, whose exceptions are the numeric-only literals). So the binder refuses, on a 2023 rule, a 1985 sort-merge entry the 1985 format admits.

**Expected (from the 1985 text).** At --std 85 a sort-merge entry's ASSIGN admits a figurative constant as literal-1 and a list of operands, and the SORT runs. The weaker arm: the list refusal may be defended under §1985:I.1.7's implementation limits (the number of operands a statement may carry), but then DOC-A.1-71 must state that limit for §1985:XI.2.3.2; it names only VII, VIII and XIII today, and a figurative-constant refusal is a restriction of kind, which no limit covers.

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** `AssignTargetRule` must take the literal rule of the entry it reads, by organization and edition, from one table, not apply the 2023 rule to every entry: at 1985 the sort-merge entry has none, the other entries have their SR3. Restate DOC-A.1-71's 1985 basis for XI.2.3.2 (its list determination), and pair the table with a drift test over the four 1985 file control formats. Sweep: the 2002 and 2014 sort-merge entries, and every other screen in `FileControlKeyRules` that applies a 2023 rule to an SD entry at 1985.
