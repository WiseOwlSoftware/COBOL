**Scenario:** ReportWriterBinder writes ctx.Refs.EnclosingReport and ctx.Refs.VaryingScope eleven times (ReportWriterBinder.cs:85-86, 97-98, 129, 134, 136, 148, 150, 187, 190), and the committer holds them for its Prober, so every report-section operand resolves under whatever value the last assignment left. BindPresentWhen ends by writing VaryingScope = null rather than restoring the caller's scope, so a call placed inside a scoped region (the VARYING loop at 129, the field loop at 134) silently drops the counters SR2 puts in scope and the next name resolves as no counter, which is the shape of defect PB3230 removed for Probing. SegmentDeferred is saved and restored by hand in two entries (ReferenceResolver.cs:299-310 ResolveHere, 536-548 ResolveForItem) and written from SubscriptBinder.MaterializePosition:901; _enclosingCapture is saved and restored in CaptureEnclosingSubscripts (SubscriptBinder.cs:155-164), whose comment still says Save/restored like Probing although Probing is now a construction constant. Each new entry point or report clause must remember the save/restore discipline, and a missed one gives a wrong resolution with no diagnostic.
