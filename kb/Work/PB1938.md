# PB1938 — OPEN

Filed by the train-1006 lander from wave 1006 group D's report (`w1006d-PB1144-report.md`, "New defects", item 2).

## Defect

PB1144 (landed in train 1006) binds §14.6.9.2's element moves ("Correspondingly numbered elements are moved according
to the rules of the MOVE statement") beside the group move, reaching each table from its group's resolved place with
`MoveBinder.TableCursor`. A level-66 THROUGH alias is a group item (§13.18.45.4 GR2), but its atoms are its span's
leaves and the tables are not subordinates of the alias item, so `TableCursor` returns null. The element move then
carries `Unsupported` and `MoveEmitter.EmitElementMoves` renders a loud run-time stop instead of moving the elements.
It only matters when the corresponding elementary elements differ in description (identical or group elements take
the image transfer and need no element move).

**Fix shape.** Walk the alias's span to the table (the renamed record's place plus the table's path), or give the
cursor a RENAMES arm. Golden: a variable-length group MOVE whose sender is a THROUGH alias over a table of `PIC 9(4)`
moved to a corresponding `PIC 9(2)V99` table.

**Site.** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/MoveBinder.cs#TableCursor` (and `ElementMove`'s
`Unsupported` arm).
