# PB5100 - a wave should not start more gates than the host has slots

**Owner (2026-11-09, relayed by Mythos at 23:59):** "We are wasting my money, tokens, and time and not accomplishing notable
work. Way too much is waste reruning the same thigns repeatedly and simply waiting far too long for nothing." Mythos's
request: hold new code-lane gates beyond the 3 slots rather than queueing them.

**Found.** `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` sets `wave.concurrency` to 6, and `plan_wave.py` passes it to
`wf_rolling_wave.js` as `CONC`. The gate pool (`scripts/gate_slot.py`, `GateSettings.effective_cap`) holds 3 slots, and the
Mythos lane's gates share it. PB4888 measured gates at 86 % of a group's fix-to-review time, so six workers keep about six
gates in flight for three slots. On 2026-11-09, wave 20261973 (8 groups) had groups B and E waiting in the slot queue at
22:55, and C's gate2 waited 1,601 s before it took a slot. PB5060's re-gate (wave 20261972) waited 1,448 s. Conformance
took 34 minutes under that load, where its median is about 10. Every waiting implementer holds an Opus or Sonnet session
open.

**Expected.** The wave's concurrency is derived from the gate pool it feeds, never hand-set. `plan_wave.py` reads
`GateSettings.effective_cap` and caps the wave's concurrency at it, less the slots other live lanes hold (read from the slot
directory's tickets), so a fourth implementer is not started while three gates run. A drift test pins the derivation: a
planted cap of 3 yields a concurrency of 3, and a `model_rules.json` value above the cap is refused. The workstream skill's
"at most 6 implementers" text and `wf_rolling_wave.js`'s `(6)` default change with it.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`plan_main`), `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` (whole: the `wave.concurrency` key is removed or becomes a ceiling), `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/wf_rolling_wave.js` (`CONC`)
