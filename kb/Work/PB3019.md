# PB3019 — the saturating intermediate float landing

Filed 2026-10-11 by the train 1059 lander from `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1059d-PB2967-report.md` (lead 2);
not re-probed by the lander.

**Site.** `CobolFloat.ToScaledSaturating` (src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolFloat.cs ~line 355), emitted by
`RuntimeApi.FloatToScaledIntermediate` from `NumericRenderer.Align` / `AlignRoundedUp` (NumericRenderer.cs ~lines
1049, 1095) and called by `CobolDate.SecondsOfReal`. PB2967 (train 1059) made every float-to-fixed STORE raise past
the carrier and kept this form for intermediate consumers, which have no store to be fooled. But two binary64 values
past the carrier both land as Int128.MaxValue, so an intermediate that compares or selects among them loses the
order: `FUNCTION MAX` of 1E300 and 1E301 float arguments, aligned to a common scale, may compare equal and select the
wrong one. The SDIDI lane's twin, `CobolDec.ToUnscaledIntermediate`, raises EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW (§14.7.5 case 5,
DOC-A.1-179 "checked") for the same fact.

**Fix shape (expected).** `ToScaledSaturating` raises EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW past the carrier, symmetric with the SDIDI
intermediate; sweep its callers (the date-seconds caller documents saturation as "out of the time form" and needs its
own decision) and the goldens that observe a saturated intermediate. Repro to write: COMP-2 items holding 1.0E+300 and
1.0E+301, `COMPUTE R = FUNCTION MAX(F1, F2)` and an ORD-MAX twin, NATIVE arithmetic.

## Landed 2026-10-12 (wave 1069 A)

**Re-probe on the wave-1069 build: the note's own repro does NOT reproduce, the mechanism does.** `FUNCTION MAX(F1, F2)`,
`ORD-MAX`, `MEDIAN`, `SUM`, `RANGE` over COMP-2 items of 1E300 / 1E301 (alone, beside a fixed-point item, as `F + 0`
expressions) all answer correctly: an all-float list rides the binary64 body, a mixed list the SDIDI selection body
(`IntrinsicRenderer`, PB65), and relations compare in binary64, so none of them aligns a float through
`ToScaledSaturating`. What still landed through it: every receiver-less `NumericRenderer.Align` / `AlignRoundedUp` consumer
of a `Real` operand, notably the native DIVIDE ... REMAINDER operand (PB3017 lead 2, observable: `DIVIDE F BY 7 GIVING Q
REMAINDER R` with F = 1.0E+40 stored a quotient of the sentinel) and the formatted-time seconds.

**Fix.** `CobolFloat.ToScaledSaturating` is replaced by `CobolFloat.ToScaledIntermediate`: NaN -> 0, in the carrier the
exact landing, past it EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW (§14.7.5 5)), the twin of `CobolDec.ToUnscaledIntermediate`. The saturating form has no
caller left and is deleted (no shim); `RuntimeApi.FloatToScaledIntermediate` emits the new one. The date functions'
"out of the standard numeric time form" answer is a membership question, so `CobolDate.SecondsOfReal` states its own screen
(NaN and |seconds| >= 10^19 saturate by sign, the bound `SecondsOfDec` uses) and lands the rest through the intermediate form.
Subscripts and reference modification do not come through `Align` (they clamp to the host int, PB2938) and are unchanged.

**Tests.** `CarrierLandingFormTests` (the kernel pair and the oracle sweep now expect the raise),
`CobolDateSecondsLandingTests.ABinary64PastTheBound_SaturatesBySign_WhereTheIntermediateLandingRaises`, golden
`2023/pb3019_native_float_intermediate_past_the_carrier_is_a_size_error`. No traceability-inventory row is claimed or closed.
