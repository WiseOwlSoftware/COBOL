## Landed (wave 1031 C, 2026-10-07, with kb/Work PB2093)

Slice 2 landed as the fix for PB2093, which it was written to close: `CsNames.Allocate(baseName, scope)` and the
seeded `CsNameScope` (`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/CsNames.cs`) replaced `DataBinder.Unique` at every caller
(the class-level roots through `CsNameScope.ProgramClass()`, a group's members through `DataItem.AddMember`, now the
only way a member joins a group); the collision fixture
`tests/conformance/2002/pb2093_member_names_like_generated_members` compiles and runs; `CsNameReservationDriftTests`
keeps both seeds total. Only programs that failed to compile get new names, so no existing golden output moved. The
design's §4.5, its change table and its slice list are updated to this state. The published scopes (host entry,
published record structs) are slice 3's and reuse the allocator.
