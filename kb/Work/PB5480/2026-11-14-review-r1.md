`NoteRecordRead` capped `LastReadLength` at `VaryMax`, but `SequentialConnector.RewriteRecordSequential` also read it as "the number of bytes in the
record being replaced" (§2023:14.9.35.4 GR16), so a REWRITE of a `VaryMax`-length record after a READ of a longer record answered `00` and half-replaced
the physical record. Two facts had been folded into one: the delivered length (DEPENDING ON) and the physical length (REWRITE). Fix: the REWRITE compares
`CurrentRecord.Length` (the physical record, unchanged by the cap). Golden `wiseowl/cases/14.9.35-REWRITE/pb5480_rewrite_after_truncated_read_85`
(85, 2002, 2014, 2023): `READ 04 LR=10`, `REWRITE 44`, the writer rereads the record unchanged.
