# PB4979 - the remaining arms of PB4923's float-content rule

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolExact.cs` (`CobolExact.SubsidiaryQuotient`, `CobolExact.Quotient`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.ExactValue.cs` (`CobolIntrinsics.VarianceExact`, `CobolIntrinsics.PresentValueExact`, `CobolIntrinsics.PresentValueBig`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Dec.cs` (`CobolIntrinsics.AnnuityDec`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs` (`NumericRenderer.Power`).

**Not PB4923.** PB4923 (landed, DOC-A.1-92b) decided that a nested selection returning a float item's content renders as floating-point and fixed SUM/MEAN/MIDRANGE/RANGE and the
arithmetic arms `+ - * / **` (the PB4923 note's Sites do not name `SubsidiaryQuotient`, `VarianceExact`, `PresentValueExact` or `AnnuityDec`; its sibling sweep says "Not covered (registered below): SubsidiaryQuotient, Quotient").
Same root: the bodies below do not read the mark `CobolExact.FromBinary64` sets.

1. **DIVIDE ... REMAINDER** over a float-content exact operand: `CobolExact.SubsidiaryQuotient` (and `Quotient`) stay exact. Unprobed.
2. **VARIANCE, STANDARD-DEVIATION, PRESENT-VALUE, ANNUITY over a nested float selection** keep the exact value (R1-2, low); sit in bodies the PB4923 change did not touch.
   Probes: `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/review-w20261921-f/p1.cob` (G1/G2) and `p2.cob`.

**Needed.** Probe each (a float item vs the same value reached through `MAX (F M2)`), decide against DOC-A.1-92b, fix at the one mark reader per body (PB4923's shape: `OfBinary64Result` / `TryBinary64Operands`), add golden lines to
`15.88.4-Returned-value-rule/pb4923_arithmetic_over_a_nested_selection_of_a_float_is_binary64`.

## Landed (wave 20261925, group B)

Re-probed on the build (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261925b/a/p88.cob`, `p89.cob`, and the reviewer's `p1.cob`/`p2.cob`). Both claims reproduced, the DISPLAY channel too: `VARIANCE (FUNCTION MAX (F M2) M)` showed .03999999999999999888977697537484347 where `VARIANCE (F M)` shows 0.04 (and STANDARD-DEVIATION .1999999999999999972 against 0.2, PRESENT-VALUE .28888888888888889259 against 0.28888888888888886, ANNUITY .4021148036253776474 against 0.4021148036253777), and `DIVIDE M BY FUNCTION MAX (F M2) GIVING FQ REMAINDER FR` (FQ PIC S9V9(5)) stored FR .00000099999999991773 where `DIVIDE M BY F` stores 0: the quotient was the binary64 5.0 but the subsidiary quotient was the exact operands' 4.99999999999999972244 truncated to 4.99999. Decision against DOC-A.1-92b: the same run-time mark makes them binary64 evaluations. Fix at the one mark reader per body: `VarianceExact`, `StdDevExact`, `PresentValueExact` and the new `AnnuityExact` return `CobolExact` and ask `TryBinary64Body` (new `floatFamily` flag: these four are the floating-math family, so their binary64 value is an approximation that lands by its shortest decimal, as `VARIANCE (F M)` does, never a float item's content); `CobolExact.SubsidiaryQuotient` truncates the binary64 quotient when an operand is a float item's content. `CobolExact.Quotient` is the exact kernel its callers choose (`DivToOdd`, `QuotientAtScale`, `DivTruncated`, `SubsidiaryQuotient`, MEAN, MEDIAN), each of which reads the mark; it needed no change. `IntrinsicRenderer.RenderDec` emits the four as `NumX.Exact`; `FunctionValueLane.NativeSdidiBodiesWithWholeResult` names them. Sibling sweep (`p90.cob`): ABS, FRACTION-PART, REM, INTEGER-PART and INTEGER over a nested selection of a float item already equal the float item's value; MOD takes integer arguments only (COBOL1627).
