# PB5538 - at --std 2002+ ENTER draws two errors for one statement (COBOL0902 and COBOL7240): BindEnter does not consult the removal row

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/StatementBinder.cs` (`BindEnter`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (`EnterStatementNotProvided`, COBOL7240).

**Mechanism.** kb/Work PB5308 made `BindEnter` refuse every ENTER with COBOL7240 ("the statement is not provided"). At --std 2002 and later (strict) ENTER is already a removed construct and draws COBOL0902 from the edition gate, so one statement draws two errors. BindEnter could consult the removal row (the construct is gated off at this edition) and stay silent when the gate already refused it, so one statement draws one diagnostic.

**Expected.** One diagnostic per ENTER statement at every edition: COBOL7240 at --std 85, COBOL0902 at 2002+.
