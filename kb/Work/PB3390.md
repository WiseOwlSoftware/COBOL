# PB3390 — LANDED — the residue run's opening and tail sets are one production for every format

Filed 2026-10-18 from the w1126b review finisher's report (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1126b-PB3373-report.md`, "New defects (note-ready)" leads 1 and 2) and the "Not admitted at the opening" and "Not closed here" paragraphs of `kb/Work/PB3373.md` (landed, PR #262). Register check: a grep of kb/Work by `residueOpening`, `residueItem`, `genericClause`, `numericLiteralCore`, `ClosedFormatPass`, `NoViableAlt`, `quotes XYZ` and `COBOL2423` found no note on these two shapes (PB3373 only). Decided from the PB3373 text: leads (a) and (b) are ONE mechanism, a single `genericClause` production whose opening and tail token sets must serve every closed format although the right reading of two tokens depends on the format. Lead (c) is filed apart as PB3391.

## What happens

(a) A bare leading-comma decimal does not open the residue run. With DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA, `OBJECT-COMPUTER. XYZ ,5.` still quotes XYZ as the unrecognized clause (the computer-name misattributed, as on main before PB3373). Repro `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1126b\fin\p\s6.cob`. The shape's one spelling is `numericLiteralCore`'s `{tokensAreContiguous(2)}? COMMA INTEGERLIT`; admitting it at the opening means copying that alternative, or splitting it into a rule that `ArithmeticFormationRules.IsBareNumericLiteral`, `DataBinder.Switches.IntegerLiteralDigits` and `SignedLiteralShapeDriftTests` would all have to follow (the implementer's words).

(b) The residue's unsigned integer is format-dependent. `SOURCE-COMPUTER. XYZ 12.` and `OBJECT-COMPUTER. XYZ 12.` read XYZ as the residue. `01 X PIC X FOO` then `05 Y PIC X.` with the period forgotten reads `FOO 05 Y` as one run and Y's PICTURE as X's second, COBOL2423. A level-number (ISO 8.3.2.2.13) can follow only the data division's lists, so the unsigned integer must not continue a run there, while in the environment-division formats it must open and continue one. One production cannot express that.

Harm: a misattributed or misleading diagnostic; every probe is refused (the lead states process_only).

## Root (the implementer's, not adjudicated here)

The residue opening's set cannot be format-dependent. Leads (a) and (b) are the two tokens (leading comma, unsigned integer) the one shared set must treat differently per format. The fix shape is a decision for the implementer reading 12.3.5.2, 12.3.6.2 and 8.3.2.2.13: a per-format residue (or a predicate on the format) rather than a single set.

## Landed (w1129b, 2026-10-19) — the error production has two forms; the data division's run holds no unsigned integer

**Re-probed on the branch's own build** (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1129b\p\a*.cob`, `before.txt` / `after1.txt` /
`after2.txt`): all four shapes reproduced on 9ea0899ea — `OBJECT-COMPUTER. XYZ ,5.`, `SOURCE-COMPUTER. XYZ ,5.`,
`OBJECT-COMPUTER. XYZ 12.` and `XYZ 1,5.` quoted XYZ (COBOL1970, and COBOL1971 "not a paragraph" for
SOURCE-COMPUTER), and `01 X PIC X FOO` then `05 Y PIC X.` drew COBOL2423 for Y's PICTURE plus COBOL1941 for FOO.

**Root, confirmed.** One production cannot hold a token in one list and refuse it in another: an unsigned integer may
be the next entry's level-number (ISO §8.3.2.2.13: "A level-number, expressed as a one-digit or two-digit number")
only after the data division's three lists (the data description entry, §13.16.2; the FD, §13.4.5.2; the SD,
§13.4.6.2), and nothing after any other list begins with one. So the error production now has TWO FORMS:
`unrecognizedClause : (residueOpening | residueInteger) (residueItem | residueInteger)*` for every list no
level-number follows, and `unrecognizedDataClause : residueOpening residueItem*` for the three data division lists.
`residueOpening` gained the leading-comma decimal (`{tokensAreContiguous(2)}? COMMA INTEGERLIT`), `residueItem` is
`residueOpening | AMPERSAND concatOperand`, and `residueInteger` is the integer and the integer-led comma decimal.
`genericClause` is gone. The data form excludes `1,5` too, though it is no level-number (a comma that no space follows
is no separator, §8.3.5 2)): its first token is INTEGERLIT, and a predicated alternative that is the only one viable
at its first token throws NoViableAlt there when its predicate fails (`05 Y` would draw one). The parse layer refuses
it instead: `CobolErrorStrategy`'s after-period exclusion spares a token that heads a longer literal
(`CobolParserCoreBase.NumericLiteralWidth`, now the one reading of a literal's extent, shared with
`numericLiteralIsLeftOperand`), and the refusal quotes the literal whole (`'1,5'`).

**After:** `XYZ 12` / `XYZ ,5` / `XYZ 1,5` after either computer-name quote the literal in the paragraph's own format;
`01 X PIC X ,5.` and `01 X PIC X 1,5.` are COBOL1941 for the literal; `01 X PIC X FOO` then `05 Y PIC X.` (and the FD
and SD twins before `01 R`) is the one COBOL0307 missing period at the level-number. `01 W-X PIC X FOO 1,5.` now quotes
`'1,5'` (the run FOO ends before it) where it quoted FOO.

**Determination (the shape).** Two named productions over a semantic predicate on the format: a predicate on the
integer items would be the sole survivor at `05` and throw NoViableAlt (the PB3373 trap) and would take the decision
off the DFA cache. `ClosedFormats.ErrorProductionRules` is the one place the parse layer learns the two rules.
`ClosedFormatDriftTests.EachList_WritesTheFormItsFollowSetCallsFor` derives the form from the generated ATN — the data
form exactly where INTEGERLIT may begin what follows the list's period — so the next closed format is held to it.

**Goldens:** `cases/12.3.6-OBJECT-COMPUTER/pb3390-object-computer-integer-clause` (COBOL1970 '12'),
`cases/12.3.5-SOURCE-COMPUTER/pb3390-source-computer-comma-decimal-clause` (COBOL1970 ',5'),
`cases/13.16-Data-description/pb3390-data-entry-level-number-after-run` (COBOL0307 at '05'), each refused at 85, 2002,
2014 and 2023; `cases/12.3.7-SPECIAL-NAMES/pb3390_integer_lists_beside_the_residue` accepted at 85 (`BCD`: integer
lists a clause owns keep their integers — SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS `ARE 2 4 IN AL-LIT`, `RESERVE 2 AREAS`, `MEMORY SIZE
8000 CHARACTERS`, `SEGMENT-LIMIT 50`; §12.3.7.4 GR11 b) and GR7 k) 2.). `ClosedFormatReservedWordTests`: sixteen
literal cases (every format with an integer or comma decimal) and the level-number control over the data entry, the
FD and the SD.

**Rows:** none re-verdicted; FMT-12.3.5.2, FMT-12.3.6.2, FMT-13.16.2, FMT-13.4.5.2 and FMT-13.4.6.2 were already
CONFORMS (every probe was refused before; the landing changes which word is named and the missing-period diagnosis).

**Not closed here:** `OBJECT-COMPUTER. XYZ FOO IS 12.` still draws the NoViableAlt of a predicated sole survivor
(`kb/Work/PB3391`): IS is reserved, so no form of the run can hold it, and the root is the text-predicated
`segmentLimitClause`, not the run's token set.
