# PB3390 — OPEN — the residue run's opening and tail sets are one production for every format

Filed 2026-10-18 from the w1126b review finisher's report (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1126b-PB3373-report.md`, "New defects (note-ready)" leads 1 and 2) and the "Not admitted at the opening" and "Not closed here" paragraphs of `kb/Work/PB3373.md` (landed, PR #262). Register check: a grep of kb/Work by `residueOpening`, `residueItem`, `genericClause`, `numericLiteralCore`, `ClosedFormatPass`, `NoViableAlt`, `quotes XYZ` and `COBOLNET2423` found no note on these two shapes (PB3373 only). Decided from the PB3373 text: leads (a) and (b) are ONE mechanism, a single `genericClause` production whose opening and tail token sets must serve every closed format although the right reading of two tokens depends on the format. Lead (c) is filed apart as PB3391.

## What happens

(a) A bare leading-comma decimal does not open the residue run. With DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA, `OBJECT-COMPUTER. XYZ ,5.` still quotes XYZ as the unrecognized clause (the computer-name misattributed, as on main before PB3373). Repro `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1126b\fin\p\s6.cob`. The shape's one spelling is `numericLiteralCore`'s `{tokensAreContiguous(2)}? COMMA INTEGERLIT`; admitting it at the opening means copying that alternative, or splitting it into a rule that `ArithmeticFormationRules.IsBareNumericLiteral`, `DataBinder.Switches.IntegerLiteralDigits` and `SignedLiteralShapeDriftTests` would all have to follow (the implementer's words).

(b) The residue's unsigned integer is format-dependent. `SOURCE-COMPUTER. XYZ 12.` and `OBJECT-COMPUTER. XYZ 12.` read XYZ as the residue. `01 X PIC X FOO` then `05 Y PIC X.` with the period forgotten reads `FOO 05 Y` as one run and Y's PICTURE as X's second, COBOLNET2423. A level-number (ISO 8.3.2.2.13) can follow only the data division's lists, so the unsigned integer must not continue a run there, while in the environment-division formats it must open and continue one. One production cannot express that.

Harm: a misattributed or misleading diagnostic; every probe is refused (the lead states process_only).

## Root (the implementer's, not adjudicated here)

The residue opening's set cannot be format-dependent. Leads (a) and (b) are the two tokens (leading comma, unsigned integer) the one shared set must treat differently per format. The fix shape is a decision for the implementer reading 12.3.5.2, 12.3.6.2 and 8.3.2.2.13: a per-format residue (or a predicate on the format) rather than a single set.
