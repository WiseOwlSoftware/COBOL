# PB4317 - The eleven syntax-screen passes are eleven whole-tree walks, 25% of corpus bind time (2,035 of 7,997 ms); six run before the bind and five after the GroupTail, so at most two fused walks are possible - measure before proposing

Found by the Mythos front-end review, gap pass, 2026-10-31 (GAP-REVIEW.md, G6-7; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/KaxkLe85tfZBZ3nZ1tDGTs). Pin d7daee1cf.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/BinderDriver.cs` (pre-bind :61-84: LevelNumberPass, AreaPlacementPass, IntegerOperandPass, ProcedureFormatPass, EndMarkerPass, DirectivePlacementPass; post-GroupTail :221-254: ExpressionFormationPass, LiteralScreen.Run, WordCharacterScreen.RunOnTree, DeclinedFacilityPass, ClosedFormatPass); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/CursorFollowingVisitor.cs`.

**Finding.** The eleven syntax-screen passes cost 2,035 of 7,997 ms of corpus bind (25.4%; LevelNumber 364, AreaPlacement 336, ExpressionFormation 261, LiteralScreen 250, IntegerOperand 245, ProcedureFormat 174, WordCharacter 170, ClosedFormat 164, EndMarker 35, Declined 34, DirectivePlacement 2 ms). The first report's framing "ten pre-bind walks one dispatching walk could serve" does not hold: BinderDriver.cs:61-84 runs six BEFORE binding (each with a stated reason, e.g. a level-number error before the bind's cascade) and :221-254 runs five AFTER the GroupTail, of which DeclinedFacility and ClosedFormat run over the bound GroupBindContext and LiteralScreen/WordCharacterScreen enumerate TOKENS, not contexts; AreaPlacementPass is a plain CobolParserCoreBaseVisitor. The candidate is at most TWO fused walks (pre-bind visitors; post-bind context visitors), keeping the bind barrier and the diagnostic order. The traversal bound (VisitChildren 5.1%/1.5%) is a sampled share of the G6-6 kind (see the PB4253 addendum), not yet a bound.

**Fix direction.** Prototype on the archive and measure with Stopwatch / allocation A/B on EVALS and the corpus before proposing a change.
