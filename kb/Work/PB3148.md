# PB3148 — the VALIDATE statement at --std 85 reports a bare syntax error instead of the edition diagnostic

Source: `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w1069g-PB2720-report.md`, "New defects (note-ready)" item 2 (branch `worktree-wf_d63f7207-db5-30`, which fixed PB2720 and PB374).

**What was seen.** A VALIDATE statement compiled with `--std 85` is rejected with a raw `COBOL0001` rather than the edition diagnostic `COBOL0900` that a later-edition construct gets elsewhere.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4` (`statement`, the VALIDATE arm), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`ParseArm.VisitValidateFacilityStatement`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/StatementBinder.cs` (`BindDeclinedStatement`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs` (`facilityWord`), `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (row `validate-statement-2002`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Constructs.g.cs`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/ConstructRegistry.g.cs`.

**Code site (as filed).** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4` line 952, the alternative `{facilityWord("VALIDATE")}? validateFacilityStatement` in rule `statement`. Sweep the other `facilityWord(...)` alternatives for the same shape (CLAUDE.md rule 4).

**Related.** `kb/Work/PB283.md` (landed: the data-division VALIDATE elements) and PB374 (the MCS siblings, same "named diagnostic instead of bare COBOL0001" theme). The edition-gate test sweep (`new-construct` skill, I4) is for the implementer. No spec clause is cited: this is a diagnostic-quality defect, not an ISO rule.

## Landed (wave 20261780 C, 2026-10-30)

Re-probed on the tree first (Debug build, 0/0): `VALIDATE WS-A.` at `--std 85` drew `COBOL0307: cannot parse construct near
'WS-A'` (the shape had moved from the COBOL0001 the note records; the cause had not), and COBOL1580 alone at 2002/2014/2023.

**Mechanism.** The arm was `{facilityWord("VALIDATE")}?`, a RESERVATION predicate: at 85, where §8.9 leaves VALIDATE a user word,
the statement arm stood down and the parse died at the operand. Every other post-85 statement (UNLOCK, ALLOCATE, INVOKE, and the
RECEIVE / SEND arms PB374 fixed) parses at every edition and names its introduction.

**Fix.** The arm is the union grammar, `{wordAhead("VALIDATE")}?`, and the edition pass names the introduction on recognition
(`VisitValidateFacilityStatement` -> the new constructs.json row `validate-statement-2002` -> COBOL0900). The binder's declined
arm for VALIDATE and the MCS one were two copies of one rule ("below the statement's introduction the facility is not this
edition's to decline"); they are now ONE method, `StatementBinder.BindDeclinedStatement(construct, descriptor, bindOperands, s)`,
so at 85 VALIDATE draws COBOL0900 alone, with no COBOL1580 beside it. No legal COBOL-85 program loses a reading: a user word
VALIDATE cannot begin a statement, `VALIDATE.` cannot match the arm (it needs an operand), and a declared VALIDATE is retyped to
IDENTIFIER by the reservation gate (PB655; `DeclinedFacilityTests.ValidateAt85_IsAUserWord_AndDrawsNoFacilityWarning` still green).
**Sweep:** the other `facilityWord` arms are COMMIT and ROLLBACK, whose statement is the bare verb: below 2023 `COMMIT.` IS a
paragraph header (probed: COBOL3734 area-A at 85, a clean paragraph at 2002/2014), so the reservation predicate is right there and
stays (`EditionReadDriftTests` now records 2 facilityWord reads, not 3).

**Witnesses.** `conformance:cases/14.9.50-VALIDATE/pb3148-validate-statement-below-2002` (refused at 85 with COBOL0900); the accepted
control at 2002+ is `conformance:wiseowl/cases/14.9.50-VALIDATE/pb3568-validate-legal-operands-inert`; `VersionMatrixTests` runs the
new construct row at every edition.
