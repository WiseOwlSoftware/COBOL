## Measured

### Finding varying-clause — ISO §13.18.64.3 SR2, SR3; §13.18.64.4 GR1, GR3, GR4 (rejects-legal-source; rows GR-13.18.64.4-1, GR-13.18.64.4-3, GR-13.18.64.4-4, SR-13.18.64.3-2, SR-13.18.64.3-3)

**Mechanism.** F2 - VARYING counters are modelled only on the PRINTABLE (leaf) entry: DataBinder.Reports collects `varyings` per entry but attaches them only via field.Varyings on a ReportFieldModel, and resolves a counter only against the entry's own list, so a group entry's VARYING is silently discarded, a subordinate entry cannot reference an ancestor's counter, and nesting-based rules cannot see ancestor counters.

**Repro.** 01 DET-A TYPE DE. 02 LINE PLUS 1. 03 OCCURS 3 TIMES STEP 5 VARYING K FROM 1 BY 1. 04 COLUMN 1 PIC 9 SOURCE K.  (probe vgrp); and 03 OCCURS 2 TIMES STEP 10 VARYING J FROM 1. 04 COLUMN 1 PIC 99 OCCURS 2 TIMES STEP 3 VARYING K FROM J * 10 + 1 SOURCE K. (probe vsr3sub)

**Expected, from the standard.** GR1 'Each entry containing a VARYING clause establishes an independent temporary integer data item'; SR2 'This definition of data-name-1 may be referenced only within the current entry or a subordinate entry'; SR3 permits it in a subordinate entry's FROM/BY. vgrp prints `1    2    3`; vsr3sub compiles and prints the subordinate counters starting at 11 and 21. SR2 must reject `VARYING K` reused in a subordinate of an entry whose VARYING already defines K (probe vsr2sub).

**Observed.** vgrp: error COBOLNET0899: RD 'R-V': SOURCE 'K' does not resolve to a data item; vsr3sub: error COBOLNET1639 'J' is not defined; vsr2sub compiles clean and prints `1  2      3  4`.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs lines ~1597-1660 (varyings capture), ~1935-1954 (FieldVaryingSource resolution / field.Varyings.AddRange), ~2664-2672 (SR2 loop over fields only); the `chain` tuple carries no counters

### Finding varying-clause — ISO §13.18.64.3 SR3; §13.18.64.4 GR3 b) (rejects-legal-source; rows SR-13.18.64.3-3, GR-13.18.64.4-3)

**Mechanism.** F3 - the SR3-permitted reference to a counter inside arithmetic-expression-2 of the same clause is staged loud (ReportVaryingCounterInExpression, COBOLNET0899) because FROM/BY expressions are bound by ReportWriterBinder through ordinary data-name resolution, which has no scope for compose-local VARYING counters (the same missing scope produces the cascading COBOLNET1639 'not defined').

**Repro.** 02 LINE PLUS 1. 03 COLUMN 1 PIC 9 OCCURS 3 TIMES STEP 3 VARYING K FROM 1 BY K SOURCE K. (probe vby)

**Expected, from the standard.** SR3 'may be referenced in arithmetic-expression-2 of the same VARYING clause'; GR3 a)/b): K = 1, then 1+1 = 2, then 2+2 = 4 -> `1  2  4`. Note the closed form FROM + n*BY used by EmitFieldPlacements is NOT equal to GR3b when BY depends on the counter - the fix needs a running accumulator (or a recurrence) across repetitions, including OCCURS replays.

**Observed.** error COBOLNET0899: RD 'R-V' VARYING 'K': a VARYING counter referenced inside the BY expression (ISO §13.18.64.3 SR3) is not yet implemented; error COBOLNET1639: 'K' is not defined

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs lines ~1763-1766; src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ReportWriterBinder.cs (FROM/BY bind ~line 58); src/Cobol.Net.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs ReportVaryingCounterInExpression

### Finding varying-clause — ISO §13.18.64.3 SR2 (under-rejects; rows SR-13.18.64.3-2)

**Mechanism.** F6 - the SR2 'defined elsewhere' screen compares each counter name only against the data-name map (ByName); it never compares VARYING names against one another, so a duplicate data-name-1 within one clause (and, with F2, within a subordinate entry) is accepted.

**Repro.** 03 COLUMN 1 PIC 9 OCCURS 3 TIMES STEP 3 VARYING K FROM 1 K FROM 2 SOURCE K. (probe vsr2dup)

**Expected, from the standard.** SR2: data-name-1 shall not be defined elsewhere in the source element except as data-name-1 in another VARYING clause of an entry NOT subordinate - a second K in the same clause is neither, so the source shall be rejected (COBOLNET1559).

**Observed.** compiles clean, runs, prints `1  2  3` (first definition silently wins).

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs lines ~2664-2672
