# PB5905 - at --std 85 a computer-name that is a literal or all digits draws COBOL3734 beside its own refusal

Filed from the lead of the Mythos lane's rulings on read pass 17 (`kb/Work/PB5683/2026-11-16-rulings-code-reads-batch-17.md`, "Leads that are not yet anything"). The ruling's bullet, unchanged: "A computer-name that is a literal or all digits draws COBOL3734 beside its own refusal (the slice-240 reader)."

**The reader's words** (`1985-240.json`, the notes of the rows `1985-SR-VI.4.3.3-1` and `1985-SR-VI.4.4.3-1`): "Reserved words in the slot are refused by the closed-format COBOL1971 rather than a computer-name message, and a literal or digit-only name draws an extra COBOL3734; both are wording, the refusal is right." The reader's probe line: "a nonnumeric literal and the all-digit 370 refused (COBOL1971/COBOL1970, with an extra COBOL3734 area-A message)" (probes `c2-r-lit.cob`, `c2-r-num.cob` under `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/coderead-1985/out/work-1985-240/src/`).

COBOL3734 is the compiler's area-A message: "an AREA-A ITEM of a fixed-form line that does not begin in area A" (the summary of `DiagnosticCatalog.AreaAItemOutsideAreaA`, kb/Work PB3147).

**Not run by the clerk.** The programs and the printed output are the reader's. **Not ruled:** which of the two messages is dropped, or whether the other wording is wrong.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs#AreaAItemOutsideAreaA` (the descriptor, found by grep); the site that raises it for a computer-name is not named by the source.

**Scope.** Edition 85, as the reader saw it.
