# PB5342 - SEND ... ADVANCING ALL ZERO is accepted at every edition: from 2002 §2023:8.3.3.6.3 SR1 a) bars ALL before ZERO where the literal is restricted to a numeric literal, and main refused it at 2002

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CommunicationBinder.cs` (`ScreenSendAdvancing`)

**Mechanism.** `ScreenSendAdvancing` (CommunicationBinder.cs:322 in the review's tree) accepts ALL ZERO as integer-1 at every edition (PB5289: the bound figurative literal answers 'Z' whatever its spelling). §2023:8.3.3.6.3 SR1 a) (`cite.py --check "§2023:8.3.3.6.3 SR1a" "If the literal is restricted to a numeric literal, the only figurative constant permitted is ZERO"` prints OK) allows only ZERO (ZEROS, ZEROES) without the ALL phrase where the literal is restricted to a numeric literal, from 2002: the VCR row `figurative-all-zero-numeric-removed-2002` (`FigurativeAssociation.GateAllZeroAsNumeric`) records that change. Main refused it at 2002; the branch accepts it silently. At 1985 the form is legal (SR5 "may be zero", PB5289).

**Fix direction.** Route the ADVANCING literal through the edition arm `GateAllZeroAsNumeric` so ALL ZERO is refused at 2002 and later and accepted at 85, with a refusal golden (2002, 2014, 2023) and an 85 acceptance golden.
