### ⚖ ANSWERED 2026-08-31 — status owner -> landed

**DECLINE, formally**, per [[PB198]] (owner protocol 2026-08-30; the survey was unanimous — no shipping COBOL
implements standard-binary, which F.2 item 3 itself records). No SBIDI implementation is scheduled; the decline
is recorded as REVISITABLE per F.2 item 3's reevaluation note.

All three conditions this note attached to the answer were met in the same change set:
- **The seven §8.8.1.4 rows re-verdict.** ⭐ And this note is why the derived selector is correct: the predicate
  was text-only through three drafts and selected just ONE of the seven, because §8.8.1.4's rules never repeat
  the phrase. PB146's hand list disagreeing with the derived selector is what exposed it — the selector gained
  a CLAUSE arm and now takes all seven, inside a population of sixteen.
- **The §4.2.6 identification survives PB135's OO OPTIONS landing** — it did not: [[PB197]] measured
  METHOD-ID and INTERFACE-ID drawing NO diagnostic at all. The screen moved to OptionsBinder, the single
  construction point, with a grammar-derived drift test.
- **The latent single-arm dispatches are pinned.** `NumericRenderer.StandardDecimal` vs `IntrinsicBinder`'s
  `== ArithmeticMode.StandardDecimal` is registered as [[PB194]] with a measured repro (at `--std 2014` under
  `ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD`, `IF 1.0E+400 > X` compiles while the IntrinsicBinder screens would apply binary64's
  ±308 to the same mode); the `DigitCapFlag` ternary is replaced by `ArithmeticModes.NumvalDigitCap`, exhaustive
  over the enum. §11.9.11.2 r2a/r2b's standard-binary rounding rules now ride the same determination
  (`GR-11.9.11.2-2`).
