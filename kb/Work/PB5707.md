# PB5707 - at --std 2002 CURRENCY ... PICTURE SYMBOL accepts a national literal-8 and accepts the same literal-8 twice, both of which 2002 forbids

**Sites:**
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.Currency.cs#CurrencyOperandOf
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.Currency.cs` (whole: RegisterCurrency)
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SpecialNamesBinder.Currency.cs#SwitchBindCurrency
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ConfigurationSlice.cs#PictureSymbolLiterals
- src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Control/DialectBehavior.cs#CurrencyPictureSymbolLiteralAlphanumericAndUnique

**Mechanism.** SpecialNamesBinder.CurrencyOperandOf accepts LiteralClass.National for literal-8 at every edition (§2023:12.3.7.3 SR26 admits national), and RegisterCurrency refuses a rebinding only when the definition differs, not when the value repeats.

**Inventory.** Rows claimed: `2002-SR-12.2.6.2-22`.

**Rules (each citation checked with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`).**

- `§2002:12.2.6.2 SR22`: "Literal-8 shall be an alphanumeric literal consisting of a single character" (`cite.py --check`: OK  §2002:12.2.6.2 SR22  (Syntax rules)  the text is in SR22)
- `§2002:12.2.6.2 SR22`: "No two occurrences of literal-8 may be the same value" (`cite.py --check`: OK  §2002:12.2.6.2 SR22  (Syntax rules)  the text is in SR22)
