# PB5134 - one rule, two writers, one of them on the statement-binding hot path

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs:568` (`ProcedureTableBuilder.DebugsUseProcedure`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/DispatchEmitter.cs:48` (`DispatchEmitter.EmitDispatcher`: `dispatchState.DebugUseProcedure = (bound.DebugSubjects ?? []).Any(s => s.SubjectPc < bound.EntryPc)`),
read at `StatementBinder.cs:474` (`Ctx.Table.DebugsUseProcedure`, in the funnel every statement of every program passes through) and
`StatementEmitter.cs:130`.

**Found.** The two copies of "a debug subject lies in the declaratives" can drift, and the binder copy is a LINQ `Any` with a
capturing lambda, re-evaluated per statement even in programs with no debugging at all.

**Owed.** One predicate on the bound program (computed once when the subjects are finalized, `FinalizeDebug`), read by both the
binder and the emitter; a drift test or a single property so the two cannot disagree.
