finisher therefore waited on a branch that lands only through the OTHER carrier's finisher, and no wave would ever plan
either. The PB3528 cross-deadlock again, one level down.

## Root cause

`plan_partition` (scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py) exempts the group's own work and maps planned work to its finisher
with `x.branch in g.continues` and `x.branch in finisher_of`: the exact branch name. A carried branch's work is its
carrier's (`branch_carrier`, the one rule for "this branch's work is carried elsewhere", kb/Work PB3170), but the
partition never asked `carrier`, although `PlanState.carrier` is the same function the finisher arms use.

## Fix

`plan_partition` reads every in-flight branch as the branch its work LANDS THROUGH (`lands_through`: `s.carrier(branch)
or branch`) in all three places: the finisher's claim takes what a continued branch carries, the own-work exemption, and
the PB3528 map to this wave's finisher (`finisher_of`). A carried branch whose carrier no group of this wave finishes still
holds, as before. The module docstring states the rule.

**Sites:**
- `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`plan_partition`)
- `scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py` (the PB4352 block after PB3528 R1-1)
