RB-CODE per pass, and the zoned arm under them formatted with `ToString` + `PadLeft`, then rebuilt the string with two

image with `ToCharArray`. Now one zoned writer (`FormatZoned` over `WriteDigits`) writes every DISPLAY image, the
STANDARD DATA FORMAT image and both unsigned formatters into one `string.Create`; `ParseDisplayMagnitude` reads the
image in place through `DigitMagnitude`'s span scan (seeded with a leading punched digit), and the binary and packed
arms of `FormatImage` write straight into their string too (the sibling arms: each built a `char[]`, and packed a
`byte[]` as well, before the string). Per call: a signed zoned image up to 5 allocations to 1, a zoned decode 1 to 0, a
packed image 3 to 1, a binary image 2 to 1. Byte-identity: a differential of the transcribed old codec against the new
one over every digit count 0-38, every `NumericSign`, both `SignEncoding`s, signed and unsigned (156,000 values, plus
random junk images for the decode and `ImageIsNegative`) and the packed/binary forms (136,800 values) agreed on every
case; it was a one-time proof and is not committed (a second copy of the rule). The structural guard is
`NumericImageAllocationTests` (one string per image, zero bytes per decode). Emitted C# is unchanged.
