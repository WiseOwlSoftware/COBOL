# PB3954 — CobolDec.Div pads an EXACT quotient to 34 digits: DISPLAY FUNCTION MEAN(1 2 3) prints 2.000000000000000000000000000000000

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolDec.cs` (`CobolDec.Div`, `CobolDec.Sqrt`, `CobolDec.ToPreferredExponent`, `Round34Wide`), `docs/NUMERIC_DESIGN.md`

`CobolDec.Div` pads an exact quotient to 34 digits instead of reducing it toward the IEEE 754 preferred exponent (ISO
§8.8.1.5.3 defers to 60559:2020 5.4.1), so `DISPLAY FUNCTION MEAN(1 2 3)` prints `2.000000000000000000000000000000000` under
native and standard arithmetic alike. Harm per the report: cosmetic output on every DISPLAY of an SDIDI quotient; MAJOR for
goldens that print one. Repro `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op/w-pb3852-g/w20261717a/p/i.cob`. Not fixed in PB3860 because many
goldens read the representation. The citation §8.8.1.5.3 is the report's; the "preferred exponent" wording was not found in
that clause by `cite.py --check`, so the adjudication must re-derive it before it is written into a golden.

## Acceptance
- Derive the exact-quotient exponent rule from the spec, make `Div` (and its `Round34Wide` caller) hold an exact quotient
  at the preferred exponent, update every golden that printed the padded form in the same change, sweep the other SDIDI
  operations (sum, product) for the same padding.

## Landing (wave 20261728 group G)

**Re-probe on the wave's build (main 1a6466e93):** the note's DISPLAY symptom no longer reproduces: `DISPLAY FUNCTION MEAN(1 2 3)`
prints `000000002` under native and STANDARD-DECIMAL alike, because PB3514's device form (`CobolNum.DeviceFunctionText`) drops the
trailing fraction zeros before it prints. The padded significand was still there and still observable wherever a value's PLACES are
read: `COMPUTE R = FUNCTION MEAN(1 2 3) / 3 * 3` stored `+002.00000000` where its literal twin `2 / 3 * 3` stores `+001.99999999`
(the nested native quotient's working scale follows the operand's places, `CobolDecWithPrecision.Own`, `CobolDec.FractionDigits`).

**Determination:** §8.8.1.5.3 "Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division are performed as specified in ISO/IEC 60559:2020,
5.4.1" (cite.py-checked; the "preferred exponent" wording is 60559's, not the ISO text's) and §15.84.4 r2 for SQRT: an exact result takes the
representation nearest the preferred exponent (division: the dividend's less the divisor's; square root: half the operand's, rounded down),
and only an inexact one fills 34 digits.

**Fix:** `CobolDec.Div` and `CobolDec.Sqrt` hold an exact result at the preferred exponent through one helper,
`CobolDec.ToPreferredExponent` (the twin of the sum reduction in `AddSigned`). Siblings swept: `Add`/`Sub` (already reduce), `Mul`
(exact product at the sum of exponents), zero results (`CobolDec.Zero`), `Pow` (built on `Mul`/`Div`), `DivToOdd` (shares `Div`).
`DivTruncatedToOdd` is deliberately unchanged: its quotient is the nested native quotient held at the working scale, whose places
are the design (NUMERIC_DESIGN, `NestedQuotientScale`). `Ln`/`Exponential` keep exponent 0 for the exact `ln 1` / `exp 0`; 60559
5.4.1 does not govern them. No golden printed the padded form, so none changed.
**Witnesses:** `CobolDecDivisionTests` (exact and inexact quotients, roots, MEAN), conformance
`15.60.4-Returned-value-rule/pb3954_exact_quotient_places` and its below-2014 refusal.
