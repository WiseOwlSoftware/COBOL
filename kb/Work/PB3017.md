# PB3017 — DIVIDE ... REMAINDER past the Int128 carrier

Filed 2026-10-11 by the train 1059 lander from two implementer leads of wave 1059 that name the same mechanism:
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1059c-PB2966-report.md` (lead 1) and
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1059d-PB2967-report.md` (lead 1). Neither was re-probed by the lander.

**Mechanism.** `ArithmeticEmitter.EmitDivideRemainder` (src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs,
~line 146) takes the dividend and divisor through `NumericRenderer.FixedLane` (NumericRenderer.cs ~line 1333), which
narrows every operand to the Int128 carrier, and then runs the Int128 `NumDivide` subsidiary-quotient kernel. Two
operand kinds that the arithmetic statements' other lanes now handle do not survive that narrowing:

1. **Unsigned-wide dividend under ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD-DECIMAL** (PB2966's sibling, not fixed there). Repro
   `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\t1059\pb3017_sd4.cob`: W = 2^128 - 1 in `PIC 9(19) COMP-5`, `DIVIDE W BY 7 GIVING Q
   REMAINDER R ON SIZE ERROR ...` takes ON SIZE ERROR through `DeU` (CobolNum.Widen), although §8.8.1.5.2 r1 rounds
   the operand into the SDIDI and the quotient (about 4.86E37) fits Q's 16-byte container. COMPUTE / ADD / the GIVING
   forms without REMAINDER take the operand since PB2966.
2. **A float dividend past 1.7E38** (PB2967's sibling, not fixed there): FixedLane lands a Real operand through the
   saturating `Align` (`CobolFloat.ToScaledSaturating`) at the float working scale, so the quotient kernel divides
   Int128.MaxValue and stores a plausible sentinel quotient instead of the size error PB2967 made every other
   float-to-fixed store raise. Probe to write: COMP-2 1.0E+40 `DIVIDE ... GIVING Q REMAINDER R ON SIZE ERROR`.

**Fix shape (expected).** A remainder kernel per lane: the SDIDI lane computes the subsidiary quotient and the
remainder on `CobolDec` (§14.9.12.4 7): "the remainder is the result of multiplying the subsidiary quotient and the divisor and subtracting the product from the dividend", with the
subsidiary quotient developed per 6) c)), the native lane keeps Int128, and a float operand past the carrier raises as
`CobolFloat.ToScaled` does. Golden per arm, at 2023 for the STANDARD-DECIMAL leg.
