# PB3017 — DIVIDE ... REMAINDER past the Int128 carrier

Filed 2026-10-11 by the train 1059 lander from two implementer leads of wave 1059 that name the same mechanism:
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1059c-PB2966-report.md` (lead 1) and
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1059d-PB2967-report.md` (lead 1). Neither was re-probed by the lander.

**Mechanism.** `ArithmeticEmitter.EmitDivideRemainder` (src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs,
~line 146) takes the dividend and divisor through `NumericRenderer.FixedLane` (NumericRenderer.cs ~line 1333), which
narrows every operand to the Int128 carrier, and then runs the Int128 `NumDivide` subsidiary-quotient kernel. Two
operand kinds that the arithmetic statements' other lanes now handle do not survive that narrowing:

1. **Unsigned-wide dividend under ARITHMETIC IS STANDARD-DECIMAL** (PB2966's sibling, not fixed there). Repro
   `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\t1059\pb3017_sd4.cob`: W = 2^128 - 1 in `PIC 9(19) COMP-5`, `DIVIDE W BY 7 GIVING Q
   REMAINDER R ON SIZE ERROR ...` takes ON SIZE ERROR through `DeU` (CobolNum.Widen), although §8.8.1.5.2 r1 rounds
   the operand into the SDIDI and the quotient (about 4.86E37) fits Q's 16-byte container. COMPUTE / ADD / the GIVING
   forms without REMAINDER take the operand since PB2966.
2. **A float dividend past 1.7E38** (PB2967's sibling, not fixed there): FixedLane lands a Real operand through the
   saturating `Align` (`CobolFloat.ToScaledSaturating`) at the float working scale, so the quotient kernel divides
   Int128.MaxValue and stores a plausible sentinel quotient instead of the size error PB2967 made every other
   float-to-fixed store raise. Probe to write: COMP-2 1.0E+40 `DIVIDE ... GIVING Q REMAINDER R ON SIZE ERROR`.

**Fix shape (expected).** A remainder kernel per lane: the SDIDI lane computes the subsidiary quotient and the
remainder on `CobolDec` (§14.9.12.4 7): "the remainder is the result of multiplying the subsidiary quotient and the divisor and subtracting the product from the dividend", with the
subsidiary quotient developed per 6) c)), the native lane keeps Int128, and a float operand past the carrier raises as
`CobolFloat.ToScaled` does. Golden per arm, at 2023 for the STANDARD-DECIMAL leg.

## Landed 2026-10-12 (wave 1069 A)

**Re-probe on the wave-1069 build.** Both operand kinds reproduced: `pb3017_sd4.cob` took ON SIZE ERROR (`rem size error`)
and COMP-2 1.0E+40 `DIVIDE F BY 7 GIVING Q REMAINDER R` stored the sentinel's quotient (`rem ok 1495604533098186245 0`).

**Fix (one kernel per lane, `ArithmeticEmitter.EmitDivideRemainder`).** The STANDARD / STANDARD-DECIMAL lane
(`SdidiSubsidiaryQuotient`) forms the GR1 quotient with `CobolDec.Div` over each operand's `DecOperand` lift (the unsigned-wide
and float operands are never narrowed), snapshots it, takes the subsidiary quotient with the new
`CobolDec.SubsidiaryQuotient` (truncation toward zero at the receiver's scale, then the digit cap, the significand left at
the quotient's own exponent so the 34-digit invariant holds), and forms the remainder as the SDIDI expression
dividend - (subsidiary quotient x divisor) through `Combine`; the stored quotient is the same SDIDI value, landed by the
receiver's own rounding (so an unsigned 16-byte receiver takes it through `ToUnscaledMagnitude`, PB2966). The native lane
(`NativeSubsidiaryQuotient`) keeps the exact Int128 kernel. The float dividend past the carrier needed no kernel change:
`NumericRenderer.FixedLane` lands it through `Align`, which PB3019 made raise EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW, and the statement's size-error
catch takes it (Q and R unchanged).

**Sibling found and fixed in the same method (I9): the subsidiary quotient's digit cap.** `DigitsOf` capped it at the
receiver's PICTURE digit positions even for a BINARY-CAPACITY receiver, which holds its whole container: `DIVIDE 123456 BY 7
GIVING Q REMAINDER R` into `Q PIC 9(4) COMP-5` stored 17636 and left R = 70004 (dividend - 7636 x 7) where the remainder is 4;
`DIVIDE 9E18 BY 1` into `PIC 9(18) COMP-5` left R = 9000000000000000000. `SubsidiaryDigitsOf` now caps at the digits the store
KEEPS (the PICTURE for a PICTURE-truncating item, none for a binary-capacity one), PB129's own rationale ("exactly the
value the no-phrase store leaves in it"). Pinned by the cap legs of `pb3019_native_float_intermediate_past_the_carrier_is_a_size_error`.

**Goldens.** `2023/pb3017_sdidi_divide_remainder_beyond_the_int128_carrier` (W = 2^128 - 1 / 7 and / 10^10, 1E30 / 3, and the
1E40 / 7 size error) and `2023/pb3019_native_float_intermediate_past_the_carrier_is_a_size_error`; values derived by hand in the headers.
No traceability-inventory row is claimed or closed.
