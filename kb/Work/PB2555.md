# PB2555

Filed by the train 1037b lander from the wave-1037 group H report (lead 4: found while writing the PB1930 golden).

**What a user sees.** In fixed-form reference format the program-text area ends at margin R (§6.3.1, cite.py --check OK:
"Margin R is immediately to the right of the rightmost character position of the program-text area"; the position is
the implementor's, column 72 here), so the compiler is right to drop what lies past it. But when the dropped text was part of a statement (a 74-column
CALL line), the statement ends early and the parser reports `cannot parse construct near STOP` on the NEXT statement, with
nothing pointing at the truncation. Repro: `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1037h\p\gb.cob`.

**Owed.** A diagnostic quality fix: when the parse fails on a line whose predecessor carried non-blank text in the
area past margin R, add a note (or a warning, at the reference-format stage) naming the line and the column where
its program text was cut. It must not fire for the ordinary sequence-and-identification use of those columns (a numeric or
blank area, or a comment line), which PB1757 / R57 measured on the conformance corpus. Related: PB1950 (a golden that
silently changed meaning under the CLI's fixed default for the same reason).
