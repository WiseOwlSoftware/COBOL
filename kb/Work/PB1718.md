# PB1718 — PB1708 pivot M14

Filed by the orchestrator from the PB1708 pivot design (workflow wf_08642598-d3c, reviewed APPROVE_WITH_CHANGES, all eleven findings resolved by the reviser), 2026-09-28.
## Mechanism (`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` section 3.14.3–4, section 3.14.9 row M14)

`scripts/test_population.py`, with a `--self-test`, answers one question: did the union of the runs equal the
discovered population? It is used in three places:
- the battery (PHASE 1);
- CI's `conformance-population` job, over the shard trx files, replacing the inline `grep -c` block (rule 4: one
  mechanism per job);
- the gate.

`gen-vcr.ps1`, `gen-diagnostics-doc.ps1`, `record_verdicts.py` and `record_impact_map.py` scrub the gate handshake
variables. This ships with `GateLegDriftTests` (6) and `docs/DRIFT_RULES.md`.

## Acceptance

- The self-test covers short, over, skipped, and definitions versus results.
- The battery's PHASE 1 prints each assembly's population line and is red on a planted dropped case.
- CI's guard is red on a planted shard overlap that keeps the count.
- b8's two inputs pass.

Independent of the other mechanisms. M13 follows it.

## Landed (wave 71, group D)

**`scripts/test_population.py`** is the ONE population check: `check` compares the multiset of `--list-tests`
names (listed by the tool itself, SCRUBBED) with the multiset union of the trx files' TEST DEFINITIONS, and names
every case NEVER RAN, RAN TWICE or NOT IN THE POPULATION; NotExecuted-only cases count as skipped; an unreadable
input, an EMPTY population or two disagreeing listings is exit 2. `--self-test` fires 19 arms, among them short,
over, skipped, definitions versus results, and a shard overlap that keeps the count. It also owns the one
`--list-tests` parser (`filter_population.py` and `record_impact_map.py` import it; their two private copies are
deleted).

**The scrub grew a channel the design did not know.** Measured on Characterization (33 cases):
`VSTestTestCaseFilter=FullyQualifiedName~Snapshot` in the environment (MSBuild imports environment variables as
properties) ran 31 cases, exit 0, AND narrowed `--list-tests` to 31. `RunSettingsFilePath` and `VSTestSetting`
naming a runsettings file with a `TestCaseFilter` did the same. A population listed in the caller's environment is
blind to that, so the tool lists scrubbed. `is_scrubbed` is the rule: the prefixes `COBOLNET_GATE_` and `VSTest`,
plus `RunSettingsFilePath`, matched case-insensitively. Every caller runs `test_population.py scrubbed dotnet test …`
or passes `env=scrubbed_env()`: `battery.sh`, `build-local.ps1`/`.sh`, `gen-vcr.ps1`, `gen-diagnostics-doc.ps1`,
`guard.sh`, `guard-fast.sh`, `measure-battery-determinism.sh`, `filter_population.py`, `record_impact_map.py`, and
the gate `record_verdicts.py` prints. CI scrubs by construction, because its hosted environment is exactly what the
workflow declares. `GateLegDriftTests` arm (6) runs `audit-callers`, which is red on any unscrubbed caller, on a
printed unscrubbed instruction, or on a workflow that sets a scrubbed variable. It found 16 unscrubbed sites in 11
files on the base, and a planted `scripts/zz_plant.sh` made it red.

**CI:** each shard lists its own build's population and uploads it beside its trx as
`test-results-<OS>-conformance-<shard>`. `conformance-population` runs `check --listing … --trx …` per platform and
needs no .NET build of its own. The inline `grep -c` count, the `Total:` capture and the `shard-count-*` artifacts
are DELETED. `FilterPopulationGuardDriftTests` recognises the new shard shape.

**Acceptance, measured:**
- Battery PHASE 1 (SKIP_GUARD/SKIP_DIFF, BelowNormal) printed `=== POPULATION Conformance: EXACT — 9,317/9,317
  cases ran (skipped 0) across 1 trx ===`, and the same for Unit at 29,705/29,705 and Characterization at 33/33.
  Unit's trx carries 29,736 results, so the definitions-not-results arm is live.
- A planted drop: a Characterization run with `VSTestTestCaseFilter` exported printed `Passed! … Total: 31`, exit 0.
  The battery's check was then `RED — 2 NEVER RAN — 31/33`, naming both, even with the variable still in the
  caller's environment. The scrubbed run of the same command ran 33.
- CI's guard, run locally on real filtered shards: shard A (`~DiagnosticSnapshotTests|~SmokeTests`, 18) and shard B
  (`~EmittedCSharpSnapshotTests|~SmokeTests`, 15) sum to the discovered 33, so the deleted count guard would have
  passed. The check was `RED — 1 NEVER RAN · 1 RAN TWICE`, and an absent platform exits 2.
- b8's two inputs, re-taken by the battery: `b8.py` reports the definitions EQUAL to `--list-tests` for Conformance
  (9,317) and Unit (29,705), and the tool reads both EXACT.
