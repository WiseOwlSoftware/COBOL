## Mechanism (`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` section 3.14.3–4, section 3.14.9 row M14)

`scripts/test_population.py`, with a `--self-test`, answers one question: did the union of the runs equal the
discovered population? It is used in three places:
- the battery (PHASE 1);
- CI's `conformance-population` job, over the shard trx files, replacing the inline `grep -c` block (rule 4: one
  mechanism per job);
- the gate.

`gen-vcr.ps1`, `gen-diagnostics-doc.ps1`, `record_verdicts.py` and `record_impact_map.py` scrub the gate handshake
variables. This ships with `GateLegDriftTests` (6) and `docs/DRIFT_RULES.md`.

## Acceptance

- The self-test covers short, over, skipped, and definitions versus results.
- The battery's PHASE 1 prints each assembly's population line and is red on a planted dropped case.
- CI's guard is red on a planted shard overlap that keeps the count.
- b8's two inputs pass.
