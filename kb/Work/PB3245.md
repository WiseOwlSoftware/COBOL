# PB3245 — no named diagnostic for an unterminated literal

Filed 2026-10-16 by the enqueue unit's clerk. Source: `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1084h-PB1804-report.md`, "New defects"; PB1804's "Not this note's" paragraph names it as a lead.

**Flags.** rejects_legal_source false, under_rejects false: the source is rightly rejected; only the diagnostic is poor.

**Repro.** `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1084\w1084h\probe\s07c.cob` (`DISPLAY "HEL` / `-LO".`, `--source-format free`) prints
`COBOL0001 unexpected '"'`, then COBOL0001 `-`, COBOL0309 and COBOL0307. The same text in pseudo-text (`s07b.cob`) gets the
named COBOL2449. Fixed form: an open literal with no column-7 continuation line after it is the same case.

**Rule.** §6.4.2: "When such a literal is incomplete at the end of a line, the incomplete portion of the literal shall be
terminated by a floating continuation indicator" (the report records `OK §6.4.2`; re-run `cite.py --check` before citing it
in code).

**Site.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4:1054`, `fragment STR_BODY` has no unterminated alternative. It
is NOT the reference-format builder: the builder runs before conditional compilation, and an unterminated literal in an
omitted `>>IF` branch is legal (§7.3.16.4 3; probe `s07d.cob` compiles and prints `OK`).

**Fix shape.** An unterminated-literal token in the lexer with one named diagnostic, so the parser reports no cascade. The
report found no other lead sharing this root. PB1804 (landed) holds the builder-side trap.

## Landed (wave 20261773, group G)

**Re-probe (this tree, before the fix).** `s07c.cob --source-format free`: COBOL0001 `unexpected '"'` (4,13), COBOL0001
`-` (5,1), COBOL0309 `LO` (5,2), COBOL0001 `"` (5,4), the cascade recorded above; `s07b.cob` COBOL2449; `s07d.cob` OK.

**Fix.** `STR_BODY`'s closing delimiter is optional, so a literal its line ends inside is that literal's own token
(STRINGLIT / NATLIT / HEXLIT / BOOLLIT, prefix kept) running to the end of the line. The four tokens' one action,
`OnDelimitedLiteral`, gives back the open literal's trailing spaces and one trailing separator period, so the sentence
still ends. `CobolLiteral.IsUnterminated` is the one question. `LiteralScreen.ReportUnterminated` reports COBOL5563 over
the unit's token stream in `Frontend.LexAndParseOnce` (first of the post-lex rules: a parse the open literal breaks is
never bound) and over each directive fragment in `ScreenFragment`. After: `s07c` draws COBOL5563 at (4,13) first; the
directive `>>DEFINE X AS "ABC` draws COBOL5563 where it drew the generic COBOL1619; `DISPLAY "HELLO` followed by
`STOP RUN.` draws COBOL5563 alone.

**Review finding R1-1 (fixed in this landing).** The open-literal token made the branch refuse a free-form comment-entry
with one apostrophe in it, which main accepted: `AUTHOR. Pat O'Brien.` at `--std 85 --source-format free` (and under
`--permissive`) drew COBOL5563 and COBOL2633. The root was the lead this note had recorded below: free-form comment-entry
text reached the lexer, where fixed form discards it in the §6.5 conversion. The free-form classifier
(`FreeFormConverter`) now discards it too, writing the header `AUTHOR. .`; the entry is the rest of the header line
(GnuCOBOL 3.2 `-free`, CLAUDE.md rule 1; docs/CONFORMANCE.md D-CE1). That closes the lead as well (COBOL2631, 2633, 2630
and 0900 on entry text, and `SECURITY. J. Smith.`'s COBOL1971). Sibling, found on the way: a free SOURCE FORMAT segment never
handed the division it read on to the next segment, so a fixed `REMARKS.` procedure paragraph after a free `PROCEDURE
DIVISION.` header was read as a comment-entry and the legal program refused (COBOL0001); `ConvertSegment` carries it in
either format. Witnesses: `conformance-suite/wiseowl/cases/6.5-Logical-conversion/pb3245_free_comment_entry_is_commentary`
(accepted at 85), `pb3245-free-comment-entry-ends-at-line-end` (refused at 85, COBOL1971, as GnuCOBOL refuses it) and
`conformance-suite/cases/6.5-Logical-conversion/pb3245_free_segment_hands_on_its_division` (accepted from 2002).

**The free-form comment-entry lead (closed here by R1-1).** The first implementer recorded that a free-form comment-entry
(AUTHOR … REMARKS, reachable at `--std 85 --source-format free` and under `--permissive`) reached the lexer as tokens, so
the post-lex rules judged its text as program text: COBOL2631/2633 on a comma, semicolon or quote in it, COBOL2630/0900 on
a prefixed literal in it, and with this change COBOL5563 on an unpaired quotation symbol. The branch review made that a
blocking regression (R1-1, above), and the review finisher fixed it at the §6.5 conversion rather than leaving it a lead.
