# PB2781 — no build rule stops a new unread private member

**Found by** the wave 1043 E implementer (PB2199, `ZeroTokenRewriter.ArithmeticOperators`), filed by the train-1043
lander.

**What.** The Delete program (PB2119) is deleting, note by note, members the R0 census measured as unreferenced. Every
PRIVATE one of them is a member the .NET analyzers already detect: IDE0051 (unused private member) and IDE0052 (unread
private member). `ZeroTokenRewriter.ArithmeticOperators` was dead from the commit that introduced it (`00f6421c3`) and
survived until the census found it. Neither rule appears in `.editorconfig` or `Directory.Build.props`, and
`EnforceCodeStyleInBuild` is not set, so the build cannot report one, and the next dead private member lands unseen
until the next census.

**Code site.** `.editorconfig` (no `dotnet_diagnostic.IDE0051.severity` / `IDE0052` entry);
`Directory.Build.props` (no `EnforceCodeStyleInBuild`).

**Fix direction.** Once the Delete program's private-member notes have landed (so the switch does not turn today's
census backlog into a build break), set `EnforceCodeStyleInBuild` and raise IDE0051 and IDE0052 to errors, with a
drift test that pins both settings. If the analyzer's cost or false-positive rate is too high on this tree (measure:
generated code, members reached only by reflection or `nameof`), the alternative is a census re-run in CI that fails on
a new unreferenced member. Non-private members are outside the analyzers' reach and stay the census's job.

**Repro.** `grep -n "IDE0051\|IDE0052\|EnforceCodeStyleInBuild" .editorconfig Directory.Build.props` prints nothing.
