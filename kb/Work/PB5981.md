# PB5981 - the report heading's lower limit at `--std 85` is borrowed from 2023

`ReportSectionBinder.GroupLimits` (ReportHeading arm) sets a report heading's lower limit to the page heading's first absolute
line minus 1, the 2023 shape (§2023 GR8 b), at every edition. At `--std 85` the 1985 rules put that limit at FIRST DETAIL
minus 1 (§1985:XIII.3.8.3 SR8a; §1985:XIII.3.10.6 GR2a), and COBOL2199 cites the 1985 rule for a limit the 1985 rule does
not state. A program whose report heading fits the 1985 limit but not the borrowed one may be refused; the fix probes that
first and sets `rejects_legal_source` if so.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReportSectionBinder.cs` (`ReportSectionBinder.GroupLimits`)

## Fix shape
The limit belongs to the edition: GroupLimits asks the edition for the report heading's lower limit, the 1985 answer is FIRST
DETAIL minus 1 and the message cites the rule that states it. Goldens at `--std 85`: a report heading at the 1985 limit
(accepted) and one past it (refused, the 1985 citation).
