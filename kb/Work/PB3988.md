# PB3988 — a FINDINGS branch that another branch replays reads DECISION, and the planner gives it a finisher of its own

**Sites:** `scripts/prune_worktrees.py` (`contained`, the `replayable` test; `answered_by_carrier`, `findings_answered_by`), `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`main`'s `answered`, which asks `branch_carrier`; `plan`'s PB3404 arm), `scripts/orchestrator/inflight.py` (`branch_replays`)

Searched first: PB3404 (the answered arm) and PB3386 are landed; PB3652 added the replay arm for an unfinished branch;
PB3668 (open) is about the replay arm's docstring only. No note owns the FINDINGS case of a replayed branch.

## What happens
A branch can carry its work to main in two ways: another branch CONTAINS it (a merge) or REPLAYS it (every commit
cherry-picked, patch-equivalent; the planner's own FINISH-FIRST pred tells a SPLIT successor to cherry-pick). The two
tools treat the FINDINGS case of each differently:

- `prune_worktrees.contained()` carries a contained DECISION row whatever its report says, but its replay arm reads only a
  row with no report or an unfinished one (`replayable`: "never a FINDINGS stamp: its finisher answers the findings").
  A DONE branch whose review stamped FINDINGS and whose every commit a carrier replays stays a DECISION row.
- `plan_wave.plan`'s PB3404 arm (`its review findings are answered by <carrier>`) asks `answered`, which asks
  `branch_carrier`, the CONTAINING carrier only. A replayed FINDINGS branch has none, so once nothing holds its lead the
  FINDINGS arm plans it a finisher of its own. That finisher answers findings the carrier has already answered, on a
  second branch that changes the same lines as the carrier, and the two conflict at landing.

## Fix direction (not decided)
A replaying carrier holds the replayed commits as its own diff, so its branch review covers them as it covers a
merged predecessor's. Read a replay as the containment it replaces, in both tools from the one rule
(`replay_view`): the survey's replay arm carries a FINDINGS row too, naming the carrier, and the planner's `answered`
accepts the replaying carrier (`branch_replays(...).carrier`) where it accepts `branch_carrier`'s, under
`findings_answered_by`'s CLEAN-review test; while the carrier is IN FLIGHT the row waits on it (`owned`). Pin it with a
self-test case in `test_plan_wave.py` and the survey's self-test: a FINDINGS branch, a carrier that cherry-picks it, and
no finisher planned.
