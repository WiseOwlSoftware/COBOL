# PB5023 - catalog rows and a citation the 712-record fleet could not trust

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (`main_2023`, `ordinal_shape`), `scripts/spec/rule_catalog_1985.py#parse`, `scripts/spec/citation.py` (`rule_designator`, `_rule`, `validate`)

**Report.**
1. Slice 03: the text of 2023 row `FMT-12.4.5.1` stops after Format 2, so the sort-merge Format 4 and sequential
   Format 3 were read from `specs/ISO_COBOL_2023.md`; two records had stayed OPEN for that reason. The cause is general
   (branch review wadj712 R1-3): `main_2023` cuts every row's text at 4,000 characters, and 18 rows are cut, among them
   `GR-14.9.30.4-21` and `GR-12.3.7.4-7`, which this batch's records cite. An agent reading a cut row can leave the
   tail's requirement out of `to`.
2. Slice 36: 1985 row `1993-GR-IX.4.4.4-19` also holds the 1993 errata for READ GR10b, GR10c and GR11b, which have no
   rows of their own and so were never decided.
3. Slice 10: the text of `SR-13.18.40.3-12` merges two branches of the rule.
4. Slices 06 and 18: the second numbered list of `§2023:9.1.14` is catalogued `GR-9.1.14-L2.x`, but `cite.py --check`
   resolves `§2023:9.1.14 GR1` to that list's item as well as to the real GR1, so a citation of either is ambiguous.

**Expected:** each row holds exactly its rule's text, no length cut (a drift test against the transcription's block), each errata rule
is its own row, and the citation format names a second list's item unambiguously, one parser (`citation.py`) for both.
