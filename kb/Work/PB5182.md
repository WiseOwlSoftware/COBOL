# PB5182 - at --std 85 DIVIDE ... GIVING an unsigned quotient REMAINDER forms the remainder from a signed subsidiary quotient: DIVIDE -11 BY 3 GIVING PIC 9 REMAINDER gives R = -02 where 1985 gives -11 - 3 x 3

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs` (`EmitDivideRemainder`, `SubsidiaryShapeOf`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:VI.6.11.4 GR6 forms the remainder from the quotient as identifier-3 holds it, and for an edited or ROUNDED quotient from an intermediate field with "the same presence or absence of a sign as the quotient (identifier-3)". `ArithmeticEmitter.EmitDivideRemainder` builds the subsidiary quotient through `SubsidiaryShapeOf` as always SIGNED, the 2023 rule, with no edition arm. For an unsigned identifier-3 receiving a negative quotient, the quotient stores its absolute value while the remainder is computed from the negative one: -11 / 3 gives Q=3 R=-02 at --std 85, the 2023 result.

Verdict, harm, evidence, repro and observed text are the records'; the rows' verdicts are recorded on another branch.

Code sites read: src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs#EmitDivideRemainder; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs#SubsidiaryShapeOf; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs#NativeSubsidiaryQuotient; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs#CarriedSubsidiaryQuotient; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs#FixedPointSubsidiary. Code read: §1985:VI.6.11.4 GR6 defines the remainder as 'the result of subtracting the product of the quotient (identifier-3) and the divisor from the dividend' and the quotient used, for a numeric edited or ROUNDED identifier-3, as an intermediate field with 'the same number of digits, the same decimal point location, and the same presence or absence of a sign as the quotient (identifier-3)'. ArithmeticEmitter.EmitDivideRemainder implements the truncated, unedited quotient at identifier-3's scale (SubsidiaryShapeOf; NativeSubsidiaryQuotient divides in the Int128 kernel under TRUNCATION, CarriedSubsidiaryQuotient/FixedPointSubsidiary truncate the final-transfer quotient) and forms dividend - subsidiary x divisor: correct at 85 for a signed identifier-3 and for any non-negative quotient, which is all the witness (11 / 3 GIVING PIC 9 ROUNDED, quotient 4, remainder 2) exercises. PARTIAL: the member has no edition arm, so the subsidiary quotient is always SIGNED, the 2023 rule (§2023:14.9.12.4 GR6 'a subsidiary quotient is developed that is signed'); at --std 85 an UNSIGNED identifier-3 (numeric PIC 9 or an edited item with no sign symbol, with or without ROUNDED) receiving a negative quotient stores its absolute value yet the remainder is computed from the negative quotient. DIVIDE -11 BY 3 GIVING QU (PIC 9) REMAINDER RM (PIC S99) stores QU = 3 and RM = -02 where the 1985 rule gives -11 - (3 x 3) = -20. No kb/Work note owns the fix (grepped 'presence or absence of a sign', VI.6.11.4, REMAINDER in PB5069-PB5130): the orchestrator files one.

**Expected (from the 1985 text, the record's).** Q=3 R=-20. §1985:VI.6.11.4 GR6: 'The remainder in COBOL is defined as the result of subtracting the product of the quotient (identifier-3) and the divisor from the dividend' and the intermediate quotient field has 'the same presence or absence of a sign as the quotient (identifier-3)'. Identifier-3 is unsigned and holds 3 (§1985:VI.6.19.4: 'When an unsigned numeric item is the receiving item, the absolute value of the sending operand is moved'), so the remainder is -11 - (3 x 3) = -20. The same holds with ROUNDED (QU = 4, intermediate field 3) and for an edited identifier-3 without a sign symbol (PIC Z9).

**Observed (the record's).** Exit 0, no diagnostic; displays 'Q=3 R=-02' at --std 85, identical to --std 2023: the remainder is -11 - (-3 x 3), formed from a signed subsidiary quotient. Also -02 for GIVING QU ROUNDED (Q=4) and for GIVING PIC Z9 (Q= 3).

**Fix direction (root cause, CLAUDE.md rule 4).** Let SubsidiaryShapeOf take the sign of identifier-3 at the editions whose rule says so (one edition test in the one shape builder, read by every subsidiary path: native, carried and fixed-point).
