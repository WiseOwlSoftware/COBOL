# PB1970

Filed by the train-1015 lander from wave 1015 group X's lead 3 (PB1407).

## Why it may be dead

ADDRESS OF a CONSTANT RECORD item is now refused (§8.4.3.11.3 SR3, COBOLNET2785, `AddressOfOperandScreen`), and the
2023 golden `pb1233_constant_record_options_initialize` lost its pointer lane for that reason. The emitter still seeds
a shared storage cell for a constant record in three places, all keyed on `DataItem.IsConstantRecord`:

- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/DataDivision/DataEmitter.cs:63`
- `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/OoEmitter.cs:110` and `:245` (object and factory data)

## Next step

Measure, do not deduce (feedback: reachability is measured): instrument or assert in those lanes and run the whole
conformance population. If nothing reaches them, delete them with a drift test that a constant record never gets a
cell; if something does (a REDEFINES, a CALL BY REFERENCE of a constant record, an external record), record it here
and keep the lane with that witness. Related: PB1233 (constant-record initial state).
