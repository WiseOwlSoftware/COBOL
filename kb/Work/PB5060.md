# PB5060 - sweep the 2002 and 1985 reserved-word lists

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/reserved_word_order.py` (`LISTS`, `WordList`, `WORD`, `SPECIAL`, `check`, `check_columns`, `_columns_1985`, `_columns_2023`, `_group`, `_gap_findings`, `main`, `self_test`), `scripts/spec/verify_publishable.py` (`main`), `specs/ANSI_COBOL_1985.md` (Section IV, paragraph 8, the table of PDF page 191), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/LEDGER-1985.json` (the sweep `reserved-word-letter-gap(PB5060)`), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-reconciliation/TRANSCRIPTION-STATE.md`. The 2002 half will change `specs/ISO_COBOL_2002.md` and add a 2002 row to `reserved_word_order.LISTS`.

**Found (PB5053, wave 20261958).** PB5053 swept the 2023 list of 8.9 pair by pair and found four entries out of alphabetical
order and two misprints, each corrected with attribution (2023 Addendum C15, C16). The 2002 list (its 8.9 table, column-wise)
and the 1985 reserved-word list had no such sweep. Run the same adjacent-pair check and a misprint read on the rendered
pages; correct each evident slip in place with attribution (the owner's rule, PB5045's standing requirements).

## 1985 DONE (wave 20261972 group A); the 2002 half stays OPEN

**1985, swept.** Rendered PDF pages 191 and 192 (folios IV-45, IV-46) and read every entry against the transcription.
The printed list has no misprint and no entry out of order: a check of every adjacent pair, down each column and
across columns and pages, finds none against the list's order (a hyphen and a digit before a letter: `END`,
`END-ADD`; `DEBUG-SUB-3`, `DEBUGGING`; `I-O`, `I-O-CONTROL`, `IDENTIFICATION`), and the list's 350 words are exactly
the 350 the compiler reserves at COBOL-85 (`tests/version-matrix/reserved-words.json`, whose 85 flag comes from
GnuCOBOL's list, an independent source). So 1985 needed no Errata entry.

**One transcription slip, repaired.** The page leaves a blank line between two initial letters. Amendment 2's
`FUNCTION` (Errata C207) had been written into the blank between `FROM` and `GENERATE`, so the F and G words ran
together. The blank is back (column 3 of the page-191 table moves down one row; the table gains a row), recorded as
the ledger sweep `reserved-word-letter-gap(PB5060)`.

**The drift test.** `scripts/spec/reserved_word_order.py`, called by `verify_publishable.py` (the gate's SPEC
CORRECTIONS audits, both editions): every adjacent pair in order, no word twice, no entry that is neither a word nor a special-character word, the special-character words last,
and for a transcription that keeps the printed columns one blank between letter groups and none inside one. It was
seen RED on the tree before the repair (`no blank line stands between FUNCTION and GENERATE`). Its 2023 row pins
PB5053's sweep, which had no kept check: the two pairs the 2023 Addendum's C16 keeps as printed are named there, and a
kept pair that stops being one is refused.

**2002, open.** The 2002 list (its 8.9) is not swept: it waits for the reconciliation of the 2002 transcription (the
owner's rule of 2026-11-07, 1985 before 2002). Until then `reserved_word_order.LISTS` has no 2002 row, and
`verify_publishable.py --edition 2002` says its order is not checked. The sweep owes: the rendered pages read, each
evident slip corrected with attribution, and a 2002 reader and row in `LISTS`.
