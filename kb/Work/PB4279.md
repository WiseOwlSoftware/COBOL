# PB4279 — COPY's text-name bypasses CobolWordRule

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs:552` (`CopyProcessor.ReadCopyName` called for text-name-1; the library-name after OF/IN is the same shape), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Common/CobolLiteral.cs` (the CobolWordRule consumer to reuse if the edge test lives there; read-only here).

## What happens

PB4234 made a COBOL word with a leading or trailing underscore draw COBOL2773 under 8.3.2.1. COPY text-name-1 is a system-name and therefore a COBOL word (8.3.2.1; 8.3.2.3.1 lists text-name), but `CopyProcessor` reads it with `ReadCopyName` and never asks `CobolWordRule`. `COPY _MEMBER.` (or `MEMBER_`) is accepted silently where the same word elsewhere is refused. The library-name (`OF`/`IN`) is the same class. Whether the member then resolves is the library's matter; the word's form is the language's.

Repro (not probed by the reviewer): `COPY MEM_.` with a file `MEM_.cpy` present; compare `01 MEM_ PIC X.`, which draws COBOL2773.

## Fix

Ask `CobolWordRule` for text-name-1 and library-name-1 in `ReadCopyName` when they are written as words (a literal form is not a word). Check the text-name rule in 7.2 and 8.3.2.3.1 first (a text-name written as a literal is exempt), validate the citations with `scripts/spec/cite.py --check`, add a golden refusal at every edition, and sweep the other preprocessor readers of a user-defined word (REPLACE operands, `>>DEFINE` names).

## Added repro (PB4278's branch review, 2026-11-01)

PB4278 made a hyphen at a word's edge one ill-formed word (COBOL2773, 8.3.2.1). `COPY BOOK-.` with a member `BOOK-.cpy` present is the hyphen form of this note's underscore repro: `CopyProcessor.ReadCopyName` reads `BOOK-` as a text-name without asking `CobolWordRule`, so it is accepted silently where `01 BOOK- PIC X.` draws COBOL2773. The leading form is `COPY -BOOK.`. The fix and its sweep cover both edge characters.

## Landed (wave 20261825 group A)

**Re-probe** (pre-fix build): `COPY PB4279U_.` and `COPY PB4279H-.`, with the members present, compiled and ran at
every edition, while `01 A_ PIC X.` draws COBOL2773.

**Fix (one rule, every reader).** `WordCharacterScreen.Check`'s per-word body is now `WordCharacterScreen.CheckWord`:
the underscore and extended-letter 2002 gates, COBOL2773, and the 2023 removal. The token walk calls it for every word
the lexer reads, and `CopyProcessor.ScreenCopyWord` calls it for text-name-1 and library-name-1 written as words (a
system-name, §2023:8.3.2.3.1, so a COBOL word, §2023:8.3.2.1), with `CobolWordRule.LengthViolation` (COBOL1567) beside it, at
the word's source position. A literal-1 / literal-2 is no word. Sweep: `>>DEFINE` and `>>TURN` names already ask
`CobolWordRule.DirectiveWordViolations`; REPLACE operands are pseudo-text, whose text-words are not COBOL words until the
resultant text is lexed, where the token walk asks them.

**Goldens:** `cases/8.1.3-COBOL-character-repertoire/pb4279-copy-text-name-edge-underscore` and
`pb4279-copy-text-name-edge-hyphen`, each with its member beside it (COBOL2773 at every edition; at 85 the underscore is
COBOL0900 too).
