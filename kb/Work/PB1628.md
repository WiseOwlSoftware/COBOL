# PB1628 — LANDED 2026-09-27

## Landing (2026-09-27)

**Rule, derived.** §12.3.7.3 SR18: "Literal-7 shall be an alphanumeric or national literal that is not a figurative
constant." A concatenation expression is one, per §8.8.3.3 GR3 ("equivalent to a literal of the same class and
value, and may be used anywhere a literal of that class may be used"). SR11's symbolic-character bar names
literal-1 through literal-6 and literal-9, not literal-7 or literal-8. So `CURRENCY SIGN IS "US" & SYM-D PICTURE
SYMBOL "$"` is legal source, and COBOLNET2473 was a rejection of legal source.
**Fix.** CURRENCY SIGN clauses are collected in the walk and bound in phase 3, after SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS (the PB1558
dependency-order discipline), so `LiteralEnvironment.SymbolicValue` finds the value whatever the source order.
CURRENCY reads no other paragraph state (the DECIMAL-POINT setting is not consulted). The dependency-order comment
lists it.
**Golden.** `2023/pb1628_currency_sign_symbolic_operand`: SYMBOLIC CHARACTERS written after CURRENCY SIGN; SYM-D IS
37 = "$"; `PIC $9(3).99 VALUE 12.5` displays `[US$012.50]`.
**Found on the way:** PB1635. `cite.py` reports §8.8.3.3 GR3 as `c)`, because those rules are numbered `N.`.

## Filing

Filed 2026-09-26 by the train-63 lander from the w62k (PB1406) implementer's lead 2. **Not probed yet: re-probe
first.**

**What.** PB1406 made a symbolic-character a concatenation operand (§12.3.7.4 GR11 a). Inside SPECIAL-NAMES,
§12.3.7.3 SR11 refuses one in literal-1…6 and literal-9 (COBOLNET2474). The CURRENCY SIGN literal (literal-7/8,
not an SR11 literal) may take one. But `DataBinder.Switches.cs` binds CURRENCY in its phase 1, before the SYMBOLIC
CHARACTERS clause is bound. So `LiteralEnvironment.SymbolicValue` finds no value, and the operand falls through to
COBOLNET2473 "not a literal". SPECIAL-NAMES clauses are order-free for name resolution (PB226), and PB1558 fixed the
same source-order shape for LOCALE.

**Check first** whether any rule forbids a symbolic-character in the CURRENCY literal. §12.3.7.3's CURRENCY rules
restrict literal-7's characters, so the answer may be a different refusal. Either way, 2473 "not a literal" is the
wrong reason.

**Code sites.** The phase order of `DataBinder.Switches.cs` SwitchBindSpecialNames; `LiteralEnvironment.SymbolicValue`
(`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/LiteralEnvironment.cs`).
