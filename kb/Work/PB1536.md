## Q2 — FMT-5.2

**Retire FMT-5.2 as an extraction artifact (and fix the FMT scan to require a numbered construct clause), or re-scope it to §5.2.1's
meta-rule?**

description-techniques clause and prints no format. `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (l.558–588) emits an FMT row for ANY

denominator — rows LOST; this is a row MANUFACTURED). Recommendation: retire, with a drift assertion that every FMT row has a diagram
or a Format label.
