# PB4485 - the qualified-index-name gate is positioned at the statement

**Where.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/NameResolution.cs` ~351: `ConstructRegistry.Check(data.Edition.Edition, data.Edition.Sink, Constructs.QualifiedIndexName2002, ...)` is
called once per written reference (`_qualifiedIndexGated.Add(writtenAt)`), but reports at the ambient `EditionContext` position, which for `SEARCH ... VARYING IX OF T` is the SEARCH
statement. The reference's own position `writtenAt` is in hand and is not used for the report.

**Repro.** A program with `SEARCH T VARYING IX OF T ...` compiled at `--std 85`: the gate reports at 12,12 (the statement); the qualified index-name IX is at 12,33.

**Fix direction.** Report at `writtenAt` (`using var at = data.Edition.At(...)` as `OptionsBinder.ArithmeticOf` does for its clause), and sweep the other gates of Annex F.2
relaxations in PB4305's list for the same shape (a gate fired from a nested resolver reports at the enclosing statement). Golden: `refuse-at`/warning position of the
qualified name at --std 85.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/NameResolution.cs:344` (`ResolveIndexName`, `WrittenStart`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ConditionBinder.cs:916` (`BindUserWordClassCondition`, `BindPartialClassName`: the item-16 sibling gate); `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/DiagnosticPositionTests.cs` (`EditionGateOfAWrittenWord_NamesTheWordNotTheStatement`).

## Landed (wave 20261849 group F)

**Re-probe** (`ix85.cob`, `--std 85`, pre-fix build): `SEARCH E VARYING IX OF T` reported at (9,12), the SEARCH statement, with IX at (9,29); `SET IX OF T` at (12,12) for IX at (12,16); `PERFORM VARYING IX OF T` at (14,12) for IX at (14,28); a subscript `E (IX OF T)` at the subscripted item (10,21) for IX at (10,24). The same shape held for the item-16 gate: `IF W = "A" AND W IS AL` reported at (13,12), the IF statement, for AL at (13,32).

**Fix.** `ResolveIndexName` positions the sink at the first token of the written reference (`writtenAt`: the `DataReferenceContext` or the name token, through `WrittenStart`) around the qualified-index-name-2002 check; `BindUserWordClassCondition` positions at the alphabet-name's word around the class-condition-alphabet-name-2002 check (its `at` parameter is now a `ParserRuleContext`, the one type its three callers pass). Post-fix every report above stands at the written word. The item-10 gate (CALL USING a level-02..49 group) stays at the statement by design: the bound arm positions at the statement (PB1925) and `BoundCallArg` carries no position of its own.

**Witness.** Six cases of `DiagnosticPositionTests.EditionGateOfAWrittenWord_NamesTheWordNotTheStatement` (SEARCH VARYING, a subscript on the WHEN line, SET, DISPLAY subscript, a PERFORM UNTIL relation, the class condition), each also asserting nothing reports at the statement's own first token. The accepted golden `pb4305_qualified_index_name_2002` and the refusal `pb4305-qualified-index-name-at-85` already cover the rule; a refusal file carries no column, so the position is a test of its own.
