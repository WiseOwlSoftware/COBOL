# PB4915 - a replayed branch stays a DECISION until someone files its abandonment

**What happened.** The planner renders FINISH-FIRST for a SPLIT or report-less predecessor ("cherry-pick its unlanded
commits ... onto current main (never merge the branch)", `scripts/orchestrator/groups.py`). The successor does that, and it
often resolves a conflict or rewrites a line while doing so: tonight it rewrote the bare citations PB4887 refused and
renamed planted cases. The successor lands. The old branch is then not patch-equivalent to anything on main, and its own
DEVLOG pending file is absent from main, because the landing stamped the successor's entry under a new name. So
`prune_worktrees.py` reads it correctly as a DECISION (PB2600: never lose work without a recorded decision), and
`plan_wave.py` holds its members as in-flight work (`inflight.hold`). The work is all on main, but no new group can be
planned on those members until a person files a kb/Work line "`<branch>` ABANDONED: superseded ...", lands it, and runs
`--apply --abandon`.

On 2026-11-07 this stopped campaign PB4812 three times: `worktree-wf_485536d1-d59-1`, `worktree-wf_c3fec2bb-f83-1` and
`worktree-wf_c3fec2bb-f83-3` (PR 1275), `worktree-wf_f12bf129-581-1` (PR 1279) and `worktree-wf_c3fec2bb-f83-2` (PR 1280).
Each time it cost a note-only landing of about 10 to 15 minutes, with the campaign idle, and the session had to notice the
"same-member collision with ... [DECISION]" line in the plan to know why nothing was planned.

**The fix.** The agent that replays a branch is the one that knows it did, so it records the decision in the same change
set as the replay:
1. FINISH-FIRST (`groups.py`) tells the successor to add, for each branch it cherry-picked, the line "Branch `<branch>`
   ABANDONED: superseded, its commits replayed by <this wave and group> (<the commit range>)" to that branch's note. The
   note is the one the predecessor's report names, or the lead note.
2. The landing then carries the record. `land_lane.py` (or `enqueue.py land`, after the merge) runs `prune_worktrees.py
   --apply --abandon` for each branch that the landed change records ABANDONED, so the branch and its member hold are
   gone when the PR merges.
3. Self-test: a planted predecessor branch, a successor that cherry-picks it with one edited line and lands it. After the
   landing, the survey has no DECISION row and the planner holds no members of the old branch.

The record stays a human-readable decision in the note (PB2600), and nothing is deleted without it. What changes is that
the agent which made the decision writes it down.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/groups.py` (whole: the FINISH-FIRST text), `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py` (whole: the post-merge abandon), `scripts/prune_worktrees.py` (`main`), `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` (`main`).
