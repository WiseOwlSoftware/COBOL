# PB4483 - who answers for the text of an imported expansion

PB4469 (landed) made a placement-(a) expansion in the group's own assembly report its text's warnings once, from its sub-compilation, at the EXPANDS entry. Three siblings
stay open on origin/main `ae067404f`; they share one root, who answers for expansion text.

1. **Imported expansions.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Repository/ExpansionPlacementPass.cs` `Place`, the `ExpansionWhere.Imported` arm (~line 151): the group's
   bind of an expansion IMPORTED from the repository reports the text's warnings under the group's state, while an own-assembly expansion reports them once from its
   sub-compilation (`ExpansionAssemblies.Write` withdraws the group's report only for `OwnAssembly`). COBOL3146 appears again at a second client's entry (repro p1/CLIENT1.cob
   then p2/CLIENT2.cob). Decide the owner of an imported expansion's warnings (the library that published it, reported once at compile of the library, or each client at
   its entry) and make both arms one rule.
2. **Parse-tree edition warnings.** Placement (b) and an imported expansion of a repository-read skeleton get no parse-tree-arm edition warnings (COBOL0903 for NEXT
   SENTENCE): the edition pass walks only the raw tree, which does not hold the substituted tokens. The expansion's tree needs the same edition walk its own assembly gets.
3. **Collapsed warnings.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Oo/OoExpansion.cs` ~594: moving every token of a published expansion to the EXPANDS entry's line AND column makes
   `EditionContext.AddOnce` collapse two distinct warnings of the text into one in placement (b), while placement (a) prints two. One rule for the position of an expansion
   token and for `AddOnce`'s key (it must include the region or the token's original position).

Fix once, at the owner of expansion text, with goldens for both client orders; update DESIGN-external-repository section 10.5.
