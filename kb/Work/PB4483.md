# PB4483 - who answers for the text of an imported expansion

PB4469 (landed) made a placement-(a) expansion in the group's own assembly report its text's warnings once, from its sub-compilation, at the EXPANDS entry. Three siblings
stay open on origin/main `0b5581dee`; they share one root, who answers for expansion text.

1. **Imported expansions.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Repository/ExpansionPlacementPass.cs` `Place`, the `ExpansionWhere.Imported` arm (~line 151): the group's
   bind of an expansion IMPORTED from the repository reports the text's warnings under the group's state, while an own-assembly expansion reports them once from its
   sub-compilation (`ExpansionAssemblies.Write` withdraws the group's report only for `OwnAssembly`). COBOL3146 appears again at a second client's entry (repro p1/CLIENT1.cob
   then p2/CLIENT2.cob). Decide the owner of an imported expansion's warnings (the library that published it, reported once at compile of the library, or each client at
   its entry) and make both arms one rule.
2. **Parse-tree edition warnings.** Placement (b) and an imported expansion of a repository-read skeleton get no parse-tree-arm edition warnings (COBOL0903 for NEXT
   SENTENCE): the edition pass walks only the raw tree, which does not hold the substituted tokens. The expansion's tree needs the same edition walk its own assembly gets.
3. **Collapsed warnings.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Oo/OoExpansion.cs` ~594: moving every token of a published expansion to the EXPANDS entry's line AND column makes
   `EditionContext.AddOnce` collapse two distinct warnings of the text into one in placement (b), while placement (a) prints two. One rule for the position of an expansion
   token and for `AddOnce`'s key (it must include the region or the token's original position).

Fix once, at the owner of expansion text, with goldens for both client orders; update DESIGN-external-repository section 10.5.

**Landed (w20261849e).** Re-probe on `32d4e4db1`: CLIENT1 then a second client of the same triple printed COBOL3146 at the second
client's entry; placement (b) of the HOLD skeleton printed COBOL3146 and no COBOL0903; a skeleton with two `IF 1 = 1` relations printed
COBOL3146 once in placement (b) and twice in (a). One rule, carried by the region of the text (DESIGN-external-repository §10.5, "Who answers
for the text of an expansion"): a skeleton written in the group is the user's own text, reported by the group at its own positions
wherever the expansion lives (the own-assembly compilation's warnings of it are dropped, an import leaves the group's report); a skeleton
read from the repository is printed at the EXPANDS entry with `[in the expansion 'NAME']`, keyed once by the place in the text, and only the
compilation that compiles it answers for its warnings: the group for placement (b), the sub-compilation for (a), and the compilation that
made an imported one (`ExpansionPlacementPass` withdraws the group's report; ISO §2023:9.3.12 "treated in all respects the same as if it were a
class that is not a parameterized class"). The parse-tree screens walk `GroupBindContext.Texts` (the written tree and each repository
expansion whose recorded state binds as the group's), held there by `GroupTextScreenDriftTests`. Witnesses:
`ExternalRepositoryExpansionTests.AnImportedExpansion_ReportsNoWarningOfItsText_WhicheverClientMadeIt` (both client orders),
`AnExpansionInTheGroupsAssembly_ReportsItsTextsEditionWarning_AtTheEntry_AsItsOwnAssemblyWould`,
`TwoPlacesOfARepositoryTextRaisingOneWarning_AreTwoReports_InEveryPlacement`,
`ASkeletonWrittenInTheGroup_IsReportedWhereItIsWritten_WhetherItsExpansionIsWrittenApartOrImported`, `DiagnosticRegionTests`.

**Review round 1 (R1-1, fixed by the review finisher).** The parse-tree arm walked every member of `Texts` with ONE `ParseArm`, whose
once-per-word set (COBOL0901, ISO §2023:8.9; COBOL1567 likewise) was shared across texts: a COBOL0901 raised first in a repository text the
group later withdraws (placement (a), or an import) hid the same word in a placement-(b) text, and a word the written tree declared hid it
in every repository text. Repro (the reviewer's): BOXLIB, LIBH and LIBK (skeletons HOLD and KEEP declaring `01 EDITING PIC X.`) and a client
expanding HOLD over BOX (a) and KEEP over its own LBOX (b), `--std 2023 --permissive --source-format free`: before, only the HOLD-BOX
report; after, `CLIENT.cob(28,5): warning COBOL0901 ... [in the expansion 'KEEP-LBOX']` beside it, and alone when HOLD-BOX is imported.
`Run` now walks each text with a walker of its own, so every per-walk state is per text by construction. Witness:
`AReservedWordOfAWithdrawnExpansionText_DoesNotHideTheSameWordInAnotherText` (with and without the word in the written tree; red on the
shared walker).
