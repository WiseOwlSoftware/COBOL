# PB4744 — Messaging slice S4: the exchange (AsyncMessaging, MessagingEmitter, CONTINUE AFTER, the EC-MCS raises); COBOL1578 and the facility's declines retire

A slice of `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-message-control.md` (§4.4, §4.5, §5, §9 S4), which the owner approved on 2026-11-04 (the owner, 2026-11-04: "yes, both approved" (the transports) and "Yes" (both documents and Q1-Q9 as recommended)). Build exactly what that section and the slice's entry say, with its goldens, tests and doc changes in the same landing.

**Prerequisites:** PB4742, PB4743.

## Landed (2026-11-07)

Slice S4 is built as DESIGN-message-control.md section 4.4, 4.5 and 5.7 say, with the corrections the design now records. The re-probe on the build before the change:
`SEND TO "SRV" FROM X RETURNING MT` compiled with the COBOL1578 warning and did nothing, so no server was ever found and `ON EXCEPTION` never ran; after it a
request and its reply cross two processes, an unserved name is EC-MCS-NO-SERVER and `EXCEPTION-STATUS` names it.

- **Runtime.** `AsyncMessaging` (`Runtime/Messaging/AsyncMessaging.cs`) runs a statement on the run unit's `MessagingPeer` and sets the condition the protocol names when the statement's
  checking profile enables it; `MessageOutcome`, `MessageWait` and `MessageWaitKind` are the public surface (PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt). `CobolTiming.Interval` is the one CONTINUE AFTER interval
  rule (`ContinueAfter` suspends by it, a RECEIVE waits by it).
- **Compiler.** `StatementBinder` binds both arms through `MessagingBinder` from 2023 (`BindIntroducedAt`; below it a no-op and COBOL0900). `MessagingEmitter` renders one `AsyncMessaging` call each, stores
  the message by the receiving inverse of a READ, the length through `StoreArith` and the tag through the place writer, and takes the condition through `EcEmitter.EmitSelection` unless ON EXCEPTION takes it.
  `EcBinder.NodeKindRaises` gains the rows of design 4.5.
- **Corrections to the design.** (1) One mechanism, the checking profile, for every EC-MCS name, not five flag declarations per fixed name; `IActivatingStatement` is renamed `IProfiledStatement` and the three nodes
  implement it (design 4.5). (2) `ElementImageCapable` does not admit the message tag (the equality and group-MOVE consumers read it and the placeholder image is not injective); a group holding tags gets `FromMessageImage` beside its one-way image and its tags travel as the message's slots (`AsyncMessaging.TagsOf`, `WithTags` over the leaf vector), design 4.4. A variable-length group holding a tag is refused with COBOL6460 (lead below).
  (3) `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` section 4 item 1 is a tombstone keeping its number, not a renumbering of items 2 to 5 (some 350 citations; a tree-wide rename lands alone).
  (4) The runner's per-test MCS directory is the WiseOwl adapter's `environment` and `CutRunner.RunExit`; `receive-poll` is `MessagingProcessTests`, not a case file.
- **Retired.** COBOL1578, the EC-MCS row of `DeclinedEcFamilies`, the EC-MCS entries of `declined-raise-sites.json`, `derived-verdicts.mcs-only` and its two drift tests, `DocumentedNonSupportWitnessTests.McsFacility_*` and `McsExceptionNames_*`.
- **Goldens.** `send-no-server`, `send-response-no-requester`, `receive-no-requester` (cases/, requires A.3-4), `pb374_ec_mcs_names_raise`, `w59_mcs_facility_witness` (rewritten), the refusal (a variable-length group holding a tag), `send-group-holding-tag`,
  `pb4744-send-from-group-holding-tag`; `MessagingProcessTests` (six: request and reply, a killed server, a server that ends without replying, a message longer than identifier-1, a forwarded exception, poll and a negative interval).
- **Registry.** Fourteen rows are CONFORMS (the general rules of both statements and GR-14.6.11-7); the witnesses of the nine syntax-rule rows and of GR-4.2.10-2 moved.
