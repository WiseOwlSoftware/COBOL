# PB3601 — the restricted data-pointer edge is 2002

## Scenario

The row `usage-pointer-to-type-2014` in `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` is `"status": "pending"` with
`"introducedIn": 2014`; its own text says the edition is UNVERIFIED (Annex E rules out 2023, leaving 2002 or 2014).
The research settles it. P2 ("ISO/IEC 1989:2002(E)", the post-ballot text of 2001-12-04, §13.16.58) has the general
format `POINTER [ TO type-name-1 ]` and its syntax rule 17 reads "If type-name-1 is specified, the TYPEDEF clause
shall be specified". The restricted data-pointer is therefore a 2002 feature, and the row's edge should be 2002 (and
its id `usage-pointer-to-type-2002`).

**Flag choice: `process_only: true`, no harm flag.** The row is pending, so no gate is installed for it: the compiler
does not refuse `POINTER TO type-name` at 2002 today (the pending row's text says the construct is live and measured
to compile; it already draws COBOLNET0900 below 2002 through the `usage-pointer-2002` and TYPEDEF gates). The wrong
edge enshrines nothing yet, and nothing legal is rejected. The defect is a registry row waiting on a fact the
research now supplies.

## Fix (for the fix lane)

1. The row's `introducedIn` becomes 2002, the row is renamed to match, `status` becomes `active`, and its
   `expectDiagnostic` is COBOLNET0900. The `vcr` and `citation` text quote P2's format and SR17. Quote the 2023
   §13.18.60.3 SR18 through `cite.py --check`. Sources and URLs: [[PB296]] "Closed by the research".
2. The edition-gate sweep (`new-construct` skill): corpus at 85 / 2002 / 2014 / 2023.
3. A 2002 acceptance golden (the row's own source, which compiles at 2002+), and a below-2002 refusal check.
