## Measured
```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. B10OC.

       01 ZC CONSTANT AS 0.
       01 T.

```

→ ONE diagnostic, for line 9 (`OCCURS 0 TIMES`: COBOLNET2386). Line 7 (`OCCURS ZC TIMES`) is accepted.

## The rule
`cite.py --check 5.5 "shall be unsigned and nonzero unless otherwise specified in the associated rules"` → `OK §5.5 1)`.
SR-5.5-1 is CONFORMS (PB859) for the literal spelling; the screen reads the TOKEN, and a constant-name is resolved
at bind time after it.

## What the fixer owes
Screen the RESOLVED value at the one place a constant-name is substituted for an integer-n (the constant arm of the
integer-operand decoder), so every integer-n position gets it — sweep OCCURS, BLOCK CONTAINS, RECORD CONTAINS/VARYING,
PAGE/LINE/COLUMN, LINAGE, ALPHABET/SYMBOLIC integers. Negative golden with a constant operand.

**Code site (located 2026-09-27).** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Constants.cs#OccursBoundValue`; also `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Validation/IntegerOperandPass.cs#IntegerOperandPass.VisitIntegerLiteral`, `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Constants.cs#ExpandPicConstants` (code). OccursBoundValue resolves the occursBound cobolWord arm to the constant's IntegerText with no nonzero screen; IntegerOperandPass.VisitIntegerLiteral screens only the literal token (COBOLNET2386)
