# PB2981 — a train of every waiting branch

**Found by:** the operator, 2026-10-10 ~21:00 PDT, with 15 branches WAITING TO LAND (six wave-1055 compiler finishers,
the loop's wave-1056 branches, four process branches). Owner: "Yes, do not try and do 15 branches at once. We'll just
hit the same issues as this morning".

## What happened this morning

Account 2's week opened at 10:00 on 2026-10-10. By 14:50 15 branches (13 clusters) waited for ONE train, because the
land unit's prompt said so. Its lander split the train into three serial lander agents, hand-applied each cluster,
found fix-ups only after merge, and at 14:51 had run 1.5 h with its Linux gate RED and nothing on main (memory
`feedback_landing_is_the_measure`; the owner's rule since then: trains of 4-6).

## Mechanism

`scripts/orchestrator/units/land.md` step 2: "The candidates are EVERY branch `prune_worktrees.py --brief` lists
WAITING TO LAND ... Carry them ALL in ONE train." MANDATORY-PRACTICES O11, the workstream SKILL §3,
`references/landing.md` and `DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md` section 3's `land` row said the same. The 4-6 band lived only
in a memory note and in the rolling wave's `train_size` (5), which governs a wave's own trains and not the land unit's.

## Fix (landed)

- `scripts/prune_worktrees.py`: `TRAIN_MAX = 6`, `next_train(rows, rank)` (the WAITING TO LAND rows ordered by their
  lead note's harm weight, a compiler note before a process-only one, then the oldest report; the first `TRAIN_MAX`),
  `note_rank()` (the planner's weights through the new `plan_wave.harm_weights()`, one reading of `.agent-fleet.json`
  `harm`), and `--train`, which prints the next train and the HELD rows (`--json` carries `train` and `held`).
- `units/land.md` step 2: the train is EXACTLY what `--train` names; the HELD rows go in `branches_pending` for the
  next land unit; an operator steer that names the train wins. O11, SKILL §3, `references/landing.md` and the design's
  `land` row say the same.
- Drift guard: `prune_worktrees.py --self-test` plants eight waiting rows (and a LANDED one) and requires the train to
  be the six most harmful, compiler before process at equal harm, oldest first, with two held. Proven to fail: with
  `TRAIN_MAX = 99` the self-test went RED (all eight in the train, none held).
- The same evening's land unit was steered by hand (`steer.ps1 -Unit land`) to the six wave-1055 compiler branches.

## Not in this change

Train 1056's members still read WAITING TO LAND while their lander is carrying them (the survey does not read an
in-flight train's manifest). A land unit started then would pick them again; the landing lease makes it wait, and the
rows read LANDED once the train is on main. The same missing manifest read is kb/Work PB2708.
