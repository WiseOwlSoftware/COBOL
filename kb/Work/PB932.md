## Why the audit passes all four

`audit_code_citations.py` verifies that the cited clause EXISTS. §8.5.1.3 exists. §13.18.7 exists. The
audit is therefore green on a citation that sends the reader to the wrong rule — which is precisely the
inheritance failure CLAUDE.md rule 1 describes, and precisely what **PB900** (registrar #6) asks the audit
to grow the ability to see. The two notes are one fill unit: PB900 builds the detector, this one is the
population it will find and the four that are already known.
