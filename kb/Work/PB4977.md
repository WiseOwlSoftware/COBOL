# PB4977 - the dynamic SEARCH prelude is held outside its WHEN phrase

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/SearchBinder.cs` (`SearchBinder.DynamicTablePath`, `prelude = host.Udf.OperandSteps(...)`, about line 95; the `whenEnclosing` / `Borrowed` consumers), `DebugTriggerPlanner.Leaves` over the `BoundSequence`, `BoundSearch.WhenSubscripts`.

**Report.** For a nested dynamic-capacity table whose WHEN subscripts contain a function or an object property, `SearchBinder.DynamicTablePath` hoists their
evaluation ahead of the scan as a separate statement step (`OperandSteps` of the WHEN subscripts' function/property pre-ops). The debug planner may then count an
operand of those steps as a reference of the statement even when no WHEN phrase is evaluated (e.g. AT END with no WHEN reached). NOT probed (too large for the round).
Same root as PB4855's R1-1, which fixed the other two consumers (`Table`, `DynTable`) with `BoundSearch.WhenSubscripts` + the implicit-operand skip: an operand borrowed from a WHEN
phrase and held outside it. Probe shape: `wdyn.cob` in `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261921e/f/` (`--std 2023 --permissive`, `C OCCURS DYNAMIC CAPACITY IN CC INDEXED BY CX` under `ROW OCCURS 2`) with a function in the WHEN subscript, plus a USE FOR DEBUGGING on the subscript's operand; expect no debug section on the AT END leg.
Witness to extend: `DebugFacilityTests.SearchWhenNamedOccurrence_IsReferencedOnlyWhenTheWhenIsEvaluated`.
