Landed (wave 20262009, group A). Re-probe on this build: the loopback program of the golden before the fix gave GC 6 status 50 and GC 4 status 00 with the group's binary bytes in the message, as the note says.

**Cause.** An elementary binary or packed item was already its standard digits (`AsTransferString` through `AsString`), so the elementary measure was right; the group was its storage image, in bytes. **Fix.** At --std 85 `CommunicationEmitter.SendingText` composes a group that holds a binary, packed or COMP-5 item in standard data format: `ItemLength.StandardLayout` states where each item sits in the group's storage (a part per item, a table repeating its element, runs of plain characters merged, none when nothing converts), `RuntimeApi.StandardDataImage` carries the layout and `CobolStandardData.Image` (runtime, `StandardDataPart`) replaces each such item by its digits, so the text length is counted against `ItemLength.StandardDataFormatCharacters` and the characters sent are the same. From 2002 a group is its storage image and the elementary arm is unchanged: the 2002 text is not in the specs this repository carries (`docs/CONFORMANCE.md` section 3.3 records the reading).
