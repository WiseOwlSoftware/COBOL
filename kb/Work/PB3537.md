# PB3537 — the fuzz harness lets a degenerate reply run to the token cap

## Facts

- The third overnight run started 16:02 PDT with nemotron-3.5-bolt (run directory
  `conformance-suite/results/fuzz-20261022-160216`).
- 19 of its first 90 replies (21 %, rising as the walk reached §13.18 and §15 seeds) were cut off at the 6,000-token cap.
- Every cut-off reply inspected is a DEGENERATE ENUMERATION (data items WS-ARG1 … WS-ARG309, VALUE lists, hundreds of
  lines differing only by a counter), with `think: false` honoured (no thinking characters). The repetition is typical of
  a very low-bit quantization: `ollama show` reports the model as 74.0B parameters at IQ2_XXS.
- The 20-program bake-off did not expose it (its 4 model failures were not inspected for this shape).
- The owner, asked "Switch tonight's run to qwen3.6:35b?", answered "yes" at about 17:25 PDT, and the run was restarted
  with `-Model qwen3.6:35b`. The nemotron rows stay on disk for the morning triage.

The evidence record is `docs/rearchitecture/evidence/fleet-optimization/2026-10-22-differential-fuzz-bakeoff.md`
(addendum).

## Repro

Open `programs/fz00088.reply0.txt` in the run directory `conformance-suite/results/fuzz-20261022-160216`: 10,540
characters, data items WS-ARG1 to WS-ARG309.

## Fix shape (for the implementer)

- Stream the reply (or poll the partial) and abort when the last N lines differ only by a counter or repeat a template (a
  measured threshold).
- Record the abort as its own NO_PROGRAM reason ('degenerate enumeration') and count it per model in the bake-off table.
- Expose Ollama's `repeat_penalty` / `repeat_last_n` as per-model options.
- A self-test over a stubbed degenerate reply covers the abort and the recording.

**Sites:** `scripts/fuzz_ollama.py` (`EnumerationWatch`, `ollama_chat`, `read_reply`, `repeat_abort`, `parse_model_options`),
`scripts/differential_fuzz.py` (`Fuzz._one`, `Settings`, `cut_off_reason`, `retry_request`, `render_report`, `bake_off`,
`bake_off_score`, `BakeOffScore`, `main`, `self_test`)

## Landed (2026-10-26, wave 20261711 group A)

Re-probed on the dispatch base: `conformance-suite/results/fuzz-20261022-160216/programs/fz00088.reply0.txt` still holds
10,540 characters of WS-ARG1 … WS-ARG309, and `ollama_chat` still read the whole reply (`stream: false`) before anything
could cut it. One mechanism, fixed at the root (the model's side of the harness is now `scripts/fuzz_ollama.py`):

- **The reply is streamed and watched.** `EnumerationWatch` normalizes each line (digit runs to one mark) and trips when the
  last 150 non-blank lines hold at most 3 distinct forms; the harness then closes the connection (Ollama stops generating),
  and the reply comes back cut four lines into the window with `done_reason` "degenerate". The threshold is MEASURED over the
  44,737 distinct replies five runs left on disk (module docstring): 0 of the 44,428 complete programs trip it, 77 of the 309
  that never completed do, and `fz00088` is cut after 5,061 of its 10,540 characters (the 77 replies: 53 % of their
  characters read before the cut). A window of 100 lines flagged 35 complete programs and 120 flagged one, so the
  threshold is not a round number.
- **Recorded as its own shape.** A cut reply never yields a program (it is the front of one the model did not finish), the
  model is asked for a SHORTER program (`retry_request`), and a program that ends so is `NO_PROGRAM` with the reason
  "degenerate enumeration: …" (`cut_off_reason`). Ollama's own repeat-limit abort is the same shape (`DEGENERATE_REASONS`),
  and its message is now the recorded reply instead of an empty file. `degenerate_replies` on the row, a line of
  `report.md` and a column of the bake-off table (`BakeOffScore`, a NamedTuple replacing a nine-element positional tuple)
  count them per model.
- **Per-model Ollama options.** `--model-option MODEL:repeat_penalty=1.1` and `repeat_last_n` (`MODEL_OPTION_TYPES`: one
  line per further option), refused when the model is not one the run uses.
- **Witnesses.** `python scripts/fuzz_ollama.py --self-test` runs a stub Ollama over a local socket (chunked NDJSON like the
  real server): an endless enumeration of 200,000 lines is cut after about 150 and the stub sees the client leave; a
  mid-reply error, a cut stream, HTTP 500 and 400, the `think` retry and the abort messages are each answered. The watch is
  checked against a brute-force reference, whole and in pieces. `differential_fuzz.py --self-test` drives `Fuzz._one` with
  degenerate replies (no program compiled although the cut front holds a PROCEDURE DIVISION) and the report and bake-off
  counts.
