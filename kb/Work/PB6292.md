**Rule it breaks:** §2023:8.4.2.3.2 writes a subscript as exactly ALL | arithmetic-expression-1 | index-name-1 [{+|-} integer-1]; an index-name is no operand of arithmetic-expression-1 (§2023:8.8.1.1 names identifiers of numeric data items, numeric literals and ZERO only), and §2023:13.18.38.3 SR7 admits index-name-1 'as a subscript', i.e. as the third alternative, not as an operand inside the second

**Scenario:** --std 2023 (strict), T OCCURS 10 INDEXED BY IX, J PIC 9: MOVE T (IX * 2) TO R, MOVE T (J + IX) TO R and MOVE T (IX + J) TO R compile with no diagnostic and run on the occurrence number (the GnuCOBOL coercion), where COMPUTE N = IX * 2 is refused COBOL1637 by the same rule (kb/Work R29). Mechanism: the direct walk's ResolveSubscriptName returns IndexCellOf for an index-name at SegmentPosition.Subscript wherever the walk meets it (Additive/Multiplicative/Unary all descend to Primary), never asking whether the index-name is the whole subscript or the left operand of one + or - and an integer; and a position the walk hands to D18 (T (IX ** 2), T (IX / 1)) binds under BindIndexNameWindowExpr, which admits an index-name as any operand. At --std 85 the shape gate VisitSubscriptPart already refuses IX * 2 and IX + N (kb/Work PB5074 coderead row 1985-FMT-IV.4.3.8.2.2), so only 2002, 2014 and 2023 under-reject.

**Measured how:** R2 batch finding `reference-resolution-1--code--f2#1` (pair reference-resolution-1--code), read in the pinned tree at 9e65164eb3a9; skeptics rule upheld, scenario upheld, site upheld.

**Spec:** §2023:13.18.38.3 "Index-name-1 may be specified only in the following contexts" (`cite.py --check` passed at collection); §2023:8.8.1.1 "An arithmetic expression may be an identifier referencing a numeric data item, a numeric literal, the figurative constant ZERO" (`cite.py --check` passed at collection)

**Target:** One subscript-shape screen applied to the written position before either route: an index-name operand is admitted only as the whole subscript or as index-name-1 {+|-} integer-1 (an unsigned integer literal); any other index-name occurrence in a subscript takes the R29 disposition (strict COBOL1637 citing §2023:8.4.2.3.2, --permissive warning plus the occurrence-number coercion), so the direct walk and the D18 route reach one verdict as BindPosition's summary promises
