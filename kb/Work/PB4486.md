# PB4486 - how is a 2002-edition citation validated?

CLAUDE.md rule 1 requires `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check <clause> "<text>"` for every citation. `specs/ISO_COBOL_2023.md` is the 2023 text. The PB4305 relaxations are
defined by "ISO/IEC 1989:2002 Annex F.2 item N" (for example item 108 "Index-names may be qualified in the same manner as data and condition-names", item 90). That text
is not in the repository's spec, so the citations in `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (the F.2 item comments), the PB4305 goldens and
docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md fail `cite.py --check`; the audit is held off by their shape or they are unchecked.

**The checkable witness.** For item 90 the 2023 text 14.2.1 Format 1 is the witness the 2023 spec holds; for the other items the 2023 clause that carries the relaxed rule
should be cited, with the F.2 item number as the edition-change pointer, not as the authority.

**Options.** (a) every 2002-edition claim cites the 2023 clause that now states the rule (checkable) plus the F.2 item as an unchecked pointer in a fixed shape the audit
recognises and skips; (b) the repo gains the 2002 change list as a second checked spec file (`specs/` copy, licensing question); (c) leave unchecked, documented in the
VCR. PB3965 (open) re-derives the other derived constructs.json edges from the F.2 list and needs the answer. Owner decision; one question: which option.
