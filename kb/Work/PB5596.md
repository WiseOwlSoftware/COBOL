# PB5596 - 1985 KEPT records are exposed to the same scope error as the 2002 sample

**Sites:** none (an inventory re-read; verdicts are recorded through the one change-record writer, PB5589).

**Evidence.**
- the blind sample (`sample-k0/`) found 16 of 100 word-identical 2002 pairs DIFFERENT through scope loss, 10 of them the
  communication section;
- `1985-SR-VI.5.5.3-1` ("In the File, Working-Storage, Communication, and Linkage Sections, data-name-1 or the key word
  FILLER ... must be the first word") is KEPT into `SR-13.16.3-4`, which names no communication section;
- `1985-SR-VII.2.5.3-2` ("must not be defined in the File Section, Report Section, or the Communication Section") is KEPT
  into `SR-12.4.5.8.3-2`.

**Exposure.**
- 297 of 1,373 1985 KEPT rows chain into 2023 data-description rules;
- 124 are in clauses a CD record can use;
- 10 name a removed element in their own text.

**Owed.**
- run the pending-clause and scope triggers of PLAN-2002-v3-K0 section 5 (T5, T6 and T8) over the 1985 KEPT records;
- re-judge the hits as CHANGED with their own verdict and a CD (or removed-form) witness at 85;
- expect about 70-90 rows to move from OK to GAP until the witnesses land.

**Rows.** The analysis claims the 124 when the trigger run lists them; they are not listed here because no run produced
the list in the filing session.
