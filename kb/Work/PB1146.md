## Measured (adjudicator / refuter evidence, verbatim)
### [procedure-division-structure] The procedureDivision grammar is `sentence* procedureUnit*` with procedureUnit = section | paragraph, so Format 1 (with sections) and Format 2 (without) are not distinguished anywhere: sentences or paragraphs before the first section, and paragraphs after END DECLARATIVES, compile silently
- **Rows:** FMT-14.2.1 — harm `under-rejects`
- **Repro:** (1) `PROCEDURE DIVISION. DISPLAY 'A'. S1 SECTION. P1. DISPLAY 'B'. STOP RUN.` compiles and prints A B. (2) `PROCEDURE DIVISION. P0. DISPLAY 'A'. S1 SECTION. P1. DISPLAY 'B'. STOP RUN.` compiles and prints A B. (3) `PROCEDURE DIVISION. DECLARATIVES. D1 SECTION. USE AFTER EXCEPTION CONDITION EC-SIZE. D1P. DISPLAY 'DECL'. END DECLARATIVES. P1. DISPLAY 'B'. STOP RUN.` compiles and prints B. Probes out/probe/pds/fmt_sent_before_section.cob, fmt_para_before_section.cob, decl_fmt2.cob; --std 2023, no warning.
- **Expected (spec):** §14.2.1 Format 1 is `procedure-division-header [DECLARATIVES. {section-name SECTION. use-statement. ...}... END DECLARATIVES.] [{section-name SECTION. [sentence]... [paragraph-name. [sentence]...]...}...]` - nothing may stand between the header (or END DECLARATIVES) and the first section header; Format 2 has no DECLARATIVES. §14.4.1: 'If one paragraph is in a section, all paragraphs shall be in sections.' All three programs shall be refused (or, if an extension is wanted, dialect-gated and documented - an owner decision, never silent).
- **Observed:** No binder pass checks the section/paragraph mix (searched src for 'first section' / 'all paragraphs' / §14.4.1: none). The leading sentences and P0 are placed in an implicit unnamed unit and executed.
- **Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#procedureDivision; src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ProcedureTableBuilder.cs

Decide the format by the first procedure unit and screen the rest (binder or grammar), including DECLARATIVES requiring sections. Render §14.2.1 before the change; check the corpus for dialect leniency (IBM accepts paragraphs before the first section) and gate it if needed.

## Landed (wave 1015, group C) — Format 1 and Format 2 told apart

**Re-probed** on base `fbcb2b3b1`: all three repros compiled with no diagnostic (A B, A B, B). GnuCOBOL 3.2 also
accepts them (a regression net, not authority; CLAUDE.md rule 1 — the ISO rule controls and no vendor dialect is
declared). **Spec:** §14.2.1 rendered from PDF page 557 — Format 1 prints ONE bracketed DECLARATIVES portion and
nothing between the header (or END DECLARATIVES) and the first section header; §14.4.1 "If one paragraph is in a
section, all paragraphs shall be in sections" (both cite-checked). **Fix:** `ProcedureFormatPass.VisitProcedureDivision`
(the pass PB351 landed with) refuses, once a division has a section or a DECLARATIVES portion, a leading sentence, a
paragraph outside every section, or a second DECLARATIVES portion (the grammar's `declarativePart*` is the superset) —
COBOLNET2797 `procedure-division-format-mixed`, one per kind at its first occurrence, at every edition; method
procedure divisions share the rule. **Corpus:** none of NIST; nine of this repo's own sources wrote a paragraph or sentence outside their sections — goldens pb1173, pb322, pb1141, pb1139, pb1094 (paragraph ahead of the sections), pb1937, pb1118, pb460 (paragraph after END DECLARATIVES) gain a `MAIN-SECTION SECTION.` header, output unchanged; `pb63_exception_location_procedure_field` exercised EXCEPTION-LOCATION's no-paragraph-no-section case with a sentence ahead of a section, so that case moved to its method's Format 2 body (line numbers and `.out` re-derived); `OoPortedTests.Ported_Method_WithSection_FailsLoudly` and `SectionDifferentialTests` (MAIN-PARA ahead of the sections) gained sections, their differential goldens re-baked against the legacy oracle with identical output. **Goldens:** `conformance:85/pb1146_procedure_division_formats`
(Format 1 with DECLARATIVES and a section opening with sentences; a nested Format 2 program); negatives
`pb1146-paragraph-before-section`, `-sentence-before-section`, `-paragraph-after-declaratives`,
`-second-declaratives`. Row FMT-14.2.1 restated CONFORMS with these witnesses.
