# PB3031 — columns after written text with supplementary characters

Filed 2026-10-11 by the train 1060 lander from the wave 1059 group A implementer's lead (new defect 2 of
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1059a-PB2982-report.md`; PB2983's body names it "not reached"). Not re-probed.

**Mechanism.** PB2983 made every diagnostic name a CHARACTER POSITION (§6.1 1) a), §6.3.1): the reference-format stage
splits an origin span at each tab and each supplementary-plane character, so within a span one resultant UTF-16 unit
is one physical position. Text a later stage WRITES has no source line to map: `OriginWriter.Write` / `Append`
(`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Common/SourceLineMap.cs`) give a REPLACE replacement one span at its start, so each
supplementary-plane character in the replacement counts two units, and every column the parser reports after it on
that resultant line is one further right per such character.

**Repro shape.** `REPLACE ==X== BY ==<two supplementary letters>==.` then a line `MOVE X TO.` — the parser's
COBOL0001 at the period names a column two to the right of the period the programmer wrote. Fix shape: the writer
splits the written text's span at each supplementary character exactly as `TextSite.Spans` does (shares the PB2986
family: columns changed after the map is built).
