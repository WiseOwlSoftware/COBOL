## Landed (wave 20261801, group A)

`build-and-test.yml`'s matrix is `linux-shard` (5 shards, Debug, under the inert watch, INV-1-strong a step of each) and
`windows-shard` (7 shards, Release), each building the solution and running all three test assemblies with
`WISEOWL_COBOL_SHARD=k/N`; `guard`, `inv1-strong-2023`, `conformance-population`, `greenfield-conformance`,
`greenfield-unit`, `windows-conformance` and `windows-build-test` are gone (Guard's one home is the nightly). The
`--built` self-tests are their own job, `linux-built-self-tests`, beside the shards: as a step their 122 s would make one
shard the longest. `ci-gate` folds the population check in: it downloads `test-results-*-shard-*` and runs
`test_population.py check` per OS and assembly against shard 1's one listing per OS (3 listings an OS, where the name
split listed 12 times), and `--method NistProgram_MatchesGolden` over INV-1's five trx. Timeouts 15 (Linux) and 20
(Windows). The shard hook, the weights file and `generate`, which the notes put in step 5, are built here: the matrix is
their one user, and step 5 builds its local orderer from them. `WISEOWL_COBOL_SHARD` is a scrubbed variable, so no local
`dotnet test` caller can be narrowed by it, and `audit-callers` lets a workflow set it only where it runs the check.

Probe (2026-11-01, this tree): `shard_weights.py generate` read runs 41284663605, 41284754898, 41286199098 (6
observations, 1,088 classes); its deal puts 5 and 7 shards within 1 % of the mean. The Unit assembly run as 7 local
shards and checked: `=== POPULATION WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit.csproj (7 probe shards): EXACT — 39,119/39,119 cases ran
(skipped 0) across 7 trx ===`; Characterization as 3: EXACT 36/36, one shard of zero cases exiting 0 with a trx.

Not verifiable off CI: the merge group's wall time (the review's estimate is about 7.7 minutes against 14.7 for run
41284754898) and the first live `shard-spread` report, which the first nightly after landing reads.
