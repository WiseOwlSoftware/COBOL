## Measured

### Finding redefines-clause — §13.18.44.3 SR14; §13.18.58.4 GR3; §13.18.57.3 SR2 (under-rejects; rows SR-13.18.44.3-14)

**Mechanism.** strong-template-internal-redefines: Sr12Sr14Violation exempts a data-name-2 whose strong root equals the subject's, so a REDEFINES written inside a TYPEDEF STRONG template is accepted; by §13.18.58.4 GR3 that clause is assumed by every TYPE subject, making data-name-2 'an item subordinate to a strongly-typed group item'. Because §13.18.57.3 SR2 forbids written subordinates under a TYPE entry, the template is the ONLY way SR14's subordinate disjunct can be met - the exemption makes it vacuous. The landed PB183 derivation (golden pb183_redefines_in_strong_typedef_ok) answered §13.18.57.3 SR4, a different rule, and never tested §13.18.44.3 SR14's letter; that golden must flip with the fix.

**Repro.** `01 T IS TYPEDEF STRONG. 05 X PIC X(4). 05 Y REDEFINES X PIC X(4). 01 S TYPE T.` PROCEDURE: `MOVE "ABCD" TO X IN S. DISPLAY Y IN S.` (sr14_in_template.cob).

## Landed (wave 1021 group E)

Re-probed: `sr14_in_template` compiled and printed ABCD before the change.

**Determination.** §13.18.44.3 SR14 (cite.py OK) bars a data-name-2 that is "an item subordinate to a strongly-typed
group item". §13.18.58.4 GR3 (cite.py OK: "All other data description clauses and subordinate data descriptions are
assumed by data defined using the type-name") makes the template's REDEFINES part of every TYPE subject, and Annex D.8.3
calls such items strongly typed ("subordinate to a type declaration with the STRONG phrase") and prohibits their
redefinition. The same-root exemption could never be bypassed any other way: a subject and its data-name-2 are siblings,
so they share one strong root, and the arm it guarded was vacuous. The RENAMES twin (§13.18.57.3 SR3, COBOLNET1532) never
had the exemption (probed: a RENAMES inside a strong template is refused).

**Fix.** `DataBinder.Sr12Sr14Violation` drops the same-root test; the clause is reported once per TYPE subject as
COBOLNET1697. `CheckStrongTypeDeclarations` keeps its SR4 exemption for the internal case on purpose so one entry draws
one diagnostic (SR4's subject is the TYPE entry and PB183's determination of that rule stands for an EXTERNAL
redefinition); its documentation now says where the internal case is reported.

**Goldens.** `conformance:2023/pb183_redefines_in_strong_typedef_ok` pinned the acceptance and is deleted;
`negative/pb1282-redefines-in-strong-typedef` replaces it, and `conformance:2002/pb1282_redefines_in_weak_typedef_ok`
pins that a WEAK template keeps its REDEFINES. An unused strong typedef holding a REDEFINES is not flagged (the
violation exists in a data item, the instance).
