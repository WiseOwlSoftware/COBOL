**Slice 5's text joined it (2026-10-29, kb/Work PB2101).** The trimmed/AOT paragraph of the chapter "Calling a WiseOwl COBOL
program from .NET" is in `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-external-repository.md` §9.4 (the feature switch

## Landed (wave 20261791, group C)

`docs/manual/` exists: the index, Install, Getting started, the User guide (command line, editions, source format and
COPY, run units, locale and collation, exceptions, files), .NET interoperability, Migrating and the release-notes
convention. Every behavioral statement was run on the compiler and cites its clause or its CONFORMANCE.md determination;
`UserManualDriftTests` keeps the options, exit statuses, diagnostic codes, links and printed programs true. The host
chapter of design section 9.4 moved into `docs/manual/dotnet-interop.md` with slice 5's trimmed/AOT paragraph, and the
Migrating page carries the clause 4.2.4 statement. What stays open elsewhere: *File types* is kb/Work PB3352 (its page
joins `docs/manual/`), the "Separately compiled units and the repository" page is kb/Work PB2103's (it describes options
the command line does not have yet), and the pages that the generators of PB1611 and PB1612 supersede in part
(`user-guide/command-line.md`'s option table, `user-guide/editions.md`'s pointer to `constructs.json`) and the packaging
of PB1613 (`install.md`) say so in their own text.
