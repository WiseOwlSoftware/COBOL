**Found by:** the R1 refuter (wave 1031, C9) re-planning PB2119; confirmed by the reviser.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py:274` (`choose_model`: the first `opus_when` match wins — file
patterns, then each note's area against `area_patterns`, then body patterns, then the failed-attempt count; the default
is Sonnet), `scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json` `routing.opus_when.area_patterns` (`"^architecture"`), and every
PB2119 note filed by `scripts/arch/file_census_notes.py` (`area: architecture`, body `**Model:** Sonnet (census-measured,
mechanical)` for the 94 Delete and Move/rename notes; the 9 Unify notes say NEEDS-OPUS).

**Repro:** from a checkout with `tools/claude-skills` initialized, `python -I scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py
--cluster PB2119 --dry-run --budget-points 1000 --no-branches --max-groups 200` → `75 groups, 15 train(s)`, every group
line `opus`, reason `PB… area architecture` (the refuter's driver `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w1031-refute/c9_driver.py`
gives the same from a worktree without the submodule; see PB2295).

**The rule it breaks:** kb/Work R69 §4 ("Sonnet: … move-and-rename and analyzer waves driven by a Roslyn rewriter,
Delete waves whose reachability the census has already measured") and the notes' own model lines. `^architecture` was
added for R0/R1 items (R69 §4's last bullet; `model_rules.json` `_why`), and it now also catches the whole Delete
program, silently: nothing reports that a note's stated model was overridden.

**Scenario:** the orchestrator plans the 65 Delete groups at Opus's rate, ≈ 2.5 times the weekly points the notes
were sized at, and the 85 % weekly cap (R69 §6) is reached that much sooner.

**Why it was not fixed in the R1 revision (MANDATORY-PRACTICES I9):** it is not a one-rule change. Narrowing
`^architecture` re-routes every other `architecture` note (Extract notes PB2296–PB2347 must stay Opus); honoring a note's
`**Model:**` line needs a new rule kind in `choose_model` (a note's declared model, checked against `routing.allowed`,
taking precedence over `area_patterns` but not over `file_patterns` or failed attempts) with its tests in
`tests/…/PlanWave*` and a decision on precedence that R69 §4 implies but does not state.

**Fix shape:** add `routing.declared_model_pattern` (the `**Model:** <model>` line) read by `choose_model` before the
area rule; a test that a Sonnet-declared `architecture` note routes Sonnet, an Opus/NEEDS-OPUS note routes Opus, and a
file-pattern hit (`.g4`) still routes Opus; re-run the PB2119 dry run and record the new split. **Model:** Opus
(orchestrator design).
