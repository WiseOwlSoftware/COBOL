# PB6164 — OPEN — stale rule numbers in the 2023 standard's cross-references

§2023:A.1 item 65 ("EXIT and GOBACK statements") and §2023:14.9.14.4 GR3 ("execution proceeds as specified in 14.9.18, GOBACK
statement, General rules 3 and 4") carry 2002's rule numbers. In 2023, GR3 and GR4 of 14.9.18.4 are the STOP-like rule and the
method rule; the latitude is §2023:14.9.18.4 GR1a, "If the activating runtime element is a non-COBOL element, execution continues in
the activating element in an implementor-defined fashion". Record it in `docs/conformance/` as a defect in the standard (CLAUDE.md
rule 1). Item 64 is not part of it: its cross-reference to GR2 of 11.9.7.4 is right (PB6130's refuter).
