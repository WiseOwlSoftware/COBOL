# PB4577 - SEARCH of a nested table with the enclosing table's subscript is accepted at 85 and 2002

**What is wrong.** `SEARCH H (GI)`, where `H` is a table nested in `G` and `GI` is the subscript of the enclosing table, compiles with no diagnostic and runs at `--std 85`, `2002`, `2014` and 2023. The 2014 and 2023 rules allow it: §2023:14.9.37.3 2) (`cite.py --check` OK) "identifier-1 shall not be subscripted at the level for which the SEARCH is applicable" bans the searched level's subscript alone, and 3) (OK) "Identifier-1 may be contained within one or more other tables, for which the subscripting is still required." The 2002 text and the 2009 committee draft (CD 1.2) say "Identifier-1 shall not be subscripted or reference modified" (`E:/COBOL-coord/specs-drafts/t2002.txt:27472` and `t2009.txt:29403`; draft text outside the tracked tree, so not `cite.py`-checkable). A conforming 85 or 2002 compiler must therefore refuse the form. At 2014 it is legal (owner decision below), and GnuCOBOL's refusal under `-std=cobol2014` is that project's defect (PB4547).

**Repro** (free format; found by fuzz night 10's vetting of PB4547, probe `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/fuzz-vet/step2/f1-misc/PB4547/prog85.cob`; `--std 2014 --source-format free` accepts the source of PB4547 as it stands, and `--std 85` needs a paragraph name before the first statement):

```cobol
IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
PROGRAM-ID. SR1.
DATA DIVISION.
WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
01 T-VALS PIC X(6) VALUE "123789".
01 T REDEFINES T-VALS.
   05 G OCCURS 2 TIMES INDEXED BY GI.
      10 H PIC 9 OCCURS 3 TIMES INDEXED BY HI.
PROCEDURE DIVISION.
MAIN-PARA.
    SET GI TO 2
    SET HI TO 1
    SEARCH H (GI)
        AT END DISPLAY "NOT FOUND"
        WHEN H (GI, HI) > 7
            DISPLAY "FOUND " H (GI, HI)
    END-SEARCH
    STOP RUN.
```

Observed: no diagnostic, the run prints `FOUND 8`, exit 0, at `--std 85`, `2002` and `2014`. Expected at 85 and 2002: COBOL2075 refuses `SEARCH H (GI)`; at 2014 and 2023 it compiles and prints `FOUND 8`, as now.

**Root.** COBOL2075 (`SearchIdentifier1Operand`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` about line 4622) is written from the 2023 SR2/SR3 wording and `ResolveSearchTable` applies it at every edition: it bans only subscripts beyond the superordinate count (`operand.SubscriptCount > superordinate`), an upper bound that is right for 2014 and 2023 and too permissive at 85 and 2002. The golden `pb3365_search_nested_dynamic_table` is `accept: [2014]` and uses both spellings (identifier-1 with the enclosing subscript, and bare), which is right; no case refuses the subscripted spelling at 85 or 2002.

**Edition boundary (decided).** Owner decision, revised 2026-11-04 (asked with this evidence, answered "Treat 2014 as allowing it"): COBOL 2014 allows the enclosing tables' subscripts on identifier-1. The 2002 text (t2002.txt:27472) and the 2009 CD 1.2 (t2009.txt:29403) say "Identifier-1 shall not be subscripted or reference modified"; the later drafts, 202x CD 1.3 (14.9.40.3, p. 794) and 2023 CD2, carry the 2023 rules 2) and 3); and 2023 Annex E, which lists the substantive changes since 2014 including relaxations (E.3, item 43), has no SEARCH entry, so the relaxation came in the final 2014 standard, after the 2009 CD (VCR row 7.13 reads Annex E the same way). The 2014 text itself is not held. The 1985 wording is not held; 1985 is taken as no more permissive than 2002, whose rule 1 it shares by the 2002 Annex F list (no relaxation of SEARCH identifier-1 is listed there). The version matrix (`docs/VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE.md`, `docs/VERSION_TEST_MATRIX_DESIGN.md`) records the edge with the fix.

**Fix shape.** Edition-gate the enclosing-table subscript on identifier-1 (a version predicate in `ResolveSearchTable`, the way other edition rules are gated), with a message naming the edition's rule; keep the golden at `accept: [2014]` (2014 and later) and add a negative case refused at 85 and 2002; sweep the sibling `SEARCH ALL` format (`CheckSearchAllFormat2`) and every other place that reads identifier-1's subscript count. A design correction updates `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` D-SRCH-DYN and the SEARCH row of the version matrix in the same change.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs#SearchIdentifier1Operand`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs#ResolveSearchTable`, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs#CheckSearchAllFormat2`, `conformance-suite/cases/14.9.37-SEARCH/pb3365_search_nested_dynamic_table.cob`, `docs/CONFORMANCE.md`.

## Landing (wave 20261863 A)

**Re-probe.** The note's repro (fixed-form, `--std 85`, `2002`, `2014`) compiled clean and printed FOUND 8 at all of them on the pre-fix build; the form is legal
at 2014 and 2023, as the owner decided.

**Fix.** Not a COBOL2075 arm: the project gates every 2002 Annex F.2 style relaxation as a `constructs.json` row (PB4305, the 14 `*-2002` rows), so this is the row
`search-identifier-1-enclosing-subscript-2014` (introducedIn 2014, COBOL0900 naming COBOL-2014, VCR row 7.49), asked by `ConstructRegistry.Check` from the
parse arm of `VersionConformancePass` (`VisitSearchStatement` and `VisitSearchAllStatement`, one body) on RECOGNITION of a subscript on identifier-1. A first
version asked it in `StatementValidation.ResolveSearchTable` (only for the enclosing count) and `EditionGateArmDriftTests.GatedConstruct_NamesItsEdition_EvenWhenItsOperandsDoNotResolve`
went red (PB353: with the table name undefined the 2002 compile named no edition), so the gate moved to the parse arm; a subscript at the searched level, which
COBOL2075 refuses at every edition, therefore also names 2014 below it (below 2014 no subscript on identifier-1 is legal). `ResolveSearchTable`/`CheckSearchAllFormat2`
keep the count rule. The COBOL2075 message tail and the
catalog description say the enclosing occurrence is left to the WHEN phrases below 2014. The bare spelling (the CCVS-85 form) stays legal everywhere.

**Sweep.** `grep -rnE "SEARCH(\s+ALL)?\s+[A-Za-z0-9-]+\s*\(" tests conformance-suite` found three suite goldens that wrote the enclosing subscript at `accept: [85]`
(`pb443_search_identifier_1`, `pb877_subscripted_subscript`, `pb1263_occurs_key_is_table_in_outer_table`); their `accept` is now `[2014]` with a comment, their
output unchanged, and the 85/2002 coverage of the bare spelling is the new `pb4577_search_bare_nested_table_85`. Other readers of identifier-1's subscript count:
`SearchBinder` (dynamic tables only, a 2014 construct).

**Goldens.** Positive `conformance:cases/14.9.37-SEARCH/pb4577_search_enclosing_subscript_2014` (2014, 2023; SEARCH and SEARCH ALL) and
`pb4577_search_bare_nested_table_85` (85 on); negatives `pb4577-search-enclosing-subscript-below-2014`, `pb4577-search-all-enclosing-subscript-below-2014` (85, 2002; COBOL0900).

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Validation/VersionConformancePass.cs` (`ParseArm.VisitSearchStatement`, `VisitSearchAllStatement`, `GateSearchIdentifier1`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs` (`ResolveSearchTable`, the message tail only), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs`
(`SearchIdentifier1Operand`), `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (`search-identifier-1-enclosing-subscript-2014`) with `ConstructRegistry.g.cs` and `Constructs.g.cs`,
