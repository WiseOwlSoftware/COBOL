### Adjudicator finding (collating-sequence-clause, harm rejects-legal-source)
- **Mechanism:** M3 — the file-control COLLATING SEQUENCE grammar shape: Format 2 takes `OF cobolWord+`, refusing a qualified data-name-1, and Format 1's FOR choice group is `collatingForPhrase+` with last-wins capture, admitting a repeated alternative
- **Repro:** Two FDs with a key field of the same name, or simply `COLLATING SEQUENCE OF IX-KEY IN IX-REC IS REV.` (probe p14) -> COBOL0312 unexpected 'IN'. And `COLLATING SEQUENCE FOR ALPHANUMERIC IS REV FOR ALPHANUMERIC IS DREV` is accepted, the second silently winning (p15 shape).
- **Expected (spec):** data-name-1 is a user-defined word including qualification where needed (§5.2 metalanguage table; §8.4.2.2 'uniqueness shall be established through qualification for each user-defined name explicitly referenced') — the qualified form must compile; SR6 forbids only subscripting. §5.2.6.4: within braces with choice indicators 'any single alternative shall be specified only once' — a repeated FOR ALPHANUMERIC shall be diagnosed.
- **Observed:** Qualified name is a parse error; the repeated FOR phrase is accepted and CaptureFileCollating overwrites alnum/nat per phrase.
- **Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolIO.g4#fileCollatingSequenceClause (OF cobolWord+ IS? cobolWord); src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.cs#CaptureFileCollating
- **Clause (adjudicator):** ISO §12.4.5.7.2 (Format 1/2); §5.2.6.4; §8.4.2.2

## What the fixer owes
`fileCollatingSequenceClause`'s Format-2 operand becomes the qualified data-reference the other key clauses use (SR6 still forbids subscripting — refuse it by name at bind, as PB481 did for the key clauses); the Format-1 FOR group stays a superset parse and `CaptureFileCollating` refuses a repeated alternative (§5.2.6.4) — the PB917 'printed once, written twice' shape, so route it through that note's mechanism if it has landed.
