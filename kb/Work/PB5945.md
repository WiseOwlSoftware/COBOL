# PB5945 - analysis: an inherited method's identity is method-name in one place and externalized name in another

Found while moving `OoInterfaceSymbol.AllPrototypes` and the §2023:11.6.3 SR5 conforming choice into the symbol table
(listing step 6b, the OO names). Behaviour was kept exactly; this note records what the move made visible.

**The two keys.**

- Every ROSTER is keyed by the method's externalized name (`OoMethodSymbol.RosterKey`, kb/Work PB303, PB4893): an INVOKE names
  its method by literal-1 or identifier-2, resolved "as described in 8.3.2.2, User-defined words" (§2023:14.9.23.4 GR2) against
  "the name of method that is externalized" (§2023:11.7.4 GR1 b)). `OoConformance.ValidateInterfaceInheritance` groups the
  inherited prototypes by that key too.
- The interface's method SURFACE (`SymbolTable.ClassNames.AllPrototypes`) presents one prototype per METHOD-NAME
  (`OoMethodSymbol.Name`), and the SR5 choice (`SymbolTable.ClassNames.ChooseInheritedPrototype`) is keyed by the chosen
  prototype's method-name. §2023:11.6.3 SR5 speaks of "a given method-name ... inherited from more than one interface".

**Where they disagree** (unprobed; a reading of the code):

1. `INTERFACE-ID I1` with `METHOD-ID. A AS "P"` and `INTERFACE-ID I2` with `METHOD-ID. A AS "Q"`, and `I3 INHERITS I1 I2`:
   the surface of I3 holds only I1's `A` (one per method-name), so `INVOKE R3 "Q"` through a reference described with I3 finds no
   method on the roster, while the grouping put the two prototypes in different groups (different keys) and asked SR5 of
   neither.
2. `METHOD-ID. A AS "Z"` in I1 and `METHOD-ID. B AS "Z"` in I2: the grouping puts both under "Z" and records the choice under the
   chosen prototype's method-name; the surface holds both (two method-names), and `INVOKE R3 "Z"` selects whichever the surface
   walk meets first, which need not be the chosen one.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SymbolTable/SymbolTable.ClassNames.cs` (`SymbolTable.ClassNames.AllPrototypes`, `SymbolTable.ClassNames.ChooseInheritedPrototype`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Oo/OoConformance.cs` (`OoConformance.ValidateInterfaceInheritance`)

**To run:** decide the identity of an inherited method for §2023:11.6.3 SR5 and §2023:9.3.10 (method-name, as SR5's text says,
or the externalized name every invocation resolves by), probe both programs at --std 2023, and make the surface, the grouping and
the choice use the one key the ruling picks.
