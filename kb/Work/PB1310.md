**ANNUITY.** For rate r = 1E-20 and n = 12, the §15.9.4 1) b) expression is r / (1 − (1 + r)^−12). Evaluated
exactly it is about 1/12 + 13/24·r, which is 0.08333333 at the receiver's eight fraction places. The program
stores **0**. That is a WRONG ANSWER on legal source, and nothing is raised.

**ACOS / ASIN.** The arccosine of 1 − 1E-30 is about √(2·1E-30) = 1.41421356E-15, so ×1E15 = 1.41421356. The
program answers 0. §15.8.4 asks for "the approximation". Whether an answer with 100 % relative error meets that
depends on the implementor's accuracy statement. The fixer settles that against the accuracy statement the
intrinsic clause (§15) or Annex A requires. Run `cite.py --find` for the clause; this note does not name it, because
it was not checked. The ACOS/ASIN row is **not** claimed here until that determination is written down.

## Mechanism — one root, two faces
`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/Intrinsics/CobolIntrinsics.Float.cs:247` `Annuity(double rate, double periods)`:
`rate / (1 - Math.Pow(1 + rate, -periods))`. `1 + 1E-20` is exactly `1.0` in binary64, so `Math.Pow(1, -12)` is
1 and the denominator is 0. The result is ∞ or NaN, and the store turns it into 0.
`CobolIntrinsics.Float.cs:131` `Acos(double x) => Math.Acos(x)`: the 30-place argument has already been narrowed
to the binary64 value 1.0 at intake.

Both are the shape PB1000 fixed for PRESENT-VALUE's discount base, which is now formed on the rate's EXACT carrier
(`PresentValueBase(CobolDec?)`). ANNUITY's base is not. The fix needs one of three things: the exact base, or a
`log1p`/`expm1`-grade evaluation, `-n·log1p(r)`, or for ACOS/ASIN near ±1 the `2·asin(sqrt((1−x)/2))` identity,
evaluated on the exact 1 − x.

## What the fixer owes
- ANNUITY's float lane evaluates 1 + rate without cancellation: one helper, shared with PB1000's `PresentValueBase`
  (one rule in one place).
- ACOS/ASIN near ±1 are evaluated on the exact complement 1 ∓ x, and the accuracy determination is written down.
- **Sibling sweep:** every float-lane body that forms `1 ± small` or `x − nearly-x` from a narrowed argument. LOG
  near 1, LOG10 near 1, SQRT's intake and the Dec twins (`AnnuityDec`) are candidates. Check ANNUITY's Dec lane with
  the same TINY; this note only measured the float lane.
- Goldens: an ANNUITY value at a rate below binary64's epsilon, and ACOS/ASIN at 1 − 1E-30, pinned at the
  receiver's precision.

## Landed

Folded into **PB1041** (same root: a binary64 step loses what the exact argument carried). Both faces fixed, the
repro above re-run on the implementer's own build: `ANN= 000.08333333`, `ACOSX1E15= 001.41421356` (and
`ASIN(NEAR1)` = 1.57079632).

- **ANNUITY** — `CobolIntrinsics.Annuity` evaluates the §15.9.4 1) b) denominator as −expm1(−n·log1p(rate))
  (`ExpMinusOne` / `LogOnePlus`, Kahan's formulations), never forming 1 + rate in binary64. This is a body fix, not
  an intake fix: a COMP-2 rate of 1E-20 cancelled identically. The standard modes keep `AnnuityDec`, which evaluates
  the EAE on the SDIDI as §15.4.1 r1 requires (1 + 1E-20 is exact in 34 digits there).
- **ACOS / ASIN** — PB1041's exact-intake arm: 2·asin(√((1 − |x|)/2)) over the EXACT 1 − |x|. The accuracy
  determination the "What the fixer owes" list asked for is written down in `COBOLNET_INTRINSICS_DESIGN.md` "An
  exact-intake float body…": the returned value is binary64's approximation of the function OF THE EXACT ARGUMENT,
  within a few ulps; its rows (RV-15.8.4-1, RV-15.10.4-1) are witnessed under PB1041.
- Sibling sweep (this note's list): LOG / LOG10 near 1 fixed under PB1041; SQRT's intake is well conditioned (½);
  PRESENT-VALUE's base was PB1000's.

Witnesses: `WholeRangeBodiesDriftTests.Annuity_KeepsATinyRate` (5 rows, 80-digit oracle, 2 ulps) and the `ANN-T`
line of `tests/conformance/85/pb1041_trig_log_arc_exact_argument` (ANNUITY(1E-17 12) = 0.08333333333333). Batch
