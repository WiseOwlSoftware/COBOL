# PB2708 — the branch survey cannot see that a landed train carried a branch

**Found by:** the land unit of 2026-10-08 13:05 PDT, right after train 1042b landed (`9fa72ad44`, ci-gate run
37834960381, main run 37836265845).

## What happened

Train 1042b carried twelve clusters, one commit each, and its lander changed several of them on the way in: conflict
resolutions (N, T, the registrar brief for U and Q), fixes in the train (H's `CobolNum.Magnitude` collapsed into
`CobolDec.UAbs`, P's `blocked: true` flipped to false, H's CONFORMANCE.md token), and G's semantic merge with H
(`FormatFloatCore` with `CobolRounding.Truncation`). After the push, `python scripts/prune_worktrees.py --brief`
printed:

- **WAITING TO LAND** `note-pb2169-reland` (T), `note-pb2688` (N, which also carried `note-trend1040`),
  `w1041p-pb1636-signed` (P): their reports say `Status: DONE` and their lead notes (PB2169, PB2688, PB1636) are still
  open or half, so `lifecycle()` calls them waiting. The next land unit takes every WAITING TO LAND row into its train,
  so it would have dispatched a lander to re-carry three branches whose work is already on main.
- **DECISION ("superseded")** `train1041b-dropped-a-pb2230` (U), `train1041b-dropped-g-pb2193` (V),
  `w1041q-devlog-scope` (Q), `worktree-wf_eb1fc3a8-908-7` (G), `worktree-wf_eb1fc3a8-908-8` (H), and from train 1042
  `worktree-wf_eb1fc3a8-908-1` (A) and `train1042-gated`. Each needs a person to prove that nothing unique remains.

The land unit proved it by a line census (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\t1042b\verify_superseded.py`: the lines each
branch's own commits add, `merge-base..branch`, that main's file lacks). Every surviving line is one of the lander's
edits listed above, or litter (`pb1466-packed.tmp` on 908-7, which the first 1042b lander excluded). The decisions
are recorded below and in each lead note.

## Mechanism

1. `scripts/prune_worktrees.py` `lifecycle()` decides from `row.content` (MERGED, LANDED: `merges_to_main_unchanged`,
   a three-way merge that leaves main's tree as it is) and `row.report` (status, `lead_terminal`). A lander that edits a
   cluster in the train makes the branch's merge change main, so the content reads CHECK. Then an open lead note makes
   the row WAITING TO LAND, and a terminal one makes it a superseded DECISION. The survey never reads the one record
   that says "this branch was carried": the train manifest (`<coord>\scratch\train<label>-manifest.json`, one entry
   per cluster with `branch`, `head` and `base`) of a train whose commits are on main.
2. `classify()` gathers `declared` names from EVERY commit in `origin/main..branch` (`git show -U0 <c>` per commit),
   so a name one WIP commit adds and a later commit on the same branch deletes still counts as "not on main".
   `worktree-wf_eb1fc3a8-908-1` read `CHECK (names not on main: RunsOfEqual)`: `RunsOfEqual` was added in `fb4c17001`
   and deleted in `5fa4b0094`, and occurs nowhere at the branch head.

## Fix direction

- Read the train manifests. A branch whose HEAD is the `head` a manifest recorded for one of its clusters, in a train
  whose landing reached main (the train's last commit, or its DEVLOG entry, is on main), is LANDED with the evidence
  "carried by train <label> (<sha>)". A branch that moved past its recorded head stays a DECISION (its new commits
  are not in the train). The lander already writes the manifest before step 6 (landing_check.py reads it), so no new
  record is needed.
- Gather `declared` from the branch's NET diff (`origin/main...branch`, three dots), not from each commit.
- Self-test plants: a train member carried with an in-train edit and its lead note open → LANDED, not WAITING TO LAND;
  the same member with one commit after its recorded head → DECISION; a name added and deleted on one branch → not
  reported as lost.
- **A branch no manifest names (operator 2026-10-10 18:10 PDT, after train 1052).** After train 1052 (`316a9d731`)
  the resume unit of 17:16 (477 s, 47 calls) hand-wrote ABANDONED lines for 19 branches (`branch-records-r1716`,
  report `w1053y-PB2614-report.md`). Fourteen were superseded by train 1052 itself (its members and its phase worktree), the case the manifest rule above
  covers. Five were
  wave-1051/1052 SPLIT predecessors whose every commit a wave-1053 finisher had cherry-picked
  (`worktree-wf_4151e8da-91b-1` → w1053g, `-91b-3`, `-91b-4`, `-91b-5`, `worktree-wf_0cae47bc-172-1`). No train
  manifest names them. Their superseded row needs a second signal, already measured by `classify`:
  every commit patch-equivalent to main by `git cherry`, no lost names (`row.lost`) and no absent added files
  (`row.absent`). Such a row is LANDED with that evidence. A superseded row with a non-empty census stays DECISION, as
  `worktree-wf_0cae47bc-172-2` (`CHECK (names not on main: HolderState StateOf)`) must. This lead was first filed as
  PB2943 and withdrawn as a duplicate of this note before it landed.

Two causes this note now also carries:
- **Cleanup is the LAST step of a unit**, and every land unit that day stopped at the 200 K soft cap first (PB2852), so
  the step never ran.
- **Remote branches are never deleted.** A squash landing leaves the pushed branch on origin (`note-budget-borrow`, and
  `r1-pb2118` since 2026-10-07: its content verified against its own rebased landing 8be230068, only the DEVLOG entry
  number and one trend sha differing); nothing in the lifecycle looks at `origin`.

Fix extension: make the cleanup a deterministic script the supervisor runs at each unit START (no model turns), covering
local branches, worktrees (refusing uncommitted paths, PB2851) and the matching `origin/<branch>`; it recognizes a
squash-carried branch from the train's `brought in (<branch> <sha>)` checkpoint records or a content check, not only
from ancestry.

## 2026-10-10 22:40 PDT: the survey half landed (operator; owner: "Fix PB2708 but land it after everything currently in process")

`scripts/prune_worktrees.py`, measured on the live branches before the change: `branch-records-r1716` read WAITING TO
LAND (the next land unit would have re-carried it) although train 1053 carried it as cluster 53Y (`69041f6c6`), and
train 1053's `worktree-wf_8d59ef7b-0cb-1` (53A) and `-0cb-7` (53G) read DECISION; at 21:30 train 1056's five members
read WAITING TO LAND while their lander carried them, and `--train` picked four of them again.

- **Carried by a landed train (`train_heads`, `carried`).** The record is the train MANIFEST plus main's commits: a
  member whose `Train-Cluster: <cluster>` trailer commit lies in its train's range (its `base` to the train's newest
  `Train <label>:` commit on origin/main), and whose branch head is the `head` the manifest recorded, is LANDED with
  the evidence "carried by train <label> as cluster <c> (<sha>)". Its diff is in main's history, so deleting the branch
  loses nothing even where a later commit withdrew the fix. The commit BODY is not the record: train 1053's lander
  rewrote 53A's and 53G's bodies and kept only the trailers, and dropped 53P's trailer (so `w1053p-PB2885` stays a hand
  DECISION). A branch that moved past the recorded head is not carried. Trains before train_apply.py (no trailers) are
  not read this way, which keeps the withdrawn 52B fix's branch (`worktree-wf_0cae47bc-172-2`) a DECISION.
- **Cherry-picked (`git cherry`).** Every commit patch-equivalent to one on main and no declared name main lacks:
  LANDED with that evidence (the five SPLIT predecessors of 2026-10-10). A non-empty census stays CHECK.
- **Net diff.** `declared` comes from `origin/main...branch`, so a name one WIP commit added and a later commit
  deleted is not lost.
- **The train landing now (`landing_train_members`).** The members of the manifest whose label the landing-lease
  holder names (`train <label> lander`) are IN FLIGHT, never WAITING TO LAND, so `--train` cannot pick them again.
- **Remote branches (`drop_remote`).** `--apply` deletes `origin/<branch>` with each removed branch unless the remote
  holds commits the local branch did not.
- **Self-test plants:** `carried` (LANDED, evidence named), `carried-moved` (CHECK), `unlanded-train` (a manifest of a
  train that never landed: DECISION), `add-del` (no lost name), `picked` (LANDED), `picked-lost` (CHECK), a train
  holding the lease (its member IN FLIGHT; `train 99990` does not bind `train 9999`; an expired lease binds nothing),
  and the remote cases (deleted; diverged kept). Each fix was proven by mutation: without `carried()` 7 checks RED;
  with the head ignored 5 RED; with per-commit names 1 RED; with `in_train` ignored 1 RED; without `drop_remote` 2 RED.
- On the live branches: `branch-records-r1716`, `-0cb-1` and `-0cb-7` read LANDED with their train-1053 evidence (the
  operator removed the three by hand at 22:05, their worktrees clean, none pushed).
