# PB4748 — the refuter on Sonnet: a measured replay

**Why.** `/usage` (2026-11-05) put `cobol-refuter` at about 44 % of a day's usage. The measurement
(E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/refuter-model/measure.md) found 113 refuter agents in 7 days, USD 546, all Opus at `xhigh`; 58 of
them (about USD 315) were review and check work in the Mythos lane's own workflows, not claim refutation (that lane now
uses `cobol-reviewer` or the default agent for those). PB4747 moved every role to effort `medium`. Whether the refuter can
also move to Sonnet was never measured.

**The experiment.** Replay the report's §3f list of 39 claims with known ground truth (12 refuter misses and 4 catches
among them) in slices of about 5, as the real runs did, once on Sonnet and once on Opus, both at `medium` (about 16
agents, an estimated USD 40-60). Score accuracy against the ground truth and cost per correct verdict (the PB3868
standard: a cheaper model is not cost-effective if it fails). **Rule:** if Sonnet matches Opus on the 16
quality-sensitive claims, `cobol-refuter` moves to Sonnet (its frontmatter, MANDATORY-PRACTICES P1 and O4); otherwise it
stays on Opus at `medium`, and the result is recorded here.

**When.** On account 1 after its weekly reset, Sunday 2026-11-08 02:00 PST (the owner, 2026-11-05: "wait for that reset
for this specific work").
