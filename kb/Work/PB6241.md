The copies already differ: ReadingParses stops BEFORE the DOT and drops it while the other four keep it; ProcedureReferencesToTheWord finds the sentence start by scanning back over every channel (Get(start-1).Type != DOT, so a DOT on a hidden or ABSENT_DEBUG_LINE channel ends the backward scan but is skipped by the forward one); the four forward scans end at any DOT token while EmbeddedBlockReading.Scan ends a sentence only at SeparatorRule.IsSeparatorPeriod (a DOT not followed by a space, T.A, is no separator). A fix to what ends a sentence must be found and made at every copy, and a missed copy makes the speculative re-reads of the reserved-word diagnoses judge a different span than the one the error strategy reports through (Through).
