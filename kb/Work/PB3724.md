# PB3724 — a notes-only gate waits for a full gate slot

**Sites:** `scripts/run_gate_legs.py` (the order of steps: audits, lock, slot, then `lane` classifies the change), `scripts/gate_slot.py` (`GateSlots`, the classes of ticket), `scripts/inert_lane.py` (`CLASSIFICATION`), `scripts/build-local.ps1`. As landed: `scripts/gate_slot.py` (new `Pool`, `GATE_POOL`, `LIGHT_POOL`, `DEFAULT_LIGHT_SLOTS`, `CAP_KEYS`; `GateSettings.effective_cap`/`KEYS`; `GateSlots.__init__`/`for_repo`/`acquire`/`_tickets_ahead`/`state`; `Slot.describe`; `main`, `_cmd_status`, `_print_pool`, `_cmd_run`), `scripts/run_gate_legs.py` (`Host.take_slot`, `Gate._inert`), `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/GateSlotDriftTests.cs` (`Arms`).

## What happens

On 2026-10-25 the PB3723 notes branch (two changed paths, `kb/Work/PB3723.md` and one DEVLOG pending file, both inert) ran `build-local.ps1 -Priority BelowNormal` at about 11:15 while the loop's wave 20261107 and the Mythos lane's wave 20261500 had implementers gating. After 10 minutes its log still read `gate-slot: waiting, 0 ahead (ticket 3138, cap 3, implementer …)`, then `1 ahead`, `2 ahead`, `0 ahead` again: later tickets passed it. The inert lane runs only the script self-tests on both hosts (PB3646), about a minute, but it holds a slot the cap exists to ration for full builds and test legs. Every owner-decision note, clerk filing and DEVLOG-only landing pays the wait, and the more implementers both lanes run, the longer it gets.

## Fix direction

Classify the change BEFORE queueing (the classification reads only the committed tree, like the audits PB2524 already moved before the slot) and give an inert change its own light path: no full-gate slot, or a separate small cap for light gates. Explain in the gate's log why a later ticket passed this one (a priority class or a defect) and pin the order with a self-test. Measure: inert-gate wall time before and after, with implementers gating.

## Landed (2026-10-25, fix-lane wave 20261703 group A, with PB3707 and PB3717)

classifies the change before `take_slot`, since PB2524/PB3646), but the inert lane still took a slot of the one pool for its
