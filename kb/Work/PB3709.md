# PB3709 — the drift rule's published text is shorter than its test

**Sites:** `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/LandingLaneDriftTests.cs` (the class `<summary>`, and the test `TheSupervisor_StartsTheLane_InTheBackground_BesideEveryUnit`).

## What happens

`drift_rules.py` derives the rule it prints for `orchestrate.ps1`, `land_lane.py`, `DESIGN-orchestrator-loop.md` and
MANDATORY-PRACTICES.md from the class summary (P11). The summary says the supervisor starts `land_lane.py tick` as a background process
"as each unit starts and on a period while it runs". Since PB3639 the test also requires `Step-Lane` in `Wait-Until` (after its STOP check,
so a budget hold and a failure backoff tick) and at the loop top between units. An implementer who runs `drift_rules.py` before editing
`Wait-Until`, reads only the unit-bound rule, and removes or reorders the `Step-Lane` call first learns the requirement from a red gate.

## Fix

Extend the summary: the lane ticks for as long as the supervisor lives, from the one timer `Step-Lane`, in `Invoke-Unit`, in `Wait-Until`
(after its STOP check) and between units (kb/Work PB3639). Small enough to land with PB3708's change if both are picked up in one group
(same lane-test area).
