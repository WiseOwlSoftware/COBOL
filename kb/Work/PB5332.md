# PB5332 - the 2002 ledger is held until the 2002 rule catalog's six findings are resolved

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/extract_rule_catalog.py` (`KINDS`); `specs/ISO_COBOL_2002.md` (whole: the two transcription
repairs are lines 17205 and 23395).

## What happened

wrote `LEDGER-2002.json` and `2002/REPORT.md` (123 claims, 83 confirmed, 17 normative; `--check` CURRENT). Gating that
branch turned the RULE CATALOGS audit RED for 2002 only. `verify_publishable.reconciled(2002)` is true once the ledger
records all 882 pages (kb/Work PB5256 review R1-4), and from then on the ISO catalog fails on the findings it records
for an unreconciled edition. The audit's 2002 findings, verbatim, are kept at
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/held-ledger-2002/catalog-2002-findings.txt` with the held ledger files beside them:

- 4 unrecognised-heading: §2002:8.8.1.3.1.3 'Rounding rules' (PDF page 144), §2002:8.8.4.1.4.2, 8.8.4.1.5.2 and
  8.8.4.1.6.2 'Rules' (pages 155-156): their rules are absent from the denominator. Type each in `KINDS` or adjudicate it.
- 1 flattened-tail: §2002:14.8.1.3 (page 436), item 2 transcribed at column 0: indent it to its printed level.
- 1 unexplained-ordinal: §2002:15.16.2 (page 607), '99. The value 00 ...' is the CURRENT-DATE table's wrapped cell
  (the reconciliation refuted p607-1 as a formatting difference); the transcription repair keeps it in its cell.
- the committed 2002 catalog then reads STALE: regenerate it (`extract_rule_catalog.py --edition 2002`, then
  `inventory_view.py --check`) in the same change.

## Fix

with them (re-written by `merge_reconciliation.py --edition 2002 --write` at that branch's head, never copied). The
2014 ledger landed separately (it has no rule catalog yet).
