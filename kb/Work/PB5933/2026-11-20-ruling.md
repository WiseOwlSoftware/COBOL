Reasoning: The No1985Rule justification is literally true (no 1985 rule SAYS "not on a sequential file"), but the refusal does have 1985 ground, the same kind of ground the codebase already cites at 85 for a phrase a module's general format does not print: 1985 is organized by functional modules (§1985:I.1.2), each module's statements serve the files of its organization, and the sequential module's Procedure Division statements are CLOSE, OPEN, READ, REWRITE, USE and WRITE (§1985:VII.1.2).
