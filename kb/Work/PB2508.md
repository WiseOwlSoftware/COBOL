# PB2508

Filed by the w1033 clerk from the PB1643 refuter (`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1033-adj\PB1643-refute.md` section 3.4, "One supporting claim is slightly overstated"; probe `negzero.cob`).
The refuter calls it "a separate, minor latitude point that could become its own note"; it does not affect the PB1643 determination.

**Observed (pinned build `pin-97006f3`).** `DISPLAY -0` prints `-0` and `DISPLAY -0.0` prints `-0.0`; GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 (default and `cobol2014`) prints `+0` and `+0.0`. This is why the PB1643 decision's claim
that WiseOwl's literal form "matches GnuCOBOL's sign ... forms" is not exact.

**Status of the question.** `§14.9.11.4 GR1` leaves the conversion of a literal to its device image to the implementor, so the precedence order applies (ISO, then GnuCOBOL, then IBM/Micro Focus). WiseOwl's own
convention is "a literal renders as its own text" (DOC-A.1-92; golden `2002/pb1230_constant_literal_as_written`: `DISPLAY +5` prints `+5`). Owed: decide whether negative zero follows the as-written convention
(current) or the GnuCOBOL form, and record the determination in `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` next to DOC-A.1-56 / DOC-A.1-92. Related: `PB1643`.

## Re-filed as a defect (analyses sweep, 2026-10-23)

Adjudicated read-only by an Opus cobol-adjudicator on main e7cf38ce4; accepted by the Mythos session.

A numeric literal whose value is zero but which is written with a minus sign displays the minus: DISPLAY -0 prints -0, DISPLAY -0.0 prints -0.0 (likewise -00, -0.00, -.0, and a constant-name declared AS -0), where GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 in its default and cobol2014 configurations prints the literal as written except that the sign of a zero value becomes + (+0, +0.0, +00, +0.00, +.0). §14.9.11.4 GR1 makes the device conversion implementor-defined; the rule-1 precedence (ISO silent, then GnuCOBOL) and DOC-A.1-56's own statement that the literal form is settled GnuCOBOL's way decide it, so no owner question is needed. Fix: BoundNumericLiteral.Image (src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Bound/BoundTree.cs:519, the one literal image) writes '+' for a '-' sign when every digit is zero; the value text (BoundNumericLiteral.Text) is unchanged. Golden pinning each spelling above, including the constant-name and a DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA leg (-0,0; not measured in this sweep, measure it against cobc when writing the golden), and one sentence in DOC-A.1-56. Flag set wrong_answer to match the register's classification of rule-1 latitude deviations (PB1540, PB1664: bytes wrong against the latitude determination, not an ISO violation).

Evidence: Re-measured 2026-10-23 (probes/pb2508/negzero.cob): e7cf38ce4 prints -0, -0.0, -00, -0.00, -.0 and, through a constant-name AS -0, -0; GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 (-std=default and cobol2014) prints +0, +0.0, +00, +0.00, +.0 and +0. Every other literal agrees (+0, 0, +5, -5, 1.50, 007). §14.9.11.4 GR1 (checked) leaves the conversion to the implementor, and DOC-A.1-56 says the literal latitude is 'settled GnuCOBOL's way (precedence tiebreaker)', so the sign of a negative zero is a measured departure from the documented choice, not a new question.

Rule: §14.9.11.4 GR1 (checked)

## Landed

Re-probed on the worktree build: `DISPLAY -0`, `-0.0`, `-00`, `-0.00`, `-.0` and a constant-name AS -0 printed `-0 -0.0 -00 -0.00 -.0 -0`; under DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA the same (cobc 3.2.0, default and cobol2014, prints `+0,0 +0 +,0 -1,50 +0,0` for the probe). `BoundNumericLiteral.Image` (BoundTree.cs) now shows `+` for a written `-` when every digit is zero; `Text`, the value, is unchanged, and the separator mapping composes. Every spelling now matches cobc. A floating-point literal cannot be a negative zero (section 8.3.3.3.3 SR4, COBOLNET1661 refuses it), so no sibling arm exists. A folded intrinsic function's value (`FUNCTION NUMVAL("-0")`) never carried the sign (prints `0`), and `MOVE -0 TO PIC X(4)` keeps the de-signed `0`; both match cobc. Goldens `pb2508_display_negative_zero_literal` and `pb2508_display_negative_zero_literal_comma`; unit theory `NumericLiteralImageDriftTests.TheLiteralImage_OfANegativeZero_ShowsAPlus_AndNothingElseChanges`; DOC-A.1-56 carries the sentence.
