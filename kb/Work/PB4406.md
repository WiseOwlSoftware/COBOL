# PB4406 - an operand on a continuation line is positioned for some screens and not for their siblings

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ExpressionBinder.cs:519` (`ExpressionBinder.RefExpr`, the ONE dataReference-to-BoundExpr mapping: its `IndexNameExpr` arm is the arithmetic sibling of the R16 index-name screen, and its LINAGE-COUNTER and keyword-omitted-function arms), `ExpressionBinder.ScreenResultant` and the verb binders' post-resolution operand screens (the arithmetic-resultant category screen COBOL1675).

PB4383 landed the per-operand position at `ReferenceResolver.ResolveImpl` (`DiagnosticCursorAt.At(dref)`) and at the four
`ScreenIndexNameOperand` callers. Diagnostics raised inside `ResolveImpl` land on the operand's own line; diagnostics
raised AFTER the resolution by `RefExpr` and by the post-resolution operand screens still land at the statement's first
token (the sink's cursor was never moved). One continued statement therefore puts COBOL1639 on the operand's line but
COBOL1637 (index-name in an arithmetic expression) or COBOL1675 (resultant category) for the same kind of operand on the
statement's line. The names are in the messages, so the diagnosis is misled by position, not lost.

**Fix shape.** Position at the mapping, not per caller: `RefExpr` and the resultant screen open `At(dref)` around their
screens, then sweep every `Screen*` call that runs after `ResolveImpl` under a statement-level cursor (the PB4383 note's
sibling sweep, `FieldOperand(` callers). A drift test enumerates the post-resolution screens and pins each to an operand
on a continuation line (extend `DiagnosticPositionTests.OperandOnAContinuationLine_NamesItsOwnLineAndColumn`).

Grepped `kb/Work` for `RefExpr`, `COBOL1675`, `continuation line` and `ScreenResultant`: only landed notes (PB128, PB215, PB4383); none covers the post-resolution positioning.

**Sites (landed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/ExpressionBinder.cs` (`ExpressionBinder.FieldOperand`, `RefExpr`, `ResolveReceiving(DataReferenceContext)`, `RefuseNonReceivable`, `ScreenResultant`, `NonNumericLiteralOperand`, `LiteralOperand`, `NumLiteral`, `NonNumericInNumericContext`, `NotNumericIdentifier`, `BindPrimary`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ArithmeticBinder.cs` (the DIVIDE REMAINDER caller of `ScreenResultant`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Conformance/DiagnosticPositionTests.cs` (`PostResolutionScreen_NamesTheOperandsOwnLineAndColumn`), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/PostResolutionOperandPositionDriftTests.cs`, `docs/VALIDATION_DESIGN.md` (Positions).

## Landed (wave 20261816 group B)

**Re-probe (2026-11-01, main b75f316a6).** On `COMPUTE N = 1` with the operand on the next line, `+ IX` (COBOL1637), `+ W`, `+ G` and `+ IDX` (COBOL0844), a keyword-omitted function (COBOL1543) and `+ LINAGE-COUNTER` (COBOL0864) were reported at the statement's `(12,12)`. So were the receivers `ADD 1 TO N` / `W` (COBOL1675, also MULTIPLY BY and DIVIDE REMAINDER), `LINE-COUNTER` (COBOL2197) and `LINAGE-COUNTER` (COBOL2026). A non-numeric literal (`ADD "A" TO N`, COBOL0844) and an out-of-range floating literal (COBOL1661) were reported at the statement's column even on the statement's own line.

**Root.** PB4383 positioned inside `ReferenceResolver.ResolveImpl`; every report made by the member that maps the resolved reference, or that screens a literal, runs after that scope closed. Those members now open `At(...)` on the operand themselves, so the position is decided at the mapping, not in each verb binder. `ScreenResultant` takes the receiving identifier's parse node (it took its text) and positions itself; its four callers pass the node.

**After.** The same probes report at the operand: `(13,18)` for the COMPUTE operands, `(13,16)` for the receivers, `(12,16)` for the literal. `PostResolutionOperandPositionDriftTests` lists the members that must open the scope.

**Sibling mechanisms found and left (same root, other files):** statement-shape diagnostics that name one operand and are raised in the verb binders (SET sender COBOL2326/COBOL0869, ADD/SUBTRACT CORRESPONDING COBOL1757, the `BoundRejected.Report` sites of SetBinder, InspectBinder, KeyedIoBinder, SortBinder and ReportWriterBinder); the subscript and reference-modification bound screens of `ReferenceResolver.ScreenPositionOperandClass` and `IndexNameInPositionError`, which stand at the outer reference; `ExpressionBinder.CheckLiteral(string)` called with text and no node from CallBinder, ConditionBinder, IntrinsicBinder and OoBinder.
