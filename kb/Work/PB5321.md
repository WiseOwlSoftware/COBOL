## What the re-read must decide

1. **D-SKE at 1985 and 2002.** The 1985 SR4c refuses the occurs-depending group key outright, and 1985 SR4e (and 2002 14.8.23.2 SR4f) refuse every key under an OCCURS entry, which is wider than the 2023 text the arm is written from. Decide: (a) the determination's basis becomes `§1985:XI.4.4.3 SR4c` / `§1985:XI.4.1.3 SR4c` and `§2002:14.8.23.2 SR4d` / `§2002:14.8.36.2 6) d)`, not GnuCOBOL and Micro Focus, and the "rejected reading" and "open to the standard itself" sentences go; (b) whether the SR4e / SR4f OCCURS arm (a key under a fixed OCCURS entry) is refused at 85 and 2002 by the same arm, by a different one, or accepted: read what the 2023 rule says on the same point (`§2023:14.9.40.3` and `§2023:14.9.24.3`, the cited SR6/SR4 items) and probe `--std 85` on a key subordinate to a fixed OCCURS entry; if the compiler accepts it, that is a defect to file, not a determination to word. Whichever way, the goldens `14.9.24-MERGE/pb1173-sort-file-key-odo-group` and `pb1173-merge-key-odo-group` keep (or gain) `refuse-at` 85 with the citation of the edition's own clause.
2. **Status 07.** Decide whether the entry's "no prior answer a gate could serve" survives. The three older texts state the same two setters as 2023, so either E.2 item 16 describes older implementations (not the standard's text) and the row stays `ref-only` with the reason restated from the texts ("the editions' own text names only OPEN and CLOSE; item 16 refers to what the 1985 text left unsaid for the other statements"), or a text-level difference between 2014 and 2023 exists in an adjacent clause (the Table 14 cells, or the I-O-warning exception condition `EC-I-O-WARNING`, `§2023:9.1.13.1`, which `pb1515_io_warning_07_raises_ec` already gates at 2023) and then a gate belongs in `DialectBehaviors` and row 22 becomes a `behavior:` row, as the entry itself says. The re-read compares `§2014:9.1.12.1 5)` against `§2023:9.1.13.2 6)`, and the Table 14 cell for CLOSE REEL/UNIT and WITH NO REWIND in both, and reads whether the 1985 relative and indexed lists name 07.
3. **Wording that goes with either outcome.** CONFORMANCE.md line 869 and line 1237 and VERSION_CHANGE_REFERENCE row 22 say the repository holds no 1985, 2002 or 2014 text; each states the edition texts it read. (Rule 6: the same change set.) The GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 probe sentence stays only as corroboration, below the standard, as rule 1's precedence orders.
