# PB1666 — OPEN

Filed 2026-09-27 by the train-68 lander. Two reports found it: wave-68 V (`w68v-PB1130-report.md`, new defect 3)
and its same-file successor V2 (`w68v2-PB1272-report.md`, new defect 1), which asked for ONE note.

**Repro.** `scratchpad/w68v/l/sum20.cob`: a `PIC 9(20)` SUM of two 6e18 addends. The counter reports a size error
and the item prints spaces; the right answer is 12000000000000000000.

**Rule.** §13.18.54.4 1) (`cite.py --check 13.18.54.4` OK §13.18.54.4 1)): "The number of decimal digits in the sum
counter, both integral and fractional, is derived from the corresponding number of digits, excluding insertion
editing characters, in the PICTURE clause of the entry containing the SUM clause." A 20-digit PICTURE makes a
20-digit counter; the counter's capacity may not be bounded by the host carrier.

**Code sites.** `PicInfo.SumCounterItem` clamp (`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Model/PicInfo.cs` ~700; its caller
`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs` ~2510) and
`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/ReportWriter.cs#SumEntry.Value` / `SumValue` / `SetSumValue` / `AddSumTerm` (all `long`).
V (298d01e48) bounded the capacity by the long carrier so no false size error fires below 19 digits; the carrier
itself is the remaining defect.

**What the fixer owes.** A counter carrier that holds the full §13.18.54.4 1) digit count (the numeric model the
compiler already uses for wide DISPLAY items), the size-error test against the PICTURE's digits (V2's
`DataBinder.SumCounterDigits`), and a golden with a 20-digit SUM.
