# PB5455 - the open 2023 rows of unnumbered text (PB479 step 5)

**Sites:** none (verdict batches through `record_verdicts.py`; a defect found becomes its own note with its own sites)

**What.** After PB5451, run the Phase-B lanes (design sections 5, 8 and 9) over every new 2023 row, all of them GAP,
clustered by clause family. Each row closes only by its own verdict, code location and spec-derived `test-ref`, or,
where it obliges the implementation to nothing observable, by a derivation of section 1.1 of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md` with its own argument, signature and UPHELD refuter record
(kb/Work PB5373); a refuter attacks every closing verdict.

- **The carved rows** (up to 121) are judged against the code AND against their parent's test: the reader says whether
  that test witnesses THIS paragraph (it is the row's `test-ref` only then), and a carved row of `RV-15.4.1-4` never
  takes its parent's derivation. Known not witnessed at design time: `GR-14.7.5-L3.5`'s NOT SIZE ERROR paragraph,
  `GR-8.8.4.2.1-13`'s alphabetic and national-group comparison sentences, and `GR-14.6.3-3`'s explicit-transfer
  sentence.
- **Order**, by what the tree already shows (none of it closes a row): the 23 paragraphs a CONFORMS record with a test
  quotes at 90 % or more, then the 19 quoted by a record of their own clause (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/pb479/review/fp_quality.py`),
  then the rows whose clause `src/` and a test tree already cite (143 of the 223 2023 hit clauses at design time), the
  quoting records and citing sites seeding each candidate's code location; then the table rows (Table 12, Table 13);
  then the rest. A DIVERGES becomes a kb/Work defect before it becomes a paragraph anywhere.

**Oracle.** The standard's text; the built compiler; spec-derived expected values.

**Done when** the new 2023 rows are at zero GAP, or each remaining one names the open note that holds it.
