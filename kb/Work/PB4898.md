# PB4898 - a wrapped box is not a four-rule rectangle

**What the reports say.** The PB4816 finisher's review lists as a standing lead: "boxes that wrap a line are not found,
`figure_raster.find_boxes`." PB4848 (landed) states: "A box that wraps a line (a polygon of two joined rectangles, the first
paragraph of pages 389 and 684) is not found: only four-rule rectangles are."

**Why a note.** PB4848 holds it as prose only; no open note carries it.

**Re-probe (wave 20261893).** On the deskewed page, page 389's first paragraph holds four boxes and page 684's three, one per
printed line, and where the boxes of two lines touch the printer drew ONE outline, open where they meet (389: `and the` over
`and the MULTIPLE`, `location ... reel.` over `FILE TAPE clause`; 684: `a message segment, ... a` over `or more`). The old
`find_boxes` measured the paragraph of 389 as no box and six choice bars (the polygons' sides), and 684's as two boxes
(`or more` lost) and two bars.

**Fixed.** `find_boxes` finds a component's boxes from the FACES its rules enclose (`_faces`: the paper its ink cuts off,
4-connected, so paper does not cross a rule at a diagonal step). A face is cut into one rectangle per printed line: a run of
rows whose left and right ends stay within `BOX_TILT` (smoothed over half a line, so a glyph fused to a side is no jump; a
run shorter than half a line joins its neighbour). Each rectangle is judged by the old four-rule test (`_box`, factored out
unchanged), with a SEAM, a rule drawn across the columns it shares with the face's next rectangle, and the ink past the seam
hidden; the erasure skips the seam, so a descender there is kept. A component with no face of a box's size (a rule the scan
broke) is judged whole, as before. Witness: the new prose crops `figure_raster_pages/p0389-prose.png` (4 boxes) and
`p0684-prose.png` (3), counted by eye, and the planted case 8h; on the old measurer all three fail (`test_figure_raster.py`
53/57), on the new one 57/57.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/figure_raster.py` (`find_boxes`, `_faces`, `_box`, the module docstring's steps and calibration),
`scripts/spec/test_figure_raster.py` (`main`: case 8h, section 9's comment), `scripts/spec/figure_raster_pages/`
(`calibration.json`, `p0389-prose.png`, `p0684-prose.png`), `kb/Work/PB4848.md` (Not right yet (2)).
