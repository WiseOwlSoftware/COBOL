# PB181

Found by the Step D design scout (2026-08-30). CallEmitter.CallStringRead routes a class-window place through
OperandText.FieldImage whose NonTextBytes lane re-renders the byte window as display digits — the §14.2.3 GR8
storage-area aliasing then carries the WRONG bytes at the WRONG width across the boundary. The read twin of the
family PB177 catalogs (its B is the write half). FIX: the boundary read of a RedefViewPlace passes the window
verbatim (the storage-area rule — PlaceRenderer.Read IS the window); sweep the BY CONTENT/copy-out/RETURNING
call sites; golden: CALL BY REFERENCE with a REDEFINES-class argument round trip. Related: [[PB177]].
