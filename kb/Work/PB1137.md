### SR-14.9.23.3-21 (PARTIAL)
|| REFUTED: PARTIAL — SUPERSEDES the earlier SR21 line and adds evidence. The rule covers identifier-5 (any identifier) and expression identifiers under BOTH the BY CONTENT and BY VALUE branches of §14.9.23.2. (1) The BY VALUE half is unexercisable: every BY VALUE argument is refused, and a BY VALUE method formal is COBOLNET0899 (open PB1051, rejects legal source). (2) Within BY CONTENT, identifier-5 formats that are legal sending operands are refused. BY CONTENT SELF in a method is a parse error (COBOL0001), yet SELF is an identifier format whose only role bar is 'shall not be a receiving operand' (§8.4.3.8.3 SR2). A BY CONTENT object-view (O AS UNIVERSAL), whose only role bar is §8.4.3.5.3 SR2 (not receiving), is refused with a false 'AS is a reserved word' COBOLNET0901. The ordinary data-item and expression cases are correct: ResolveSending (pe3), BindExpr/BindBoolExpr, no write-back (pd9), and BY CONTENT EXCEPTION-OBJECT is accepted (re9).
- **Code location (record):** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/OoBinder.cs#OoBindInvocationArg

## What the fixer owes
Route every INVOKE argument through CALL's ONE operand chokepoint (PB132's ScreenCallOperand) for the shared rules — section, bit alignment, literal forms, ADDRESS-IDENTIFIER, constant-name — on the typed AND universal paths; a property reference is a conceptual temporary (content, never reference). Delete the INVOKE-private literal decode.
