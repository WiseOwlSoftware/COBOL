# PB5837 - after a conflict finisher the branch survey does not offer the branch (a fixed tip read as BOUNCED; a gated tip of a finisher that died read as IN FLIGHT for three hours), and both lanes leave it idle

**Sites:**
- `scripts/prune_worktrees.py#lifecycle` (the rule `pr.bounced and at_head`, and the age rule that holds a branch for three hours)

Kind: defect. `process_only: true`. No harm flag. Severity MAJOR is the filer's to weigh (the filer's source leaves it to the clerk; the clerk chose MAJOR because each case held a finished, gated branch with dozens of 1985 rows out of review for 25 to 45 minutes until an attended session noticed). Area: tooling/orchestrator. Related: PB3023, PB3275, PB3628, PB3723, PB2600, PB5754, PB5759. Measured on Monday 2026-11-16 by the Mythos lane, on two of its branches within one hour.

## Case 1: kb/Work PB5754, read BOUNCED after it was fixed
- 23:58 the landing lane queued pull request 1763. 00:02 the survey foresaw a conflict with the entry ahead of it
  and the review lane started a conflict finisher. 00:06:35 the finisher committed the merge `5d397d209e`; its gate
  (run 000004) ended GREEN and it pushed. 00:11:54 GitHub recorded the pull request's removal from the queue
  (merge_conflict). 00:27 the finisher ended DONE, saying the new tip needed a review before the landing.
- From then `python scripts/prune_worktrees.py` printed
  `BOUNCED worktree-agent-a348499a20097e810 — pull request #1763 was removed from the merge queue (merge_conflict at 2026-11-16T08:11:54Z ...): ONE finisher fixes the branch and returns DONE`.
  The rule is `pr.bounced and at_head` in the lifecycle function: the pull request's head was already the
  finisher's merge when the removal was recorded, so the FIXED tip read as the bounced head. Neither lane acted for
  25 minutes. The Mythos lane ran the round-2 review by hand (CLEAN) and `enqueue.py land` by hand; the pull request
  merged at 01:14.

## Case 2: kb/Work PB5759, read IN FLIGHT after its finisher died
- 00:47 the landing lane's `enqueue.py land` stopped on a conflict in one design document. 00:52 the review lane
  started a conflict finisher. 00:53:51 it committed the merge `5f01fc177f`; its gate (run 000004 of that worktree)
  ended GREEN at that head. Its log ends at 01:01:49 with no result line and its process is gone: the session ended
  before it rewrote the report.
- From then the survey printed `IN FLIGHT worktree-agent-a1369f767c02c7fc0 — touched 43 min ago (under --min-age 180)`:
  the report still named the old head, so the branch fell to the age rule, which holds a branch for three hours.
  After the Mythos lane wrote the gated head into the report (from the run's verdict.json), the survey printed
  `WAITING FOR REVIEW`; a hand-dispatched round-2 review ended CLEAN at 01:41 and the landing lane took the branch
  at 01:43.

## What a fix must decide (not decided here)
- A removal from the queue is a fact about a HEAD AT A TIME. A tip whose own gate record is GREEN and newer than
  the commit the queue tested is not the bounced head, whatever GitHub's head field says.
- A finisher that ends without a result leaves a gated commit nobody announced. The survey can see it: the
  worktree's newest gate record names a head the report does not. That is a branch to offer for review, not one to
  hold for three hours.
- Whether the review lane should start the round-2 reviewer itself when a conflict finisher's process ends, as it
  starts the finisher.
