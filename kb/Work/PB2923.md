Filed 2026-10-10 by the train 1052 lander (phase 1b) from the wave 1052 D implementer's report
(`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1052d-PB2694-report.md`, "Leads" item 1; the train manifest's `leads` for 52D

**Repro:** `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w1052d\p3.cob`: `01 FE PIC +9.99E+99.`, `01 HUGE PIC 9(25) VALUE
3000000000000000000000000.`, `01 R2 PIC 9V99.`; `DIVIDE 1 BY HUGE GIVING FE ROUNDED REMAINDER R2` displays
`FE = +0.00E+00` and `R2 = 1.00`. The quotient 3.33E-25 is representable in FE; zero is not its rounded value.

**Rule.** §14.9.12.4 6) c): "a subsidiary quotient is developed that is signed and derived from the quotient by
truncation of digits at the least significant end and that has the same number of digits and the same decimal point
location as the data item referenced by identifier-3. The remainder is calculated as indicated in General rule 7".
A floating-point edited item has a number of significand digits but no fixed decimal point location: the phrase does
not determine the subsidiary quotient for it, so the remainder (GR7: dividend minus the product of the subsidiary
quotient and the divisor) is not determined either.

**Where.** `ArithmeticEmitter.EmitDivideRemainder` (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Verbs/ArithmeticEmitter.cs`, from
line 129; about lines 133-160) truncates the subsidiary quotient to `ScaleOf(d.Quotient.Place)`, which for a
float-edited receiver is the mask hint's fixed scale (2 for +9.99E+99), so a quotient below 0.005 truncates to zero and
the remainder becomes the whole dividend; the stored quotient then comes from that same truncated value. PB2694 (train
1052) made COMPUTE and DIVIDE GIVING render a root quotient per receiver; this single-receiver arm was left as it was.

**Adjudicate, then fix.** Options: (a) the subsidiary quotient keeps the receiver's significand digits (3 here) at the
quotient's own exponent (truncation of digits at the least significant end, read literally), so the remainder is
dividend - 3.33E-25 * HUGE; (b) the implementor defines it (an Annex A.1 determination in CONFORMANCE.md); (c) the
precedence of CLAUDE.md rule 1 (GnuCOBOL, then IBM / Micro Focus) if the standard does not decide. Whatever is
decided, the STORED quotient must not be derived from the truncated subsidiary one (§14.9.12.4 6) b): the quotient
"is then formed as specified in General rule 1 and stored in identifier-3 as specified in 14.7.7"; 6) c) only feeds
the remainder, which GR7 defines as dividend - (subsidiary-quotient * divisor)). Golden at 2023 with the repro's cases.

## Landed (wave 20261714 B, 2026-10-26)

**Re-probe on the fix lane's build (base 624115327):** every number of the adjudication reproduced: `DIVIDE 1 BY HUGE GIVING FE ROUNDED REMAINDER R2`
-> `[+0.00E+00] [1.00]`, `1/3000` -> `[+3.30E-04] [+00000.010000]`, FLOAT-LONG `1000/3` -> `[333] [+00001.000000]`, FLOAT-SHORT `10/3` -> `[3]`; the
STANDARD-DECIMAL program stored the quotients right and left the remainders wrong (1.00, 1.000000, 0.010000).

**Mechanism (one root, three forms).** The REMAINDER arm described identifier-3's subsidiary quotient by a (scale, digit cap) pair, which only a
fixed-point item has; a floating-point identifier-3 was forced into it through `ReceiverScale` (a float-edited item's mask hint) or 0 (a float usage).
The native lane also formed the STORED quotient by the Int128 kernel at that scale, so the REMAINDER phrase changed what identifier-3 held (§14.9.12.4
6) b) says it does not). `ArithmeticEmitter.SubsidiaryShapeOf` now answers "identifier-3's shape under truncation" once per form
(`SubsidiaryForm`: FixedPoint, FloatEdited, BinaryFloat), and a floating-point identifier-3 takes `CarriedSubsidiaryQuotient` in every mode:
the quotient is `FinalQuotient` (the one former DIVIDE GIVING uses too), the subsidiary quotient is derived from it by the form's arm
(`CobolEdit.TruncateToFloatShape` for a float-edited item, the store's own normalization; `FloatResultant` under TRUNCATION for a binary
float, the item's own store landing and never a second rounding routine) and the remainder is GR7's expression over it. Also: `EmitDivideRemainder` builds its
context with `ReceiverContext.Of` (it hand-built a copy that dropped the float-edited flag), `IntDigitsOf` and `SubsidiaryDigitsOf` are deleted (no
reader left), `PicInfo.ReceiverScale`'s docstring no longer says a float-edited receiver's scale is 0 and unused.

**Determination** (docs/CONFORMANCE.md section 3, "DIVIDE ... GIVING identifier-3 REMAINDER identifier-4 with a floating-point identifier-3"):
the subsidiary quotient has identifier-3's own shape under truncation; ISO decides (IBM/Micro Focus reject the form, GnuCOBOL 3.2 has no float-edited
item and truncates a float subsidiary at a fixed scale that is not identifier-3's).

**Goldens:** `pb2923_divide_remainder_float_edited` (2002, 2014, 2023; 10 legs), `pb2923_divide_remainder_float_usage` (2002, 2014, 2023; 5 legs), their
STANDARD-DECIMAL twins (2023) and the negative `pb2923_divide_remainder_float_below_2002` (COBOL0900 at 85). Unit: `FloatSubsidiaryQuotientTests`;
drift: `DivideQuotientOneFormerDriftTests` (the division is rendered by `FinalQuotient` alone, both statements call it, every form has its arm).
