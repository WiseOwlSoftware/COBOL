## Observation
`CopyProcessor.ParseCopyStatement` (`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ParseCopyStatement`, the
§7.2.3.2 general-format parser landed by PB1354) accepts `SUPPRESS [PRINTING]` and a `literal-1` library-text name
with no version predicate. `constructs.json` has no row for either, so `VersionMatrixTests` never samples them below
2023, and no edition gate says which edition introduced them.

## To decide

standards — and state the introducing edition of each phrase with its citation. If either is post-1985, gate it
(`new-construct` skill: the predicate, the constructs.json row, the edition-gate sweep, a positive golden at the
introducing edition and a negative below it). If both are 1985, record that with its evidence and close this note.
