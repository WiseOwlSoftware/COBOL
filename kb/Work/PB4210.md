# PB4210 — the deep-dive states the semantic R2-1 removed

**Sites:** `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md:415-422` (the paragraph whose sentence begins "The recovery from that
report reads the word the same way" and says the word is consumed "only when the statement it would begin does not parse
(`CobolParserCoreBase.LookaheadBeginsAStatementThatParses`, a speculative `ParserInterpreter` parse of `statement` up to the
separator period ...); where it parses, the recovery resumes at it"), and as the code it must describe
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolParserCoreBase.cs` (`CobolParserCoreBase.ReadingParses`, `CobolParserCoreBase.FollowSet`,
`CobolParserCoreBase.LookaheadBeginsAStatementThatParses`).

**The lag (a lead from the branch review, R3-1, medium).** Commit e48103c6f (PB3248's round-2 fix) changed `ReadingParses` to
require a COMPLETE reading: the rule parses AND the reading ends at the separator period or at a token of the rule's
context-free FOLLOW set (`FollowSet`, read off the generated ATN, cached per rule). A prefix parse was too wide:
`MOVE GOBACK TO W-X`, `IF GOBACK = 1 ...`, `ADD VALIDATE W-N TO W-N` and `MOVE RAISE W-X TO W-Y` parse a statement as a prefix,
so the recovery resumed at the operand. The commit did not update the paragraph (CLAUDE.md rules 2 and 6: a design doc
describes the current compiler and is corrected in the same change set). The doc's line ~538 (`entryNameHere`, "asked of the
grammar by a speculative `ParserInterpreter` parse of ...") shares the helper; check it too.

**Fix.** Rewrite the paragraph to the complete-reading rule, naming `ReadingParses`, `FollowSet` and
`ReadingParsesFollowDriftTests` as the pin. No history in the doc (the narrative lives in DEVLOG).
