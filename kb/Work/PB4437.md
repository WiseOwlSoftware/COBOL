# PB4437 - false COBOL0306 for an IF first in a WHEN group faulting at ELSE

**Defect.** Introduced by the R2-2 fix (commit 70b66101b) on the branch now queued as PR #1021: an IF that is the first
statement of an EVALUATE WHEN group (or a SEARCH WHEN) and faults at ELSE loses its `_leftByRecovery` record before its own
END-IF, so that legitimate END-IF draws a false COBOL0306, the PB4382 cascade that round-2 head df87a3682 and round-1
head 5f8f30bca both got right. The report lists it apart from PB4436 (this one drops a record too early; PB4436 is a
lost true diagnostic); the two do not share a stated mechanism.

**Witness.** `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/reports/w20261823a-PB4299-report.md`, R3-2 (medium, not blocking at round 3);
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs:169`. No repro path in the report.

**Fix direction.** The report states none; the record's `StillOpen`/`EnclosingStatement` drop rule must keep an IF that opens a WHEN group.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs#StillOpen`

## Resolution

**Root cause (re-probed: `EVALUATE W-N WHEN 1 IF W-K = 1 ELSE DISPLAY W-A END-IF DISPLAY "X" END-EVALUATE.` drew a false
COBOL0306 at the IF's END-IF).** The WHEN group's `statementBlock` cannot take the left IF's END-IF, so its
`statement+` loop decision fails (`Recover` in `statementBlock`) and the EVALUATE returns BEFORE the next statement
without its END-EVALUATE. `StillOpen` read "the enclosing EVALUATE returned" as "its terminator ended the IF too"
(§2023:14.5.3.2 2)), but the EVALUATE ended by its optional END-x, not by its terminator, and the EVALUATE itself was never
recorded left, so its END-EVALUATE was stray too. **Fix.** A record lives until its statement ends IN its own terminator
(`EndedWithItsTerminator`, used by `StillOpen` and by the record loop), and `Recover` in a `statementBlock` records the
owning statement (`OwningStatement`) as left.
