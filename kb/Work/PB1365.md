## Finding — compiler-directives (under-rejects)

turn-trailing-words: TurnDirectiveProcessor.ParseTurn stops its word loop at CHECKING ON|OFF [WITH LOCATION] with a 'break' and never checks that the operand ends there. TURN is a Stage-owned directive, so CompilerDirectiveCatalog.CheckOperand (the PB794 COBOLNET1911 producer that screens trailing words for the closed-word directives) skips it by design, and no stage-side replacement check exists.

**Rows:** SR-7.3.3-3, SR-7.3.3-4, SR-7.3.3-6

**Repro:**

```
PROCEDURE DIVISION. '       >>TURN EC-ALL CHECKING OFF JUNK' / DISPLAY "S4B". STOP RUN. Also '>>TURN EC-ALL CHECKING ON JUNK' and '>>TURN EC-ALL CHECKING ON WITH LOCATION JUNK' (E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2\out\probe\cd\S4B.cob, T1.cob, T2.cob).
```

**Expected from the spec:** §7.3.3 SR3/SR4: a compiler directive 'may be followed only by space characters and an optional inline comment'; §7.3.3 SR6 composes compiler-instruction 'as specified in the syntax of each directive', and §7.3.25.2 ends the TURN format at CHECKING {ON [WITH LOCATION] | OFF}. Each line must be rejected (COBOLNET0718, the TURN malformed-directive code).

**Observed:** All three compile clean and run ('S4B', 'T1', 'T2'); the same trailing word after every closed-word directive (PROPAGATE, LISTING, REF-MOD-ZERO-LENGTH, LEAP-SECOND, CALL-CONVENTION, PUSH, POP, SOURCE) draws COBOLNET1911 and after DEFINE/IF/EVALUATE/WHEN draws COBOLNET1619. Siblings with the same shape already filed: >>ELSE/>>END-IF/>>END-EVALUATE (kb/Work PB806, open) and >>DISPLAY (PB807, open) - worth landing with them as one 'the stage parser must consume the whole operand' fix.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/TurnDirectiveProcessor.cs#TurnDirectiveProcessor.ParseTurn (the CHECKING arm's 'break')

## Finding — turn-directive (rejects-legal-source)

The >>TURN operand parser treats ON as a required word and stops reading at the CHECKING phrase: ParseTurn sets the ON/OFF state only when the word after CHECKING is literally ON or OFF, and `break`s out of the word loop immediately after, so (a) the legal implied-ON spellings are rejected and (b) any words after ON/OFF are silently ignored.

**Rows:** FMT-7.3.25.2, GR-7.3.25.4-6

**Repro:**

```
PROCEDURE DIVISION. / >>TURN EC-SIZE CHECKING / ADD 1 TO A ON SIZE ERROR DISPLAY "SE [" FUNCTION EXCEPTION-STATUS "]" END-ADD (A PIC 9 VALUE 9). Also '>>TURN EC-SIZE CHECKING WITH LOCATION'. Under-rejection: '>>TURN EC-SIZE CHECKING ON JUNK' and '>>TURN EC-SIZE CHECKING OFF WITH LOCATION'.
```

**Expected from the spec:** §7.3.25.2 printed diagram (PDF p115): ON is not underlined (optional word), so 'CHECKING' alone and 'CHECKING WITH LOCATION' mean ON; §7.3.25.4 GR6 'If the ON phrase is specified or implied'. Expected output 'SE [EC-SIZE-TRUNCATION ...]' exactly as with explicit ON. 'CHECKING ON JUNK' and 'CHECKING OFF WITH LOCATION' match no format and must be diagnosed.

**Observed:** Pinned cobol.exe: TRNP1/TRNP3 -> error COBOLNET0718 '>>TURN: malformed directive'; TRNP5 (ON JUNK) and TRNP4 (OFF WITH LOCATION) compile clean and run.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/TurnDirectiveProcessor.cs#TurnDirectiveProcessor.ParseTurn (the CHECKING branch)

## Finding — turn-directive (under-rejects)

The >>TURN operand parser does not apply §7.3.25.3 SR1's second sentence: after an EC-I-O exception-name every non-'EC-' word other than CHECKING is taken as file-name-1, so compiler-directive words (§8.12: ON, OFF, WITH, LOCATION, ALL, ...) are silently accepted as file-names instead of being interpreted as directive words (which makes the directive malformed).

**Rows:** SR-7.3.25.3-1

**Repro:**

```
'>>TURN EC-I-O ON CHECKING ON' or '>>TURN EC-I-O LOCATION CHECKING ON' before a READ ... AT END on a declared file.
```

**Expected from the spec:** §7.3.25.3 SR1: 'Any user-defined word that duplicates a compiler-directive word is interpreted as a compiler-directive word rather than as file-name-1'; ON and LOCATION are §8.12 compiler-directive words, so neither can be file-name-1 and the directive matches no format -> a diagnostic (COBOLNET0718 class).

**Observed:** TRNF4/TRNF5 compile clean and run; the directive is recorded as (EC-I-O, 'ON') / (EC-I-O, 'LOCATION') file-scoped toggles that match no file. The check needs the §8.12 word set (plus the §14.6.13.1 exception-names, reserved in directives) at the file-name position.

**Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/TurnDirectiveProcessor.cs#TurnDirectiveProcessor.ParseTurn (the `else if (currentEc is not null)` file-name arm)

## What the fixer owes (and the sibling sweep)

Parse TURN from its general format (exception-name/file-name list, CHECKING, optional ON (implied) / OFF, optional WITH LOCATION, then END) and reject anything after; consult the §8.12 word list (PB1366 makes it data) in the file-name arm. Goldens: `>>TURN EC-ALL CHECKING` enables checking; trailing junk rejected; `>>TURN EC-I-O ON CHECKING` rejected.
