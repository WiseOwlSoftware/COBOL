# PB6149 - CALL messages name the 2023 clause at --std 2014

**Mechanism.** `CallBinder.ScreenCallOperand` (`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/CallBinder.cs`) writes the clause in the message text of
its SR3, SR7, SR10 and SR12 refusals as a fixed §2023 reference. Under `--std 2014` the compiler judges by the 2014 text, so the message sends the reader
to a clause that is not the rule applied (the clause numbers and rule numbers can differ between the editions). PB5714 made only the two messages it
touched pick their citation by the compiling edition (`edition.Edition.Cite`).

**Fix shape.** Every message of the method takes its citation from the edition, with each (edition, rule) pair checked by `cite.py`; a drift test that no
CALL binder message carries a literal `§2023` outside an edition switch. Look for the same fixed-edition citation in the sibling verb binders.
