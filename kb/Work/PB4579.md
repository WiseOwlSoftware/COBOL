# PB4579 — the triage briefs never require the Annex A check

**Sites:** `scripts/fuzz_triage_briefs/common.md`, `scripts/fuzz_triage_briefs/refute.md`, `scripts/fuzz_triage_briefs/slice.md`, `scripts/fuzz_triage.py#plan_filing`.

**Observed (night 10, 47 GnuCOBOL-side records filed, then vetted in two steps).** A record is a defect of another project only if the standard requires the behavior of an implementation that CLAIMS the element. Annex A decides that: A.1 lists implementor-defined behavior, A.3 processor-dependent (device-dependent) behavior, and A.4 the optional elements (A.4.1 says a claimed element's syntax and rules are part of the claim, 4.2.7 and 4.2.6 govern a partial or unclaimed one). Nothing in the common, slice or refute brief requires the adjudicator or the refuter to check an element against Annex A and against whether the implementor claims it. The result: the vetting ended four of the five records it ended as not reportable on a latitude or an unclaimed-element ground that the triage had already passed: PB4545 (DYNAMIC LENGTH, A.4.5 1), not claimed) and PB4546 (RESUME, A.4.12 1), not claimed, `-Wpending`) were upheld by their refuter, PB4551 (OPEN WITH NO REWIND, A.3 33), device-dependent and not claimed) was vetted SOUND and overturned only on the second refutation, and PB4519 (native arithmetic, implementor-defined 8.8.1.3) was LATITUDE under ISO alone. The fifth, PB4538, was already fixed upstream (PB4580). Step 1 also found the refuters of one night upholding DYNAMIC LENGTH and RESUME while two others overturned a record on Annex A: the rule was applied by luck of the slice.

**Fix shape.** Put one section in `common.md` that every role renders (not a per-role copy): before a GnuCOBOL-side verdict, name the element, look it up in Annex A.1, A.3 and A.4 of `specs/ISO_COBOL.md`, and state whether the implementor claims it (reserved-word list, `-Wpending`, NEWS, `cbrese.tex`); an unclaimed optional element is `OPTIONAL_UNCLAIMED` and a processor-dependent one needs the claim shown. Add the verdict value to the vocabulary `fuzz_triage.py` validates, so `plan_filing` refuses to file it, and make the refuter's brief require the same lookup independently.
