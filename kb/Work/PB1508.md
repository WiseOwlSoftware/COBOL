# PB1508 — OPEN

## The rule (every citation re-run through `cite.py --check`)

> When enclosed by brackets, zero or more of the alternatives contained within the choice indicators shall be specified, but any single alternative may be specified only once. … The alternatives may be specified in any order.

`OK  §5.2.6.4   (Choice indicators)`


The §11.9.2 general format prints the OPTIONS clauses in a FIXED sequence of independent brackets and the trailing `[.]` as its own bracket; render PDF p. for §11.9.2 (`scripts/render-spec-page.py`) before changing the production — the OCR'd diagram is not load-bearing evidence.

## Finding — `options-paragraph` (under-rejects; rows `FMT-11.9.2`)

**Mechanism.** The OPTIONS paragraph production `OPTIONS DOT (optionsClause+ DOT)?` is an order-free, repeatable `optionsClause+` loop, and OptionsBinder folds it last-wins: the general format's fixed clause sequence and at-most-once brackets are enforced nowhere, so out-of-order and repeated clauses compile clean with no diagnostic and no documented/flagged extension

**Repro.** probe/options-paragraph/reorder.cob: `OPTIONS.` / `DEFAULT ROUNDED MODE IS TRUNCATION` / `ARITHMETIC IS NATIVE.` then COMPUTE B ROUNDED = A (A = 1.5, B PIC 9) -> compiles clean and prints 1 at --std 2014 and 2023. probe/options-paragraph/dup.cob: `OPTIONS.` / `DEFAULT ROUNDED MODE IS TRUNCATION` / `DEFAULT ROUNDED MODE IS NEAREST-EVEN.` -> compiles clean and prints 2 (the second clause silently wins).

**Expected from the spec.** Both programs are nonconforming source and draw a diagnostic (an error, or at minimum the §4.2.10 nonstandard-extension warning with a CONFORMANCE.md entry): §11.9.2 lists seven separate brackets in a fixed sequence with no choice indicators, so arithmetic-clause precedes default-rounded-clause and each clause is written at most once; only choice indicators grant 'The alternatives may be specified in any order' (§5.2.6.4), and no §11.9.3 syntax rule relaxes the order (§5.3.2: syntax rules 'define or clarify the order in which words or elements may be written').

**Observed / reasoning.** Measured with the pinned cobol.exe: reorder.cob and dup.cob both compile with zero diagnostics at 2014 and 2023; dup.cob runs under the LAST DEFAULT ROUNDED (NEAREST-EVEN, 1.5 -> 2). The grammar comment itself records the choice ('order-independent (a superset of the spec's fixed clause order)'), but docs/CONFORMANCE.md carries no determination for it and no warning flags it, which §4.2.10 requires of a nonstandard extension. The fix shape is one check at the single construction point (OptionsBinder.BindParagraph already walks optionsClause() in order): a rank per clause kind from the §11.9.2 sequence, diagnosing a rank that does not increase (covers both out-of-order and repetition), so a future clause gets ordered by adding its rank. Every options-bearing production (program, class, factory, object, interface, method) reaches BindParagraph, so all six arms are covered by the one check.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#optionsParagraph (line ~229); src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/OptionsBinder.cs#BindParagraph / #Apply

## Finding — `options-paragraph` (rejects-legal-source; rows `FMT-11.9.2`)

**Mechanism.** The same production binds the trailing `[.]` to the clause loop, `(optionsClause+ DOT)?`, where the general format prints it as an independent bracket; `OPTIONS. .` (no clauses, the optional period written) is refused by the parser

**Repro.** probe/options-paragraph/twodots.cob: `OPTIONS. .` as the paragraph -> COBOL0001 unexpected '.' at --std 2014 and 2023; twolines.cob (`OPTIONS.` then a line holding only `.`) -> the same.

**Expected from the spec.** Accepted, with the same meaning as `OPTIONS.`: §11.9.2 prints `[.]` as its own bracket after the seven clause brackets, and §5.2.6.2 lets any bracketed portion be written or omitted independently; §11.9.3 SR1 constrains only the case where a clause IS written (then the period is required) and does not forbid it when none is.

**Observed / reasoning.** Measured: rejected at 2014 and 2023. Low user impact (a pathological spelling), recorded because it is the literal derivation of the printed format (PDF p302 rendered and inspected) and it falls out of the same production fix: `OPTIONS DOT optionsClause* DOT?` plus SR1 enforced as 'clauses present => trailing DOT present'. The registrar should confirm no separator rule forbids two consecutive separator periods before landing a positive golden; none was found in §5.2.8 or §11.9.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#optionsParagraph

## Finding — `float-decimal-clause` (rejects-legal-source; rows `FMT-11.9.9.2`)

**Mechanism.** The OPTIONS FLOAT-DECIMAL clause grammar spells the §5.2.6.4 choice-indicator group as an ORDERED sequence (encodingPhrase endiannessPhrase? | endiannessPhrase), so the legal endianness-then-encoding order is a raw parse error - while the USAGE clause's twin group (floatFormatPhrase*) accepts either order. Two-arm dispatch: PB174 fixed the USAGE arm, not the OPTIONS arm.

**Repro.** IDENTIFICATION DIVISION. PROGRAM-ID. FDB. OPTIONS. FLOAT-DECIMAL IS HIGH-ORDER-RIGHT DECIMAL-ENCODING. PROCEDURE DIVISION. DISPLAY "OK". STOP RUN.  (cobol FDB.cob --std 2023)

**Expected from the spec.** Accepted exactly as 'FLOAT-DECIMAL IS DECIMAL-ENCODING HIGH-ORDER-RIGHT' is: the braces enclose choice indicators (PDF p305, figure_geometry.py) and §5.2.6.4 says 'The alternatives may be specified in any order'. Since the clause is Not claimed (A.3 item 13), the accepted clause should draw the §4.2.6 non-support warning (second finding), never a parse error.

**Observed / reasoning.** FDB.cob(4,46): error COBOL0307: unexpected 'DECIMAL-ENCODING'. The encoding-first spelling compiles and runs (OK). The fix is the shape the USAGE clause already has: an unordered choice-indicator group over {encodingPhrase, endiannessPhrase} plus the binder's repeat screen (COBOLNET1718 posture) - ideally ONE rule shared by both citing sites.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#floatDecimalEncoding

**FIXED 2026-09-24 (wave 59 H, owner decision R43 items 1 and 5).** `floatDecimalClause` now reuses the USAGE clause's ONE unordered group, `FLOAT_DECIMAL DEFAULT? IS? floatFormatPhrase+`, and the ordered copy `floatDecimalEncoding` is deleted, so every legal spelling reaches the §4.2.6 decline warning COBOLNET2424 (pinned by `conformance-test:DocumentedNonSupportWitnessTests.FloatDecimalClause_EveryPhraseOrder_ReachesTheWarning_NotAParseError`, four spellings × 2014/2023). The clause is declined whole (docs/CONFORMANCE.md §2 row 13), so no duplicate-phrase screen is added — §4.2.6 frees the implementation from diagnosing syntax errors inside unsupported syntax — and FMT-11.9.9.2 was re-verdicted DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT by batch (`scratchpad/w59h/batch-7-r43-residue.json` + `batch-8-float-decimal-order.json`). The other two findings of this note remain open.

## Finding — `initialize-clause` (under-rejects; rows `FMT-11.9.10.2`)

**Mechanism.** OPTIONS INITIALIZE clause's storage-section choice-indicator group does not enforce §5.2.6.4 'only once': grammar optionsInitializeSection+ and OptionsBinder.InitializeOf fold duplicates with a bitwise OR instead of routing through the shared ChoiceIndicators.AtMostOnce reader (COBOLNET2104) that GOBACK, INITIALIZE REPLACING, CODE-SET FOR and SOURCE-COMPUTER already use.

**Repro.** OPTIONS.
    INITIALIZE WORKING-STORAGE WORKING-STORAGE TO X"5A".
(also LOCAL-STORAGE LOCAL-STORAGE, SCREEN SCREEN) -- pinned cobol.exe --std 2023 compiles with no diagnostic and runs, W=[ZZZZ].

**Expected from the spec.** §11.9.10.2 encloses LOCAL-STORAGE/SCREEN/WORKING-STORAGE in choice indicators inside braces; §5.2.6.4: 'When enclosed by braces, one or more of the alternatives contained within the choice indicators shall be specified, but any single alternative shall be specified only once' -> the repeated WORKING-STORAGE is nonconforming source and must be diagnosed (COBOLNET2104 choice-alternative-repeated), once per repeated alternative.

**Observed / reasoning.** InitializeOf: target.optionsInitializeSection().Aggregate(OptionsSections.None, (acc, s) => acc | SectionOf(s, edition)) -- a repeat is idempotent under OR and never counted; no grep hit for AtMostOnce in OptionsBinder.cs. Probe confirmed clean compile.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/OptionsBinder.cs#InitializeOf (line ~182); src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#optionsInitializeTarget

If the order-free spelling is kept as a leniency it must be a dialect-gated, documented extension with a diagnostic under strict. Grammar changes are pre-authorized; regenerate the grammar diagrams. Goldens: FDB (HIGH-ORDER-RIGHT DECIMAL-ENCODING), `OPTIONS. .`; negatives: reorder.cob, dup.cob, INITIALIZE WORKING-STORAGE WORKING-STORAGE.
