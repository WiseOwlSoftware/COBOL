# PB4059 — the register-key paragraph states an invariant PB3840 broke

**Sites:** `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` (the register-key paragraph near line 3583, "THE ITEM KEY IS THE INVENTORY RULE-ID"),
`scripts/spec/audit_annex_a1.py` (module docstring, lines 16-20)

Since PB3840 the second determination under A.1 item 92 is filed as `DOC-A.1-92b`. The CONFORMANCE.md paragraph still says each
row's first cell is `DOC-A.1-<n>`, the rule-id of the P14 inventory row for A.1 item n. The audit's module docstring says
"every key parses as a rule-id of a real A.1 item". A lettered key is neither, so both statements are false for that row.

## Acceptance

- Both texts describe the key as `DOC-A.1-<n>` or `DOC-A.1-<n><letter>`, say which of the two the inventory row's computed
  `code-location` resolves, and name what the audit checks for a lettered key (read the audit, do not paraphrase this note).
