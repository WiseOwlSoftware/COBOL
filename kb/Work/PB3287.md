## Landed

Re-probed on the Debug build: `SET P TO ADDRESS OF L-REC` over an unreferenceable LINKAGE record compiled clean (while
`MOVE L-REC TO X` drew COBOLNET2746); `ADDRESS OF A` with two `A` drew COBOLNET0869 "names none"; `ADDRESS OF IX` the same.

**Fix.** `ReferenceResolver.Identify` is now the ONE identification step (registers and implicit names, the ref-mod count,
the §8.4.2.2 lookup and its one report, the §13.7.3 SR4 screen, the written subscripts). `ResolveImplCore` takes the place half
after it and `ResolveForAddressOf` the ADDRESS OF half (ref-mod admission, bit place, OCCURS displacement, now through the
one `FixedLevelTerm` shared with `WindowScopeOf`); `forAddressOf` is the single difference (no object-property / object-view
chain, which §8.4.3.11.3 SR1 does not admit and which would register a pending §8.4.3.9.4 operation). `ReportUnidentified`
gained a constant-name arm (a constant-name is declared, not "not defined"). `PtrBinder.PtrBindAddressOf` keeps its operand-shape
message only for the answers that are no data item (a register, a sum counter, a property chain).

**Siblings found in the same file and fixed (I9).** The other two entries that named a TABLE and not a place
(`ResolveTableOperand`: SEARCH / SORT table operand; `IntrinsicBinder` table(ALL) argument) also stopped at `FindItem` and
never asked the §13.7.3 SR4 screen: `SEARCH L-TABLE` and `FUNCTION SUM (L-TABLE (ALL))` over an unreferenceable LINKAGE table
compiled clean. Both now call `ReferenceResolver.FindReportedItem` (lookup + report + screen). `ResolveForItem` (a
condition-name's conditional variable) already screens through its own legs (PB1249).

**Goldens.** Positive `conformance:2002/pb3287_address_of_linkage_sr4_admitted` (a USING operand, its REDEFINES, a
subordinate of the redefinition, a subordinate of a USING group and a BASED item: each address is THE address, checked by
storing through a BASED item set at it). Negatives `pb3287-address-of-linkage-not-referenceable` (2746),
`-address-of-ambiguous-name` (1639), `-address-of-index-name` (1637), `-address-of-constant-name` (2023 only, where CONSTANT
exists), `-search-linkage-table-not-referenceable` (85..2023) and `-all-subscript-linkage-table-not-referenceable`.
`ReferenceIdentificationDriftTests` pins that both entries ask `Identify` and keep no front-half call of their own;
`SubscriptAdmissionDriftTests` now counts the `ReadSubscripts` call sites as `Identify` and `ResolveForItem`.
