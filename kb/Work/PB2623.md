**Rule it breaks:** ISO §4.2.8 (an implementation recognizes as reserved the words specified in §8.9) and §4.2.10 3) (reserved words added beyond the standard are a nonstandard extension that the documentation shall specify); CLAUDE.md rule 1 (validate every citation mechanically, render the PDF when the transcription is in doubt) and rule 4 (no relabelling).

**Scenario:** scripts/gen-reserved-words.ps1:141 adds METHOD to the ISO-2023 set with the comment '# OCR omission (DEVLOG 578)', and the generated row src/Cobol.Net.Editions/ReservedWords.Table.cs:276 (new("METHOD", false, true, true, true, "high", "added 2002 (GnuCOBOL 2002 list; ISO 2023 §8.9)")) and tests/version-matrix/reserved-words.json:2372 carry the provenance 'ISO 2023 §8.9'. Rendering the PDF (scripts/render-spec-page.py 236, printed page 206) shows the M column of §8.9 is MERGE, MESSAGE-TAG, METHOD-ID, MINUS, MODE, MOVE, MULTIPLY. METHOD is not there, so this is not an OCR omission. §8.10 has no METHOD either, and Annex E.2 item 1 removes EXIT METHOD at 2023. ReservedWordSet.RejectsAt (ReservedWords.cs:82-87) therefore rejects `01 METHOD PIC X.` at --std 2023 with COBOLNET0901 ('reserved word in COBOL-2023 ... (ISO §8.9)'), quoting a clause that does not reserve the word. docs/CONFORMANCE.md does not document METHOD as an added reserved word (§4.2.10 3)), and the transcription's Addendum has no correction for it (C1 records the analogous EMD-START typo with evidence). Either the standard's omission is a typo, since END METHOD still spells the word, and it belongs in the Addendum as C-next with that evidence, or 2023 un-reserves METHOD. Today the decision is made silently in a generator comment that says something else.

**Owner question:** ISO 2023 §8.9 as printed omits METHOD although END METHOD still uses it. Should it be treated as a typo in the standard and recorded as an Addendum correction (METHOD stays reserved at 2023), or followed literally (METHOD becomes a user word at 2023)?

**Landed (owner question answered by the standard itself).** Re-probe on the wave-1070 build: `01 METHOD PIC X VALUE "A".` / `DISPLAY METHOD.` was refused with COBOLNET0901 at 2002, 2014 and 2023. The printed page 236 (printed page 206) confirms the note: M reads MERGE, MESSAGE-TAG, METHOD-ID, MINUS, MODE, MOVE, MULTIPLY. The decision is the one every other word the 2023 list dropped already follows (POSITION, COBOL, ...): §4.2.8 makes the §8.9 words THE reserved words, so METHOD is reserved at 2002/2014 and a user word at 2023 (and at 85, which has no OO). `gen-reserved-words.ps1` no longer adds it; the table and `reserved-words.json` regenerate to `R2023 = false`, provenance "dropped by 2023 §8.9". Documented as `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` D-RW2 (owner may overturn). **Sibling arm found by the fix:** the §8.9 reservation gate retypes EVERY METHOD token of a program that declares the word, including the keyword of `END METHOD` (a 2023 program declaring `01 METHOD` plus any method failed with COBOL0001). Fixed at the grammar: one rule `methodKeyword : METHOD | {wordAhead("METHOD")}? IDENTIFIER` (`CobolOO.g4`) serves the END METHOD marker and EXIT METHOD, the three C# readers of `ExitStatementContext.METHOD()` read `methodKeyword()`. The other words 2023 dropped have no 2023 format that spells them.
