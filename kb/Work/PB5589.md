# PB5589 - The lineage screen and the one change-record writer

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/lineage_screen.py` (whole: a new module), `scripts/spec/data/lineage-screen-triggers-2002.json` (whole:
new), `docs/rearchitecture/spec-lineage-screen-2002.json` (whole: generated), `tests/version-matrix/lineage-screen-labels-2002.json`
(whole: new), `scripts/spec/audit_batch.py#change_records`, `scripts/spec/audit_batch.py#change_batch`,
`scripts/spec/audit_batch.py#change_of`, `scripts/spec/lineage.py#next_errors`, `scripts/gate_audits.py#AUDITS`,
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/DocumentedFactsDriftTests.cs#Facts`.

**The defect.** The only writer of an older edition's change records (`audit_batch.change_records`) hard-coded 1985, and the
reads of the 2002 pairs had no list of what each pair's identical words may hide. A 2002 rule word-identical to a 2023 rule
can still govern more in 2002: through a pending 2002 clause no catalog row holds (PB5584: §2002:8.8.1.3.2 truncates a sum
to 32 significant digits), a term §2002:4 defines, a behaviour the compiler applies by edition, the records a 2002 CD entry
holds, a band of formats, an element §2002:G marks obsolete or archaic, or rules it defers to. The prototypes (scratch
measure7-10.py) were not repository tools.

**The fix (design: DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md section 10.5).** The screen FLAGS such pairs; it decides none. Every pair
is decided by a read of the 2002 rule in the 2002 text, attacked by a refuter (owner 2026-11-14: "2002 spec covers 2002
rules"). The triggers are data, one table row each, naming its mechanism (the only code), parameters, source and reason. A
change record for a flagged pair answers each flag by the trigger's name (`next.answers`), or the writer and the gate's
LINEAGE SCREEN audit refuse it. An unjudged changed pending clause, and a behaviour placed in no clause, fail closed (they
flag) and are printed as owed, never a red gate. `change-batch --edition` is one writer for every older edition and writes
only an audit run's decisions (`--state`); without one it is refused by name. The regression fixture holds two blind
samples: the first (`caught`, 16 DIFFERENT) must stay flagged; the second (`known-misses`, 13 DIFFERENT and 3 DOUBTFUL among
pairs the screen left unflagged) is measured. The fixture pins what the screen catches, never that it suffices.

**Measured 2026-11-14.** P0: 10 identical, 43 near, 30 changed, 6 removed of 89 pending clauses; the table judges 25 of the 36
changed or removed and promotes 1 near; 11 are owed and fail closed. The screen flags 1,220 of 1,770 pairs: all 16 of the
first sample's DIFFERENT, 3 of the second sample's 16 known misses, 30 of the 168 SAME of both.

**Still owed.** The 11 owed `pending` judgments and the review of the 14 seeded from the prototype, by an adjudicator and a
refuter (a draft is in scratch, `w20262039a/governs-draft.json`, not adopted: under it the pending-clause trigger flags 313
pairs where the table now flags 621, and it judges two clauses the diff now finds identical); the reads of every 2002 pair (PB5574).
