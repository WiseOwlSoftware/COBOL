# PB5589 - The rule stage's screen and the edition-parameterized KEPT writer

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/lineage_screen.py` (new), `scripts/spec/data/rule-stage-triggers-2002.json` (new),
`docs/rearchitecture/spec-lineage-triggers-2002.json` (new, generated), `tests/version-matrix/rule-stage-labels-2002.json`
(new), `scripts/spec/audit_batch.py#change_records`, `#change_batch`, `scripts/gate_audits.py` (LINEAGE SCREEN),
`tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/DocumentedFactsDriftTests.cs` (`changedIn`).

**The defect.** The only writer of an older edition's change records (`audit_batch.change_records`) hard-coded 1985, and
nothing decided which 2002 candidate pairs may be KEPT by rule. A word-identical pair can still differ in meaning: through
a pending clause no catalog row holds (PB5584: §2002:8.8.1.3.2 truncates to 32 significant digits, 2023 has no such
clause), a definition 2023 changed or dropped, a behaviour the compiler switches, or a loss of scope the K0 blind sample
found in 16 of 100 pairs (the CD entry's records, a removed form, a split band of formats, a deferral). The prototypes
(scratch measure7-10.py) were not repository tools and their counts were pinned nowhere.

**The fix (design: DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md section 10.5).** The triggers are data: one table row each, naming its
mechanism, parameters, source and reason; the mechanisms are the only code. The pending-clause diff is the
`pending-clause` mechanism with an adjudicated `pending` judgment per changed or removed clause. The regression fixture
holds the K0 sample's 100 labels and fails the audit if a DIFFERENT pair is ever unmarked. `change-batch --edition` is one
writer for every older edition: an audit run's records with `--state`, the rule stage's without.

**Measured 2026-11-14.** P0: 10 identical, 43 near, 30 changed, 6 removed of 89; it marks 540 of 1,543 eligible pairs,
equal to the prototype. T5-T8 over v2's 674 keep 524, equal to `k0-v3-keep.json`. The writer writes 461 KEPT and refuses
1,309 pairs: the 63 of the 524 it also refuses are T3 keyed by sense (59: `class (of a data item)`, the four senses of
`character`, `interface (of an object)`, `property`) and the whole-text 2014 check (4). Fixture: 16 of 16 DIFFERENT
marked, 20 of 84 SAME marked. `record_verdicts.py --edition 2002 --dry-run` accepts all 461; nothing is recorded.

**Still owed.** The `pending` judgments of 14 changed clauses that the prototype left without a trigger, and the review of
the seeded ones, by an adjudicator and a refuter (the plan's governs table); the second-target finder of T7; a fresh blind
sample of the written set before any 2002 rule-stage record is stored (PB5574).
