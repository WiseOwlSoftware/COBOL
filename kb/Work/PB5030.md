# PB5030 - real-word misprints in the printed 2002 standard

**Found.** PB4984 ran a real-word misprints reader (`scripts/spec/audit_batch.py`, pass `misprints`) over the 1985 rule
catalog only. The 2002 text carries the same slip that the 2023 text corrected in INSPECT's AFTER rule: §2002:14.8.21.3 GR8 c)
reads "the associated literal-1 or the implied operand of the CHARACTERS phrase participate only in those comparison cycles"
(`specs/ISO_COBOL_2002.md` line 19134), while the BEFORE rule b) one paragraph up and the 2023 rule read "participates".
Checked with `cite.py --check "§2002:14.8.21.3 GR8c"`.

from the catalog and the map it plans from), then read each lead on the rendered page and record the corrections the way the 1985
