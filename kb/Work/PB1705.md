## Finding

**Repro.** `E:\Temp\claude\E--COBOL\eb162d4b-e0bb-4027-b8a0-86fd190d20be\scratchpad\w68u\p\dline85.cob` at
`--std 85`: a program whose SOURCE-COMPUTER paragraph carries `WITH DEBUGGING MODE` and whose PROCEDURE DIVISION has
indicator-`D` lines. The debugging lines are dropped as comments; under the 1985 debug module they are compiled when
the clause is present. Wrong answer, silent.

**Code site.** `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.LogicalConversion.cs:99` (line as of `dbea12428`) always emits the
comment carrier for a `D` line; it never asks the `DebuggingModeDeclared` question PB1088 fixed on the binder side.
The reference-format pass runs before the SOURCE-COMPUTER paragraph is bound, so the fix has to carry the debug lines
forward (for example as a distinct carrier the binder keeps or drops) rather than decide in the text pass.

**Independent root** from PB1088 (which fixed contained-program inheritance of the clause). Related: PB1494 (the
column-7 indicator dispatch is not dialect-gated; `D` is accepted at 2002+ although §6.2.2 no longer lists it).
`docs/CONFORMANCE.md` has no entry for the 1985 debug facility.
