**Found by:** the PB2118 Draft 10 reviser (PB2118 "Draft 10", "What a tenth refuter should try first" 2). Draft 10
made a landing's own MEMBERS known by its train manifest and by content (N1–N5), but the landing's own NOTES still come
from ids leading its commit subjects and from its `status:` flips.

**Harm:** a lander that squashes or re-commits under `WIP checkpoint:` before flipping any status lands R3 work that the
check treats as fix-lane work: check (1) (an R3 landing's diff must lie in its declared set) does not run on it.

**Fix direction (rule 5):** read the landing's notes from its train manifest (the manifest already names its groups and
their notes) and fall back to subjects only for a landing with no manifest; one reader for both member and note
identity; a self-test case with a WIP subject and no flip.
