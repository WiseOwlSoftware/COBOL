# PB6070 - the record extent a clause declares and the extent the runtime uses disagree in three places

1. **LINE SEQUENTIAL, RECORD IS VARYING ... TO integer-3 above every description.** The reader truncates at `MaxRecordSize`
   (the description width, `SequentialConnector.cs:1954`, remainder kept in `_lineRemainder`), so a line within the clause's
   maximum gets status 06 and is split across READs. The clause's GR15 needs its own adjudication (the reviewer's words) before a
   fix; a probe: a 100-column line, FD record 80, clause `TO 120`.
2. **RELEASE of a record shorter than a fixed SD's RECORD CONTAINS** stores the short image (probe `rsort`: `RETURN INTO` gave
   `ABCDE`). Related to PB322. Not probed against its golden.
3. **A file with RECORD CONTAINS/VARYING above the descriptions whose records hold a variable-length member**
   (`FileModel.FixedFormRecords`) keeps the area image for INTO, where the connector record is the fixed-form carriage. Sites
   `OperandText.CurrentRecordImage`, `FileModel.RecordClauseExceedsDescriptions`.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/IO/SequentialConnector.cs`, `OperandText.CurrentRecordImage`, `FileModel.cs`.
