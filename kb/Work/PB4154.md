# PB4154 — dref.cobolWord() is the qualifier in the register alternatives, at about 40 sites

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/ConditionBinder.cs:1626` (`ConditionBinder.ConditionOf`, `ConditionBinder.ReportMisqualifiedCondition`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs:229` (`SearchAllFormat2Rules.CheckKeySide`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReferenceResolver.cs:1557` (`ReferenceResolver.WrittenWordOf`); the remaining readers are found by the sweep the body names and added to this block.

New defect 2 of PB1906's finisher report: a latent wrong-name hazard, found by that branch's SET fix and not yet swept. In the
`dataReference` alternatives for LINAGE-COUNTER, LINE-COUNTER and PAGE-COUNTER the rule's `cobolWord` is the QUALIFIER, not the
register's own name: `LINAGE-COUNTER OF F` gives `F` to every `dref.cobolWord()?.GetText()` reader. PB1906 added
`ReferenceResolver.WrittenWordOf(dref)` (the user-defined word a `dataReference` names, or null for the three register
alternatives) and moved the SET path onto it (`SetBinder.BindSetCondition`, `SetAlterBinder.SwitchCondOf`; before it,
`SET LINAGE-COUNTER TO TRUE` crashed with a NullReferenceException in `SetBinder.BindSetCondition` and `LINAGE-COUNTER OF F` passed
F, the qualifier, to the declaration query).

About 40 other readers of a `dataReference`'s `cobolWord()` remain (the report's count; `git grep` finds 160 lines in all, most of them other rules); each should ask `ReferenceResolver.WrittenWordOf`, or the grammar should
stop giving the qualifier the field name `cobolWord` in those alternatives so that the next reader cannot misread it (prefer the
structure over a hand list, CLAUDE.md rule 5, and pair it with a drift test that no binder reads `dref.cobolWord()` directly).
Examples named by the report: `ConditionBinder.cs:1626`, `:1706` and `SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs:229`. Enumerate them with
`git grep -n "cobolWord()" src` and probe each with the register spelling (`LINAGE-COUNTER OF F`, `LINE-COUNTER OF R`,
`PAGE-COUNTER OF R`) to record which ones answer wrongly today; PB1906's repros under
`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w20261769b\f\` (`r_q.cob`, `s1.cob`, `s2.cob`, `r_pc.cob`) are the starting set.

It shares its root with PB1906's R1-1.
