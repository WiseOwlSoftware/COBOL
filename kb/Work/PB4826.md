# PB4826 - a BOUNDED list that leaves the carrier (PB621 arm) still takes the SDIDI's 34-digit image and can round a value whose exact form the Int128 lane held; needs an owner call because pb4592 pins the 34-digit rounding

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** New defects 1. A BOUNDED list that leaves the carrier (PB621 arm) still takes the SDIDI's 34-digit image and can round a value whose exact form the Int128 lane held (same family as R1-1, other arm). Site: IntrinsicRenderer.cs the cross-aligning arm (RenderDec), IntrinsicCarrierRules.AlignmentMayLeaveCarrier bounds computation. Needs an owner call because pb4592 pins the 34-digit rounding for the SDIDI lane. OWNER CALL NEEDED, as the report words it; this note decides nothing and adjudicates nothing.

**R1.** kb/Work PB621, PB4592-adjacent notes, PB4792 and PB4417 mention AlignmentMayLeaveCarrier; the bounded-list arm is not stated in any open note by grep.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs` (`IntrinsicRenderer.RenderDec`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Bound/IntrinsicCarrierRules.cs` (`IntrinsicCarrierRules.AlignmentMayLeaveCarrier`).

Filed from a review lead of a merged fix-lane branch (wave 20261879 to 20261882) by the enqueue clerk; the lead's claim is the reviewer's, not adjudicated here.

**Fix (landed, wave 20261885; the note's "owner call" did not arise).** Re-probed: `COMPUTE R ROUNDED = FUNCTION SUM (A B)` with `A PIC 9(31)` holding 26 digits, `B PIC V9(9)` = .004999999 and `R PIC 9(26)V99` stored `.01`, where `A + B` stores `.00`: the PB621 arm (a list whose aligned bound leaves the Int128 carrier) took the SDIDI body, whose 34 digits round the 35-digit sum at its last digit, and that rounding carried through the receiver's ROUNDED phrase. pb4592 pins the 34-digit rounding of a value the exact family cannot hold in 34 digits (`SUM (A*A*A, 1.0E0)`, a 92-digit value on the SDIDI/wide lanes), a different arm from this one: every argument of the PB621 arm is an exact scaled item, so the arm takes the same whole-value bodies PB4792 gave the unbounded case (`SumWhole` and siblings, rounded ONCE by the receiver, §14.7.4.3; §15.4.1 leaves the approximation to the implementor and CLAUDE.md rule 1 sends that to GnuCOBOL's exact decimal). A standard mode keeps the SDIDI body (§15.4.1 1)). The pb4592, pb621 and pb4792 goldens are unchanged and run at the gate. Golden: `pb4826_a_bounded_list_leaving_the_carrier_is_the_exact_value` (SUM, RANGE, MIDRANGE, MEDIAN, expected values by Python Decimal) with its `_85` refused twin.

**Sites (as changed):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/IntrinsicRenderer.cs` (`IntrinsicRenderer.RenderNum`, `IntrinsicRenderer.RenderWholeAligned`; the renderer's `AlignmentShiftsUnboundedOperand` wrapper is deleted, `IntrinsicCarrierRules.AlignmentShiftsUnboundedOperand` stays the one reader inside `AlignmentMayLeaveCarrier`), `docs/NUMERIC_DESIGN.md` D26, `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/FunctionValueField.cs` (`FunctionValueField.Median`, the DISPLAY channel's even mean), `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` DOC-A.1-123 and DOC-A.1-92b.
