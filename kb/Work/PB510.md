# PB510

## Mechanism — blank-when-zero-clause
bwz-lexer-zero-synonyms - the lexer folds three distinct reserved words into one token. CobolLexer.g4:722 declares `ZERO : 'ZERO' | 'ZEROS' | 'ZEROES' ;`, so blankWhenZeroClause (`BLANK WHEN? ZERO`) accepts all three spellings and the distinction is unrecoverable downstream. ZERO/ZEROS/ZEROES are interchangeable only as the FIGURATIVE CONSTANT (the figurative-constant general format lists all three as required alternatives); in the BLANK WHEN ZERO clause ZERO is a required word of the clause's own general format, which admits no synonym.

### Expected, from the standard
ISO/IEC 1989:2023 13.18.8.2 general format, rendered from the canonical PDF page 409 (scripts/render-spec-page.py 409): `BLANK WHEN ZERO` with BLANK and ZERO UNDERLINED and WHEN NOT underlined. WS-1 (`BLANK ZERO`, the optional word omitted) is legal. WS-2 and WS-3 are not: the format's required word is ZERO, and the clause's own format is the only licence for the words it contains.

## Landed (wave 58, 2026-09-23) — one token per reserved word

**Re-probed first** on the worktree cut from c54434a8d: `BLANK ZERO` / `BLANK WHEN ZEROES` / `BLANK WHEN ZEROS`
printed `1[     ]2[     ]3[     ]` — all three compiled and blanked. The SWEEP found the same fold accepting every
spelling at the two other formats that print a figurative word as a KEYWORD: the sign condition
(`IF W IS ZEROS` / `IS ZEROES` compiled as `IS ZERO`, §8.8.4.7.2 prints ZERO only) and OPTIONS INITIALIZE
(`TO BINARY ZERO` / `BINARY ZEROS` zero-filled, `TO SPACE` filled spaces — §11.9.10.2 prints BINARY ZEROES,
HIGH-VALUES, LOW-VALUES and SPACES, each in its one plural spelling).

**Fix (structural, at the lexer).** Each figurative spelling is its own lexer token (`ZERO`, `ZEROS`, `ZEROES`,
`SPACE`, `SPACES`, `HIGH_VALUE(S)`, `LOW_VALUE(S)`, `QUOTE_`, `QUOTES`); the interchangeability is written ONCE,
as the parser rules `zeroWord` / `spaceWord` / `highValueWord` / `lowValueWord` / `quoteWord` that
`figurativeConstant` references (§8.3.3.6.2). The keyword sites write the one printed token: `BLANK WHEN? ZERO`,
the sign arms' `ZERO`, `BINARY ZEROES | HIGH_VALUES | LOW_VALUES | SPACES` — so a format added tomorrow that names
one spelling is exact by construction. A misspelling is refused BY NAME as **COBOLNET2418** from the parse layer
(`CobolErrorStrategy` arm 0d, families read from the ATN; the sign condition's early-reported shape recognized
explicitly); an INITIALIZE singular (`TO SPACE`) falls to literal-1 and COBOLNET1727, whose message now names the
four fill keywords. Every figurative consumer (new compiler + legacy oracle) reads the family rules; the
ZERO→ZERO_ARITH rewriter and the two term-start sets admit all three zero spellings. `>>COBOL-WORDS` gains exact
per-word reach for free (the figurative words now carry vocabulary literal names). The only multi-spelling keyword
rule left is `PIC` (§13.18.40.3 SR5) — `KeywordSpellingDriftTests` derives that from the spec tables and the lexer.

Goldens: `conformance/85/pb510_figurative_keyword_spellings` (every legal spelling at its legal position);
negatives `pb510-blank-when-zeros`, `pb510-sign-condition-zeroes`, `pb510-options-initialize-binary-zero` (all four
editions, COBOLNET2418). Design: DESIGN-frontend-grammar.md §3.12.
