## Landed (wave 1037 G, with PB1927) — the OR PAGE heading chain is walked from the heading above

Re-probed before the fix: `pb1927_cf_abs3` (CF FOR MO `LINE 3`) and the refuter's `r3` (DE `LINE 3` below two OR PAGE headings) compiled clean (exit 0) and `r3` overprinted `M=1  D 1` on page 2 line 3.
Root cause, one rule in one place: `DataBinder.Reports.cs#GroupLimits` took "the line following the last line of" a heading from `MinimumLastLine(h)`, which places EVERY body group's first line at FIRST DETAIL (§13.18.35.4 GR5 b) 3., the first body group on a page), so a heading below another OR PAGE heading was taken to start where the higher one starts. `MinimumLastLine` now takes the `seed` the previous group ended on (a relative first line then follows it), and a local `HeadingReach(h)` walks the chain: the last line of h is its lines walked from the last line of the nearest OR PAGE heading ABOVE it (every page advance prints those first, GR6 c)), recursively. `AfterHeading` reads it, so d) 2. (a lower heading), d) 3. (a detail, below the lowest OR PAGE heading) and d) 4. (a footing) all use the one chain; `MinimumLastLine`'s other callers (report/page heading, page/report footing) pass no seed and are unchanged. Sibling arms checked: the heading arm (d) 1./2.), the detail arm (d) 3.) and the footing arm (d) 4.) of `BodyUpper` share `AfterHeading`, so one fix covers the three.
Result: `pb1927_cf_abs3` and `r3` are COBOLNET2199 "lies above the upper limit 4" at all four editions; CF FOR MO `LINE 4` (the edge) compiles and prints correctly.
Doc drift removed (rule 6): `docs/COBOLNET_REPORT_WRITER_DESIGN.md` ("realised by placement, not modelled as limits") and `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` A.4.11 ("the engine models no GR7 upper limits"). The XML doc of `GroupLimits` states the chain.
Goldens: positive `2002/pb2513_cf_absolute_at_or_page_limit` (the edge, with its derived 3-page table); negatives `pb2513-cf-absolute-above-or-page-limit`, `pb2513-detail-absolute-below-two-or-page` (reject-at 85 2002 2014 2023: the screen is edition-independent). The pb1927 positives hold.
