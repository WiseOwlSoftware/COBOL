# PB4145 — no hold when the merge queue's red belongs to everyone

## What happened

At 18:35Z merge group 41107427092 (#811, worktree-wf_f1d1c695-17b-15) went red on
`ConformancePartitioningDriftTests.TheSuiteRunner_KeepsItsPartitionCount_AndBoundsEveryPartition`. The conformance suite
held 12,005 rows against a bound of 24 x 500. That check fails for every merge group whose cumulative suite rows stay
over the bound, whatever the branch, until the partition count is raised. The landing lane went on queueing (#825,
#826, #827), and the operator re-landed #811 by hand, so CI ran matrices whose failure was already known. The account
had already hit "Egress is over the account limit". The operator then stopped it by hand: it dequeued the pull requests
(GraphQL `dequeuePullRequest`), force-cancelled their runs, landed the fix (#828) alone and first, and held the lane
with `<coord>/land-lane/HALT`.

## The defects

1. **No hold for a red that is not the branch's.** The lane's queue watch treats every merge-group red as the branch's
   own (BOUNCED, then ONE finisher, kb/Work PB3023). It cannot tell a red the branch caused from a red every group will
   meet. For example, a check that also fails at main plus any golden-adding branch, or a test of a shared bound. The
   lane should hold its own landings on such a red, say so to the operator, and resume when main carries the fix. The
   test it needs is decidable from data the lane has: the failing check's name and whether the same check failed in
   other groups, or a declared list of shared-bound checks.
2. **The operator has no hold of its own.** `HALT` is written when a run ends QUEUE NOT ACTIVE (exit 8), and the lane
   reports it with that cause. An operator who holds the lane for any other reason must write HALT, and every status line
   then misstates the cause. A hold needs its own state, its reason, and a clear verb (`land_lane.py hold <reason>` /
   `release`), shown by `status`, `session-probe` and the enqueue unit.
3. **Nothing cancels the doomed groups.** When the lane holds, the queued pull requests whose groups will fail should be
   dequeued and their running groups cancelled by the same verb, not by hand.

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py#tick` (launch gate: `HALT`, `loop_stop`), `scripts/orchestrator/land_lane.py#watch_queue`
(the BOUNCED arm), `scripts/orchestrator/lane_status` view and `scripts/session-probe.ps1` (whole: a hold line in its report), and the
enqueue unit's reading of the lane (`scripts/orchestrator/units/enqueue.md`).
