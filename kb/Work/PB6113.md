# PB6113 - `SEARCH E AT END MOVE 1 TO.` draws the bare COBOL0001 'unexpected .'

A MOVE whose receiver is missing, written inside an AT END phrase, is refused at the period with the bare `COBOL0001` "unexpected '.'". The
message does not say that the MOVE lacks its receiver (identifier-2 of §2023:14.9.25.2, which every format of MOVE requires after TO).

**Repro.** `SEARCH E AT END MOVE 1 TO.` (the MOVE without a receiver, the error at the period). The reader's file is `s5.cob` in the scratch
directory of wave 20262143 group E (line 15). Re-probe on current main before fixing; PB6096's branch (`CorrespondingReceiverMessage`,
hint 0c') adds neighboring arms in the same method and may change what the bare message looks like.

**Site.** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (`CobolErrorStrategy.GuessCobolIntent`): no arm reads "a statement inside an AT END
phrase stopped at a period where its operand list needs a receiver".

**Mechanism to sweep (CLAUDE.md rule 4).** The missing receiver after TO is the general shape; AT END only makes the statement sit inside a
phrase of the enclosing statement. Probe the same unfinished statement (`MOVE 1 TO.`, `ADD 1 TO.`, `SET ... TO.`) outside a phrase and in the
other phrases that hold imperative statements (`INVALID KEY`, `NOT AT END`, `ON SIZE ERROR`, `WHEN`), and name the missing operand wherever
the parser can read it from the completed prefix.

**Next.** Add the arm beside PB6096's, read from parser state (the statement prefix the parser completed), with a refusal golden per edition
and an `.err` pinning the new message in place of the bare `COBOL0001`.
