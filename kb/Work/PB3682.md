## Repro (measured by the group A implementer on its branch build)

`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\w20261032a\p\lgu.cob`: a TYPEDEF `GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL` group over a `GROUP-USAGE BIT` subgroup
compiles silently; the same shape in storage is refused COBOLNET1653.

## Fix shape

The same structure PB494 applied: run the 13.18.29.3 SR1-SR3 screens over every tree (typedef and storage) in the one
pre-order walk, not only the Roots, and sweep the other Roots-only checks still left in `UsageInheritanceGroup`. Goldens:
a negative case per screen on a TYPEDEF tree.

## Landed (wave 20261505, group D)

Re-probed on the branch's own build: `lgu.cob` (a TYPEDEF `GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL` over a `GROUP-USAGE BIT` subgroup) compiled
clean while its storage twin drew COBOLNET1653. The root is wider than the GROUP-USAGE block: `UsageInheritancePass` walked
`Roots` only, so NONE of the §13.18.60.4 GR1 screens reached a TYPE declaration (probes on the same build: a TYPEDEF group
`USAGE BINARY` over a `USAGE PACKED-DECIMAL` member, over a `PIC X` member, and `USAGE PACKED-DECIMAL WITH NO SIGN` over
`PIC S9` were all silent) — and the opposite hole beside it: a VALID template with a picture-less group usage (`01 TG TYPEDEF
USAGE BINARY-LONG. 05 A. 05 B.`) was REFUSED (COBOLNET2191 beside COBOLNET0881), because the template header kept the profile
entry bind synthesized for it.

**Fix (three parts, one root).**
1. `UsageInheritancePass` walks the roots and then every `AllTypeDecls()` template, so a declaration answers for itself (the
   PB494 shape, for the whole walk). A copy of a description takes the walk's APPLICATION but not its SCREENS where the fact is
   its declaration's (`AnsweredByTypeDeclaration`: the entry is a copy or a TYPE subject, and the clause reached it from nothing
   or from the same copy), through `EditionContext.Muted`; so a TYPEDEF referenced twice draws one diagnostic naming the
   declaration. A clause from OUTSIDE the copy, and §13.18.29.3 SR1's "not strongly typed" (the composed subject's), are still the
   copy's. A copy of a template the container's bind already walked (a GLOBAL declaration in a contained program) arrives with its
   leaves' profiles in place; the elementary arm leaves a `PicIsUsageSynthesized` item alone.
2. THE COPIES ASK THE ENTRY. `ExpandTemplate`, `ExpandType` and `ExpandSameAsEntry` run before the usage walk and asked `IsGroup`
   (`Pic is null`), which is false of `01 G USAGE BINARY-LONG. 05 A.` until the walk sheds the header's profile: a SAME AS
   subject of such a group was ONE elementary item a quarter of its source's size (a silent wrong answer: `01 X USAGE
   BINARY-LONG. 05 XA. 05 XB. 01 V SAME AS X.` gave BYTE-LENGTH 8 for X and 4 for V), a TYPE subject of the template got no
   members, a `TYPEDEF STRONG` of the shape drew COBOLNET1529, and `SAME AS H` of a level-5 group with a usage passed SR7. They ask
   `DataItem.HasSubordinates` now (pinned by `TheDescriptionCopies_AskTheEntry_NotTheCompletedForm`).
3. `CompleteDescriptionAhead` (a constant's LENGTH OF operand) records the profile as written for a later copy, and recorded
   the profile but not its provenance (`PicIsUsageSynthesized`) or the deferred mark, so a SAME AS copy of such a group read the
   header's profile as a WRITTEN PICTURE on a group (`PictureAsWritten` keeps all three).

**Witnesses.** Nine refused goldens, one per screen, on a TYPEDEF tree (`pb3682-typedef-*`, `pb3682-same-as-group-with-usage-not-level-1`);
four accepted goldens (`pb3682_typedef_picture_less_group_usage`, `pb3682_global_typedef_picture_less_group_usage`,
`pb3682_same_as_picture_less_group_usage`, `pb3682_same_as_after_length_constant`); `UsageInheritanceDriftTests` gains the third
spelling (`01 TG TYPEDEF USAGE u. 05 A pic.`, typed and unreferenced, over the whole usage × phrase × picture matrix: 49 of its
77 cells fail with the template walk removed), the once-per-declaration test and the copies' subordinate test. Rows: witnesses
added to 15 rows (batch `pb3682-typedef-usage-walk`), no GAP closed. **Sibling sweep:** the SIGN walk (`InheritSignClauses`) was
Roots-only too — it only APPLIES (it has no screens), so a template's leaves missed a group SIGN — and is fixed the same way
(`foreach AllTypeDecls`). The two walks together are what made a template and its TYPE subject DIFFERENT descriptions to the
§8.5.3.1 comparison: `01 PT TYPEDEF USAGE POINTER TO TG. 01 P TYPE PT. 01 W TYPE TG. SET P TO ADDRESS OF W.` over a
`TYPEDEF STRONG` group with a group USAGE or SIGN was refused COBOLNET0869 ("not the same type") and is accepted now
(`pb3682_set_restricted_pointer_to_typedef_group_clauses`).

**Review round 1 (finisher).** R1-1 BLOCKING, fixed: SAME AS of a picture-less LEAF under a group-level picture-less usage (`01 G USAGE
BINARY-LONG. 05 E. 01 S SAME AS E.`, legal by §13.18.49.3 SR7) was refused as not elementary. Same root as part 2: SR7 read `IsElementary`
(`Pic`), and the §13.18.49.4 GR3 ancestor transfer read `ItemCategory.GroupKindsOf` (`IsGroup`) of an ancestor whose header still held the
synthesized profile, so no usage crossed. SR7 now asks `!HasSubordinates`; `GroupKindsOf` (and `IsStrongGroup`, `IsVariableLength` under
it) ask the new `DataItem.IsGroupEntry` (subordinates and no PICTURE the program wrote: equal to `IsGroup` after the walk); the transfer
hands the subject what the walk would hand E (the group's synthesized profile and its deferred NATIONAL / BIT mark), through the one
`PictureAsWritten.GiveTo` that `CopyEntryDescription` now uses too. R1-2 LEAD, fixed (same file): the SR2/SR3 subgroup screen muted on
`ClonedFrom` alone; `GroupUsagePairIsTheDeclarations` mutes only a pair the declaration holds (a clone group, or a subject whose clause its
declaration's header wrote), so a clause implied from an enclosing group OUTSIDE the copy is reported again.

**For the next implementer.**

  `DataItem.HasSubordinates`, never `IsGroup` (a group header still holds entry bind's picture-less profile); and a template the CONTAINER's bind
  already walked is copied again by a contained program (the elementary arm's `acquired && !PicIsUsageSynthesized` guard is what lets it).
- `DataBinder.DescriptionCompletion.cs#CopyClauseDescription` / `PictureAsWritten` — the PICTURE side as written (profile, provenance, deferred

- `Model/DataItem.cs#HasSubordinates` / `IsGroupEntry` — the entry's structure; `IsGroup` is the completed form (`Pic is null`). The group-kind
