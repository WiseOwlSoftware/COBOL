## Design in brief

One local gate (`scripts/gate.py`, launched as `build-local.ps1`) with three lanes decided by the diff's paths: **inert** runs the tree audits (about 40 s); **tooling** (`scripts/`, `.github/`, `tools/`, `.claude/`, any `*.py`/`*.ps1`/`*.sh`) runs the audits plus the self-tests whose recorded inputs the diff touches; **code** (everything else, tests included) builds once and runs the WHOLE Unit, Conformance and Characterization populations on Windows, three assemblies at once, longest collections first, the audits beside them. No impact map, no planner, no tiers, no timings store, no post-build self-test, no Linux half per change. The merge group runs the whole population on Linux and Windows in 12 time-weighted shards plus the audits with all Linux self-tests, and is the FIRST run of the merged tree, of Linux, of Release and of GitHub's runners. PR and push events shrink to the required check. A nightly runs the CLI NIST guard, every self-test on both OSes and the GnuCOBOL differential.

Headline numbers: local gate, code change, median 11.1 min to 5.8 (6 to 7 once every co-running gate is whole-population), p90 21.4 to an estimated 10 to 13, inert 187-200 s to about 40 s; merge group 15.1 min and 116 runner-min to about 7.7 min and 77 runner-min; PR created to merged 21.5 to about 9 min; merge-queue red incidents per 20 days 31 (3.5 % of landings) to about 18-21, SELECTION reds 5 to 0 on Windows; about 14,000 of 28,200 pipeline lines deleted against about 700 added.

## The rule that replaces the CI invariant (2026-10-04)

Every case the merge group runs on Windows already ran green at the pushed head, on this host, under the same `toolchain.env`. The merge group is the first run of (a) the merged tree, (b) Linux, (c) Release with a cold compile cache, (d) the Windows tooling self-tests when a tooling path changed, (e) GitHub's runners. A merge-group red is one of those five, a flake, or infrastructure, never an untested case; the landing lane classifies it (PB4271) before anyone is blamed. The owner earlier said, about weakening the CI invariant: "This does weaken the CI must pass if local passes rule. I'm okay with that."
