# PB1941 — OPEN

Filed by the train-1007 lander from wave 1007 group C's report (`w1007c-PB1231-report.md`, "New defects" item 1).
[[PB1231]] (train 1007) made constant entries unordered: a length operand described LATER binds its whole record out
of source order on demand. What it cannot do yet is split a record that is still OPEN.

## Defect

`01 R. 05 A PIC X(K). 05 W PIC X(7). 01 K CONSTANT AS LENGTH OF W.` — A's PICTURE demands K while R is being
described, K measures W, and W is a later entry of the same open record R. §13.10.3 SR4 ("The length of data-name-1
or data-name-2 shall not be dependent, directly or indirectly, upon the value of constant-name-1", `cite.py --check
13.10.3` → `OK §13.10.3 4)`) is not violated: W's length does not depend on K. The same shape arises in the REPORT
SECTION for an elementary report item in a group the report binder has not reached (§13.10.3 SR11 admits it).
Both draw COBOLNET0899 (the bound-later descriptor), a recognized-not-implemented refusal of legal source.

## Code sites and fix shape

`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Constants.cs#LengthOperandItem` and `#ReportLengthOperand`;
`DataBinder.EntryOrder.cs#BindLaterRecords` binds only whole records. The fix needs part-record binding: bind the
operand's entry with its ancestor chain (the open groups on the level stack) without completing the record, or
measure the operand's description directly when its length does not depend on the open entry (the SR4 test already
knows which).
