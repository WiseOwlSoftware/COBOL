# PB1078 — OPEN — `assignTarget : cobolWord | STRINGLIT` gets §12.4.5.2 SR4 wrong in both directions: the zero-length literal `ASSIGN TO ""` compiles (then OPEN answers 30), and 

## Rule text (every citation re-run by the registrar)
- `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check 12.4.5.2 "Literal-1 shall be an alphanumeric literal and shall be neither a figurative constant nor a zero-length literal"` → `OK  §12.4.5.2 4)  (Syntax rules)`

### Adjudicator finding (file-control-entry, harm rejects-legal-source)
- **Mechanism:** assign-literal-1-shape: `assignTarget : cobolWord | STRINGLIT` both admits a literal SR4 forbids (the zero-length "", STRINGLIT's STR_BODY allows it) and refuses one SR4 allows (the hexadecimal-alphanumeric X".." form, a different token), with no bind-time screen of literal-1's shape
- **Repro:** (1) SELECT F1 ASSIGN TO "". FD F1 ... (probe/fce/sr4zero; with FILE STATUS and OPEN OUTPUT: probe/fce/sr4zero_open) (2) SELECT F1 ASSIGN TO X"612E646174". (probe/fce/sr4hex)
- **Expected (spec):** §12.4.5.2 SR4: 'Literal-1 shall be an alphanumeric literal and shall be neither a figurative constant nor a zero-length literal.' (1) is a compile-time error wherever zero-length literals exist; (2) is legal from 2002: §8.3.3.2.1 'Alphanumeric literals are of the class and category alphanumeric' and §8.3.3.2.2 prints 'Format 2 (hexadecimal-alphanumeric)'; its value equals "a.dat", so SR5's implementor rules on the VALUE cannot distinguish it from the accepted spelling.
- **Observed:** (1) compiles clean at --std 2023 and --std 85; OPEN OUTPUT then answers FS=30 at run time (under-rejects). (2) is refused COBOL0312 unexpected 'X"612E646174"' (rejects legal source). Figurative constants (SPACE, ALL "A") and N".." are refused at parse, correctly but anonymously (COBOL0312).
- **Code site:** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolIO.g4#assignTarget; src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/AssignTargetRule.cs#Resolve (decodes, never screens shape)
- **Clause (adjudicator):** 12.4.5.2 SR4

## What the fixer owes
Admit every alphanumeric literal token (incl. X"..") in `assignTarget` and screen SR4 at bind in `AssignTargetRule.Resolve` (zero-length and figurative refused BY NAME, not by COBOL0312). Sweep the sibling literal-operand positions of the file-control entry for the same token-level shortcut.

Rows claimed: SR-12.4.5.2-4. Flip `status` and write `closes_rows` in the landing commit (CLAUDE.md rule 8).
