# PB4592 - the past-77-digit fallback of the exact form keeps a 34-digit residue

**Witness.** `COMPUTE R = (BIG*BIG*K3*1.0E0) ** 2 / (BIG*BIG*K3) - BIG*BIG*K3` stores 99001 (GnuCOBOL gives 0); `(P + 1.0E-50) - P` into a V9(4) receiver stores -999.0000
(probe `f1.cob`, leg PX2, directory above). The intermediate is past 77 digits beside exact operands and keeps the residue of its 34-digit rounding.

**Mechanism.** DOC-A.1-123's published fallback past 77 digits (the SDIDI determination, stated for powers by PB4575) in `CobolExact.cs`: `Additive`, `DivTruncated` and `Pow` hold the wide form to
77 digits and past it round to 34 digits, so a cancellation of two equal large images leaves the rounding residue.

**Question (DESIGN section 1.2).** Exact results past 77 digits need more than 256 bits of intermediate. Whether the compiler widens the exact form (and what the
published limit then is) or keeps DOC-A.1-123's fallback and documents the residue was a design decision; the owner decided it on 2026-11-04 (below).

**Determination (adjudicator, wave 20261870 c).** No ARITHMETIC clause means native arithmetic (§8.8.1.3). The floating-point
literal changes neither the mode nor its own exact value (§8.3.3.3.3 5)), so no SDIDI rule rounds these intermediates. Native
evaluation is an implementor-defined, specified technique (§8.8.1.3, §11.9.5.2 1), §11.9.11.2 1), A.1 123), which may check a
bounded intermediate range (§14.7.5 5)). The standard fixes no value for W1 and W2. Under STANDARD-DECIMAL it fixes 0 for both,
and the compiler answers 0. For the native latitude CLAUDE.md rule 1 makes GnuCOBOL the answer, and GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 is exact at
every size probed: W1 0, W2 0.0000, `(P + 0.5E0) ** 2 - P ** 2 - P` 0.2500, `MOD (P*P + 1, 1000)` 2, `IF P*P + 1 > P*P` true,
`(P ** 250 + 1) - P ** 250` 1. The pin answers 99001, -999.0000, -999.0000, 0, false and a size error. That residue belongs to
neither technique: an exact operand within 77 digits meets a value past them that was rounded to 34 digits. The owner ruling of
2026-11-04 (PB4569) did not decide it: its question kept "the wide form, then the SDIDI", and the 77-digit bound comes from
DESIGN §1.2 invariant 2's owner-locked `BigInteger` ban, not from D19 (a). Widening past 256 bits needs an arbitrary-precision
integer, so the choice was the owner's, and the owner has made it (next paragraph).

**Owner decision (2026-11-04):** asked "when a native arithmetic intermediate exceeds 77 digits, should WiseOwl compute it
exactly as GnuCOBOL does, which lifts DESIGN §1.2 invariant 2's BigInteger ban for that one fallback in CobolExact.cs, or
keep the 34-digit rounding and document the residue in DOC-A.1-123?", the owner answered "Exact, lift the ban": an
arbitrary-precision third form in `CobolExact.cs`, used only where the 256-bit form already fails, with the ban and the
semgrep rule `wiseowl-cobol-no-biginteger` narrowed to that one file in the same change set (DESIGN §1.2,
NUMERIC_DESIGN, DOC-A.1-123).

**Owed (the exact arm of the determination's fix shape):**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Numeric/CobolExact.cs`: the fallback past the form stops being `ToDec(mode)` plus a `CobolDec`
  operation. A third representation, an arbitrary-precision integer at a scale, takes its place in `#Additive` (the `CobolDec.Add`
  / `Sub` tail), `#Mul`, `#TruncatedQuotient` (the `DivTruncatedToOdd` tail; shared by `DivTruncated` and `SubsidiaryQuotient`),
  `#Pow` (the `PowNativeDec` tail for a non-negative integer exponent), `#DivToOdd` (`Significand` of the big form), `#TryAlign`
  (so the intrinsic `*Exact` bodies and `IntegerArgExact` take every digit: the MOD and SUMW legs) and `#Compare`.
- The settling members read it as they read a wide value. `Image` is a round-to-odd 34-digit lowering. `ToUnscaled` rounds once at
  the receiver's scale and mode, and an unchecked overflow keeps the value's own low-order digits, so `COMPUTE R = P*P / 7` stores
  571571143 as GnuCOBOL does (S5). `ToDouble` reads through `Image`.
- The published bound: keep the SDIDI range, |value| < 10^6145. Past it, raise EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW as the §14.7.5 5) checked
  intermediate range, as the pin does today for `(P) ** 250` (q4). This bound is implementor latitude.
- Nothing changes on the fast path. The big form is reached only where `CobolWide`'s `Try...` already fails, so lanes within 77 digits
  keep their current code (no-added-overhead).
- Docs in the same change: `docs/DESIGN.md` §1.2 invariant 2 and the D19 intrinsic-internals line (the owner's amendment of the
  `BigInteger` ban, scoped to the exact intermediate past the wide form); `scripts/semgrep/invariants.yml`
  `wiseowl-cobol-no-biginteger` (scoped to the one file that holds the big form); `docs/NUMERIC_DESIGN.md` (the "past 77 digits"
  sentences at lines 39, 61 and 65, D19's heading and body at line 937 onward, the wide-lane paragraph at lines 1232 to 1275);
  `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` DOC-A.1-123 (the "determination past 77 digits" sentences become the exact rule plus the 10^6145 checked
  range) and DOC-A.1-179 (the power); `docs/INTRINSICS_DESIGN.md` ("What still takes the image").
- Golden: W1, W2, PSQ, ADDF, ADDI, MOD, RELI, SUMW and ABCD at their expected values (+000000000, +000000000.0000, +000000000.2500,
  +000000001, +000000001, +000000002, GT, +000000001, +000000001) plus a STANDARD-DECIMAL twin with SDW1 and SDW2 (0 and 0.0000), as
  the determination's section 3 gives them. Probes: `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-w20261870/c/p/q1.cob` to `q5.cob`.

**Owner decision (2026-11-04), implemented:** "Exact, lift the ban". `CobolBig` (new file, an arbitrary-precision integer at a scale) is the THIRD form of `CobolExact`, reached only where the 256-bit `CobolWide` operation fails and normalized back onto it when the result fits: `Additive`, `Mul`, `TruncatedQuotient`, `Pow`, `Compare`, `Image` (one round-to-odd lowering), `ToUnscaled` (one rounding in the receiver's mode, an unchecked overflow keeps the value's own low-order 38 digits, so `COMPUTE R = P*P / 7` stores 571571143 as GnuCOBOL does), `ToDouble` through `Image`. The `mode` parameter left every operation that had it (`Add`, `Sub`, `Mul`, `DivTruncated`, `SubsidiaryQuotient`, `Pow`) and its emitters: no intermediate rounding is left to take. The intrinsic bodies SUM, RANGE, MEAN, MEDIAN, MIDRANGE, MOD and REM form their value from `CobolExact.Add` / `Quotient` / `Remainder` and round it once (`CobolIntrinsics.ExactValue.cs`); `IntegerArgExact`, `FloorExact`, `TruncateExact` and `FractionPartExact` have the third-form arm. Published bound: |value| < 10^6145 (the SDIDI range, EC-SIZE-OVERFLOW past it, §14.7.5 5)) and an integer of at most 2^22 bits (so `X ** N` stays finite). The fast path has no new code. DESIGN §1.2 invariant 2 and §18 item 19, the semgrep rule `wiseowl-cobol-no-biginteger` (CobolBig.cs exempt; `verify.py` PASS, counts unchanged), NUMERIC_DESIGN (the "past 77 digits" sentences, D19, the wide-lane paragraph), CONFORMANCE DOC-A.1-123 and INTRINSICS_DESIGN ("What still takes the image") state it.
