## Landed (the grammar half)

`invokeArgument` gained the keyword-less `{boolExprAhead()}? booleanExpression` and `arithmeticExpression` arms (CobolOO.g4), the twin of `callArgument`'s, and `InvocationArg.OfInvokeArgument` marks such an argument `Expression` so `OoBindInvocationArg` takes it BY CONTENT (§14.9.23.3 SR9, §14.9.23.4 GR6 a) 2). `USING N * 2`, `USING (N + 1)` and `USING B1 B-AND B2` — parse errors before — now compile and pass one argument each. Witness: `conformance:2023/pb1135_invoke_keywordless_expression_argument`. **Not adopted, and why the note stays half:** the finding expects a bare `USING N + 1` to be ONE expression argument (its p3 probe: one argument for two formals must be refused). CALL's grammar records the opposite determination (kb/Work PB130): `N + 1` is BOTH one expression and the two arguments N and +1, both legal Format-2 lists, the LIST reading wins and a parenthesis selects the expression — and the two verbs must not answer one question two ways. Reversing that for CALL and INVOKE together is a determination for docs/CONFORMANCE.md, not part of this fix, so GR-14.9.23.4-6 (and the p3 leg) remain claimed here.

## Task brief (research 2026-09-30)

Re-measured on `4a1dda60e`: `INVOKE O "TAKEN" USING N + 1` → COBOLNET0828 "2 USING argument(s) for 1 formal" plus
COBOLNET2155 "the numeric literal `+ 1` has its sign separated from its digits". The second diagnostic shows the LIST
reading PB130 recorded is not even what the parser does: `+ 1` with a space is not a literal (§8.3.3.3.2 SR2 — a
sign "shall appear as the leftmost character of the literal", cite-checked OK 2)), and §8.7.1 requires an arithmetic
operator to be "preceded by a space and followed by a space" (cite-checked OK §8.7.1). So `N + 1` has exactly two
grammatical readings: ONE argument (the binary expression) or TWO arguments (`N` and the UNARY expression `+ 1`).

- **⚖ OWNER DECISION (bare question, for CALL and INVOKE together):** *Is `USING N + 1` one expression argument or the
  two arguments `N` and `+ 1`?* (a) one expression — the arithmetic-expression rule is greedy, and the unary reading
  needs parentheses `USING N (+ 1)`; (b) two arguments (PB130's recorded determination) — then `+ 1` must bind as a
  unary expression argument, not be diagnosed as a malformed literal (COBOLNET2155 is wrong under (b) too). ISO gives
  no disambiguation rule. GnuCOBOL 3.2 cannot answer: its `cobc/parser.y` `call_param` → `call_x` admits an
  identifier, literal, function, LENGTH OF / ADDRESS OF and a few registers — no arithmetic expression at all (read
  2026-09-30, read-only); the next precedence step is IBM / Micro Focus. Record the answer in docs/CONFORMANCE.md
  beside PB130's determination.
- **Then:** quarter slot in the frontend (CALL and INVOKE grammars answer identically); golden per the answer.
