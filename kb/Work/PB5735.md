# PB5735 - INSPECT refuses (COBOL0847) a GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL group as identifier-3 ... identifier-n, which the GROUP-USAGE rule treats as an elementary item of usage national: ItemCategory.IsElementaryReference answers 'group' for it (2002 and 2023)

**Sites:**
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/ItemCategory.cs#IsElementaryReference` (`place is RefModPlace || !IsGroupItem(place.Item)`: a national group is a group here)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs#IsInspectElementaryCharacterItem` (the INSPECT predicate built on it)
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs#CheckInspectOperandUsage` (COBOL0847)

**Mechanism.** kb/Work PB856 wrote the national half of SR2 for elementary operands and PB5216 the reference-modified group;
neither treats a GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL group as the elementary item of usage national GR2b says it is "unless stated otherwise".
The sweep with the fix: every other caller of `IsElementaryReference` (InspectBinder's character operands) and every other
statement rule that says "elementary" where a national group is meant to qualify (the rules §2002:E.11.2 lists).

**Inventory.** `2002-SR-14.8.21.2-2` (a KEPT record into `SR-14.9.22.3-2`; it reads GAP through its chain once the 2023 row is
non-conforming) and `SR-14.9.22.3-2` (re-recorded PARTIAL naming this note by the registrar landing records-sample-pb5692).

**Rules (each citation checked with `python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`, verdict OK).**
- `§2002:14.8.21.2 SR2`: "shall reference an elementary item described implicitly or explicitly as usage display or national"
- `§2002:13.16.27.3 GR2b`: "a national group is treated as though it were an elementary data item of usage national"
- `§2023:14.9.22.3 SR2`: "shall reference an elementary item described implicitly or explicitly as usage display or national"
- `§2023:13.18.29.4 GR2b`: "a national group is treated as though it were an elementary data item of usage national"

## Retired 2026-11-23 (wave 20262150 group C): the refusal no longer reproduces

Re-probed on a build of main 7ea61d2e85 (`python scripts/orchestrator/run_probe.py ... s2n2.cob`, the note's own repro): `--std 2002` and
`--std 2023` both compile it and print `002`. The refusal was removed by commit 7470049259 (2026-11-15, the PB5646 review round R1-1:
"INSPECT SR2 admits a national group operand"), the same day this note was filed against an older build (main 95e8e476ad):
`StatementValidation.IsInspectElementaryCharacterItem` asks `ItemCategory.IsElementaryOrBitNationalGroupReference`, which treats a
GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL group as the elementary item of usage national (§2023:13.18.29.4 GR2b), and the usage half
(`IsDisplayOrNationalUsage`) keeps a bit group refused. The sibling sweep re-ran every identifier-3 ... identifier-n position with
national groups (REPLACING pattern and replacement, CONVERTING pair, BEFORE and AFTER delimiters): all four are accepted and give the values
§2023:14.9.22.4 GR derives. Golden `14.9.22-INSPECT/pb5735-inspect-national-group-every-operand-position` (accepted at 2002, 2014 and 2023) pins them;
`pb5646-inspect-national-group-operand` already pinned the TALLYING position. Both inventory rows are re-verdicted: `SR-14.9.22.3-2`
PARTIAL to CONFORMS, and `2002-SR-14.8.21.2-2` (its KEPT record into it) now reads through a conforming row.

**Sites (unchanged):** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Model/ItemCategory.cs#IsElementaryOrBitNationalGroupReference` and
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/StatementValidation.cs#IsInspectElementaryCharacterItem`.
