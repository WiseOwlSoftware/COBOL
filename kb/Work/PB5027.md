# PB5027 - attribution arms 1 and 2 over-credit

**Found.** Wave 20261941 group A (PB5000). In `attribute`, arms 1 and 2 credit a row to every note its `notes` text names.
Example: DOC-A.1-169 names PB1087 and PB1535 but PB1636 closed it, so both named notes are credited with a closure they did
not land. A separate mechanism from PB5000, which narrows arm 3's message set.

**Owed.** Arms 1 and 2 credit the note whose landing changed the row, and a note a row merely mentions is not credited. A
self-test plants a row naming two notes that a third closed.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/backfill_closes_rows.py` (`attribute`, arms 1 and 2).
