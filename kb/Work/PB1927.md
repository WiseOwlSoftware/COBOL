# PB1927 — OPEN

Filed by the train-1004 lander from wave 1004 group E's report (`w1004e-PB1226-report.md`).

## Question

§13.18.57.4 GR6 c): an OR PAGE control heading is reprinted after a page advance, provided the advance "did not take
place just before the printing of a control footing at a lower control level". GR7 d) 4. places a footing's upper limit
after the OR PAGE headings at its own level or higher. For a footing at a lower level than an OR PAGE heading the two
point opposite ways. Wave 1004 group E (kb/Work PB1298, which records the conflict in its landed section) follows the
proviso as written.

**Site.** `src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/ReportWriter.cs#PresentOrPageHeadings` (called from `CobolReport.AdvancePage`).
Golden `conformance:2002/pb1298_control_heading_or_page` does not exercise the conflicting case.

## Orchestrator analysis 2026-10-03 (NOT a verdict; the note stays OPEN)
Both clauses were re-run through `cite.py --check` (§13.18.57.4 6) c) 2., OK; 7) d) 4., OK). Reading them again: a footing at a
LOWER level than an OR PAGE heading has that heading at a HIGHER level, and "at the same level as the control footing, or
higher" in GR7 d) 4. includes it, so d) 4. gives the footing an upper limit BELOW the heading's last line. GR6 c)'s proviso
says that heading is NOT reprinted when the advance comes just before such a footing. If the heading is not printed, there is
no "last line of the heading" on the page for the limit to follow. The two can be reconciled by reading d) 4. as applying
only when the heading is on the page, but that is a reading, not text, and I earlier called the rules non-conflicting without
that caveat; that was too confident. **Not closed from the spec.**
**GnuCOBOL 3.2.0 (cobc, WSL) does not settle it either:** it accepts `TYPE CONTROL HEADING ... OR PAGE` syntactically, but my probe
(PAGE LIMIT 4 to 8, three details, then a lower-level footing) never emitted a page advance, so the proviso case was never
reached. Next step: build a probe that forces the advance (explicit FIRST DETAIL / LAST DETAIL), then record the outcome as
an implementor decision in `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` per "a defect in the standard is recorded, never silently coded around".

**Verdict needed.** The reading that reconciles the two rules (cite both), and a golden with a lower-level footing that
forces a page advance.
