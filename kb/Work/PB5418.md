# PB5418 - COPY REPLACING refuses the COBOL words BY, LEADING and TRAILING as word-1 or word-2: ReadOperand takes any BY as a missing operand, and below 2002 ParseReplacingOperands still reads LEADING and TRAILING as the 2002 partial-word phrases

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ReadOperand`
- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Preprocessor/CopyProcessor.cs#ParseReplacingOperands`

**Mechanism.** The REPLACING format alternates operand BY operand, so position decides whether a BY is the keyword or an operand. `CopyProcessor.ReadOperand` treats any word BY where an operand is expected as a missing operand (COBOL2449), and `ParseReplacingOperands` takes LEADING or TRAILING before an operand as the partial-word phrase at every edition, so at --std 85, where those phrases do not exist and LEADING and TRAILING are ordinary reserved words, it reports COBOL0900 and then COBOL2449. The 1985 rule admits any COBOL word but COPY, and reserved words are COBOL words.
