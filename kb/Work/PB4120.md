# PB4120 — a bare figurative ZERO in a grammar slot typed arithmeticExpression alone is never rewritten to ZERO_ARITH (th

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/ZeroSignReading.cs` (`ZeroSignReading.Revise`, `ZeroSignReading.Try`, `ZeroSignReading.Trial`), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/ZeroTokenRewriter.cs` (`AZeroWrittenAtIsArithmetic`, now private again: the rewrite is its one reader), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/AdmittedKinds.cs` (`Refusal`, the ZERO_ARITH narrowing parameter deleted), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/CobolErrorStrategy.cs` (hint 0i's call). As filed: `ZeroTokenRewriter.cs:100` (`ZeroTokenRewriter.Rewrite`), `ZeroSignReading.cs:30` (`ZeroSignReading.Revise`), `CobolParserCore.g4` (`primaryExpression`, the ZERO_ARITH arm).

Review leads R1-1 (low) and R1-2 (info, "PRE-EXISTING (not introduced by this diff; to be filed as its own note, a rejects-legal-source
defect, sibling of landed PB432)") of PB3961.

**R1-2.** `ZeroTokenRewriter.Rewrite` (ZeroTokenRewriter.cs:136 on the reviewed branch) turns a figurative ZERO into the virtual token
`ZERO_ARITH` by ADJACENCY to an arithmetic operator only. A BARE figurative ZERO in a slot the grammar types `arithmeticExpression`
alone (no alternative admits the figurative) is never rewritten, and the `primaryExpression` primary admits only `ZERO_ARITH`
(CobolParserCore.g4, "primaryExpression : numericLiteral | ZERO_ARITH | functionCall | dataReference | ( arithmeticExpression )"), so
legal source is refused with COBOL0001. Sibling of landed PB432.

**R1-1.** `ZeroSignReading.Revise` (ZeroSignReading.cs:49 on the branch) flips a ZERO only when the syntax-error offender is its touching
sign. Where the arithmetic-expression slot refuses the figurative ZERO itself, the offender IS the ZERO, so PB3981's defect (`ZERO -1`
in an arithmetic expression draws a generic parse error where `Y -1` draws COBOL2993) survives in those slots. One root with R1-2:
the slot refuses the figurative.

**Fix:** decide which slots are arithmeticExpression-only by the grammar (not by a hand list: derive them), let the rewriter produce
`ZERO_ARITH` for a bare figurative ZERO there; then `Revise` sees the sign as the offender. Needs repros first (R2: the reports give
no runnable probe; start from PB432's and PB3981's): a bare `ZERO` as the whole expression of each arithmeticExpression-only slot.

Source: report `w20261769f-PB3961-report.md`, review leads R1-1 and R1-2; PB3961 (landed).

## Landed (wave 20261780 C, 2026-10-30)

Re-probed on the tree first (Debug build, 0/0): `SET IX UP BY ZERO.`, `SET IX TO ZERO.`, `CONTINUE AFTER ZERO SECONDS.` and
`ALLOCATE ZERO CHARACTERS RETURNING P.` each drew `COBOL0001: unexpected 'ZERO'` at 85 and 2023; `COMPUTE N = ZERO.` compiled
(COMPUTE's boolean arm, PB51). Legal: ISO §8.8.1.1 "An arithmetic expression may be [...] the figurative constant ZERO (ZEROS,
ZEROES)", and §14.9.39.2 prints arithmetic-expression-1 / -2 in SET Formats 1 and 2. PB4172 (filed separately the same day from the
PB3428 report) is the same defect from the slot side and lands with this note.

**Fix (derived from the grammar, not a slot list).** `ZeroSignReading`, the parse-informed round PB3981 added to the one parse
loop (`ReservationGateRewriter.ParseToFixpoint`), now takes a second kind of offender: a FIGURATIVE ZERO the parse refused is put
on trial as `ZERO_ARITH`, the expression's operand, and the source is parsed again; a trial still refused AT the ZERO goes back
and is never tried again (`MOVE X TO ZERO` keeps its first error). So every slot the grammar types arithmeticExpression alone
takes a bare ZERO, the next one included, while a slot that admits the figurative parses it and never reaches the round (the
figurative keeps its §8.3.3.6.4 GR4 meaning there). R1-1 falls out: in those slots `ZERO -1` is now refused at the ZERO, read as
`ZERO_ARITH - 1`, and draws §8.7.1's COBOL2993 as `Y -1` does. `AdmittedKinds.Refusal` lost its `aZeroWrittenHereIsArithmetic`
narrowing (PB3861 R1-1): a ZERO written wherever the parse expects ZERO_ARITH now parses, so COBOL5506 names "the figurative
constant ZERO" there (`SET IX TO "A"`).

**Sweep.** Probed with a bare ZERO: SET TO / UP BY / DOWN BY, CONTINUE AFTER, ALLOCATE, READ ... RETRY n TIMES, START ... WITH LENGTH,
B-SHIFT-L: all compile. `PERFORM ZERO TIMES` stays refused: its slot is integer-1 (`integerLiteral`), not an arithmetic
expression, and whether §8.3.3.6.3 SR1 a) admits ZERO for integer-1 is a separate question, reported as a lead.

**Witnesses.** `conformance:cases/14.9.39-SET/pb4120-set-index-bare-zero` (85-2023, output C / B),
`conformance:cases/14.9.39-SET/pb4120-set-up-by-zero-touching-sign` (COBOL2993 at every edition),
`ZeroSignReadingTests` (the trial protocol, and `ABareZeroInAnArithmeticExpressionOnlySlot_Compiles` over the eight slots),
`WrittenKindRefusalTests` (the SET rows now name ZERO and the theory proves a ZERO written there parses), and the re-pinned
`conformance:cases/14.9.39-SET/pb3861-set-index-to-alphanumeric-literal`.
