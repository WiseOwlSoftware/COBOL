## Finding — `qualification` (under-rejects; rows `FMT-8.4.2.2.2`)

**Mechanism.** The identifier grammar allows subscripts and reference modifiers between qualifiers, and the binder never screens the order. `dataReference : cobolWord dataReferenceSuffix*` with `dataReferenceSuffix : subscriptPart | refModPart | qualification`, and `qualification : (OF | IN) cobolWord (subscriptPart | refModPart)*`, accept `E (1) OF T` and `S (2:2) OF G`. The binder resolves them as if written in standard order. No diagnostic is issued at any edition, including under strict (non-permissive) mode, and no CONFORMANCE.md extension row records the leniency.

**Repro.** probe/qual/n2.cob (--std 85 and 2023): 01 G. 05 T. 10 E PIC 9 OCCURS 3 VALUE 5. 05 S PIC X(4) VALUE "ABCD".  MOVE 7 TO E (2) OF T  /  DISPLAY "N8=" E (1) OF T E (2) IN T OF G  /  DISPLAY "N9=" S (2:2) OF G. Observed: compiles clean and prints N8=57, N9=BC at both editions.

**Expected from the spec.** A compile-time diagnostic for each misordered reference, or at minimum a dialect-gated extension warning documented in docs/CONFORMANCE.md. §8.4.2.2.2 Format 1 (the qualified-data-name) carries no subscript. The identifier is §8.4.3.1.2 Format 2 `qualified-data-name-with-subscripts-1` (--check OK), which is §8.4.2.3.2 Format 1 `qualified-data-name-1 [ ( subscript … ) ]` (--check OK): every qualifier precedes the subscripts. Reference modification is §8.4.3.1.2 Format 3 `identifier-1 reference-modifier-1`, which follows the whole identifier. The conforming spellings are `E OF T (1)` and `S OF G (2:2)`.

**Observed / reasoning.** Measured with the pinned cobol.exe. The grammar interleaving is deliberate: the grammar comment makes `qualification` carry its own subscript/refMod suffixes. No binder check compares the suffix order with the standard's, and the pinned tree has no dialect gate for it (grep for an ordering screen in ReferenceResolver.cs and in the grammar found none). Project doctrine is that every leniency is dialect-gated, and a non-ISO extension must be recorded in CONFORMANCE.md. Neither is true here.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/CobolParserCore.g4#dataReferenceSuffix and #qualification (~line 684-695); the binder's reference resolution (ReferenceResolver / DataBinder.WrittenText path) that accepts the interleaved suffixes

## What the fixer owes

Either narrow the grammar to the printed order (qualifiers, then subscripts, then reference modification) or keep the superset and screen the order at bind — the choice is a leniency, so if kept it must be dialect-gated and recorded in docs/CONFORMANCE.md (project doctrine; owner precedence ISO → GnuCOBOL → IBM/MF for the option). Strict mode must reject. Negative golden n2 at 85 and 2023.
