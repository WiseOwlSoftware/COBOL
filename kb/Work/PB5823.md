# PB5823 - at --std 85 the Report Writer sends a relative group of line sum 0 (a control footing with LINE PLUS 0) to the next page after a saved absolute NEXT GROUP, where the 1985 fit test keeps it on the page

**Ruling: UPHELD (a), harm wrong_answer.** Cause (a) is wrong at 85 only: at 2023 the forced page advance is the rule. The checker's confidence: high. Severity MAJOR is the clerk's choice (a wrong-answer defect is at least MAJOR); the ruling names none.

**What happens (the ruling's row for mechanism 1, copied).** Report Writer. After a body group's absolute NEXT GROUP saves its integer, the next relative group whose LINE integers sum to zero (a control footing with LINE PLUS 0) is sent to the next page. The 1985 fit test keeps it on the page: a body group HAS been presented there, so the saved integer is not consulted. The forced page advance is the 2023 rule, applied at every edition. Narrow: only a relative group of integer sum 0 whose lower limit is at least the FOOTING line. The rules it rests on are printed text (folios XIII-32 to XIII-36), not reconstruction.

**The checker's evidence.** All the cited rules are printed text (folios XIII-32 to XIII-36), not the reconstruction. After a body group's absolute NEXT GROUP saves its integer (GR6a: LINE-COUNTER = FOOTING), the next body group meets fit test 3b first paragraph because a body group HAS been presented on the page; the saved integer is consulted only in the paragraph "If no body group has yet been presented". A relative group whose LINE integers are all zero has trial = FOOTING, which is not greater than a control footing's lower limit (FOOTING), so it is presented on the current page at LINE-COUNTER + 0 (GR4b second paragraph). The compiler advances the page unconditionally whenever the save is set (`PresentBody` to `PlaceAfterSavedNextGroup`, the 2023 GR4a forced advance) and places the control footing at saved + 1 on page 2. At 2023 the forced advance IS the rule, so 2023 is right and 1985 lacks its own arm. Narrow: only a relative group of integer sum 0 whose lower limit is >= FOOTING (any control footing; a control heading or detail when LAST DETAIL = FOOTING); absolute and NEXT PAGE groups can never fit at LINE-COUNTER = FOOTING.

**Secondary reading (the checker's; NOT decided and NOT needed for the verdict).** Whether the save still applies on the next page after a non-setting body group was presented below it ("was set when the final body group was presented on the preceding page") decides only where the FOLLOWING group lands (page 2 line 9 against page 2 line 3), not the control footing's page.

**Expected:** the control footing CF-K of the break on page 1 line 12 (trial 12 + 0 = 12 is not greater than FOOTING 12, GR3b; it is presented at LINE-COUNTER + 0, GR4b second paragraph); the save stays for the next group. **Observed** at `--std 85`: CF-K on page 2 line 9 (saved 8 + 1); PAGE-COUNTER 2 after it; the TERMINATE control footing goes on to page 3.

**Where** (the checker's reading): the `_nextGroupSave != 0` branch in `PresentBody` bypasses the fit test at every edition, and `PlaceAfterSavedNextGroup` advances the page unconditionally. There is no DialectBehavior switch.
