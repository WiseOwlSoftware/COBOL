# PB3941 — the frontier-model hold read prose, and held the owner's approved fix

**Sites:** `scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py#plan_held`, `scripts/orchestrator/clusters.py#Note`,
`scripts/orchestrator/clusters.py#note_of`, `scripts/orchestrator/coord.py#frontier_models`,
`scripts/orchestrator/model_rules.json`, `scripts/spec/work.py#frontier_model_shape`

## What happened

The owner approved the fix of PB3869 (a NOT-only SIZE ERROR phrase takes the SIZE ERROR phrase's disposition, a wrong
answer) at about 10:10 PDT on 2026-10-27, in the Mythos session, and the operator's handoff told the next wave to plan
it first. Wave unit 20261723 ran `plan_wave.py --from-budget` and planned **0 groups**. It listed PB3869 as "needs the
owner's approval of a frontier model for this dispatch (P1)".

The hold was `model_rules.json` `routing.owner_approval_patterns`, one regex over the note's body:
`(?i)\b(mythos|fable)\b[\s\S]{0,160}?\bapprov`. PB3869's body records the owner's decision as "in chat (Mythos session
ef566665): \"Approve fixing PB3869\"", and the regex matched it. The owner works in a session and a lane both named after
the Mythos model, so every note that records an approval given there reads as one that needs a frontier model. Over
the open register on 2026-10-27 the regex matched eight notes, and four of the matches were false:

- **False:** PB3869 (the approved fix), PB3765 ("in the Mythos-lane session ... I approve the immediate merge"), PB3836
  ("the Mythos session ... approved wording fixes"), R69 (it states the frontier rule itself).
- **Historical:** PB3160 (the owner's approval of one Mythos review of the design, which ran on 2026-10-14).
- **True:** PB3330 (the owner chose Fable for each of its design's finisher rounds), PB3726 (the owner handed the
  restructure's planning and design to a Mythos model), PB1754 (released to the Mythos lane; R1 Mythos-authored).

## The fix (root cause)

Whether a note's work runs on a frontier model is a fact about the note, so it is stated in the note's frontmatter
and never inferred from prose: **`frontier_model: fable | mythos`**.

- `model_rules.json` `routing.frontier` names the frontier families (with the why). It replaces
  `owner_approval_patterns` and `_owner_approval_why`, which are deleted (CLAUDE.md rule 4: no compatibility layer).
- `coord.frontier_models()` is the one reader of that list. `clusters.Note` carries `frontier_model` from the
  frontmatter (`note_of`, the one note reader).
- `plan_wave.py` holds a lead whose `frontier_model` is set, and its waiting line names the model.
- `work.py check` refuses a `frontier_model` that names no frontier family (`frontier_model_shape`), so a typo
  (`opus`, a dated id) cannot leave a frontier note unheld. `work.py --self-test` holds that check to both answers.
- The migration gives the field to the three true notes (PB3330 `fable`; PB3726 and PB1754 `mythos`), each with a
  paragraph naming why, so their holds are unchanged. The false and historical matches get no field.
