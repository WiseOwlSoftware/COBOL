# PB4906 - transcription line numbers written as section numbers

**What the report says (the implementer's words).** (carried) About 25 transcription line numbers written as `§NNNN` in docs
(`git grep -nE "§[0-9]{4,5}([^0-9.:]|$)" -- docs kb`). process_only. Own root.

**Read on main (2026-11-06).** The same grep lists 19 lines, among them `docs/CONDITIONS_EXCEPTIONS_DESIGN.md:1154` ("ISO §11409"),
`docs/DESIGN.md:1188` (`§12063`) and `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-SPEC-RECONCILIATION.md:121,128,139` (which quotes `§6046` and `§12063` as
the mis-cited finding itself). `kb/Work/PB285.md` records the COBOL0822 message's "ISO §12063" as stale. Clauses of ISO/IEC
1989:2023 are dotted, so no such number resolves, and PB4887's rewrite cannot give them an edition form. Some of the 19 are the
reconciliation document quoting the bad citation and must stay.

**Sites:** `docs/CONDITIONS_EXCEPTIONS_DESIGN.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md`.
