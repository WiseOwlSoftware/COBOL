# PB4894 - the InFlight offer is decided once, at CompleteReceive, and never again

**What the report says (the reviewer's words).** R2-1 LEAD (low, not blocking at round 2):
`src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Messaging/MessagingProtocol.cs:175` An offer kept InFlight only because a reply was owed is not
revisited when that reply is answered: `BeginSendResponse` removes the last `_active` entry and leaves the link open, so the
requestor stays granted to a server that may not RECEIVE again, where `CompleteReceive` would by then have closed the link and
started a new round.

**Read on main (2026-11-06).** `CompleteReceive` sets `claimed.InFlight = true` when `_active` still holds an entry for that
requestor (the line near `MessagingProtocol.cs:698`); `BeginSendResponse` (line 168) removes the `_active` entry and writes the
`ReplyFrame`, and touches no offer.

**Landed (wave 20261918 B).** Re-probe: `MessagingProtocolTests.AReplyThatAnswersTheLastOwedMessageEndsTheLinkOfAnOfferKeptInFlightForIt` failed before the fix
(after the reply the requestor stayed granted: no second OFFER). Fix: `BeginSendResponse` calls `ReleaseKeptOffers`, which ends the link of an offer kept InFlight
when the requestor is owed nothing more, the decision `CompleteReceive` makes; `Owes` is the one test of "a reply is owed" for both. Design section 5.4.2 says so.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Messaging/MessagingProtocol.cs` (`MessagingProtocol.BeginSendResponse`, `MessagingProtocol.ReleaseKeptOffers`, `MessagingProtocol.Owes`, `MessagingProtocol.CompleteReceive`).
