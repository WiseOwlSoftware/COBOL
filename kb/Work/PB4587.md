# PB4587 — a native power past 77 digits is a round-to-odd chain, not the exact power reduced once

**Found by** the determination of PB4569 (wave 20261861 a, pin fb6fcc048), sibling S7. `2.5 ** 100` and `X25 ** 100` show
6223015277861141707144064053780127000000 (probes p5 L2 and L3 under `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-w20261861/a/p/`). The 34-digit
correct rounding is 6.223015277861141707144064053780124E+39, and the exact value, GnuCOBOL's, is
6223015277861141707144064053780124240590.25... `X3 ** 200` (p7 K2) shows 17 digits today; after PB4569 it takes the same chain.

**Determination (adjudicator, wave 20261870 b).** §8.8.1.3 and §11.9.11.2 1) leave the native power and the rounding of a native
intermediate to the implementor (§A.1 123 asks for the technique), and §8.8.1.5.4 2) e)'s chain binds STANDARD-DECIMAL only, so
CLAUDE.md rule 1 makes GnuCOBOL the answer. Its `cob_decimal_pow` is exact at any size. The owner's ruling of 2026-11-04 (PB4569)
takes that exactness to the 77-digit wide form, and the owner-locked ban on arbitrary-precision integers bounds it there. Past
the form, the 34-digit value that answers as GnuCOBOL does at every receiver of at most 32 digit positions is the EXACT power reduced
ONCE by round-to-odd, the reduction every other exact native intermediate takes (`CobolWide.ToDec`, `MulToOdd`, `DivToOdd`).
`PowMagnitudeToOdd`'s chain jams at every product and drifts 2 to 8 units in the 34th digit. Probes are under
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-w20261870/b/p/`. On the pin, `COMPUTE R30 = 1.01 ** 119` into `PIC 9V9(30)` stores …852 for …851,
`COMPUTE R30 ROUNDED = 1.01 ** 181` …877 for …876, and `COMPUTE R6 = 2.5 ** 100 / 2.5 ** 98` 6.249999 for 6.250000 (GnuCOBOL: …851,
…876, 6.250000). The note's "…0124" was the nearest-even rounding. The owed SDIDI value of `2.5 ** 100` is
6223015277861141707144064053780125000000. Nearest-even would round a second time at the final transfer (`1.09 ** 115` into
`PIC 9(5)V9(26)` would store …715 for …714).

**Owed:** one routine, used by `PowNativeDec`, `CobolExact.Pow` and the product past 77 digits. It strips the base's trailing zeros,
stays exact on the wide form while the power fits, and past it runs square-and-multiply on two directed 77-digit bounds (a 512-bit
`CobolWide` product). It reduces the agreeing 34-digit truncation once, jammed odd, and takes the reciprocal of the same bounds for a
negative exponent. The residue is a run of 38 nines or zeros after the 34th digit (0 of 322,660 powers swept). DOC-A.1-123 names it.
`CobolExact.Pow` passes the exact base (not `ToDec(mode)`), and its `mode` parameter goes. `NumericRenderer.Multiply`'s past-77 arm and
`CobolExact.Mul`'s fallback take the exact 512-bit product reduced once, so `(A * B) ** 2 = (A * B) * (A * B)` is true. Docs:
NUMERIC_DESIGN D19 (heading, (a), the PB69 paragraph's chain sentence, the closing paragraph that names this note) and D26, and
CONFORMANCE DOC-A.1-123 (the power, nested product and PB4575 past-77 sentences). Goldens G1 to G15 and the STANDARD-DECIMAL twin are
in the determination's section 3; `pb4575_wide_beside_sdidi_operand`'s R1-1 residue leg is re-derived. Determination:
`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/op-w20261870/b/determination.md`.

**Siblings folded into the fix (S1 to S4):**
- S1: `CobolExact.Pow` rounds a 39-77-digit base first (`(A * B) ** 2` …349 for …351).
- S2: a negative exponent past the form takes `DivToOdd(1, chain)` (`2 ** -300` …273 for …275).
- S3: the wide × wide product past 77 digits reduces images (`(A * B) * (A * B)` …355 for …351).
- S4: the scaled-base arm.

**Lead, not claimed:** `CobolExact.Add`, `Sub` and `DivTruncated` past the form over `ToDec(mode)` images. It needs a repro before a note.

**Landed (wave 20261872, group A).** Re-probed on the build (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261872a/pbw/p1.cob` .. `p5.cob`): `1.01 ** 119` into `PIC 9V9(30)` stores 3.267709796607589156313825958851, `1.01 ** 181` ROUNDED …876, `2.5 ** 100 / 2.5 ** 98` 6.250000, `ABS (2.5 ** 100)` shows 6223015277861141707144064053780125000000 (round-to-odd, not the note's nearest-even …0124 and not the chain's …0127), `(A * B) ** 2 = (A * B) * (A * B)` EQ, `1.09 ** 115` into `9(5)V9(26)` …714, `1.08 ** 297` ROUNDED into `9(10)V9(21)` …687 (the pin: …852, …877, 6.249999, …0127, NE, …, …688). Every expected value was computed with Python fractions (the determination's table) and the STANDARD-DECIMAL twin (`CobolDec.Pow`, §8.8.1.5.4 2) e)) is unchanged (S1 …0126, S2 …851).

**Fix: no second routine, the third form of PB4592 is the routine.** The determination owed "one routine" (a 512-bit two-bound square-and-multiply, with a residue of 38 nines or zeros); the owner's same-day decision to lift the `BigInteger` ban for the exact fallback (PB4592) makes the residue-free shape the right one: `CobolExact.Pow` is the exact power (`CobolWide.TryPow` while it fits, `CobolBig.Pow` — `mpz_pow_ui`, scale × n, the base's trailing zeros moved into the scale first — past it), reduced ONCE by round-to-odd wherever a consumer lowers it (`Image`); a negative exponent is one rounding of the exact quotient `1 / (b ** |e|)`; the §8.8.1.2 rule-6 screen is `CobolIntrinsics.CheckPowRule6`'s (now internal). `CobolIntrinsics.PowNativeDec` and `PowMagnitudeToOdd` (the chain) are deleted, `NumericRenderer.Power` routes every integer-exponent base (integer, scaled, SDIDI, exact) to `CobolExact.Pow`, and `CobolExact.Pow` / `Mul` lost the `mode` parameter (S1: the 39-77 digit base is no longer rounded first; S2: a negative exponent is one rounding; S3: the wide-by-wide product past 77 digits is the exact product; S4: the scaled-base arm). The 512-bit `CobolWide` primitive the determination asked for is not needed.
