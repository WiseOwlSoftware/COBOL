# PB646 — LANDED — a category refused by name

Found 2026-09-05 by the `PB231` NATIONAL-third implementer (report §"New defects" (B)), which measured it while
trying to build a drift row for the byte-window gate's national-form-numeric arm: the arm is reachable only through
an item that `PictureAnalyzer` refuses earlier in the pipeline, so the arm is a table nothing has ever contradicted
([[a_dead_lookup_is_also_unverified]]) — a fact `ByteWindowResidueDriftTests` now states rather than pretends to
cover. No `kb/Work` note owned the staging itself ([[PB70]] and [[PB15]] mention it in passing;
`docs/PHASE4_RECONCILIATION.md` item 8 records it as residue).

## Repro (`--std 2023`)

```cobol
       01 G EXTERNAL.
          05 L PIC 9(3) USAGE NATIONAL.
```

```
error COBOLNET0899: national-form numeric data (a numeric or numeric-edited PICTURE 9(3) with USAGE NATIONAL —
national digits) is recognized but not yet implemented (Phase 4a residue) — … (ISO §13.18.60.3 SR12)
```

The same at every edition from 2002 up; the WORKING-STORAGE spelling without EXTERNAL is refused identically.

## The rule

§13.18.60.3 SR12: "An elementary data item with usage national shall be described with a picture character-string
that describes a boolean, national, national-edited, numeric, or numeric-edited data item." (`OK §13.18.60.3 12)`)
— the rule the diagnostic itself cites ADMITS a numeric PICTURE; §13.18.60.4 GR8 fixes the representation of
a national character at "a uniform size equal to or a multiple of" an alphanumeric one (`OK §13.18.60.4 8)`), which
under D-N1 is two bytes per digit. A numeric item of usage NATIONAL is legal source with a defined storage; the
compiler refuses it by name.

## The mechanism

`src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs:252` — the `else` arm of the USAGE NATIONAL compatibility check
raises `DiagnosticCatalog.NationalData` for a numeric or numeric-edited PICTURE and then sets `usage =
Usage.Display`; `:688` is the numeric-EDITED twin of the same staging. Downstream, the storage model already
answers the question: `NationalWindow.PositionsOf` (PB231's NATIONAL third) names both national spellings and the
byte-window gate carries a national-form numeric leaf at two bytes per digit — unreachable today.

## Fix shape

Implement the category, not the diagnostic: a national-form numeric item is a numeric item whose digit characters
are national (UTF-16 `0`–`9`), so its arithmetic value is the same `CobolNum` as its DISPLAY twin and only its
CHARACTER form differs — the sending/receiving conversions (§14.6.8's MOVE table rows for national numeric, the
simple class condition §8.8.4.4's NUMERIC test on national digits, DISPLAY, the edited form for the `:688` twin) route
through the same one national codec the storage channel uses. Remove the two staging arms; the byte-window arm PB231
wrote becomes reachable and its drift row is written; goldens at 2002/2014/2023 for MOVE in both directions,
arithmetic, the class condition, DISPLAY and an EXTERNAL/BASED spelling (the shape that found this); re-verdict the
`§13.18.60` rows the registrar's batch left PARTIAL on this staging (grep the inventory for COBOLNET0899 notes).
Sweep: every other `Phase 4a residue` staging in `DataBinder.cs` (`:2296`, `:3526`, `:4322`) is the same class —
each is a note or a fix, never a permanent decline without an owner decision (CLAUDE.md rule 3).

## Landed — 2026-09-13

§13.18.60.3 SR12's permission is **five-fifths live**. The three shapes it admitted and the compiler refused by
name — `PIC 9(n) USAGE NATIONAL`, `PIC ZZ9 USAGE NATIONAL`, `PIC 1(n) USAGE NATIONAL` — bind, store, compute,
compare, edit, de-edit, initialize, ride every byte surface, and gate at 2002 exactly as their DISPLAY twins do.

**The shape is data-model design D-N7: a national-form item is its DISPLAY twin COMPOSED with the one national
byte transform** — it is not a new category and it did not need one. §13.18.40.4 GR1 ("When the usage of the
subject of the entry is national, each symbol representing a character position defines a national character
position") says the item's digits, its SIGN IS SEPARATE position and its insertion characters are the SAME
characters the twin holds, in a different character set; §13.18.60.4 GR8 pins only the SIZE of one of those
characters (D-N1: two bytes, UTF-16BE). So the VALUE CARRIER is the twin's (`long`/`Int128`, the edited image
string, the `'0'`/`'1'` run — `PicInfo.ClrType` needed no arm), the CHARACTER IMAGE is the twin's
(`PicInfo.ByteForm` is `Zoned` for usage NATIONAL exactly as for DISPLAY, so `CobolNum` owns every digit, sign
and de-edit rule once — a second "NationalZoned" byte form would have had to re-derive all of them), and only
the CHARACTER→BYTE step differs, applied once by `NationalWindow.PositionsOf` + `CobolBits.NatBytes` at every
byte boundary. `GroupImageCodec` COMPOSES the two (`CarrierImageOf` / `CarrierFromChars`); they used to be
mutually exclusive arms, sound only while the shape could not reach emit — a national numeric leaf would have
stored a string into a `long` field.

**FOUR staging arms, not two, and two of them were the only §13.18.60.3 screen their path had.** Beside the
boolean arm and the numeric/numeric-edited arm of `ScreenUsageAgainstPicture`, `AnalyzeFloatEdited` and
`AnalyzeLocaleEdited` each carried a private hand-written SR12 staging **and nothing else** — so a
floating-point-edited or a format-2 LOCALE picture under `USAGE BINARY`/`COMPUTATIONAL`/`PACKED-DECIMAL` met no
§13.18.60.3 SR3 screen at all. Both now call `ScreenUsageAgainstPicture`, the ONE screen its own doc comment
already claimed to be.

**`NationalWindow.PositionsOf` is now ONE test.** It named two shapes and read two different widths for them
(`p.Length` for the category, `ElementaryImageWidth` for the numeric); the two are equal for every
category-national item, so collapsing to `Pic.Usage is Usage.National → ElementaryImageWidth` changed no answer
and made all five SR12 shapes ride at once — §13.18.60.4 GR8 is written about the USAGE, not about a category.

**Five defects the note did not have, each measured before it was claimed:**

1. **§13.18.63.3 SR7 was asked at the wrong TIME, and the GR1 spelling got the opposite verdict.** SR7 reads
   §8.5.2.1 Table 2's CLASS of a numeric-edited item, which IS its usage — and §13.18.60.4 GR1 supplies a usage
   AFTER the entry binds. `01 G USAGE NATIONAL. 05 GZ PIC ZZ9 VALUE N"  1".` was refused as a class mismatch
   against a DISPLAY item while its written-clause twin bound. The verdict moved to a declared pass,
   `CheckValueLiteralClasses`, placed after `UsageInheritancePass` (the resolved usage) and after
   `ResolveTableValues` (format 2's per-occurrence literals), covering formats 1, 2 and 3/5 alike.
2. **§13.18.8.4 GR2 was conditioned on usage DISPLAY** — `PIC 9(3) USAGE NATIONAL BLANK WHEN ZERO` silently
   stored `000` where GR1 requires spaces, even though §13.18.8.3 SR2 admits the clause on a usage-national item
   by name. A SILENT WRONG ANSWER, invisible only while the shape was staged.
3. **`PicInfo.SignKindFor` sent every non-DISPLAY usage to `BinaryMinus`**, but §13.18.52.3 SR2 names "display
   **or national**" and GR6a makes a SEPARATE sign a character position. `PIC S9(3) USAGE NATIONAL SIGN IS
   LEADING SEPARATE` measured `FUNCTION LENGTH` 3 and `BYTE-LENGTH` 6 against its DISPLAY twin's 4 — the item
   claimed a width its own character image contradicts. Now 4 and 8. The group-SIGN inheritance walk carried the
   same display-only conjunct and took the same fix.
4. **§15.50.4 r2 re-derived the national position count as `pic.Length`** instead of reading the one authority,
   so the same SIGN SEPARATE position r3's DISPLAY arm counted was dropped by r2's national arm — one rule, two
   arms, disagreeing. It reads `NationalWindow.PositionsOf` now.
5. **Reference modification was a two-arm dispatch with one arm fixed.** `ReferenceResolver.RefModExclusion`
   already ADMITTED `{ Category: Numeric, Usage: Display or National }` (§8.4.3.3.3 SR1) and `RefModView`'s wrap
   gate named DISPLAY alone, so `RM(2:3)` over a national-form numeric passed the exclusion and then fell to the
   Tier-C runtime loud: "a COBOL feature that is not yet implemented was reached at run time". §8.4.3.3.4 GR6c's
   category answer (`RefModPlace.CategoryOf`) was already written and merely unreachable.

**`PicInfo.IsCharacterFormNumeric` is the extracted predicate** for "this item's image is a run of digit
characters" — eleven hand-written copies of `{ Category: Numeric, IsFloat: false, Usage: Usage.Display }`, the
national half missing from every one. ⛔ It is read only where an item is VIEWED through its characters
(ref-mod, a RENAMES span's `NumericImagePlace`, a STRING receiver). The sites that ask whether the CARRIER may be
REPLACED by that string — `MarkImageForced`, `StorageFormPass`'s whole-group promotion — stay DISPLAY-only and
say why: promotion pins the carrier at `ImageWidth` CHARACTER positions, which is a DISPLAY item's whole storage
and half of a national one, and promoting a national-form numeric leaf was MEASURED to corrupt a plain
group-to-group MOVE between two of them. The consequence is recorded rather than hidden: a group move that
deposits characters incompatible with such a leaf's description leaves it holding §14.6.13.2's value rather than
those characters, which is the posture every other non-DISPLAY numeric usage already takes.

Goldens: `{2002,2014,2023}/pb646_national_form_numeric` (the whole behaviour, every expected line computed from
the standard in the file header) and `2023/pb646_national_form_numeric_bytes` (the §13.18.60.4 GR8 / D-N1
determination OBSERVED through a REDEFINES — the low byte of every pair is the character, the high byte of every
pair is zero, for a numeric, a numeric-edited and a boolean national item alike); negatives
`pb646-national-form-numeric-at-85` (COBOLNET0900, written AND group-inherited spellings),
`pb646-national-numeric-edited-value-class` and `pb646-numeric-edited-value-national-class` (COBOLNET0898 —
SR7 in both directions). **No new diagnostic code was needed**: COBOLNET1978–1980 were allocated and none used;
SR7 joins the existing 0898 VALUE-literal-class band. Registry row `national-data-2002` re-texted; the
COBOLNET0899 `national-data` descriptor re-texted too — its last caller is the FD/SD byte-surface gate and no
national shape is staged anywhere any more.

Rows re-verdicted (6; GAP 2533 → 2527): **CONFORMS** — `SR-13.18.60.3-12`, `GR-13.18.40.4-1`,
`GR-13.18.40.4-2`, `GR-13.18.40.4-8`, `SR-13.18.63.3-7`, `SR-13.18.63.3-8`.

## Finished — 2026-09-13 (the five pinned tests, and TWO more arms of the same shape)

The implementer split at the turn cap with the mechanism landed and FIVE gate reds, every one of them a test or
table that PINNED the retired COBOLNET0899 staging or a hand-rolled `Usage.Display` union. All five are closed,
and none was a behaviour regression:

1. `DataSkeletonEditionTests`' NAT1 row was the LAST member of the W2 staged-skeleton set, and a green theory
   asserting `COBOLNET0899` on `PIC 9(4) USAGE NATIONAL` is a green test holding the gap open. The row became a
   POSITIVE edition-gate theory over four national-form shapes (compiles at 2002/2014/2023, COBOLNET0900 naming
   COBOL-2002 at 85), and the two now-empty staged theories became `NoDataSkeleton_StagesAt0899` — which
   compiles every shape that was ever in the set and fails if any of them returns to a staged posture. The set
   is asserted empty, not described as empty.
2. `NumericByteFormDriftTests` pinned `Usage.National -> NumericByteForm.None`. It is `Zoned` (D-N7): the
   national form's FORM is the zoned digit run and only the CHARACTER->BYTE step differs. ⛔ The old row had
   never been contradicted because the population it described could not be compiled.
3. `DisplayUsageUnionDriftTests` is re-derived as TWO counts per file — the DISPLAY-ONLY union and the
   `Usage.Display or Usage.National` PAIR — because after this landing both are spelled with the same text and
   they are different questions; the PAIR is what the standard itself writes (§8.4.3.3.3 SR1, §13.18.52.3 SR2,
   §14.9.48.3 SR4) and the singleton is the drift-prone one. A single total would have let a site move between
   them silently. The IMAGE-FORM question has left the inventory entirely: it is `IsCharacterFormNumeric`.
4. `LoudGuardTests.Analyze_UsagePictureConformance_0881And0899Shapes` split: the 0881 half (SR20, SR5) is
   unchanged, and the 0899 half became `Analyze_Sr12Shapes_BindUnderUsageNational`, a theory asserting that all
   FIVE SR12 picture shapes bind with the usage and category intact. Its doc comment cited §13.18.60.4 for
   rules that live in §13.18.60.3; corrected.
5. `ConstructRegistryDriftTests` — `ConstructRegistry.g.cs` regenerated with `scripts/gen-constructs.ps1`
   (never hand-edited) after the `national-data-2002` row's re-texting.

**TWO further defects, both the shape this landing kept fixing — one rule written down twice, one copy widened.**
Both were found by the four-lens review of the landing's own diff and both were MEASURED before being claimed:

- **The §13.18.49.4 GR5 SAME-AS sign transform kept a DISPLAY-only guard.** `PicInfo.SignKindFor` names the
  §13.18.52.3 SR2 pair and `InheritSignClauses` was widened to it, but `ExpandSameAs` carried a SECOND copy of
  the transform. `01 TN USAGE NATIONAL SIGN IS LEADING SEPARATE. 05 TNE PIC S9(3). 01 SNX SAME AS TNE.`
  measured `LEN=3 BYTES=6 [04N]` for SNX against TNE's `LEN=4 BYTES=8 [-045]` — the SAME description, two
  answers — though GR5 names "an alphanumeric group item, **national group item**, or strongly-typed group
  item" explicitly. Fixed by extracting `DataBinder.ApplyEffectiveSign`, the SIGN twin of `ApplyEffectiveUsage`,
  so both acquisition routes are one method. Golden: the `GSE`/`SAMES` leg of all three positive goldens.
- **A GROUP-LEVEL VALUE over a NATIONAL GROUP dropped its whole distribution.** `GroupValueSlicer`'s numeric
  arms named DISPLAY, and the landing's own self-review had recorded them as unreachable "by rule" on the
  strength of §13.18.63.3 SR14 — but SR14's usage-DISPLAY requirement is written about items "subordinate to an
  **alphanumeric** group item", and a `GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL` group is not one: §13.18.29.3 SR3 requires its
  subordinates to be usage national and contemplates numeric ones by name. `01 NG GROUP-USAGE NATIONAL VALUE
  N"AB123". 05 NGA PIC N(2). 05 NGB PIC 9(3).` measured `NGA=[  ] NGB=[000]` — the failing leaf made the WHOLE
  subtree non-distributable — against its alphanumeric twin's `[AB]` / `[123]`. Both arms now read
  `IsCharacterFormNumeric`. This was a SILENT WRONG ANSWER that the staging had hidden: before PB646 the shape
  could not compile at all. Golden: the `NG`/`AG` leg of all three positive goldens.

Both are recorded in `docs/COBOLNET_DATA_MODEL_DESIGN.md` under D-N7, which now also states that an inherited
SIGN clause reaches `SignKind` through ONE method and that the group-VALUE slicer is the one site where the
national form is not screened out upstream.
