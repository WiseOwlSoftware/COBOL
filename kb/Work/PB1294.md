## Measured

### Finding source-clause — ISO §13.18.53.4 GR3; §13.18.54.4 GR6 (rejects-legal-source; rows GR-13.18.53.4-3)

**Mechanism.** The GR3 exception 'except where the entry is referred to by means of a SUM clause' is unreachable: a SUM data-name-1 naming a SOURCE entry is staged (rolled total) and, for an unprintable entry, misreported as an SR5 resolution failure

**Repro.** probe out13/probe/source-clause/p3.cob: RD R2 CONTROL IS FINAL. DE: `05 UNP PIC 9(3) SOURCE WS-AMT.` (no COLUMN) and `05 PRN COLUMN 23 PIC 9(4) SOURCE WS-AMT.`; CF FINAL: `05 COLUMN 1 PIC 9(5) SUM UNP.` and `05 COLUMN 7 PIC 9(5) SUM PRN.`; GENERATE DET with WS-AMT 5 then 7; TERMINATE.

**Expected, from the standard.** §13.18.53.4 GR3 excepts an unprintable SOURCE entry referred to by a SUM clause, and §13.18.54.4 GR6 adds 'the operand of the SOURCE or VALUE clause' for such a data-name-1; the CF FINAL line prints 00012 at column 1 and 00012 at column 7.

**Observed.** error COBOLNET0899: SUM addend 'UNP' does not resolve to a data item outside the report section (ISO §13.18.54.3 SR5) - the unprintable entry is not recognized as a report-section data-name-1 at all - and error COBOLNET0899: SUM addend 'PRN' names a report section data item (data-name-1 - a rolled total ...) - not yet implemented. The rolled-total staging is the SUM clause's rows (SR-13.18.54.3-4 / GR-13.18.54.4-6); this finding records that it also holds the SOURCE clause's GR3 exception unreachable and that the unprintable arm gets the wrong diagnostic. No open kb/Work note owns rolled totals (COBOLNET_REPORT_WRITER_DESIGN lists them as staged).

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs#ResolveSumAddend

### Finding sum-clause — ISO/IEC 1989:2023 13.18.54.3 SR4; 13.18.54.4 GR6, GR7 a)/b)/c) 2., GR8, GR11 (rejects-legal-source; rows SR-13.18.54.3-4, GR-13.18.54.4-6, GR-13.18.54.4-7, GR-13.18.54.4-8, GR-13.18.54.4-11, GR-13.18.54.4-9)
