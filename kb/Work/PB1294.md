## Measured

### Finding source-clause — ISO §13.18.53.4 GR3; §13.18.54.4 GR6 (rejects-legal-source; rows GR-13.18.53.4-3)

**Mechanism.** The GR3 exception 'except where the entry is referred to by means of a SUM clause' is unreachable: a SUM data-name-1 naming a SOURCE entry is staged (rolled total) and, for an unprintable entry, misreported as an SR5 resolution failure

**Repro.** probe out13/probe/source-clause/p3.cob: RD R2 CONTROL IS FINAL. DE: `05 UNP PIC 9(3) SOURCE WS-AMT.` (no COLUMN) and `05 PRN COLUMN 23 PIC 9(4) SOURCE WS-AMT.`; CF FINAL: `05 COLUMN 1 PIC 9(5) SUM UNP.` and `05 COLUMN 7 PIC 9(5) SUM PRN.`; GENERATE DET with WS-AMT 5 then 7; TERMINATE.

**Expected, from the standard.** §13.18.53.4 GR3 excepts an unprintable SOURCE entry referred to by a SUM clause, and §13.18.54.4 GR6 adds 'the operand of the SOURCE or VALUE clause' for such a data-name-1; the CF FINAL line prints 00012 at column 1 and 00012 at column 7.

**Observed.** error COBOLNET0899: SUM addend 'UNP' does not resolve to a data item outside the report section (ISO §13.18.54.3 SR5) - the unprintable entry is not recognized as a report-section data-name-1 at all - and error COBOLNET0899: SUM addend 'PRN' names a report section data item (data-name-1 - a rolled total ...) - not yet implemented. The rolled-total staging is the SUM clause's rows (SR-13.18.54.3-4 / GR-13.18.54.4-6); this finding records that it also holds the SOURCE clause's GR3 exception unreachable and that the unprintable arm gets the wrong diagnostic. No open kb/Work note owns rolled totals (COBOLNET_REPORT_WRITER_DESIGN lists them as staged).

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Reports.cs#ResolveSumAddend

## Landed 2026-10-03 (wave-1010 implementer A)

**Re-probe (the note's p3, own build of fb6a6747e).** `UNP`/`PRN` under `SUM` drew COBOLNET0899 `SUM addend 'UNP' does
not resolve to a data item outside the report section` (the unprintable entry was not a report-section name at all) and
`PRN` the staged `report-sum-rolled-total`; the p7 shape drew the same staged error. Citations checked:
`13.18.54.3` SR4 a)-g), `13.18.54.4` GR6, GR7 a)/b)/c) 2., GR8, GR9, GR11, `13.18.53.4` GR3.

**Mechanism (one root, three arms staged by it).** A SUM clause's data-name-1, its report-name-qualified spelling (SR4 g))
and an UPON naming a detail of another report each need a sum counter fed by something that is not a GENERATE of its own
report, and the engine had no way to say "add THIS when THAT group is processed". The ROOT is that the unit of rolling
was never modelled: an ENTRY that carries a value — a SUM, SOURCE or VALUE entry, printable or not. Now
`ReportEntryFamily` (the written entry: name, qualification, group, report and the repetition geometry a sum counter
already had; `ReportSumFamily` and the new `ReportSourceFamily` derive from it) owns one `ReportItemOccurrence` per replay
and COLUMN operand, with its counter occurrence or SOURCE/VALUE operand and its FULL presence chain.
`ResolveRolledAddend` resolves the name once (qualifiers against each entry's hierarchy, report-name last), screens SR4
once per written addend (COBOLNET2730: numeric/value/unsubscripted, a), b)/c)/d), e); COBOLNET2731: f), none across
reports per g)) and maps per counter occurrence by GR8 (innermost levels correspond, the addend's outer levels are the
table totalled). The addition is registered with the GROUP that contains the addend — `CobolReport.AddRolled`, stored on
`ReportGroup.Rolled`, performed by the one group prologue `BeginGroup` (not on an OR PAGE reprint) — through the same
closure a GENERATE-driven term builds (`ReportWriterEmitter.SumAddition`), after every engine is constructed
(`RegisterCrossEngineAdditions`, which also orders a group's chain and registers the cross-report UPON via
`AddGenerateTrigger`). Deleted: the three staged descriptors (`report-sum-rolled-total`, `report-sum-cross-report`,
`report-sum-upon-cross-report`). Design: `docs/COBOLNET_REPORT_WRITER_DESIGN.md` "A ROLLED TOTAL …".

**Witnesses.** `conformance:85/pb1294_report_rolled_unprintable_source` (the p3 shape, 00012 00012),
`2002/pb1294_report_rolled_totals` (detail → control footing → FINAL chain, VALUE, unprintable SOURCE, PRESENT WHEN
absent, a mixed addend list), `2002/pb1294_report_rolled_repeating` (GR8 a) and b)), `2002/pb1294_report_rolled_cross_report`
(SR4 g) and the cross-report UPON), `2002/pb1294_report_rolled_varying` (a VARYING counter as SOURCE: 4+6+8); and nine
negatives `negative/pb1294-sum-data-name-*`, one per rule (numeric, group, subscripted, UPON, b), c), d), e), f)). Each
expected line was derived from the cited rule and the program run against it.

**Sibling sweep.** Which arm of the dispatch was fixed, and where is the other: the SUM clause has three addend arms —
data-name-1 (this note), identifier-1 (SR5, PB482/PB1830) and arithmetic-expression-1 (SR6, PB883) — all three now
resolve through `ResolveSumAddend`, and a term splits them by when each adds (GR9). The GENERATE-driven arm's UPON is the
other half of the same registration problem and now carries its owner. Probed and left as measured: a SOURCE operand
that is not numeric (an alphanumeric item feeding a numeric entry) is refused when a SUM names it, not before — the
printable item's implicit MOVE keeps compiling.

**Code sites for the next implementer.**
- `DataBinder.Reports.cs#ResolveRolledAddend` / `ScreenRolledAddend` — the ONE resolution and SR4 screen of a SUM data-name-1;
  reuse `ReportEntryFamily.Linear` / `CoordinatesOf` for any occurrence arithmetic; a new rule over a rolled addend goes in
  `ScreenRolledAddend` (once per WRITTEN addend, memoised by its parse node — a repeating SUM entry binds its clause once
  per counter occurrence and would report each rule N times).
- `DataBinder.Reports.cs#RecordSourceOccurrences` / `SourceFamilyOf` — an entry with SOURCE/VALUE and no SUM; the trap is
  that a printable entry's occurrence is recorded after its field (operands cycle by the field's `RepetitionOrdinal`) and an
  unprintable one has no field at all.
- `ReportWriterEmitter.cs#SumAddition` — the one ADD-consistent closure (GENERATE-driven and rolled), and
  `RegisterCrossEngineAdditions` — registrations must be emitted AFTER every report's engine is constructed (a cross-report
  registration names the other engine); a rolled addition that reads a counter must follow the additions into it.
- `ReportWriter.cs#BeginGroup` / `ApplyRolled` — the single group prologue; do not add a second path that processes a group.
- Two drift tests pin the report binder: `GlobalReportScopeTests` counts reads of `Data.Reports` in the procedure binder
  (read it once and pass the list), `ReportGroupResolutionDriftTests` bans a `Groups`-by-name search outside
  `ReportGroupResolution`.
