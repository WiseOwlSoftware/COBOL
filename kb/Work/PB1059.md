# PB1059 — OPEN

## Measured
`RD RR PAGE LIMIT 20 LINES 10 COLUMNS.` → `error COBOL0307: unexpected '10'. A period may be missing at the end of the
previous sentence.` `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` A.4.11 lists 'PAGE COLS' as having no grammar surface, 'tracked' — no
kb/Work note tracked it.

## The rules
`cite.py --check 13.18.39.2 "COLUMNS"` → OK (§13.18.39.2 general format, rendered: `PAGE [LIMIT IS | LIMITS ARE]
{integer-1 | [integer-1 {LINE|LINES}] [integer-2 {COLS|COLUMNS}]}`).
`cite.py --check 13.18.14.3 "Neither integer-1 nor integer-2 shall exceed the page width"` → OK (§13.18.14.3 — the
transcription prints this rule as `6.` without its parenthesis, so cite.py shows an empty rule path; a PB903-class
transcription defect).
