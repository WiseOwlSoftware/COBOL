# PB4304 - The PICTURE character-string limit is 63 at every edition; ISO 2002 says 50 and COBOL-85 said 30, so 85 accepts 31-63 symbols and 2002 accepts 51-63

Found by the Mythos front-end review, gap pass, 2026-10-31 (GAP-REVIEW.md, G5-4; artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/KaxkLe85tfZBZ3nZ1tDGTs). Pin d7daee1cf.

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/PictureAnalyzer.cs` (`PictureAnalyzer.MaxStringLength`, `PictureAnalyzer.BoundProvenance`, `PictureAnalyzer.AnalyzeCharacterString`, `PictureAnalyzer.MaxPictureStringLength`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs` (`DiagnosticCatalog.PictureStringTooLong`, COBOL2146); `tests/version-matrix/constructs.json` (rows `picture-string-50-2002`, `picture-string-63-2014`); `conformance-suite/wiseowl/cases/13.18.40-PICTURE/pb531_picture_repetition_factor.cob` (its R3 leg is now the 85 boundary, 30).

**Finding.** `PictureAnalyzer.MaxPictureStringLength = 63` (:760, COBOL2146) at every edition. 2002 section 13.16.39.3 SR4 says 50 (t2002.txt:16902; 2002 F.2 item 94 "increased from 30 to 50" :44179), so 85 is 30 (GnuCOBOL cobol85.conf pic-length 30; cobc refuses `p12-pic-long.cob`, 38 X symbols). The pin accepts 38 at 85 and 2002 and 51 (`p12b`) at 2002, rc 0; the 2023 64-symbol refusal (`p12c`) is correct. The 2014 half was REFUTED: GnuCOBOL cobol2014.conf sets pic-length 63 (while keeping word-length 31, so it distinguishes the limits per edition) and 2023 Annex E item 11 records the word-length increase but no picture-string increase, which it would have to if 2014 still said 50; the CD's 50 is the CD, not the final. Edition facts: 85:30, 2002:50, 2014:63, 2023:63. PB532 landed the 2023 limit without edition reasoning.

**Fix direction.** Per-edition limit joined to the word-length table. The fix must rework `pb531_picture_repetition_factor.cob` (accept: [85], a 63-character boundary leg, a header claiming SR4 "read identically at 85, 2002, 2014 and 2023").

**Citations checked** (`python scripts/spec/cite.py --check`): section 13.18.40.3 "The maximum number of characters allowed in character-string-1 is 63".

**Evidence.** E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/GAP-REVIEW.md (sections 2 and 5.1); G5/p12-pic-long.cob, p12b, p12c; review-G5.md. Probe names are copied from the shard reports; the refuter folders are E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/G*-refute/. Shard reports: E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w-gr2/review-G*.md.
