A pointer to either kind of storage, used after that storage's life ends, is therefore a non-NULL invalid address. The runtime's only validity notion is the StorageCell.Freed flag, which FREE sets. CobolPtr.Deref checks NULL, non-window carrier, Freed and the window bound, and nothing else. No activation end or CANCEL ever invalidates a cell, so both shapes read stale storage silently with checking on. The adjudicator's FREE, out-of-window, before-start and tail-straddle branches do conform. I re-probed the before-start and tail-straddle branches myself.
