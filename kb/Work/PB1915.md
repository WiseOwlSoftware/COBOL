# PB1915 — OPEN

Filed by the train-1003 lander from wave 1003 group D's report (`w1003d-PB1660-report.md`, new defect 3; the
implementer's self-review, silent-failure lens).

## Finding

**Mechanism.** `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Parsing/DebuggingLineRewriter.cs#Rewrite` finds unit boundaries and the
SOURCE-COMPUTER clause by token TYPE (`CobolLexer.PROGRAM_ID` … `METHOD_ID`, `END` + unit kind, `SOURCE_COMPUTER`,
`DEBUGGING MODE`). It runs FIRST in `Frontend.LexAndParse` (`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Pipeline/Frontend.cs`), before the
>>COBOL-WORDS retype (ISO §7.3.10.4) turns a de-reserved word into an IDENTIFIER or a synonym into its keyword. So a
compilation group that de-reserves or substitutes one of those words gives the rewriter the wrong unit structure, and
§12.3.5.4 GR1's per-unit scope of WITH DEBUGGING MODE is read wrongly.

**Repro shape.** A >>COBOL-WORDS directive that makes a synonym of `SOURCE-COMPUTER` or of `DEBUGGING`, used in a
program that declares the mode through the synonym and has `D` lines at `--std 85`. (>>COBOL-WORDS is a 2014 directive
and debugging lines are removed at 2014, so the combination needs a dialect that admits both; the implementer rated it
rare.)

**Fix shape.** Run the rewriter after the COBOL-WORDS retype, or have it read the retyped types; a unit test in
`DebuggingLineRewriterTests` with a substituted header word.
