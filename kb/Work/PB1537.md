## Mechanism and code site

`DataBinder.TryClauseTextLiteral` (the one SR11 screen, `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/DataBinder.Switches.cs`, wired by landed
kb/Work PB226 to all seven literals) classifies ANY figurative constant as the symbolic-character kind. Fix: refuse only a
symbolic-character figurative constant (and a zero-length literal); every other figurative constant is a literal of its class.
§7 rows DOC-A.1-42 and DOC-A.1-181 state the SR11 restriction and flag this over-rejection; they are recorded PARTIAL on it.

## Sibling sweep

All seven SR11 literals share the helper (ALPHABET literal-1/-2/-3, CLASS literal-5/-6): after the fix, `ALPHABET A IS SPACE` and
`CLASS C IS SPACE` must follow their own rules. Also PB1544 (a symbolic character refused as an intrinsic argument — another
place a figurative constant stands for a literal).
