**Rule it breaks:** CA1031 (do not catch general exception types), reported once in this shard at AnalysisLevel=latest-all; CLAUDE.md rule 4 (no papering over) and the front end's own stated rule at Frontend.ParsePass (Frontend.cs:532-537): 'A NON-parse exception (a predicate/lexer-action bug) now propagates instead of being silently retried'

**Scenario:** LexProbe (CobolKeywordTokens.cs:96-123) runs the real CobolLexer over a word a >>COBOL-WORDS directive names. Every legal outcome is already a return value: the word is pre-screened to letters, digits, '-' and '_' (:98-100), and lexer errors go to CountingErrorListener, never thrown. The 'catch (Exception) { return 0; }' (:119-122) therefore catches only a defect, such as a NullReferenceException or InvalidOperationException from a lexer action (the lexer's NextToken runs the >>COBOL-WORDS plan and context actions, CobolLexer.g4:316-317). Then 0 means 'not a keyword token', so Plan.For leaves the word out of the retype and '>>COBOL-WORDS UNDEFINE "PICTURE"' or an EQUATE onto a multi-spelling keyword silently does nothing. The class remark (:14-25) records that this exact symptom, a silently inert UNDEFINE, already cost one bug. The answer is also memoized in Probed (:76-79), so it stays wrong for the life of the process.
