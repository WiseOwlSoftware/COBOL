## Landing (wave 20262135 group B)

Re-read at 2061bdb7bb: `CobolString.CompareFig` (native) still built `FigToWidth(seed, width)` on every evaluation, and
`ThruMemberFig` (native) built up to two sized ends through `SizedFig` before `ThruMember` compared them.

Fix: `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Runtime/Values/Text/CobolString.cs#SizedOperand`, a private `ref struct` view of an operand: a
value as it is, or a figurative seed sized to its associated operand without building the string (length
`max(width, 1)`, character i `seed[i % seed.Length]`, a space for an empty seed: exactly `FigToWidth`'s characters,
§2023:8.3.3.6.4 GR2). There is now ONE native comparison loop, `Compare(in SizedOperand, in SizedOperand, char)`, and ONE
native THROUGH test, `ThruMember(in SizedOperand ×3, char)` (§2023:14.7.8 GR2's inversion check, then the inclusive
bounds). The public `Compare(string?, string?, char)`, `CompareFig(.., char)`, `ThruMember(.., char)` and
`ThruMemberFig(.., char)` each build their views and call those two, so the plain and the figurative-aware carriers can no
longer drift. A figurative relation on the native sequence allocates nothing. `FigToWidth` stays the one materializing
form: the compile-time fold (`EmitText.RepeatToWidth`) and the `CobolCollation` arms still read it (the locale arm
trims, so it needs the string; walking `LiteralPhraseCollation.Weight` over the view is left for when a measurement
asks for it).

Drift test: `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/FigurativeReaderDriftTests.cs` compares `CompareFig` on both sides and
`ThruMemberFig` with the materialized `FigToWidth` forms over nine seeds (empty, one character, ALL literals shorter
than, equal to and longer than the operand) and associated widths 0 to 12, with both pads. Behavior-neutral: no emitter
change; the public signatures are unchanged.
