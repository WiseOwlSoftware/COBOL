## Why the fold is real, and where it stops

`AlphabetFigurative` serves **§12.3.7.4 GR10** — the ALPHABET clause's SPECIAL-NAMES mapping — which is a
genuinely different rule with different VALUES from §8.3.3.6.2: under GR10, HIGH-VALUE and LOW-VALUE are the
extremes of the **native** character set, never of the sequence being defined. That half must NOT be folded away.

**The WORD-RECOGNITION half is the duplicate.** "Which figurative constant did the programmer write, and was it
written with the ALL phrase?" is one question, and §8.3.3.6.2 is the one place the standard answers it. The
implementer's measured estimate is ~6 lines: ask `FigurativeConstants.KindOf` for the kind, then map the KIND to
GR10's character. That leaves GR10's value table where it belongs and deletes the second spelling of the word list.
