# PB5564 - at --std 85 a subscripted subscript compiles (ELEM (IE (2)), ELEM (IE (2) + 1)): the 1985 subscript gate takes any data reference as data-name-2

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Frontend/Parsing/Cobol85OperandForms.cs` (`Cobol85OperandForms.IsSubscript`, `Cobol85OperandForms.SubscriptDataName`); `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/SubscriptBinder.cs` (`SubscriptBinder.ScreenCobol85SubscriptDataNames`)

**Mechanism.** The 1985 subscript (as Amendment 1 printed it) is ALL, integer-1, data-name-2 [{+|-} integer-2] or index-name-1 [{+|-} integer-3]. Since kb/Work PB5074 the --std 85 gate is `VersionConformancePass.ParseArm.VisitSubscriptPart` over `Cobol85OperandForms.IsSubscript` (the shape, and the sign and zero of the integers), with `SubscriptBinder.ScreenCobol85SubscriptDataNames` asking the scale of data-name-2. But `IsSubscript` accepts ANY `dataReference` as data-name-2 (it tests `dataReference() is not null`), so a data-name that is itself subscripted passes, and the screen asks only the referenced item's class and scale. This is the line-18 case of PB5074's own repro, which its landing left open. At --std 2002 the same programs are legal (the arithmetic-expression subscript) and compile, as they should.
