## Landed (wave 1111 group E, branch `worktree-wf_71ab13ab-689-5`)

Re-probed on a fresh Debug build: `RECORD CONTAINS 11` over two `PIC 1(3) USAGE BIT` items and `PIC X DYNAMIC LENGTH LIMIT 10`
was refused COBOL2010 `1 to 12 bytes`, and compiled once the dynamic item was `X(10)`. Fixed at the root in
`FileModel.MaxDynamicExtent`: it is now `ByteWidth` (bits laid out by the one 8.5.1.6.3 walk, a dynamic-length leaf zero
bytes) plus `DynamicLeafSum` over the dynamic-length leaves (`DynamicLeafMaximum` each, times the occurrence counts
between the leaf and the record), so a record is `1 to 11` and `RECORD CONTAINS 11` compiles and runs.

Siblings found in the same two members and fixed (I9): `StructureOverheadBeneath` (the minimum twin) did not multiply a
dynamic-length item's structure overhead by the occurrence count of the table around it, and `MinExtent`'s bit-bearing
occurs-depending arm left the overhead out entirely. Both now read `MinimumStructureOverhead`, the same
`DynamicLeafSum` walk at `MinimumOccurrences`; `3 x (10 + 1) = 33` / `3 x (0 + 1) = 3` for a DELIMITED item in a three-element
table, and `3 to 14` for a bit item, a DELIMITED item and a bit ODO table, are pinned in `RecordSizeModelTests`. Goldens:
`pb3253_record_contains_bits_beside_dynamic` (accept 2014, run) and `pb3253-record-contains-bits-beside-dynamic-one-over` (refused
at 2014 and 2023, COBOL2010).
