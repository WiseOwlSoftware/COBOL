# PB2672 — a landed train's members cannot be pruned by the tool

## What happened (2026-10-08, the loop's land unit after train 1039b landed as c799a62b1)

`python scripts/prune_worktrees.py` listed eight of the train's ten member branches (`w1038t-loop-control`,
`w1038u-session-checks`, `w1038v-branch-lifecycle`, `w1039x-battery89`, `w1039y-goto-conditional`,
`worktree-wf_2d68bf0d-aa5-1`, `-7` and `-8`) as `DECISION … DONE, but PB… is already landed: superseded (its work
reached main through another branch); confirm nothing unique remains, then delete it; CHECK (names not on main: )`.
The other two (`w1039-note-pb2613`, `r2-batch1-file`) were IN FLIGHT only by their touch time. Every member's head
equalled the head that `E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\train1039b-manifest.json` recorded, and every worktree was clean. The
land unit removed all ten by hand, with a one-off script that checked those two facts. `--apply` could not do it.

## Mechanism

`scripts/prune_worktrees.py#classify` calls a branch LANDED only when `merges_to_main_unchanged` holds: `git
merge-tree --write-tree origin/main <branch>` must equal main's tree. Train 1039b's V cluster (PB2600) introduced that
proof so that a branch which only edits existing files is never called LANDED by its added files alone. But a lander
changes its members' content as it lands them: review fixes in a cluster's commit, re-sited witnesses, conflict
resolutions against the earlier clusters, renumbered DEVLOG entries. A merge of the member branch therefore yields a
tree that differs from main, and the member is CHECK. `lifecycle` then makes it a superseded DECISION, and the only
deletion `--apply` offers for a DECISION row is `--abandon <branch>`. That flag requires a kb/Work note to name the
branch with the word ABANDONED. For work that landed, that record is false. The tool never reads the train manifests
(`<coord>/scratch/train*-manifest.json`), although they are the record of which branch heads a train carried.

## Harm

Process only. Every landed train leaves four to ten member worktrees that the tool will not remove. The land unit has to
remove them by hand (the lander-train brief's step 8 gives that job to the orchestrator), which is the unprotected path
the lifecycle exists to replace. Or the worktrees accumulate, and later landing checks then treat them as in-flight work
(PB2609's stale-worktree arm).

## Fix shape

- A branch whose head equals a member head recorded in a train manifest, where that train's landing commit is an
  ancestor of origin/main, is LANDED (`landed through train <label>`). Its worktree is still removed only when clean and
  idle. Prove the train landed by its head: record the train branch's final sha in the manifest at push-main, or match
  the DEVLOG entry's commit.
- A member whose head moved past its manifest head stays CHECK, because the commits after the manifest head are
  unlanded work.
- A `--self-test` arm: a member branch whose content the train changed (a fixed-in-train commit) is LANDED by its
  manifest, and a member with one commit beyond its manifest head is not.
