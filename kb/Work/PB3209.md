## Landing

Re-probed on the worktree's tree (base 8958152ce) before the change: `A1_ROW` matched 35 lines of docs/CONFORMANCE.md,
all in §2 (Annex A.3, lines 76-115), none in §7 (lines 2786-3104); for clause 12.4.5 `doc_citing` tagged §2 lines 99
and 110 as `a1-item` 26 and 40 (A.3 item numbers) and left all 16 §7 determinations untagged.

Fixed at the root: the register keeps ONE parser, `inventory_schema.register_section_at` (each data row with its
1-based line; `register_section` is its cell-only view, and lines are split on `\n` as the C# twin
`ConformanceRegister.cs` splits them), and `section7_rows_at` keys each §7 row `DOC-A.1-<n>` with its line
(`section7_rows` is its row-only view, unchanged for `audit_annex_a1.py`). `phase_b_batch.register_rows_by_line` reads it
and `doc_citing(lines, pat)` tags a citing line with its §7 row key; `A1_ROW` and phase_b's `CATALOG`/`INVENTORY`/
`CONFORMANCE_DOC` paths are deleted — `main` reads `inventory_schema.load_catalog()`/`load_inventory()` and the dossier
reads `inventory_schema.REGISTER_PATH`. After: the 16 §7 hits carry `DOC-A.1-151` … `DOC-A.1-164`, and §2 lines 99, 110,
111 and §8 line 3170 carry none. `phase_b_batch.py --self-test` (new; found by `scripts/self_tests.py`) plants a §2,
a §7 and a §8 row citing one clause and asserts only the §7 row is tagged — the old `A1_ROW` fails it twice (the §2 row
tagged, the §7 row not). `section7_rows` over the live document is identical before and after (206 rows).
Design §8.2 carries the rule ("a register document has one Python reader").

For the next implementer:
- `scripts/spec/inventory_schema.py#register_section_at` — THE register-table parser, with lines; `section7_rows_at` for §7. Never parse a CONFORMANCE.md table elsewhere.
- `scripts/spec/phase_b_batch.py#doc_citing` — takes the document's lines; `register_rows_by_line` caches the §7 line → key map. Trap: §2's first cell is a bare number too (an A.3 item), so a "numbered row" pattern is not an A.1 row.
