# PB5869 - the loop's stall watch reports a hand-dispatched group as stalled while its agent is committing

**Sites:** none (the site of the watch is not named by the finding: what the watch reads as progress is the first thing a fix decides).

Noticed on Monday 2026-11-16 by the Mythos lane: three notices in two hours, all false.

kind: defect. process_only: true. No harm flag. Severity: MINOR. area: tooling/orchestrator. related: PB5851, PB5847, PB5846.

## What happened
The Mythos lane dispatches fix groups by hand (an Agent call on a spec rendered by `make_dispatch_specs.py`, which records the group in the dispatch ledger so that the loop's planner leaves its notes alone). The loop's watch then sent the lane's mailbox a notice for three of them:
- 05:42, `STALL w20262075a (PB5851): no progress for 24 min on running (24 min since it began)`. At 05:42 its worktree had a checkpoint commit made at 05:40 and scratch logs written at 05:41.
- 06:22, `STALL w20262076a (PB5847): no progress for 27 min on running (27 min since it began)`. Its worktree had a checkpoint commit made at 06:18.
- 07:28, `STALL w20262079a (PB5846, PB5859, PB5850, PB5848): no progress for 24 min on running (24 min since it began)`. Its worktree was six commits ahead of main, the last at 07:25.
Each notice says the time without progress equals the time since the group began: the watch saw no progress at all for a group whose agent was committing.

## Why it matters
The lane's standing rule is that a stall notice is an alarm and is verified each time. Each false notice cost a check of the worktree, the scratch directory and the agent's transcript. A watch that is always wrong for one kind of group will be ignored for it, and a real stall of a hand group would then go unseen.

## What a fix must decide (not decided here)
- What the watch reads as progress (a milestones file a loop wave's agents write, the wave's own state) and why a hand group, which is in the ledger and has a worktree with commits, shows none.
- Whether a ledger group dispatched outside the loop's wave is the watch's to watch at all; if it is, it reads the branch's commits; if it is not, it sends nothing for it.
