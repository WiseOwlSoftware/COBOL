# PB1663 — LANDED

Filed 2026-09-27 by the train-67 lander from the wave-67 P implementer report (`w67p-PB1168-report.md`, new
defect 1). Rejects legal source.

**Measured on the train-67 build** (repro `scratchpad\w67p\probe\pi.cob`, re-run by the lander):
```
pi.cob(7,12): error COBOLNET1757: INSPECT operand 'X"41"' is a numeric literal; each INSPECT literal shall be an
alphanumeric, boolean, or national literal (ISO §14.9.22.3 SR3)
```

**Rule.** §14.9.22.3 3): "Each literal shall be an alphanumeric, boolean, or national literal" (`cite.py
--check` OK). A hexadecimal-alphanumeric literal is format 2 of §8.3.3.2.2's general format, and §8.3.3.2.1:
"Alphanumeric literals are of the class and category alphanumeric" (`cite.py --check` OK).

**Code site.** `src/Cobol.Net.Compiler/Binding/Procedure/Verbs/InspectBinder.cs:372` — after the NATLIT / BOOLLIT
arms, every other literal token reaches the "is a numeric literal" error, so the hex alphanumeric token (and, to
check in the sweep, the hex national `NX"…"` and the null-terminated `Z"…"` forms) is refused.

**What the fixer owes (rule 4).** Classify the literal by the ONE literal-category reader the other verbs use
rather than a token list in this binder; golden with X"41" in each INSPECT phrase (TALLYING, REPLACING, CONVERTING,
BEFORE/AFTER); sweep other binders that enumerate literal tokens.

## Landed

INSPECT over a hexadecimal alphanumeric literal; witness `conformance:2023/pb1663_inspect_hexadecimal_alphanumeric_literal`.
