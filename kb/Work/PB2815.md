# PB2815 — the fix-lane plan ignores waves another dispatcher has in flight

Severity MAJOR: a duplicate wave spends 2-3 weekly points and lands a conflict; on 2026-10-08 it was prevented only by a
manual steer repeated at every loop choice.

## Found by

The Mythos session planning fix-lane wave 1045 on account 1 at 15:06 PDT 2026-10-08 while the orchestrator loop ran on
account 2 (owner 15:05: both sessions burn their remaining quota; the operator answered GO at 16:47 and steered the loop
to the campaign lane at every choice "until 1045 lands" to keep its own planner off 1045's notes).

## Evidence

`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` builds the fix-lane candidate set from `fix_clusters.py`'s open notes minus `taken`
(notes on UNLANDED branches that have a w<wave><letter>-PB<lead>-report.md: SPLIT becomes a finisher, DONE is held for the
train) and from the open ids. `inflight_file_sets()` (worktrees, unlanded branches, the dispatch ledger's non-terminal
groups, the open external-repository slices) is computed ONLY under `--cluster` (`busy = None` otherwise, main(): the block
`if a.cluster:` near line 888; `file_set_collisions` is applied in the group loop near line 635 only when `busy` is
truthy). A wave recorded in the ledger whose implementers have written no report yet is therefore re-planned by the next
fix-lane plan. The dry run of 15:06 listed wave 1045's own candidates again with nothing excluded; the dispatch ledger
held 1045 A-H from 15:06.

## Sites

`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py` main() (`busy` only under `--cluster`), `plan()` (the `taken` set and the `busy`
check), `inflight_file_sets()`, `record_dispatch()`; `scripts/orchestrator/next_unit.py` (the loop's wave choice has no
"another dispatcher is in flight" input); `steer.ps1` (the manual workaround used today).

## Fix shape

CLAUDE.md rule 5, the shape that makes the next case automatic: the fix-lane path excludes every note of a non-terminal,
non-expired dispatch-ledger group (the same filter `inflight_file_sets` already applies to the ledger: terminal notes and
expired `hand` entries drop out), and the plan prints those notes as "dispatched by w<wave><letter>, in flight" in its
waiting list, so a second dispatcher's plan over the same register is disjoint by construction and no steer is needed.
Related: PB2427 and PB2348 (wave numbers also have no shared allocator: the Mythos session had to pass `--wave 1045` by
hand), PB2614 (over-broad ledger entries would then over-exclude; fix both), PB2699 (the loop plans from a stale register),
PB2424 (the campaign path's own in-flight check).

## Test

`scripts/orchestrator/test_plan_wave.py` pins it: with a ledger group {wave 9001, notes [X]} whose note X is open and has
no report, a fix-lane plan over a register containing X plans no group holding X and lists X as in flight; after X's note
turns `landed` the entry drops and X is plannable again.
