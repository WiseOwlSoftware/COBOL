# PB1233 — OPEN

## Measured

**constant-record-clause** — CONSTANT RECORD content is produced by the ordinary initial-state machinery (VALUE clauses over the implementor default / OPTIONS INITIALIZE fill background) instead of by the §13.18.15.4 GR1 INITIALIZE ... WITH FILLER ALL TO VALUE THEN TO DEFAULT semantics; DataItem.IsConstantRecord's remark asserts the two coincide, and they do not for VALUE-less numeric-edited items, under an OPTIONS INITIALIZE fill, or under a group-level VALUE.

- **Repro:** WORKING-STORAGE: 01 CR CONSTANT RECORD. 05 A1 PIC X(3). 05 FILLER PIC X(2). 05 E1 PIC ZZ9.99. 05 V1 PIC X(2) VALUE "OK". ... DISPLAY "[" E1 "]" -> observed [      ]. Add `OPTIONS. INITIALIZE WORKING-STORAGE SECTION TO X"5A".` and DISPLAY CR -> observed Z-filled A1/FILLER/E1. Group variant: 05 GV VALUE "WXYZ". 10 GA PIC XX. 10 GB PIC XX. -> observed WXYZ. Probes: E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d1\out13\probe\constant-record-clause\gr1a.cob, gr1b.cob, gr1c.cob (pinned cobol.exe --run, --std 2023 and 2014).
- **Expected (spec):** §13.18.15.4 GR1 + §14.9.20.4 GR5c3/GR6c: every elementary item (FILLER included) with no data-item/table VALUE clause of its own receives its category default by MOVE: numeric-edited ZZ9.99 <- ZEROES = "  0.00"; alphanumeric <- SPACES regardless of any OPTIONS INITIALIZE fill (the INITIALIZE overwrites the background); GA/GB under a group VALUE receive SPACES.
- **Observed:** gr1a: E1=[      ] (spaces). gr1b: [ZZZZZZZZOK] (fill X"5A" in A1, FILLER, E1). gr1c: G2=[WXYZ]. InitialStateBackground.FillFor explicitly lets CONSTANT RECORD roots take the fill (the §11.9.10.4 GR7 exception is about external items being initialized at all, not about the DEFAULT content), and PicInfo.DefaultInitializer gives numeric-edited items spaces - legitimate for an ordinary VALUE-less item (§13.18.63.4 GR4 undefined) but not for a structured constant, whose content GR1 defines.
