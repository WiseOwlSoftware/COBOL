# PB1006 — OPEN

## Repro — measured
`scratchpad/w49f/probe/p5_srcif.cob`: `>>SOURCE FIXED`, `>>DEFINE V AS 1`, `>>IF V = 2`, `>>SOURCE FREE`, `>>END-IF`,
then a fixed-form sequence-numbered line → `p5_srcif.cob(9,12): error COBOL0001: cannot parse construct near 'DISPLAY'`.

## The rule
`OK  §7.3.16.4 2)` — "If constant-conditional-expression-1 evaluates to TRUE, all lines of text-1 are included in the
resultant text and all lines of text-2 are omitted from the resultant text." (GR3 is the FALSE mirror.) An omitted line
is not in the resultant text, so its >>SOURCE directive has no effect.

## What the fixer owes
Evaluate conditional compilation and the reference-format switch in ONE pass in source order (the format directive is
itself subject to the >>IF state), or make the normalizer consult the >>IF state it would otherwise precede. Golden for
>>SOURCE and for >>PUSH/>>POP SOURCE in an omitted branch, both forms.
