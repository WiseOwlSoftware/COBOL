# PB5865 - the code-read store refuses a truthful DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT read and a DOUBTFUL with no corrected verdict

**Sites:**
- `scripts/spec/code_reads.py#reader_problems` (requires `harm`, `repro`, `expected`, `observed` and `cause` of a DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT read)
- `scripts/spec/code_reads.py#refuter_problems` (requires `corrected` of a DOUBTFUL case)
- `scripts/spec/code_read_store.py#outcome` (counts every reader's verdict other than CONFORMS as a contradiction of the shared verdict)

Noticed on Monday 2026-11-16 by the Mythos lane, when two slices were refused by the importer for the second time.

kind: defect. process_only: true. No harm flag. Severity: MAJOR is the filer's to weigh: twelve rows of 1985 cannot
enter the store, and every row whose shared verdict is DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT meets the same refusal.
area: tooling/inventory. related: PB5759, PB5842, PB5854, PB5844.

## What happened
Batch 10's slices 1985-196, 1985-203 and 1985-210 were refused by `scripts/spec/code_reads.py import`
(`my-20261115/registrar-code-reads-2/refused-slices-b10.md`). Their rows were read again by other agents as slices
1985-221, 1985-222 and 1985-223, and 221 and 223 were refused again, for the same reasons
(`my-20261115/registrar-code-reads-2/refused-slices-b13.md`):
- a reader's record whose verdict is DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT (the RECORD DELIMITER rows `1985-GR-VII.2.8.4-2`, `-3`,
  `-5`, `1985-SR-VII.2.8.3-1`, `-2`) with empty `harm`, `repro`, `expected`, `observed` and `cause`: "`harm` must be
  a non-empty string", "a DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT read names its `cause`";
- a refuter's case whose refutation is DOUBTFUL with an empty `corrected` (`1985-GR-VII.2.8.4-1`): "a DOUBTFUL says
  the right verdict: `corrected` must be a non-empty string", which then leaves "a CONFORMS read with no refuter's
  case".
Two independent readers wrote the same shape, and the reads are sound in substance: each names the requirement,
the code, the probes it ran and the citations. Twelve rows (the six of each slice) are therefore not in the store,
among them two new findings of the re-read (kb/Work PB5860 and PB5861) whose rulings cannot be recorded.

## Why the importer is wrong, not the readers
- `harm`, `repro`, `expected`, `observed` and `cause` describe a DEFECT. A DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT read says the
  implementation declines the element and says so: there is no harm to state and nothing to reproduce. In the
  inventory that verdict closes a row.
- The store's outcome makes every reader's verdict other than CONFORMS a contradiction of the shared verdict
  (`code_read_store.py#outcome`). For a row whose shared verdict IS a documented decline (its 2023 rule is recorded
  DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT, as the 2023 rules of these rows are), a reader that finds the decline AGREES with the
  shared verdict. The earlier batches hid this: such rows were adjudicated CONFORMS by a ruling ("rejected"), which
  records the right outcome under the wrong word.
- A refuter that doubts and cannot say which verdict is right has said all it knows. Requiring a corrected verdict
  of an OVERTURNED is right; of a DOUBTFUL it forces an invented one.

## The shape decided (the Mythos lane; to be built by a tooling change of its own, reviewed)
1. THE READ'S SHAPE: the defect fields are required of a reader's verdict that does not resolve (the inventory
   schema says which verdicts resolve); a DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT read carries none of them. A DOUBTFUL case needs
   its reason and no `corrected`.
2. THE OUTCOME, one function as today: a record is `confirmed` when its reader's verdict is the verdict the row
   SHARES (the verdict of its chain at the next held edition, the one function the inventory already has for a
   kept row) and, for CONFORMS, the refuter upheld it; a DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT read of a row whose chain conforms
   is `contradicted` as today and names its `work`. A row whose chain is mixed is decided by what that one
   function answers, and where it answers nothing the record is `undecided`.
3. The records that were adjudicated CONFORMS only to say "the documented decline stands" keep their
   adjudication: it is true and the outcome is the same.
4. A drift test over planted records: a decline read of a declined row (confirmed), of a conforming row
   (contradicted, needs `work`), a DOUBTFUL with no `corrected` (undecided), an OVERTURNED with none (refused).
Then slices 1985-221 and 1985-223 import as they are, and the rulings on their rows are recorded.
