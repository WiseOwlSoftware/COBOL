# PB4276 — the required-clause drift test misses an unbracketed clause behind a bracketed optional-word group

**Sites:** `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/FileControlKeyRuleDriftTests.cs:94` (`FileControlKeyRuleDriftTests.EveryFormat_PrintsExactlyTheRequiredClausesOfTheTableUnbracketed`; the line scan at about :380 on the PB4236 branch).

## What happens

PB4236's drift test re-reads the four §12.4.5.1 diagrams and takes every line that OPENS with a letter as an unbracketed clause. A line that opens with a bracketed optional-word group is not seen: Format 1 prints `[ ORGANIZATION IS ] INDEXED` and Format 2 prints `[ ORGANIZATION IS ] RELATIVE`, both required members, written unbracketed apart from the optional words. The test's claim (the next unbracketed clause a later edition prints becomes a red automatically) has that hole; a required clause printed in that shape would be added to the standard with no red.

## Fix

Strip a leading bracketed optional-word group before the opens-with-a-letter test (a structural reading of the printed line, not a list of the two known lines), and pin it with a case whose source line opens with a bracketed group. Sweep the other drift tests that scrape a diagram line by its first character.
