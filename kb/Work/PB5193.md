# PB5193 - at --std 85 SEARCH ALL refuses WHEN K (IX + 1) = 2 and a condition-name subscripted by another index or with a relative subscript (COBOL1965): CheckKeySubscript adds the 2023 rules at every edition

**Sites:**

- `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs` (`CheckKeySubscript`, `CheckConditionNameSide`)

**Mechanism.** §1985:VI.6.22.3 SR4 asks only that each key data-name be subscripted by the first index-name associated with identifier-1, along with other subscripts as required; it puts no rule on a relative subscript or on a condition-name's subscript. `SearchAllFormat2Rules.CheckKeySubscript` adds the 2023 sentences (the index-name subscript not followed by + or -, a condition-name subscripted by the first index-name) at every edition, so WHEN K (IX + 1) = 2, WHEN C1 (IX - 1) and WHEN C1 (JX) are refused at --std 85.

Verdict, harm, evidence, repro and observed text are the records'; the rows' verdicts are recorded on another branch.

**Inventory.** `work.py check` refuses a 1985 rule id in `inventory_rows` (kb/Work PB5094, open), so `inventory_rows` is empty and the rows this note claims are the rule ids of the list below.

Checked: §1985:VI.6.22.3 SR4: "Each data-name-1, data-name-2 must be subscripted by the first index-name associated with identifier-1 along with other subscripts as required". Code sites read: src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs#CheckKeySubscript; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs#CheckKeySide; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs#CheckConditionNameSide; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs#CheckSendingSide; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs#CheckSendingIdentifier; src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/Validation/SearchAllFormat2Rules.cs#CheckPrecedingKeys. Code read: No kb/Work note owns the fix. Every requirement §1985:VI.6.22.3 SR4 states is enforced at 85 by SearchAllFormat2Rules (no edition arm), each probed: a condition-name with two values or a THRU range, a condition-name on a non-key item, a key operand not in the KEY IS phrase (the witness) or subscripted by a second index-name, a literal or a data-name, a sending identifier (alone or in an arithmetic expression) that is a key or is subscripted by the first index-name, and a key referenced without its preceding key are refused (COBOL1965, COBOL1966); a qualified key, a key in an inner table with its outer subscript and two keys joined by AND are accepted. The divergence is what CheckKeySubscript adds from §2023:14.9.37.3 SR8 and SR9 at every edition: "The index-name subscript shall not be followed by a '+' or a '-'" and the requirement that a condition-name be subscripted by the first index-name. The 1985 rule says only "Each data-name-1, data-name-2 must be subscripted by the first index-name associated with identifier-1 along with other subscripts as required" and puts no subscript rule on a condition-name, so at --std 85 WHEN K (IX + 1) = 2, WHEN C1 (IX - 1) and WHEN C1 (JX) are refused with COBOL1965 where the 1985 text has no refusal (the row's change record reads the two 2023 sentences as added refusals). Caveat on the reading: the 1985 words do not say whether a relative subscript (index-name + integer) is "subscripted by the first index-name", and the 2002 standard, which adds the sentence, does not list it among its changes; kb/Work PB445 (landed) wrote the screen for every edition on the statement that the rules are unchanged at 85.
