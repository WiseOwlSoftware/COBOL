# PB4569 - native `2 ** 130` keeps 16 significant digits

**Lead, not investigated (search the register before acting).** Under native arithmetic `ABS (2 ** 130)` shows `1361129467683754000000000000000000000000`.
STANDARD-DECIMAL gives `1361129467683753853853498429727073000000`. The exact value is `1361129467683753853853498429727072845824`.
GnuCOBOL prints 0, so it is no guide for the value; the spec's native latitude (§8.8.1.3, §15.4.1) and the register decide whether 16 digits is the documented binary64
carrier or a defect.

To determine: where the native power's 16-digit carrier is documented (DOC-A.1-92 family), and whether the exact integer power, which fits 40 digits, is owed.
