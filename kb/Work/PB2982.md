# PB2982 - RETIRED (wave 1059 group A): the premise is false

## Retirement (wave 1059 group A, 2026-10-11)

**Re-probe on a fresh build** (`cobol.exe --std 85` and `--std 2023`, scratch `w1059a/a_tab_led.cob`, `b_tab_after_hyphen.cob`,
`c_national_fixed.cob`, `d_surr_seq.cob`):

- The note's repro, `<TAB>-    "BBB".` after `DISPLAY "AAA`, is NOT a continuation line. DOC-A.1-157 expands a tab to the
  next stop (positions 1, 9, 17, ...), so the tab fills positions 1-8, position 7 (the indicator area) is a SPACE and the
  hyphen stands in position 9 of the program-text area. Neither build raises any continuation gate; the only diagnostics
  are the parser's own (`a_tab_led.cob(4,20)` and `(5,7): unexpected ...`, the line being program text).
- A tab can never PRECEDE an indicator that is not a space: a tab written at positions 1-7 fills through position 8, and a
  tab written after the indicator (`b_tab_after_hyphen.cob`, hyphen, tab, literal) leaves the indicator at position 7 -
  the gates name `(5,7)`, which is its physical column.
- So every site the note lists (`ReferenceFormatDiagnostics.cs` lines 48-173 and `LogicalLineBuilder.cs:187`) passes
  `FixedFormLine.IndicatorColumn` (0-based 6), and on every line those gates fire on, that IS the physical column of the
  indicator, in the one unit the diagnostics now share (the character position, PB2983). ISO 6.3.1: "The indicator area is the 7th
  character position of a line" (`cite.py --check 6.3.1 "The indicator area is the 7th character position of a line"` -> OK).
- The one real variable was the unit: six supplementary-plane characters in the sequence area (`d_surr_seq.cob`) put the
  indicator at UTF-16 index 12. The gates name `(5,7)`, the character position - consistent with the rest after PB2983.

**Why nothing was changed in the gates:** deriving a column from the `TextSite` would return the constant. The determination
is written where the next reader looks, `FixedFormLine.IndicatorColumn`, and pinned by
`DiagnosticPositionTests.FixedForm_IndicatorGates_NameColumn7_WhateverTheSequenceAreaHolds` (a supplementary-plane
sequence area keeps `(8,7)` for COBOL2685 and COBOL0903; a TAB-led hyphen raises no continuation gate).

LEARNED: `Preprocessor/FixedFormLine.cs#IndicatorColumn` is the physical column of every indicator a gate can name (a tab in
positions 1-7 fills through 8, so a non-space indicator is never tab-shifted; positions are characters); do not derive it from
a `TextSite`.

# PB2982 — indicator-column gates ignore a TAB in the sequence area

Filed 2026-10-10 by the train 1055 lander from the wave 1055 B report
(`E:\COBOL-coord\scratch\reports\w1055b-PB1688-report.md`, lead 1; first seen as w1053c lead 1). Same family as
PB1688 / PB1801 (train 1055: a diagnostic's column is the SOURCE column, mapped through the logical line), a site
those notes did not reach.

**Repro:** a fixed-form line whose sequence area is a TAB, then the indicator: `<TAB>-  "X".` (a continuation
line written after a TAB). The line's indicator is at the TAB-expanded position, but every indicator-column
diagnostic reports column 7, the EXPANDED column, never the source column the indicator character sits in.

**Sites:** `src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatDiagnostics.cs` lines 48, 72, 80, 93, 150, 161,
173 (each passes `FixedFormLine.IndicatorColumn` as the column) and
`src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.LogicalLineBuilder.cs:187`
(`gates?.OnTwoContinuationForms(file, site.Line, FixedFormLine.IndicatorColumn)`).

**Fix shape:** the gates take the `TextSite` (whose `Col(text, i)` already maps a TAB-expanded index back to the
physical column, `ReferenceFormatProcessor.LogicalLineBuilder.cs:371`) instead of the constant, as PB1801 did for
the other reference-format diagnostics; a negative golden per gate with a TAB-led line pins the column.
