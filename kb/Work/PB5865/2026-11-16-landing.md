# PB5865 - landing (2026-11-16)

The importer's shape checks and the one outcome function are changed as decided in the note. `code_reads.reader_problems`
requires the defect fields of a verdict that does not resolve (`Schema.resolving`), so a DOCUMENTED-NON-SUPPORT read carries
none; `refuter_problems` requires `corrected` of an OVERTURNED only. `code_read_store.outcome` takes the verdict each row
shares (`lineage.chain_verdict`, computed by `code_read_store.shared_verdicts` and carried by `Context.shared`): a resolving
non-CONFORMS read is confirmed by the verdict its row shares, contradicted by another, undecided where the chain shares none;
every other verdict contradicts as before. The store still reads the inventory's rows and never kb/Work.

Proof: `scripts/selftests/spec/code_reads/test_code_reads.py` case 18 over planted records (a decline read of a declined row
confirmed, of a conforming row contradicted and owed its `work`, a DOUBTFUL with no `corrected` undecided, an OVERTURNED with
none refused); against the old shape four of its expectations failed (the decline read's defect fields, the DOUBTFUL's
`corrected`, the decline read of a declined row, the row sharing nothing). No stored record changes outcome (1,334 records,
before and after identical), because the earlier decline rows were adjudicated CONFORMS. Design: section 10.6.1 of
`docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-spec-conformance-review.md`.
