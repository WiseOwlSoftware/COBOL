# PB4641 - lead from a landed branch's report

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/CodeGen/Emit/NumericRenderer.cs` (`NumericRenderer.DecBinary`)

Unprobed lead from PB4599's report; no repro. Whether ISO requires a single rounding here is not decided by this clerk (kind analysis); needs a probe at an exact 35th-digit tie first.

**Audit 2026-11-07** (the operator's audit of the 37 open analyses that set no harm flag (kb/Work PB4949; four read-only adjudicators, evidence in E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/analyses-audit/)). HIDDEN DEFECT, wrong answer, probed: `9.999...9E-1 / 2.0E0` gives R = 1 under ROUNDED, and a truncating receiver gets .5000... where GnuCOBOL gives 0 and .4999.... The DecBinary "/" path (NumericRenderer.cs:802-806) rounds twice, against DOC-A.1-123's single rounding.

**Landed (wave 20261921, group D).** Re-probed on the build (`E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/w20261921d/probe.cob`): `COMPUTE R ROUNDED = 9.999999999999999999999999999999999E-1 / 2.0E0` stored 1, and a truncating `PIC V9(30)` receiver stored .500000000000000000000000000000, where the exact quotient .49999...95 lies below .5. After the fix: 0 and .499999999999999999999999999999. Cause: the native lane's final-transfer quotient of two SDIDI values (`DecBinary` "/") called `CobolDec.Div` at the program's INTERMEDIATE ROUNDING mode, a 34-digit rounding the receiver rounded again. Fix: `CobolDec.DivFinal` (new) reduces the quotient by round-to-odd, PROHIBITED excepted (it screens, as before), emitted by `RuntimeApi.DecDivFinal`; the STANDARD-DECIMAL lane keeps `Div` (its quotient is an SDIDI value, 2023:8.8.1.5). The sibling arms (`ExactFinalQuotient`, `Divide`) already round once.
