# PB4348 - The package-cache job saves ~/.nuget/packages under the exact key even when the continue-on-error dotnet restore before it failed, so a partial package set becomes main's exact-key entry

**Sites:** `.github/workflows/build-and-test.yml` (job `package-cache`: the step "Restore the solution's packages (dotnet restore)", which carries `continue-on-error: true`, and the step "Save the NuGet packages under main", whose `if` tests only `steps.warm.outcome` and `steps.nuget.outputs.cache-hit`, around lines 1092-1104; as landed, the steps `restore` and `fetch` and the two saves after them), `tests/WiseOwl.Cobol.Tests.Unit/CiBoundsDriftTests.cs` (`UntestedProducerOffenders`, `NoCacheSavesWhatAFailedStepLeftBehind`, `TheAudits_FireOnEveryShapeTheyForbid`), `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-test-build-ci.md` (§1.4, the bounds paragraph).

Found by the branch review of PB4324 (w20261792b-review-r1, lead R1-1, low). The save step does not test the outcome of the restore step before it. When `dotnet restore WiseOwl.Cobol.sln` fails partway (a feed outage, a timeout), `continue-on-error` lets the job go on, and `actions/cache/save` stores whatever `~/.nuget/packages` holds under the exact key `${{ runner.os }}-nuget-<hash of csproj and Directory.Packages.props>`. Every later restore then hits that key exactly, finds a partial package set, and nothing re-saves it until a csproj or `Directory.Packages.props` changes, because the save step runs only when `cache-hit != 'true'`. Each consumer falls back to restoring the missing packages from the feed, so the damage is slower jobs and renewed exposure to feed outages, not a wrong result.

Fix at the root: give the restore step an `id` and gate the save on `steps.<id>.outcome == 'success'`, so a failed restore saves nothing. Add an arm to `CiBoundsDriftTests` (or its sibling) that fails when a cache-save step follows a `continue-on-error` step in the same job without testing that step's outcome, then sweep the `toolchain-cache` job and `.github/actions/setup-toolchain/action.yml` for the same shape.

**Landed (kb/Work PB4348, wave 20261805).** The two producers in `package-cache` that continue on error, `dotnet restore` and the GnuCOBOL fetch, gained the ids `restore` and `fetch`, and their saves run only `if: steps.<id>.outcome == 'success'` (a producer skipped on a cache hit or a failed toolchain warm-up is not `success`, so the old conditions are implied). `CiBoundsDriftTests.NoCacheSavesWhatAFailedStepLeftBehind` reads every workflow job and composite action: for each `actions/cache/save@` step the producer is the nearest earlier step that is not a cache step, and when that step continues on error the save must test its outcome. A rule over every earlier `continue-on-error` step would make the tarball's save depend on `dotnet restore`, so the audit names the producer. Run against the old workflow it fired on both saves of `package-cache`. The sibling sweep (`git grep -n 'actions/cache/save@' -- .github`: three saves) cleared the third, `setup-toolchain`'s, whose producer `install` does not continue on error, so a failed install ends the composite before its save.
