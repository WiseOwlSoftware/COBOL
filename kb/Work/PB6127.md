PB2652 shipped `--suppress` (a named warning is muted, owner decision D20) and recorded in `kb/Work/PB2652/2026-11-22-suppress-shipped-and-decisions.md`
(Decision 2) that §2023:4.2.2 also requires "a suboption for selection or suppression of checking for violations of the set of conformance
rules" (both phrases checked with `cite.py --check` in that file), a GAP "filed as a lead in the wave report for its own note". The wave report

implement it to the design, add the traceability row if none exists. Decide scope in a decision note under rule 3.
