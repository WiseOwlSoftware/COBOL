# PB3559 — the EC-FLOW checking-off choice is not in CONFORMANCE.md

## Lead (R2-1, low, not blocking at round 2; verbatim from the PB3542 report)

conformance-suite/cases/14.9.36-ROLLBACK/pb3544_item8_rollback_under_recursive_element_unchecked.cob:26 The new golden attributes its checking-off expectation to 'docs/CONFORMANCE.md A.4.3: a forbidden statement with checking off is not executed', but the A.4.3 row says no such thing (its (d) covers only a forbidden ACTIVATION leaving the clause inactive). The determination lives only in docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-runtime-library.md:2053-2054. It is the implementor's choice under 14.6.13.1.3 item 8 ('the implementor defines whether or not execution will continue'), and CONFORMANCE.md documents item 8 only for EC-SIZE-TRUNCATION and EC-DATA-NOT-FINITE (DOC-A.1-70), not for EC-FLOW-COMMIT, EC-FLOW-ROLLBACK or EC-FLOW-APPLY-COMMIT. The same misattribution sits in the round-1 sibling pb3544_item8_rollback_in_sort_procedure_unchecked.cob:18.

The reviewer's scenario: a reader checks why WS-COUNT stays 0002 after item 8 under the recursive P3544RR with checking off,
opens docs/CONFORMANCE.md A.4.3 as the golden directs, and finds no determination there (`grep 'forbidden statement'
docs/CONFORMANCE.md` finds 0 hits). The expected output itself is consistent with the design doc and with §14.9.36.4 GR2 /
§9.1.18.7, so no program result is wrong.

## Fix shape

- `docs/CONFORMANCE.md` gains the DOC-A.1 row for the choice (it is a required documentation item under Annex A.1 for
  implementor-defined behaviour), covering EC-FLOW-COMMIT, EC-FLOW-ROLLBACK and EC-FLOW-APPLY-COMMIT with checking off.
- The two goldens' comments (`pb3544_item8_rollback_under_recursive_element_unchecked.cob:26` and
  `pb3544_item8_rollback_in_sort_procedure_unchecked.cob:18`) point at that row.
- `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-runtime-library.md` (lines 2053 to 2054) cross-references the row.
