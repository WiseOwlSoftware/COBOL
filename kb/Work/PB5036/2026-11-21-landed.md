# PB5036 - landed (train 1985 b, group t, 2026-11-21)

which no pair of held editions spans: its citations must name at least one held edition's rule, and no rule they name may
hold a word of the construct (`Gate.words`, the capitals of its `display`). A cited rule holding the word, a citation of no
held rule, and a display naming no word are each refused. `repository_inputs` joins each view row with its catalog text.

(unproven); at the head it prints `✓ constructs/remarks-removed-85 [1985, 1985) in no edition: 1985-FMT-VI.3.2.1.1 hold no
REMARKS`, and `--check` is `=== EDITION GATES: GREEN ===`. The self-test plants the four arms (proven, a cited rule holding
the word, no held rule cited, no word) and `audit` routing an empty window to them: `=== EDITION GATES SELF-TEST: PASS ===`.
