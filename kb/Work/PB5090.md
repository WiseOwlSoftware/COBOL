# PB5090 - three refusals that the 1985 rules require are reported at the enclosing entry or section header instead of the clause or statement that breaks the rule (CD duplicate names, SUM UPON a non-detail group, a file in two USE statements), and COBOL6169 spells a 2002 citation inline

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/CommunicationSectionBinder.cs#ReadClauses` (the `names.Values.GroupBy` loop reports under the CD entry's position and spells the 2002 citation inline; descriptor `DiagnosticCatalog.CommunicationEntryRule`, COBOL6169), `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Compiler/Binding/ReportSectionBinder.cs#ResolveUponDetail` (reports under the report's position, where `ResolveSumAddend` narrows to the addend). The USE duplicate (COBOL0897) site is not located (the records name none).

**Mechanism.** The three programs ARE refused, so these are not under-rejects, but the message points at the wrong construct: COBOL6169 is positioned at the CD entry's first line, not at the repeated data-name clause; COBOL2046 for SUM ... UPON a non-detail group is positioned at the RD entry (line 14) rather than at the SUM clause (line 21), though the sibling diagnostic for a SUM addend is positioned at the operand; COBOL0897 is positioned at the section header rather than the USE statement naming the file a second time. The 1985 text gives a diagnostic no line, so the mismatch is against the conformance suite's site rule; the user-visible harm is a diagnostic that points away from the fault. These messages also cite a 2002 or 2023 rule at --std 85 (kb/Work/PB4950).

**Refute pass (2026-11-09; Mythos 5.2 cobol-refuters, `E:/COBOL-coord/scratch/refute-85/out/` slices 06, 08, 09, 10).** All
five verdicts stand as misleads-diagnosis, but each refuter upheld only the citation limb (a 2002 or 2023 rule cited at
`--std 85`, kb/Work PB4950's population): the 1985 text gives a diagnostic no line, so the position limb is not a 1985
conformance defect. It stands as a diagnostic-quality defect against the conformance suite's site rule
(docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-conformance-suite.md section 3.2) and the compiler's own sibling diagnostics (the SUM addend's
refusal is positioned at the operand). Fixing this note fixes the positions; the citations are fixed by PB4950.

**Found by.** The 1985 `--std=85` draft triage (kb/Work PB4995 item 3): each rule below is a conformance-suite draft that did not MATCH at `--std 85`, and its adjudicator decided COMPILER-DEFECT. Harm and observed text are the records'; nothing here was re-derived.

- `1985-SR-XIV.2.2.3-10` - `§1985:XIV.2.2.3 SR10` (record harm: misleads-diagnosis, slice-11): §1985:XIV.2.2.3 SR10: "Data-name-1, data-name-2, data-name-3, data-name-4, and data-name-5 must be unique within a CD entry." - `1985-SR-XIV.2.2.3-15` - `§1985:XIV.2.2.3 SR15` (record harm: misleads-diagnosis, slice-11): §1985:XIV.2.2.3 SR15: "Data-name-1, data-name-2, data-name-3, data-name-4, data-name-5, and data-name-6 must be unique within the CD entry. - `1985-SR-XIII.3.19.3-2` - `§1985:XIII.3.19.3 SR2` (record harm: misleads-diagnosis, slice-14): §1985:XIII.3.19.3 SR2: "Data-name-1 must be the name of a detail report group described in the same report as the control footing report group in which the SUM clause appears." The draft reads the rule correctly (its UPON operand names the control footing report group itself), but it carries an unrelated error that refused its control: it names that group CF, and CF is in the list of §1985:IV.8 ("| CF | DESTINATION | FINAL | LINES |"); §1985:IV.4.2.2.1.3: "A reserved word is a COBOL word that is one of a specified list of words which may be used in COBOL source programs, but which must not appear in the programs as user-defined words or system-names." The corrected draft renames the group CFG and changes nothing else. Its control then compiles and runs, and the compiler refuses the test, as SR2 requires; the corrected draft still does not match, because the diagnostic is reported at the RD entry (line 14), not at the entry holding the SUM clause (line 21) nor at the group the operand names (line 19). The 1985 text gives a diagnostic no line; the mismatch is against the suite's site (docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-conformance-suite.md section 3.2: the site is 'the whole statement or entry, both places for a rule that relates two'), and the compiler's own sibling diagnostic for a SUM addend is positioned at the operand.
- `1985-SR-VIII.4.8.3-3` - `§1985:VIII.4.8.3 SR3` (record harm: misleads-diagnosis, slice-08): §1985:VIII.4.8.3 SR3 (as amended by X3.23b-1993): "The same file-name must not appear in more than one USE AFTER EXCEPTION statement within the same Procedure Division." RFILE1 appears in the USE statement of line 19 and again in the USE statement of line 23; line 23 is the violating statement. The draft quotes the pre-amendment wording as its basis, but its program and marked line test the amended rule correctly. The compiler does refuse the program; the defect is in the diagnostic.
