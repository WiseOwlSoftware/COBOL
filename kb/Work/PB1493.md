## Finding — `floating-indicators` (under-rejects; rows `SR-6.2.3.2-2`, `SR-6.2.3.2-3`)

**Mechanism.** The floating comment indicator is recognized wherever the two characters '*>' occur outside a literal - the lexer's COMMENT_START and the fixed-form StripInlineComment have no separator-space look-behind, and the col-7 continuation join runs BEFORE comment recognition, so a '*>' that is not preceded by a separator space, or whose two characters sit on different lines, silently starts a comment.

**Repro.** free form: `MOVE "ABC" TO X*> comment` (probe E:\Temp\claude\E--CobolSharp\a700c052-f9e4-4942-84ff-a13c7505d6ec\scratchpad\adj-d2b\out\probe\floating-indicators\n09_inline_nospace) and `MOVE \"ABC\"*> comment` / `TO X.` (n09b); fixed form: line `           MOVE \"ABC\" TO X *` followed by col-7 continuation `      -    > comment here` (n12_split_comment_ind)

**Expected from the spec.** Each is diagnosed: §6.2.3.2 SR2 'The floating comment indicator of an inline comment shall be preceded by a separator space'; SR3 'All the characters forming a multiple-character floating indicator shall be specified on the same line'.

**Observed / reasoning.** All three compile with no error (n12 only gets the COBOLNET0903 obsolete-continuation warning) and run [ABC         ], the '*>' text discarded as a comment.

**Code site.** src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Grammar/Core/CobolLexer.g4 line 188 COMMENT_START; src/Cobol.Net.Frontend/Preprocessor/ReferenceFormatProcessor.cs#StripInlineComment and #HandleContinuation (non-literal join arm)

## What the fixer owes

Comments are removed by §6.5 rules 2 and 3 BEFORE any text analysis, so remove them in the logical-conversion pass (one place) and let neither the lexer's PICMODE nor CopyProcessor's REPLACE/COPY operand scanner see comment text: then a comment line between PIC and X(4), a `*>` inside REPLACE pseudo-text operands, and every other stage behave alike. The recogniser must require the separator space before `*>` (SR2), refuse the two characters split across lines (SR3; §6.3.5's multiple-character sentence) and carry an edition gate + constructs.json row for the floating comment indicator (probe n22_inline_85 compiles at --std 85). Goldens per rule; negatives for SR2/SR3 and the 85 gate.
