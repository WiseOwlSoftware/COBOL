neither, deliberately: *"Left to the owner of that doc rather than edited from inside another group's change

Line 192 sits inside the doc's `Emit/` subtree; line 263 spells `CodeGen/Emit/FigurativeConstants.cs` outright.

who follows the doc lands in a real directory, finds no such file, and concludes the service does not exist.

## Why it matters more than an out-of-date line

Train 43's cluster 2 landed **PB933 arm 1** and made `FigurativeConstants.Classify` the single reader of
§8.3.3.6.2's figurative vocabulary — `InitializeFigurativeKind` deleted, and a seventh private copy folded in
`ValueInitializer`. **`kb/Work/PB940`** (this pass) is the eighth copy, still standing. DEVLOG 1625 records that
`InitializeBinder` — a BINDER — now calls it.

So the compiler's rule is: *every phase asks this one service.* Its FILE says: *this belongs to the Roslyn
backend.* Its DOC says: *it is in the emitter's `Emit/` folder.* Three answers, and the two wrong ones are
precisely the ones a binder author consults before writing their own copy. Seven copies of one word list
accumulated behind that; the placement is not the cause of all seven, but it is the standing invitation to a
ninth. `feedback_one_rule_one_place`.

## The two halves, and they are one change set

1. **The doc** — three lines in `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-module-topology.md` (192, 263, 438). Pure
   documentation; CLAUDE.md rule 6 says it should have been current in the change set that moved the file.
2. **The placement** — `CodeGen/Roslyn/` is the BACKEND directory, and a classifier that binders call is not
   backend-private. Moving it is a one-file `git mv` plus namespace, but it is a DESIGN decision about the
   module topology and therefore belongs in `DESIGN-module-topology.md` with the move (rule 2), not in a
   drive-by. The natural homes are the shared `CodeGen/Emit/` the doc already claims, or a cross-phase location
   the doc names explicitly as such — the fixer picks and the doc records why.

Doing only (1) is also a legitimate outcome, if the owner of the topology doc decides `Roslyn/` is right: then
the doc says `CodeGen/Roslyn/FigurativeConstants.cs` and says WHY a cross-phase service lives in the backend
folder. What is not legitimate is the current state, where the doc and the tree disagree and neither explains
itself.

## The sibling sweep the fixer owes

`DESIGN-module-topology.md` is a MAP, and a map with one wrong road probably has others. Mechanically check every
