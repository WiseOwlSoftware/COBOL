# PB1919 — OPEN

Filed by the train-1004 lander from wave 1004 group B's report (`w1004b-PB1368-report.md`).

## Finding

**Site.** `src/Cobol.Net.Cli/Program.cs#RunCompile` — the three-line `foreach (var transfer in result.CompileOutput)` loop
that writes each transferred line to `Console.Out` or `Console.Error` (§7.3.12.4 GR1/GR5, DOC-A.1-53, kb/Work PB1538).
The carrier is pinned by `DisplayDirectiveTests.CompilerDriverResult_CarriesTheTransferredLines` and the stream choice
by unit tests, but nothing runs the CLI and reads its two streams, so a regression that drops or misroutes the write
would pass every gate.

**Fix shape.** One CLI-level test (the Characterization or Unit assembly's process-runner) compiling a `>>DISPLAY "X"
UPON SYSERR` / `>>DISPLAY "Y"` source and asserting `Y` on stdout and `X` on stderr.
