## Finisher (w69x, 2026-09-28)

The train-68b lander's review found the comment arm of `ForbiddenIn` DEAD: it asked whether a TEXT-WORD starts
with `*>`, and `TextWordScanner.TryNext` never yields one (a comment is skipped as a single space). So
`REPLACE LEADING ==Q== BY ==*==` over `Q>1` produced `*>1` silently and the rest of the line vanished (probe
`scratchpad\w69x\probe\gr9c.cob` printed `A`; the COPY twin `gr9d.cob` the same). Fixed at the root: the question
is asked of the produced TEXT - `TextWordScanner.HoldsComment` scans the gaps between text-words with ONE
`CommentStartsAt` that `TryNext` also uses, so a `*>` inside a produced literal is still literal content.
Both statements share it (§7.2.4.4 GR9 / §7.2.3.4 GR13, `cite.py --check` OK); COBOLNET2574. Witnesses:
`conformance:negative/pb1356-replace-produces-comment`, `unit:TextManipulationStatementTests.ReplaceResult_ShallNotHoldAComment_ButALiteralMayHoldItsCharacters`,
`unit:TextManipulationStatementTests.CopyResult_ShallNotHoldAComment` (batch `scratchpad\w69x\batch-w69x-finisher.json`).
