# PB809

**Found by:** the kb/Work PB245 golden round, 2026-09-09, while measuring which lane conforming source reaches
for `FUNCTION YEAR-TO-YYYY`.

## The claim, and why it is false now

`tests/conformance/2002/pb119_windowing_float_args.cob` states its own mechanism in its header: *"The float
ARGUMENTS force the Real lane."* Every one of its probes writes a literal — `FUNCTION YEAR-TO-YYYY(50.0 60
3000)`, `FUNCTION DATE-TO-YYYYMMDD(590401.0)` and so on — and a literal cannot reach that lane at HEAD.

`IntrinsicRenderer.RenderFloat` is entered only through `AnyRealArgument(ic)`, which is
`ic.Args.Any(a => Landed(num.AsNum(a, num.Receiver)).Real)`. `NumericRenderer.LiteralNum` has exactly two arms
and NEITHER produces `Real: true` — a floating-point-form literal becomes a `Dec` (SDIDI) operand and any other
numeric literal becomes `EmitText.UnscaledLit`'s scaled integer. `NumericRenderer.Landed` only ever *de*-Reals
(`if (x.Real && StandardDecimal) …`); it never sets the flag. So an argument run made entirely of literals is
never Real, and the golden runs on the EXACT lane.

That is not a defect in the renderer: the literal rendering is owner decision D-B (2026-08-30, kb/Work PB156 +
PB195) — "a FLOATING-POINT literal is its EXACT ISO §8.3.3.3.3 rule-5 value … carried on the Dec (SDIDI) lane".
The defect is that the golden was written BEFORE that decision, was never re-read after it, and still asserts a
mechanism it no longer exercises.

## Harm

1. **A CONFORMS row rests on it.** `AR-15.100.3-5` (§15.100.3 r5 — an omitted argument-3 windows on the
   execution year) names `conformance:2002/pb119_windowing_float_args` as its witness together with
   `unit:CobolDateWindowingTests.YearToYyyy_DefaultArg3_IsExecutionYear`, which calls the EXACT overload through
   the C# API. Both witnesses are therefore exact-lane; the row's note credits the golden with the float half.
2. **The RealArgs date lane may have NO strict-mode corpus witness at all.**
   `CobolIntrinsics.RealArgs.YearToYyyyReal`, `DateToYyyymmddReal` and `DayToYyyydddReal` are reachable only
   when an argument lands `Real`, and a floating-point *item* at any of those §15.3 type-6 positions is rejected
   at bind (COBOLNET1627 — kb/Work PB248), as is a float-bearing arithmetic expression. Measured 2026-09-09: both
   forms rejected at `--std 2002`. The lane is therefore reachable only under `--permissive`, which the corpus
   runner never uses.
3. **It is the `green_gates_arent_evidence` shape**: a passing check that proves nothing about what it claims to
   cover, and whose claim is written down where a reader will believe it.

## What was measured, and what is NOT wrong

The lane itself is CORRECT. Under `--permissive` at `--std 2002`, with the clock at 2026, over
`01 F USAGE COMP-2` holding 76: `FUNCTION YEAR-TO-YYYY(F)` = 2076, `(F 23)` = 1976, `(F 60 3000)` = 2976 — every
one the value derived from §15.100.3 r3/r5 + §15.100.4 r1/r2 before the run. kb/Work PB119's overload split holds.
So this is an EVIDENCE defect, not a behaviour defect, and no user program is wrong today.

## Fix shape

Two parts, and the second is the one that keeps it fixed.

1. Re-read `pb119_windowing_float_args`'s header against HEAD and either (a) make the probes actually reach the
   Real lane — which needs an operand that lands `Real` and survives the type-6 screen, so the honest answer may
   be that no CONFORMING source can, and the header should say so — or (b) restate the header to what the golden
   now measures and give the Real lane a witness of the kind it can have (a unit test on
   `CobolIntrinsics.RealArgs`, or a `--permissive` fixture if the corpus ever gains that mode).
2. Sweep for the siblings: every golden whose header asserts a LANE (`Real`, `Dec`, `alwaysDec`, "forces the
   float lane") rather than a VALUE is making a claim no `.out` comparison can check, and D-B moved the lane
   boundary under all of them at once. A drift test that reads those claims and asserts the lane is the shape
   that makes the next one automatic; a hand re-read is not.
