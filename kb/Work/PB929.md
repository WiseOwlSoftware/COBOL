## What it does and does not prove

⛔ It is deliberately NOT a gate, and the note says so where a reader will see it. A landed note NAMED in a
row's prose is not proof the row is stale: the note may have closed one arm of a multi-arm rule, or been
landed for a different reason, or the prose may name it as context. That asymmetry is exactly why the
ENFORCED back-link (`ClosesRowsBackLinkDriftTests`, landed in train 42) runs NOTE→ROW and not ROW→NOTE.

What the number IS: a 75-row SUSPECT LIST, each member cheap to settle, and a direct measurement of how
much of the campaign's headline GAP figure may be bookkeeping.

## Why ONE note and not seventy-five

The work is a GOLDEN ROUND, not seventy-five investigations: for each suspect row, build the witness the
row needs and re-verdict it on the witness, or record why the row is genuinely still open. That is one
input set, one implementer pattern, one batch of `record_verdicts` records. Filing seventy-five notes would
put seventy-five entries in front of `work.py next` that are all the same task —
`feedback_fill_by_rows_per_mechanism` in reverse.

`kb/Work/PB190` is ONE member of this population, found by hand weeks ago; that is the shape of the manual
alternative.

## The neighbour this rounds out

**PB902** (registrar #6) asks for a sweep of rows whose witnesses all vary ONE AXIS — rows that read
CONFORMS on evidence that never probed the shape in question. This note asks for the opposite population:
rows that read GAP on work that may already be done. Together they are the two ways the inventory can be
wrong about itself, and they want the same machinery.
