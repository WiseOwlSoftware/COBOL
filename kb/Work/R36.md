# R36 — adjudicate: REPLACE/COPY partial-word replacement (LEADING/TRAILING), including the GCOS/ACU BY-SPACES deletion form

> Found by the R21+R22+R30 battery's differential leg (2026-08-08): flip `syn_copy:686` ("REPLACE:
> partial replacement BY SPACE", AT_KEYWORDS "gcos acu"), AGREE_ACCEPT → WE_REJECT_THEY_ACCEPT —
> `REPLACE LEADING "PREFIX-" BY SPACES` should turn `PREFIX-VAR1` into `VAR1`; our REPLACE does not,
> so VAR1 draws COBOLNET1639 since R30 (compiled clean and was silently broken before).

TWO QUESTIONS, adjudicate both against §7.2.2's general format before any code: (1) does ISO's
partial-word replacement (the LEADING/TRAILING phrase) admit the shapes GnuCOBOL runs here, and do we
implement the ISO subset AT ALL — measure with a literal-BY-literal LEADING case; (2) the BY
SPACES/figurative DELETION form is a GCOS/ACU extension (GnuCOBOL gates it behind
-fpartial-replace-when-literal-src) — supporting it is a vendor-dialect decision on the
[[dialect_two_axes]] strictness axis, not a conformance gap. GnuCOBOL's own default rejects some of
these shapes.
