# R36 — adjudicate: REPLACE/COPY partial-word replacement (LEADING/TRAILING), including the GCOS/ACU BY-SPACES deletion form

> Found by the R21+R22+R30 battery's differential leg (2026-08-08): flip `syn_copy:686` ("REPLACE:
> partial replacement BY SPACE", AT_KEYWORDS "gcos acu"), AGREE_ACCEPT → WE_REJECT_THEY_ACCEPT —
> `REPLACE LEADING "PREFIX-" BY SPACES` should turn `PREFIX-VAR1` into `VAR1`; our REPLACE does not,
> so VAR1 draws COBOLNET1639 since R30 (compiled clean and was silently broken before).

TWO QUESTIONS, adjudicate both against §7.2.2's general format before any code: (1) does ISO's
partial-word replacement (the LEADING/TRAILING phrase) admit the shapes GnuCOBOL runs here, and do we
implement the ISO subset AT ALL — measure with a literal-BY-literal LEADING case; (2) the BY
SPACES/figurative DELETION form is a GCOS/ACU extension (GnuCOBOL gates it behind
-fpartial-replace-when-literal-src) — supporting it is a vendor-dialect decision on the
[[dialect_two_axes]] strictness axis, not a conformance gap. GnuCOBOL's own default rejects some of
these shapes.

## LANDED 2026-08-08 (adjudicated)

(1) **The ISO subset CONFORMS — measured, not assumed**: `REPLACE LEADING ==PRE-== BY ==NEW-==`,
`TRAILING ==-OLD== BY ==-NXT==`, and the empty-pseudo-text DELETION form all work end-to-end
(`ReplaceKind.Leading/Trailing` in the preprocessor; probed, and pinned by the new golden
`replace_partial_pseudo_text` in the 2002 corpus, which also exercises a second REPLACE statement
swapping the active set mid-source). (2) The syn_copy:686 shapes are the GCOS/ACU VENDOR spellings —
LITERAL partial-words and the `BY SPACES` figurative deletion — which no ISO edition ever admitted in
REPLACE (COPY's literal operands were what 2023 removed; REPLACE's operands were always pseudo-text).
Verdict: no conformance gap; the vendor semantics stay a [[dialect_two_axes]] vendor-axis candidate,
unimplemented by decision. **The probe found the adjacent real defect and it is [[R39]]**: the illegal
REPLACE statement itself is silently half-parsed with no diagnostic — the failure surfaces downstream as
a misleading COBOLNET1639. The differential's syn_copy:686 flip attributes here (vendor construct,
WE_REJECT divergence expected) with R39 owning the diagnostic-quality half.
