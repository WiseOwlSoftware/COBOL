# PB5948 - a link's target scrolls under the HTML listing's sticky navigation bar

**Sites:** `src/WiseOwl.Cobol.Listing/Html/HtmlListingRenderer.cs` (`Style`, `Script`)

**Found** by the owner (2026-11-19 19:00), viewing NC105A's page: clicking PROGRAMS or DATA MAP in the bar scrolled the heading
"one line or so too far", hidden by the bar.

**Measured** in Chrome on the page of origin/main 8dd07d2eab (NC105A, `--nist --listing-sections all --listing-format html`): after
each section link, the heading's top was at 0 px and the bar's bottom at 34 px, for all seven sections the page could scroll to.
The bar is `position: sticky; top: 0`, and the page reserved no room for it. A fixed `scroll-margin` would not be the root: the bar
wraps to more lines as the window narrows (112 px at a 420 px body).

**Fix:** `html { scroll-padding-top: var(--navh, 5em) }`, and the script sets `--navh` to the bar's rendered height plus 8 px on load
and through a `ResizeObserver` on the bar, so every link target (section heading, line, data item, diagnostic, method) lands below it
at any width; a page opened at a `#fragment` scrolls once the padding is set. Measured after: every heading at 42 px under a 34 px
bar; at a 420 px body, 120 px under a 112 px bar; line `L1` at 42 px. Witness:
`HtmlListingRendererTests.ALinkTarget_ScrollsBelowTheStickyNavigation`. DESIGN-listing.md section 7.2 says it.
