## 2026-09-02 — a CONCRETE instance, handed here by fix-lane cluster D (kb/Work PB153)

This analysis asks which pre-2023 facts are derivable from the sole in-repo 2023 document. Here is one that
is **not**, blocking exactly one thing:

> **What edition introduced `USAGE POINTER TO type-name-1` — the RESTRICTED data-pointer (ISO §13.18.60.2
> general format; §13.18.60.4 GR23; narrated in Annex D.9.2.2)?**

**What is settled.** The grammar and the semantics are LIVE as of PB153 — `dataPointerUsage` parses the `TO`
tail, `PicInfo.RestrictedTypeName` carries the restriction, and the consumption screens are enforced
(§14.9.3.3 SR4/SR5 on ALLOCATE, §14.9.39.3 SR19/SR20 on SET ADDRESS OF in BOTH arms, §14.8.2.3.2 on a nested
CALL). The `constructs.json` row `usage-pointer-to-type-2014` also had its WITNESS repaired: the previous
source was DOUBLY nonconforming and had never been compiled, so nothing had contradicted it.

**What is not, and why the row stays `pending`.** `introducedIn: 2014` was self-labelled "provisional" and
the row has never been exercised. Annex E (the 2014→2023 substantive-changes list) carries no pointer entry,
which RULES OUT 2023; the feature's dependencies — STRONG TYPEDEF (§8.5.3), USAGE POINTER, BASED, ALLOCATE —
are all COBOL-2002 in this registry. So the answer is **2002 or 2014**, and the 2023 document cannot decide
between them. Activating the row installs an edition GATE, so a guessed number would not stay a guess: it
would become the compiler's answer, enforced.

**No conformance hole is held open by the wait** (measured): a restricted-pointer program already draws
`COBOLNET0900` below 2002 through the existing `usage-pointer-2002` and TYPEDEF gates.

**What would settle it**, in the order this note's own thesis prefers:

1. the COBOL-2002 (ISO/IEC 1989:2002) and COBOL-2014 (ISO/IEC 1989:2014) texts — the direct authority, and
   the acquisition this analysis exists to argue for;
2. failing that, the surveyed model implementations per the standing owner-decision protocol
   (feedback_owner_decision_protocol / feedback_survey_compilers_on_latitude) — which edition each of the
   other compilers gates the restricted form at, recorded with its evidence.

Until one of those is done the row is `pending`, and that is a DECISION not to guess, not an oversight.
