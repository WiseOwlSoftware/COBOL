# PB4614 — the test suites' architecture, reviewed in depth

**The direction.** The owner (2026-11-04) asked for a deep architectural design of the test suites "at some point",
naming three questions: is a case keyed to an ISO section reference, or to each alternative of a statement's general
format; how is proper error reporting tested; and, from the goal restated the same day (PB4613), how the COBOL-85 suite
comes to subsume and replace NIST-85 and each later edition gets an equivalent best-in-class suite (85, 2002, 2014, 2023,
and 2027 when released).

**What exists.** `docs/rearchitecture/DESIGN-conformance-suite.md` (PB3160) was approved by the owner on 2026-10-14
(revision 2). It already decides: one case is one program witnessing one rule (or a few tightly related ones), keyed by
inventory row ids (`SR-`, `GR-`, `FMT-` rows derived from the ISO text); a metadata header with `accept` editions;
must-diagnose cases as `flag: {kind, at}` with one marked line and a control program, because the standard requires a
compiler only to flag, never to refuse (ISO 4.2.2); run-time violations observed through EC- names from 2002 on;
WiseOwl's own refusals kept in `wiseowl/diagnostics/`. Its implementation is the slices of its section 13.

**What this review decides** (questions the approved design leaves open or answers only implicitly):
1. Coverage unit: one case per rule row, or per alternative of each general format (every choice indicator, optional
   phrase and repetition), or both; how a combinatorial format is covered without an explosion; how the inventory
   expresses it.
2. Diagnostics: is the section 3.2 shape (flag, marked line, control) enough for every kind (syntax, obsolete, archaic,
   nonstandard, processor-dependent), at every edition, including the 85 edge where the warning mechanism differs; how
   WiseOwl's own codes and messages are pinned without making the portable suite WiseOwl-specific.
3. Completeness: a measured map from NIST-85's modules and tests to suite cases for COBOL-85, and an equivalent
   per-edition coverage measure (rows witnessed per edition, alternatives witnessed per format), reported by the burn-down.
4. Editions: how a case is placed per edition when the published 2014 text is not held (PB4608: the 2014 FCD with its
   limits), and how 2027 enters when it is published.
5. Derivation and intake. The suite is derived SYSTEMATICALLY from the ISO text (owner 2026-11-04: "fuzz tests are only
   one possible input. Fuzz generation is random and we want a comprehensive ISO spec test suite"): the rule catalog and
   traceability inventory enumerate what must be witnessed, and the design says how each row (and each format
   alternative) gets its case by construction, so coverage is complete by derivation, not by chance. Fuzz programs,
   vetting repros and report repros (PB4613) are supplementary inputs that fill or confirm cases under these rules,
   never the plan.

**Deliverable.** A revision 3 of DESIGN-conformance-suite.md (the one canonical doc; no new design doc), reviewed
adversarially and approved by the owner, with its implementation slices as kb/Work notes. PB4613's golden campaign waits
for it; until then programs are preserved, not converted.
