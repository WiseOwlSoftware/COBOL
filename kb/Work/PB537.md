## Mechanism — sign-clause
sign-sr1-sr2-placement-unscreened

### Expected, from the standard
SR1: 'The SIGN clause may be specified only for: - a numeric data or screen description entry whose picture character-string contains the symbol S - a numeric report group description entry whose picture character-string contains the symbol S - an alphanumeric group item, national group item, or strongly-typed group item.' SR2: 'The usage of an elementary item for which the SIGN clause is specified shall be display or national.' Each of the following shall be diagnosed: 01 A PIC 9(3) SIGN IS LEADING. (no S); 01 A PIC X(3) SIGN IS LEADING. (an alphanumeric ELEMENTARY item -- the third bullet admits an alphanumeric GROUP item); 01 A PIC N(3) SIGN IS TRAILING. (a national elementary item); 01 A PIC ZZ9 SIGN IS LEADING. (numeric-edited, no S); 01 G GROUP-USAGE IS BIT SIGN IS LEADING. 05 A PIC 1(4). (a bit group item is none of SR1's three group kinds); and, for SR2, 01 A PIC S9(4) USAGE COMP SIGN IS LEADING SEPARATE., the COMP-3 and COMP-5 twins, and 01 A USAGE INDEX SIGN IS LEADING.

### Observed
Every one of those nine shapes compiles clean, with no diagnostic, at --std 85, 2002, 2014 and 2023. There is no usage screen and no category/group-kind screen anywhere: DataBinder.cs:2378 assigns `ownSign` straight from the parse tree, and the two checks that DO exist were both written for other reasons -- the float-edited one is the single SR1 shape anyone has implemented, and the LOCALE one enforces §13.16.3 SR19. Grep of src/Cobol.Net.Editions/Diagnostics/DiagnosticCatalog.cs finds no descriptor for either rule. The harm is UNDER-REJECTS and that was VERIFIED rather than assumed to be benign: PicInfo.SignKindFor (PicInfo.cs:593-599) is consulted only for a signed NUMERIC subject and returns BinaryMinus before it inspects the SignSpec for any non-DISPLAY usage, so each illegally-placed clause is inert -- the COMP item is byte-for-byte the same with and without it (BYTE-LENGTH 2, image -0012), the PIC X(3) and PIC ZZ9 items are unchanged. The shape to fix is ONE screen at the point of capture (the subject's category, whether it is a group and of which kind, and its usage), not another special case beside the float-edited one; the report-group arm at DataBinder.Reports.cs:428 needs the same screen from the same rule and today has none at all.
