# PB3369 — a standing owner rule is bound to one campaign instead of to the work it governs

Found by the operator (account 2), 2026-10-18 02:25 PDT, while it checked wave 1117's specs against the owner's
requirement. Grepped `kb/Work` for `exception-path`, `abnormal`, `Standing requirements` and `outlets design`: PB3359
(landed) is the only note on the requirement, and it scopes the binding to campaign membership.

**The requirement.** The owner, 2026-10-17 ~22:25 PDT, verbatim: "Note: out test cases for sort and file I/o must cover
both the success and abnormal exception cases. Therefore they also validate outlets design." It is general: it covers
every SORT, MERGE and file I/O test, not the tests of one campaign.

**What PB3359 built (PR #223).**
- `plan_wave.group_requirements` reads the bullets of a campaign lead note's `## Standing requirements` section.
- It binds them to every group whose notes name that lead in `cluster:`.
- They are written into the spec, groups.json and the args, and the branch reviewer raises a missing one as a finding.
- Two lead notes carry the section, each with its own copy of the owner's text:
  - `kb/Work/PB3353.md`, on main;
  - `kb/Work/PB3330.md`, added by hand at line 135 by the PB3330 finisher's `5ee56d4c3` (2026-10-18 03:26). It is on
    `design-pb3330-keyed-storage-20261017` and not yet on main.

**The gap.** A group is bound only if one of its notes is in a cluster whose lead carries the section. So:
1. **The binding needs a hand copy per campaign, and the copies drift.** The keyed-store engine's notes (PB3331-PB3338,
   PB3340; `cluster: ["PB3330"]`, areas `runtime/io` and `compiler/validation`) are bound only because someone copied
   the owner's text into PB3330 by hand, an hour after this note's first draft said they were not. The register will
   then hold the same owner sentence in two places, and a later amendment by the owner has to find both. This is the
   hand-maintained copy rule 5 forbids: each new campaign needs someone to remember to copy it, and each copy can go
   stale on its own.
2. A SORT or file I/O defect filed after the campaign, in no cluster, is planned by an ordinary fix-lane wave
   without it.
3. A hand-dispatched finisher on a SORT or file I/O branch that is no campaign's member gets nothing either.

**Repro.**
- Take a group whose note is `area: runtime/io` with `cluster: []`.
  `plan_wave.group_requirements` (`scripts/orchestrator/plan_wave.py`, from line 1196) collects leads only from the
  notes' `cluster:` lists and keeps a lead only when its note has the section. It returns `{}` for that group, so
  nothing is written into its spec or handed to its reviewer. This was read from the code, not run.
- `git grep -n "out test cases for sort" -- kb/Work`, on main once the design branch lands, finds the owner's sentence
  in both PB3353.md and PB3330.md: two homes for one rule.
- Wave 1117 (planned at 02:13 on 2026-10-18, minutes before PR #223 landed) shows the before state: none of its eight
  specs carried the rule, and the operator appended it by hand to the two whose agents had not started.

**The bar for the fix.**
- The rule binds by what the group CHANGES or what its notes are ABOUT, computed, never by a list someone maintains.
  For example, the group's `file_set` touches the runtime's I/O, sharing, locking or SORT code, the SORT/MERGE and
  file I/O statement binders and emitters, or the I/O conformance cases. Or the note's `area` falls in the I/O and
  SORT family; the register's areas are not normalized today (`io`, `runtime/io`, `binder/sort`, `codegen/sort`,
  `binding/io`, `binder/file-io` and more), so an area rule needs a normalization or a drift test of its own.
- The requirement text lives in ONE place (a standing owner rule, not a campaign's copy). The fix moves the owner's
  sentence out of BOTH existing copies (PB3353.md, and PB3330.md once the design branch has landed) into that home,
  and deletes it from the leads, in the same change (rule 4: no second copy). Campaign leads may still add
  campaign-specific bullets on top, such as PB3330's engine cases.
- A drift test pins the binding: every open note in the I/O or SORT family resolves to the requirement, and a group
  planned from one carries it.
- The finisher and the reviewer of such a branch get it on the same path, whoever dispatches them.
