# PB3781 — the doctrine files restate the practices file and one of them states a retired shape

**Sites:** `CLAUDE.md` (the "Testing" paragraph, rule 9, "Where things live"), `PROMPT.md` (§5 "Token-frugal, restart-safe workstreams"), `.claude/skills/workstream/templates/MANDATORY-PRACTICES.md` (nothing moves out of it; it is the home the pointers name), `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/ArchReviewFleetDriftTests.cs` (`ArchReviewFleetDriftTests`, the doctrine drift assertion)

## Measured

CLAUDE.md is 14,521 chars of every dispatched agent's turn-1 baseline (3 of 27 implementers also read it again). Its
"Testing" paragraph (2.6K chars) is MANDATORY-PRACTICES I1, I2, I8, L1 and L2 in a fourth wording (the overlay's §2
gate bullet, the agent definition and the spec's GATE paragraph are the others); rule 9's second half is O9; "Where
things live" repeats the session-start skill's §1-§2. PROMPT.md §5 says "keep to the concurrency budget (1 lander + ≤3
implementers + one 4-wide read-only chunk), land finished work first with one lander on main at a time": the lander
retired with the train (kb/Work PB3023), the budget is 6 implementers (overlay §2), and main takes only the merge
queue's pull requests. A doc that describes a shape the compiler's process no longer has breaks rule 6 on its own.

Saving: about 4.5K chars of CLAUDE.md, about 1.1K tokens a turn across every agent and main thread: **about $20 a
week** (2 % of the baseline, at the threshold). The correction of PROMPT.md §5 is owed regardless.

## Fix

CLAUDE.md's Testing paragraph becomes three lines: the one gate command, the landing script, and the pointer to the
rows (I1, I2, I8, L1, L2) in the practices file; rule 9 keeps the owner's words and drops the restated hook
description (O9 holds it); "Where things live" keeps the one-line map and drops the paragraph that session-start
owns. PROMPT.md §5 states the current shape in one sentence and points at the overlay for the numbers (so no number
lives in PROMPT.md to go stale: the same rule PB3701 applied to turn caps). The owner's rule text (the nine rules'
own words) is not touched; only the restated process mechanics are.

Guard: `ArchReviewFleetDriftTests` asserts that PROMPT.md and CLAUDE.md name no concurrency number, lander or train
(the retired words), the way `LandingPipelineDriftTests` keeps the landing's retired steps out.

## Acceptance

CLAUDE.md under about 10K chars with the nine rules intact; PROMPT.md §5 names no retired mechanism; the drift test is
green and goes red on the word "lander" in either file; a dispatched agent's `api-request-shape` shows the smaller
CLAUDE.md block.
