## Landed 2026-10-04 (wave-1011 implementer B2)

**Re-probed on the wave's own build** (`p/c19`, `c33`, `c23`, `ctype`, `ct1`..): `OCCURS 1 TO 3 DEPENDING ON N` and `OCCURS DYNAMIC FROM 2`
under a CONSTANT RECORD compiled and ran before the change (rc 0, `RAN`); the STEP spelling (`OCCURS 1 TO 5 DEPENDING ON N STEP 1`) did too.

**Mechanism: a TWO-ARM dispatch, found by the sweep.** The note's finding was one missing OCCURS arm in the attach-time screen of
`BindEntries`. That screen asks the entries the programmer WROTE; an item a `TYPE` / `SAME AS` clause brings into the record
(`01 T TYPEDEF. 05 X PIC 9 BLANK WHEN ZERO. 01 C CONSTANT RECORD. 05 Y TYPE T.`) was never asked, for ANY of the
subordinate rules (§13.16.3 SR13, §13.18.40.3 SR32), and the SR13 list covered five of the rule's eight clauses. Adding OCCURS to
the attach-time list would have fixed the note and left the clone arm open for all three rules. The rules are about an item's POSITION,
so the root fix moves them into ONE post-expansion pass: `CheckConstantRecordSubtrees` (`DataBinder.ConstantRecord.cs`, a
`BindPipeline` pass immediately after `ExpandTypes`, over `CompositionForest()`), which asks every item under a CONSTANT RECORD: SR13's
excluded clauses (the five flags plus DYNAMIC LENGTH — select-when and the validation clauses are declined facilities, refused
where written), SR32's format 2 PICTURE, SR19/SR23 (an OccursSpec with DEPENDING ON, any format spelling) and SR33
(`OccursSpec.IsDynamic`, the CONSTANT RECORD item itself included). The attach-time blocks are deleted, not kept; the same-entry half of
SR13 stays in `BindEntry` where the written clause set exists (PB487). A clone's error is positioned at the TYPEDEF template's entry
(the item's `DeclaredAt`), which names the offending item.

**Citations** (all `cite.py --check` OK): 13.18.38.3 SR19, SR23, SR33; 13.16.3 SR13; 13.18.40.3 SR32.

**Sibling sweep.** The other positional CONSTANT RECORD rules: SR1 (section) is not positional-subtree and stays at attach (it reads the
root); §13.18.63.3 SR25 (format 3 VALUE, COBOLNET2406) and the receiving-operand bar (§13.18.15.3 SR2, COBOLNET1548) are separate
mechanisms with their own sites. FOUND, NOT MINE: `05 T PIC X VALUE "A" OCCURS 1 TO 5 TIMES DEPENDING ON N STEP 1.` (a VALUE clause

(see the report's leads).
