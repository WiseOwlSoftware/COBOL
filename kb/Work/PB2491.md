# PB2491 — OPEN

Filed 2026-10-07 by the train-1034 lander from the wave-1034 group F implementer report (`w1034f-PB1071-report.md`,
lead 2).

**Shape.** PB1071 (landed in train 1034) replaced the space placeholders a pointer leaf occupied in a byte image with
its `PointerImage` (NULL = eight X"00"); the two `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Conformance/TierCRejectionTests.cs` methods
named above now assert the image but keep their old names, because the names are `conformance-test:` witnesses in
`tests/version-matrix/traceability-inventory.json` (rows citing `TierCRejectionTests.DisplayPointerGroup_RendersPlaceholderPositions`,
e.g. the §14.9.11 DISPLAY rows) and `audit_witness_loss.py` turns a dropped witness red.

**Correction to the report.** The implementer wrote that a rename "needs an inventory retirement mechanism". One exists:
a `record_verdicts.py` batch statement with `retire-witnesses: <ref>` and `retire-reason:` records
`retired-witness: <ref> (<reason>)` in the row's notes, which `audit_witness_loss.py` excuses. Fix shape: rename both
methods (e.g. `..._RendersPointerImage`, `..._SendsPointerImage`), and in one batch add the new refs and retire the old
ones on every row that cites them.
