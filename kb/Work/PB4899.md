# PB4899 - page 602 measured, not counted by eye

**What the reports say.** The PB4816 finisher's review lists as a standing lead: "Report Writer page 602 measured, not counted
by eye." PB4848 (landed) records: "Report Writer (page 602) was run and not counted by eye".

**What the lead asks, nothing more.** Count page 602's brackets, braces, underlines and boxes by eye against
`scripts/render-spec-page.py --edition 1985`, as the other twelve calibration pages were, and put the counts beside
PB4848's calibration table. The clerk did not do the count.

**Sites:** `scripts/spec/figure_raster_pages/` (new crop `p0602.png`; `calibration.json`, its page 602 entry),
`scripts/spec/figure_raster.py` (the module docstring's calibration paragraph), `scripts/spec/test_figure_raster.py`
(`main`, section 9's comment), `kb/Work/PB4848.md` (the calibration table). No measurer code changes; a miscount it
finds becomes its own note.

**Landed (2026-11-06, wave 20261891).** Page 602 (Report Writer, 3.9.2 the report group description entry, both
formats) rendered with `scripts/render-spec-page.py --edition 1985` and counted by eye over the band 200 to 670 pt:
brackets 3/3 (the two LINE NUMBER clauses and NEXT GROUP; the typed `[ ]` glyphs are text), braces 13/13 (the 14-line
TYPE IS brace, its nine two-line inner braces, the two LINE NUMBER braces and the three-line NEXT GROUP brace), boxes 0,
underlines 41. The measurer agrees on brackets, braces and boxes and leaves no shape unclassified. It reports 42
underlines: the extra one is a 9.0 pt run along the fused foot serifs of "am" in "data-name-3" (y 437.3 pt, x 318 to
327 pt), which `split_fused_rules` takes for an underline fused to its word (the run is exactly `FUSED_MIN_RUN` long).
The crop, stamp margins excluded, is `figure_raster_pages/p0602.png`; `calibration.json` asserts its brackets, braces and
boxes and, because of that one false rule, no underline count. The false rule is a measurer defect of its own, handed
to the orchestrator as a lead for a note.
