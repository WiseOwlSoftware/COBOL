# PB1244 — LANDED

## Landed (w66j, 2026-09-27 12:35 PDT)

Re-probed on the w66j worktree build (base `1dd1b63a7`): gip1 printed `'         DB'` for DB's first GENERATE, gip2 blanked line 2's `BBBBB`, gip5 was refused COBOLNET0899 — all three reproduced. ONE root: GROUP INDICATE was modelled as a report-wide freshness flag plus a per-group column mask blanked after composing the line, where §13.18.28.4 GR1 (`cite.py --check 13.18.28.4 "true only on the first occasion that a GENERATE is issued for the current detail group"` → OK 1)) makes it "the same effect as a PRESENT WHEN clause" on the ITEM with a PER-DETAIL-GROUP condition.

- **Runtime** (`src/Cobol.Net.Runtime/IO/ReportWriter.cs`): `ReportGroup.GroupIndicatePending` per detail group; `ArmGroupIndicate` sets it on every detail at INITIATE (a), `PageFeed` (b) and a detected control break (c); `GenerateCore` clears the GENERATEd detail's flag AFTER its presentation (so the group's own page-fit advance re-arms first; a SUPPRESSed or all-absent presentation still consumes it — the GENERATE was issued). `PresentBody` publishes it as `CobolReport.GroupIndicatePresent`. `_indicateFresh`, `IndicateFields` and the post-compose blanking in `PresentLine` are deleted (and `PresentLine`'s now-dead group parameter).
- **Emitter** (`ReportWriterEmitter.EmitFieldPlacements`): the indicated item's presence test ANDs `__RPT_n.GroupIndicatePresent` beside its PRESENT WHEN chain and OCCURS DEPENDING guards — the three suppressors §13.18.63.4 GR22 names. A non-presented item is ABSENT (§13.18.41.4 GR2b): no column, no horizontal-counter move — so the relative-COLUMN case is automatic and `report-indicate-relative-column` (COBOLNET0899) is retired.
- **Golden** `tests/conformance/2002/pb1244_group_indicate_per_detail_group` (two detail groups, a two-line group, relative COLUMN before and after an indicated item, all three events), expected output derived line by line in its header.
- ⚠ The gip5 expectation above (`'BBBBB  AAAAA'`, two spaces) was wrong: §13.18.14.4 GR8 places a PLUS 2 item at horizontal counter + 2 (NOTE 2: "integer-2 minus 1 is the number of blank columns"), so the line is `'BBBBB AAAAA'` — which is what the fixed build prints.
