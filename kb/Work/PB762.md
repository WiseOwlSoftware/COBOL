central id allocation. **Measured here across all 4348 rows of
`tests/version-matrix/traceability-inventory.json` against main `3d75a747`** — the count is confirmed at seven,
by a different route than the report took, and the internal shape of those seven is not what the report
describes.

## ⛔ Premise correction — PB338's batch DID re-record five of them, and that is the more interesting defect

The report reads as though the seven were simply missed. Measured, they split two ways:

| rows | state |
|---|---|
| `GR-14.9.30.4-7`, `-8`, `-9`, `-10`, `-12` | **carry a dated erratum** appended to `notes`: *"\|\| 2026-09-06 (kb/Work PB338): code-location symbols renamed by the peek-then-commit landing - ReadLockGovern became ReadKeyedShared (it now OWNS the Format-2 physical retrieval) and ApplyReadLockDiscipline became ApplyPostReadLockActions"* |
| `GR-14.9.30.4-22`, `FMT-14.9.30.2` | **no correction of any kind** |

So PB338 **did** re-record five rows' `notes` — it just **appended an erratum instead of rewriting the prose it
corrects**, leaving each of those five self-contradicting: the body still narrates the dead symbol in the present
tense, and a tail paragraph says it is gone. Read in order, `GR-14.9.30.4-9` tells a reader that
*"ConflictOnLockedRecord … is reached from ReadShared (Format 1 …) and from **ReadLockGovern** (Format 2)"* and
then, further down, that ReadLockGovern no longer exists. `GR-14.9.30.4-12` says *"Keyed: **ReadLockGovern**
wraps only the conflict block"*; `GR-14.9.30.4-22` says the Format-2 random read *"keeps the post-read
**ReadLockGovern**, which is correct for it"* — a present-tense claim about a method that is not there, with no
erratum after it at all.

⭐ `GR-14.9.30.4-10` is the exception that proves the point: its prose was actually **rewritten** —
*"The Format-2 post-read patch ReadLockGovern is GONE, replaced by FileRegistry…"* — and reads correctly. It
names a dead symbol and is not defective, which is precisely why the fix cannot be "no dead symbol may appear in
`notes`" (see below).

## Why nothing is red, stated as the architecture rather than as luck

`code-location` resolves on all seven — they point at `LocksEffective`, `OpenShared`,
`ApplyPostReadLockActions`, `ReadShared`, `ReadKeyedShared`, `ConflictOnLockedRecord`,
`FileConnector.cs#Peek…`, `KeyedIoEmitter.cs#EmitRead`, `SequentialIoEmitter.cs#EmitReadSharedCall` — every one
of which exists. And that is the whole explanation:

- `tests/Cobol.Net.Tests.Unit/SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests.cs:20-21` states its own contract:
  *"`state` is RECOMPUTED here rather than believed, a **`code-location` must name a file and a symbol that
  still exist**, and a `test-ref` must resolve to a test that is really on disk."* Three fields checked;
  `notes` is not among them.
- `scripts/spec/record_verdicts.py` validates **shape only**, and says so at length, deliberately: *"Whether a
  `code-location` symbol still exists … is checked by the C# battery gate `SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests`
  — deliberately NOT here … implementing it in both places would be exactly the duplication CLAUDE.md rule 5
  forbids."*

So the referential predicate is correctly sited and **correctly scoped to two fields out of four**. The forensic
prose — the field a human actually reads when asking *why does this row have this verdict* — has never been in
its domain. `feedback_green_gates_arent_evidence`: the gate is green because it never looked at what changed.

## Why the harm is real even though it is `process_only`

Nothing a user compiles is affected, and the flag says so. What is affected is the artifact the P14 burn-down is
FOR: a CONFORMS row is a claim that a rule was located in the code, and the `notes` are the evidence a later
reader re-checks that claim against. Seven rows now hand that reader a method to go look at that is not there —
in the READ statement's lock-governance family, which is the most re-adjudicated corner of the inventory
([[PB338]], [[PB346]], [[PB713]], [[PB739]], [[PB753]] all live in it). The failure mode is a future adjudicator
concluding the row is wrong, or worse, re-deriving a mechanism from a dead symbol's name.

## The fix shape

⛔ **Not a hand edit of `traceability-inventory.json`.** It is a generated, all-or-nothing register; the only
supported write is a batch (`feedback_verdict_recording_forms` — and check the GAP delta, since
`record_verdicts.py` drops bad rows PER RECORD).

1. **A `record_verdicts.py` batch re-recording the seven rows' `notes`** — rewriting the body prose into the
   present tense of the current code (`ReadKeyedShared` OWNS the Format-2 physical retrieval;
   `ApplyPostReadLockActions` is the post-read discipline), and **folding the appended PB338 erratum into it**
   rather than leaving a correction stapled to the text it corrects. Verdicts and `code-location` unchanged, so
   the GAP must not move: **assert that**, do not assume it.
2. ⭐ **The drift assertion — and its shape is the whole design question.** A naive *"every symbol named in
   `notes` exists in `src/`"* rule **fails on `GR-14.9.30.4-10`, which is correct**: a note legitimately
   narrates history, and *"the post-read patch ReadLockGovern is GONE"* is a true and useful sentence. That is
   the trap, and it is why the report's own suggestion ("a `notes`-scoped symbol check") cannot be implemented
   as stated. Two shapes that survive it:
   - **(a) Scope the check to the `code-location` SPELLING inside `notes`.** Rows quote locations in the
     `File.cs#Symbol` form; require only *those* to resolve, and let bare prose mention anything. Mechanical,
     narrow, and it catches the real regression — a note that re-states a location that has moved.
   - **(b) Make the batch carry the obligation.** When a record changes `code-location`, require it to also
     supply `notes` — enforceable in `record_verdicts.py` as a SHAPE rule about the record, which is exactly
     what that script's stated contract admits (it is decidable from the record alone; no tree resolution).
   ⭐ **(a) and (b) are complements, not alternatives**: (b) stops the next divergence at the source, (a) catches
   the ones already recorded and the ones a future rename creates under a row nobody is editing. Implement both;
   they live in the two places the architecture already assigns.
   ⛔ **If a reviewer concludes neither is affordable, the outcome is a documented reason in
   `SpecTraceabilityInventoryDriftTests`'s own doc comment beside the three-field contract it already states** —
   not silence. An unstated exclusion is how this one lasted.
3. ⛔ **Prove it fails once** (`feedback_green_gates_arent_evidence`): point a row's `notes` at a symbol that does
   not exist, watch the new assertion go red, put it back. A drift test that has never failed is a hypothesis.

## Sweep

- **The family, not the case.** This measurement covered eight lock/sharing symbols because that is where the
  report found it. The general shape — *a landing renames a symbol, re-points `code-location` because a gate
  makes it, and leaves `notes` alone because none does* — is not specific to I-O, and the sweep that shape asks
  for is over **every** `File.cs#Symbol`-shaped string in every row's `notes`, across all 4348 rows. That sweep
  is fix shape (a) above, which is why (a) is preferred: it performs the sweep and then keeps performing it.
- **[[PB338]] is landed and its own note body also narrates the old symbols** — correctly, because a `kb/Work/`
  note is HISTORY once landed (`feedback_docs_current_state_only` exempts nothing else, and DEVLOG/landed notes
  are the exception by design). Do not sweep the register for these names; the inventory is the artifact that
  claims to describe the CURRENT compiler.
- **Not this note:** [[PB720]] (`work.py check` never reads `inventory_rows`, and `load()` returns the ids still
  quoted) is the mirror-image hole one register over — a field a checker does not read. Same shape, different
  file, and worth landing near this one.

⚠ **Premise correction to the source report.** `PB353-report.md` §(B) says that after PB353

**What it changes here:** nothing about the fix VEHICLE — it is the same `record_verdicts.py` batch this
